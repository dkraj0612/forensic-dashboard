> ACME Solar Holdings Limited has announced the commissioning of an additional 52.32 MW / 209.28 MWh Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) component for its FDRE Project in Rajasthan, with a Commercial Operation Date (COD) set for November 19, 2026. commissioned capacity of 247.67 MW / 990.68 MWh (Discharge Capacity) of the
