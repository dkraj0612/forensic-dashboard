Sub: ACME Solar Holdings Limited wins 300 MW capacity in SECI’s 6000 
MWh (1500MW X 4Hrs) Assured Peak Power Tender (FDRE-IX)  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
ACME Solar Holdings Limited (ACME) has been emerged as a successful bidder in 
SECI’s e-reverse auction for the “Selection of RE Power Developers for assured 
Peak Supply of 6000 MWh (1500 MW x 4 Hrs.) from ISTS-Connected RE Projects 
in India, under Tariff-Based Competitive Bidding (SECI-FDRE-IX).”  
Under this tender, the company secured 300 MW of contracted capacity at a 
discovered tariff of Rs. 6.00/kWh and will supply 1,200 MWh (300 MW X 4) supply, 
during four-hour duration.  
ACME already has the requisite connectivity grant and has secured a substantial 
portion of the land required for the project. This development readiness is 
expected to significantly shorten the project execution timeline and facilitate 
timely commissioning. 
Upon execution of the PPA, the project will add 1.2 GWh to ACME’s battery energy 
storage portfolio and increase the company’s PPA-signed BESS pipeline to 
approximately 15 GWh. 
 
You are requested to take the same on your record. 
 
Thanking you, 
 
For ACME Solar Holdings Limited
