Mr. Nikhil Dhingra is a seasoned business leader with over 21 
years of multi-sectoral experience across infrastructure, 
renewable energy, investment banking, consulting, and 
technology. An alumnus of NIT Kurukshetra (Electrical 
Engineering) and IIM Bangalore, his distinguished career 
includes leadership roles as CEO of Oriental Tollways and ACME 
Solar Holdings, alongside key stints at ICICI Securities, PwC, 
Infosys, and SAP Labs.  
 
Under his stewardship since February 2023, the Company 
successfully raised INR 2,900 crore via IPO and INR 2,800 crore 
via QIP, scaling its standalone net worth to INR 4,951.59 crore 
alongside a credit rating upgrade to AA-/Stable. Driven by 52% 
EBITDA CAGR since FY24 (reaching INR 2,265 crore in FY26), 
he has steered the Company’s transformation into an integrated 
renewable energy platform, expanding operational capacity to 
2,990 MW and battery storage capacities to ~5GWh, securing a 
robust ~ 8.4 GW contracted portfolio. The Company continues 
to benefit from his extensive strategic vision and execution 
capabilities as it scales its footprint in the clean energy sector.
