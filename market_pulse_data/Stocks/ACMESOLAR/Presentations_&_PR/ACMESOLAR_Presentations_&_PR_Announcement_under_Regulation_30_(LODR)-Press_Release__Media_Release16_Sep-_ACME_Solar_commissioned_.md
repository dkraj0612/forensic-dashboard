ACME Solar commissions 66.68 MW Solar Capacity and 300 
MWh BESS Capacity in Rajasthan 
 
Gurugram – September 16, 2026: ACME Solar Holdings Limited (ACME 
Solar), through its subsidiaries has commissioned 66.68 MW of Solar and 300 MWh 
of Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) capacities in Bikaner, Rajasthan, 
expanding its operational portfolio. 
 
Commissioning of Phase-I of ACME Solar’s first hybrid project 
 
ACME Solar, through its subsidiary, ACME Renewtech Private Limited, has 
commissioned Phase-I, comprising 66.68 MW solar out of the total 300 MW Solar 
capacity. The solar capacity is located at Kelan Village in Bikaner, Rajasthan.  The 
capacity forms part of ACME Solar’s first hybrid renewable energy project, 
combining 300 MW of solar capacity with 100 MW of wind capacity. The Power 
Purchase Agreement (PPA) for the project has been signed with NTPC Limited and 
long-term project financing with PFC. ACME Solar expects to commission  entire 
300 MW solar capacity by the end of Q3 FY27. Following the commissioning of this 
project, the renewable energy operational capacity of ACME Solar is now 
approximately 3,057 MW. 
 
Commissioning of Phase-I of BESS capacity 
 
Separately, ACME Solar, through its subsidiary ACME Greentech Seventh Private 
Limited, has commissioned Phase-I of the Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) 
component of its FDRE project located at the same site. Phase-I comprises 53.63 MW 
/ 300 MWh of BESS capacity out of the project’s total 300 MW / 1581 MWh BESS 
capacity. The project marks ACME Solar’s first assured off-peak power supply 
project for which the Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) for the project has been 
signed with SJVN Limited and long term project finance is with REC. ACME Solar 
expects to commission  entire BESS capacity for this project by Q3 of the current 
fiscal.  
 
With this commissioning milestone, ACME Solar has crossed 4 GWh milestone in 
operational BESS capacity with total operational capacity of 4.16 GWh.
