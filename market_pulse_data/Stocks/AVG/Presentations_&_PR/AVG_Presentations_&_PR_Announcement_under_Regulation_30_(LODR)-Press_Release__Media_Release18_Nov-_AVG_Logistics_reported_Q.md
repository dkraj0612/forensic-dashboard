AVG Logistics Limited 
Q2 FY27 & H1 FY27: AVG Logistics Delivers Rs. 151 Cr Revenue 
Standalone PBT up 58.06% YoY to ₹10.29 Cr; PAT grew 45.02% YoY to ₹7.28 Cr 
New Delhi, 18th November 2026: AVG Logistics Limited (BSE – 543910, NSE – AVG), a leading multimodal logistics solutions 
provider, has announced its unaudited financial results for Q2 FY27 & H1FY27. 
Key Highlights — Q2 FY27 & H1 FY27 
• Strong Profit Growth: Revenue from operations grew 6.23% YoY to ₹151.41 Cr (Q2 FY26: ₹142.53 Cr). PBT rose 58.06% 
YoY to ₹10.29 Cr (Q2 FY26: ₹6.51 Cr), while PAT increased 45.02% YoY to ₹7.28 Cr (Q2 FY26: ₹5.02 Cr). 
• Margin Expansion: PBT margin improved by 222 Bps to 6.79% (Q2 FY26: 4.57%), and PAT margin improved by 129 Bps 
to 4.81% (Q2 FY26: 3.52%). 
• Deployment of 68 Vehicles for Haldiram, Nagpur: The Company has successfully deployed 68 dedicated vehicles for 
Haldiram, Nagpur, which deals in sweets, namkeen and other products. Under this contract, AVG is providing logistics 
services to FMCG customers across the Western and Southern regions, as well as key eastern states including Odisha, 
Bihar and Jharkhand. 
• Deployment of 30 EV Heavy Duty Vehicles for Dalmia Cement: The Company has successfully deployed 30 dedicated 
EV heavy duty vehicles with Dalmia Cement, a leading player in the cement industry. Under this contract, AVG is 
providing logistics services for the transportation of cement across various plant locations, using its EV heavy duty fleet 
to support the customer’s move towards cleaner and more sustainable logistics. 
• Effects of New Deployments: The Company’s performance in Q2 FY27 reflects the positive impact of additional 
business from new deployments that started operating during the quarter, with part of this impact already visible in a 
higher top line and improved profitability. Further capacity additions with existing clients and new customers under 
deployment are expected to improve the Company’s profitability further, supporting its overall valuation and 
delivering better returns to investors.

Management Commentary 
“Q2 FY27 reflects a positive step forward in our growth journey, with continued improvement in both operational performance and 
profitability. We have delivered a 6.23% year-on-year growth in revenue, while Profit Before Tax and Profit After Tax have grown by 
58.06% and 45.02%, respectively. The significant improvement in profitability margins reflects our continued focus on operational 
efficiencies, better fleet utilization and disciplined execution. 
During the quarter, we further strengthened our business portfolio by successfully deploying 68 dedicated vehicles for Haldiram in 
Nagpur and 30 heavy-duty electric vehicles for Dalmia Cement. These strategic additions reinforce our ability to secure and execute 
large-scale, long-term contracts across diverse industry segments. Importantly, these deployments have started contributing to our 
financial performance, with their full operational and financial potential expected to progressively reflect in the coming quarters. 
We continue to invest in technology-led operational capabilities, with our in-house developed fleet management systems, real-time 
vehicle tracking and monitoring solutions enhancing transparency, service reliability and overall asset productivity. Our focus remains 
on building an integrated, scalable and technology-enabled logistics platform that can deliver customized solutions to India's leading 
businesses. 
Looking ahead, we remain confident about our growth prospects. The addition of new customers, expansion of business with existing 
clients and progressive deployment of additional capacities provides a strong foundation for sustained business growth. We remain 
focused on improving profitability, strengthening our market position and maintaining financial discipline while pursuing 
opportunities across conventional and sustainable mobility solutions. 
As we move forward, our priority will be to translate our expanding business portfolio and operational capabilities into consistent 
financial performance and long-term shareholder value.” 
Mr. Sanjay Gupta, Managing Director & CEO, AVG Logistics Limited 
About AVG Logistics Limited 
AVG Logistics Limited (‘AVG Logistics’ or ‘The Company’) is a leading multimodal logistics solutions provider based in India. Founded 
in 2010, the Company has rapidly grown to become a trusted partner for businesses across India. With a team of dedicated logistics 
experts and a modern fleet, AVG Logistics provides customized and technology-driven solutions across transportation, warehousing, 
distribution, and supply chain management. Furthermore, the Company also offers Third-Party Logistics Services (3PL), effectively 
complementing its wide range of logistics solutions. 
The company specializes in road & rail transportation, reefers/cold chain, and warehousing segment with over 70+ fully automated 
branches pan India. AVG’s esteemed customers include Nestle, Ferrero, HUL, Kwality Wall’s, Maruti Suzuki, DS Group, Brillion 
Consumers, INTAS, Amara Raja, Mother Dairy, Apollo Tyres, JK Tyres, ITC, Bharti Airtel, TATA Steel, NCERT, Dalmia Cement, Marico, 
Godrej, MRF, Jubilant Foods, Ultra Tech Cement, Coca Cola, Haldiram & many other retail and multinational companies. 
The company’s 600+ workforce comprising dedicated & skilled professionals provides 24x7 integrated logistics services in an 
efficient manner to customers across different industries in India. The company operates a 3000+ fleet of hired & owned vehicles 
along with 7,40,571 Sq. Ft. of warehousing space Pan India to provide several value-added services.
