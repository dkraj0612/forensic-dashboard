The AVG Advantage
Six advantages that compound as we scale
One partner.
Every mode.
Sustainable · Reliable · Integrated
01
Truly Multimodal
Customers can move goods by road or rail, store 
them in our warehouses, keep them cold in our 
reefers and ship bulk liquids in ISO tanks – all with 
one partner. We also shift suitable cargo from road to 
rail to cut costs.
02
Rail Advantage
What began as a cargo express train now runs on 6+ 
active routes. We have won 6 Indian Railways 
tenders worth ₹5,100 Mn and a ₹105 Cr contract, and 
were first to move automobile tyres by rail rake.
03
Green First-Mover
We were the first company in India to buy a fleet of 
55-tonne electric trucks, now running on Tata Steel 
routes. LNG trucks with Blue Energy Motors and our 
Baidyanath JV take this further.
04
Sticky Blue-Chip Clients
Nestlé was our first client in 2010 and still works with 
us today. We now serve 1,000+ customers, many on 
long-term dedicated contracts such as Haldiram's in 
Nagpur and Dalmia Cement in Assam.
05
Asset-Right Fleet
We own 850+ trucks and reefers for dedicated and 
specialised work, and draw on 3,000+ partner 
vehicles when volumes rise. This lets us take on large 
contracts while staying flexible.
06
Tech-Led Control
Our in-house command centre tracks every vehicle in 
real time and finds the nearest free truck or 
warehouse for each shipment, cutting empty trips. 
ADAS and onboard cameras keep drivers safe.
