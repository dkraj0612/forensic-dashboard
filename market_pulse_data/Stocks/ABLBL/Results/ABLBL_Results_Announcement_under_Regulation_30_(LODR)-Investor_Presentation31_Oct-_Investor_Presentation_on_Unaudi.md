### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) reported a strong Q2 FY27, achieving 11% consolidated revenue growth and a significant 126% surge in Profit After Tax, primarily driven by operating leverage and substantial margin expansion in its high-growth Emerging Business segment. This performance was underpinned by robust multi-channel execution and aggressive store network expansion, successfully navigating rising input costs and a festive calendar shift. While Net Debt increased materially, this appears aligned with the company's strategic capital deployment for growth initiatives.
