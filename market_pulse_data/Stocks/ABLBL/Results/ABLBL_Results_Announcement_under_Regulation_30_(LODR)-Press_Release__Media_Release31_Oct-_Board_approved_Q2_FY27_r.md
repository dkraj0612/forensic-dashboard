Business Performance 
 
Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) comprises of -  
 
• 
Lifestyle brands – Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, Allen Solly, Peter England and Simon Carter 
• 
Emerging Business – American Eagle, Sportswear brand Reebok and Innerwear business under Van 
Heusen 
Lifestyle Brands - The business delivered another quarter of profitable growth, with healthy performance 
across brands and channels. Revenue grew 9% YoY to Rs. 1906 Cr, while EBITDA rose 10% to Rs. 371 Cr, with 
margin at 19.5%. Growth was built upon 5% retail LTL, complemented by sustained traction across wholesale 
and e-commerce. 
 
Business continues to make progress on key strategic levers of product innovation, brand rejuvenation and retail 
expansion. Sharper assortments, new launches and targeted digital initiatives further enhanced brand relevance 
among younger and Gen Z consumers. Network expansion gained pace with the addition of larger, elevated 
stores, alongside renovations aimed at enhancing the overall shopping experience. 
 
The business is consistently building its own e-commerce business as a key growth lever for future. This channel 
has built capabilities around online-exclusive merchandise, enhanced app and brand.com experiences, expanded 
hyperlocal capabilities and impactful go-to-market initiatives, helping it maintain strong growth momentum. 
 
The Emerging Businesses portfolio sustained strong growth, supported by healthy performance across 
brands and channels, alongside consistent improvement in profitability. The portfolio has now crossed an 
annualised revenue run-rate of Rs. 1400 Cr, contributing over 16% of ABLBL's overall H1 revenue, reflecting its 
growing significance within the business. 
 
Q2 revenue grew 27%, driven by 11% retail LTL growth and healthy traction across wholesale and e-commerce 
channels. Strategic retail expansion continued with the addition of 30+ stores across key markets this quarter.

About Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited 
 
Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL), a part of the Aditya Birla Group, is a leading branded apparel company 
with a comprehensive portfolio of marquee lifestyle brands. As of September 30, 2026, the Company operates a retail 
footprint of ~5.1 million sq. ft., comprising 3,434 exclusive brand outlets, presence across 40,500+ multi-brand 
outlets and over 6,800+ shop-in-shops in department stores across the country. 
 
The Company’s brand portfolio includes India’s most established and trusted names - Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, 
Allen Solly and Peter England - each with a legacy of over 25 years. These brands have consistently delivered 
sustained growth, driven by a sharp focus on design-led innovation, category expansion, and operational excellence 
across product lines spanning formal, casual, and occasion wear.  
 
ABLBL has built a strong presence in the innerwear and athleisure market through Van Heusen Innerwear, which has 
rapidly scaled its distribution network across India.  
 
The Company’s portfolio is further enhanced through strategic partnerships with leading international brands including 
Simon Carter, American Eagle and Reebok. American Eagle has emerged as one of India’s most preferred premium 
denim brands, while Reebok – transitioned under the Company’s management in 2022 - has expanded its footprint 
across multiple channels. Simon Carter continues to strengthen its position in the premium menswear segment 
through its distinctive design language and curated collections.
