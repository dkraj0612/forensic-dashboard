Rating Update 
October 30, 2024 | Mumbai 
 
Behari Lal Ispat Private Limited 
 
Update as on October 30, 2024 
 
This update is provided in continuation of the rating rational below. 
 
The key rating sensitivity factors for the rating include: 
 
Upward factors: 
 
• 
Continuous improvement in operating income supported by volumetric growth of more than 30% 
along with sustenance of operating margin leading to higher-than-expected net cash accrual. 
• 
Efficient management of working capital cycle leading to sustained healthy financial risk profile. 
 
Downward factors: 
 
• 
Decline in volumetric growth by over 20%, or operating margin leading to lower-than-expected net 
cash accrual. 
• 
Large debt-funded capex or substantial increase in working capital requirements thus weakening the 
liquidity and financial risk profiles. 
 
CRISIL Ratings has a policy of keeping its accepted ratings under constant and ongoing monitoring and 
review. Accordingly, CRISIL Ratings seeks regular updates from companies on the business and financial 
performance. CRISIL Ratings is, however, awaiting adequate information from Behari Lal Ispat Private 
Limited (BLIPL) which will enable us to carry out the rating review. CRISIL Ratings will continue provide 
updates on relevant developments from time to time on this credit. 
 
CRISIL Ratings also identifies information availability risk as a key credit factor in the rating assessment 
as outlined in its criteria ‘Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings’. 
 
 
About the Company 
BLIPL, incorporated in 1995, is promoted by Mr Dinesh Garg, and is based in Mandi Gobindgarh 
(Punjab). Until 2012, the company was trading in various ferroalloys, sponge iron, and other related 
products. In 2012, it set up a facility for manufacturing metal rolls for rolling mills, engineering castings 
and steel ingots.

Detailed rationale
CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘CRISIL BBB+/Stable/CRISIL A2’ ratings on the bank loan facilities of Behari Lal Ispat Pvt
Ltd (BLIPL).
 
The ratings continue to reflect the estabilished market position of BLIPL and its healthy financial risk profile. These strengths
are partially offset by the modest scale of operations and moderate working capital requirements.
Analytical approach:
Unsecured loans of Rs 6.2 crore as on March 31, 2023 have been treated as debt as there have been consistent
withdrawals over a couple of years.
Key rating drivers and detailed description
Strengths:
Estabilished market position: The promoter’s experience of over three decades in steel - rolled/ structural products
industry and rolls and casting manufacturing, in-depth understanding of the dynamics of the market and healthy
relationships with suppliers has helped the company to develop an estabilished market position which has in return
aided the company to diversify its product and customer mix. The estabilished market position would continue to provide
revenue visibility and support the business risk profile of the company.
 
Healthy financial risk profile: Low gearing of 0.19 time as on March 31, 2023 offers sufficient headroom to avail
additional debt for business purposes. Further, with no major debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) proposed to be
undertaken, capital structure should continue to be comfortable over the medium term. Debt protection metrics are
above average with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCAAD) ratios of more than 16 and 1.3
times, respectively, in fiscal 2023. With sustenance of operating margin at 9-10% and no major debt proposed to be
undertaken, debt protection metrics should remain comfortable over the medium term.
 
Weaknesses:
Modest scale of operations: Revenue declined by ~10% in fiscal 2023 over previous fiscal to ~Rs 305 crore majorly
on account of volumetric degrowth ~15% of steel rounds. The moderation in operating income in FY23 was higher than
CRISIL Ratings expectations. Operating income, at ~Rs 71 crore year-to-date (April -June 2023), is expected at Rs 300-
310 crore for fiscal 2024, which shall remain below CRISIL Ratings expectations. Continuous improvement in volumetric
growth supporting sustained improvement in operating income amid sustenance of operating margin would therefore
remain a key monitorable.
 
Moderate working capital requirements: The operations of the company are moderate in terms of working capital
requirements with GCA days historically ranging between 100-120 days for last 3 fiscals through FY23, majorly driven
by inventory days in range of 45-60 days. With sustained improvement in operating income, the working capital

Liquidity: Adequate
Liquidity is comfortable driven by expected net cash accrual of Rs 22-24 crore with cash and equivalents projected at ~Rs
1.0 crore over the medium term. Working capital limits of Rs 26.5 crore were utilised ~22%, on average, over the 12 months
through July 2023. Company has repayment obligations of less than ~Rs 0.5 crore and annual capex plans of Rs 8-9 crore
over the medium term. CRISIL Ratings expects internal accruals, cash and equivalents and unutilised bank lines to be
sufficient to meet its repayment obligations as well as incremental working capital requirements.
Outlook: Stable
CRISIL Ratings believes BLIPL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established
relationships with clients.
Rating sensitivity factors

Continuous improvement in operating income supported by volumetric growth of more than 30% along with sustenance
of operating margin leading to higher-than-expected net cash accrual.
Efficient management of working capital cycle leading to sustained healthy financial risk profile.
 
Downward factors:
Decline in volumetric growth by over 20%, or operating margin leading to lower-than-expected net cash accrual.
Large debt-funded capex or substantial increase in working capital requirements thus weakening the liquidity and
financial risk profiles.
