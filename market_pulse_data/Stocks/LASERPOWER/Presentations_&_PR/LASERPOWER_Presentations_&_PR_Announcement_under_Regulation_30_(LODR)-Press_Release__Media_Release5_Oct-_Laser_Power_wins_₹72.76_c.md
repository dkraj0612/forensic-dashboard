Laser Power & Infra secures ₹72.76 crore HTLS 
reconductoring order from POWERGRID 
First commercial HTLS conductor order marks a major breakthrough in the Company’s 
technology-led transmission journey 
 
Kolkata, October 05, 2026: Laser Power & Infra Limited (“LPIL” or “the Company”) (BSE: 
544822, NSE: LASERPOWER), a leading integrated power infrastructure company, has secured 
contracts aggregating to ₹72.76 crore from Power Grid Corporation of India Limited for the 
supply of High Temperature Low Sag (“HTLS”) conductors and the reconductoring of two existing 
132 kV transmission lines under the North Eastern Region Expansion Scheme-XXIV (NERES-
XXIV). 
The project covers approximately 164 circuit-km of existing transmission corridors and is to be 
completed within 12 months from the Notification of Award. 
The award represents Laser Power’s first commercial order for the supply of HTLS conductors, 
marking the transition of its HTLS technology from product development and qualification to 
commercial execution. Through its licensing arrangement with TS Conductor, USA, the Company 
has access to an advanced conductor platform. Participation in this specialised segment involves 
significant product-testing, utility qualification and execution requirements. 
The integrated nature of the POWERGRID mandate, covering both conductor supply and 
reconductoring services, brings together Laser Power’s capabilities across engineering, 
manufacturing, supply, installation and commissioning. It provides the Company with an 
opportunity to take advanced conductor technology from manufacturing through to on-ground 
execution. 
 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Deepak Goel, Chairman and Managing Director, Laser 
Power & Infra Limited, said: 
“This order is a watershed moment in Laser Power’s journey. It translates nearly 18 months of 
focused work across product development, testing, qualification and execution readiness into our 
first commercial HTLS conductor order. More importantly, it gives us an entry into a specialised 
segment of transmission infrastructure where technology access is only the starting point. Product 
qualification, utility acceptance and execution capability are equally critical. 
Our HTLS platform has been designed to address several practical requirements of transmission 
modernisation. It can support higher power transfer through existing corridors while managing 
thermal sag and has the potential to offer benefits in conductor efficiency, transmission losses and 
installation.
