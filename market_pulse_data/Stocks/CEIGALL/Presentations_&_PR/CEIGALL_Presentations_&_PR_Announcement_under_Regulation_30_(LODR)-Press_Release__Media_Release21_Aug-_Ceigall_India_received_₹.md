CEIGALL INDIA LIMITED 
“Ceigall India Strengthens Pan-India Growth Momentum with ₹225 Crore Bulk Drug Park Order in 
Himachal Pradesh” 
• 
New order reinforces the company’s growing presence across geographies and its contribution to 
India’s infrastructure and industrial development 
National, August 21, 2026: Ceigall India Limited (CIL), one of India’s leading infrastructure development 
companies, has received a Letter of Award (LOA) from Himachal Pradesh State Industrial Development 
Corporation Limited (HPSIDC) for the ₹225 crore development of the Bulk Drug Park at Una, Himachal 
Pradesh. The project involves formation cutting, construction of internal roads, storm water drains, a 
bridge and fencing as part of PH-I of the development. 
The order, awarded as an item-rate contract, has an execution period of 18 months. The project adds to 
Ceigall India’s growing portfolio of infrastructure projects across the country and further strengthens its 
presence in the development of critical infrastructure supporting India’s industrial and economic growth. 
The development of the Bulk Drug Park is expected to create essential infrastructure for a strategically 
important industrial ecosystem, with the project scope encompassing road connectivity, drainage 
infrastructure, bridge construction and associated development works. 
Commenting on the order, Ramneek Sehgal, Managing Director, Ceigall India Limited, said, “This order 
for the development of the Bulk Drug Park in Una marks another important milestone in Ceigall India’s journey 
of sustained growth across the country. Our expanding order portfolio across diverse geographies and 
infrastructure segments reflects the trust placed in our execution capabilities and our ability to undertake 
projects of national and regional significance. As India progresses towards the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047, 
we remain committed to contributing to the creation of robust infrastructure that strengthens industrial 
development, improves connectivity and supports long-term economic growth. We look forward to bringing 
our execution expertise to this project and further expanding our contribution to India’s infrastructure 
transformation.” 
With this project, Ceigall India continues to build on its strategy of expanding its geographic presence and 
diversifying its infrastructure portfolio. The company remains focused on leveraging its execution 
capabilities to participate in projects aligned with India’s evolving infrastructure requirements and the 
broader objective of building a stronger, more connected and industrially resilient economy. 
About Ceigall India Limited - (BSE: 544223 | NSE: CEIGALL) 
Ceigall India Limited is an infrastructure engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company 
engaged in the development of highways, expressways, bridges, flyovers, railway overbridges, tunnels, and 
runways. The Company executes projects under EPC as well as Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) formats and 
has a strong track record of timely execution across multiple states in India. With a growing order book 
and diversified project portfolio, Ceigall India continues to play a key role in India’s infrastructure 
development journey.
