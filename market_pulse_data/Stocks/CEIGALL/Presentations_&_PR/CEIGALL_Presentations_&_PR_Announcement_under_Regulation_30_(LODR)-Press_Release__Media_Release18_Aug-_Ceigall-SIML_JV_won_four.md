Ceigall India Strengthens Presence in Northeast with ₹2,149.62-Crore MoRTH Contracts 
● CIL - SIML JV receives four Letters of Acceptance from the Ministry of Road Transport & 
Highways for road construction packages on NH-913 (Frontier Highway) in Arunachal Pradesh  
● Projects to be executed on EPC mode across multiple sections with construction periods 
ranging from 36 to 48 months 
New Delhi, August 18, 2026: Ceigall India Limited (CIL), through its joint venture with Sushee Infra 
Mining Limited (SIML), has received four Letters of Acceptance (LOAs) from the Ministry of Road 
Transport & Highways (MoRTH) for the construction of multiple road packages on NH-913 (Frontier 
Highway) in Arunachal Pradesh. The four projects have an aggregate bid cost of ₹2,149.62 crore. 
The CIL-SIML joint venture comprises Ceigall India Limited with a 74% stake and Sushee Infra Mining 
Limited with a 26% stake. The contracts have been awarded on an Engineering, Procurement and 
Construction 
(EPC) 
basis. 
The four packages involve the construction and development of Intermediate Lane roads across key 
sections of NH-913 (Frontier Highway) in Arunachal Pradesh. The projects include: 
● Construction of the road from km 0.000 to km 55.725 of the Huri–Taliha section, with a bid 
cost of ₹521 crore 
● Construction of the road from km 0.000 to km 78.38 of the Sarli–Huri section, with a bid cost 
of ₹611.10 crore 
● Construction of the road from km 55.725 to km 113.25 of the Huri–Taliha section, with a bid 
cost of ₹525 crore 
● Construction of the road from km 160.75 to km 209.90 of the Huri–Taliha section, with a bid 
cost 
of 
₹492.52 
crore 
The first, second and fourth packages have a construction period of 36 months, while the third 
package is scheduled for completion over 48 months. Each project also includes a five-year 
maintenance period. 
Commenting on the development, Ramneek Sehgal, Chairman & Managing Director, Ceigall India 
Limited, said, “We are pleased to secure these four important packages from the Ministry of Road 
Transport & Highways for the development of the Frontier Highway in Arunachal Pradesh. These 
projects further strengthen our presence in India’s strategic road infrastructure development and 
reflect our capabilities in executing large and complex projects across challenging geographies. We 
remain committed to delivering these projects with the highest standards of quality, safety and timely 
execution while contributing to improved infrastructure connectivity in the region.” 
The new contracts further strengthen Ceigall India’s order book and reinforce its presence in the road 
and highway infrastructure segment. The projects also mark another significant addition to the 
Company’s portfolio of EPC infrastructure projects across India.

About Ceigall India Limited - (BSE: 544223 | NSE: CEIGALL) 
Ceigall India Limited is an infrastructure engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company 
engaged in the development of highways, expressways, bridges, flyovers, railway overbridges, tunnels, 
and runways. The Company executes projects under EPC and Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) formats 
and has a strong track record of timely execution across multiple states in India. Building on its proven 
execution capabilities, the Company is also expanding its presence in the renewable energy sector 
through utility-scale solar and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) projects, further diversifying its 
infrastructure portfolio. With a growing order book and diversified project portfolio, Ceigall India 
continues to play a key role in India’s infrastructure development journey.
