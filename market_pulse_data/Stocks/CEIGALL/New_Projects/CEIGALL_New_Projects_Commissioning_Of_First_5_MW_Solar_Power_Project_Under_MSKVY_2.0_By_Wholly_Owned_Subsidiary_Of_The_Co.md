Subject: Commissioning of First 5 MW Solar Power Project under MSKVY 2.0 by Wholly

Dear Sir / Madam, 
In continuation of our letter dated March 25, 2026, and in accordance with the provisions of 
Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
are pleased to inform you that Ceigall Green Energy MH2 Limited, a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) 
being a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has successfully commissioned its first 5 MW 
Solar Power Plant at Ranjangaon Deshmukh, Taluka Kopargaon, District Ahilyanagar, 
Maharashtra, well ahead of schedule commissioning date and thereby eligible for the applicable 
incentive, under the Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Vahini Yojana 2.0 (MSKVY 2.0). 
The aforesaid 5 MW Solar Power Project forms part of the aggregate 147 MW Solar Power Project 
being developed and executed by the said SPV under MSKVY 2.0.  
The successful commissioning of the project marks a significant milestone in the Company's 
expansion into the renewable energy sector and strengthens its presence in solar power 
generation. 
The above intimation is also available on the Company's website at www.ceigall.com
