Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
This is to inform that the Board of Directors of Finkurve Financial Services Limited (“the Company”), at its 
meeting held on Thursday, October 1, 2026, has, inter alia, considered and approved the following: 
 
1. To Change the  Registrar & Transfer Agent (RTA) of the Company from M/s. MUFG Intime India Private Limited 
(“MUFG”) to M/s. Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited (“Purva”) (SEBI Registration Number: 
INR000001112) for its Equity shares; 
 
MUFG will continue to serve as the Company’s RTA until the completion of data transition, shifting of electronic 
connectivity, and other related procedures, along with receipt of necessary confirmations/ approval from NSDL, 
CDSL and other authority, if any. The formalities for change of RTA including documentation, execution of 
agreement, shifting of electronic connectivity and transition of data will be done by the Company in due course. 
The details as required under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read 
with the SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 
2024, is annexed as Annexure A to this letter. 
 
2. The Resignation of Mr. Naveen Kottala as the Chief Executive Officer of the Company w.e.f. the close of the 
business hours of October 20, 2026. 
 
3. On the Recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors appointed Mr. 
Siddharth Purandare as the Chief Executive Officer of the Company w.e.f. December 13, 2026. 
 
The relevant details pursuant cessation and appointment to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 
dated 11th November 2024 are enclosed as Annexure B and C respectively. 
 
4. The appointment of Mr. Hussain Mohammed Pittalwala as Chief Compliance Officer, pursuant to Reserve Bank 
of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies - Compliance Function) Directions, 2026,with effect from October 
1, 2026. 
 
5. On the Recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors of the Company 
has approved, the grant of 4,50,000 (Four Lakh Fifty Thousand Only) Employee Stock Options (“Options”) under 
the “Finkurve ESOP Plan -2018” is annexed as Annexure D to this letter.
