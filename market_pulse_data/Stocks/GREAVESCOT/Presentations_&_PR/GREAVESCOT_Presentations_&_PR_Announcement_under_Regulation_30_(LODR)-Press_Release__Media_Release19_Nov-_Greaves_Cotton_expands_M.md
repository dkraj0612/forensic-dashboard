Greaves Cotton Accelerates Global Marine Business Expansion Through Multiflex Marine; 
Targets Europe as a Key Growth Market 
Amsterdam (NETHERLAND) / Mumbai (INDIA), November 19, 2026: Greaves Cotton Limited, one of 
India's leading diversified engineering companies, is accelerating its global marine business expansion 
through Multiflex Marine, leveraging its engineering capabilities, expanding product portfolio and 
international distribution network to build a globally competitive marine solutions business.  
With Europe emerging as a priority growth market, Greaves Cotton is focused on strengthening OEM and 
distributor partnerships, expanding customer relationships and taking its India-developed engineering 
solutions to new international markets. These ambitions are showcased at METSTRADE 2026, being held 
at RAI Amsterdam, Netherlands, from November 17–19, 2026. 
The expansion reflects Greaves Cotton's GREAVES.NEXT strategy of evolving into a diversified engineering 
solutions provider and building future-ready businesses with global relevance. Through its subsidiary Excel 
Controlinkage Private Limited, the company is strengthening Multiflex Marine as an integrated brand 
within its diversified portfolio. Today, Multiflex Marine has a presence in over 65 countries, serving more 
than 400 customers through approximately 200 distributors. 
As part of its next phase of growth, Multiflex Marine is expanding its marine control solutions portfolio to 
address evolving customer requirements across recreational boats, commercial vessels, workboats, 
personal watercraft and boat trailers. At METSTRADE 2026, the brand officially launched its Multiflex 
Hydraulic Steering System for 90HP to 500HP outboard applications under the campaign theme 'Master 
Every Turn'. It also marked the soft launch of its Multiflex Electric Trim Tabs under 'Level Up Your Ride', 
while strengthening engagement with OEMs, distributors and industry stakeholders and enhancing brand 
visibility across key international markets. The showcase further featured EDGE Premium Engine Control 
Cables, engineered to deliver enhanced control and performance. 
Parag Satpute, MD & Group CEO, Greaves Cotton Limited, said, "The global marine industry presents a 
significant opportunity for Greaves Cotton to leverage its engineering heritage and build a differentiated 
international business. Through Multiflex Marine, we are focused on scaling our presence across key 
markets, with Europe being an important priority, while expanding our product portfolio and 
strengthening partnerships with OEMs and distributors. Our approach is centred on understanding 
evolving customer needs and delivering reliable, globally competitive marine solutions from India. This is 
an important part of our GREAVES.NEXT journey towards building future-ready engineering businesses 
with sustainable global growth potential."

About Greaves Cotton Limited: 
With a legacy of over 165 years, Greaves Cotton Limited is a diversified, future-ready engineering company 
delivering innovative and sustainable solutions to customers across key sectors. Renowned for its 
precision engineering and technology leadership, the Company’s growth strategy is anchored on three 
core dimensions: accelerating its core businesses, building new muscle onto the core, and expanding into 
new horizons across Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions, and Industrial Solutions. 
As one of India’s largest manufacturers of single-cylinder diesel engines, and a pioneer in introducing them 
for microcar applications in the European market, Greaves continues to reinforce its commitment to 
accessible, efficient, and reliable mobility. Guided by its purpose of ‘Empowering Lives’, Greaves is 
committed to providing reliable products, sustainable technology, and customer-centric solutions. With 
its strong engineering foundation and continuous innovation, the Company aims to enable people, 
businesses, and communities to progress with confidence in a future rooted in engineering excellence and 
driven by efficient energy. 
The investee companies of Greaves include Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML) with its diverse 
portfolio of electric 2 & 3 wheelers for passenger and cargo mobility, and Greaves Finance Limited (GFL) 
with ev.fin, a 100% EV-focused NBFC that leverages cutting-edge technology to offer customised financing 
options & seamless buying experience. Both these businesses play a significant role in accelerating EV 
adoption in India.
