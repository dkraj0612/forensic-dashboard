Ampere partners with CSC Grameen eStore  
to strengthen presence in rural and emerging markets 
 
The partnership will enable customers to connect with authorised Ampere dealership network via the CSC 
Grameen eStore network 
 
Bengaluru/ New Delhi, 26 November 2026: Ampere, the electric two-wheeler brand of Greaves Electric 
Mobility Limited, with proven performance with 6 national records and 1 international record, has 
announced a strategic partnership with CSC Grameen eStore Private Limited to bring its wide range of 
award-winning Electric Scooters to customers in rural and emerging markets. CSC Grameen eStore is the 
e-commerce arm of the Common Service Centre (CSC) network, promoted by CSC e-Governance Services 
India Limited under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India. 
Through its network of Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs), CSC Grameen eStore will connect customers in 
rural and emerging markets with Ampere’s electric two-wheeler portfolio and authorised Ampere dealer 
partners. The partnership begins as a pilot in Tamil Nadu, Bihar, Odisha and Maharashtra. 
 
Customers interested in an Ampere electric scooters can submit an enquiry through the CSC Grameen 
eStore app, with the support of VLEs. Ampere will validate the enquiry and forward it to the nearest 
authorised dealer, who will facilitate test rides, booking, retail and delivery. The partnership creates 
additional customer touchpoints in Tier II and Tier III towns and rural areas, connecting customers with 
Ampere’s established retail network. 
 
Ampere’s wide range of electric scooter portfolio will be available through the partnership. Within the 
portfolio, the Ampere Nexus EX+ is powered by a new powerful 5 kW motor and backed by multiple 
national and international records demonstrating its performance across challenging terrain. The Magnus 
GMax brings together everyday practicality, connected technology and affordability, while the Magnus 
Neo focuses on ergonomics and comfortable city riding. The recently launched Ampere Reo Vyb further 
expands the portfolio for younger riders, including students and college-goers. The Nexus EX+ and Magnus 
GMax variants come with Intellipack, bringing 30+ intelligent features through the Ampere Connect App, 
including Cruise Control, Hill Hold Assist, Find My Scooter and Turn-by-Turn Navigation. The portfolio is 
backed by Next-Gen LFP battery technology and a standard battery warranty of up to five years/75,000 
km, offering customers greater confidence in everyday electric mobility. 
 
Speaking on the development, Mr. Manoj MP, Chief Business Officer, Greaves Electric Mobility Limited, 
said, “At Greaves Electric Mobility, we are focused on making electric mobility more accessible by 
strengthening customer touchpoints across markets. Our partnership with CSC Grameen eStore provides 
an additional channel for customers in rural and emerging markets to explore and experience Ampere 
electric scooters.” 
 
Ampere’s ‘Bharat Ki Smart Ride’ positioning is backed by real-world engineering and validation across 
varied Indian terrain. The Magnus GMax with Intellipack recently completed the Ultimate Endurance Ride, 
covering  4,000+ km in 15 days from Dong Valley in Arunachal Pradesh to Guhar Moti in Kutch, traversing 
mountains, plains and desert. The Nexus platform has also navigated the 70 hairpin bends of Kolli Hills 
and reached Shipki La Pass at 13,200 feet, demonstrating its performance across challenging terrain. This 
is relevant for customers in smaller towns and villages, where roads and riding conditions vary widely.

About Greaves Cotton Limited: 
With a legacy of over 165 years, Greaves Cotton Limited is a diversified, future-ready engineering company 
delivering innovative and sustainable solutions to customers across key sectors. Renowned for its 
precision engineering and technology leadership, the Company’s growth strategy is anchored on three 
core dimensions: accelerating its core businesses, building new muscle onto the core, and expanding into 
new horizons across Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions, and Industrial Solutions. 
 
As one of India’s largest manufacturers of single-cylinder diesel engines, and a pioneer in introducing them

About Greaves Electric Mobility Limited: 
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), the electric mobility business of Greaves Cotton Limited (GCL), 
is one of the first companies in India to focus on the evolving market of electric vehicles (“EV”) and has 
been among the frontrunners at driving EV adoption in the country. With over 17 years of experience 
designing and manufacturing electric vehicles, GEML serves both the electric 2-wheeler and 3-wheeler 
segments. GEML along with its subsidiaries, operates three manufacturing plants located in Hyderabad, 
Noida, and Ranipet. The company supports customers through 600+ Ampere touchpoints across India, 
enabling wider access, dependable service and an enhanced ownership experience. GEML holds 8 India 
Book of Records* and 1 Asia Book of Records* titles, reflecting its focus on technology and design led 
innovative & durable mobility solutions. Subsidiaries MLR Auto Limited help extend GEML’s market 
presence. Committed to affordability and sustainability, GEML provides clean mobility solutions 
contributing to India’s shift toward electric transportation.
