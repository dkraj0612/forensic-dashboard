Ather Energy introduces Konarc, its new electric scooter to make EVs mainstream  
Embargoed until 12.30 pm IST on 29th August, 2026 
~ Offers IDC range options of up to 200 km, with six variants across the S and Z product lines 
~ Doubles service interval to 10,000 km, with fewer service checkpoints and easier access to 
wear-and-tear components 
~ Introduces 10-year/100,000 km warranty on its new fifth-generation BedrockTM battery pack 
~ New suite of features includes Advanced Electronic Braking System (AeBS™), MagicKey, AutoPop and 
AirWalk™ 
~ Konarc priced from ₹99,999 (effective ex showroom Bengaluru), with deliveries beginning in a phased 
manner across select variants and select cities before ramping up across India 
National, 29th August 2026: Ather Energy, one of India’s leading electric two-wheeler manufacturers, 
today introduced KonarcTM at the fourth edition of Ather Community Day 2026 in Bengaluru, taking 
another step toward making EVs mainstream. Built on Ather’s new EL platform, Konarc brings Ather’s 
engineering and technology to a wider base of Indian riders, with a refined experience built around ride 
comfort, range, durability and ease of ownership. 
Konarc will be available across the S and Z product lines, with six variants in total. The S line comprises 
four variants with IDC ranges of 100 km, 125 km, 161 km, and 200 km, while the Z line comprises two 
variants with IDC ranges of 125 km and 161 km. Konarc will be available in Quartz White, Lumen 
Copper, Celestial Gold, and Majestic Grey, with Steel Blue and Claret Red available exclusively on the 
higher-end Z variants. 
Konarc also brings a simpler ownership proposition, with service required only once a year for most 
riders and a 10-year/100,000 km (whichever is earlier) warranty on its fifth-generation BedrockTM battery. 
The scooter introduces a suite of new features including MagicKey, AutoPop, AirWalk™ and Ather’s 
Advanced Electronic Braking System (AeBS™). 
Tarun Mehta, Co-Founder & CEO, Ather Energy, said, “Konarc has been designed to make EVs 
mainstream in India. We have understood the needs of the vast majority of buyers who are still 
waiting to go electric and have engineered Konarc for them. Be it the metal panels, 200 km range, 
once-a-year service interval, fast home charging, or the plush and comfortable ride, we have pushed 
the envelope on every fundamental that matters to this buyer, without compromising on the 
premium experience and advanced technology that Ather is known for. Konarc has also been 
designed to scale, and with the vertical integration and manufacturing capacity we are building at 
AURIC, we can take the Ather experience to many more riders and markets across India.” 
Built around the realities of Indian roads 
Konarc has been engineered around the way scooters are ridden in India, with a focus on stability and 
comfort. It gets a 14-inch front alloy wheel and a 12-inch rear alloy wheel, both featuring a new alloy 
wheel design. Its 1,352 mm wheelbase is among the longest in the scooter segment, while a relaxed

Comfort is at the heart of Konarc’s design, with a seamless rider and pillion experience spanning the

Rethinking Braking: Advanced Electronic Braking System (AeBSTM) 
Konarc introduces Ather’s Advanced Electronic Braking System (AeBS™), a technology developed 
entirely in-house to make braking performance superior across different road surfaces. Unlike 
conventional braking systems, which apply a fixed distribution of braking force between the front and 
rear wheels, AeBS™ dynamically adjusts this balance based on the road conditions while braking. It 
does this using software and sensors, working with Konarc’s existing Ather Drive Controller and motor, 
without the need for additional hardware. AeBS™ also uses regenerative braking to add braking force, 
helping reduce stopping distances. At the same time, its dynamic modulation helps reduce wheel 
lock-ups and improve stability and control. 
Swapnil Jain, Co-founder & CTO of Ather Energy said, “Ather has spent over a decade building its 
technology and engineering capabilities. We started working on software-led braking, fast charging and 
connected scooter experiences well before they became expected in the market. That head start matters 
because it gives us the experience to integrate these technologies deeply into the vehicle and bring the 
engineering depth we have built over the years to a much larger set of riders. AeBS™, MagicKey, 
AutoPopTM and AirWalk™ are examples of that approach, each designed to make everyday riding more 
effortless.” 
Everyday technology, made effortless 
Konarc brings Ather’s technology-first approach to everyday scooter interactions, making them simpler, 
easier and more intuitive. Its new Magic features turn familiar moments, from unlocking and accessing 
storage to moving the scooter around, into effortless experiences. 
MagicKey redefines the keyless experience for two-wheelers and is featured on Ather’s first scooter built 
around this technology. Its SmartWakeTM feature allows riders to wake and unlock the scooter through a 
simple interaction, without ever needing to take the key out of their pocket. Konarc also automatically 
locks when the rider walks away. MagicKey can also be used to locate the scooter remotely and even 
open the seat. A ping function on the key fob can locate Konarc from 30 metres away by triggering a

AutoPopTM makes the under-seat storage easier to access when a rider's hands are full. Unlike a boot 
release that only works once the rider is standing next to the scooter, AutoPop can also be triggered 
from a few metres away using the MagicKey™, so the seat is already open by the time the rider reaches 
Konarc. A button on the scooter offers the same function at close range. The seat lifts fully to a vertical 
position and stays there, giving riders clear, hands-free access with no risk of it swinging shut mid-reach. 
AirWalk™ makes moving your scooter by hand noticeably easier. As soon as the scooter detects a 
manual push or pull, it engages the motor to provide mild assistive torque in the same direction. This 
makes it easier to navigate tight parking spaces, back out or move the scooter around manually, while 
also reducing the effort required when moving the scooter with under-inflated tyres. 
Simplifying Charging and Servicing  
Every Konarc comes with an onboard charger and charging cable, eliminating the need for a separate 
charging brick. The charger is integrated into the scooter using the same drive controller that powers the 
motor, making the charging cable lightweight and easy to carry. Riders who want faster home charging 
can add a 450W offboard charger to the scooter's built-in 450W charger. Together, they deliver charging 
at 900W through Combined Charging, reducing home charging time by up to half. Exclusive to Konarc, 
Combined Charging gives riders the flexibility to use the lightweight cable for regular charging or add the 
offboard charger when they need to charge faster. 
Konarc also doubles Ather’s service interval to 10,000 km, which is roughly once a year for most riders 
(whichever is earlier). The number of checkpoints at each service visit has also been reduced, while the 
platform has been designed to make wear-and-tear components easier to access and replace. 
Battery Warranty 
All Konarc variants come with Ather’s fifth-generation Bedrock™ batteries, backed by a new warranty 
that covers the battery for 10 years or 1,00,000 km, whichever is earlier. Building on Ather’s earlier 
eight-year warranty, the new warranty extends the coverage period to 10 years while continuing to 
guarantee a minimum 70% state of health at the end of the coverage period. It also covers battery 
failures and defects, including periods when the scooter is not in use, giving riders greater confidence in 
long-term battery ownership. Sales of the 10-year battery warranty will begin soon. 
The Konarc S 125 km and Konarc S 161 km variants will be available initially, with bookings and 
deliveries beginning in mid-September in a phased manner. The rollout will start with select variants and 
states before expanding to more locations across the country. The Konarc S 100 km is priced at 
₹99,999, the Konarc S 125 km at ₹1,21,999, and the Konarc S 161 km at ₹1,44,999, effective 
ex-showroom Bengaluru.

About Ather Energy Limited 
Ather Energy Limited (ATHERENERG | 544397 | INE0LEZ01016) is a pioneer in the Indian electric two-wheeler 
("E2W") market (CRISIL Report). Founded in 2013 by Tarun Mehta and Swapnil Jain, Ather designs and 
manufactures high-performance electric scooters. Ather launched its first electric scooter in 2018. Its current E2W 
portfolio consists of two distinct product lines: the Ather 450 series, focused on performance-oriented customers, 
and the Ather Rizta, a convenience first family scooter line launched in 2024. Together, these product lines offer a 
total of 9 variants. Ather was the first two-wheeler OEM in India to establish a dedicated fast charging network, the 
Ather Grid, which is designed and built in India. Today, Ather operates the widest 2W fast charging network in the 
country. With a strong emphasis on R&D and innovation, as of June 30, 2026, Ather holds 323 registered 
trademarks, 270 registered designs and 52 registered patents, along with pending applications for 142 trademarks, 
32 designs and 667 patents globally.
