Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform you 
that Cleanmax Energy (Thailand) Company Limited (“Cleanmax Energy”) and Cleanmax IHQ 
(Thailand) Company Limited (“Cleanmax IHQ”), both are wholly owned subsidiaries of Cleanmax 
Solar MENA FZCO (Dubai), which is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. Cleanmax Energy 
and Cleanmax IHQ have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement with Massmunch Company 
Limited and Promoters of Massmunch Company Limited on 9 October 2026 for acquisition of 100% 
share capital of Massmunch Company Limited. 
 
Consequently, Cleanmax Energy and Cleanmax IHQ will hold 99.99% and 0.01% of the share capital 
of Massmunch Company Limited, respectively. As a result, Massmunch Company Limited will become 
a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. 
 
In compliance with the SEBI Listing Regulations and Securities and Exchange Board of India Master 
Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/l/3762/2026 dated 30 January 2026 (as amended), 
the details of the transaction are provided in Annexure A enclosed herewith. 
 
The same will be made available on the Company’s website i.e., www.cleanmax.com. 
   
This is for your information, record, and appropriate dissemination.
