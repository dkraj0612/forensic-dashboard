Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, the Stakeholders Relationship Committee 
of the Board of Directors of the Company today i.e. 03 September 2026 has approved the allotment 
of a total of 46,978 equity shares of the face value of INR 1 (Indian Rupee One Only) each fully paid 
up, comprising (i) 23,489 (Twenty-Three Thousand Four Hundred and Eighty-Nine) equity shares 
pursuant to exercise of vested stock options at exercise price of INR 1 per option, and (ii) 23,489 
(Twenty-Three Thousand Four Hundred and Eighty-Nine) bonus shares allotted pursuant to the bonus 
issuance in the ratio of 1:1 approved by the shareholders by way of resolution dated  
8 August 2025, under Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited Employee Stock Option Scheme 
2015 as amended from time to time (“CMES ESOS”) to the eligible employees.  
 
The equity shares so allotted shall rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the Company 
in all respects. 
 
Consequent to the aforesaid allotment, the paid-up share capital of the Company shall stand 
increased from INR  11,74,84,732 to INR 11,75,31,710. 
 The details as required under SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30 January 2026, with respect to above 
allotment is enclosed as Annexure A. 
 
The same will be made available on the Company’s website www.cleanmax.com. 
 
This is for your information, record, and appropriate dissemination. 
 
Thank you.
