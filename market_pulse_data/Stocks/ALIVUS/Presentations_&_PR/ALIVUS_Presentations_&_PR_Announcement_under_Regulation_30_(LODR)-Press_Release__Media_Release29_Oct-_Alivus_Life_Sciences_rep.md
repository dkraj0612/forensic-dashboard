Alivus Life Sciences Delivers 16.0% YoY Revenue Growth in Q2FY27 
EBITDA Margin at record high of 41.8% 
 
Mumbai, October 29, 2026: Alivus Life Sciences Limited (formerly Glenmark Life Sciences Limited), a 
leading developer and manufacturer of select, high-value, non-commoditized, Active Pharmaceutical 
Ingredients (APIs) in chronic therapeutic areas, today announced its financial results for the second 
quarter and half-year, ended September 30, 2026. 
 
Revenue from operations for Q2FY27 came at Rs. 6,820 Mn, a growth of 16.0% YoY, driven by 
broad-based momentum in the non-GPL business, which grew at 20.8% YoY. 
Revenue from operations for H1FY27 was Rs. 13,224 Mn, a growth of 11.1% YoY. 
 
EBITDA for Q2FY27 stood at Rs. 2,851 Mn, a growth of 47.0% YoY with margins at 41.8%, up 520 bps 
QoQ & 880 bps YoY, driven by favourable product mix, new launches, and the benefits of operating 
leverage. 
EBITDA for H1FY27 stood at Rs. 5,192 Mn, a growth of 38.4% YoY with margins at 39.3%, up 780 bps 
YoY. 
 
PAT for Q2FY27 stood at Rs. 1,968 Mn, a growth of 51.3% YoY. PAT margins were at 28.9%, up 390 bps 
QoQ & 680 bps YoY. 
PAT for H1FY27 stood at Rs. 3,569 Mn, a growth of 41.9% YoY. PAT margins were at 27.0%, up 590 bps 
YoY. 
 
The company generated a strong free cash flow of Rs. 2,702 Mn, leading to Cash and Cash Equivalents 
(including short-term investments) of Rs. 10,116 Mn as of September 30, 2026. 
 
Commenting on the company’s performance Dr. Yasir Rawjee, MD & CEO, Alivus Life Sciences Limited 
said, “Q2FY27 was another strong quarter for us, with revenue growing 16.0% YoY, supported by healthy 
demand across all key markets and robust contributions from recent launches. Non-GPL business momentum 
was maintained, while CDMO business saw a meaningful pick-up during the quarter. 
The improving business mix and greater operating efficiencies also translated into better underlying profitability. 
Looking ahead, our ongoing capacity expansion and healthy development pipeline give us confidence in 
sustaining this momentum and delivering consistent growth.” 
 
Tushar Mistry, CFO, Alivus Life Sciences Limited said, “We delivered a strong financial performance in 
Q2FY27, with healthy revenue growth, improved profitability and robust cash generation. Revenue grew 16.0% 
YoY, supported by a favourable product mix.  
Healthy operating performance translated into strong cash generation, with cash flow from operations of Rs. 
270 crores during H1FY27. With cash and cash equivalents of Rs. 1,012 crores as of September 30, 2026, we 
have ample financial flexibility to support our growth initiatives while maintaining a healthy, debt-free balance 
sheet. 
Going forward, we remain focused on sustaining profitable growth, maintaining financial discipline and creating 
long-term value.”

About Alivus Life Sciences Ltd (ALIVUS) 
Alivus Life Sciences Limited (Formerly Glenmark Life Sciences Limited) (BSE: 543322, NSE: ALIVUS), is a 
leading developer and manufacturer of select, high-value, non-commoditized, active pharmaceutical 
ingredients (APIs) in chronic therapeutic areas such as cardiovascular disease, central nervous system 
disease, pain management and diabetes. The company is increasingly providing CDMO services to a range 
of multinational and specialty pharmaceutical companies. It has a diversified portfolio of 183 molecules & 
supplies its products to customers in India, Europe, North America, Latin America, Japan, and the Rest of 
the World (RoW).  
 
The company’s four manufacturing facilities are located in Ankleshwar, Dahej, Mohol and Kurkumbh with 
a total installed capacity of 1,424 KL, which are regularly inspected by global regulators such as USFDA, 
PMDA (Japan) and EDQM (Europe). Alivus Life Sciences is listed on BSE and NSE.
