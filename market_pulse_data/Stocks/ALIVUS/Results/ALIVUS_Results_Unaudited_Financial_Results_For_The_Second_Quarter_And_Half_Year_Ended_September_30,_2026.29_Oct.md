labour codes comprising gratuity amounting to Rs. 170.02 million and compensated absences amounting to Rs. 
86.55 million, recognised as past service cost.

Brazil, on September 15, 2026. As at September 30, 2026, no investment has been made by the Company in 
the subsidiary, and the subsidiary has not commenced operations or undertaken any transactions. Accordingly, 
there were no financial transactions or balances of the subsidiary for the period requiring consolidation. In view 
of the above, the financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026 have been presented 
on a standalone basis.
