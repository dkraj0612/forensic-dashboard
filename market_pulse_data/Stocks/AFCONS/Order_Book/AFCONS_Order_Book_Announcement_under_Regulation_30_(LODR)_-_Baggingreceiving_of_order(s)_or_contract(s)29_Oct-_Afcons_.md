Afcons to build connecting Expressway for 
Jewar Airport & Underground Lucknow Metro 
Secures EPC orders worth ₹3,469 crores 
Mumbai: Afcons Infrastructure Ltd has secured two signiﬁcant engineering, 
procurement and construction (EPC) contracts in Uttar Pradesh with a combined value 
of nearly ₹3,469 crore, reinforcing its strong position in the transportation and urban 
infrastructure segment. The projects comprise the development of the Jewar Link 
Expressway and a key underground section of the Lucknow Metro Phase-1B. 
 
Mr Krishnamurthy Subramanian, Executive Chairman, Afcons, said, "The award of these 
prestigious projects reﬂects the conﬁdence that leading infrastructure agencies place in 
Afcons' engineering capabilities and execution excellence. The Jewar Link Expressway 
and the Lucknow Metro underground corridor are transformative projects that will 
enhance mobility, support economic growth, and improve quality of life for millions of 
people across Uttar Pradesh and NCR."  
 
Mr S Paramasivan, Managing Director, Afcons, said, "These wins further strengthen our 
order book and reinforce our commitment to delivering complex, nation-building 
infrastructure projects. With these two projects, the fresh orders secured in FY27 stands 
at ₹19,438 crores.  We look forward to partnering with our clients to deliver these projects 
eƯiciently and create long-term value for all stakeholders.”   
 
The Uttar Pradesh Expressways Industrial Development Authority (UPEIDA) has awarded 
the contract, valued at approximately ₹2,086 crore (excluding GST), for the construction 
of the 24.5-km access-controlled Greenﬁeld Jewar Link Expressway. The six-lane 
corridor, expandable to eight lanes, will connect the Noida International Airport at Jewar, 
creating an integrated high-speed transportation network across the state. 
 
The expressway is expected to signiﬁcantly enhance airport accessibility, reduce travel 
times, and improve logistics eƯiciency by linking two of Uttar Pradesh’s most important 
expressway corridors - Yamuna Expressway and Ganga Expressway. The project will 
support regional economic development while strengthening connectivity for 
passengers and freight traƯic across western and central Uttar Pradesh. 
 
In addition, Afcons has received notice of award for a major contract from Uttar Pradesh 
Metro Rail Corporation (UPMRC) for the design and construction of a 6.52-km 
underground stretch under the Lucknow Metro Phase-1B project. The scope includes 
seven underground metro stations, associated civil and architectural works, utility 
systems, ﬁre safety infrastructure, and connectivity between underground and elevated 
sections. The project value is approximately ₹1383 crore.
