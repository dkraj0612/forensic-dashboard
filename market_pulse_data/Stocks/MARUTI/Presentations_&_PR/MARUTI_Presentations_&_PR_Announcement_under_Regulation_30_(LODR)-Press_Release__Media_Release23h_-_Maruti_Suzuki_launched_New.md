Maruti Suzuki Launches New Brezza Turbo Boosterjet with Powerpacked Performance 
•
Envisioned to appeal to multiple personas, the New Brezza has been developed to appeal to today’s
dynamic and youthful customers, who expect their SUV to adapt to their multi-faceted lifestyles.
•
Designed for a young India that is evolving, aspirational and constantly on the move, the New Brezza
introduces a new dimension to India’s favourite compact SUV with a Bolder, More Youthful Exterior
Design, enhancing the Signature Brezza SUV stance.
•
Conceptualised with the idea of ‘Spontaneous Play’, the New Brezza Turbo Boosterjet with a 6-speed
manual transmission and Progressive Smart Hybrid Technology offers a power-packed performance.
•
New Brezza S-CNG customers can enjoy the full SUV experience with the new underbody CNG tank
configuration offering uncompromised boot space.
•
With its positioning of ‘Strategic Play’, the New Brezza with the Proven & Refined K15C engine with a
new 6-speed manual transmission for effortless urban and highway performance.
New Delhi, 24 July, 2026: Maruti Suzuki India Limited (Maruti Suzuki) announces the launch of the New 
Brezza Turbo Boosterjet, delivering exciting driving performance for young India. Created for today’s multi-
faceted customers, the New Brezza has been designed to appeal to their different personas in every walk 
of life. The New Brezza Turbo Boosterjet has been conceptualised with the idea of ‘Spontaneous Play’, 
engineered to deliver excitement for young trendsetters who seek playfulness and boldness in every walk 
of life. The New Brezza Turbo Boosterjet is available with introductory prices starting at INR 7 39 900.  
The New Brezza is also available with the Proven & Refined K15C Dual Jet Dual VVT engine with Progressive 
Smart Hybrid Technology. Catering to a strong legacy of over 1.4 million customers, New Brezza K15C 
features a new 6-speed manual transmission, alongside a 6-speed automatic transmission with steering-
mounted paddle shifters. The New Brezza K15C commands supremacy in its segment and finds favour 
amongst ambitious professional achievers with its positioning of ‘Strategic Play’, in sync with customers 
whose choices revolve around their goals and aspirations.

Commenting on the introduction of the New Brezza, Mr. Partho Banerjee, Senior Executive Officer, 
Marketing & Sales, Maruti Suzuki India Limited, said, “The New Brezza, is an SUV that truly resonates with 
the aspirations of young India. With the New Brezza Turbo Boosterjet we add a new dimension of driving 
excitement that matches the energy and vibe of Young India. The New Brezza has been curated to find 
favour with diverse customer preferences as it switches effortlessly to appeal to the different moods and 
occasions of customers. It is an SUV that appeals to every side of a customer’s personality, be it at work or 
play, appealing to various aspects of their lifestyles and enabling them to ‘Lead Every Play’. This is the true 
hallmark of a great product, one that adapts to customers. I am confident the New Brezza will continue to 
build on the brand’s legacy as India’s favourite compact SUV.” 
Power-Packed Performance – Engineered to deliver youthful, energetic performance, the New Brezza 
Turbo Boosterjet is designed to excite customers and turn every drive into a power-packed performance. 
With a maximum power output of 81 kW @ 5500 rpm and 170 Nm of torque @ 2000–3500 rpm, the New 
Brezza Turbo Boosterjet delivers powerful performance with excellent fuel-efficiency of 20.47 km/l^. 
Complementing the powerful performance of the New Brezza Turbo Boosterjet is an all-new 6-speed manual 
transmission for enhancing driver engagement and control. 
Developed to appeal to a wide demographic of customers, the New Brezza is available with a range of 
powertrain offerings to suit varied customer preferences and tastes. The Proven & Refined K15C engine 
with Dual Jet Dual VVT & Progressive Smart Hybrid technology offers enhanced drivability and lower NVH 
with the introduction of a new 6-speed manual transmission. The new 6-speed transmission enables 
smoother urban and highway driving experience. Catering to a strong legacy of over 1.4 million customers, 
the New Brezza K15C is also available with the choice of a 6-speed automatic transmission with steering-
mounted paddle shifters. The New Brezza K15C with 6-speed transmission options offers effortless 
performance with lower NVH levels and a fuel-efficiency of 21.09 km/l^.
