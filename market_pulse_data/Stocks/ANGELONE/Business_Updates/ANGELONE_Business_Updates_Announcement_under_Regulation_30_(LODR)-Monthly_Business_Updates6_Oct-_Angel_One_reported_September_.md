FY27 Q2 underscored the underlying depth of our user franchise amid a challenging market environment. Although subdued market 
activity led to a sequential moderation in average daily orders, our core business metrics remained healthy. We maintained our 
turnover market share in equity derivatives, expanded the client funding book to a record high, and sustained robust SIP momentum. 
In the commodities segment, ADTO grew 105.5% YoY, although our market share softened as the market expanded significantly. 
 
While cash market share moderated in September 2026, exit trends improved, with sequential growth in average daily orders and 
turnover market share across overall equities and equity derivatives.
