Angel One Limited
Commenting on Angel One’s performance, Mr. Dinesh Thakkar, Chairman & Managing Director 
said, “India is witnessing one of the largest wealth creation opportunities in its history, and 
technology is making financial services more accessible than ever before. We believe the next 
decade will belong to platforms that combine innovation, trust and execution at scale.
Angel One is evolving into an AI-powered financial platform designed to serve clients across 
trading and investing, wealth, credit and global markets. Every product we build, every capability 
we add and every investment we make is guided by a simple objective: helping clients make better 
financial decisions and achieve their aspirations.
Our ambition is to build a platform that becomes an indispensable part of every client's financial 
journey. With strong foundations, multiple growth engines and a relentless focus on client 
outcomes, we are confident in our ability to create enduring value while contributing meaningfully 
to India's financial future.
Commenting on Angel One’s performance, Mr. Ambarish Kenghe, Group CEO said, “The second 
quarter reinforced the resilience of our client franchise despite significant changes in India’s 
market structure including revised bank funding norms and introduction of the Closing Auction 
Session in cash equities. While these changes temporarily impacted broking activity, client 
engagement recovered steadily, with our average daily orders reaching 5.8 million in September 
2026, approaching pre-CAS levels. More importantly participation deepened as our average client 
funding book reached a record high of ₹ 74.5 billion, growing by 40.5% year-on-year.
Customer preference remained strong across our platform. We increased our share of retail equity 
derivatives turnover to 22.1%, up 43 basis points year-on-year, while commodity turnover more 
than doubled.
We continue to evolve our platform into a broader financial-services ecosystem. During the 
quarter, we began the early beta of Loan Against Securities within our NBFC and will scale the 
product with discipline and responsibility. Supported by our AI and ML-powered underwriting and 
risk models, monthly credit distribution reached a record high in September 2026. We also 
received authorization to operate as a Global Access Provider in the IFSC, creating a pathway to 
offer customers investment opportunities beyond domestic markets.
Combined with a standalone operating margin of 48.9%, above our guided range, these 
investments position us well to deepen customer relationships across investing, borrowing and 
wealth creation, while capitalizing on India’s long-term financialization opportunity.”
