LCL operations: 
 
LCL volume stood at 667 thousand cubic meters in October 2026, down 3% year-on-year and 
month-on-month. The decline witnessed is linked to the gradual easing of peak season 
demand.  
 
Global fleet remains tied up due to port congestion, which is leading to pressure on capacity, 
which in turn has kept the rates well above same period last year. 
 
On a year-on-year basis volume increased across North America, while there was a decline in 
other regions.
