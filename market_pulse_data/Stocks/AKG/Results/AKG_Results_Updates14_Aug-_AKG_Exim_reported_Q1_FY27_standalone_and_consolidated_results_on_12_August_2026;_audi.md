### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> AKG Exim Limited's Q1 FY2027 consolidated financial results show a significant year-over-year revenue decline, dropping from 2,855.54 Lakhs to 2,071.84 Lakhs. Despite this, net profit increased to 8.85 Lakhs (from 7.77 Lakhs), primarily driven by a favorable change in inventories, largely offsetting a near-complete disappearance of other income compared to the prior year. While the auditor issued a clean limited review report, the substantial revenue contraction and the reliance on inventory adjustments for profit improvement, alongside the vanishing other income, suggest underlying operational pressures.
