Sub: Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 29(2) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and 
Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 in relation to Ambuja Cements Limited 
As you would note, we had made disclosures on 25 October 2023, 27 August 2024 and 30 September 
2024 under Regulation 29(2) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) 
Regulations, 2011 in relation to certain facilities availed by Endeavour Trade and Investment Ltd pursuant 
to the facilities agreement dated 20 October 2023 entered into, inter alia, between (a) Endeavour Trade 
and Investment Ltd (as the borrower) (“Endeavour”), (b) the lenders thereunder, (c) Deutsche Bank AG, 
Hong Kong Branch (as agent) and (d) Deutsche Bank AG, Hong Kong Branch (as security agent) , as may 
be amended, novated, supplemented, extended or restated from time to time (“Original Facilities 
Agreement”), as more particularly described therein as well as under Annexure A hereto. 
Endeavour has now entered into a facility agreement dated 21 October 2026, the proceeds from which will 
be used, inter alia, to refinance and repay one of the facilities availed under the Original Facilities Agreement 
(being the “Original Facility C”), as more particularly set out in Annexure A hereto.  
In light of the foregoing, pursuant to Regulation 29(2) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares 
and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, please find attached the disclosure (as set out in Annexure A) in 
respect of the encumbrance over certain equity shares of Ambuja Cements Limited for the benefit of certain 
lenders and other finance parties, as described in Annexure A.
