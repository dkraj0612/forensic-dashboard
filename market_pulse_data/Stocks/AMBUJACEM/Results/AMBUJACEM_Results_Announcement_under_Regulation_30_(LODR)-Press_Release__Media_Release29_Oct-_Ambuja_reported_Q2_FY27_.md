EDITOR’S SYNOPSIS 
 
Consolidated Financial Highlights Q2 FY27: 
 Cement sales volume stood at 16.5 MnT during the quarter; trade volumes increased by 
8.6 % YoY & trade share increased by 6 pp to 74% YoY and premium products rose by 2 
pp to 37% of trade sales. 
 Revenue from operations Rs 9,197 Cr, with Operating EBITDA of Rs 1,271 Cr, EBITDA 
margin of 13.8% and EBITDA per tonne of Rs 770. 
Business Updates: 
 Commissioned in Q2 (4.6 MTPA): Dahej (1.2 MTPA), Salai Banwa (2.4 MTPA), Kalamboli 
(1.0 MTPA). 
 Stabilised & Ramping Up in H2 (3.2 MTPA): Bathinda (1.2 MTPA) and Jodhpur Penna (2 
MTPA); expected commissioning in H2 (2.4 MTPA); Warisaliganj (2.4 MTPA). 
One Cement Platform: 
 Ambuja shareholders approved the proposed amalgamations of ACC and Orient Cement 
with Ambuja Cements, advancing the One Cement Platform; the schemes remain subject 
to requisite approvals and are expected to be completed in FY’27. 
Ahmedabad, 29 October 2026: Ambuja Cements Limited, part of the diversified Adani 
Portfolio, announced its result for the quarter ended 30th September 2026.  
Mr. Vinod Bahety, Whole-time Director and CEO, Ambuja Cements Limited, said, “Our 
Q2 and H1 FY’27 performance underscores Ambuja’s focus on quality growth despite 
seasonal and external headwinds. Strengthening trade and premium product mix 
improved revenue quality, while disciplined execution and cost management supported 
profitability. 
We continue to strengthen the structural drivers of earnings through manufacturing 
excellence, energy efficiency, logistics optimisation and digitalisation, supported by a 
debt-free balance sheet. Our growth journey continues, with 4.6 MTPA commissioned 
in Q2 and another 5.6 MTPA ramping up in H2 FY’27.  
The implementation of BCFC infrastructure, a higher share of renewable power, the 
ramp-up of new capacities and their additional incentives are expected to support 
performance in H2 FY’27.  
We are on track achieving our cost target of Rs 4,250 per tonne by FY’27. Our growth 
remains trade-led, in line with our strategy, while we continue to strengthen the 
institutional business through a comprehensive solutions approach with a focus on 
value and profitability. Volumes are expected to grow ahead of the industry in H2 FY’27, 
supported by accelerated trade volumes and calibrated institutional sales. Following 
the recent Rs 10-15 price increase, the industry expects further pricing improvement 
over the next couple of months.

Industry Outlook: 
Looking ahead, near-term conditions remain challenging amid geopolitical conflicts, 
global uncertainty and higher energy costs, following the monsoon-related headwinds 
during the quarter. At the same time, structural demand remains supported by 
infrastructure spending, urbanisation, rural housing and industrial activity. 
With our focus on quality growth, premiumisation, operational efficiency, disciplined 
capital deployment and progress towards the One Cement Platform, we remain well 
positioned to deliver sustainable, value-led growth. 
Business Performance: 
Elevated fuel and logistics costs, driven by higher petcoke and coal prices, constrained 
coal availability, and increased diesel and packaging costs, continue to weigh on 
industry margins. Ambuja prioritised market discipline, improved product mix and 
sustainable profitability. 
Logistics costs were moderately higher YoY due to higher diesel costs, partly offset by 
the reduction in primary lead from 265 km to 254 km. Planned shutdowns at major 
plants have also been successfully completed. Total cost in Q2 stood at Rs 4,410 per 
tonne, and excluding the impact of geopolitical conflicts, at Rs 4,182 per tonne.

About Ambuja Cements Limited  
Ambuja Cements Limited is the 9th largest cement company globally, a key part of the diversified Adani 
Portfolio – the country’s fastest-growing portfolio of sustainable businesses. With a cement capacity of 
113.5 MTPA across 24 integrated manufacturing plants and 24 grinding units, Ambuja Cements is at the 
forefront of building a greener, stronger India. The Company is accelerating its decarbonisation journey 
through investments in 1 GW of renewable energy (solar + wind), 376 MW of Waste Heat Recovery Systems 
(WHRS) by FY’28, a partnership with Leilac Limited for low-carbon cement production and a partnership 
with Coolbrook to deploy zero-carbon RotoDynamic Heater™ (RDH™) technology. Ambuja Cements 
continues to remain water positive and plastic negative. It is committed to net-zero by 2050, being 
amongst the four large-scale building materials companies in the world, with its near-term and net-zero 
targets validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi). Its blended cement products are GreenPro 
certified, and its innovative products are listed in the GRIHA product catalogue. The Company operates a 
captive port network with ten terminals for cleaner, cost-effective bulk cement shipments. Recognised 
among ‘India’s Most Trusted Cement Brand’ by TRA Research, Ambuja Cements is the world’s first cement 
manufacturer to join the Alliance for Industrial Decarbonization (AFID), a global alliance facilitated by 
IRENA and a member of LeadIT, first global high-level initiative on reaching net-zero emissions from heavy 
industry.
