Media Release 
Adani Cement Flags Off Heavy-Duty Electric Trucks 
for Clinker & Cement Transport 
EDITOR'S SYNOPSIS 
• 
Adani Cement to deploy 410 EV trucks by March 2027 as 1st Phase plan for cement & clinker 
movement. 
• 
Deployment commenced at Sanghi Plant in Gujarat. 
• 
410-truck fleet is projected to reduce over 25,000 tonnes of CO₂ annually. 
• 
Targeting 10% logistics road volume through EV trucks by 2030, with deployment of 2000+ 
EVs. 
• 
Renewable-powered EV trucks enable zero freight emissions, supporting Scope 3 emission 
reduction and our net-zero goals. 
Ahmedabad, 18th November 2026: Adani Cement, part of the Adani Portfolio and one of 
India's leading building materials solutions providers, flagged off Heavy-Duty Electric 
Trucks at its Sanghi Plant in Gujarat. This is the first-ever deployment of EV trucks by Adani 
Cement and a defining milestone in the company's sustainability and green logistics 
journey.  
Mr. Vinod Bahety, CEO – Cement Business, Adani Group, said: "The deployment at Sanghi 
is just the beginning. We have identified multiple routes across India for EV-based 
transport of clinker and cement. Every truck we electrify reduces our carbon footprint and 
insulates our logistics cost base from diesel price volatility. This is smart sustainability — 
it benefits the people, planet and the balance sheet. This is our commitment in action — 
to build a greener, more competitive cement company that serves India's growth while 
protecting its future." 
The economic case for heavy-duty EV trucks in India's cement sector has reached a 
structural tipping point. Over the past decade, diesel prices rose 60%, while Li-ion battery 
pack costs fell by approximately 70% and solar tariffs declined 47%. 
Diesel trucks emit approximately 0.7375 Kg CO₂ per km and when powered by renewable 
energy, EV trucks can eliminate up to 100% of these freight emissions, making logistics 
electrification a powerful lever for Scope 3 decarbonisation. Adani Cement's 410-truck 
fleet is projected to reduce over 25,000 tonnes of CO₂ annually, displacing the equivalent 
of 11 million litres of diesel per year, with plans to expand across additional routes and 
plants. 
India is the world's second-largest cement producer, and the movement of limestone, 
clinker, and finished cement across vast supply chains is an essential, but carbon-intensive, 
part of the sector's operations. Adani Cement's EV fleet deployment addresses both the 
cost and climate dimensions of this challenge directly.

About Adani Cement 
Adani Cement, comprising Ambuja Cements Limited and its subsidiaries ACC Limited and Orient Cement 
Limited, is the building materials solutions business of the Adani Group. With 113.5 MTPA of installed capacity, 
it offers a broad portfolio of cement, concrete and specialised building solutions backed by advanced R&D and 
technical services. Adani Cement is committed to net zero by 2050, with near-term and net-zero targets 
validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi). Its decarbonisation initiatives include investments in 
1 GW of renewable energy, 376 MW of Waste Heat Recovery Systems by FY’28, and partnerships with Leilac 
and Coolbrook to advance low- and zero-carbon cement technologies. The business is water positive and 
plastic negative; its blended cements are GreenPro certified and its innovative products feature in the GRIHA 
product catalogue. Adani Cement is also the world’s first cement manufacturer to join the Alliance for 
Industrial Decarbonization and is a member of LeadIT. Adani Cement’s mission is to build a stronger nation by 
delivering quality, innovation, and reliability in construction materials, supported by extensive technical 
services and a customer-centric approach. For further information on this release, please contact:
