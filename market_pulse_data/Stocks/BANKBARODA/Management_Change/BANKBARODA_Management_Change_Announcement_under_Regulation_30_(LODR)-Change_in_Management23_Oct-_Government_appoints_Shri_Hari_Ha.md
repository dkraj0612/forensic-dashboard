  Madam/Dear Sir, 
   
  Re: Bank of Baroda – Change in Government of India’s Nominee Director 
 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, we hereby report change in GOI Nominee 
Director on the Board of Bank of Baroda as under:- 
 
1 
Nature of Change 
Nomination of Shri Hari Har Mishra as GOI Nominee Director on the Board 
of Bank of Baroda vice Shri Ashish Madhaorao More (Existing GOI Nominee 
Director on the Board of Bank of Baroda) 
 
2 
Reason  for Change 
In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (3) of section 
9 of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 
1970, the Central Government, vide notification No. eF.No.6/2/2022-BO.I/1 
dated 23.10.2026 has nominated Shri Hari Har Mishra (Additional Secretary, 
Government of India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Financial Services) 
as Director on the Board of Bank of Baroda, with immediate effect and until 
further orders vice Shri Ashish Madhaorao More. 
 
3 
Date of Appointment  
 
23.10.2026 
 
4 
Brief Profile 
Shri Hari Har Mishra, aged 54 years, is an officer of the Indian Defence 
Accounts Service (IDAS) of 1997 batch and is presently serving as Additional 
Secretary in the Department of Financial Services. 
 
Shri Hari Har Mishra is a Post Graduate in Archaeology from Allahabad 
University and holds an MBA (Finance) from the National Institute of Financial 
Management, Faridabad. 
 
During his distinguished career, he has held several key assignments such 
as IFA, HQ Training Command (Air Force), Joint CGDA (HRD), Joint CGDA 
(Offset Audit), Chief Vigilance Officer for Defence Accounts Department, 
Director in Ministry of Environment & Forests and Additional Mission Director 
in Ministry of Ganga Rejuvenation. 
 
He possesses over 28 years of rich and diverse experience in financial 
management, audit, vigilance, policy formulation, expenditure management, 
human resource development and public administration across various 
Ministries and Departments of the Government of India, including the Ministry 
of Defence, Ministry of Environment & Forests and Ministry of Ganga 
Rejuvenation. 
 
He has been the recipient of several commendations and awards, including 
Raksha Mantri Award for overall performance and Chief of Army Staff 
Commendation and others.
