Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, it is hereby informed that the Company has 
received a Show Cause Notice dated September 28, 2026 from the Office of the Assistant 
Commissioner, CGST, Gurugram in relation to the financial year 2022-23. The relevant details pursuant 
to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with the applicable SEBI Circulars are annexed

was made by the Company on November 22, 2024.  
 
In the said writ, the Hon’ble High Court vide its interim order stayed the further proceedings in the 
concerned matter. The Company is in the process of filing a writ before the Hon’ble High Court of 
Punjab & Haryana, Chandigarh in relation to the aforesaid Show Cause Notice dated September 28, 
2026 and to connect the matter with the previous writ. 
 
In respect of the captioned matter, we hereby also state and declare that the information and details 
provided in Annexure A, in compliance with Regulation 30(13) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, are
