### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Artemis Medicare Services Ltd. has successfully reaffirmed its credit ratings with CARE Ratings Limited, maintaining strong investment-grade ratings of 'CARE A; Stable' for long-term facilities and 'CARE A; Stable / CARE A2+' for long/short-term facilities. No red flags are present, reinforcing the company's healthy credit profile.
