Artemis’ Consolidated Revenue from Operations up 13.2% 
in Q2 FY27 (YoY); Adj. EBITDA* up 22.7%; EBITDA up 7.9%; 
Net Profit up 3.7% 
 
October 30, 2026: The Board of Directors of Artemis Medicare Services Ltd today approved the 
company’s financial results for the Q2 FY27. 
 
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Artemis Medicare Services Ltd, on a consolidated 
level, registered a revenue from operations of Rs 31,104 Lacs, an increase of 13.2% over the 
same period last fiscal year.  
 
YoY Artemis Hospital Gurgaon Performance Highlights 
Q2 FY27 vs Q2 FY26 
• Revenue from operations up by 14.5% at Rs 29,844 Lacs, against Rs 26,059 Lacs  
• EBITDA of Rs 7,096 Lacs against Rs 5,828 Lacs, growth of 21.8% 
• PAT of Rs 4,043 Lacs against Rs 3,210 Lacs, growth of 25.9%  
• Bed capacity utilisation stood at 68.1% against 64.1% 
• Average revenue per occupied bed (ARPOB) at Rs 86,631, against Rs 81,248 
• Revenue from overseas patients increased by 7.6% to Rs 8,877 Lacs from Rs 8,249 Lacs, 
contributing 29.7% to Revenue from operations  
 
YoY Consolidated Performance Highlights 
Q2 FY27 vs Q2 FY26 
• Revenue from operations up by 13.2% at Rs 31,104 Lacs, against Rs 27,470 Lacs 
• EBITDA of Rs 6,289 Lacs against Rs 5,827 Lacs, growth of 7.9% 
• Adjusted EBITDA* of Rs 7,150 Lacs against Rs 5,827 Lacs, growth of 22.7% 
• PAT of Rs 3,110 Lacs against Rs 3,000 Lacs, growth of 3.7%  
• Diluted EPS of Rs 1.96 against Rs 1.89, growth of 3.7% 
 
QoQ Artemis Hospital Gurgaon Performance Highlights 
Q2 FY27 vs Q1 FY27 
 
• Revenue from operations up by 7.2% at Rs 29,844 Lacs, against Rs 27,849 Lacs 
• EBITDA of Rs 7,096 Lacs against Rs 6,163 Lacs, growth of 15.1% 
• PAT of Rs 4,043 Lacs against Rs 3,306 Lacs, growth of 22.3%  
• Bed capacity utilisation stood at 68.1% against 65.7% 
• Average revenue per occupied bed (ARPOB) at Rs 86,631 against Rs 85,690 
• Revenue from overseas patients increased by 16.0% to Rs 8,877 Lacs from Rs 7,651 Lacs, 
contributing 29.7% to Revenue from operations 
 
QoQ Consolidated Performance Highlights 
Q2 FY27 vs Q1 FY27 
• Revenue from operations up by 8.3% at Rs 31,104 Lacs, against Rs 28,732 Lacs 
• EBITDA of Rs 6,289 Lacs against Rs 6,182 Lacs, growth of 1.7% 
• Adjusted EBITDA* of Rs 7,150 Lacs against Rs 6,182 Lacs, growth of 15.7% 
• PAT of Rs 3,110 Lacs against Rs 3,144 Lacs, degrowth of 1.1%  
• Diluted EPS of Rs. 1.96 against Rs 1.98, degrowth of 1.0% 
 
* Adj. EBITDA excluding the performance of Raipur Unit launched in July-2026

## Page 3

Media Release 
 
 
 
 
Commenting on the company’s performance, Mr. Onkar Kanwar, Chairman, Artemis 
Medicare Services Ltd said “We have delivered a steady performance in the second quarter 
of FY27, with consolidated revenue growing 13.0% year-on-year. This reflects the resilience of 
our business model, sustained demand for quality healthcare services, and our continued 
focus on disciplined execution. 
The commencement of operations at our Raipur hospital in July 2026 marks an important 
milestone in Artemis’ growth journey, expanding our presence into new markets and extending 
access to advanced healthcare services beyond our established Gurugram centre. As we 
build out our healthcare platform, we remain committed to a calibrated approach to 
expansion, with a focus on clinical excellence, operational sustainability, and prudent capital 
allocation. 
Looking ahead, we will continue to strengthen our core operations while progressing our 
strategic expansion plans. With a clear long-term vision and an unwavering commitment to 
quality healthcare, we remain focused on building a resilient healthcare platform that creates 
sustainable value for our patients, employees, stakeholders, and shareholders.” 
Dr. Devlina Chakravarty, Managing Director, Artemis Medicare Services Ltd mentioned “The 
second quarter of FY27 reflects continued momentum in our core Gurugram operations, 
supported by healthy revenue growth, improved occupancy, and stronger operating 
profitability. The hospital recorded year-on-year revenue growth of 14.5%, while Ops. EBITDA 
and PAT grew by 26.3% and 25.9%, respectively. Improved bed occupancy of 68.1% and 
higher average revenue per occupied bed further demonstrate the strength of our operating 
performance and the sustained demand for our specialised clinical services. 
At the consolidated level, revenue from operations grew 13.2% year-on-year, while Adjusted 
EBITDA* increased by 22.7%, reflecting underlying operating progress. Our priorities remain 
centred on enhancing operational efficiencies, maintaining disciplined cost management, 
strengthening clinical capabilities, and delivering consistently high standards of patient care. 
With the commencement of operations at our Raipur hospital in July 2026, we have entered 
an important new phase of expansion. Our focus is now on progressively building clinical 
capabilities, expanding specialised services, and establishing the facility as a trusted 
destination for advanced healthcare in Central India. Going forward, we will continue to 
strengthen our existing operations and pursue measured expansion, with the objective of 
building a future-ready healthcare platform that delivers superior clinical outcomes and 
sustainable long-term value for all our stakeholders.”
