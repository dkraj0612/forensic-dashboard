BLS Polymers Reports ~131% YoY Growth in PAT to ₹10.54 Crore in 
Q2 FY27 
• Q2 FY27, Operating Revenue y-o-y grew 28.92% to ₹125.56 Cr 
• Q2 FY27, EBITDA rose 92.98% y-o-y to ₹17.31 Cr and PAT 130.63% to ₹10.54 Cr 
• H1 FY27, Operating Revenue increased 35.63% y-o-y to ₹232.59 Cr  
• H1 FY27, EBITDA grew 41.71% to ₹33.57 Cr and PAT 51.16% to ₹20.77 Cr y-o-y 
* All reported numbers are on a consolidated basis 
New Delhi, November 18, 2026: BLS Polymers Limited (NSE: BLSPOLY, BSE: 545011), a manufacturer of 
multiple grades of custom-formulated polymer compounds catering to critical industries such as 
telecommunications, power, railways, water, and oil and gas distribution, today announced its unaudited financial 
results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. 
The Company reported a strong financial performance for Q2 FY27, with Operating Revenue increasing by 
28.92% YoY to ₹125.56 crore, compared to ₹97.39 crore in Q2 FY26. EBITDA witnessed a significant growth 
of 92.98% YoY to ₹17.31 crore, as against ₹8.97 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. Profit 
After Tax (PAT) more than doubled, registering a growth of 130.63% YoY to ₹10.54 crore, compared to ₹4.57 
crore in Q2 FY26. 
For H1 FY27, Operating Revenue grew by 35.63% YoY to ₹232.59 crore, compared to ₹171.49 crore in H1 FY26. 
EBITDA increased by 41.71% YoY to ₹33.57 crore, against ₹23.69 crore in the corresponding period of the 
previous year, reflecting improved operating performance. PAT registered a growth of 51.16% YoY to ₹20.77 
crore, compared to ₹13.74 crore in H1 FY26, while EPS improved to ₹4.62 from ₹3.04. The overall performance 
was supported by healthy revenue growth, an emphasis on value-added products and continued efforts towards 
enhancing manufacturing efficiencies and strengthening the product portfolio.

Business Performance and Growth Outlook 
BLS Polymers remains focused on strengthening its market position through capacity expansion, operational 
efficiencies and a greater emphasis on value-added products. The proposed investment in expanding the 
manufacturing capacity of select existing products is expected to enhance production capabilities and enable the 
Company to address evolving customer requirements. Going forward, the Company will continue to focus on 
optimising its product mix, strengthening customer relationships and improving working capital management to 
support sustainable growth. 
About BLS Polymers Limited 
Established in 1991, BLS Polymers Limited manufactures multiple grades of custom-formulated polymer 
compounds for critical industries, including telecommunications, power, railways, water, and oil and gas 
distribution. Its diversified product portfolio includes HDPE, MDPE, LLDPE, XLPE, HFFR, PVC, cable filling 
compounds and adhesive coating compounds. The Company operates a manufacturing facility in Goa, spread 
across approximately 24,220 square metres, with a current installed capacity of 44,339.20 MTPA. Equipped with 
specialised manufacturing machinery, the facility is strategically located near Goa Port (Mormugao), facilitating 
the sourcing of imported raw materials and exports to international markets. BLS Polymers has a presence across 
23 states and union territories in India and exports its products to 13 countries. The Company is part of the BLS 
Group, which has an operating history of over three decades across diverse business sectors.
