Dear Sir / Madam, 
In compliance with the provisions of Regulation 30(6) read with Para A(1) of Part A of 
Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the Company, on 24th
September, 2026, has acquired additional 2,00,00,000 Equity Shares of face value of 
Rs.1 each at a price of Rs. 10 each of Ecosis Limited (Ecosis), Wholly Owned Subsidiary 
for an aggregate amount of Rs. 20.00 crore. Till date, the Company has invested around 
Rs.85 crores in the said subsidiary Company. 
The said investment amount will be primarily used to finance the ongoing Polyester 
Textiles Recycling Project, Repayment of existing loan taken from Filatex and Working 
capital requirements. 
The transaction is between the Company and its wholly owned subsidiary and hence a 
related party transaction. It is on arm's length basis. None of the Company's promoter / 
promoter group / other group companies have any interest in the above transaction. 
No governmental or regulatory approval is required for the above acquisition. 
This is for information and records.
