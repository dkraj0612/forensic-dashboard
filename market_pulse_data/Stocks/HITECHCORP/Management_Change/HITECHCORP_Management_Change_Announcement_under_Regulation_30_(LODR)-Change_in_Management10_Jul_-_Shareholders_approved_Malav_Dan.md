In accordance with Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("Listing Regulations") and other 
applicable provisions of Listing Regulations, as amended from time to time, and in continuation of our 
intimation dated June 10, 2026, approval of the shareholders of the Company was sought by Postal 
Ballot for the re-appointment of Mr. Malav Dani (DIN: 01184336) as Managing Director of the 
Company, subject to Central Government approval and appointment of Mr. Mehli Golvala (DIN: 
02234105) as an Independent Non-Executive Director. The results of the Postal Ballot were announced 
today and shareholders approved, by requisite majority, the aforesaid resolutions.

Circular ref no. LIST/COMP/14/2018-19 and NSE Circular ref no. NSE/CML/2018/24 dated June 20, 
2018. Further, as affirmed by them, they are not disqualified from holding the office as a Director 
pursuant to the provisions of Section 164 of the Companies Act, 2013.
