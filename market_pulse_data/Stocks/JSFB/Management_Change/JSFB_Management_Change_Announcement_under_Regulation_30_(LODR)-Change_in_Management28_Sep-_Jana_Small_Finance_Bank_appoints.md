Sub: Appointment of new Head of Internal Audit and retirement of current Head of Internal Audit - Change in Senior 
Management Personnel (SMP) 
Ref: Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), this is to inform the changes in the 
Senior Management Personnel of Jana Small Finance Bank Limited (“the Bank”). 
1. Mr. Ramachandran, currently working as Head of Internal Audit has expressed his intention to retire from 
the services after seven (7) years of service in the bank and after attaining the age of 67 years. Accordingly, 
he will relinquish charge as Head of Internal Audit immediately and will subsequently retire from the bank 
on 2nd December 2026.  
2. Mr. Rajeev K, a seasoned banker having about 30 years of total experience, including extensive exposure 
across audit themes relevant to the Bank's business joins the bank as Head of Internal Audit effective 
September 28, 2026 and will take over from Mr. Ramachandran. Prior to joining Jana Bank, Mr. Rajeev 
has worked for Axis Bank, ICICI Bank and State Bank of India. Rajeev has done his Masters of Business 
Administration from ICFAI University, Masters of Commerce from Annamalai University and Bachelors of 
Commerce from Calicut University.  
3. The Audit & Compliance Committee of the Board (ACB), Nomination & Remuneration Committee (NRC) 
have approved the appointment on 11th August 2026; and the said agenda has been approved by the 
Board in its meeting held on 13th August 2026. 
Further, the disclosure as required under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations read 
with SEBI Master Circular having reference no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/023/120 dated July 11, 2023, and 
SEBI Circular having reference no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, and other 
applicable circulars, is provided in Annexure-A. 
Kindly take the same on record and oblige. 
Thank you
