Dear Sir/ Ma’am,  
With reference to our earlier intimation dated November 14, 2025, wherein the Company had intimated the acquisition of 
27% equity stake in M/s. Sharvesh Power Projects Private Limited and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Clause 1 of 
Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish 
to inform that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, has approved the disinvestment of the 
entire 27% equity shareholding held by the Company in M/s. Sharvesh Power Projects Private Limited, pursuant to the 
rescission of the Power Purchase Agreement entered into between the Company and M/s. Sharvesh Power Projects Private 
Limited. 
The aforesaid investment was made in connection with the Power Purchase Agreement for procurement of electricity for 
the Company's business operations. Consequent upon the rescission of the said Power Purchase Agreement, the underlying 
purpose for which the investment was made no longer subsists. Accordingly, the Board has approved the disinvestment of 
the Company's entire 27% equity shareholding in M/s. Sharvesh Power Projects Private Limited. 
The required details under SEBI Circular no. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are 
enclosed herewith as Annexure-A.
