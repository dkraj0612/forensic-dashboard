Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that Almondz Global 
Infra - Consultant Limited (“AGICL”), a Step-Down Subsidiary of the Company, has more

AGICL New Business; New Orders Cross Rs. 150 Crore 
Avonmore Capital’ infrastructure consultancy step down subsidiary secures Rs. 154 crores of 
new order book in the 1st Half year as on 30th September 2026, against Rs. 72 crores in the 1st 
Half year in the previous; orders to be executed over three years 
New Delhi, 12th October 2026: Avonmore Capital & Management Services Limited (AGSL) today 
announces that its step-down subsidiary, Almondz Global Infra Consultant Limited (AGICL), has 
secured more than double new order book in the 1st Half year 2026-27 as compared with the 1st 
Half year 2025-26. AGICL has secured new order book of Rs. 154 crores in the 1st Half year as on 
30th September 2026, as against Rs. 72 crores in the 1st Half year as on 30 September 2025, a 
growth of about 214%. With this, AGICL’s Total orders book have crossed the Rs. 400 crores mark 
as on 30th September 2026. 
The new orders will be executed over a period of three years, giving the Company a clear, multi-
year pipeline of work and strong visibility on delivery and revenue as these mandates are 
mobilized.

A diversified order inflow 
The new business spans highways, bridges, railways, urban and industrial infrastructure, and an 
overseas mandate, and deepens AGICL’s presence in eastern India and Uttar Pradesh. Some of 
select mandates secured during the year in H1 2026-27 include: 
• IDCO, Odisha: Programme Management Consultant for various IDCO projects, valued at 
approximately Rs. 33.15 crore (excluding GST) over two years, the highest-value sole bid 
won by AGICL to date. 
• Mission 3000 MT traction power works, West Central Railway: Authority Engineer 
assignment valued at Rs. 21.23 crore. 
• Kulgaon Badlapur Municipal Council, Maharashtra: DPR and PMC for conservation of the 
Ulhas River, flood control and disaster management, for Rs. 15 crore.
