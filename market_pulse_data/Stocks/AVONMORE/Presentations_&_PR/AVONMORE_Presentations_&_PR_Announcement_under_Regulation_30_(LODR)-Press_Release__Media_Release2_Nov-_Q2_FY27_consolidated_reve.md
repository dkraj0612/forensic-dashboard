PRESS RELEASE 
New Delhi, November 02, 2026 
Subject: Financial Performance Update for 2Q FY 2026-27 and Business 
Outlook for 3Q FY 2026-27 
Avonmore Capital & Management Services Limited (“the Company”) is pleased to 
provide an update on its consolidated financial performance for the second 
quarter (2Q) of the financial year 2026-27, together with its guidance for the 
upcoming third quarter (3Q) of FY 2026-27. 
Consolidated Financial Highlights 
The Company has reported consolidated total revenue of Rs. 44.36 crore with a 
profit of Rs. 10.84 crore for 2Q FY 2026-27, as compared to total revenue of Rs. 
37.04 crore with a profit of Rs. 5.99 crore in 2Q FY 2025-26.

Performance Overview – Financial Services 
During 2Q FY 2026-27, the Company achieved total revenue of Rs. 9.93 crore

Performance Overview – Green Fuel Business 
The Company is engaged in the production of green biofuel and distillery 
operations through its joint venture company, Premier Green Innovations Private 
Limited (PGIPL), in which the Company holds 40.99% of the total equity. The 
holding company, Avonmore Capital & Management Services Limited, also holds 
an 8.88% equity stake in PGIPL. 
During 2Q FY 2026-27, PGIPL recorded total revenue of Rs. 202.24 crore and a 
profit of Rs. 12.59 crore, as compared to revenue of Rs. Rs. 129.90 crore and a 
profit of Rs. 3.66 crore in 2Q FY 2025-26.

The capacity of the Himachal Plant is 285 KLPD and is operational at full capacity. 
Status of Odisha Plant – PGIPL 
The Odisha plant has not yet commenced commercial production on a full scale, 
as the procurement agreement with the Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) is 
awaited. Nevertheless, the plant produced 71 lac litres of ethanol during 2Q FY 
2026-27. 
The OMCs have floated the tender for the supply of ethanol for FY 2026-27, and 
PGIPL has participated in the tender. The Company expects full-fledged 
production and supply of ethanol to commence from the third week of November 
2026, by which time the allocation process is expected to be completed by the 
OMCs. 
The capacity of Odisha Plant is 200 KLPD and is expected to go fully operational 
at 80% capacity in the third quarter.

Outlook

to be 20-25% compared to the previous financial year. Almondz Global Infra 
Consultant Limited (AGICL) has secured more than double new order book in the
