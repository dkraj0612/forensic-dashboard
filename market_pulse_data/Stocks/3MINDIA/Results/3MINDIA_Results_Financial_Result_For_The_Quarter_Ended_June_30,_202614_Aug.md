Commenting on the first quarter ended June 30, 2026, financial results Mr. Aseem Joshi, Managing 
Director of 3M India Limited, said “the Company delivered 19.0% sales growth in the first quarter

exceptional items. The company’s profitability excluding the exceptional items declined YoY 
primarily due to the impact of Indian Rupee depreciation. I thank our teams for their disciplined 
execution during the quarter, and for maintaining a strong customer focus.
