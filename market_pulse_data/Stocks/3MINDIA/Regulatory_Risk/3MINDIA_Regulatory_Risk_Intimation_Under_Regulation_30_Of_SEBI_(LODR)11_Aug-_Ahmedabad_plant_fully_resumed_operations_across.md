We refer to our announcement dated July 24, 2026, wherein the Company had informed about 
the temporary suspension of manufacturing operations and production at its Ahmedabad Plant 
due to flooding caused by unprecedented heavy rainfall. 
 
We wish to inform you that the Company has completed the necessary restoration activities at 
the plant. Consequently, manufacturing operations and production, which had previously 
resumed on a partial basis, have now fully resumed across all value streams with effect from 
August 10, 2026. The operations at the facility have returned to normal. 
 
The Company continues to assess the overall impact, if any, arising out of the said natural 
calamity. All assets of the Company are adequately insured and the matter has been intimated 
to the insurance company.
