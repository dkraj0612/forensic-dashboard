Sub:  Intimation of event under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 viz. 
temporary closure/shutdown of operations of company at its Ahmedabad Plant due to

In reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform that the manufacturing operations at 
the Company's Ahmedabad Plant have been temporarily suspended due to heavy rainfall and 
flooding in the region experienced yesterday and today. 
 
The water level has begun to recede; however, the restoration of operations is expected to take 
a few days. We are currently assessing the extent of any damage to assets. We would like to 
clarify that all assets of the Company are adequately insured. 
 
Considering the Company's sufficient manufacturing capacities at other locations, we do not 
foresee any material impact on its overall operations. 
 
The Company continues to closely monitor the situation and will provide timely updates 
regarding the resumption of normal operations.
