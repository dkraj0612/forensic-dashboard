PRESS RELEASE 
 
ROYAL ENFIELD POSTS BEST-EVER FESTIVE SEASON WITH 2,67,400 
MOTORCYCLES SOLD  
 
Chennai: November 01, 2026: Royal Enfield recorded its highest-ever monthly sales in October 2026, 
delivering 1,33,442 motorcycles and achieving a 7% growth year-on-year. Driven by strong festive 
demand across September and October 2026, cumulative sales reached 2,67,400 units - surpassing the 
previous year's full festive total even before the season had concluded.   
Speaking about the performance, B. Govindarajan, Managing Director of Eicher Motors Ltd. and 
Chief Executive Officer of Royal Enfield, said, “It has been an exceptional festive season for us at 
Royal Enfield. Riders across the country continue to choose our brand and motorcycles, making this our 
best-ever festive performance. The strong response across our portfolio - from iconic favourites like the 
Classic 350 to new additions like the Himalayan 440 - reflects the enduring bond we share with our 
riding community. As the festive season enters its final stretch, we are ramping up production to meet 
the growing demand. Our key international markets have also begun showing a positive growth 
trajectory. Furthermore, we continued expanding the Flying Flea footprint across India, extending its 
retail presence to two additional major urban hubs: Hyderabad and Pune. The weeks ahead will be even 
more exciting as we gear up to celebrate with our community at Motoverse.”

Brand & Business Updates  
 
125th Anniversary Edition Hypershift Classic 650 Sold Out in India in Under 360 Seconds ​
Adding another stylish tribute to its commemorative celebrations, Royal Enfield opened registrations for 
the 125th Anniversary Edition Hypershift Classic 650. Limited to 125 units per key region globally, the 
motorcycle is built on the proven 650 Twin platform and features a distinctive Hypershift finish that 
transitions between red, green and gold. Complemented by a gilded 125-year crest, special side-panel 
badging and hand-painted pinstripes, the exclusive 125 units in India sold out in less than 360 seconds 
on the sale day. 
 
Flying Flea Expanded Its Retail Presence to Hyderabad and Pune ​
Building on the strong customer response to the FF.C6 in Bengaluru, Flying Flea, Royal Enfield’s city+ 
electric mobility brand, has expanded its presence to Hyderabad and Pune. The brand entered 
Hyderabad with one company store and three additional stores, followed by Pune with ten sales and 
service centres. The steady city expansion reflects Flying Flea’s continued focus on building a robust 
retail and ownership ecosystem.

Royal Enfield and the Madhya Pradesh Tourism Board commenced a two-year partnership to promote 
responsible riding, cultural immersion and exploration across the state. The first expedition covered 
over 1,520 kilometres across key wildlife sanctuaries, heritage corridors and lesser-travelled routes, with 
20 riders exploring Madhya Pradesh on the Bullet 650. The collaboration will continue with curated 
heritage rides, off-road trail explorations and experiential motorcycling journeys. 
 
Pledged Over ₹3 Crore Towards Snow Leopard Conservation​
On International Snow Leopard Day, Royal Enfield announced over ₹3 crore for snow leopard 
conservation, delivering on the pledge made at One Ride 2026. Themed “Every Rider. Every Road. One 
Mission.”, the ride drew a record 156,085 riders from 67 countries on September 20. The funds will back 
scientific research, population assessments, community stewardship and human-wildlife conflict 
mitigation across more than 10,500 sq. km of Himalayan snow leopard habitat.  
 
International Business​
Royal Enfield launched the Classic 650 125th Anniversary Edition across the APAC region, as well as the 
Guerrilla Apex 450 in Malaysia. In Taiwan, the brand opened a new and exclusive store, along with the 
launch of helmets in the country. Expanding its community initiatives, Royal Enfield participated in the 
Retro Racing Trophy at the Korea International Circuit in Korea, where the Continental GT 650 claimed 
both top spots overall, showcasing the brand’s racing spirit and strengthening its presence in Korea’s 
motorsport scene. It also concluded the first edition of the Moroccan Odyssey in Rabat bringing 
together 29 riders over 12 days and 2000kms, marking a milestone for its Global Marquee Rides 
programme.

About Royal Enfield 
The oldest motorcycle brand in continuous production, Royal Enfield has created beautifully crafted motorcycles 
since 1901. From its British roots, a manufacturing plant was established in Madras in 1955, a foothold from which 
Royal Enfield spearheaded the growth of India’s mid-sized two-wheeler segment. Royal Enfields are engaging, 
uncomplicated, accessible and fun to ride; a vehicle for exploration and self-expression. It’s an approach the brand 
calls ‘Pure Motorcycling’. Royal Enfield’s premium line-up includes Bear 650, Classic 650, Bullet 650 and the 
Guerrilla 450 modern roadster, Hunter 350, Meteor 350, Super Meteor 650, Interceptor 650 and Continental GT 650 
twins, the Shotgun 650, the Himalayan 450, the Himalayan 440, the Scram 440 ADV Crossover, the iconic Bullet 350, 
Classic 350 and Goan Classic 350. The company has recently unveiled its dedicated electric mobility brand – the 
Flying Flea – a fresh take on electric two-wheelers, blending authentic design with advanced technology. The Flying 
Flea C6, its first product, has been launched in India, Europe and the UK. 
 
Riders and a passionate community are fostered with a rich profusion of events at a local, regional and 
international level. Most notable are Motoverse (previously Rider Mania), the greatest celebration of motorcycling 
culture attended each year by thousands of riders from across the globe in Goa, and Himalayan Odyssey; a yearly 
pilgrimage over some of the toughest terrain and highest mountain passes. 
 
A division of Eicher Motors Limited, Royal Enfield operates through more than 2074 stores across all major cities 
and towns in India and through nearly 1212 stores in 80+ countries around the globe. Royal Enfield also has two 
world-class technical centres, in Bruntingthorpe, UK, and in Chennai, India. The company’s state-of-the-art 
production facilities are located at Tiruvottiyur, Cheyyar, Oragadam and Vallam Vadagal, near Chennai. Across the 
world, Royal Enfield has seven modern CKD assembly facilities in Bangladesh, Nepal, Brazil (2), Thailand, Argentina 
and Colombia.
