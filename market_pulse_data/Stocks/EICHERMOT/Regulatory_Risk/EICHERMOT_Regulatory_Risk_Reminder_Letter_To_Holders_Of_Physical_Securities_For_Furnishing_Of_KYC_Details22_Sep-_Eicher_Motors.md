In case of non-updation of PAN or Address with PIN code or Mobile Number or Bank Account Details or Specimen Signature in respect 
of physical folios: - 
(i) The dividend will be paid to the shareholder (through electronic mode) only upon furnishing all the aforesaid details. Upon updation

shareholder.
(ii) Shareholders will be ELIGIBLE to lodge any grievance or avail of any service from the Company only upon furnishing all the aforesaid
details.
In view of the above, we request you to submit at the earliest the KYC Form printed overleaf and Form ISR-1 duly signed along with 
the required supporting documents, as applicable.
