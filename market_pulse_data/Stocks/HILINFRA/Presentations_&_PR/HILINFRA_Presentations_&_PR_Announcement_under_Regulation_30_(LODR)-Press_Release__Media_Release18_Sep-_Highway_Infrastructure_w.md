Highway Infrastructure Limited Secures Rs. 220.66 Crore Toll Operations 
Contract of 2 years for Gorakhpur Link Expressway  
 
Madhya Pradesh, India 18 September 2026: Highway Infrastructure Limited (HIL) is an  integrated 
infrastructure platform with established capabilities across Tolling, EPC, and Real Estate, is pleased to 
announce that it has received a Letter of Acceptance (LOA) from the Uttar Pradesh Expressways 
Industrial Development Authority (UPEIDA) for toll collection and allied operations on the 
Gorakhpur Link Expressway. 
The contract covers the collection of user fees and operation of toll plazas, along with the deployment of 
four patrol-cum-safety vehicles with the required personnel. It has been awarded for an initial period of 
two years, with a provision for extension of up to six months at UPEIDA’s discretion. 
The total contract value for the two-year period is Rs. 220.66 crore. The amount payable for the 
first year is Rs. 105.08 crore, followed by a 10% escalation in the second year. This win strengthens 
HIL’s tollway collection portfolio and expands its footprint across India’s growing expressway network. 
The Gorakhpur Link Expressway represents a transformational infrastructure initiative for Eastern Uttar 
Pradesh, enhancing regional connectivity, improving logistics efficiency, and unlocking new 
opportunities for trade, industry, and investment. By integrating key economic corridors, the expressway 
is expected to accelerate economic activity and support sustainable development across the region. 
Speaking on the development, Mr. Arun Kumar Jain, Managing Director, Highway Infrastructure Limited 
said: “We are pleased to secure the Rs. 220.66 crore tollway collection contract from UPEIDA. This significant 
order win enhances our medium-term revenue visibility and demonstrates HIL’s ability to execute large-
scale toll management mandates. It further strengthens our credentials to pursue larger, higher-value 
projects while expanding our toll operations footprint across key highway and expressway corridors. 
Alongside scaling our operations, we continue to invest in developing proprietary technology solutions 
aimed at improving toll collection efficiency, transaction accuracy, and operational effectiveness. With our 
growing scale, proven execution capabilities, technology-led approach, and diversified operating footprint, 
HIL is well positioned to capitalize on emerging opportunities in India’s expanding road infrastructure 
sector."
