In connection with and in furtherance of our earlier letters concerning the subject captioned Scheme, 
we are pleased to inform that the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Chennai Bench (‘NCLT’), 
has sanctioned the Scheme of Amalgamation between Dr. Agarwal’s Eye Hospital Limited (‘AEHL’) and 
Dr. Agarwal’s Health Care Limited (‘AHCL’ / ‘the Company’) and their respective shareholders and 
creditors, providing for the amalgamation of AEHL with and into AHCL.  
 
The Order sanctioning the aforesaid Scheme (uploaded on the website of the Hon’ble NCLT today, i.e., 
October 05, 2026), was formally pronounced by the Hon’ble NCLT, at its hearing held on September 
30, 2026. The certified copy of the Order is awaited, which shall be made available on the website of 
the Company promptly upon its receipt. 
 
In terms of the provisions of the Scheme and the aforesaid NCLT Sanction Order dated September 30, 
2026, the Scheme shall be effective from the Appointed Date (i.e., opening of business on April 01, 
2026) and operative from the Effective Date being the first day of the calendar month immediately 
following the calendar month in which all the conditionalities mentioned in Clause 24 of the Scheme 
are completed. 
 
The Company shall keep the stock exchanges duly informed regarding the effectiveness of the Scheme, 
fixation of Record Date, allotment of shares as per the Share Exchange Ratio, and other subsequent 
developments / future course of action in connection with the Scheme, as and when appropriate.  
 
We request you to kindly take the above on record. 
 
For Dr. Agarwal’s Health Care Limited
