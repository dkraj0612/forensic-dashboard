Pursuant to the captioned subject, reference and our earlier intimation dated October 20, 2026, 
regarding the effectiveness of the Scheme and fixation of the Record Date, we wish to inform you that 
the Board of Directors of Dr. Agarwal’s Health Care Limited (“the Company”), by way of circular 
resolution dated November 19, 2026, have approved the allotment of 1,51,90,373 (One Crore Fifty-
One Lakh Ninety Thousand Three Hundred and Seventy-Three) fully paid-up Equity Shares of face 
value of ₹ 1/- each to the eligible equity shareholders of Dr. Agarwal’s Eye Hospital Limited (“Transferor 
Company”) as on the Record Date, i.e., Wednesday, November 18, 2026, in accordance with the 
approved Share Exchange Ratio under the Scheme as below:  
 
“23 (Twenty-Three) fully paid-up Equity Shares of face value of ₹1 each of Dr. Agarwal’s Health Care 
Limited for every 2 (Two) fully paid-up Equity Shares of face value of ₹10 each held in Dr. Agarwal’s Eye 
Hospital Limited” 
 
The said Equity Shares shall rank pari passu in all respects with the existing Equity Shares of the 
Company. In terms of Clause 18.5 of the Scheme, the Company will take necessary steps to obtain 
listing and trading approvals for the aforesaid Equity Shares from BSE Limited and National Stock 
Exchange of India Limited. 
 
Consequent to this allotment, the paid-up equity share capital of the Company stands increased from 
₹ 31,70,99,111 consisting of 31,70,99,111 equity shares of ₹ 1 each to ₹ 33,22,89,484 (Indian Rupees 
Thirty Three Crores Twenty Two Lakh Eighty Nine Thousand Four Hundred and Eighty Four) consisting 
of 33,22,89,484 equity shares of ₹ 1 each. 
 
Further, pursuant to the Scheme, 35,11,925 equity shares of face value of ₹ 10/- each held by Dr. 
Agarwal’s Health Care Limited in the Transferor Company prior to the effectiveness of the Scheme 
stand cancelled and extinguished. 
 
This information is also made available on the website of the Company at: www.dragarwals.co.in.
