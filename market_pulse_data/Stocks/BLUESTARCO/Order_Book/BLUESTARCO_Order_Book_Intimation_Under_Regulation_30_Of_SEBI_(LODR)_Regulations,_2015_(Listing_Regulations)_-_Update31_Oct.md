Sub.: Intimation under Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(‘Listing Regulations’) – Update 
 
We refer to our disclosures dated October 2, 2024 and April 3, 2024 in relation to the 
arbitration proceedings filed before the International Chamber of Commerce (“ICC”) by 
W.J. Towell & Co LLC (‘WJT’), an entity based in Oman, with whom the Company had 
entered into a Shareholders’ Agreement for managing the affairs of Blue Star Oman Electro 
Mechanical Company. 
 
The Company vide its disclosure dated July 23, 2026 had informed that the Arbitrator has 
issued the Award in favour of the Company, dismissing the entire claim of OMR 
2,11,80,748 by WJT against the Company. 
 
As part of the legal remedies available to WJT, the Company had, vide its disclosure dated 
September 15, 2026 (attached), informed that WJT had approached the Dubai Court of 
Appeal seeking annulment of the Award. 
 
Consequent to the annulment proceedings initiated by WJT, the Company made 
appropriate submissions before the Dubai Court of Appeal asserting that the grounds of 
annulment raised by WJT are baseless. 
 
Dubai Court of Appeal has dismissed WJT’s application for annulment of the Award 
in entirety. 
 
WJT, as part of the legal remedies available to it, may appeal against Award. The Company 
will make appropriate submissions for dismissal of such appeal, if filed. 
 
Kindly take the same on record and note that it pertains to a subsequent development in 
the matter and does not constitute a material update.

Sub.: Intimation under Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
 Update 
 
This has reference to the arbitration proceedings relating to the claim filed before the 
October 1, 2015 for managing the affairs of Blue Star Oman Electro Mechanical Company. 
 
The Company had, vide its disclosure dated July 23, 2026 (attached), informed that the 
Arbitrator had issued an Award in favour of the Company, dismissing all the claims made 
by WJT. 
 
WJT, as part of the legal remedies available to it, has approached the Dubai Court of 
Appeal seeking annulment of the Award. Annulment proceedings do not constitute an 
appeal or reconsideration of the merits of the Award and are confined to limited grounds.  
 
Consequent to the annulment proceedings initiated by WJT, the Company will make 
appropriate submissions before the Dubai Court of Appeal. The Company believes that 
the grounds of annulment raised by WJT are baseless. 
 
Kindly take the same on record and note that it pertains to a subsequent development in 
the matter and does not constitute a material update.
