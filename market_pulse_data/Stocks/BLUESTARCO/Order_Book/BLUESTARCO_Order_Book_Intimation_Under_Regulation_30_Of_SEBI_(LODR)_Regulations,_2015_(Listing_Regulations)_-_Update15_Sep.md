This has reference to the arbitration proceedings relating to the claim filed before the 
International Chamber of Commerce by W.J. Towell & Co LLC (‘WJT’), an entity based in 
Oman, with whom the Company had entered into a Shareholders’ Agreement dated 
October 1, 2015 for managing the affairs of Blue Star Oman Electro Mechanical Company.

Arbitrator had issued an Award in favour of the Company, dismissing all the claims made 
by WJT.

Appeal seeking annulment of the Award. Annulment proceedings do not constitute an 
appeal or reconsideration of the merits of the Award and are confined to limited grounds.  
 
Consequent to the annulment proceedings initiated by WJT, the Company will make 
appropriate submissions before the Dubai Court of Appeal. The Company believes that 
the grounds of annulment raised by WJT are baseless.

the matter and does not constitute a material update.

Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Sub.: Intimation under Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
 Material Update 
 
We refer to our letter dated October 2, 2024 and April 3, 2024 (enclosed), and in 
accordance with Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations  and relevant SEBI Circular, wherein the Company had informed the Stock 
Exchanges regarding arbitration proceedings filed against the Company before the 
dated October 1, 2015 for managing affairs of Blue Star Oman Electro Mechanical 
Company. 
 
Initially, the arbitration proceedings filed against the Company was for OMR 103,18,000 
(approx. Rs 223.60 crores) plus interest @7% on the total claim amount which was then 
revised by WJT to OMR 2,11,80,748 (approx. Rs 461.74 crores). The intimation in this 
regard was duly filed with the Stock Exchange on October 2, 2024.  
 
The Company contested the said arbitration proceedings reiterating that the claims filed 
by WJT are frivolous, unsubstantiated, premised on fundamental factual misstatements 
and contrary to the overwhelming facts and evidence. 
 
After going through the submissions made by the legal counsel of both the parties, the 
Arbitrator has pronounced its Award, which was received by the Company vide email dated 
July 23, 2026 at 3:06 p.m.  (I.S.T.) 
 
The Arbitrator has issued the Award in favour of the Company, dismissing all the 
claims of WJT.
