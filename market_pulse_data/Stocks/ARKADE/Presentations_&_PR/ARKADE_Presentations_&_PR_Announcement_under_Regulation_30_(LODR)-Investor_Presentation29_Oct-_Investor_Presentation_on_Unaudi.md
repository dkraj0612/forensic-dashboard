Management Commentary
The Company delivered a healthy performance during Q2 FY27, supported by improved
gross margins and strong growth in collections. Gross margins improved to 35% during
the quarter, while collections grew by 22% year-on-year to ₹183 crore, reflecting our
continued emphasis on execution, customer engagement and timely project delivery.
While pre-sales remained moderate amid evolving market dynamics, we remain
focused on strengthening customer confidence and driving sustainable business
growth.
Our growth strategy continues to be supported by a robust development pipeline with
an estimated Gross Development Value (GDV) of approximately ₹19,000 crore across
5.7 million square feet of saleable carpet area. We are targeting the launch of two
projects with a combined GDV of approximately ₹2,800 crore in Q3 FY27. In parallel, we
continue to pursue strategic land acquisitions across key micro-markets in the Mumbai
Metropolitan Region, further strengthening our development portfolio and enhancing
long-term growth visibility.
We remain committed to disciplined financial management, quality development and
timely project execution. As of September 30, 2026, the Company maintained a net
liquid cash position of ₹63 crore, providing financial flexibility to support its growth
plans. With a strong project pipeline, a prudent financial approach and a continued
focus on operational efficiency, we are well positioned to pursue our growth ambitions
and create sustainable long-term value for our customers and stakeholders.
Amit Mangilal Jain | Promoter & CMD
