We are pleased to inform you that the Board of Directors of Asahi India Glass Limited at its Meeting held on 
27t May, 2026 have recommended Final Dividend of  2.00/- per Equity Share of ¥ 1.00/- each (200%) for the

As you are aware, pursuant to the provisions of the Income Tax Act, 2025 ("the Act"), dividends paid or distributed 
by a company are taxable in the hands of the shazeholders. The Company shall therefore be required to deduct tax at 
source at the time of making payment of the dividend to the sharcholders at the applicable rates if declared at the 
aforesaid AGM.

documents submitted to and accepted by the Company under the provisions of the Act.
