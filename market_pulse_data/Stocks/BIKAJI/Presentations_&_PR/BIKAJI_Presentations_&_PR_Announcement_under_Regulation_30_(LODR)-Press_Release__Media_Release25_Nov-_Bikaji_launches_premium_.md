Bikaji Foods International Forays into the Premium Bakery Segment with the Launch 
of Mahè under Bikaji Bakes 
In partnership with renowned Master Baker T K Khaleel, the launch brings European baking traditions and 
artisanal expertise to India 
 
Mumbai, 25 November 2026: Bikaji Foods International Limited, one of India's leading ethnic snacks brands, 
is expanding its food portfolio with its entry into the premium bakery segment through Mahè, a European-
inspired bakery brand under Bikaji Bakes. In partnership with Master Baker T K Khaleel, Mahè brings together 
European baking traditions, artisanal craftsmanship and contemporary production capabilities. The launch 
marks a strategic step in Bikaji's efforts to diversify its portfolio and build capabilities in new food categories. 
Mahè will be unveiled at Bakery Biz Mumbai, scheduled to take place from 25 to 27 November 2026. The launch 
marks Bikaji's entry into the commercial bakery and foodservice space, a specialised segment that calls for 
distinct technical expertise, product capabilities and a deep understanding of evolving customer requirements. 
Through Bikaji Bakes, the company aims to build a presence in this segment by combining its experience in food 
manufacturing and distribution with specialised expertise in artisanal baking. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Deepak Agarwal, Chairman & Managing Director, Bikaji Foods 
International Limited, said, "Bikaji has grown by understanding consumer preferences and creating food 
offerings that respond to evolving tastes. As we look to expand beyond our established categories, bakery 
represents an exciting opportunity for us. Through Bikaji Bakes and Mahè, we are bringing together Bikaji’s 
experience in the food business and the specialised expertise of T K Khaleel. We recognise that entering a new 
category requires respect for its craft, processes and consumer expectations. This partnership reflects our 
approach to building the capabilities and expertise needed to grow meaningfully in the segment." 
At the heart of Mahè is T K Khaleel, whose association with the baking industry spans more than four decades. 
Trained by European master bakers in Qatar, Khaleel went on to establish and develop several bakery businesses 
across the Middle East. Through Mahè, he brings his expertise in European baking traditions and contemporary 
production practices to India. 
Speaking about his approach to baking, T K Khaleel, Master Baker, Mahè, said, "The future of baking isn’t about 
choosing between tradition and technology. It’s about bringing the two together, allowing innovation to 
elevate craftsmanship without ever losing the human touch that makes baking special." 
Mahè's portfolio will include premium European viennoiseries, artisan breads, morning goods, savouries and 
cakes. The brand will also offer specialised ‘naked’ base products for chefs and foodservice professionals, giving 
them the flexibility to customise and finish products to suit their culinary requirements. The venture is supported 
by a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Karnataka, designed to integrate artisanal baking processes with 
structured production capabilities. The facility will enable Mahè to serve both retail consumers and foodservice 
customers while maintaining product consistency and supporting scale. 
Through Bikaji Bakes, the company is building a platform to expand into new food categories by drawing on its 
established manufacturing capabilities and bringing specialised expertise into the business. Mahè is an important 
step in this direction, opening up opportunities across India's premium retail bakery and foodservice markets.

About Bikaji Foods International Ltd. (https://www.bikaji.com/) 
Bikaji Foods International is the third-largest ethnic snacks company in India, with an international footprint, 
selling Indian snacks and sweets. It is also the second-fastest-growing company in the Indian organised snacks 
market (Source: F&S Report). Late Shri Shiv Ratan Agarwal launched the Bikaji brand in 1993, guided by a deep 
understanding of Indian tastes and preferences. As of 31 March 2026, the company has operations in 27 states 
and 7 union territories in India. It also exports its products to 47 countries across North America, Europe, the 
Middle East, Africa and Asia Pacific. Bikaji Foods' product range spans six principal categories: bhujia, namkeen, 
packaged sweets, papad, western snacks and other snacks.
