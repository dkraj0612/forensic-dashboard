Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 – Update on Credit Rating 
 
This is with reference to our earlier communication dated 07 May 2026, wherein we had informed that 
CRISIL Ratings Limited (“CRISIL”) had reaffirmed the rating on the long-term bank facilities of Sun 
Pharmaceutical Industries Limited (“Company”) at ‘CRISIL AAA’ and had placed the said rating on 
‘Rating Watch with Developing Implications’ following the Company’s announcement dated 27 April 
2026. 
 
CRISIL has now removed the ‘Rating Watch with Developing Implications’ status and has reaffirmed the 
rating assigned to the Company's long-term bank facilities at ‘CRISIL AAA/Stable’ (Reaffirmed; Removed 
from ‘Rating Watch with Developing Implications’).
