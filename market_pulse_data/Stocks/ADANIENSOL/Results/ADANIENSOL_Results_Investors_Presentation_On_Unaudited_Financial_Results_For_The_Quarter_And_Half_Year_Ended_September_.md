### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) demonstrated strong financial growth for Q2 and 1H FY27, reporting significant year-on-year increases in operational revenue (+51%), EBITDA (+67%), and PAT (+145%) for Q2FY27. This performance was fueled by robust contributions from its expanding transmission and smart metering businesses, alongside an exponential revenue surge from the Energy Solutions Platform, supported by substantial capital expenditure and new project wins. While the unaudited results highlight impressive operational expansion, the accompanying information notes a substantial net debt of Rs 41,067 crore as of 1HFY27 without providing comparative leverage context.

Key Highlights Q2 FY27 and 1H FY27
Financial Performance:
–
The operational revenue of Rs 7,167 crore in Q2FY27 was up 51% driven by stronger 
operating performance across the portfolio.
–
EBITDA reached record high to Rs 3,548 crore in Q2FY27, up 67% YoY, supported by 
strong growth in the transmission, smart metering, energy solutions platform and 
steady performance in the distribution business
–
PAT was up 145% and 137% YoY to Rs 1,364 crore and Rs 2,600 crore in Q2FY27 
and 1HFY27 respectively, translating from double-digit EBITDA expansion and 
inclusion of energy solutions platform
Transmission Business:
–
System availability remains robust at 99.8% in Q2 FY27
–
During the quarter, AESL secured two projects Vizag Power Transmission Limited and 
Satara Power Transmission Limited, collectively adding ~2,000 ckm of line capacity 
with ~ INR 1,474 Cr of annual transmission tariff
Distribution Business (AEML Mumbai):
–
Total units sold in the Mumbai circle increased by 11% from 2,650 MUs in Q2FY26 to 
2,930 MUs in Q2FY27
–
RAB stands at Rs 10,456 Cr as of Q2FY27, registering a growth of 11% YoY
Smart Metering:
–
In Q2FY26, the company installed 1.3 million new meters, reaching a total mark of 
14.8 million smart meters
–
The untapped country-level market opportunity stands at 86 million smart meters
Energy Solutions Platform:
–
Supplied 25,333 MUs in 1HFY27, across C&I and utilities
–
supply tie-up ~4.6 GW across solar & wind, ~3.5 GWh of BESS which will cater robust 
energy portfolio across C&I, data centers and utilities, delivering integrated renewable 
and round-the-clock digital energy solutions
