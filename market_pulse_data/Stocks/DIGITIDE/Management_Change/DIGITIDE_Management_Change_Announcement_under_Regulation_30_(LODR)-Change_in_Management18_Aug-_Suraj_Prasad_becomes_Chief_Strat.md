Pursuant to the Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, we hereby inform that the Board of 
Directors of the Company, at its meeting held on August 18, 2026, has inter-alia approved the 
following: 
 
1. Transition of Mr. Suraj Prasad, Chief Financial Officer, as Chief Strategy and 
Corporate Development Officer of the Company and Senior Management Personnel with 
effect from October 01, 2026. Mr. Suraj Prasad will continue to discharge the 
responsibilities of Chief Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company 
up to the close of business hours on September 30, 2026. 
 
2. Appointment of Mr. Harigovind Krishnasamy, as Chief Financial Officer (Designate), 
with immediate effect, who shall form part of Senior Management of the Company.      
Mr. Harigovind Krishnasamy will be assuming office of Chief Financial Officer and Key 
Managerial Personnel of the Company w.e.f. October 1, 2026.

Brief profile of Mr. Harigovind Krishnasamy 
 
Mr. Harigovind Krishnasamy is an accomplished finance leader 
with over 21 years of experience across large conglomerates and 
high-growth digital businesses, including more than five years of 
international 
experience. 
His 
expertise 
spans 
financial 
controllership, business finance, treasury, project finance, 
taxation, corporate compliance, governance and strategic finance. 
 
Hari has served as Chief Financial Officer of Matrimony.com 
Limited and Indiavidual Learning Limited - Embibe, the edtech 
business within the Reliance Jio group. At Matrimony.com 
Limited, he led several cost saving initiatives and a high impact revenue enhancement project 
as well. At Embibe, he helped scale the organisation and strengthen its finance and support 
infrastructure. 
  
Across his earlier roles, he led several complex initiatives, including US GAAP conversion, 
SOX compliance and significant debt refinancing activities. His experience includes multi-
entity consolidation involving more than 100 subsidiaries, listed-company compliance and 
governance, financial controls, treasury management, business partnering and finance and 
business transformation. Earlier in his career, he held senior finance roles at Vedanta, Den 
Networks and Jio Platforms, building extensive experience across complex, high-growth and 
multinational business environments. 
 
Hari is a Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, having 
qualified in 2004. He holds a B. Com from Madurai Kamaraj University and completed all 
three levels of the US CFA Program in 2013.
