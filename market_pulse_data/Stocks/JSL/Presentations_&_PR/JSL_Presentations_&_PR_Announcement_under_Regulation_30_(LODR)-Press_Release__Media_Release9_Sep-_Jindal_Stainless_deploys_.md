Management, Strengthening Supply Chain Resilience

National, September 09, 2026:  Jindal Stainless, India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, today 
announced the successful deployment of SAP Ariba Supplier Risk with Environmental, Social, and 
Governance (ESG) risk capabilities. The deployment marks a significant milestone in the company's digital 
transformation and sustainability journey, establishing a technology-enabled framework to assess, 
monitor and manage supplier risks integrated within the Business ERP platform while strengthening 
transparency, resilience and responsible sourcing across its procurement ecosystem. 
 
This initiative builds on the Company's existing supplier risk assessment practices, transitioning from 
periodic, largely manual assessments to a digital-first model with continuous monitoring and enhanced 
visibility of risk indicators. 
 
With ESG indicators embedded within supplier profiles, procurement teams can identify risk signals 
earlier, support more informed sourcing decisions and maintain structured, audit ready information for 
governance and compliance. The deployment also strengthens the integration of supplier risk 
management with the Company's broader digital transformation agenda. 
 
Commenting on the development, Managing Director, Jindal Stainless, Mr Abhyuday Jindal, said, 
“Digital transformation and sustainability have become critical pillars in building resilient and responsible 
businesses. At Jindal Stainless, we are using technology not only to strengthen operational efficiency, but 
also to embed greater transparency, accountability and environmental focus across our value chain. This 
approach brings together our digital and sustainability priorities to create stronger systems of governance 
and enable more informed, responsible business decisions.” 
 
The implementation brings together ESG focused capabilities including supplier risk classification, 
questionnaire based ESG evaluation, risk scoring, continuous monitoring of risk signals and structured 
visibility for procurement stakeholders. These capabilities are integrated across key stages of the supplier 
lifecycle, including onboarding, sourcing and award related decision making. 
 
Commenting on the development, Manish Prasad, President and Managing Director, SAP Indian 
Subcontinent said, “The future competitiveness of India's industrial sector will increasingly depend on the 
resilience, transparency, and sustainability of its supply chains. Organizations that can effectively embed 
ESG considerations into operational decisions will be better positioned to navigate evolving stakeholder

About Jindal Stainless 
India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,955 crore (USD 4.86 
billion) in FY26 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes. It has 16 stainless steel manufacturing and 
processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Spain and Indonesia, and a worldwide network in 12 countries, 
as of March 2026. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service centres, as of March 2026. The company’s 
product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, plates, sheets, precision strips, wire rods, rebars, blade 
steel, and coin blanks. 
 
Jindal Stainless relies on its integrated operations to enhance its cost competitiveness and operational efficiency. 
Founded in 1970, Jindal Stainless continues to be inspired by a vision for innovation and enriching lives and is 
committed to social responsibility. 
 
Jindal Stainless remains focused on a greener and sustainable future. The company manufactures stainless steel 
using electric arc furnace, a process that significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions and allows for recyclability 
of scrap without compromising on quality.
