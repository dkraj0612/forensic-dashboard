Jindal Stainless receives Awarding Body recognition from National Council for 
Vocational Education and Training 
 
• 
Recognition strengthens Jindal Stainless’ role in developing industry-led qualifications and building 
a skilled talent pipeline for the stainless steel ecosystem 
 
National, September 18, 2026: Jindal Stainless, India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer has formally 
signed an agreement with the National Council for Vocational Education and Training (NCVET), under the 
Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), for its recognition as an Awarding Body. This 
will enable Jindal Stainless, to develop and offer Government recognised, industry-led qualifications and 
certification programmes aligned with the evolving skill requirements of the stainless steel ecosystem. 
 
The signing of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was attended by Chairperson, NCVET and 
Secretary, Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India, Ms Debashree 
Mukherjee; Secretary to the Council cum Director, NCVET, Lt. Col. Vikram Singh Bhati; Director – 
Corporate Affairs, Jindal Stainless, Mr Vijay Sharma; and Head – Stainless Academy, Jindal Stainless, Ms 
Bhavna Chopra Srikrishna. 
 
 
 
NCVET is the apex national regulator for vocational education, training and skill development in India. As 
an Awarding Body, Jindal Stainless will be eligible to award, assess and certify learners for approved or

Commenting on the development, Managing Director, Jindal Stainless, Mr Abhyuday Jindal, said, 
“India’s manufacturing ambitions cannot be realised through infrastructure and technology alone; they 
also depend on the capabilities of the people who operate, innovate and build within these systems. As the 
nature of industrial work evolves, the relationship between government, industry and the institutions that 
prepare people for it must evolve as well. Stainless Academy was established with this larger purpose in 
mind, and its recognition by NCVET marks an important step in giving industry a stronger role in shaping 
and recognising the capabilities that India’s stainless steel sector will need.” 
 
The recognition builds on Jindal Stainless’ work through Stainless Academy, its education and skilling 
initiative focused on developing a skilled blue- and white-collar workforce for the stainless steel sector. 
Since its inception, the Academy has worked across industry aligned training, curriculum development, 
specialised programmes for ITIs, Polytechnics and Engineering colleges, and MSME cluster development. 
With the NCVET recognition, Jindal Stainless can build on this foundation by developing qualifications and 
certification programmes that reflect the practical requirements of stainless steel manufacturing, 
processing, fabrication and applications.  
 
Director – Corporate Affairs, Jindal Stainless, Mr Vijay Sharma, said, “At Jindal Stainless, Stainless 
Academy has evolved beyond conventional training to work across academic institutions, MSMEs, 
fabricators and other stakeholders across the stainless steel value chain. The NCVET recognition gives us 
the opportunity to bring greater structure to this work and create a platform that brings together industry, 
academia and Government in developing skills that are aligned with the sector’s evolving requirements.” 
 
The NCVET recognition marks another step in Jindal Stainless’ efforts to address workforce readiness gaps 
among fabricators, workers, employers and students through an industry-led approach, while 
strengthening collaboration between industry, academia and Government to support the development 
of a skilled workforce for India’s growing stainless steel ecosystem. 
 
About Jindal Stainless 
India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,955 crore (USD 4.86 
billion) in FY26 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes. It has 16 stainless steel manufacturing and 
processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Spain and Indonesia, and a worldwide network in 12 countries, 
as of March 2026. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service centres, as of March 2026. The company’s 
product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, plates, sheets, precision strips, wire rods, rebars, blade 
steel, and coin blanks.
