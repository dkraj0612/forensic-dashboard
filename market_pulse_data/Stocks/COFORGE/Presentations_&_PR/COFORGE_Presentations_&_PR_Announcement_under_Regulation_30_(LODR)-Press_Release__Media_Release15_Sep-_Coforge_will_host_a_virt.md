Coforge to Host Executive Briefing on Operationalizing 
Enterprise Autonomy 
 
Coforge will demonstrate how to bridge the AI execution gap through data enablement, 
agentic operations, and forward-deployed engineers 
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | September 15, 2026 – Coforge Limited (NSE: 
COFORGE), a global AI-native engineering services leader, today announced it will host a virtual 
briefing for analysts and investors entitled “Operationalizing Enterprise Autonomy,” on 
September 17, 2026 from 9:00–10:30 a.m. EDT (6:30–8:00 p.m. IST).  
 
This invitation-only, 90-minute session will explore the technologies, operating models, and execution 
frameworks required for global enterprises to move beyond AI experimentation to deliver measurable 
business outcomes at scale.  
 
The executive briefing builds on Coforge’s broader vision for enterprise autonomy, which focuses 
on enabling AI systems to make increasingly autonomous, context-aware decisions across enterprise 
data, systems, processes, and policies. The discussion will demonstrate how organizations can 
operationalize that vision through a combination of data foundations, infrastructure modernization, 
agentic operations, and outcome-based delivery models.  
 
The executive briefing will feature presentations from Coforge executives and business leaders and 
will conclude with live Q&A session.  
 
Attendance is by invitation only. Analysts, investors and other qualified attendees may request an 
invitation by contacting investors@coforge.com. 
 
About Coforge 
 
Coforge is an AI-native engineering services leader, where AI is the foundation of how we design, 
build, and deliver intelligent solutions for our clients. We use AI and hyperspecialized industry 
expertise to engineer autonomous enterprises. We combine AI agents with our AI-enabled workforce, 
including specialized FDEs in hybrid pod-based delivery units. With a deep focus on trusted AI, our 
solutions are secure, governed, and enterprise-grade. We are outcome-led by design. Moving beyond 
AI experimentation, we deliver measurable business outcomes—including lower operating costs, 
faster cycle times, higher conversion rates, and sustained margin growth.
