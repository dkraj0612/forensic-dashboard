Coforge Global Event Advances Workforce Readiness for Enterprise 
AI Adoption 
 
TechCon 2026 combines AI training, innovation, and ecosystem partnerships to help enterprises 
operationalize AI at scale 
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | September 14, 2026 – Coforge Limited (NSE: COFORGE), an 
AI-native engineering services leader, today announced TechCon 2026, its flagship global technology and 
innovation event, taking place September 15-16, 2026 across India, North America, the UK, Europe, Australia, 
and LATAM. The event will bring together Coforge's 46,000 employees with technology leaders, clients, 
hyperscalers, frontier model providers, and ecosystem partners to help enterprises move from AI 
experimentation to enterprise-wide adoption. 
 
The greatest barrier to AI adoption is no longer the technology itself. It is the ability to reinvent operating models, 
decision-making and business processes. Through keynotes, masterclasses, panel discussions, and hands-on 
practical demonstrations, TechCon is designed to develop the workforce capabilities required to build intelligent 
enterprises and create lasting competitive advantage. 
 
“The question facing enterprises is no longer whether to adopt AI. It’s whether they have the talent and 
operational discipline to deploy it responsibly across the business,” said Sudhir Singh, Chief Executive Officer 
and Executive Director, Coforge. “We are building the skills, engineering discipline, and execution intensity 
required to embed intelligence into business operations responsibly and effectively. TechCon reflects that 
commitment and the role our people play in helping clients navigate this transformation.” 
 
The centerpiece of the event is a company-wide AI hackathon that attracted more than 3,000 participants, 
alongside an Innovation Showcase highlighting how AI can transform operations, accelerate decision-making, 
reimagine customer experiences, and unlock new sources of growth and productivity. Together, these initiatives 
demonstrate how organizations can move beyond experimentation and translate AI into tangible business results. 
 
“The AI era is creating a competitive reset across every industry, with a small group of winners pulling away 
from the pack,” said Sunil Fernandes, Chief Operating Officer, Coforge. “Through TechCon, we are building 
the skills, ecosystem partnerships, and innovation culture needed to help clients operationalize intelligence 
across the enterprise and reinvent how work gets done.” 
 
About Coforge 
Coforge is an AI-native engineering services leader, where AI is the very foundation of how we design, build, 
and deliver intelligent solutions for our clients. We use AI and hyperspecialized industry expertise to engineer 
autonomous enterprises. We combine AI agents with our AI-enabled workforce, including specialized FDEs in 
hybrid pod-based delivery units. With a deep focus on trusted AI, our solutions are secure, governed, and 
enterprise-grade. We are outcome-led by design. Moving beyond AI experimentation, we deliver measurable 
business outcomes—lower operating costs, faster cycle times, higher conversion rates, and sustained margin 
growth.
