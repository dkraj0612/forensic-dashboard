New services combine AI agents, FDEs, and human oversight and approval checkpoints to

Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | November 25, 2026 – Coforge Limited (NSE: 
COFORGE), a global AI-native engineering services leader, today announced that Coforge Nuuron 
has been expanded with new capabilities designed to provide an agentic remedy that helps enterprises 
reduce security backlogs and accelerate vulnerability closure across infrastructure, applications, cloud 
environments, data platforms, and AI systems. 
 
As enterprises expand digital operations and AI adoption, the cybersecurity threat landscape is evolving 
rapidly. Attackers are increasingly using AI and autonomous agents to identify vulnerabilities, exploit 
weaknesses, and adapt their tactics at machine speed. While security teams can effectively discover 
vulnerabilities quickly, most remediation activities remain manual. As vulnerability volumes grow, 
security teams struggle to prioritize, coordinate, and execute fixes quickly enough to reduce risk. The 
result is a widening gap between detection and closure, increasing the operational burden and the 
organization’s exposure. In large enterprises, remediation often remains constrained by fragmented 
workflows, resource shortages, and operational complexity. 
 
Coforge Nuuron continuously identifies, contextualizes, prioritizes, and remediates vulnerabilities across 
applications, infrastructure, cloud environments, AI systems, and digital platforms. Multiple human-in-
the-loop (HITL) governance checkpoints enable security and operations teams to review 
recommendations, approve high-risk actions, handle exceptions, and maintain full oversight of 
autonomous activities.   
 
"The cybersecurity industry has spent years optimizing vulnerability discovery, but most enterprises 
already know where their risks are,” said Ashish Kumar, Global Head, Cloud and AI 
Infrastructure, Coforge. "Agentic attackers can find a weakness and exploit it in a matter of hours, 
yet traditional remediation takes weeks. That’s the gap that Coforge Nuuron’s new agentic remedy 
capabilities were built to close. We combine AI agents, human governance, and FDE expertise to help 
enterprises move from finding vulnerabilities to eliminating them consistently, quickly, and at scale." 
 
The service leverages the Coforge Nuuron enterprise AI enablement suite to establish a scalable 
remediation framework that combines the speed of agentic AI with the control, transparency, and 
accountability required for enterprise operations. Coforge Nuuron provides the intelligence, 
orchestration, governance, auditability, policy enforcement, and learning framework that allows 
autonomous remediation agents to operate safely within enterprise environments while maintaining 
compliance and operational controls. 
 
The agents continuously learn from enterprise-specific policies and operating environments, and 
multiple approval and governance checkpoints ensure full visibility and authority over material changes. 
Remediation is led by expert forward deployed engineers (FDEs) that work inside client environments
