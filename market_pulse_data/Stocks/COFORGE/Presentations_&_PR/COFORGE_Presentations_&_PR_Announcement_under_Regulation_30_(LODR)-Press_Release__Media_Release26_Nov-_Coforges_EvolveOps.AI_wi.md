Coforge EvolveOps.AI Wins “AI Platforms, Infrastructure & Developer 
Tools” Award at ET’s Most Innovative AI Product Awards 2026 
 
Recognition highlights Coforge’s proven impact in accelerating autonomous enterprise 
operations and infrastructure management 
 
Greater Noida, India – November 26, 2026 – Coforge Limited (NSE: COFORGE), a global AI-native 
engineering services leader, has been named the winner of the "AI Platforms, Infrastructure & Developer 
Tools" in enterprise category at The Economic Times’ Most Innovative AI Product Awards 2026 for 
Coforge EvolveOps.AI™ (part of the Coforge Nuuron enterprise AI enablement suite). The award 
recognizes outstanding AI products that demonstrate architectural originality, production scalability, and 
transformative enterprise value. 
 
The awards followed an evaluation by an independent jury of industry leaders and technology experts, 
who reviewed nominees across criteria including technical innovation, agentic reasoning capabilities, 
infrastructure reliability, and responsible governance. Coforge was cited for its ground-up engineering 
of EvolveOps.AI, which establishes a unified, autonomous control plane across compute, multi-cloud 
environments, and business applications. 
 
“This award from The Economic Times validates our vision to engineer truly autonomous, resilient 
enterprise systems,” said Ashish Kumar, Global Head, Cloud & AI Infrastructure, Coforge. “With 
EvolveOps.AI, we have moved beyond reactive monitoring to deliver autonomous, self-healing 
technology operations. By combining open-source foundations, purpose-built small language models, 
and multi-agent systems, we are enabling our clients to achieve zero disruption, zero touch, and zero 
friction across their enterprise estate.” 
 
Operating in live production for 27 enterprise clients, Coforge EvolveOps.AI actively manages more than 
110,000 nodes, 500,000 assets, and 3,100+ GPUs, serving more than 45 million end users. Powered by 
28 operationalized AI agent personas collaborating over the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the platform 
harmonizes more than 16 million events daily across 85 telemetry systems, achieving a 97.5% reduction 
in operational noise.  
 
In production, enterprises leveraging the platform have achieved a 40% autonomous resolution of 
incidents, a 60% reduction in combined MTTD and MTTR, a 25% reduction in downtime, and a 40% 
reduction in IT operational expenses, with zero P1 incidents across 193 critical systems over the last 
twelve months.
