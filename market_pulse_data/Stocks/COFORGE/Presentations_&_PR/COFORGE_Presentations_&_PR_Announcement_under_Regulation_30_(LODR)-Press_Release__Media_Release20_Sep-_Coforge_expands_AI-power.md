Coforge Advances AI-Powered Vehicle Lifecycle Intelligence 
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | September 20, 2026 – Coforge Limited (NSE: 
COFORGE), a global AI-native engineering services leader, today announced expanded capabilities 
that help automotive lenders, fleet operators, and remarketing organizations transform vehicle 
inspections into AI-powered lifecycle intelligence, enabling faster and more informed business 
decisions throughout the remarketing process.  
 
When a vehicle is returned, organizations often lack a trusted view of its condition, delaying critical 
decisions around repair, certification and resale. The solution creates a single, trusted vehicle condition 
report that brings together vehicle history, inspection data, operational context, and evidence from 
multiple enterprise systems.  
 
The solution assembles information including VIN, mileage, maintenance history, and return data 
before inspections begin. During the inspection process, standardized workflows capture 360-degree 
condition evidence, assessments and wear. The result is a trusted digital record that can be used 
throughout the vehicle lifecycle.  
 
"Vehicle inspections should not end with documentation. They should create intelligence that powers 
every downstream decision," said Niraj Patel, Senior Vice President, Coforge. "By creating a 
trusted condition record, organizations can make faster, more confident decisions about repair, 
certification, routing, and remarketing."  
 
The solution is already operating at scale and supporting more than 60,000 vehicles annually. By 
transforming inspection data into actionable intelligence, Coforge helps clients reduce delays and 
optimize vehicle remarketing outcomes.
