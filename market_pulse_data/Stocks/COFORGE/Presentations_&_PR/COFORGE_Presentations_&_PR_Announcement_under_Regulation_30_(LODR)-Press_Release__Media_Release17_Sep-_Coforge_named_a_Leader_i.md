Coforge Positioned as a Leader Across Six Segments in 
NelsonHall’s 2026 NEAT for AI-Enabled Cloud Infrastructure 
Management Services  
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | September 17, 2026 – Coforge Limited 
(NSE: COFORGE), a global AI-native engineering services leader, announced that it has been 
named a “Leader” in six evaluation segments within NelsonHall’s 2026 Vendor Evaluation & 
Assessment Tool (NEAT) for AI-Enabled Cloud Infrastructure Management Services. 
 
The report evaluated 19 global systems integrators to assess their AI-based cloud 
infrastructure management capabilities. The findings positioned Coforge as a Leader across 
multiple categories, including Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud 
Platform (GCP), Cloud Management and Orchestration Services, Overall Cloud Infrastructure 
Management Services, and AI Capabilities. 
 
The NelsonHall report analyzes the performance of vendors offering AI-enabled cloud 
infrastructure management services, allowing strategic sourcing managers to assess their 
capability across a range of criteria and business situations and identify the best performing 
vendors.  
 
John Laherty, IT Services Research Director at NelsonHall, said “Coforge is investing 
in its EvolveOps.AI platform to support hybrid cloud services and enable clients to build, 
operate, analyze, and autonomously manage their hybrid cloud estates. It has further 
integrated GenAI and agentic AI capabilities into each layer of the operating model across 
cloud services. This is integrated with its agentic framework, which powers all Coforge 
solutions, including agentic-AI-powered operations and assistants, an AI agent factory, and 
AI-powered FinOps.”  
 
“We are honored to be recognized as a ‘Leader’ in cloud infrastructure management services 
by NelsonHall,” said Ashish Kumar, Senior Vice President and Cloud Business Unit 
Head, Coforge. “This recognition reflects the strength of our cloud engineering capabilities, 
deep hyperscaler partnerships, and our ability to deliver complex cloud transformations at 
scale. We have invested heavily in building AI-powered operational platforms such as 
EvolveOps.AI that enable autonomous, self-healing cloud operations that deliver real business 
outcomes.”
