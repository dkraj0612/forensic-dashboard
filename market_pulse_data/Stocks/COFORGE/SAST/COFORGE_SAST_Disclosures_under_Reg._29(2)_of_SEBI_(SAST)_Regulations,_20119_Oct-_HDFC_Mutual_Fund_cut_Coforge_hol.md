Sub: Regulation 29(2) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial
Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations. 2011
We refer to our letter dated August 25, 2023 wherein we had mentioned that as on August 24,
2023, the aggregate holding of the Schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund in "Coforge Limited" ("The
Company") was 6.31% of the paid up equity share capital of the Company (Paid up Equity
Share Capital being Rs. 61,10,72,630/- comprising 6,11,07,263 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/-
each).
Further, in accordance with the captioned regulation and other applicable laws / regulations,
if any, we wish to inform you that there has been a decrease in the shareholding of the
Company from the last reporting by the Schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund by 2.01% as of
October 06, 2026.
As on October 06, 2026 the aggregate holding of the Schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund in the
Company is 4.30% of the paid-up equity share capital of the Company (Paid up Equity Share
Capital being Rs. 88,53,81,622/- comprising 44,26,90,811 Equity Shares of Rs. 21- each).
As per Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and
Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 please find enclosed the details of change in the shareholding
in the prescribed format enclosed as Annexure 1.
Kindly acknowledge receipt of the same.
Thanking you,
Yours faithfully,
