PRESS RELEASE 
 
Almondz Global Securities Limited acted as Transaction Advisor to Greenjoules on its 
₹150 crore Series B fundraise 
 
Almondz Global Securities Limited (AGSL) announces that it acted as a Transaction Advisor 
(via its subsidiary Almondz Financial Services Limited) to Greenjoules Private Limited for the 
company's ₹150 crore Series B funding round.  
 
The round was co-led by Amazon's Smbhav Venture Fund and Climate Pledge Fund, with 
participation from Rainmatter (Zerodha's investment arm) and Singularity AMC's Strategic 
Autonomy Fund. Blue Ashva Capital, which led Greenjoules' Series A in 2021, remains the 
company's largest shareholder. Alongside the equity round, Greenjoules has secured a ₹100 
crore soft loan from the Technology Development Board (TDB), Department of Science & 
Technology, Government of India.  
 
About the transaction 
Greenjoules Private Limited is in the business of manufacturing 2G “drop-in” biofuel. The 
company has developed innovative proprietary technology to manufacture biofuel that uses 40 
different types of agri-wastes, so there is no impinging on human/cattle consumption. The 
process also produces biochar as a byproduct, which can act as a soil supplement or as an input 
to Green Construction. Its products are tested and validated by IIT Chennai and ARAI and meet 
with the Indian diesel standards (BIS:1460) as claimed by the company. The company has its 
manufacturing facility in Chakan, Pune with a production capacity of 350 KL every month. The 
manufacturing process has already been developed and tested at Greenjoules’ Chakan plant 
near Pune, where the technology has been refined in operating conditions rather than in a 
controlled research setting.  
Greenjoules plans to produce 200,000 litres of biocrude a day from agricultural waste. The 
project is now partly funded by Amazon, Singularity and Rainmatter. In addition, Greenjoules 
has received a soft loan commitment to the extent of Rs. 100 crores from Technology 
Development Board (TDB), Department of Science & Technology, Government of India for 
setting up greenfield bio-fuel project. 
These project initiatives have the potential to establish a sustainable model for converting crop 
residue into an economically viable renewable energy resource, thereby discouraging stubble 
burning. Beyond strengthening fuel supply, this initiative could generate an additional source of 
income for farmers while contributing to improved air quality in regions affected by seasonal 
stubble burning. 
 
About Almondz Global Securities Limited  
Almondz Global Securities Limited (AGSL) is a diversified group operating through three 
verticals: financial services (broking, wealth advisory, corporate advisory, merchant banking, 
and debt and equity operations), infrastructure consulting through its subsidiary viz. Almondz 
Global Infra Consultant Limited (AGICL), and green fuel through its associate company viz. 
Premier Green Innovations Private Limited (PGIPL), which runs ethanol plants in Himachal 
Pradesh (285 KLPD, at full capacity) and Odisha (200 KLPD).
