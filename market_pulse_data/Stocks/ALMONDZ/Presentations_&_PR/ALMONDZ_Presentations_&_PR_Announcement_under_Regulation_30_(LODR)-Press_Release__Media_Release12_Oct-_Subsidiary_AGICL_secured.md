AGICL New Business; New Orders Cross Rs. 150 Crore 
Almondz Global Securities’ infrastructure consultancy subsidiary secures Rs. 154 crores of new order book in 
the 1st Half year as on 30th September 2026, against Rs. 72 crores in the 1st Half year in the previous; orders to 
be executed over three years 
New Delhi, 12th October 2026: Almondz Global Securities Limited (AGSL) today announces that its subsidiary, 
Almondz Global Infra Consultant Limited (AGICL), has secured more than double new order book in the 1st Half 
year 2026-27 as compared with the 1st Half year 2025-26. AGICL has secured new order book of Rs. 154 crores in 
the 1st Half year as on 30th September 2026, as against Rs. 72 crores in the 1st Half year as on 30 September 
2025, a growth of about 214%. With this, AGICL’s Total orders book have crossed the Rs. 400 crores mark as on 
30th September 2026. 
The new orders will be executed over a period of three years, giving the Company a clear, multi-year pipeline of 
work and strong visibility on delivery and revenue as these mandates are mobilized.

A diversified order inflow 
The new business spans highways, bridges, railways, urban and industrial infrastructure, and an overseas 
mandate, and deepens AGICL’s presence in eastern India and Uttar Pradesh. Some of select mandates secured 
during the year in H1 2026-27 include: 
• IDCO, Odisha: Programme Management Consultant for various IDCO projects, valued at approximately Rs. 
33.15 crore (excluding GST) over two years, the highest-value sole bid won by AGICL to date. 
• Mission 3000 MT traction power works, West Central Railway: Authority Engineer assignment valued at 
Rs. 21.23 crore. 
• Kulgaon Badlapur Municipal Council, Maharashtra: DPR and PMC for conservation of the Ulhas River, 
flood control and disaster management, for Rs. 15 crore. 
• Tindivanam–Nagari New Broad-Gauge Line, Southern Railway: PSSA assignment valued at Rs. 12.13 
crore.
