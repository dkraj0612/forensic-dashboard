PRESS RELEASE 
New Delhi, October 31, 2026 
Subject: Financial Performance Update for 2Q FY 2026-27 and Business Outlook for 3Q FY 
2026-27 
Almondz Global Securities Limited (“the Company”) is pleased to provide an update on its 
consolidated financial performance for the second quarter (2Q) of the financial year 2026-27, 
together with its guidance for the upcoming third quarter (3Q) of FY 2026-27. 
Consolidated Financial Highlights 
The Company has reported consolidated total revenue of Rs. 40.09 crore with a profit of Rs. 9.13 
crore for 2Q FY 2026-27, as compared to total revenue of Rs. 34.00 crore with a profit of Rs. 3.86 
crore in 2Q FY 2025-26.

Performance Overview – Green Fuel Business 
The Company is engaged in the production of green biofuel and distillery operations through its 
joint venture company, Premier Green Innovations Private Limited (PGIPL), in which the Company 
holds 40.99% of the total equity. The holding company, Avonmore Capital & Management Services 
Limited, also holds an 8.88% equity stake in PGIPL. 
During 2Q FY 2026-27, PGIPL recorded total revenue of Rs. 202.24 crore and a profit of Rs. 12.59 
crore, as compared to revenue of Rs. Rs. 129.90 crore and a profit of Rs. 3.66 crore in 2Q FY 2025-
26.

The capacity of the Himachal Plant is 285 KLPD and is operational at full capacity. 
Status of Odisha Plant – PGIPL 
The Odisha plant has not yet commenced commercial production on a full scale, as the procurement 
agreement with the Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) is awaited. Nevertheless, the plant produced 
71 lac litres of ethanol during 2Q FY 2026-27. 
The OMCs have floated the tender for the supply of ethanol for FY 2026-27, and PGIPL has 
participated in the tender. The Company expects full-fledged production and supply of ethanol to 
commence from the third week of November 2026, by which time the allocation process is expected 
to be completed by the OMCs. 
The capacity of Odisha Plant is 200 KLPD and is expected to go fully operational at 80% capacity 
in the third quarter. 
Performance Overview – Infrastructure Consulting Business 
During 2Q FY 2026-27, the Company earned revenue of Rs. 31.35 crore with a profit of Rs. 2.90 
crore, as compared to revenue of Rs. 25.89 crore with a profit of Rs. 2.42 crore in 2Q FY 2025-26. 
The increase in revenue is mainly attributable to higher revenue from DPR projects. The Company 
is hopeful of sustaining the growth trend in both revenue and profitability over the previous year.
