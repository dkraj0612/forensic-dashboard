### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Almondz Global Securities Ltd. has approved the strategic demerger of its Infrastructure Advisory Business, which contributed a minor 4.58% to its total FY 2025-26 turnover, into a new, independent entity, Almondz Global Infra-Consultant Limited. This move aims to enhance strategic focus, optimize resource allocation, and unlock value for both the core securities business and the specialized infrastructure advisory segment. A key red flag is the significant number of required regulatory and shareholder approvals, including from the NCLT and public shareholders, which introduces execution risk and an extended timeline for this corporate restructuring.

Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

1. Scheme of Arrangement between Almondz Global Securities Limited and Almondz Global Infra – Consultant Limited 
and their respective shareholders (“Scheme”) after considering the recommendations of the Audit Committee and the 
Committee of Independent Directors.

The Demerged Company is presently engaged in business of stock broking 
& wealth advisory providing professional advisory/consultancy services in 
the equity and debt capital markets and infrastructure advisory. The 
business and activities forming part of the Demerged Undertaking are 
carried on by the Demerged Company directly and through AGICL. In order 
to have focused management on each business activity, it is proposed to 
restructure the business in such a way that the Infrastructure Advisory 
Business is carried through an independent listed entity. 
The following benefits shall accrue on demerger of the Demerged 
Undertaking into the Resulting Company: 
a) 
The infrastructure consultancy assignments involve long gestation 
periods, government and multilateral agency interactions, technical 
teams, and sector-specific expertise. This business is completely 
different from broking business. Both sets of businesses carry 
significant potential for growth and profitability. The nature of risks, 
rewards, financial profile, competition and opportunities are separate 
and distinct for the Infrastructure Advisory Business and the broking 
business. Housing multiple businesses, including the Infrastructure 
Advisory Business, under a single entity may not optimally serve the 
interests of investors, lenders and other stakeholders, thus the 
segregation of the Infrastructure Advisory Business into the Resulting 
Company would enable better allocation of resources, streamlined
