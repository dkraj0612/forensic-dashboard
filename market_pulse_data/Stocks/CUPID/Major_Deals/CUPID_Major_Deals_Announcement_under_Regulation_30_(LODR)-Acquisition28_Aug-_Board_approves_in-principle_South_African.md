Dear Sir / Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III and other applicable provisions of the 
SEBI Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors has accorded in-
principle approval for the establishment of a South African manufacturing venture with a South 
African partner. 
The proposed venture envisages establishment of a suitable entity in South Africa for manufacture 
and supply of Male condoms and related products, considering the increasing emphasis in the 
South African domestic market on domestic manufacturing, localisation and local value addition, 
with Cupid Limited proposed to hold up to 49% of the equity share capital and providing its 
technical and manufacturing expertise, know-how, technology-transfer support, quality-control 
systems and training, while the required capital expenditure, working capital and operating 
funding are proposed to be arranged by the South African partner.  
The in-principle approval is subject to finalisation of definitive agreements and arrangements.
