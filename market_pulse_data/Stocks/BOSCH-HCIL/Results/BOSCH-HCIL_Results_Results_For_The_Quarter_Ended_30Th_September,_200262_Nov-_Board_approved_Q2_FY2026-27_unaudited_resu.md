Notes to financial results: 
The above statement of unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026 ('the Statement') of Bosch Home Comfort India Limited 
(formerly known as Johnson Controls-Hitachi Air Conditioning India Limited) ('the Company') is reviewed by the Audit Committee and thereafter approved by the 
Board of Directors at their respective meetings held on November 02, 2026. The unaudited financial results are prepared in accordance with the Companies (Indian 
Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015 (as amended) (Ind AS) prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, other recognised accounting practices and 
policies to the extent applicable. 
2 
In accordance with Ind AS 108 - Operating Segment, the Company has identified two business segments viz., i) Cooling products for comfort and commercial use 
and ii) Design and development services. The segment wise information as required by Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 is furnished in Annexure 1. 
3 
Exceptional items include: 
(a) During the year ended March 31 , 2026, Robert Bosch GmbH completed the acquisition of the Residential & Light Commercial HVAC business of the joint venture 
between Johnson Controls and Hitachi Air Conditioning globally, including its Indian operations. Following this change in ownership and to ensure brand alignment, 
the Company has been renamed to Bosch Home Comfort India Limited. The name change was approved by the shareholders on October 18, 2025, and by the 
Ministry of Corporate Affairs with effect from November 4, 2025. 
Further, pursuant to global acquisition by Robert Bosch GmbH, the Company had recognized a one time retention bonus expense of Rs. 65.0 million to support 
continuity in key managerial rotes during the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025 and year ended March 31, 2026. 
During the previous quarter ended June 30, 2026, Bosch Global Software Technologies Private Limited had sold 20,92,440 Equity Shares of the Company 
(representing 7.70% of the Paid-up Equity Share Capital of the Company) through offer for sale via stock exchange mechanism to achieve minimum public 
shareholding (MPS) requirements. Post successful completion of Offer for Sale, the Promoter Shareholding had reduced from 82.22% to 74.52% of the Paid-up 
Equity Share Capital of the Company and the Company achieved the MPS requirements, as mandated under rule 19A of the Securities Contracts (Regulations) 
Rules, 1957 and Regulation 38 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
(b) On November 21 , 2025, the Government of India notified four Labour Codes effective immediately replacing the existing 29 labour laws. The Company during the 
year ended March 31, 2026 had assessed and disclosed the impact of changes in labour laws amounting to Rs. 140.8 million on the basis of information available as 
an exceptional item consistent with the guidelines provided by Institute of Chartered Accountant of India. 
(c) Expenses pertaining to Severance pay amounting to Rs. 26.1 million, Rs. 59.1 million and Rs. 89.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, half year 
ended September 30, 2025 and for the year ended March 31 , 2026 respectively paid to employees/workers pursuant to realignmenUreorganisation/voluntary 
retirement scheme. 
(d) On June 24, 2025, a fire incident occurred at one of the Company's warehouses, resulting in an estimated inventory loss of Rs. 311 .0 million and a reversal of 
GST input tax credit amounting to Rs. 95.7 million. During the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025, the Company further recognized an expense of Rs. 
8.6 million on account of standard deductions as per the terms of the insurance policy. 
During the year ended March 31, 2026, pursuant to the final assessment by the surveyor, the Company received Rs. 503.7 million as full and final settlement. 
Additionally, the Company had sold scrap of damaged inventories amounting to Rs. 13.4 million as agreed with insurance company. On settlement of insurance claim 
basis realisable value and sate of damaged inventories excess amount received of Rs. 112.0 million was disclosed as exceptional item for the year ended March 31 , 
2026. 
4

not available to the Company. 
5 
The Company predominantly operates in the air conditioning business which is seasonal in nature and major market demand is generated during the first and last 
quarter of every financial year.
