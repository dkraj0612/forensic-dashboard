Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”), read with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated

and Tubular Towers and for project execution of 153.7 MW of wind power projects to be set up in the State 
of Madhya Pradesh (the “Project”). The details required under the SEBI Listing Regulations read with the 
SEBI Master Circular are set out in Annexure-A.
