RATING ACTION/OUTLOOK:  
Brickwork Ratings (BWR) has reaffirmed the long-term rating at BWR AA with a Stable outlook 
for the Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Jindal Saw Ltd (JSL or the company).  
 
The ratings continue to reflect the company’s strong business risk profile, which is supported by its 
market leadership in the carbon steel pipes segment, diversified revenue streams, and a healthy 
financial risk profile characterized by low gearing and comfortable debt protection metrics. The 
ratings also benefit from the experienced promoters, an established track record of approximately 
four decades with demonstrated execution capability, a healthy order book, a diversified customer 
base, and a product portfolio applicable across various end-user industries. However, these 
strengths are partially offset by exposure to subsidiaries and group companies, susceptibility to 
fluctuations in raw material prices and foreign exchange rates, inherent industry risks, and intense 
competition. 
 
Brickwork Ratings (BWR) continues to monitor JSL’s persistently high exposures to group and 
subsidiary companies. This financial exposure is expected to increase due to recently announced 
capital expenditure (capex) plans in the MENA region (Abu Dhabi and Saudi Arabia, June 2025). 
The timely and budget-compliant completion of these capex projects, along with the generation of 
operating cash flows as envisaged from the new international facilities, will remain a key 
monitorable. 
 
Additionally, BWR will continue to assess the impact (if any) of the Honourable Supreme Court’s 
ruling regarding state governments' power to tax mineral rights and mineral-bearing lands 
(judgment dated August 14, 2024) on the company's operations. The company operates a low-grade 
iron ore mine in Rajasthan. Ratings will be reviewed appropriately as further clarity emerges.

KEY RATING DRIVERS 
 
Credit Strengths-:  
Established track record of operations and experienced management: The company 
commenced its operations in 1984 and has a successful track record of operations of around four 
decades in the existing business line. JSL’s long-standing track record and the extensive experience 
of its promoters have helped it establish relationships with various stakeholders for its business. The 
company’s board consists of six independent directors, ensuring it is a professionally run 
organization. Furthermore, the company’s board is supported by an experienced and qualified 
management team that looks after the day-to-day operations. 
 
Strong business risk profile supported by diversified revenues and geographically diversified 
operations: JSL is a diversified steel pipe manufacturer specializing in Stainless Steel Tube Pipes 
(SSTP) and carbon steel pipes, which find application in various sectors including power, oil and 
gas, food and chemicals. The company holds a dominant market position in both longitudinal and 
helical Submerged Arc Welded (SAW) and ductile iron (DI) pipe segments, on account of its 
extensive capacity, loyal customer base, and advanced technology, which consistently delivers 
above-average profitability. JSL operates nationally with manufacturing units spread across seven 
states. With the acquisition of Sathavahana Ispat Limited (SIL) in 2023, JSL has strengthened its 
footprint in South India's DI pipe market, utilizing SIL's manufacturing, coke oven, and power plant 
assets to cater to that region. 
 
Healthy Order Book Position: JSL currently boasts a strong revenue outlook for the next 3-4 
quarters, supported by a robust order book valued at nearly US$1.31 billion as of March 31, 2025. 
The order book is primarily concentrated in ductile iron (DI) pipes and HSAW pipes. Export order 
as a percentage of order book has improved to 32% for June 30, 2025, providing a natural hedge to 
the company’s export-import mix. While steel pipe demand—driven by consistent government 
focus on infrastructure, water, and sanitation, and renewed investment in the oil and gas sector—is 
expected to moderately slow in the short term, the medium-to-long term prospects remain steady.  
 
Improved operational performance in FY25, albeit a moderation in Q1FY26- In FY25, JSL's 
Total Operating Income (TOI) experienced a marginal moderation, predominantly due to a general 
decline in steel prices, while sales volumes remained consistent with the previous year. Despite this 
price pressure, profitability improved, a result of favorable margins from export orders, a strategic 
shift in product mix, and softened raw material prices.

This moderation can be attributed to several challenges faced in Q1FY26, including slower 
execution by EPC customers, strategic maintenance shutdowns, and geopolitical instability in the 
Middle East. Brickwork Ratings (BWR) anticipates an improvement in operational performance 
during the second half of the year. Despite the expected decline, the company's profitability and 
financial risk profile are projected to remain healthy. 
 
Moderate financial risk profile: JSL has a moderate financial risk profile characterised by 
moderate debt coverage indicators and a comfortable standalone overall gearing. The overall 
gearing of the company, on a standalone basis, improved and continued to remain healthy at 0.25x 
as on March 31, 2025 (FY24: 0.39x). The TOL/TNW also improved to 0.60x as of March 31, 2025, 
from 0.85x in FY25. The interest service coverage ratio also improved to 6.66x in FY25 from 5.30x 
in FY24.  
 
Competitive advantage in cost structure for Pellet Plant: JSL operates a captive mine in 
Bhilwara, Rajasthan, with estimated reserves of nearly 180 MMT of low-grade iron ore and extracts 
about 6-7 million tonnes annually. The pellets division reported sales of Rs. 1791 cr. in FY25 
(FY24: Rs. 1814 cr.). The captive availability of iron ore leads to a competitive cost structure for 
the pellets manufactured by the company and relatively stable operating profit margins for the 
company as a whole. 
 
Credit Weakness-: 
Exposure to subsidiaries/group companies: JSL maintains substantial investments, totaling 
₹2,090 crore as of March 31, 2025, in various domestic and international subsidiaries and joint 
ventures (JVs). Furthermore, JSL has issued corporate guarantees of ₹682 crore supporting these 
entities. This financial exposure is set to increase due to recently announced capital expenditure 
(capex) plans in the MENA region (Abu Dhabi and Saudi Arabia, June 2025). Timely and 
budget-compliant completion of these capex projects with the generation of operating cash flows as 
envisaged from the new international facilities will remain a key monitorable going forward. 
 
Susceptibility to fluctuation in raw material prices and forex: Steel and coking coal, the two 
primary raw materials for the company, experience high price volatility. A sharp adverse movement 
in either commodity's price could impact the company’s profitability. To mitigate this risk, the 
company procures steel based on its order book, minimizing time lags between new order 
acquisition and steel procurement, thus avoiding speculative practices to stabilize profitability 
margins. Additionally, the majority of the company's foreign exchange exposure is naturally 
hedged. The remaining exposure is managed by booking forward contracts. 
 
Inherent risk of the industry and intense competition: The sector is characterized by significant 
fragmentation, encompassing a diverse range of both organized and unorganized entities that offer 
comparable products and services. This landscape fosters intense competition, particularly from 
regional players, which consequently leads to pricing pressures and impacts the company’s 
profitability margins.

LIQUIDITY INDICATORS (Adequate): JSL has adequate liquidity marked by healthy net cash 
accruals of Rs. 2339 Crs in FY25 and cash and ash equivalents of Rs. 589 cr. as of March 31, 2025 
against scheduled debt repayment obligations aggregating to ~Rs. 323 crores for FY26. The 
maintenance capex towards de-bottlenecking and efficiency improvement requirements of Rs. 
600-700 crore are modular and will be funded through internal accruals. The current ratio of the 
company is also comfortable at 1.52x as of March 31, 2025.

Jindal Saw Ltd (JSL), the flagship company of PR Jindal group, was incorporated as Saw Pipes Ltd 
in 1984, and the name of the company was changed to Jindal Saw Ltd in February 2005. It 
manufactures LSAW pipes, HSAW pipes, DI pipes, seamless pipes, and pellets. The promoter 
group held a 63.26% equity stake in the company as on 30 June 2025. It is listed on both the 
Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE). The company’s 
manufacturing units in India with a capacity of 24,50,000 metric tonne per annum, are located in 
Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh. 
Besides this, it had an iron ore mine at Bhilwara (Rajasthan) with a beneficiation plant and pellet 
plant with an installed capacity of 16,50,000 MTPA, as on 31 March 2025.
