The brief profiles of the Senior Management Personnel: 
1. Mr. Rishi Pratim Ray currently serves as Director - Finance (Corporate Development) at Capillary 
Technologies India Limited, having been associated with the Group for over seven years since his 
appointment in February 2019. In his present role, he oversees Business Finance, Corporate 
Development, FP&A, and Investor Relations. He is a Chartered Accountant qualified from the 
Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), with prior audit and assurance experience at S.R. 
Batliboi & Associates LLP (EY) and PwC. He was conferred the COO Award of Excellence in 2024, in 
recognition of his contribution to the Group’s corporate finance infrastructure and investor reporting. 
2. Mr. Hipin Jain serves as Vice President – Customer Success at Capillary Technologies India Limited, 
bringing over four years of leadership in his current tenure, alongside a prior two-year term with the 
Group. He drives customer success strategies, product adoption, retention, and P&L expansion 
across India, the Middle East, and Africa. As a Loyalty, MarTech, and AI thought leader, he guides 
enterprises in scaling CRM and loyalty initiatives. He holds an MBA in Marketing from the 
University of Pune, and brings over 15 years of industry experience across Loyalty, CRM, Customer 
Success, and Sales gained through prior roles at Hansa Cequity and HCL Infosystems, delivering 
growth and ROI. 
3. Mr. Sumit Kumar serves as President - Middle East, Africa & India at Capillary 
Technologies, having joined the organisation in February 2025. He leads regional strategy, 
sales, digital ecosystems, customer engagement, and P&L growth across key international 
markets. He holds an MBA in Marketing from the University of Lincoln, and brings over 28 
years of global experience as a digital and commercial leader across the Retail, FMCG, 
Healthcare, and SaaS sectors.

Group, and Marks & Spencer, where he successfully scaled major regional loyalty platforms 
including Shukran and BLUE Rewards. He has been recognised among the "Top 100 Retail 
Minds".
