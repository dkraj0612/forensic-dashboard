CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT 
• 
Listing: The Company listed on BSE and NSE on November 5, 2026, following its IPO of 
2,23,24,120 fresh equity shares at ₹82 per share, raising gross proceeds of ₹1,830.58 Mn. 
• 
Deleveraging: ₹1,250 Mn of the IPO proceeds is earmarked for repayment of borrowings. 
Debt-to-equity, at 2.48x as of September 30, 2026 (pre-IPO), is targeted to fall to around 
1x. 
• 
Ghiloth efficiency: ₹151.85 Mn of the proceeds will fund the purchase of Siemens-leased 
equipment at the Ghiloth facility, reducing lease costs. An adjacent 11,250 sq. m. plot has 
been acquired at Ghiloth for an additional processing line. 
• 
Supa expansion: On November 19, 2026, the Company took possession of MIDC Plot 
E-5 at Supa, Maharashtra (34,300 sq. m.), to serve the home appliance and automotive 
clusters around Pune and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. 
 
MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY 
Mr. Nitin Vijaykumar Gupta, Managing Director, said: "Our first results as a listed company reflect 
strong demand from our appliance and engineering customers. H1 revenue grew nearly 38% and 
profit after tax grew over 80%, driven by higher volumes, deeper relationships with existing 
customers and lower finance costs. Q2 is seasonally softer for air-conditioner demand, and 
higher input and employee costs weighed on margins, but volumes still grew over 21%. With 
possession of our Supa site and added capacity at Ghiloth, we are well placed to serve growing 
appliance and automotive demand across India." 
Mr. Ashish Sonthalia, Chief Financial Officer, said: "Strengthening the balance sheet is our near-
term priority. Using ₹1,250 Mn of IPO proceeds to repay borrowings should bring our debt-to-
equity ratio towards 1x, lower finance costs and improve PAT margins and return on capital 
employed. Buying out the leased equipment at Ghiloth will further reduce fixed costs." 
ABOUT BOMBAY COATED & SPECIAL STEELS LIMITED 
Established in 1983, Bombay Coated & Special Steels Limited is a precision steel processing 
company with over four decades of industry experience. The Company provides value-added 
steel processing solutions, including slitting, cut-to-length, embossing and shearing, catering to 
OEMs and ODMs across the Home Appliances, Automotive, HVAC and General Engineering 
industries. 
The Company operates four strategically located manufacturing facilities at Wada, Bhiwandi, 
Ghiloth and Sri City, enabling efficient logistics and Just-in-Time deliveries. It has a long-standing 
strategic association with JSW Steel Coated Products Limited as an authorized service partner 
since 2021. 
With established customer relationships, advanced processing capabilities and a pan-India 
manufacturing presence, the Company continues to focus on customer diversification,
