Bombay Coated & Special Steels Limited Strengthens 
Growth Trajectory with 8.48-Acre Industrial Land Addition 
at Supa, Maharashtra 
Industrial land secured to support future infrastructure development and expansion 
opportunities, catering to the growing Home Appliances and Automotives industry. 
 
Mumbai, November 20, 2026: Bombay Coated & Special Steels Limited (BSE: 545018 | NSE: 
BCSSL), a precision steel processing company with over four decades of industry experience, has 
achieved a significant infrastructure milestone with the physical possession of an industrial plot 
measuring 34,300 square metres (approximately 8.48 acres) at Supa Parner Industrial Park, 
Taluka Parner, District Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra. 
The physical possession was formally handed over by the Maharashtra Industrial Development 
Corporation (MIDC) on November 19, 2026. 
 
STRENGTHENING INFRASTRUCTURE FOR KEY GROWTH INDUSTRIES 
The addition of this industrial land strengthens the Company's long-term infrastructure base and 
provides an opportunity to evaluate future expansion of its steel processing capabilities, 
particularly catering to the growing requirements of the Home Appliances and Automotive 
industries. 
The proposed development is aligned with the Company's focus on strengthening its value-
added processing capabilities, including slitting, cut-to-length, embossing and shearing. These 
services enable customized steel solutions, improve material handling and reduce customers' 
in-house processing requirements. 
The strategic location also provides an opportunity to enhance connectivity with key industrial 
clusters, improve logistics efficiency and facilitate Just-in-Time (JIT) deliveries, supporting 
customers in optimizing inventory levels and maintaining uninterrupted manufacturing 
operations. 
With four strategically located manufacturing facilities across Wada, Bhiwandi, Ghiloth and Sri 
City, the Company continues to strengthen its operational footprint and customer reach across 
India. The new land parcel provides additional flexibility to evaluate future infrastructure 
development in line with business requirements and market opportunities. 
The Company will execute the Agreement to Lease within 30 days from the date of possession, in 
accordance with the terms specified in the MIDC possession receipt.

ABOUT BOMBAY COATED & SPECIAL STEELS LIMITED 
Established in 1983, Bombay Coated & Special Steels Limited is a precision steel processing 
company with over four decades of industry experience. The Company provides value-added 
steel processing solutions, including slitting, cut-to-length, embossing and shearing, catering to 
OEMs and ODMs across the Home Appliances, Automotive, HVAC and General Engineering 
industries. 
The Company operates four strategically located manufacturing facilities at Wada, Bhiwandi, 
Ghiloth and Sri City, enabling efficient logistics and Just-in-Time deliveries. It has a long-standing 
strategic association with JSW Steel Coated Products Limited as an authorized service partner 
since 2021. 
With established customer relationships, advanced processing capabilities and a pan-India 
manufacturing presence, the Company continues to focus on customer diversification, 
operational efficiency and strengthening its position in India's precision steel processing 
industry.
