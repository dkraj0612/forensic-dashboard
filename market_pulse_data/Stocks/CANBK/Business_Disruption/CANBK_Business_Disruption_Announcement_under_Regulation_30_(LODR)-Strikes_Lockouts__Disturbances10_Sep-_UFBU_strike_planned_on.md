Ref: SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 
  
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
The Stock Exchanges are hereby informed that the United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU) consisting of seven 
unions, AIBEA, AIBOC, BEFI, NCBE, AIBOA, INBEF, INBOC has informed their decision to go on strike 
on following days: 
 
i. 
 11th September 2026,  
ii. 
28th, to 30th September 2026 and 
iii. Continuous strike from 26th October 2026. 
 
Though, the Bank is taking all the necessary steps for smooth functioning of Bank’s branches and offices on 
the days of Strike, in the event the strike materializes, the functioning of the branches and offices may be 
affected.
