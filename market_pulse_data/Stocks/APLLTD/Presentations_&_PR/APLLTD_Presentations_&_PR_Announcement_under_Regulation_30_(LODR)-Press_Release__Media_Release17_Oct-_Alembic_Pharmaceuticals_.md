PRESS RELEASE 
17th October, 2026,  Vadodara, India  
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited receives USFDA Final Approval for Ciprofloxacin and 
Dexamethasone Otic Suspension USP, 0.3% / 0.1%. 
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (Alembic) today announced that it has received final approval from 
the US Food & Drug Administration (USFDA) for its Abbreviated New Drug Application (ANDA) for 
Ciprofloxacin and Dexamethasone Otic Suspension USP, 0.3% / 0.1%. The approved ANDA is 
therapeutically equivalent to the reference listed drug product (RLD), Ciprodex Otic Suspension, 0.3% 
/ 0.1%, of Sandoz Inc. Ciprofloxacin and Dexamethasone Otic suspension is indicated for the treatment 
of infections caused by susceptible isolates of the designated microorganisms in the specific 
conditions: i) Acute Otitis Media (AOM) in pediatric patients (age 6 months and older) with 
tympanostomy tubes, and ii) Acute Otitis Externa (AOE) in pediatric (age 6 months and older), adult, 
and elderly patients. Refer label for a detailed indication. 
 
According to IQVIA, the estimated market size for Ciprofloxacin and Dexamethasone Otic Suspension 
USP, 0.3% / 0.1%, was US$110 million for twelve months ending June 2026. 
  
Alembic has a cumulative total of 246 ANDA approvals (226 final approvals and 20 tentative approvals) 
from USFDA.
