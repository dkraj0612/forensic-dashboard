Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited receives USFDA Final Approval for Olopatadine 
Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution USP, 0.7% (OTC). 
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (Alembic) today announced that it has received final 
approval from the US Food & Drug Administration (USFDA) for its Abbreviated New Drug 
Application for Olopatadine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution USP, 0.7% (OTC). The 
approved ANDA is therapeutically equivalent to the reference listed drug product (RLD), 
Pataday Once Daily Relief, 0.7%, of Alcon Laboratories, Inc. (Alcon). Olopatadine 
Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution USP, 0.7%, is indicated for the temporary relief of itchy 
eyes due to pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair and dander. Refer label for a detailed 
indication and usage. 
  
According to IQVIA, Olopatadine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution USP, 0.7%, had an 
estimated market size of US$20 million in the United States for twelve months ending 
September 2026. 
 
This ANDA was developed in collaboration with Orbicular Pharmaceutical Technologies Pvt 
Ltd. 
   
Alembic has a cumulative total of 248 ANDA approvals (229 final approvals and 19 tentative 
approvals) from the USFDA.
