Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that Ascension Mercantile 
Private Limited (“AMPL”), a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has 
informed us today that it has incorporated a wholly owned subsidiary, namely AMPL Green 
City Solutions Private Limited, which has allotted 10,000 equity shares (100% equity stake) at 
par to AMPL on September 17, 2026.

no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 are provided in the
