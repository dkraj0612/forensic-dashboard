Subject: Disclosure regarding transactions in Equity Shares by Members of the Promoter and 
Promoter Group 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
The Company wishes to inform that certain transactions in the Equity Shares of the Company 
were undertaken by the Members of the Promoter and Promoter Group of the Company 
through block deals on the stock exchanges on September 24, 2026, pursuant to which Godrej 
Industries Limited and Godrej Seeds & Genetics Limited have sold an aggregate of 1,02,30,000 
Equity Shares of the Company aggregating to 1.00% of paid up Equity Share Capital of the 
Company, while Mr. Nadir Godrej, Ms. Nisaba Godrej, Mr. Pirojsha Godrej and Ms. Tanya 
Dubash, have in aggregate acquired 1,02,30,000 Equity Shares of the Company aggregating to 
1.00% of Equity Shares of the Company.  
 
The transactions therefore do not result in any change in the overall shareholding of the 
Promoter and Promoter Group of the Company. 
 
The pre transaction and post transaction shareholding of the Promoter and Promoter Group of 
the Company remains unchanged at 53.05%. 
 
The aforesaid information is being provided in the interest of transparency and with a view to 
providing clarity to the stakeholders in relation to the shareholding position of the Promoter 
and Promoter Group. 
 
Kindly take the above on record.
