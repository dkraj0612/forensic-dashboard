Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

We refer to our letter dated 10 November, 2025 vide which we had informed the Exchanges 
that the Board of Directors, at its meeting held on 10 November, 2025 had approved the 
proposal to make investment by way of purchase and acquisition of 14,000 equity shares of 
OMR 1/- each representing 70% shareholding of Arabian Water Tech LLC based in Oman, 
held by Arabian (DIFC) SPC1 Limited and Arabian (DIFC) SPC2 Limited. 
 
In continuation to the above referred intimation and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI Listing 
Regulations"), we would like to inform you that the Company has paid an amount equivalent 
to OMR 21,000/- for acquisition of 14,000 equity shares of OMR 1/- each representing 70% 
shareholding of Arabian Water Tech LLC by the Company. 
 
We would like to further inform you that consequent upon the above remittance by the 
Company, the Company has received the Share Certificate for 14,000 equity shares of OMR 
1/- each representing 70% shareholding of Arabian Water Tech LLC, duly transferred in the 
name of the Company and accordingly, Arabian Water Tech LLC has become a subsidiary of 
the Company.
