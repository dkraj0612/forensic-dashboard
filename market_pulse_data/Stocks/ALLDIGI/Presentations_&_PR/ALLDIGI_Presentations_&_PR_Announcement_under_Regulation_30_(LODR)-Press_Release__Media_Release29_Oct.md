Key Business and Performance highlights of Q2 FY’27 
• Revenue up 4.0% YoY to ₹153.3 Cr; EBITDA up 28.5% YoY to ₹46.3 Cr  
• EBITDA margin expands 576 bps YoY to 30.2% on AI-led efficiencies and improved revenue mix  
• PAT grows 50.9% YoY to ₹26.6 Cr; Operating Cash Flow surges 11.0% YoY to ₹37.1 Cr  
• Tech & Digital revenue rises 12.9% YoY, reinforcing strategic growth momentum  
• International BPM revenue grows 12.6% YoY.  
• 51 lakhs+ employee records processed in the quarter, up 7.2% YoY, demonstrating platform scale   
• Continued focus on AI adoption and premium accounts to drive profitable growth 
• Board approved ₹48 Cr share buyback at ₹1,000 per share through the tender offer route subject 
to shareholder’s approval 
 
Commenting on the results, Chief Executive Officer Natarajan Laxsmanan said:  
“Q2 was a strong quarter for Alldigi. Our EBITDA margin rose above 30%, PAT grew by more than 50% and 
international business now makes up about 70% of our revenue. Our shift to a higher-value, technology-led 
model is working: Tech & Digital Revenue grew by 12.9% at margins of about 44%, and international BPM 
grew 12.6% while we continue to rationalise low-margin domestic BPM. 
We continue to invest in AI across our delivery and in our HR and payroll products, and we have strengthened 
our sales leadership in the US and Southeast Asia. With deeper relationships with existing clients and a 
growing base of new ones, we remain focused on delivering sustainable, profitable growth and long-term 
value for all our stakeholders”

About Alldigi Tech Limited: 
Alldigi Tech Limited (BSE: 532633, NSE: ALLDIGI), headquartered in Chennai and founded in 1998, is a 
Digitide Solutions company backed by Fairfax Holdings and a global business process leader with ~5,000 
employees across India, the Philippines and the US. It is India's leading Payroll Services Provider, processing 
~5.0 million pay slips each quarter on AI-enabled HR and payroll platforms, and its BPM business serves 
global clients, including Fortune 100 companies, across customer experience, collections, insurance, 
healthcare, transaction processing and compliance, with multilingual delivery from Manila in 12+ languages. 
For further details on Alldigi Technologies Limited, please visit Digital Business Services and Payroll 
Outsourcing | Alldigi Tech.
