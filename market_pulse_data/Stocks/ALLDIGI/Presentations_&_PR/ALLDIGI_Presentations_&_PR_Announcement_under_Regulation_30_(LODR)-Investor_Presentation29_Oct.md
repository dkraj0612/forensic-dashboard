updates, salary revisions, attendance and resignations. Future-dated 
changes are held for the correct pay period.
Employee personal data is masked before it reaches the AI model, 
each client has its own database and sensitive fields are encrypted. 
Validated records move to the payroll engine, with dashboards, plain-
language error reports and one-click reprocessing.
