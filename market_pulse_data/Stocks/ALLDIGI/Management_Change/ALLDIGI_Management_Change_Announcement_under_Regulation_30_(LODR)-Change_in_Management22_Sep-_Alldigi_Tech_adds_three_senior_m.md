Brief Profiles of Senior Management Personnel 
Ms. Saranya R 
Ms. Saranya R is Vice President – Operations at Alldigi Tech Limited and has over 
22 years of experience across business services, pre-sales strategy, payroll solutions, 
service delivery and operations management. She has extensive experience in 
managing complex global service transitions, including payroll and HR functions, 
and in building scalable service delivery models across high-volume operations. 
Prior to joining Alldigi, she served as Director – APJ Pre-Sales and Payroll Solutions 
at Strada. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Corporate Secretaryship.  
 
Mr. Vijay Krishna Subramanyam Naidu 
Mr. Vijay Krishna Subramanyam Naidu is Chief Business Officer – International 
BPM Operations and has over 25 years of leadership experience across healthcare, 
technology and global business operations. His experience includes building and 
scaling businesses, leading multi-country operations, driving transformation and 
strengthening client and partner relationships. In his current role, his mandate 
includes accelerating growth across international markets, strengthening go-to-
market execution and driving sustainable business outcomes. Prior to joining the 
organisation, he served as Vice President – Operations at EXL. He holds a Doctorate in Business 
Administration.  
Mr. Jeemit Rajesh Kothari 
Mr. Jeemit Rajesh Kothari is Vice President – Enterprise Sales Leader at Alldigi 
Tech Limited and has over 23 years of experience in sales, business development 
and growth leadership. His expertise spans enterprise B2B and SaaS sales, new 
client acquisition, key account management, go-to-market strategy, solution selling 
and P&L management. He has experience across managed services, HCM SaaS, 
CX automation, AI/ML platforms, cloud managed services and contact centre 
solutions. Prior to joining Alldigi, he served as Associate Vice President – Sales at 
PeopleStrong. He holds a Master’s degree in Business Management.
