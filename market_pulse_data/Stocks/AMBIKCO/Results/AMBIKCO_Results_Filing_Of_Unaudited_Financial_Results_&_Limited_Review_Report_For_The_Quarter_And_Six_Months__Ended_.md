### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Ambika Cotton Mills Limited reported robust financial performance for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026, demonstrating significant year-over-year growth in both revenue and net profit. However, this strong profitability is notably offset by a sharp decline in net cash generated from operating activities, which plummeted from Rs. 16,796 lakhs in the prior half-year to just Rs. 99 lakhs, primarily due to substantial increases in various current assets and higher tax payments. This significant reduction in operational cash flow, despite improved earnings, represents a material impact and a key red flag regarding the company's short-term liquidity and cash conversion efficiency.
