Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 
Change in Senior Management 
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 read with Clause 7 of Para A Part A of Schedule III, we advise that 
following senior executives are superannuating w.e.f. 31st July 2026:  
 
1. Shri Ashok Kumar Sharma, Deputy Managing Director & Chief Credit Officer 
and Chief Sustainability Officer. 
 
2. Shri Kameshwar Rao Kodavanti, Chief Financial Officer. 
 
New responsibility entrusted to the undernoted Deputy Managing Directors: 
 
1. Shri Satyendra Kumar Singh, as Deputy Managing Director & Chief Credit 
Officer and Chief Sustainability Officer. 
 
2. Shri Varinder Khanna, as Deputy Managing Director (CCG-II) Commercial 
Client Group. 
 
Further, in terms of our intimation vide letter No.: CC/S&B/AND/2026-27/298 dated 
15.07.2026, Shri Sunil Ramgopal Agrawal will take charge as Chief Financial Officer 
and Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) from 1st August 2026.
