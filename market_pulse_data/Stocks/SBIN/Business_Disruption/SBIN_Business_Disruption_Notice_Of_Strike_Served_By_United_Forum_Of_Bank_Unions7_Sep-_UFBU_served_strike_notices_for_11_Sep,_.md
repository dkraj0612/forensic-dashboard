Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 
Notice of Strike Served by United Forum of Bank Unions 
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, we advise that United 
Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU) on behalf of its seven constituents Unions/ 
Association, viz. All India Bank Employees' Association (AIBEA), All India Bank 
Officers' Confederation (AIBOC), National Confederation of Bank Employees 
(NCBE), All India Bank Officers' Association (AIBOA), Bank Employees Federation 
of India (BEFI), Indian National Bank Employees Federation (INBEF) and Indian 
National Bank Officers' Congress (INBOC), have served strike notices informing that 
the members of unions / associations propose to go on strike on following days: 
 
i. 
11th September 2026, 
ii. 
28th, 29th and 30th September 2026; and  
iii. Continuous strike from 26th October 2026. 
 
2. In this connection, we advise that while the Bank has made all necessary 
arrangements to ensure normal functioning of the branches and offices on the days 
of strike, it is likely that work in the Bank may be impacted by the strike.
