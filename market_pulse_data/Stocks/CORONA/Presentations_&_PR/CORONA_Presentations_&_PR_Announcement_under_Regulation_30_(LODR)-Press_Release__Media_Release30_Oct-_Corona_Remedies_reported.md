Commenting on the results, Mr. Nirav K. Mehta, Managing Director & CEO, CORONA Remedies 
Limited, said:  
“We are pleased to announce another quarter of strong, consistent growth. Revenue grew 22.2% 
year-on-year in Q2FY27 and 22% in H1FY27, ahead of our stated guidance. Our domestic 
business grew at 22.9%, well ahead of the Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM) growth of 12%, 
and our chronic portfolio grew at nearly twice the rate of its market segment.  
This outperformance reﬂects our disciplined execution and consistent focus on our long-term 
strategy. We remain committed to strengthening our core therapies, building powerful brands, 
driving operational excellence and investing in capabilities that will support our future growth. 
The Indian pharmaceutical industry is underpinned by strong structural demand drivers. Backed 
by our diversiﬁed portfolio, established brands, expanding market reach and experienced 
leadership team, we are well positioned to capitalise on these opportunities and sustain our 
growth momentum.”

About CORONA Remedies Limited  
Founded in 2004, CORONA Remedies Limited is an India-focused pharmaceutical company 
engaged in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of products across key therapeutic 
areas, including Women’s Healthcare, Cardio-Diabetes, Pain Management, and Urology. 
The company has established a strong presence in the Indian pharmaceutical market through its 
focus on scientiﬁc expertise, brand building, manufacturing excellence, and quality-driven 
operations. Its manufacturing facilities and research capabilities support its commitment to 
delivering high-quality healthcare solutions.
