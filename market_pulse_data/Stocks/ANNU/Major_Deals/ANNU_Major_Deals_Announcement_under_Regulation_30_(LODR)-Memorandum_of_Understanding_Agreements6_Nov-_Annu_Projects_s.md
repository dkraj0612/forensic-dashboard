Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) - 
Execution of Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with GEIE Solar Products India 
Private Limited 
 
With reference to the above-mentioned subject, we wish to inform you that Annu Projects 
limited (“the Company”) has executed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with GEIE 
Solar Products India Private Limited (“GEIE”), in connection with procurement and 
supply of materials for a rooftop solar project awarded to GEIE by Jaipur Vidyut Vitran 
Nigam Limited (“JVVNL”) 
 
Pursuant to the MoU, the Company is responsible for procurement and supply of specified 
materials, while GEIE remains the project executing agency and is responsible for 
installation, commissioning, operation and maintenance and other obligations under the 
award documents. The arrangement provides for a project account and an agreed payment 
mechanism for the supply consideration, subject to the terms of the MoU. 
 
Additional Details as required in accordance with the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with Master Circular bearing no. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 for compliance with the SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015 on January 30, 2026 are enclosed herewith. 
 
We request you to kindly take the above intimation on record.
