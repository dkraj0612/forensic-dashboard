Annu Projects Limited signs MoU with GEIE Solar Products 
India for supply of materials for 8,000 rooftop solar systems 
under PM-Surya Ghar scheme 
Agreement covers 8,000 grid-connected rooftop solar systems in Dausa, Rajasthan, 
marking Annu Projects' expansion into the renewable energy segment 
New Delhi, 06th November 2026: Annu Projects Limited, an established diversified 
infrastructure EPC company with over two decades of experience across telecom, 
sewerage, gas pipeline and railway signalling infrastructure, has signed a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with GEIE Solar Products India Private 
Limited for the procurement and supply of materials required for 8,000 grid-
connected rooftop solar systems in Dausa, Rajasthan.  
Each system will have a capacity of 1.1 kWp and will be installed under the utility-
led aggregation model of the Government of India's PM-Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, 
which aims to promote rooftop solar adoption among households. Under the 
arrangement, Annu Projects will be responsible for procuring and supplying the 
required materials, while GEIE Solar Products India, the awardee of the project, will 
undertake installation, commissioning and other project execution responsibilities. 
The project is scheduled to be completed within six months, as stipulated under the 
award. 
The project constitutes Lot-3 (Dausa) of a tender floated by Jaipur Vidyut Vitran 
Nigam Limited (JVVNL), which was awarded to GEIE Solar Products India. Under the 
MoU, Annu Projects will procure and supply solar PV modules, mounting structures, 
DC and AC cables, grid-tied inverters, internet dongles for remote monitoring and 
other balance-of-system components required for the installations. The materials will 
be procured in accordance with the technical specifications stipulated in the award 
documents and will be subject to verification by JVVNL officials prior to purchase. 
GEIE Solar Products India will be responsible for installation, commissioning, net 
metering, beneficiary coordination and the applicable operations and maintenance 
obligations under the project. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Sanjay Sarraf, Chairman and Managing 
Director, Annu Projects Limited, said, "This MoU marks an important step in 
expanding our participation in the renewable energy segment, leveraging our 
established procurement and infrastructure capabilities. India's renewable energy 
transition is creating significant opportunities across the infrastructure value chain, 
with decentralised solar power emerging as an important driver of this transformation. 
Government initiatives such as the PM-Surya Ghar scheme are further accelerating 
household adoption of rooftop solar and strengthening the ecosystem for clean energy 
solutions. Through this association, we look forward to contributing to the successful 
implementation of the Dausa project while exploring opportunities in the evolving 
renewable energy ecosystem. We believe that our experience across infrastructure 
segments will enable us to participate in emerging opportunities and support our long-
term growth and diversification objectives."

Mr. Krishna Kadariya, Managing Director and Co-director, GEIE Solar Products 
India Private Limited, said, "A dependable supply of materials is central to 
completing installations on schedule. With rooftop solar adoption gaining momentum 
in India, initiatives such as PM-Surya Ghar are creating opportunities to accelerate the 
deployment of decentralised renewable energy solutions across households. 
Successful execution of such large-scale programmes requires strong coordination 
across procurement, technology, supply chains and on-ground implementation. This 
MoU gives both companies a clear framework for the Dausa project, and we will work 
with Annu Projects to meet the requirements of the award and support the timely 
implementation of the installations." 
The MoU represents an expansion of Annu Projects' participation in the renewable 
energy ecosystem, complementing its established presence across infrastructure 
segments. The company aims to leverage its procurement capabilities and project-
related experience to explore emerging opportunities across renewable energy and 
allied infrastructure sectors. 
About Annu Projects Limited: 
Annu Projects Limited (formerly Annu Infra Construct (India) Private Limited) is a 
New Delhi-headquartered infrastructure and EPC solutions company with a track 
record spanning over two decades. Established in 2003, the company specialises in 
the design, development, implementation and operations and maintenance of 
infrastructure projects across telecom, sewerage, gas pipeline and railway signalling 
segments. Its diversified capabilities encompass overhead and underground utility 
infrastructure, catering to the requirements of government departments, public 
sector undertakings and private sector organisations. 
Supported by its fleet of specialised machinery and equipment and an experienced 
leadership team, Annu Projects focuses on delivering infrastructure solutions across 
multiple sectors. The company is led by Mr. Sanjay Kumar Sarraf, Chairman and 
Managing Director, and Mr. Krishna Ranjan, Whole-Time Director.
