Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with sub-para 8 of Para B and/or sub-para 20 of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of 
the SEBI Listing Regulations, and in accordance with the Company’s Policy on Determination of Materiality for 
Disclosure of Events or Information, and further to our earlier communication dated February 4, 2025, whereby the 
Company had informed about the receipt of an assessment order dated January 28, 2025, issued by the Deputy 
Commissioner, Ranchi South Division (“GST Authority”) under Section 74 of the Jharkhand State Goods and 
Services Tax Act, 2017, read in conjunction with the relevant sections of the CGST Act, 2017 and the IGST Act, 2017, 
imposing a penalty of Rs. 32.50 lakh and interest of Rs. 8.46 Lakh on account of alleged non-payment of interest on 
the reversal of GST Input Tax Credit in Form GSTR-3B for the FY 2017-18 (“Assessment Order”), we hereby provide 
the following update: 
 
• 
The Company has received a favourable order for FY 2017-18, issued by the Office of the Commissioner 
(Appeals), Central Goods and Service Tax & Central Excise, Ranchi, Jharkhand (“Appellate Authority”), which 
was received by the Company on October 1, 2026, at 12:05 p.m. (“Appeal Order”). Through the Appeal Order, 
the Appellate Authority annulled the Assessment Order, waived the entire penalty amount of Rs. 32.50 lakh 
and redetermined the interest amount by reducing it from Rs. 8.46 lakh to Rs. 1.28 lakh. Please note that the 
revised interest of Rs. 1.28 lakh has already been discharged by the Company during the appellate 
proceedings. 
 
The financial impact of this order is limited solely to the aforesaid relief granted and interest amount paid as per 
the Appeal Order, with no other impact on the operational, financial, or other activities of the Company. 
 
We request you to take the above on record and treat it as compliance under the applicable provisions of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations, if any.
