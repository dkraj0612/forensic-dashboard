Krishnan Sundaram Appointed as Chief Growth & New Business Officer at Bajaj 
Electricals Limited  
11 August, 2026, Mumbai: Bajaj Electricals Limited announces the appointment of Krishnan Sundaram 
as Chief Growth & New Business Officer, effective August 11, 2026. In this role, Krishnan will be 
responsible for identifying, evaluating and scaling new business opportunities that can accelerate Bajaj 
Electricals' growth journey, strengthen the company’s portfolio, and create new avenues of value 
creation for customers and stakeholders. 
Krishnan Sundaram is an accomplished business leader with over 26 years of diverse experience across 
business management, growth strategy, marketing transformation, innovation, category creation, and 
P&L leadership. He joins Bajaj Electricals from Vini Cosmetics, where he served as Chief Executive 
Officer. Prior to this, he spent more than two decades with Hindustan Unilever Limited and Unilever 
PLC, holding several leadership roles across India and global markets. 
Shekhar Bajaj, Chairman, Bajaj Electricals Limited, said, “We are pleased to welcome Krishnan 
Sundaram to Bajaj Electricals. His extensive experience in building businesses, driving innovation and 
creating new categories will bring valuable perspective to our leadership team. We look forward to his 
contribution as we continue to strengthen our portfolio and create meaningful value for our customers 
and stakeholders.” 
Sanjay Sachdeva, Managing Director & CEO, Bajaj Electricals Limited, added, “We are delighted to 
welcome Krishnan to Bajaj Electricals, where he will lead our growth and new business agenda. His 
strong consumer-centric mindset, commercial acumen and experience in driving transformational 
growth will be valuable as we identify and scale new opportunities. We are confident that his leadership 
will contribute meaningfully to the company’s next phase of growth.”

About the company 
Bajaj Electricals Limited is a globally renowned Indian Consumer Appliances and Lighting Solutions 
company. Headquartered in Mumbai with a turnover of INR 4,462 crores (FY 25-26), it is a part of over 
$150 bn multinational conglomerate Bajaj Group and founded by Shri Jamnalal Bajaj.  
The Company was established in 1938 and has stood the test for more than 87 years. It continues to enjoy 
consumer trust across various sectors it operates in and remains at the forefront of developing modern, 
technologically superior, and aesthetic products and solutions. Bajaj Electricals’ business portfolio spans 
Consumer Products (Appliances and Fans) and Lighting Solutions (Consumer and Professional Lighting). 
They are a front runner in the industry, with their key brand offerings comprising BAJAJ and Morphy 
Richards. Under their Lighting Solutions business, they offer consumers a wide range of innovative LED 
lamps and fixtures, including panels and battens, as also wires and switchgear. They are known for their 
prowess in street lighting, sports lighting, industrial, commercial, and infrastructural lighting, and 
undertaking specialized illumination projects on a turnkey basis amongst other solutions.
