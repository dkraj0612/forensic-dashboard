Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) – Update on investment in Sicona Battery 
Technologies Pty Ltd  
 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This has reference to our earlier announcement dated 13 May 2025, wherein the Board of Directors 
(“Board”) of the Company approved further investment in Sicona Battery Technologies Pty Ltd 
(“Sicona”) through the subscription of Compulsorily Convertible Notes (“CCNs”) in agreed tranches. 
We would like to inform you that the Company has already invested AUD 1,67,53,000 in cash towards 
the subscription of 1,67,53,000 CCNs of face value AUD 1.00 each.  
This is to further inform you that an investment of amount AUD 16,94,000, pertaining to the last tranche 
has been remitted in the form of cash towards the subscription of 16,94,000 CCNs of face value AUD 
1.00 each.  
Consequently, the Company now cumulatively holds 1,84,47,000 CCNs in Sicona.  
Since the aforesaid investment is being made in the form of CCNs, the Company has not acquired any 
additional voting rights/ control in Sicona at present. The CCN will be convertible into shares of Sicona 
as per agreed terms. 
Further, the information as required under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI Listing 
Regulations read along with SEBI Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 
November 11, 2024 has already been provided to the Stock Exchange(s) vide our letter Ref. No HSCL 
/ Stock-Ex/2025-26/26, dated 13/05/2025 and there is no change in the said disclosure except as 
mentioned in this intimation. 
 
We request you to kindly take on record the same.
