Dear Sir/ Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations™), this is to inform you that the Company’s subsidiary Trancemarine 
and Confreight Logistics Private Limited (“Trancemarine”) has entered into an agreement for 
divestment of its stake in its subsidiary i.e. Sturdy Niketan Private Limited (“Sturdy”), by selling 34% 
of its stake in the form of equity shares of Sturdy. 
After this sale, Sturdy continues to be a subsidiary of Trancemarine and step down subsidiary of the 
Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd (“Company™) with 65% holding of Trancemarine in Sturdy. Neither 
Trancemarine nor Sturdy is a material subsidiary of the Company, hence, the transaction does not result 
in sale or disposal of shareholding or assets of material subsidiary. 
The transaction would not fall within related party transactions and the buyers are not related to the 
promoter/ promoter group/group companies of the Company. The transactions are expected to be 
completed within 10 (Ten) days from the date of signing of the Agreement or such date as may be 
mutually agreed between the parties. 
We request you to kindly take on record the same.
