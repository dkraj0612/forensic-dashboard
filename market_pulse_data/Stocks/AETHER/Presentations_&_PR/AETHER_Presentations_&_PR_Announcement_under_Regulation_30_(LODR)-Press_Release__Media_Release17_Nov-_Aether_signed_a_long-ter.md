PRESS RELEASE 
Aether Industries to Build India’s First Dedicated Drag Reducing Agents 
Manufacturing Facility 
 
Aether selected by Baker Petrolite LLC, a Baker Hughes company, as exclusive manufacturing 
supplier at Panoli, Gujarat for Drag Reducing Agents (DRAs) 
Surat, 17 November, 2026 – Aether Industries Limited (NSE: AETHER, BSE: 543534), one of 
India’s leading specialty chemical manufacturers and provider of contract research and 
manufacturing services (CRAMS), today announced that it has entered into a Facility Development 
and Supply Agreement with Baker Petrolite LLC, a Baker Hughes company. Under the long-term 
agreement, Aether will set up and operate a dedicated facility for the manufacture of Drag Reducing 
Agents (DRAs) for Baker Petrolite. Aether is Baker Petrolite’s exclusive manufacturing supplier for 
this program, and the facility will be the first of its kind in India. The facility will be located at Aether’s 
Site Magnum (Site 5) in Panoli, Gujarat. 
Drag Reducing Agents (DRAs) are performance chemicals that reduce friction in crude oil and 
refined product pipelines. They allow operators to move more volume through existing pipelines and 
to use less pumping energy. Demand is rising as pipeline operators seek greater capacity and 
efficiency from existing infrastructure. Manufacturing DRAs in India for the first time strengthens 
domestic capability in a high-value specialty chemical segment. 
The agreement builds on Aether’s existing relationship with Baker Petrolite. It brings together Baker 
Petrolite’s leadership in oilfield and pipeline chemicals with Aether’s strengths in complex chemistry, 
process scale-up and large-scale manufacturing. The long-term, dedicated nature of the 
collaboration gives Aether strong visibility on future revenues and opens a new end-market in energy 
infrastructure. 
Commenting on the agreement, Dr. Aman Desai, Co-Founder and Director / SVP, Research and 
Operations, Aether Industries, said: “We are proud to be chosen by Baker Hughes as its exclusive 
manufacturing supplier to bring this technology to India for the first time. This agreement reflects the 
trust in Aether’s chemistry, technology, execution and safety capabilities. We look forward to 
delivering the facility on schedule and supporting Baker Hughes and its customers worldwide for 
years to come.” 
Rohan Desai, Co-Founder and Director / SVP, Commercials, Aether Industries, added: “This 
long-term agreement is an important milestone in Aether’s strategy of building deep, multi-year 
collaborations with global leaders. It adds a new end-market for Aether in energy infrastructure and 
gives us strong visibility on future revenues. It is also a meaningful contribution to world-class 
specialty chemical manufacturing in India.” 
Luciana Pinho, President, Baker Petrolite, said: "Demand for pipeline capacity and efficiency 
keeps rising around the world, and our customers need partners who can grow with them. This 
collaboration with Aether adds dedicated manufacturing capacity in the Eastern Hemisphere and
