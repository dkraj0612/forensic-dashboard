Aurionpro secures another significant win from a global 
hyperscale data centre operator. 
Deepening its partnership with the global hyperscaler, Aurionpro wins an order to build AI-
ready digital infrastructure for its Navi Mumbai data centre, further strengthening its position 
in the fast-growing data centre market. 
Mumbai, August 13, 2026 - Aurionpro Solutions Limited (BSE: 532668; NSE: 
AURIONPRO), a leading end-to-end data centre solutions provider, is pleased to announce 
another significant order win from a global hyperscale data centre operator. Valued close to 
INR 100 Crores, the order involves Aurionpro building and project-managing the digital 
infrastructure for the customer’s upcoming AI-ready data centre in Navi Mumbai, 
Maharashtra, India.  
This latest win further expands Aurionpro’s existing partnership with the global hyperscaler 
and reflects the customer’s continued confidence in Aurionpro’s technology capabilities and 
project execution expertise. The order further strengthens Aurionpro’s position as a partner of 
choice in the rapidly expanding data centre market, particularly in the emerging AI-ready 
infrastructure segment. 
Aurionpro’s mission is to contribute actively to India’s digital and AI transformation and has 
established itself as a full stack centre solutions partner, combining deep expertise across 
Mechanical, Architectural, Structural, and Fit-out disciplines with advanced technology 
capabilities. This integrated approach enables Aurionpro to build and project manage 
innovative, scalable, and future-ready data centre infrastructure, addressing the evolving 
requirements of hyperscale operators and the rapidly emerging AI-driven data centre 
ecosystem. 
Bhaskar Bhattacharya, EVP & Head – Enterprise Business, Aurionpro Solutions, 
commenting on the win, said:  
      “We thank our customer for their continued trust and confidence in 
Aurionpro and congratulate our team on securing this important 
win. This order further strengthens our engagement with global 
hyperscalers and is an important milestone in our journey of 
establishing Aurionpro as a trusted partner for complex, AI-ready 
data centre builds and implementations. With a strong pipeline 
driven by demand for data centre capacity and AI-ready 
infrastructure, we are well positioned to accelerate growth, backed 
by our expanding capabilities, experienced team, and proven 
execution track record.”
