Aurionpro and Mastercard partner to bring contactless 
payments to public transportation in North America  
 
(L-R) Sanjay Bali, CEO, Aurionpro Transit, and Chapin Flynn, SVP, Mastercard 
Urban Mobility 
 
Mumbai, India –October 22, 2026: Aurionpro Transit, a global provider of smart 
ticketing and payment systems, is building on its partnership with Mastercard to 
support public transport agencies and government authorities across North 
America in upgrading transit payment systems and enhancing the passenger 
experience. 
The collaboration aims to make contactless transit payment solutions easier to 
evaluate, procure, and deploy. This includes supporting practical procurement 
approaches, repeatable implementation methods, and clear pathways that can 
reduce complexity while supporting local operational and policy requirements.  
Together, the companies aim to help transit agencies and authorities introduce 
convenient, secure and seamless payment experiences across public 
transportation, with deployment models that can scale to meet their needs.
