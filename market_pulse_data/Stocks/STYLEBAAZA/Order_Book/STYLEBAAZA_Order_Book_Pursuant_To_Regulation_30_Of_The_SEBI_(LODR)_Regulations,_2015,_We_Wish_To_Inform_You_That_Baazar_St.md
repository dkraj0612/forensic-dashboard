PRESS RELEASE 
Style Baazar Unveils the World’s Largest Fashion Wardrobe, Receives 
Acclaimed Guinness World Record Title 
• Hon’ble Chief Minister of West Bengal Shri Suvendu Adhikari graced the occasion 
• 50 ft. × 25 ft. spectacle celebrates Style Baazar’s largest festive collection 
Kolkata, September 2026: Fashion is an expression of who we are — and during the festive 
season, it becomes an even more vibrant celebration of colour, tradition, style and 
individuality. Building on this spirit, Style Baazar is bringing its festive fashion story to life in an 
extraordinary way with the World’s Largest Fashion Wardrobe, a spectacular installation 
measuring 50 feet × 25 feet at City Centre 1, Salt Lake, Bidhannagar. 
Adding another remarkable dimension to the celebration, Style Baazar was bestowed with a 
coveted Guinness World Record™ title for this larger-than-life wardrobe, and created a 
record around an object that is at the very heart of fashion and everyday life. 
The occasion was graced by the presence of Shri Suvendu Adhikari, Hon’ble Chief Minister 
of West Bengal, along with Dr. Swapan Dasgupta, Hon’ble Finance Minister, Government 
of West Bengal, and Shri Manoj Kumar Agarwal, Chief Secretary, Government of West 
Bengal. 
The concept takes inspiration from something familiar to everyone’s wardrobe. Style Baazar 
transforms this everyday symbol of fashion into a larger-than-life experience, creating a 
wardrobe on a scale that is as spectacular as the festive season itself. 
The installation showcases the spirit of Style Baazar’s largest festive collection, bringing 
together an expansive celebration of fashion for the season. From traditional-inspired looks to 
contemporary styles, the collection reflects the many ways people dress up, express 
themselves and celebrate during the festive period. 
Speaking about the initiative, Mr. Shreyans Surana, Chairman & Managing Director, Style 
Baazar said “We want to present fashion in a manner that is as joyful, inclusive and 
extraordinary as the festive season itself. Pujo is a time when Kolkata comes alive with colour, 
celebration and a renewed sense of togetherness, and fashion becomes an integral part of 
that spirit. A wardrobe is something that connects with everyone — it represents our choices, 
our celebrations and the way we express ourselves. With this initiative, we wanted to take 
that everyday symbol of fashion and transform it into an experience that brings people 
together and celebrates the spirit of Pujo. Our Guinness World Record™ title adds another 
dimension to this celebration, and we are delighted to bring Style Baazar’s largest festive 
collection to life on such a remarkable scale.” 
The 50 ft. × 25 ft. World’s Largest Fashion Wardrobe takes this celebration beyond the 
confines of a store, turning fashion into a public spectacle and an experience for everyone to 
witness and participate in.

At its core, the World’s Largest Fashion Wardrobe is a celebration of fashion at its most joyful 
and inclusive — where the scale of the wardrobe reflects the scale of the festive spirit, and 
Style Baazar’s largest festive collection becomes part of an experience designed to be

The installation will be open for visitors from 11th to 13th September 2026 at City Centre Salt 
Lake, Bidhannagar, from 12.00 PM to 8.00 PM inviting people to witness this extraordinary 
celebration of fashion and festive spirit. 
 
About Style Baazar:  
Style Baazar (Baazar Style Retail Limited) is one of Eastern India’s fastest-growing value 
fashion retail chains, committed to making quality, trend-led apparel accessible to every 
Indian family. The Company began its journey in 2013 with its first store in Berhampore, West 
Bengal. Today, it operates a strong and expanding network of 282 stores across 10 states, 
with an ambition to reach 500 stores. 
Style Baazar caters to men, women and children across diverse consumer segments, while also 
offering cosmetics, accessories and home utility products. 
Listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE), Style 
Baazar offers a comprehensive portfolio spanning men’s wear, women’s wear, kids’ wear, 
accessories and seasonal collections—delivering a compelling blend of style, comfort and 
affordability. 
Backed by data-driven merchandising and deep consumer insights, the Company focuses on 
regionally relevant fashion that aligns with evolving consumer trends. With a customer-first 
approach, a robust supply chain and value-driven pricing, Style Baazar continues to drive 
sustainable growth while strengthening its position as a trusted fashion destination across real 
Bharat.
