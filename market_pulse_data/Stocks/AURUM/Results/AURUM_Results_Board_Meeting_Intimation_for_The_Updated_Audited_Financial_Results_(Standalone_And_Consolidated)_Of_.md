With reference to our disclosures dated April 23, 2026 and June 12, 2026, and pursuant to

scheduled to be held on Friday, August 14, 2026, at the Registered Office of the 
Company.

Solutions Private Limited (the “Scheme of Merger”), both wholly owned subsidiaries of the

approved by the Board on April 23, 2026.
