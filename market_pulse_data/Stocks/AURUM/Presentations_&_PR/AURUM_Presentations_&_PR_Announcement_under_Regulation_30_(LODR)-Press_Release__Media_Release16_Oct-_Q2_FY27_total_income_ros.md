Management Commentary 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Onkar Shetye, Executive Director, Aurum PropTech Limited, said: 
“Q2 FY27 reflects the growing strength of Aurum PropTech’s operating model, with total income growing 
57% to ₹137 crore, Adjusted EBITDA margins expanding to 11.5%, and PBT margins reaching ~5%. Our 
fourth consecutive quarter of positive PBT reflects disciplined execution, improving monetisation and a 
structurally more efficient operating model. 
With Housing.com bringing consumer intent at scale, we are building India’s first AI-native real estate 
operating system, connecting Discovery, Intelligence, Transactions, Financing and Living. Our next phase of 
growth will be driven by deeper monetisation of consumer demand, AI-led operating efficiencies and the 
network effects of an integrated ecosystem. 
We see AI as a structural driver of revenue productivity, operating leverage and long-term margin 
expansion.”
