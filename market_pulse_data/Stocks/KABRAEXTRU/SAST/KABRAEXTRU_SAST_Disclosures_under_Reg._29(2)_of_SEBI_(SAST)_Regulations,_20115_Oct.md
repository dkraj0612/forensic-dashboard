Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
We, Garudlaxmi Ventures LLP (Acquirer), forming part of the Promoter group of Kabra Extrusiontechnik 
Limited (Target Company), along with the Persons Acting in Concert (PAC), hereby submit the disclosure 
under Regulation 29(2) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, in 
the prescribed format, in respect of the acquisition described below. 
 
The Acquirer has been allotted 18,93,334 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each of the Target Company, representing 
4.89% of the post-allotment paid-up equity share capital of 3,87,32,836 equity shares, by way of preferential 
allotment on October 02, 2026. Consequent to the said allotment, the aggregate shareholding of the Acquirer 
and the PAC in the Target Company has changed from 2,11,38,635 equity shares (60.44%) to 2,30,31,969 
equity shares (59.46%). 
 
You are requested to kindly take the above disclosure on record.
