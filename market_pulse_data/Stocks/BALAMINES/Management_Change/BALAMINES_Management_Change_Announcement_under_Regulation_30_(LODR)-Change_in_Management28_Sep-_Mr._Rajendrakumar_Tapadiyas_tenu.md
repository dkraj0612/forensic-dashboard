Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that Mr. 
Rajendrakumar Mohanprasad Tapadiya (DIN: 00103949), who served as an Independent Director in 
the Balaji Speciality Chemicals Limited, Material Subsidiary of the Company, from September 28, 2026 
has concluded his tenure as an Independent Director on the Board of the Balaji Speciality Chemicals 
Limited. His term officially ended from the close of business hours on September 28, 2026, following 
completion of his second term. The Board and the Management of the Balaji Speciality Chemicals 
Limited extend their sincere appreciation for the valuable contributions made by Mr. Rajendrakumar 
Mohanprasad Tapadiya during his term.
