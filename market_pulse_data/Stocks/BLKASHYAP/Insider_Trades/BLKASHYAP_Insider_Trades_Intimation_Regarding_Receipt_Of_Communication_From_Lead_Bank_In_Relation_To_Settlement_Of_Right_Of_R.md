Subject: Intimation regarding receipt of communication from Lead Bank in
relation to Settlement of Right of Recompense (ROR) 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Company has received a communication from
the Lead Bank of the Consortium Lenders in relation to the settlement of Right of
Recompense (ROR).
As per the aforesaid communication, the Lead Bank has communicated the Right of
Recompense (“ROR”) amount of ₹52.5 crore payable by the Company to the CDR lenders,
to be repaid before 31.03.2027, along with the other terms and conditions applicable for
Settlement.
The aforesaid ROR amount and the proposed terms and conditions are subject to
consideration, approval and/or confirmation by the other consortium lenders, as applicable.
The Company shall take the requisite steps for fulfillment of the applicable terms and
conditions upon receipt of the necessary approvals/confirmations from the consortium
lenders.
The Company will make further disclosures to the Stock Exchanges as and when there are
material developments in the matter.
