> **Executive Summary:**  Anupam Rasayan India Ltd. reported strong consolidated Q1 FY27 revenue (36% YoY) and EBITDA (35% YoY) growth, driven by new product commercialization and strategic acquisitions like Jayhawk Fine Chemicals, positioning the company for global expansion and diversification into high-value specialty chemicals.
