Consolidated Financial Highlights for Half-year ended September 30th, 2026: 
▪ 
Total Revenue for H1FY27 was Rs. 1,489 Cr as compared to Rs.1,230 Cr in H1FY26; up 21% YoY 
▪ 
EBITDA (incl. other income) came in at Rs.377 Cr in H1FY27 as against Rs.273 Cr in H1FY26; up 38% YoY, 
translating into EBITDA margin of 25% for the Half year. 
▪ 
Profit After Tax stood at Rs. 116 Cr in H1FY27 as compared to Rs. 106 Cr in H1FY26. 
 
Consolidated Financial Highlights for Quarter ended September 30th, 2026: 
▪ 
Total Revenue for Q2FY27 was Rs. 821 Cr as compared to Rs. 739 Cr in Q2FY26; up 11% YoY. 
▪ 
EBITDA (incl. other income) came in at Rs.202 Cr in Q2FY27 as against Rs.144 Cr in Q2FY26; up 41% YoY, 
translating into EBITDA margin of 25% for Q2FY27. 
▪ 
Profit After Tax stood at Rs.65 Cr in Q2FY27 as compared to Rs.57 Cr in Q2FY26. 
 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Anand Desai, Managing Director, Anupam Rasayan India Limited, 
said, “H1 FY27 was marked by steady progress across our strategic priorities, expansion into new end markets 
and continued strengthening of our business portfolio. We are pleased to have achieved our highest-ever 
quarterly consolidated revenue during Q2 FY27, reflecting the resilience of our diversified business and the 
progress made across key segments. 
 
Our focus continue to remain on building a globally integrated specialty chemicals platform by deepening 
relationships with existing customers, expanding into new applications and strengthening our capabilities across 
Pharmaceuticals, Performance Materials and other specialised segments. 
 
During the quarter, we signed a six-year long-term supply agreement with a US-based speciality metal 
manufacturer. This marks an important step towards establishing our presence in the geological resources 
sector, diversifying our customer base and building a long-term strategic partnership with a global customer. 
 
We also successfully completed the acquisition of a 50.07% stake in Bliss GVS Pharma, securing a controlling 
interest and marking a significant step in our vision of building a diversified pharmaceutical platform. The 
acquisition brings complementary capabilities in finished dosage formulations and provides opportunities to 
strengthen our presence across the pharmaceutical value chain, leverage operational and commercial synergies

About Anupam Rasayan India Limited  
 
Anupam Rasayan India Limited (Anupam) is one of the leading companies engaged in the custom synthesis 
(CSM) and manufacturing of specialty chemicals in India. Incorporated in 1984, the specialty chemicals major 
has two verticals: Life science related Specialty Chemicals comprising products related to Agrochemicals, 
Personal Care and Pharmaceuticals, Performance Materials comprising Electronics & EV Chemicals and 
Polymers. It is currently manufacturing products for over 200+ domestic and international customers. Anupam 
operates via its 14 manufacturing facilities, with 6 facilities located in Gujarat, 6 facilities in Maharastra, 1in Tamil 
Nadu and 1 in U.S. with an aggregate installed capacity of ~2,00,000+ MT for Speciality Chemicals and 4,800+ 
Mn Units for Pharmaceutical Formulations as of September 30, 2026. Anupam offers multistep synthesis and 
undertakes complex chemical reactions technologies, for a diverse base of Indian and global customers.
