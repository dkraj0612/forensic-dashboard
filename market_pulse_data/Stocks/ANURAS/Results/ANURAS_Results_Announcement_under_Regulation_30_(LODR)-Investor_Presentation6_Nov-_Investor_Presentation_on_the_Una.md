“H1 FY27 was marked by steady progress across our strategic priorities, expansion into 
new end markets and continued strengthening of our business portfolio. We are pleased 
to have achieved our highest-ever quarterly consolidated revenue during Q2 FY27, 
reflecting the resilience of our diversified business and the progress made across key 
segments.
Our focus continue to remain on building a globally integrated specialty chemicals 
platform by deepening relationships with existing customers, expanding into new 
applications and strengthening our capabilities across Pharmaceuticals, Performance 
Materials and other specialised segments.
During the quarter, we signed a six-year long-term supply agreement with a US-based 
speciality metal manufacturer. This marks an important step towards establishing our 
presence in the geological resources sector, diversifying our customer base and building a 
long-term strategic partnership with a global customer.
We also successfully completed the acquisition of a 50.07% stake in Bliss GVS Pharma, 
securing a controlling interest and marking a significant step in our vision of building a 
diversified pharmaceutical platform. The acquisition brings complementary capabilities in 
finished dosage formulations and provides opportunities to strengthen our presence 
across the pharmaceutical value chain, leverage operational and commercial synergies 
and expand our growth opportunities.
Going forward, we remain focused on disciplined execution, optimal utilization of our 
capital employed, successful integration of our strategic investments and continuous 
enhancement of our manufacturing and technological capabilities. We believe these 
initiatives will strengthen our business resilience, support sustainable growth and create 
long-term value for all stakeholders.”
