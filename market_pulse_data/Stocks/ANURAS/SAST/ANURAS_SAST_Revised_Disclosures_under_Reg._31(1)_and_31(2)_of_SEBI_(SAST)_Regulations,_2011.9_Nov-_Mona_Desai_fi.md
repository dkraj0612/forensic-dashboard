Dear Sir/Madam, 
1. This is in reference to the email received from BSE Limited dated November 02, 2026, 
noting that the disclosure submitted on October 05, 2026 ("Takeover Code Disclosure") 
by Mona Anandbhai Desai, Promoter of Anupam Rasayan India Limited ("Company"), 
identified only the trustee under the details of the entity in whose favour the shares were 
encumbered and did not identify the relevant lender(s). Further, BSE Limited requested 
that the disclosure be revised to identify both the trustee and the lender(s) for whose benefit 
the encumbrance has been created. 
2. As requested, and in supersession of the aforesaid Takeover Code Disclosure, please find 
enclosed the revised disclosure under Regulation 31 (1) and Regulation 31 (2) of the SEBI 
SAST Regulations, at Annexure I and Annexure II. The aforesaid debenture holders are 
identified as of the date on which the relevant encumbrance was created and their names 
have been extracted from the records of the relevant depository. 
3. Furthermore, the Takeover Code Disclosure has certain inadvertent errors, which are 
corrected in Annexure I and Annexure II, and the contents of Annexure I and Annexure II 
is aligned with the system driven disclosure generated by the depositories in respect of such 
encumbrances. Particularly, the name of the issuer has been corrected to Pure bliss Pharma 
Solutions Private Limited instead of Mates Visa Consultancy Private Limited.
