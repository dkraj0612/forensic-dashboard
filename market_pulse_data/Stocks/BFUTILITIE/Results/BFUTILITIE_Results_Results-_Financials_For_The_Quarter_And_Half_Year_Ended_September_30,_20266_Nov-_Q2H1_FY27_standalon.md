the Company and other Promoters and Sponsors that since these shares form part of a 
separate agreement, no claim in respect of the same can form subject matter of arbitration

18% p.a. on any monetary amount that may be awarded to the Claimants by the arbitral 
tribunal. Sponsors and Promoters including the Company have been legally advised that 
the claim filed by the Claimants lack merits on several counts and that they have strong 
defences and a good case on merits to contest the Claimants' claim. Company along with 
other Promoters and Sponsors (named in the SHA) have filed a statement of defence

production took place between 21 st January 2026 and 8th April 2026. The Claimants filed 
their reply to the SOD on 13th May 2026 and in response thereto, Company and other
