1. This has reference to the original shareholding pattern filings made by Camlin Fine 
Science Limited ("the company") under Regulation 31 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("LODR Regulations") for the 
quarters ended June 30, 2016 till March 31, 2023. ('relevant period') 
2. In this regard, it is noted that Ms. Radha Pandit, being the spouse of Mr. Ashish 
Dandekar, a promoter of the company, and Mr. Anand Pandit, being the father of Ms. 
Radha Pandit, fell within the promoter/promoter group category and were required to 
be disclosed accordingly in the shareholding pattern filings of the company for the 
relevant period. 
3. However, the company had incorrectly classified/disclosed Ms. Radha Pandit and Mr. 
Anand Pandit under the public shareholder category instead of the promoter/promoter 
group category in the shareholding pattern filings for the relevant period.
