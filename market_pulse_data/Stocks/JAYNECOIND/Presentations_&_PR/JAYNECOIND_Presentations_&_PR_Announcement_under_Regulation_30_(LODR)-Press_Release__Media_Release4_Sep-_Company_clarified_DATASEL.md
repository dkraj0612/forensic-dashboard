Clarification Regarding Media Reports Concerning DATASEL S.R.L., Italy

Jayaswal Neco Industries Limited (JNIL) is issuing this statement in response to recent media
coverage concerning the Neco Group Entity "DATASEL S.R.L".
DATASEL S.R.L. is a Subsidiary of Neco Defence Munitions Private Limited. There is no direct
relation of these Companies with Jayaswal Neco Industries Limited except that both these
entities are owned by the same promoters group. Further, Shri Anand Jayaswal, Non-
Executive Director of JNIL is the only common director in DATASEL S.R.L. Further JNIL does
not have any transactions with DATASEL S.R.L. and Neco Defence Munitions Private Limited.
It is important to clarify to all the stakeholders that any consequences arising therefrom will
not have any financial impact on JNIL, as the DATASEL S.R.L is not an associate/ joint
venture/ subsidiary company of JNIL and operates independently.
The Company is making this disclosure voluntarily, in the interest of transparency. The
Company requests its shareholders, investors and the public at large to exercise due caution
and not to rely. on or act upon unverified information, speculation or market rumours, and to
refer only to information disseminated by the Company through authorised and regulatory
channels.
