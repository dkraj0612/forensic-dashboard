M/s. B M Chatrath & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants: 
 
M/s. B M Chatrath & Co. LLP is a more than 100 years old Firm of Chartered Accountants 
founded in 1923 by late B. M. Chatrath in Lahore and later on shifted to Kolkata. In the 
year 1955, Shri M. M. Chatrath (Son of Late B. M. Chatrath) became a partner of the firm. 
He is currently the guiding force to the firm with his vast experience of over 60 years. 
 
Over a period of time the size of the Firm has increased significantly. With effect from 
05.04.2017 the Firm has been converted into Limited Liability Partnership (LLP). 
 
Shri Anand Chatrath (Grand Son of Late B.M.Chatrath) is now the Managing Partner of 
the Firm. Over the years the firm has received numerous Internal/Management Audit 
assignments of various Corporates and today it has more than 200 satisfied clients in its 
clientele. 
 
The firms has got its offices at Kolkata, Delhi, Noida, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Jaipur, 
Vijayawada and Bengaluru. The firm has Five Divisions (1) Audit, (2) Taxation (3) Back 
End Support (4) Financial Planning & Feasibility Study (5) Compliance of Labour Laws. 
 
Currently the firms has seventeen (17) Partners and the staff strength of the firm is more 
than 300 Audit Staffs which includes Qualified CA, CMA, CS, LL.B and Semi Qualified CA 
and CMA. 
 
The firm aspires to become a leader in the field of Accountancy, Audit, Taxation, Financial 
Services & Management Consultancy and Strategic Support Services and have 
repositioned as an Integrated Service Provider that renders complete solutions to the 
clients’ business challenges. 
 
M/s. SRB & Associates, Chartered Accountants: 
 
M/s. SRB & Associates was founded in 1978 and is a leading Chartered Accountancy firm 
in India, with its head office in Bhubaneswar and branches in key cities like New Delhi, 
Kolkata, Hyderabad, Patna, and Jajpur Road.  
 
With a workforce of over 300, the firm specializes in Assurance, Taxation, Corporate, and 
Transaction Advisory and business support Services. Its success is driven by strong client 
relationships, experienced staff, and a commitment to delivering innovative solutions 
across diverse industries. Guided by the values of trust, integrity, client focus, and social 
responsibility, SRB continues to prioritize client service and long-term partnerships.
