Bluspring Shifts Gear in Q2 FY27: EBITDA Up 130% and PAT Up 95% YoY, 
Outpacing 49% Revenue Growth 
 
Bengaluru, November 6, 2026: Bluspring Enterprises Limited [NSE: BLUSPRING, BSE: 544414], India’s 
leading integrated infrastructure services enterprise, today announced its financial results for the second 
quarter of FY 2026-27, ended September 30, 2026.

Commenting on the performance, Executive Director & CEO, Kamal Pal Hoda said,  
“Our growth trajectory is now translating into stronger earnings. In Q2 FY27, revenue grew 49% year on year and 
profit after tax grew 95% to ₹32 crore. EBITDA margin expanded by 188 basis points year on year from 3.5% to 5.4%. 
This performance reflects our deeper presence in Energy through our acquisition of STEAG India and our expertise in 
Engineering services, which is supported by India’s growing power demand and its focus on a robust domestic 
industrial base. The addition of LSG Sky Chefs India extends our Food & Hospitality business into high-margin aviation 
catering. Our priority now is to integrate our recent acquisitions fully into the Bluspring enterprise, which will help us 
unlock the benefit of our expanded scale, increase cross selling and deliver better returns to our shareholders.”

Bluspring Enterprises Limited (NSE: BLUSPRING, BSE: 544414) is India's only nationally scaled, people-
powered, compliance-first, and tech-enabled integrated infrastructure services enterprise. It delivers services 
across Facilities, Food and Hospitality, Security (Powered by Terrier Security Services), Engineering 
(Powered by Hofincons), Smart Infrastructure (Powered by Vedang Cellular Services) and Energy (Powered 
by STEAG Energy Services India). With a workforce of over 100,000 operating across 28 states, Bluspring 
supports more than 1,000 customers across healthcare, education, BFSI, commercial, IT, manufacturing and 
industrial, government, and PSU sectors.
