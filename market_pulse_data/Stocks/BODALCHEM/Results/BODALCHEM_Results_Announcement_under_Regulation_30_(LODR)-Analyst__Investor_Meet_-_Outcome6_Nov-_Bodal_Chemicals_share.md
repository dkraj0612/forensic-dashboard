inflation and significant central bank interest rate increases, geopolitical disruptions arising from 
the Russia-Ukraine conflict, disruptions to shipping routes in the Red Sea, tariffs imposed by 
the U.S. 
 
Apart from these external factors, stabilization of our benzene downstream plant took longer 
than expected because of the integration of several foreign technologies, initial operational 
issues and delays in approvals from pharmaceutical customers.
