### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> CMR Green Technologies Limited has received significant credit rating upgrades from CRISIL, with its long-term rating improved to 'Crisil AA-/Stable' (from 'A+/Stable') and its short-term rating to 'Crisil A1+' (from 'A1'). This positive re-assessment, alongside an increase in total bank loan facilities to Rs. 953 Crore (from Rs. 760.74 Crore), materially enhances the company's financial standing, indicating improved creditworthiness and potentially better access to capital at favorable terms. No red flags are identified in this rating intimation; rather, it reflects a strengthened financial profile.
