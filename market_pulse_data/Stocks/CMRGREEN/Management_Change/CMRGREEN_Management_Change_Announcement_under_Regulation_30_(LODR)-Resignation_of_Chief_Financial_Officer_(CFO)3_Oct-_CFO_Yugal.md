Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform that Mr. Yugal Kishor Garg, Chief Financial Officer 
(CFO) and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company, has tendered his resignation from the position of 
Chief Financial Officer of the Company vide resignation letter dated 03rd October 2026. Mr. Yugal Kishor 
Garg will cease to hold his designation of CFO and KMP w.e.f. 23rd October, 2026  
 
The resignation has been tendered due to his personal commitments and priorities. 
 
The requisite details pertaining to the resignation are enclosed as Annexure A. 
 
Further, we wish to inform you that the Company proposes to designate Mr. Satish Kumar Kaushik, who 
is appointed as President Finance of the Company w.e.f. 28th September 2026 as the Chief Financial 
Officer and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company as per Section 203 of the Companies Act, 2013, 
subject to the approval of the Board of Directors at its ensuing meeting scheduled to be held in November 
2026. 
 
Upon approval by the Board, the Company shall make the requisite disclosure to the Stock Exchanges 
in accordance with the applicable provisions of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations. 
 
The above information is also being made available on the website of the Company at www.cmr.co.in . 
 
The same is for your information and record.
