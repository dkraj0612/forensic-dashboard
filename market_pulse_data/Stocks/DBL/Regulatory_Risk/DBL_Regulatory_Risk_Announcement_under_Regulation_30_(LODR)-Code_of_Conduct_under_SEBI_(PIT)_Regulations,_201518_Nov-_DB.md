Details of violations observed under

Execution of Trades in shares of the Company by the DP during the 
closure of trading window.  
 
Mr. Amit Surendra Sharma, has taken position in Futures and Options 
(F&O) segment and kept the shares of the Company in margin as security 
against the said F&O Position and due to an inadvertent delay in 
replenishing the requisite margin, the system has automatically liquidated 
the 34 shares of the Company on September 11, 2026.  
5. 
Action taken by Listed company/ 
Intermediary/ Fiduciary 
As per the provisions of the Code of Conduct of the Company and SEBI 
(Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, the Company has instructed 
Mr. Amit Surendra Sharma to remit 200% of the amount of the profit 
made on sale of shares i.e Rs. 1636.00/- as penalty to SEBI for credit to the 
Investor Protection and Education Fund. The Company had also issued a 
warning letter and cautioned him not to repeat any violation in future 
under the Code. 
 
DP has remitted an amount of Rs. 1636.00, to the Investor Protection and 
Education Fund (IPEF) administered by SEBI.

compliance with the applicable provisions in future.
