BRIEF PROFILE OF INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS 
Shri Pankaj Joshi 
Shri Pankaj Joshi, is a Graduate in Commerce (B.com) from Rajasthan University, Jaipur. He is a 
member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) since 2007 and has 20 years of 
Professional experience in internal as well as statutory audit of various private sector companies and 
public sector banks/financial institutions. 
Shri Ankush Munjal 
Shri Ankush Munjal is a qualified Chartered Accountant, Advocate and Finance and legal professional 
with over 25 years of professional and industry experience across Corporate Finance, Banking, 
Treasury, Regulatory Compliance, Taxation, Insolvency, Audit, Risk Management and Legal Advisory. 
Smt. Vandana Agarwal 
Smt Vandana Agarwal is a practicing chartered Accountant with over 22 years of professional 
experience. Her core areas of expertise includes Statutory Audits, Tax Audits, Internal Audits, Stock 
Audits, TDS, Direct and Indirect Taxation (GST), Accounting, MCA & Corporate Compliance, 
Consulting, and Appeals.
