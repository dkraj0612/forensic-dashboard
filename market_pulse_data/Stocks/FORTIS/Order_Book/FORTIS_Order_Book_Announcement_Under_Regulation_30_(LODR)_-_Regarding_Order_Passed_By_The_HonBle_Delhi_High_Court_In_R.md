Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
In furtherance of our earlier disclosure(s) dated September 23, 2022, we would like to inform you that a single judge bench of the 
Hon’ble Delhi High Court has passed an order dated August 31, 2026, in relation to certain applications filed by Daiichi Sankyo 
Company, Limited against inter alia the Company (the “Order”).  
 
By way of the Order, the Hon'ble Court has directed the appointment of a forensic auditor with a mandate to primarily undertake a 
factual enquiry into the dissipation of the shareholding of the Singh Brothers (i.e., the Company's erstwhile promoters) in the 
Company, the subsequent change in control of the Company, and the role of the relevant stakeholders (including inter alia various 
banks / financial institutions, erstwhile promoters and their controlled entities, and the Company) in regard to the same. The Hon’ble 
Court has not imposed any liability, penalty or fine on the Company. 
 
It is worthwhile to mention that the Company was neither a party to the original dispute nor a judgment debtor in the execution 
proceedings. 
 
The Company is reviewing the judgment in detail in consultation with its legal counsels and will evaluate the appropriate course 
of action in accordance with applicable law. The Company remains committed to the highest standards of corporate governance, 
transparency and regulatory compliance and continues to remain focused on its operations and the interest of all its stakeholders. 
 
The details of the Order, as required to be disclosed under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master 
Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are attached as Annexure-I. 
 
The date and time of occurrence of event is August 31, 2026  at 04:31 PM (IST).
