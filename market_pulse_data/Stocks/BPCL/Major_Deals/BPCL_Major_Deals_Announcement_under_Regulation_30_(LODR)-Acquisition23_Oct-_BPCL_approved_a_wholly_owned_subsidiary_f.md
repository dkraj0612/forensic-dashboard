Dear Sir/Madam, 
  Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 - Incorporation of a subsidiary 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) and the applicable SEBI Master 
Circular, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of Bharat Petroleum Corporation 
Limited, at its meeting held today, i.e., 23 October 2026, has approved the incorporation of a Wholly 
Owned Subsidiary (WOS) in India, as a dedicated project company for the proposed Greenfield 
Refinery & Petrochemical Complex and Associated Facilities at Ramayapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, 
subject to requisite Government, statutory and regulatory approvals. 
 
Establishing the subsidiary will provide a dedicated legal and governance structure to carry out 
project activities with an initial authorised share capital of upto Rs.4900 crores. The incorporation 
of this entity will support due diligence activities, engagement with lenders and potential partners 
and development of an optimal financing and partnership structure. The detailed feasibility reports 
are under finalisation and accordingly the financial investment decision and financial commitments 
will be considered separately by the competent authorities at the appropriate stage. 
 
Further details of the WOS as per the format will be intimated at later date.  
 
We request you to take the same on record. 
  
 Thanking You,  
 
 Yours faithfully,  
 For Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited
