### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) has received two orders from Gujarat stamp authorities, demanding approximately Rs. 46.13 lakhs in unpaid stamp duty and penalties related to a past amalgamation and older land transactions. The authorities rejected BPCL's objections, indicating non-compliance, and the company plans to appeal these orders. Despite the penalties exceeding the duty in some cases, BPCL asserts there will be no material financial impact on the company.
