In terms of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform that Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, Govt. of India vide letter dated 
17th November 2026 has communicated the nomination of Dr. M. Beena, IAS, Principal Secretary, 
Industries Department, Government of Keralam (DIN 03483417) on the Board of Directors of the 
Company for a period of three years on co-terminus basis or until further orders, whichever is 
earlier.  Accordingly, Dr. M. Beena has been appointed as a Government Nominee Director and 
Additional Director on the Board w.e.f. 17th November 2026.

any other authority. Further, she is not related to any of the Directors and Key Managerial Personnel 
in the Company.

Brief profile 
Dr. M. Beena, IAS is a 1999-batch Indian 
Administrative Service officer from the Kerala 
cadre and a medical doctor by qualification, 
currently serving as Principal Secretary of the

Prior to her current assignment, she served on 
central deputation in New Delhi as the 
Development Commissioner for Handlooms 
under the Ministry of Textiles (in both Joint 
Secretary and Additional Secretary equivalent 
ranks) and as Chairperson of the Cochin Port 
Trust. Over a distinguished career spanning 
more than two and half decades across 
infrastructure, 
industrial 
development, 
healthcare, and public sector management, she 
has held numerous key positions in the state, 
including Managing Director of KSIDC, Roads 
& Bridges Development Corporation, and
