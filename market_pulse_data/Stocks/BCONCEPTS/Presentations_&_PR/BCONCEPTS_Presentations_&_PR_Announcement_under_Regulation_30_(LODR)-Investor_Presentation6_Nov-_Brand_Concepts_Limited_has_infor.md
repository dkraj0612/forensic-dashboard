## Page 9

Revenue / Sales / Topline
•
Revenue was broadly flat at INR 96.21 Cr. vs INR 97.62 Cr. as 
the later onset of the festive season moved some part of the 
demand into Q3. The course correction undertaken in existing 
brands is aimed at long-term brand positioning and 
profitability, and is going as per plan.
Financial Highlights — Q2 FY27
•
Gross margin remained stable at 53.93% in Q2 FY27, against 54.25% 
in Q2 FY26. Channel-level margins were steady, and the slight dip of 
0.32 percentage points is due to a change in sales mix.
•
EBITDA margin remained resilient at 10.91% vs 11.61%, with EBITDA 
at INR 10.49 Cr. vs INR 11.33 Cr. The marginal dip was mainly due to 
a slight reduction in sales. New brand sales come with upfront 
investment in inventory, stores and marketing. Despite this, and a 
course correction in existing brand sales, EBITDA was broadly 
maintained, reflecting the flexibility of the business model in a 
disruptive environment.
•
PBT stood at INR 1.35 Cr. vs INR 2.64 Cr. with PBT margin at 1.41% vs 
2.71%, mainly due to a slight reduction in sales and higher finance 
cost on account of inventory funding and new stores INR 5.10 Cr. vs 
INR 4.07 Cr. Finance cost for inventory of new brands also includes 
the impact of new stores on account of lease accounting as per Ind 
AS. This was partly offset by lower depreciation of INR 4.04 Cr. vs INR 
4.63 Cr. Despite a course correction in existing brands and continued 
investment in new brands, the business remained profitable at the 
PBT level.
Financial Highlights — Balance Sheet & Cash Flow
•
Current ratio improved to 1.25x in Sep-26 from 1.14x in Mar-
26, reflecting a better liquidity position.
•
Cash flow from operations turned positive at INR 1.12 Cr. in 
H1 FY27, the first time since Sep-24. While modest, it marks a 
clear turning point as the business transitions from the 
investment phase to the returns phase.
•
Total debt rose by only INR 6.92 Cr. in Sep-26 to INR 142.36 
Cr. from INR 135.44 Cr. in Mar-26, even though INR 16.6 Cr. 
of ECLGS came in. Short-term debt reduced by INR 8.69 Cr. 
to INR 106.57 Cr. from INR 115.26 Cr., reflecting a conscious 
pause on fresh borrowing. Excluding ECLGS, total debt 
reduced by INR 9.68 Cr.
Focus Areas for Upcoming Quarters
•
Monetizing New Brands: Focus is on monetizing the upfront 
marketing investment by scaling sales across new brands.
•
Positive Cash Flow from Operating Activities: With the 
investment phase largely complete, the focus now shifts to 
delivering consistent positive cash flow from operations.
Other Key Updates
•
Off-White: Opened 2 new Off-White exclusive stores this 
quarter, at DLF Promenade, Delhi and Quest Mall, Kolkata. 
The initial response to the brand is very positive and indicates 
a good outlook for the future.
•
Hard Luggage Plant: Started production of PP luggage from 
Sep-26, with a healthy production pipeline for Q3. Q2 also 
saw strong product development, including front-opening 
luggage and UV printing capabilities.
•
CSD: Acquired the rights for UCB Luggage from Benetton 
India in Q1 this year and immediately started the paperwork 
for the index transfer. We are happy to announce that we have 
received CSD approval to move forward, and sales are 
expected to begin by the end of Q4 this year.
•
PMS (Product Management Services): Started this division 
to leverage our product development and manufacturing 
capabilities. A couple of high-profile customers have been 
onboarded, and PMS is seen as a growth driver in the future.

OUR APPROACH
20
INVESTOR  PRESENTATION
Brand Concepts is built on the thinking of our Founders. They believe that brand and lifestyle licensing is 
mutually beneficial to both the licensor and the licensee. While the brand owner/licensor benefits from generating a 
new revenue stream, increasing their brand awareness, and expanding into new product categories, geographies as 
well as retail channels; the licensee generates a new revenue stream at the same time as having an association with 
the brand name, and differentiating its offerings from competitors.
Since Brand Concepts works with highly reputed global and 
domestic brands, it follows the highest standards of quality 
checks, inspite of outsourcing the manufacturing of its 
products. With a dedicated team of in-house professionals 
that include merchandisers, account specialists and retail 
planners, Brand Concept services its key retail relationships 
with unmatched execution and program management
The team is backed by a strong sourcing network, in-house trend-
spotting and design teams, coupled with robust logistics and 
warehousing network. 
We sell our products through exclusive THTG stores, EBOs as well 
as  several large multi brand outlets (MBOs).
We have also appointed master distributors (on cash and carry) to 
service our distributor and retail business.
