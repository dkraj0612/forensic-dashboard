Sub: Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited - Updates for Q2 FY2027 
 
You are requested to take note of the following updates for Q2 FY27: 
  
In Q2 FY27, we estimate the overall disbursement at approximately Rs. 16,490 crore, YoY growth of ~22% over 
previous year and for H1 FY27 disbursement at approximately Rs. 32,050 crore, a growth of ~22% YoY 
(excluding finance lease). 
  
Business Assets at approximately Rs. 1,46,300 crore grew by ~15% over September 2025. 
  
The Collection Efficiency (“CE”) is estimated at 97% for Q2 FY27 (In Q2 FY26, CE was 96%). 
  
As at September 30, 2026, Stage-3 is estimated in the range of 3.35% to 3.45% (vs. 3.45% as at June 30, 2026, 
and 3.94% as at September 30, 2025) and Stage-2 is estimated in the range of 4.7 % to 4.8% (vs. 4.9% as at 
June 30, 2026, and 5.8% as at September 30, 2025). 
  
The Company continued to enjoy a comfortable liquidity position on its Balance Sheet, with a liquidity chest of 
over Rs. 16,200 crore.
