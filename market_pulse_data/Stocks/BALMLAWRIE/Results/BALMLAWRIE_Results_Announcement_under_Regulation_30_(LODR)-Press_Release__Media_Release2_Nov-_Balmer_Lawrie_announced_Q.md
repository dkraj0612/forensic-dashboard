Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd. announces second quarter results  
 
 
November 02, 2026, Kolkata - Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd., a Miniratna I PSE with diversified 
business portfolios, has announced the second quarter results ending 30 September 2026 
as per the accounts adopted. The results were approved by the Board of Directors in its 
Meeting held at Hampi, Karnataka on 02 November 2026. 
 
The net total income for the second quarter increased by 26.34% and stood at Rs. 818.04 
crores as compared to Rs. 647.48 crores in the same period last year. The Profit Before Tax 
(PBT) saw a jump of 42.95% and rose to Rs. 93.63 crores for the quarter ending 30 
September 2026 as compared to Rs. 65.50 crores for the same quarter last year. 
Correspondingly, the net profit (PAT) during the quarter increased by 39.56% to Rs. 67.66 
crores compared to Rs. 48.48 crores for the corresponding period last year. 
 
The total income, Profit Before Tax (PBT) and Net Profit After Tax (PAT) for the six months 
period ended 30 September 2026 stood at Rs. 1570.58 crores, Rs. 172.75 crores and Rs. 
125.32 crores respectively whereas the numbers for the corresponding period of the 
previous year were Rs. 1332.76 crores, Rs. 140.88 crores and Rs. 104.41 crores 
respectively.
