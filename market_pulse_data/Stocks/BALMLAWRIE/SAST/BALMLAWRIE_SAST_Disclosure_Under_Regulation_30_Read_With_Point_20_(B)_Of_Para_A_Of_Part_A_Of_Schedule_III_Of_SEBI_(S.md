### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd. has been fined ₹14,19,540 by the National Stock Exchange for material non-compliance with board composition regulations during the quarter ended June 30, 2026, specifically lacking independent directors, woman directors, and sufficient non-executive directors. This represents a significant governance red flag, as the company's board structure deviates from SEBI Listing Regulations. While the direct financial impact is limited to the fine, the company attributes these issues to its Central Public Sector Enterprise status and reliance on government appointments, and is currently seeking a waiver.
