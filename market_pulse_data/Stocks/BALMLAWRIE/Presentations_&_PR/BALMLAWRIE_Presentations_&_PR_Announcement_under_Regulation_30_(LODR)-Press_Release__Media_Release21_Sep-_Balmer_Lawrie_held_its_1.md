Kolkata, September 21, 2026 
The 109th Annual General Meeting of Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd., a Public Sector Enterprise under the 
Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India was held on 21st September 2026. 
Below is an extract of the Chairman’s Speech: 
As Balmer Lawrie celebrates a landmark milestone — the 160th year since inception; three major 
qualities shape the defining features — a 160-years legacy of resilience, a deepening alignment with 
the nation’s priorities and a decisive embrace of technology as the engine of our next chapter. 
 
Balmer Lawrie continues to operate in an era of unprecedented volatility, where uncertainty has become 
the new normal — this year was shaped by the escalation of the Gulf Crisis, the rise of global Tariff 
Protectionism and the resulting disruption to established supply chains. Geopolitics and national security 
are now overtaking traditional economic efficiency as the primary drivers of global trade. Rather than 
merely weathering global storms, the Company has pivoted towards aligning its operations towards 
catering to robust domestic demand — expanding the supply chain touchpoints, including increased 
focus into domestic logistics operations through Rail Logistics and 3rd Party Logistics. Domestic tourism, 
meanwhile, remains the backbone of the travel sector, evidenced by a nearly 52.7% surge in visits 
between January and September 2025; Balmer Lawrie has aligned its tour packages, retail vacations 
and MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions) capabilities accordingly. By catering 
directly to the nation’s internal economic engines, the Company remain well-poised to improve operating 
efficiency and continue its legacy of resilience across business cycles. 
 
Overall Financial Performance 
Despite navigating a highly complex operating environment shaped by global headwinds, Balmer Lawrie 
delivered a robust financial performance in FY 2025-26. Net Turnover reached Rs.2,78,459.58 Lakhs, a 
healthy growth of 8.03% over the Rs.2,57,762.84 Lakhs recorded in the previous fiscal year. This topline 
momentum translated into strong profitability, with Profit Before Tax (PBT) strengthening to 
Rs.33,086.61 Lakhs, up from Rs.31,378.99 Lakhs in FY 2024-25 — driven largely by the exceptional 
performance of the Travel & Vacations and Logistics businesses, which capitalised on optimised 
operational efficiencies.  
 
Reserves and Surplus increased to Rs.1,38,448.31 Lakhs as of 31st March 2026, compared to 
Rs.1,35,694.55 Lakhs at the close of the prior year. 
 
Performance of Strategic Business Units (SBUs) 
Balmer Lawrie is a diversified PSU with a presence in both manufacturing and service sectors. 
Industrial Packaging (SBU: IP) – Balmer Lawrie sustained its formidable market leadership through 
continuous technological upgradation and a pan-India manufacturing footprint of six plants, achieving 
growth in both production volume and turnover in FY 2025-26.
