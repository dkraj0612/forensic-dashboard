PRESS RELEASE 
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited receives Letter of Intent 
from Aurionpro Solutions for cable supply to Hyderabad data 
centre campus 
Vadodara, 22 August 2026 — Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (NSE: DIACABS | BSE: 522163), one of India's 
integrated manufacturers of power cables and conductors operating under the DICABS brand, has received a Letter 
of Intent (LOI) from Aurionpro Solutions Limited (NSE: AURIONPRO | BSE: 532668) for the supply of HT and LT 
electrical cables to a hyperscale data centre campus at Hyderabad. 
The LOI, valued at approximately ₹52.86 crore (Including GST), covers the HYD-20 package of the campus, for which 
Aurionpro is the appointed EPC contractor. Supplies commence immediately and are scheduled on a staggered 
basis in line with the site's construction programme, delivered DAP Hyderabad. The contract is on a price-variation 
basis under the IEEMA formula with April 2026 base indices 
A copper-led order 
The package is overwhelmingly copper-intensive. It comprises approximately 130 kilometres of cable, of which 
over 116 kilometres is 1100 V grade, XLPE-insulated, FRLS-sheathed 1C x 300 sq mm copper conductor cable — 
the backbone distribution cable for high-density server halls. The balance covers 33 kV (E) grade aluminium XLPE 
armoured FRLS cable, 1.1 kV aluminium armoured LT power cables and copper control cables, all built to the client's 
IEC 61914 cleat and fire-performance specifications. 
The order draws directly on the copper wire drawing and copper cable manufacturing line recently commissioned 
by DICABS, which has a capacity of 1,500 MT of copper cables per month. The line was set up specifically to address 
high-intensity power applications such as data centres and power plants and marks the Company's entry into 
copper-based cables alongside its established aluminium cables and conductors’ business. 
Management comment 
Mr. Umesh Chayya, Whole time Director, Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, said: 
"Data centres are the single fastest-growing load centre in India, and they demand cable of a quality and 
consistency that very few plants in the country can deliver at scale. This order validates two things — the decision 
to invest in copper capability, and our ability to hold delivery schedules against a construction programme that 
moves week to week. We are supplying into this campus through more than one EPC partner, and we intend to 
be the reference cable supplier for India's data centre build-out,our Order book for Data Center at Present value 
exceeds Rs 575 cr – we expect to target this segment aggressively"
