Annexure A 
PRESS RELEASE 
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DICABS) secures ₹116.49 crore of 11 kV power 
cable orders for Gujarat’s underground distribution network 
Vadodara, 21 September 2026: Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (“DPIL” or “the Company”; BSE: 522163, NSE: 
DIACABS), one of India’s established manufacturers of power cables and conductors under the DICABS brand, has 
secured orders aggregating ₹116.49 crore (including GST; ₹98.72 crore excluding GST) for the supply of 11 kV XLPE-
insulated medium voltage power cables. The orders have been received directly from a Gujarat-based EPC contractor 
executing underground cabling projects for state power distribution companies in Gujarat. 
The orders cover approximately 655 km of 11 kV three-core aluminium XLPE cables in sizes of 300, 240, 95 and 70 sq 
mm, to be supplied for urban underground distribution projects in the Bhavnagar and Ahmedabad regions. The cables 
will be manufactured at the Company’s integrated plant near Vadodara, which draws aluminium from DICABS’ own rod 
mills, and will be supplied in lots against manufacturing clearances, subject to utility-specified inspection and testing. 
The orders are on a variable-price basis; the value stated is the base price set out in the orders, with final realisation 
linked to the applicable price-variation formula. Orders aggregating ₹79.83 crore (including GST) are subject to receipt 
of the Letter of Award by the EPC contractor from the end utility. 
Commenting on the orders, Mr. Kavish Shah, Vice President, Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, said: 
“Gujarat’s distribution utilities are taking large parts of their urban networks underground to improve reliability and 
resilience, and medium voltage cables are at the heart of that programme. Winning four orders in a single day for projects 
across Bhavnagar and Ahmedabad reflects the trust EPC contractors and utilities place in DICABS’ quality, testing 
standards and delivery. With our resolution plan fully implemented and the Company out of the NCLT framework, we are 
scaling up our MV and EHV cable business and building a strong, diversified order book, and we look forward to executing 
these orders on time.” 
About Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DICABS) 
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, a company with a five-decade legacy, manufactures EHV, HV, MV and LV power 
cables and conductors under the DICABS brand. Its manufacturing facilities near Vadodara, Gujarat have an installed 
capacity of 250,000 MT per annum across 125 operational machines, supported by backward integration into four in-
house aluminium rod mills and captive wind power. The Company serves power utilities, transmission and distribution 
EPC contractors, renewable energy developers and industrial customers across India. In September 2026 the Company 
prepaid its resolution-plan obligations in full, a year ahead of schedule, and has exited the NCLT mechanism.
