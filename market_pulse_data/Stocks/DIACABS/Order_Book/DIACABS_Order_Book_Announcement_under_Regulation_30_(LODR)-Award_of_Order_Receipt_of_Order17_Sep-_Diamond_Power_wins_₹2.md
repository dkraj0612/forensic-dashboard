Annexure A 
PRESS RELEASE 
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DICABS) bags ₹263.25 crore order 
for cables and conductors for MSEDCL urban power infrastructure in 
Maharashtra 
Vadodara, 17 September 2026: Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (“DPIL” or “the Company”), 
manufacturer of power cables and conductors under the DICABS brand, has received a purchase order 
worth approximately ₹263.25 crore (inclusive of GST) for the supply of DICABS make power cables and 
conductors for an urban power infrastructure project of the Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution 
Company Limited (MSEDCL) in the Bhandup region of Maharashtra. The order has been placed on the 
Company by a domestic EPC contractor executing the project. 
Order highlights 
• Order value: ~₹263.25 crore, inclusive of GST 
• End client: Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL) 
• Segment: Urban power distribution infrastructure – underground cabling and overhead line 
strengthening 
• Scope: MV & LV XLPE underground power cables, overhead conductors and control cables, 
manufactured to MSEDCL specifications 
• Manufacturing: at the Company’s integrated facility at Vadadala, Vadodara, Gujarat 
• Guarantee: 66 months from supply or 60 months from commissioning, whichever is earlier 
 
Medium-voltage XLPE underground cables form the core of the order. Underground cabling is central to 
urban distribution upgrades as utilities move to more reliable, weather-resilient and space-efficient 
networks in dense city areas. The order reinforces DICABS’ position as a preferred supplier to state 
distribution utilities and adds to the Company’s growing MV cable order book. 
The order will be priced on a variable basis linked to prevailing aluminium and copper prices and currency, 
in line with the Company’s practice of protecting margins against raw material volatility. Supplies will be 
made against a delivery schedule to be finalised following approval of technical particulars and drawings 
by MSEDCL. 
Commenting on the order, the management of DPIL said: “This order from a major state utility’s urban 
infrastructure programme is a strong endorsement of DICABS’ capability across medium-voltage cables, 
low-voltage cables and conductors under a single roof. Urban underground cabling is one of the fastest-
growing segments of India’s distribution sector, and with our backward-integrated aluminium rod mills and 
expanding MV capacity, we are well placed to execute large, multi-product utility orders with quality and 
speed.” 
About Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited 
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, with its DICABS brand and a five-decade legacy, is a manufacturer of power 
cables (LV, MV, HV and EHV) and overhead conductors, with an installed capacity of 250,000 MT per annum at 
Vadodara, Gujarat. The Company is backward integrated with its own aluminium rod mills and captive wind power,
