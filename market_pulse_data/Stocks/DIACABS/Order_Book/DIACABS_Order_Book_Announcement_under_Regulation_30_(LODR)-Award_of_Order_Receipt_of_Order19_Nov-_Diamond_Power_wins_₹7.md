Annexure A 
PRESS RELEASE 
Diamond Power enters HVDC transmission with Rs. 708 crore conductor order for the 
±800 kV Bhadla–Fatehpur HVDC line 
Order from Adani Energy Solutions for over 9,000 km of AL-59 conductors for one of India’s largest green 
energy corridors 
• Purchase order value Rs. 707.80 crore including GST (Rs. 599.83 crore excluding GST) 
• Value is at base indices of LME aluminium USD 2,550 per MT and USD/INR 87; commodity on back-
to-back basis under the price variation formula 
• 9,059 km of AL-59 Lapwing and AL-59 Moose conductors for an ±800 kV HVDC transmission line 
• Marks Diamond Power’s entry into the HVDC transmission business 
Vadodara / Ahmedabad, [●] November 2026: Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (“Diamond Power” 
/ “DICABS”) (BSE: 522163 | NSE: DIACABS), India’s fastest growing cables and conductors company, today 
announced that it has received a purchase order from Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) for supply 
of AL-59 conductors for the ±800 kV HVDC Bhadla–Fatehpur transmission project. 
The order covers 8,948 km of AL-59 Lapwing conductor and 111 km of AL-59 Moose conductor, 
aggregating 9,059 km, on delivered-at-site basis. The purchase order value is Rs. 707.80 crore including 
GST (basic value Rs. 599.83 crore excluding GST). Deliveries will be made month-wise in line with the 
project schedule and are to be completed by 31 December 2027. 
Base indices and commodity back-to-back pricing 
The Purchase Order value is based on LME aluminium of USD 2,550 per MT, premium of USD 106 per MT 
and an exchange rate of USD/INR 87. The order is on commodity back-to-back basis: the final price of 
each month’s supplies will be determined under the price variation formula on the average LME 
aluminium price and USD/INR rate of the month preceding scheduled dispatch, so that movement in 
aluminium price and exchange rate is passed through. The Company therefore does not carry aluminium 
price or currency risk on this order beyond the pricing period. 
Entry into HVDC 
This is Diamond Power’s first major order for an ±800 kV HVDC transmission line and marks the 
Company’s entry into the HVDC business. HVDC is the technology used to move very large blocks of power 
over long distances with low losses, and ±800 kV is the highest HVDC voltage level in operation in India.

Conductors for such lines are large-diameter, high-capacity products made to demanding quality and

About the project 
The Bhadla–Fatehpur HVDC link is being developed by Adani Energy Solutions as a ±800 kV, 6,000 MW 
corridor of about 950 km connecting Bhadla in Rajasthan with Fatehpur in Uttar Pradesh, to evacuate 
renewable energy from Rajasthan to demand centres in northern India. 
Management comment 
Mr. Kavish Shah, Vice President – Corporate Affairs, Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, said: “This 
order is a milestone for Diamond Power. Supplying conductors for an ±800 kV HVDC line places us in a 
segment where very few manufacturers are qualified, and it is our entry into the HVDC business. We thank 
Adani Energy Solutions for the confidence placed in us. With our integrated rod mills, enhanced conductor 
capacity and in-house testing facilities, we are well prepared to deliver this order on schedule and to 
participate in the HVDC and green energy corridor projects planned across the country.” 
Diamond Power’s conductor capability 
Diamond Power is amongst the largest conductor manufacturers in India, with integrated aluminium rod 
mills and a conductor range covering ACSR, AL-59, AAAC and HTLS conductors. The Company’s plant at 
Vadodara is supported by NABL-accredited in-house laboratories with high-voltage test facilities rated up 
to 765 kV. 
About Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd 
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DICABS) is an integrated manufacturer of power transmission 
equipment, offering LV, MV, HV and EHV power cables, control and instrumentation cables, fire-resistant 
and flame-retardant cables, solar DC and EV charging cables, and conductors, serving sectors including 
power, infrastructure, oil & gas, renewables, real estate and data centers.
