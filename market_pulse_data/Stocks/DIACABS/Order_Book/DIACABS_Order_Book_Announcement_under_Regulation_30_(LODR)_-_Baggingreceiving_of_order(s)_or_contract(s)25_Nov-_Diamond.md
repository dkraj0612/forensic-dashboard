Annexure A 
PRESS RELEASE 
Diamond Power bags further conductor orders of Rs. 488 crore for a ±800 kV Bhadla–
Fatehpur HVDC line 
Two orders from Adani Energy Solutions for over 6,600 km of AL-59 conductors for one of India’s largest 
green energy corridors 
• Aggregate purchase order value Rs. 488.18 crore including GST (Rs. 413.71 crore excluding GST) 
• 6,660 km of AL-59 Lapwing and AL-59 Zebra conductors for the Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh 
portions of an ±800 kV HVDC transmission line 
• Further strengthens Diamond Power’s position in the HVDC transmission business 
Ahmedabad, 25 November 2026: Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (“Diamond Power” / “DICABS”) 
(BSE: 522163 | NSE: DIACABS), India’s fastest growing cables and conductors company, today announced 
that it has received two purchase orders from Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) for supply of AL-59 
conductors for the Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh portions of the ±800 kV HVDC Bhadla–Fatehpur 
transmission project. 
The orders together cover 5,693 km of AL-59 Lapwing conductor and 967 km of AL-59 Zebra conductor, 
aggregating 6,660 km, on delivered-at-site basis. The aggregate purchase order value is Rs. 488.18 crore 
including GST (basic value Rs. 413.71 crore excluding GST). Deliveries will be made month-wise in line 
with the project schedule and are to be completed by 31 December 2027. 
Growing presence in HVDC 
These orders follow Diamond Power’s earlier order of Rs. 707.80 crore for the same project, taking the 
Company’s total orders for the Bhadla–Fatehpur HVDC line to about Rs. 1,196 crore including GST, and 
further strengthen its position in the HVDC business. HVDC is the technology used to move very large 
blocks of power over long distances with low losses, and ±800 kV is the highest HVDC voltage level in 
operation in India. Conductors for such lines are large-diameter, high-capacity products made to 
demanding quality and surface-finish requirements, and are supplied by a limited set of qualified 
manufacturers. 
About the project 
The Bhadla–Fatehpur HVDC link is being developed by Adani Energy Solutions as a ±800 kV, 6,000 MW 
corridor of about 950 km connecting Bhadla in Rajasthan with Fatehpur in Uttar Pradesh, to evacuate 
renewable energy from Rajasthan to demand centres in northern India.

Management comment 
Mr. Kavish Shah, Vice President – Corporate Strategy, Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, said: 
These repeat orders for the Bhadla–Fatehpur HVDC line reflect the continued confidence Adani Energy 
Solutions has placed in Diamond Power. Conductors for a ±800 kV HVDC line must meet some of the most 
demanding specifications in the industry, and being entrusted with them strengthens our position in this 
specialised segment. With integrated aluminium rod mills, expanded conductor capacity and in-house 
testing facilities, we are well placed to execute on schedule and to participate in the HVDC and green 
energy corridor projects being planned across the country" 
Diamond Power’s conductor capability 
Diamond Power is amongst the largest conductor manufacturers in India, with integrated aluminium rod 
mills and a conductor range covering ACSR, AL-59, AAAC and HTLS conductors. The Company’s plant at 
Vadodara is supported by NABL-accredited in-house laboratories with high-voltage test facilities rated up 
to 765 kV. 
About Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd 
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DICABS) is an integrated manufacturer of power transmission 
equipment, offering LV, MV, HV and EHV power cables, control and instrumentation cables, fire-resistant 
and flame-retardant cables, solar DC and EV charging cables, and conductors, serving sectors including 
power, infrastructure, oil & gas, renewables, real estate and data centers.
