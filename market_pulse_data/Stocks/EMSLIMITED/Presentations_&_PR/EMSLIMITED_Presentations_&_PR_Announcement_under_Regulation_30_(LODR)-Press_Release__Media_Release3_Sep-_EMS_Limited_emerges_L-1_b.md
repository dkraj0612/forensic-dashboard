EMS Limited Emerges as L-1 Bidder for Rs 218.65 Crore 
Rajasthan Sewerage Project 
 Latest L-1 status strengthens order pipeline and reinforces the 
company’s positioning in water and wastewater infrastructure 
Ghaziabad, September 2, 2026: EMS Limited (BSE: 543983, NSE: EMSLIMITED), 
a leading multi-disciplinary EPC company specialising in water, wastewater and 
sewerage infrastructure, has emerged as the Lowest Bidder (L-1) for a major 
sewerage project awarded by the Directorate of Local Bodies, Government of 
Rajasthan. 
The project has an estimated value of ~Rs 218.65 crore (including GST), and covers 
sewerage and septage management works across six Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) in 
the districts of Ganganagar, Hanumangarh and Churu in Rajasthan. 
The scope of work includes sewerage systems with Sewage Treatment Plants 
(STPs), along with laying, jointing, testing and commissioning of sewerage networks. 
The project also includes a one-year defect liability period and 10 years of 
Operations & Maintenance (O&M), with an execution period of 18 months. 
The latest L-1 status further strengthens EMS Limited’s order pipeline. It reinforces 
its growing presence in Rajasthan, while adding to its portfolio of projects focused on 
urban sanitation, wastewater management and sustainable water infrastructure. 
Order Pipeline 
EMS Limited continues to witness healthy tender activity across its core business 
segments. As of June 30, 2026, the company’s order book stood at Rs 2,328.91 
crore. 
In June 2026, the company received L-1 status for another sewerage project in 
Varanasi valued at approximately Rs 102.85 crore, further demonstrating the 
company’s strong participation in urban water and sanitation projects. 
Management Commentary 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Ramveer Singh, Chairman, EMS Limited, 
said: 
“The latest L-1 status for the Rajasthan project is a significant addition to our growing 
project pipeline and reinforces our capabilities in executing large-scale sewerage 
and wastewater infrastructure projects. The project also strengthens our presence in 
Rajasthan and provides an opportunity to leverage our expertise across sewerage 
networks, treatment infrastructure and long-term O&M services. 
The continued focus on urban infrastructure, sanitation and wastewater management 
is creating meaningful opportunities for specialised EPC players. With our 
established execution capabilities and strong order pipeline, we remain focused on

The latest project development comes amid improving operating performance for

About EMS Limited 
Incorporated in 2010, EMS Ltd provides a range of services, including EPC and 
O&M in sewerage solutions, water supply systems, and wastewater schemes for 
government authorities and local municipal bodies. The company also engages in 
electricity transmission and distribution and the manufacture of items used for 
construction purposes. Headquartered in Ghaziabad, EMS Ltd offers water and 
sewerage infrastructure solutions, including laying sewerage networks and building 
sewerage and water treatment plants across Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Bihar, 
Uttarakhand, Rajasthan and West Bengal. 
EMS Ltd also has a major interest in the electrical contracting business, undertaking 
turnkey projects across India. It specializes in the construction of 33/11 KV, 66/33 KV, 
and 132/133 KV substations, internal and external electrification work, and the 
erection, testing, and commissioning of transformers. The company provides EPC 
services for building and road works, successfully delivering numerous projects to its 
clients.
