Blue Dart Expands Delivery Reach into Deeper Pockets Through India Post 
Partnership 
Serving Bharat with stronger delivery connectivity across Tier III and Tier IV towns 
 
Mumbai, October 9, 2026: Blue Dart Express Limited, South Asia’s premier express air and 
integrated transportation and distribution company, has signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) with India Post to further extend its delivery reach. Building on Blue Dart’s 
extensive national network, the collaboration will strengthen last-mile connectivity for 
businesses and customers across Tier III and Tier IV towns and remote locations. The expanded 
coverage will be introduced in a phased manner. 
 
The collaboration brings together Blue Dart’s express logistics capabilities and India 
Post’s extensive postal network to provide customers with access to a wider range of 
markets. Shipments to these additional locations will be delivered through India Post’s 
network, complementing Blue Dart’s infrastructure. The partnership creates a scalable 
model for extending market coverage through the complementary strengths of both 
organisations and the efficient use of existing infrastructure. 
 
Commenting on the partnership, Sh. Harpreet Singh, Member (Operations), 
Department of Posts, “On World Post Day, this partnership bodes well for both 
organisations. The Department of Posts is committed to this partnership and looks 
forward to it. The Department has strong last-mile connectivity, and we will offer all 
possible support to make this partnership stronger. We look forward to a much broader 
and constantly evolving partnership ahead.” 
 
Highlighting the strategic significance of the collaboration, Balfour Manuel, 
Managing Director, Blue Dart, said: “Highlighting the strategic significance of the 
collaboration, Balfour Manuel, Managing Director, Blue Dart, said: “With a legacy 
spanning over 42 years, Blue Dart has grown alongside Indian businesses, building a 
trusted national network that supports the country’s commerce. As the economy 
expands and customer expectations evolve, this strategic partnership has an important 
role to play in strengthening parcel delivery. Our collaboration with India Post builds on 
this foundation, combining the strengths of two established institutions to support 
businesses in reaching more customers across smaller towns and remote locations 
across the country.” 
 
Speaking on the opportunities for customers, Dipanjan Banerjee, Chief Commercial 
Officer, Blue Dart, said: “For SMEs, e-commerce sellers and enterprises, reaching 
customers beyond established markets is an increasingly important driver of growth. 
Across Bharat’s smaller towns, demand for greater product choice creates opportunities 
that businesses need dependable logistics to realise. Our collaboration with India Post 
strengthens our delivery reach across Tier III and Tier IV towns, enabling customers to 
enter new markets, deepen their presence and serve locations that were previously 
harder to reach.”
