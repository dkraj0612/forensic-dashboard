Sub:  Change in Key Managerial Personnel of the Company -  Intimation under

2015’) 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements), 
Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’), we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of 
the Company in its Meeting held today i.e. October 9, 2026, basis the recommendation of the 
Nomination and Remuneration Committee, inter alia, considered, noted and approved the 
following changes in the Key Managerial Personnel viz; Head (Legal & Compliance) & 
Company Secretary of the Company: 
 
i) 
In order to facilitate smooth and seamless transition of the Company Secretarial, Legal 
& Compliance functions in favour of the incumbent candidate, Mr.Tushar Gunderia, 
Head (Legal & Compliance) & Company Secretary (ICSI Membership No. F5508), shall 
continue employment as a `Corporate Affairs and Legal Counsel’ upto December 31, 
2026 viz; superannuation date.  Accordingly, Mr. Tushar Gunderia shall cease to hold 
the office of Head (Legal & Compliance) and Company Secretary and as the Key 
Managerial Personnel of the Company w.e.f. close of business hours on October 9, 
2026.   
 
The Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, noted valuable 
contribution of Mr. Tushar Gunderia during his remarkable tenure of about 28 years with 
Blue Dart Express Ltd. The Board conveyed its best wishes to him as he embarks on 
the next chapter of his journey.  
 
ii) 
Appointment of Mr.Rabindra Purohit (ICSI Membership No. F4680), as Vice President, 
Legal, Compliance & Company Secretary of the Company and Compliance Officer 
under Listing Regulations who would also be Key Managerial Personnel and part of 
Senior Management Personnel with effect from October 10, 2026.   
 
Mr.Rabindra Purohit is a seasoned legal and governance professional with around 25 
years of experience leading legal, secretarial, compliance, and corporate governance 
functions across diverse industries. A qualified Company Secretary, Fellow Member of
