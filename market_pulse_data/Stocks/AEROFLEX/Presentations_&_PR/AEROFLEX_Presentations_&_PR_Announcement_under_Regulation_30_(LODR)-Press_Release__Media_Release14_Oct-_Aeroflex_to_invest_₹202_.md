Aeroflex Industries Limited to Invest Rs 202 Crore by March 2028 to 
Strengthen Manufacturing Prowess 
 
 
Investment to capitalize on high-growth, new-age industries, particularly data centre infrastructure and 
liquid cooling. 
 
Expected to generate incremental revenue of Rs 500–550 crore at peak capacity utilization. 
 
Mumbai, October 14th, 2026: Aeroflex Industries Limited (NSE: AEROFLEX, BSE: 543972), a leading 
manufacturer of flow control solutions with a growing presence in high-value liquid cooling systems for next-
generation AI data centres, today announced a capacity expansion programme with a total capital expenditure 
outlay of Rs 202 crore. The investment will focus on expanding the Company's manufacturing capabilities across 
SS Hose & Braiding, Assemblies and Liquid Cooling Skid Assemblies. 
 
The proposed expansion is aimed at strengthening the Company's manufacturing infrastructure and supporting 
its long-term growth ambitions through the following capacity enhancements: 
 
 
SS Hose & Braiding: Manufacturing capacity to increase from 20 million metres p.a. to 30 million 
metres p.a., enhancing the Company's production capabilities. 
 
Hose Assemblies: Increased hose manufacturing capacity is expected to support an expansion in the 
production capacity of associated hose assemblies. 
 
Liquid Cooling Skid Assemblies: Capacity to expand to 30,000 pieces p.a., strengthening Aeroflex's 
capabilities in the growing liquid cooling infrastructure segment. 
 
The proposed capital expenditure will be funded through a combination of equity, bridge loan and internal 
accruals. The expansion programme will comprise a mix of brownfield expansion at the Company's existing 
Taloja facility and a new manufacturing facility for Hose Assemblies. The investment will be undertaken in a 
phased manner, with the target to complete the expansion by March 2028 
 
Commenting on the capacity expansion plan, Mr. Asad Daud, Managing Director, Aeroflex Industries 
Ltd., said, “The proposed investment reflects Aeroflex’s strategic focus on strengthening its core manufacturing 
capabilities while building capacity in high-growth and emerging industries. 
 
While newer industrial applications and evolving customer requirements are driving demand for SS Hoses & 
Braiding and Assemblies, the rapid expansion of AI workloads, data centre infrastructure and high-density 
computing is creating significant opportunities in liquid cooling solutions. 
 
The planned expansion across SS hoses & Braiding, Assemblies and Liquid Cooling Skid Assemblies is expected 
to enhance Aeroflex’s ability to capitalize on these opportunities, cater to evolving customer needs and drive 
sustainable long-term growth. At peak capacity utilization, the expanded capacities are expected to generate 
incremental revenue of Rs 500 - 550 crore.”

About Aeroflex Industries Limited  
 
Aeroflex Industries Limited (Aeroflex) is one of the leading manufacturers of flow control solutions in India. The 
Company specializes in manufacturing flexible flow solutions used for flow of liquids and gases across a wide 
range of industries and applications. Aeroflex caters to a diverse base of domestic and international customers 
and has established a presence across multiple end-user industries. The Company is expanding its product 
portfolio and manufacturing capabilities, with a growing focus on high-potential segments such as data centre 
liquid cooling infrastructure, including liquid cooling skid assemblies. Through its engineering capabilities, 
product innovation and expanding manufacturing footprint, Aeroflex aims to strengthen its position in the 
global flow solutions market.
