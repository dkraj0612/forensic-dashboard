Sub: - Intimation under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and

Ref: Disclosure filed on November 26, 2025 under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 relating to GST proceedings for Financial Year 2021-22 
 
Dear Madam/Sir, 
 
This is in furtherance to the disclosure made by Company on November 25, 2025 with stock exchanges, 
pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI Listing Regulations"), regarding the 
GST proceedings initiated by the Joint Commissioner, LTU, Corporate Division, West Bengal for Financial 
Year 2021-22. Pursuant thereto, the said authority vide order dated 25th November 2025 (“Original Order”) 
had raised a total demand of INR 4,67,740/- on the Company. 
 
Please be informed that the Company had preferred an appeal before the Sr. Joint Commissioner of Revenue, 
LTU (Appeal), West Bengal ("Appellate Authority") against the Original Order and the Appellate 
Authority, vide its order dated October 30, 2026, has partly allowed the appeal and substantially reduced the 
tax demand, interest and penalty liabilities arising therefrom. 
 
The appellate authority accepted the Company's submissions regarding eligibility of Input Tax Credit 
("ITC") on services availed in connection with its manufacturing activities and accordingly set aside a 
significant portion of the demand raised in the original order. Consequently, the original order has been 
modified and the aggregate liability stands reduced to INR 1,28,577/- (comprising Tax of INR 51,941/-, 
Interest of INR 46,636/- and Penalty of INR 30,000/-). 
 
In terms of SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026, and Industry Standard Note on Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations, the information required 
are enclosed herewith as Annexure -1 and Form - A.
