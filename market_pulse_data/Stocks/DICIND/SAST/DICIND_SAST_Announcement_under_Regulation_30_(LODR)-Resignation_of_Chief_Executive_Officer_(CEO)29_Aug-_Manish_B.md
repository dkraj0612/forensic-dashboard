Dear Mr Anand & Mr Navapol, 
 
After careful consideration, I wish to tender my resignation from the position 
of Managing Director & CEO of DIC India Limited. 
 
This decision has not been easy, as my journey with the organisation has been 
professionally fulfilling and personally enriching. It has been a privilege to 
lead the company during an important phase of its revival , growth and 
transformation, and to work alongside a committed team, valued customers, 
trusted partners, and an experienced Board. 
 
I remain deeply grateful for the trust, support, and guidance extended to me 
throughout my tenure. Together, I believe ,we have strengthened the 
organisation’s capabilities, advanced key strategic priorities, and built a strong
