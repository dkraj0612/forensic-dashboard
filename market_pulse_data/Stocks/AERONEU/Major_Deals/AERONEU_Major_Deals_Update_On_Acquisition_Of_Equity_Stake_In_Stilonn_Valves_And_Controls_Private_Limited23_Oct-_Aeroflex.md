Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
With reference to our earlier intimation dated October 15, 2026 regarding the proposed acquisition 
of equity stake in Stilonn Valves and Controls Private Limited ("Stilonn"), we wish to inform you that 
the Company has completed the acquisition of an aggregate 7,27,040 equity shares of Stilonn through 
a combination of purchase of equity shares from existing shareholders pursuant to the Share 
Purchase Agreement and subscription to additional equity shares of Stilonn pursuant to the Share 
Subscription and Shareholders' Agreement and ancillary Transaction Documents. 
 
Consequent to completion of the aforesaid acquisition, the Company's aggregate shareholding in 
Stilonn stands at 51.01% of its issued and paid-up equity share capital and, accordingly, Stilonn has 
become a subsidiary of the Company. 
 
The acquisition forms part of the Company’s initiative in the AI Infra and Data Centre sector to 
establish an integrated platform for high-tech and critical engineering solutions.
