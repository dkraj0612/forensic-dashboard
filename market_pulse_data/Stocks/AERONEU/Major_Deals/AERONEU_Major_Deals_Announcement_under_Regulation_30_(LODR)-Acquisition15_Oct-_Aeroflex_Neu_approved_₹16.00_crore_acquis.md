Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held on October 
15, 2026, has, inter alia, approved further acquisition of 7,27,040 equity shares of Stilonn Valves and Controls 
Private Limited ("Stilonn"), Company engaged in the business of manufacturing of precision engineering products 
in the high-growth AI Infra and Data Centre space along with critical pharmaceutical and healthcare sector, including 
valves, sanitary fittings and allied goods, for an aggregate consideration of ₹16,00,21,504/- (Rupees Sixteen Crore 
Twenty-One Thousand Five Hundred and Four Only). The acquisition shall be undertaken in one or more tranches 
through a combination of purchase of equity shares from the existing shareholders of Stilonn and subscription to 
additional equity shares proposed to be issued by Stilonn, pursuant to the execution of a Share Purchase Agreement, 
Share Subscription and Shareholders' Agreement and such other ancillary Transaction Documents as may be 
necessary. 
 
Consequent to the completion of the proposed acquisition, the Company's shareholding in Stilonn shall increase 
from 19.58% to 51.01% of its issued and paid-up equity share capital, thereby resulting in Stilonn becoming a 
Subsidiary of the Company. 
 
This acquisition forms part of the Company’s initiative in the AI Infra and Data Centre sector and establish an 
integrated platform for high-tech and critical engineering solutions. 
 
Further, the details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026, is enclosed as Annexure A. 
 
The meeting of the Board of Directors held today commenced at 03:30 p.m. and concluded at 04:15 p.m.
