the terms of the definitive agreements to be signed by the parties.

expand into the manufacturing of wire and wire-related products business. It will broaden the 
Company’s product range and strengthen its position in the AI Infra and Data Centre sector to establish 
an integrated platform for high-tech and critical engineering solutions.
