TEMBO GLOBAL INDUSTRIES FORMS STRATEGIC JOINT VENTURE 
WITH JR-UAV EUROPE AND JR PROPO JAPAN TO ADVANCE INDIGENOUS 
UAV MANUFACTURING IN INDIA 
Tembo Group is pleased to announce a landmark strategic joint venture with JR-UAV Europe S.r.l. 
(Italy) and JR PROPO (Japan) to establish an advanced ecosystem for technology transfer, localisation 
and manufacturing of next-generation Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) and autonomous systems in 
India. 
The strategic collaboration brings together Japanese innovation, European aerospace engineering and 
Indian manufacturing excellence to build mission-ready UAV platforms for defence, surveillance, 
logistics and industrial applications. The initiative reinforces India's vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat by 
enabling indigenous production of globally benchmarked UAV technologies while creating a strong 
export-oriented manufacturing base. 
Under the partnership, global technologies spanning UAV platform design, autonomous flight systems, 
mission integration, flight electronics, servo actuators, telemetry, composite airframe technologies and 
propulsion systems will be industrialised and manufactured in India. The association also leverages the 
expertise of leading global technology partners across Japan, Europe, the United States and the 
Netherlands, creating a comprehensive technology ecosystem capable of delivering complete UAV 
solutions for domestic and international markets. 
The venture aims to strengthen India's aerospace and defence manufacturing capabilities through 
technology transfer, engineering excellence, local production, supply chain development and skill 
creation. By combining international technological expertise with India's manufacturing strengths, the 
partnership seeks to deliver high-quality, mission-critical UAV systems while supporting long-term 
capability building within the country. 
The initiative aligns with Tembo Global Industries' strategic diversification into the defence 
manufacturing sector and reflects the Company's commitment to becoming a significant contributor to 
India's rapidly growing aerospace and defence ecosystem. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Sanjay J. Patel, Managing Director, Tembo Global 
Industries Limited, said: 
"This strategic joint venture marks a defining milestone in Tembo Global Industries' journey towards 
becoming a key contributor to India's defence and aerospace manufacturing ecosystem. By bringing 
together the technological excellence of JR PROPO Japan, the advanced aerospace engineering 
capabilities of JR-UAV Europe and our manufacturing expertise in India, we are creating a strong 
foundation for indigenous production of next-generation UAV systems. Our vision is not merely to 
manufacture drones but to build a globally competitive technology ecosystem that enables innovation, 
strengthens national capabilities, supports the Government's Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative and 
positions India as an export hub for advanced autonomous systems. We believe this collaboration will 
accelerate technology transfer, generate high-value employment, enhance engineering capabilities and 
deliver world-class, mission-ready solutions for both Indian and international markets."

About Tembo Global Industries Limited: 
Incorporated in 2010, Tembo Global Industries is a prominent entity in the industrial sector, specializing 
in the production and assembly of metal components for Pipe Support Systems, Fasteners, Anchors, 
HVAC, Anti-Vibration Systems, and Equipment for a range of installations, including industrial, 
commercial, utility, and OEM. The Company is a fabrication and installation specialist in ductile pipes, 
HDB pipes and fittings, and MS plate, and also engages in the trade of metal products that complement 
its manufacturing operations.  
Its products are certified and approved by Underwriter’s Laboratory Inc. (USA) and FM Approval 
(USA) for Fire Sprinkler System Installations. As an export-driven enterprise, Tembo has earned the 
distinction of a 2-Star Export House. In 2023, Tembo ventured into the EPC (Engineering, Procurement, 
and Construction) contracting arena, securing orders from prestigious infrastructure clients. 
Additionally, the Company has interests in textile trading, manufacturing of defence products, and 
renewable energy.   
About JR-UAV: 
JR-UAV is a System Company enabling the competence and experience of world-renowned RC-
dedicated operations to jointly engineer and produce cutting-edge UAV-UAS components, such as 
piston engines, servo actuators and advanced transmission/receiving systems to support defense and 
commercial applications.  
JR-UAV’s engineering, production, and components supply chain is secured within Australia, Asia, 
Europe, and North America.
