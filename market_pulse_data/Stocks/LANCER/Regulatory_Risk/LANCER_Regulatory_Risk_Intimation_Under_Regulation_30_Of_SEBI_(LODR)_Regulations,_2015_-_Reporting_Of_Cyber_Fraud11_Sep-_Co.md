Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform the Exchange that the Company 
has been subjected to a cyber fraud incident involving impersonation and fraudulent payment 
instructions, pursuant to which certain payments were made by the Company to third-party bank 
accounts. No disruption to the business operations of the Company has been identified. 
 
The incident came to the notice of the Company upon identification of certain transactions that 
were not in accordance with the Company’s normal payment and authorisation processes. The 
Company immediately initiated a detailed investigation into the matter and took necessary steps 
to contain and mitigate the impact of the incident. 
 
The Company’s management, internal technical team and cybersecurity professionals have been 
engaged in investigating the incident, including examination of the relevant IT systems, 
communication channels, payment processes and authorisation mechanisms. Appropriate 
containment, remediation and strengthening measures are being undertaken. 
 
The Company promptly notified its bankers and instructed them to initiate necessary measures, 
including recall of the relevant transactions and freezing/tracing of the beneficiary accounts, to 
the extent possible. The Company has also lodged a complaint Cyber Police Station, Navi Mumbai 
and has reported the matter to the relevant investigating authorities.  
 
The Company continues to cooperate fully with the investigating authorities and is taking all 
necessary steps to safeguard the interests of the Company and its stakeholders. 
 
The particulars of the aforesaid event, in the format prescribed under the SEBI LODR Regulations, 
are enclosed as Annexure A and form part of this intimation. 
 
Any further material developments in the matter, including recovery of funds, determination of 
the final financial impact and outcome of the investigation, shall be intimated to the Stock 
Exchange in accordance with the applicable regulatory requirements.
