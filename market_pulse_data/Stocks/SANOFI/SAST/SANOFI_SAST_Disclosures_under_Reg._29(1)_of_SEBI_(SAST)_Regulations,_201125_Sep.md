Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Sub: Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 29(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011  
(“SAST Regulations”) 
This is to inform you that Sanofi Healthcare India Private Limited, an entity part of the promoter 
group of Sanofi India Limited (“Target Company”) has acquired 3,500,000 equity shares 
representing 15.20% of the equity share capital of the Target Company from Hoechst GmbH, 
a promoter of the Target Company. The acquisition has been undertaken pursuant to the 
exemption available under Regulation 10(1)(a)(iii) of the SAST Regulations, on 
24th September 2026 by way of inter se transfer of shares amongst members of the promoter 
and promoter group of the Target Company through a block deal mechanism on the Stock 
Exchange. 
Pursuant to the Regulation 29(1) of the SAST Regulations, please find attached the disclosure 
in respect of the acquisition of the equity shares of the Target Company. 
 
This is for your information and records. 
 
Thanking you. 
 
Yours faithfully,
