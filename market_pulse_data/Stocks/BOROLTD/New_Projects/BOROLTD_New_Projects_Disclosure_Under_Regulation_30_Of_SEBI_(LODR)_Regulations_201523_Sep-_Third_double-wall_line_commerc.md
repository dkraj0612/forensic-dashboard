Dear Sirs, 
 
Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
 
We refer to our earlier intimation dated July 01, 2026, wherein it was, inter alia, informed 
that commercial production from the third double-wall line at the manufacturing unit set up 
through the Company’s Wholly Owned Subsidiary, Stylenest India Limited, was expected 
to commence during Q2 FY 2026-27. 
 
We wish to inform you that the work on the third double-wall line is currently in progress, 
and the commencement of commercial production from the said line is now expected by 
January 2027. The revised timeline is primarily attributable to certain logistical and supply-
chain constraints in the receipt of imported equipment from China, coupled with the 
scheduling and availability of the supplier’s technical personnel for installation and 
commissioning. 
 
The Company is closely coordinating with the supplier to expedite the balance activities 
relating to installation and commissioning and is taking necessary steps to ensure 
completion of the pending activities in line with the revised timeline. 
 
This is for your information and record, please. 
 
This intimation is also being uploaded on the Company's website at www.borosil.com.  
Thank you.
