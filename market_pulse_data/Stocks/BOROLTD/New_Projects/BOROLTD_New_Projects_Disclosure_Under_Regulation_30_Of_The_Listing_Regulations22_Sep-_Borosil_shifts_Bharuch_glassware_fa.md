Dear Sirs, 
 
  Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
 
We refer to our earlier disclosure dated March 10, 2026, regarding the setting up of a new 
manufacturing facility of the Company at Bharuch, Gujarat, for the manufacture of glassware 
products with dedicated mass-production lines, involving an estimated capital expenditure of Rs. 
42 crores. 
 
In this regard, we wish to inform you that, following a comprehensive review of the Company’s 
evolving business environment and overall manufacturing strategy, the Board has reconsidered 
the proposal and decided to pursue an alternative approach to the proposed setting up of the 
manufacturing facility. Accordingly, instead of the Company setting up and funding the new 
manufacturing facility directly, the facility will now be set up by Borosil Scientific Limited (“BSL”) at 
Bharuch, Gujarat, with the capital expenditure for the facility to be incurred by BSL, subject to the 
requisite approvals. 
 
The Company’s long-term requirements for the relevant glassware products will continue to be 
supported through the existing manufacturing arrangement between the Company and BSL. 
 
The Company remains committed to pursuing its growth plans, expanding its market presence and 
strengthening its position across its key glassware categories, in alignment with its strategic 
priorities. 
 
This intimation is also available on the Company’s website at www.borosil.com. 
 
Thanking you.
