Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“the SEBI Listing Regulations”) - Final Findings 
of DGTR recommending imposition of anti-dumping duty on imports of Borosilicate 
Table and Kitchen Glassware from China PR 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that 
the Company has received the Final Findings dated September 24, 2026, issued by the 
Directorate General of Trade Remedies (“DGTR”), Department of Commerce, Ministry of 
Commerce & Industry, Government of India, in relation to the anti-dumping investigation 
concerning imports of “Borosilicate Table and Kitchen Glassware” originating in or 
exported from China PR. 
 
The investigation was initiated pursuant to the application filed by the Company as the 
domestic industry. The DGTR, upon examination of the facts and submissions on record, 
has concluded, inter alia, that: 
 
• 
the subject goods originating in or exported from China PR have been exported to 
India at dumped prices;  
• 
the dumping margins are positive and significant;  
• 
the Company, being the sole producer of the like article in India, constitutes the 
domestic industry;  
• 
the dumped imports from China PR have materially retarded the establishment 
of the domestic industry; and  
• 
the imposition of anti-dumping duty is not against the public interest.  
 
Accordingly, having regard to the lesser-duty rule, the DGTR has recommended 
imposition of anti-dumping duty on imports of the subject goods originating in or 
exported from China PR for a period of five (5) years from the date of the notification 
to be issued in this regard by the Central Government.
