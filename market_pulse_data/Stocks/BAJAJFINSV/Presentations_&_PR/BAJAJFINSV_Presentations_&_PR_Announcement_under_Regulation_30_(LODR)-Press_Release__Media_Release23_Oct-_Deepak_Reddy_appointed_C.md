Mumbai/Pune, October 23, 2026: The Board of Directors of Bajaj General Insurance 
Limited (formerly known as Bajaj Allianz General Insurance Company Limited), a material 
unlisted subsidiary of Bajaj Finserv Limited, approved the appointment of Deepak Reddy 
as CEO Designate of the company, eąective January 1, 2027. He will take charge as MD & 
CEO on 1st April 2027, subject to approval of IRDAI. 
 
Dr. Tapan Singhel will continue as MD & CEO until March 31, 2027, ensuring a seamless 
transition. Subject to approval from the Insurance Regulatory and Development 
Authority of India (IRDAI), Deepak Reddy will take charge as MD & CEO from April 1, 2027.  
 
Sanjiv Bajaj, Chairman and Managing Director, Bajaj Finserv Limited, said, “Few 
leaders have the distinction of building an institution from the ground up. Tapan is one of 
them. From its early years as a joint venture to its evolution into a wholly Bajaj-owned 
company, he has played a deﬁning role in shaping Bajaj General Insurance into one of 
India’s most trusted general insurers. Under his leadership, the company has earned a 
reputation for underwriting discipline, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to 
customers. His legacy is not just a successful business, but a strong institution built to 
endure. On behalf of the Board of Directors and the Bajaj Group, I thank Tapan for his 
exceptional leadership and lasting contribution to the company and to Indian insurance. 
 
As India’s insurance sector enters its next phase of growth, the opportunity to protect 
more lives, livelihoods and businesses, is immense. We are delighted to welcome Deepak 
to lead Bajaj General into its next chapter. I am conﬁdent that Deepak will build on the 
strong foundation laid by Tapan and his team, unlock new opportunities and take the 
company to greater heights.” he added.

About Bajaj Finserv 
 
Bajaj Finserv Ltd (BFS), an unregistered Core Investment Company (Unregistered CIC) 
under Core Investment Companies (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2016, as amended, is one 
of India’s leading promoters of ﬁnancial services businesses with consolidated revenue 
of over Rs. 1,50,530 crore (USD 15.9 billion) and consolidated proﬁt after tax of over Rs. 
8,800 crore (USD 1 billion) for FY2025- 26.  
 
BFS and its subsidiaries have served over 308 million customers with a diversiﬁed 
portfolio that helps meet ﬁnancial needs and build ﬁnancial resilience of individuals and 
businesses. Its suite of ﬁnancial solutions includes consumer and commercial loans, 
mortgages, auto ﬁnancing, ﬁxed deposit products, payments, securities brokerage 
services, general and life insurance, and investments.  
 
BFS is focused on continuous innovation through smart use of technology, data and 
analytics to drive seamless, simpliﬁed and personalized experiences for its customers. 
BFS is included in the benchmark BSE Sensex and Nifty 50 index of large cap stocks.

country. The company’s ﬂagship self-implemented skilling programme called the
