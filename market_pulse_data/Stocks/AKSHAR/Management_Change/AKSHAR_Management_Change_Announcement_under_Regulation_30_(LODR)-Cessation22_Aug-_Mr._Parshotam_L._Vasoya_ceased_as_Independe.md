SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 we hereby inform you that the

been completed and his term expired on 22nd August, 2026. 
 
The Company places on record its sincere appreciation for the valuable contribution and services rendered by Mr. 
Parshotam L. Vasoya during his tenure as an Independent Director of the Company. 
 
 
Following the cessation, the Company will initiate the process of identifying and evaluating a suitable candidate for 
appointment as an Independent Director in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the 
SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, with a view to ensuring compliance with the 
prescribed Board composition requirements. The Company shall make the requisite disclosure to the Stock Exchanges 
upon finalisation of the appointment, in accordance with the applicable regulatory requirements.
