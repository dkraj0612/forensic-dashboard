### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aarti Pharmalabs reported robust Q2 FY27 year-over-year revenue and profit growth, driven by strategic capacity expansions in Xanthine derivatives and CDMO/CMO, while projecting an optimistic 15-18% revenue and EBITDA CAGR for the coming years. However, the company experienced a sequential decline in Q2 EBITDA and PAT margins, impacted by elevated raw material costs, exceptional items, and volatility from fair value adjustments on a USD forward contract. Significant ongoing capital expenditure has also led to a substantial increase in borrowings and working capital, which warrants close monitoring.
