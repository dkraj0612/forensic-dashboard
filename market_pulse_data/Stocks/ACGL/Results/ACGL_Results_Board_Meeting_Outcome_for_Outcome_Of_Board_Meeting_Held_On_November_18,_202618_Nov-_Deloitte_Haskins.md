today, i.e., November 18, 2026, approved the appointment of M/s. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, 
Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 117366W/W-100018), as the Statutory Auditors 
of the Company with effect from November 18, 2026, until the conclusion of the 47th Annual 
General Meeting of the Company, to fill the casual vacancy arising as a result of the resignation 
of M/s. B S R & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), 
the erstwhile Statutory Auditors of the Company.

to the Stock Exchange vide our letter dated November 13, 2026. 
 
The Company shall seek the approval of the Members for the aforesaid appointment within the 
prescribed timelines, in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013.
