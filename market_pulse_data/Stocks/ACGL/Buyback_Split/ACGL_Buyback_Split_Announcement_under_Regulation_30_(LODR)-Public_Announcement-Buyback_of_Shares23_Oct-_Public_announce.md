### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Automobile Corporation of Goa Limited (ACGL) is undertaking a buyback of up to 1,44,639 shares at INR 2,005 each, totaling approximately INR 29 crore, to return surplus funds and enhance shareholder value through improved financial ratios. This tender offer allows eligible shareholders to exit at a significant premium over recent market prices. Notably, the promoter group will not participate, leading to a proportional increase in their ownership without further investment, which is a material impact rather than a red flag given the buyback's compliance and internal funding.
