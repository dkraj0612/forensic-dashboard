Stanley Lifestyles Strengthens Presence in Rajasthan with New Store Launch in Jaipur 
Expands Sofas & More footprint with a new store in the Pink City 
Jaipur, 3rd August 2026: Stanley Lifestyles Limited (BSE: 544202 | NSE: STANLEY), one of India’s 
leading integrated super-premium and luxury furniture manufacturers and retailers, today 
announced the launch of its new Sofas & More by Stanley store in Jaipur, Rajasthan. 
Located at 10, Ajmer Road, Hathroi, Jaipur – 302001, the new 6,500 sq. ft. store marks another step 
in Stanley Lifestyles' retail expansion strategy, strengthening the Company's presence in Rajasthan 
and bringing its portfolio of design-led home furniture solutions closer to consumers in Jaipur and 
the surrounding markets. 
Designed to offer an immersive and experience-led shopping environment, the new Sofas & More – 
Jaipur store showcases a curated portfolio of sofas, recliners, beds, mattresses, dining tables, 
armchairs and accessories. The collection brings together contemporary design, comfort, 
functionality, quality craftsmanship and personalisation to address the evolving preferences of 
modern homeowners. 
The store was inaugurated by Sunil Suresh, Founder & Chairman, Stanley Lifestyles Limited and 
Venkataramana Gorti, Managing Director, Stanley Lifestyles Limited, in the presence of Anil Solanki, 
Director, NDA Corp Ltd. and distinguished guests Dr. Sandeep Bakshi & Dr. Preeti Bakshi, Chancellor 
& ED, Jaipur National University. 
Store Highlights & Offerings 
The Sofas & More – Jaipur store offers a comprehensive range of home furniture solutions across 
living, dining and bedroom categories. The portfolio has been thoughtfully curated for consumers 
seeking a balance of contemporary aesthetics, comfort, functionality and exceptional quality. 
Within an experience-led retail format, customers can explore a variety of designs, configurations, 
materials, colours and finishes available beyond the products displayed in-store. This enables them 
to customise and curate furniture solutions suited to their individual tastes, lifestyles and preferences 
for living spaces. 
The Jaipur store reflects Sofas & More’s vision of making thoughtfully designed, premium-quality 
furniture accessible to a diverse consumer base while providing customers with a personalised and 
immersive retail experience. 
Commenting on the launch, Sunil Suresh, Founder & Chairman, Stanley Lifestyles Limited, said: 
“Rajasthan is an important market for us, with Jaipur emerging as a strong destination for premium 
home and lifestyle products. Consumers today are increasingly looking for furniture that brings 
together design, comfort, quality and functionality. With the launch of Sofas & More in Jaipur, we 
look forward to bringing our design-led furniture solutions closer to customers in the region and 
strengthening our presence in this growing market.” 
Venkataramana Gorti, Managing Director, Stanley Lifestyles Limited, said: 
“Our expansion into Jaipur reflects our strategy of strengthening the Sofas & More network across 
high-potential markets in India. Jaipur has a growing base of discerning homeowners with increasing 
aspirations for well-designed and high-quality furniture. Through this store, we aim to offer customers

About Stanley Lifestyles Limited: 
Stanley Lifestyles Limited is a leading Indian luxury furniture company offering a broad spectrum of 
home solutions under brands such as Stanley Level Next, Stanley Boutique and Sofas & More. The 
Company designs, manufactures and retails a diverse array of premium and luxury furniture and 
interior products including sofas, recliners, dining sets, kitchen cabinetry, beds, wardrobes and 
accessories. Its vertically integrated model ensures end-to-end control over design, production and 
retail, with two manufacturing facilities in Bengaluru spanning over 300,000 square feet. These 
facilities support a wide range of offerings across ultra-luxury, luxury and super-premium categories. 
With 71 retail outlets across major Indian cities, Stanley Lifestyles operates through both company-
owned and franchise formats to maximize reach and customer engagement. The Company’s strategic 
strengths lie in its design-led innovation, skilled craftsmanship and consistent expansion in high-
opportunity markets. Backed by experienced promoters and professional leadership, Stanley 
Lifestyles continues to leverage its retail and manufacturing capabilities to address growing demand 
in India’s premium and luxury home interiors market.
