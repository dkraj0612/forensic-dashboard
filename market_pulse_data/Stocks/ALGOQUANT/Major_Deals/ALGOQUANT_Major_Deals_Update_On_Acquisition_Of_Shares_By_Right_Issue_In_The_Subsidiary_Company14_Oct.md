### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Here is the executive summary:  Algoquant Fintech Limited has invested ₹52.5 lakhs into its 75%-owned, non-operational subsidiary, AQ Capital Services Private Limited (AQCSPL), through a rights issue. This cash infusion is intended to fund AQCSPL's initial setup, including essential SEBI registration to operate as a Registrar and Transfer Agent, and cover initial running costs. A significant red flag is that AQCSPL is a pre-revenue startup with zero turnover, indicating a high-risk investment heavily dependent on successfully obtaining regulatory approvals to commence operations.
