> **Executive Summary: Adani Power Limited Investor Presentation (Sept 2026)**  Adani Power Limited (APL) presents itself as India's leading private baseload power generator, strategically positioned for substantial growth driven by India's robust economic expansion and surging electricity demand. The company highlights strong operational excellence, evidenced by 90%+ plant availability and 40% EBITDA margins in FY26, alongside a secure financial model with 23.7 GW of future capacity largely self-funded and derisked by long-term power purchase agreements. A potential red flag is the strong emphasis on coal-based power to meet India's baseload needs, which, despite being presented as critical, creates a tension with the broader decarbonization goals also noted in the presentation.
