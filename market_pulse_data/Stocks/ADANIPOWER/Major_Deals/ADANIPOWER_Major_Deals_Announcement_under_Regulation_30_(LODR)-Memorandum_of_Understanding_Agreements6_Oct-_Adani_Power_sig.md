ANNEXURE B 
                   
                            Media release 
Adani Power and Druk Green Power to set up the 770 MW 
Chamkharchhu-I Hydropower Project in Bhutan 
 
• 
49:51 equity partnership between Adani Power and Druk Green Power 
• 
Project to be delivered within six years of ground-breaking 
• 
Part of the larger 5,000 MW hydropower development strategic partnership between 
Adani Group and the Royal Government of Bhutan 
 
Thimpu/ Ahmedabad, October 5, 2026: Adani Power, India’s leading private power producer, 
and Druk Green Power Corp. Ltd. (DGPC), Bhutan’s state-owned generation utility, today 
signed the Shareholders Agreement (SHA) for setting up a 770 MW Chamkharchhu-I 
Hydroelectric Project in the Kingdom of Bhutan.  
 
The signing ceremony was graced by Hon’ble Prime Minister of Bhutan, Lyonchen Dasho 
Tshering Tobgay, and attended by Hon’ble Minister of Finance and the Hon’ble Minister of 
Energy and Natural Resources, along with representatives from Druk Holding & Investments 
Limited (DHI), Bhutan Power Corporation Limited (BPC), the DGPC Board, DGPC Management, 
and Adani Power. 
 
The project marks the second major hydropower collaboration between the two partners 
following the 570 MW Wangchhu Hydroelectric Project agreement signed in September 
2025, and forms part of the broader 5,000 MW hydropower development Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) signed between the Adani Group and DGPC in May 2025. 
 
The peaking run-of-river project is located in central Bhutan’s Zhemgang District and on the 
Chamkarchhu River and the project will be implemented under the Build, Own, Operate and 
Transfer (BOOT) model through a 49:51 equity partnership between Adani Power and DGPC 
with a concession of 30 years from COD. The project will strengthen Bhutan’s clean energy 
generation capacity and enhance regional energy cooperation, with construction expected 
to commence in the first half of 2027 and commissioning targeted for six years from 
groundbreaking. 
 
“We are delighted to strengthen our partnership with the Royal Government of Bhutan and 
DGPC through the Chamkharchhu Hydroelectric Project. Hydropower will play a pivotal role 
in accelerating South Asia’s energy transition, and this project will contribute significantly to 
enhancing energy security, reducing carbon intensity, and advancing shared sustainability 
goals. While the project is expected to help meet Bhutan’s peak winter electricity 
requirements, it will also enable the export of surplus green power to India during the summer 
months, creating long-term value for both nations,” said Anil Sardana, Managing Director, 
Adani Power Ltd.

About Adani Power Limited 
Adani Power (APL), a part of the Adani portfolio, is the largest private thermal power producer 
in India. The Company has an installed thermal power capacity of 18,290 MW spread across 
14 power plants in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Madhya 
Pradesh, Jharkhand, and Tamil Nadu, apart from a 40 MW solar power plant in Gujarat. With 
the help of a world-class team of experts in every field of power, Adani Power is on course to 
achieve its growth potential. The company is harnessing technology and innovation to 
transform India into a power-surplus nation and provide quality and affordable electricity for 
all. 
 
About Druk Green Power Corporation Limited 
Druk Green Power Corporation Limited (DGPC) is Bhutan’s leading renewable energy 
generation company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Druk Holding & Investments Limited, 
the commercial arm of the Royal Government of Bhutan. With a generation portfolio of over 
3,500 MW, DGPC is at the forefront of Bhutan’s ambition to expand its renewable energy 
capacity to 25,000 MW by 2040. Established in 2008, DGPC has built extensive expertise 
across the energy value chain, particularly in hydropower development and operations, and 
is now expanding into solar and other renewable energy opportunities. Through strategic 
partnerships, innovation and sustainable project development, DGPC is working to harness 
Bhutan’s renewable energy potential while strengthening energy security and enabling 
greater regional energy cooperation.
