Sub.: Update on pending litigation - Intimation under Regulation 30 of the Securities 
and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”). 
 
We refer to our disclosure dated August 19, 2023, intimating the pendency of material 
litigations, as required to be disclosed under SEBI Listing Regulations. 
 
As an update to the litigation mentioned at Srl # 2 of the above disclosure, relating to 
appeal filed by the Company before Hon'ble Appellate Tribunal for Electricity 
(“APTEL”), we would like to inform that the APTEL has allowed the Company’s appeal 
and set aside the order of Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission’s (“MERC”). 
APTEL has further directed MERC to issue consequential orders strictly in line with 
APTEL's earlier judgment and compute Change in Law compensation, based on the 
operational parameters prescribed therein. 
 
The detailed disclosure with respect to the above as required under the SEBI Master 
Circular HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is 
enclosed herewith as ‘Annexure – A’.
