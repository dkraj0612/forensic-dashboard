Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Letter of Award 
 
This is to inform that Adani Power Limited (“the Company”) has received a Letter of 
Award (“LoA”) from Uttarakhand Power Corporation Limited (“UPCL”) for 1,234 MW 
ex-bus net supply (1,320 MW installed capacity) of Thermal Power under a long-term 
Power Supply Agreement (“PSA”), upon the Company being declared as the 
successful bidder in terms of the tender invited and issued by UPCL. 
 
The LOA is subject to the Hon’ble Uttarakhand Electricity Regulatory Commission’s 
approval for the adoption of tariff under Section 63 of the Electricity Act, 2003. 
 
The details, as required under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Master Circular bearing reference number 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is attached 
herewith as Annexure A. 
 
The LoA has been received by the Company at 05:09 PM on October 13, 2026. 
 
You are requested to take the same on your record.
