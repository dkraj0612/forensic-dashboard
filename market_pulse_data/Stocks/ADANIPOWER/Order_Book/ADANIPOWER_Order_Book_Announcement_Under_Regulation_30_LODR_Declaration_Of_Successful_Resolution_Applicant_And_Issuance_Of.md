Dear Sirs / Madam, 
 
 Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Declaration of the Successful Resolution 
Applicant and issuance of Letter of Intent for acquiring GVK Energy Limited  
 
This is to inform that the Committee of Creditors ("COC") of GVK Energy Limited 
(“GVKEL”), a company undergoing Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process ("CIRP") 
under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016 ("IBC") has approved the Resolution 
Plan submitted by Adani Power Limited (“APL”).  
 
In this regard, APL has received a Letter of Intent ("LOI") from the Resolution 
Professional ("RP") on September 07, 2026, at 9.35 a.m.  
 
GVKEL owns and operates a 330 MW hydroelectric power plant located in Uttarakhand 
through its subsidiary Alaknanda Hydro Power Company Limited. The implementation 
of the resolution plan is subject to the terms of the LOI and requisite approvals from 
the National Company Law Tribunal, Hyderabad and / or any other regulatory authority 
/ courts / tribunal (as the case may be) under applicable laws.  
 
This is for your information and taking on record.
