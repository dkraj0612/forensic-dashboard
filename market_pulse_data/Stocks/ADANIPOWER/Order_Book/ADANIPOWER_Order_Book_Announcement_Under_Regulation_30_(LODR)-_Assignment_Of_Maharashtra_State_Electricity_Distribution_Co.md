Dear Sirs / Madam, 
 
 Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Assignment of Maharashtra State Electricity 
Distribution Co. Limited’s 2500 MW RE RTC Power Supply Contract by Adani 
Power Limited to Powerpulse Trading Solutions Limited 
 
We refer to Adani Power Limited’s (“APL” or “Company”) disclosure dated April 2, 2026, 
intimating about receipt of a Letter of Award (“LOA”) from Maharashtra State 
Electricity Distribution Co. Limited (“MSEDCL”) for supply of 2500 MW RE RTC 
(“Renewable Energy Round-The-Clock”) power for a period of 25 years from Scheduled 
Commencement Date of Supply.  
 
This RE RTC contract envisages supply of power aggregated from various sources with 
minimum supply obligations including a minimum ratio of renewable power. 
Subsequent to the receipt of the LOA, in view of APL’s long-term plans to invest in 
capacity expansion in the thermal, nuclear, and international hydroelectric projects, it 
has been decided to assign the LOA to M/s Powerpulse Trading Solutions Limited 
(“PTSL”), which is a wholly owned subsidiary of Adani Energy Solutions Limited.  
 
Accordingly, APL has, after securing requisite approvals, entered into an agreement to 
transfer the rights and obligations under the said LOA to PTSL. This transfer will allow 
APL to focus on its core activity of baseload power generation under long-term 
arrangements to ensure steady revenue and predictable profitability.  
 
This is for your information and taking on record.
