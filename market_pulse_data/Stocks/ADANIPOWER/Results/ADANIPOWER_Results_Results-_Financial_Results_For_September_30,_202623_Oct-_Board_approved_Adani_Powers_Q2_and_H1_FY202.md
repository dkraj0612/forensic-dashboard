Power market update: 
During Q2 FY27, India’s power demand grew by 11.5% at 502 BU as compared to Q2 FY26, 
driven by rapid economic growth and a weak monsoon. Growing demand resulted in 11% 
higher power generation year-on-year to reach 536 Billion Units (“BU”), which coupled with 
lower hydropower potential resulted in higher contribution in power supply from Coal and 
Lignite-fired power generation at 66% as compared to 65% in Q2 FY26.  Coal and Lignite-
based power accounted for 73% of the incremental power generation in the quarter.  
Aggregate Plant Load Factor (“PLF”) of coal-based power plants in India rose to 68% in Q2 
FY27, as compared to 62% in Q2 FY26.  
 
Peak power demand reached 270 GW in Q2 FY27, up 18% from 230 GW recorded in the 
previous year. Large states like Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra accounted for 27 GW of 
peak demand each during Q2 FY27, followed by Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka with 
23 GW, 20 GW, and 16 GW respectively.  
 
During Q2 FY27, the Day-Ahead Market Clearing Price on the Indian Energy Exchange rose 
by 46% year-on-year to Rs. 5.7/unit. The Real-Time Market Clearing Price also increased by 
49% year-on-year to Rs. 5.2/unit.
