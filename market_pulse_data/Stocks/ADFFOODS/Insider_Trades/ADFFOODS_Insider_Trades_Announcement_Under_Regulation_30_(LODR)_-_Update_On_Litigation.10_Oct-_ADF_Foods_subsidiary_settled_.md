Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Update on Litigation. 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
Further to our intimations dated 28th June, 2026 and 14th August, 2026 and pursuant to Regulation 30 
read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we inform you that ADF Foods (USA) Ltd., the Company’s step-down 
wholly owned subsidiary, has executed a Forbearance Agreement dated 9th October, 2026 
with Ascot Valley Foods Ltd (‘Ascot’) and its affiliates in relation to the litigation before the 
United States District Court, Southern District of New York. 
The monetary judgments in favour of the subsidiary aggregate approximately US$ 2.31 million plus 
applicable interest. Under the Forbearance Agreement, the subsidiary has conditionally agreed to accept 
US$ 1.75 million plus applicable interest, towards satisfaction of these judgments. The initial payment 
of US$450,000 was received by the subsidiary on 9th October, 2026. The balance of US$1.30 million 
plus interest is payable in monthly instalments of at least US$25,000 over a maximum of 52 months 
commencing November 2026.  
Ascot is required to withdraw its appeal. The permanent injunction protecting the subsidiary’s 
proprietary recipes and trade secrets remains fully effective. The subsidiary retains its rights to enforce 
the original judgments, after crediting payments received, in the event of default. Satisfaction of the 
judgments is conditional upon full payment under the agreement. 
Receipt of the initial payment marks progress in monetary recovery under a structured payment 
framework, while preserving the subsidiary’s intellectual property protection.  
The disclosure as required under Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular 
dated 30th January, 2026 is enclosed as ‘Annexure A’. 
The above information shall also be made available on the website of the Company at 
www.adf-foods.com.  
Please take the same on record.
