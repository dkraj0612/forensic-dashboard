NOTICE
 
Heard the learned Senior Counsel Sri. B.C. Thiruvengadam, appearing for the appellants.
The appellants institute a suit before the Commercial Court invoking the Commercial Courts Act, 2015, seeking
injunction against the respondents herein. Before the Concerned Court, the respondents filed application under
Order VII Rule 11 of the CPC seeking rejection of the plaint on the score that there was no commercial dispute
as obtaining under the Commercial Courts Act subsisting in the proceedings. The Court accepts the same,
allows the application in part, permits the plaint to be presented before the appropriate Court having jurisdiction
by passing an order under Order VII Rule 10 of CPC.
The appellant is now before the Court on the score of projection of an error by the hands of the concerned
Court.
The learned Senior Counsel would take this Court through the documents appended to the petition to
demonstrate that the nature of the transaction is what is to be looked into and mere existence or non-existence
of an agreement would not be the only reason to assess whether a suit is a commercial suit or otherwise. He
would seek to place reliance upon several judgments of the Apex Court on the issue. The matter would require
consideration.
The learned counsel submits that throughout the proceedings before the concerned Court, there was an
injunction operating in favour of the appellants.
In the light of the said submission, the same would continue till the next date of hearing.
Issue emergent notice to the respondents, returnable by 04.11.2026.
List this matter on 04.11.2026.
Registry is directed to show the name of the appellants’ counsel as Sri. Roshan Saheb Chabbi.
