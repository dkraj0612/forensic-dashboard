by A-One Steels India Limited:  
1. W.P. No. 33933/2026 – Pushpa Agarwal vs. SEBI & Ors.: filed by Pushpa Agarwal, the 
Petitioner against SEBI, Union of India, A-One Steels India Limited, PL Capital Markets 
Private Limited, and Khambatta Securities Limited, the Respondents, before Hon’ble High 
Court of Bombay alleging lack of proper disclosure of material information concerning 
Respondent No. 3 in the RHP. The petition sought directions from the Hon’ble High Court to 
issue writ of mandamus or other appropriate writ, order or direction to commanding, SEBI to 
consider and decide by reasoned order on pending complaints/representations, set aside RHP 
filed dated 11th September 2026, equity shares allotted to Respondent not to be listed, to 
command SEBI in the event and addendum, corrigendum or supplementary disclosure 
including publication of public notice in the newspaper and deferment of listing and take 
corrective measures or supplementary disclosures.
