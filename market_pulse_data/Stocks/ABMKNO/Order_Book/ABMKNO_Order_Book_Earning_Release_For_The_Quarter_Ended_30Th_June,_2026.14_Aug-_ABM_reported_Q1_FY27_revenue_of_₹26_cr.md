 Analysis Of The Financial Results 
 
  
ABM Knowledgeware Limited (BSE: 531161), operates in three high tech verticals i.e. E-
governance, Cybersecurity and Agritech. ABM announced its results on August 14, 2026, for 
the quarter ended June 30, 2026. 
Summary of Key Financial Parameters 
On a Consolidated basis across Digital Governance, Cybersecurity and Agritech, ABM reported 
operational revenue of ₹26 crores for Q1 FY 2026–27. The Company posted consolidated 
EBITDA, PBT and PAT of ₹8.17 crores, ₹6.28 crores and ₹2.74 crores respectively for the 
quarter. 
ABM achieved a Gross Profit Margin of approximately 31% and an Operating Profit Margin of 
18%. As highlighted over the past 1–2 years, the moderation in profitability is primarily 
attributable to continued investments in AgriTech (Scanit), scaling efforts and competitive 
pressures in Cybersecurity (InstaSafe), and margin pressures during the transition phase of 
diversification in Digital Governance business. 
Segment Highlights 
Digital Governance 
Standalone revenue for Q1 FY27 stood at ₹20.64 crores, compared with ₹20.16 crores in the 
corresponding quarter of the previous year, registering a marginal year-on-year growth of 2%. 
During the quarter, the business maintained a Gross Profit Margin of 18% and a Net Profit 
Margin of 15%.  
Previous year Quarter included deferred revenue of ₹2.50 crores, with no corresponding cost 
impact. Excluding this one-time impact, the current quarter PAT margin has improved from 
11% to 15% in comparison with the previous year quarter.  
Rs 7 Crores orders were won during the quarter in the focus areas of the diversification strategy. 
Cybersecurity 
InstaSafe Technologies Private Limited, ABM’s cloud-based cybersecurity subsidiary, delivered 
a strong performance in Q1 FY27, with revenue increasing to ₹5.52 crores from ₹4.95 crores 
in Q1 FY26, registering a growth of 12%. PAT also increased significantly to ₹2.29 crores from 
₹1.47 crores, reflecting a year-on-year growth of 56%.

Agritech 
Scanit continued to strengthen its position as a global agricultural intelligence and pathogen 
surveillance platform during the quarter. The business achieved several commercial and 
strategic milestones across North America, Europe, Africa, Latin America and India. In the 
United States, Scanit secured its first multi-year deployment for row-crop disease monitoring 
covering approximately 500,000 acres, expanded acreage coverage in the Midwest by over 2x, 
and added new pilots with leading agribusiness, research and academic partners. Positive 
customer outcomes and repeat subscriptions further validated the value of Scanit's technology.  
The company also expanded its international opportunity pipeline, including advanced 
discussions with leading seed and agricultural organizations in Europe and Africa, while 
establishing new significant partnerships in India with AgriPilot.ai and DeHaat to broaden 
market access. Product enhancements, including the launch of Spore Insights v2.0 and 
continued evolution of the SporeCam platform, further strengthened Scanit's technology 
offering. During the quarter, Scanit's growing industry recognition was reflected through 
invitations to present at leading agriculture and AI forums in the United States. Management 
remains focused on converting the expanding global pipeline into recurring revenues while 
scaling the pathogen surveillance network and data subscription business.  
The journey from innovation to scale is a gradual process. It has a long gestation cycle. But

validations, more repeat orders, more use cases and more partners.

Defending the Global Breadbasket: A New Milestone for SporeCam 
Scanit has reached a major breakthrough in our mission to protect global food security. Our 
SporeCam is now ready to identify Fusarium graminearum spores, one of the world’s most 
destructive wheat pathogens.This training of algorithm was done by India’s premier R&D lab, 
in Pune with the help of Scanit’s US R&D team. While this fungus is a natural threat, it is also 
a known risk in agro-terrorism due to its ability to rapidly decimate crops and contaminate 
grain. By integrating this detection capability, Scanit is providing a critical defensive layer for 
national biosecurity. We can now help partners distinguish between natural outbreaks and 
intentional threats, enabling immediate intervention before an infection takes hold. This 
achievement isn't just about better tech; it's about safeguarding the world's food supply and 
alerting on possible bio-terrorism attempt.
