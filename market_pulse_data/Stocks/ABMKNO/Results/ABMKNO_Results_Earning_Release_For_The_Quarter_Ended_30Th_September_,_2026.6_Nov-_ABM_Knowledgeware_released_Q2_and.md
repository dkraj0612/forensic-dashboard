 Analysis Of The Financial Results 
 
  
ABM Knowledgeware Limited (BSE: 531161), operates in three high tech verticals i.e. E-
governance, Cybersecurity and Agritech. ABM announced its results on November 6, 2026, for 
the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. 
Summary of Key Financial Parameters 
On a Consolidated basis across Digital Governance, Cybersecurity and Agritech, ABM reported 
operational revenue of ₹26 crores for Q2 FY 2026–27up by 21% from Q2 FY 2025. The Company 
posted consolidated EBITDA, PBT and PAT of ₹3.83 crores, ₹1.88 crores and ₹1.80 crores 
respectively for the quarter. 
ABM achieved a Gross Profit Margin of approximately 18% and an Operating Profit Margin of 
7%. Profitability continues to reflect the impact of strategic investments in AgriTech (Scanit), 
expansion and competitive pressures in Cybersecurity (InstaSafe), and margin constraints 
arising from the ongoing diversification and transition in the Digital Governance business. 
Segment Highlights 
Digital Governance 
Standalone revenue for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹25.21 crores, compared with ₹20.40 crores in the 
corresponding quarter of the previous year, registering year-on-year growth of 23%. During 
the quarter, the business maintained a Gross Profit Margin of 19% and a Net Profit Margin of 
11%.  
Cybersecurity 
InstaSafe Technologies Private Limited, ABM’s cloud-based cybersecurity subsidiary, reported 
revenue of ₹1.49 crores for Q2 FY 2026–27. EBITDA and PAT stood at ₹ (44.47) lakhs and 
₹(91.37) lakhs, respectively. The quarterly performance reflects the alternate-half yearly billing 
cycle of a major customer, with the next billing expected in Q3 FY 2026–27.  
For the half year ended September 2026, the Company achieved revenue of ₹7.02 crores, 
representing a growth of 6% over the corresponding period of the previous year. EBITDA 
improved by 41% to ₹3.38 crores, while PAT increased to ₹1.38 crores from ₹68.31 lakhs, 
registering a growth of 102%.

Agritech 
Scanit Technologies Inc. continued to strengthen the commercial foundation of its pathogen 
surveillance and disease intelligence platform during the quarter through expanded customer 
deployments, advancement of strategic partnerships, product innovation, and growing market 
validation across multiple geographies. 
During the quarter, Scanit made tangible progress in expanding adoption of its disease 
monitoring solutions. In the United States, the successful implementation of on-demand farmer 
enrolment for the Iowa SporeWarn Network demonstrated the scalability of its service delivery 
model, while ongoing discussions around data integration and revenue-sharing arrangements 
with established Agri-tech platforms could expand future commercialization opportunities. The 
advancement of multiple grant applications with leading research institutions and Universities 
further validates the interest of the scientific communities in Scanit's technology and the 
potential to support future product development and market penetration. 
In India, the company deepened its engagement with large agricultural ecosystems through 
deployments with strategic partners. Disease monitoring services are currently supporting 
approximately 325 grape growers across nearly 550 acres, while a tomato disease intelligence 
pilot has been deployed in Chhattisgarh. These initiatives provide important validation of the 
platform's ability to deliver actionable disease intelligence in diverse crop environments and 
create opportunities for wider subscription-based adoption through partner networks. 
Across international markets, Scanit continued to build its commercial pipeline through multiple 
proposals in Latin America and engagement with new enterprise customers. The initiation of 
discussions with Mars-Wrigley following successful vendor onboarding and continued progress 
with partners in South Africa reflect growing interest in the company's technology beyond its 
existing customer base. 
The company also enhanced its technology platform with the launch of Sentinel View, enabling 
customers to monitor multiple deployments through a unified interface. Continued 
improvements to the SporeCam platform, customer analytics capabilities, and internal software 
infrastructure are expected to improve product scalability, customer experience, and 
operational efficiency. 
Management believes the quarter's progress reinforces several key trends: increasing market 
acceptance of disease intelligence as a decision aiding tool, growing validation from commercial 
growers and strategic partners, expanding opportunities for recurring subscription revenues, 
and continued strengthening of Scanit's position as a differentiated technology platform in the 
emerging agricultural pathogen surveillance market.
