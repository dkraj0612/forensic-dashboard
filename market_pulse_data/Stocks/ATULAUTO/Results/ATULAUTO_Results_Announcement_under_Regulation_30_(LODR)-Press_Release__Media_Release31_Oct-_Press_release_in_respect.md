Press Release - Financial Results for the Quarter and Half Year Ended September 30, 2026 
 
Atul Auto reports 38% growth in Consolidated Revenue and 44% growth in Profit After Tax 
for H1 FY 2026-27 
 
Ahmedabad, October 31, 2026: Atul Auto Limited, one of India's established manufacturers 
of three-wheelers, announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year 
ended September 30, 2026, today. 
 
The Company delivered a strong performance at the consolidated level during the first half of 
FY 2026-27, recording healthy growth in revenue and profitability. The performance was 
supported by an increase in three-wheeler sales volumes and contributions from the Group's 
automobile and financial services businesses. 
 
Key Operational Highlights 
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company sold 11,916 three-wheelers, 
compared with 9,248 three-wheelers in the corresponding quarter of the previous year, 
registering a growth of approximately 28.8%. 
 
For the half year ended September 30, 2026, sales stood at 21,794 three-wheelers, as against 
16,177 three-wheelers in the corresponding period of the previous year, reflecting a growth 
of approximately 34.7%. 
Consolidated Financial Performance 
The Company's consolidated financial performance reflects a strong improvement during the 
first half of FY 2026-27.
