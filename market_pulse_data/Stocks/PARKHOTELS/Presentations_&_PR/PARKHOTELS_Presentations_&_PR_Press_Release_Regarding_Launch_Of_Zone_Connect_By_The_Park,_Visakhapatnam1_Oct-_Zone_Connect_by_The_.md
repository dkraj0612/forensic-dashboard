Zone Connect by The Park launches in Visakhapatnam,  
marking ASPHL’s second hotel in the city 
 
National, 01 October 2026: Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels Limited (ASPHL) has further 
strengthened its presence in Visakhapatnam with the launch of Zone Connect by The 
Park Visakhapatnam, its second hotel in the city. Strategically located on Beach Road, 
the new hotel brings Zone’s contemporary design, vibrant social spaces and warm, 
connected hospitality to one of Andhra Pradesh’s most dynamic coastal destinations. 
Positioned along Visakhapatnam’s iconic Beach Road, the hotel places guests close to 
the city’s key business, commercial and transport hubs: 
 Visakhapatnam Railway Station: 4.9 km 
 Visakhapatnam Port: 2.5 km 
 Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport: 48.6 km 
The 52-key hotel oƯers a contemporary stay experience designed for both business and 
leisure stays, alongside spaces designed for social occasions. The property brings 
together Café C, an all-day dining restaurant; an open-air coƯee shop overlooking the 
Bay of Bengal; and a terrace swimming pool oƯering panoramic sea views. Indoor 
banquet spaces accommodating up to 300 guests cater to weddings, celebrations, 
conferences and corporate gatherings, while Grey Zone oƯers a dedicated setting for 
intimate meetings and smaller corporate requirements. Bright, spacious and clutter-free 
interiors add to the relaxed, contemporary character of the hotel. 
Commenting on the launch, Mr Botcha Suresh Kumar, Managing Director and Mr Veda 
Vyas Botcha, Director, M/s Hema Abodes Private Limited, said, “We are delighted to 
partner with Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels Limited to bring Zone Connect by The Park to 
Visakhapatnam. We have great conﬁdence in ASPHL’s expertise and proven track record 
in hospitality, and believe that its brand strength will add signiﬁcant value to this 
partnership. The development will create local employment opportunities and contribute 
to the wider hospitality ecosystem.” 
Commenting on occasion, Mr Vikas Ahluwalia, AVP and National Head Zone by The 
Park, said, “The opening of Zone Connect by The Park Visakhapatnam marks an 
important milestone in our growth journey, further strengthening our presence and 
commitment to Andhra Pradesh as a high-potential market. Visakhapatnam’s strong mix 
of business, leisure and coastal appeal, makes it a natural ﬁt for Zone Connect. The 
hotel’s location and oƯering are well suited to the city’s evolving hospitality needs, and 
we believe it will add a distinctive new address to the destination.”
