Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that Hexco Global FZCO, 
Dubai, a subsidiary of Jinkushal Industries Limited ('the Company'), has incorporated a wholly 
owned subsidiary in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates under the name 'HEXCO GLOBAL 
MACHINES L.L.C. - S.P.C.' on 03rd August 2026. 
 
Consequently, HEXCO GLOBAL MACHINES L.L.C. - S.P.C. has become an indirect (step-
down) wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. 
 
The newly incorporated entity has been established to support and expand the Group's international 
business activities relating to trading of heavy equipment, machinery, machinery spare parts and 
allied activities in the Middle East region. 
 
The entity is presently in the initial incorporation and commercial establishment stage. It has not 
commenced commercial operations.
