Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’), we wish to 
inform you that Mr. Abhishek Gupta, Corporate Head – Information Technology, has tendered his

Management of the Company with effect from the said date. The resignation is on account of personal 
reasons/pursuit of opportunities outside the organization, and there is no other material reason for the 
same.
