Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to provide an update regarding the servicing of debt obligations of 
Jain Irrigation Systems Limited (“the Company™). 
We are pleased to inform that the Company has fully discharged all its debt repayment 
obligations falling due up to September 30, 2026, ahead of their respective scheduled due 
dates. 
During the period from July 2026 till date, the Company has made aggregate debt repayments of 
NCD’s / Term Debt of approximately ¥ 246.72 crore, including repayments towards Non- 
Convertible Debentures and other debt obligations falling due during this period. 
These repayments have been met through a combination of business cash flows, collections and 
available liquidity, while continuing to support the working capital requirements of the business. 
The timely and ahead-of-schedule servicing of these obligations reflects the Company’s continued 
focus on financial discipline, prudent liquidity management and honouring its financial 
commitments. 
The Company continues to focus on improving operating cash flows, collection of receivables and 
strengthening its overall liquidity position.
