PRESS RELEASE 
JAIN IRRIGATION SECURES ₹117.96 CRORE EMPANELMENT FROM 
MSEDCL 
FOR 5,000 SOLAR PUMPS 
Jalgaon, Maharashtra | September 17, 2026: Jain Irrigation Systems Limited has secured a Letter 
of Empanelment from Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited 
(MSEDCL) for the supply and installation of 5,000 Off-Grid DC Solar Photovoltaic Water 
Pumping Systems (SPWPS) across Maharashtra. 
Valued at ₹117.96 crore, inclusive of GST, the empanelment has been awarded under the Magel 
Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana, a Maharashtra Government initiative aimed at promoting solar-
powered irrigation solutions for farmers. 
Under the empanelment, Jain Irrigation will undertake the complete scope of work, including the 
design, manufacture, supply, transportation, installation, testing and commissioning of 
agricultural solar pumping systems of 3 HP, 5 HP and 7.5 HP capacities. The project is scheduled 
to be completed within 60 days. 
The latest empanelment further strengthens Jain Irrigation’s longstanding association with 
MSEDCL in Maharashtra’s solar irrigation sector. 
Jain Irrigation has been working with MSEDCL since 2015, beginning with the Atal Solar Pump 
Project, under which the Company completed the supply and installation of 5,039 solar pumps 
across Maharashtra. Subsequently, during 2019–20, the Company completed another 1,539 
pumps under the Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Pump Yojana. 
More recently, under the Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana, which commenced in 
December 2024, Jain Irrigation has successfully completed the supply and installation of 12,457 
pumps, with a total project value of ₹307.62 crore, according to the Company. 
With the addition of 5,000 more solar pumps under the latest empanelment, Jain Irrigation 
continues to expand its presence in Maharashtra’s solar-powered agricultural irrigation 
infrastructure. The Company’s cumulative execution across these projects reflects its experience in 
the supply, installation and commissioning of solar pumping systems for agricultural 
applications.
