Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Subject: - Amanta Healthcare Limited Announces Commissioning of New SteriPort Line  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time, 
we would like to inform your good office that Amanta Healthcare Limited, a niche 
pharmaceutical company specialising in sterile liquids and integrated LVP and SVP 
manufacturing is happy to announce commencement of production at its new SteriPort facility 
situated at Kheda campus.  
SteriPort has unique features like absolute sterility, superior transparency and far more tensile 
strength. This enhances the container integrity by manifold. It’s two-port system ensures leak-
proof IV administration and medication. SteriPort also demonstrates superior compatibility 
with potent drugs especially in Onco and Intensive Care. The new facility incorporates 
technological advancements and higher degree of automation primarily aimed at reduced 
human interference, enhanced quality assurance and patient safety. 
The brownfield facility is expected to scale up the operational and financial efficiency, resulting 
into EBITDA margin expansion.  
This is for your information and records.
