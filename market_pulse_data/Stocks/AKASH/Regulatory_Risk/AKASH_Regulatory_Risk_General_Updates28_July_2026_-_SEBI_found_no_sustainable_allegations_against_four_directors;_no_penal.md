### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Akash Infra-Projects Limited announced that the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has dismissed allegations against four of its directors regarding violations of fraudulent and unfair trade practices due to a lack of evidence, with no penalties imposed. This favorable ruling resolves a significant regulatory inquiry that began with a Show Cause Notice, thereby preventing potential material financial or reputational impacts on the company and its leadership. The initial allegations of SEBI Act and PFUTP Regulation violations, while serious, are now deemed unsustainable.
