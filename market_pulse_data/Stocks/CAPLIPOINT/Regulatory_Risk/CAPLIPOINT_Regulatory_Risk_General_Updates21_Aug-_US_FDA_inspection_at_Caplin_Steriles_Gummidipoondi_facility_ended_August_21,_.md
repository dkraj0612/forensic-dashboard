Sub: Completion of Unannounced US FDA Inspection at the facility of Caplin Steriles Limited (Company’s 
Subsidiary) 
  
This is to inform you that the United States Food and Drug Administration (US FDA) conducted an   
unannounced inspection of Caplin Steriles’ injectable and ophthalmic manufacturing facility located at 
Gummidipoondi.  The inspection was conducted between August 13, 2026, and August 21, 2026. At the 
end of the inspection, the Company received Form 483 with 10 (ten) observations. The observations are 
procedural in nature and does not pertain to data integrity and none of them were repeat observations.  
 
The Company is addressing the observations comprehensively and will respond to the US FDA within the 
stipulated timeframe.
