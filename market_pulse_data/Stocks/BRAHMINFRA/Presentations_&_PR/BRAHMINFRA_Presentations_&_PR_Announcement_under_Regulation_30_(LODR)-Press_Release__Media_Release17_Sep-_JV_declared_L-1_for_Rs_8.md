PRESS RELEASE 
New Delhi, September 17, 2026 
Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (BSE: 535693) Through its JV M/s BIL 
– GKCPL (JV)  Declared L-1 for Rs. 89.73 Crore High Level Bridge over 
River Ravi in Punjab 
44-span, 1,606-metre High Level (HL) major river bridge with 4,144 metres 
of approaches at Makoura Pattan, Districts Gurdaspur and Pathankot; 
funded under the Central Road and Infrastructure Fund (CRIF); 24-month 
execution period; Letter of Award awaited 
Key Highlights 
• Announcement: Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (BIL) through its 
M/s BIL – GKCPL (JV)  declared L-1 (lowest bidder). This is an L-1 
declaration; the contract has not yet been awarded. 
• Total bid value: Rs. 89.73 crore (INR 897.3 million). 
• Scope: High Level (HL) major bridge over the River Ravi – 44 spans of 
36.50 metres each (bridge length 1,606 metres) plus 4,144 metres of 
approaches, about 5.75 km in all. 
• Client: Executive Engineer, Construction Division, PWD (B&R) 
Branch, Pathankot, Government of Punjab. 
• Funding: Central Road and Infrastructure Fund (CRIF). 
• Execution period: 24 months from receipt of the Letter of Intent (LOI) 
/ Letter of Award (LOA). 
• Status: L-1 declared; LOI / LOA awaited. 
New Delhi, September 17, 2026: Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited 
(“BIL” or the “Company”) (BSE Scrip Code: 535693), an infrastructure EPC 
company executing road and bridge projects, today announced that it has 
been declared the lowest bidder (L-1) through its Joint Venture M/s BIL – 
GKCPL (JV)   for the construction of a High Level (HL) major bridge over 
the River Ravi at Makoura Pattan, Punjab. The tender was floated by the 
Executive Engineer, Construction Division, PWD (B&R) Branch, Pathankot, 
Government of Punjab. The JV’s L-1 bid is valued at Rs. 89.73 crore.

A Technically Demanding River Bridge 
The project is a High Level (HL) bridge: its superstructure is kept above the 
highest flood level with the required clearance, so that traffic can move in 
all seasons, including the monsoon. Classified as a major bridge, it 
comprises 44 spans of 36.50 metres each, giving a total bridge length of 1,606 
metres, along with 4,144 metres of approach roads linking the structure to 
the road network in Districts Gurdaspur and Pathankot. 
Building a bridge of this length across the Ravi, a perennial Himalayan river, 
calls for specialised well foundation and pier works across the river bed and 
flood plain, repetitive construction of the superstructure over 44 spans, and 
careful scheduling of site activity around monsoon flows.  
Site Context and Connectivity Impact 
Makoura Pattan (also spelt Makora Pattan or Makkoran Pattan) lies on a 
flood-prone reach of the Ravi near its confluence with the Ujh river, in the 
border belt of Gurdaspur district. Villages on the far bank are currently 
linked by a pontoon bridge that is dismantled every monsoon. The High 
Level bridge is intended to provide a permanent, all-weather crossing at this 
location. 
Significance for the Company 
Upon receipt of the LOI / LOA, the project will add to the Company’s order 
book. The project adds a technically complex major river bridge to BIL’s 
portfolio of road and bridge works for government authorities, and the joint 
venture structure allows the Company to bid for and execute a larger, 
specialised project alongside its partner. The project also further enhances 
BIL's focus on flood-resilient infrastructure, extending its flood protection 
and river works into permanent, all-weather crossings over flood-prone 
rivers. 
This press release accompanies the Company’s intimation under Regulation 
30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015. Further updates, including receipt of the LOI / LOA, will 
be communicated to BSE Limited in due course.
