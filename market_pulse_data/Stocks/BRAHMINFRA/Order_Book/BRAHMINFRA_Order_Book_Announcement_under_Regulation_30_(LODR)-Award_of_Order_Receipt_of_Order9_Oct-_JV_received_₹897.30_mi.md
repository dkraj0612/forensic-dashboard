PRESS RELEASE 
New Delhi, October 9, 2026 
Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited, through M/s BIL – GKCPL (JV) 
Receives Letter of Acceptance for Rs. 897.30 Million Bridge Project in 
Punjab 
Order for construction of a 1,606-metre, 44-span high-level major bridge 
over the River Ravi at Makoura Pattan and its approaches under the CRIF 
Scheme, to be executed within 24 months 
New Delhi, October 9, 2026: Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (“BIL” or 
the “Company”) (BSE Scrip Code: 535693), a leading infrastructure 
construction company, today announced that its Joint Venture, M/s 
Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited – Ganesh Kartikey Pvt. Ltd. (JV), has 
received a Letter of Acceptance from the Chief Engineer (Central), Punjab 
PWD (B&R), Patiala, Government of Punjab, vide Memo No. 1417/CRIF 
dated October 8, 2026, for a major bridge construction project valued at Rs. 
897.30 Million. 
 
About the Project 
The project involves the construction of a 44-span high-level major bridge, 
with each span measuring 36.50 metres, over the River Ravi at Makoura 
Pattan, along with its approach roads. The bridge will span a total length of 
1,606 metres, while the approaches extend a further 4,144 metres, connecting 
communities across District Gurdaspur and District Pathankot in the state 
of Punjab. The project is being undertaken under the Central Road and 
Infrastructure Fund (CRIF) Scheme, underscoring its strategic importance 
to regional road and bridge connectivity. 
In terms of the Letter of Acceptance, the project is to be completed within a 
period of 24 (twenty-four) months and will be executed in accordance with 
the approved DNIT / Bidding Document, MoRT&H specifications and 
CRIF Guidelines.
