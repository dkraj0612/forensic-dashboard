PRESS RELEASE 
New Delhi, September 17, 2026 
Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited in the name of M/s BIL – GKCPL (JV)  
Emerges as Lowest Bidder (L-1) for Rs. 89.73 Crore Bridge Project in 
Punjab 
Company declared sole L-1 bidder for construction of a 1,606-metre, 44-span 
major bridge over the River Ravi at Makoura Pattan under the Centrally 
Sponsored CRIF Scheme; formal Letter of Award awaited 
New Delhi, September 17, 2026: Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (“BIL” 
or the “Company”) (BSE Scrip Code: 535693), a leading infrastructure 
construction company, today announced that it has been declared L-1 
(Lowest Bidder) in the name of M/s BIL – GKCPL (JV)  for a major bridge 
construction tender floated by the Executive Engineer, Construction 
Division, PWD B&R Branch, Pathankot, Government of Punjab. The tender, 
valued at Rs. 89.73 crores, was won by the Company in the name of M/s BIL 
– GKCPL (JV) . 
 
About the Project 
The project involves the construction of a 44-span high-level major bridge, 
with each span measuring 36.50 metres, over the River Ravi at Makoura 
Pattan, along with its approach roads. The bridge itself will span a total 
length of 1,606 metres, while the approaches extend a further 4,144 metres, 
connecting communities across District Gurdaspur and District Pathankot 
in the state of Punjab. The project is being undertaken under the Centrally 
sponsored Road Infrastructure Fund (CRIF) Scheme, underscoring its 
strategic importance to regional road and bridge connectivity. 
Upon receipt of the formal Letter of Intent (LOI) / Letter of Award (LOA) 
from the awarding authority, the Company will be required to complete the 
project within a period of 24 (twenty-four) months from the date of such 
receipt.
