Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (BSE: 535693) ("BIL" or "the Company") has received a Letter of 
Award (LoA) dated 18 November 2026 from National Highways Logistics Management Limited (NHLML). 
The award is for developing, operating and maintaining wayside amenities at Site AS/L/01, at km 
273+400 (RHS) of NH-27 on the Guwahati–Nagaon section, at Borghat, District Nagaon, Assam. 
NHLML is a wholly owned special purpose vehicle of the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), 
under the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways. The site, spread over approximately 17 acres, The 
Company was declared the Highest Bidder under Clause7.2 of the RFP, at an annual lease rent of Rs. 
3,11,86,000 (Rs. 3.12 Crores), following the opening of financial proposals on 12 November 2026.

## Page 5

Scope of work 
• 
Develop the wayside amenities facility at the Borghat site on NH-27. 
• 
Operate and maintain the facility for highway users over the 30-year lease period. 
• 
Indicative facilities: fuel station, food court / restaurant, clean toilets, parking for cars and 
trucks, driver rest area, convenience store. 
Development timeline 
Construction is planned to commence within three months of the date of this release. 
The project will be developed in phases, with Phase 1 targeted to become operational within 10 months 
of the date of this release (by September 2027). 
 
Why this award matters to BIL 
 
• 
Operating income model: unlike an EPC contract, the site is leased. The Company invests in 
development and earns from operating it over a 30-year lease. 
• 
Home region: the site lies on the Guwahati–Nagaon corridor of NH-27, close to the Company's 
operating base in the North Eastern region. 
• 
Platform for more sites: NHLML is developing wayside amenities across the national highway 
network. 
 
 
Management comment 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Umang Prithani, Joint Managing Director, Brahmaputra 
Infrastructure Limited, said: 
"The Letter of Award from NHLML for the Borghat site extends our Real Estate Division into highway 
wayside amenities, building on our experience of developing and operating City Centre in Guwahati. It is 
a segment where we develop and operate an asset rather than only build it. The site sits on a busy 
stretch of NH-27 in Assam, our home region. We plan to commence construction within the next three 
months and develop the project in phases, with the first phase targeted to become operational within 
10 months of the date of this release. Our focus will be on developing a facility that serves highway 
users well."
