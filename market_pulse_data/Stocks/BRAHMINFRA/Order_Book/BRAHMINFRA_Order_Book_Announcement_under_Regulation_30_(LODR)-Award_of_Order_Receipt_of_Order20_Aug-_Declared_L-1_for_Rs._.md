PRESS RELEASE 
Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited declared L-1 for Rs. 78.09 Crore 
tunnelling and access-works package at the 1,000 MW Turga Pumped

Marks the Company’s entry into the pumped storage hydro-power segment and into 
West Bengal 
New Delhi / Guwahati, 20 August 2026 
Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (BSE: 535693) (NSE : BRAHMINFRA) 
("BIL" or "the Company") has been declared L-1 (Lowest Bidder) for the 
construction of the project road, ancillary structures and the Main Access 
Tunnel (MAT) including the Power House Adit for the 4 x 250 MW Turga 
Pumped Storage Project at Baghmundi, District Purulia, West Bengal. 
The tender has been invited by the Pumped Storage Project Department 
(PSPD) of West Bengal State Electricity Distribution Company Limited 
(WBSEDCL). The Company’s quoted value is Rs. 78.09 Crores, with an 
execution period of 18 months. The formal Letter of Intent / Letter of Award 
from the Authority is awaited.

Scope of work 
• Construction of the project road and associated ancillary structures. 
• Construction of the Main Access Tunnel (MAT) — the primary 
construction access route for the project’s underground works.

• Construction of the Power House Adit, providing access to the

Why this package matters to BIL 
• Underground works credentials: the MAT and the Power House Adit 
are hydro-electric tunnelling works. Execution of this package would 
add tunnelling experience of the kind that forms a standing pre-
qualification requirement in hydro-power, pumped storage, metro and 
highway tunnel tenders. 
• New segment: this would be the Company’s first package in the 
pumped storage hydro-power segment. 
• New client and new geography: the Company’s first engagement with 
WBSEDCL, a State power utility, and its first project in West Bengal, 
extending its footprint beyond its established base in the North Eastern 
region. 
• Early-cycle positioning: access works and the MAT are enabling 
packages on the critical path to the underground power house, placing 
the contractor on site at an early stage of the project cycle. 
Management comment 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Umang Prithani, Joint Managing 
Director, Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited, said: 
“Being declared L-1 on the Turga access and tunnelling package is a 
meaningful step for us. The Main Access Tunnel and the Power House Adit 
take us into underground works for a pumped storage scheme — a segment 
where the qualification bar is high and where credentials are built one 
package at a time. It also takes us into West Bengal and into a first working 
relationship with WBSEDCL. We now await the formal award, and our 
focus will be on mobilising early and executing this package to programme.” 
 
About the Turga Pumped Storage Project 
The Turga Pumped Storage Project is a 1,000 MW closed-loop pumped 
storage scheme being developed by WBSEDCL at the Ayodhya Hills in 
Baghmundi block, District Purulia, West Bengal. It comprises an upper and
