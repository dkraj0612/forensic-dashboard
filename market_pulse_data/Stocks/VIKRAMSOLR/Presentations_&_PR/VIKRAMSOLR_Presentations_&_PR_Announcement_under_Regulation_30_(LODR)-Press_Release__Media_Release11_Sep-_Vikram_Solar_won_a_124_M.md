Vikram Solar Secures 124 MW Module Supply Order for a Solar Project in 
Andhra Pradesh 
Kolkata, September — Vikram Solar, one of India’s leading solar PV module manufacturers, has 
secured a 124 MW module supply order for a solar project in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh, reinforcing 
its growing presence in India’s utility-scale and large-scale solar ecosystem. 
Under the agreement, Vikram Solar will supply its high-efficiency G12R TOPCon modules, with rated 
capacities ranging from 620 Wp to 630 Wp. Module supplies are scheduled to commence in October 
2026. 
This order underscores Vikram Solar's strengthening position to serve this growing market, building 
on its recent scale-up to 15.5 GW of module manufacturing capacity. With an expanding 
manufacturing footprint and an evolving technology portfolio, the company is strengthening its ability 
to serve the requirements of utility-scale projects while supporting India's broader transition towards 
greater domestic manufacturing and energy self-reliance. 
Mr. Sameer Nagpal, Chief Executive Officer, Vikram Solar, said, "Every module supply order we sign 
is a vote of confidence - not just in our capacity, but in the technology and consistency we bring to the 
table. Solar developers today are looking for partners who can match the pace of India's energy 
ambitions with equally advanced solutions. Our G12R TOPCon platform represents that commitment 
to staying ahead of the curve, and this partnership reaffirms the confidence the industry continues to 
place in Vikram Solar as India builds out its utility-scale solar infrastructure. We remain focused on 
deepening these partnerships as the country's clean energy story continues to unfold.” 
About Vikram Solar Limited: 
Vikram Solar Limited is one of the leading Indian solar module manufacturers, specializing in efficient 
photovoltaic (PV) module manufacturing, with an international presence across 39 countries. Headquartered in 
Kolkata, West Bengal, it is one of the largest PV module manufacturers in India. Vikram Solar is a 9th time ‘Top 
Performer’ in PVEL’s PV Module Reliability scorecard and has been included in the Tier 1 solar PV modules 
manufacturer list of Bloomberg NEF for 9 consecutive quarters. Vikram Solar Limited has established a pan-India 
presence through an extensive distributor network of 119 authorized distributors and more than 750+ dealers.
