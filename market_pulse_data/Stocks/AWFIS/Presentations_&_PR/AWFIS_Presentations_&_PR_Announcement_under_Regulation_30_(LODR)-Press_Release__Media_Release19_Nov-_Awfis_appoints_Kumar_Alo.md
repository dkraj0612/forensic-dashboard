Awfis strengthens leadership team with Kumar Alok Gupta as Chief 
Business Officer 
 
National, November 19th, 2026: Awfis Space Solutions Ltd., India’s largest flexible workspace 
solutions platform, today announced the appointment of Kumar Alok Gupta as Chief Business 
Officer. In this role, Alok will lead strategic demand and supply initiatives across Awfis’ 
Managed Office and Coworking verticals, driving revenue expansion, institutional landlord 
partnerships, and enterprise client growth across India. 
 
A commercial real estate leader with over 24 years of experience, Alok brings a proven track 
record of building and scaling institutional platforms. His core expertise spans portfolio 
strategy, high-value leasing transactions, and risk management for global funds, institutional 
investors, and large developers. 
 
Before joining Awfis, Alok served as Managing Director at Cushman & Wakefield, where he led 
the national agency leasing business with profit and loss (P&L) responsibility and a strong 
focus on large landlord mandates. Earlier in his career, he held the position of Associate 
Executive Director at CBRE India. 
 
Commenting on the appointment, Amit Ramani, Chairman & MD, Awfis Space Solutions 
Limited, said, “We are delighted to welcome Alok to the Awfis leadership team. As demand 
for flexible and managed workspaces continues to surge among Global Capability Centres 
(GCCs) and large enterprises, Alok’s deep institutional relationships and over two decades 
of commercial real estate leadership will be pivotal. His strategic vision across supply, 
demand, and premiumization will further strengthen our market leadership and accelerate 
our next phase of growth.” 
 
Speaking on his new role, Kumar Alok Gupta, Chief Business Officer, Awfis Space Solutions 
Limited, said, “Awfis has redefined the flexible workspace landscape in India. Its scale, asset-
light model, and enterprise-first focus have set the industry standard. Flexible workspaces 
are now central to corporate real estate strategy, and I am delighted to join Awfis at this 
point. I look forward to draw upon my experience in institutional leasing and landlord 
partnerships to build the next phase of growth by driving strategic supply expansion and 
tailored workspace solutions for enterprises.” 
 
As the company enters its next phase of growth, this appointment further strengthens its 
leadership team. Alok’s deep understanding of the commercial real estate ecosystem and his 
strong institutional relationships will be instrumental in accelerating the company’s 
momentum and consolidating its market leadership. The move reflects the company’s 
continued focus on building a robust, future-ready organisation and expanding its footprint 
across key markets.

About Awfis Space Solutions Limited  
Awfis Space Solutions Ltd. (‘Awfis’) is India’s largest and first listed flexible workspace solutions provider 
of modern workspaces. The company enables small and large corporates to seamlessly book and utilise 
workspaces as per their requirement and convenience. The comprehensive suite of solutions includes 
Flex Space Solutions (Coworking and Enterprise Solutions), Transform (Design & Build), and Allied 
Services, enhancing customer service. With a strong presence in 18 cities and 250+ centres, Awfis serves 
3700+ clients across diverse industries, ensuring scalable and adaptable workspace solutions for 
businesses of all sizes. The figures include operational and fit-out centres and seats. For more 
information, please log on to www.awfis.com. In addition, connect with us on Facebook, Instagram, 
YouTube, and LinkedIn.
