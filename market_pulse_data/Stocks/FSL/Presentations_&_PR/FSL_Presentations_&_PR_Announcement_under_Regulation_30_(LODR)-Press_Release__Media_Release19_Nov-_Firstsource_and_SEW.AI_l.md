Firstsource and SEW.AI Partner to Launch Smart Revenue 
Operations and Smart Revenue Collections for Energy and Water 
Utilities 
 
Jointly developed offerings combine Firstsource’s digital collections platform and meter-
to-cash expertise with SEW.AI’s Vertical AI 
 
Las Vegas, United States | November 19, 2026: Firstsource Solutions Limited (NSE: FSL, BSE: 532809), a global 
intelligence partner and an RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group company, and SEW.AI have announced a strategic partnership at 
the WE3 Global AI Summit 2026, introducing two jointly developed offerings for energy and water utilities: Smart 
Revenue Operations (Smart RO) and Smart Revenue Collections (Smart RC). Together, they bring AI-driven intelligence 
and accountable delivery to billing and receivables, from the first meter read to the last payment. 
 
The offerings combine Firstsource’s proprietary digital collections platform, its meter-to-cash domain intelligence and its 
track record running revenue, collections, and payment-assistance operations for utilities with SEW.AI’s Vertical AI 
platforms for energy and utilities. The result is a single, outcome-focused solution: technology, domain knowledge and 
operations designed together from day one, not assembled after the fact. 
 
Smart RO: Unlocking the revenue cycle 
Smart RO pairs SEW.AI’s Vertical AI with Firstsource’s billing exception management expertise. Billing teams get end-
to-end visibility into exceptions, can prioritize the work that matters most, and can scale billing with demand while their 
people focus on higher-value work. 
 
Smart RC: Meeting customers where they respond 
Smart RC is powered by Firstsource’s digital collections platform and enhanced with SEW.AI’s Vertical AI. It is a digital-
first, omnichannel approach that reaches customers on their preferred channels, with empathy and respect, and stays 
aligned with utility collections regulations. 
 
The partnership will enable energy and utilities to: 
• 
Lower cost to serve by freeing teams to focus on complex issues and customer needs. 
• 
Proactively identify exceptions that impact the timeliness and accuracy of customer bills. 
• 
Increase cash collections at scale while optimizing collection spend. 
• 
Support customers facing affordability challenges with flexible payment options and accessible help. 
 
Ritesh Idnani, CEO, Firstsource, said “Utilities need more than technology. They need intelligence that understands 
their business and takes responsibility for results. With Smart RO and Smart RC, we’re bringing our own digital 
collections platform and decades of meter-to-cash experience together with SEW.AI’s Vertical AI. That’s what 
Intelligence That Operates means to us: AI built into everyday work and accountable for how it performs. For energy 
and water utilities, that means more accurate billing, healthier revenue, and more ways to support customers, especially 
those facing affordability challenges.” 
  
Deepak Garg, Founder, Chairman, and Group CEO, SEW.AI shared, "Affordability and operational excellence are 
closely connected for energy and utilities. Stronger collections and better customer experiences help utilities improve 
financial performance while supporting the people they serve. Our strategic partnership with Firstsource brings together 
Vertical AI platforms with utility domain expertise and operational delivery experience to make progress on these 
priorities. This is People + Vertical AI in practice, helping energy and utilities operate more efficiently and giving 
customers better ways to manage their bills."
