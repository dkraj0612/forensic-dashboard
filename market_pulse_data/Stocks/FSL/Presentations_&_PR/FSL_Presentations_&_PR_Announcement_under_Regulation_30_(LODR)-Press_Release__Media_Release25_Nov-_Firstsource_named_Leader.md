Firstsource named a Leader in BFS Agentic Business Operations 
and a Major Contender and Star Performer in FCC Operations 
Services by Everest Group 
 
Mumbai, India | November 25, 2026: Firstsource Solutions Limited (NSE: FSL, BSE: 532809), a global intelligence 
partner and an RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group company, has been named a Leader in Everest Group's BFS Agentic 
Business Operations PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026 and a Major Contender and Star Performer in its Financial 
Crime and Compliance (FCC) Operations Services PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026.  
 
Financial institutions are moving beyond rules-based automation and AI-assisted workflows towards agentic operations, 
where human teams, intelligent platforms and AI agents work together to execute, orchestrate and optimize recurring 
business processes. Everest Group estimates the BFS agentic business operations market at US$500–800 million in 
CY2025, with the market having grown two to three times as pilots move into production. Growth is strongest across 
lending, KYC/AML, fraud, disputes, servicing and collections. In FCC, institutions are looking to improve productivity 
while maintaining explainability, governance and human accountability 
 
Vivek Sharma, Head, CMT, BFS and Emerging Geos, Firstsource, said, “Most banks have run AI pilots. The real 
test is getting agents into live, regulated operations without losing control. That’s what this recognition is about. In 
mortgage, AML and collections, our agents now do the heavy lifting. They assemble the evidence, flag the exceptions 
and hand over decision-ready cases. Our people make the judgement calls and own the outcomes. That balance is how 
agentic AI earns trust in banking, and it’s why clients are moving it from pilot to production with us.” 
 
Dheeraj Maken, Vice President, Everest Group, said, “As financial institutions seek to improve productivity and reduce 
reliance on labor-intensive processes, they are looking for providers that can pair operational expertise with AI-enabled 
transformation across banking operations, including Financial Crime and Compliance (FCC). Firstsource is responding 
through its UnBPO™ strategy and Kairos operating framework, integrating workflow agents into day-to-day execution 
alongside human teams across areas such as mortgage underwriting preparation, quality checks, AML investigations, 
and collections. In FCC, it has broadened its service capabilities and invested in agentic AI and specialist technology 
partnerships. Clients recognize Firstsource’s operational excellence, process maturity, scalability, and ability to deliver 
cost efficiencies while maintaining service quality. These capabilities contributed to its recognition as a Leader in Everest 
Group’s BFS Agentic Business Operations PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026, and as a Major Contender and Star 
Performer in the FCC Operations Services PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026.” 
 
Rajiv Malhotra, Head, EMEA, Firstsource, said, “UK banks can’t pause operations to transform them. Change has to 
land inside live workflows, alongside legacy systems, existing controls and the regulator’s expectations. We don’t hand 
over a platform and walk away. We run the work with our clients, redesign it in flight and put our commercials behind 
the results. Everest Group’s recognition reflects that model: transformation that keeps service steady while it changes 
how the work gets done.” 
 
Everest Group's BFS assessment highlights Firstsource’s approach to converting selected banking workflows into 
exception-led, outcome-oriented managed operations. This approach is rooted in UnBPO™, Firstsource’s thinking for 
reimagining business process operations are designed and run, and is put into practice through Kairos, its operating 
system for AI-native operations. Kairos' brings process context, exceptions and decision paths into workflows, helping 
agents prepare decision-ready work packets and route cases requiring human judgement to the right teams.  
 
The assessment recognizes Firstsource’s production evidence across consumer lending, mortgage, customer 
experience and servicing, collections, AML and card decline workflows. It also highlights the company's outcome-linked 
commercial constructs, multi-vendor technology orchestration and operational change management in regulated BFS 
environments. 
 
Through Kairos, Firstsource is bringing its Intelligence That Operates approach to FCC Operations, applying domain 
context and agentic AI across KYC/KYB, screening, transaction monitoring, AML screening, ongoing due diligence, and 
policy compliance. This combines AI-led execution with human oversight for cases requiring investigation and 
judgement. Everest Group also recognizes Fraud 360's capabilities across scams, investigations, disputes, 
chargebacks, recoveries and application fraud, alongside Firstsource's FCC delivery footprint in India, the UK, the US 
and Mexico.
