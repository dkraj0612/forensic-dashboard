> Aarti Drugs Limited's subsidiary, Pinnacle Life Science, received three US FDA Form 483 observations following an inspection of its manufacturing facility. While Form 483s indicate potential compliance issues, the absence of data integrity concerns and the company's "no impact" statement mitigate immediate red flags.

inspected the Formulations manufacturing facility of our Subsidiary, Pinnacle Life Science 
Private Limited at Baddi, Himachal Pradesh from 2nd to 6th of November 2026.  
On conclusion of the inspection, the company has received 3 inspectional observations in 
Form 483, where none of the observations are related to Data Integrity. The company will 
work closely with US FDA in resolving all points. 
There is no impact on financial, operation or other activities of the Company pursuant the 
observations received.
