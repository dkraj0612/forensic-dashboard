Pursuant to the outcome of the Board meeting dated July 31, 2026 and approval of the 
shareholders in 41st Annual General Meeting dated September 26, 2026, kindly take the 
note of following changes in the Directorate and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company: 
A) Cessation of Shri Prakash M. Patil from the positions of Chairman, Managing 
Director and Chief Executive Officer 
With reference to the earlier intimation bearing reference no. ADL/SE/2026-27/28 dated 
July 31, 2026, regarding the decision of Shri Prakash M. Patil to take early retirement 
and resign from the positions of Chairman, Managing Director and Chief Executive 
Officer of the Company, we hereby inform that he has ceased to hold the aforesaid 
positions with effect from the close of business hours on September 30, 2026. 
A copy of the letter received from Shri Prakash M. Patil communicating his decision in 
this regard is enclosed herewith as Annexure-II. 
B) Relinquishment of office of Chief Financial Officer by Shri Adhish P. Patil 
Pursuant to the approval of the Members of the Company at the 41st Annual General 
Meeting held on September 26, 2026, Shri Adhish P. Patil has been promoted to the 
position of Managing Director of the Company with effect from October 1, 2026. 
Consequently, Shri Adhish P. Patil has relinquished the position of Chief Financial 
Officer of the Company with effect from the close of business hours on September 30, 
2026. 
A copy of the letter received from Shri Adhish P. Patil communicating his decision in this 
regard is enclosed herewith as Annexure-III. 
The Company will appoint a Chief Financial Officer in due course, in compliance with 
Regulation 26A(2) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015.
