Dear Sir/Ma’am, 
In compliance with Regulation 30 read with Para B of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with the SEBI Master Circular dated January 30, 2026, we 
hereby notify that, in line with the objects of the Initial Public Offer of the Company, as mentioned in the 
Prospectus dated February 25, 2026, the Company has commenced operations at the Company’s new IVF 
Centre/Hub at Gurgaon, Haryana with effect from September 24, 2026. 
The Centre/Hub is designed to provide comprehensive fertility and reproductive healthcare services under one 
roof, supported by experienced fertility specialists, advanced reproductive medicine, modern medical 
infrastructure and standardised clinical protocols. The Centre features Gaudium IVE’s proprietary Gaudium 
Signature Lab, which integrates advanced embryology, Al-powered technologies and internationally 
benchmarked quality standards to provide an optimal environment for embryo development and support high 
standards of precision, safety and clinical excellence.
