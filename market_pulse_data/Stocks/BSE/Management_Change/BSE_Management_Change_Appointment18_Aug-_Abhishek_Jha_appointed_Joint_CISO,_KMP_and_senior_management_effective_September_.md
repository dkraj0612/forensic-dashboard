Brief Profile 
Shri Abhishek Jha is a cybersecurity leader with 23+ years of experience across 
financial services, engineering and defence, with expertise in cybersecurity 
strategy and governance, enterprise resilience, regulatory compliance and 
crisis management. He currently serves as Global Chief Information Security 
Officer (CISO) at Tata Technologies Limited and has previously served as BISO 
for Citi India Retail and CISO for Citi Global Markets at Citibank. Earlier, he 
served with the Indian Air Force. 
 
He holds a Gold Medal in Master of Computer Applications (MCA), a Post 
Graduate Diploma in Cyber Law and Cyber Forensics, and an MBA in FinTech. 
He is also a frequent speaker on cybersecurity, cyber resilience and emerging 
technology risks at industry and professional forums.
