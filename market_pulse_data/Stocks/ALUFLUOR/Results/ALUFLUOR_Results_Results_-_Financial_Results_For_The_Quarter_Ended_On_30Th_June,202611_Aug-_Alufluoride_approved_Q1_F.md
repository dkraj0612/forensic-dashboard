> **Executive Summary: Alufluoride Limited Q1 FY27 Financials**  Alufluoride Limited reported a significant decline in Q1 FY27 standalone revenue (down ~40% YoY) and net profit (down ~10% YoY), primarily due to disruptions in critical raw material (Silicic Acid) supply caused by geopolitical tensions between the USA and Iran. While statutory auditors issued an unmodified limited review, management anticipates improved supplies and financial performance in subsequent quarters following a completed project expansion.
