Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 – Notifications under Jute and Jute Textiles Control Order, 2016 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) and SEBI 
Master Circular dated 30th January, 2026, please be informed that the Office of the Jute Commissioner, in 
exercise of the powers conferred by clause 3(6) of the Jute and Jute Textiles Control Order, 2016, has 
notified revision in the maximum ex-factory prices of 580 gms B-Twill Sacking Type-A & Type–B Jute bags 
supplied by the Jute Mills to the State Procurement Agencies for the period specified in the notifications 
issued from time to time with the latest gazette notifications published on 18 August 2026 and 19th August 
2026 on the official website of the Office of the Jute Commissioner.  
 
Except for the uniform revision in the maximum ex-factory prices of the Jute bags supplied by Jute Mills to the 
State Procurement Agencies for the period specified in the notifications, all other terms and conditions of the 
orders / contracts remain unchanged. 
 
The value or the expected impact in terms of value on sales realisation arising from such price revision in 
respect of supplies made by the Company for the period stated in the notifications is expected to exceed the 
threshold specified in Regulation 30(4)(i)(c) of the Listing Regulations, necessitating disclosure under 
Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations as provided in  Annexure A attached herewith. 
 
Kindly take the above on record. 
 
Thanking you

For Cheviot Company Limited
