### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Arvind Limited reported robust Q1 FY27 results, with significant revenue (+25%) and PAT (+47%) growth, primarily driven by strong demand across its core textile and advanced materials businesses, complemented by the strategic acquisition and consolidation of Dalco-GFT. While EBITDA margins expanded by 100 bps, profitability faced headwinds from increased raw material costs and supply chain disruptions. Material impacts include higher working capital due to raw material procurement and a notable increase in overall debt attributed to the Dalco-GFT acquisition.
