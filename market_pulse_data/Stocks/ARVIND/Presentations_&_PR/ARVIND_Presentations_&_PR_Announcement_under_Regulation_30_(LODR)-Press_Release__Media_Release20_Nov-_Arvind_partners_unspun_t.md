Press Release  
Arvind Limited Becomes First Manufacturer to Deploy 
unspun's OneWeave™ 3D Weaving Technology 
Two industrial 3D weaving machines are expected to be operational at Arvind's 
Ahmedabad facility by December 2026, with a path to 30+ additional machines across India 
and the U.S. — a step toward more responsive, automated, and lower-waste apparel 
production at industrial scale. 
AHMEDABAD, India & SAN FRANCISCO, November 20, 2026, unspun, the B Corp-certified 
fashion technology company behind OneWeave™ 3D weaving, and Arvind Limited, one of India’s 
leading textile-to-retail conglomerates, today announced a strategic partnership to bring yarn-to-
garment 3D weaving into industrial-scale apparel manufacturing. 
The deployment marks Arvind Limited's first installation of OneWeave™ technology and the first 
time unspun's 3D weaving platform will move from its own microfactory into the production 
environment of a global Tier-1 manufacturer — an important step in the company's broader vision 
for more responsive, automated and lower-waste apparel supply chains. 
The fashion industry's operating model still depends heavily on forecasting demand months in 
advance, committing to bulk orders, and correcting for inevitable misses through markdowns, 
deadstock and disposal. McKinsey has reported that U.S. retailers alone were sitting on 
approximately $740 billion in unsold goods in 2023. As of July 19, 2026, new EU rules prohibit 
large companies from destroying unsold clothes, clothing accessories and footwear across the 
EU — a policy shift that makes overproduction not just a sustainability problem, but a compliance 
and business-model risk. In apparel manufacturing, conventional cut-and-sew production also 
creates fabric offcuts before a garment ever reaches a customer — making waste, excess 
inventory and long lead times structural problems rather than edge cases. 
Under the partnership, Arvind will deploy two OneWeave™ machines at its facility in Ahmedabad, 
India, with operations expected to begin by December 2026. The phased partnership includes a 
path to 30+ additional machines across India and/or North America in 2027–28, scaling with 
demand from global apparel brands. unspun will provide installation, commissioning, training, 
software, and ongoing service and support; brands will contract with Arvind to produce using the 
technology. 
Demand for the new capacity is already building, with a pipeline of leading global apparel brands 
preparing programs. With initial capacity limited to the first two machines, production slots will be 
allocated on a first-come, first-served basis, giving early-moving brands priority access as the 
deployment scales.

OneWeave™ weaves yarn directly into three-dimensional garment forms, bypassing 2D fabric 
production, most cutting, and most sewing. A pant leg is woven in roughly 10 minutes, and each 
machine can produce approximately 20,000 pants per year — creating a new foundation for 
faster, more flexible apparel production. By moving OneWeave™ from unspun's microfactory into 
a global Tier-1 manufacturing environment, and by building a path toward additional capacity in 
India and the U.S., the partnership brings the industry closer to a future in which brands can 
replenish closer to demand, produce nearer to end markets, and rely less on long-range 
forecasting and bulk inventory bets. 
That shift matters because unsold inventory remains one of fashion's most persistent financial 
and sustainability problems. Overproduction — locked in by long lead times and bulk ordering — 
ends in markdowns, deadstock and landfill. Responsive and nearshored manufacturing capacity 
gives brands a path to make smaller, smarter commitments; react to real consumer demand in-
season; reduce overstock and waste; cut markdown exposure; and move closer to a true on-
demand production model over time.
