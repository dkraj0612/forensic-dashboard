Change in Senior Management. 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, it is hereby informed that vide office order No. CON/HR/CN -
7892/ dated 05.10.2026, Shri Rama Kant Singh, GGM (C&O)/E8 grade has been 
redesignated as Executive Director (C&O) in E-9 grade w.e.f. 16.09.2026.

Safety and Vigilance department of Railway at various levels at Division, Zonal 
Head-Quarter and Railway Board. He has also worked in Ministry of Food, Ministry 
of Heavy Industries and Ministry of Coal. He has done B.E. (Mechanical) and M. 
Tech.
