i. Name of the authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). 
ii. Nature and details of the action(s) taken or order(s) passed: Caution letters have 
been issued to the persons listed at S.No.(2) and the Compliance Officer. 
iii. Date of receipt of direction or order, including any ad-interim or interim orders, 
or any other communication from the authority: 18.11.2026. 
iv. Details of the violation(s)/contravention(s) committed or alleged to be 
committed: The Contra Trade executed by the persons stated at S.No.(2) were in 
contravention of the provisions of SEBI (PIT) Regulations, 2015 and Company's Code of 
Conduct. Further, as per the PIT regulations, the Compliance Officer has failed to take suitable 
action in respect of Contra trades. 
v. Impact on financial, operation or other activities of the listed entity, quantifiable 
in monetary terms to the extent possible: 
No fine or penalty has been imposed. 
Therefore, there is no financial impact of the caution notices on the Company. 
4. 
On the above subject, the Company would like to state that the non-compliance of 
Shri Aniket Khandelwal (son of Mr Manoj Kumar Gupta - DP) was noted and apprised to Audit 
Committee and Board of Directors in their meetings held on 07.08.2024 and 08.08.2024 
respectively. As per the decision of the Board, caution letter was issued to him and gains made 
on the Contra Trade were deposited in SEBI-IPEF fund on 28.11.2024. This compliance was 
also informed to the Stock Exchanges by the Company vide letter dated 02.12.2024. 
The trades and contra trades executed by Shri Vijay Namdeo Janrode, as informed by SEBI, 
were in the same quantity and were executed within the same week, thereby not getting 
reflected in the weekly BENPOS made available to the Company, and hence did not come to 
the notice. Further, the same were also not reported by Designated Person. The gains made 
from the trades have already been deposited in the designated fund on 28.10.2026. 
The particulars of above two cases of contra trades and the non-compliance by the designated 
persons, deposit of gains in the designated funds, etc. have already been apprised to the 
Audit Committee and Board of Directors of the Company in its meetings held on 08.08.2024 
(for Sh. Aniket Khandelwal) and 02.11.2026 (for Sh. Vijay Namdeo Janrode). 
This is for your information and record.
