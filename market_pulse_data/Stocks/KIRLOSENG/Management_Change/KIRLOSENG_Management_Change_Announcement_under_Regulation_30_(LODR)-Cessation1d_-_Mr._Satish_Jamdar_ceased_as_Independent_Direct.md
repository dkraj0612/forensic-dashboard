Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
The Members of the Company in its meeting held on 11th August 2022 had approved the re-
appointment of Mr. Satish Jamdar (DIN 00036653), as an Independent Director of the Company, 
for a second term of 4 (four) consecutive years with effect from 4th August 2022. His current term 
is valid upto 3rd August 2026. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, including amendments thereunder, this is to inform you that                      
Mr. Satish Jamdar (DIN 00036653), Independent Director of the Company, has ceased to be a 
Director of the Company and member of the Committee(s), as applicable, on completion of his 
tenure with effect from close of working hours of 3rd August 2026.  
 
The Company places on record its sincere appreciation for the valuable contribution made by                     
Mr. Satish Jamdar during his tenure as an Independent Director of the Company.
