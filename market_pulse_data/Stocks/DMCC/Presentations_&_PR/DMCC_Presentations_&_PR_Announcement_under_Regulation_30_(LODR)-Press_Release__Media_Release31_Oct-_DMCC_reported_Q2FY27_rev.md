MUMBAI, OCTOBER 30, 2026
DMCC Speciality Chemicals Limited, India’s leading Sulphur chemistry solutions manufacturer reported its 
financial performance for Q2FY27.
Q2 FY27 was a challenging quarter for the Company. Sulphur prices stayed elevated throughout the period 
and rose to a level that could not be absorbed or passed on through sulphuric acid prices. The resulting 
divergence between the two caused significant demand destruction across our end markets. A large share 
of sales in the market also took place at prices below the cost of production. Given how unfavourable the 
commodity cycle was, we took a conscious decision to run our plants below optimum capacity and avoid 
uneconomic volumes.
The decline in profitability this quarter therefore reflects mainly the commodity cycle and the complete 
divergence between sulphur and sulphuric acid prices. Since mid-October, sulphur prices have come off 
their highs and availability has improved significantly. We have ramped up production to match these 
better conditions.
Our borrowings rose during the quarter. The increase in short-term borrowings comes from a higher 
working capital requirement, driven by elevated sulphur prices. The increase in long-term borrowings 
comes from funds we raised at subsidised rates under a Government scheme for businesses adversely 
affected by the conflict in the Middle East.
Our speciality chemicals business continued to make good progress. In the Boron segment, we increased 
our output further. The wider speciality chemicals portfolio saw steady demand, particularly in export 
markets. Capacity utilisation has started to inch up and is now higher than in earlier quarters.
The macro environment is still too uncertain for us to predict or give guidance. However, once the 
operating environment stabilises and sulphur prices cool off, I am confident we will deliver performance 
that is more sustainable and more representative of DMCC’s capability and capacity.

ABOUT DMCC SPECIALITY CHEMICALS LIMITED (DMCC)
DMCC Speciality Chemicals Limited (DMCC), began its humble journey in 1919 with just one product and one manufacturing unit. It 
was the first manufacturer of sulphuric acid and phosphate fertilisers in India. The Company evolved over the years to establish its 
own brand, ‘Ship’, which soon became the trusted source for quality Single Superphosphate (SSP). Today, DMCC is a fully integrated 
speciality chemical company that specialises in sulphur, boron and ethanol chemistry, exporting its products to markets worldwide. 
The Company takes pride in its heritage, and borrows from its experience, to deliver tailor-made solutions to its customers from 
across the globe. Its products find application in a variety of end-use industries, such as, pharmaceuticals, detergents, dyes, 
fertilisers, pigments and cosmetics.
