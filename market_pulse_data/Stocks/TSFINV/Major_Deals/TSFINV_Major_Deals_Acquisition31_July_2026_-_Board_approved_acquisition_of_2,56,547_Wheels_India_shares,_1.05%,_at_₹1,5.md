Further Acquisition of Shares in Wheels India Limited  
 
We wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of our Company, at the meeting held on 
31.07.2026 (today), had approved  further acquisition of 2,56,547 equity shares of ₹10/- each 
representing 1.050% in the paid-up equity share capital of  M/s.Wheels India Limited (WIL)., 
from M/s. Trichur Sundaram Santhanam & Family Private Limited (TSSFPL). 
 
The transaction is proposed to be executed between the two promoters in the form of an inter-
se transfer through the block deal window mechanism of the stock exchange(s) or any other 
mechanism at a price that may be permitted under the regulatory framework on the date of 
execution of the transaction. 
 
We hereby confirm that the proposed transaction with M/s. Trichur Sundaram Santhanam & 
Family Private Limited., is a related party transaction under section. 2(76) read with section.188 
of the Companies Act, 2013 and Reg. 23 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.The Audit 
Committee has approved this transaction. Further we hereby confirm that Sundaram Finance 
Limited, the promoter of TSFIL, does not hold any shares in WIL. The persons belonging to 
the promoter group/group companies of TSFIL are interested in WIL only to the limited extent 
of their shareholdings, if any (forming part of public shareholding).
