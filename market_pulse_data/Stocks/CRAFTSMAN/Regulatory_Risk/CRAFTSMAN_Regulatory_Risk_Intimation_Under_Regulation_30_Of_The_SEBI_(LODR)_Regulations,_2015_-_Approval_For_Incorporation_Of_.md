Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI LODR Regulations"), we wish to inform you that the 
Board of Directors of the Company, by way of Circular Resolution dated 21st August, 2026, has 
approved the proposal for incorporation of Craftsman Fronberg Precision GmbH in Germany by 
Craftsman Germany GmbH, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, subject to completion of 
statutory and regulatory formalities and such approvals as may be required under applicable laws.  
 
Upon incorporation, Craftsman Fronberg Precision GmbH shall become a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Craftsman Germany GmbH and consequently a step-down wholly owned subsidiary 
of Craftsman Automation Limited.
