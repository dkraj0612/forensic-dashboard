During the quarter, the Company reassessed the valuation of its inventories in view of 
the suspension of manufacturing and trading operations since January 2026, prolonged 
non-movement and ageing of inventory, expiry of certain finished goods, deterioration 
in the condition of certain tobacco materials and obsolescence of brand-specific 
packaging materials. Based on the Commodity Condition Survey and Assessment made 
by registered valuer vide its report dated 14 October 2026, the estimated net 
realisable value of inventories was assessed at ₹ 2,925.64 Lakhs as against their 
existing carrying cost of ₹ 8,179.39 Lakhs. 
 
Accordingly, the Company has recognised an inventory write-down of ₹ 5,253.75 Lakhs 
during the quarter, being the difference between the carrying cost and the estimated 
NRV, in accordance with Ind AS 2. Such write-down is recognised as an expense in the 
period in which it occurs. 
 
The assessment has considered the physical condition and ageing of inventory, 
prevailing market conditions, estimated realisable prices and regulatory restrictions in 
respect of certain inventory sealed by the F&DA authorities. The Company shall 
reassess the NRV of inventories in subsequent reporting periods and recognise any 
reversal of write-down, where warranted, subject to the limits prescribed under Ind AS 
2. Our conclusion is not modified in respect of this matter.
