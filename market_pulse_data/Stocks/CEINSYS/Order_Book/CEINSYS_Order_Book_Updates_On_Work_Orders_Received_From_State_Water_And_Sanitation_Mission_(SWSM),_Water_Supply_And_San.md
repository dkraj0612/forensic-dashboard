Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Further to our letter dated October 22, 2023, and October 9, 2024, informing you about receipt of Letter of Award from 
State Water and Sanitation Mission (SWSM), Water Supply and Sanitation Department (WSSD), Government of 
Maharashtra for Appointment of System Integrators (SI’s) for IoT Deployment including Design, Implementation & 
Maintenance with Centralized IoT Platform for Jal Jeevan Mission Projects in State of Maharashtra amounting to  
Rs. 248.39 Crores and Rs.331.61 Crores respectively, we hereby inform you that SWSM vide its letter dated August 21, 
2026, has informed the Company that due to the sanctioned amount for the implementation of IoT has been exhausted 
under the allocated schemes in MJP MVS Schemes of SWSM, the allocation for 617 ZP schemes has been cancelled by 
SWSM.  
 
The overall reduction in the value of the pending orders to be executed is Rs.112.42 Crores with no impact on the 
revenue already booked.  Even after this reduction the pending order book remains at healthy Rs.865 Crores.
