Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of the Listing Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that 
the Company and Grammer AG, Germany have mutually agreed to extend the validity of the Joint Venture Agreement 
(“JVA”) dated November 26, 2026, entered between them in relation to their Joint Venture through Allygram Systems 
and Technologies Private Limited as a Joint Venture subsidiary Company of the Company.  
 
Accordingly, the parties have entered into an amendment to the aforesaid JVA, whereby the validity of agreement has 
been extended for a period of 3 years from April 1, 2027 to March 31, 2030. 
 
The extension is intended to facilitate the continued implementation of the JVA and enhance the business and area of 
implementation and fulfillment of the obligations contemplated under JVA. 
 
Except for the aforesaid extension, all other terms and conditions of the JVA shall remain unchanged and shall and 
continue to be in full force and effect 
 
The above changes do not have any material impact, nor do they impact the management and control of the Company.  
 
The details as required under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with 
SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is enclosed as an 
Annexure A. 
 
We request you to kindly take on record the information and disseminate the same.
