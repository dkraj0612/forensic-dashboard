The 41st Annual General Meeting (AGM) of CONFIDENCE FUTURISTIC ENERGETECH LIMITED

A.M.) to Tuesday, 29th September, 2026 (5.00 P.M.). Further those shareholders who could not vote

meeting.

the year ended 31st March, 2026 were taken as read. Since there was no physical attendance of 
Members and in compliance with the Circulars issued by the MCA and SEBI, the members were 
informed that the requirement of appointing proxies was not applicable.

CONSOLIDATED)
