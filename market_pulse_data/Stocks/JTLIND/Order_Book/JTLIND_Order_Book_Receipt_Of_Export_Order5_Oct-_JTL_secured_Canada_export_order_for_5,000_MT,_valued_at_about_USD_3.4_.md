## Page 2

Press Release
 
Secures Export Order from Canada for Approximately 5,000 MT of 
Black Welded Hollow Sections 
 
Chandigarh, 5th October 2026: JTL Industries Limited (“JTL” or “the Company”) (BSE: 
534600 | NSE: JTLIND), a leading manufacturer of structural steel tubes and pipes, is pleased 
to announce that it has secured a sizeable export order from Canada for approximately 5,000 
MT of Black Welded Hollow Sections. 
The order is valued at approximately USD 3.4 million and is scheduled to be executed within 
the current quarter. This order further strengthens JTL Industries’ growing international export 
footprint. The order also reflects the increasing acceptance of the Company’s products across 
global markets and its ability to cater to international customer requirements.  
The export order supports JTL Industries’ focus on expanding its presence in international 
markets and building a diversified customer base beyond India. With its growing export 
capabilities and established manufacturing platform, the Company continues to pursue 
opportunities across key global markets. 
 
Commenting on the order, Mr. Madan Mohan, Managing Director of JTL Industries said: 
“We are pleased to secure this sizeable export order from Canada. The order is an important 
addition to our growing international business and reflects the increasing acceptance of JTL 
Industries’ products in global markets. Our focus remains on expanding our presence across 
international geographies, while continuing to build long term relationships with customers and 
consistently meet their requirements. We see meaningful opportunities to further develop our 
export business and strengthen JTL Industries’ position as an Indian steel products 
manufacturer serving customers across global markets.”

About JTL Industries Limited 
JTL Industries Limited is among the fastest growing steel tube and non ferrous metal products manufacturers, with 
its registered office situated in Chandigarh. The company operates manufacturing facilities in Punjab, Maharashtra, 
Chhattisgarh and Himachal Pradesh (including its subsidiaries). Its cumulative pipe manufacturing capacity is 
approximately 9,36,000 MTPA, with around 3,00,000 MTPA dedicated to backward integration. The company is a 
recognized Three Star Export House. Its product range includes DFT Structural Pipes, GI Pipes, MS Black Pipes, 
Hollow Sections, Solar Structures, HR Coils and Phosphorous Bronze, Copper and Brass Alloys, among others, 
which cater to various industrial and infrastructural applications.
