Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015
 
Today, at 11:29 a.m., ICICI Bank Limited (the Bank) has received a show cause notice 
(SCN) under Section 73 of West Bengal Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 from                        
Joint Commissioner of Revenue, West Bengal. The aforesaid SCN includes a Goods and 
Services Tax (GST) demand amounting to ₹ 16,75,96,536/- (Tax ₹ 9,30,73,770/- ;    
interest ₹ 6,52,15,388/- and penalty ₹ 93,07,378/-) on services provided by the Bank to 
customers maintaining specified minimum balances in their accounts. 
 
While the Bank is in litigation (including writ petition) on a similar issue raised in 
orders/SCNs in the past, since the aggregate/cumulative amount involved in the above 
crosses the materiality threshold, this matter is being reported. 
 
The Bank will file a reply to the said SCN within prescribed timelines. 
 
Please take the above information on record.
