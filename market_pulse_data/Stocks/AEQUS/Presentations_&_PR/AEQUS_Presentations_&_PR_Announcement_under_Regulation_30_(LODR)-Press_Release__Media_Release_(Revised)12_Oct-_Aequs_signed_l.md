Press Release  
Aequs Announces Long-Term Supply Agreements with 
Safran for Aerospace Applications 
Bengaluru, 12 October 2026: Aequs, a leading India-based precision manufacturing 
company, today announced the signing of long-term supply agreements with Safran, 
a global leader in the aerospace industry for critical components. 
These agreements reflect the commitment of both companies to strengthen their 
collaboration and highlight Safran's dedication to working with trusted, high-quality 
industrial partners. The contracts will support Safran's supply chain and ongoing 
production programs, while also contributing to the growth and development of 
Aequs within the aerospace sector. 
This milestone represents a significant opportunity for Aequs to further expand its 
activities alongside one of the world's leading aerospace companies.  
The agreements build on Aequs’ longstanding engagement with Safran Landing 
Systems with new contracts, while also extending the relationship to other Safran 
entities Safran Aerosystems and Safran Electronics & Defense. With this Aequs 
today has relationships with six Safran entities.  
While broadening the scope of collaboration, the agreements involve major work 
being carried out at the upcoming Aequs Engine Components Ecosystem (AECE), at 
Hosur in Tamil Nadu. The awards cover the machining of superalloys parts, along with 
the supply of complex machining coating, and assembly of landing gear components 
for the Airbus A320, A330, A350 and Boeing 787 programs. 
Aequs will leverage its integrated aerospace manufacturing ecosystem and end-to-
end capabilities across forging, precision machining, special processes, surface 
treatment, and assembly to manufacture these products. The agreements further 
strengthen the role of Aequs’ Belagavi Aerospace Cluster as an integrated 
manufacturing base serving global aerospace supply chains. This capability base will 
be further expanded with the upcoming Aequs Engine Components Ecosystem 
(AECE) in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, where the manufacture of as all medium and large parts 
will be taken up. 
Aravind Melligeri, Executive Chairman & CEO, Aequs, said: “These agreements 
reflect what Aequs was built for: long-horizon partnerships anchored in capability, 
reliability, and scale. The components Aequs will manufacture for Safran are among

Today’s announcement comes close on the heels of the agreement signed between 
Aequs and Safran Landing Systems in July 2026 for the supply of fully assembled A320 
family aircraft wheels. The wheels agreement is Aequs’ first serial production 
agreement extending beyond 15 years, through 2042, and will see complete aircraft 
wheels manufactured entirely in India – from Indian-sourced aluminum to final 
assembly and testing, creating 100% in-country manufacturing value addition. 
The latest agreements build further on a relationship between Aequs and Safran that 
spans more than a decade and extends across multiple Safran businesses. Together, 
the agreements reflect the growing depth of Aequs’ aerospace manufacturing 
capabilities and its expanding role in supporting global aerospace supply chains from 
India. 
About Aequs Limited:  
Aequs Limited is the only engineering-led, vertically integrated precision manufacturer 
operating within a single SEZ in India, with operations across the aerospace and 
consumer segments. The Company operates a unique ecosystem with co-located 
capabilities spanning forging, precision machining, surface treatment, and assembly, 
enabling end-to-end manufacturing of complex, high-precision components. 
In aerospace, Aequs is among India’s largest precision component manufacturers, with 
a portfolio of parts across engine systems, structures, actuation systems, landing 
systems, and assemblies. The Company is a Tier-1 supplier to leading global aerospace 
OEMs and system integrators, including Airbus, Boeing, Safran, and Collins Aerospace, 
and maintains long-standing relationships with global customers.  
Alongside aerospace, Aequs operates a diversified consumer manufacturing 
ecosystem spanning consumer electronics, plastics, and durables, leveraging its 
advanced engineering, tooling, and large-scale manufacturing capabilities to serve 
leading global brands. It operates within integrated manufacturing clusters in Belagavi, 
Hubballi, and Koppal (Karnataka), supported by international operations in the United 
States and France, providing proximity to key global customers.
