### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Here's an executive summary of the document:  Aequs Limited's Board has approved a preferential issue of warrants totaling INR 6.5 billion to Mellwood Trustee Services, a promoter entity, at a regulatory floor price of INR 231.55 per warrant. This significant capital infusion will bolster the company's finances, while also increasing the promoter group's shareholding from 59.09% to 60.73% upon full conversion of the warrants by December 2027. Although the issue price meets regulatory requirements, the preferential allotment to a promoter warrants observation regarding potential control consolidation, despite the company stating no immediate change in control.
