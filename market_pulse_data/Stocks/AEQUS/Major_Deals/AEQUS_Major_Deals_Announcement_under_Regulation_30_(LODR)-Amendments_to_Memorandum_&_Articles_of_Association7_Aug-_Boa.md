Engineered Plastics Private Limited and Aequs Force Consumer Products Private Limited, which has been 
approved by the shareholders of the Company through Postal Ballot process and which is further subject 
to other statutory and regulatory approvals as required.

ANNEXURE 1 
 
Brief details of Alteration in the Memorandum of Association (“MOA”) of the Company  
 
Alteration of Main Object Clause III(A) of MOA by insertion of clauses 5 to 10, as provided below 
subject to the approval of the shareholders at the ensuing Annual General Meeting, and receipt of 
any regulatory/statutory approvals, as may be required under applicable laws 
 
Proposed Clauses: 
 
“5. To carry on the business in India or elsewhere as manufacturers, buyers, sellers, dealers, importers, 
exporters, contractors, factors, agents and suppliers for polythene, polypropylene, ABS, nylon, polystyrene, 
P.V.C. polyester strips, plastic powder, thermoplastic and thermosetting polymers and such other articles 
required to manufacture profile plastic, moulded industrial articles, industrial components and articles and 
auxiliary plastic products, extrusion, injection moulding, blow moulding, compressor moulding, rotational 
moulding, thermoforming, vacuum forming, plastics pellets, plastic luggage articles, light and heavy 
automobile parts, components and accessories for vehicles including aircrafts.  
 
6. To carry on the business in India or elsewhere as manufacturers, buyers, sellers, dealers, importers, 
exporters, contractors, factors, assemblers, agents and suppliers for consumer products including but not 
limited to all sorts of cookware, kitchenware and appliances for domestic, commercial or industrial purposes 
and all types of electric, electronic and non-electronic products for domestic, commercial and / or industrial 
use whether made out of metal, non-metal, ferrous and non-ferrous, polypropylene, ABS, nylon, polystyrene, 
P.V.C. polyester strips, plastic powder, thermoplastic and thermosetting polymers or made out of any other 
substance including aluminum and such other articles including light and heavy parts, components and 
accessories for such consumer & electronic products and such other articles required to manufacture profile 
plastic, moulded industrial articles, industrial components and articles and auxiliary plastic products, 
extrusion, injection moulding, blow moulding, compressor moulding, rotational moulding, thermoforming, 
vacuum forming, plastics pellets, plastic luggage articles, components and accessories for consumer & 
electronic products.  
 
7. To manufacture and otherwise carry on the business of developing, designing, manufacturing, import, 
export, buying, selling or otherwise dealing in highly precision equipment, engineering products including 
but not limited to components and other systems and their accessories, precision turned, machined products, 
components or parts either metal, plastic, silicon or any other substance, involving precision manufacturing 
or molding or products made using molds as a process and parts, components and items that can be 
produced, manufactured or conceptualized using precision manufacturing methods including but not limited 
to smart devices and its components, products, accessories, peripherals and parts involving general 
engineering, hydraulic and pneumatic uses, applications and industries.
