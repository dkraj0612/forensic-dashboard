In accordance with SEBI Listing Regulations and Code of Practices and Procedures for Fair 
Disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information in terms of Regulation 8 of SEBI (Prohibition 
of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, the following is the Business Update for the quarter ended 
September 30, 2026 (“Q2 & H1 FY27”):   
 
Operational Highlights 
 
Q2FY27 was a quarter of resilience for the Company. Despite an exceptionally heavy monsoon and 
the temporary suspension of energy operations at the Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC) Waste-to-Energy 
(WtE) facility, total tonnage managed grew ~0.8% year-on-year (YoY) to approximately 1.28 million 
tonnes. Collection & Transportation (C&T) volumes rose ~11.5% YoY  to ~0.60 million tonnes, led by 
the ramp-up of the newly commenced C&T operations in Mumbai  awarded by Brihanmumbai 
Municipal Corporation (BMC) and higher volumes from existing C&T operations, including those at 
Navi Mumbai, Panvel and Noida. 
 
Processing volumes stood at ~0.68 million tonnes, ~7.2% lower YoY, primarily on account of the 
suspension of WtE operations at PCMC. Adjusting the PCMC WtE facility from Q2 FY26, processing 
volumes grew by ~3.3% YoY, supported by a ~70% YoY increase in volumes at the Construction & 
Demolition (C&D) waste processing facility in Mumbai. For the first half of the fiscal year, the total 
tonnage stood at ~ 2.71 million tonnes representing growth of ~ 4.5% on YOY basis. 
 
Core revenue remained broadly stable YoY (~1% lower), due to the absence of power sales from 
the PCMC WtE facility, which was largely offset by ~12% YoY growth in C&T revenue. Excluding 
power sales from the base quarter, core revenue grew ~2% YoY, underscoring the strength and 
diversity of the Company’s contract portfolio. For the first half of the fiscal year, core revenue 
growth stood at ~ 3.8%.

PCMC WtE Facility – Incident and Restoration Update 
 
As disclosed in the Company’s Business Update for Q1 FY27, the quarter witnessed an unfortunate 
and tragic incident at the PCMC WtE facility operated by the Company’s material subsidiary, Antony 
Lara Renewable Energy Private Limited (“ALREPL”). On July 8, 2026, excessive and incessant 
monsoon rainfall caused a legacy waste landfill located adjacent to the facility — outside the 
Company’s operational perimeter — to collapse onto the site, resulting in severe structural damage 
and the tragic loss of nine lives. The Company once again extends its heartfelt condolences to the 
bereaved families. 
 
Efforts to bring the WtE facility back into operations are going on in close coordination with PCMC 
and the relevant authorities. All Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) have been working 
diligently alongside the Company’s project and operations teams on the inspection, repair and 
recommissioning of the affected equipment and systems. Based on current progress, the WtE 
facility is expected to be functional by December 2026. 
 
In the interim, Material Recovery Facility (MRF) and composting operations at the site have 
resumed, processing ~33,600 tonnes of waste during the quarter. Collection and Transportation 
services across all project sites, including PCMC, continued uninterrupted. 
 
Support to Bereaved Families 
 
The Company continues to honour, in full, the commitments made to the families of the deceased 
and to the injured employees, as disclosed in the Q1 FY27 Business Update, including financial 
assistance, employment to the next of kin, educational support for minor children, and medical 
coverage for the injured.  
 
The safety and well-being of every employee remain the Company’s highest and most non-
negotiable priority. Further updates on the restoration of the WtE facility will be provided at the end 
of every quarter throughout the current financial year.
