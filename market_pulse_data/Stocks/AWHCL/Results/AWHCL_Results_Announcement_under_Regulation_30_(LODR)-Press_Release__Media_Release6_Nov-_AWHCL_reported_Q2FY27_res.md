Commenting on the results, Jose Jacob, Chairman & Managing Director of Antony Waste Handling Cell Limited, 
said, “During Q2FY27, our operating revenue stood at ₹321 crores, registering a growth of 25% year-on-year, 
while H1FY27 operating revenue increased by 15% year-on-year to ₹582 crores. This performance reflects the 
strength of our diversified contract portfolio, driven by continued growth in our Collection & Transportation (C&T) 
business. The growth in C&T was supported by the ramp-up of newly commenced Mumbai operations awarded by 
the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC), along with higher volumes across existing projects and the 
recognition of cost escalations as per the contracted terms. 
 
EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹58 crores, with margins at 17.6%, while H1FY27 EBITDA stood at ₹103 crores, 
with margins at 17.2%. Revenue growth in the quarter was driven partly by certain past escalation claims that the

client approved during the quarter. Because these escalations relate to earlier periods, the Company has made 
an expected credit loss (ECL) provision against the resulting receivables, in line with Ind AS 109 and applicable 
audit requirements. As a result, margins rose less than the revenue increase would otherwise suggest. These 
provisions are expected to be written back as the amounts are realised from the client. Excluding the ECL 
provision, the Company would have reported stronger EBITDA growth and margin expansion. 
 
This quarter, we witnessed an unfortunate incident at the PCMC WtE facility in July 2026, which disrupted 
operations. Restoration and recommissioning efforts are underway in close coordination with PCMC, the relevant 
authorities and our OEM partners. While energy operations remained suspended, MRF and composting operations 
resumed, processing ~33,600 tonnes of waste. The facility is expected to be functional by December 2026. During 
the quarter, we recognized ₹20 crores as exceptional item relating to this incidence. This exceptional item 
comprising impairment of property, plant and equipment, restoration and other related costs and compensation 
paid to employees/ their next of kin.  
 
Looking ahead, our focus remains on the timely restoration of the PCMC WtE facility, strengthening operational 
efficiencies and sustaining growth across our diversified portfolio. The continued ramp-up of our Mumbai C&T 
operations, healthy traction in C&D processing and growth across existing projects provide a strong foundation 
for our business. With a strong order book and an expanding project pipeline, we remain focused on disciplined 
execution, prudent capital allocation and operational excellence, while remaining confident in our long-term 
growth prospects and our ability to create sustainable value for all stakeholders.” 
 
About Antony Waste Handling Cell Limited 
 
Antony Waste Handling Cell Limited is leading player in the Indian Municipal Solid Waste Management industry 
with an established track record of more than two decades, providing full spectrum of MSW services which includes 
solid waste collection, transportation, processing and disposal services across India, majorly catering to 
municipalities. The Company has pioneered both MSW collection and transportation business in the country. We 
are also key players in the landfill construction and management sector with in-house expertise for construction 
and management of landfills. We are focus on the emerging waste management areas in India such as waste to  
energy. During our journey of over two and half decades, we started the business with MSW C&T and built their 
way in the solid waste management business, having worked with more than 23 Municipal Corporations. At 
Kanjurmarg, Mumbai, the Company is operating the largest single location waste processing plant in Asia. The  
PCMC Waste-to-Energy is the first Waste-to-Energy Plant in Maharashtra selling power under Green Energy Open 
Access Rules. Further strengthening its renewable energy portfolio, the Company has also secured two new Waste-
to-Energy projects in Kadapa and Kurnool in Andhra Pradesh, reinforcing its commitment to sustainable and 
technology-driven waste management solutions.
