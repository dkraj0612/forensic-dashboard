PRESS RELEASE 
Augmont Wins First-Ever ‘Best Digital and E-Commerce Company of the Year’ Award 
at IIJA 2026 
Augmont Enterprises Limited has been recognised as the ‘Best Digital and E-Commerce Company 
of the Year’ at the inaugural edition of the India International Jewellery Awards (IIJA) 2026. The 
recognition celebrates Augmont’s contribution to digital innovation, consumer engagement and the use 
of technology to shape the evolving jewellery and gold/silver ecosystem. 
The award was presented at the IIJA 2026 Awards Ceremony, held on October 2, 2026, at The Westin 
Mumbai Powai Lake. Organised by the India Bullion & Jewellers Association (IBJA) and Tefla’s, 
the awards brought together leaders, entrepreneurs, creators and stakeholders from across the jewellery 
value chain to recognise excellence and emerging contributions to the industry. 
Selected by the IIJA 2026 jury, the award recognises Augmont’s technology-led approach to making gold 
more accessible and convenient for both consumers and businesses. At the heart of its digital ecosystem are 
SPOT, its platform for jewellers and bullion traders, and Augmont.com, which enables consumers to invest 
in Digital Gold and Gold SIPs, as well as purchase gold and silver coins and jewellery online. 
As of 30 June 2026, Augmont’s ecosystem comprised 231 fintech partnerships, 5,271 registered members 
on SPOT and 5,517 retail touchpoints. Through its platforms and partnerships, Augmont had also served 
more than 50.6 million registered consumers, underscoring the scale and reach of its technology-enabled 
gold ecosystem 
As the first-ever recipient of the award, Augmont becomes part of IIJA’s founding chapter and sets 
the benchmark for the editions that follow. 
Commenting on the recognition, Ketan Kothari, Whole-Time Director, Augmont, said, “This 
recognition reflects the collective efforts of our team and our continued focus on using technology to 
make gold more convenient and accessible for consumers. The evolution of online bullion trading, 
digital gold and e-commerce has opened new ways for jewellers and consumers to engage with gold. 
We have always focused on building platforms like augmont.com and Augmont SPOT that make these 
experiences more seamless and trusted. 
Being the first recipient of this award at the inaugural IIJA is particularly meaningful for us. It 
reinforces our belief that technology will continue to play an important role in shaping how consumers 
access and engage with gold and encourages us to keep strengthening our digital capabilities as the 
industry evolves.” 
The recognition at IIJA 2026 adds to Augmont’s growing list of industry honours and reflects its 
continued focus on technology, accessibility and innovation across India’s evolving gold, silver and 
jewellery ecosystem

ABOUT AUGMONT 
Augmont is one of India’s leading integrated gold and silver companies, with a presence across the precious 
metals value chain spanning refining, wholesale trading, digital gold, jewellery, gold-backed lending and 
retail services. 
Combining physical infrastructure with technology-led platforms, Augmont seeks to make gold more 
accessible, transparent and convenient for consumers, jewellers, bullion traders and businesses. Its 
ecosystem includes Augmont SPOT, its B2B platform for physical gold and silver bullion, as well as 
offerings across coins and bars, Digital Gold, Gold SIPs, gold loans, old-gold exchange, jewellery and lab-
grown diamonds. 
As of 30 June 2026, Augmont’s ecosystem included two refineries, 231 fintech partnerships, 5,271 registered 
members on SPOT, 20 SPOT delivery centres, 5,517 retail touchpoints and 118 Gold For All stores across 
India. Through its platforms and partnerships, Augmont had served more than 50.6 million registered 
consumers. 
Augmont has been recognised as ‘India’s No. 1 Gold Platform’ at IGC for two consecutive years, in 2025 
and 2026, and as ‘India’s No. 1 Silver Platform’ in 2025. 
Guided by its vision of “Gold For All”, Augmont is focused on making gold more accessible, trusted and 
useful for Indian households and businesses.
