Commenting on the Q1FY27 results Mr. Vinay Tripathi, Managing Director of the company said:
“The logistics industry continues to operate in a dynamic environment, with evolving global trade conditions, escalated fuel prices, 
freight-rate movements and geopolitical developments influencing trade flows and realizations. In this environment, the Company 
remained focused on strengthening its core logistics operations while continuing to build a more diversified and resilient business 
platform.
During Q1FY27, Accuracy Shipping demonstrated resilience in its operating performance. Revenue stood at ₹140.8 crores, with 
Logistics Services continuing to anchor the business and contributing 71% of the total revenue. Commercial Vehicles contributed 
28%, while the Petroleum & Petroleum Products segment accounted for 2%, reflecting the Company's progressively diversified 
revenue profile.
Our focus on improving the quality of earnings continued to yield positive results. Gross Profit increased to ₹16.2 crores, with the 
Gross Profit margin improving to 11.5% from 8.3% in Q1FY26. EBITDA increased to ₹6.7 crores, while EBITDA margin expanded to 
4.8% from 3.2% in the corresponding quarter. The improvement in margins reflects a stronger business mix and continued focus on 
operational efficiencies.
Our diversification strategy continues to progress steadily. The Commercial Vehicles business has emerged as an important 
contributor, accounting for 28% of revenue in Q1FY27, while Transportation & Last Mile contributed 15% of the Company's revenue. 
The increasing contribution from these businesses, alongside our continued expansion across industries such as Marble & Granite, 
Ceramic Tiles, Agri, Rubber, Wood and other sectors, is helping us reduce concentration and create multiple avenues for sustainable 
growth.
Going forward, our focus remains on strengthening the core Clearing & Forwarding franchise, scaling Transportation & Last Mile 
capabilities, improving margins and expanding our presence across high-potential industries and business verticals. We remain 
committed to disciplined execution, operational efficiency and customer-centric growth, which we believe will enable Accuracy 
Shipping to build a stronger, more diversified and profitable logistics platform and create sustainable long-term value for our 
stakeholders.”
