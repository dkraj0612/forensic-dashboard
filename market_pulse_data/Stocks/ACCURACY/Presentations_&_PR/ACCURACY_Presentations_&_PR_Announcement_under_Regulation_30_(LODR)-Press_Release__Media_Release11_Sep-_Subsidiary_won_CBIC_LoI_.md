Accuracy shipping Receives LOI to set up CFS at Kandla, Gujarat 
 
Gandhidham, September 11, 2026: Accuracy Shipping Limited today announced that A.R.S. Terminals 
(India) Private Limited, a subsidiary in which Accuracy Shipping Limited holds a 99.80% stake, has 
received a Letter of Intent (LoI) dated September 10, 2026, from the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and 
Customs (CBIC) for setting up a Container Freight Station (CFS) at Kandla, Gujarat. 
 
The proposed CFS, located at Plot No. 7, Sector 10B, Kandla, Gandhidham, Kachchh - 370201, Gujarat, 
marks a strategic expansion of Accuracy Shipping's presence in the container logistics and cargo 
handling segment. The facility will cater to the handling of import and export cargo and is expected to 
be operationalized within one year from the date of the LoI. With a planned storage capacity of 
approximately 5,000 containers per month and 80,000 containers annually, the facility is expected to 
strengthen the Company's ability to cater to growing cargo handling requirements in the region. 
 
The project entails an estimated capital expenditure of approximately ₹25 crore, which is proposed to 
be funded through a combination of debt and internal accruals. At peak operations and full utilization, 
the CFS is expected to generate revenue of approximately ₹175–200 crore, with the EBITDA margin 
expected to grow to double-digit levels as operations scale up.  
 
Commenting on the development, Vinay Tripathi, Chairman & Managing Director, Accuracy Shipping 
Limited, said: 
 
"We are pleased to mark this important milestone in our growth journey with the addition of a new 
Container Freight Station at Kandla. This strategic expansion will further strengthen our integrated 
logistics capabilities, enable us to serve a wider customer base and create new opportunities for revenue 
growth.  
 
We believe the facility will also enhance our operating leverage and support improved profitability as 
utilization scales up. We remain focused on expanding our presence across key logistics hubs and 
delivering sustainable long-term value to our stakeholders."
