Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to announce that A.R.S. Terminals (India) Private Limited, a 
subsidiary company in which Accuracy Shipping Limited holds a 99.80% stake, has received a 
Letter of Intent (LoI) dated September 10, 2026, from the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and 
Customs (CBIC) for setting up a Container Freight Station (CFS) at Kandla, Gujarat. 
 
The proposed CFS will be located at Plot No. 7, Sector 10B, Kandla, Gandhidham, Kachchh - 
370201, Gujarat, and will facilitate the handling of import and export cargo. The CFS is expected 
to be operationalized within one year from the date of the LoI. 
 
The proposed facility is expected to have a monthly storage capacity of approximately 5,000 
containers and an annual storage capacity of approximately 80,000 containers. 
 
We request you to take the above information on record. 
 
Thanking You.
