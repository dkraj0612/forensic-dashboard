https://www.in.mpms.mufg.com  > Resources > Downloads > KYC > Formats for KYC.\

Choice of Nomination, Contact details, Bank Account details and Specimen Signature updated, shall be eligible for any 
payment including dividend, interest or redemption payment in respect of such folios, only through electronic mode with effect 
from April 1, 2024.
