SIGMAC & Co., Statutory Auditors.

Regulations, 2015. The financial results will also be made available on the Company's 
website at www.bedmutha.com 
2. Sale of Non-Operational Land Property:  The Board was informed that the Company 
has completed the sale of non-operational land bearing Gat No. B-113, admeasuring 
1,000 sq. mtrs., situated at STICE, Musalgaon MIDC, Sinnar, Nashik, 
Maharashtra, to M/s. Pradeep Transport (an unrelated third party) for a total 
consideration of ₹1.09 Crore. The enabling proposal for the sale of the said land was 
previously approved by the Audit committee, Board of Directors and Shareholders, and 
duly disclosed in the outcome of the said meeting(s’). The land was previously used for 
manufacturing operations at Sinnar and was surplus to requirements following 
operational closure at Sinnar taluka. The transaction does not constitute a sale of an 
undertaking (either fully or partially) and will have no impact on the current business 
operations of the Company.
