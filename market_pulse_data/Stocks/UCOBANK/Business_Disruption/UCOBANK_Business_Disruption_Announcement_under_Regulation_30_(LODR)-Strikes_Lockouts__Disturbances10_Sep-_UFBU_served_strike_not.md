Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Notice of Strike by UFBU 
 
We wish to inform that the United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU), on behalf of its 
seven constituent unions/associations, viz. AIBEA, AIBOC, NCBE, AIBOA, BEFI, 
INBOC and INBEF, has served a notice informing of its decision to proceed on 
strike on 11 September 2026, from 28 September 2026 to 30 September 2026, 
and continuous strike from 26 October 2026, on various issues/demands. 
 
The Bank has taken necessary steps to ensure continuity of its operations. 
However, in the event the proposed strike materialises, the functioning of the 
Bank's Branches and Offices may be affected. 
 
The above information is being submitted pursuant to Regulation 30 of the 
SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
