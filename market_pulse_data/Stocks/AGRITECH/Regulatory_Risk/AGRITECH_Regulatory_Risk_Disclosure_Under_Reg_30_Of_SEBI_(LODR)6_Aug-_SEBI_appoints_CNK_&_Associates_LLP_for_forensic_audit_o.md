Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Intimation of Initiation of Forensic Audit by SEBI. 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Company has 
received a communication dated 05 August 2026 from the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (“SEBI”), informing the Company that SEBI has appointed CNK & Associates LLP as the 
Forensic Auditor to conduct a forensic audit in respect of the Company. 
 
As stated in the communication, the forensic audit shall, inter alia, examine the books of 
account and records of the Company for the financial years 2020-21 to 2025-26 with 
reference to the matters specified by SEBI in its communication. 
 
The Company is extending and shall continue to extend its full cooperation to the Forensic 
Auditor and shall provide all information, records and assistance as may be required for 
completion of the audit. 
 
The Company remains committed to maintaining the highest standards of corporate 
governance, transparency and regulatory compliance. 
 
This disclosure is being made in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. 
 
You are requested to take note of the above.  
 
Thanking You.  
Yours faithfully,  
For Agri-Tech (India) Limited,
