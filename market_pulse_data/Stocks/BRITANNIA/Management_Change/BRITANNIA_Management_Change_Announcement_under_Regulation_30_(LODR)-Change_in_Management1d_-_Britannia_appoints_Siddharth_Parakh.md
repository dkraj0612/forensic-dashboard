Brief Profile 
Mr. Siddharth Parakh brings over 13 years of diverse experience in 
Corporate Strategy, Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A), Investments 
and Capital Allocation.  
 
Prior to joining Britannia, he was part of the Group CFO & Group 
Strategy Office at Tata Sons, where he worked on Strategic 
Initiatives, Investments and M&A across the Tata Group of 
Companies for the last 4.5 years.  
 
Prior to joining Tata Group, Mr. Siddharth worked as an Assistant 
Vice President in the investment banking team at Edelweiss 
(Nuvama) where he advised Indian and global clients on M&A, 
Corporate Finance and Fund Raising activities across several 
sectors including consumer, new age businesses, information 
technology and healthcare. He has also worked with the 
organizations like MAPE Advisory Group, Grant Thornton & 
PwC, advising on Corporate Finance & several Marquee 
Transactions. 
 
Mr. Siddharth was recognized with the prestigious Economic Times 
Young Leader Award, recognizing India’s emerging business 
leaders.  
 
He is a Chartered Accountant, a Company Secretary and holds a 
Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) from S. P. Jain 
Institute of Management & Research, Mumbai. He has also 
pursued Advanced International Finance Program from Cornell 
University, New York.
