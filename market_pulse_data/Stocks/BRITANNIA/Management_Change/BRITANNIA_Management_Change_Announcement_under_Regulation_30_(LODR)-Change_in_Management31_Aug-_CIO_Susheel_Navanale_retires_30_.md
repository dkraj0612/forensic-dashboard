Brief Profile 
Mr. Rejin Surendran brings over 24 years of diverse experience in leading 
and driving large-scale digital and technology transformations. 
 
Prior to joining Britannia, Mr. Rejin was working as the Global Chief 
Information Officer at Wipro Enterprises Private Limited, where he led a 
global technology team spanning across 25 Countries for over 4.5 years.  
 
Prior to Wipro Enterprises Private Limited, Mr. Rejin was working as the 
Head of Digital & ERP CoE at the Bottling Investments Group, Coca-Cola, 
where he led and drove the digital transformation journey for the 
enterprise. He also worked for over 13 years at Unilever, where he led the 
Digital Supply Chain roadmap for the organisation, including the 
digitisation of factories and logistics. 
 
Mr. Rejin holds an MBA from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), 
Bombay, and a degree in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from the 
National Institute of Technology (NIT), Warangal.
