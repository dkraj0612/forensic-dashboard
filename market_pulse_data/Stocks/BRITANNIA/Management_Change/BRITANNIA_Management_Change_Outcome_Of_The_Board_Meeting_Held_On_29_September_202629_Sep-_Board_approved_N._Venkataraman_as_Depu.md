 Appointment and Re-designation of Mr. N. Venkataraman (DIN: 05220857), Whole-Time Director 
designated as Executive Director and Chief Financial Officer, as the Deputy Managing Director of the 
Company, with effect from 1 October 2026, for the balance period of his existing term, i.e., up to          
29 July 2030 (both days inclusive), liable to retire by rotation, on the terms and conditions including 
remuneration, as recommended by NRC and approved by the Board, subject to the approval of the 
Members of the Company. Consequently, Mr. N. Venkataraman shall cease to be the Chief Financial 
Officer of the Company and shall continue as the Whole-Time Director and Key Managerial Personnel of 
the Company, designated as the Deputy Managing Director of the Company with effect from 1 October 
2026. He shall be responsible for Finance, Strategy, Legal, Secretarial and IT functions of the Company and 
will work along with Mr. Rakshit Hargave, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director to drive the 
transformation agenda of the Company. 
 
 Appointment of Mr. Ramamurthy Jayaraman, Vice President – Corporate Finance, as the Chief Financial 
Officer and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company with effect from 1 October 2026. He will head the 
entire Finance function comprising Financial planning & analysis, Taxation, Treasury, Shared Services and 
Business Commercial. 
 
The other details as required under the SEBI Listing Regulations, 2015 read with the SEBI Master Circular No.
