Vikran Engineering Successfully Commissions Strategic 132 kV Miao–Namsai 
Transmission Line in Arunachal Pradesh 
Mumbai, July 29, 2026: Vikran Engineering Limited, one of India's leading engineering, 
procurement and construction (EPC) companies in the power transmission sector, has 
successfully commissioned the strategically significant 132 kV Miao–Namsai Transmission Line 
in Arunachal Pradesh. The project marks a key milestone in strengthening power transmission 
infrastructure in the North Eastern Region and supports the development of a more resilient and 
reliable electricity network. 
The project comprises 40.630 kilometres of transmission line and 138 tower foundations, 
including two highly challenging pile foundations executed under difficult geological 
conditions. Successfully delivering the project across one of India's most demanding terrains 
underscores Vikran Engineering's engineering excellence, technical expertise, and execution 
capabilities. 
Recognising its strategic importance for strengthening Arunachal Pradesh's power infrastructure, 
the project was closely monitored by the Ministry of Power, Government of India, throughout 
its execution. 
The commissioning of the transmission line is expected to significantly strengthen the power 
supply network in Arunachal Pradesh, improve the reliability of the existing transmission system, 
connect remote areas to the National Grid, and facilitate the evacuation and integration of 
upcoming hydropower projects across the North Eastern Region (NER). The project will play an 
important role in enhancing energy accessibility while supporting the region's long-term 
economic growth and infrastructure development. 
Commenting on the achievement, Mr. Rakesh Markhedkar, Chairman & Managing Director, 
Vikran Engineering Limited, said: 
"The successful commissioning of the 132 kV Miao–Namsai Transmission Line is a proud milestone for 
Vikran Engineering and reflects our unwavering commitment to building critical infrastructure that 
powers India's growth. Executing a strategically significant project in the challenging terrain of Arunachal 
Pradesh demanded exceptional engineering expertise, meticulous planning, and the relentless dedication of 
our project teams. We are grateful to the Ministry of Power, our client, partners, and all stakeholders for 
their trust and collaboration throughout this journey.
