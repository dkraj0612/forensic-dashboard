Good Afternoon, Ladies and Gentlemen.
It gives me immense pleasure to welcome all our esteemed shareholders, representatives of the
Ministry of Coal, Coal India Limited, my colleagues on the Board, Statutory Auditors, Secretarial
Auditor, Scrutinizer and all other stakeholders to the 51st Annual General Meeting of Central Mine
Planning & Design Institute Limited.
At the outset, I thank each one of you for joining us today through Video Conferencing and for your
continued trust, confidence and support towards CMPDIL.
The requisite quorum being present as confirmed by company secretary, I call the Meeting to order.
The Notice convening this Annual General Meeting together with the Integrated Annual Report
containing the Audited Financial Statements for the financial year ended 31st March, 2026, the
Directors' Report, the Report of the Statutory Auditors, the Comments of the Comptroller and Auditor
General of India and the Secretarial Audit Report have already been circulated electronically to all the
Members.
With your permission, I take the Notice convening the Meeting, the Directors' Report, the Audited
Financial Statements and the Reports of the Statutory Auditors, the Comptroller and Auditor General
of India and the Secretarial Auditor as read.
I am pleased to present before you the highlights of the Company's performance during the financial
year 2025–26.
Dear Shareholders,
The year under review has been one of the most significant and eventful years in the history of our
Company.
The successful listing of CMPDIL on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited and BSE Limited
marks a defining milestone in our journey. It reflects the confidence of investors and stakeholders in
the Company's technical excellence, sound governance framework and sustained financial
performance built over more than five decades.
Our transition to a listed company is not merely a change in status, it is the beginning of a new chapter
that places greater responsibility upon us to deliver sustainable growth while maintaining the highest
standards of transparency, accountability and corporate governance.
Since its inception in 1975, CMPDIL has remained the premier consultancy and engineering
organisation in the Indian mining sector. Over the years, we have continuously strengthened our
expertise in exploration, mine planning, engineering consultancy, environmental management,

digital mine planning, geographic information systems, satellite imagery, 5G enabled applications and
advanced laboratory infrastructure.
The Company also continued to support the Ministry of Coal in several strategic national initiatives,
including commercial coal block auctions, First Mile Connectivity projects, exploration management
systems and adoption of advanced digital technologies in mining.

impact assessments, mine water management studies, biodiversity conservation initiatives,
ecological restoration and greenhouse gas estimation, CMPDIL remains committed to promoting

position, consistent profitability and disciplined approach towards resource management have
