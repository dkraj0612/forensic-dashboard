Hyundai BAYON: Generating Soon… 
Hyundai Motor India Announces the Name of Its Much-Awaited Intelligent SUV 
Gurugram | September 14, 2026: Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) today announced the 
name of its much-anticipated upcoming intelligent SUV, Hyundai BAYON. A new global SUV 
from Hyundai, BAYON marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter for Hyundai in India.  
As a new addition to HMIL's strong SUV line-up, Hyundai BAYON reflects a new approach to 
intelligent mobility, where innovation, expression and emotion come together with a clear 
sense of purpose. Crafted for an evolving India, the new SUV embodies Hyundai’s global 
design and product philosophy while bringing an intelligent experience to a new generation of 
customers. 
Commenting on the name reveal of the upcoming SUV, Mr. Tarun Garg, Managing 
Director & CEO, HMIL, said, “With Hyundai BAYON, we are introducing more than a new 
SUV. BAYON represents a new mindset – one that reflects our ambition to create experiences 
that are intelligent, intuitive and meaningful for our customers. At Hyundai, we believe that 
every new product is an opportunity to push ourselves further, guided by our passion for 
creating mobility experiences that enrich people’s lives. With BAYON, we have once again 
challenged ourselves to pursue perfection in the details that matter, while staying true to our 
belief that technology creates its greatest value when it becomes effortless and seamlessly 
integrated into our customers’ everyday lives. We look forward to sharing more about Hyundai 
BAYON with India in the coming weeks.” 
Bringing technology and storytelling together, Hyundai BAYON's name reveal film creates a 
futuristic world where intelligence and imagination converge. Inspired by the precision of a 
next-generation lab, the film follows a series of intelligent elements as they seamlessly come 
together, piece by piece, to form the name BAYON - symbolizing the emergence of a greater 
intelligence and a new expression of Hyundai's bold design, technology and innovation. The 
film culminates in a simple yet powerful thought: Intelligence ON. 
What is the inspiration behind the name Hyundai Bayon?  
 
The name BAYON draws inspiration from Bayonne, a city in the French Basque Country, 
known for its distinctive character and dynamic spirit. The name reflects Hyundai’s global 
design philosophy while marking the arrival of a new SUV created for an evolving India, with 
a sense of exhilaration and a new spirit of adventure. 
 
The announcement marks the first chapter in the Hyundai BAYON journey, with more details 
about the intelligent SUV to be revealed in the coming days.
