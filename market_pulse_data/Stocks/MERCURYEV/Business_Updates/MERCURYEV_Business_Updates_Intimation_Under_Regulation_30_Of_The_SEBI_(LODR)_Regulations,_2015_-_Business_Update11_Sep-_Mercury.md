Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform you that we have achieved significant progress in 
expanding its distribution network and strengthening its market presence across India as 
mentioned below: 
1. Expansion of Pan-India Dealer Network 
The Company has appointed 100 new dealers across India during the last four months, 
strengthening its retail distribution and after-sales service network across key electric vehicle 
markets. 
Building on this momentum, the Company has set a target of appointing an additional 200 
dealers by March 2027. The expansion is aimed at enhancing the Company's geographical 
reach, improving last-mile accessibility for customers and strengthening its presence across 
urban and semi-urban markets in India. 
The expansion of the dealer network is also aligned with the Company's objective of scaling its 
distribution infrastructure in line with its growing manufacturing capabilities and increasing 
demand for electric mobility solutions. 
2. Eligibility under GEDA Subsidy Scheme 
The Company has also received clearance from the Gujarat Energy Development Agency 
(GEDA) for marketing its battery-operated two-wheelers and three-wheelers in the State of 
Gujarat, making eligible customers entitled to applicable subsidies under the prevailing GEDA 
scheme. 
As per the applicable scheme and eligibility conditions: 
 
Electric Two-Wheelers (2W): Eligible for subsidy of ₹12,000 per vehicle. 
 
Electric Three-Wheelers (3W): Eligible for subsidy of ₹48,000 per vehicle. 
The availability of such subsidy is expected to enhance the affordability of the Company's 
electric vehicles for eligible customers and support the adoption of electric mobility in Gujarat.
