## Page 2

The Bombay Dyeing and Manufacturing Company Limited (Bombay 
Realty – Real Estate Division) recognized as "India’s Most Trusted 
Employer for Women 2026–2027" 
Mumbai, 20th November, 2026: The Bombay Dyeing and Manufacturing Company Limited 
(Bombay Realty – Real Estate Division) is pleased to announce that it has been recognized as 
one of India’s Most Trusted Employers for Women 2026–2027. The recognition is based on 
comprehensive research; industry benchmarking and insights conducted by Coherent Market 
Insights, the official research partner for the initiative. This recognition reflects the Company’s 
continued commitment to providing a trusted, inclusive and employee-centric workplace that 
supports and empowers women across all levels of the organization.  
In addition, the Company is proud to share that its HR leadership has been honoured with the 
“People’s Champion of the Year 2026” award, recognizing its efforts in promoting progressive 
HR practices, and creating an environment where employees can grow and succeed.  
 
Both awards were presented on November 19, 2026, at a gala felicitation ceremony held at five-
star hotel in Mumbai. These recognitions reaffirm the Company’s commitment to building a 
workplace where employees feel valued, supported and empowered, and further strengthen its 
position as an employer of choice.
