During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received the requisite regulatory approvals to commence its real estate project, namely, THREE ICC; the project was launched during the quarter ended
June 30, 2026. Accordingly, in terms of Ind AS 115, "Revenue from Contracts with Customers", the Company has commenced recognising revenue for this THREE ICC project ‘over time’ based on contracts
