Subject: Resignation from the Position of Chief Executive Officer 
Dear Sir, 
Please accept this letter as my formal resignation from the position of Chief Executive Officer of 
Bombay Realty. I have decided to step down from my role purely for a specific leadership role 
outside the organization. 
I would like to express my gratitude to you for all the support, guidance and the high degree of 
empowerment you gave me during my tenure. It has been a privilege working with you and to 
serve the company. The opportunity to lead the organization with your encouragement has been 
an invaluable learning experience, both professionally and personally. I will always be grateful for 
all of these. I sincerely hope our paths cross again in the future, and I would be delighted to have 
the opportunity to collaborate with you and the company in any capacity where I can add value. 
I remain fully committed to ensuring a smooth and orderly transition and will extend my complete 
support to facilitate the handover of my responsibilities in the best interests of the company. 
Request you to relieve me of my responsibilities by 31 st July 2026. 
I wish you, the Board of Directors, and everyone at Bombay Dyeing continued success and many 
more achievements in the years ahead. Thank you once again for the opportunities, the 
empowerment, and the many learnings that I will always cherish. 
Yours sincerely, 
ROH~
