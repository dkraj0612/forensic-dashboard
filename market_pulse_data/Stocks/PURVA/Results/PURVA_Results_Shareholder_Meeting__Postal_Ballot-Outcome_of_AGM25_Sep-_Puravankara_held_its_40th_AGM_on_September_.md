ANNEXURE – A 
Summary of the proceedings of the 40th Annual General Meeting 
 
The 40th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Members of Puravankara Limited was held on Friday, 
September 25, 2026, at 2:00 P.M. (IST) through Video Conferencing (VC) / Other Audio-Visual Means 
(OAVM) to transact the business as stated in the Notice dated August 14, 2026. The meeting was 
convened in compliance with the General Circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), 
the circulars issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), and the applicable provisions 
of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Rules made thereunder. 
 
Mr. Sudip Chatterjee, Company Secretary, welcomed the Members to the AGM and briefed them on 
the process for participation in the AGM. As Mr. Ravi Puravankara, Chairman of the Company had 
expressed his inability to attend the AGM due to his pre-occupied business exigencies, Mr. Ashish Ravi 
Puravankara, Managing Director of the Company, chaired the meeting in accordance with Article 75 
of the Articles of Association of the Company and attended the AGM through VC. 
 
The Chairman welcomed the Members to the AGM and, upon ascertaining that the requisite quorum 
was present, called the meeting to order. The Directors of the Company, members of Senior 
Management, the Scrutinizer, and the representatives of the Statutory Auditors and Secretarial 
Auditors attended the AGM. The Chairpersons of the Audit Committee, Stakeholders Relationship 
Committee and Nomination and Remuneration Committee were also present at the AGM to answer 
the queries of the Members, if any. 
 
With the concurrence of the Members, the Notice convening the 40th AGM and the Annual Report, 
including the Audited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Statements for the financial year ended 
March 31, 2026, together with the Reports of the Statutory Auditors and the Secretarial Auditors, 
were taken as read. 
 
The Chairman informed the Members that the Statutory Auditors’ Reports on the Standalone and 
Consolidated Financial Statements and the Secretarial Audit Report did not contain any qualification, 
observation, adverse remark or disclaimer on financial transactions or other matters that could 
adversely affect the functioning of the Company. Accordingly, the aforesaid reports were taken as 
read, in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Act.

The Chairperson read out his speech and thereafter invited the Members who had registered 
themselves as speakers to raise their queries and seek clarifications. The queries raised by the 
Members were duly addressed and satisfactorily answered by the Chairperson. 
 
The Members were informed that the Board of Directors had appointed Mr. Nagendra D Rao, 
Practicing Company Secretary, as Scrutinizer to Scrutinize the remote e-voting process and e-voting 
conducted during the AGM in a fair and transparent manner. The Company Secretary informed the 
Members that in accordance with the provisions under Section 108 of the Companies Act, 2013 read 
with Rule 20 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, the Company had 
provided the remote e-voting facility to the Members in respect of businesses to be transacted at the 
AGM. The facility of casting votes by remote e-voting was provided to the Members from Tuesday, 
September 22, 2026, at 09:00 A.M. to Thursday, September 24, 2026 at 05:00 P.M. Further, the 
members who were present at the AGM through Video conferencing and who had not cast their votes 
through remote e-voting, were provided an opportunity to cast their votes at the AGM. 
 
The Chairperson informed the members that consolidated results of the remote e-voting and e-voting 
conducted during the AGM, together with the Scrutinizer’s Report would be announced within two 
working days of the conclusion of the AGM and the same would be intimated to the Stock Exchanges 
and uploaded on the website of the Company, National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) and 
Central Depository Services Limited (CDSL). 
 
The Chairperson then thanked all the Directors, Members and Auditors for attending and participating 
at the meeting. There being no other business to be transacted, the meeting concluded at 2:55 P.M. 
(IST) with a vote of thanks.
