BRIEF PROFILE OF AUDITORS 
 
Rama K Gupta & Co. 
Rama K Gupta & Co. is a Chartered Accountants firm established in 1989 and practicing in India for 
over 3 decades. The firm has 25 partners, 12 Chartered Accountants, 38 Articled Assistants and more 
than 150 professional staff and has presence through 18 branch offices across 10 States. The firm 
provides services in the areas of Audit & Assurance, Taxation, Corporate Advisory, Risk 
Management, Forensic Audit, Regulatory Compliance and Financial Consulting. The firm has 
experience across Oil & Gas, Power, EPC, Banking & NBFC, Insurance, IT, Pharmaceuticals, Real 
Estate and other sectors. The firm undertakes Statutory, Internal, Concurrent, Stock and Forensic 
Audits and is empaneled with RBI, C&AG, LIC, IBA and NHAI. 
 
Borkar & Muzumdar 
Borkar & Muzumdar is a Chartered Accountants firm established in 1946 and practicing in India for 
over 8 decades. The firm has 20 partners, more than 50 Chartered Accountants and over 250 
employees and has presence across various locations in India. The firm provides services in the areas 
of Audit & Assurance, Tax & Regulatory, Risk Advisory, Consulting, Business Support, Investigations 
& Forensics, Due Diligence and Financial Advisory. The firm has experience in Statutory & Special 
Audits, Internal & Process Reviews, Management Audits, Regulatory Audits, Certifications, 
Forensics, Due Diligence and Ind AS Advisory across Oil & Energy, PSUs, Banking & Financial Services, 
Mutual Funds, Listed Entities, NBFCs, Insurance, IT and other sectors. The firm has also undertaken 
assignments relating to RBI, SEBI, NCLT, C&AG and other regulatory and special-purpose 
engagements. 
 
S Guha & Associates 
S Guha & Associates is a Chartered Accountants firm established in 1960 and practicing in India for 
over 6 decades. The firm comprises 21 partners and has multi-location presence across India. The 
firm provides services in the areas of Audit & Assurance, Accounting, Taxation, GST, Advisory and 
Corporate Services. The firm has experience in assignments relating to PSUs, Government, Banking, 
Insurance, Private Sector and Government-funded Projects, including World Bank and ADB-funded 
projects. The firm is empaneled with C&AG and RBI and has experience in assignments for various 
Government agencies. 
 
MKPS & Associates LLP  
M K P S & Associates LLP is a Chartered Accountants firm established in 1952 and practicing in India 
for over 7 decades. The firm comprises 30+ partners and more than 130 professionals and has 
presence across India through its offices and branches. The firm provides services in the areas of 
Statutory and Internal Audits, Bank Audits, Regulatory Compliance, Forensic Investigations, 
Transaction Due Diligence, Project and Infrastructure Audits and Tax Structuring. The firm has 
experience across Banking, Insurance, Oil & Gas, Infrastructure, Government, Renewable Energy, 
Steel and Mines sectors. The firm undertakes Statutory & Internal Audits, Limited Reviews, 
Group/Consolidation Audits, Ind AS/IFRS, Special-Purpose Audits, Forensic Investigations, 
Project/Infrastructure Audits and regulatory compliance assignments and is empaneled with C&AG, 
RBI, NHAI, SEBI and IBA.
