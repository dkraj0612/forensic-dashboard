Aarti Industries Sustains Growth Momentum in Q2 FY27, Led by 
Higher Volumes  
 
Records highest quarterly revenue and EBITDA, reflecting sustained business growth and 
operating performance 
 
Mumbai, October 30, 2026: Aarti Industries Limited (AIL), a leading global speciality chemicals 
company, today announced its financial results for the second quarter and half-year ended 
September 30, 2026, reporting continued revenue and EBITDA growth, supported by 
higher volumes. 
 
The Company sustained its growth momentum, with volumes up by over 30%. Higher export 
demand and effective pass-through of elevated raw material costs drove revenue growth. 
Reflecting this growth, the quarterly EBITDA breached the 400 cr mark to close at 418 cr, the 
highest ever for AIL. 
 
Evolving global trade dynamics are creating new opportunities for Indian speciality chemical 
manufacturers. China’s initiatives to rationalise industry capacity, alongside India’s expanding 
trade agreements with developed economies, including the UK and European markets, could 
strengthen India’s competitive position in global supply chains over the medium to long term. 
AIL sees opportunities to leverage these developments through its integrated manufacturing 
capabilities and established global customer relationships. The impact of this phenomenon is 
visible both in margin and volume improvements in NCB value chains. The momentum is 
expected to continue and extend to other value chains like DCB, NT, etc.  
 
Financial Highlights – Q2 FY27 
 
● Revenue from Operations: ₹3,887 crore, registering growth of 73% YoY and 48% QoQ. 
● EBITDA: ₹418 crore, compared with ₹291 crore in Q2 FY26, representing 44% YoY 
growth. 
● Profit After Tax: ₹169 crore, registering growth of 60% YoY. 
● H1 FY27 Performance: Revenue of ₹6,513 crore, EBITDA of ₹802 crore and PAT of 
₹324 crore. 
 
The company’s robust pass-through model supported passing on elevated input prices to 
customers, while absolute EBITDA remained protected, keeping EBITDA margins suppressed.

Business Highlights & Outlook 
 
● Zone IV Commissioning:  Following the commissioning of Phase I in September 2026, 
the Calcium Chloride (CaCl2) facility and part of the Multipurpose Plant (MPP) have 
commenced operations, with activities focused on stabilisation, customer qualifications 
and progressive commercialisation. Further capacities are scheduled for commissioning 
during Q3- Q4 FY27. With its multiproduct and multitechnology capabilities, Zone IV is 
expected to emerge as a key growth engine for AIL over the next 1-2 years, supporting 
portfolio diversification and expansion into value-added chemistries. 
 
● Strategic Partnerships: On October 13, 2026, AIL entered into a manufacturing and 
supply agreement with a global speciality chemicals company, spanning more than 10 
years and representing cumulative revenue potential of approximately ₹1,500 crore over 
the contract period. Commercial operations at the proposed dedicated facility are 
targeted for FY29. The Augene Chemicals JV is progressing towards commercial 
production, while Circularity JV remains targeted for commissioning in H2 FY27.  
 
● Capacity Expansion & Operational Efficiency: Fuel additives capacity expanded from 
290 KTPA to 360 KTPA, supporting higher volumes during the quarter. AIL continued to 
focus on cost optimisation, yield improvement, energy efficiency, and digital-led 
operational improvements across its manufacturing value chains. 
 
● Health & Safety Excellence: Aarti Research and Technology Centre (ARTC), AIL’s 
R&D arm, was awarded the Sword of Honour 2026 by the British Safety Council after 
receiving a Five Star rating in health and safety management, reinforcing the Company’s 
commitment to safe and responsible innovation. 
 
● Outlook: AIL expects to sustain its growth momentum, supported by export demand, 
progressing customer engagements and the commercialisation of new capacities. It 
remains focused on improving asset utilisation, operating efficiency, working capital 
management, and cash generation, alongside developing value-added speciality 
chemical platforms. 
 
Mr Suyog Kotecha, Managing Director & CEO, Aarti Industries Limited, said: 
 
“Over the past two years, our efforts have been directed towards strengthening the 
foundations for AIL’s next phase of growth. We have focused on expanding market 
participation, deepening customer partnerships, and building capabilities that let us 
serve a wider range of global requirements. These efforts are now driving stronger 
business momentum.

About Aarti Industries Limited 
 
Aarti Industries Limited (AIL) is a leading global speciality chemicals company with over four 
decades of expertise in complex process chemistries, scale-up and manufacturing. Established 
in 1984, AIL serves leading global and domestic customers across diverse end-use industries 
through a portfolio of 100+ products, supported by 16 manufacturing plants, four R&D centres 
and 250+ scientists. 
 
With strong capabilities spanning research, process development, engineering and commercial-
scale manufacturing, AIL continues to advance innovation, customer partnerships and 
sustainable growth. The Company has earned the EcoVadis Platinum Medal, placing it among 
the top 1% of companies assessed globally, and the 2026 Gallup Exceptional Workplace 
Award. Reinforcing its commitment to world-class safety standards, AIL's Aarti Research & 
Technology Centre (ARTC) has also received the prestigious Sword of Honour from the 
British Safety Council, along with a Five-Star Rating in its Occupational Health and Safety 
Audit. 
 
With deep expertise across multiple chemistry platforms, an integrated manufacturing footprint, 
and strong R&D capabilities, AIL continues to strengthen its position as a trusted Global 
Partner of Choice.
