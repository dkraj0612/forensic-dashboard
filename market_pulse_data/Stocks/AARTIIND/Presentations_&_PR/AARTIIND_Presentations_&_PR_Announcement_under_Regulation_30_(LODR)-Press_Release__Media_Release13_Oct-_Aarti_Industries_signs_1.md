Aarti Industries enters into a 10+ year strategic and dedicated manufacturing & 
supply agreement with a global speciality chemical company 
 
The dedicated long-term arrangement offers an estimated revenue potential of ₹1,500 
crores over the contract period. 
 
Mumbai, October 13, 2026: Aarti Industries Limited (AIL), a leading global manufacturer of 
speciality chemicals, today announced that it has entered into a 10+ year dedicated 
manufacturing and supply agreement with a leading global speciality chemical company serving 
diverse industries with a cumulative revenue potential of approximately Rs 1500 crores (about 
US$150 million) over its contract term. The agreement establishes a new customer relationship 
for AIL and supports the customer’s expanding requirements in India. 
 
AIL will set up, commission, and operate a dedicated manufacturing facility in India for a speciality 
chemical used in high-performance applications, based on the customer's proprietary process 
technology and a structured financial arrangement to finance CAPEX of less than US$25 million. 
The model combines global technology with AIL’s process scale-up, engineering, and operating 
capabilities to provide a critical manufacturing outsourcing solution to global companies. 
Commercial operations are expected to commence in FY2028–29, subject to applicable 
approvals.  
 
By localising manufacturing through a dedicated Indian setup, this arrangement will support 
supply-chain resilience and the customer’s growth plans in India. 
 
For AIL, the agreement broadens its global customer base, strengthens its strategic 
manufacturing portfolio and creates a platform to deepen the relationship as the customer’s India 
requirements grow. 
 
Commenting on the development, Mr Suyog Kotecha, Managing Director and Chief 
Executive Officer, Aarti Industries Limited, said: 
 
“This agreement brings a new global customer into AIL’s portfolio and reflects the 
confidence placed in our ability to establish and operate dedicated manufacturing assets 
to global standards. The model combines the customer’s proprietary technology with AIL’s 
strengths in scale-up engineering, execution and reliable operations. 
 
The long-term domestic supply arrangement provides sustained visibility while creating a 
capital-efficient platform for growth. It also gives both organisations a foundation to 
expand the engagement as the customer’s India requirements grow. We look forward to 
building this into a trusted and enduring manufacturing partnership.”
