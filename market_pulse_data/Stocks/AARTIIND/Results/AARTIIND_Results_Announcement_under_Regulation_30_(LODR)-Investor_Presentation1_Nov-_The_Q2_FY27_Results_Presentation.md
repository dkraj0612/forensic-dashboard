### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aarti Industries Limited (AIL) reported strong Q2 FY27 revenue and volume growth, bolstered by the commissioning of its Zone IV project and a significant 10+ year strategic manufacturing partnership providing substantial future revenue visibility. Long-term partnership with a global speciality chemical company, creating

Key Deal Highlights:
• Dedicated manufacturing and supply arrangement spanning 10+ years.
• Estimated cumulative revenue of approximately ₹1,500 crore (~US$150 million) 
over the contract period.
• AIL to set up, commission and operate a manufacturing facility in India using the 
customer’s proprietary process technology.
• Customer led financing for CAPEX of less than US$25 million.
• Operations expected to commence in FY29.
Strategic Significance:
• Adds a new global customer to AIL’s portfolio. Leverages AIL’s process scale-up, 
engineering and manufacturing capabilities.
• Supports customer localisation and supply-chain resilience in India. Creates a 
platform to deepen the relationship as the customer’s India requirements grow.
