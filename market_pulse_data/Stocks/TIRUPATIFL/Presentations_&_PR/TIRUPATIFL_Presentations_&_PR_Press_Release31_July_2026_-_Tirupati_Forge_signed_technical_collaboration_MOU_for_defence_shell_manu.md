## Page 2

Press Release 
 
Subject: Intimation of Technical Collaboration MOU 
 
Date: July 31, 2026 
  
Dear Sir/ Madam 
 
Tirupati Forge Limited is pleased to inform the Stock Exchange that the Company 
has entered into a Technical Collaboration Agreement with an experienced defence 
technology professional from Brazil for the development and manufacturing 
support of defence products. 
 
The collaboration will focus on the development of the following products: 
 
• 155 mm M107 Empty Shell Body 
• 155 mm ERFB BB/BT Empty Shell Body 
• Other Bigger size of bombs and ammunition components of various 
calibres, as may be mutually agreed. 
 
The objective of this collaboration is to leverage international technical expertise 
for product design, development, manufacturing processes, and technical guidance 
in 
line 
with 
internationally 
accepted 
defence 
standards, 
including 
applicable NATO requirements, wherever relevant. This MOU is valied till 
successful development of products. 
 
The Company believes that this technical collaboration will strengthen its product 
development capabilities, enhance its competitiveness in the global defence 
market, and support its long-term strategy of expanding its defence manufacturing 
portfolio. 
 
This collaboration does not involve any equity participation or change in the 
shareholding pattern of the Company. The agreement is expected to support the 
Company’s future growth in the defence sector.
