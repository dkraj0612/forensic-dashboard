Happiest Minds’ Arttha deployed by Absa's Spark in Mauritius for Digital 
Banking Services 
Bengaluru, Kolkata & Nairobi, November 18, 2026: Happiest Minds Technologies Limited 
(“Happiest Minds”) (NSE: HAPPSTMNDS), an AI First, customer-centric digital engineering 
company, today announced the deployment of its cloud-native digital banking platform, 
Arttha, to support selected capabilities within Spark by Absa, the digital banking and lifestyle 
app from Absa Bank (Mauritius) Limited. 
Developed and built in Mauritius, Spark is proudly “Made in Moris” and brings payments, 
banking services, offers and lifestyle experiences together for Absa and non-Absa 
customers. 
Arttha's Open Banking and Financial Marketplace modules power selected Spark capabilities, 
including account linking, payment options, offers and experience bookings. Happiest Minds 
provided technical integration and implementation support, while Absa Mauritius led the 
development of the Spark proposition and customer experience. 
Arttha is Happiest Minds Technologies' cloud-native digital banking platform, 
supporting banks and financial technology companies across markets with capabilities 
spanning payments, lending, core banking and open banking. 
Joseph Anantharaju, Co-Chairman & CEO, Happiest Minds Technologies, said: “The 
launch of Spark by Absa reflects the strength of our relationship with Absa Mauritius and our 
shared commitment to making digital services more relevant and accessible. Through Arttha, we 
are pleased to provide the underlying platform capabilities that support the bank in bringing its 
vision for Spark by Absa to market and creating greater value for customers in their everyday 
lives.” 
Prathamesh Kulkarni, CEO, BFSI, Healthcare & Life Sciences, and Retail & CPG, Happiest 
Minds Technologies, added: “Arttha is an important part of our digital banking portfolio. Its 
deployment in support of Spark by Absa demonstrates how underlying technology platforms can 
support banks in responding to changing customer needs and delivering more connected digital 
experiences. We value the opportunity to support Absa Mauritius as it advances its digital 
strategy.” 
Udeet Bhagat, Arttha Business Head, Happiest Minds Technologies, said: “Absa has been 
a valued customer in Arttha’s growth journey, and the launch of Spark by Absa further 
strengthens this relationship. Arttha provides selected underlying platform capabilities that 
support Absa Mauritius’ proposition, bringing banking, payments and lifestyle services closer 
together with the customer at the centre.”
