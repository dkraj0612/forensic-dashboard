Rating Update 
September 30, 2025 | Mumbai 
 
Banco Products India Limited 
 
Update as on September 30, 2025 
 
This update is provided in continuation of the rating rational below. 
 
The key rating sensitivity factors for the rating include: 
 
Upward Factors: 
 
• 
Improvement in the business risk profile, driven by geographical as well as product diversification, 
aiding revenue growth and increasing market share in the industry 
• 
Sustainable improvement in the operating performance, driven by revenue of more than Rs 4,000 
crore with sustenance of operating margin, while maintaining the financial risk profile and working 
capital cycle 
Downward Factors: 
 
• 
Significant fall in revenue or operating margin dropping below 12%, resulting in stretched working 
capital cycle or weakening of the financial risk profile 
• 
Weakening of the capital structure because of large, debt-funded capex or acquisition. 
• 
Sizeable reduction in cash surplus owing to higher-than-expected shareholder payout or buyback. 
Crisil Ratings has a policy of keeping its accepted ratings under constant and ongoing monitoring and 
review. Accordingly, Crisil Ratings seeks regular updates from companies on the business and financial 
performance. Crisil Ratings is, however, awaiting adequate information from Banco Products India Limited 
(BPIL; part of Banco group) which will enable us to carry out the rating review. Crisil Ratings will continue 
provide updates on relevant developments from time to time on this credit. 
 
Crisil Ratings also identifies information availability risk as a key credit factor in the rating assessment as 
outlined in its criteria ‘Information Availability Risk in Credit Ratings’. 
 
 
About the Group 
BPIL, incorporated in 1961, manufactures engine cooling systems including radiators, change air coolers, 
oil coolers – transmission and hydraulic, fuel coolers, battery coolers, inverter coolers, in aluminium and 
cooper/brass configurations and accessories expansion tanks, fan motor assembly, fan shroud and guard 
and miscellaneous mounting and plumbing accessories. Mr Mehul K Patel is the group’s chairman; he is 
supported by a qualified team. BPIL is listed on Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange. 
  
BGIL was hived off from BPIL in fiscal 2012 for better operational efficiency. BGIL is a wholly owned 
subsidiary of BPIL and manufactures metal and rubber gaskets, which have application as a sealing 
material in automobile and industrial sectors. 
  
NRF was acquired by the Banco group in fiscal 2010 and is a leading European manufacturer and trader 
of automotive, industrial, railway and marine, automobile air conditioning and heat exchanger products. 
Operations and distribution of NRF are located in major western and eastern European countries. 
  
BNECSL, incorporated in May 2021, is wholly owned subsidiary of BPIL and manufactures heat exchangers 
for railway and automotive industries.

Detailed Rationale
CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its 'CRISIL AA/Stable' rating on the long-term bank facility of Banco Products India Ltd (BPIL;
part of Banco group).
 
The rating factors in the established market position, with better-than-expected revenue for fiscals 2023 and 2024. Revenue
of the group increased at a compound annual growth rate of 21.43% for past five years, with year-on-year growth of 20% to
Rs 2,806 crore in fiscal 2024 (against Rs 2,344 crore in the previous year) backed by growth in revenue from Nederlandse
Radiateuren Fabriek B V (NRF), BPIL and Banco Gaskets India Ltd (BGIL) led by improved capacity utilisation with
enhancement of capacity in BGIL and repeat orders from existing original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), dealers,
distributors and better average realisation. The operating margin improved to 16% for fiscals 2023 and 2024, from 14% in
fiscal 2022. However, net cash accrual declined due to higher-than-expected shareholder payout and will remain a key
sensitivity factor. Also, operations are expected to remain moderately working capital intensive, with gross current assets
(GCAs) projected at 190-200 days for the medium term.
 
Financial risk profile remained robust, with networth of Rs 1,048 crore as on March 31, 2024, backed by healthy accretion to
reserve on account of improving revenue and sustained higher profitability. Despite increase in the overall working capital
limit utilisation and term loan availed in the foreign entity over the two fiscals through 2024, gearing and total outside
liabilities to tangible networth ratio (at group level) remained comfortable at 0.21 time and 0.89 time, respectively, as on
March 31, 2024. The financial risk profile should remain robust over the medium term, in the absence of any large, debt-
funded capital expenditure (capex).
 
The ratings continue to reflect the established market position, reputed clientele with low customer concentration risk and
robust financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to volatile raw material prices, large working
capital requirement in subsidiaries, significant dividend outflow and exposure to cyclicality in demand in the domestic
automobile industry.
Analytical Approach
CRISIL Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of BPIL with its 100% wholly owned subsidiaries --
BGIL, NRF and Banco New Energy Cooling System Ltd (BNECSL). The consolidated entity is referred to herein as the
Banco group.
 
Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation.
Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description
Strengths:
Established market position, reputed clientele base with low customer concentration risk: The promoters have
more than six decades in the industry; their technical expertise, management skills, strong understanding of market
dynamics and healthy relationships with suppliers and customers should continue to support the business. Besides this,
the group has a Department of Scientific & Industrial Research-approved research and development (R&D) facility
wherein new products are developed in consultation with various OEMs. This enables the group to work with OEMs and
develop customised products. It also gives a competitive edge to BPIL over many other automotive ancillary
manufacturers. The Banco group is the largest manufacturer of radiators in India and have been dealing directly with
various OEMs (55-60% of revenue), aftermarkets (~15%), and export (~30%).

Revenue is derived from various industries such as automobiles, agricultural equipment, power generation applications,
off-road vehicles (including farm equipment, industrial and earth moving equipment) and rail locomotives; this insulates
the business from adverse downfall in any particular sector. Revenue (of the group) has been rangebound at Rs 1,400-
3,000 crore during the past five fiscals and is estimated at Rs 2,742 crore in fiscal 2024.
 
Key customers are reputed and include Cummins India Ltd, Mahindra and Mahindra Ltd ('CRISIL AAA/Stable/CRISIL
A1+'), J C Bamford Excavators Ltd, Ashok Leyland Ltd, TVS Motor Company Ltd, Maruti Suzuki India Ltd, Hero
Motocorp Ltd, WABCO India Ltd, Tata Motors Ltd ('CRISIL AA+/Stable/CRISIL A1+') and Caterpillar. Revenue of BPIL is
well diversified with the top five customers contributing less than 35% of revenue, which indicates lower customer
concentration risk.
 
Robust financial risk profile: The financial risk profile should remain robust, in the absence of any large, debt-funded
capex over the medium term. Networth stood strong at Rs 1,048 crore as on March 31, 2024, backed by healthy
accretion to reserve on account of improving revenue and sustained higher profitability. Gearing remained robust though
moderated in fiscal 2023 as NRF availed a term load and reported increase in working capital utilisation. However,
gearing of the group was below 0.30 time during the eight years ended March 31, 2024, despite various dividend
payout. Debt protection metrics were strong, with interest coverage ratio of 21.5 times and net cash accrual to adjusted
debt ratio of 0.47 time for fiscal 2024. Cash and bank balance of over Rs 63 crore as on March 31, 2024, also aids the
financial risk profile.
 
Weaknesses:
Profitability is exposed to volatile raw material and large working capital requirement in subsidiaries: The
operating margin of the group was rangebound at 9-16% during the 10 fiscals through 2024 and 16.5% in fiscal 2024
due to fluctuation in raw material prices (50-60% of sales value) and volatility in operating margin in the subsidiaries --
NRF and BGIL. The operating margin of NRF significantly declined in the past due to fluctuation in input cost, increase
in employee cost, increase in other expenses driven by R&D costs and exchange rate difference. Furthermore, the
operating margin remains low for NRF as over 75% of revenue is being derived from the trading division. Due to
volatility in subsidiaries, the operating margin of BPIL at consolidated level gets impacted and maintenance of operating
margin over 15% would be monitorable.
 
Operations of the group have been working capital intensive, with GCAs of 199 days as on March 31, 2024, and are
largely dominated by inventory of 143 days and moderate debtors of 64 days. Inventory is higher as the group imports a
large portion of its aluminum requirements. However, the group has a running order of 60 days from OEMs, which
protects profitability against fluctuation in raw material prices. GCAs are expected at 190-200 days over the medium
term.
 
Significant dividend outflow: The Banco group followed an aggressive dividend policy, having paid out of its profit
after tax (PAT) as dividend over the three fiscals through 2024. The company paid an overall dividend of Rs 200 crore in
fiscal 2023 followed by Rs 243.16 crore in fiscal 2024, which impacted the overall net cash accrual over the two fiscals
through 2024. However, dividend payout over the medium term remains monitorable. Nevertheless, dividends are paid
out after meeting the internal business and financial requirements. The group will not be leveraging its balance sheet to
pay out dividends to its subsidiaries and not make any special dividend payments.
 
Exposure to cyclicality in demand in domestic automobile industry: The products of the group finds application in
various industries such as automobiles, industrial and earth moving equipment, agricultural equipment power generation
applications, off-road vehicles including farm equipment, rail locomotives and marine. Although key end-user industries
are diversified, they are correlated to economic cycles. Subdued performance of automobiles led to slowdown in the
industry, which in turn restrained the performance of the radiator industry and the turnover of the Banco group. As a
result, revenue is expected to remain exposed to any sustained downturn in demand in the domestic automobile
industry as over 40% of BPIL’s revenue is derived from the automobile segment.
Liquidity: Strong
Bank limit utilisation was just around 0.25% for the 12 months through March 2024. Cash accrual is expected at more than
Rs 130 crore per annum, against yearly debt obligation of Rs 2-3 crore over the medium term; the surplus cash will aid
financial flexibility. Current ratio was healthy at 1.76 times on March 31, 2024. Cash and bank balance was over Rs 60 crore
as on March 31, 2024 (against over Rs 29 crore a year ago). Low gearing and moderate networth should also support
liquidity.
Outlook: Stable
The Banco group will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of the promoters along with its established market
position and robust financial risk profile.
