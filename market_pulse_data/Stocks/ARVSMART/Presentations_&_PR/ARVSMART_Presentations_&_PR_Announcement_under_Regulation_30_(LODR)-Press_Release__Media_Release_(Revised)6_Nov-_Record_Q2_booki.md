PRESS RELEASE 
 
Arvind SmartSpaces delivers record Q2 performance  
with 110% surge in bookings 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
November 06, 2026 – Financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, have 
been announced by Arvind SmartSpaces Limited (ASL), the real estate development arm of the Lalbhai 
Group. 
 
Performance Summary – Q2 FY27 
 
Bookings stood at ₹ 906 Cr, as compared to ₹ 432 Cr in previous year, driven by both new 
launches & strong sustenance sale 
 
Collections amounted to ₹ 418 Cr, as against ₹ 236 Cr in previous year 
 
Revenue from operations stood at ₹ 157 Cr, vs. ₹ 141 Cr in previous year 
 
Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹ 45 Cr, vs. to ₹ 31 Cr in previous year 
 
Profit After Tax (PAT) for the quarter stood at ₹ 23 Cr, vs. ₹ 18 Cr in previous year 
 
Net Debt (interest-bearing funds) to Equity ratio stood at 0.26x as on September 30, 2026, 
compared to 0.29x as on June 30, 2026 
 
Launched Arvind Sylva, ASL’s largest vertical project launched till date, located at Sarjapur 
Road, Bengaluru in August 2026. Achieved sales bookings of ₹ 500 crore representing ~60% of 
total inventory within 30 days of its launch 
 
Launched Arvind Iconia in Vastrapur, Ahmedabad towards the end of the quarter. Achieved 
sales bookings of ₹ 81 crore representing ~21% of total inventory 
 
During H1 FY27, bookings stood at ₹ 1338 crore, registering 120% YoY growth, while collections 
increased by 77% to ₹ 754 crore. 
 
Business Development Highlights 
 
In September 2026, the Company entered into a joint development agreement for a 
residential high-rise project in Sarjapur Road, Bengaluru, with an estimated revenue potential 
of approximately ₹470 crore and a saleable area of around 3.60 lakh sq.ft. 
 
This acquisition, together with last quarter’s additions, takes the Company’s FY27 new 
business development to a total top-line potential of approximately ₹ 3,100 crore. 
 
Commenting on Q2 & H1 FY27 performance, Mr. Priyansh Kapoor, Managing Director & CEO, Arvind 
SmartSpaces Ltd.  said "We are pleased to report our highest-ever quarterly and half yearly bookings 
as well as collections during the quarter, this achievement is driven by successful launches, strong 
sustenance engine and disciplined execution across our projects. We have also made strong progress 
Highlights for Q2 FY27 (Comparison on YoY basis) 


 
Collections increased 77% to ₹ 418 Cr 




About Arvind SmartSpaces:  
Built on ~128+ years legacy of the Lalbhai Group, and established in year 2008, Arvind SmartSpaces is India’s
