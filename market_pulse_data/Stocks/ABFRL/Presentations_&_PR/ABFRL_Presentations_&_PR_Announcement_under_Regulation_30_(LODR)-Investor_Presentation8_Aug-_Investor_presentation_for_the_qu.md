### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited reported an 11% revenue growth in Q1 FY27, driven by strong performances in Pantaloons and Luxury segments, alongside narrowing losses in TMRW. However, this top-line expansion was overshadowed by a significant decline in consolidated profitability, with the net loss widening from Rs. 234 Cr to Rs. 249 Cr and EBITDA margin dropping from 9.3% to 8.2% due to lower other income and investments in newer ventures. This widening loss despite revenue growth, coupled with emerging input cost pressures and moderated demand, presents a key red flag regarding overall operational efficiency and profit conversion.
