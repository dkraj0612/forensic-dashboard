### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) disclosed a demand order totaling Rs. 33.42 lakhs, including tax, interest, and penalty, from the Assistant Commissioner, Chennai-South, related to alleged discrepancies in credit notes and E-waybills.
