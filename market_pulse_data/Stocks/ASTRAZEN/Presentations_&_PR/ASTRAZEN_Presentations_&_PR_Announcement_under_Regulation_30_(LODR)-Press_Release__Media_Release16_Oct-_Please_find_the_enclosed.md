AstraZeneca Pharma India announces new Mumbai corporate office, marking the next phase 
of growth through innovation 
Mumbai, 16 October 2026: AstraZeneca Pharma India Limited today inaugurated its new corporate office 
in Mumbai, marking an extension of company’s presence in India, reinforcing its ambition to transforming 
care by unlocking the power of science. 
The new office spread across 25,600 sq ft was inaugurated today by Honorable Shri Devendra Fadnavis, 
Chief Minister of Maharashtra alongside senior government officials, dignitaries from embassies and 
AstraZeneca team. 
AstraZeneca has introduced more than 25 new medicines and indications in India over the past three years 
and is currently running about 80 global clinical trials in the country, supporting the development of new 
treatments, strengthening India’s contribution to global research and getting the right medicines to the 
right patients, faster.  
Shri Devendra Fadnavis, Hon’ble Chief Minister of Maharashtra, said “Maharashtra has a strong and 
growing pharmaceutical and life sciences ecosystem, supported by skilled talent, leading institutions and 
robust healthcare infrastructure. AstraZeneca’s decision to establish its India commercial office in Mumbai 
reflects confidence in the state’s capabilities and its potential as an innovation hub. We welcome 
AstraZeneca’s continued commitment to Maharashtra and look forward to the opportunities this will 
create for collaboration, capability building and skilled employment.” 
“India is at an important inflection point in healthcare, with a growing need for earlier diagnosis, innovative 
treatments and more equitable access,” said Praveen Rao Akkinepally, Country President and Managing 
Director, AstraZeneca Pharma India Limited. “Our stronger presence in Mumbai strengthens our ability 
to work with partners across the healthcare ecosystem and advance solutions that address the needs of 
patients in India. Our strong workforce of nearly 5,000 colleagues across the country are united by a 
common ambition to follow the science, advance access to life saving therapies and help more people live 
better, healthier lives.” 
At the center of its ambition is the organization’s unwavering focus on pioneering newer access pathways 
so life-saving therapies can reach every patient that needs them. Working closely with governments and 
industry, AstraZeneca has focused on leveraging AI and building solutions across the continuum of care – 
from prevention, awareness, to diagnosis and treatment, at scale. This strategic move presents several 
opportunities to the organization for a closer and effective partnership with a vibrant and fast-evolving 
healthcare ecosystem across therapy areas it is present in-Oncology, Biopharmaceuticals and Rare Disease. 
Arun Krishna, Non-Executive Director on the Board of AstraZeneca Pharma India Limited and Area Vice 
President, Asia, AstraZeneca, said “India is an increasingly important contributor to AstraZeneca’s growth 
across Asia. The momentum of our business, the evolution of our portfolio, and the depth of talent and 
capabilities in the country provides a strong foundation for the opportunity ahead. Our new commercial

AstraZeneca is a global science-led biopharmaceutical company, it focuses on the discovery, development
