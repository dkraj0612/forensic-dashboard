Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform that the Company has received an order dated September 
17, 2026, from the Registrar of Companies (‘ROC’), whereby a penalty has been imposed on 
the Company and its erstwhile Officers, pertaining to a delay in filing Form MR-2 in 
connection with the appointment of Dr. Sanjeev Kumar Panchal as Managing Director of the 
Company in the year 2023. Dr. Panchal served as the Managing Director of the Company from 
January 1, 2023 to June 30, 2025.  
 
The delay in filing was on account of technical glitches experienced on the filing portal of the 
Ministry of Corporate Affairs (‘MCA’), whereby Dr. Sanjeev Kumar Panchal's DIN, was 
erroneously reflected as inactive due to alleged non-filing of DIR-3 KYC, despite 
inapplicability of this requirement at the time. 
 
In this regard, please find the details as required under the SEBI Master Circular 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 attached herewith as 
Annexure – A. 
 
Kindly take the above information on record.
