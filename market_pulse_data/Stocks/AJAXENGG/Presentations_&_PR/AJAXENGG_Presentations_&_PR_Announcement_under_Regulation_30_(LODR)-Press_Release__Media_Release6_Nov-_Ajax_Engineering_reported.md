Ajax Engineering Limited 
Resilient performance in H1 FY27; Increased Market Share in SLCM segment 
 
Bengaluru  |  06th November 2026 
Ajax Engineering Limited, one of the leading Concrete Equipment manufacturers in India, 
announced its unaudited financial result for the quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026. 
The company delivered resilient performance amidst a soft demand environment in the industry, 
led by disciplined execution, operational agility and prudent financial management. 
 
Key Operational and Financial Highlights for H1 FY27: 
➢ 
Increased market share – Amid softness in demand, Ajax’s retail market share in SLCMs 
increased to 75.5% in H1 FY27 compared to 70.7% in H1 FY26. This underpins the strength of 
the Ajax brand, the depth and reach of our distribution network and strong customer trust. 
➢ 
Growth – Revenue for H1 FY27 grew by 5.5% over H1 FY26, driven by volumes and price hikes. 
Robust growth in Non-SLCM business and exports in H1 FY27. 
➢ 
Margins largely stable – Gross Margin remained largely stable, with price hikes offsetting 
higher raw material costs. EBITDA Margin too remained broadly stable compared to H1 FY26 
despite certain one-time costs. 
➢ 
Strong focus on financial and operational excellence – Company remains focused on 
maintaining balance between growth, profitability, cash generation and working capital 
management. 
➢ 
Robust working capital and cash position – Working Capital for H1 stood at a healthy 22 days 
compared to 21 days in FY26. Cash balance stands at ₹ 11,511 million, providing considerable 
flexibility to pursue growth / inorganic opportunities. 
➢ 
Outlook – Company anticipates customer sentiment and demand momentum to improve in 
the second half of the year. As consistently maintained, the business is best evaluated on an 
annualised basis.

Commenting on the H1 FY27 performance, Mr. Shubhabrata Saha, Managing Director & Chief 
Executive Officer, said: 
“Infrastructure remains a key driver of India’s economic growth, with sustained investments and 
increasing mechanisation presenting significant long-term opportunities. However, near-term 
challenges, including lower-than-budgeted state infrastructure spending and delayed contractor 
payments, have impacted project execution and equipment demand. While growth in several states 
remains strong, it has been offset by regional softness elsewhere. Additionally, rising steel and 
crude-linked commodity prices led to a close to mid-single-digit increase in raw material costs 
during H1 FY27. 
We are expanding SLCM applications through greater private-sector engagement, accelerating 
UDAAN adoption through focused market activation and stronger dealership execution, while 
preparing to launch the ARGO 4000 to address a key gap in our product portfolio and strengthen 
our overall value proposition. Our SLCM retail market share increased to 75.5% in H1 FY27, 
representing an expansion of approximately 480 bps over 70.7% in H1 FY26. 
We have made significant progress in expanding our Non-SLCM business, leveraging the B2B 
channel, brand equity and distribution network to strengthen market presence, particularly in 
Batching Plants and Pumps. Encouraging customer engagements and contract opportunities 
position the business for steady growth in the coming years. 
Our focus on operational excellence remains unwavering, driven by lean manufacturing, stronger 
engineering capabilities, supply-chain resilience and cost optimisation. Key initiatives include 
supplier diversification and competitive sourcing. We have also implemented a further 1% price 
increase in September and will evaluate additional calibrated hikes over the coming quarters. 
Exports represents another growth driver for Ajax. We are witnessing encouraging traction and 
growing customer interest across regions such as Africa and Southeast Asia. Export revenue grew 
48% on a Y-o-Y basis in H1 FY27. While exports currently contributes a small portion of our overall 
revenue, we see good headroom for growth. 
Ajax has consistently demonstrated its ability to navigate challenging cycles earlier and emerge 
stronger. A track record of mid-to-high-teens CAGR over the past decade reflects the resilience 
of our business model, disciplined execution, adaptability and operational agility. We remain 
confident that these strengths, coupled with a measured, long-term approach and prudent 
financial management, will enable us to navigate the current environment while sustaining our 
growth trajectory. 
We expect customer sentiment and demand momentum to improve in the second half of the year. 
Cash balance of ₹ 1,151 crores provides significant financial flexibility to capitalise on future growth 
opportunities, including strategic inorganic opportunities.”

About Ajax Engineering Limited 
Ajax Engineering is one of India’s leading concrete equipment manufacturers. Ajax is the pioneer 
and market leader in the Self-Loading Concrete Mixer (SLCM) market in India, holding more than 
75% market share. The company manufactures a comprehensive range of products catering to the 
entire concrete value chain. The product portfolio includes SLCMs, Batching Plants, Transit Mixers, 
Concrete Pumps and Boom Pumps. Ajax is also the first Indian company to develop and supply 
Slip-Form Paver and 3D Concrete Printer. 
The company’s focus is on designing & engineering and operates on a lean and asset-light 
distributor-led model across 26 states in India. Ajax has the widest dealer network in the industry, 
with 68 Dealers having a total of 132 customer touch points across India. 
Ajax’s products are supplied to small contractors, mid-size contractors and rental companies, 
serving a diverse range of end use cases including rural real estate, rural roads, railways, renewable 
energy, irrigation, etc.
