## Page 10

Performance Summary and Outlook
9
Shubhabrata Saha
Managing Director & 
Chief Executive Officer
Reflecting on the H1 FY27 performance, Mr. Shubhabrata Saha, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, said:
“Infrastructure remains a key driver of India’s economic growth, with sustained investments and increasing mechanisation presenting 
significant long-term opportunities. However, near-term challenges, including lower-than-budgeted state infrastructure spending and 
delayed contractor payments, have impacted project execution and equipment demand. While growth in several states remains strong, it 
has been offset by regional softness elsewhere. Additionally, rising steel and crude-linked commodity prices led to a close to mid-single-
digit increase in raw material costs during H1 FY27.
We are expanding SLCM applications through greater private-sector engagement, accelerating UDAAN adoption through focused market 
activation and stronger dealership execution, while preparing to launch the ARGO 4000 to address a key gap in our product portfolio and 
strengthen our overall value proposition. Our SLCM retail market share increased to 75.5% in H1 FY27, representing an expansion of 
approximately 480 bps over 70.7% in H1 FY26.
We have made significant progress in expanding our Non-SLCM business, leveraging the B2B channel, brand equity and distribution 
network to strengthen market presence, particularly in Batching Plants and Pumps. Encouraging customer engagements and contract 
opportunities position the business for steady growth in the coming years.
Our focus on operational excellence remains unwavering, driven by lean manufacturing, stronger engineering capabilities, supply-chain 
resilience and cost optimisation. Key initiatives include supplier diversification and competitive sourcing. We have also implemented a 
further 1% price increase in September and will evaluate additional calibrated hikes over the coming quarters.
Exports represents another growth driver for Ajax. We are witnessing encouraging traction and growing customer interest across regions 
such as Africa and Southeast Asia. Export revenue grew 48% on a Y-o-Y basis in H1 FY27. While exports currently contributes a small 
portion of our overall revenue, we see good headroom for growth.
Ajax has consistently demonstrated its ability to navigate challenging cycles earlier and emerge stronger. A track record of mid-to-high-
teens CAGR over the past decade reflects the resilience of our business model, disciplined execution, adaptability and operational agility. 
We remain confident that these strengths, coupled with a measured, long-term approach and prudent financial management, will enable us 
to navigate the current environment while sustaining our growth trajectory.
We expect customer sentiment and demand momentum to improve in the second half of the year. Cash balance of ₹ 1,151 crores provides 
significant financial flexibility to capitalise on future growth opportunities, including strategic inorganic opportunities.”
