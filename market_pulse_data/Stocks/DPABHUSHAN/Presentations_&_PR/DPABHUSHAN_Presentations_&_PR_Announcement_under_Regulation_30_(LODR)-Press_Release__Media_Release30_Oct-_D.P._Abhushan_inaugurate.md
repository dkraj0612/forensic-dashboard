PRESS RELEASE 
D. P. Abhushan Limited Inaugurates its First FOCO Showroom in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh 
Madhya Pradesh, 30th October 2026: D. P. Abhushan Limited (NSE: DPABHUSHAN | BSE: 544161), one of 
India's leading jewellery retailers operating under the trusted "D.P. Jewellers" brand, is pleased to announce the 
inauguration of its new showroom in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh. The showroom marks the Company’s first 
operational outlet under the Franchisee Owned Company Operated (FOCO) model, representing an 
important milestone in its retail expansion strategy & this will be the 14th Showroom under the brand D.P. 
Jewellers. 
 
Located at Plot Nos. 104, 110 & 111, House No. 896-1/2, Niwarganj Extension, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh 
- 482001, the showroom has an approximate retail area of 3,750 square feet. The new showroom expands D. P. 
Abhushan’s presence in Madhya Pradesh and provides the Company with an opportunity to cater to customers 
across Jabalpur and surrounding catchment markets. The Company intends to leverage its established brand and 
retail operating capabilities, together with the franchise partner’s local presence, to progressively strengthen 
customer outreach and brand visibility in the region. 
 
Under the FOCO arrangement, the franchise partner has funded the inventory and showroom capital 
expenditure, while D. P. Abhushan will manage the day-to-day operations of the outlet. The Jabalpur showroom 
will operate as an exclusive D. P. Abhushan outlet, with its product offering and retail operations aligned with 
the Company’s established framework. The arrangement follows a pre-agreed revenue-sharing structure, under 
which the franchise partner’s participation is linked to the sales generated by the showroom. 
 
Importantly, the Jabalpur showroom is being introduced on a pilot basis. The Company intends to closely 
monitor its operational performance, customer response and overall effectiveness. The learnings from this pilot 
will form the basis for evaluating the scalability of the FOCO model and determining its potential adoption 
across other markets. 
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Santosh Kataria Managing Director said: “The inauguration of our Jabalpur 
showroom marks an important milestone for D. P. Abhushan as we commence operations under the FOCO 
model for the first time. We are approaching this format on a pilot basis, with a clear focus on understanding its 
operating dynamics, customer response and overall effectiveness before considering a wider rollout. 
 
The FOCO model provides us with an opportunity to expand into new markets with lower upfront capital 
deployment, while retaining control over showroom operations and customer experience. At the same time, we 
intend to remain measured in our approach. We will closely monitor the performance of the Jabalpur showroom, 
assess the learnings from this pilot and refine the model wherever required. Any future scaling of the FOCO

About D. P. Abhushan Limited 
D. P. Abhushan Limited, a renowned name in the jewellery sector, has been synonymous with trust and quality 
since its inception in 1940 under the brand “D.P. Jewellers.” Listed in 2017, the Company carries forward an 86-
year legacy in Central India, now managed by the 4th generation of the founding family. Renowned for its 
commitment to purity and transparency, D. P. Abhushan Limited has established itself as one of the most 
trustworthy and honest jewellery brands. 
 
With a rich history dating back to founders Late Shri Pannalalji Kataria and his sons, the Company has 
flourished into a prominent retail chain featuring 13 Showrooms across Ratlam, Indore, Udaipur, Bhopal, 
Ujjain, Bhilwara, Kota, Banswara, Ajmer, Neemuch, Dhar, a second showroom in Ratlam, and most recently, 
Dahod, marking its entry into the Gujarat market & the above New Store at Jabalpur takes the total to 14 
Showroom. D. P. Abhushan Limited offers a diverse range of high-quality products in Gold, Silver, Diamond, 
and Platinum, catering to various occasions with collections including Wedding, Valentine, Traditional, Flower, 
Mewar, Dohra, Lightweight, and Trendy Jewellery, alongside an array of Chains, Armlets, Rings, Pendants, and 
Necklaces.
