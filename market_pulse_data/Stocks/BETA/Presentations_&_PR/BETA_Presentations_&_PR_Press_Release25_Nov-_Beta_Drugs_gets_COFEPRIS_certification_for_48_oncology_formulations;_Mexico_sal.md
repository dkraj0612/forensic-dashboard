Beta Drugs Limited receives COFEPRIS certification for select Oncology Products, reinforcing Its 
global quality credentials and strengthening its position in the high-growth Mexican Oncology 
market 
November 25, 2026, Panchkula — Beta Drugs Limited (“Beta” or “the Company”) has received 
certification from Mexico’s Federal Commission for the Protection against Sanitary Risks 
(“COFEPRIS”) for select oncology products, including oral solid dosage forms and injectable 
formulations. The certification covers 48 oncology formulations including injectables, oral solids 
and oral suspensions. The certification paves the way for sales to commence from financial year 
ending 2027 
COFEPRIS is Mexico’s apex health regulatory authority, responsible for the sanitary registration 
and oversight of pharmaceutical products and is recognized by the Pan American Health 
Organization (PAHO/WHO) as a Level IV National Regulatory Authority — a distinction held by 
only a select group of regulatory agencies globally. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Rahul Batra, MD of Beta Drugs, said: 
“Receiving the COFEPRIS certification is a significant milestone for Beta. It is a testament to our 
unwavering commitment to global quality standards. This certification validates the robustness of 
our quality systems, our manufacturing processes, and our ability to meet the stringent regulatory 
requirements of one of Latin America’s most respected health authorities.” 
A Gateway to a Large and Fast-Growing Market 
The certification positions Beta to address a significant share of Mexico’s oncology 
pharmaceuticals market, which is valued at approximately USD 3.5 billion in 2025 and is projected 
to grow at a CAGR of approximately 10–11%. Mexico is the second largest pharmaceutical 
markets in Latin America, and oncology represents one of its fastest-growing therapeutic 
segments, driven by rising cancer incidence, expanding healthcare coverage, and an evolving 
regulatory environment that is increasingly supportive of high-quality generic and biosimilar 
products.

Reinforcing Beta’s Credentials as a Global Partner of Choice 
The COFEPRIS certification further strengthens Beta’s growing roster of international regulatory 
approvals and reinforces its credentials as a reliable partner for global pharmaceutical 
collaboration. By demonstrating compliance with the high standards demanded by a PAHO/WHO

companies, regional distributors, and institutional buyers seeking quality-assured oncology 
products from a dependable supply partner. 
Varun Batra, Join MD, Beta Drugs added. “This milestone is not just about market access — it is

healthcare systems, and ultimately patients can place in Beta. We see this as a strong foundation

regulated markets worldwide. Mexico represents a compelling growth opportunity for our 
oncology portfolio. With this certification in hand, we are well-positioned to capture a meaningful 
share of this large and rapidly expanding market, while making quality oncology treatments more 
accessible to patients across the country,”
