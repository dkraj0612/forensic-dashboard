Beta Drugs Limited (“Beta”) forms Oncology Joint Venture with 
IMGSA Groupe SPA (“IMGSA”)  
JV to acquire IMGSA’s oncology formulation plant for US$8.7 million; Beta to hold 49% and 
manage the operations of the JV 
 
 
Panchkula, Nov 19th, 2026 – Beta Drugs Limited (“Beta” or “the Company”) today announced 
that it has entered into a joint venture (“JV”) with IMGSA Groupe SPA (“IMGSA”), a prominent 
and leading pharmaceutical and medical devices company in Algeria. Beta will hold a 49% stake 
in the JV, with the remaining 51% held by IMGSA (the “Transaction”). The JV will acquire 
IMGSA’s oncology formulation plant for a total consideration of US$8.7 million. The plant is 
among few plants in Algeria for manufacturing of oncology formulations and has been qualified 
by Algerian regulatory authority for supply in Algeria tenders. Algeria oncology market is primarily 
a tender market and there has been a consistent impetus provided by the government of Algeria 
for localisation of pharmaceutical manufacturing. 
From a long-standing partnership to an equity partnership 
IMGSA is one of Algeria’s leading healthcare companies, with an established presence across 
antibiotics, insulins and medical devices. The group has a diversified industrial presence in 
Algeria through its operations in the field of agriculture, chemicals and other industrial sectors 
including healthcare and pharmaceuticals. Beta has had a long-standing relationship with IMGSA 
over the last 4 years, during which Beta has supplied semi-finished and finished formulations to 
IMGSA and assisted IMGSA in product registrations for Algeria market. The product registrations 
would be transferred to the JV as part of the Transaction. 
The JV is intended to deepen the partnership between Beta and IMGSA. By moving from a 
supplier to an Equity investor, the two companies expect to broaden the relationship and align

Acquisition of Oncology formulation plant 
As part of the Transaction, the plant being acquired by the JV has manufacturing capabilities in 
both injectable and oral solid dosage forms, giving the JV a broad platform to produce a range of 
oncology products locally in Algeria. The plant is among a few oncology dedicated plants in 
Algeria and has been qualified by Algeria FDA for supply for national tender. The JV will file 
dossiers for products registrations from the plant with support from our partner to enable 
accelerated launch of our products in Algeria and other North African markets 
Positioned for a fast-growing market 
The Algerian oncology market is valued at approximately US$1 billion and growing at a fast clip. 
Oncology is a tender driven market in Algeria and the government of Algeria has been 
incentivising local manufacturing of critical life-saving pharmaceutical products. The JV, with the 
acquisition of oncology formulation plant, is well positioned to capture significant market share in 
the national tender by combining Beta’s research, development, manufacturing and supply chain 
capabilities with the marketing and distribution infrastructure of IMGSA. 
Governance and management 
Under the terms of the agreement, the JV’s Board will comprise five directors, three appointed 
by Beta and two by IMGSA. The CEO/ MD of the JV will be appointed by Beta and will be 
responsible for the day-to-day operations of the JV. 
Management commentary 
Rahul Batra, MD, Beta Drugs Limited, said: “Our relationship with IMGSA has grown steadily 
over many years, and this joint venture marks a natural next step for us to deepen our relationship 
and commitment to the Algerian market. By bringing our manufacturing technology and know-
how to a well-equipped and qualified oncology plant and pairing it with IMGSA’s strong market 
presence in Algeria, we aim to improve access to quality cancer therapies for patients in Algeria.” 
Mr. Toufik Haouam, MD, IMGSA Groupe SPA, said: “Beta has been a trusted partner for many 
years. This equity partnership strengthens our oncology capabilities and supports our 
commitment to local manufacturing to meet the growing needs of Algerian patients. We believe
