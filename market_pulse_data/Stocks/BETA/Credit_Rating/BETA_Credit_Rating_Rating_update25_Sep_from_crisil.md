Detailed Rationale
Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB+/Positive’ rating on the long-term bank facilities of Beta Drugs Ltd (BDL; part of
the Beta group).
 
Crisil Ratings has taken note of the recent announcement made by BDL on January 09, 2026, regarding its acquisition of
66.09% equity stake in Nivian Life Sciences Private Limited (Nivian) for Rs 69.4 crore (enterprise value of Rs 105 crore).
Nivian is engaged in branded formulations focusing on In-Vitro Fertilization (IVF) therapy. This will enable Beta group to
broaden and enhance its product portfolio and bring in operational efficiencies for Nivian backed by Beta group’s
established manufacturing capabilities in complex formulations and strong supplier network. The acquisition will be funded
by cash consideration (Rs 20.8 crore) and share swap (Rs 48.6 crore). The acquisition is expected to be completed within
three months.
 
The rating continues to reflect the group’s established market position and diversified clientele, healthy financial risk profile,
and sound operating efficiencies. These strengths are partially offset by large working capital requirement, and exposure to
regulatory risks and raw material price volatility.
Analytical Approach
To arrive at  the  rating,  Crisil  Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of BDL with its  wholly
owned subsidiaries, Adley Formulation Pvt Ltd (AFPL) and Adley Lab Ltd (ADL); acquired during fiscal 2019 and 2020. This
is because all these entities, together referred to as the Beta group, are in the same line of business.
 
CCDs of Rs. 117 crores (coupon rate of 0.65% per month and a maturity of 18 months) issued by the group in November
2024 have been treated as 100% equity considering the compulsory convertible nature.
 
Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation.
Key Rating Drivers - Strengths
Established market position and diversified clientele: The promoter’s experience of more than two decades, strong
understanding of market dynamics, and healthy long-term relationships with customers and suppliers has aided the revenue
to grow with healthy CAGR of 25% in the last three fiscals ending 2025. Clientele of the group mainly include renowned
pharmaceutical companies and large government and private hospitals. Group manufactures oncology products and derives
revenue from diverse streams such as contract manufacturing, branded and export segments. It has also recently
diversified into derma segment. The acquisition of Nivian is expected to contribute to incremental revenue growth of the
group from the new IVF segment. Entry into a high growth segment supports medium to long term revenue expansion. The
diverse product portfolio, accreditations and strong sales network will continue to support the group’s business risk profile.
 
Healthy financial risk profile: Networth is expected to remain strong at around Rs. 350-355 crores as on March 31, 2026
(Rs. 305.5 crores as on March 31, 2025) driven by healthy accretion to reserves. Due to low reliance on external debt, total
outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOL/ANW) is expected to remain around 0.35 times as on March 31, 2026 (0.40
times as on March 31, 2025). Debt protection metrics were comfortably aided by heathy profitability: Net Cash Accrual to
Adjusted Debt (NCAAD) and Interest Coverage (ICR) Ratios are expected to be 3.5-4.0 times and 7-8 times respectively in
fiscal 2026 (NCAAD and ICR were 2.38 times and 10.7 times, respectively, in fiscal 2025). These metrics are likely to
remain healthy over the medium term.

Sound operating efficiencies: Operating efficiencies are expected to remain healthy over the medium term on account of
economies of scale and presence in critical ailment segment. Operating margin and Return on Capital Employed are
expected to remain healthy and in the range of 19-21% (20.6% in fiscal 2025) and 21-22% (27.1% in fiscal 2025),
respectively, in fiscal 2026.
Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses
Large working capital requirement: Operations have remained working capital intensive and are expected to continue in
the same line: gross current assets (GCA) are expected at around 260-270days on March 31, 2026, driven by inventory and
receivables of 75-80 days and around 100 days, respectively and large cash and bank balance. However, these are
sufficiently supported by creditors, internal accruals and working capital limits. GCAs are expected to remain in the range of
260-280 days going ahead. Efficient working capital management would remain a key monitorable going forward.
 
Exposure to regulatory risks, raw material price volatility: The pharmaceutical industry is closely monitored and
regulated and as such, there are inherent risks and liabilities associated with the products and their manufacturing.
Furthermore, the prices of key raw material (active pharmaceuticals ingredients) are volatile in nature. Therefore,
susceptibility to the risk of variations in commodity prices persists.
Liquidity Adequate
Liquidity is adequate as reflected from moderate bank limit utilization of around 33% at group level for the past twelve
months ended May 2025. Net cash accruals are projected to be Rs 60-65 crore in fiscal 2026 and will sufficiently cover
repayments of Rs 5.3-5.4 crore. Net cash accruals are expected to improve to around Rs. 80-90 crore per annum which
would be more than adequate against annual term debt repayment obligations of Rs 3.5-4.0 crores annually going ahead.
Current ratio is projected around 2.7 times as on 31st March 2026. The group had cash and cash equivalent of around Rs.
122.5 crores as on September 30, 2025.
Outlook Positive
Crisil Ratings believes that Beta group’s business risk profile will continue to improve backed by steady revenue growth and
sustained improvement of operating margins underpinned by diverse revenue stream  and healthy, long-standing
relationships with clients.
