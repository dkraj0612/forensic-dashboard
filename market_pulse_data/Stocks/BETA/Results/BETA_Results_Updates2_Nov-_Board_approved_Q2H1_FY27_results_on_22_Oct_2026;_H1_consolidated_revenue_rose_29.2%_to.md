[Management Discussion & Analysis| 
Beta recorded another strong quarter with impressive performance across its key businesses. 
Despite global headwinds, the Company demonstrated exceptional operating performance and 
is on track for a sustained growth in the medium term. Consolidated revenues from operations 
for the first six months of FY27 grew by 29.20% to INR 263.04 Crs. compared to INR 203.6 Crs. in 
the same period last year. 
The Branded Oncology segment grew by 17%. The growth was impacted by production shutdown 
due to debottlenecking and capacity expansion at Adley Formulation Plant. Sales were also 
hampered by supply constraints in platin APIs; consequently, the orders have moved to H2 of 
FY27. The Company expects to close FY27 Branded Oncology at INR 150 Crs. in line with the 
guidance. The Branded Oncology segment is poised for a sustained growth of 20%-22% over the 
medium term supported by accelerated new product launches and stronger brand adoption by 
oncologists. We have evaluated the recent NPPA notification related to trade margin capping at 
30% and are of the view that the recent amendments have no significant bearing on our 
realisations and / or our margins. We maintain our growth guidance for Branded Oncology and 
CMO in light of the recent NPPA guidelines 
The company’s growth was led by a 21% rise in CMO business. The high growth was on the back 
of new customer additions and inclusion of new products developed by Beta and offered to new 
and existing customers. 
Nivan delivered an exceptional performance in H1FY27, recording a pro-forma growth of 75%. 
The growth was aided by deeper penetration of key brands and increasing acceptability among 
prescribers. Nivian’s strategy of focused brand building among doctors witnessed stronger 
traction and the company has entered into marquee IVF chains as a supplier of key products. 
Exports segment rebounded in Q2 delivering 95% growth in Q2-FY’27. The Company filed 58 
dossiers across select markets of LATAM, MENA, APAC in H1FY27 laying the foundation for future 
growth. In Derma segment Beta signed an exclusive licensing agreement with Frezyderm for India 
market for high end cosmeceutical products. APl segment witnessed strong growth for H1 FY’27 
at29% with the additional capacity expected to go onstream in Q4FY27. 
The Gross margins expanded by 486 basis points to 56.21% in H1FY27 compared to 51.35% in 
H1FY26. The improvement in Gross Margins is a reflection of higher realisations across 
businesses. Going forward we expect to maintain the Gross Margin levels through cost reduction 
efforts which will be supplemented by backward integration at our API plant. Consolidated 
EBITDA stood at INR 61 Crs. compared to INR 47 Crs. for H1FY26 growing by ~30%. In terms of
