 Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations and other applicable regulations of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), 
Regulations 2015 (the Listing Regulations), we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of Beta Drugs Limited (“Beta”) at its meeting held 
today (i.e. November 19, 2026) have inter-alia considered and approved the proposal to enter into a joint venture agreement (“JVA”) with IMGSA 
Groupe SPA (“IMGSA”), a company incorporated under the laws of Algeria, for the purpose of establishing a joint venture company (“JV”) in 
Algeria to manufacture & market oncology pharmaceutical products.  
The JVA sets out the terms governing the relationship of Beta and IMGSA as shareholders, and the governance and operation of the JV. Beta 
will own 49% of the JV with 51% owned by IMGSA as per the local regulations of Algeria which limit non-resident ownership to 49% in the 
pharmaceutical sector. Beta will be responsible for running day to day operations of the JV and will have majority on the Board of the JV. 
Founded in 2003, IMGSA has emerged as a leading Algerian conglomerate having presence across Healthcare, Real Estate, Agriculture etc. It 
is a leading pharmaceutical and medical devices manufacturer and distributor based out of Algeria with presence across anti-biotics, biotech, 
medical disposables, APIs etc. Its products are also exported across North Africa and Europe including Tunisia, Morocco, Yemen, Mauritania, 
Italy and Spain. IMGSA recorded a turnover of US$ 100 Million for the year ended December 2025 with 30% of the turnover coming from 
exports. Beta has an established track record of supplying semi-finished/ finished formulations to IMGSA for 4 years and has supported 
IMGSA in oncology product registrations for the Algeria market. The JV cements the supply relationship into a long-term partnership. 
As part of the transaction, the JV will take over the Oncology Plant (“Plant”), currently owned by IMGSA for a total consideration of USD 8.7 
Mn. The Plant is equipped with production lines for Injectables and Oral Solid dosage forms and has been supplying to Algeria national 
tenders. The transaction is subject to customary closing formalities. The Company will make further disclosures in accordance with the Listing 
Regulations upon the occurrence of material developments in this regard. 
 
The meeting of the Board of Directors commenced at 9.00 a.m. and concluded at 10:50 a.m. 
Disclosures as required under the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 issued 
on 11 July 2023 and last updated on 30 January 2026 (“SEBI Master Circular”) are set out in ANNEXURE A and ANNEXURE B.
