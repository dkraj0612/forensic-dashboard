DLF Introduces ‘The Aureva’, a Thoughtfully Curated Luxury Retirement Housing 
Address in Gurugram, Designed Around Wellbeing and Convenience 
 
October 4, 2026, Gurugram, Haryana: DLF, India's leading real estate developer, 
today announced the complete sell-out of The Aureva, its thoughtfully curated luxury 
residential community in Sector 63, Gurugram in the retirement segment. The project has 
achieved sales of ~₹1985 crore, underscoring strong demand for thoughtfully curated 
luxury retirement living communities that seamlessly integrate luxury, wellbeing, 
convenience, healthcare access, and meaningful community engagement.  
 
Spread across approximately 1.6870 hectares (4.17 acres), The Aureva comprises 172 
luxury residences with a total carpet area of over 37,540 square meter (over 4 lakh 
square feet) with a total saleable area of over 7.5 lakh square feet. Designed as an exclusive 
enclave, the development caters to discerning homeowners seeking a vibrant and fulfilling 
lifestyle supported by world-class amenities, wellness offerings, hospitality-inspired 
services, integrated healthcare access, and opportunities for meaningful social 
connection. 
 
Commenting on the vision behind the project, Aakash Ohri, Managing Director and 
Chief Business Officer, DLF Home Developers Ltd., said: "The Aureva represents 
DLF's vision of redefining luxury retirement housing in India. As expectations evolve 
beyond traditional markers of luxury, residents increasingly seek homes that enhance 
everyday life through thoughtful design, holistic wellbeing, seamless convenience, and 
a strong sense of community. Conceived around this understanding, The Aureva brings 
together wellness, hospitality, healthcare, and luxury living within a carefully curated 
residential ecosystem. Every element of the development has been designed to create an 
elegant, future-ready environment that empowers residents to embrace their next 
chapter with confidence, dignity, and purpose." 
 
Conceived as an iconic standalone tower rising G+45 floors, The Aureva offers spacious 
4 BHK residences with dedicated staff dormitories, expansive private decks, premium 
finishes, and universally accessible design elements that enhance comfort, safety, and 
ease of living. Each residence is complemented by three dedicated parking spaces and 
access to a comprehensive suite of wellness, recreational, and lifestyle amenities.  
 
The development has been envisioned around the principles of wellbeing, independence, 
and community. Residents will benefit from a holistic amenity ecosystem featuring a 
luxury clubhouse, fitness and wellness facilities, an indoor swimming pool, spa, yoga 
pavilion, meditation centre, library, banquet space, landscaped garden, outdoor gathering 
areas, and curated social infrastructure. A professionally managed on-site medical centre 
with emergency response support further strengthens the project's integrated living

The Aureva has been designed in collaboration with globally renowned consultants, 
including HB Design as master planner, SHMA as landscape architect, BO Steiber 
Lighting Design, Hewshott for acoustics, and ESD Global for sustainability and 
green building advisory. 
 
Situated in the heart of Gurugram's premium residential corridor, the development 
combines expansive open spaces, curated landscaping, integrated healthcare 
infrastructure, and future-ready residences to create a distinctive luxury living 
destination in this segment. Strategically located in Sector 63, Gurugram, abutting The 
Arbour - The Aureva enjoys seamless connectivity to Golf Course Extension Road, 
Southern Peripheral Road, NH-48, and the city's leading commercial, educational, and 
lifestyle destinations.
