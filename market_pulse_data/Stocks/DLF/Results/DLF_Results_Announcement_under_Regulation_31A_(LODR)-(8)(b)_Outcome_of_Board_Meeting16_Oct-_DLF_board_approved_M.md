Dear Sir/ Madam, 
This is in continuation to our intimation dated 7th October 2026, regarding receipt 
of request from Madhukar Housing and Development Company (‘Madhukar’), 
an entity belonging to the ‘Promoter/ Promoter Group’ and holding nil equity 
shares/ voting rights of the Company for its reclassification to ‘Public’ category.  
In this regard and based on the recommendation of the Stakeholders 
Relationship Committee, the Board of Directors at its meeting held today i.e. 
Friday, 16th October 2026, has considered and approved the request for 
reclassification of Madhukar as ‘Public’, subject to the approval of Stock 
Exchanges. Certified true copy of the Board Resolution is enclosed herewith. 
The Board inter-alia noted that Madhukar has complied with the requisite 
conditions of reclassification as required under Regulation 31A of the SEBI Listing 
Regulations and would also continue to satisfy the requisite conditions, post 
approval of its reclassification. 
The reclassification will not have any impact on the operations of the Company, 
financial or otherwise, and will also have no impact on the existing shareholding 
structure including public shareholders. Accordingly, post reclassification, the 
Promoter/ Promoter Group will continue to hold 1,83,36,36,385 equity shares, 
representing 74.08% of the Company's paid-up equity share capital. 
In view of the above, the detailed application will be submitted to the Stock 
Exchanges within the stipulated timelines.
