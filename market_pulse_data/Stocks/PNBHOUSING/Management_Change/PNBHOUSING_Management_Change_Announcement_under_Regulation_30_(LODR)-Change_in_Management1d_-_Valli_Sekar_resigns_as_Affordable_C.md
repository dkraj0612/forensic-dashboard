Sub: Change in Management – Resignation of Senior Management Person 
-------------- 
 
With reference to the captioned subject, we hereby inform that Ms Valli Sekar, Chief Business Officer 
(Affordable business) has tendered resignation to pursue other opportunities. She will be relieved 
from the service of the company with effect from September 01, 2026. Copy of the resignation letter 
is attached as Annexure 1. 
 
We wish to inform that Mr. Satish Kumar Singh, has been designated as the Chief Business Officer 
– Affordable Business with effect from August 03, 2026. In his new role he will be responsible for 
both Sales and Collections in Affordable Business. 
 
The details of the aforesaid change as prescribed under Regulation 30 of LODR read with SEBI 
Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are 
enclosed herewith as Annexure 2. 
 
This submission is pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements), Regulations 2015. 
 
The above intimation will also be made available on the website of the Company viz. 
https://www.pnbhousing.com/ 
 
This is for your information and records.
