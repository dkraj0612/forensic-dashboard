Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is in context of the Composite Scheme of Amalgamation & Arrangement amongst 
erstwhile GMR Airports Ltd, erstwhile GMR Infra Developers Limited and the 
Company, which became effective from July 25, 2024. As informed vide our earlier 
intimation dated July 25, 2024, the Company had issued and allotted 2,79,40,26,763 
equity shares to Aeroports de Paris S.A. and 25,75,82,066 equity shares to GMR 
Infra Services Private Limited (subsidiary of Aeroports de Paris S.A.) ("GISPL"). As 
part of the said Scheme, GISPL was also issued 2,60,44,40,880 Optionally 
Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (“OCRPS”) of Rs. 40/- each, convertible 
into 40 equity shares of Re. 1/- each of the Company. 
  
In accordance with the terms of the issue of the said OCRPS, GISPL, issued a Notice 
of Conversion to convert a part of its OCRPS, being 25,50,000 OCRPS into Equity 
Shares of the Company. 
  
Accordingly, in line with the terms of the issue, 25,50,000 OCRPS have been 
converted, and the Company has allotted 10,20,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each 
(being 0.96% of the post allotment capital). The said equity shares will rank pari-
passu with the existing equity shares of the Company in all respects. 
 
This is for your information and records. 
 
Thanking you, 
 
For GMR Airports Limited
