Dear Sir / Madam,

Financial Results (Standalone and Consolidated) of the Company for the quarter and

Trading window closure  
 
In continuation to our earlier intimation dated September 28, 2026 and pursuant to

Designated Persons have been advised not to trade in securities of the Company 
either by themselves or by their immediate relatives during the period of closure of the 
trading window.
