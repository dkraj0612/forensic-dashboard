Press Release – Execution of Indicative Non-Binding Term Sheet for Proposed Acquisition

This is to inform you that Empower India Limited (“Empower” or the “Company”) has received and 
accepted, on an indicative and non-binding basis, a Term Sheet proposed by Mark AB Capital 
Private Limited (“Mark AB”) for the proposed acquisition by the Company of 100% of the equity 
share capital of Valiance Engineers Private Limited (“Valiance”), to be implemented primarily by 
way of a non-cash share swap through a preferential issue of fresh equity shares of the Company. 
 
The proposed transaction is subject to, inter alia, completion of due diligence, independent 
valuation, execution of definitive documentation and requisite corporate, stock exchange and 
regulatory approvals. Upon completion of the proposed preferential allotment, Valiance is proposed 
to become a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, while Mark AB is proposed to emerge as the 
principal shareholder of the Company pursuant to the shares to be allotted as part of the share swap 
consideration.

Empower India Limited Signs Non-Binding Term Sheet to Acquire 100% of Valiance Engineers in 
All-Equity Transaction 
Strategic consolidation to integrate global EPC and engineering capabilities including US-based 
Lauren Engineers & Constructors, with EIL’s digital infrastructure platform while maintaining 
balance-sheet discipline. 
Mumbai, India – September 16, 2026 — Empower India Limited (NSE: EMPOWER / BSE: 504351), a 
technology and digital infrastructure company, announced today that it has executed an indicative, 
non-binding term sheet with Mark AB Capital Private Limited (“Mark AB”) to acquire 100% of the 
equity share capital of Valiance Engineers Private Limited (“Valiance Engineers”).  
The proposed transaction will be structured as a 100% non-cash share swap via the preferential 
allotment of fresh equity shares of Empower India, establishing Valiance Engineers as a wholly 
owned subsidiary.  
The proposal comes as Empower India continues to explore opportunities in emerging technology, 
infrastructure, and engineering-led businesses. The Company has recently announced its strategic 
expansion into sustainable digital infrastructure solutions, reflecting its focus on participating in 
new-age infrastructure opportunities.

Key Investment Highlights & Strategic Rationale 
• 
Capital-Efficient Balance Sheet Protection: Structuring the acquisition as an all-equity share 
swap eliminates the need for debt financing or cash depletion, preserving Empower India’s 
working capital for scalable operational deployment.  
• 
Direct Ownership Alignment: Mark AB Capital, which currently holds ~70% of Valiance 
Engineers, will transition into an equity shareholder of Empower India, aligning institutional 
promoter-partner interests with long-term public shareholder value.  
• 
Immediate Global EPC & Engineering Scale: Valiance Engineers contributes an established 
operating base across Realty, Civil, Oil & Gas, and Public Infrastructure. Through its wholly 
owned US subsidiary, Lauren Engineers & Constructors Inc. (Texas, USA), the transaction 
provides EIL immediate access to tier-one global capabilities in modularization, process 
engineering, piping, instrumentation, and plant layout design.  
• 
Convergence with Next-Gen Digital Infrastructure: The engineering, modular fabrication, 
and procurement depth of Valiance directly complements Empower India’s expansion into 
sustainable digital infrastructure, high-density data center builds, and energy-transition 
projects driven by AI and enterprise digitalization.  
Management Commentary: 
"This proposed transaction represents a disciplined, value-accretive milestone in Empower India’s 
roadmap. By utilizing a share-swap framework, we are expanding our core operating platform into 
complex engineering and infrastructure while keeping our balance sheet unleveraged and liquid.  
Valiance Engineers brings critical process engineering expertise, an extensive domestic footprint, and 
proven North American delivery capabilities through Lauren Engineers. This structural combination 
equips Empower India to execute large-scale, sustainable infrastructure projects at the intersection 
of energy transition and digital growth."  
— Mr. Rajesh Chavan, Managing Director, Empower India Limited  
Target Overview: Valiance Engineers Private Limited 
• 
Establishment & Reach: Incorporated in 2017 (registered office in Delhi) and backed by 
Mark AB Capital since 2019, Valiance has built a multi-disciplinary infrastructure platform led 
by industry veterans with over a century of collective technical experience.
