### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Apex Frozen Foods Limited demonstrated robust Q1FY27 financial performance, achieving substantial year-over-year increases in profitability with EBITDA up 79% and Profit After Tax up 138%, driven by higher shrimp realization and effective cost management. Despite a slight decline in net revenue and lower sales volumes attributed to external factors like labor shortages and war-related transport disruptions, the company successfully expanded its margins significantly. While an increase in short-term borrowings and a decrease in cash on hand warrant monitoring, management asserts that debt levels remain prudently managed.
