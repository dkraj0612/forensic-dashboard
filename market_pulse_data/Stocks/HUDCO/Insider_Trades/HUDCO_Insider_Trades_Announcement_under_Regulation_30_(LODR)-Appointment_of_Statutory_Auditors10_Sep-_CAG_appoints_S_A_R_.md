Sub: Corporate Disclosure regarding appointment of Statutory Auditors 
        ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 
 
Sir/Madam, 
 
In line with the corporate disclosure requirement under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 2015, we wish to inform that: 
 
The Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG) has, vide letter No./CA. V/ 
COY/CENTRAL GOVERNMENT, HUDCO (1)/579 dated 8th September, 2026, in 
exercise of powers conferred under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, conveyed 
the appointment of M/s S A R C & Associates, Chartered Accountants as Statutory 
Auditors of Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO), for the 
financial year 2026-2027. The above firm of Chartered Accountants shall also be the 
auditors of Consolidated Financial Statements for the financial year 2026-2027 under 
Section 139 read with Section 129(4) of the Companies Act, 2013. 
 
Brief profile of the Statutory Auditors is attached as Annexure-A. 
 
This is for your kind information.
