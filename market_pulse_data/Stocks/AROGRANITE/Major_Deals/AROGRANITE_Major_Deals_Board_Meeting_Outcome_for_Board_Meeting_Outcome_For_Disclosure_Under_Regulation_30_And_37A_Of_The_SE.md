Sale on a going concern basis, subject to the approval of the shareholders by way of a Special 
Resolution via Postal Ballot, including the special public voting thresholds prescribed under 
Regulation 37A of the SEBI LODR and other stakeholders including Banks and other 
regulatory authorities.
