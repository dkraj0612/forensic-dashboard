Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 - Change in Senior 
Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company 
 
We hereby inform you that Mr. Ravi Kethana who is the Chief Platform Officer and one of the 
Senior Management Personnel of the Company has tendered his resignation on 18th September 
2026 to pursue the next phase of his professional journey.  
 
The Company has accepted his resignation, and he will be relieved from his responsibilities with 
effect from the close of business hours on December 10th, 2026 or any other date as may be 
mutually agreed. Accordingly, he will cease to be the Chief Platform Officer and Senior 
Managerial Personnel of the Company from the date of relieving.  
The Company places on record its sincere appreciation for his valuable contributions during his 
tenure and wishes him the very best in his future endeavours.  
 
The relevant details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, read with SEBI 
Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are 
enclosed as ‘Annexure A’. A copy of the resignation letter received from Mr. Ravi Kethana, 
containing the reasons for his resignation, is enclosed as ‘Annexure B’.  
 
This intimation is also being uploaded on the Company's website at www.camsonline.com 
 
We request you to take the above on records and disseminate the same on your respective 
websites. 
 
Thanking you, 
 
Yours faithfully, 
For Computer Age Management Services Limited
