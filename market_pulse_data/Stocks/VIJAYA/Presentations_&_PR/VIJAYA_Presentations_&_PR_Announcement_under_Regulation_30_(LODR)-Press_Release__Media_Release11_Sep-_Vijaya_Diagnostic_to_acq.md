Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Makes Strategic Foray into Assam, Strengthening its 
Eastern India Presence through the Acquisition of B2C Focused Integrated 
Diagnostic Centre, Arya Wellness Centre in Guwahati  
 
India, Hyderabad, 11th September 2026: Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited (NSE: VIJAYA | BSE: 543350), India’s 
leading Integrated Diagnostic Chain, today announced 100% acquisition of a well-established B2C focused integrated 
diagnostic centre, Arya Wellness Centre in Guwahati, Assam through a Business Transfer Agreement (BTA) for a 
consideration of ~INR 46 Cr. marking the Company’s entry into the North-East India diagnostics market. The 
transaction is expected to close within ~ three months, subject to the fulfilment of customary Conditions Precedent (CPs) 
and other terms and conditions as set out in the BTA.  
Aligned with Vijaya’s long-term growth strategy and its core principles of B2C-focused businesses, an integrated ‘All 
Under One Roof’ model and strategic geographic expansion, the transaction is a natural fit for the Company. It 
complements Vijaya’s growing presence in West Bengal and extends its proven concentric-circles approach into the 
North-East, creating a strategic platform for further expansion across the region. 
 
A Strategic Entry into an Attractive New Geography  
Guwahati serves as the principal healthcare hub for Assam and the wider North-East region, catering not only to the city 
but also to patients travelling from neighbouring districts and states for advanced healthcare services. The acquisition 
provides Vijaya with an established operating platform in this attractive market. 
Established in 2021, as a Joint Venture / AOP between North-Eastern Medical Research Institute Ltd. (NEMRIL), 
and Hexagonal Research Pvt. Ltd. (HRPL), Arya Wellness Centre is a leading B2C focused integrated diagnostic 
centre located in GS Road in the heart of the Guwahati’s prominent healthcare hub surrounded by several leading 
hospitals. It offers a comprehensive suite of diagnostic services across radiology and pathology, with capabilities 
spanning MRI, CT, ultrasound, mammography, X-ray, pathology tests and specialised services including high-end 
interventional radiology procedures and histopathology. 
The business, which operates a single centre, has built a strong B2C business, with over 85% of its revenues generated 
from the B2C segment, supported by an experienced team of doctors with strong clinical credentials. In FY26, the 
business recorded revenue from operations of ~₹26 cr. (Business Mix: 56% comes from Pathology and balance 44% 
comes from Radiology) with IND AS EBITDA of ₹5.6 cr. and IND AS Adjusted PAT of ₹2.5 cr. 
 
Commenting on the acquisition, Ms. Suprita Reddy, Managing Director & CEO, Vijaya Diagnostic Centre 
Limited, said: Our entry into Guwahati marks another important milestone in Vijaya’s journey towards building a high-
quality, integrated diagnostics network. We have consistently pursued a disciplined approach to expansion, focusing on 
markets with strong long-term demand for comprehensive and advanced diagnostic services. Guwahati is a natural 
strategic extension of our growing presence in West Bengal and provides an attractive gateway to the North-East. Arya 
Wellness Centre has been built on an ethos that closely aligns with Vijaya’s philosophy of delivering high-quality 
diagnostics. It is a unique asset in Guwahati with a balanced mix of advanced and basic pathology and radiology services, 
making it a strategically attractive asset for us. We believe this acquisition provides Vijaya with a strong platform to 
establish a meaningful presence in Assam and progressively expand across the North-Eastern region.

Mr. Narasimha Raju KA, Chief Financial Officer, Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited, said: The acquisition reflects 
our disciplined approach to capital allocation and our focus on pursuing opportunities that create long-term shareholder 
value. The transaction has been concluded through a slump sale structure by execution of a Business Transfer 
Agreement (BTA) at valuation of ~8–9x of FY26 IND AS EBITDA and is expected to be EPS accretive from the 
outset. Importantly, the acquisition provides us with an established and profitable operating base in Guwahati while 
maintaining our focus on capital efficiency and return thresholds. The Company will continue to evaluate both organic 
and inorganic opportunities where it sees the potential to combine strong market fundamentals, strategic fit, attractive 
valuations and healthy long-term returns on invested capital. 
Mr. Siva Rama Raju Vegesna, Chief Operating Officer, Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited, said: Arya Wellness 
Centre brings together a strong B2C business with comprehensive radiology and pathology capabilities, and an 
experienced clinical team, making it a strategic fit with Vijaya’s integrated diagnostics model. Our immediate focus will 
be on ensuring a seamless transition, strengthening the team, and ensuring the continuity of patient care and service 
quality. Over time, we look forward to leveraging Vijaya’s operating expertise, clinical capabilities and established 
processes to build upon our presence in Guwahati and support our calibrated expansion across Assam and the broader 
North-East region. 
 
About Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited 
Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited (Vijaya), is one of India’s largest integrated diagnostic chains, providing one-stop 
solution for pathology and radiology investigations through an extensive operational network spanning 160+ diagnostic 
centres and 27 reference laboratories in 27 cities and towns spread across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, 
Karnataka, NCR and West Bengal. The company offers comprehensive diagnostic services ranging from basic 
pathology to high-end radiology.
