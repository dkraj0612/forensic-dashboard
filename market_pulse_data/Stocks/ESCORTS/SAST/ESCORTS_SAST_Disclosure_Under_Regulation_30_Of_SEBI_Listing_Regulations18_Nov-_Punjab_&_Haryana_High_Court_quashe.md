Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) 
 
Dear Sir/ Ma’am, 
 
In compliance with Regulation 30 read with Para B (8) of Part A of Schedule III of SEBI Listing 
Regulations and Para 8 of Industry Standards Note on Regulation 30 of  SEBI Listing Regulations, it 
is to inform that the Hon’ble High Court of Punjab & Haryana, Chandigarh has vide its order dated 
November 04, 2026 allowed the Writ petition filed by the Escorts Benefit & Welfare Trust 
(Subsidiary of Escorts Kubota Limited) against the initiation of reassessment proceedings u/s 147 of 
the Income Tax Act, 1961, quashing the notice u/s 148 and subsequent order disposing off the 
objections raised by the Trust against the initiation of proceedings u/s 147 of the Income Tax Act, 
pertaining to the AY 2013-14. As last disclosed in the quarterly governance filing for the quarter 
ended September 30, 2026 the potential tax demand of Rs. 75.08 Crore was expected before the 
current update. Considering the above High Court order, no potential tax demand is expected to 
arise in this matter. 
 
The requisite disclosure pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-
POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is enclosed as Annexure I for Para B (8) of Schedule III 
of SEBI Listing Regulations.  
 
The date and time of occurrence of the event is November 18, 2026 at 12:44 P.M. (IST). 
 
Please take the same on record.  
                                                                                                                                                                 
Thanking You, 
Yours faithfully,  
for Escorts Kubota Limited
