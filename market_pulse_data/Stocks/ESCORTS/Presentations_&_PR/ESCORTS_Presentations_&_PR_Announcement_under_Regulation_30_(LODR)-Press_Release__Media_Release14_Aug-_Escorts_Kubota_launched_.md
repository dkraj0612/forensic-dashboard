Escorts Kubota introduces MU4502 and MU5002 with industry-first 8 Year Transferable 
Warranty 
 
Faridabad, 14, 2026: Escorts Kubota Limited has introduced two new tractor models, the Kubota MU4502 
at 45 horsepower (HP) and the Kubota MU5002 at 50 HP. They strengthen the company’s premium utility 
tractor portfolio in the 41–50 HP category, one of the largest and fastest-growing segments in the Indian 
tractor market. Both models carry an industry-first 8 Year transferable warranty, reflecting the company’s 
high confidence in the build quality and working life of these products. 
Both tractors are powered by the proven Kubota KQP4 Japanese engine. They are equipped with an 
advanced 12F + 4R Multi-Speed Synchromesh Gearbox that lets farmers pick the ideal speed for every job, 
from heavy tillage to haulage, so they can work faster, cover more ground and finish operations in less 
time. Pompa Lift with DSS (Dual Stabilizer Spring) Technology keeps implements steady and maintains a 
consistent working depth even on uneven ground, giving more uniform results. 
Escorts Kubota operates three tractor brands in India, Farmtrac, Powertrac and Kubota, catering to entry-
premium, value and premium segments respectively. The new MU4502 and MU5002 sit within the 
premium Kubota brand. With these two additions, the MU range now includes the MU4201, MU4501, 
MU4502, MU5002 and MU5502, offering farmers a comprehensive premium utility portfolio. 
The new MU4502 and MU5002 have been engineered for modern Indian farmers looking for higher 
productivity, comfort and multi-application performance. The tractors feature Kubota’s global design, 
inspired by the strength and agility of a bull, with full LED lighting for better visibility and a premium 
appearance. The fully covered flat-deck platform, premium bucket seat, suspended pedals, double-acting 
power steering and ergonomically placed controls keep operators comfortable through long working 
hours, and balancer shaft technology keeps vibration down. 
As a true multi-utility platform, the new models work with almost all primary and secondary tillage and 
farming implements, including super seeders, seed drills, potato planters, mini round balers and 
cultivators. The tractors handle major crops such as paddy, wheat, maize, pulses and soyabean, and take 
on haulage and commercial tasks as well. A reverse power take-off (PTO) supports specialised applications 
such as rotavators and straw reapers. Both models suit landholdings of 5 acres and above, and Escorts 
Kubota has launched them as a pan-India offering. 
Commenting on the launch, Nikhil Nanda, Chairman and Managing Director, Escorts Kubota Limited said, 
“The 41–50 HP category is one of the largest and most important segments in the Indian tractor market, 
and the new MU4502 and MU5002 strengthen our position in it with a more complete premium utility 
offering. Aimed at progressive farmers, existing tractor owners looking to upgrade, contract farming 
operators and custom hiring businesses across diverse cropping patterns and farming practices, these 
tractors reflect our strategy of focusing on the segments that matter most to India’s farmers.” 
 
Akira Kato, Deputy Managing Director, Escorts Kubota Limited said, "The MU4502 and MU5002 carry 
the strength of Kubota's Japanese engineering, built for performance and durability over a long working 
life. The proven KQP4 engine, matched with a 12F + 4R Multi-Speed Synchromesh Gearbox and Pompa
