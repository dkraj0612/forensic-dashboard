Sub: Regulation 29(2) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial
Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations. 2011
We refer to our letter dated January 09, 2026 wherein we had mentioned that as on January
08, 2026, the aggregate holding of the Schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund in "FIEM INDUSTRIES
LIMITED" ("The Company") was 7.07% of the paid up equity share capital of the Company
(Paid up Equity Share Capital being Rs. 26,31,96,600/- comprising 2,63,19,660 Equity Shares
of Rs. 10/-each).
Further, in accordance with the captioned regulation and other applicable laws / regulations,
if any, we wish to inform you that there has been an increase in the shareholding of the
Company by the Schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund by 2.36% as of August 13, 2026.
As on August 13, 2026 the aggregate holding of the Schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund in the
Company is 9.43% of the paid up equity share capital of the Company (Paid up Equity Share
Capital being Rs. 26,31,96,600/- comprising 2,63,19,660 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each).
As per Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and
Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 please find enclosed the details of change in the shareholding
in the prescribed format enclosed as Annexure 1.
