Brief Profiles of proposed Senior Management Personnel 
Mr. Amit Govil 
Mr. Amit Govil is a civil engineering professional with over 33 years of experience in the construction 
industry. He has worked with organisations including Kalpataru Ltd., Tricon Infra Buildtech Pvt. Ltd., Vascon 
Engineers Ltd. and Shapoorji Pallonji, among others. He brings extensive experience in construction 
operations and project execution. 
Mr. Rakesh Kumar Singh 
Mr. Rakesh Kumar Singh is a civil engineering professional with over 23 years of experience in the 
construction industry. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering and has completed a Senior 
Leadership Programme from XLRI. He has worked with reputed organisations including B.L. Kashyap & Sons 
Ltd., Shapoorji Pallonji, JMC Infraprojects Ltd. and Tata Projects, among others. He brings extensive 
experience in project execution, operations and leadership of large construction projects. 
Mr. Vishnudas Shanbhag 
Dr. Vishnudas Shanbhag holds a doctorate from Thames International University, France, and a Bachelor's 
degree in Civil Engineering from the University of Mysore. With over three decades of experience in the 
construction industry, he has held key leadership positions with renowned organisations including JMC 
Projects (India) Ltd., Godrej & Boyce Co. and Capacit'e Infraprojects Ltd. His expertise spans project 
execution, operational excellence and strategic leadership. 
Mr. Ram Mohan Rai 
Mr. Ram Mohan Rai is a seasoned construction professional with over 26 years of experience in the 
construction industry. He has significant experience in project execution and operations and has previously 
worked with HCC, R.L Dalal, Capacit'e Infraprojects Ltd., Adani Enterprises, etc. He brings extensive 
experience in managing construction operations and leading large and complex projects.
