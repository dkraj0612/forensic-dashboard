ARSSBL reports strong Q2 FY 27 financial performance:  Revenue grows by 20% YoY, 
PAT rises by 50% YoY and MTF Book rises by 38% YoY
▪
ARSSBL recorded 20% YoY growth in Revenue from Operations to ₹2,732 million despite a 
volatile market environment; H1FY27 Revenue from Operations rose 21% YoY to ₹5,193 
million.
▪
Q2FY27 EBITDA stood at ₹999 million, with a EBITDA margin of 37%, while PAT was ₹419 
million, with PAT Margin of 15%. H1FY27 EBITDA was ₹1,972 million, with a EBITDA margin of 
38%, and PAT stood at ₹810 million before an exceptional item with PAT Margin of 16%.
▪
Growth was largely driven by Non-Broking revenues, with Interest on MTF growing 36% YoY to 
₹496 million and Income from Distribution increasing to ₹327 million, reaffirming the 
increasing contribution of diversified revenue streams.
Mumbai, October 13, 2026: Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited (BSE:544530) (NSE:ARSSBL), 
announced its unaudited consolidated financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
The quarter witnessed broad-based growth across business segments. Revenue from Operations grew 20% 
YoY, supported by strong momentum in the MTF business, steady contribution from distribution income and 
continued client acquisition. Non-broking revenues increased 21% YoY, reinforcing ARSSBL's strategy of 
building a diversified and balanced revenue profile.

Commenting on the results, Mr. Pradeep Gupta, Chairman and Managing Director, said
The quarter was characterised by heightened market volatility arising from geopolitical issues 
and regulatory changes. Despite these headwinds, investor participation remained resilient, 
supported by sustained domestic inflows and continued growth in mutual fund assets.
Against this backdrop, we delivered a steady performance, with Revenue from Operations 
growing by 20.3% YoY to ₹2,732 million in Q2FY27. For H1FY27, Revenue from Operations 
increased by 21.2% YoY to ₹5,193 million, reflecting sustained business momentum amid 
volatile market conditions.
Growth was driven by healthy traction across both our broking and non-broking businesses. 
During the quarter, revenue from broking and related services increased by 34.0% YoY, while in 
non broking, our Margin Trading Facility (MTF) book grew by 38.2% YoY to ₹14,993 million. 
Assets under Management increased by 29.2% YoY to ₹99,990 million, further strengthening 
the diversification of our revenue profile and supporting a growth trajectory for the non-broking 
segment. Meanwhile, Assets under Custody grew by 13.2% YoY to ₹11,54,190 million, 
demonstrating the depth of our client relationships and the continued trust in our platform.
The Company continues to invest in technology to improve the customer experience. Our 
customer-centric approach is further supported by serving clients through our physical 
network. We remain confident that the Company will continue to progress along its long-term 
growth path.”

Mr. Roop Kishor Bhootra, Wholetime Director, added
ARSSBL delivered a resilient operating performance during the quarter, reflecting the 
strength of our balanced and scalable business model. EBITDA stood at ₹999 million in 
Q2FY27 and ₹1,972 million in H1FY27, with margins of 37% and 38%, respectively. PAT 
grew by 50.3% YoY to ₹419 million in Q2FY27 and by 28.7% YoY to ₹653 million in H1FY27, 
after exceptional items. PAT before exceptional items grew by 59.7% YoY to ₹810 million in 
H1FY27.
We continued to expand our trail-based revenues, which now cover 76% of our employee 
costs, further strengthening the resilience and visibility of our earnings profile. Our 
performance continued to benefit from multiple growth drivers. Notably, 55% of our clients 
have been associated with us for more than three years, reflecting the strength of our long-
standing client relationships. MTF interest income increased by 36.3% YoY to ₹496 million, 
while distribution income rose to ₹327 million during the quarter. The growing contribution 
from these businesses reflects our progress in building a diversified earnings profile with a 
higher share of recurring and non-broking revenues.
Going forward, we will remain focused on deepening client relationships, strengthening 
our technology infrastructure, and broadening our product and advisory capabilities. These 
efforts will support sustainable growth and create long-term value for all stakeholders.”
