ANNEXURE-I 
 
Brief Profile of M/s. S K Agrawal and Co., Chartered Accountants LLP 
Statutory Auditor of the Company for FY 2026-27 
 
M/s. S K Agrawal and Co., Chartered Accountants LLP, is a longstanding audit firm of 
Eastern India. The firm was established in the year 1968 and completed 57 years in 
the field of profession of chartered accountancy.  
 
At present, the firm is having total 24 Chartered Accountants out of which 17 
(seventeen) are FCA partners with several years of experience of whom 4 (four) are 
DISA qualified. The firm has 7 (seven) qualified chartered accountants and 1 (one) 
qualified cost & management accountant in its employment. With more than 200 staff 
and article assistants, the firm is capable of handling audit assignments including 
internal audit of large-scale enterprises both in public and private sectors.  
 
The firm has conducted statutory audit of 21 public sector undertakings in various 
sectors namely mining, steel, manufacturing, insurance, NBFC, agriculture, transport, 
engineering, telecommunications etc. and also performed as central statutory audit of 
UCO Bank for the FY 2020-21 to 2022-23. They have rich experience in conducting 
internal audit of PSUs and Banks in the last several years.
