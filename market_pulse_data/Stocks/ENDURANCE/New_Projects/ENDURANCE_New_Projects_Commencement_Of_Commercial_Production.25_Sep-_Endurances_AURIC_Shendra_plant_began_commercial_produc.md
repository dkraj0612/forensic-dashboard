Sub.: Commencement of commercial production at the plant in AURIC

Ref.:  Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is in reference to our earlier communication dated 16th May, 2024, a copy of 
which is enclosed for reference, regarding the approval of capital expenditure by the 
Company for setting up a greenfield manufacturing plant at AURIC Shendra, Chh. 
Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra.  
 
In continuation thereto, we wish to update that the said manufacturing facility has 
commenced commercial production effective today i.e. 25th September, 2026. The 
facility will manufacture aluminium die casting components catering to automotive 
sector, including ICE and EV segments as well as non-automotive applications. 
 
The plant has an initial installed capacity to manufacture 900 MT castings parts per 
month, expandable up to 1,100 MT per month. The plant has been strategically 
designed to support future capacity expansion by installing balance equipment. 
Such expansion will be undertaken when the Company secures further orders from 
its OEM customers.  
 
You are requested to take this intimation on record.
