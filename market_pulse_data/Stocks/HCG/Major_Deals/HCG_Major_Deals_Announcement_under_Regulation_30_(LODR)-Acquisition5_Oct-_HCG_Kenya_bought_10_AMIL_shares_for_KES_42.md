Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we would like to inform that Healthcare Global (Kenya) Private 
Limited (“HCG Kenya”), a step-down subsidiary of Healthcare Global Enterprises Limited 
(“Company”), has today, i.e., October 05, 2026, entered into a Share Purchase Agreement (“SPA”) with 
Meditec Systems Limited (“Meditec”) for acquisition of 10 (Ten) ordinary shares of Advanced 
Molecular Imaging Limited (“AMIL”) held by Meditec, for an aggregate consideration of 42,150 
Kenyan Shillings (approximately INR 31,288 as on October 05, 2026).  
 
The consideration payable under the SPA has been paid by HCG Kenya today. Consequent to the 
acquisition, HCG Kenya’s shareholding in AMIL has increased from 50% to 50.10% of the share capital 
of AMIL. Accordingly, AMIL, which was an associate of the Company prior to the acquisition, has 
become a subsidiary of HCG Kenya and, consequently, a step-down subsidiary of the Company. 
 
We are enclosing herewith the brief details of the above acquisition, as prescribed under the SEBI 
Listing Regulations read with the applicable SEBI Master Circular, as Annexure A. 
 
We request you to kindly take the above information on record and bring the same to the notice of all 
concerned. 
 
The above information is also available on the website of the Company.
