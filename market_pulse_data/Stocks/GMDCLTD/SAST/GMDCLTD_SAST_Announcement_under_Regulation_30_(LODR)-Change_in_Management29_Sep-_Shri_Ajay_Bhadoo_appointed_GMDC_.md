ANNEXURE-II 
 
Brief Profile of Shri Ajay Bhadoo, IAS 
Shri Ajay Bhadoo, IAS is an Indian Administrative Service officer of the 1999 batch of the Gujarat 
cadre, having over two decades of experience in public administration, policy formulation and 
implementation across diverse sectors. 
He holds a degree in Civil Engineering and a Master’s degree in Business Law from the National 
Law School of India University, Bengaluru. 
Shri Bhadoo has held several senior positions during his career, including Deputy Election 
Commissioner at the Election Commission of India, Joint Secretary to the former President of 
India, Secretary to former Chief Minister of Gujarat, Vice Chairman & Chief Executive Officer of 
the Gujarat Maritime Board and Commissioner of Rajkot and Vadodara Municipal Corporations. 
Shri Bhadoo worked as Additional Secretary in the Department of Commerce, Government of 
India and was also appointed as Chief Executive Officer of Government e Marketplace (GeM).  
Shri Ajay Bhadoo has now been appointed as the Managing Director of Gujarat Mineral 
Development Corporation Limited with effect from 29th September 2026, vice Shri Roopwant 
Singh, IAS.
