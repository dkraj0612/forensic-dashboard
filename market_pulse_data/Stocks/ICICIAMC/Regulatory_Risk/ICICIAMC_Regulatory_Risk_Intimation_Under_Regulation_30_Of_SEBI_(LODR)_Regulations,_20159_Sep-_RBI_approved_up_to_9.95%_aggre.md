Sub:   Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (SEBI Listing Regulations) 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations, we inform you that today at                       
6:24 p.m., ICICI Bank Limited has informed about receipt of the letters dated September 8, 
2026 from Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regarding the approvals granted to ICICI Prudential 
Asset Management Company Limited (the AMC) to acquire “aggregate holding” of up to 
9.95% of the paid up equity capital or voting rights in the following banks: 
 
• 
CSB Bank Limited 
• 
DCB Bank Limited 
• 
Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited 
• 
AU Small Finance Bank Limited 
 
The approvals are pursuant to the applications made in accordance with Reserve Bank 
of India (Commercial Banks - Acquisition and Holding of Shares or Voting Rights) 
Directions, 2025 dated November 28, 2025 (Master Direction). Accordingly, the term 
“aggregate holding” shall be construed as per the said Master Direction. 
 
The aforesaid approvals by RBI are subject to certain conditions including compliance with 
the relevant statutory and regulatory provisions.  
 
It is hereby clarified that the AMC shall undertake acquisition of the equity shares of the 
aforesaid banks as an investment manager for and on behalf of: 
 
• 
Schemes of ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund (MF), including investment strategies under 
Specialised Investment Fund; 
• 
Schemes under Alternative Investment Funds (AIF); 
• 
Clients of Portfolio Management Services (PMS) of the AMC.
