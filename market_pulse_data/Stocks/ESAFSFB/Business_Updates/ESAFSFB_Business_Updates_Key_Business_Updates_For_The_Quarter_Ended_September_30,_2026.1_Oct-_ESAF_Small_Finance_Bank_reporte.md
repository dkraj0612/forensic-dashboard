## Page 2

Management Commentary: 
 
1. 
Deposits: 
• 
Total deposits increased by 24.34% YoY to ₹28,466 Crore as of September 30, 2026. 
• 
CASA deposits witnessed a 9.91% YoY growth, reaching ₹6,645 Crore as of 
September 30, 2026.  
• 
The CASA ratio is 23.34% as on September 30, 2026 as against 26.41% as on 
September 30, 2025. 
 
2. 
Advances: 
• 
Gross advances increased by 25.05% YoY to ₹23,931 Crore as of September 30, 
2026.  
• 
The Bank had undertaken Technical Write off of the NPAs during Q2 amounting to 
₹625 Crore.  Had it not been done, the Year-on-Year growth would have been 28.32% 
and Quarter-on-Quarter growth would have been 5.78%. Accordingly, the gross 
advances as on September 30, 2026, would have stood at ₹24,556 Crore. 
• 
Advances including IBPC increased by 30.54% YoY to ₹24,981 Crore as on 
September 30, 2026. 
• 
MARG (MSME, Agri, Retail & Gold Loan) - MARG advances grew by 38% on YoY 
basis and stands at ₹13,938 Crore as of September 30, 2026.  
• 
Secured advances constitutes 65.19% of the gross advances as on September 30, 
2026 as against 61.20% as on September 30, 2025.   
• 
Disbursements remained stable at ₹16,608 Crore in FY27, compared to ₹16,607 
Crore in FY26. 
• 
CD Ratio computed as a percentage of gross advances to deposits stood at 84.07% 
as on September 30, 2026 as against 83.59% as on September 30, 2025. 
 
3. 
Total Business 
• Total business of the Bank (including IBPC) stands at ₹53,447 Crore as on September 
30, 2026 as against ₹42,031 Crore as on September 30, 2025. 
 
4. 
Distribution Network: 
• 
As on September 30, 2026, the Bank’s distribution network comprised 831 branches, 
722 ATMs and 1060 Customer Service Centres spanning across 24 states and 2 
union territories.
