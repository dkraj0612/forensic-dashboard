Subject:Intimation about Execution of Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) 
–Disclosure under Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
With reference to the above-mentioned subject, we wish to inform you that the Company has 
executed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Oremus Corporate Services Private 
Limited, appointing it as the Strategic Finance & Accounting Partner under the CDX 
framework. 
Under this partnership, Oremus will provide finance, accounting, compliance and advisory 
services to the Constituency Development and Sustainability Bridge (CDSB) Foundations being 
established by EQUIPPP in partnership with the Telangana State Legislature Secretariat. The 
engagement will follow a scalable model designed to support the foundations throughout their 
growth journey, commencing with the five pilot foundations. 
Oremus is a trusted finance and accounting solutions partner serving businesses across India, 
the United States and the United Kingdom. With over 24 years of experience and a team of 
more than 400 professionals, its service portfolio spans accounting, fractional CFO services, 
finance strategy and FP&A, taxation and compliance, payroll, HR, business process 
management, and risk and assurance. Oremus helps organisations streamline financial 
operations, strengthen reporting and drive sustainable growth through domain expertise, 
technology and process excellence.
