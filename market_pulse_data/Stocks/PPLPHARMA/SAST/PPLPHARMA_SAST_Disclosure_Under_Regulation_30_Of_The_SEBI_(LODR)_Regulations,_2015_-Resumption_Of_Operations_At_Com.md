Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is further to our communication dated 24th July, 2026, regarding temporary suspension 
of operations at the Company's manufacturing facilities located at Plot Nos. 18 and 19 – 
PHARMEZ - Pharmaceutical Special Economic Zone, Village Matoda, Sarkhej Bawla, NH 
8A, Taluka Sanand, Ahmedabad - 382 213, Gujarat, India, due to exceptionally heavy rainfall 
and flooding across parts of Gujarat. 
 
We wish to inform you that operations have resumed across substantially all areas of the 
aforesaid facilities, with only limited restoration and recommissioning activities ongoing in 
certain sections. 
 
The Company has completed a preliminary assessment of the impact on its assets and 
operations and has initiated the insurance claim process. Based on the assessment undertaken 
to date, the Company does not expect any material impact on its operations or financial 
performance.
