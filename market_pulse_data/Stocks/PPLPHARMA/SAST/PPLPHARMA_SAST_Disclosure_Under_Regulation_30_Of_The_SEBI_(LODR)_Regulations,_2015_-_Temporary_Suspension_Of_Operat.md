Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Temporary suspension of operations at Company’s 
facilities located at Ahmedabad, Gujarat  
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is to inform that due to exceptionally heavy rainfall and flooding across parts of Gujarat 
resulting in waterlogging, the operations at the Company's manufacturing facilities located at 
Plot Nos. 18 and 19 – PHARMEZ - Pharmaceutical Special Economic Zone, Village Matoda, 
Sarkhej Bawla, NH 8A, Taluka Sanand, Ahmedabad - 382 213, Gujarat, India, have been 
temporarily suspended as a precautionary measure. All employees, including contract staff, at 
the affected facilities are safe. 
 
The Company has initiated necessary actions to assess the condition of the facilities and to 
facilitate a safe and orderly resumption of operations at the earliest. The extent of the impact, 
if any, on assets, operations is currently under evaluation. Subject to the improvement in the 
weather conditions, the operations are expected to gradually normalize over the next few days. 
The assets at the affected facilities are adequately covered under the Company's insurance 
policies. 
 
For context, the aforesaid facilities together account for less than 3% of the Company's 
consolidated revenues. 
 
The Company shall provide further updates, including any material developments or financial 
implications, as appropriate. 
 
This is for your information and records 
 
Thanking you, 
 
Yours truly, 
For Piramal Pharma Limited
