Annexure A 
Brief Profile of Mr. Brian Guy 
 
Mr. Brian Guy is a seasoned pharmaceutical industry executive with over 25 years of 
experience in business leadership, commercial operations, strategy execution, and enterprise 
management. He currently serves as President & Chief Commercial Officer of Breckenridge 
Pharmaceutical, Inc., a subsidiary of Towa Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., where he has held full 
U.S. P&L responsibility since 2018. 
 
Mr. Guy has extensive experience in driving business growth, portfolio development, strategic 
partnerships, and organizational transformation within the pharmaceutical sector. He has led 
the expansion of specialty and institutional pharmaceutical businesses, managed global partner 
networks across multiple countries, and played a key role in corporate transactions, including 
the sale of Breckenridge Pharmaceutical to Towa Pharmaceutical in 2020. His prior leadership 
experience was with Aveva Drug Delivery Systems, Inc. where he was Director – Business 
Development. 
 
He holds an MBA from the University of Dayton, a Master of Science in Administration from 
Central Michigan University, and a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from Wright State 
University. 
 
He will leverage his extensive experience to drive accelerated growth and expansion of the 
Company’s Complex Hospital Generics business to make a positive difference by delivering 
innovative solutions that serve consumers and bring to life Piramal Pharma’s purpose of ‘Doing 
well and Doing good’.
