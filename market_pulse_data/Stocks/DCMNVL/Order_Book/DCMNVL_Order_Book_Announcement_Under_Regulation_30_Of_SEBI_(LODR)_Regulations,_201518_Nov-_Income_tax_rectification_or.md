This is to inform you that the Company has received a Rectification Order dated November 16, 2026  from 
DCM Limited (“Demerged Company”) via mail at 2:13 PM on November 18, 2026, passed under section 
154 read with section 147 of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (“Act”) from the office of Deputy Commissioner of 
Income Tax, Central Circle, Dehradun, along with the revised Computation Sheet and the consequential 
Notice of Demand under section 156 of the Act, for the Assessment Year 2019-20. By the said order, the 
tax on the addition of Rs. 25,89,48,981/- (Rupees Twenty-Five Crore Eighty-Nine Lacs Forty-Eight

disclosed by the Company vide its intimation dated April 09, 2024, has been recomputed by applying the 
special rate prescribed under section 115BBE of the Act, as a result of which the earlier notice of zero 
demand stands revised to a demand of Rs. 41,65,89,240/- (Rupees Forty-One Crore Sixty-Five Lacs 
Eighty-Nine Thousand Two Hundred Forty Only). The said order pertains to the erstwhile Textile 
Business Undertaking of DCM Limited which was demerged into DCM Nouvelle Limited under a 
scheme of arrangement.

Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2025/25 dated February 25, 2025 and Industry 
Standards note on Regulation 30 of LODR Regulations, the information required is given in the ‘Form A’. 
 
I, the undersigned state and declare that the information and details provided in ‘Form A’, in compliance 
with Regulation 30(13) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
is true, correct, and complete to the best of my knowledge and belief.
