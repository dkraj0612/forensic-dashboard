Regulatory Position and Remaining Process 
The GEDA letter confirms registration of the rooftop solar PV project. It is important to distinguish 
registration from final commissioning. The letter records a number of subsequent technical and 
regulatory steps, including: 
 Technical feasibility assessment by the relevant DISCOM. 
 Execution of the connectivity agreement with the DISCOM after technical feasibility is established. 
 Installation of the appropriate bi-directional meter in accordance with DISCOM specifications. 
 SLD approval and charging permission from the competent electrical authorities, where applicable. 
 Compliance with the Gujarat Integrated Renewable Energy Policy - 2025, GERC regulations and 
applicable ALMM requirements. 
Disclosure discipline 
Accordingly, the project should be described as "GEDA registered and under execution" rather than 
as fully commissioned. The commercial and operating benefits will begin only after completion of 
installation, statutory / utility processes and successful commissioning. 
 
Strategic & Operational Relevance 
Captive renewable power: The project is registered in CAPEX mode and the electricity generated is 
intended for Falcon's own use. This directly links the renewable asset with manufacturing operations 
rather than a merchant-power model. 
Potential energy-cost efficiency: After commissioning, captive solar generation can reduce the 
quantum of grid electricity required during solar-generation hours. Any actual savings will depend on 
irradiation, plant availability, captive consumption, tariff structure and other operating factors. 
Execution visibility: The DPR and site photographs demonstrate that the project is not limited to a 
regulatory application; rooftop marking and mounting-structure installation activity is physically 
underway. 
Sustainability of manufacturing platform: A dedicated rooftop solar asset can increase the 
renewable-energy component of power consumed at the Falcon manufacturing site and supports the 
Company's broader focus on operational efficiency and sustainability. 
Scalable operating infrastructure: Energy infrastructure at a manufacturing facility is an important 
part of supporting production operations. Progress on the solar project strengthens the supporting 
infrastructure around the Falcon facility as the broader manufacturing platform is developed.
