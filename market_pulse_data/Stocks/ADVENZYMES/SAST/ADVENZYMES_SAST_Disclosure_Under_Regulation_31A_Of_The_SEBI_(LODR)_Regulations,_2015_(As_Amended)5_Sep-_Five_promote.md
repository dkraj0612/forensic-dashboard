1.

2.

4. I do not have any special rights in the Company through any formal or informal arrangement,
including through any shareholder agreement or any other arrangement.
5.
I am not represented on the Board of Directors of the Company and am not a Key Managerial
Personnel of the Company.
6. I am not acting as a promoter of the Company and do not hold myself out as a promoter of
the Company.
7. I am not a person acting in concert with the existing Promoters or Promoter Group of the
Company with respect to the Company.
8. I have no direct or indirect control or influence over the management or affairs of the
Company,and there are no circumstances or arrangements pursuant to whichI could exercise
such control or influence.
