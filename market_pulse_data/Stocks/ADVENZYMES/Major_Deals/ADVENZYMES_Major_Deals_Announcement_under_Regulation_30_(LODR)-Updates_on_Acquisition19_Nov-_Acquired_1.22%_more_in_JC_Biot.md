Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Sub: Update on acquisition of additional stake of 1.22% in JC Biotech Private Limited, 
subsidiary i.e. increase in shareholding from 95.72% to 96.94%  
Ref.: Intimation dated August 08, 2026 
 
With reference to our intimation dated August 08, 2026, we would like to inform that the 
Company has completed the acquisition of additional stake of 1.22% in JC Biotech Private 
Limited, subsidiary [“JCB”] by way of purchase of 2,53,297 Equity Shares from few selling 
shareholders of JCB, for a consideration of ₹ 22.80 million i.e. ₹ 90 per Equity Share. In view 
of this, the shareholding of the Company in JCB has increased from 95.72% to 96.94% of 
paid-up share capital of JCB effective from November 18, 2026. 
 
Further, the Company is in the process of acquiring the remaining 3.06% equity stake in JCB. 
Upon completion of the said acquisition, JCB will become a Wholly Owned Subsidiary of the 
Company.
