November 25, 2026 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Sub: Update on acquisition of additional stake of 1.22% in JC Biotech Private Limited, 
subsidiary i.e. increase in shareholding from 96.94% to 98.16%  
Ref.: Intimation dated August 08, 2026 and November 19, 2026 
 
With reference to our intimation dated August 08, 2026 and November 19, 2026, we would 
like to inform that the Company has completed the acquisition of additional stake of 1.22% 
in JC Biotech Private Limited, subsidiary [“JCB”] by way of purchase of 2,53,294 Equity 
Shares from few selling shareholders of JCB, for a consideration of ₹ 22.80 million i.e. ₹ 90 
per Equity Share. In view of this, the shareholding of the Company in JCB has increased 
from 96.94% to 98.16% of paid-up share capital of JCB effective from November 25, 2026. 
 
Further, the Company is in the process of acquiring the remaining 1.84% equity stake in JCB. 
Upon completion of the said acquisition, JCB will become a Wholly Owned Subsidiary of the 
Company.
