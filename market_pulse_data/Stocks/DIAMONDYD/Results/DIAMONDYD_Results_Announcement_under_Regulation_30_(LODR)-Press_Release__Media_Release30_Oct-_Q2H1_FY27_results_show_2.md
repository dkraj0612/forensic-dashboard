Prataap Snacks Limited (PSL) 
REPORTS STRONG Q2 & H1 FY27 TOPLINE PERFORMANCE 
INCOME FROM OPERATIONS HIGHER BY 26% YOY  
QUARTERLY REVENUES SURPASS Rs. 500 Cr MILESTONE  
Indore, October 30, 2026: Prataap Snacks Ltd. (PSL), (BSE- 540724), (NSE- DIAMONDYD) a leading 
Indian Snacks Food Company has announced its financial results for the quarter and half year 
ended September 30, 2026. 
 
In Q2 FY27 (YoY Comparison), PSL reported: 
o 
Income from operations of Rs. 5,409.5 million, an increase of 25.9% over Q2 FY26 
o 
Operating EBITDA of Rs. 129.0 million compared to Rs. 229.0 Mn in Q2 FY26 
o 
PAT of Rs. (25.0) million compared to Rs. 46.4 Mn in Q2 FY26 
o 
Cash Profit of Rs. 139.7 million compared to Rs. 211.8 million in Q2 FY26 
o 
Cash EPS of Rs. 5.84 compared to Rs. 8.86 in Q2 FY26 
 
In H1 FY27 (YoY Comparison), PSL reported: 
o 
Income from operations of Rs. 10,313.8 million, an increase of 23.0% over H1 FY26 
o 
Operating EBITDA of Rs. 319.5 million compared to Rs. 409.1 Mn in H1 FY26 
o 
PAT of Rs. (0.3) million compared to Rs. 53.3 Mn in H1 FY26 
o 
Cash Profit of Rs. 327.7 million compared to Rs. 390.2 million in H1 FY26 
o 
Cash EPS of Rs. 13.69 compared to Rs. 16.33 in H1 FY26

Commenting on the Q2 & H1 FY27 performance, Mr. Amit Kumat – MD, Prataap 
Snacks Limited said.  
 
“We are pleased to report another strong quarter, with the Company crossing milestone of ₹500 
crore in quarterly revenue for the first time in its history. Income from operations grew 26% year-on-
year and 10% sequentially to ₹541 crore in Q2 FY27, building on the record performance delivered 
in the preceding quarter. This achievement reflects the sustained momentum in our business and 
the growing contribution of strategic initiatives undertaken over the past several quarters. 
Growth during the quarter was supported by healthy momentum in our Namkeen and Potato 
Chips portfolios, encouraging consumer response to recently launched products, and greater 
efficacy of our distribution network. Our investments in technology, including Sales Force 
Automation, are also contributing to improved sales productivity, wider market reach and 
stronger execution. Strategy for channel diversification continues to benefit us as quick commerce 
and modern trade are developing steadily as additional growth channels. Together, these 
initiatives have created multiple avenues for growth and strengthened our ability to capitalise on 
opportunities across the snacks category. 
On the profitability front, elevated input costs continue to present challenges for the industry. 
Prices of key raw materials, particularly palm oil and packaging laminates, increased further from 
elevated levels, while fuel and logistics costs also continued to exert pressure. We have responded 
through calibrated grammage rationalisation, selective adjustments to channel margins and 
trade schemes, and continued cost optimisation across operations. These measures are helping 
us offset a meaningful part of the inflationary impact while balancing margin protection with 
consumer affordability and market competitiveness. 
We have also achieved an important milestone in our proposed greenfield manufacturing facility 
near Indore. We have completed the acquisition of RLOP which holds leasehold rights, thereby 
securing access to the land required for the project. Going ahead, we will initiate the process to 
merge RLOP into PSL, thereby simplifying the corporate structure. We have commenced site 
development activities starting with preparatory work which will be followed by civil construction. 
We will simultaneously proceed with the procurement of plant and machinery, especially items 
requiring longer lead time. This facility will be instrumental in enhancing manufacturing efficiency, 
optimising operating costs and supporting our next phase of growth along with an enhanced 
margin profile. 
Looking ahead, we remain confident of delivering strong double-digit revenue growth in FY27, 
supported by sustained momentum in our core categories, distribution expansion, product 
innovation and growing participation across alternate channels. While the input cost environment 
remains challenging, our continued focus on productivity, cost discipline and operational 
efficiencies will enable us to protect margins and deliver sustainable, profitable growth.”

About Prataap Snacks Ltd 
 
Prataap Snacks Limited (PSL) is a leading Indian Snacks Food Company. It offers multiple variants 
of products across categories of Potato Chips, Extruded Snacks, Namkeen (traditional Indian 
snacks) and Cakes under the popular and vibrant Yellow Diamond and Avadh brands. PSL is 
focused on offering deep value to consumers through a variety of pack sizes at attractive price 
points. Its products are present across 27 states and 4 union territories in India, and it is one of the 
fastest growing companies in the organized snacks industry. 
Headquartered in Indore, India; PSL operates 13 manufacturing facilities of which 6 facilities 
(Indore 1&2, Assam, Kolkata and Rajkot 1&2) are owned and 7 facilities (Kolkata, Jharkhand, 
Tumkur, Karnal, Patna, Hissar and Gwalior) are on contract manufacturing basis. Its distribution 
network includes more than 5,000 super/sub distributors allowing it extensive reach across the 
country. PSL has a wide presence that is equally spread in metro cities and urban clusters as well 
as in rural areas and Tier 2 and 3 cities and towns. Its products are available at independent 
grocers and small retail stores in the lanes and bylanes of its key markets and it is now building up 
its presence in modern trade outlets and quick commerce platforms. 
Led by an able and experienced leadership and guided by an accomplished Board of Directors, 
PSL is a socially responsible corporate citizen with a strong focus on Corporate Governance and 
Internal controls. PSL is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE:540724) and National Stock 
Exchange (NSE:DIAMONDYD) in India.
