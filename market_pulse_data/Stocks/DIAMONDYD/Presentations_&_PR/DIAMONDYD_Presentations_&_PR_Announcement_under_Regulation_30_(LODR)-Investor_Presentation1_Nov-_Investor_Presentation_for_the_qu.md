## Page 28

MD & CEO’s Message
“We are pleased to report another strong quarter, with the Company crossing milestone of ₹500 crore in quarterly revenue for 
the first time in its history. Income from operations grew 26% year-on-year and 10% sequentially to ₹541 crore in Q2 FY27, 
building on the record performance delivered in the preceding quarter. This achievement reflects the sustained momentum in 
our business and the growing contribution of strategic initiatives undertaken over the past several quarters.
Growth during the quarter was supported by healthy momentum in our Namkeen and Potato Chips portfolios, encouraging 
consumer response to recently launched products, and greater efficacy of our distribution network. Our investments in 
technology, including Sales Force Automation, are also contributing to improved sales productivity, wider market reach and 
stronger execution. Strategy for channel diversification continues to benefit us as quick commerce and modern trade are 
developing steadily as additional growth channels. Together, these initiatives have created multiple avenues for growth and 
strengthened our ability to capitalise on opportunities across the snacks category.
On the profitability front, elevated input costs continue to present challenges for the industry. Prices of key raw materials, 
particularly palm oil and packaging laminates, increased further from elevated levels, while fuel and logistics costs also 
continued to exert pressure. We have responded through calibrated grammage rationalisation, selective adjustments to 
channel margins and trade schemes, and continued cost optimisation across operations. These measures are helping us offset a 
meaningful part of the inflationary impact while balancing margin protection with consumer affordability and market 
competitiveness.
We have also achieved an important milestone in our proposed greenfield manufacturing facility near Indore. We have 
completed the acquisition of RLOP which holds leasehold rights, thereby securing access to the land required for the project. 
Going ahead, we will initiate the process to merge RLOP into PSL, thereby simplifying the corporate structure. We have 
commenced site development activities starting with preparatory work which will be followed by civil construction. We will 
simultaneously proceed with the procurement of plant and machinery, especially items requiring longer lead time. This facility 
will be instrumental in enhancing manufacturing efficiency, optimising operating costs and supporting our next phase of growth 
along with an enhanced margin profile.
Looking ahead, we remain confident of delivering strong double-digit revenue growth in FY27, supported by sustained 
momentum in our core categories, distribution expansion, product innovation and growing participation across alternate 
channels. While the input cost environment remains challenging, our continued focus on productivity, cost discipline and 
operational efficiencies will enable us to protect margins and deliver sustainable, profitable growth.”
