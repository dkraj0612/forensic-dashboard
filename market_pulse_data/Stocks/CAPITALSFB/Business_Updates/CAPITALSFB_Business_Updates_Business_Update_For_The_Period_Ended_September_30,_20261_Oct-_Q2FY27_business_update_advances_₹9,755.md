Management Commentary 
🏦 Loan Portfolio 
Continuing with the growth momentum, the gross advances grew to ₹9,755 crores as of September 30, 2026, 
reflecting a year-on-year growth of 23.4% and quarter-on-quarter growth of 7.5%.  
 
The disbursements during the quarter increased to ₹1,095 crores, as against ₹805 crores in Q2FY26 reflecting 
a growth of 36.0%. The loan book remains well-diversified, with ~97% being secured, consistent with the Bank’s 
prudent, retail-focused lending philosophy. 
📊 Asset Quality 
Asset quality shows further improvement, during the quarter with Gross NPAs at 2.36% as of September 30, 
2026, against 2.47% as of June 30, 2026 and 2.70% as of September 30, 2025. The improvement reflects the 
Bank’s disciplined underwriting standards, proactive recovery mechanisms and the inherent resilience of its 
well-secured, granular and diversified loan portfolio. 
💰 Deposits 
Total deposits increased to ₹10,831 crore as of September 30, 2026, registering a growth of 16.2% year-on-
year and 2.2% quarter-on-quarter. CASA deposits remained healthy and stood at ₹3,734 crore, representing 
34.5% of total deposits, as compared to 33.9% as of September 30, 2025, reflecting continued strength of the 
Bank's retail deposit franchise and deeper customer engagement across its branch network. The Bank 
continues to have a stable, diversified and cost-efficient liability franchise to support the long-term growth. 
🔁 Loan-to-Deposit & Liquidity Ratio  
The average^ CD ratio of the Bank for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 stood at 85.4% which was 81.5% 
for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.  The Bank’s liquidity position remains comfortable, supported by 
a well-capitalised balance sheet that provides adequate headroom to sustain and accelerate growth going 
forward.  
 
^ average is calculated basis daily outstanding balances
