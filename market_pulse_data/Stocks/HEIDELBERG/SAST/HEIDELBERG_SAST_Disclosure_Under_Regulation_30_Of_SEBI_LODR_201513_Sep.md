Sub.: Disclosure under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to 
inform that the Company has entered into a Mine Development and Production Agreement (“MDPA”) for a period of 50 years 
with the Department of Mineral Resources, Government of Madhya Pradesh, in respect of the Sukha–Satpara Limestone Block 
situated in District Damoh, Madhya Pradesh. The said mining block, admeasuring approximately 216 hectares, was secured 
by the Company through an auction conducted in 2023 and has estimated limestone resources of approximately 63 million 
tonnes, as per the Geological Report of the Government. 
This intimation is also being placed on the website of the Company at Corporate Announcements. 
We request you to kindly take the above intimation on record.
