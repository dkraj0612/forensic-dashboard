Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is to inform you that the Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board (“APPCB”), vide its order dated 
September 5, 2026, addressed to the Company, has levied an Environmental Compensation of 
%21,01,80,000/- (Rupees Twenty-One Crores One Lakh Eighty Thousand only), alleging breach of the 
consent conditions and directions issued by the APPCB. The said order was received by the Company 
by email on September 7, 2026 at about 3:41 p.m. 
The said order constitutes a material event in terms of Regulation 30(4)(i)(c) of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with the Company's Policy for 
Determination of Materiality of Events and Information. 
The Company is presently examining the aforesaid order and the basis for levy of the Environmental 
Compensation and shall take appropriate steps, including responding to the same and pursuing such 
remedial and legal measures as may be considered necessary to safeguard its interests. 
The Company remains committed to compliance with applicable environmental laws and regulatory 
requirements and continues to engage with the concerned regulatory authorities in relation to 
environmental and operational matters.
