Aavas Financiers Limited (Aavas) Crosses Rs. 25,000 Crore AUM Milestone, Reinforcing its 
Commitment to Financing the Dreams of Aspirational India 
 
Jaipur, November 1, 2026:  Aavas, one of India’s leading affordable housing finance companies, has 
crossed Rs. 25,000 Crore milestone in Assets Under Management (AUM), marking a significant 
achievement in its journey of enabling homeownership and expanding access to formal housing 
finance across underserved and emerging markets. 
 
The achievement reflects the trust and confidence of its customers, the dedication of employees, and 
the enduring support of its stakeholders. Since inception, Aavas has enabled more than 3.8 lakh 
families to access housing finance, supported by a strong network of 443 branches and a permanent 
workforce of over 7,500 employees. 
 
More than a financial milestone, Rs. 25,000 Crore AUM represents the aspirations of families seeking 
the security, dignity, and pride of owning a home. It reinforces Aavas’ commitment that housing 
finance is not simply about extending credit, but about creating opportunities, strengthening 
communities, and enabling lasting economic progress. 
 
A Journey Built on People, Performance and Perseverance 
 
Aavas’ growth has been anchored in its commitment to serving low- and middle-income households, 
particularly self-employed individuals, small business owners, and customers with informal or 
assessed income profiles who have traditionally faced barriers in accessing formal credit. 
 
The Company’s progress reflects a differentiated business model built on deep local market 
understanding, prudent underwriting, responsible lending, technology-enabled processes, and strong 
customer relationships. 
 
Guided by its philosophy of People. Performance. Perseverance., Aavas continues to strengthen its 
distribution capabilities, enhance operational efficiency, and invest in digital transformation while 
remaining firmly committed to asset quality, governance, and sustainable growth. 
 
Leadership Perspective 
 
“Crossing Rs. 25,000 Crore in AUM is a proud and defining milestone for Aavas. However, the true 
measure of our success lies not merely in the size of our loan book, but the difference we have made 
in the lives of more than 3.8 lakh families who have placed their trust in us. This achievement belongs 
to our customers, our 7,500-plus colleagues, and the many stakeholders who have supported and 
believed in our journey. We express our sincere gratitude to the National Housing Bank, the Reserve 
Bank of India for its guidance and oversight; to our lending and financial partners for their continued 
confidence; to the rating agencies for their independent assessment and engagement; and to our 
investors and shareholders for their enduring trust and support. As we enter the next phase of our 
journey, our focus remains on building a stronger, more agile, technology-enabled, and customer-
centric institution. We are committed to scaling responsibly, maintaining the highest standards of 
governance and risk discipline, and taking affordable housing finance closer to every deserving Indian 
household. For us, this milestone is not a destination. It is a foundation for the opportunities and 
responsibilities that lie ahead.” 
 
Manu Singh, Managing Director & CEO

Beyond Rs. 25,000 Crore: Building Homes, Enabling Futures 
 
Homeownership remains one of the most meaningful financial aspirations for Indian families. It 
represents stability, dignity, security, and the promise of a better tomorrow. 
 
As India progresses towards its vision of Viksit Bharat, Aavas remains committed to advancing 
financial inclusion and supporting the country’s housing aspirations by making responsible housing

With an expanding distribution network, a committed workforce, and a growing customer franchise, 
Aavas is well-positioned to pursue the opportunities presented by India’s evolving affordable housing 
landscape. 
 
The company remains focused on creating enduring value for customers, employees, investors, 
lending partners, and the communities it serves. 
 
About Aavas Financiers Limited 
 
Aavas Financiers Limited is one of India’s leading affordable housing finance companies, focused on 
providing housing loans to low- and middle-income households, particularly self-employed individuals 
and customers with informal or assessed income profiles. 
 
Headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, the company has served more than 3.8 lakh families since 
inception and operates through a network of 443 branches, supported by over 7,500 permanent 
employees. 
 
Driven by its purpose of enabling homeownership, Aavas combines deep market understanding, 
responsible lending practices, and technology-enabled customer solutions to expand access to formal 
housing finance.
