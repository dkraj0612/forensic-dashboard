PRESS RELEASE 
 
Aartech Solonics Limited’s Wholly Owned Subsidiary - Faradigm Ultracapacitors Pvt. Ltd. Signs 
MoU with Shenzhen Titan Smart Energy Technology Co. Ltd. to explore Energy Storage Cooperation 
in India 
 
Bhopal, India | October 19, 2026: Faradigm Ultracapacitors Pvt. Ltd., a wholly-owned subsidiary of 
Aartech Solonics Limited, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Shenzhen Titan Smart 
Energy Technology Co. Ltd., China, to explore strategic cooperation in advanced energy storage solutions 
and the development of localized manufacturing and assembly capabilities in India. 
 
The proposed collaboration will bring together Titan’s expertise in energy storage technology and 
manufacturing with Faradigm and Aartech’s industry experience, market presence and planned 
manufacturing infrastructure in India. The initiative is aimed at exploring solutions suited to the growing 
requirements of commercial, industrial and other energy storage applications in the country. 
 
 
 
A key enabler of this vision is FaraDigm’s upcoming manufacturing facility at the Renewable Energy 
Manufacturing Cluster at Mohasa-Babai, Madhya Pradesh, which is planned to commence operations 
in FY 2027–28. The facility is expected to strengthen the company’s capabilities in advanced energy 
technologies and support the development of energy storage solutions and localized manufacturing 
opportunities in India.

The proposed cooperation will initially focus on Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP)-based Battery Energy 
Storage Systems (BESS), covering capacities from 100 kWh to 5 MWh, including Commercial and 
Industrial (C&I) energy storage applications and integrated hybrid solutions combining solar power, 
diesel generators and battery storage. Plans to enter into Utility Scale projects are currently under review. 
The partnership will explore manufacturing and technology licensing opportunities with the objective of 
addressing India’s growing demand for reliable, efficient, and sustainable energy storage solutions. 
 
Driven by plummeting lithium-ion costs and stringent green mandates, India’s Commercial and 
Industrial (C&I) BESS opportunity is transitioning from a sub-1 GWh base in 2025 to a massive 22–31 GWh 
projected capacity by 2032, capturing a sub-market CAGR of 36.2% (contributing heavily to a broader 
national sector projected to grow at a 91% CAGR up to 302 GWh by FY32). 
 
Aartech Solonics Limited’s participation in the MP Solar + Storage Expo 2026 further reflects the 
company’s interest in understanding emerging technologies and exploring opportunities in the energy 
storage sector. 
 
 
 About Faradigm Ultracapacitors Pvt. Ltd. 
Faradigm Ultracapacitors Pvt. Ltd. is a wholly owned subsidiary of Aartech Solonics Limited, an Indian 
engineering company with over four decades of experience in specialized electrical and power solutions. 
Faradigm is focused on advanced energy storage technologies and aims to contribute to the 
development of innovative and sustainable energy solutions. 
 
 
About Shenzhen Titan Smart Energy Technology Co., Ltd. 
Shenzhen Titan Smart Energy Technology Co., Ltd. is a China based energy storage technology company 
engaged in research and development, intelligent manufacturing, and digital operation and maintenance 
of energy storage systems for global markets. The company reports a portfolio of more than 300 projects, 
cumulative deployments exceeding 3GWh, and over 200 proprietary patents and intellectual property 
assets in the energy storage domain.
