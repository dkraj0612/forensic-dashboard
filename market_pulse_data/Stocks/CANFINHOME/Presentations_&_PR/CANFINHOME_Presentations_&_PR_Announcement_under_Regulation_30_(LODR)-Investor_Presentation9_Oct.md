### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Can Fin Homes Limited reported robust H1 FY27 growth in loan book, Net Interest Income, and Profit After Tax, coupled with improved asset quality through lower NPA ratios. However, Q2 FY27 disbursements saw a year-over-year decline, materially impacted by operational disruptions during the rollout of "PROJECT TEJAS," a significant IT transformation initiative, which explicitly reduced disbursals in affected branches. This operational challenge, alongside a slight compression in Q2 profitability metrics like Net Interest Margin and Return on Equity, presents a key area for monitoring despite the strategic focus on digital acceleration.

PROJECT TEJAS
It gives me immense pleasure to announce the successful completion of the IT Transformation Program at 
Can Fin Homes Limited, a landmark initiative that represents a significant milestone in our journey towards 
becoming a more agile, customer-centric, and digitally enabled organization.
In an increasingly dynamic financial services landscape, the ability to leverage technology effectively is critical 
to delivering superior customer experiences, enhancing operational efficiency, strengthening governance, and 
supporting sustainable growth. Recognizing this imperative, Can Fin Homes embarked on a comprehensive IT 
transformation journey aimed at modernizing our technology ecosystem and building a future-ready digital 
foundation. The successful completion of this program is a testament to our unwavering commitment to 
innovation and excellence. The transformed platform equips our organization with enhanced operational 
capabilities, improved scalability, stronger security frameworks, streamlined business processes, and enriched 
digital experiences for our customers, employees, and business partners.
I would like to place on record my sincere appreciation for the dedication and hard work demonstrated by our 
employees, leadership team, and project stakeholders who contributed tirelessly to the success of this strategic 
initiative. Their commitment, resilience, and collaborative spirit ensured that this complex transformation 
was executed effectively while maintaining our focus on customers and business continuity. I would also 
like to extend our heartfelt gratitude to IBM, our System Integration partner, for their valuable expertise, 
professionalism, and steadfast support throughout the transformation journey. IBM’s deep domain knowledge, 
technological capabilities, and collaborative approach played a pivotal role in helping us realize our vision and 
successfully deliver this large-scale program.
This achievement is not merely the completion of a project; it marks the beginning of a new chapter for Can Fin 
Homes. The modern technology foundation we have established today will enable us to accelerate innovation, 
respond more effectively to evolving customer needs, strengthen risk management practices, and pursue 
growth opportunities with greater confidence and agility.
As we move forward, we remain committed to leveraging technology as a strategic enabler to create long-term 
value for all our stakeholders and to further strengthen our position as a trusted housing finance institution. On 
behalf of the Board of Directors, I congratulate everyone involved in this remarkable accomplishment and look 
forward to the continued success of Can Fin Homes Limited in the years ahead.
Shri. Hardeep Singh
