PRESS RELEASE 
 
Brigade Group’s BuzzWorks Announces Foray into Chennai with 65,000 sq ft 
 
New facilities at Brigade Tech Boulevard and Brigade Vantage to generate an annual revenue of ₹10 crores that 
will cater to demand from GCCs, IT-BPM, Engineering, Manufacturing and BFSI sectors. 
 
Company to add 1 lakh sq ft in the city by FY28. 
 
Chennai, November 26, 2026: BuzzWorks, the flexible workspace and managed office solutions arm of 
Brigade Group, has announced its foray into Chennai with 65,000 sq. ft. managed office space across two 
new facilities at Brigade Tech Boulevard on Pallavaram–Thoraipakkam Radial Road and Brigade Vantage 
on Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR). The new facilities, which will cater to the evolving workspace 
requirements of Global Capability Centres (GCCs), IT-BPM, engineering, manufacturing and BFSI 
companies in Chennai, is expected to generate a combined annual revenue of approximately ₹10 crore. 
The company is also planning a further expansion of 1 lakh sq. ft. in the city by FY28. 
 
Commenting on the development, Siddharth Verma, Business Head, BuzzWorks said, “Our foray into 
Chennai marks an important milestone in BuzzWorks’ growth journey, as we enter a market with strong 
fundamentals and significant potential for sustained growth, with the city’s expanding GCC ecosystem, 
technology-led industries and diverse enterprise base creating increasing demand for high-quality, 
flexible and scalable workspace solutions. BuzzWorks is well equipped to address this opportunity with 
world-class facilities, professionally managed workspaces and solutions that can adapt to the evolving 
needs of businesses, whether companies establishing their presence in Chennai and project-based teams 
or larger enterprises and long-term office requirements. We see significant headroom for growth in the 
city and intend to expand our presence in line with this demand” said Siddharth Verma, Business Head, 
BuzzWorks. 
 
With Chennai emerging as one of India’s leading destinations for global businesses, supported by its 
strong talent pool, established industrial and technology ecosystem, and growing prominence as a 
preferred location for GCCs, BuzzWorks will continue to focus on creating workspaces that bring together 
flexibility, technology, connectivity and community, enabling businesses to focus on their core operations 
while their workplace requirements are managed seamlessly. 
 
About BuzzWorks: 
 
Brigade Group entered the flexible office space segment with the launch of BuzzWorks in 2019. The brand 
offers managed, customisable workspaces through two key formats: Built-to-Suit solutions for enterprises 
with specific design and operational needs, and Ready-to-Occupy offices for businesses seeking high-
quality, immediately functional spaces. BuzzWorks primarily caters to large enterprises and growth-
focused companies. Managed offices are increasingly seen as enablers of productivity, culture, and scale 
in today’s dynamic business landscape. With a sharp focus on experience, flexibility and scalability, the 
brand goes beyond workspaces; curating events, activations, and collaborative touchpoints that foster 
meaningful connections and enable professional and cultural exchange among its members.
