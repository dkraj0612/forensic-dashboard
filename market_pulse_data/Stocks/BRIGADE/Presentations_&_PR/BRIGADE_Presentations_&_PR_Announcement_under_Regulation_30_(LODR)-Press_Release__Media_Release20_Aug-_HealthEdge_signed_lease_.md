Brigade Group Signs Lease Agreement with US based HealthEdge at Brigade Square, 
Thiruvananthapuram 
 
1.62 lakh sq. ft. leased at Brigade Square, Brigade Group’s first IT office building in Technopark 
 
Thiruvananthapuram, August 20, 2026: Brigade Group has signed up a lease agreement with HealthEdge, 
a U.S. headquartered healthcare technology company backed by Bain Capital, for a super built-up area of 
approximately 1.62 lakh sq. ft. across 11 floors of Brigade’s first IT Office building, Brigade Square in 
Thiruvananthapuram. HealthEdge provides AI-powered software and technology-enabled services that 
help health plans modernize healthcare administration. With an integrated platform spanning claims 
administration, payment accuracy, provider network management, care management, and risk adjustment, 
HealthEdge enables health plans to harness automation and AI to deliver frictionless, cost-effective 
healthcare experiences. 
 
Located in  Technopark – Phase1, Brigade Square is a 2 lakh sq. ft.  IT office building designed to support the 
evolving requirements of technology-driven businesses.  This marks an important milestone in Brigade 
Group’s expansion in Thiruvananthapuram and reinforces the city’s growing appeal as a destination for 
global technology and healthcare companies.  
 
Commenting on the development, Nirupa Shankar, Joint Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited, 
said, “Our objective is to create high-quality, technologically equipped workspaces that attract global 
businesses to the city. Brigade Square was therefore envisioned as a future-ready workplace that can meet 
the evolving technology, infrastructure and employee experience requirements of world-class organisations.  
Having a global healthcare technology company such as HealthEdge as a key occupier is a strong validation 
of Thiruvananthapuram’s potential as a technology destination. It also contributes to the creation of high-
quality employment opportunities and strengthens the wider business ecosystem.” 
 
Kevin Adams, CEO, HealthEdge said, “Health plans are under real pressure to control costs and operate 
more efficiently, and they need services and technology partners who deliver measurable results. Our teams 
in India are central to that work, with deep expertise in AI and engineering, product development, and 
operations. This new campus in Thiruvananthapuram reflects our long-term commitment to those teams 
and to the growth we're building together at HealthEdge." 
 
HealthEdge’s new campus brings together teams across Engineering, Product Development, Technology, 
Operations and Corporate Functions. The new facility has been designed to support collaboration, 
innovation and employee experience, with a range of dedicated workplace and employee amenities. The 
campus includes an executive floor with a boardroom, meeting rooms and executive dining, along with a 
dedicated cafeteria, training rooms, collaborative workspaces, wellness rooms and a mothers’ room.  
 
Brigade Group has significant expansion plans for Thiruvananthapuram. The Group has signed a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Government of Kerala to develop a 2 million sq. ft. World 
Trade Center within Technopark- Phase 1. Brigade Square will form part of this larger development, further 
strengthening the Group’s presence in the city and its contribution to the growth of Thiruvananthapuram’s 
commercial and technology ecosystem. The proposed World Trade Center is expected to further enhance the 
city’s ability to attract multinational companies, technology businesses and other knowledge-driven 
enterprises, while creating a platform for global businesses to establish and expand their operations in 
Kerala.
