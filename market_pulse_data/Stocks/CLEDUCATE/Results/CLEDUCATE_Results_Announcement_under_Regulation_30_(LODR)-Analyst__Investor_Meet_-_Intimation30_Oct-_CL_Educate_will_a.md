About CL Educate 
CL Educate Limited, listed on both BSE and NSE since 2017, is a well-diversified, technology-enabled 
company operating across the EdTech, MarTech & Digital Assessments segments. Since its inception 
in 1996, CL has grown from a single MBA test-prep center into a pan-India conglomerate, delivering a 
wide range of offerings—from test preparation and training to publishing, content development, 
corporate marketing services, and integrated institutional solutions—across both physical and digital 
platforms. 
Led by a team of highly qualified professionals, including IIT-IIM alumni, the company has 
consistently leveraged strong synergies between its consumer- and enterprise-focused businesses to 
build an asset-light, scalable model. 
Further strengthening its position, CL Educate has forayed into the rapidly expanding Digital 
Assessments space through the acquisition of DEXIT Global Limited (formerly NSEIT Limited), an 
erstwhile step-down subsidiary of NSE Limited. With a 20% relative market share in a ₹4,500+ crore 
industry, DEXIT marks the largest and most strategic addition to CL’s EdTech and MarTech portfolio
