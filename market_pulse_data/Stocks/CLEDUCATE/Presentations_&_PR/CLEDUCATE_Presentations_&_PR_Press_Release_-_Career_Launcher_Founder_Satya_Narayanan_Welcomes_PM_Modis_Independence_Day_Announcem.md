Career Launcher Founder Satya Narayanan welcomes PM Modi's Independence Day Announcement of Free 
Online Coaching and AI Skilling for 1 Crore Youth 
New Delhi, August 15, 2026 — CL Educate Limited (BSE|NSE-listed), the diversified education services group 
behind Career Launcher, DEXIT Global, and mySATHI Foundation, today welcomed Prime Minister Narendra 
Modi's announcement — made during his 80th Independence Day address from the Red Fort — of free online 
coaching for competitive examinations and a nationwide programme to train one crore youth in Artificial 
Intelligence over the coming year. 
In his address, the Prime Minister noted that the burden of coaching costs on middle-class and poor families had 
become unsustainable, and that India's Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), together with its base of talented 
teachers and educators, gave the country the foundation to solve this at national scale. 
"This is precisely the vision Career Launcher was built to serve — that access to quality preparation and quality 
skilling should never be a function of a family's postal code or income," said Satya Narayanam R, Founder and 
Group Chairman, CL Educate Limited.  
"For nearly three decades, we have believed that education is nation-building infrastructure, not a private privilege. 
The Prime Minister's announcement validates a direction we have invested in for years, and we stand ready to 
contribute our platforms, our people, and our on-ground experience to make this a success.", he said.  
A Track Record Built for This Moment 
CL Educate's constituent businesses bring credentials directly relevant to the scale and intent of the Prime 
Minister's announcement: 
- Career Launcher, the group's flagship test-preparation network, has trained lakhs of students across 
examinations and age groups since 1995.  
When the COVID-19 pandemic struck in 2020, Career Launcher was among the first to move an entire school 
system of over 200,000 students across 1500 schools online — supporting Delhi government and private schools 
within 48 hours of the lockdown, at a time when most institutions had no digital continuity plan in place.  
- DEXIT Global, the group's AI-powered assessment and credentialing platform, today conducts over 11 million 
assessments annually  making it one of India's largest examination and certification technology infrastructures — 
spanning school boards, entrance tests, and professional certification bodies. This is precisely the class of secure, 
high-integrity technology backbone that a national free-coaching and AI-skilling programme will require. 
- mySATHI Foundation, the group's employability and skills arm, is currently piloting India's first district-level 
Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) for Jobs, Higher Education and Skills, in a district in Uttar Pradesh, in consortium 
with other foundations and the district administration. The pilot goes beyond technology deployment: it is actively 
building a network of local teachers and educators onto the DPI layer itself, ensuring that the platform's value is 
delivered through trusted, hyper-local relationships rather than a one-size-fits-all digital rollout — a model 
designed explicitly for the kind of scalable, last-mile success the Prime Minister's vision demands.

Looking Ahead 
CL Educate said it looks forward to working with the Ministry of Education, NTA, and state governments to extend 
the learnings from the Ghaziabad DPI pilot and DEXIT's assessment infrastructure to the national programme, and 
reaffirmed its commitment to India's Viksit Bharat 2047 vision of a digitally empowered, AI-ready youth 
population. 
About CL Educate 
CL Educate Ltd. (BSE: 540403 | NSE: CLEDUCATE), listed since 2017, is a technology-driven, diversified group 
operating across EdTech (Career Launcher), MarTech (Kestone) and Digital Assessments (DEX). Founded in 1996 
as a single MBA test-prep centre, the Group has grown into a nationwide, increasingly global business serving 
consumer and enterprise clients through physical and digital channels — spanning test-preparation and training, 
content development and publishing, integrated marketing solutions for corporates, comprehensive solutions for 
educational institutions, and digital assessments at global scale. Kestone, the Group's MarTech arm, delivers event 
management and experiential marketing — conferences, product launches, hybrid and virtual events — for 
leading Indian and global enterprises across India, the US, the UAE and Singapore. On 20 February 2025, CL 
Educate acquired DEX (DEXIT Global), the world's fourth largest standalone digital assessments company, with 
over 20% relative market share across Professional Certifications, Recruitment & Promotion Exams, Vocational 
Assessments, Entrance Exams and Employability Enhancement in a market growing at ~16% CAGR. Guided by a 
team of accomplished professionals, including IIT-IIM alumni, the Group continues to expand globally.
