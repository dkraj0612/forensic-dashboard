Aptech Limited and Film and Television Institute of India (FTII) Enter into 
Partnership to Democratise Access to Film and Creative Education Across India 
 
~Strategic collaboration to offer workshops, certified programmes and a co-developed filmmaking 
programme, combining FTII’s cinematic expertise with Aptech’s industry-oriented skilling ecosystem~ 
 
Mumbai/Pune, October 23, 2026: Aptech Limited,  a pioneer in vocational training and global learning 
solutions has entered into a collaboration with The Film and Television Institute of India (FTII)  to expand 
access to quality film education and creative skills across India. 
 
The partnership brings together FTII’s academic excellence and rich cinematic legacy with Aptech’s 30+ 
years of experience in vocational education across Media & Entertainment, creative careers and the 
AVGC-XR ecosystem, backed by its expertise in curriculum development and established extensive 
training network. Together, the institutions will create structured, industry-relevant learning programs for 
aspiring filmmakers, content creators, animators, VFX professionals and other creative talent, enabling 
learners to build the skills, creative capabilities and practical exposure needed to thrive in the rapidly 
evolving Media & Entertainment and AVGC-XR sectors. The collaboration will offer learners access to FTII-
led learning experiences through Aptech’s ecosystem, while creating learning opportunities that range 
from focused workshops and micro-credentials to specialised certification and an immersive, co-
developed filmmaking programme. 
 
The partnership will be executed through three distinct engagement models creating multiple routes to 
creative learning.  
 
1. FTII Workshops & Micro-Credentials 
Under the first model, selected FTII workshops will be made accessible to eligible students at Aptech 
centres. The workshops will provide learners with opportunities to engage with specialised areas of 
filmmaking and cinematic storytelling, enabling them to complement their existing learning with focused, 
expert-led interventions from FTII. Eligible participants will receive applicable FTII certificates or micro-
credentials, creating opportunities for learners to build specialised knowledge and demonstrate 
additional competencies. 
 
2. Aptech–FTII Joint Certification Programme 
The second model will integrate a two-month FTII module with Aptech’s training brands offering VFX and 
3D programmes. The programme will combine existing structured, industry-oriented training with FTII’s 
academic and cinematic expertise, giving learners exposure to advanced filmmaking concepts and 
practices. Successful students will receive a joint certificate, subject to meeting the prescribed 
programme and certification requirements. The model is designed to strengthen the bridge between 
technical skills and cinematic application, enabling students to develop a more holistic understanding of 
visual storytelling. 
 
3. Co-Curated Joint Certification Programme 
The third and most comprehensive model will be a co-developed programme, including a two-month 
immersive learning experience at the FTII campus in Pune. Initially planned across Aptech’s own centres

in Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru, the programme will combine structured learning at Aptech’s training 
centres with intensive academic immersion and mentorship at FTII. A key outcome of the programme will

Mr. Sandip Weling, Whole-time Director & Chief Business Officer, Global Retail, Aptech Limited, said, 
“The future of creative education lies in bringing together strong academic foundations, industry-relevant 
skills and opportunities for experiential learning. Our collaboration with FTII brings together two 
complementary strengths- FTII’s unparalleled cinematic legacy and Aptech’s expertise in vocational 
education and nationwide delivery. Through these three models, we aim to give learners greater access to 
specialised knowledge, mentorship and real-world creative experiences, ultimately enabling them to build 
stronger capabilities, portfolios and career pathways in the Media & Entertainment and AVGC-XR 
ecosystem.” 
 
Mr. Santosh Ojha, Head of Strategy, FTII, added, “FTII has a rich legacy of nurturing filmmakers and 
storytellers, and we believe this learning should reach many more aspiring creative professionals. Our 
collaboration with Aptech gives us the opportunity to take FTII’s academic and cinematic expertise beyond 
our campus, leveraging Aptech’s extensive network of creative education centres across India. Cinema is 
as much about storytelling, imagination and creative expression as it is about technical skills. Through this 
association, we hope to bring that perspective to more learners through specialized programmes, 
workshops and hands-on experiences, helping shape the next generation of filmmakers and creative 
professionals.” 
 
Mr. Abir Aich, Executive Vice President, Aptech Limited, said, “One of the key objectives of this 
collaboration is to bring the art and practice of filmmaking closer to students pursuing careers in the 
creative industry. By combining Aptech’s technology and production-focused learning with FTII’s expertise 
in cinema and storytelling, we want to help students develop a more rounded understanding of how films 
and visual content are actually created. Importantly, this opportunity will be available to both new and 
existing students, giving them access to specialized learning, industry mentorship, and internship 
opportunities to work on projects. For the industry, we believe this can help build a ready pool of future 
creative talent, who bring together technical proficiency, cinematic understanding and practical 
filmmaking skills, and are better prepared to contribute from the outset.” 
 
The partnership aims to move beyond conventional skill training towards applied, experiential and 
outcome-oriented creative education, equipping learners with the technical proficiency, storytelling 
capabilities, creative thinking and industry exposure needed to succeed in the evolving Media & 
Entertainment and AVGC-XR sectors. This may also lead to internship opportunities for the top talent. 
Through three progressive learning models from specialised workshops and micro-credentials to 
integrated certification and immersive filmmaking the collaboration seeks to expand access to quality 
creative education and build a future-ready talent pool for India’s growing creative economy.

ABOUT APTECH LIMITED 
With over three decades of strong experience in the vocational skill training and non-formal academic 
curriculum-based training programs, Aptech Limited is a pioneer in the non–formal vocational training 
business in the country with a significant global presence. Ever since its commencement in 1986 and with 
a current presence of  over 750 centres globally, Aptech Limited has effectively ventured into diverse 
sectors ranging from IT training, media & entertainment, virtual production, beauty & wellness, retail & 
aviation, pre-school segment, amongst others. Aptech Limited has successfully trained students, 
professionals, universities & corporates through its two main streams of business- Individual training and 
Enterprise Business Group.  
 
Under Individual Training, Aptech offers career and professional training through its multi-brands- Arena 
Animation & Maya Academy of Advanced Creativity (both in Animation & Multimedia), Lakmē Academy 
powered by Aptech (beauty & grooming), Aptech Aviation & Hospitality Academy, Aptech Learning, and 
Aptech International Pre-school, amongst others. 
 
Enterprise business includes Training and Assessment Solutions for Corporates & Institutions (Aptech 
Training Solutions, Aptech Assessment & Testing Solutions). Aptech Limited has been recognized as a  
 
Great Place to Work (2017 & 2019) won the Golden Peacock National Training Award (2019) and appraised 
at Maturity Level 5 of People Capability Maturity Model (PCMM) in the year 2020. For further information 
please visit- https://www.aptech-worldwide.com/ 
 
About FTII:  
 
The Film and Television Institute of India (FTII) was set up by the Government of India in 1960, in the 
premises of the erstwhile Prabhat Studios in Pune. 
 
Today, FTII is recognised as a centre for excellence in audio visual media across the world and one of the 
best film institutes in India. FTII alumni work everywhere: from Los Angeles, Paris, and London to Mumbai, 
Hyderabad, Trivandrum, Chennai, and Kolkata. Over the years they have gained popularity and have 
earned various laurels as accomplished technicians and superstars. The alumni have also won popular film 
and television awards from the ‘National Film Awards’ to ‘Oscars’, to the ‘Dada Saheb Phalke award’ to 
the prestigious ‘Padma Awards’.
