### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Archean Chemical Industries Limited (ACIL) has had its long-term bank facilities rating downgraded from 'CRISIL A/Negative' to 'Crisil A-/Stable' due to moderation in its core business performance and significant financial strain from a large, unrelated silicon carbide semiconductor project. This new project, facing cost overruns and not generating revenue until after fiscal 2029, will substantially increase debt, with net debt-to-EBITDA projected to more than double from 1.53x to 3.50-3.80x, severely impacting debt metrics. Key red flags include a substantial increase in promoter share pledge to support a group entity, an ongoing Income Tax investigation, and significant execution and market risks associated with the semiconductor venture.
