From Order Book to Output: Balu Forge ramps up Artillery Shell 
Dispatches, Clears Key 155 mm ERFB milestone & Lands marquee new 
customers from the United States & Europe 
 
152mm shells dispatched, 155 ERFB quality-approved at forging tolerance of TIR 3mm and 
machined wall tolerance of 0.3mm, Aerospace jet engine components approval, 105 mm 
empty shell order from major Indian defence factory, new orders from a leading U.S. oil & 
gas major and a Czech Armoured vehicle maker. 
 
 
Mumbai, November 2, 2026: Balu Forge Industries Ltd, a leading force in heavy engineering 
and precision machining has announced a series of execution milestones across defence, 
aerospace, energy and heavy mobility. The company has moved previously announced defence 
orders into dispatch, cleared one of the most demanding artillery components in its portfolio, 
and added two new international customers & one domestic defence unit after rigorous audits.  
 
Defence: from new order wins to dispatches 
 
 152mm empty shells: The company has made multiple successful dispatches of 152mm 
empty shells against the order announced earlier, demonstrating on-time conversion of 
its defence order book into revenue.  
 
 155mm ERFB: In its most significant technical milestone yet, the company successfully 
produced and obtained quality approval for the 155mm ERFB shell, achieving a Total 
Indicated Runout forging tolerance (TIR) of 3mm and machined wall tolerance of 
0.3mm. This validates the company's process capability and the standard of its 
manufacturing facilities. Serial dispatches are expected to commence shortly. 
 
 105mm shells: The company has received approval & new orders for its 105mm empty 
shells from a large Indian Government Defence unit. The dispatches will commence 
shortly. 
 
Aerospace: precision entry into a high-barrier sector 
 
Jet engine components developed and quality-approved: The company has received approval 
for jet engine components to the aerospace sector, approved against the tightest tolerance. This 
marks the company's entry into one of the most quality-intensive segments of precision 
engineering. A further expansion into a much larger product portfolio in the aerospace domain 
is now underway. 
 
Energy: Marquee Oil & Gas Customer 
 
Large-component order from a leading oil & gas player from the United States: The company 
has secured an order for large components weighing over 450 kg from one of the largest oil 
and gas companies in the United States. The order follows a successful plant audit and drawing 
approval, validating the company’s manufacturing capabilities and quality standards for heavy,

About Balu Forge Industries: 
Balu Forge Industries Limited, founded by Mr. Prehlad Singh Chandock, is a leading Indian precision 
engineering company delivering forged and machined components across multiple global industries. 
The company offers a comprehensive product portfolio ranging from 1 kg to 1,500 kg and up to 3 meters 
in length, supporting diverse applications in automotive, industrial vehicles, earthmoving equipment, 
wind energy, aerospace, defence, oil and gas, railways, marine, and agriculture. Its operations include 
fully integrated forging and machining capabilities, with advanced manufacturing facilities in Belgaum, 
Karnataka, spread over a 46+ acres campus. Equipped with high-capacity hydraulic hammers and 
forging presses, and supported by a dedicated in-house tool room, metallurgical labs, and CNC 
machining units, Balu Forge ensures consistent precision and quality. The company’s strategy is driven 
by innovation, with a specialized R&D division focusing on new materials and rapid prototyping. 
Strategic initiatives emphasize expanding defence production, enhancing automation, and strengthening
