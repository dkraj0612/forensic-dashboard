SUMMARY OF PROCEEDINGS OF THE MEETING OF THE EQUITY SHAREHOLDERS OF ARISINFRA 
SOLUTIONS LIMITED (“THE COMPANY”) CONVENED AS PER THE DIRECTIONS OF THE HON’BLE 
NATIONAL COMPANY LAW TRIBUNAL MUMBAI BENCH, VIDE ITS ORDER DATED OCTOBER 7, 2026.  
The Meeting of the Equity Shareholders of Arisinfra Solutions Limited (“the Company”) convened 
pursuant to the directions of the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench (“NCLT”), was 
held on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at 3:30 P.M. (IST) through Video Conferencing (“VC”) / Other 
Audio Visual Means (“OAVM”) (“Meeting”). The Meeting was conducted in compliance with the 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 (“the Act”), read with the applicable general circulars issued by 
the Ministry of Corporate Affairs for holding general meetings through VC/OAVM, Regulation 44 of the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), other applicable SEBI Circulars and Secretarial Standard on General 
Meetings as issued by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India ('SS-2').  
As directed by the Hon’ble NCLT, Dr. Mansi Jain, chaired the proceedings of the Meeting and Ms. Taruna 
Kumbhar, Scrutinizer was present at the meeting through VC. Mr. Latesh Shah, Company Secretary and 
Compliance Officer of the Company, welcomed the Shareholders to the Meeting and requested Dr. 
Mansi Jain to commence the proceedings of the Meeting. Dr. Mansi Jain welcomed the members 
present at the meeting of the Equity Shareholders of the Company, convened in accordance with the 
directions of the Hon’ble NCLT, Mumbai Bench. He stated that the purpose of the meeting was to 
consider and, if deemed appropriate, approve the Scheme of Amalgamation of Arisunitern Re Solutions 
Private Limited (“AUSPL” OR “Transferor Company”) and Arisinfra Solutions Limited (“ASL” or 
“Transferee Company”) and their respective shareholders (“Scheme”), in accordance with sections 230 
to 232, and other relevant provisions of the Companies Act, 2013. The meeting was scheduled at 3:30 
P.M. (IST) and upon availability of the requisite quorum the Chairperson commenced the meeting at 
3:30 P.M. (IST) calling the meeting in order in accordance with the NCLT order dated October 7, 2026.  
The Chairperson informed the members that the Company had made all necessary arrangements to 
enable their seamless participation and voting at the meeting. The Company had engaged National 
Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) to provide facilities for remote e-voting, participation through 
video conferencing, and e-voting during the meeting. Members requiring any assistance during the 
meeting, whether technical or otherwise, were advised to contact the helpline numbers specified in the 
notice of the meeting.  
At the request of the Chairperson, Mr. Latesh Shah, Company Secretary introduced and welcomed the 
Board of Directors and other officials who participated in the meeting through Video Conferencing.

Mr. Latesh Shah briefed the members about the conduct of the meeting along with the e-voting process 
in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable SEBI Listing Regulations. 
He stated that remote e-voting facility was provided through the NSDL platform from 9:00 A.M. (IST) 
on Sunday, 22nd November 2026 to 5:00 P.M. (IST) on Tuesday, 24th November 2026. Members who had 
not exercised their vote through remote e-voting were given the opportunity to vote during the meeting 
through the NSDL e-voting system. He further requested members to refer to the instructions provided 
in the notice for participation and voting through video conferencing. 
He informed that members were kept on mute by default to ensure smooth conduct of the meeting and 
that the proceedings were being recorded.  
He further outlined the flow of the meeting, briefed the members on the salient features of the Scheme, 
followed by the discussion and Q&A session.  
Following the explanation, the equity shareholders were invited for their comments and queries.  
As registered speaker shareholders participated, the query raised regarding the Scheme was 
satisfactorily addressed by the management, and the discussion was concluded.  
The Chairperson thereafter thanked the members and stakeholders for their participation and support 
and declared the meeting closed at 4:08 P.M. (IST), being 15 minutes after the closure of the e-voting 
facility, which was kept open for 15 minutes post conclusion of the meeting.
