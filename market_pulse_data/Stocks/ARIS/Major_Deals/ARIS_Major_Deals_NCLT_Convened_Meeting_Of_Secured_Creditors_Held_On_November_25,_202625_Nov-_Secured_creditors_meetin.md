SUMMARY OF PROCEEDINGS OF THE MEETING OF THE SECURED CREDITORS OF ARISINFRA 
SOLUTIONS LIMITED (“THE COMPANY”) CONVENED AS PER THE DIRECTIONS OF THE HON’BLE 
NATIONAL COMPANY LAW TRIBUNAL MUMBAI BENCH, VIDE ITS ORDER DATED OCTOBER 7, 2026.  
The Meeting of the secured creditors of Arisinfra Solutions Limited (“the Company”) convened pursuant 
to the directions of the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench (“NCLT”), was held on 
Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at 11:30 A.M. (IST) through Video Conferencing (“VC”) / Other Audio 
Visual Means (“OAVM”) (“Meeting”). The Meeting was conducted in compliance with the provisions of 
the Companies Act, 2013 (“the Act”), read with the applicable general circulars issued by the Ministry 
of Corporate Affairs for holding general meetings through VC/OAVM, other applicable SEBI Circulars 
and Secretarial Standard on General Meetings as issued by the Institute of Company Secretaries of 
India ('SS-2').  
As directed by the Hon’ble NCLT, Ms. Mansi Jain, chaired the proceedings of the Meeting and Ms. Taruna 
Kumbhar, Scrutinizer was present at the meeting through VC. Mr. Latesh Shah, Company Secretary and 
Compliance Officer of the Company, welcomed the secured creditors to the Meeting and requested Ms. 
Mansi Jain to commence the proceedings of the Meeting. Ms. Mansi Jain welcomed the secured 
creditors present at the meeting of the secured creditors of the Company, convened in accordance with 
the directions of the Hon’ble NCLT, Mumbai Bench. He stated that the purpose of the meeting was to 
consider and, if deemed appropriate, approve the Scheme of Amalgamation of Arisunitern Re Solutions 
Private Limited (“AUSPL” OR “Transferor Company”) and Arisinfra Solutions Limited (“ASL” or 
“Transferee Company”) and their respective shareholders (“Scheme”), in accordance with sections 230 
to 232, and other relevant provisions of the Companies Act, 2013. The meeting was scheduled at 11:30 
A.M. (IST) and upon availability of the requisite quorum the Chairperson commenced the meeting at 
11:30 AM (IST) calling the meeting in order in accordance with the NCLT order dated October 7, 2026.  
The Chairperson informed the secured creditors that the Company had made all necessary 
arrangements to enable their seamless participation and voting at the meeting. The Company had 
engaged National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) to provide facilities for remote e-voting, 
participation through video conferencing, and e-voting during the meeting. Secured creditors requiring 
any assistance during the meeting, whether technical or otherwise, were advised to contact the helpline 
numbers specified in the notice of the meeting.  
At the request of the Chairperson, Mr. Latesh Shah, Company Secretary introduced and welcomed the 
Board of Directors and other officials who participated in the meeting through Video Conferencing.  
Mr. Latesh Shah briefed the secured creditors about the conduct of the meeting along with the e-voting 
process in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable SEBI Listing
