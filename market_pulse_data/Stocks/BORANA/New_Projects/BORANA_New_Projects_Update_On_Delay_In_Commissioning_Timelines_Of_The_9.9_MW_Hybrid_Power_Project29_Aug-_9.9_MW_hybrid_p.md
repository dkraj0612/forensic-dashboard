Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, and in continuation of the Company's earlier intimation dated June 18, 2026, we wish to inform 
you that the commissioning timeline of the 9.9 MW Hybrid Power Project at Kalikanagar, Gujarat, has 
been revised. 
As per the latest communication received from the project developer, the commissioning is now 
tentatively scheduled as follows: (i) one WTG location along with the 7.65 MW solar component is 
expected to be commissioned by September 30, 2026; and (ii) the remaining two WTG locations are 
expected to be commissioned by the end of October 2026. 
The delay is attributable to factors beyond the project developer's control, including high wind speeds 
affecting WTG erection, heavy monsoon conditions requiring additional logistics-road rework, and 
delay in obtaining shutdowns of existing 33 kV and 11 kV lines for the 33 kV transmission-line work. 
The Company will keep the Stock Exchanges and stakeholders informed of any developments in 
accordance with applicable regulations. You are requested to take the above on record and disseminate 
the same to all concerned.
