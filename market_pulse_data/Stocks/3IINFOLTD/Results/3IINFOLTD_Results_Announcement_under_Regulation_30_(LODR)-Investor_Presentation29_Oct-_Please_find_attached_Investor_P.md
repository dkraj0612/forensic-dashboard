3i Infotech Reported Q2FY27 Revenue of ₹ 182.0 crore (4.1% Growth YoY) 
➢ Profitability remains resilient with PAT of ₹6.7 crore in Q2 FY27 
➢ EBIDTA at ₹13.7 crore and Margin of 7.5%  
➢ 25 new client wins in Q2 FY27 
 
Mumbai, October 29th, 2026: 3i Infotech Limited (BSE: 532628) (NSE: 3IINFOLTD), a leading provider of digital 
transformation, technology services and technology solutions, announced its consolidated financial results for 
the second quarter & half year ended September 30,2026. The Company continued to maintain healthy 
operational discipline while investing in strategic growth initiatives. 
 
Consolidated Performance Highlights for Q2 & H1FY27  
➢ Operating Revenue increased to ₹182.0 crore in Q2 FY27 from ₹174.9 crore in Q2 FY26, representing 
4.1% YoY growth. For H1 FY27 revenue stood at ₹360.0 crore compared to ₹345.4 crore in H1 FY26, 
representing 4.2% YoY growth. 
➢ Further, EBITDA stood at ₹13.7 crore in Q2 FY27 compared to ₹30.5 crore in Q2 FY26, (Last year 
numbers included a one-time other income from US on ECS credit of ₹20.5 crore) with an EBITDA margin 
of 7.5%. For H1 FY27, EBITDA stood at ₹ 25.2 crore compared to ₹ 48.6 crore in H1 FY26 (Last year 
numbers included a one-time other income from US on ECS credit of ₹38.4 crore) with an EBITDA margin 
of 7.0 %. 
➢ PAT stood at ₹6.7 crore in Q2 FY27 compared to ₹ 18.2 crore in Q2 FY26, with a PAT margin of 3.7%. 
For H1 FY27, PAT stood at ₹13.2 crore compared to ₹ 25.8 crore in H1 FY26, with a PAT margin of 3.7%. 
➢  The Company secured order bookings of TCV ₹467.3 Crore (ACV ₹379.5 Crore) during the quarter. 
 
Business & Geographic Performance Highlights for Q2 & H1FY27 
Business Segment Performance: 
The Company's revenue mix remained well balanced during Q2 & H1FY27, reflecting the strength of its 
diversified business portfolio, broad industry presence, and geographically balanced customer base. This 
diversification continues to provide resilience against sector-specific fluctuations while enabling sustainable 
growth across key markets. 
 
• 
Digital Engineering Services (DES) continues to be the Company's largest business segment, contributing 
74.9% & 74.1% of total revenue in Q2 & H1FY27, supported by sustained demand for application 
modernization, digital transformation, data & analytics, and AI-led solutions. 
• 
Cloud Infrastructure & Security Services (CISS) Contributed 17.1% & 17.9% of total revenue in Q2 & H1 
FY27 respectively, reflecting stable demand for cloud, cybersecurity, digital workplace, and managed 
infrastructure services. 
• 
Business Process Services (BPS) Accounted for 7.8% of total revenue in Q2 & H1FY27, with the business 
continuing to focus on operational excellence, customer experience, and process transformation 
initiatives. 
 
Industry & Geographic Performance: 
 
➢ The IT sector remained the largest industry vertical, contributing nearly 43.7% & 44.8% of revenue, 
followed by BFSI at approximately 30.8% & 31.8% in Q2FY27 & H1FY27 respectively.  
 
➢ Geographically, the Company continued to maintain a balanced global footprint.

About 3i Infotech Limited 
3i Infotech Limited incorporated in 1993 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, provides information technology 
services and software solutions. Since its inception the company has 30+ years of experience in delivering 
business value to its clients across multiple industry verticals. 3i Infotech has emerged as a leading name in 
propelling the current wave of digital transformation initiatives, with deep domain expertise across BFS, IT, 
Insurance, Manufacturing, Retail and Government sectors. It operates through three key business segments, (i) 
Digital Engineering Services unit anchors 3i Infotech’s digital services by integrating Application Development & 
Maintenance, Automation, and Analytics to drive enterprise transformation. It delivers scalable applications, 
streamline operations using RPA, and enables detailed decisions through business intelligence and strategic 
analytics. (ii) Cloud Infrastructure & Security Services include end-user computing, data centre management, 
cloud enablement, and network management, delivered via 3i Infotech’s Global Command Centre with 24x7 
support, ITIL-aligned practices, and SLA driven delivery. The offerings cover cloud transformation, remote 
infrastructure management, hybrid cloud readiness, proactive monitoring, and integrated cybersecurity. (iii) 3i 
Infotech’s Digital BPS provides services in claims, fund accounting, and collections—driving efficiency, cost 
savings, and better customer experience. The Company has over 3700+ employees and over 300+ clients 
globally.
