Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This is with reference to our letter dated June 29,2026, vide Ref. No. DOMS/SE/26-27/23 and January 30, 2026, 
vide Ref. No. DOMS/SE/25-26/82, wherein it was, inter alia, informed that, the Board of Directors of DOMS 
Industries Limited (‘the Company’), approved the formation of Joint Venture Company in India and execution of 
joint venture agreement (‘said transaction’) with Seven SpA, a F.I.L.A. - Fabbrica Italiana Lapis Ed Affini S.p.A., 
Group Company. 
 
With reference to the above, the Company informs that the documentation and other requirements for the 
proposed transaction are progressing well and nearing finalisation. The parties have, however, mutually agreed 
to extend the deadline for incorporating the joint venture company in India and executing the joint venture 
agreement from September 30, 2026, to January 31, 2027, to allow for the completion of necessary procedural 
compliances and other requisite formalities. 
 
Accordingly, the said transaction will be completed on or before January 31, 2027. 
 
 
Except for the extension of the timelines, all the other details of the said transaction shall remain unchanged.  
 
The Company shall keep the Stock Exchange informed of any further material developments in relation to the 
said transactions. 
 
Further, in compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR Regulations, read with SEBI Circular Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, the Company shall submit the detailed 
disclosure once the joint venture agreement is executed between the parties to the said transaction. 
 
 
We request you to take the above information on record.
