PITHAMPUR, October 12, 2026
Adroit Industries (India) Limited (NSE: ADROITIND | BSE: 544950), A leading manufacturer of
specialized propeller shafts and driveline components, announced its unaudited consolidated
financial results for the first quarter ended June 30, 2026. This marks the company's inaugural
financial disclosure following its successful listing on the Main Board of the NSE and BSE
Limited on September 30, 2026.

Operational Milestones & Growth Strategy
Capacity Expansion On Schedule: Following the recent completion of its IPO raising
₹132.62 Crore in fresh capital, the company's planned CapEx program is progressing on
schedule. The proceeds will be deployed to double overall manufacturing capacity,
addressing anticipated domestic demand and scaling export order execution.
Tapping into High-Growth Defence Opportunities: The company is actively executing a
milestone purchase order from a leading domestic private defence OEM. This
engagement marks Adroit’s strategic entry point into India’s high-growth defence
manufacturing sector, validating its engineering capabilities in high-specification, mission-
critical tactical mobility platforms. 
Global Market Diversification: To mitigate dependence on North American commercial
vehicle seasonality and insulate the business against prevailing tariff uncertainties, Adroit
is accelerating its geographic diversification strategy. The company is aggressively
expanding its distribution footprint by enlisting authorized dealers across South America,
Canada, and other strategic international territories, unlocking resilient, high-margin
replacement and OEM export demand.
Commenting on the company’s performance and future outlook, Mr. Saurabh Sangla, Chairman
& Managing Director, Adroit Industries (India) Limited Said; 
"Our Q1 performance reflects the resilience of our business model. Even in what is traditionally a
softer quarter for our industry, we expanded our EBITDA margins to nearly 31% and delivered a
32% year-on-year growth in net profit.
With our listing on the NSE and BSE now successfully executed, our primary operational focus is
the swift deployment of IPO proceeds to double our manufacturing capacity. Simultaneously,
our strategic push into untapped global geographies notably Canada and South America,
combined with prestigious orders from leading domestic private defence OEM, positions Adroit
for sustained long-term growth."
 
Adroit Industries (India) Limited is an engineering and automotive components manufacturer
specializing in precision-engineered propeller shafts and driveline assemblies for commercial
vehicles, defense mobility systems, industrial machinery, and off-highway equipment.
Headquartered in Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh, the company caters to prominent domestic
automotive manufacturers while actively exporting to global aftermarket and OEM distribution
networks.
