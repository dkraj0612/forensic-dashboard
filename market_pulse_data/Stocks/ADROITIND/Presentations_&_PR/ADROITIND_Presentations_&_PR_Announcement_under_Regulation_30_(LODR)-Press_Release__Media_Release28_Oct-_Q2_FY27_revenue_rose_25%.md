Management Commentary 
Mr. Saurabh Sangla 
Chairman & Managing Director 
Adroit Industries (India) Limited 
“Our Q2 FY27 performance reflects strong growth in revenue and profitability. Revenue increased 25% 
quarter-on-quarter to ¥426.73 million, while EBITDA grew 13% quarter-on-quarter to ¥ 119.56 million, with a 
margin of 28.0%. Profit after tax increased 14% quarter-on-quarter to ¥ 85.47 million. 
We added 13 new customers during Q2 FY27. Our entry into the industrial shaft and defence segments 
broadens our addressable market, opens opportunities to serve new customers and strengthens business 
diversification. 
Our IPO-funded capacity expansion remains on track. Machinery worth ¥ 50 million has already been 
purchased and commissioned, and orders worth ¥ 300 million have been placed. We are on schedule to 
operationalise the additional machining capacity from Q4 FY27 and forging capacity by Q1 FY28. 
We are targeting full ramp-up by Q4 FY28, following which the expanded capacity could support peak annual 
revenue of ¥ 2,500 million. Our focus remains on timely execution, customer acquisition and building a 
strong foundation for sustained, profitable growth.”

About Adroit Industries (India) Limited 
Adroit Industries (India) Limited is an engineering and automotive components manufacturer 
specialising in precision-engineered propeller shafts and driveline assemblies for commercial vehicles, 
defence mobility systems, industrial machinery and off-highway equipment. 
Headquartered in Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh, the company caters to prominent domestic automotive 
manufacturers while exporting to global aftermarket and OEM distribution networks.
