PRESS RELEASE 
AXISCADES’ XIDA to acquire Silicon Valley-based ITSJ business — a 
ready-made US base inside the AI hardware supply chain 
Board approves the acquisition of ITSJ Group’s business by XIDA Inc.: qualified supplier positions 
with leading semiconductor-equipment makers and a hyperscaler, and a San Jose engineering and 
manufacturing base 
• 
Inside the AI build-out from day one: ITSJ supplies sub-systems to two of the world’s top-five 
semiconductor-equipment manufacturers and to a leading hyperscale data-centre operator, with live 
purchase orders in hand. 
• 
A ready-made Silicon Valley platform: design-to-build capability in San Jose — precision machining, 
PCB design and assembly, cable and box build, and full system integration, from prototype to 
production. 
• 
Disciplined, performance-linked terms: consideration of up to US$ 7.0 million (≈ ₹67 crore), of 
which only US$ 2.5 million is cash at completion. The balance is XIDA shares vesting over 30 months 
and an earn-out of up to US$ 2.0 million tied to customer, revenue and margin milestones. As an 
asset purchase, ITSJ’s legacy liabilities stay with the seller. 
Bengaluru, India — 15 October 2026. AXISCADES Technologies Limited (NSE: AXISCADES; BSE: 532395) 
announced that its Board of Directors has approved on October 14, 2026, the acquisition of substantially all 
the business assets of ITSJ Group, Inc. (“ITSJ”), a San Jose-based precision engineering and electronics 
manufacturer, by XIDA Inc. (“XIDA”), the group’s US AI-infrastructure company and a step-down subsidiary of 
AXISCADES. The transaction gives XIDA an operating base in Silicon Valley and direct supplier positions in two 
of the fastest-growing parts of AI hardware: semiconductor equipment and data-centre infrastructure. 
Two sides of the AI build-out, one platform 
Every advanced AI chip is made on equipment from a handful of toolmakers, and every AI rack has to be 
powered and cooled. ITSJ already sits in both supply chains. Founded in 2023, it has in under three years 
become a qualified supplier of sub-assemblies for semiconductor capital equipment and of data-centre cooling 
and infrastructure systems. Qualification with customers of this calibre usually takes years to earn; XIDA gains 
it from the start. 
ITSJ has grown on the strength of its engineering and customer relationships. XIDA brings what is needed to 
scale it: capital, finance and quality systems, management depth, and the engineering bench of the AXISCADES 
group’s India-based design and manufacturing platform. 
A US headquarters in Silicon Valley, and a tariff-resilient supply chain 
On completion, XIDA plans to strengthen its global delivery capabilities, building on the engineering team that 
supports ITSJ today. This will create a diversified delivery model combining a US front end with Indian 
engineering capabilities, enhancing resilience to tariffs and evolving global trade dynamics. The acquisition 
advances XIDA’s focus on the physical layer of the AI build-out — data-centre cooling and semiconductor-

About AXISCADES Technologies Limited 
AXISCADES Technologies Limited (NSE: AXISCADES; BSE: 532395) is an Indian technology group serving 
aerospace, defence, electronics and semiconductor customers worldwide. Its XIDA business focuses on AI 
infrastructure and deep technology — electronics design, semiconductor-equipment sub-systems, data-centre 
thermal systems and edge AI — from engineering centres in India and the United States. 
About ITSJ Group 
ITSJ Group, Inc., founded in 2023 and headquartered in San Jose, California, is a precision engineering and 
electronics manufacturing company. It serves semiconductor-equipment and data-centre customers with CNC 
machining, PCB design and assembly, electrical cabinets, cable and harness assemblies, box build and system 
integration, from prototype to production.
