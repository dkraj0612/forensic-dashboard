PRESS RELEASE 
AXISCADES Initiates Voluntary Solvent Liquidation of German 
Subsidiary add-solution GmbH 
Orderly closure of a non-core, loss-making unit that completes the Group's exit from automotive 
engineering services. 
Bengaluru, India - 05 October 2026: AXISCADES Technologies Limited ("AXISCADES" or the 
"Company") today announced that its wholly owned German step-down subsidiary, add-solution 
GmbH ("add-solution"), held through AXISCADES GmbH, has been placed into voluntary solvent 
liquidation. The members' resolution was adopted on 24 September 2026, with the dissolution 
effective from 25 September 2026 which has been registered with the German Commercial 
Register office. 
add-solution, based in Wolfsburg, provides engineering design services to the automotive industry. 
The downturn in the German automotive sector reduced demand, and the business recorded 
losses for several consecutive quarters. Following an evaluation of strategic alternatives, the Group 
determined that a voluntary solvent liquidation was the appropriate course. 
Strategic and financial rationale 
• 
Completes the exit from automotive engineering services. Following the recently 
completed transfer of the Automotive Engineering Services business, the liquidation removes 
the Group's remaining automotive engineering exposure. 
• 
Addresses recurring losses and supports earnings quality. add-solution contributed 
₹15.62 crore (1.35%) of FY26 consolidated turnover and had a negative net worth of ₹14.37 
crore as at 31 March 2026. On a standalone basis, add-solution incurred a PAT loss of ₹ 34.48 
crores between FY25 to Q1FY27 and continues incur losses 
• 
Orderly, solvent process under German law. The process is a voluntary solvent liquidation, 
not an insolvency proceeding, and will be conducted in accordance with applicable German 
law. 
• 
Supports focus on Power 930. The decision enables management attention to remain on the 
Company's growth platforms: aerospace manufacturing, defence, XIDA and space. 
• 
No material impact expected. The liquidation is not expected to have any material impact on 
the Company's operations or profitability, while removing a recurring drag on consolidated 
profitability.

The liquidation process 
The liquidation will be carried out in accordance with applicable German law. The liquidator will 
realise assets, settle liabilities and complete the winding-up process. Any accounting effects will be 
recognised in the Company's results for the relevant periods in accordance with applicable 
accounting standards. The Company will keep the stock exchanges informed of material 
developments as required under the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
Management commentary 
“This is a financially disciplined decision to address a non-core, loss-making exposure. The 
orderly, solvent process supports our focus on earnings quality, capital efficiency and disciplined 
execution of Power 930.” 
– Shashidhar SK, Group CFO, AXISCADES Technologies Limited 
About AXISCADES Technologies Limited 
AXISCADES Technologies Limited is a publicly listed technology company headquartered in 
Bengaluru, India, with operations across Europe, the Americas and the APAC region. The 
Company delivers product-based Aerospace and Defence solutions, and advanced Electronics, 
Semiconductor and AI products, working with long-term strategic partners across global OEMs and 
Tier 1 suppliers. 
About add-solution GmbH 
add-solution GmbH, headquartered in Wolfsburg, Germany, provides engineering design services 
to the automotive industry. It became part of the AXISCADES Group in June 2023 and entered 
voluntary solvent liquidation with effect from 25 September 2026.
