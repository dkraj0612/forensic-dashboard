Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) - Execution of Transaction Documents 
and completion of the acquisition of 90% of the share capital of Cloud Wave Technologies 
Private Limited by AXISCADES Technologies Limited. 
 
Ref: Our earlier intimation dated August 28, 2026 
 
Further to our intimation dated  August 28, 2026 and in terms of Regulation 30 of the Listing 
Regulations read with sub-paragraph 1 of Paragraph A of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations, we hereby inform you that AXISCADES Technologies Limited (“AXISCADES” or 
“Company”) has executed the Share Purchase Agreement with Cloud Wave Technologies Private 
Limited (“Cloud Wave”), Protohubs System Solutions Private Limited (“Protohubs”), Aureate 
Polymet Private Limited (“Aureate”), Mr. Satish Kumar Pinninti, Mr. Chandrashekar Ramegowda 
Devalapura, Mr. Mahadevapura Raghavendra Rao Prakash, Mr. Ann Antony and Mrs. Deepika 
(collectively the “Sellers”, and such agreement the “Share Purchase Agreement”), and the 
Shareholders’ Agreement with Cloud Wave and Mr. Satish Kumar Pinninti (the “Shareholders' 
Agreement” and collectively with the Share Purchase Agreement the “Transaction Documents”) 
on September 10, 2026, and has simultaneously completed the acquisition of 90% (ninety 
percent) of the share capital of Cloud Wave (and the indirect acquisition of 90% (ninety percent) 
of the share capital of Cloud Wave's wholly owned subsidiaries, Protohubs and Aureate), for a 
purchase consideration of INR 234 crores, subject to post-closing adjustments as per the 
provisions of the Share Purchase Agreement.  
 
Consequent to completion of this acquisition and other closing conditions as mentioned in the 
Transaction Documents, Cloud Wave, Protohubs and Aureate have become subsidiaries of the 
Company, and Protohubs and Aureate have become step-down subsidiaries of the Company.
