Dear Sir/Madam, 
Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Acquisition of the business assets of ITSJ Group, Inc. by 
XIDA Inc., a step-down subsidiary of the Company 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) read with Schedule III thereto, we 
wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e., 
October 14, 2026, has approved, in principle, the acquisition by XIDA Inc. (formerly Mistral 
Solutions Inc.), a step-down subsidiary of the Company incorporated in the State of Delaware, 
USA (“XIDA”), of substantially all the business assets of ITSJ Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation 
with its principal place of business in San Jose, California, USA (“ITSJ”), by way of an asset 
purchase (the “Proposed Transaction”). The Proposed Transaction is subject to the execution 
of an Asset Purchase Agreement among XIDA, ITSJ and its promoter, and to the satisfaction of 
the conditions precedent set out therein. XIDA is not acquiring any shares of ITSJ, and ITSJ will 
not become a subsidiary of the Company or of XIDA. 
The aggregate consideration for the Proposed Transaction shall not exceed US$ 7.0 million 
(United States Dollars Seven Million only), comprising cash, restricted shares of XIDA and a 
performance-linked earn-out, as detailed in Annexure-I. 
The relevant details pertaining to the above, as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing 
Regulations, read with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 
issued on July 11, 2023, and last updated on January 30, 2026, are enclosed as “Annexure-I”.
