Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“LODR Regulations”), read with Schedule III thereto, we wish to inform 
you that add solution GmbH, Germany, a step-down subsidiary of AXISCADES Technologies 
Limited, has been placed under voluntary solvent liquidation. 
 
As per the German Commercial Register extract dated 2 October 2026, add solution GmbH, 
which was into continuous losses, for the past several quarters, due to downturn in the 
automotive industry in Germany, has been dissolved for the purpose of winding up its affairs. 
The voluntary liquidation is being undertaken as part of the Group's business rationalization 
initiatives and is not expected to have any material impact on the operations,  or profitability 
of the Company. The liquidation process shall be carried out in accordance with the applicable 
laws of Germany.

of Schedule III of the LODR Regulations and SEBI Master Circular HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-
