CLARIFICATION: 
 
As you are aware that the Company is currently undergoing Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) vide 
an order dated 15th May, 2026 of the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Hyderabad Bench-1. Pursuant to 
Section 17 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, the powers of the Board of Directors of the Company 
stand suspended and are being exercised by the Resolution Professional, Ms. Medarametla Srinivasa Manoranjani. 
Accordingly, the holding of Annual General Meeting and submission of Annual Report as per Regulation 34 of the 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the period ended 31st March 2026 will be considered and approved by the 
Resolution Professional assisted by the Management Committee. 
  
Pursuant to Regulation 34 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 the Company is required to submit to the Stock 
Exchanges and publish on its website a copy of the Annual Report sent to the shareholders along with the notice of 
the annual general meeting on or before the date of commencement of dispatch to its shareholders.  
 
The Company submits that, despite efforts to convene the 106th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) for the financial 
year ended 31 March 2026 on or before 30 September 2026, pursuant to Section 96 of the Companies Act, 2013, it 
was unable to do so primarily due to the non-availability of the updated Benpos statement, which was essential for 
finalising the shareholder list, issuing notices with the mandatory 21 clear days’ notice under Section 101 of the 
Companies Act, and arranging related corporate actions, including e-voting.  
 
The last available Benpos was dated 19 July 2026, and despite repeated requests, CDSL initially withheld the 
updated Benpos citing pre-CIRP dues, compelling the Company to approach the NCLT, Hyderabad through I.A. No. 
1205/2026. The Resolution Professional subsequently assured the Tribunal that the requisite payments would be 
made, however, the updated Benpos was provided only on 25 September 2026.  
 
It is submitted that CDSL’s withholding of the Benpos statement on the ground of pre-CIRP dues was not in 
consonance with the moratorium under Section 14 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, which bars any 
action to recover or enforce dues that arose prior to the insolvency commencement date. The Company, through the
