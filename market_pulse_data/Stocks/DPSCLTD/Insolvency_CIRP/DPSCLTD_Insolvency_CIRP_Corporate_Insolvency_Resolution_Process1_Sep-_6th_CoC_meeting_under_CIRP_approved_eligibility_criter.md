Dear Sir(s),  
 
Further to the Company’s intimation dated 16th May 2026 regarding commencement of the Corporate Insolvency 
Resolution Process (“CIRP”) of India Power Corporation Limited (“Company”) and appointment of the Interim 
Resolution Professional, who was subsequently confirmed as the Resolution Professional, we would like to inform 
that the 6th Meeting of the Committee of Creditors (“CoC”) of the Company was convened on Tuesday, 1st 
September, 2026 at 3:00 P.M (IST) through Video Conferencing and concluded at 5:00 P.M. (IST). 
 
The Chairperson welcomed the members of the Committee of Creditors and other participants and recorded 
appreciation to the financial creditors for consenting to convene the meeting.  
 
The agenda items as per the notice convening the meeting and other matters placed before the Committee were 
deliberated upon by the CoC and the agenda item relating to approval of  the eligibility criteria for Prospective 
Resolution Applicants, the Invitation for EOI and the Form G was approved by the CoC by show of hands. 
 
The Agenda item relating to engagement of advocates/law firms for CIRP-related legal services was decided to be 
taken up in the next CoC meeting.
