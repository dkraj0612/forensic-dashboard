## Page 2

– Allcargo Logistics, one of the largest integrated logistics solutions
companies in India, is using Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications to support its mission of delivering customer-
centric, digitally enabled, and well-governed logistics services worldwide. With Oracle Fusion
Applications for finance, Allcargo Logistics has standardized core business processes on a single integrated
platform, reduced manual work through automation, strengthened governance and operational visibility,
and reduced month-end close time by 30 percent.
Founded in 1993, Allcargo Logistics now operates more than 700 facilities in India - with customers across
diverse industries such as e-commerce, automobile, pharmaceutical and more. To support continued
growth, the company needed to move off its legacy on-premises systems and onto a scalable cloud platform
that uses AI to automate routine business processes, provide real-time visibility, and strengthen controls.
After careful evaluation, Allcargo Logistics decided to move its finance operations to Oracle Fusion
Applications.
“Digital transformation is central to our long-term growth strategy and our commitment to operational
excellence,” said Sanjay Khiyani, chief information officer, Allcargo Logistics. “With Oracle Fusion
Applications for finance, we have been able to bring more than 4,000 users and over 700 operating units
onto a single integrated platform, giving us a foundation to use AI to simplify how work gets done,
strengthen visibility, and support our continued growth.”
With Oracle Fusion Cloud Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Allcargo Logistics has standardized and
automated finance processes across its operations, helping teams spend less time on manual process
execution while strengthening controls and improving financial reporting. Oracle Fusion Cloud
Procurement, part of Oracle Cloud ERP, has also helped Allcargo Logistics automate end-to-end
procurement processes and provide teams with a consistent view of purchasing activity across its
distributed operations.
“As customer expectations continue to rise and supply chains become more dynamic, logistics providers
need applications that can increasingly take on more of the work required to keep their operations moving,”
said Shailesh Singla, vice president, Cloud Applications, Oracle India. “With extensive AI and Fusion
Agentic Applications, Oracle is helping Allcargo Logistics automate routine tasks and move work forward,
enabling its teams to spend less time managing processes and more time applying judgment to the decisions
and priorities that drive business outcomes.”
The implementation was managed by Oracle PartnerNetwork (OPN) partner, Grant Thornton Bharat.

## Page 3

Allcargo Logistics Limited, under the composite Scheme of Arrangement, has merged its Domestic Supply
Chain business and demerged its International Supply Chain (ISC) business as Allcargo Global. The
Domestic Supply Chain business houses Express Distribution and Consultative Logistics. Allcargo Logistics
combines legacy, innovation, and customer-centricity with a robust distribution network, deep expertise,
and a digital-first approach to provide reliable logistics services for MSMEs, retailers, and enterprises. With
a nationwide network covering 100% of India’s districts and a growing presence across Asia, the company
offers extensive reach and service capability. Its key business verticals include Express Distribution, Air
Freight, E-commerce Logistics, First and Last Mile Delivery, and more. The company also offers specialized
B2C services such as Laabh, Bike Express, and Student Express, designed to meet the evolving needs of
diverse customer segments.
The Allcargo Group remains committed to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) standards and
continues to invest in its people, technology, business processes, and operations. The Group has set a goal
of achieving 100% carbon neutrality by 2040.
Allcargo Logistics Limited is listed on BSE Limited (Scrip Code: 532749) and National Stock Exchange of
India Limited (Symbol: ALLCARGO).
Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications are a complete suite of cloud applications that form the enterprise system
of record, spanning finance, HR, supply chain, manufacturing, sales, service, and marketing. The suite
includes Oracle Fusion Cloud Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Oracle Fusion Cloud Human Capital
Management (HCM), Oracle Fusion Cloud Supply Chain & Manufacturing (SCM), and Oracle Fusion Cloud
Customer Experience (CX).
Fusion Agentic Applications extend that foundation into a system of outcomes. They can coordinate and
execute end-to-end work toward business objectives, and are designed to operate within the policies,
controls, and authority each organization defines. With Oracle AI Agent Studio for Fusion Applications,
customers and partners can create, extend, and manage these agents and agentic applications combining
trusted enterprise data with the security, data governance, and performance needed to operate at scale.
Oracle offers integrated suites of applications plus secure, autonomous infrastructure in the Oracle Cloud.
For more information about Oracle (NYSE: ORCL), please visit us at oracle.com.
