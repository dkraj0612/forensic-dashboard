HCLTech and CrowdStrike Expand Strategic Partnership to Advance AI Security and 
Resilience 
 
NEW YORK, NOIDA, India, AUSTIN, Texas
, Sept . 14, 2026 – HCLTech , a leading global technology 
company, and CrowdStrike (NASDAQ: CRWD) today announced an expansion of their strategic 
partnership to advance AI security and resilience by integrating 
CrowdStrike Falcon® Guardian
 with 
HCLTech’s AI Security and Resilience services.
 
 
Together, HCLTech and CrowdStrike combine Falcon Guardian with HCLTech's cybersecurity 
advisory, AI governance, implementation and managed services delivered through HCLTech's 
Cybersecurity Fusion Centers, to help enterprises secure AI applications, models
, agents, identities 
and data while improving visibility into emerging risks.
 
 
Building on the Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM)
 services launched in March 2026, 
the expanded partnership brings AI security deeper into enterprise security operations. It combines 
Falcon Guardian's AI detection and response 
(AIDR) capabilities with HCLTech's TRiBe framework for 
AI trust, risk governance and resilience, and 
VERITY Frontier AI Resilience
, a cybersecurity operating 
model that continuously identifies, validates and mitigates exploitable cyber exposure across hybrid 
environments. 
 
“AI is becoming a new enterprise attack surface, and securing it requires visibility and protection 
everywhere AI operates,” said
 Daniel Bernard, Chief Business Officer, CrowdStrike. “CrowdStrike 
pioneered AI Detection and Response to secure the full AI at
tack surface. Together 
with HCLTech’s global cybersecurity
 expertise and services, we’re bringing these capabilities to 
more enterprises as they operationalize AI at scale.”
  
 
“The next generation of enterprise security will require organizations to address both sides of the AI 
risk equation, defending against AI
- powered threats while securing the AI systems they deploy,” said 
Amit Jain, EVP and Global Head of Cybersecurity at H
CLTech. “By bringing together our 
complementary strengths in cybersecurity innovation and enterprise resilience, CrowdStrike and 
HCLTech are helping organizations scale AI securely and responsibly while driving greater 
operational resilience and business c
onfidence.”
