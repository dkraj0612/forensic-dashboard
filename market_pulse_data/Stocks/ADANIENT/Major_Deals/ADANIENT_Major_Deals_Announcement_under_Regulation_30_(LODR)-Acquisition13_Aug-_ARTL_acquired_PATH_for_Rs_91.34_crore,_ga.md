Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We wish to inform that Adani Road Transport Limited (“ARTL”), a wholly owned subsidiary of 
the Company and Adani Road GRICL Limited (“GRICL”), a wholly owned subsidiary of ARTL 
and step down subsidiary of the Company, have acquired 99.99% and 0.01% stake 
respectively, in Path Highway LLP (“PATH”) from Prakash Asphalting and Tolls Highway 
(India) Limited (“Seller 1”) and Mrs. Neeti Agarwal (“Seller 2”).  
Intimation regarding the aforesaid acquisition was received by the Company on 13th August, 
2026 at 12:58 PM. 
Prior to above acquisition, ARTL and PATH were holding 74%:25.99% stake in Mancherial 
Repallewada Road Limited (“MRRL”); Suryapet Khammam Road Limited (“SKRL”) and Bilaspur 
Pathrapali Road Limited (“BPRL”). Consequent to the above acquisition, ARTL will hold 
99.99% shareholding in MRRL, SKRL and BPRL.
