> **Executive Summary: Bajaj Finance Limited Q2 FY27 Investor Presentation**  Bajaj Finance Limited reported an excellent Q2 FY27, showcasing robust financial growth with a 26% increase in AUM to ₹ 584,624 crore and a 29% rise in both PBT and PAT, alongside strong customer acquisition and improved asset quality metrics (GNPA 1.06%, NNPA 0.42%). Operating efficiencies
11. Net interest income grew by 24% to ₹ 13,335 crore as against ₹ 10,785 crore in Q2 FY26. 
12. Net total income grew by 23% to ₹ 15,974 crore as against ₹ 12,982 crore in Q2 FY26.
13. Opex to Net total income was 33.0% as against 33.1% in Q2 FY26. The Company expects 25-40 bps improvement in Opex 
to NTI in FY27
14. Full-time employee count stood at 75,845 as of 30 September 2026. The Company added 2,584 employees in Q2 FY27. 
Adjusted for Gold Loan and MFI, the net addition was 1,058. Employee attrition was 15.1% in Q2 FY27.
15. The Fixed Term Contract (FTC) resource count stood at 89,484 as of 30 September 2026. 
Credit cost
16. Loan losses and provisions in Q2 FY27 were ₹ 2,166 crore as against ₹ 2,080 crore in Q2 FY26. Q2 FY27 includes a prudent 
management and macro-economic provision of ₹ 337 crore. Excluding this, loan losses and provisions were ₹ 1,829 crore, 
a decline of 12% as against Q2 FY26.
17. Loan loss to average AUF was 1.56% in Q2 FY27 as against 1.88% in Q2 FY26. Excluding prudent management and macro-
economic provision of ₹ 337 crore, it was 1.32% in Q2 FY27.
18. Stage 2 & 3 assets increased by ₹ 1,601 crore in Q2, including a ₹ 1,047 crore increase in the gold loan business due to 
short-tenor schemes that are no longer being offered, taking Stage 2 & 3 assets to 2.02% of loans from 1.87% in Q1 
FY27. The Company does not expect impact on its credit cost from this transitional increase in gold loan stage 2.
19. Adjusted for transient movement in gold loans, Stage 2 & 3 assets improved to 1.88% of loans from 1.90% in Q1 FY27, 
reflecting improving asset quality trends in portfolio.
