Additionally, as communicated earlier, the Resolution Plan provided an option for the Lead Member and 
the Company to agree on terms and conditions for infusion by the Company of debt / equity in WWIL. 
However, since the scheme of arrangement forming part of the Resolution Plan, was not approved by the 
NCLT vide the NCLT Approval Order, the Consortium revised the transaction structure / structure for 
implementation of the Resolution Plan (pursuant to such flexibility under the Resolution Plan), which was 
approved by the implementation and monitoring committee vide its resolution dated August 31, 2026 
(“Revised Structure”). In terms of the Revised Structure, the Lead Member and the Company had an 
option to agree on terms and conditions for infusion by the Company of debt / equity in Vibhav Energy 
Private Limited, one of implementation entities of the Lead Member (“VEPL”).  
 
In view of the above, pursuant to an inter corporate deposit agreement dated September 28, 2026 (“ICD 
Agreement”), the Company had disbursed an amount of INR 100,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees One Hundred 
Crores) to VEPL, which amount has, in terms of the ICD Agreement, on October 09, 2026, been converted 
into equity shares of VEPL representing 25% (twenty-five percent) of the paid-up share capital of VEPL 
(as calculated on a fully diluted basis), and the Company has, on October 09, 2026, received intimation 
from VEPL that such equity shares have been allotted to the Company.
