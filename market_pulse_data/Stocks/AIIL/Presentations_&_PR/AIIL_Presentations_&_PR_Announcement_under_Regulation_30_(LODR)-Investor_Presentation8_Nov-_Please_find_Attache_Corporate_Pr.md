> Authum Investment & Infrastructure Limited is undergoing a significant strategic transformation, shifting from a pure-play investment firm to a diversified credit and alternative assets platform, marked by substantial fresh capital deployment in credit (over Rs. 4,100 Cr by Sep-26) and the acquisition of NBFCs and an ARC.
