Sub:  Disclosure under Regulation 29(2) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and

Dear Sirs,  
Enclosed is a disclosure by Catalyst Trusteeship Limited under Regulation 29 (2) of the SEBI 
(Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 (the “Takeover Code”). 
Catalyst Trusteeship Limited (acting in the capacity of the security agent) had previously made a 
disclosure on 3 December 2025 in relation to encumbrance over the shares of Apollo Tyres Limited 
by way of creation of pledge.  
This disclosure is in respect of the release of pledge over 63,050,966 equity shares, constituting 
9.93% of the paid-up share capital of Apollo Tyres Limited, created by Emerald Sage Investment 
Ltd in favour of Catalyst Trusteeship Limited (acting in the capacity of the security agent) for the 
benefit of certain lenders.
