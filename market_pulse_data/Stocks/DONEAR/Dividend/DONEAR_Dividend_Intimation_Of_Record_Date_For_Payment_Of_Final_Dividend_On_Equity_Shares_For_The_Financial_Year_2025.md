Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 42 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, the Company has fixed Friday, 18th September, 2026, as the Record Date for determining entitlement 
of Members for payment of final dividend of Rs. 0.20/- per equity share having face value of Rs. 2/- each 
for the financial year ended 31st March, 2026. If the final dividend as recommended by the Board of 
Directors is approved at the AGM, payment of such dividend, subject to deduction of tax at source, will be 
made on or after 26th September, 2026,  as under: 
a. To all Beneficial Owners in respect of shares held in dematerialized form as per the data as may be made 
available by the Depositories, as of close of business hours on 18th September, 2026; and 
b. To all Members in respect of shares held in physical form after giving effect to valid transmission or 
transposition request lodged with the Company as of the close of business hours on 18th September, 2026. 
Kindly take the above information on your records.
