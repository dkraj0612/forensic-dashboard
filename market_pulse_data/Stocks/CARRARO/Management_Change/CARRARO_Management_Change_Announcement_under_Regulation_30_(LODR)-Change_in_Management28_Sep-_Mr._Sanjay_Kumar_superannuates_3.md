Ref.: Regulation 30 (read with Part A of Schedule III) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and

Pursuant to above-mentioned Listing Regulations, this is to inform you that, basis the recommendation 
of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors has taken note and approved 
the following today, ie., 28th September, 2026: 
 
a. Superannuation of Mr. Sanjay Kumar, Director of Manufacturing and consequent cessation as 
the Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company, to be effective from the close of 
business hours on Wednesday, 30th September, 2026. 
 
b. Appointment of Mr. Dheeraj Kumar Rai as Director of Manufacturing and designate him as the 
Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company, to be effective from Thursday, 01st 
October, 2026.
