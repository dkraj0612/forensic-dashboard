Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
In continuation of the Company’s earlier disclosures dated December 04, 2024, January 
31, 2025 and August 07, 2026, we wish to inform you that a Joint Venture Agreement 
(“JVA”) has been executed between Carnation Hotels Private Limited (“Carnation”), a 
wholly owned subsidiary of the Company and RJ Corp Limited (“RJ Corp”) with respect to  
Arum Hotels Private Limited (“Arum”), the Special Purpose Vehicle (“SPV”) and step-
down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, for the development and execution of 
the Aurika Shillong project. 
 
Pursuant to the execution of the JVA, RJ Corp shall acquire 49% stake by subscribing to 
fresh issuance of equity shares of Arum. As contemplated under the JVA, Carnation shall 
hold 51% of the equity share capital in Arum. Upon completion of the aforesaid 
transactions, Arum shall cease to be a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company, and shall continue as a step-down subsidiary of the Company. 
 
The details of the aforesaid transaction, as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read with SEBI 
Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026, are enclosed as Annexures A and B. 
This is for your information and record please. 
 
Thanking You
