Dear Sir/Ma’am, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of E2E Networks Limited (“the 
Company”), at its meeting held on October 16, 2026, took note of the non-compliance with Regulation 29 of the 
SEBI Listing Regulations, pertaining to the non-submission of prior intimation to the Stock Exchanges in 
connection with the Board Meeting held on August 31, 2026. 
 
The Board took note of the fines of Rs.10,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand only), plus applicable taxes, imposed 
separately by NSE and BSE in this regard. The Company has duly paid the respective fines, along with applicable 
taxes, to the Stock Exchanges. 
 
Further, in accordance with the directions of the Stock Exchanges, the Company has brought the provisions of the 
applicable circular to the notice of the promoter of the Company. 
 
The Board advised the management to exercise due diligence and ensure strict adherence to the applicable 
regulatory requirements and prescribed timelines under the SEBI Listing Regulations to avoid the recurrence of 
such instances in the future. 
 
The Company shall continue to strengthen its internal compliance monitoring mechanisms to ensure timely 
submission of all applicable prior intimations and disclosures to the Stock Exchanges.
