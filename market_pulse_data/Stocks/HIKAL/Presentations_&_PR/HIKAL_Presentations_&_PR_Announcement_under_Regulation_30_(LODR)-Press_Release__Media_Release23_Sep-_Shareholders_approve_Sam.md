Mumbai, September 23, 2026: Hikal Limited, a trusted life sciences partner to global pharmaceutical, 
crop protection, animal health and specialty chemical companies, today announced that its 
shareholders have approved the appointment of Mr. Sameer Hiremath as Chairman & Managing 
Director of the Company for a five-year term, effective October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2031. 
The appointment was approved at Hikal’s 38th Annual General Meeting. Shareholders also approved 
the audited financial statements for FY2025-26, the final dividend of Rs. 0.40 (Forty Paise) and the re-
appointment of Mr. Sarangan Suresh as Executive Director. 
Mr. Sameer Hiremath has been associated with Hikal’s Board since May 1999 and was last reappointed 
as Managing Director at the Company’s 2021 Annual General Meeting. His continued association with 
the Company spans almost three decades, during which Hikal has expanded its capabilities across 
pharmaceuticals, crop protection and emerging life-sciences businesses.  
Commenting on the occasion, Mr. Sameer Hiremath, Chairman & Managing Director, Hikal Limited, 
said: “FY2025-26 was a year of resilience, disciplined execution and capability building for Hikal. We 
strengthened our core businesses while making focused investments in high-value capabilities, 
including complex chemistries, HPAPI, CDMO and advanced R&D infrastructure.  
As we move forward, our focus will remain on strengthening our core businesses, accelerating our 
diversification into new geographies and growing our emerging business verticals like Animal Health 
and Specialty Chemicals which include Personal Care. Under the current plan, we have healthy cash 
flows and strong financials allowing us to focus on deepening customer partnerships and continuing 
to invest in technology, quality and manufacturing excellence” 
The Company recently announced the commencement of commercial production of Personal Care 
products at its new cGMP-compliant multipurpose facility at Panoli, Gujarat. The facility marks Hikal’s 
entry into commercial-scale manufacturing for the global beauty and personal care industry. 
Hikal has also been awarded the EcoVadis Gold Medal rating, placing it among the top 5% of 
companies assessed globally for sustainability performance. 
About Hikal Limited 
Established in 1988, Hikal is a reliable partner to companies in the Pharmaceutical, Animal Health, Biotech, 
Personal Care and Specialty Chemical industries. We manufacture and supply quality Active Ingredients, 
Intermediates, Biocides to the global life sciences industry. Hikal has five manufacturing facilities in India at 
Maharashtra (Taloja and Mahad), Gujarat (Panoli) and Bengaluru (Jigani) and a world-class Research & 
Technology centre at Pune. Our facilities have been inspected and certified by the Global Regulatory Authorities. 
We create and deliver sustainable solutions for our partners and customers across the life-sciences value chain.  
Hikal believes in inclusive growth that embraces all constituents. Hikal is one of the first Indian life-sciences 
companies to receive the Responsible Care® certification and has also been awarded the EcoVadis Gold Medal
