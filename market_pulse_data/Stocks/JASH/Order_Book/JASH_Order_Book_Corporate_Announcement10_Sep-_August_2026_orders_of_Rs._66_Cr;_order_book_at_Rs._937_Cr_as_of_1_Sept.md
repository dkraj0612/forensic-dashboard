MONTHLY ORDER INTAKE: In the month of August 2026, the consolidated orders received by the

         The significant contributors to the monthly order booking of August 2026 are Enviro Infra Engineers - 
New Delhi & Vishwaraj Environment Ltd. - Nagpur from India and Calgary Fish Creek WWTP, AB - 
USA & Fullink Technologies Ltd. - Hong Kong from international market. 
 
2) 
CONSOLIDATED ORDER BOOK POSITION: The total consolidated order book position of the 
company as on 1st September 2026 is Rs. 937 Cr out of which orders worth Rs. 296 Cr are for Indian 
market and orders worth Rs. 641 Cr are for markets outside India 
 
Out of the consolidated orders worth Rs. 641 Cr for markets outside India, Rs. 374 Cr worth orders are 
for USA market, Rs. 35 Cr are from Waterfront-UK, Rs. 31 Cr are from Mahr-Austria and remaining Rs. 
201 Cr constitute orders for rest of the World. 
 
3) 
ORDERS NEGOTIATED BUT NOT YET RECEIVED: As on 1st September 2026, consolidated 
orders worth Rs. 86 Cr have been negotiated with clients and formal purchase orders are awaited. Out of 
this Rs. 26 Cr are for Indian market and Rs. 60 Cr are for projects outside India. 
 
        After negotiating an order, it may take up to 2 months to receive a formal Purchase Order from the client. 
Once a Purchase Order is received, we remove its value from the list of “negotiated orders” and move it to 
list of order received in a given month and add it to the total outstanding consolidated order book position 
of the Company.
