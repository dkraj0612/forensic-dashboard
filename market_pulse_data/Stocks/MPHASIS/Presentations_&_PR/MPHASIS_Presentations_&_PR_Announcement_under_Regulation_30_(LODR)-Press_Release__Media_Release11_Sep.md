SGBS Unnati Foundation and Mphasis join hands for a greener Bengaluru 
 
 
 
 
Bengaluru, September 11, 2026 
Mphasis, (BSE: 526299; NSE: MPHASIS), a global AI-led, platform-driven technology solutions provider, is supporting an 
environmental sustainability initiative led by SGBS Unnati Foundation in collaboration with Bangalore Metro Rail 
Corporation Limited (BMRCL). This initiative recognizes the importance of collective action in advancing urban 
sustainability. As Bengaluru continues to evolve as a rapidly growing urban centre, creating greener and more sustainable 
public spaces requires greater collaboration across sectors.  
This commitment takes shape through the current collaboration, under which SGBS Unnati Foundation has signed a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with BMRCL for the maintenance of Pier Nos. BP-382 to BP-437. The initiative will 
support the upkeep of the designated metro corridor while contributing to a cleaner and more sustainable public 
environment. The initiative builds on Mphasis’ broader CSR commitment of working with social organisations, 
government institutions and community stakeholders to address environmental and community priorities.  
SGBS Unnati Foundation made history by becoming the first NGO in India to be listed on the Social Stock Exchanges of 
both NSE and BSE in December 2023, marking a milestone in governance, transparency, and measurable social impact. 
“Building sustainable communities requires more than individual efforts. It calls for organisations to come together and 
take shared responsibility for the places in which we live and work. At Mphasis, our CSR efforts are guided by this belief, 
with partnerships enabling us to support issues that are relevant to the communities around us. Our association with 
SGBS Unnati Foundation and BMRCL is a continuation of this efforts, enabling us to support an initiative that will 
strengthen the upkeep of Bengaluru’s shared spaces while fostering a greater sense of collective responsibility towards 
the city’s future,” said Deepa Nagraj, Senior Vice President & Global Head - ESG, Sparkle Innovation Ecosystem, and 
Communications, Mphasis.  
“Meaningful and sustainable community development is possible when organisations come together with a shared sense 
of responsibility. Our collaboration with Mphasis and BMRCL is a testament to this spirit, enabling us to contribute 
towards cleaner, better-maintained public spaces and a stronger sense of community ownership in Bengaluru,” said Dr. 
Ramesh Swamy, Director, SGBS Unnati Foundation. 
Bringing together BMRCL, SGBS Unnati Foundation and Mphasis, the initiative represents a cross-sector approach to 
addressing an urban priority. It combines institutional support, CSR participation and community-oriented 
implementation to create a model for sustained stewardship of shared urban spaces. 
The initiative is part of a continued effort to support programmes that respond to local priorities through meaningful 
partnerships and sustained engagement. By bringing together organisations with complementary strengths, the 
collaboration aims to contribute to Bengaluru’s environmental sustainability while creating value for the communities that 
call the city home. 
Over the years, Mphasis has championed a range of environmental and community development initiatives, focusing on 
water conservation, lake rejuvenation, afforestation, and community welfare through strategic partnerships that drive 
long-term impact. As part of its commitment to ecological restoration, Mphasis supports the planting and maintenance of
