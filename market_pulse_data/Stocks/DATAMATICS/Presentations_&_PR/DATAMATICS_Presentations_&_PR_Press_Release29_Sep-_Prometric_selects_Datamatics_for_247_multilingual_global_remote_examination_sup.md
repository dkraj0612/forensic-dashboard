Prometric selects Datamatics to support its Global 
Remote Examination Operations 
 
Mumbai, India, September 29, 2026: Datamatics, a leading global Digital Operations, 
Experiences and Technologies Company, today announced that Prometric, a global 
leader in credentialing and skills development, has selected Datamatics to support its 
remote examination operations globally. 
Datamatics will provide 24/7/365 multilingual support in English, Spanish, and French 
through its delivery centers across India, the Philippines, and Peru. The scalable global 
delivery model will support key stages of the remote examination journey, including 
candidate technical readiness, remote exam proctoring, candidate support, security 
monitoring, quality checks, workforce planning, and performance reporting and 
governance. 
The engagement is designed to help Prometric minimize candidate wait times and 
service interruptions while maintaining examination security, service quality, and 
candidate satisfaction scores above 90%. The global delivery model will enable 
Prometric to scale its remote testing operations while delivering a consistent, seamless, 
and secure candidate experience across markets. 
Joel Beauchamp, Director - Remote Testing Operations, Prometric, said, "For 
candidates, even a small delay or technical issue on exam day can significantly impact 
their testing experience. As our remote testing operations grow, we need to ensure 
candidates receive timely support while maintaining the security and integrity of every 
examination. Datamatics’ multilingual, 24/7 delivery model provides the operational 
scale, flexibility, and resilience needed to deliver a consistent experience across markets." 
Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Datamatics, said, "The 
Prometric engagement demonstrates our ability to manage complex, high-volume 
operations across geographies and languages while maintaining consistent service 
levels. We build dedicated teams aligned to each client’s specific requirements and 
combine this operating model with technology, automation, and AI-led capabilities. This 
enables testing and credentialing organizations to improve operational efficiency while 
delivering a secure, reliable, and seamless candidate experience." 
Datamatics partners with leading global technology companies and testing & 
assessment providers to manage and scale their credentialing and certification 
programs worldwide.
