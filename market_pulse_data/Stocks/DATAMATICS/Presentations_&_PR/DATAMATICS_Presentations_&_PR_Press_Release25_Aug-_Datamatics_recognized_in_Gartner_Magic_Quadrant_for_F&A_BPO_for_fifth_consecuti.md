Datamatics Recognized in the 2026 Gartner® Magic 
Quadrant™ for Finance & Accounting Business Process 
Outsourcing Report for the Fifth Consecutive Year 
 
Mumbai, India – 25th August 2026: Datamatics, a global Digital Operations, Technologies, 
and Experiences company, today announced that it has been recognized as a Niche Player 
in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Finance & Accounting Business Process 
Outsourcing report for the fifth consecutive year. We believe that this recognition solidifies 
Datamatics' position as a preferred partner for CFOs seeking AI-powered autonomous 
finance. 
The Gartner Magic Quadrant for Finance & Accounting Business Process Outsourcing 
evaluated 16 Finance & Accounting BPO providers globally based on their Ability to Execute 
and Completeness of Vision, assessing vendors across product and service capabilities, 
innovation, customer experience, geographic strategy, and market responsiveness. 
Datamatics’ Finance & Accounting BPO offering is powered by its proprietary FINATO 
platform. FINATO integrates Datamatics’ proprietary technologies across AI, automation, 
analytics, and intelligent document processing to transform finance operations across 
Procure-to-Pay (P2P), Order-to-Cash (O2C), and Record-to-Report (R2R).  
Mitul Mehta, EVP and Chief Marketing Officer, Datamatics, said, "We're pleased to be 
recognized in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for the fifth consecutive year. In our opinion, 
this recognition reflects our approach of pairing AI with professional judgment. AI and 
agentic technology now sit at the core of FINATO's platform, built for enterprises for the 
next decade, not just today."
