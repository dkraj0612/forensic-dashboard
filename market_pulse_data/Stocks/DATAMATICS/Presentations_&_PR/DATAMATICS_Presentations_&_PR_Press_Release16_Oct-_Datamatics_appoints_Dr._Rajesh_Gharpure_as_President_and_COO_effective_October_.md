Datamatics Appoints Dr. Rajesh Gharpure as President &  
Chief Operating Officer to Accelerate AI-led Growth. 
 
Mumbai, India – October 16, 2026: Datamatics Global Services Limited, a global provider 
of Digital Technology, Operations, and Experiences, today announced the appointment of 
Dr. Rajesh Gharpure as President & Chief Operating Officer (COO). Reporting to Rahul 
Kanodia, Vice Chairman & CEO, Dr. Rajesh will lead key business units and corporate 
functions, strengthening Datamatics’ strategic focus on AI-led services, profitable growth, 
and operational excellence. 
With over 30 years of global experience in 
technology services, P&L management, digital 
transformation, and large-scale operations, Dr. 
Rajesh brings a proven track record of scaling 
businesses, 
expanding 
margins, 
integrating 
acquisitions, and building high-performing global 
teams. 
In his new role, he will oversee Digital Technologies, 
Digital Operations & Experiences, AI Consulting, 
Solutions & Presales, Corporate Quality, and IT 
Operations. His appointment comes at a pivotal time as Datamatics sharpens its focus on 
embedding Artificial Intelligence across its service offerings, accelerating AI-led 
transformation, and scaling solutions that deliver measurable business outcomes. His 
leadership will help strengthen synergies across all business units, enhance execution, and 
deliver exceptional customer value. 
Prior to joining Datamatics, Dr. Rajesh served as Chief Delivery Officer at Persistent 
Systems, where he led the Technology Services organization. He played a key role in 
driving growth, improving margins, scaling managed services, and integrating multiple 
business units. 
Earlier, he spent over two decades at L&T Infotech (LTI), now LTM, as Executive Vice 
President & Global Delivery Head and a member of its Management Council. He managed 
a portfolio across Manufacturing, Automotive, and Energy, serving global customers.

Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman & CEO, Datamatics, said: “Rajesh brings a proven track 
record of business leadership, technology expertise, and operational discipline. As we 
accelerate our strategic shift towards AI-led services, his experience in scaling global 
businesses, driving profitable growth, and transforming service delivery will be invaluable. 
Together, we aim to strengthen our capabilities, expand customer relationships, and build a 
differentiated, AI-driven business that creates sustainable value for our customers, 
employees, partners, and shareholders.” 
Dr. Rajesh Gharpure, President & Chief Operating Officer, Datamatics said,“I am 
delighted to join Datamatics at an exciting stage in its growth journey. Datamatics is already 
on a strong path towards AI-led services, backed by its proprietary platforms and deep 
domain expertise. As AI reshapes how enterprises operate and compete, I see significant 
opportunities to build on this foundation, scale our AI-led capabilities, and deliver greater 
business value to customers. I look forward to working with Rahul and the leadership team 
to accelerate this journey, strengthen operational excellence, and create sustainable value 
for our customers, partners, employees, and shareholders.” 
Dr. Rajesh holds a Ph.D. in Management from Symbiosis International University and 
professional qualifications as a Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Company Secretary (CS), 
and Cost & Works Accountant (ICWA). 
About Datamatics: 
Datamatics (BSE: 532528 | NSE: DATAMATICS) enables enterprises to go Deep in Digital to boost 
their productivity, customer experience, and competitive advantage. Datamatics’ portfolio spans 
across three pillars including Digital Technologies, Digital Operations, and Digital Experiences. It 
has established products in Intelligent Document Processing, Robotic Process Automation, AI/ML 
models, Smart Workflows, Business Intelligence, & Automatic Fare Collection. Datamatics caters 
to a diverse global clientele across Banking, Financial Services, Insurance, Healthcare, 
Manufacturing, International Organizations, and Media & Publishing. The Company has a 
presence across four continents with significant delivery centres in the USA, India, and the 
Philippines. To learn more about Datamatics, visit www.datamatics.com.
