### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Bhagyanagar India Limited has announced October 8, 2026, as the record date for its NCLT-sanctioned Composite Scheme of Arrangement, under which eligible shareholders will receive one equity share of Tieramet Limited for every one share held. A material impact is that these new shares will only be issued in dematerialized form. This poses an administrative red flag for shareholders holding physical shares, as they must provide demat account details by the record date, or their shares will be held in trust, potentially delaying access.
