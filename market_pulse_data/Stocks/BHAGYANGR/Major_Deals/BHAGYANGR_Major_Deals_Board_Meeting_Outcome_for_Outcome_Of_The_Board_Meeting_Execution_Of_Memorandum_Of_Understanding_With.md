under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 — Execution of Memorandum of Understanding with M/s. Om Sree 
Builders and Developers LLP for the Sale of the Company's land at IDA Uppal, Hyderabad 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("Listing Regulations") and in 
compliance with the provisions of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the Board 
of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e. on 12 October, 2026, has inter alia 
approved and executed, a Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU™) with M/s. Om Sree Builders 
and Developers LLP (LLPIN: AAV-7704) (“Developer”) subject to approval of sharcholders of 
the Company which shall be initiated in due course. 
The MOU relates to the Company's land admeasuring approximately 20,080.52 square yards 
situated in Survey No. 1, Block No. III, Plot Nos. A3/P and A2/P, Industrial Development Area 
(IDA), Uppal, Uppal Khalsa Village, Uppal Mandal, Medchal-Malkajgiri District, Telangana 
("Property"). It provides for a hybrid transaction comprising: 
L. Outright sale of 7,260 square yards (1.50 acres) to the Developer at a consideration of Rs. 
60 Crore. 
2. Joint development of the balance of approximately 12,820.52 square yards (approximately 
6489 acres) under a Development Agreement-cum-Irrevocable General Power of Attorney 
(“DAGPA”), with the saleable area shared 30% to the Company admeasuring about 3 lac 
square feet of developed area. 
The details required under the SEBI LODR are given in Annexure A.
