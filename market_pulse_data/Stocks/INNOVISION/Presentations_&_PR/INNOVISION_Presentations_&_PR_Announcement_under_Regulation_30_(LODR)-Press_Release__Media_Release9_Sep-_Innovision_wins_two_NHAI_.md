Innovision Ltd secures Two New NHAI Toll Plaza Mandates in Madhya Pradesh 
 
New Delhi, India, 9th September, 2026 - Innovision Limited, an integrated infrastructure and 
services platform, has received two separate Letters of Award (LoA) by the National Highways 
Authority of India (NHAI) to operate as the user fee collection agency at the IDTL A and IDTL B 
Fee Plazas, on the six-laning of the Indore-Dewas section of NH-3 in Madhya Pradesh, and at 
the Pachwara Fee Plaza, on the four-laning of the Jhansi-Khajuraho section of NH-39, also in 
Madhya Pradesh. The Company has already disclosed these developments to the stock 
exchanges under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.  
Contract highlights: 
Both mandates, granted on September 7, 2026, follow competitive e-tender processes. Beyond 
toll collection, the scope for each also extends to the upkeep and maintenance of adjacent 
toilet blocks, including recouping of consumable items.  
Each engagement runs for a term of one year. The IDTL A and IDTL B Fee Plazas mandate 
carries a broad commercial consideration of ₹219,07,29,635 (Rupees Two Hundred Nineteen 
Crore Seven Lakh Twenty-Nine Thousand Six Hundred and Thirty-Five only), while the Pachwara 
Fee Plaza mandate carries a broad commercial consideration of ₹24,52,79,635 (Rupees Twenty-
Four Crore Fifty-Two Lakh Seventy-Nine Thousand Six Hundred and Thirty-Five only).  
These awards mark a further addition to Innovision's toll management footprint, strengthening 
the Company's presence in Madhya Pradesh and building on its standing within NHAI's user fee 
collection framework.

The underlying model continues to be asset-light in nature, with working capital forming 
the primary requirement, which allows us to expand the portfolio without a proportionate 
rise in capital intensity. 
 
We would also note that the commercial consideration disclosed as part of our regulatory 
filing reflects the gross value of the engagement under the toll collection framework, 
where collections are remitted to NHAI and the Company retains an agreed spread. This 
figure should not be interpreted as an expected direct or proportionate addition to net 
revenue; the more meaningful measure of performance in this vertical remains the 
aggregate contribution across our expanding plaza portfolio. 
 
NHAI's continuing pipeline of toll asset tenders, together with the sector-wide shift toward 
fully digital fee collection, may provide opportunities for participation in future tenders, 
subject to competitive bidding and applicable eligibility criteria. We intend to remain 
disciplined in our participation across this pipeline, while maintaining the execution 
standards that have underpinned our presence across toll, manpower, and skill 
development businesses.”
