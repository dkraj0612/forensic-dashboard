Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
read with Clause 20(b) of Para A of Part A of Schedule III thereto, we wish to inform that the Company has 
received a letter dated September 10, 2026 (Ref. No. NHAI/CO/13013/547/22-23/Dinengpasoh FP/E-
276473/843) from the National Highways Authority of India (“NHAI”), whereby the Competent Authority of 
NHAI has approved forfeiture of part Performance Security amounting to ₹1,60,00,000/- (Rupees One Crore Sixty 
Lakh only) furnished by the Company under the Contract Agreement dated June 10, 2022 for engagement as the 
user fee collection agency at Diengpasoh Fee Plaza (Km 24.700) on the Shillong Bypass connecting NH-40 and 
NH-44, in the State of Meghalaya. 
The aforesaid forfeiture has been imposed under Clause 18(a) of the Contract Agreement, pursuant to findings of 
a Forensic Audit that alleged excess collection of User Fee through Electronic Toll Collection (ETC) at the said 
Fee Plaza. 
In this regard, as a remedial measure and to protect its interests, the Company intends to file a Writ Petition 
before the appropriate Hon’ble Court, challenging the said order of forfeiture and seeking appropriate 
relief in accordance with applicable law. 
The Company will keep the Stock Exchanges informed of any further developments in this regard, as and when 
received.
