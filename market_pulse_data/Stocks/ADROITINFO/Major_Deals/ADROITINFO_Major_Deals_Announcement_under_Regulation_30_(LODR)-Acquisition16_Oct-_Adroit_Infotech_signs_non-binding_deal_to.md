Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable regulations, read with Schedule III of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform the 
Exchanges that Adroit Infotech Limited (the" Company") has entered into a non-binding agreement 
to acquire a majority equity stake in VERSO ALTIMA GROUP D.O.O., a prominent business integrator 
and digital solutions provider headquartered in Zagreb, Croatia. 
Financial Scale: Verso Altima Group d.o.o. has a current standalone revenue of approximately 13 
million Euros. Upon successful completion of the transaction, the consolidated revenue of the 
Company (Adroit Infotech Limited) is projected to reach an estimated $23-25 Million USD 
(approximately Rs. 225-250 Crores). 
• Source of Funding: The transaction is structured without third-party debt and will be financed 
entirely through a combination of internal accruals, Preferential Issue and Rights Issue proceeds. 
 
• Strategic & Operational Synergies: Both entities operate in highly complementary IT services 
portfolios. The acquisition establishes an undisputed global practice in SAP Billing and Revenue 
Innovation Management (BRIM)and advanced telecom engineering. 
 
• Hybrid Delivery & Profitability: By unifying Verso Altima Group's European on-site front-end with 
the deep technical core and proprietary products of the Company's subsidiary, Verso Altima India 
Limited, the transaction creates an optimized on-site/offshore model positioned to maximize 
resource utilization and drive up overall corporate profitability. 
 
The transaction remains subject to the execution of definitive Share Purchase Agreements (SPA), 
satisfactory completion of comprehensive legal and financial due diligence, necessary board 
authorizations, and customary regulatory approvals in India and Croatia. detailed copy of the Press 
Release being issued by the Company in this regard is enclosed herewith as Annexure-A, and the 
SEBI structural disclosure table is enclosed as Annexure-B.

HYDERABAD, INDIA — Adroit Infotech Limited today announced it has entered into a non-binding agreement 
to acquire a majority equity stake in Verso Altima Group d.o.o., a highly regarded business integrator 
headquartered in Zagreb, Croatia. The transaction instantly onboards Verso Altima's standalone revenues of 
approximately 13 Million Euros. Following finalization, the combined revenue is projected to surge to an 
estimated $23-25 Million USD (approximately Rs. 200-250 Crores), driven by a combined employee strength 
of 400+ employees. 
Funding Mechanism: Adroit Infotech plans to complete the transaction without relying on third-party debt. 
The acquisition will be financed through a prudent mix of Internal Accruals, preferential equity issue and Rights 
Issue Proceeds. 
European Market Expansion and Global Delivery Capabilities 
The acquisition serves as a strategic anchor for Adroit Infotech's expansion into Continental Europe. By 
merging Verso Altima’s robust European footprint with Adroit’s existing infrastructure, the combined entity 
establishes a highly scalable global delivery model. This framework connects localized, high-touch European 
front-end consulting seamlessly with cost-effective, deep technical offshore and nearshore delivery centers, 
fully supported by our expanded global talent pool of over 400 professionals. 
Strengthening SAP Strategic Partnerships and Systems Integration Opportunities 
This integration substantially amplifies the company's capabilities as an elite SAP partner, opening up high-
value avenues across multiple core domains: 
• 
SAP Licensing and Software Sales: Expanding SAP licensing and subscription-related opportunities 
through relevant partner engagement models. 
• 
Systems Integration (SI): Pursuing larger and more complex SAP-led digital transformation and systems 
integration engagements. 
• 
Implementation and Consulting: Delivering SAP advisory, implementation, migration, modernization, 
and business process transformation services.

Innovation and Emerging Technologies: Accelerating the adoption of SAP Business Technology Platform

Deepened Technical Core & SAP BRIM Powerhouse 
The combined entity will possess elite technical capabilities geared toward managing high-volume 
monetization and digital network architectures. This includes specialized global practices in SAP Billing and 
Revenue Innovation Management (BRIM) spanning subscription billing, flexible pricing orchestration, partner 
revenue settlements, and order-to-cash lifecycle automation. Additionally, Verso Altima Group brings a legacy 
of high-performance telecom integrations, incorporating advanced mediation and application server 
frameworks. 
Industry-Focused Solutions, Accelerators and Product Innovation 
The transaction unifies proprietary product frameworks and industry-specific accelerators to fast-track digital 
transformation. This leverages the expanded delivery capabilities of Adroit's existing subsidiary, Verso Altima 
India Limited (VAI). Based in Navi Mumbai, VAI is a CMMI Level 3, certified SAP PartnerEdge – Sell partner with 
an agile workforce of 250+ SAP professionals. VAI brings a proprietary product suite—including VAI 360 CRM, 
VAI Check Box, and VAI IntelHubX—extending tailored, high-margin solutions across the Media, 
Telecommunications, Utilities, Retail, and Transport verticals. 
Expansion of Non-SAP Technology and Engineering Services 
Beyond the core SAP ecosystem, the acquisition significantly diversifies the company’s engineering 
capabilities. It expands Adroit's portfolio into advanced networking, Internet of Things (IoT) architectures, 
customized software product engineering, and high-performance telecom infrastructure design across four 
continents.
