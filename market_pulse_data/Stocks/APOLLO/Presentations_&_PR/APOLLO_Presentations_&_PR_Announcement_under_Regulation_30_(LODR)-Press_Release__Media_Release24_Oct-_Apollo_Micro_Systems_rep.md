### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Apollo Micro Systems Limited reported record-breaking consolidated results for Q2 and H1 FY27, driven by robust revenue growth (Q2: +54.5% QoQ to ₹388.1 Cr) and a doubling of EBITDA (+106.9% QoQ to ₹111.2 Cr), along with an all-time high order book of ₹1765 Cr. While showcasing strong operational execution and future potential, a significantly higher effective tax rate of approximately 50% in Q2FY27 materially dampened net profit, causing the PAT margin to decline compared to the prior year despite strong pre-tax profit growth and posing a potential red flag regarding future profitability if sustained.

POWERING INDIA’S DEFENCE
Apollo Micro Systems Limited is a Hyderabad-based publicly-listed defence and aerospace technology company. 
Founded in 1985, AMS designs, develops, and manufactures advanced defence electronics, embedded systems, 
electronic warfare suites, electro-optic systems, and complete weapon system solutions for the Indian Armed Forces 
and global customers. The Company is an established Tier-1 supplier to DRDO, HAL, BEL and the Ministry of Defence, 
and is now licensed to manufacture missile-class weapon systems under the Arms Act, 1959.
