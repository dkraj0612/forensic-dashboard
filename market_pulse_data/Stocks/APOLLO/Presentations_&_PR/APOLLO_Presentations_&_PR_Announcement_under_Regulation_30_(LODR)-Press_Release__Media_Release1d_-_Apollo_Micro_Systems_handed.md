Hyderabad, India — 31st July 2026: Apollo Micro Systems Limited (“AMS” / “the Company”), one 
of India’s leading indigenous defence electronics and weapon manufacturing company, today 
announced the formal handing over of its indigenously designed and developed Safety & Detonation 
Device (SDD) to the Indian Navy during a ceremony held in the distinguished presence of Rear Admiral 
Rupak Barua, VSM, Director General of Naval Inspection (DGNAI), Indian Navy, alongside senior 
officers of the Indian Navy and the leadership team of Apollo Micro Systems Limited on 30th July 2026. 
The handing over of the SDD marks the successful culmination of a rigorous design, development, 
qualification and validation journey and stands as a proud testimony to what Indian industry can 
deliver when entrusted with the most safety-critical elements of a weapon system.

A SAFETY-CRITICAL SYSTEM AT THE HEART OF NAVAL FIREPOWER 
The Safety Arming Device is among the most critical and unforgiving sub-systems of any modern 
munition. It is the final guardian between a weapon and its target ensuring that ordnance remains 
absolutely safe through storage, handling, transportation and launch, and arms only when every 
prescribed condition of a valid engagement sequence is unambiguously satisfied. In the demanding 
maritime environment characterised by shock, vibration, humidity, salt-fog and severe electromagnetic 
conditions the reliability of the Safety Arming Device directly determines both the safety of the platform 
and its crew, while ensuring the assured performance of the weapon at the moment of truth. 
For decades, systems of this criticality were accessible to India largely through import channels, with 
attendant constraints of cost, availability, obsolescence and denial regimes. Indigenous mastery of 
Safety Arming technology is therefore not merely an engineering achievement it is a strategic capability 
that secures the operational readiness and sustenance of the Indian Navy's weapon inventory on 
sovereign terms. 
PROVEN THROUGH RIGOUR: HOW APOLLO ESTABLISHED THE CAPABILITY 
 
 
 
 
 
Working in close partnership with the Indian Navy's professional teams, AMS took the SDD from 
concept to a fully proven, deliverable system. The device has been engineered to stringent international 
fuze-safety design philosophies and has been subjected to an exhaustive validation, including: 
• Design conforming to globally accepted fuze safety and arming principles, with independent, redundant 
safety interlocks and out-of-line safety architecture; 
• Comprehensive environmental qualification mechanical shock, vibration, temperature extremes, 
humidity and marine environmental stress screening; 
• Electromagnetic compatibility and interference testing appropriate to shipborne and underwater 
weapon environments; 
• Extensive functional, endurance and safety-margin testing, followed by joint evaluation and acceptance 
trials with Indian Navy. 
Through this journey, AMS worked shoulder-to-shoulder with the Indian Navy to convert a clearly 
articulated operational problem statement into a certified, production-ready indigenous solution 
exemplifying the collaborative industry–Navy model championed under the Swavalamban framework.

AATMANIRBHAR BHARAT IN ACTION — AND A PLATFORM FOR GROWTH 
This handing over is a direct expression of the Government of India's Aatmanirbhar Bharat vision in 
one of the most technologically guarded domains of defence. Every successfully indigenised safety-
critical sub-system deepens the nation's strategic autonomy, compresses supply chains, and builds a 
sovereign industrial base for the future. 
For Apollo Micro Systems, the SDD establishes a proven, qualified capability in safety and arming 
technologies a capability that is directly extensible across a wide spectrum of naval and tri-services 
munitions, underwater weapons, missiles and guided ordnance. The Company expects this 
indigenisation success to translate into a healthy pipeline of production orders and follow-on 
programmes, as the Indian Navy and other users progressively induct indigenous safety and arming 
solutions across their weapon inventories. AMS is fully geared, in terms of design strength, 
manufacturing infrastructure and quality systems, to support series production and lifecycle sustenance 
of the SDD and its derivatives. 
 
“The Safety & Detonation Device is where engineering meets absolute responsibility there is no 
margin for error. Handing over this fully indigenous, fully proven system to the Indian Navy is a 
moment of deep pride for every member of the Apollo family. We thank the Indian Navy for its 
trust and partnership, and we see this as the beginning of a much larger journey in indigenous 
weapon safety and arming technologies for the nation.” 
— Mr. Baddam Karunakar Reddy, Managing Director, Apollo Micro Systems Limited 
 
About Apollo Micro Systems Limited 
 
Apollo Micro Systems Limited (NSE: APOLLO | BSE: 540879) is a pioneer in defence technology,
