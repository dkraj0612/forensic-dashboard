There are milestones that add to a company's history, and there are those that shape its future. 
For Apollo, the Make-II Prototype Sanction Order from the Indian Navy for the development of 
the SAVIOR-ASW autonomous anti-submarine warfare platform is one such milestone. 
We see this programme as an opportunity to do what Apollo has always believed in, solve 
complex engineering challenges with indigenous innovation and contribute meaningfully to 
India's strategic capabilities.
One of the year's defining achievements was our empanelment by the Indian Air Force as 
a Prime Development Agency for the IPREK programme. It is a testament to the depth of our 
engineering capabilities and years of sustained investment in indigenous technologies. As 
precision-guided systems become increasingly central to modern defence, this milestone 
strengthens Apollo's position in a strategically important and technologically demanding 
domain.
The successful handover of our indigenously developed Safety & Detonation Device (SDD) to 
the Indian Navy marks another important step in Apollo’s evolution as a defence technology 
company. This achievement demonstrates our ability to develop and deliver highly specialised, 
safety-critical systems that meet the stringent requirements of the Armed Forces. More 
importantly, it strengthens a technology capability that can be leveraged across multiple 
defence applications, creating a foundation for future programmes and reinforcing our position 
as a trusted partner in India’s defence ecosystem.
