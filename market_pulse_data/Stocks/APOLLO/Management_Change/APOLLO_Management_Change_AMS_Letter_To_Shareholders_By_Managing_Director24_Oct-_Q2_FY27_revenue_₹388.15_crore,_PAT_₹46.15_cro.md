Beyond Four Decades of Innovation, Building the Next

Dear Shareholders,
Every meaningful achievement is built on years of
effort, conviction and the courage to keep moving
forward. As we reflect on Apollo Micro Systems
Limited’s performance in Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27, I
am filled with a deep sense of gratitude for the
people, partners and shareholders who have been
part of this journey.
Our performance in Q2 and H1 FY27 reflects the
collective efforts we have made to strengthen
Apollo’s business, expand our capabilities and
advance our long-term strategic priorities. During
Q2 FY27, we recorded revenue of ₹388.15 crore,
EBITDA of ₹111.18 crore and profit after tax (PAT) of
₹46.15 crore, representing year-on-year growth of
72.3%, 87.8% and 53.7%, respectively. For H1 FY27,
revenue stood at ₹639.44 crore, EBITDA at ₹164.91
crore and PAT at ₹71.37 crore, registering year-on-
year
growth
of
78.2%,
64.7%
and
49.6%,
respectively.

“Coming together is a beginning; keeping together is 
progress; working together is success.”
H E N R Y F O R D
This thought captures the spirit of Apollo’s journey. The progress we have 
made over more than four decades has been possible because we have 
continued to work towards a common purpose: developing indigenous 
capabilities that strengthen India’s defence preparedness.

S E L F - R E L I A N C E
Resilience Is the New Firepower
Today, that purpose has become more 
relevant than ever.
The recent approval by the Hon’ble Raksha Mantri for the
transfer of technology of DRDO-developed missile systems
to the Indian defence industry marks an important moment
in India’s journey towards self-reliance. It reflects the
Government’s confidence in domestic industry and its
recognition that a strong defence ecosystem must be
supported
by
indigenous
technologies,
reliable
manufacturing capabilities and resilient supply chains.
For Apollo, this development holds particular significance.
For more than forty years, we have been building
capabilities
across
mission-critical
avionics,
weapon
electronics, fuzes, actuators, rugged computing, precision
machining and system integration. Our journey has been
shaped by the exacting requirements of DRDO, the Armed
Forces and Defence PSUs, and by our commitment to
delivering dependable solutions for some of India’s most
demanding defence programmes.
D R . A . P . J . A B D U L K A L A M
“Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.” These words capture the
spirit of India’s journey towards self-reliance and the philosophy that has guided Apollo Micro Systems Limited for
over four decades. Building indigenous defence capabilities has never been an overnight endeavour; it has required a
willingness to dream beyond existing limitations, the perseverance to develop complex technologies and the
conviction to keep investing in India’s ability to design and manufacture for itself.
