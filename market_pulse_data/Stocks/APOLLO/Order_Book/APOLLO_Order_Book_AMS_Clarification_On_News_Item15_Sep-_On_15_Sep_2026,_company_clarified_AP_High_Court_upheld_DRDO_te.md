Sub: Clarification on the news article “HC Orders Review of ₹1, Other Extremely Low Defence Bids” 
– The Economic Times, 15th September 2026 – Judgment dated 09.09.2026 of the Hon’ble High 
Court of Andhra Pradesh in W.P. No. 2450 of 2026 
Ref: Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
With reference to the captioned news article published in The Economic Times on the judgment of the 
Hon'ble High Court of Andhra Pradesh in the writ petition filed against the Union of India, DRDO and NSTL 
by an unsuccessful bidder, M/s Jaykay Enterprises Limited, challenging the award of the contract to the L-1 
bidder, Apollo Micro Systems Limited ("AMS" / "the Company"), the Company wishes to clarify as under.  
At the outset, it is clarified that the reference to a "₹1 bid" appearing in the headline of the article is not part 
of the judgment of the Hon'ble High Court and does not pertain to the Company or to the subject tender in 
any manner. 
The judgment is substantially in favour of the Company and the procuring authorities. The Hon'ble Court 
rejected the petitioner's prayers for cancellation of the tender and disqualification of bidders, and no lapse, 
fault, mala fide or arbitrariness could be established by any party against the Company or against 
DRDO/NSTL. 
The tender award and the financial approval in favour of the Company continue to hold, subject only to 
completion of  pre-award  verification by the competent authority under Clause 6.42(b)(ii) of the DRDO 
Procurement Manual, 2025. The Hon'ble Court having upheld the tender conditions, the bid-opening and the 
evaluation in all other respects, and having recorded the Company's legitimate commercial basis for its 
competitive bid and the Company's position stands correct and unimpeached on merits. 
Apollo Micro Systems has been in the service of the nation and of DRDO programmes for over four decades. 
The Company's sustained investments in R&D, indigenous development and vertical integration enable it to 
deliver cost-effective, high-quality solutions to its customers, a capability that itself constitutes a formidable 
entry barrier for new entrants without proven expertise in such critical technologies. The Company's 
competitive pricing reflects this strength. 
The Company has already developed Underwater Mine technologies. The specific PBMM-NG technology 
was developed by the Company two years ago on a No Commitment – No Cost (NC-NC) basis and was 
offered to DRDO for validation and acceptance. However, as the procurement framework does not permit 
direct acceptance of a technology under the DcPP route, a tender was floated providing equal opportunity to 
multiple vendors, in which the Company emerged as L-1.
