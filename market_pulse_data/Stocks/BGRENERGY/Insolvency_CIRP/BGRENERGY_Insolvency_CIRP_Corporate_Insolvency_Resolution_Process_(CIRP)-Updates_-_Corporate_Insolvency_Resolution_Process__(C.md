Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Company had filed certain Interlocutory 
Applications before the Hon’ble National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), Chennai 
Bench, in Company Appeal (AT) (CH) (Ins.) No. 252 of 2026, seeking, inter alia, completion of the 
withdrawal process of the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under Section 12A of 
the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, restoration of the Company's access to and control 
over statutory filings and records maintained on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal, and 
consequential directions to the Registrar of Companies (ROC) and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy 
Board of India (IBBI). 
 
The Hon’ble NCLAT had passed an order directing the ROC and IBBI to give effect to its earlier 
orders, including updating the status of the Company from "Under CIRP" to "Active" in the records 
maintained by the ROC/MCA portal and undertaking all consequential actions required for 
implementation thereof. 
 
The matter was adjourned by two weeks. The interim stay granted by the Hon’ble NCLAT continues 
to remain in force. 
 
The Company has not yet received a copy of the order passed on the aforesaid date. The same 
will be submitted to the Stock Exchanges upon receipt.
