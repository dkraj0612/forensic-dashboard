Commenting on the performance, Mr. Vimal Kumar, Managing Director, Best Agrolife Limited,
said, "Q2FY27 was a challenging quarter for the domestic crop protection industry. lrregular
monsoon rainfall delayed and uneven sowing, moisture stress and limited irrigation availability
across several agricultural regions affected crop establishment and the timing of crop protection
applications. These factors, coupled with cautious farmer spending and heightened competitive
intensity, created significant headwinds for the business.
Despite these challenges, we delivered a strong improvement in profitability, with EBITDA
growing 30% in Q2FY27 and 45o/o in H1FY27. An important contributor to our margin expansion
has been our strategic shift over the past two years towards a stronger patented product portfolio,
complemented by our continued focus on cost discipline and operational efficiency. We intend to
build on this progress by further strengthening our patented portfolio, with innovation and
intellectual property at the core of our long-term growth strategy.
We had made a higher provision for sales returns in anticipation of the impact of the irregular
monsoon. However, we are pleased to see that the sales return percentage has also declined
this year, with sales returns in H1 Fy27 lower than in H1 Fy26
During the period, we secured a patent grant for the combination of Fluxametamide, Abamectin
and Trifloxystrobin and advanced our international business through a 47-metric-tonne shipment
to Sudan, registration of our patented product Ronfen in Nepal, and registration of Lambda
technical in Mexico. With four additional products submitted for registration in Nepal, we continue
to pursue opportunities to expand our international presence and broaden our grovyth platform.
As we enter the second half of FY27, the domestic outlook will depend on the progress of Rabi
