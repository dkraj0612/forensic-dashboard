“Q2FY27 was a challenging quarter for the domestic crop protection industry. Irregular monsoon
rainfall delayed and uneven sowing, moisture stress and limited irrigation availability across several
agricultural regions affected crop establishment and the timing of crop protection applications.
These factors, coupled with cautious farmer spending and heightened competitive intensity, created
significant headwinds for the business.
Despite these challenges, we delivered a strong improvement in profitability, with EBITDA growing
30% in Q2FY27 and 45% in H1FY27. An important contributor to our margin expansion has been
our strategic shift over the past two years towards a stronger patented product portfolio,
complemented by our continued focus on cost discipline and operational efficiency. We intend to
build on this progress by further strengthening our patented portfolio, with innovation and
intellectual property at the core of our long-term growth strategy.
During the period, we secured a patent grant for the combination of Fluxametamide, Abamectin and
Trifloxystrobin and advanced our international business through a 47-metric-tonne shipment to
Sudan, registration of our patented product RonfenTM in Nepal, and registration of Lambda technical
in Mexico. With four additional products submitted for registration in Nepal, we continue to pursue
opportunities to expand our international presence and broaden our growth platform.
As we enter the second half of FY27, the domestic outlook will depend on the progress of Rabi
sowing, soil moisture conditions, irrigation availability and farmers’ investment in crop protection.
Our priorities remain strengthening domestic market execution, expanding our patented portfolio
and pursuing international growth opportunities. While market conditions remain uncertain, we will
continue to focus on innovation, operational discipline and sustainable, profitable growth over the
long term.”
Mr. Vimal Kumar
Managing Director
