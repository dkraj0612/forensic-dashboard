PRESS RELEASE 
 
Castrol India launches first skilling centre under its flagship CSR 
programme ‘Ujjwal Kal’ in Kolkata 
 
 
New centre to equip the city’s youth with future-ready skills and create pathways to 
sustainable livelihoods 
 
Kolkata, 1 October 2026: Castrol India Limited, in association with Smile Foundation, today 
unveiled its first Ujjwal Kal skilling centre in Kolkata, aimed at equipping young adults from 
economically disadvantaged communities build industry-relevant skills and connecting them to 
opportunities for employment and sustainable livelihoods. 
 
Located at the Smile Foundation STeP Centre in Santoshpur, Kolkata, the centre will offer training 
in digital technologies, Artificial Intelligence (AI), retail, and beauty & wellness, alongside 
communication, communication readiness and digital literacy. The learners will also receive 
recognized certificates and placement assistance. 
 
‘Ujjwal Kal’, Castrol India’s flagship CSR initiative, focuses on strengthening livelihoods through 
youth upskilling, women empowerment and community development. Launched in 2021, the 
programme has a particular focus on improving access to opportunities for young women and 
underserved communities.  
 
Speaking about the initiative, Mr. Saugata Basuray, Managing Director, Castrol India Limited, 
said, “Castrol India has always believed that talent is universal, but access to opportunity is not. 
Through Ujjwal Kal, we are working to bridge that gap by giving youth the skills, confidence, and 
support to build better futures for themselves and their families.The launch of our first skilling 
center in Kolkata is an important step in that journey, particularly in creating greater opportunities 
for young women. As the world of work continues to evolve, enabling young adults to participate 
meaningfully in it can create an impact that goes well beyond the individual.”  
 
In 2025, Ujjwal Kal’s initiatives across in education, upskilling, and healthcare benefited close to 
5,000 individuals.The programme primarily targets individuals aged 18-28, with young women 
currently accounting for nearly 85% of participants. It aims to achieve a 50–55% of graduates to 
secure direct employment or pursue self-employment opportunities. 
 
About Castrol India Limited
