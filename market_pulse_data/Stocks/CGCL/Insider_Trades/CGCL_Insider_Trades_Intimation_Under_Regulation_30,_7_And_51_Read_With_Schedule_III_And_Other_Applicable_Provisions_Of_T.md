BIPARTITE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE ISSUER AND INDIA INTERNATIONAL 
DEPOSITORY IFSC LIMITED 
This Bipartite Agreement made and entered into on the date mentioned herein below between the Issuer, 
which is a company incorporated and registered under the provisions of the Companies 
Act, 1956 
(hereinafter called the "Issuer" which expression shall unless it be repugnant to the context or meaning 
thereof mean and include its successors in title), Depository INDIA INTERNATIONAL DEPOSITORY 
IFSC LIMITED who will also act as Registrar and Transfer Agent having its registered office at Unit 
2NW & 2SW, 2nd Floor, Building 12B, IFSCA Headquarters, Block 12, Road IA, Zone 1 (SEZPA), GIFT 
SEZ PA, GIFT City, Gandhinagar - 3 82050 (hereinafter called the "IIDI") 
Witnesseth as Follows 
WHEREAS The Issuer has furnished, to the IIDI an application for its securities being admitted as an 
"eligible security" in the depository system and also working as the R&T Agent of the Issuer. 
NOW THEREFORE in consideration of the IIDI having agreed to admit the securities of the Issuer as 
being eligible for being admitted on the depository system, the parties to this Bipartite Agreement hereby 
covenant and agree as follows: 
1. 
The Issuer agree and undertake to comply with the provisions of the Bye Laws and the Business 
Rules of IIDI, wherever applicable. 
2. The Issuer and IIDI hereby agree that the Bye Laws and Business Rules of IIDI shall be part of the 
terms and conditions of every agreement, contract or transaction between the parties to the 
agreement. 
3. The Issuer shall be bound by any amendment to the Bye Laws and Business Rules of IIDI with 
respect to any transaction occurring subsequent to the time of such amendment. Such amendment 
shall take effect as if it were originally a part of the Bye Laws and Business Rules of IIDI. 
4. The agreement and all contracts and transactions effected by the Issuer under the Bye Laws and 
Business Rules of ITDI or through the facilities of ITDI shall be governed by and be construed in 
accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws as well as the rules and regulations of the 
Regulatory Bodies having jurisdiction in respect of the same from time to time. 
5. The Issuer shall furnish a list of authorized officials who shall represent and interact on behalf of 
the Issuer with IIDI within fifteen days of the execution of this agreement and any changes 
including additions/deletions, thereof shall be communicated to IIDI within fifteen days of such 
change. 
6. The Issuer shall send to IIDI copies of the letters approving listing and commencement of trading 
issued by the relevant stock exchanges in respect of securities held in dematerialised form with 
IIDI. 
7. IIDI shall allocate unique identity codes to the Issuer. 
8. The Issuer shall establish continuous electronic means of communication with TIDI and JIDI shall 
provide necessary manuals & procedural guidelines to the Issuer as is necessary for effective and 
prompt conduct of the business of the Depository. The Issuer shall maintain such systems, 
procedures, means of communication, adequate infrastructure, hardware, software security devices 
and back- up facilities as may be prescribed by TIDI from time to time. 
9. The Issuer shall strictly follow the backup procedure recommended by IIDI.

is listed. 
15. The Issuer undertakes that the dematerialisation and rematerialisation requests are processed within 
fifteen and thirty days respectively. However, it is agreed that in case of bulk dematerialisation 
requests, this period may be extended to thirty days.

prohibitory order, stop transfer, attachment order, or disputed title, on the day of such request. It is 
agreed that where a court order has been received by the Issuer or where there are court orders 
against any transfer request, if such a request is entertained, the Issuer shall be entirely responsible.

such date or fifteen days from the date of receipt of such request by the IIDI whichever is later.

Client, furnished by TIDT or its Participant, TTDI shall indemnify such losses. 
19. The Issuer shall indemnify IIDT in respect of any loss or liability incurred, or any claim arising in 
respect of any incorrect information furnished by the Issuer in respect of the operations of the 
Depository.
