Press Release 
Capri Gold Loans launches ‘Vyapar Mahotsav’ across India, offers 
Gold Loans at interest rates starting 11% p.a. 
Four day initiative from September 16 - 19, also offers zero processing fees and maximum value on 
eligible gold loans 
Mumbai, September 16, 2026: Capri Global Capital Limited, also known by the brand name ‘Capri 
Loans’, one of the leading non-banking financial companies, today announced the launch of ‘Vyapar 
Mahotsav’, a four day, pan-India gold loan promotional offer aimed at providing customers with 
convenient and cost-effective access to funds against their gold assets, with added benefits. 
Available from September 16 to 19, 2026, the limited period initiative will offer gold loans at interest 
rates starting from 11% p.a. It also offers zero processing fees and maximum eligible loan value 
against gold, subject to applicable terms. Customers can avail these benefits by visiting their nearest 
Capri Gold Loan branches.  
Commenting on the initiative, Mr. Ravish Gupta, Chief Business Officer – Gold Loans, Capri Global 
Capital Limited, said, "At Capri Loans, our focus is on making credit more accessible, transparent and 
customer centric. Gold loans have emerged as one of the most efficient financing solutions for 
customers, especially those seeking quick liquidity for business purposes. Through Vyapar Mahotsav, 
we are bringing together attractive interest rates, zero processing fees and higher value realization to 
help these customers access funds with greater ease and affordability. We believe this initiative will 
support traders, entrepreneurs and small businesses across India as they prepare for the upcoming 
festive and business season." 
Through 'Vyapar Mahotsav', Capri Loans aims to help customers unlock the financial value of their 
gold without having to sell it. The offer is intended to address a range of funding requirements, 
including business working capital, inventory purchases, seasonal expenditure and other personal or 
commercial financial needs.  
Capri Gold Loan offers its Gold Loan services to customers through 1100+ branches across 16 States 
and UTs. It has served over 14.5 Lakh customers till date. 
About Capri Global Capital Limited: 
Capri Global Capital Limited (“Capri Loans”) is a well-diversified retail focused Non-Banking Financial 
Company listed on the BSE and the NSE. Capri Loans has an AUM of over Rs. 40,000 crores and serves 
its customers through 1,400+ branches with an employee base of 13,700+ as of June 30th, 2026 across 
India. Capri Loans offers a wide range of secured and collateralized loans across four primary lending 
segments – Gold Loans, MSME Loans, Construction Finance and Housing Loans offered through its 
100% subsidiary, Capri Global Housing Finance Limited (“CGHFL”). Capri Loans also has fee-based 
businesses such as insurance distribution through corporate agency 
license and distribution of car loans through its subsidiary Capri Loans Car Platform Private Limited 
(“CLCPPL”).
