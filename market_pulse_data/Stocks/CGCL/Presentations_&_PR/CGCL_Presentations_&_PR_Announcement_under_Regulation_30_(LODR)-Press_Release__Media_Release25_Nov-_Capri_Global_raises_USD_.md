Press Release 
 
Capri Global Raises USD 30 Million from the BlueOrchard 
Microfinance Fund in Its First-Ever Impact Fundraise 
 
The fundraise will support Capri Global's MSME lending business and expand access to credit for 
businesses across India 
 
Mumbai, 25 November 2026: Capri Global Capital Limited also known by the brand name ‘Capri 
Loans’, one of the leading non-banking financial companies, has raised USD 30 million from the 
BlueOrchard Microfinance Fund, managed by BlueOrchard Finance Ltd, an impact investment firm. 
This marks Capri Global's first-ever impact fundraise and represents a significant milestone in the 
company's efforts to strengthen its MSME lending business. 
 
The funds will be deployed towards Capri Global's MSME financing activities, supporting businesses 
that rely on access to timely and appropriate credit to sustain operations and pursue growth 
opportunities. The fundraise reflects the role of institutional capital in supporting lending to the MSME 
segment, which is an important contributor to India's economic activity and entrepreneurship. 
 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Rajesh Sharma, Managing Director, Capri Global Capital 
Limited, said, “India’s MSME sector has significant potential to drive economic growth, 
entrepreneurship and employment. Realising this potential will require a stronger credit ecosystem 
that can meet the diverse financing needs of businesses at every stage of their growth. Institutional 
capital and long-term partnerships will be key to this effort. Our partnership with BlueOrchard 
strengthens our ability to deepen our reach in the MSME segment and support a more inclusive credit 
landscape.” 
 
Mr. Normunds Mizis, Chief Credit Officer, BlueOrchard Finance Ltd, said, “For impact investing to 
deliver meaningful outcomes, capital must be channelled through institutions with the ability to reach 
businesses that are critical to local economies. We see an important role for specialised lending 
institutions in translating investment into financing opportunities for MSMEs. Our investment in Capri 
Global reflects our approach of partnering with financial institutions to support the flow of capital to 
this segment, while contributing to broader economic resilience and sustainable growth.” 
 
The partnership underscores the importance of impact investment in strengthening credit availability 
for India's MSME sector.  
 
Capri Global has been creating positive impact in the lives of thousands of borrowers since 2013,  
through MSME lending while serving India’s underserved segments.

About Capri Global Capital Limited: 
Capri Global Capital Limited (“Capri Loans”) is a well-diversified retail-focused Non-Banking Financial 
Company listed on the BSE and NSE. As of September 30, 2026, Capri Loans had consolidated AUM of 
approximately Rs 43,878 crores and operated through a network of 1,609 branches with over 15,400 
employees across India. The Company offers secured and collateralised loans across four principal 
lending segments – Gold Loans, MSME Loans, Construction Finance and Affordable Housing Loans, the 
latter through its wholly owned subsidiary, Capri Global Housing Finance Limited (“CGHFL”). Its fee-
based businesses include insurance distribution through a corporate agency license and car loan 
distribution through Capri Loans Car Platform Private Limited (“CLCPPL”). 
 
About BlueOrchard: 
BlueOrchard is a leading global impact investment manager with more than two decades of 
experience partnering with financial institutions in emerging and frontier markets. Established in 2001 
as part of a United Nations initiative, the firm has been a pioneer in impact investing and is the 
manager of the world’s largest and oldest commercial microfinance fund.  
  
With deep local expertise and experience across more than 100 countries, BlueOrchard provides 
flexible, long-term capital solutions tailored to financial institutions serving micro, small and mid-sized 
enterprises, as well as direct lending to high-impact companies. Beyond financing, the firm works 
closely with portfolio companies to strengthen efficiency and effectiveness, helping to deepen and 
scale positive impact in their communities.  
  
As part of Schroders, a global asset manager with a heritage spanning more than 200 years, 
BlueOrchard combines specialist impact investing capabilities with institutional-grade infrastructure 
and governance.

Annexure – Key terms and background of the transaction 
 
The Fund raise of USD 30 million from BlueOrchard is through a private placement of senior secured 
USD-denominated bonds. The bonds have a tenor of 36 months and mature in November 2029. The 
issuance was completed through a private placement, with BlueOrchard as the sole investor, and the 
bonds will be listed on NSE IFSC Limited.  
 
The fundraise further diversifies Capri Global’s liability profile and demonstrates its ability to access a 
broad range of domestic and international funding sources. Alongside development and impact-
focused investors, the Company’s funding sources include domestic banks and financial institutions, 
domestic bond issuances, co-lending arrangements, direct assignment transactions and international 
capital market programmes. 
 
The fundraise was structured through bilateral discussions rather than a traditional book-building 
process. It was executed as a standalone private placement bond issuance outside Capri Global’s 
existing Global Medium-Term Note (GMTN) programme 
 
The Company reaffirms that it has no plans to access the conventional GMTN dollar bond market or 
any broadly marketed US dollar bond issuance in this financial year.
