ARAPL Secures ₹19 Crore Order for Its Next-
Generation AGV-Based Automated Car Parking 
Technology 
Largest-ever single order in the company's AGV car parking business marks a major 
milestone in the commercialisation of its robotic parking solutions under the Relipark 
brand. 
Pune, November 09, 2026: Affordable Robotic and Automation Limited (ARAPL) (BSE: 
541402 | NSE: AFFORDABLE), India's first listed robotics company, has secured an order 
worth approximately ₹19 crore for its next-generation Automated Guided Vehicle (AGV)-
based car parking technology. This is the largest single order in the company's AGV car 
parking business since it commercially launched its product in September 2026 and 
marks a significant milestone in the commercialisation of its next-generation robotic 
parking solutions. 
The company markets its advanced automated parking solutions under Relipark, a 
dedicated brand created to bring its next-generation parking technologies under a 
distinct identity. Relipark builds on ARAPL's engineering, manufacturing and execution 
capabilities developed through its experience in robotic welding lines, warehouse 
robotics and automated parking systems for manufacturers and real estate developers. 
The newly secured order represents an important commercial step for ARAPL's parking 
technology, which uses battery-operated autonomous mobile robots to transport and 
position vehicles within a parking facility. The technology is designed to optimise parking-
space utilisation, reduce dependence on conventional mechanical infrastructure and 
provide greater operational flexibility. 
A new generation of robotic parking technology 
ARAPL's new parking architecture represents a shift from conventional mechanical multi-
level parking systems, which typically rely on interconnected chains, bearings, 
conveyors, couplers, pallets and multiple motors. 
Mr. Rahul Padole, CIO and Director, ARAPL Group, said: 
“We have been in the automated parking business since 2012 and have experienced first-
hand the limitations of conventional multi-level parking systems. Traditional designs 
involve numerous mechanical components, each of which can become a potential point

of failure. A typical car pallet may require two to three motors, adding to system 
complexity and maintenance requirements. 
In 2019, we began developing a new generation of parking robots with the objective of 
moving away from conventional mechanical architectures and embracing autonomous 
mobility technology. 
Our mobile-robot-based approach uses a fleet of intelligent robots to perform parking 
and retrieval operations. If one robot becomes unavailable, another can take over its task, 
helping maintain operational continuity. We have focused on fleet-level redundancy, 
reliability and lower lifecycle maintenance. We believe this technology has the potential 
to make automated parking more flexible and dependable.” 
Product portfolio and technology roadmap 
Mr. Melind Padole, Founder and Director, ARAPL Group, said: 
“We named our first product 2M because our objective is to retrieve a parked vehicle in 
as little as two minutes, subject to project configuration and operating conditions. Our 
next model, VaLe, is designed around a valet-parking concept, offering a convenient 
parking experience for users. 
We have already launched the 2M mobile robot model. The 2M Slim is scheduled for 
launch by the end of 2026. This will be followed by VaLe, our third and most advanced 
model, which we expect to launch by October 2027. We are also developing two 
additional models, planned for 2028, for outdoor parking applications.” 
Relipark's expanding product portfolio is intended to address different parking 
requirements, from compact urban developments and premium residential projects to 
large commercial facilities and outdoor parking infrastructure. Its modular, battery-
operated and autonomous architecture is designed to provide flexibility in system 
configuration, capacity expansion and operations. 
Flexible business models and lower lifecycle costs 
Relipark aims to offer developers and infrastructure owners flexible approaches to 
implementing automated parking infrastructure. Under proposed commercial models, 
eligible developers may be able to deploy parking infrastructure with zero upfront 
investment or on a cost-neutral basis, depending on project feasibility and the agreed 
commercial structure. Company has aimed to offer 20 such projects where they will offer 
cost neutral parking. With limited offering there is huge demand for this. 
The systems are modular and compact, enabling efficient use of available space. Based 
on the company's estimates, the simplified robotic architecture is expected to reduce 
post-installation maintenance costs by approximately 40% compared with conventional

Relipark will showcase its next-generation automated parking solutions at two industry

Airport Parking Network London: 18–20 November 2026

UK airport parking opportunity 
At Airport Parking Network London, Relipark will present its technology to international 
airport operators, parking management companies and infrastructure stakeholders. The 
company sees opportunities to modernise existing airport parking facilities in the UK and 
other international markets. 
Its brownfield solutions are developed for existing parking facilities where extensive 
structural modifications are difficult or commercially impractical. According to the 
company's preliminary engineering assessments, the technology may increase parking 
capacity by approximately 40–50% at suitable sites, depending on the existing layout, 
clearances, circulation arrangements, structural constraints and project-specific 
feasibility. 
ARAPL has submitted proposals to several airport stakeholders and is exploring potential 
joint ventures with parking management companies in the UK to support market entry 
and project execution. Any project will remain subject to technical evaluation, 
commercial negotiations and the required approvals. 
Indian real estate and infrastructure opportunity 
At ACETECH 2026 in Mumbai, Relipark will showcase solutions primarily targeting real 
estate developers, architects, infrastructure companies and institutional stakeholders. 
These offerings will focus on greenfield developments, where parking infrastructure can 
be planned around robotic mobility from the initial design stage. 
The exhibition will give developers, architects and other industry participants an 
opportunity to experience the technology and explore its potential integration into 
residential, commercial and mixed-use developments.

About ARAPL 
Affordable Robotic & Automation Limited (ARAPL), established in 2005 and 
headquartered in Pune, India, provides turnkey industrial automation and robotics 
solutions. The company serves automotive, non-automotive, general industrial and 
government-sector customers, with operations and customer relationships across India, 
China and other Asian markets. 
ARAPL's solutions include robotic welding lines, robotic inspection stations, automated 
assembly systems, warehouse robotics and automated car parking systems. The 
company operates approximately 350,000 square feet of facilities and employs more 
than 400 people. Its sales and service network includes offices in Faridabad and Mumbai. 
About Humro 
Humro, an ARAPL RaaS brand, is an Indian deep-tech robotics company specialising in 
autonomous mobile robotic solutions for manufacturing, warehousing and industrial 
logistics. Its AMRs and AGVs use iWare navigation technology and FleetOps fleet-
management software to support automation in industrial and logistics environments. 
Through commercial models including Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS), pay-per-use, 
leasing and perpetual licensing, Humro enables enterprises to adopt automation with 
lower upfront capital commitments. For more information, visit www.humro.com.
