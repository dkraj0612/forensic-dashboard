Pursuant to the Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
and subject to the provisions of the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) 
Regulations, 2021, we wish to inform you that the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (‘NRC’ 
or ‘Compensation Committee’) at its Meeting held today, i.e., Friday, September 18, 2026, has, inter 
alia transacted the following businesses: 
 
1. Allotment of Equity Shares pursuant to Employee Stock Option Scheme 2025 (“HFL ESOS 
2025” or “Scheme”) 
 
 The Compensation Committee has approved and allotted  1,46,300 Equity Shares to certain 
eligible employees of the Company and its Subsidiary Companies, pursuant to exercise of the 
Options vested under HFL ESOS 2025.   
 
Consequently, the Issued, Subscribed, and Paid-Up Equity Share Capital of the Company stands 
increased from INR 24,23,25,404/- (Indian Rupees Twenty Four Crores Twenty Three Lakhs 
Twenty Five Thousand Four Hundred and Four Only), consisting of 12,11,62,702 (Twelve Crores 
Eleven Lakhs Sixty Two Thousand Seven Hundred and Two) Equity Shares of INR 2/- each, to INR 
24,26,18,004/- (Indian Rupees Twenty Four Crores Twenty Six Lakhs Eighteen Thousand and 
Four Only), consisting of 12,13,09,002 (Twelve Crores Thirteen Lakhs Nine Thousand and Two) 
Equity Shares of INR 2/- each. 
 
 
2. Grant of fresh 1,05,900 Employee Stock Options under the existing Employee Stock 
Option Scheme 2025 (“HFL ESOS 2025” or “Scheme”) 
 
The Compensation Committee approved the grant of fresh 1,05,900 Stock Options to the eligible 
employees of the Company and its Subsidiary Companies, in terms of the HFL ESOS 2025 and under 
the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
