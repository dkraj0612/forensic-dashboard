Subject: Resignation from the position of Managing Director and Director of AQYLON NEXUS LIMITED 
Dear Members of the Board, 
I, Mr. SRIVATSAVA SUNKARA, holding DIN: 01725431, presently serving as the Managing Director and 
Director of AQYLON NEXUS LIMITED (“Company”), hereby tender my resignation from the office of 
Managing Director and Director of the Company, with effect from 3rd September 2026. 
I have taken this decision after careful consideration of my personal circumstances and existing professional 
and other prior commitments. Due to increasing personal commitments and other prior obligations 
requiring my attention and time, I am presently unable to devote the requisite time and attention necessary 
for effectively discharging the responsibilities and duties attached to the position of Managing Director and 
Director of the Company. 
In these circumstances, I believe that it would be in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders 
that I step down from my position so that the Company may make appropriate arrangements for the 
effective management and leadership of its affairs. 
For the avoidance of doubt, I confirm that my resignation is purely on account of personal reasons, existing 
commitments and my inability to devote sufficient time to the affairs of the Company. My decision is 
voluntary and has not arisen on account of any disagreement with the Board of Directors, the management, 
the operations, policies or practices of the Company. 
I further confirm that, to the best of my knowledge and belief, there are no other material reasons for my 
resignation other than those stated above. 
I request the Board to kindly take note of my resignation in accordance with Section 168 of the Companies 
Act, 2013, and to complete all necessary corporate, statutory and regulatory formalities arising from my 
resignation, including the necessary filings with the Registrar of Companies and the requisite disclosures to 
the recognised stock exchange(s) under the applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. 
In particular, I understand that the resignation is required to be disclosed in accordance with Regulation 30 
read with Schedule III, Part A, Para A, Clause 7 and Clause 7C of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, including 
furnishing my resignation letter and the detailed reasons for resignation as stated herein. 
I shall extend all reasonable cooperation to the Company and its officers during the transition period and 
for completion of any pending matters, handover requirements and statutory or regulatory formalities 
relating to my tenure, subject to my other commitments.
