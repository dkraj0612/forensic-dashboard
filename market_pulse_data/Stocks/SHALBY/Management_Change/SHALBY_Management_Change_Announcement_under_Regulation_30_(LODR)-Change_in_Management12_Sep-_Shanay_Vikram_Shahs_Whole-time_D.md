Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that:- 
 
The Board of Directors at its meeting held on August 12, 2026, approved the appointment of Mr. Shanay 
Vikram Shah (DIN: 02726541) as Additional Director designated as Whole-time Director of the Company, 
subject to approval of the shareholders at the 22nd Annual General Meeting (AGM). 
 
Based on the voting results of 22nd AGM declared on September 11, 2026, it is observed that Ordinary 
Resolution no. 3 relating to appointment of Mr. Shanay Vikram Shah (DIN: 02726541) as Director of the 
Company and Ordinary Resolution no. 4 relating to his appointment as Whole-time director of the Company, 
as set out in the Notice of said AGM, did not secure requisite majority by the shareholders of the Company.  
 
In view of the above and in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, Schedule IV thereto, 
and the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, Mr. Shanay Vikram Shah automatically ceases to hold office as an 
Additional Director (Whole-time Director) of the Company with effect from September 10, 2026 (i.e., the 
date of the AGM), however, he will continue to act as President of the Company. 
 
The details required under Reg. 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of Listing Regulations are provided in 
attached Annexure. 
 
You are requested to take the same on your record.
