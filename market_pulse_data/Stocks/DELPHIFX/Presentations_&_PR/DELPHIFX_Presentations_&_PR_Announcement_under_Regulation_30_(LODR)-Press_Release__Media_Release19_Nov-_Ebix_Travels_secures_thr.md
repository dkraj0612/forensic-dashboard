DELPHI WORLD MONEY STRENGTHENS ITS INTEGRATED TRAVEL PLATFORM 
WITH THREE-YEAR MADHYA PRADESH GOVERNMENT TRAVEL EMPANELMENT 
 
Three-year empanelment of Ebix Travels by MPSTDC expands Delphi's presence in 
government and institutional travel, covering domestic and international travel, group 
mobility and MICE services 
 
New Delhi, 19 November 2026:  Delphi World Money Limited (“Delphi”), an emerging 
integrated Travel, Financial Services and Technology platform, continues to expand its Travel 
Business as Ebix Travels Private Limited (“Ebix Travels”), has been empanelled by the Madhya 
Pradesh State Tourism Development Corporation (MPSTDC), a Government of Madhya Pradesh 
undertaking, as a tour operator for domestic and international end-to-end travel services. The 
three-year engagement represents an important addition to Delphi's growing institutional travel 
portfolio and strengthens its capabilities across government travel, group mobility, international 
delegations and Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions (MICE) services. 
 
Expanding Presence in Government and Institutional Travel 
 
The empanelment follows a competitive tender process initiated by MPSTDC, the implementing 
agency for the Madhya Pradesh Government's Mukhya Mantri Tirth Darshan Yojana. Ebix 
Travels successfully qualified on both the technical and financial parameters prescribed under 
the tender and will remain valid for a period of three years. 
 
Under the engagement, Ebix Travels will manage domestic and international travel requirements 
assigned by MPSTDC, offering a comprehensive range of services including air and railway 
ticketing, hotel accommodation, airport transfers, ground transportation, visa and passport 
assistance, foreign exchange, travel insurance and airport protocol arrangements. The scope also 
includes customised travel itineraries, coordination of connecting flights and transfers, and end-
to-end assistance for cancellations, rebookings and other travel-related requirements. 
 
Comprehensive Travel Management and Group Mobility Solutions 
 
The engagement extends beyond conventional ticketing and accommodation services to 
encompass the coordination and management of travel and logistical requirements for official 
delegations and large groups travelling across India and overseas. 
 
These arrangements include coordination of interpreters, liaison officers, meeting rooms, 
banquet facilities and temporary office arrangements at various destinations. For international 
group travel, Ebix Travels will deploy dedicated tour managers to accompany delegations and 
oversee travel logistics throughout the journey, ensuring coordinated execution and on-ground 
assistance. 
 
The mandate provides an opportunity for Ebix Travels to leverage its established travel 
management capabilities across multiple service categories and deliver an integrated travel 
proposition for government and institutional customers.

Leveraging Established MICE and Destination Management Capabilities 
 
Ebix Travels will draw upon the experience and capabilities of its established travel businesses, 
including Mercury Travels and Leisure Corp, to support the requirements under the engagement. 
The businesses have capabilities spanning corporate conferences, incentive programmes, 
destination management, group travel, event planning and associated logistics. 
 
Its MICE business has received the National Tourism Award twice and has served as a preferred 
MICE partner for more than 150 companies, reflecting its experience in managing complex travel 
programmes and large-scale corporate and institutional engagements. 
 
These capabilities complement Delphi's broader strategy of building an integrated travel 
platform by bringing together corporate travel, leisure travel, MICE, hospitality and ancillary 
travel services, enabling the Company to pursue opportunities across a wider spectrum of 
customer segments. 
 
Positioned to Participate in India's Expanding Travel Ecosystem 
 
India's travel and tourism industry continues to offer significant growth opportunities, supported 
by rising domestic mobility, increasing international travel and growing demand for organised 
travel management solutions. According to the Ministry of Tourism, India recorded 
approximately 428.7 crore domestic tourist visits in 2025, registering growth of around 45.5% 
over the previous year, while outbound departures by Indian nationals increased by 6.3% to 
approximately 3.3 crore. 
 
The corporate travel segment also represents a significant opportunity, with Deloitte estimating 
that India's corporate travel market could reach approximately US$20.8 billion by FY2030, 
growing at around 10.1% CAGR. 
 
Against this backdrop, Delphi is focused on expanding its Travel business beyond traditional 
ticketing services towards a broader, technology-enabled and integrated travel management 
platform. The MPSTDC empanelment further broadens the Company's addressable customer 
base and provides an additional avenue to participate in the growing government and 
institutional travel ecosystem. 
 
Building a Diversified and Scalable Travel Platform 
 
The latest development adds to Ebix Travels' existing portfolio of government and institutional 
tourism engagements. The business also operates and manages the Deccan Odyssey luxury train 
in partnership with the Maharashtra Tourism Development Corporation (MTDC), providing 
experience in tourism operations, destination management and travel services associated with 
government-linked initiatives. 
 
As Delphi continues to consolidate and expand its Travel operations, engagements of this nature 
are expected to complement its existing corporate travel business, strengthen institutional 
relationships and create opportunities for cross-selling and wider adoption of its integrated travel 
services. 
 
The Company remains focused on building a scalable Travel platform through deeper customer 
engagement, expansion into new business segments, technology-led service delivery and the 
utilization of synergies across its travel businesses.
