### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Ador Welding Limited has approved the vesting of 6,625 employee stock options under its 2025 ESOP plan, part of an initial grant of 29,500 options. These options carry a low exercise price of Rs. 10 per share, which, while beneficial for employee retention and aligning interests, presents a potential dilution impact for existing shareholders once exercised, particularly if the market price is substantially higher. No immediate share issuance or earnings per share dilution has occurred as options are vested but not yet exercised.
