Management’s Message
“Ador has sustained its momentum in Q2 FY27, with revenue growing 23% YoY to Rs. 345 Cr. This takes H1 FY27 revenue to Rs. 654 Cr, also 
up 23% YoY. Demand stayed healthy across our core user industries: infrastructure, power, oil & gas, railways, shipbuilding, defence and 
heavy fabrication. Strong capital goods activity in India during the quarter supported this.
Gross margins were 34.2% in Q2 FY27, reflecting higher steel and other raw material prices in an inflationary input environment. EBITDA 
grew 9% YoY to Rs. 38 Cr in Q2 FY27, and H1 FY27 EBITDA margin held steady at around 11%. We continue to take calibrated pricing actions 
and to monitor raw material and geopolitical developments closely.
Three key drivers continue to underpin our growth:
•
Exports: The Middle East, served through our Dubai hub, remains our primary export market, and we continue to monitor developments 
in the region closely. We are expanding our international distributor network and exploring new opportunities in European markets..
•
Product Mix: We are scaling up technology-led, higher-value offerings, particularly in welding equipment, including advanced digital 
and automation-ready machines. In consumables, we continue to grow our range of specialised, high-performance products for 
demanding applications.
•
Customer Engagement: We are deepening direct relationships with key customers to drive new business, while maintaining our 
existing distributor partnerships. Technical support, training and application support help us here.
Automation remains a strategic priority as rising labour costs increase demand for productivity and efficiency. Ador is strengthening its 
robotics, cobot and Industry 4.0 capabilities, including Ador WelDoS, our digital welding management solution. We continue to evaluate 
acquisition opportunities to accelerate growth in this segment.
As Ador celebrates 75 years of creating the best welding experience, the Board has declared an interim dividend of Rs. 7.50 per equity 
share to reward our shareholders for their continued trust. We remain confident of carrying this momentum through the rest of FY27.”
