Ms. Shaili Tyagi has more than 25 years of experience in various 
facets of HR function. Her professional journey includes key 
leadership roles across prominent organizations such as Sheela

Telenor India, Syngenta India Limited, Matrix Laboratories Ltd (A

supplemented by advanced HR qualifications including a 
PGCHRM from XLRI Jamshedpur and a PGDBA from Symbiosis 
University (SCDL). Throughout her career, Ms. Tyagi has 
established core expertise in Talent & Leadership Management 
(executive hiring, succession planning, and competency mapping), 
Organization Design & Development (enterprise restructuring and 
RACI governance), Change & Culture Management (M&A people 
integration and APAC-wide HR transformation), Performance & 
Rewards (merit-based and flexi-compensation frameworks), and 
driving Group DEI initiatives along with strategic Employer 
Branding.
