KMEW Secures Its Third Green Tug Contract 
Mumbai, India | September 19, 2026: Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited 
(“KMEW”) (BSE: 543273 | NSE: KMEW), an integrated marine infrastructure and dredging 
company, is pleased to announce that it has secured its Third Green Tug contract from Mumbai 
Port Authority, one of the Major Ports of India. 
The contract, valued at approximately ₹279.33 crore (including taxes), involves the construction 
and hiring of a battery-operated Green Tug for a tenure of 15 years. 
With this order, KMEW has further strengthened its presence in India's emerging Green Tug 
segment, having won three Green Tug contracts awarded by Major Ports within a relatively short 
period. These contracts represent the Company's growing portfolio of next-generation marine 
assets with long-term operating commitments. 
Building a Scalable Green Maritime Platform 
The latest order strongly adds to the two Green Tug contracts secured earlier by KMEW and will 
be supported through its subsidiary, Knowledge Shipyard Limited, which is also undertaking the 
construction of the Company's other Green Initiatives. 
The simultaneous construction of multiple Green Tugs at a common shipbuilding platform 
provides KMEW with an opportunity to develop greater standardisation, repeatability and 
execution efficiencies, while building specialised technical capabilities in the construction of next-
generation marine assets. 
The Company intends to leverage its integrated capabilities across shipbuilding, marine operations 
and asset management throughout the lifecycle of these vessels—from construction and 
commissioning to operations and maintenance. 
This integrated approach is expected to create a scalable platform for KMEW as it expands its 
presence beyond its established dredging and marine services businesses into specialised 
shipbuilding and next-generation marine infrastructure. 
From Dredging to Next-Generation Marine Infrastructure 
The Green Tug business represents an important extension of KMEW's existing marine 
capabilities. 
Over the years, KMEW has developed experience in operating and managing a diversified fleet of 
specialised marine assets across dredging, port operations and allied marine services. The 
Company's growing Green Tug portfolio enables it to leverage this operational experience while 
developing capabilities in advanced vessel construction and long-term asset management. 
The Company is therefore progressively building an integrated marine platform spanning: 
Dredging → Marine Assets → Green Vessels → Indigenous Shipbuilding → Lifecycle Marine Infrastructure 
The development of these capabilities is intended to strengthen KMEW's participation in India's 
evolving maritime infrastructure ecosystem and create opportunities in specialised marine assets 
beyond conventional dredging.

Supporting India's Green Maritime Vision 
The Green Tug Transition Programme, announced on 22 May 2023 as part of the Government of 
India's broader maritime sustainability initiatives under the Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047, 
seeks to accelerate the transition of harbour tugs from conventional fossil-fuel-powered vessels 
towards cleaner and more sustainable alternatives. 
KMEW's growing Green Tug portfolio enables the Company to participate in this transition while 
contributing to the development of indigenous capabilities in the design, construction, operation 
and maintenance of advanced marine assets. 
The Company believes that building these technologically advanced vessels in India can contribute 
to the broader objectives of Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat, while supporting the 
development of India's domestic shipbuilding ecosystem. 
With multiple Green Tugs being developed on a common shipbuilding platform, KMEW also 
intends to build institutional knowledge and technical capabilities that can be leveraged for future 
specialised marine asset programmes. 
Management Commentary 
Mr. Sujay Kewalramani, CEO, Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited, said: 
“We are delighted to announce our third Green Tug contract with this ₹279.33 crore order 
from Mumbai Port Authority. Securing three Green Tug contracts from Major Ports reflects 
the confidence placed in KMEW's technical capabilities, execution strength and our long-
term vision for sustainable maritime infrastructure. 
This order is particularly significant as we intend to construct this vessel alongside our 
other two Green Tugs at our shipbuilding platform. Having multiple vessels under 
construction simultaneously will allow us to develop greater standardisation, strengthen 
execution capabilities and build deeper technical expertise in this specialised segment. 
Our objective is not merely to construct individual vessels, but to develop a scalable 
capability around next-generation marine assets. By combining our experience in marine 
operations with our growing shipbuilding capabilities, we aim to build expertise across 
the complete lifecycle of these assets—from construction and commissioning to operations 
and maintenance. 
We are equally proud that these technologically advanced vessels will be built in India. 
We see this as a meaningful contribution to the Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat 
vision. Our objective is to develop domestic capabilities to design, construct, operate and 
maintain next-generation marine assets within the country, while creating a scalable 
platform for future opportunities in green and specialised marine infrastructure.”
