In accordance with the provision of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that 
the Board of Directors of the Company at their Meeting held today i.e. Wednesday, September 
02, 2026 has approved allotment of 29,305 equity shares of the Company having a face value of 
₹1/- (Rupee One only) each, as fully paid up, to eligible employees, upon exercise of vested 
options under BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle limited – Employee Stock Option Plan 2014.

of the Company stands increased from ₹ 15,27,49,400 (consisting of 15,27,49,400 equity shares of 
face value of ₹ 1 each) to ₹ 15,27,78,705 (consisting of 15,27,78,705 equity shares of face value of
