### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited disclosed a SEBI settlement order concerning its Managing Director, Mr. Karan Adani, and former CFO, Mr. B. Ravi, for alleged violations of SEBI Listing Regulations, specifically Regulation 17(8) related to board compliance reviews. While the company stated there is "nil financial impact," both executives individually paid a settlement amount of INR 13,65,000 each. The involvement of top management in alleged compliance breaches, even without admitting guilt, poses a red flag regarding potential corporate governance issues within the company.
