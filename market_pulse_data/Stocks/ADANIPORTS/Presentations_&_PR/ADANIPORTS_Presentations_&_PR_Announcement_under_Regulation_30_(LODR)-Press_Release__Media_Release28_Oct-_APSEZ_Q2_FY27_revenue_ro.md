Domestic ports: The earnings anchor  
Domestic Ports continued to provide scale, stability and strong cash generation. Revenue increased 
13% YoY, supported by cargo growth and higher realizations. EBITDA margin remained best-in-class 
at 72.5%. Cargo volume during the quarter stood at 118 MMT, while APSEZ’s all-India cargo and 
container market share was at 27.3% and 44.4% respectively. Domestic ports’ capacity stood at 671 
MMT. 
During the quarter, APSEZ secured 30-year concession for Paradip Port’s CQ-I and CQ-II dry bulk 
berths, adding 18 MMT of mechanized capacity. The addition strengthens APSEZ’s east coast 
network, enhances access to mineral-rich hinterlands and supports long-term cargo growth. During 
the quarter, APSEZ also commenced EXIM operations at the Vizhinjam port.  
International Ports: moving from scale-up to meaningful earnings contribution 
The International Ports portfolio sustained its strong momentum, delivering record revenue and 
EBITDA of ₹1,839 crore and ₹878 crore respectively, with the EBITDA margin reaching a record 
47.7%. Volumes increased to 24 MMT from 10 MMT in Q2 FY26. 
The performance was led by the continued ramp-up of Colombo West International Terminal (CWIT) 
and Tanzania operations and contribution from NQXT Australia. CWIT handled 2 million TEUs within 
its first 18 months of operations, demonstrating strong customer confidence and operational scale. 
Building on the success of Phase I, APSEZ inaugurated Phase II, doubling CWIT’s capacity from 1.6 
million TEUs to 3.2 million TEUs. The expanded terminal aims to handle nearly 25% of the Port of 
Colombo’s targeted capacity of 13 million TEUs by 2028. 
Tanzania also delivered its highest ever quarterly volume at 265,518 TEUs, including its highest ever 
monthly volume at 91,625 TEUs during July 2026.   
Logistics: expanding the shore-to-door opportunity 
Logistics business demonstrated strong recovery from Q1FY27 that was impacted by the West Asia 
crisis. TEU volumes jumped 15% to 167,453 TEUs in Q2 FY27 from 145,310 TEUs in Q1 FY27. Trucking 
revenue was up 28% YoY. IFN revenue more than doubled YoY to ₹289 Cr.  
The changing business mix reflects APSEZ’s strategy of building an integrated, asset-light logistics 
platform that connects ports with industrial and consumption centers across India. 
Marine: building a global maritime services platform 
Marine revenue and EBITDA increased 57% YoY and 32% YoY respectively supported by fleet 
additions (138 vessels in Q2 FY27 v. 127 in Q2 FY26), higher contribution from offshore services, and 
foray into subsea services.

About APSEZ 
 
APSEZ, part of the globally diversified Adani Group, a leading Integrated Transport Operator--across cargo 
origination (International Freight Network) through port handling, rail transport, multi-modal logistics parks, 
warehousing, and final delivery via road transport to customer gates.   
 
This comprehensive "shore-to-door" capability, supported by cutting-edge digital infrastructure and AI-driven 
optimization, positions APSEZ as India's preeminent integrated logistics solutions provider. The company 
operates a comprehensive ecosystem of 16 strategically located ports and terminals across India's west, south, 
and east coasts, combined with a diversified marine fleet of 138 vessels, integrated logistics capabilities 
including 12 multi-modal logistics parks, 3.1 million sq. ft. of warehouses, and 40,000+ trucks operating on its 
proprietary platform, thus providing capabilities to handle vast amounts of cargo from both coastal areas and 
the hinterland. APSEZ also operates 4 international ports across Australia, Colombo, Israel and Tanzania.  With 
a current cargo handling capacity of 671 million tonnes per annum, APSEZ commands approximately 27% of 
India's total port volumes, targeting 1 billion tonnes throughput by FY31.  
 
Recognized among the Top 5% of global transportation and transportation infrastructure firms in the 2025 
S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment (95th percentile globally), with five ports featuring in the 
World Bank's Container Port Performance Index 2024, APSEZ combines scale, operational excellence, and 
integrated capabilities to enable seamless global trade.
