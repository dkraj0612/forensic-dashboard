Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”), we hereby inform the exchanges that, IL 
JIN Electronics (India) Limited (Formerly known as IL JIN Electronics (India) Private Limited) (“IL JIN” / “Purchaser”), a 
Material Subsidiary of the Company, has entered into Share Swap Agreement on 18th October 2026 with Mr. Ramanna 
Rajesh Mayasandra, Mr. Harsha Venkatesh, Dwaith Advisory Private Limited, Mrs. Doddabidere Honappa Veena 
(“collectively referred to as the “Sellers”) and Power-One Micro Systems Private Limited (“Power-One”), a step-down 
subsidiary of the Company. 
In terms of the Share Swap Agreement, IL JIN shall acquire 1,75,680 equity shares of Power-One, representing the 
balance 40% equity stake on a fully diluted basis, from the Sellers and shall issue and allot 1,76,000 Compulsorily 
Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS A3) of face value Rs. 10 each at an issue price of Rs. 10,000 per CCPS A3 (including 
a premium of Rs. 9,990 per CCPS A3), to the Sellers, against the purchase of equity shares in accordance with the terms 
and conditions stipulated under the Share Swap Agreement. 
 
Upon completion of the aforesaid transaction, IL JIN shall hold 100% of the equity share capital of Power-One and, 
consequently, Power-One shall become a wholly owned subsidiary of IL JIN and the Company. 
 
The disclosures required under SEBI (LODR) Regulations in line with SEBI Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-
POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January 2026 are given in “Annexure – A” and “Annexure – B” to this letter. 
 
The above intimation is also available on the website of the Company at www.ambergroupindia.com.
