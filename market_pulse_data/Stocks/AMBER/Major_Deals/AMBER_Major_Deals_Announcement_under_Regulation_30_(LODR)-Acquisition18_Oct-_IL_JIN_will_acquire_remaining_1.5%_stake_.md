Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”), we hereby inform the exchanges that, IL 
JIN Electronics (India) Limited (Formerly known as IL JIN Electronics (India) Private Limited) (“IL JIN” / “Purchaser”), a 
Material Subsidiary of the Company, has entered into Share Swap Agreement on 18th October 2026 with Ms. Manju 
Thomas (“Seller”), Mr. Mampra Chacko Thomas and Ascent Circuits Private Limited (“Ascent Circuits”), a step-down 
subsidiary of the Company. 
 
In terms of the Share Swap Agreement, IL JIN shall acquire 23,401 equity shares of Ascent Circuits, representing the 
balance 1.50% equity stake on a fully diluted basis, from the Seller and shall issue and allot 13,120 Compulsorily Convertible 
Preference Shares (“CCPS A2”) of face value Rs. 10 each at an issue price of Rs. 10,000 per CCPS A2 (including a premium 
of Rs. 9,990 per CCPS A2), to the Seller, against the purchase of equity shares in accordance with the terms and 
conditions stipulated under the Share Swap Agreement. 
 
Upon completion of the aforesaid transaction, IL JIN shall hold 100% of the equity share capital of Ascent Circuits and, 
consequently, Ascent Circuits shall become a wholly owned subsidiary of IL JIN and the Company. 
 
The disclosures required under SEBI (LODR) Regulations in line with SEBI Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-
POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January 2026, are given in “Annexure – A” and “Annexure – B” to this letter. 
 
The above intimation is also available on the website of the Company at www.ambergroupindia.com. 
 
You are requested to take the aforesaid information on record and disseminate the same on your website.
