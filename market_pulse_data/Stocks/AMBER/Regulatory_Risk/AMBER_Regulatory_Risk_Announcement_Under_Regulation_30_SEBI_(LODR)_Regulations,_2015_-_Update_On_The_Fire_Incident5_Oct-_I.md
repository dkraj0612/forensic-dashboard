In continuation of our earlier intimation dated 4th August 2026, and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI LODR 
Regulations"), we wish to update the Exchanges on the insurance claim recovery process pertaining to the fire 
incident that occurred during the early hours of 4th August 2026 at the Factory Unit of the Company's material 
subsidiary, IL JIN Electronics (India) Limited (formerly known as IL JIN Electronics (India) Private Limited) ("IL JIN"), 
situated at Plot No. 28, Ecotech-III, Extn-II, Udyog Kendra, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh – 201 306. 
  
Based on the assessment undertaken by the Management, the loss arising from the aforesaid incident has been 
estimated at ₹38.28 crore, plus applicable GST. Accordingly, the Company has lodged an insurance claim with the 
insurance company for the aforesaid amount, together with applicable GST. 
 
Pursuant to the initial preliminary assessment and considering the urgency of the situation, the insurer has already 
released an account payment of ₹10 crore against the estimated loss. 
  
The insurer has commenced its detailed assessment of the submitted claim and will release the balance insurance 
claim amount, in accordance with the terms and conditions of the relevant insurance policies, upon completion of 
such assessment. 
  
The Company is confident of recovering a majority portion of the losses incurred as a result of the aforesaid incident, 
and continues to closely engage with the insurer and other concerned stakeholders for expeditious processing of 
the pending claim. The Company is also undertaking necessary measures for restoration of the affected facilities 
and normalization of operations. 
  
In terms of Para 6.2 of Para B of Part A of Schedule III to the SEBI LODR Regulations, read with SEBI Master Circular 
No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January 2026, the Company shall continue to keep the 
Stock Exchanges informed of any further material developments in relation to the insurance claim recovery, 
restoration activities, and the operational and financial impact arising from the aforesaid incident.
