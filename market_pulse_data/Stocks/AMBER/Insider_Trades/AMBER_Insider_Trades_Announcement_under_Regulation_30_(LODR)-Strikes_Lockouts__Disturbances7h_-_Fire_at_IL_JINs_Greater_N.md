Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI LODR Regulations"), we wish to inform the 
Exchange(s) of a fire incident occurred during the early hours of 4th August 2026 at the factory of our 
material subsidiary, IL JIN Electronics (India) Private Limited ("IL JIN"), situated Plot No. 28, Ecotech - III, 
Extn - II Udyog Kendra, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh - 201306, in accordance with SEBI Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January 2026 and other applicable SEBI Circular(s): 
 
This is to inform the Exchange(s) that the fire was brought under control with the assistance of the 
factory's fire and safety team and the local fire authorities. There has been no loss of life of any employee 
or worker of IL JIN due to the incident, whereas one employee of IL JIN sustained minor injuries and has 
got discharged from the hospital after providing necessary medical treatment. 
 
However, the Company deeply regrets the unfortunate demise of two personnel from the local fire 
authorities during the firefighting operations. The Company extends its sincere condolences to the 
bereaved families and will provide all necessary support while extending full cooperation to the 
concerned authorities. Further, three other personnel from the local fire authorities sustained injuries 
during the firefighting operations and are currently undergoing medical treatment. 
 
The Company is currently in the process of assessing the impact on the operations and extent of the 
damage caused to the property, plant and machinery and/or inventory while maintaining the highest 
standard of safety and compliances. The assets are adequately insured, and the necessary insurance claim 
process will be initiated, as applicable. 
 
Operations in the affected area of the facility have been temporarily impacted. However, operations in 
the unaffected areas are expected to be restored as soon as practicable.
