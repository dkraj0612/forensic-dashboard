Brief Profile  
Mr. Chandramouli Akkiraju holds a Master’s degree in 
Business Management from Andhra University and a 
certification in risk management from the Asian 
Institute of Management, Philippines. A dedicated and 
innovative professional, he brings over 25 years of 
experience in financial services. He has previously 
been associated with BASIX, KBS Local Area Bank, 
Belstar Financial Services, Heritage Finlease Limited 
and Namra Finance Limited. 
As Chief Operating Officer of Namra Finance Limited 
from 2017 to 2023, he oversaw the growth of the 
company’s microfinance AUM from approximately Rs. 
90 crore to Rs. 1,500 crore and was instrumental in 
steering the company through the COVID-19 
pandemic. 
His work across domestic and international markets 
has given him valuable insight into cross-cultural 
customer behaviour and business opportunities. He 
has professional exposure of 22 states in India and 12 
countries overseas, the majority of them in Africa. As 
a consultant, he successfully bid for two prestigious 
assignments: agricultural value chain financing with 
IFC in India, and Micro Lead Cameroon with UNCDF. He 
has also provided advisory services on agricultural 
value chain risk management to OIKO Credit’s 
subsidiary in India.
