Brief Profile of Mr. Dinkar Ayilavarapu 
Mr. Dinkar Ayilavarapu is a business leader with over two decades of experience in 
building, transforming and scaling large enterprises across digital commerce, retail and 
technology. He has expertise in the areas of P&L Leadership, enterprise transformation 
and long range strategy. 
He was previously leading Flipkart Wholesale, an omnichannel B2B business serving 
more than a million customers across India, focusing on reshaping a legacy business 
into a digital-first, customer-centric platform balancing commercial performance with 
technology modernisation, operational discipline and cultural renewal. He was earlier 
Group Head of Strategy for Flipkart and prior to that, he has 15 years experience at 
Bain & Company, where he led large-scale transformation, merger and turnaround 
programs for telecom and technology clients globally.
