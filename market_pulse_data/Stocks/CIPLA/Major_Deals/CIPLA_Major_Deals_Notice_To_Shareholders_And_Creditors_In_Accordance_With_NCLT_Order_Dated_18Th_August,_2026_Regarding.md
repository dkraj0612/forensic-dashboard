Dear Equity Shareholders, 
 
Notice is hereby given in pursuance of sub-section (5) of Section 230 of the Companies Act, 2013 and as 
directed by the Mumbai Bench of the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal ('NCLT' or 'Tribunal') by an 
order dated 18th August, 2026 under sub-section (1) of Section 230 of the Companies Act, 2013 ('the Act') 
in relation to the Scheme of Amalgamation (by Absorption) of Inzpera Healthsciences Limited ("Transferor 
Company") with Cipla Limited ("Transferee Company") and their respective shareholders ("Scheme"). 
 
Hon'ble NCLT in terms of order passed for directions in C.A. (CAA)/ 114 (MB)/ 2026 dated 18th August, 
2026 ("NCLT Order") has dispensed with the meetings of the equity shareholders and unsecured creditors 
of the Transferee Company. However, as per NCLT Order, a notice is being sent to all the shareholders and 
unsecured creditors of the Transferee Company, informing them that they may submit their 
representations, if any, in respect of the proposed Scheme to the Hon'ble NCLT within 30 days of the receipt 
of the Notice. As there are no Secured Creditors in the Transferee Company, no meeting of Secured 
Creditors is required.
