U.S. FDA Approves First ANDA Generic Flovent® HFA in 110 mcg and 220 mcg 
strengths for the Treatment of Asthma

Mumbai, India, November 25, 2026 – Cipla Limited (BSE: 500087; NSE: CIPLA EQ; and 
hereafter referred to as "Cipla") today announced the U.S. FDA approval and launch of 
Fluticasone HFA MDI, the first generic equivalent Flovent® HFA, in110 mcg and 220 mcg 
strengths, approved under an Abbreviated New Drug Application (ANDA).   
Cipla and Teva Pharmaceuticals (hereinafter referred to as “Teva”), jointly developed 
the product. Cipla is responsible for manufacturing and supplying the product, while 
Teva holds the ANDA to the product and will commercialize the product in the United 
States. 
Fluticasone HFA is an inhaled corticosteroid indicated for the long-term maintenance 
and prophylactic treatment of asthma in patients aged 4 years and older, as well as for 
patients requiring oral corticosteroid therapy for asthma. 
Commenting on the milestone, Achin Gupta, Managing Director & Global Chief 
Executive Officer, Cipla, said, “Respiratory care has long been a core strength for Cipla, 
and our purpose remains rooted in improving the lives of patients. This product 
reinforces our commitment to expanding access to trusted treatment options for 
patients living with asthma”.  
Marc Falkin, Chief Executive Officer, Cipla North America, said, “We are delighted to see 
this opportunity create meaningful impact and support our future US growth journey. Our 
team remains focused on consistent supply to service the market.” 
According to IQVIA, the U.S. market for fluticasone HFA 110 and 220 mcg is approximately 
$350 million.
