Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we hereby inform you that Mr Venkata Sai Mungara, 
Global Head Supply Chain, has transitioned to the role of Chief of Manufacturing and Supply Chain Operations 
of Cipla USA Inc. (wholly owned subsidiary of the Company) with effect from 1st October, 2026. Consequent to 
this transition, he will cease to be a Senior Management Personnel of the Company. 
 
The details required under the SEBI Listing Regulations, read with the SEBI circular no. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-
CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January, 2026 have been provided in Annexure A.
