FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER / HALF YEAR ENDED 30th 
SEPTEMBER 2026. 
 
RESULTS AT A GLANCE (Including IFSC Banking Unit) – 
BUSINESS- 
➢ Total Global Business grew by 20.54% to ₹ 889541 crore (Q2FY-26 - ₹ 737938 crore). 
➢ Total Domestic Deposits increased by 14.41% to ₹ 508509 crore (Q2FY-26 - ₹ 444450 
crore). 
➢ CASA Deposits stand at 44.65% of total deposits. 
➢ Global Advances increased by 29.83% to ₹ 381032 crore (Q2FY-26 - ₹ 293488 crore). 
o Domestic Advance stood at ₹ 372939 crore. 
o Overseas Advance (Gift City- IBU) stood at ₹ 8093 crore. 
➢ Business per Employee has improved to ₹ 25.26 crore on YoY basis, as against ₹ 21.48 
crore for the quarter ended 30/09/2025. 
➢ RAM (Retail, Agriculture & MSME) Business grew by 21.11%. The individual sector 
wise growth stood at 24.49% (₹ 112299), 19.92% (₹ 67778) & 17.36% (₹ 74196), 
respectively for Retail, Agriculture & MSME. 
PROFITABILITY – (Y-O-Y)- FOR SEPTEMBER QUARTER- 
➢ Net Profit for the Quarter has increased by 21.10% to ₹ 1469 crore from ₹ 1213 crore. 
➢ Operating Profit for the Quarter stood at ₹ 2268 crore from ₹ 1786 crore, with a growth 
of 26.9%. 
➢ Net Interest Income (NII) grew by 26.28 % on YoY basis to ₹ 4146 crore in Q2FY27 
as against ₹ 3283 crore in Q2FY26. 
➢ Total Income (Interest Income plus Non-Interest Income) for Q2FY27 improved by 
14.92 % from ₹ 10213 crore for Q2FY26 to ₹ 11737 crore for Q2FY27. 
➢ Cost to Income ratio reduced to 58.95 % for Q2FY27 as against 62.42 % Q2FY26.

PROFITABILITY – (Y-O-Y)- FOR SEPTEMBER HALF YEAR- 
➢ Net Profit increased by 17.25 % to ₹ 2793 crore for the Half year ended September 
2026 on YoY basis against ₹ 2382 crore. 
➢ Operating Profit for the Half Year stood at ₹ 4454 crore as against ₹ 4090 crore, 
registering a growth of 8.90%. 
➢ Net Interest Income grew by 20.91 % to ₹ 8060 crore for the Half year ended September 
2026, on YoY basis as against ₹ 6666 crore. 
➢ ROA (Return on Asset) remained stable at 1.02%. 
➢ ROE (Return on Equity) improved to 15.50% from 14.24%. 
 
PROFITABILITY – Q2 over Q1- FY 2026-27 
➢ Net Profit grew by 10.95% (₹ 145 crore) to ₹ 1469 crore. 
➢ Operating Profit grew by 3.75% (₹ 82 crore) to ₹ 2268 crore. 
➢ Net Interest Income grew by 5.93% (₹ 232 crore) to ₹ 4146 crore. 
➢ Interest Income grew by 6.87% (₹ 666 crore) to ₹ 10357 crore. 
➢ ROA improved by 3 bps & stood at 1.03%. 
 
ASSET QUALITY- 
➢ Gross NPA stood at 2.39%, registering an improvement of 62 bps, from 3.01%, when 
compared on Y-o-Y basis. 
➢ Net NPA stood 0.31%, registering an improvement of 17 bps, from 0.48%, when 
compared on Y-o-Y basis. 
➢ Provision Coverage Ratio stood at 97.16%, registering a growth of 28 bps, from 
96.88%, when compared on Y-o-Y basis. 
➢ Slippage Ratio stood at 0.24% for Quarter & 0.45% for the Half Year, registering an 
improvement of 6 bps / 11 bps, respectively. 
 
BRANCH SEGMENTATION- 
➢ Bank is having pan India presence with total 22215 Touch Points, (85% of touchpoints 
in RU/SU centres) with 4622 Branches, with network of Branches in rural & semi urban 
areas (65.24%) & 1 IFSC Banking Unit, 3820 ATMs and 13752 BC outlets & 20 BC 
Maxx Centres as on 30/09/2026. 
 
CAPITAL ADEQUACY- 
➢ Total BASEL III CRAR for the Quarter / Half Year ended 30/09/26 is 17.68% (with 
Tier I of 15.97 %) as against 17.34 % for the quarter / Half Year ended 30/09/25, 
registering an improvement of 34 bps.
