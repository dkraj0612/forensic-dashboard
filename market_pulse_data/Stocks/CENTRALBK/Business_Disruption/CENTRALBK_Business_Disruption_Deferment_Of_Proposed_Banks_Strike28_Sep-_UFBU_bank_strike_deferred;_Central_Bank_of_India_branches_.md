Dear Sir/Madam, 
Sub: Deferment of proposed Bank’s Strike.    
Ref: Our earlier letter no. CO:IRD:2026:27:148 dated 09.09.2026 
 
With reference to captioned subject, this is inform that the proposed Bank strike called by United Forum 
of Bank Unions (UFBU) consisting of seven unions-(AIBEA, AIBOC, BEFI, NCBE, AIBOA, INBEF 
and INBOC) on 28th to 30th September 2026 & continuous strike from 26th October 2026 is now deferred 
pursuant to the discussion and understanding between Indian Bank’s Association (IBA) and Bank 
Unions on their key demands/issues. 
 
As such, all the Branches/Offices of the Bank across India are functioning as usual, ensuring 
uninterrupted Banking services for its customers. 
 
We request you to take note of the above pursuant to the Regulation 30 and 51 of SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015.
