Press Release 
Revenue up by 33% and PAT up by 49% 
(2nd Quarter FY 2027 Consolidated Results) 
 
 
Mumbai, 30th October, 2026: Ajanta Pharma Ltd., a specialty pharmaceutical formulation 
company today reported its excellent performance for 2nd quarter, and half year ended 
30th September 2026. 
 
Q2 FY 2027 performance highlights (compared to Q2 FY 2026): 
 
• 
Revenue from operations at Rs. 1,794 cr. against Rs. 1,354 cr.; up 33%. 
• 
Adjusted EBITDA (excluding forex loss) at Rs. 543 cr. & 30% against Rs. 369 cr., up 47%.  
• 
Profit after tax at Rs. 388 cr. against Rs. 260 cr.; PAT at 22%; up 49%. 
 
H1 FY 2027 performance highlights (compared to H1 FY 2026): 
 
• 
Revenue from operations at Rs. 3,420 cr. against Rs. 2,656 cr.; up 29%. 
• 
Adjusted EBITDA (excluding forex loss) at Rs. 997 cr. & 29% against Rs. 745 cr., up 34%.  
• 
Profit after tax at Rs. 722 cr. against Rs. 516 cr.; PAT at 21%; up 40%. 
• 
ROCE stood at a healthy level of 41% and RONW at 31%.

About Ajanta Pharma Limited 
 
Ajanta Pharma is a speciality pharmaceutical formulation company having major focus on 
branded generic business across India, Asia & Africa. Company has ground presence in each 
of these 34+ countries. Many of company’s products are 1st to market and are leading in their 
sub-therapeutic segments. Company also has presence in USA in generic business and 
institution business in Africa. 
 
Company’s state of the art R&D centre is in Mumbai. Company has 7 world class 
manufacturing facilities located in India. 
 
For the last 3 financial years, company has posted healthy Revenue and PAT CAGR of 14%.
