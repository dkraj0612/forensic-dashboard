Ashika Global Securities Ltd reports H1FY27 PAT of ₹118.08 crore
Steady path, higher rise: every business vertical is now on a growth path
Mumbai, 31 October 2026: Ashika Global Securities Limited (formerly Ashika Credit Capital Limited), the listed 
holding company of the Ashika Group, today announced its consolidated financial results for the half year ended 
30 September 2026 (H1FY27), as approved by its Board of Directors.

●Key highlights
1
Credit Rating Upgrade: Credit rating of Ashika Stock Services Limited, a material subsidiary, upgraded from BBB+ 
to A-, reflecting improved credit standing.
2
Private Equity: Ashika Growth Equity Fund achieved its first close with commitments exceeding ₹215 crore, 
marking a significant milestone in the Group's alternative investment business.
3
Private Credit: Ashika Credit Opportunities Fund achieved its first close with commitments of approximately ₹300 
crore, with deployment activities scheduled to commence in Q3 FY27.
4
Healthcare Fund: Launched Ashika DNA Fund, a specialized Category III Alternative Investment Fund focused on 
the healthcare and pharmaceutical sectors.
5
Digital Transformation: Successfully completed the full rollout of Dhanush 2.0, with 100% migration of customers 
from the legacy application to the integrated wealth platform.
6
Merchant Banking: Filed two Draft Red Herring Prospectuses (DRHPs) for mainboard IPOs, strengthening the 
Group's presence in the capital markets and investment banking business.
7
Mutual Fund: Progressing towards securing final regulatory approval for the Mutual Fund business, with the 
launch of the first scheme targeted for the coming quarter.
This half was about building momentum across the platform. Our Private Equity and Private Credit funds 
achieved their first closes, we launched the Ashika DNA Fund, and Dhanush 2.0 moved every customer onto our 
integrated wealth platform. The upgrade of Ashika Stock Services Ltd's credit rating to A- recognises the 
discipline behind this growth. As we work towards launching our Mutual Fund, our focus remains on executing 
steadily and scaling each vertical responsibly.
Mr. Chirag Jain, CEO, Ashika Global Securities Ltd.
 
●About Ashika Group
Ashika Group is a diversified financial services group with an established presence across lending, capital markets, 
investment banking and wealth management. Ashika Global Securities Limited (formerly Ashika Credit Capital Limited), 
the Group's listed holding company, is a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) listed on BSE Limited. The Group offers a 
comprehensive range of financial services, spanning NBFC lending, stock broking, depository services, financial product 
distribution, merchant and investment banking, wealth management, Category II and Category III Alternative Investment 
Funds, mutual fund and an integrated digital wealth management platform through the Dhanush app.
