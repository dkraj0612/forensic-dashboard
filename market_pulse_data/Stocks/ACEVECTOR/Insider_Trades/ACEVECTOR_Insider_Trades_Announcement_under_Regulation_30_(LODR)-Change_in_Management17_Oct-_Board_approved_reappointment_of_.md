Kasaragod Ullas Kamath is the Chairperson of our Board 
and Independent Director of our Company. He holds a 
bachelor’s degree in law from the University of Mysore, 
Karnataka and a master’s degree in commerce from the 
Kakatiya University, Warangal, Telangana. He holds a 
master’s degree of science in management from the 
London School of Economics and Political Science and 
has also completed the advanced management 
programs from the Wharton School at University of 
Pennsylvania and Harvard Business School and has over 
28 years of experience. He is a certified fellow of the 
Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and an 
associate member of the Institute of Company 
Secretaries of India. He is also an independent director 
on the board of our listed subsidiary Unicommerce 
eSolutions Limited. He is the founder of M/s. UK & Co., 
Advisors: Family Business, a partnership firm. He also 
served as the chairperson for FICCI Karnataka State 
Council and currently serves as a mentor for the FICCI 
Karnataka State Council. He has received the ‘CA 
Business Achiever’ award in the SME category for the 
year 2008 by Institute of Chartered Accountants of 
India, and the ‘CNBC Awaaz Best CEO’ award for the year 
2018 by CNBC. Previously, he was the joint managing 
director of Jyothy Labs Limited.
