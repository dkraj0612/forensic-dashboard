As previously disclosed by the Company, UC Credit has 
filed Commercial Arbitration Application No. 489 of 2026 
under Section 9 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 
1996 (the “Application”) before the Civil Court, against 
the Company and Agarwal Bulkactives Private Limited 
(“ABPL”). The Application is based on an alleged corporate 
guarantee dated January 31, 2024, said to have been 
given by the Company for a term loan of Rs. 5,00,00,000 
extended by UC Credit to ABPL.  
 
In relation to the said Application, the Civil Court passed an 
ex-parte ad-interim impugned order dated November 6, 
2026, by issuing: (a) notice to the Company and ABPL to 
appear on December 4, 2026, and show cause why they 
should not furnish security for Rs. 8,27,15,948; and (b) a 
conditional order of attachment in certain properties 
belonging to the Company and ABPL, pending further 
orders. (the “Interim Order”).  
 
Aggrieved by the Interim Order, the Company filed an 
appeal before the Hon’ble High Court of Karnataka seeking 
to set aside the Interim Order. The Hon'ble High Court of 
Karnataka heard the appeal and directed: (i) the Company 
to file the recall application for recall of the Interim Order 
at the Civil Court; and (ii) the Civil Court to hear and 
decide such application within 3 days of filing the same by 
Company (“HC Order”). 
 
Pursuant to the HC Order, the Company filed an 
application before the Civil Court, seeking to set aside the 
Interim Order.

The Civil Court in proceedings ongoing in relation to the 
Application, vide its order dated November 25, 2026, has: 
(i) continued the conditional attachment of certain
