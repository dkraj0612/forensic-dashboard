Sub: Changes in Senior Management Personnel of the Company  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Regulations”), we wish to inform you that             
Mr. Salil Raghavan, Head (HR & Admin), is retiring from the services of the Company on October 
04, 2026. However, he will continue to be associated with the Company as an advisor.  
Further, please note that Ms. Savitha Krishnamoorthy, who is currently General Manager, Human 
Resources has been appointed as Head (HR & Admin) w.e.f. October 05, 2026 in his place. She 
will be categorized as ‘Senior Management Personnel’ of the Company w.e.f. the same date as 
per the provisions of Regulation 16(1)(d) of SEBI Regulations. 
 
The details as required under SEBI Master Circular dated January 30, 2026 are enclosed as 
“Annexure A”.
