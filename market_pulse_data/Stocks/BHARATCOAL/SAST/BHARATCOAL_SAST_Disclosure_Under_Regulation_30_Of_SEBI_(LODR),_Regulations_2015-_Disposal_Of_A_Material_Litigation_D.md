Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is in continuation to the litigation disclosed in the offer documents relating to an 
application filed before the National Green Tribunal, Eastern Zone, Kolkata alleging 
violations of the Environment Protection Act, 1986, by our Company and others contending 
that illegal mining is occurring at Central Surunga Paharigora, Parbad Laxmi Colliery, and 
other sites located at Alakdiha OP, District Dhanbad, Jharkhand. 
 
The Company has been informed that the National Green Tribunal, Eastern Zone Bench, 
Kolkata, vide order dated 08.10.2026, has disposed of Original Application No. 
57/2023/EZ, Ajay Rajak v. Ministry of Coal & Ors. The Tribunal did not consider it proper 
to exercise parallel jurisdiction when identical reliefs are pending before the Hon’ble High 
Court of Jharkhand at Ranchi. 
 
This is for your information and record please.
