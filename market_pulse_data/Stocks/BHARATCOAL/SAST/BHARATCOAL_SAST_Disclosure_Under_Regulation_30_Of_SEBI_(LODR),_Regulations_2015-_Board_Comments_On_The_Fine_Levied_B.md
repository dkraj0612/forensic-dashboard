### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL) has been fined a cumulative ₹37.35 Lakhs by both the NSE and BSE for repeated non-compliance with SEBI LODR Regulations concerning the composition of its Board and key committees (Audit, Nomination & Remuneration, Stakeholder Relationship) for the quarters ended March and June 2026. This recurring failure to appoint requisite independent directors, including a woman director, presents a significant governance red flag, indicating systemic issues despite BCCL's assertion that appointments are beyond its direct control due to government processes. The company's board has decided to request a waiver of the fines and continue pursuing the matter with the Ministry of Coal.
