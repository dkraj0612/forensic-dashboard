Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements), Regulations 2015- Ongoing Litigation which has become Material 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III thereto, this is to inform 
that the following litigation has become material and is accordingly being disclosed. 
 
The matter pertains to a contractual dispute arising out of a contract awarded to AT-
Libra BPL (JV), a project-specific Joint Venture, pursuant to the Notice Inviting Tender 
dated 27.12.2010, for hiring of Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM) for removal 
and rehandling of overburden and transportation of coal at Golukdih NC Project, 
Dhanbad. 
 
The Hon’ble Supreme Court of India, vide Order dated 08.10.2026, dismissed the 
petition filed by Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL) in CA No. 1629/2026, BCCL v. 
AT Libra and Ors., against the Order dated 08.08.2024 of the Hon’ble High Court of 
Jharkhand in Commercial Appeal No. 18 of 2023. The High Court had affirmed the 
decree dated 08.05.2023 passed by the Ld. Commercial Court, Dhanbad, directing 
BCCL to pay ₹46.20 crore along with interest at the rate of 10.5% per annum. 
 
The Company has been advised by its conducting counsel to file a Review Petition 
against the aforesaid Order. 
 
Further developments in the matter, as applicable, shall be intimated in accordance with 
the applicable regulatory requirements. 
 
This is for your information and record, please.
