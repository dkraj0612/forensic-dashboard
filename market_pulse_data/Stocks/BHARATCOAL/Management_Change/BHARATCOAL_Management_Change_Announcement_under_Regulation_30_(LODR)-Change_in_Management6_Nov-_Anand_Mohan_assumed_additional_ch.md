Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements), Regulations 2015- Change in Director 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations 2015, it is hereby informed that Shri 
Anand Mohan has assumed the additional charge of the post of Director (Technical) Project & 
Planning, Bharat Coking Coal Limited w.e.f. 06.11.2026 consequent to the approval accorded 
by Hon’ble Minister of Coal, Government of India and subsequent order by Coal India Limited. 
Accordingly, Shri Anand Mohan becomes a Director & Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) of 
Bharat Coking Coal Limited. 
 
Brief Profile of Shri Anand Mohan is as under: 
 
Shri Anand Mohan is holding the post of Director (Technical) of CMPDIL, a subsidiary of 
Coal India Limited. He holds an M. Tech in Open Cast Mining from the Indian School of 
Mines, Dhanbad, and a B. Tech in Mining Engineering from the Karnataka Regional 
Engineering College, Surathkal. He is also a holder of the First-Class Mines Manager's 
Certificate of Competency (DGMS), a testament to his technical proficiency and statutory 
expertise. In recognition of his outstanding performance and exemplary leadership, he has been 
conferred several prestigious honours, including the Coal Minister's Award and the Best Area 
General Manager/Individual Excellence Award. 
  
Shri Anand Mohan has over 36 years of experience in Coal India Limited subsidiaries (NCL, 
CCL and ECL). His extensive career encompasses expertise in mine planning, operations, 
project management, rehabilitation, environmental management, and digital transformation 
initiatives.
