Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements), Regulations 2015 - Outcome of Board Meeting dated 13.10.2026 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI 
(LODR) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of Bharat 
Coking Coal Limited, at its meeting held on 13th Oct, 2026, approved the 
Rationalisation of Basic Prices of certain grades of Raw Coking Coal for Non-
Regulated Sector.   
With a view to correct disproportionate inter-grade price differentials, the revision 
covers Steel Grades (I and II), Direct Feed, PCC Washery Grades (W-II to W-VI) and 
HVMC Washery Grades (W-I to W-VI) for Non-Regulated Sector only. The basic 
prices of different grades have been increased by approximately 0.1% to 14% over the 
existing prices. 
The Board meeting commenced at 17:30 Hrs and concluded at 20:05 Hrs.
