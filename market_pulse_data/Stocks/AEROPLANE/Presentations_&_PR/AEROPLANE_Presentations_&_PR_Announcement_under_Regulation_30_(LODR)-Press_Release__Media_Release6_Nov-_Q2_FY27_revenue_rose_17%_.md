About Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Ltd.
Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Ltd, established in 2003, is one of India’s leading producers and exporters 
of basmati rice. Operating under the flagship brand Aeroplane Rice, the company also markets a diverse portfolio 
of rice varieties through other in-house brands such as La-Taste, Alibaba, World Cup, Hani, Palm Tree, Budallah, 
Sophia, and Al Jazira.
The company ranks 3rd among the peer set in terms of revenue, it is also among the few Indian branded rice 
players that have ventured into FMCG staples. Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Ltd has the 3rd largest 
installed production capacity as compared to peers.

Commenting on the Results Mr. Jagdish Kumar Suri, Chairman & Managing Director of Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar 
(Exports) Ltd. said, - 
“We are pleased to report a strong performance in Q2 FY27, with the Company continuing its growth momentum across key financial 
parameters. Consolidated revenue grew by 17% YoY to INR 693.9 crore during the quarter, while H1 FY27 revenue increased by 32.9% YoY 
to INR 1,357.7 crore, reflecting sustained business momentum and continued demand across our markets.
Our operational performance remained strong, with EBITDA growing by 20.4% YoY to INR 73.9 crore in Q2 FY27. For H1 FY27, EBITDA 
stood at INR 135.8 crore, registering a growth of 28.4% YoY. PAT grew by 26.2% YoY to INR 41.1 crore during the quarter, while H1 FY27 
PAT stood at INR 77.7 crore, registering a growth of 59.7% YoY. This reflects our continued focus on operational efficiency, cost 
management and improving profitability.
During the quarter, we strengthened our international presence with the incorporation of Amir Chand Trading Company, our wholly-
owned subsidiary in Saudi Arabia. The subsidiary will focus on trading and distribution of rice and FMCG products, including import and 
export activities, enabling us to establish a direct presence in the Saudi market. 
We also continued our focus on brand building and consumer engagement through the appointment of Rashmika Mandanna as brand 
ambassador along side Fatima Sana Sheikh.
Looking ahead, we remain focused on strengthening our distribution network, expanding our international presence and building our 
brands across markets. We will continue to work towards improving our domestic and international market penetration, widening our 
consumer reach and strengthening our position in the rice and FMCG segments. With continued investments in brand building, 
operational efficiencies and market expansion, we remain committed to sustaining our growth momentum and creating long-term value 
for our stakeholders.”
