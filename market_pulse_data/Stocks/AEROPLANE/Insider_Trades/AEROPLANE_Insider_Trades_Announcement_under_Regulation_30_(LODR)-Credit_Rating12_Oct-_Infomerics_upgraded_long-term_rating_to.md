Rationale 
Infomerics Ratings has upgraded the long-term rating assigned to the bank facilities of Amir 
Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Limited (ACJKEL) to IVR A+/Stable from IVR A/Positive and 
reaffirmed the short-term rating at IVR A1.  
The upgrade in the long-term rating reflects ACJKEL’s growth in operating scale and profitability 
during FY26, significant strengthening of the capital structure following ACJKEL’s initial public 
offering (IPO), and improvement in debt protection metrics. The ratings continue to derive 
strength from the promoters’ extensive experience in the rice industry, established market 
presence under the Aeroplane brand, widespread distribution network and healthy export order 
visibility.  
The rating strengths constrained by the working capital-intensive nature of basmati rice milling 
and processing operations, exposure to changes in paddy prices and crop availability, regulatory 
risks associated with agricultural trade, and intense competition in the fragmented rice industry. 
 
Outlook: Stable 
The Stable outlook reflects Infomerics’ expectation that Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) 
Limited will sustain its operating scale and financial profile over the medium term, supported by 
its established position in the basmati rice industry, integrated operations, experienced 
promoters and favourable demand propsects.

Key Rating Drivers with Detailed Description 
Strengths 
Growth in operating scale and profitability 
Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Limited’s total operating income grew by 14.3% to Rs 
2,287.13 crore in FY26, supported by an approximately 21% increase in sales volume in its 
key product category, despite a roughly 9% decline in average realisation. EBITDA margin 
improved to 9.85% from 8.18%, while PAT rose to Rs 103.29 crore from Rs 60.85 crore. 
In Q1 FY27, operating income grew by approximately 55% year on year on higher volumes. 
EBITDA margin moderated to 9.30% from 10.40%, although PAT increased by approximately 
127%, aided by lower interest costs 
 
Improved financial risk profile 
The financial risk profile strengthened significantly following the IPO completed in March 
2026. Net worth increased to Rs 922.86 crore as at March 31, 2026, from Rs 377.91 crore a 
year earlier, primarily due to fresh equity raised through the IPO and retained profits. 
Consequently, overall gearing improved to 0.82 times from 2.07 times, while TOL/ATNW

Established distribution network and location advantage 
The company enjoys an established market presence under its flagship Aeroplane brand and

Healthy export order book 
The company has an export order book of Rs806.24 crore for the supply of 1,11,592 MT of

Experienced promoters 
The Suri family has longstanding experience in the rice industry. Mr. J. K. Suri, Chairman 
and Managing Director, has more than five decades of experience in the processing, 
Marketing and trading of food grains. The promoters’ experience supports the company’s 
procurement, customer relationships and overall operations. 
 
   Weaknesses 
        Working capital intensive operations 
The company’s operations remain working capital intensive due to seasonal paddy 
procurement and ageing requirements for basmati rice. Its operating cycle was 227 days in 
FY26 (audited), compared with 223 days in FY25. Paddy is procured largely during the 
September–January harvest season, while basmati rice is typically aged for 3–24 months 
before sale. Consequently, average inventory holding remained elevated at 174 days in FY26, 
compared with 171 days in FY25. The business continues to require substantial working 
capital to support inventory and receivable levels.

     Exposure to agro-climatic and regulatory risks 
The availability and cost of paddy depend on factors such as rainfall and crop yields. Changes 
in government policies on procurement, minimum support prices and exports, as well as trade 
regulations in destination markets, could affect the company’s costs, sales volumes and 
profitability. 
 
     Intense competition in a fragmented industry 
The basmati rice industry includes established brands and numerous regional participants. 
Competition may limit the company’s ability to pass increases in paddy and other input costs 
on to customers, putting pressure on margins. 
 
         Liquidity – Adequate 
The liquidity profile is supported by satisfactory cash flow from operations and low scheduled 
debt repayment obligation during FY27-FY29. In FY26, net cash accrual was ~Rs.109.74 crore 
against its debt repayment obligation of Rs.0.46 crore in FY26. Liquidity is further supported 
by moderate working capital utilisation, which has historically remained at ~85% of the 
sanctioned limit, indicating some cushion to support near term increase in scale of operations. 
The company’s liquidity profile as of June 2026 appears strong, supported by bank balances of 
Rs. 95.91 crore, along with cash holdings of Rs. 0.08 Crore and Mutual fund investment of Rs. 
0.93 Crore.

About the Company 
Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Limited, incorporated in 2003, is an integrated 
processor, marketer and exporter of basmati rice and other food products. The company 
operates across the entire basmati rice value chain, including procurement, storage, processing, 
packaging, marketing and distribution. Its rice portfolio comprises basmati rice as well as 
specialty varieties such as Kolam, Sona Masuri, Idli and Ponni rice, catering to premium, 
medium, value and HORECA segments.  
The company has also diversified into FMCG staples, including atta, maida, sooji, besan, salt, 
sugar and other kitchen essentials. Its products are marketed under the flagship “Aeroplane” 
brand and various sub-brands including Aeroplane La-Taste, Aeroplane Classic, Ali Baba, 
World Cup and Jet.
