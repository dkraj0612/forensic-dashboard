### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Alembic Limited's Q1 FY2027 consolidated results report a 5.3% year-over-year increase in Net Profit (including associate share) to Rs. 6,489 Lakhs, significantly bolstered by a 12% rise in the share of associate's profit. However, core operational performance shows weakness, with revenue from operations declining 2% YoY and 19% QoQ, mainly due to decreased Real Estate activity and a YoY dip in the API business. This indicates a material reliance on associate contributions and other income to offset declining segment revenues and rising operational expenses, which could be a red flag regarding the company's standalone profitability and growth sustainability.
