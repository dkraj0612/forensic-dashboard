### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> The Anup Engineering Limited reported a mixed H1 FY27, demonstrating a strong sequential recovery in Q2 with revenue up 46% QoQ and significantly improved profitability, yet experiencing substantial year-over-year declines in both revenue and PAT (down 88%). This material impact was driven by elevated raw material costs due to geopolitical tensions and under-absorption of fixed costs from lower volumes. While a healthy ₹941 crore order book and strategic expansion into thermal power and clean energy sectors provide future growth visibility, a potential red flag is the balance sheet showing net short-term borrowings, contradicting a "net debt free company" claim in the annexures.

OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
HIGHLIGHTS FOR Q2 & H1 FY27
•
Consistent with our commentary during first quarter call, we delivered a strong sequential recovery, with revenue in Q2 growing 46% QoQ and 
improved EBITDA margins.
•
Revenue and EBITDA stood at ₹308 crore and ₹27 crore, respectively.
•
Geopolitical uncertainties led to elevated raw material costs, creating additional pressure on margins during the quarter.
•
Despite these challenges, we remained disciplined in our execution and pricing approach, prioritizing profitability and value creation over 
short-term volume growth.
•
EBITDA margins were impacted by lower revenue levels and the under-absorption of fixed costs; however, the underlying business 
fundamentals remained resilient.
•
Our order book, including Letters of Intent (LOIs), remained robust at ₹941 crore as on date, providing strong revenue visibility.
•
We secured orders exceeding ₹150 crore for thermal power plants, marking our entry into the elite group of manufacturers capable of supplying 
critical heat exchangers for this sector, which is expected to witness significant growth in the coming years.
•
In line with our strategy to expand into high-value niche segments, we secured orders for three proprietary licensed products.
•
Technical and site services business has gained good traction, with 3 site execution in progress. Currently on track for a revenue of about 50 
crore this year from this profitable vertical.

OUTLOOK FOR FY27
• Healthy pending orderbook (including LOI) of ₹941 Cr as on date(of which ~₹390 Cr booked for FY28)
• Orderbook between Domestic (67%) and Exports (33%), suggesting pick up in domestic demand.
• Encouraging Order inquiry pipeline of ₹1200 Cr, including sector of Oil Gas, Fertilizer, Thermal power and Clean energy storage,
providing good opportunities in the coming month to secure our 20% growth plan for next year.
• With large Thermal Power projects on the cards, this segment is poised for a strong growth for at list 3 years. Our strong presence 
now provide good opportunity. 
• Good traction seen in the Clean energy storage project at specially in USA, this should be about 20% of our revenue next year.
• Considering the current global volatile business scenarios due to wars & geopolitics, the focus of the company this year will be more 
on Stabilization of current operations, better Execution, Consolidation and Risk mitigation.
• Continuous endeavor to add new critical and proprietary products to create more niche space. Expect to add 5 proprietor equipment 
to our product list this year making a even more strong entry into tier one vendor list.
