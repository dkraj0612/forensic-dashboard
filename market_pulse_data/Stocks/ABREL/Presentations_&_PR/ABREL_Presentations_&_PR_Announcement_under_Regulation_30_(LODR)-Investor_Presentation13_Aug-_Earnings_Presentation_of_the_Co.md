### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited reported strong Q1 FY27 collection growth of 31% YoY, strategically securing partnerships with IFC and Mitsubishi, and notably divesting its non-core pulp and paper business for ₹34.98 billion to fully concentrate on real estate. Despite this operational momentum and a robust project pipeline, the company recorded continuing losses and negative net cash flow from real estate activities for the quarter, driven by significant land and project development investments. The substantial divestment proceeds are critical to bolster capital for its ambitious expansion plans and enhance its financial position.
