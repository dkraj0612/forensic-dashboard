Birla Estates Expands Pune Portfolio with Premium residential 
project in Hinjewadi with a revenue potential of ₹1,650 Crore  
New development marks the company's entry into Pune's high-velocity North-West growth cluster 
Mumbai /Pune, October 23, 2026: Birla Estates Pvt. Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Aditya Birla 
Real Estate and part of the Aditya Birla Group, today announced a new development in Hinjewadi, 
undertaken through a Joint Development Agreement (JDA). The project carries a total revenue potential of 
approximately ₹1,650 crores. The development spans a land parcel of 5.62 acres (approx. 22,737.27 sq. 
mts.) and will deliver a total saleable area of approximately 1.25 million sq. ft. The company plans to 
develop a premium residential homes along with a retail component.   
Located in Hinjewadi, one of Pune's most established IT and employment hubs, the project enjoys proximity 
to Rajiv Gandhi InfoTech Park and a large campus base of technology companies including Infosys, Wipro 
and Tata Technologies. It is served by the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and the Mumbai-Bengaluru Highway, 
and the upcoming Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar Metro is expected to further strengthen connectivity to central 
Pune. The neighbourhood is also well supported by social infrastructure, with Metro station at 500 mts. 
and Grand Highstreet Mall about 200 mts. away coupled with leading schools, colleges and hospitals at a 
short drive from the site. The project has been conceived with IGBC Gold certification considered at the 
concept design stage, in line with Birla Estates' broader sustainability commitments across its portfolio. 
Mr. KT Jithendran, MD & CEO Birla Estates said, "Pune continues to be a focus market for our growth 
plans. Hinjewadi is one of Pune's most active residential markets, with demand anchored by a large and 
growing professional workforce. This development strengthens our presence in Pune and reflects our focus 
on high-growth micro-markets. We intend to create a premium, thoughtfully planned community with an 
integrated retail offering” 
 
The project builds on Birla Estates’ presence in Pune, with Birla Punya in Central Pune and Birla Evam in 
Manjari, and reflects the company’s continued focus on high-growth residential markets across India.  
 
About Birla Estates - Birla Estates Private Limited, is a wholly owned subsidiary of Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited 
(formerly Century Textiles and Industries Limited) and part of the Aditya Birla Group. In a short span of time Birla 
Estates has established itself as a brand of choice in the real estate industry. Birla Estates develops premium residential 
housing in key markets. The company is developing land parcels both through outright purchases as well as asset light 
Joint Ventures apart from developing its own land parcels. In the long term, the company is focused on developing 
world class residential, commercial and mixed use properties and aims to be amongst the top real estate companies in 
India. The company’s focus is on differentiating through sustainability, execution excellence, customer centricity and 
thoughtful design. The company is headquartered at Mumbai and currently has regional offices in NCR, Bengaluru and 
Pune and also has a well- established commercial portfolio with 2 grade-A commercial buildings located in Worli, 
Mumbai with ~6 lakh square feet of leasable area.
