PRESS RELEASE 
 
BENARES HOTELS LIMITED (BHL) ANNOUNCES Q2 AND H1 FY 2026-2027 FINANCIAL RESULTS 
 
MUMBAI, OCTOBER 16, 2026: Benares Hotels Limited (BHL), a subsidiary of The Indian Hotels 
Company Limited (IHCL), reported its results for the second quarter and half year ending September 
30, 2026. 
FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 
Dr. Anant Narain Singh, Chairman, Benares Hotels Limited (BHL) said, “BHL for the second quarter 
reported a 29% growth in revenue at INR 32.8 crores, EBITDA of INR 11.5 crores with a healthy EBITDA 
margin of 35% and PAT of INR 6.6 crores. This double-digit revenue growth of 30% in the first half at 
INR 68 crores with an EBITDA of INR 25.3 crores and PAT of INR 14.8 crores is reflective of the market 
demand and resultant absorption of additional inventory at Taj Ganges. This performance will be 
surpassed in the seasonally strong quarters ahead.” 
 
Key Highlights: 
 
• 
IHCL’s iconic brand Taj is ranked as India’s Strongest Brand 2026 and World’s Strongest Luxury 
Hotel Brand 2026 as per Brand Finance-UK.   
• 
Taj Ganges and Taj Nadesar Palace hosted the FHRAI convention for 2026. 
• 
Taj Nadesar Palace is the proud recipient of the Michelin Golden Key for the second year. 
 
 
About Benares Hotels Limited  
Benares Hotels Limited (BHL) is a listed public limited company incorporated in 1971. The Company operates its 
hotels, viz. Taj Ganges and Taj Nadesar Palace in Varanasi and Ginger, Gondia in Maharashtra.
