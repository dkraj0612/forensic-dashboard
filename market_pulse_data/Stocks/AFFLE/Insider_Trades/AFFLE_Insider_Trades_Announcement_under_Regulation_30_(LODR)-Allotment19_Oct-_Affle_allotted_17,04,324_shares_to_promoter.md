Further to our intimation dated October 16, 2026, regarding receipt of funds from Affle Holdings Pte. Ltd., 
the Promoter Company (“allottee”), towards exercise of warrants, we would like to inform that the 
Company has allotted 17,04,324 (Seventeen Lakhs Four Thousand Three Hundred Twenty Four) equity 
shares of face value of Rs. 2/- (Rupees Two) each to the allottee. 
 
Consequently, the issued, subscribed and paid-up share capital of the Company stands increased to Rs. 
285,237,344 divided into 142,618,672 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each. 
 
The allotment of equity shares for the remaining USD 14.99 million (Equivalent to Rs. 1424.345 million) 
remitted by the Promoter Company shall be done subject to currency conversion from USD to INR. 
 
The Company is in the process of applying for the listing of the aforesaid shares with the stock exchanges 
i.e. NSE and BSE.
