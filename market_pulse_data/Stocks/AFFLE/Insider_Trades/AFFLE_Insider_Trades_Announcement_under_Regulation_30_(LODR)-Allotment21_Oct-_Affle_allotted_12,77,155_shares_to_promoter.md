Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Further to our intimation dated October 19, 2026, regarding allotment of 17,04,324 equity shares to Affle 
Holdings Pte. Ltd., the Promoter Company (“allottee”), we would like to inform that the Company has 
further allotted 12,77,155 (Twelve Lakhs Seventy-Seven Thousand One Hundred Fifty-Five) equity shares 
of face value of Rs. 2/- (Rupees Two) each to the allottee pursuant to exercise of warrants. 
 
With reference to our intimations dated August 14, 2026 and October 19, 2026, we further wish to inform 
that, as on date, 30,16,443 equity shares have been allotted pursuant to conversion of warrants out of the 
total 74,00,000 warrants allotted to the Promoter Company, against receipt of an aggregate amount of 
Rs.6,115.04 million out of the total amount of Rs.11,003.80 million including upfront amount of 25% and 
balance amount for exercise of warrants. 
 
Consequently, the issued, subscribed and paid-up share capital of the Company stands increased to Rs. 
287,791,654 divided into 143,895,827 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each. 
 
The Company is in the process of applying for the listing of these aforesaid shares with the stock exchanges 
i.e. NSE and BSE.
