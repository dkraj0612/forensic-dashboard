Combined business strengthens AI-powered consumer conversions, expands global scale, delivers

Gurugram, November 1, 2026: Affle 3i Limited (BSE: 542752 & NSE: AFFLE), a global technology 
company that delivers premium consumer conversions for advertisers with its verticalized AI-powered 
Consumer Platform, today announced that its Israel-based step-down subsidiary, Affle 3iL Ltd. 
(“Affle”), has signed a definitive agreement to acquire all outstanding shares (including unvested 
equity awards as per vesting schedule) of Perion Network Limited (“Perion”) (NASDAQ & TASE: PERI) 
at US$ 11.70 per share in an all cash transaction for an implied equity value of ~US$ 420 million.   
Affle has an established track record of acquiring and successfully transforming businesses, 
complementing its organic growth journey with strategic acquisitions. Our approach focuses on 
identifying businesses that complement our capabilities, expanding our access to customers and 
markets, and using our tech to seamlessly integrate their strengths into our broader platform 
ecosystem. By leveraging our AI-powered Consumer Platform Tech Stack, proprietary audience 
intelligence and differentiated cost per converted user (“CPCU”) business model, we have successfully 
transformed acquired businesses to unlock high value strategic outcomes and ROI for customers, 
expanded margins through tech and data synergies, and created long-term value for our shareholders 
with disciplined execution delivering over 20% annual organic growth and over 20% EBITDA 
consistently. 
Perion is an Israeli technology company headquartered in Tel Aviv, with a strong North American 
footprint and dual listings on NASDAQ and the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange. It is an AI-powered tech stack 
providing omnichannel advertising solutions across Retail Media, Connected TV (CTV), DOOH and other 
digital channels. Over the last three years, the company has undergone a significant transformation, 
completing three strategic acquisitions and unifying these expanding segments under its Perion One 
platform.

The transaction has been approved by the Boards of Directors of both Affle and Perion and is subject

Transaction Summary 
● 
Affle will acquire 100% of Perion’s issued and outstanding shares including unvested equity 
awards as per vesting schedule for an equity value of ~ US$ 420 million.  
● 
Perion does not have any financial debt. As on September 30th, 2026, it had a cash balance of 
~US$ 228 million. 
● 
As Perion is publicly held, the transaction costs and related expenses would be recorded by 
Affle in its opening balance sheet.  
● 
Affle plans to fund the acquisition through a combination of committed financing from banks 
and available cash resources. 
● 
The transaction will be effected by way of a reverse triangular merger, pursuant to which a 
wholly-owned subsidiary of Affle will merge with and into Perion, with Perion surviving the 
merger as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Affle. Upon closing, Perion’s ordinary shares will be 
delisted from Nasdaq and the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange. Perion will continue to operate under 
the Perion brand. 
● 
The transaction is expected to close in early 2027, subject to closing conditions, including 
regulatory and Perion’s shareholder approval. 
● 
Affle would be net debt free immediately post this acquisition and with a FY2028 revenue run 
rate of over US$ 700 Mn.  
Post acquisition, we expect to update Perion’s revenue recognition in view of the planned CPCU 
strategic transformation including review of business contracts leading to a significantly improved 
bottom line performance based on the immediate strategic synergies. We expect our complementary 
technologies, planned business model transformation and disciplined focus on profitability to drive 
sustainable growth to make this acquisition accretive to earnings per share (“EPS”) by the end of 1st 
year following closing.
