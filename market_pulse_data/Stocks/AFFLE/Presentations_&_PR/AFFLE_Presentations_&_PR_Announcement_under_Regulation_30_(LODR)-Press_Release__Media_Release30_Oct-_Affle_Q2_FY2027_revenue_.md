Press Release 
Affle reports robust performance for Q2 & H1 FY2027 
Q2 FY2027: Revenue growth of 21.2% y-o-y | EBITDA growth of 24.2% y-o-y 
H1 FY2027: Revenue growth of 20.8% y-o-y | EBITDA growth of 22.1% y-o-y 
October  30, 2026: Affle 3i Limited, a consumer intelligence driven global technology company, today 
announced results for the second quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Affle reported robust performance for Q2 FY2027 with a consolidated revenue from operations of INR 
783.6 crore, an increase of 21.2% y-o-y from revenue of INR 646.7 crore in Q2 last year. EBITDA stood 
at INR 181.4 crore, an increase of 24.2% y-o-y. EBITDA margin was at 23.2% in Q2 FY2027, an increase 
of 56 basis points on a y-o-y basis. PAT increased by 17.4% y-o-y to INR 129.8 crore from INR 110.5 
crore in Q2 last year. This growth was broad-based coming across India & International markets. 
In H1 FY2027, consolidated revenue from operations stood at INR 1,530.8 crore, an increase of 20.8% 
y-o-y. EBITDA was at INR 349.1 crore, an increase of 22.1% y-o-y and EBITDA margin stood at 22.8%, an 
increase of 25 basis points on a y-o-y basis. PAT increased by 19.5% y-o-y to INR 258.2 crore and PAT 
margin stood at 16.4%. 
The CPCU business noted strong momentum delivering 12.9 crore converted users in Q2 FY2027, an 
increase of 18.2% y-o-y and taking the total converted users delivered in H1 FY2027 to 25.3 crore. The 
CPCU Revenue stood at INR 781.9 crore in Q2 FY2027. The top industry verticals for the company 
continued their strong momentum, helping it register a robust growth anchored on the CPCU business 
model along with an operating margin expansion on both y-o-y and q-o-q basis.

About Affle 
Affle 3i Limited is a global technology company enabling verticalized AI-led consumer conversions for 
advertisers globally, powered by the differentiated CPCU business model across Mobile, CTV and other 
agentic/autonomous intelligent connected devices (AICDs).  
Affle 3i Consumer Platform enables advertisers to optimize targeting, personalize user engagement 
and maximize ROI by combining proprietary data, deep audience intelligence and advanced agentic AI 
capabilities. With innovative technology integrations, Affle maximizes user engagement at every stage 
of the consumer journey across connected devices with algorithms that go beyond automation and 
efficiency to deliver next-gen experiences through deep human-vs-non-human data distillation. Affle 
3i Limited is listed on the NSE (AFFLE) and BSE (542752).

Commenting on the results, Anuj Khanna Sohum, the Chairperson, MD & CEO of Affle said: 
“Affle continued its established growth trajectory in H1 FY2027. We achieved our 15th consecutive 
quarter of sequential topline growth, while registering our highest-ever quarterly revenue run-rate, 
EBITDA, PAT and consumer conversions. This performance reflects the strength of our differentiated 
CPCU business Model in sustaining ROI for advertisers globally, despite prevailing geopolitical 
tensions, and regulatory and macroeconomic headwinds. 
As we progress towards our vision of 10X, we continue to advance on our ambition of building a 
globally scaled, AI-powered full funnel Consumer Platform Stack that delivers measurable value for 
advertisers through premium consumer conversions. Our focus remains on strengthening our 
competitive differentiation, deepening our presence across Developed and Emerging Markets, and 
expanding the capabilities of our platform across mobile, Connected TV and other 
Agentic/Autonomous Intelligent Connected Devices.”
