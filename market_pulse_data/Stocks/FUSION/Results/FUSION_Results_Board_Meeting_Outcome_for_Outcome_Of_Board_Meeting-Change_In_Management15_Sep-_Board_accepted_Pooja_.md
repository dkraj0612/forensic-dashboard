Brief Profile of Ms. Chinmayi Trivedi: 
Ms. Chinmayi Trivedi is a seasoned Human Resources leader with over 20 years of experience across 
banking, housing finance, asset management, and financial services organizations. In her recent role, 
she served as Executive Vice President - Human Resources at Truhome Finance Ltd., where she was 
responsible for building and scaling the people function, driving talent acquisition, performance 
management, employee engagement, diversity & inclusion, digital HR transformation, and 
organizational effectiveness. 
Prior to joining Truhome Finance, she served as Head HR - Wholesale Finance Group at Piramal 
Capital & Housing Finance Ltd., where she led HR strategy, talent management, compensation & 
benefits, employee experience, digital transformation, and governance initiatives. Earlier, she held 
leadership roles at IDFC Ltd. and HDFC Bank Ltd., partnering closely with business leaders on 
organization design, performance management, employee engagement, culture transformation, and 
leadership development. 
Throughout her career, Ms. Trivedi has successfully led several large-scale HR transformation and 
digitalization initiatives, developed high-performance cultures, and implemented award-winning 
diversity, talent, and employee engagement programs. Under her leadership, organizations have 
received multiple recognitions, including Great Place to Work and HR excellence awards. 
Ms. Trivedi holds a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Management (HR) from N. L. Dalmia Institute 
of Management Studies & Research and a Bachelor of Commerce degree from Mithibai College of 
Commerce & Arts.
