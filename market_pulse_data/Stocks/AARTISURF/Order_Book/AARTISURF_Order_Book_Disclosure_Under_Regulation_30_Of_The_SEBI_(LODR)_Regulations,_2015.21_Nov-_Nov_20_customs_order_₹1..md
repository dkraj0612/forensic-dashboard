### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aarti Surfactants Limited has received a customs order demanding approximately Rs. 1.28 crore in duty, along with equivalent penalties and interest, related to alleged import classification irregularities predating its demerger. This order further directs the confiscation of goods worth Rs. 5.22 crore (with a redemption option) and enforcement of bonds, establishing a material and unquantified financial liability. The company is evaluating legal remedies, but the long-standing nature of this dispute and potential for significant financial outflow represent a notable compliance risk.

aforesaid Show Cause Notice were transferred to the Company, being the Resultant Company under the
