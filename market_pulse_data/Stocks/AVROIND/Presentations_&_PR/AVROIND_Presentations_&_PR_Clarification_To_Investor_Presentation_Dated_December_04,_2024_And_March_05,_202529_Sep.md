Pursuant to Reg. 30 read with Para A of Part A of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and with reference to Investor presentation dated December 04, 
2024 on financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2024 and Investor 
presentation dated March 05, 2025 on financial results for the quarter and nine months ended 
December 31, 2024, we would like to clarify you the following: 
 
1. The Management decided to start and commence recycling of rigid plastic which was informed 
to exchanges via announcement dated September 29, 2025. Currently, the plant has a capacity 
of 6000 MT per annum. 
 
2. The Combined capacity of flexible and rigid plastic is cumulatively 18000 MT per annum. 
 
3. Since the new capacity enhancement for flexible recycling business has not been undertaken, 
the given estimated revenue also changes due to the said reason. 
 
4. The volume of plastic furniture has been increased as compared to the financial year 2024-25, 
but due to intense competition from the local players, the desired revenue could not be 
achieved. The Company is working aggressively for new territorial expansion and is focusing 
on value added products for online sales and offline sales.  
 
You are requested to kindly take the same on record.
