Notes: 
As disclosed in our disclosure dated 20 December 2024: 
(a) PAGAC Ecstasy Pte. Ltd. (“PAG Ecstasy”) had entered into a facilities agreement dated 17 
December 2024 (the “Original Facility Agreement”) pursuant to which PAG Ecstasy had availed 
a loan facility from certain lenders (including their assigns, transferees, successors and novates 
from time to time) (the “Lenders”).  
(b) PAG Ecstasy II Pte. Ltd. (the “Parent”) had, pursuant to the terms of a borrower share pledge 
agreement dated 18 December 2024 executed by and between the Parent and DB Trustees (Hong 
Kong) Limited (the “Offshore Security Agent”) acting as an offshore security agent for the Lenders, 
created a pledge over all of the equity shares of PAG Ecstasy constituting 100% of the paid-up 
equity share capital of PAG Ecstasy held by the Parent, for the benefit of the Lenders. Additionally, 
the Parent had, by way of covenants under the Original Facility Agreement, agreed to restrictions 
on creating any security on or disposing of its assets (including the entire shareholding in PAG 
Ecstasy), other than as permitted under the Original Facility Agreement. 
No further pledge or encumbrances have been created over any additional equity shares of the TC held 
by PAG Ecstasy. However, the amounts secured by the aforementioned encumbrances are increasing 
from USD 265,000,000 to USD 450,000,000 pursuant to the terms of the Original Facility Agreement 
as amended by the amendment and restatement agreement dated 9 September 2026 executed by and 
between inter alia PAG Ecstasy as the borrower, the Lenders and the Offshore Security Agent and a 
supplemental borrower pledge agreement dated 9 September 2026 executed by and between the 
Parent and Offshore Security Agent. 
As on 9 September 2026, DBX Advisors LLC (a Deutsche Bank entity) independently holds 160 equity 
shares of the TC, aggregating to approximately 0.0001% (rounded off to 0.00% to two decimal places) 
shareholding in the TC.
