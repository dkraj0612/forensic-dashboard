Subject: Updates on the Project awarded by Diriyah Company, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, 
intimated on February 06, 2026  
 
In continuation to the announcement made by Ashoka Buildcon Limited (“the Company”) on 
February 06, 2026, about the receipt of Letter of Acceptance (LOA) from Diriyah Company, 
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, (“Employer”) by its wholly owned subsidiary in Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, 
(“KSA”) viz. ‘Ashoka Buildcon Limited for Contracting’ (“the SPV”) along with ‘BEC Arabia 
Contracting Co’. in Joint Venture for the Project Viz. ‘Main Construction Works of Diriyah II – 
Delivery Partner for One Hotel’ (“Project”) at accepted Project Price of SAR 351,372,308.67 (excl. 
VAT) Rs.846.38 Crore, a SPV’s share of work.   
 
However, the Employer has now notified to the Joint Venture that the said Project has been 
cancelled due to internal reasons attributable to the Employer. 
 
The Company is in the process of approaching the Employer to understand the reason in better 
detail and the next course of action.  
 
This disclosure is pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
