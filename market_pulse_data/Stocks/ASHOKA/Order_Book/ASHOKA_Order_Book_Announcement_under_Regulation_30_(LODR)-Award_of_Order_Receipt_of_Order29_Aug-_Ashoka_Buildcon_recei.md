Subject: Intimation for Receipt of Letter of Acceptance (LOA) from Rail Vikas Nigam Limited (RVNL)  
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, Ashoka Buildcon Limited (“the Company”) informs that the Company had submitted 
bid, for the following Project to Rail Vikas Nigam Limited (RVNL). 
 
Package-3: Supply, Erection, Testing & Commissioning of Electro Mechanical (E&M) System comprising 
of 33/11kv & 11/0.433kv GIS Substations, HT & LT Cables, DG Sets, Lighting, Ups System, Ventilation 
Systems & Fire Fighting Systems and other associated works for Tunnel T-13, T-14, T-15 & T-16 under 
package E-4 of Rishikesh Karnprayag New BG Rail Line Project in the State of Uttarakhand (“Project”) 
 
Further to apprise that the Company has received Letter of Acceptance for the said Project.  
 
The Trading Window for Designated Persons of the Company and their immediate relatives will remain 
closed till 48 hours after the disclosure is made to the Exchanges.
