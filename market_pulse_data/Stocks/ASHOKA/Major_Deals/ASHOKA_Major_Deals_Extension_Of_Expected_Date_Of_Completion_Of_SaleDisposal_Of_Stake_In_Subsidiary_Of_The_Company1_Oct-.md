Sub: Intimation regarding extension of expected date of completion of sale/disposal of stake in 
Subsidiary of the Company  
This is in continuation to the announcement made by the Company on December 31, 2024 informing 
about the Company and Ashoka Concessions Limited (“ACL”), a, material unlisted subsidiary of the 
Company, entering into Share Purchase Agreements and other transaction documents (“Transaction 
Documents”) with Epic Concesiones 2 Private Limited, Infrastructure Yield Plus II and Infrastructure 
Yield Plus IIA (both schemes of Infrastructure Yield Trust and managed by their respective 
investment manager, EAAA India Alternatives Limited (formerly known as Edelweiss Alternative 
Asset Advisors Limited)) (“Proposed Investor”) inter alia for sale of the entire share capital (including 
repayment of any shareholder loans) and management control of the SPVs held by the Company 
and/or ACL in the following subsidiaries: 
 
1) Ashoka Kharar Ludhiana Road Limited;  
2) Ashoka Khairatunda Barwa Adda Road Limited; 
3) Ashoka Ranastalam Anandapuram Road Limited;  
4) Ashoka Ankleshwar Manubar Expressway Private Limited;  
5) Ashoka Kandi Ramsanpalle Road Private Limited;  
6) Ashoka Mallasandra Karadi Road Private Limited; (TS-1) 
7) Ashoka Karadi Banwara Road Private Limited;  (TS-2) 
8) Ashoka Banwara Bettadahalli Road Private Limited; (TS-3) 
9) Ashoka Baswantpur Singnodi Road Private Limited (BS) 
10) Ashoka Belgaum Khanapur Road Private Limited; (BK) and  
11) Ashoka Bettadahalli Shivamogga Road Private Limited; (TS-4) 
 
collectively, the “SPVs”, 
 
Further to update that in continuation to the announcement made by the Company on September 
30, 2025, informing about the sale of entire stake held in SPVs from serial no. 1 to 5 from above list 
and in continuation to the further announcements made by the Company on April 01, 2026, and 
June 30, 2026, regarding expected date of completion of sale of rest of 6 SPVs, respectively.  
 
We further update that the Company and the Proposed Investor have mutually agreed to extend the 
indicative timelines for the completion of the sale of Ashoka Baswantpur Singnodi Road Private 
Limited (BS). The extension will allow for the individual completion of certain conditions precedent 
and other terms outlined in the transaction documents of BS.
