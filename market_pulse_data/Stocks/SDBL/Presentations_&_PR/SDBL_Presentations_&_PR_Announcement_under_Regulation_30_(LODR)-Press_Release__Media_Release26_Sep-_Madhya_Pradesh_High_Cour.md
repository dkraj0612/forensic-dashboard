SUBJECT:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) – Judgement 
passed by Hon'ble High Court of Madhya Pradesh. 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 ("SEBI LODR Regulations") read with Part B of Schedule III thereto, the SEBI Circulars relating to 
compliance with the SEBI LODR Regulations and the applicable Industry Standards on disclosure of 
material events, we hereby inform you that the Company has received the Judgement passed by the 
Hon'ble High Court of Madhya Pradesh in connection with a litigation involving the Company. 
 
Som Distilleries & Breweries Limited ("SDBL" or the "Company") is pleased to inform that the 
Hon'ble High Court of Madhya Pradesh, vide its judgment dated September 24, 2026, has quashed 
the order dated June 18, 2026 rejecting the Company's applications for renewal of its excise licences. 
 
The Hon'ble Court has directed renewal of all the Company's manufacturing licences under the 
applicable provisions of the Madhya Pradesh Excise Act within 15 days from the order date. The 
order marks an important step towards the resumption and normalization of the Company's 
operations in Madhya Pradesh.  
 
The Hon'ble High Court has also awarded costs of ₹1 lakh payable to us by the Government of 
Madhya Pradesh. 
 
The Hon'ble Court has also ordered and preserved the Company's right to pursue damages/losses 
arising from the period of operational closure before an appropriate forum. The judgment has 
thereby vindicated the Company's stand throughout the period and demonstrates that there was no 
compromise on our corporate governance, ethical standards or commitment to lawful conduct. The 
Company consistently acted to protect its legitimate interests and safeguard the interests of all its 
stakeholders.
