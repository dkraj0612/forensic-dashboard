Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 – Subscription of Shares of

Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that One Point One Solutions Limited 
("the Company" or "OPOSL") has subscribed to 74,480 equity shares of One Point One 
Technology Labs Private Limited ("OPO Tech Lab" or "the Subsidiary"), a subsidiary of the 
Company, at an issue price of INR 3,467.10/- per share (including a premium of INR 3,457.10/- 
per share), aggregating to INR 25,82,29,608/-. The shares have been issued by OPO Tech Lab to 
OPOSL on a private placement basis, for consideration other than cash, being extinguishment of 
the outstanding liability owed by the Subsidiary to the Company. Consequent to the aforesaid 
subscription of shares, OPOSL's holding rose by 2.75% in OPO Tech Lab, increasing from 6,05,999 
shares (60.00%) to 6,80,479 shares (62.75%). 
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, are enclosed as “Annexure I”. 
 
We request you to take the above information on your record.
