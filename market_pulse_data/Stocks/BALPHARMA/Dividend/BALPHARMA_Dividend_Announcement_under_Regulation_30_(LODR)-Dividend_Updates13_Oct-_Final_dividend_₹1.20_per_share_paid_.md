We wish to inform you that the Members of Bal Pharma Limited (“the Company”), at their 39th Annual 
General Meeting held on Thursday, September 24, 2026, approved a final dividend of ₹1.20 per equity 
share of ₹10 each for the financial year 2025–26. 
The Company has deposited the declared dividend amount into its designated bank account within the 
prescribed timeline and has completed the disbursement of the final dividend on October 9, 2026, to 
eligible shareholders whose names appeared in the Register of Members or the records of the 
Depositories as on the record date, i.e., September 17, 2026. The dividend was remitted through 
electronic modes, including NACH/NECS/direct bank remittance, wherever valid bank account details 
were available. 
Shareholders are requested to verify the credit of the final dividend in their respective bank accounts. 
They are also advised to check their Form 26AS/AIS or the Income Tax e-filing portal, as applicable, for 
details of tax deducted at source (“TDS”) on the dividend, wherever applicable. 
 
Further, the dividend amounts pertaining to shares already transferred to the Investor Education and 
Protection Fund (“IEPF”) account will be duly transferred to the prescribed IEPF account in accordance 
with the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, and the IEPF Rules. The details of such 
transfers will also be available on the Company's website for the information of the shareholders.

Withholding of Dividend: Shareholders Holding Shares in Physical Form 
The dividend payable to physical shareholders whose mandatory KYC details and/or bank account 
particulars are incomplete or have not been updated with the Registrar and Transfer Agent (“RTA”) has 
been withheld in accordance with the applicable SEBI circulars and guidelines.  
The dividend amounts that could not be disbursed have been deposited into the Company’s bank 
account designated for this purpose. The amounts remaining unpaid after the expiry of 30 days from the 
date of declaration of the dividend shall be transferred to the Company’s Unpaid Dividend Account 
within the statutory period, in accordance with Section 124 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the 
applicable rules. 
KYC updation and release of withheld dividend 
Physical shareholders are requested to update their KYC and bank account details by submitting the 
applicable forms and supporting documents to the Company's RTA, MUFG Intime India Private Limited 
(Unit: Bal Pharma Limited). 
The applicable forms are as follows: 
Form ISR-1 
For Updation KYC details ie PAN, Address, Bank Particulars, Mobile No. Email ID 
etc alongwith self attested Aadhar card, PAN card copy, Client Master List (CML) if 
having demat account. (If shares are in joint name, PAN/Aadhar are required for 
all joint shareholders). 
 
Form ISR-2 
For banker attestation of Signature along with Original Cancelled Cheque with 
your name(s) printed thereon or self-attested copy of bank passbook/statement. 
 
Form ISR-3 
For “ Opt-out the Nomination, if nomination is not required. 
 
Form SH-13 
For Updation of Nomination.

Withholding of Dividend: Shareholders Holding Shares in Dematerialised Form 
Shareholders holding shares in dematerialised form whose dividend could not be credited due to invalid 
bank account details or bank accounts that are blocked or closed are requested to update their bank 
account details with their respective Depository Participant (“DP”). 
Upon updating the bank account details, shareholders are advised to contact their DP and, where 
necessary, inform the Company and/or the RTA so that the updated particulars can be appropriately 
reflected in the relevant records and the dividend payment can be processed, subject to applicable 
procedures. 
The Company is taking necessary steps to facilitate the release of withheld dividend upon receipt and 
verification of the requisite KYC and bank account documents from the concerned shareholders.
