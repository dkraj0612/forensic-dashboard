## Page 4

Message from Vice – Chairman
4
"We are pleased to share our Q2 and H1 FY27 performance, reflecting steady progress across our business 
operations. For Q2 FY27, Total Income stood at Rs.358.4 crore, with EBITDA of Rs.36.5 crore at a margin of 
10.2%, and Profit After Tax of Rs.8.1 crore. For H1 FY27, Total Income reached Rs.680.8 crore, with EBITDA of 
Rs.66 crore at a margin of 9.7% and Profit After Tax of Rs.12.5 crore. 
The Yarn Division witnessed sequential improvement in domestic sales during Q2 FY27, supported by increased 
captive consumption within the Fabric Division. Despite elevated raw material and export freight costs and supply 
chain disruptions, seasonal domestic demand and anticipated export orders are expected to support performance 
in H2 FY27. 
The Fabric Division delivered a steady performance, with H1 FY27 results broadly in line with expectations despite 
relatively subdued business across certain export markets and the Simone business. Encouraging order visibility 
and capacity enhancement initiatives are expected to support H2 FY27, with a continued focus on strengthening 
order inflows and customer relationships.
In the Garment Division, sales momentum improved during Q2 following a softer start to the year, with exports 
contributing significantly to sales. Despite some dispatch delays due to logistical challenges, jacket orders 
remained strong. We also see potential opportunities to strengthen exports through the UK and EU Free Trade 
Agreements.
As we move into H2 FY27, our focus remains on strengthening business momentum, improving operational 
efficiency and enhancing customer relationships. Supported by encouraging order visibility, seasonal demand and 
improving capacity utilisation, and given the seasonally stronger nature of the second half, we remain confident 
in achieving our FY27 guidance.”
Commenting on the Results, Mr. Ravindra Kumar Toshniwal, Vice - Chairman said

Yarn Division
6
▪
The Yarn Division recorded revenue of Rs. 103 crore in Q2 FY27 and Rs. 199 crore in H1 FY27, with sales volumes of 37 lakh kg and 74 lakh kg, 
respectively. Capacity utilisation stood at 84% in Q2 FY27 and 77% for H1 FY27
▪
Domestic yarn sales improved sequentially during Q2 FY27, supported by outsourced processing of certain yarn varieties, while increased 
internal transfers to the Fabric Division reflected higher captive consumption for in-house fabric manufacturing
▪
Performance continued to be impacted by higher raw material and export freight costs, along with supply chain disruptions, which affected 
overall business operations
▪
Looking ahead, performance is expected to improve in H2 FY27, supported by seasonal and specialised products in the domestic market and 
anticipated orders from regular export customers
▪
The Company expects production output to improve in H2 FY27, supported by planned customer dispatches and better capacity utilisation. 
Inventory management efforts remain focused on reducing slow-moving yarn stocks while maintaining adequate inventory of regularly 
demanded products to support domestic sales

Garment Division
8
▪
The Garment Division recorded revenue of Rs.90 crore in Q2 FY27, up 13% YoY and 30% QoQ, with sales volumes of 10 lakh pieces and 
capacity utilisation of 78%. For H1 FY27, revenue stood at Rs.160 crore, compared to Rs.155 crore in H1 FY26, while sales volumes were 18 
lakh pieces and capacity utilisation stood at 74%, compared to 78% in the corresponding period last year
▪
Sales momentum improved during Q2 FY27 following a soft start to the year, with exports contributing significantly to overall sales. However, 
dispatches were impacted by vessel delays and logistical challenges, resulting in the deferment of certain customer shipments
▪
Jacket orders remained strong, with production running at healthy levels. The division entered H2 FY27 with strong business momentum and 
encouraging order visibility, supporting the outlook for improved sales performance
▪
The UK and EU Free Trade Agreements are expected to provide favourable tailwinds for export order inflows, creating opportunities to 
strengthen the division’s presence in both markets
▪
Looking ahead, strong order visibility, robust demand and improving export opportunities are expected to support sales momentum in H2
