Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we hereby inform you that a fire incident occurred around 10.05 A.M. on 30th October, 2026, in  
U Thermopac Machine, at – unit – BTM, Industrial Area, Dahod Road, Post Box No. 21, Banswara, 
Rajasthan – 327001. 
 
The cause of fire is currently under investigation. There were no casualties or injuries to any human 
life arising from this incident. We are currently assessing the full extent of the damage or loss caused 
due to fire and its impact on production, if any. 
 
Further, we would like to clarify that the Company has adequate insurance coverage in place and we 
are in the process of filing insurance claims to cover the damage and losses. The Company is taking all 
necessary and adequate steps to restore normal operations at the unit at the earliest. 
 
Any further development related to this incident will be intimated to the exchanges in due course.
