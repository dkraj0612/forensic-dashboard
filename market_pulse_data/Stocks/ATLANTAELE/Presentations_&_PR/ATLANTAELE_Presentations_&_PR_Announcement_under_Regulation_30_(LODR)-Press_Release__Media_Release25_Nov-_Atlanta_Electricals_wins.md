MEDIA RELEASE 
Atlanta Electricals strengthens order book with Rs 523.21 crore 
APTRANSCO contract for 4,750 MVA transformer capacity 
Company wins order for supply of 95 units of 50 MVA, 132/33 kV Power Transformers 
Anand (Gujarat), November 25, 2026: Atlanta Electricals Limited (NSE, BSE: ATLANTAELE), a 
leading manufacturer of power transformers, has secured an order worth approximately Rs 523.21 
crore from Transmission Corporation of Andhra Pradesh Limited (APTRANSCO) for the design, 
manufacture, testing and supply of power transformers. 
 
As per the Letter of Intent received from APTRANSCO, the order comprises the supply of 95 units of 
50 MVA, 132/33 kV Power Transformers, aggregating 4,750 MVA of transformation capacity, 
including 50 units under Lot-1 and 45 units under Lot-2, with an aggregate contract value of 
approximately Rs 523.21 crore, net of GST. 
 
This order builds on Atlanta Electricals' existing engagement with APTRANSCO, following the 
Company's earlier order for the supply of transformers aggregating 3,120 MVA, valued at 
approximately Rs 270 crore, further strengthening its relationship with one of India's leading state 
transmission utilities. 
 
"India's power sector continues to witness sustained investments in transmission infrastructure, 
driven by rising electricity demand.  Utilities across the country are strengthening their 
transmission networks to ensure reliable and efficient power evacuation, creating significant 
opportunities for technologically advanced transformer solutions. This order from APTRANSCO 
reflects our longstanding partnership and reinforces Atlanta Electricals' commitment to supporting 
the development of resilient and future-ready transmission networks. We remain focused on 
delivering high-quality products and execution excellence as utilities undertake capacity expansion 
and network strengthening projects across the country," said Mr. Niral Patel, Chairman and 
Managing Director, Atlanta Electricals Limited. 
 
The order has been awarded under Tender Specification No. PMM23-E-68/2026 and covers the 
design, manufacture, testing and supply of the transformers on a free-at-destination basis, 
including unloading, in accordance with the technical specifications and commercial terms 
stipulated by APTRANSCO. Deliveries are scheduled to commence within eight months from the 
date of the Letter of Intent, with subsequent supplies to be executed in a phased manner. 
 
This order further strengthens Atlanta Electricals' presence in the domestic power transmission 
segment and reinforces its capability to execute large-scale transformer projects for leading 
transmission utilities. The addition of this order provides strong revenue visibility and reflecting the 
growing demand for transmission infrastructure investments across India. 
 
About Atlanta Electricals Limited 
 
Atlanta Electricals Limited manufactures and supplies a wide range of power transformers starting 
from 5 MVA/11 kV up to 500 MVA/765 kV, as well as auto transformers, inverter duty transformers, 
furnace transformers, generator transformers and special duty transformers in India, across its 
manufacturing facilities in Gujarat and Karnataka.
