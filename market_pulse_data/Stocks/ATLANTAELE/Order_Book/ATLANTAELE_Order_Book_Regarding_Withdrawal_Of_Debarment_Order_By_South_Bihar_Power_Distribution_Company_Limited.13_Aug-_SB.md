Further to our disclosure dated 14th May, 2026 under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, regarding receipt of a Debarment Order dated 24.04.2026 from 
South Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited (“SBPDCL”) in relation to Purchase Order No. 58 dated 
12.10.2023, we wish to inform that the said Debarment Order has now been withdrawn. 
SBPDCL has, vide its Letter No. 1100 dated 13.08.2026, informed the Company that the Debarment Order 
issued vide Letter No. 516 dated 24.04.2026 has been withdrawn with immediate effect, pursuant to the 
Company’s appeal and detailed submissions before the competent authority. 
As stated in our earlier disclosure, the Company had submitted its representation and observations before 
the authorities concerned, clarifying that the matter pertained to a single isolated observation during post- 
test inspection at CPRI, Bhopal, and did not involve any established design failure or systemic quality issue. 
SBPDCL, having considered the Company’s appeal, submissions and the facts and circumstances of the 
matter, has decided to withdraw the Debarment Order in its entirety. 
This disclosure is being made in the interest of transparency, good corporate governance and adherence to 
compliance requirements.
