Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that Atlanta Electricals Limited (“the Company”) has 
received a Letter of Intent from Transmission Corporation of Andhra Pradesh Limited 
(APTRANSCO) for the design, manufacture, testing and supply of 12 Nos. of 160 MVA, 
220/132 kV Auto Transformers, against Tender Specification No. PMM21-31/2026 (Lot-2). 
The total contract value of the order is Rs 193.92 crore (including GST). 
The order comprises the supply of 12 units of 160 MVA, 220/132 kV Auto Transformers, with 
the scope covering design, manufacture, testing and supply. 
The Company is pleased to receive this order from APTRANSCO and will execute the same 
in accordance with the technical specifications, commercial terms and delivery schedule 
stipulated under the tender. 
We request you to take the above on record and disseminate the information.
