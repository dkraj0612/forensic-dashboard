Niral Krupeshbhai Patel
Chairman and Managing Director
"We have delivered a resilient performance during Q2 FY27 despite a challenging operating environment. Revenue from 
operations grew 23.1% year-on-year to ₹390.1 crore, while EBITDA increased 8.8% to ₹59.6 crore and PAT grew 28.0% to 
₹32.1 crore. For H1 FY27, revenue grew 35.5% and PAT increased 40.4% year-on-year, reflecting the underlying strength of 
our business and sustained demand across key end markets.
During the quarter, our operating performance was impacted by elevated commodity prices and unusually heavy rainfall, 
which temporarily disrupted manufacturing operations and delayed customer deliveries. While higher input costs affected 
margins, particularly on certain firm-price contracts, these challenges are largely temporary in nature. With operations 
having normalised and customer dispatches resuming, we expect execution momentum to improve in the second half of the 
financial year.
Our order book remained healthy at ₹3,196.97 crore as of September 2026, providing strong revenue visibility. We also 
made encouraging progress in expanding our international presence by securing our first order from the United States, 
marking an important milestone in our export strategy. Additionally, our continued investments in higher-voltage 
transformer capabilities, including the development of 400 kV and 765 kV products, along with the upcoming 
commissioning of our dedicated Inverter Duty Transformer facility, are expected to strengthen our competitive positioning 
and expand our addressable market.
Looking ahead, we remain confident in the structural growth opportunities across India's power transmission and 
distribution sector, supported by investments in renewable energy integration, battery energy storage systems and grid 
infrastructure. Our immediate priorities remain accelerating execution of deferred deliveries, improving operational 
efficiencies, advancing our backward integration initiatives and strengthening our presence in domestic and international 
markets. With a robust order pipeline and expanding manufacturing capabilities, we remain well-positioned to capitalise on 
these opportunities and deliver sustainable long-term growth.”
