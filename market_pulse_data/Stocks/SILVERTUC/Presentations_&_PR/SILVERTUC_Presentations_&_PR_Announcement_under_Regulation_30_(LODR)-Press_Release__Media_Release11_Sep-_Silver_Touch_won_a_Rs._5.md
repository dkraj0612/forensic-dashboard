Business Update Release 
Business Update – Silver Touch Technologies Strengthens Digital Infrastructure Footprint 
in the Power & Energy Sector 
The Company has been engaged by Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL) — the Government of Gujarat's 
holding company overseeing generation, transmission and distribution of power across Gujarat under the 
state's Energy and Petrochemicals Department — to provide comprehensive management and OEM-
authorised support of the core IT infrastructure underpinning GUVNL's enterprise systems. This engagement 
covers the computer servers, storage Systems, SAN-Switches, Tape Library and backup architecture with 
Silver Touch committed to ensuring application uptime services at a 99% SLA that empower GUVNL's e-Urja 
platform, the ERP backbone connecting the state's electricity generation, transmission and distribution 
utilities and serving over a crore consumer. This reaffirms Silver Touch's role as a trusted technology partner, 
keeping Gujarat's critical government infrastructure running reliably, around the clock. 
Strategic significance 
Power and energy utilities sit at the heart of national critical infrastructure — their IT backbones must run 
continuously, securely and without compromise, as any disruption carries consequences far beyond the 
enterprise itself. This engagement positions Silver Touch Technologies as a trusted partner safeguarding that 
continuity for one of Gujarat's most vital public utilities, and reflects three areas of deliberate strategic focus 
for the Company: 
● Deepening presence in Power & Energy: extending the Company's engagement with Gujarat's 
power sector ecosystem beyond project delivery into sustained, mission-critical operations support. 
● Critical infrastructure reliability: delivering the always-on, high-availability infrastructure 
management that utilities, government and other essential-services clients increasingly demand as 
they modernise legacy environments. 
● Digital Transformation of public sector enterprises: reinforcing Silver Touch's role in enabling large 
public utilities to run resilient, future-ready digital cores as they scale citizen and enterprise services. 
This engagement builds on the Company's long-standing track record in e-Governance and Government/PSU 
technology delivery, and reflects its continued strategy of expanding into infrastructure management and 
managed services for sectors where uptime and reliability are non-negotiable.
