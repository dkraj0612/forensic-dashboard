Garware Hi-Tech Films and Lubrizol sign MoU to collaborate on 
advanced TPU-based film solutions 
The collaboration will apply Lubrizol’s TPU technology and Garware’s 
manufacturing excellence to localize Premium PPF and new specialty TPU film 
applications 
Mumbai, 23-09-2026: Garware Hi-Tech Films Limited (GHFL), one of India’s leading specialty films 
manufacturers, and Lubrizol, a global leader in specialty chemicals, today announced the signing 
of a Memorandum of Understanding to collaborate on the development and manufacturing of 
Lubrizol’s world-class Thermoplastic Polyurethane (TPU) technology in India. 
The partnership marks an important step in GHFL’s strategy to build a technology-led TPU product 
platform across new specialty applications, while also enabling backward integration into TPU 
films for its premium Paint Protection Film (PPF) business. 
Advancing TPU-Based Film Solutions 
The collaboration will combine Lubrizol’s global TPU technology and material science expertise 
with GHFL’s engineering, processing and manufacturing capabilities to develop and 
commercialize new, high-value TPU-based products in India. 
It will also create opportunities across automotive, architectural, industrial, electronics and 
other high-value specialty applications, expanding GHFL’s addressable market and enabling the 
company to participate in new technology-driven product categories. 
This dedicated TPU film extrusion capability is expected to accelerate product development, 
prototyping and commercialization, while enabling GHFL to respond faster to evolving customer 
requirements. Importantly, nearly 25% of GHFL’s approximately ₹118 crore investment in the 
platform is expected to be allocated toward new product development and technology 
capabilities, highlighting the strategic focus on building future products and applications. 
Strengthening Backward Integration in Premium PPF 
The collaboration will also enable GHFL to backward integrate a critical input for its premium 
PPF business. The facility is expected to be India’s first dedicated TPU extrusion platform for 
premium PPF and will support the company’s expansion of PPF manufacturing capacity to over 
600 LSF.

In-house TPU extrusion is expected to provide greater control over a critical input, improve 
product consistency, manufacturing efficiency and supply-chain resilience, and reduce 
dependence on imported TPU films. Closer integration between TPU processing and PPF 
manufacturing is also expected to support faster product development and commercialization. 
The initiative is expected to strengthen the economics of the PPF business and support an 
incremental improvement in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps on a consolidated level. 
The premium PPF market continues to benefit from rising premium vehicle ownership, increasing 
consumer awareness and the expansion of automotive detailing and customization networks. 
The initiative supports the Government of India’s "Make in India" objective by developing 
advanced material processing and specialty film manufacturing capabilities within the country. 
Commenting on the development, Ms. Monika Garware, Joint Managing Director & Vice 
Chairperson, GHFL, said: “This association marks a significant milestone in GHFL’s journey 
towards building a globally competitive, technology-led specialty films business. This 
collaboration gives us the opportunity to develop new, high-value products across multiple 
specialty applications, while also strengthening our backward integration in PPF. By combining 
Lubrizol’s world-class TPU technology with GHFL’s engineering and manufacturing capabilities, we 
aim to accelerate innovation and build differentiated solutions for the Indian and global markets.” 
Commenting on the partnership, Mr. Deepak Joshi, Director - Sales & Marketing, GHFL, said: 
“GHFL’s ₹118 crore investment in advanced TPU film extrusion and manufacturing capabilities is 
expected to be operational by December 2026, together with the expansion of PPF manufacturing 
capacity to over 600 LSF, marks a significant step in strengthening our technology-driven, value-
added portfolio. This will enable us to develop new TPU-based products across PPF and other 
specialty applications, while providing greater backward integration for our PPF business. With 
nearly 25% of the investment directed towards new product development and technology 
capabilities, we see TPU as an important foundation for future innovation, faster 
commercialization and incremental EBITDA margin improvement in PPF.” 
Commenting on the partnership, Mr. Mittal Shah, Business Head - Engineered Polymers & 
Medical Devices IMEA, Lubrizol, said: “As customers increasingly seek PPF solutions that combine 
premium aesthetics with long-term protection, Lubrizol’s ESTANE® TPU technology helps enable 
the performance, durability and appearance retention required for next-generation PPF 
applications. We look forward to supporting Garware on its TPU extrusion setup for PPF in India 
leveraging our global TPU technologies to help deliver best-in-class solutions.” 
Commenting on the partnership, Mr. Abhishek Shrivastava, VP and Managing Director, Lubrizol 
IMEA, said: “India is emerging as a significant hub for advanced manufacturing, supported by 
growing domestic demand, strong industrial capabilities and an increasing focus on localization.
