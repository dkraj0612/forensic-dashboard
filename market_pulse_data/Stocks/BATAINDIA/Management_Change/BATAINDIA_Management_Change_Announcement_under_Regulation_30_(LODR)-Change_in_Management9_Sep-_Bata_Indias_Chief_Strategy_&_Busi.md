As discussed, and in line with my career aspirations, I am tendering my resignation from the position of

My tenure at Bata India has been deeply rewarding, both professionally and personally. I am grateful to 
have been part of the Company's transformation journey and to have contributed to several of its strategic 
agendas — modernising our retail operations, reimagining marketing for the new age with a strong digital 
focus, and driving end-to-end supply chain transformation. I leave with genuine pride in what we have built 
together, and with every confidence in the road ahead for the Company. 
I request that you kindly accept this resignation and relieve me from my duties by the end of working hours 
on 14th September 2026. 
I thank the management, my colleagues, and the entire Bata family for the support and collaboration 
extended to me over the years, and I wish the Company continued success in all its future endeavours.
