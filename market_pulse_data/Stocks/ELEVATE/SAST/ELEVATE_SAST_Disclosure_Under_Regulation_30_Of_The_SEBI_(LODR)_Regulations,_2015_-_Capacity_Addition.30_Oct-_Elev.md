Dear Sir/Madam,  
Subject: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”) – Capacity Addition 
This is to inform you that, at the meeting of the Investment Committee of the Board of 
Directors of Elevate Campuses Limited (“the Company” or “Elevate”) held on October 30 
2026, the Investment Committee has approved the execution of definitive documents 
pursuant to which GHS Sonipat II Private Limited (“GHS Sonipat II”), a wholly owned subsidiary 
of the Company, will acquire (i) the hostel accommodation undertaking of 2 (two) hostel 
blocks (SH12 and SH14) to extend provision of hostel accommodation services to students 
residing in the said 2 (two) hostel blocks (SH12 and SH14); and (ii) the other allied services 
undertaking of 3 (three) hostel blocks (SH11, SH12 and SH14), to extend other allied services, 
including mess, laundry and security services to students residing in 3 (three) hostel blocks 
(SH11, SH12 and SH14), located in the campus of O. P. Jindal Global University (“University”), 
Sonipat, Haryana  from the University (“Proposed Transaction”).  
The Company, had commenced provision of hostel accommodation and allied services in the 
campus from 2020 and has since then consistently endeavoured to deepen its engagement 
with the University. Specifically, GHS Sonipat II currently provides services in a hostel block (SH 
11) in the University campus, and the Proposed Transaction is intended to expand the scope 
of GHS Sonipat II’s services to the additional hostel blocks (SH12 and SH14). GHS Sonipat II 
also proposes to acquire the mess, laundry and security services presently being provided by 
the University to the students residing in hostel blocks SH 11, SH 12 and SH 14. Pursuant to 
the Proposed Transaction, Elevate would own and manage 10,500 beds at the University 
through its subsidiaries.  
The consummation of the Proposed Transaction is subject to completion of due diligence, 
execution of definitive documents, completion of conditions precedent, closing actions set 
out in the definitive documents, and the receipt of all necessary approvals as may be required.  
The Investment Committee meeting commenced at 11:05 a.m. (IST) and concluded at 12:00 
noon (IST).
