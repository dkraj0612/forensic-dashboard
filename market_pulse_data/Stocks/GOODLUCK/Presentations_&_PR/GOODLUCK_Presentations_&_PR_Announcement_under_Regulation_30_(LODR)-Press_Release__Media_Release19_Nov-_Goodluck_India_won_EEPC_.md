19th November, 2026 
Goodluck India Ltd has been honoured with the prestigious Golden Trophy, Top Exporter of the Year 2023-
24 (Large Enterprise Category) at the 56th Export Awards for Export Excellence, organised by the Engineering 
Export Promotion Council of India (EEPC India), Northern Region. The award was presented by the Hon'ble 
Chief Minister of Uttarakhand, adding further significance to this prestigious recognition. 
The award recognises Goodluck's outstanding contribution to India's engineering exports and its continued 
commitment to delivering world-class products, maintaining international quality standards, and 
strengthening India's presence in global markets. 
This prestigious recognition reflects the company's consistent focus on export excellence, operational 
capabilities, technological advancement, and its ability to meet the evolving requirements of customers 
across international markets. 
Receiving this distinguished honour from the Hon'ble Chief Minister of Uttarakhand is a proud milestone for 
Goodluck India Ltd and a testament to the dedication, hard work, and collective efforts of its employees, 
management, business partners, and stakeholders who have contributed to the company's export 
achievements. 
This recognition further strengthens Goodluck India Ltd's resolve to expand its international footprint, 
explore new global opportunities, and contribute meaningfully towards India's vision of becoming a globally 
competitive manufacturing and engineering powerhouse. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Mahesh Chandra Garg, Chairman, Goodluck India Limited, stated 
"We are deeply honoured to receive the Golden Trophy as the Top Exporter for 2023–24 in the Large 
Enterprise Category from EEPC India, Northern Region, presented by the Hon'ble Chief Minister of 
Uttarakhand. This recognition reinforces our commitment to engineering excellence, superior quality, 
innovation, and customer satisfaction. We remain dedicated to contributing to India's growing global stature 
as a trusted manufacturing and engineering export hub."
