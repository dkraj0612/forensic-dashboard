Gist of the proceedings of the 19th (Nineteenth) Annual General Meeting (AGM) of Bharat Road 
Network Limited held on September 30, 2026 
 
The 19th (Nineteenth) Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Company has been duly convened and 
held on Wednesday, September 30, 2026 at 2:30 P.M. (IST) through Video Conferencing (VC) in 
accordance with the guidelines stipulated by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and the Securities 
and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). 
 
Dr. (Ms.) Manta Dey was elected as the Chairperson to conduct the proceedings of the meeting and 
accordingly, she chaired the Meeting. The Meeting was attended by 74 (Seventy-Four) Members 
(including authorised representatives). Participation of Members through video conference was 
reckoned for the purpose of quorum as per the circulars issued by MCA and Section 103 of the 
Companies Act, 2013. 
 
The Chairperson informed the Members that the requisite quorum was present to conduct the 
proceedings of the Meeting and called the Meeting to order. The Quorum was present throughout the 
Meeting. 
 
The Meeting was attended by all the Directors. The Chief Executive Officer, Company Secretary, Chief 
Financial Officer, representatives of Statutory Auditors and the Secretarial Auditors were also present 
during the meeting. 
 
The Chairman of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee and Stakeholders Relationship 
Committee of the Company also attended the Meeting. All the members of the Audit Committee were 
present at the Meeting to answer members queries. 
 
The Chairperson and the Company Secretary informed the Members about the regulatory aspects 
pertaining to participation at the Meeting through VC. The Chairperson stated that the Company had 
taken necessary steps to ensure that the Members were able to attend and vote at the Meeting through 
electronic mode in a seamless manner. 
 
The Company Secretary informed that the statutory registers and other relevant documents, as 
mentioned in the Notice of the AGM, have been made available electronically for inspection by the 
Members during the AGM. Thereafter, the Chairperson delivered her speech by giving an overview of 
the financial and operational performance of the Company for the Financial Year ended on March 31, 
2026, the challenges faced by the Company and also briefed the members about future outlook and way 
ahead. 
 
She further informed that the Statutory Auditors of the Company have given Modified Opinion in their 
Auditor’s Report dated 30th May, 2026 on the Annual Standalone and Consolidated Financial 
Statements of the Company for the Financial Year ended March 31, 2026. Such qualification in the 
Annual Financial Statements was then read out at the Annual General Meeting and the attention of 
Members present was drawn to the explanations / comments given by the Board of Directors in their 
Report. 
 
She also stated about the qualification given by the Secretarial Auditor in the Secretarial Audit Report 
dated 14th August, 2026. The said qualification in the Secretarial Audit Report was then read out at the 
Annual General Meeting and the attention of Members present was drawn to the explanations / 
comments given by the Board of Directors in their Report. 
 
With the consent of the Members present, the AGM Notice, Report of Board of Directors, Auditors' 
Report and Secretarial Audit Report for the financial year ended on March 31, 2026 were taken as read. 
 
The Chairperson informed the Members that in compliance with the provisions of Section 108 of the 
Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 20 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 
2014 and the provisions of Regulation 44 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (hereinafter referred to as 'SEBI Listing
