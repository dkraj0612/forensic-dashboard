Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

been postponed. 
 
Reason for Postponement:  
 
The postponement is necessitated due to the continued illness of the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of 
the Company. Consequently, the financial results and related supporting documents could not be 
finalised, thereby rendering the Management unable to present the same before the Audit Committee 
and the Board of Directors for their consideration and approval. In view of the above, the Board Meeting 
has been postponed.

for dealing in the securities of the Company will remain closed from till 48 (Forty Eight) hours after 
the declaration of Financial Results (Standalone & Consolidated) for the Quarter and Half Year ended 
30th September, 2026.
