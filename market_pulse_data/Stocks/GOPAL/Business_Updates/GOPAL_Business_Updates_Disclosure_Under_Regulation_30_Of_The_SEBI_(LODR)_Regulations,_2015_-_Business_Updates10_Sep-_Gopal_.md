Gopal Snacks Strengthens Backward Integration with  
New Besan Manufacturing Unit at Nagpur 
 
Rajkot, 10th September 2026: Gopal Snacks Limited, (“Gopal Snacks Ltd” or the “Company”) (BSE: 
544140; NSE: GOPAL) a leading FMCG company in India renowned for its high-quality snack products, 
has commissioned a new Besan manufacturing unit at its existing Nagpur manufacturing facility in 
Maharashtra. The unit has an installed capacity of 19,642 MT per annum and is aimed at further 
strengthening the Company’s backward-integrated manufacturing operations. 
With this addition, the Company will reduce the transportation of Besan from its Rajkot manufacturing 
locations to Nagpur. This will lower transportation costs, simplify material movement and further 
integrate the Nagpur operations with Gopal Snacks’ in-house manufacturing model. 
As the capex is largely one-time in nature, the unit is expected to deliver recurring cost benefits over 
the coming years, improving the overall efficiency of the Nagpur operations. 
Over the years, Nagpur has developed into an critical manufacturing unit for Growth outside Gujarat. 
The facility manufactures multiple product categories, including Gathiya, Namkeen, Snack Pellets, 
Wafers, Extruded Snacks and Papad. The addition of Besan manufacturing further strengthens the 
facility by bringing the production of a key input closer to its manufacturing operations. 
 
Commenting on the performance Mr. Bipinbhai Hadvani, Chairman and MD said, 
“The Nagpur Besan unit reflects our strategy of investing in areas that deliver lasting operating 
benefits. By bringing production closer to consumption, we are reducing transportation costs and 
creating recurring savings from a one-time investment. These efficiencies will support our growth 
plans, strengthen our competitiveness in an Inflationary challenging environment. 
The record monthly sales performance reflects the Company's continued progress in restoring 
operations and strengthening its presence across key markets. With the Rajkot Main Plant 
operational, the Company is focused on building on this momentum, enhancing market penetration 
across existing geographies and increasing its share in the packaged snacks market through 
improved product availability and distribution reach.”

About Gopal Snacks 
Gopal Snacks Limited is a leading manufacturer of packaged snacks in India, offering a diverse portfolio that 
includes gathiya, namkeen, wafers, snack pellets, extruded snacks, and other products such as papad, besan, 
spices, and bakery items. With six manufacturing facilities supported by in-house cold storage and a network 
of ancillary units, the company applies a vertically integrated approach to ensure quality and agility in 
production. Its products reach consumers through an extensive distribution network of over 1,000 distributors 
and nearly 300 owned logistics vehicles, supported by advanced distribution management systems. Gopal 
Snacks exports to markets including Canada, the UAE, the UK, and the USA, expanding its global footprint. 
Strategic initiatives focus on strengthening partnerships with retail chains, advancing e-commerce channels, 
introducing innovative flavors, and deepening market penetration through marketing and branding initiatives. 
Committed to sustainability through renewable energy adoption and resource efficiency, Gopal Snacks aims 
to drive growth, enhance its product appeal, and capitalize on evolving consumer preferences in the growing 
packaged foods industry. For more details, please visit: https://www.gopalnamkeen.com/
