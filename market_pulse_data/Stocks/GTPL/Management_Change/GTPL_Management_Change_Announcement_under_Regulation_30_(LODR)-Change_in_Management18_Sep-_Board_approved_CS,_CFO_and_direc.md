Brief Profile of Mr. Ashwinkumar Patel is as under: 
 
Mr. Ashwinkumar Patel is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of 
India (ICSI). He has over 20 years of overall experience in secretarial and legal functions, 
corporate law compliance for listed and unlisted companies, securities law, corporate 
restructuring, due diligence, litigation, and legal case management, among other areas. He 
has served in various positions/roles at Electrotherm (India) Limited for approximately 
18 years. 
 
2) Appointment of Mr. Piyush Pankaj, currently serving as the Business Head – B2B (CATV 
& Broadband) and Chief Strategy Officer of the Company, as Chief Financial Officer (Key 
Managerial Personnel) of the Company with effect from October 1, 2026, in place of Mr. 
Saurav Banerjee who superannuates on September 30, 2026 (close of business hours).   
 
Brief Profile of Mr. Piyush Pankaj is as under: 
 
Mr. Piyush Pankaj has over 28 years of experience and has been associated with the 
Company for the last 10 years and has held various positions during this tenure, currently 
serving as Business Head – B2B (CATV & Broadband) and Chief Strategy Officer.  
 
Mr. Piyush Pankaj is a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) and holds an MBA in Finance 
& IT from the Institute of Chartered Financial Analysts of India (ICFAI).
