Airtel Business launches IQ Gateway – India’s first business communications platform 
with telco-grade network intelligence for safe enterprise messaging 

The DPDP-compliant unified platform combines Airtel’s industry leading fraud protection solutions
with network insights to make every customer conversation safer and more trusted

To enable enterprises to manage multiple service providers and customer communication channels
(SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, and business voice) on a single, sovereign control plane, reducing delivery
failures, spam risks, and regulatory challenges

Enterprises can now easily optimize their customer conversation channels (including fallback
options), ensure easy identity verifications and curb artificially inflated traffic costs
New Delhi (India), October 5, 2026: Airtel Business, India's leading provider of secure and reliable 
connectivity, technology, and communications solutions, today launched Airtel IQ Gateway, India’s first ever 
telco-grade network intelligence platform which brings different customer communication channels onto one 
safe control plane. 
Enterprises now won’t need to manage multiple vendors separately if they opt for Airtel IQ Gateway, a DPDP-
compliant platform which combines SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, voice communications and number verification 
(silent authentication) together in one place. It will enable enterprises to easily manage their business 
communications at scale, while protecting customer trust and making safety foundational to every customer 
interaction. 
This offering from Airtel Business is of critical importance today, when businesses have to manage fragmented 
customer communication stacks across multiple service providers and tools to reach their customers, with 
limited visibility into the real delivery of messages. Messages are often reported delivered even when they fail, 
while International OTP pumping by bots inflates bills. Moreover, managing pre-approved message 
templates is manual and often leads to mistakes. For enterprises, especially in sectors like BFSI, government, e-
commerce and logistics, this not only hurts customer trust when consumers are targeted by scams, this also 
damages their brand reputation. 
Airtel IQ Gateway solves this with a single control center for all business communications. It brings together 
Airtel’s fraud protection with automatic integrations of native message template system (DLT), automatic 
switching to back-up service providers, and intelligent routing across SMS, WhatsApp, RCS and voice.  
By leveraging Airtel’s built-in network intelligence and trust layers for enterprise use, such as IQ Verify for 
fraud protection and Silent Authentication for SIM based login, with capabilities like spotting fraudulent 
account creations and takeovers and artificial traffic spikes, Airtel IQ Gateway protects enterprises from fraud 
losses and billing spikes caused by industry-wide SMS pumping. 
Launched nationwide today after successful trial deployments across select banks, financial institutions and 
other relevant industry collaborations, the platform extends Airtel’s proven expertise in fraud prevention for end 
customers to enterprises, enabling them to build customer trust at scale through safe communications. 
Sharat Sinha, CEO – Airtel Business, said, “As India accelerates towards a safe, inclusive digital economy, 
trust must become the foundation of every digital interaction. Secure-by-design customer engagement is today 
the bedrock on which enterprises can scale with confidence. At Airtel, we have already demonstrated the power
