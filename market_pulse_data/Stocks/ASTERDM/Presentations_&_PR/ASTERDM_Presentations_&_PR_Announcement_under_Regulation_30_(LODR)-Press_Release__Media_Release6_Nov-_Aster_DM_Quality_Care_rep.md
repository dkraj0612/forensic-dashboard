Bengaluru, November 06, 2026: Aster DM Quality Care Limited, one of the leading integrated 
healthcare service providers in India, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended 
September 30, 2026. The quarter was the company’s first as a combined entity, and the company 
delivered a robust performance, while bringing together diverse teams, aligning them around a 
shared purpose, and navigating the organizational changes associated with integration. 
 
Commenting on the merger, Dr. Azad Moopen, Executive Chairman, said: 
“Completing our first 100 days as a combined entity is a milestone we are proud of. We began as one integrated 
platform on July 1, 2026, and the progress since then reflects the alignment of our leadership and the 
commitment of our clinicians and colleagues across the network. 
The entity delivered a strong performance in Q2 FY27, with revenue growing 19% to INR 2,829 crore and 
normalised PAT increasing 34% to INR 281 crore. Our emerging hospitals portfoilio (incl Kasargod & Nagarcoil) 
recorded an impressive 61% growth, highlighting the growing contribution and long-term potential of these 
units. 
Alongside our financial performance, we continued to strengthen our clinical capabilities and quality standards. 
CARE Hospitals, HITEC City, Hyderabad, received JCI accreditation, while MIMS Kasargod achieved NABH 
accreditation within a year of commencing operations. These recognitions reaffirm our commitment to clinical 
excellence, quality and patient safety. 
We are equally proud of our progress in Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) initiatives and Corporate 
Social Responsibility (CSR). Our recognition at the Global ESG Awards 2026, including awards for supporting 
economically weaker sections, renewable energy integration and CSR excellence, reflects our commitment to 
responsible and sustainable healthcare. Through Aster Volunteers and our various community initiatives, we 
remain focused on extending quality healthcare to underserved communities. 
Looking ahead, we remain focused on expanding our healthcare network, including progressing the Aster 
Trivandrum project towards commissioning, while continuing to invest in clinical talent, advanced infrastructure 
and technology. Our priority remains delivering better patient outcomes, strengthening operational excellence 
and creating sustainable long-term value for all stakeholders.”

Commenting on the performance for Q2 FY27, Mr. Varun Khanna, Managing Director & Group 
CEO, said:  
“Q2 FY27 marks our first full quarter as Aster DM Quality Care, following the successful merger of Aster DM 
Healthcare and Quality Care India. Building on the strong foundation laid in the previous quarter, I am delighted 
with the progress we have made as one organisation. Our teams have demonstrated tremendous commitment 
in bringing together our people, processes and systems, while maintaining an unwavering commitment to our 
patients, doctors and employees, ensuring seamless patient care and operational excellence.  
One of the most encouraging developments has been the way our people have embraced our shared culture. 
The foundation laid through GO GREATER, our unified culture initiative launched across nearly 40 locations, is 
now coming to life through the enthusiasm and active participation of our doctors, nurses and employees. It is 
heartening to see our teams coming together, learning from one another and embracing a shared sense of 
purpose. 
We are encouraged by the strong performance in our first quarter together and remain grateful for the trust 
that patients continue to place in us. During the quarter, we served 2.2 million patients, a 12% year-on-year 
growth and our occupancy improved by 335 bps to 68%, reflecting improved utilisation. We witnessed 
encouraging growth across complex clinical procedures, with robotic surgeries, kidney transplants and joint 
replacements growing by approximately 39%, 31% and 29%, respectively. Our Medical Value Travel (MVT) 
volume grew by 31% year-on-year, despite a challenging external environment, demonstrating the continued 
confidence of international patients in our doctors, clinical outcomes and healthcare capabilities. 
This quarter we made significant advances in clinical capabilities, with several complex and specialised 
procedures performed across our network. The exchange of clinical expertise and best practices across the 
combined platform enabled CARE Banjara, Hyderabad, perform its first adaptive Deep Brain Stimulation 
procedure, enabling personalised, real-time stimulation for Parkinson’s disease management. Aster Medcity, 
Kochi, performed Kerala’s first liquid nitrogen cryoablation for breast fibroadenomas, offering a minimally 
invasive, breast-tissue-preserving treatment option. We also strengthened our specialised care offerings with 
the launch of three Centres of Excellence: the ARISE Robotic Spine Institute at Aster CMI, Bengaluru; the CARE 
Cancer Institute at Ramkrishna CARE, Raipur; and the Fetal Medicine Centre of Excellence at Aster Medcity, 
Kochi.  
Higher volumes, greater clinical complexity and a favourable payor mix drove revenue growth of 19% YoY to 
INR 2,829 Cr, and normalised PAT grew by 34% to INR 281 Cr, a PAT margin of 10%. Going forward, our focus 
will be on expanding our CONGO and other high-end specialties, augmenting capacity across geographies, 
strengthening clinical talent and improving access to quality healthcare, while unlocking the full potential of our 
combined network.”

About Aster DM Quality Care 
Aster DM Quality Care Limited is one of India’s leading integrated healthcare providers, formed through the merger 
of Aster DM Healthcare Limited and Quality Care India Limited. The merged entity brings together four leading 
healthcare brands - Aster DM, CARE Hospitals, Evercare and KIMSHEALTH, creating a scaled and diversified 
healthcare platform with a network of 38 hospitals across 27 cities and over 10,840 beds. With a strong presence 
across South and Central India, the merged entity will deliver comprehensive healthcare services spanning primary, 
secondary, tertiary and quaternary care, supported by centers of excellence across key specialties including 
oncology, cardiac sciences, neurosciences, gastro sciences, orthopaedics, nephrology, organ transplantation, mother 
and child-care, and critical care.
