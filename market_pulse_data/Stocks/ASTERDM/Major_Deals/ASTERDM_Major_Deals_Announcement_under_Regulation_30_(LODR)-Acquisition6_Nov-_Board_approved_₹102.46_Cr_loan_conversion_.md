Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“Listing Regulations”) 
 
With reference to the above-captioned subject, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of 
Aster DM Quality Care Limited (“the Board” and “the Company”, respectively) at its meeting held 
today i.e. 6 November 2026, inter alia, has approved: 
 
1. conversion of a pre-existing loan of INR 102.46 Cr extended by the Company to Sri Sainatha 
Multispeciality Hospitals Private Limited (“Sri Saintha”), a wholly owned subsidiary Company, into 
equity shares, as part of internal capital restructuring, pursuant to which Sri Sainatha will issue 
10,24,62,779 equity shares of face value of INR 10/- (Rupees ten only) to the Company. The 
transaction does not have any impact on the ownership or control of the subsidiary. 
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master 
Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are provided as  
Annexure I. 
 
2. seeking approval of shareholders for special business through Postal Ballot and Notice of Postal 
Ballot. The Notice of Postal Ballot will be sent to the shareholders and stock exchanges in due 
course.  
 
3. termination/closure of the Operation & Management Services Agreement dated 4 March 2016, 
as amended and extended from time to time (“O&M Agreement”), and the Medical Services 
Agreement dated 1 April 2026 (“MSA”), entered into with DM Education and Research Foundation 
(“DMERF”), and consequent cessation of operations undertaken by the Company pursuant to the 
said agreements 
 
The termination is proposed to be effected by mutual consent of the parties, subject to execution 
of necessary termination/closure documents and settlement of the respective rights, obligations 
and outstanding dues between the parties.
