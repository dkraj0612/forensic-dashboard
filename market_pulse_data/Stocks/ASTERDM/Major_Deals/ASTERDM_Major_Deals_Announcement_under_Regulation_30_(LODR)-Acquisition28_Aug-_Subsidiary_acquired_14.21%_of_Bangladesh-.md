With reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform you that Chemistry Intermediate Holdings 
Limited, company incorporated in Mauritius and a subsidiary of Aster DM Quality Care Limited 
(“Company”), has acquired equity shares of STS Holdings Limited, a company incorporated in Bangladesh. 
The said acquisition is in line with the Merger Framework Agreement executed on 29 November 2024, 
amongst Quality Care India Limited, Aster DM Healthcare Limited, Dr. Azad Moopen, Union Investments 
Private Limited, Union (Mauritius) Holding Limited, BCP Asia II Topco IV Pte. Ltd., Centella Mauritius 
Holdings Limited and Sri Sainatha Multispeciality Hospitals Private Limited. The acquisition has also been 
disclosed in the Scheme of Amalgamation between Quality Care India Limited and Aster DM Healthcare 
Limited, which has been approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Hyderabad Bench, vide 
its order dated 19 June 2026.  
 
The requisite details, as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with the SEBI Master 
Circular dated January 30, 2026, are enclosed as Annexure A.
