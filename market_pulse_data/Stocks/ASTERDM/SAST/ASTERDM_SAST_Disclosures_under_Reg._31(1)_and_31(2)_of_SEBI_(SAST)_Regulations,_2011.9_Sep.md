### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Union (Mauritius) Holdings Limited, a promoter of Aster DM Quality Care Limited, has restructured its share encumbrances, releasing a direct pledge while simultaneously creating a non-disposal undertaking over 100% of its 2.86% stake in the company to secure an enhanced USD 210 million debt. This action, layered on top of an existing indirect pledge of the promoter entity itself, indicates substantial financial leverage and a complete restriction on the promoter's holding. A significant red flag is the unusual future date of this regulatory filing, September 9, 2026.
