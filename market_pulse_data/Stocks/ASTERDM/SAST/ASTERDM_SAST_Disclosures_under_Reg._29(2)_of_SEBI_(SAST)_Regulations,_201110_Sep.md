### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Catalyst Trusteeship Limited has disclosed a net increase in the encumbrance of Aster DM Quality Care Limited shares from 9.76% to 10.33%. This arises from the release of an existing ~2.29% share pledge for an amended loan, immediately followed by the creation of a new non-disposal undertaking over ~2.86% shares to secure a new USD 50 million loan for Union Investment Private Limited (UIPL). The overall increase in encumbered shares, utilizing nearly all of Union (Mauritius) Holding Limited's stake for new debt, signals increased financial obligations for the promoter group and could pose a risk of share invocation if debt covenants are breached.
