### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) is making a material investment of ₹125 Crores into its subsidiary, Minakshi Agro Industries LLP, primarily to establish a new malt distillery and maturation warehouse, signaling a strategic move into the premium single malt whisky segment. This expansion aims to strengthen ABDL's manufacturing footprint and premium portfolio for long-term growth. However, a portion of the investment includes ₹10 Crores to cover cost overruns on previously approved projects, which, despite justifications, flags potential past budgeting or project management challenges.

Dear Madam/Sir,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30(6) of SEBI Listing Regulations read with Schedule III thereto, we wish to 
inform you that the Management Committee of the Board of Directors of the Company, at its Meeting 
held today, i.e. September 11, 2026, has, inter alia, considered and approved additional capital 
contribution / financial assistance of an amount not exceeding ₹115 Crores to Minakshi Agro 
Industries LLP (“MAILLP”), a Subsidiary of the Company, in one or more tranches,  for setting up a 
Malt Distillery with capacity of ~3 MN BL per year cum Maturation Warehouse at Aurangabad, 
Maharashtra.  
The Committee also approved additional capital contribution/financial assistance of an amount not 
exceeding to ₹10 Crores to MAILLP to meet the cost overruns in relation to the previously approved 
projects, as intimated on January 29, 2026.
