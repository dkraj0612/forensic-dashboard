ABD COMMENCES COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS AT MALT DISTILLERY IN 
TELANGANA 
~ New facility strengthens ABD’s in-house malt capability and supports future product 
development ~ 
Mumbai, 22nd October 2026: Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), one of India’s 
leading spirits companies, today announced the commencement of commercial operations at 
its malt distillery at Rangapur, Telangana. The facility has an approved manufacturing capacity 
of ~ 4.4 million BL per annum. The greenfield facility marks a key milestone in expanding ABD’s 
domestic production footprint and vertically integrating its core supply chain. 
The facility will enable ABD to manufacture malt spirit in-house, giving the Company greater 
control over the important input used in its whisky portfolio. This will help ensure consistent 
quality and provide a steady, dependable supply of malt spirit as production scales up. 
Along with meeting ABD’s internal needs, the distillery will drive new product development 
and maturation. Crucially, it allows ABD to begin laying down malt spirit to craft its own 
homegrown single malt whisky in the coming years and support its growing Prestige & Above 
portfolio. 
Commenting on the commencement, Amar Sinha, Managing Director, ABD, said, “The start 
of operations at the malt distillery is an important step at ABD, this underscores ABD’s 
disciplined approach to backward integration and process excellence. Moving critical 
production inputs in-house enhances operating margins, insulates core brands from market 
fluctuations, and establishes a robust foundation for next-generation spirits development.” 
Rangapur has evolved into the operational anchor of ABD’s growth story. With grain 
distillation, PET bottle production, bottling operations, and now with malt distillation, ABD has 
created an end-to-end, integrated manufacturing hub. This complete self-reliance provides 
total control over cost, quality, and supply continuity, while giving the teams the agility and 
scale to innovate and premiumise with speed.

About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (www.abdindia.com) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is one of the leading spirits companies in India and the largest 
exporter in terms of volume with presence in 39 international markets. The company operates across five 
main categories: whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, and gin. Its portfolio is anchored by "millionaire" brands, 
including the flagship Officer’s Choice, which remains among the largest-selling whiskies globally. This is 
complemented by Sterling Reserve and the rapidly scaling ICONiQ White, which is currently the world’s 
fastest-growing millionaire whisky brand. As part of its premiumisation journey, ABD has expanded into the 
super-premium and luxury segments through its subsidiary- ABD Maestro Pvt Ltd. The company maintains 
a robust manufacturing footprint of 40 units including 10 owned bottling facilities, 2 owned distilleries, 1 
owned PET bottle plant and 27 non-owned manufacturing units (including 1 in Malaysia), supported by a 
pan-India distribution network that ensures scale and supply chain flexibility.
