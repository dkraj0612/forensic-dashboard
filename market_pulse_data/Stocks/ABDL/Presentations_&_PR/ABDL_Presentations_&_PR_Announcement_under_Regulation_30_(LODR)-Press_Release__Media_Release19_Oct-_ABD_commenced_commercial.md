ABD COMMENCES COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS AT MORADABAD BOTTLING 
FACILITY 
~ 6 million cases per annum capacity strengthens ABD’s manufacturing footprint and 
advances its backward integration programme ~ 
Mumbai, 19th October, 2026: Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), one of India’s 
leading spirits companies, today announced the commencement of commercial operations at 
its bottling facility in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh. The unit, with an initial capacity of 6 million 
cases per annum, marks a major milestone in ABD’s integrated manufacturing programme to 
serve the state’s growing spirits demand. 
The facility forms part of the assets acquired in January 2026 for ~ ₹70 crore and subsequently 
upgraded by ABD with an investment of ~ ₹40 crore. The commissioning enhances the 
Company’s bottling capacity in Uttar Pradesh and is expected to improve service efficiency 
while delivering cost benefits through increased in-house bottling and savings on franchise 
fees. 
Uttar Pradesh, one of India’s fastest-growing spirits markets, is central to ABD’s growth plans. 
The Company has announced an investment of over ₹600 crore in the state, including the 
acquisition and upgradation of the Moradabad unit and the development of an integrated 
facility comprising a dual-mode distillery and additional bottling capacity. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Amar Sinha, Managing Director, ABD, said, “The 
commencement of operations at the Moradabad unit is an important step in strengthening 
ABD’s manufacturing base and presence in Uttar Pradesh. Backward integration is a key pillar 
of our strategy, enabling greater control over the value chain, improved supply chain agility 
and stronger cost efficiencies. The unit will support our growth in the state and provide a 
platform for further integration, including increased captive ENA capacity. 
Uttar Pradesh is central to this backward integration journey. The State’s progressive excise 
policy framework and conducive investment environment provide a strong foundation for the 
industry’s long-term growth and bolster state’s economy.”

About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (www.abdindia.com) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is one of the leading spirits companies in India and the 
largest exporter in terms of volume with presence in 39 international markets. The company operates 
across five main categories: whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, and gin. Its portfolio is anchored by 
"millionaire" brands, including the flagship Officer’s Choice, which remains among the largest-selling 
whiskies globally. This is complemented by Sterling Reserve and the rapidly scaling ICONiQ White, 
which is currently the world’s fastest-growing millionaire whisky brand. As part of its premiumisation 
journey, ABD has expanded into the super-premium and luxury segments through its subsidiary- ABD 
Maestro Pvt Ltd. The company maintains a robust manufacturing footprint of 40 units including 10 
owned bottling facilities, 2 owned distilleries, 1 owned PET bottle plant and 27 non-owned 
manufacturing units (including 1 in Malaysia), supported by a pan-India distribution network that 
ensures scale and supply chain flexibility.
