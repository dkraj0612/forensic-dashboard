ABD Maestro Named ‘Company of the Year 2026’ at Spiritz Conclave 
& Achievers’ Awards 2026 
 
~Receives Eight Honours Across Blind Tasting and Excellence Categories~ 
 
Mumbai, 15th October 2026: ABD Maestro Private Limited, the super-premium and luxury 
spirits subsidiary of Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), has been named ‘Company 
of the Year 2026 (Domestic)’ at the Spiritz Conclave & Achievers’ Awards 2026. The company 
also received medals in multiple blind tasting product categories and trophies in Excellence 
Awards, reflecting the strength of its portfolio for super-premium, globally benchmarked 
spirits. 
 
In the blind tasting categories, ABD Maestro's portfolio secured four medals across whisky, 
vodka and gin. RANGEELA Contemporary Indian Vodka won 'Grand Gold' in the Vodka 
category, the highest recognition in the round. YELLO Designer Whisky and AODH Irish 
Whiskey each won ‘Gold’ in the Indian Whisky - Blended and Irish Whiskey - Blended 
categories, respectively, while ZOYA Premium Mixed Berries Gin received ‘Silver’ in the Gin 
category. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
In the Excellence Awards, ABD Maestro Private Limited was named 'Company of the Year 
2026 (Domestic)’ and received ‘Gold’. The company's recent launches were also recognised, 
with RANGEELA Contemporary Indian Vodka winning Gold for 'Product Launch 2026 - Vodka' 
and AODH Irish Whiskey sharing the Gold for 'Product Launch 2026 - Whisky Premium'. 
 
Commenting on the recognition, Bikram Basu, Vice Chairman, ABD Maestro, said, “Being 
named Company of the Year, along with medals across categories - whisky, vodka and gin, is 
a validation of building each of our brands. The blind tasting results are really meaningful as 
they reflect the quality of what is in the bottle. We thank the expert jury and the Spiritz team 
for this recognition and reiterate our commitment to raise the bar for premium spirits.” 
 
The recognition marks another important chapter for ABD Maestro, affirming its role in 
shaping India’s super-premium and luxury spirits’ landscape.

About ABD Maestro Private Limited 
 
ABD Maestro Private Limited is a super-premium and luxury spirits company, and a subsidiary of Allied 
Blenders and Distillers Limited, one of the leading domestic Spirits companies in India. Bollywood 
superstar Ranveer Singh is a co-founder and creative partner at ABD Maestro. 
 
The ABD Maestro portfolio includes The Collective Limited Edition, Arthaus Blended Malt Scotch Whisky, 
Aodh Irish Whiskey, Woodburns Contemporary Indian Malt Whisky, Yello Designer Whisky, Zoya Special 
Batch Gin and its flavours, Rangeela Contemporary Indian Vodka, Pumori Small Batch Gin and Pink Gin, 
Segredo Aldeia Café and White Rums. Through its partnership with Roust Corporation, the company has 
introduced Russian Standard Vodka to India in three distinct segments: Original, Gold, and Platinum.
