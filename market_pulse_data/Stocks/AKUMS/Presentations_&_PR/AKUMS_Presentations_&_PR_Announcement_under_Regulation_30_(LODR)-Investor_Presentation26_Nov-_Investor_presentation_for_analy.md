Managing Directors’ Message
Dear Investors
It gives us immense pleasure connecting with you to update on Akums’ journey and our vision forward. It has been 22 years since Akums 
started its journey and over 2 years since we listed. Over the last two decades, Akums has been at the forefront of augmenting access to 
quality medicines at affordable prices in India and globally. The trust of more than 1,700 clients has established us as the largest India-
focused formulations CDMO, with presence across 60+ dosage forms and 4,000+ formulations. With over 50 billion capacity across 13 
operational plants, we are one of the leading global players in terms of scale and diversity of offerings. Since we started this business, our 
focus has been on providing exceptional client service and becoming a “one stop” solution provider across product development and 
commercial production.
CDMO vertical, our flagship business, commands over 30% market share in the domestic CDMO market through diverse capabilities and 
extensive product pipeline. Our volumes have grown over 20% during last 12 months, driven by healthy order book, ramping up of our 
new facilities and deeper client penetration. Our annual investment in R&D has doubled in the past 5 years and accounts of ~4% of our 
revenues. With multiple tailwinds in place, we expect CDMO vertical to maintain strong growth in near future as well.
As we prepare ourselves for the 3rd decade of our journey, one of the strategic priorities is to meaningfully expand our geographical 
footprints. Regulated markets, incl. Europe, Latin America, Russia, Middle East, Australia and Canada are our focus markets for CDMO 
offerings. Our Plant 1 (oral solids) and Plant 2 (oral liquids) are EUGMP approved. Our Plant 3 (injectables), approved by Brazil ANVISA, and 
Plant 4 (hormones), approved by Russian regulators, are expected to trigger and receive EUGMP accreditation soon along with our β-
Lactam antibiotic facility. This will allow us to serve our global customers with a robust infrastructure of globally accredited facilities across 
diverse dosage forms and therapeutic capabilities. Our R&D product pipeline for European and other regulated markets is shaping up well 
with 2 approved, 8 filed and over 30 in pipeline. Our European contract, worth EUR 200 million+, is a testimony to our global CDMO 
capabilities. In order to diversify our manufacturing base and cater to localization opportunities across high potential, emerging countries, 
we intend to selectively set up facilities globally. Our manufacturing JV with Govt. of Zambia is a key step in that direction. It would 
meaningfully open opportunities for us in Zambia and neighboring countries.
Beyond pharma, we are looking to add impetus to our growth journey by tapping into fast growing BPC (Beauty and Personal Care) 
category with specific focus on nutraceuticals and cosmetics, for the Indian as well as global markets,. Driven by our formulation expertise, 
supply chain efficiency, manufacturing prowess and strong need for quality CDMO partners, we see this as an exciting opportunity.
Sustainability is core to Akums in whatever we do. We have been awarded EcoVadis Bronze Medal and are on our journey to be among 
the top global companies focused on environment, labour rights, ethics and sustainability. We remain committed to driving sustainable 
growth, maintaining market leadership and creating long term shareholder value.
