Respected Sir/Ma’am, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR 
Regulations”), this is to inform you that Pure and Cure Healthcare Private Limited (“Pure & Cure”), a wholly-owned 
subsidiary of Akums Drugs and Pharmaceuticals Limited (“the Company”) has received a Demand Notice from National 
Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority, Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Government of 
India dated October 13, 2026 for manufacturing of formulation “Lanol 1000 mg injection” 100ml (the product),  for our 
client Hetero Healthcare Limited (“the client”), the notice states that the client has fixed MRP of the product, in excess 
to the prescribed limits under the applicable provisions of The Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013. 
 
The above referred demand notice dated October 13, 2026 was issued to three (03) parties which includes Hetero 
Healthcare Limited as a Marketer, Abaris Healthcare Private Limited and Pure & Cure as a manufacturer (hereinafter 
referred to be as the “Parties” to the demand notice). However, the prime responsibility to respond on the notice is of 
Hetero Healthcare Limited, being the marketer of the product.  
 
Further in the ordinary course of business, the Company and its subsidiaries execute supply agreements with the 
marketing entity(ies) containing a clause that marketing entity shall be solely held liable for violation of DPCO 
provisions; and any penalty, fine, interest and other amount, as may be specified by the regulatory or statutory 
authority, in the event of any discrepancy found in the maximum retail price of the supplied medicine. 
 
Details of action/order required to be furnished under Regulation 30 read with Clause 20 of Para A of Part A of Schedule 
III of SEBI LODR Regulations is annexed as “Annexure-A” 
 
The above-mentioned demand notice was received by the Company on October 15, 2026 at 11:05 A.M. 
 
This is for your kind information and record.
