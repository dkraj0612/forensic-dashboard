OnEMI Technology Solutions Limited - Business Update for Q2FY27 
 
 
October 03, 2026: OnEMI Technology Solutions Limited (BSE: 544754 & NSE: KISSHT), a technology-first 
digital lender to India's mass market and mass affluent segments, wishes to provide the following provisional 
business update on consolidated basis for the quarter ended September 30, 2026: 
 
 Registered user base stood at 79.54 Mn as of September 30, 2026, compared to 59.96 Mn as on 
September 30, 2025, an increase of 32.6%. 
 
 Cumulative customers served stood at 12.80 Mn as of September 30, 2026, as compared to 10.45 Mn 
as of September 30, 2025, an increase of 22.5%. Customers served increased by 0.55 Mn in Q2FY27. 
 
 Assets under Management (AUM) grew by 68.4% to approximately ₹9,317 Cr as of September 30, 2026, 
as compared to ₹5,533 Cr as of September 30, 2025. AUM increased by approximately ₹1,316 Cr during 
Q2FY27, representing a 16.4% QoQ increase. 
 
 Loan Against Property (LAP) portfolio mix increased to 7.9% of AUM as of September 30, 2026, as 
compared to 4.1% as of September 30, 2025 and 7.7% as of June 30, 2026. 
 
 Overall disbursements were approximately ₹4,612 Cr in Q2FY27, as compared to ₹2,963 Cr in Q2FY26 
and ₹3,812 Cr in Q1FY27. 
 
The above information is provisional and subject to review by the statutory auditors of the Company. We 
request you to bring the above to the notice of all concerned.
