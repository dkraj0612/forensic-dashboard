> **Executive Summary: Force Motors Limited Q1 FY27 Financial Results**  Force Motors Limited reported mixed Q1 FY27 results (ended June 30, 2026), showcasing strong year-over-year growth in both revenue and net profit, though both metrics experienced a notable sequential decline, with net profit falling approximately 22.5% from the prior quarter. Material impacts include the acquisition of Veera Tanneries Private Limited as a wholly-owned subsidiary and the recognition of provisions related to new Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligations for vehicle scrapping. The significant sequential profit downturn, despite clean limited review reports from auditors, warrants further investigation into its drivers.
