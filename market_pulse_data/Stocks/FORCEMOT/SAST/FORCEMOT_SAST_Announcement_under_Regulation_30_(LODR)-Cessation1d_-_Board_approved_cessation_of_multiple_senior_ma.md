Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
In terms of the provisions of Regulation 30 (read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III) of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (Listing Regulations), we hereby inform you 
that based on the recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC), the Board of 
Directors of the Company; in its meeting held today, i.e. on Wednesday, 29th July, 2026, has considered 
and approved the changes to Senior Management Officials (“SMO”) of the Company w.e.f. 29th July, 2026. 
 
The details required to be disclosed pursuant to Regulation 30, read with Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations and SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th 
January, 2026, are attached at Annexure-A to this letter. 
 
Further, the disclosure requirements pertaining to submission of resignation letter, pursuant to Regulation 
7(C) of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of Listing Regulations is not applicable, since the aforesaid changes 
in SMO pertains to cessation of officials as SMO. 
 
The aforesaid information will also be made available on the Company's website at www.forcemotors.com. 
 
The meeting of the Board of Directors commenced at 02.56 p.m. and concluded at 04.23 p.m.
