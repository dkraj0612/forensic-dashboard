### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Centum Electronics is a growing Indian company specializing in high-reliability electronics for defense, aerospace, and critical industrial sectors, boasting a strong INR 18 Bn order book and 25% 3-year revenue CAGR. The company executed a significant strategic shift in FY26 by divesting loss-making subsidiaries, resulting in a substantial one-time exceptional loss of INR 2,033 Mn that led to a net loss for the year. This action, however, is intended to improve future profitability and streamline operations, positioning Centum for stronger growth in its core, high-entry-barrier mission-critical segments.
