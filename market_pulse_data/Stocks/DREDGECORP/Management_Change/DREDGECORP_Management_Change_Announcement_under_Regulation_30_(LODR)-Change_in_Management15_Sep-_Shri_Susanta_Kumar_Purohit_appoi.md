Shri Susanta Kumar Purohit, IRSEE (1996 batch), Chairperson of 
V.O. Chidambaranar Port Authority, has assumed the additional
charge of Chairman, Paradip Port Authority (PPA) by the Ministry of
Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Government of India.
Shri Purohit is a senior officer of the Indian Railway Service of
Electrical Engineers with extensive experience in administration,
engineering, infrastructure development, policy formulation and
public-sector management.
Prior to his present assignment, he served as Joint Secretary,
Department of Chemicals & Petrochemicals, Government of India,
where he was associated with policy formulation and sectoral
development. During his career, he has also held key positions in
Indian Railways, the Ministry of Power and the Government of
Odisha, gaining wide experience in infrastructure development,
technology-driven initiatives and public administration.
With his rich experience in port management, infrastructure
development, public policy and sustainable development, Shri
Purohit will provide strategic leadership to Paradip Port Authority
while continuing his responsibilities as Chairperson of V.O.
Chidambaranar Port Authority.
Under his additional charge, the focus will be on further
strengthening Paradip Port's infrastructure and connectivity,
enhancing operational efficiency, modernising cargo-handling
facilities and advancing green and sustainable port initiatives.
Paradip Port Authority looks forward to benefiting from Shri
Purohit's experience and leadership in further strengthening the
Port's capabilities and contributing to the growth of India's maritime
sector and the vision of Viksit Bharat.
