This is with reference to the communication received from the Stock Exchange(s) seeking 
clarification from Genesys International Corporation Limited ("Company") regarding the 
significant movement observed in the price of security across Exchanges, in the recent past. 
 
In this regard, we wish to submit and clarify as under: 
The Company has been and continues to be in compliance with the disclosure requirements 
prescribed under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), and 
has been making timely disclosures to the Stock Exchanges of all events and information which 
are required to be disclosed thereunder. 
As on the date of this clarification, there is no undisclosed material event, information or 
impending announcement in the knowledge of the Company which is required to be disclosed 
under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations and which, in the opinion of the Company, 
may have a bearing on the price/volume behaviour of the Company’s securities. 
The Company has taken note of a news article published in the online edition of Gujarat 
Samachar on August 24, 2026, inter alia, reporting that Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation 
(“AMC”) has awarded a contract to the company stated to be valued at approximately ₹240 
crore for undertaking a comprehensive 3D GIS survey/mapping project for the city of 
Ahmedabad. 
In this connection, the Company wishes to clarify that as on the date and time of this 
clarification, the Company is yet to receive a formal communication from Ahmedabad 
Municipal Corporation confirming the award of the aforesaid contract to the Company. 
Accordingly, no disclosure regarding award/receipt of the aforesaid contract has been made by 
the Company under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, since no formal award/order 
or other definitive communication confirming such award has been received by the Company 
as on date.
