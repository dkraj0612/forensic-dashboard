TVS MOTOR COMPANY BRINGS THE NEXT 
GENERATION OF APACHE PERFORMANCE TO 
SRI LANKA WITH THE WHOLE RANGE 
• Built on over four decades of TVS Racing heritage, the new 
Apache line-up brings performance, technology and racing-
inspired innovation to Sri Lanka 
Singapore/Colombo, September 11, 2026: TVS Motor Company (TVSM), part of TVS VENU, and a 
reputed global manufacturer of two and three-wheelers, today launched its expanded TVS Apache 
motorcycle portfolio in Sri Lanka, comprising the Flagship TVS Apache RR 310, TVS Apache RTR 310 , 
TVS Apache RTR 200 4V TFT and TVS Apache RTR 160 4V TFT. 
The launch marks the introduction of the globally celebrated TVS Apache brand to the customers in Sri 
Lanka. For over 20 years, TVS Apache has embodied TVS Motor Company's racing DNA and built a 
passionate community of more than 7 million riders worldwide. Developed on TVS Racing's "Track to 
Road" philosophy, the brand brings race-derived technology, performance, and innovation from the 
racetrack to the street, delivering motorcycles engineered for thrill, precision, and everyday rideability. 
Speaking about the launch, Mr. Peyman Kargar, President – International Business – TVS Motor 
Company, said, “Sri Lanka is an important market for TVS Motor Company, with a passionate and 
evolving community of motorcycle enthusiasts. The launch of the TVS Apache portfolio marks another 
significant step in our premium motorcycle journey in the country. By bringing the TVS Apache RTR 160 
4V TFT, Apache RTR 200 4V TFT, RTR 310 and RR 310 together, we are offering customers a wider 
portfolio that combines performance, advanced technology and distinctive design. Backed by over four 
decades of TVS Racing heritage, the Apache brand has evolved into a global performance motorcycle 
family, with over 7 million customers across 90 countries. We are confident that this expanded portfolio 
will further strengthen our connection with Sri Lankan riders and give them an opportunity to experience 
the racing spirit, technology and performance that define the Apache brand.” 
Mr. Geethal Anthony, CEO – TVS Lanka Pvt. Ltd  “The introduction of the TVS Apache range in Sri 
Lanka is an important milestone for us and an exciting opportunity for motorcycle enthusiasts in the 
country. Sri Lankan riders have a strong appreciation for performance, technology and distinctive design, 
and we believe this new portfolio will offer something for every kind of performance-oriented rider. These 
three vehicle bring a different riding experience, while staying true to the racing DNA of the Apache brand. 
We are delighted to bring these motorcycles to Sri Lanka and look forward to seeing the response from 
customers and the growing riding community.” 
Built on 44 years of TVS Racing heritage and inspired by the racetrack, TVS Apache represents the 
perfect blend of performance, agility, innovation, and rider-centric engineering. Every motorcycle in the 
TVS Apache lineup is designed and developed with insights gained from TVS Racing, delivering a truly 
exhilarating riding experience.

A PERFORMANCE PORTFOLIO FOR EVERY KIND OF RIDER 
The TVS Apache RR 310, the flagship supersport motorcycle in the TVS Apache line-up, brings TVS 
Racing's supersport DNA to Sri Lanka. Featuring an aggressive fully-faired design, race-focused 
ergonomics, and segment-first aerodynamic winglets, the motorcycle is powered by a 312.2 cc 
reverse-inclined DOHC engine, delivering 38 PS at 9,800 rpm and 29 Nm at 7,900 rpm. 
Engineered to deliver a race-inspired riding experience, the TVS Apache RR 310 comes equipped with a 
Bidirectional Quickshifter, Cruise Control, Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS), Transparent 
Clutch Cover, and four ride modes that allow riders to tailor performance to varying riding conditions. 
The motorcycle also features the advanced RT-DSC (Race Tuned Dynamic Stability Control) suite, 
comprising Launch Control, Cornering Drag Torque Control (CDTC), Cornering ABS, Cornering 
Traction Control, Cornering Cruise Control, Wheelie Control, Rear Lift-off Control, and Slope 
Dependent Control, delivering exceptional safety, precision, and rider confidence. Additional rider-
focused features include a Gen-2 Race Computer with multi-language support, further elevating the 
riding experience. Together, these technologies make the TVS Apache RR 310 a true embodiment of TVS 
Racing's "Track to Road" philosophy, offering riders a supersport experience that seamlessly blends 
performance, technology, and control. 
The TVS Apache RTR 310 combines aggressive streetfighter styling with performance-focused 
engineering and advanced rider technology. Powered by a 312.2 cc reverse-inclined engine, the 
motorcycle delivers 35.6 PS at 9,700 rpm and 28.7 Nm at 6,650 rpm.

Designed to redefine street performance, the TVS Apache RTR 310 comes loaded with segment-leading 
technologies, including a Bidirectional Quickshifter, Cruise Control, Tyre Pressure Monitoring 
System (TPMS), Transparent Clutch Cover, Keyless Ride, and five ride modes that offer a highly 
personalized riding experience. The motorcycle also features the advanced RT-DSC (Race Tuned 
Dynamic Stability Control) package with Cornering Drag Torque Control (DTC), Cornering ABS, 
Cornering Traction Control, Cornering Cruise Control, Wheelie Control, Rear Lift-off Control, and 
Slope Dependent Control, enabling enhanced control, stability, and confidence across varied riding 
conditions. These advanced electronic rider aids, combined with its aggressive streetfighter character and 
race-derived engineering, make the TVS Apache RTR 310 one of the most technologically advanced 
motorcycles in its segment. 
The TVS Apache RTR 200 4V TFT raises the benchmark in the premium performance motorcycle 
segment by combining race-derived engineering with advanced rider technology. Powered by a 197.75 
CC Engine Delivering 20.82 PS power at 9000 RPM and 17.25 Nm at 7250 RPM, the motorcycle 
delivers exhilarating performance while ensuring superior rideability across conditions. Equipped with a 
segment-leading Class D projector headlamp, 37 mm Upside Down (USD) front suspension, and 
an advanced 5-inch TFT instrument cluster with TVS SmartXonnect™, it offers a perfect blend of 
style, connectivity, and control. Enhancing the riding experience further are three ride modes (Sport, 
Urban, and Rain), Dual-Channel ABS, Traction Control System, and TVS Racing's race-derived O3C 
engine technology, providing riders with exceptional performance, confidence, and precision on every 
ride. 
The TVS Apache RTR 160 4V TFT brings premium technology and performance to the entry level 
performance Motorcycle segment. Powered by a 159.7 cc engine delivering 17.55 PS power at 9,250 
RPM and 14.73 Nm Torque at 7500 RPM, the motorcycle features a segment-first Class D projector 
headlamp, 37 mm Upside Down (USD) front suspension and a 5-inch TFT cluster with TVS 
SmartXonnect™. Complementing these features are dual-channel ABS, rider-focused technologies such 
as Traction Control System, Assist & Slipper Clutch and three ride modes – Sport, Urban and Rain, 
delivering enhanced control and an engaging riding experience.
