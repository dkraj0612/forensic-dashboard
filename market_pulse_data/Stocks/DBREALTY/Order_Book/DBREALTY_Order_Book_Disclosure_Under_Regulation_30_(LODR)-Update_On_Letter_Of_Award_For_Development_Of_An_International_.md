Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, and further to our letter dated 19th March, 2026 informing the Exchanges about the receipt of 
the Letter of Award dated 18th March, 2026 from the Government of Goa (Department of Public 
Private Partnership) (“Authority”) for development of an International Convention Centre, Convention 
Hotel and associated downstream facilities at Dona Paula, Goa on a Design, Build, Finance, Operate 
and Transfer (DBFOT) basis, we wish to update the Exchanges as under: 
● The project shall be implemented through Blue Crest Properties Private Limited, a wholly 
owned subsidiary of the Company (“SPV”), incorporated as the special purpose vehicle for 
undertaking the project. 
● The SPV has deposited a Performance Bank Guarantee of ₹57,90,00,000/- (Rupees Fifty-Seven 
Crore Ninety Lakh Only) with the Authority, in accordance with the terms of the Letter of 
Award. 
● The SPV has, on 28th July, 2026, executed the Concession Agreement with the Director, 
Department of Public Private Partnership, Government of Goa, for the development of the 
aforesaid project. 
With the execution of the Concession Agreement, the detailed rights and obligations of the parties, 
including development timelines, revenue rights, concession terms and other project parameters, 
shall be governed by the Concession Agreement, read with the Letter of Award. 
This project is material in nature and is expected to create significant long-term value for the Company 
and its shareholders.  
This is for your information and record.
