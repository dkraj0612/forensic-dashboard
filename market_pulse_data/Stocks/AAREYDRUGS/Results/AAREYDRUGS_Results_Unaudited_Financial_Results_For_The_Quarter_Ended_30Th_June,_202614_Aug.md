> **Executive Summary: Aarey Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Limited Q1 2026 Unaudited Results**  Aarey Drugs & Pharmaceuticals reported significantly weaker Q1 2026 results, with a ~56% year-over-year drop in revenue from operations (from Rs. 10,220.83 Lakhs to Rs. 4,462.55 Lakhs) and a ~25% decline in net profit (from Rs. 137.13 Lakhs to Rs. 102.37 Lakhs), indicating a material negative impact on performance.
