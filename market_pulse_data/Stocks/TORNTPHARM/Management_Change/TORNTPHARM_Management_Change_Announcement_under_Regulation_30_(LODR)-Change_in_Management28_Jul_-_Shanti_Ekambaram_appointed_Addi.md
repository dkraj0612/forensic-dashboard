banking business and governance leader with 39 years of 
financial services experience, including an exceptional 35

most respected universal banks. As Deputy Managing 
Director and Whole-time Director (2022-2025), she

Treasury, Human Resources, Internal Audit, Vigilance, 
CSR/ESG, and Capital Market subsidiaries.

building capabilities across multiple business verticals and

business experience. On the India's capital markets side, she 
served as the CEO of Kotak Mahindra Capital Company 
(1997-2003), the investment banking joint venture with 
Goldman Sachs.
