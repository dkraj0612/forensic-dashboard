Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
we hereby inform that, name of our step-down subsidiary company in the US has been changed from “Altek 
Electronics, Inc.” to “Cyient DLM Electronics Inc” 
 
We further inform that the name change is to align our U.S. operations with the global Cyient DLM brand 
and will not result in any change to its ownership, business operations, assets, liabilities, contractual 
arrangements, or legal status of the entity.  
 
The aforesaid information will also be made available on the Company's website at  www.cyientdlm.com
