Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we would like to inform you that: 
 
Jaykay Enterprises Limited (the “Company”) has obtained “AS9100D and ISO 9001:2015” 
Certifications from MTIC InterCert India Private Limited (“MTIC”), for “Manufacture and Sale of 
Precision Machined Mechanical Components for the Aerospace, Defence and Aviation 
Industries”. 
 
The assessment was performed in accordance with the requirements of Standard AS 9104/1 ISSUE 
2012-01, covering the Company’s operations at Bengaluru, Karnataka under a Single Site certification 
structure. MTIC is accredited by the National Accreditation Board for Certification Bodies (NABCB) 
under the AQMS/ICOP Certification Scheme for certification against AS9100 Rev. D, a 9100-series 
Aerospace Quality Management System standard established by the International Aerospace Quality 
Group (IAQG). 
 
The aforesaid certifications reinforce the Company’s commitment to maintaining robust quality 
management systems and strengthen its capabilities in the manufacture and sale of precision machined 
mechanical components for the Aerospace, Defence and Aviation Industries.  
 
A copy of the aforesaid certificate is enclosed herewith. 
 
This disclosure will also be hosted on Company’s website viz. www.jaykayenterprises.com.     
 
Kindly take the same on record.
