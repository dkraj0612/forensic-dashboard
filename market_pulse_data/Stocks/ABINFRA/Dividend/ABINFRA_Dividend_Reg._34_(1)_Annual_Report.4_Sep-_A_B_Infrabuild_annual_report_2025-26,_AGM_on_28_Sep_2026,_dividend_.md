Dear Shareholders,
It gives me immense pleasure to present the Annual Report of your Company for the 
financial year ended 31st March, 2026. The year has been one of remarkable progress, 
reflecting our resilience, execution, and unwavering commitment to creating sustainable 
value for all our stakeholders. Despite evolving market dynamic and increasing 
competition, your Company has continued to strengthen its position through disciplined 
execution, operational efficiency, and a relentless focus on quality.
Warm Regards,
Amit Mishra
Managing Director 
India’s infrastructure sector continues to play a pivotal role in the nation’s economic 
transformation, driven by sustained Government investments in transportation, 
urban development, railways, and public infrastructure. As a trusted infrastructure 
partner, your Company has remained at the forefront of this growth journey by 
delivering critical engineering and construction projects that contribute to building 
a stronger and more connected India.
Key Highlights of the year.
Strong Financial Performance:
During the year, your Company delivered a robust financial performance with healthy 
growth in revenue from operations and improved profitability. Our focus on efficient project execution, 
disciplined cost management, and prudent financial practices has enabled us to strengthen our margins while maintaining a healthy 
financial position. These results reflects the resilience of our business model and our commitment to delivering long-term value to our 
shareholders.
Operational Excellence:
We successfully completed several prestigious infrastructure projects for esteemed clients, including the various divisions of the 
Indian Railways. The timely execution of Road Over Bridges (ROBs), Foot Over Bridge (FOBs), station infrastructure, and other 
complex civil engineering works stands as a testament to our engineering expertise, execution capabilities, and uncompromising 
commitment to safety, quality, and client satisfaction.
Strategic Growth and Business Development:
Our continued emphasis on expanding our operational footprint and strengthening execution capabilities has resulted in a healthy 
order book, providing strong revenue visibility for the coming years. The confidence reposed in us by our clients is reflected in the 
successful award of new projects across infrastructure segments. We continue to diversify our project portfolio while taking on 
increasingly complex and technically challenging assignments.
The year also presented challenges, including intense industry competition, fluctuating input costs, and evolving project execution 
requirements. However, through proactive planning, effective risk management, and the unwavering dedication of our employees, we 
successfully navigated these challenges and continued our growth trajectory.
Looking ahead, we remain optimistic about the opportunities emerging from India's ambitious infrastructure development agenda. 
With significant investments planned in railways, roads, urban infrastructure, and public utilities, we believe the sector offers 
substantial long-term growth potential. Your Company is well positioned to capitalize on these opportunities through its strong 
execution capabilities, experienced workforce, sound financial discipline, and commitment to innovation.
Our strategic priorities for the coming year include strengthening project delivery capabilities, enhancing operational efficiencies 
through technology adoption, promoting sustainable construction practices, expanding our geographical presence, and investing in 
the development of our people. We remain committed to maintaining the highest standards of corporate governance while delivering 
consistent and sustainable value to all stakeholders.
On behalf of the Board of Directors, I extend my sincere gratitude to our employees for their dedication, professionalism, and 
unwavering commitment to excellence. I also thank our clients for their continued trust, our bankers, suppliers, and business partners 
for their valuable support, our Board of Directors for their guidance and strategic oversight, and our shareholders for their continued 
confidence in the Company.
Together, we are not merely constructing infrastructure—we are building enduring assets that 
contribute to India's growth and creating a stronger foundation for generations to come.

down a consistent approach to dividend declaration.

the shares held by the shareholders. As per the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the dividend can be paid as 
interim or final.

Meaning of Internal Financial Controls over Financial Reporting 
 
A company's internal financial control over financial reporting is a process designed to provide 
reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and the preparation of Ind AS 
Financial Statements for external purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.  
A company's internal financial control over financial reporting includes those policies and procedures   
that (1) pertain to the maintenance of records that, in reasonable detail, accurately and fairly reflect the   
transactions and dispositions of the assets of the company; (2) provide reasonable assurance that 
transactions are recorded as necessary to permit preparation of  Ind AS Financial Statements in  
accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, and that receipts and expenditures of the  
company are being made only in accordance with authorisations of management and directors of the
company;  and (3) provide reasonable assurance regarding prevention or timely detection of 
unauthorised acquisition, use, or disposition of the company's assets that could have a  material effect on
the Ind AS Financial Statements.
 
 
Inherent Limitations of Internal Financial Controls over Financial Reporting 
 
Because of the inherent limitations of internal financial controls over financial reporting, including the  
possibility of collusion or improper management override of controls, material misstatements due to  
error or fraud may occur and not be detected. Also, projections of any evaluation of the internal financial 
controls over financial reporting to future periods are subject to the risk that the internal financial control    
over financial reporting may become inadequate because of changes in conditions, or that the degree  
of compliance with the policies or procedures may deteriorate.

The Company is exposed to market risk, credit risk and liquidity risk. Risk management is carried out by the company under policies approved by the board of 
directors. The Company’s documented risk management policies are effective tool in mitigating the various financial risk to which the business is exposed to in the 
course of daily operations This Risk management plan defines how risks associated with the Company will be identified, analysed, and managed. It outlines how 
risk management activities will be performed, recorded, and monitored by the Company. The basic objective of risk management plan is to implement an integrated 
risk management approach to ensure all significant areas of risks are identified, understood and effectively managed, to promote a shared vision of risk management 
and encourage discussion on risks at all levels of the organisation to provide a clear understanding of risk/benefit trade-offs, to deploy appropriate risk management 
methodologies and tools for use in identifying, assessing, managing and reporting on risks, and to determine the appropriate balance between cost and control of risk 
and deploy appropriate resources to manage/optimize key risks. Activities are developed to provide feedback to management and other interested parties (e.g. Audit 
committee, Board etc.). The results of these activities ensure that risk management plan is effective in the long term.
Market risk is the risk that the fair value or future cash flows of a financial instrument will fluctuate because of changes in market prices. Market prices comprise 
three types of risk: foreign currency rate risk, interest rate risk and other price risks, such as equity price risk and commodity risk.
Foreign currency risk is the risk that the fair value or future cash flows of a financial instrument will fluctuate because of changes in foreign exchange rates. The 
Company’s exposure to the risk of changes in foreign exchange rates relates primarily to the Company’s operating activities. Currently, the Company does not have 
any transaction in Foreign Currencies. 
Interest rate risk is the risk that the fair value or future cash flows of a financial instrument will fluctuate because of changes in market interest rates .In order to 
optimize the Company’s position with regards to interest expenses and to manage the interest rate risk treasury performs a comprehensive corporate interest rate risk 
management by balancing the proportion of fixed rate and floating rate financial instruments in its total portfolio.
The Company is engaged in construction work and commodities like ferrous and non ferrous metal materials, Welded pipes, MS TMT bars & Metals, Cements, etc 
are the basic commodity for consumption. Commodity price risk arises due to fluctuation in prices of metal products. The Company mitigate the risk by natural 
hedge as any increase/ decrease in materials price directly reflect the changes in finished goods price. 
Credit risk is the risk that a counter party will not meet its obligations under a financial instrument or customer contract, leading to a financial loss. The Company is 
exposed to credit risk for trade receivables, other bank balances, loans, other financial assets and financial guarantees.
The Company extends credit to customers in normal course of business. The Company considers factors such as credit track record in the market and past dealings 
with the Company for extension of credit to customers. The Company monitors the payment track record of the customers. Outstanding customer receivables are 
regularly monitored.
The Company seeks to limit its credit risk with respect to banks by only dealing with reputable banks.
Liquidity risk is the risk that the Company may not be able to meet its present and future cash and collateral obligations without incurring unacceptable losses. The 
Company’s objective is to, at all times, maintain optimum levels of liquidity to meet its cash and collateral requirements. The Company limits its liquidity risk by 
ensuring funds from trade receivables.
