Company Overview
A B Infrabuild Limited, formerly A B Infrabuild Private Limited, was incorporated on March 16, 2011, 
transitioning from A B Enterprises - a proprietary concern established in 1999 by visionary Mr. Amit 
Bholananth Mishra
Holds Grade “AA” contractor registration with the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai and 
Class 1(A) Contractor status with the Public Works Department of Maharashtra
Specialized in construction of various types of Bridges, Platforms, and Road & Rail Works
A leading player in the Civil Construction Industry with Annual Turnover of Rs. 25,621 Lakhs and 
CAGR of 41% during FY22 to FY26; EBIT Margin improving YoY from 7.2% (FY22) to 14.2% (FY26)
Targeting revenue of ~₹45,000 lakhs by FY28, implying a ~32.5% CAGR over FY26–FY28, subject to 
order inflows, execution timelines and prevailing business conditions.
