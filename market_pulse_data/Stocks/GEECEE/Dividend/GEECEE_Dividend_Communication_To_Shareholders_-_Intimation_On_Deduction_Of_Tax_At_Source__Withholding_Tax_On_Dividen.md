Where PAN has not been furnished, PAN has not been linked with Aadhaar which has subsequently 
made the PAN invalid/ inoperative and where incorrect PAN details have been provided by the 
investors, the Company is liable to deduct TDS at a higher rate on the amount of dividend payable as

As per Section 262 of the Act, every person who has been allotted a PAN and who is eligible to obtain 
Aadhaar is required to link the PAN with Aadhaar. Where the PAN has not been linked with Aadhaar 
within the prescribed time, the PAN shall become invalid/inoperative, and TDS will be deducted at the 
higher rate prescribed under Section 397(2) of the Act, as if PAN has not been furnished.
