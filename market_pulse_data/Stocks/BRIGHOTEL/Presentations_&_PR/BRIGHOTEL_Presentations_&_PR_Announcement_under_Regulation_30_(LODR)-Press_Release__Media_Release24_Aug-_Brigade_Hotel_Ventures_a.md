PRESS RELEASE 
Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited appoints Vinay Gupta as CEO 
Bengaluru, August 24, 2026: Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited (BHVL) has announced the 
appointment of Vinay Gupta as Chief Executive Officer. In his new role as CEO, Vinay will 
be responsible for leading a dynamic team of seasoned professionals to take BHVL to greater 
heights. 
"Vinay's appointment comes at a significant stage in BHVL's journey and reflects our 
commitment to building a strong leadership team for the future. His proven track record in 
driving business growth, scaling operations, and creating long-term value will be instrumental 
as we focus on our next phase of expansion. With his strategic vision and deep industry 
expertise, I am confident Vinay will make a meaningful contribution to BHVL's continued 
success and growth. We have known Vinay for the past 20 years, as he was the first GM of our 
first hotel. Him coming back as CEO is a full circle moment for us,” said Nirupa Shankar, 
Managing Director, Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited. 
"I am honoured to be part of an organisation with a clear vision for the future. I look forward 
to working closely with the team to build on the organisation’s strengths. Our main focus will 
be to deliver a world-class hospitality experience while catering to the evolving needs of our 
guests and the market. I am excited about the journey ahead and look forward to contributing 
to BHVL’s continued growth and success,” said Vinay Gupta, CEO, Brigade Hotel Ventures 
Limited. 
Previously, Vinay was associated with InterGlobe Hotels & Accor, during which time he led 
the transformation and repositioning of a 22-hotel portfolio, driving significant revenue 
growth, operational excellence, and leadership development. He also headed a portfolio of 
more than 25 hotels across India for SAMHI, with Marriott, Hyatt, and IHG as brand partners. 
Vinay has also held multiple General Manager roles across India and Australia with Hilton, 
Accor, and Mirvac, building deep expertise in luxury and upscale hotel operations while 
consistently improving asset performance, and had earlier held leadership roles in the UAE, 
the UK, and Australia. 
Vinay is an alumnus of IHM Pusa, the University of Central Lancashire, and Lancaster 
University 
About Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited: Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited (BHVL) is an 
owner and developer of hotels in key cities in India primarily across South India. The Company 
is the second largest owner of chain-affiliated hotels and rooms in South India among major 
private hotel asset owners. BHVL is a subsidiary of Brigade Enterprises Limited (BEL) which 
is one of the leading Indian real estate developers in India. The Company has a portfolio of 
nine operating hotels across Bengaluru (Karnataka), Chennai (Tamil Nadu), Kochi (Kerala), 
Mysuru (Karnataka) and the GIFT City (Gujarat) with 1,604 keys. The hotels are operated by
