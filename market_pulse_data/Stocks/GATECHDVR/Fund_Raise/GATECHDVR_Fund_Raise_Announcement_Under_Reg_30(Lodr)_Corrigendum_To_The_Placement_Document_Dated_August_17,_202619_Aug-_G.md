REGULATIONS, 2015, AS AMENDED 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
We wish to inform the Stock Exchanges and the members/investors of the Company that GACM 
Technologies Limited (“Company”), in connection with its proposed Qualified Institutions Placement 
(“Issue”), has appointed Infomerics Valuation and Rating Limited as the Monitoring Agency for the 
Issue. 
The Issue Size is ₹4,950 lakhs and, in terms of Regulation 173A of the SEBI (Issue of Capital and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, as amended (“SEBI ICDR Regulations”), the appointment 
of a monitoring agency is not mandatory for the Issue. 
However, with a view to ensuring an independent monitoring mechanism for the utilisation of the Net 
Proceeds of the Issue and enhancing transparency in the utilisation of funds, the Company has 
voluntarily appointed Infomerics Valuation and Rating Limited as the Monitoring Agency for the Issue. 
The Company considers that the appointment of the Monitoring Agency, although not mandatory for 
the Issue, will provide an additional level of oversight and transparency in relation to the utilisation of 
the Net Proceeds. 
Infomerics has provided its consent to act as the Monitoring Agency for the proposed Issue being a 
SEBI-registered credit rating agency.  
The Monitoring Agency will monitor the utilisation of the Net Proceeds of the Issue in accordance with 
the applicable provisions of the SEBI ICDR Regulations and other applicable laws, regulations and 
guidelines.
