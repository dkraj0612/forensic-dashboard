ABOUT US
12
Established in 1983, Dishman Carbogen Amcis Limited is a fully integrated CDMO 
(Contract Development and Manufacturing Organisation) company with strong 
capabilities right from process research & development to late stage clinical and 
commercial manufacturing and supply of API to innovator pharmaceutical companies.
The Company has global presence with development and manufacturing sites in 
Switzerland, UK, France, Netherlands, India and China.
Dishman provides end-to-end integrated high-value niche CDMO offering and 
has comprehensive product offerings which include APIs, High Potent APIs, 
Intermediates, Phase Transfer Catalysts, Vitamin D Analogues, Cholesterol, 
Lanolin-related products, Antiseptic and Disinfectant formulations.
