Ahmedabad, India, September 18, 2026: Advait Battery Ecosystem Private Limited (“Advait 
Group”), a subsidiary company of BSE and NSE-listed parent company, Advait Energy 
Transitions Limited, announced that it has entered into a Master Supply Agreement (MSA) with 
HiTHIUM, a BloombergNEF Tier 1 global energy storage manufacturer, for the supply of lithium iron 
phosphate (LFP) prismatic battery cells.
The agreement anchors cell supply for Advait’s upcoming 2.5 GWh annual capacity cell-to-container 
Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) assembly facility and is structured to support cell offtake of up to 1 
GWh over the next year, establishing a dependable, high-volume supply chain as Advait scales domestic 
BESS assembly for grid-scale and commercial and industrial energy storage applications across India. 
Under the agreement, HiTHIUM will supply its 314 Ah LFP prismatic cells to Advait, bringing one of the 
most widely deployed cell platforms in global energy storage into Advait’s domestic manufacturing 
ecosystem as the company builds capabilities spanning module and pack assembly, container integration 
and system commissioning. 
Commenting on the agreement, Bhushan Shahane, Chief Operating Officer, Advait Battery Ecosystem 
Private Limited, said: 
“This partnership with Hithium reflects our belief in partnering with the best: the best cell 
technology, the best quality and the best service available in the market today. As we scale 
our 2.5 GWh BESS assembly capacity, a dependable, world-class cell supply chain is 
central to our ability to deliver cost-optimised, high-performance energy storage solutions 
to customers across India.” 
Hithium is recognised as a BloombergNEF Tier 1 global energy storage manufacturer and is 
among the leading energy storage battery cell manufacturers globally. The company supplies 
battery cells and energy storage systems for utility-scale, commercial and industrial, and 
residential applications across international markets.

The agreement establishes Hithium as a key cell supply partner for Advait as the company builds 
its domestic BESS manufacturing capacity. The cell supply arrangement is expected to provide 
Advait with access to high-volume LFP cells as its facility progresses towards commercial 
production. 
“We partnered with Hithium because they represent the gold standard in cell 
manufacturing: proven quality, consistent performance and a track record with some of the 
world’s most demanding energy storage developers. Bringing that standard into our supply 
chain lets us offer the Indian market a cost-optimised solution without compromising on 
quality, performance or service,” Mr. Shahane added. 
Strengthening India’s Domestic Energy Storage Manufacturing Base 
Advait’s 2.5 GWh BESS cell-to-container assembly line, currently under development, is central 
to the company’s strategy of building an integrated, India-based battery energy storage 
manufacturing ecosystem. The planned capabilities span cell sourcing, module and pack 
assembly, container integration and system commissioning, providing an integrated 
manufacturing framework for BESS applications. 
The Hithium agreement provides a cell supply framework as Advait prepares to scale production 
at the facility. With an offtake structure of up to 1 GWh over the next year, the agreement aligns 
cell sourcing with the planned ramp-up of Advait’s domestic assembly capabilities. 
Advait’s collaboration with Hithium also builds on the company’s broader strategy of developing 
an integrated BESS supply chain through specialised technology partnerships. Advait Battery 
Ecosystem has already entered into a strategic collaboration with Adaptive Engineering for 
engineering design, Energy Management System (EMS) architecture, PLC/SCADA and system 
integration capabilities. With Hithium now strengthening the critical cell supply side, Advait is 
advancing this partnership-led ecosystem to the next level, bringing together cell sourcing, EMS 
and engineering expertise, module and pack assembly, container integration and system 
commissioning under its growing domestic BESS manufacturing platform. 
The facility, in Gangad, Gujarat, is scheduled to begin commercial production soon. It is being 
developed to serve utility-scale, commercial and industrial, and renewable integration 
applications as Advait expands from project-level BESS deployment towards volume 
manufacturing. 
Advait Energy Transitions Limited, the group’s listed parent company, is a Gujarat-based new & 
renewable energy & power company with an active portfolio spanning battery energy storage 
manufacturing, green hydrogen project development, electrolyser manufacturing, fuel cell

About Advait Energy Transitions Limited 
Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL), formerly Advait Infratech Limited, is a publicly listed energy
