Key Highlights  
• 
Consolidated revenue grew 40.84% in Q2 FY27 and 45.37% in H1 FY27 to ₹400.21 crore. 
• 
Consolidated EBITDA margin improved by 342 basis points year-on-year to 14.45% in Q2 FY27. 
• 
Consolidated EBITDA margin improved from 13.82% in Q1 FY27 to 14.45% in Q2 FY27, 
reflecting operating leverage. 
• 
Consolidated H1 FY27 profit after tax increased 77.89% to ₹33.99 crore, while EPS grew 
78.64% to ₹29.53 and Return on Equity stood at 20.87%.

Key Business Highlights  
• 
The Company’s order book stood at ₹1,574.83 crore as of September 30, 2026, representing 
47% year-on-year growth, with PTS contributing 68% and NRE accounting for 32%. 
• 
Advait Battery Ecosystem Private Limited is setting up a 2.5 GWh BESS assembly facility spread 
across approximately 4,00,000 square feet at Gangad, Gujarat, supported by a Master Supply 
Agreement with Hithium for up to 1 GWh of LFP battery cells. 
• 
The Power Transmission Solutions business secured orders worth approximately ₹462 crore, 
led by underground cabling for PGVCL and Emergency Restoration Systems orders from 
MPPTCL and GETCO. 
• 
The New & Renewable Energy business secured a ₹116 crore solar EPC order for a 200 MW 
project from KPI Green Energy. 
 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Shalin Sheth, Founder and Managing Director, Advait Energy 
Transitions Limited, said: 
 
“The first half of FY27 has been encouraging, with strong revenue growth and a significant 
improvement in profitability. These results reflect our continued focus on execution, operational 
efficiency and strengthening the fundamentals of our business. 
 
Our Power Transmission Solutions (PTS) business remains central to our growth strategy, supported 
by sustained investments in transmission infrastructure, grid modernisation and renewable energy 
integration. We are focused on expanding our capabilities and enhancing our ability to serve a 
broader set of customers and projects. 
 
At the same time, we are progressing our initiatives across New and Renewable Energy (NRE), with a 
focus on manufacturing of battery energy storage systems (BESS), electrolysers and Other Integrated 
renewable energy solutions. These areas represent important opportunities to build complementary 
capabilities and participate in India’s evolving energy landscape.  
 
As we move into the second half of FY27, our focus will remain on timely execution, capacity 
expansion, working capital discipline and creating sustainable long-term value for our shareholders.”

About Advait Energy Transitions Limited 
Advait Energy Transitions Limited (formerly Advait Infratech Limited), established in 2009 and 
headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, provides products and solutions for power transmission, 
substation and telecommunication infrastructure. Its Power Transmission Solutions business 
manufactures and supplies stringing tools, OPGW, ACS wires, Emergency Restoration Systems and 
insulators, and executes EPC projects. Its New & Renewable Energy business covers solar EPC, battery 
energy storage systems and green hydrogen. For more information, visit www.advaitgroup.co.in.
