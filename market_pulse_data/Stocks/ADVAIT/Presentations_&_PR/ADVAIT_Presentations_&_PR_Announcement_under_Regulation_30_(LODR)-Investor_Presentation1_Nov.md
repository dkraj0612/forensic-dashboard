India's power sector is entering a significant transformation phase, driven by rising electricity consumption, increasing peak power
demand and the growing need to bridge the gap between power availability and demand. Over the next decade, substantial investments
will be required in power generation, transmission and distribution infrastructure to ensure reliable and uninterrupted electricity supply.
The increasing share of renewable energy in the power mix is creating greater demand for grid-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems
(BESS), grid modernization and integrated energy solutions. Simultaneously, Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia are emerging as
important components of India's future energy mix.
Amid geopolitical uncertainties, volatile global fuel prices and supply-chain challenges, energy security and indigenous manufacturing of
critical technologies, particularly electrolysers, BESS and renewable energy solutions, are becoming strategic priorities.
This evolving landscape presents significant opportunities for Advait Energy Transitions Limited to strengthen its manufacturing
capabilities, expand its technological footprint and contribute to India's long-term energy security while creating sustainable value for all
stakeholders.
