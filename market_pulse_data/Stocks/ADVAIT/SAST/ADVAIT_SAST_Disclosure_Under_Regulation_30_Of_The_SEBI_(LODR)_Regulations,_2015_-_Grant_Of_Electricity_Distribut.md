Advait Energy Transitions Marks Milestone in India’s Power Distribution Sector 
Following HERC Licence Grant as Consortium Member 
Ahmedabad, October 21, 2026: Advait Energy Transitions Limited (“Advait” or “the 
Company”), a player in India’s power transmission and distribution infrastructure sector, 
today announced its participation as a consortium member in the consortium led by Eleven 
Power Private Limited, which has been granted an electricity distribution licence by the 
Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission (HERC). As part of the consortium, Advait will 
contribute its expertise and experience in the power sector by offering a range of product 
solutions in the new and renewable energy segments to support decarbonisation initiatives. 
The licence pertains to electricity distribution in the authorised area comprising the Gurugram 
and Nuh revenue districts of Haryana, subject to the terms and conditions stipulated in the 
licence. 
The development marks an important milestone in the evolution of Haryana’s electricity 
distribution sector and reflects the growing focus on strengthening and modernising power 
distribution infrastructure to support the region’s evolving electricity requirements. 
Advait’s participation in the consortium represents a significant development in the 
Company’s journey within India’s power infrastructure ecosystem. The development aligns 
with the broader transformation underway in the country’s electricity sector, driven by the 
need for efficient distribution networks, infrastructure modernisation and the adoption of 
advanced technologies. 
As India continues to strengthen its power infrastructure to meet rising electricity demand 
and support its energy transition objectives, opportunities across the transmission and 
distribution value chain are becoming increasingly important. Advait remains focused on its 
strategic priorities and on opportunities aligned with the evolving requirements of India’s 
power infrastructure sector. 
The development marks another milestone in Advait’s growth journey and reinforces the 
strategic relevance of the power transmission and distribution sector within the country’s 
broader energy infrastructure landscape. 
Commenting on the development Mr. Shalin Sheth, Founder & Managing Director of 
Advait Energy, said,  
“India’s power sector is witnessing a significant transformation, with the modernisation and 
expansion of electricity distribution infrastructure emerging as key priorities. Advait’s 
participation in this consortium marks an important milestone in our growth journey and 
reflects our continued engagement with the evolving power transmission and distribution 
landscape. We look forward to the opportunities emerging from India’s expanding energy 
infrastructure ecosystem as the country advances towards a more resilient and future-ready 
power sector.”

About Advait Energy Transitions Limited 
Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL), formerly Advait Infratech Limited, is a publicly 
listed energy transition and infrastructure company headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, 
and listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). The 
company operates across power transmission infrastructure, renewable energy, Battery 
Energy Storage Systems (BESS), green hydrogen and clean energy technologies, with 
capabilities spanning EPC execution, manufacturing and advanced energy infrastructure 
solutions.
