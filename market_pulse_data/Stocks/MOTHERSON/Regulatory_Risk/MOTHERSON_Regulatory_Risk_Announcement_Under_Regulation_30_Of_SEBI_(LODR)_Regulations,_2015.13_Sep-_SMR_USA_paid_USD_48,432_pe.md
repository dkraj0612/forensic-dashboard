The penalty has been imposed pursuant to the 
Corporate Income Tax audit carried out by the 
State of Michigan, USA for the periods April 1, 
2022 to March 31, 2024.  
 
The short payment of tax has led to levy of 
penalty of USD 48,432 (US Dollar Forty Eight 
Thousand Four Hundred Thirty Two only) on 
SMR USA.

Department of Treasury.

on operations or other activities of the Company.
