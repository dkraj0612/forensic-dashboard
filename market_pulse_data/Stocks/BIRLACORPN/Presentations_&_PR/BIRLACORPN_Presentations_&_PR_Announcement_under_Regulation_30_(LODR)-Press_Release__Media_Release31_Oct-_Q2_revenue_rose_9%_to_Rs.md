Birla Corporation September quarter revenue up 9%,  
cement sales grow 7% 
KOLKATA, 31 Oct.--Birla Corporation Limited’s September quarter consolidated 
revenue rose 9% to Rs 2,441 crore from Rs 2,233 crore in the same period last year. 
The quarter saw weak cement prices and disruption in construction activities in 
core markets owing to excessive rainfall. 
The Company’s cement sales, by volume, during the quarter grew 7% year-on-year 
(yoy) to 4.54 million tons (mt), while realization rose 2% to Rs 4,925 per ton. It was, 
however, marginally lower sequentially as cement prices weakened through July 
and August in core markets. 
For the full quarter, realization for the cement industry declined 3% in the east and 
remained flat in central India. Modest improvements were witnessed in the west 
and north. 
Capacity utilization for the quarter was maintained at 85%—the same level as last 
year—but profitability was impaired owing to unprecedented increase in input 
costs, which couldn’t be offset due to subdued prices. Clinker cost rose 13% while 
power and fuel cost rose 15% yoy as pet coke prices rose 64% over the same period 
last year. The Cement Division’s EBITDA per ton for the quarter was at Rs 482 versus 
Rs 712 in the same period last year.

To mitigate cost pressure, the Company scaled up consumption of green power to 
32% for the quarter from 30% in the same period a year ago. Also, the Company 
started extracting coal from its Bikram coal mine in Madhya Pradesh during the 
September quarter. Production from the mine is being ramped up and it is 
expected to deliver 180,000 tons of coal in the second-half of the current financial 
year, and significantly reduce fuel cost. 
Birla Corporation’s consolidated EBITDA for the quarter was at Rs 254 crore, 
compared to Rs 332 crore in the same period a year ago. Consolidated net profit for 
the quarter was at Rs 37 crore against Rs 90 crore a year earlier. The Cement 
Division’s EBITDA margin fell 490 basis points, from a year earlier, to 9.8%. 
 
In line with its stated strategy, Birla Corporation Limited continued to expand sales 
of blended cement, which accounted for 86% of total cement sales during the 
quarter. Sales by volume of blended cement grew 4% yoy with significant gains 
coming from Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and Rajasthan. Sales from the 
newly commissioned production facilities at Kundanganj and Mukutban rose 20% 
and 8%, respectively. 
Similarly, sales of premium products registered healthy growth of 17% during the 
quarter. Sales by volume stood at 2.38 mt compared to 2.02 mt in the same period 
a year earlier. Sales of Perfect Plus, the flagship brand, continued to strengthen with 
21% yoy growth during the quarter. Premium cement accounted for 64% of the 
Company’s sales through the trade channel compared to 61% in the same period a 
year earlier. 
Given its sustained high capacity utilization, Birla Corporation Limited remains 
focused on sales through the more remunerative trade channel. The Company’s 
sales through the trade channel during the September quarter was at 82% versus 
79% in the same period last year.

However, thanks to better price realization, Birla Jute registered a 17% growth in 
revenue during the quarter. With 143% jump in export sales and 64% growth in 
sales of shopping bags, the Jute Division’s revenue for the quarter rose to Rs 155 
crore versus Rs 132 crore in the same period a year earlier. As result, Birla Jute’s 
operating profit for the quarter was at Rs 6.16 crore against Rs 4.02 crore in the same 
period a year earlier. 
Outlook: Realization from cement sales has started to improve from October as 
construction activities regain momentum. Government spending on infrastructure 
is expected to be the key growth driver. Construction activities in the private sector, 
too, is gradually improving. But cost pressure will persist and may continue to 
impact overall profitability. 
 
Birla Corporation Limited is the flagship Company of the MP Birla Group. 
Incorporated as Birla Jute Manufacturing Company Limited in 1919, it was given 
shape by Syt MP Birla. The Company has interest in cement and jute goods. Its Birla 
Jute Mills is the first jute mill started by an Indian entrepreneur. The Company and 
its subsidiary, RCCPL Pvt Ltd, have 10 cement plants in eight locations across the 
country, with an annual installed capacity of 21.4 million tons. The Company 
produces an array of cement products, under the MP Birla Cement brand, suited to 
different climatic conditions as well as consumer segments. It also sells 
construction chemicals and wall putty. 
For more information, visit www.birlacorporation.com
