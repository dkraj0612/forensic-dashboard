Pursuant to Regulation 30 and 51 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’), this is to inform you that: 
1. Shri Kalidas Pramanik, Chief Marketing Officer of the Company had requested for early 
superannuation from the employment of the Company with effect from close of business hours 
of 9th September, 2026 owing to ongoing family health challenges that require his personal 
attention and commitment. Accordingly, he shall cease to be the Senior Management Personnel 
of the Company with effect from 10th September, 2026. Further, the Company has received 
confirmation from Shri Pramanik stating that there is no other material reason for his early 
superannuation other than those stated above. Shri Pramanik will, however, continue to be 
associated with the Company in an advisory capacity. 
 
A copy of the letter received from Shri Kalidas Pramanik intimating his early superannuation is 
enclosed herewith. 
 
2. Shri Tamal Kumar Pal, currently serving as President – Corporate Development has been 
designated as President- National Sales Head and as a Senior Management Personnel of the 
Company with effect from 10th September, 2026. 
 
The details as required under Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January, 2026 are enclosed as 
“Annexure - A”. 
 
The aforesaid event occurred on 9th September, 2026 at around 3.00 p.m.  
 
This is for your information and record. 
 
 
Thanking you,  
Yours faithfully,
