Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform that the Company has received notices from 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”) and BSE Limited (“BSE”) dated August 14, 2026 regarding 
imposition of fine for alleged non-compliance with Regulation 31 of the SEBI Listing Regulations pertaining to Non-
submission of shareholding pattern within the period prescribed. 
 
The NSE and BSE have levied a fine amounting to Rs. 48,000/- along with applicable GST aggregating to Rs. 56,640/- 
each on the Company for the aforesaid alleged non-compliance. 
 
The Company is examining the notices received from the Exchanges and shall take appropriate steps in the matter. 
 
The aforesaid matter shall also be placed before the Board of Directors of the Company in the ensuing Board Meeting 
in compliance with the applicable provisions of SEBI Listing Regulations and the circulars issued thereunder.
