Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform that the Company has 
received notices from National Stock Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”) and BSE Limited (“BSE”) dated 
August 20, 2026 regarding imposition of fine for alleged non-compliance with Regulation 13(3) of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations with respect to Non-submission of the statement on shareholder complaints within the 
period prescribed, and Regulation 76 of the SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018 with 
respect to Non-submission of Reconciliation of share Capital audit Report. 
 
The NSE and BSE have levied a fine amounting to Rs. 21,000/- along with applicable GST aggregating to 
Rs. 24,780/- each on the Company for the aforesaid alleged non-compliance. 
 
The Company is examining the notices received from the Exchanges and shall take appropriate steps in the 
matter. 
 
The aforesaid matter shall also be placed before the Board of Directors of the Company in the ensuing Board 
Meeting in compliance with the applicable provisions of SEBI Listing Regulations and the circulars issued 
thereunder. 
 
Relevant details as required under Schedule III to the Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 are enclosed herewith as Annexure A.
