PRESS RELEASE 
Autoline Industries Reports Q2 FY27 Revenue of ₹304.25 Crore, Up 76.1% YoY 
EBITDA rises 48.1% YoY to ₹24.86 crore; PBT increases to ₹6.15 crore 
 
Pune, 6 November 2026: Autoline Industries Limited (BSE: 532797 | NSE: AUTOIND), a manufacturer 
and supplier of automotive components and assemblies to major OEMs in India, today announced 
its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half-year ended 30 September 2026. 
 
The Company reported Q2 FY27 revenue from operations of ₹304.25 crore, compared with ₹172.79 
crore in Q2 FY26, an increase of 76.1% YoY. EBITDA rose to ₹24.66 crore from ₹16.79 crore, up 46.9% 
YoY. Sequentially, revenue increased 14.8% and EBITDA increased 28.9% over Q1 FY27, reflecting 
higher customer volumes and improved manufacturing utilisation. 
 
EBITDA margin improved to 8.11% from 7.22% in Q1 FY27, a sequential increase of 89 basis points, 
although it remained below 9.72% in Q2 FY26. Margins during H1 FY27 lower, primarily due to elevated 
costs arising from the impact of the war during Q1 and Business mix. However, a sequential recovery 
was witnessed in Q2. The company expects margins to improve further in H2, driven by higher capacity 
utilisation, improved fixed-overhead absorption and continued focus on operational efficiencies. 
 
PBT increased to ₹6.15 crore from ₹2.83 crore in Q2 FY26 and ₹2.00 crore in Q1 FY27. PAT stood at 
₹6.63 crore, compared with ₹3.15 crore in Q2 FY26. For H1 FY27, revenue from operations increased 
to ₹569.34 crore from ₹324.30 crore in H1 FY26, while EBITDA grew to ₹43.99 crore from ₹30.07 crore. 
H1 PAT was ₹8.33 crore, against ₹16.65 crore in the previous year; the earlier period included 
exceptional income and a MAT-credit write-off, affecting comparability.

Management Comment 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Shivaji Akhade, Managing Director, Autoline Industries Limited, 
said: 
“Q2 FY27 marks an important inflection point in Autoline's operating performance, reflecting stronger 
business momentum, improving capacity utilisation and a sequential recovery in profitability. The 
Company's strategic focus is now firmly on converting revenue growth into sustainable margins, 
improved cash generation and long-term stakeholder value.” 
 
Strategic Priorities for H2 FY27 
The H2 FY27 execution agenda centers on four priorities: protecting delivery performance for 
expanding CV and PV programmes; recovering input-cost and conversion margins through customer 
price revisions, VAVE and procurement measures; improving cash conversion through receivables, 
tooling billing and inventory discipline; and maintaining rigorous review of financing costs, capacity 
ramp-up and capital allocation. 
 
About Autoline Industries Limited 
Autoline Industries Limited manufactures and supplies automotive components and assemblies to 
leading OEMs in India. Its portfolio includes sheet-metal components, fabricated assemblies, tooling, 
welded structures and value-added engineering solutions. With manufacturing facilities across key 
automotive hubs, the Company supports customers through engineering capability, scalable 
manufacturing and programme execution.
