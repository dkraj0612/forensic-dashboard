PRESS RELEASE: FINANCIAL RESULTS 
Akme Fintrade (India) Limited crosses the Rs. 1,000 crore AUM milestone; 
AUM up 35% YoY, H1 FY27 PAT up 19% YoY 
Q2 disbursements up 44% QoQ | Capital adequacy at 44.95% 
Udaipur, October 30, 2026: The Board of Directors of Akme Fintrade (India) Limited, an RBI-registered NBFC focused 
on vehicle and MSME finance in rural and semi-urban India, at its meeting held on October 30, 2026 approved the 
unaudited standalone financial results for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026, which have been subjected 
to a limited review by the Statutory Auditors. 
Key Highlights – H1 FY27 (vs H1 FY26) 
• Assets Under Management (AUM) crossed the Rs. 1,000 crore milestone to reach Rs. 1,039.35 crore as of 
September 30, 2026, up 35.43% YoY from Rs. 767.46 crore. 
• Disbursements own books grew 22.18% YoY to Rs. 205.10 crore, against Rs. 167.87 crore in H1 FY26. 
• Interest Income rose 30.92% YoY to Rs. 84.80 crore, against Rs. 64.77 crore. 
• Net Interest Income (NII) increased 18.45% YoY to Rs. 47.77 crore, against Rs. 40.33 crore. 
• Profit After Tax (PAT) grew 19.48% YoY to Rs. 23.49 crore, against Rs. 19.66 crore. 
• Earnings Per Share (EPS) increased 17.39% YoY to Rs. 0.54, against Rs. 0.46. 
• Net Interest Margin (NIM) remained healthy at 11.48% (annualised) for H1 FY27, reflecting the Company's ability 
to maintain strong spreads despite a rising interest-rate environment. 
• Return on Assets (RoAA) at 4.68% and Return on Equity (RoAE) at 10.45% (annualised). 
• Gross NPA improved to 2.88% (2.96% in September 2025; 2.91% in June 2026) and Net NPA to 1.39% (1.43% in 
September 2025; 1.41% in June 2026), with Provision Coverage Ratio at 51.74%. 
• Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) stood at 44.95% (Tier I: 44.15%), nearly three times the regulatory minimum of 
15%, providing ample headroom for growth.

Management Commentary 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Akash Jain, Chief Executive Officer, Akme Fintrade (India) Limited, said: 
"The first half of FY27 has been a milestone period for Akme Fintrade. Our AUM crossed Rs. 1,000 crore and grew 35% 
year-on-year, well ahead of the roughly 20% growth expected for the NBFC sector this quarter. Disbursements picked up 
sharply in the second quarter, rising 44% over Q1, as pre-festive demand for vehicle and MSME credit strengthened across 
our rural and semi-urban markets. Just as importantly, we grew without loosening our standards: both GNPA and NNPA 
improved for the quarter. 
The second half of the year is seasonally our strongest. Navratri this year saw strong two-wheeler demand compared with 
two years ago and electric-vehicle sales rising by more than a quarter. We expect Dhanteras and Diwali, the wedding 
season and kharif harvest cash flows to keep vehicle and small-business credit demand firm in our markets. Through Akme 
GreenX we are well positioned to finance the steady shift toward electric two-wheelers, clean mobility and rooftop solar, 
while our insurance partnership adds fee income and value for customers. 
We will stay watchful of pockets of stress in small-ticket MSME lending and of the impact of high fuel prices on transport 
operators, and will continue to put asset quality ahead of volume. We remain confident of sustaining our growth momentum 
through H2 FY27, while investing in technology to improve turnaround times, collections and customer experience." 
About Akme Fintrade (India) Limited 
Akme Fintrade (India) Limited ("Aasaan Loans") is an RBI-registered, non-deposit-taking Non-Banking Financial 
Company listed on BSE and NSE. Incorporated in 1996 and headquartered in Udaipur, Rajasthan, the Company provides 
vehicle finance and MSME/business loans to individuals and small business owners in rural and semi-urban India, 
segments that are often underserved by formal credit. Its vehicle finance portfolio covers used commercial vehicles, new 
and used two-wheelers and electric vehicles, while its business finance products serve the working-capital and growth 
needs of small enterprises.
