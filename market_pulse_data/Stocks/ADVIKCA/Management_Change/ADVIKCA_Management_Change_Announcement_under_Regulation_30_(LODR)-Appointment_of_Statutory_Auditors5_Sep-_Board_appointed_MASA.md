Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
In compliance with the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), Regulations 2015, we wish to inform you that the Board 
of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. September 05, 2026 at the Registered 
Office of the Company, inter-alia, transact the following businesses: 
 
1. APPOINTMENT OF STATUTORY AUDITORS TO FILL CASUAL VACANCY 
 
The Board of Directors, based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee, approved the 
appointment of M/s. MASAR & Co., Chartered Accountants, as the Statutory Auditors of the 
Company, with effect from September 5, 2026, to fill the casual vacancy caused by the 
resignation of M/s. KSMC & Associates, Chartered Accountants, subject to the approval of the 
Members of the Company. 
 
The Board further recommended to the Members for their approval at the ensuing 41st Annual 
General Meeting, the appointment of M/s. MASAR & Co., Chartered Accountants, as the 
Statutory Auditors of the Company for a term of five years, to hold office from the conclusion 
of the 41st Annual General Meeting until the conclusion of the 46th Annual General Meeting to 
be held in the year 2031. 
 
The requisite details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read with the SEBI Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFDPOD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are enclosed 
herewith as Annexure-1. 
 
2. APPROVAL OF RELATED PARTY TRANSACTION 
 
The Board, based on the approval of the Audit Committee, considered and approved the 
Related Party Transactions pursuant to Section 188 read with Regulation 23 and other 
applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, subject to the approval of the Members of the Company.

3. CESSATION OF INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS AND RECONSTITUTION OF VARIOUS 
COMMITTEES OF THE BOARD 
 
The Board took note of the resignation letters tendered by Mr. Devender Kumar Garg (DIN: 
02316543) and Mr. Rajnish Kumar Gupta (DIN: 08483800), Independent Directors of the 
Company, and accordingly noted and accepted their resignations from the office of 
Independent Director of the Company with immediate effect.  
 
The Company hereby confirms that there are no material reasons for the resignations other 
than those stated in the respective resignation letters. 
 
Copies of the resignation letters tendered by the aforesaid Directors are enclosed herewith as 
Annexure-2. 
 
Further, the requisite details under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read with the applicable SEBI Master Circular, in 
respect of the aforesaid cessation of Directors, are enclosed herewith as Annexure-3. 
 
Consequent upon the aforesaid cessation, the Board approved the reconstitution of various 
Committees in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. The revised composition of 
the Committees is enclosed herewith as Annexure-4. 
 
4. CONVENING OF THE 41ST ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF THE COMPANY 
 
The Board approved the convening of the 41st Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Company 
on Wednesday, September 30, 2026 at 2:00 P.M. through Video Conferencing (“VC”)/Other Audio-
Visual Means (“OAVM”), in accordance with the applicable provisions.  
 
The Board also approved the Notice of the AGM and the Annual Report of the Company for the 
Financial Year 2025-26, comprising, inter alia, the Directors’ Report, Corporate Governance Report, 
Secretarial Audit Report and other requisite reports, statements and documents, for circulation to 
the Members. 
 
The Board further approved the closure of the Register of Members and Share Transfer Books of 
the Company from Thursday, September 24, 2026 to Wednesday, September 30, 2026, both days 
inclusive, for the purpose of the 41st AGM. 
 
The Board also approved the arrangements for conducting the remote e-voting and e-voting process 
at the AGM and authorised NSDL to provide the remote e-voting and e-voting facility, along with 
such other matters as may be necessary and incidental for convening and conducting the AGM. 
 
The Notice of the 41st AGM, together with the Annual Report for FY 2025-26, shall be circulated 
separately to the Members in due course in accordance with the applicable provisions. 
 
The Board Meeting commenced at 10:45 A.M. and concluded at 12:20 P.M.
