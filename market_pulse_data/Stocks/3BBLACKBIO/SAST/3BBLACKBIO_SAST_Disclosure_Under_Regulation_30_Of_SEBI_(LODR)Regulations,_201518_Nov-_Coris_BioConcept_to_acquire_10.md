BUSINESS UPDATE: Coris BioConcept Announces Strategic Acquisition of 
100% shareholding of Bilex SRL to Optimize Costs and Secure Facility 
Ownership 
 
November 18th 2026, Gembloux, BELGIUM: Coris BioConcept SRL, a leading 
innovator in rapid diagnostic kits for AMR, today announced a strategic agreement to 
acquire 100% of the shares of Bilex SRL from existing shareholders Mr. Thierry 
Leclipteux and Mr. Joseph Bertinetti.  
Bilex SRL is the owner of land and building where Coris office and manufacturing 
plant is situated. Following the closing of this transaction, Bilex SRL will operate as a 
wholly-owned subsidiary of Coris BioConcept. This corporate acquisition transitions 
Coris BioConcept from a tenant to an owner-occupier. By consolidating Bilex SRL 
into its corporate structure, intercompany rental payments will get eliminated. 
This ultra-modern building was built in 2023  is located in the Créalys® Science Park 
Gembloux on a 6,443 m2 land parcel. The building has a surface area of 2,000 m² 
which brings together the company’s administrative, offices and labs. It includes 
state-of-the-art laboratories equipped with containment airlocks and temperature-
controlled and humidity-controlled environments. These are essential to ensure that 
research conducted here is accurate and safe. This building is equipped with 256 
solar panels, two 20,000-litre rainwater-collection tanks to supply the bathrooms, a 
thermodynamic tank and optimized insulation. These installations reflect the 
company’s desire to minimize its environmental impact while continuing to develop. 
The restructuring will generate an immediate net annual savings of €140,000 before 
depreciation. Moving forward, the only property-related expense reflected on the 
consolidated financial statements will be the direct bank interest paid on Bilex’s 
existing long term building loans apart from depreciation. 
This transaction aligns with Coris BioConcept's commitment to, long-term cost 
optimization, and securing the permanent infrastructure required to support its global 
diagnostic pipeline. 
Mr. Sébastien Goenen (CEO – Coris Bioconcept SRL) said, “We are pleased to 
announce the acquisition of Bilex SRL. Transitioning from tenant to owner-occupier 
secures long-term control over our core operating infrastructure and delivers 
immediate, recurring savings to our cost base. Owning our Gembloux facility gives 
us the stability and flexibility to invest in our manufacturing capabilities and to scale 
operations in line with growing global demand for our rapid diagnostic solutions.”  
Mr. Dhirendra Dubey (Founder CEO – 3B BlackBio Dx Limited) said, “The 
acquisition of Bilex marks a prudent step in strengthening the operational foundation 
of Coris, our European subsidiary and a recognized leader in lateral flow assay 
technology for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) diagnostics. Securing ownership of its 
Gembloux headquarters and manufacturing facility optimizes its rental outflows, and 
is expected to provide long-term certainty over a critical operating asset.”

About 3B BlackBio Dx Limited: 
 
3B is a research-driven company dedicated to the development, validation, and 
manufacturing of high-quality in vitro diagnostic (IVD) products. Under its brands, 
TRUPCR®, TRURAPID®, and TRUNGS®. 3B offers a comprehensive portfolio 
focused on oncology and infectious disease diagnostics. 
 
With over 14 years of industry expertise and more than 100 successfully launched 
products, 3B is committed to advancing patient care through innovative and reliable 
diagnostic solutions. All molecular diagnostic kits are developed and manufactured in 
compliance with the highest quality standards, backed by ISO 13485:2016 
certification through BSI, UK. 
 
About Coris Bioconcept SRL: 
 
Founded in 1996 and headquartered in Gembloux, Belgium, Coris develops, 
manufactures and distributes in vitro diagnostic (IVD) rapid test kits, with a primary 
focus on lateral flow assays designed for use by healthcare professionals. 
 
Coris is ISO 13485 certified, ensuring the highest standards of quality and regulatory 
compliance across its operations. The company distributes its products through a 
global network of distributors in over 60 countries, reaching markets in Europe, Asia, 
South America, Africa, and Oceania.
