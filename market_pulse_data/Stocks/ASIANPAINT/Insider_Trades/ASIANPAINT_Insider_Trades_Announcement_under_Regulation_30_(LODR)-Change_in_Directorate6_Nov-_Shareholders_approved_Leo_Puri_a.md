Sub:  Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

The Company, vide its letter no. APL/SEC/57/2026-27/35 dated 7th September 2026, had 
informed the stock exchanges  that the Board of Directors of the Company (‘the Board’), at its 
meeting held on the same day, based on the recommendation of the Nomination and 
Remuneration Committee, had unanimously approved the appointment of Mr. Leo Puri  
(DIN: 01764813) as an Additional and Independent Director of the Company for a first term of five 
(5) consecutive years commencing from 7th September 2026 to 6th September 2031, subject to 
the approval of the shareholders of the Company through Postal Ballot. Mr. Leo Puri shall assume 
office as Chairman of the Board with effect from 23rd January 2027, upon completion of the term 
of Mr. R. Seshasayee (DIN: 00047985) as Chairman and Independent Director on 22nd January 
2027. 
 
This is to inform you that, based on the voting results of the Postal Ballot conducted through 
remote e-Voting, the shareholders of the Company have approved the appointment of  
Mr. Leo Puri as an Independent Director of the Company. The special resolution shall be deemed 
to have been passed on Friday, 6th November 2026, being the last date specified for remote  
e-Voting. 
 
The voting results and the Scrutinizer’s Report have been submitted separately pursuant to 
Regulation 44 of the Listing Regulations. 
 
The brief profile of Mr. Leo Puri, as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, was 
disclosed by the Company vide its letter no. APL/SEC/57/2026-27/35 dated 7th September 2026 
and is available on the website of the Company at www.asianpaints.com. 
 
Mr. Leo Puri is not related to any of the Directors and/or Key Managerial Personnel and/or 
members of the Promoter(s) & Promoter(s) Group of the Company.
