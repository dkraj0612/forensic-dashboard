Sir/Madam, 
 
Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Commencement of commercial production at the 
VAE manufacturing facility at Dahej, Gujarat  
 
This is with reference to our earlier disclosures regarding the establishment of manufacturing 
facilities for Vinyl Acetate Ethylene Emulsion (VAE) and Vinyl Acetate Monomer (VAM), together 
with an ethylene storage and handling facility, at Dahej, Gujarat.  
 
We wish to inform you that the Company has today commenced commercial production of VAE 
at its VAE manufacturing facility in Dahej, Gujarat, having an installed capacity of 1,50,000 tons 
per annum. The VAE manufactured at the facility is intended to be primarily utilised for captive 
consumption at the Company’s paint manufacturing facilities.  
 
The commencement of commercial production at the facility marks a significant milestone in the 
Company's ongoing backward integration initiatives. 
 
The construction of VAM manufacturing, ethylene storage and handling facilities is in progress. 
The Company will continue to make necessary disclosures on any material developments in this 
regard from time to time. 
 
You are requested to take the above information on record.
