We write to inform you that the Eighty-Sixth Annual General Meeting (‘AGM’) of the Members of

Conferencing.

the Rules framed thereunder and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’) read with the relevant circulars issued

2026, 5:00 p.m.) and e-voting during the AGM (‘Instapoll’), in accordance with the provisions of the Act 
and the Listing Regulations.
