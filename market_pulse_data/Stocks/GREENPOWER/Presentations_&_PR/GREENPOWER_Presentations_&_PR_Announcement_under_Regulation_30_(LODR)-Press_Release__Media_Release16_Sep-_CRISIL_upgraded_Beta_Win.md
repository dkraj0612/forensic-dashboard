Rating upgrade for Material Subsidiary 
CRISIL Ratings Limited upgrades the rating for Beta Wind Farm Private Limited (BETA), material subsidiary of OGPCL. 
Chennai, September 16, 2026: Orient Green Power Company Limited (OGPCL), a leading renewable energy 
producer in India, announces that rating agency CRISIL Ratings Limited (CRISIL) has reviewed and upgraded the 
credit ratings for its material subsidiary, Beta Wind Farm Private Limited.  
Beta Wind Farm Private Limited (BETA) 
Beta Wind Farm is a key subsidiary of OGPCL and operates 241.6 MW of wind energy projects across Tamil Nadu, 
Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, and Karnataka. 
● Rating Improvement: CRISIL upgrades the rating of Beta to CRISIL BBB / Stable (upgraded from CRISIL 
BBB- / Positive) on the Rupee term loan facility of Rs. 617.79 crore availed from Indian Renewable 
Energy Development Agency Limited (IREDA) (outstanding IREDA amount as on date Rs.456.68 crores 
(Rs.478.74 crores as on March 31, 2026)). This upgrade is a one notch improvement from the CRISIL 
BBB-/ Positive assigned by Crisil earlier. This revision reaffirms the rating assigned by ICRA Limited to Beta on 
September 08, 2026. 
● This upgrade is assigned after considering the healthy operational performance resulting from 
improved generation and cash accruals while strengthening the debt servicing capacity of BETA.
