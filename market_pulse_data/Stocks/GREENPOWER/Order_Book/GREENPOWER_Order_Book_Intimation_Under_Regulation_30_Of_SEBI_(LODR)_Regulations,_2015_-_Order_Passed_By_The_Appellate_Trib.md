In furtherance to our earlier intimation dated October 04, 2023 with regard to pendency 
of litigations or disputes (if any), we wish to inform you that the Appellate Tribunal for 
Electricity (APTEL), vide its judgment dated November 17, 2026, has pronounced its order 
in the appeals filed by various wind power generators, including Beta Wind Farm Private 
Limited (Beta), a material subsidiary of the Company. 
 
The order is favourable to Beta and entitles Beta to receive payment for the energy 
supplied beyond the normative CUF, together with applicable interest, in accordance with 
the terms of the PPA.
