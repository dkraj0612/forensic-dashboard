Coal India Limited and ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel India Private Limited   
 
Reference the captioned subject, kindly note that Coal India Limited 
(CIL) and ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel India Private Limited (AMNS), have 
executed a Non-Binding Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 
12.08.2026 for exploring the possibility of utilizing syn-gas produced from a 
coal gasification facility, proposed to be established by CIL adjacent to/at the 
AMNS Paradip Pellet Plant coupled with CCUS. 
 
Under this Non-Binding MoU, initially the focus shall be to assess the 
technical, commercial, and implementation modalities of the project and to 
facilitate preparation of a Preliminary Feasibility Report (“PFR”) for evaluating 
project viability.
