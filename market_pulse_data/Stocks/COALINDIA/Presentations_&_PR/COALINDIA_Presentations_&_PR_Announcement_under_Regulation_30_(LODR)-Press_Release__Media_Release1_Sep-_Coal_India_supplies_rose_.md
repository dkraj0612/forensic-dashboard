Coal India’s Supplies Rise 5.5% in August FY’27 
Kolkata, September 1, 2026: Coal India Limited (CIL) increased its total coal supplies to 
60.60 million tonnes (MT) in August FY 2026–27, registering a 5.50% growth over the 57.40 
MT supplied during the corresponding month last year. 
The company also recorded a 4.5% growth in coal supplies to the power sector during the 
month, reaching 48.46 MT, compared to 46.39 MT in August FY 2025–26. 
Coal supplies to the non-regulated sector (NRS) also registered robust growth, increasing by 
9.6% to 12.12 MT in August FY 2026–27 from 11.06 MT in August last year. 
Coal India’s total coal supplies during the first five months of FY 2026–27, till August, reached 
322.90 MT, compared to 302.60 MT during the corresponding period of the previous year, 
registering a growth of 6.70%. 
 
The higher supplies also enabled Coal India to liquidate around 55 MT of pithead coal stocks 
during the first five months of FY 2026–27. With approximately 76 MT of coal currently 
available at its pitheads, CIL has sufficient inventory to support power generation requirements 
in the coming months.  
As the intense rainy spells gradually recede and drier months approach, the company is 
preparing itself to ramp up both production and supplies.
