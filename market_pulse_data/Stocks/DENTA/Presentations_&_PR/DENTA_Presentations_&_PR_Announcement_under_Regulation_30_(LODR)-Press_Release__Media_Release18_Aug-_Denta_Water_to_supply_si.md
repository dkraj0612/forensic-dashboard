Denta Water to supply 6 tanks from Tungabhadra River for ground water 
recharging and enhancement for its flagship Mukkumpi lift irrigation project 
  
 Tanks to pump out water from Tungabadra river to recharge ground water 
 Will also enhance underground water to boost irrigation facilities 
 
Bengaluru, August 18, 2026: Denta Water and Infra Solutions Limited ("Denta Water") (NSE: DENTA, BSE: 
544345), a leading civil engineering contractor specializing in water infrastructure projects, today announced 
that it is supplying a total of six tanks for its flagship Mukkumpi lift irrigation project. 
 
As part of the project, the tanks will be used to pump out water from Tungabadra river to recharge ground water 
and enhance underground water to boost irrigation facilities. 
 
The six tanks to be used for the project are Hirebenakal Tank, Venktagiri Tank, HRG Camp Tank, Lingadalli 
Tank, Mukumpi Tank and Chikkbenakal Tank. 
 
Key local representatives who participated and offered Ganga puja were MLA Shri Gaali Janardhan Reddy, Shri 
Manohar Gowda (Jilla Adhyaksha BJP & Gramina Gataka Adhyaksha), Shri DK Aagoli & Shri Anand Gowda, 
TP Chikkbenakal & Shri Veeresh Hirebenakal, and Shri Venktesh Nayak & Shri Mallesh Danapur Hirebenkal. 
 
Commenting on the company’s flagship project, Mr. C. Mruthyunjaya Swamy, Chairman of Denta Water, 
said: “This is a significant milestone for Denta Water & Infra Solutions and an encouraging development for 
the region’s irrigation infrastructure. The Mukkumpi Lift Irrigation Project, with its focus on groundwater 
recharge and improved irrigation facilities, has the potential to deliver long-term benefits to farmers and local 
communities.” 
 
The Company reported operating revenue of ₹586.64 million in Q1FY27, while EBITDA stood at ₹154.31 
million and Profit After Tax was ₹111.68 million. The Company continues to witness healthy order inflows and 
remains well positioned to capitalize on opportunities in the water infrastructure sector. The order book 
remained robust at ₹8,508.96 million as of 31 July 2026, providing strong revenue visibility for the coming 
quarters.  
 
About Denta Water and Infra Solutions Limited  
Denta Water and Infra Solutions Limited is a premier civil engineering company focused on water revitalization,
