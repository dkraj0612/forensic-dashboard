Committee and after due consideration, the Board of Directors of the Bank at its meeting held today,

from October 19, 2026.

deepen customer relationships and strengthen the Bank’s presence across these businesses. He would 
be a part of the Senior Management of the Bank.

Brief Profile 
Rahul Shukla, aged 57 years, is an accomplished banking leader 
with over three decades of experience spanning Wholesale, 
Corporate, Investment, Commercial and Rural Banking across 
India and Asia. At HDFC Bank, he led the Commercial & Rural 
Banking group, having earlier led Wholesale Banking. Prior to HDFC 
Bank, Rahul held senior leadership roles at Citi, including Head of 
the South Asia Corporate Bank and Asia-Pacific TMT Investment 
Banking. He brings deep expertise in managing large banking 
franchises, business strategy and franchise building, with a 
consistent track record of scaling businesses, improving returns and 
building market-leading positions. He is an MBA from IIM Bangalore 
and B.Tech. (Electrical Engineering) (Hons.) from IIT Varanasi.
