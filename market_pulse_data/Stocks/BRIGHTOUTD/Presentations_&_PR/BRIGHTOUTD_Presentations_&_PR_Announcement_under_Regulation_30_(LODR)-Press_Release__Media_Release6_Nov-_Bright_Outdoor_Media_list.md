Bright Outdoor Media Limited Makes Its Debut On The Main Boards 
Of BSE And NSE, Marking A New Chapter In Its Growth Journey 
India's First Listed Outdoor Media Company Strengthens Its Position in the OOH Advertising 
Industry as It Enters Its Next Phase of Growth. 
Mumbai, 6th November 2026, Bright Outdoor Media Limited, one of India's established players in the Out-
of-Home (OOH) advertising industry, has achieved a signiﬁcant milestone with its successful listing on 
the Main Boards of BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE). The development marks 
an important chapter in the Company's corporate journey, further strengthening its presence in India's 
capital markets. 
With this achievement, Bright Outdoor Media Limited continues to reinforce its position as India's First 
Listed Outdoor Media Company, a distinction it established when it became the ﬁrst company from the 
OOH advertising industry to list on the BSE SME Platform on March 24, 2023. 
The transition from the SME platform to the Main Boards of BSE and NSE represents the Company's 
progress over the years and its continued efforts towards building a scalable, diversiﬁed and future-ready 
outdoor advertising business. The listing also provides the Company with a wider platform to engage with 
investors, market participants and other stakeholders as it pursues its long-term growth objectives. 
From an Industry First to a New Chapter on the Main Boards 
Bright Outdoor Media Limited's journey has been deﬁned by its focus on expanding its advertising 
network, strengthening its market presence and identifying emerging opportunities across the OOH 
advertising landscape. 
Over the years, the Company has expanded its footprint through strategic advertising locations, 
government and infrastructure-led projects, railway advertising opportunities and an expanding portfolio 
of traditional and digital advertising assets. 
Its participation in projects such as the Navi Mumbai Metro Line advertising tender and the Vile Parle to 
Kandivali Railway Road Over Bridge (ROB) advertising tender with Indian Railways reﬂects its focus on 
accessing high-visibility advertising locations and strengthening its presence across public 
infrastructure and transit networks. 
The Company currently operates an extensive network of more than 1,350 hoardings across strategic 
geographies, alongside more than 50 large-format Digital LED Billboards. Its growing inventory enables 
the Company to offer advertisers a combination of traditional outdoor formats and technology-enabled 
digital advertising solutions. 
Bright 360: Expanding Beyond Traditional Outdoor Advertising 
A key part of the Company's forward strategy is its Bright 360 initiative, through which it aims to expand 
beyond conventional outdoor advertising and develop integrated branding and advertising solutions. 
Bright 360 reﬂects the Company's approach towards offering a wider range of services to clients by 
bringing together multiple advertising formats and brand visibility solutions under one platform.

About Bright Outdoor Media Limited 
Founded in 1980 and headquartered in Andheri, Mumbai, Bright Outdoor Media Limited is an established 
name in India's Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising industry, with over four decades of experience. The 
Company operates an extensive network of more than 1,350 hoardings across strategic geographies and 
owns more than 50 large-format Digital LED Billboards in Mumbai. It also trades in hoardings acquired 
from government, semi-government and private entities, further strengthening its market presence. 
The Company's portfolio includes strategic advertising ventures and contracts across major transit 
locations, government and infrastructure-led projects, traditional outdoor advertising and digital 
displays. Through its Bright 360 initiative, the Company is also working towards expanding its capabilities 
into integrated branding and advertising solutions. 
Bright Outdoor Media has also undertaken sustainability-led initiatives, including the installation of solar 
panels on hoardings to supply electricity to Indian Railways through a joint venture partner. In addition, 
its real estate operations contribute to its diversified business interests. 
Bright Outdoor Media Limited became India's first outdoor media company to be listed on the stock 
exchange when it debuted on the BSE SME Platform on March 24, 2023. Its successful listing on the Main 
Boards of BSE and NSE marks another important milestone in its journey as it continues to pursue 
opportunities across India's growing outdoor advertising industry.
