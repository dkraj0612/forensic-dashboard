> **Executive Summary:**  Acutaas Chemicals Limited announced a significant capital expenditure plan of up to Rs. 212 crores by FY2027-28 to establish a new greenfield/brownfield manufacturing plant in Gujarat for electronic grade chemicals. This substantial investment marks a key strategic initiative to enter high-value product segments and drive future growth. Other board approvals included routine re-appointments of key directors and the extension of an employee stock option scheme to subsidiary employees, supporting governance and talent retention.
