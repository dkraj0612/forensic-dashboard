With reference to our earlier intimation dated September 24, 2026, and pursuant to Regulation 30 
read with Schedule 11l of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that Acutaas Chemicals Limited (“the Company”) 
has completed the acquisition of 100% of the equity share capital of Enchem Ami Organics Private 
Limited, a step-down subsidiary of the Company, through the transfer of 10,000 equity shares of face 
value of Rs. 10/- each from Acutaas Chemicals Electrolytes Private Limited, a subsidiary of the 
Company. 
Consequent to the completion of the aforesaid acquisition on October 17, 2026, Enchem Ami Organics 
Private Limited has become a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company.
