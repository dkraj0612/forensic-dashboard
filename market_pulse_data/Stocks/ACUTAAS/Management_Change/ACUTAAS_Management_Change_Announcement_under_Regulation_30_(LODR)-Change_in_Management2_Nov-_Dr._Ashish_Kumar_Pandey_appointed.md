### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Acutaas Chemicals Limited has appointed Dr. Ashish Kumar Pandey, a seasoned professional with over 30 years of industry experience, as Assistant Vice President - Operations (Cluster Head - Unit 2 & 3), effective November 2, 2026. This strategic organizational restructuring involves two existing Senior Management Personnel, Mr. Anurag Shukla and Mr. Uday Desai, being declassified as SMPs due to revised reporting lines under Dr. Pandey, though they retain their unit head roles. While the company assures continued employment for the declassified executives, this shift in formal designation suggests a consolidation of operational leadership and merits monitoring for its broader impact on internal structure and morale.
