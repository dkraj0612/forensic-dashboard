### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Chambal Fertilisers and Chemicals Limited (CFCL) is acquiring a 26% stake in Clean Max Hachi Private Limited, a newly incorporated Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) developing a 5 MW captive hybrid wind-solar power generation facility in Rajasthan. This strategic investment of up to Rs. 6 Crore aims to achieve substantial cost savings by partly replacing purchased grid power with renewable energy and supports CFCL's sustainability objectives. A key point for consideration is that the SPV has not yet commenced operations and lacks historical financials, indicating an investment in a greenfield project with inherent development risk.
