### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  AIA Engineering Limited reported strong 12.4% revenue growth in Q1 FY2026-27; however, Profit After Tax (PAT) marginally declined due to a significant compression in EBITDA margins from 40.46% to 36.35%. This margin erosion was primarily driven by a substantial increase in "Other expenses" and a noticeable deterioration in working capital efficiency, as indicated by increased inventory and receivable days, which are key areas of concern. Despite these headwinds, finance costs were significantly reduced, and a strong order book of Rs. 977 Crores provides positive future revenue visibility.
