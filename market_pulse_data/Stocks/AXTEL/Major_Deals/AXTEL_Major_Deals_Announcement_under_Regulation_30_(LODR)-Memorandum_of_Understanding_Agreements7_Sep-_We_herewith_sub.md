Date: 7 September 2026 
Axtel Industries and FSID, IISc Enter into Technology Licensing Agreement for Advanced 
Cryogenic Grinding Technology 
Vadodara / Bengaluru, India – 2026 
Axtel Industries Limited (“Axtel”) and the Foundation for Science, Innovation and 
Development (FSID), Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru are pleased to announce 
that they have entered into a Technology Transfer agreement for the commercialization, by Axtel 
on an exclusive basis, of an advanced High-Performance Grinding Technology incorporating 
a Counter-Rotating Pin Mill and Cryogenic Conveying System.  Under this agreement, FSID 
will also transfer to Axtel the prototype of this system to permit accelerated commercialization 
by Axtel. FSID–IISc designed and manufactured entire machinery and plant indigenously under 
Phase II of the Capital Goods Scheme of the Ministry of Heavy Industries, Government of 
India. The scheme aims to develop indigenous technologies that reduce import dependence 
and can subsequently be commercialized in partnership with Indian companies. 
Commenting on this development, Mr. Ajay Desai, Executive Director of Axtel said that this 
unique cryogenic grinding technology, currently not available with Indian companies, will permit 
the manufacture of powders of polymers, rubbers, resins and other natural materials to fine 
sizes which cannot be achieved by conventional technologies.   
Mr. Ajay Parikh, Executive Director of Axtel said that making such equipment in India will 
significantly reduce India’s dependence of importation of these very high-cost machines, as 
also the importation of such powders which will result in substantial savings in foreign 
exchange.  
Mr. Desai and Mr. Parikh said they were excited by this partnership with FSID/IISc and are proud 
to be contributing to Prime Minister, Shri Narendra Modiji’s, objectives of a Atmanirbhar Bharat.   
About FSID: The Foundation for Science Innovation and Development (FSID) serves as the 
single-window interface for IISc to engage with industry, government and multiple funding 
agencies. It facilitates collaborative research, technology development and the execution of 
strategic programs. FSID connects IISc faculty, expertise and research infrastructure with 
national priorities and industrial requirements. It also supports technology transfer, product 
commercialization, deep-tech incubation and industry partnerships. Through these activities, 
FSID translates IISc’s scientific capabilities into meaningful economic, industrial and societal 
impact.
