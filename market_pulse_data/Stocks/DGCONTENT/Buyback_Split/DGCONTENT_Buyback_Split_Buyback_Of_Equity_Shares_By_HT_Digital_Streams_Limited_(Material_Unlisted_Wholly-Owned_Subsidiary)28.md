Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is to inform you that, the Board of Directors of HT Digital Streams Limited, material unlisted 
wholly-owned subsidiary (HTDS) today i.e., 28th September, 2026, has approved the proposal 
to buy-back upto 29,26,820 (Twenty Nine lacs twenty six thousand and eight hundred twenty) 
fully paid-up equity shares of Rs. 10/- each (representing 25% of total equity share capital of 
HTDS), at a price of Rs. 87.68 per equity share. 
The aforesaid buy-back will not entail any change in the shareholding pattern of HTDS, as it will 
continue to be a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company.
