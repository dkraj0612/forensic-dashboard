Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), and in continuation of our earlier disclosures dated April 5, 2025 and 
April 7, 2025, regarding the acquisition of Genrx Pharmaceuticals Private Limited (in Liquidation), we wish 
to inform you that the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (“NCLT”), Mumbai Bench, vide its order 
dated October 13, 2026, has approved the acquisition of Genrx Pharmaceuticals Private Limited on a going 
concern basis. 
 
The Company downloaded the aforesaid order from the official website of the Hon’ble National Company 
Law Tribunal on October 13, 2026. 
 
The brief details of the aforesaid order, as required under Regulation 30(13) of the SEBI Listing Regulations 
read with the Industry Standards Note on Regulation 30, are enclosed herewith as “Annexure I” 
 
Further, pursuant to the provisions of SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 as amended 
& as per the Company’s code of Internal Procedures and Conduct for Regulating, Monitoring & Reporting 
of trading in securities, the Trading window for dealing in securities of the Bajaj Healthcare Limited shall 
continue to remain closed for all Designated Persons including their immediate relatives in terms of our 
earlier disclosure dated September 23, 2026. 
 
You are requested to take the same on record. 
  
Thanking You, 
 
 
For and on behalf of Bajaj Healthcare Limited
