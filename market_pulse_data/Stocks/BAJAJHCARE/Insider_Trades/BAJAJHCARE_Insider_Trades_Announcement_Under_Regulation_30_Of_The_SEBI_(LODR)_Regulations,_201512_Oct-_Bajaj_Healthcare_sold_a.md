Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 
2015, this is to inform you that the Company has executed a Deed of Assignment with Spa Vet Private 
Limited and Indosol Pure Technologies Private Limited for the sale of an undertaking of the Company 
situated at Plot No. D-2/CH/42 & D-2/CH/43 Dahej Industrial Area, GIDC, Bharuch, Gujarat.  
 
We further confirm that none of the promoter/ promoter group/ group companies have any interest in the 
aforesaid buyers and the transaction does not fall within the purview of related party transactions. 
 
Pursuant to the provisions of SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 as amended & as per 
the Company’s code of Internal Procedures and Conduct for Regulating, Monitoring & Reporting of trading 
in securities, the Trading window for dealing in securities of the Bajaj Healthcare Limited shall continue to 
remain closed for all Designated Persons including their immediate relatives in terms of our earlier 
disclosure dated September 23, 2026.
