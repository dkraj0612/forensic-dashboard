INVESTOR RELEASE 
Bajaj Healthcare reports Q2 & H1 FY27 results 
Revenue Up 18.9% YoY; EBITDA Up 12.2% and PAT Up 26.9% YoY 
Mumbai, 17th October 2026: Bajaj Healthcare Limited, one of the leading manufacturers of APIs, Intermediates and 
Formulations, announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026. 
Highlights of Q2 FY27 Results 
● 
Revenue from operations at Rs. 175.9 Crore, up 18.9% YoY and 6.2% sequentially 
● 
Gross profit at Rs. 85.4 Crore, up 12.4% YoY; gross margin at 48.3%, in line with Q1 FY27 
● 
EBITDA at Rs. 32.1 Crore, up 12.2% YoY; EBITDA margin improved to 18.2% from 17.8% in Q1 FY27 
● 
Profit after tax at Rs. 14.1 Crore, up 26.9% YoY; PAT margin improved to 8.0% from 7.5% in Q2 FY26 
Highlights of H1 FY27 Results 
● 
Revenue from operations at Rs. 341.5 Crore, up 15.1% YoY 
● 
Gross profit at Rs. 165.8 Crore, up 14.2% YoY; gross margin at 48.3% 
● 
EBITDA at Rs. 61.7 Crore, up 14.2% YoY; EBITDA margin at 18.1% 
● 
Profit after tax at Rs. 27.8 Crore, up 21.1% YoY; PAT margin improved to 8.1% from 7.7% in H1 FY26 
Business Performance 
API — Domestic 
Domestic API sales for the quarter were Rs. 71.6 Crore, as compared to Rs. 76.3 Crore in Q2 FY26. For the first half of the 
year, domestic API sales grew 10.1% YoY to Rs. 163.9 Crore. 
API — Exports 
API export sales were Rs. 62.6 Crore for the quarter, up 28.5% YoY from Rs. 48.7 Crore in Q2 FY26 and 25.1% sequentially. 
Formulations 
Formulations revenue was Rs. 41.7 Crore for the quarter, up ~82% YoY from Rs. 22.9 Crore in Q2 FY26 and ~79% 
sequentially. During the quarter, the Company launched Cenobamate through its marketing partners in India. The segment 
has normalised as the Company had expected at the end of Q1 FY27. 
Quarterly Business Updates 
• Bajaj Healthcare became the first company in India to receive approval from the Drug Controller General of India 
(DCGI) to manufacture and market Cenobamate API and Tablets in six strengths, following the SEC recommendation 
received in Q1 FY27. Cenobamate is a next-generation anti-seizure medication for the treatment of partial-onset 
seizures in adults. 
• Commercial launch of Cenobamate Tablets commenced in Sept 2026 through 8 marketing partners. 
• India Ratings & Research upgraded the Company’s long-term rating from INA A-/Stable to IND A/Stable and affirmed 
the short-term rating at IND A1, reflecting the strengthened balance sheet and improved operating performance

Management Commentary 
Commenting on the Results, Mr. Anil Jain – Managing Director said, 
“We have delivered a strong second quarter, with healthy growth across key financial parameters. In fact, our API exports 
and formulations business combined crossed Rs. 100 Cr in revenue in Q2FY27. Further, revenue from operations grew 18.9% 
YoY to Rs. 175.9 Crore, EBITDA increased 12.2% YoY, with EBITDA margin improving sequentially to 18.2%, and PAT grew 
26.9% YoY. For the first half, revenue grew 15.1% and PAT grew 21.1% YoY. 
As we had expected, formulations rebounded strongly, growing 82% YoY, while API exports grew 28.5% YoY. Further, we 
have received NCLT approval for acquisition of GenRx which will enable us to further expand our formulation capabilities. 
The quarter also marked an important milestone in our innovation journey. We became the first company in India to receive 
DCGI approval for Cenobamate API and Tablets, reinforcing our commitment to building a differentiated specialty portfolio. 
Our peptide facility remains on track for commissioning in Q4FY27 and the upgrade in our credit rating reflects the stronger 
balance sheet that we have built. 
With a strong foundation and clear priorities, we remain committed to delivering sustainable growth and enhancing long-
term value for all our shareholders.”
