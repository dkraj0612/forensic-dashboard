### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Crown Lifters Limited successfully passed three ordinary resolutions at its 24th Annual General Meeting on September 24, 2026, including the adoption of financial statements and director re-appointments. However, a material impact occurred as a special resolution to significantly increase the overall limit of director remuneration failed, securing only 19.82% of valid votes. This failure stemmed from the exclusion of a substantial 7.86 million votes cast by related parties, which were deemed invalid for this specific resolution, indicating potential governance scrutiny around executive compensation.
