Catanduanes Integrated Digital Healthcare Project Awarded to Time Medical Philippines  – 
Advancing the Digitalization of Universal Health Care in the Philippines 
Time Medical Philippines (TM-P), a step-down subsidiary of Fischer Medical Ventures Limited 
(FMV), has been awarded a province-wide digital healthcare project for rollout across 
Catanduanes Province, deploying an integrated solution combining Health Kiosks with the 
PhilHealth-accredited FlynnCare Clinic Management System (CMS). The project represents the 
first province-wide implementation of this integrated healthcare model in the Philippines. 
 
The project marks an important milestone toward the digitalization of Universal Health Care (UHC) in 
the Philippines. Fischer Medical Ventures and Time Medical Philippines are fully committed to supporting 
the Philippine Government’s Universal Health Care priority program under President Ferdinand 
“Bongbong” Romualdez Marcos Jr., with a focus on making quality healthcare more accessible, connected 
and digitally enabled across communities. The Catanduanes Province rollout represents an important first step 
in translating this vision into a scalable province-wide digital healthcare model. 
 
Time Medical Philippines (TM-P). is proud to announce that it has been awarded the Catanduanes 
Province project to strengthen digital healthcare delivery and support the digitalization of 
Universal Health Care across the province. Together with its digital health partner, FlynnCare, 
TM-P will deploy an integrated healthcare solution combining Health Kiosks with the PhilHealth-
accredited FlynnCare Clinic Management System (CMS), connecting community-level healthcare 
delivery with structured digital clinical workflows and patient information. 
This integrated approach is particularly important for municipalities and barangays where access to 
comprehensive healthcare services and diagnostic infrastructure may be limited. By bringing together 
community-level 
screening, 
connected 
diagnostics, 
digital 
clinical 
workflows 
and 
teleconsultation, the solution is designed to bring healthcare closer to communities while strengthening 
coordination between frontline healthcare providers and the broader healthcare system. 
The Catanduanes rollout also demonstrates how digital technology can support the objectives of the 
Philippines’ Universal Health Care program. Connecting community healthcare delivery with a PhilHealth-
accredited CMS provides a foundation for more standardized clinical workflows, structured patient information 
and improved continuity of care, supporting a healthcare environment that is increasingly accessible, 
connected, coordinated and data-driven. 
With Catanduanes serving as an important implementation milestone, Time Medical Philippines, together 
with FlynnCare, aims to establish a scalable model for provincial healthcare digitalization. The 
project represents one of the early steps toward demonstrating how an integrated digital ecosystem can 
support the broader digitalization of Universal Health Care in the Philippines, with the potential to extend 
similar connected healthcare models across additional municipalities and provinces. 
Time Medical Philippines, together with FlynnCare, is also exploring similar large-scale 
opportunities across the Philippines and other markets with comparable healthcare needs, where 
integrated digital health, preventive screening and AI-enabled diagnostics are gaining increasing importance. 
With capabilities spanning Health Kiosks, the PhilHealth-accredited FlynnCare CMS, AI-powered 
screening, preventive health, TeleRadiology, Tele-Diagnosis and connected care pathways, the 
partnership is positioned to support broader healthcare digitalization initiatives. As healthcare systems 
continue to modernize, this integrated approach provides opportunities for scalable deployments, long-term 
technology support and sustainable recurring revenue models across multiple markets.

At Fischer Medical Ventures and Time Medical Philippines (TM-P), we remain deeply committed to 
supporting the transformation of healthcare delivery through integrated medical technology and digital health 
solutions. The province-wide Catanduanes project represents an important milestone in this journey. 
Together with our digital health partner, FlynnCare, we are integrating Health Kiosks with the PhilHealth-
accredited FlynnCare Clinic Management System (CMS) to enable municipal and barangay health teams 
with connected screening, structured clinical workflows, teleconsultation capabilities, and timely access to 
patient information. This integrated approach reflects our commitment to making accessible and technology-
enabled healthcare available to communities, irrespective of geography or resource availability. 
 
As the Universal Health Care vision advances across the 
Philippines, digitally connected healthcare will play an increasingly 
important role in improving access, strengthening coordination, reducing 
delays in care, and enhancing patient outcomes. By seamlessly connecting 
community-level screening, digital clinical workflows, patient information, 
and telehealth through an integrated platform, the Catanduanes rollout 
demonstrates how healthcare digitalization can be implemented 
across an entire province. We are proud to support this important 
national healthcare transformation and believe this model can provide a 
scalable foundation for expanding connected, accessible, and digitally 
enabled healthcare across additional provinces in the Philippines. 
 
 
 
— Ravindran Govindan, Chairman and Managing Director 
About Time Medical Philippines (TM-P). 
Time Medical Philippines (TM-P) is a subsidiary of Time Medical 
International Ventures (India) Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary 
of Fischer Medical Ventures Limited, a company listed on the BSE and NSE. 
Through its parent group’s capabilities spanning medical imaging, 
diagnostics, preventive healthcare, digital health and treatment solutions, 
TM-P is focused on bringing advanced, affordable and accessible 
healthcare technologies to the Philippines. Together with its technology 
partners, TM-P aims to deliver integrated healthcare solutions that combine 
medical technology and digital platforms to support the modernization and 
digitalization of healthcare delivery across the country. 
 
 
 
 
About Flynncare Health Innovations Private Limited :  
FlynnCare, a wholly owned subsidiary of Fischer Medical Ventures is a 
Chennai-based HealthTech company advancing AI-powered screening 
and early detection to transform preventive and community healthcare. 
Through its eHAP ecosystem, it integrates multi-domain diagnostic tools 
spanning vitals, cardiovascular, lung, mental, eye, oral, women’s, and 
cancer health, along with palliative care support. By combining intelligent 
technology with data-driven insights, FlynnCare delivers accessible, 
affordable, and inclusive solutions that empower individuals and 
strengthen healthcare systems.
