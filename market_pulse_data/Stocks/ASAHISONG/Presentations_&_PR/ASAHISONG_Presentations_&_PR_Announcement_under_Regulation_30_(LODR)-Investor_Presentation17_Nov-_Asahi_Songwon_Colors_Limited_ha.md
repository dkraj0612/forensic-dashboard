M A N A G I N G D I R E C T O R
“Q2 was a challenging quarter for the Azo business, and its performance was below our 
expectations. Sales have recovered to earlier levels after a difficult first quarter, and we see 
a positive trend of steady improvement. We have grown volumes well. Our focus now is on 
winning larger customers, which will be key to better profitability. The business remains 
comfortably EBITDA-positive and cash-positive. The pricing environment is still tough, and 
the industry changes we expected globally have not played out as we anticipated. The next 
step for us is to improve on both product quality and cost, and our teams are working on 
both. We expect H2FY27 to be better than H1FY27. 
In the API business, the business has grown volumes consistently over the last three years 
at almost 18% per year, however price pressure has kept performance subdued. As price 
pressure eases, we expect its performance to improve in the second half.
Strong cash generation across our businesses has let us continue to deleverage. We 
reduced gross borrowings by about ₹49 crore during the first half, and our balance sheet is 
now considerably stronger. As debt falls and profitability improves, we are carefully 
evaluating the right opportunities for the Group’s next phase of growth”
