### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Advent Hotels International Limited announced the resignation of Independent Director Mr. Rajeev RA, effective September 25, 2026. This resignation was necessitated by an RBI circular restricting NBFCs from granting facilities to companies with common directors, as Mr. RA held directorships in both Advent Hotels and Authum Investment & Infrastructure Limited, an NBFC from which Advent is seeking a financial loan. This action, while addressing a regulatory compliance issue, highlights Advent's reliance on this specific financing and required a structural change to facilitate the loan.
