Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 31(2) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and 
Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 – Release of encumbrance over equity shares of Advent Hotels 
International Limited 
We wish to inform you that 31,80,433 (Thirty One Lakh Eighty Thousand Four Hundred and Thirty Three) 
equity shares of Advent Hotels International Limited (formerly known as Shiva Realtors Suburban Private 
Limited) (“Company” / “Resulting Company”) held by us have been released from pledge. 
At the outset, we wish to inform that we held equity shares in Valor Estate Limited (formerly known as D 
B Realty Limited) (“Amalgamated Company” / “Demerged Company”), which shares were pledged in 
favour of IDBI Trusteeship Services Limited, acting as the Share Pledge Trustee on behalf of the lender, in 
connection with the facility sanctioned by HDFC Limited (“Lender/ HDFC Limited now merged with 
HDFC Bank Limited”) to MIG (Bandra) Realtors & Builders Private Limited (“Borrower”), a wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Demerged Company. 
Pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Amalgamation and Arrangement amongst Valor Estate Limited 
(Amalgamated Company / Demerged Company), Esteem Properties Private Limited (“Amalgamating 
Company”) and Advent Hotels International Limited (Resulting Company), and their respective 
shareholders and creditors (“Scheme”), as sanctioned by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, the 
shareholders of the Demerged Company were allotted equity shares of the Resulting Company in the 
prescribed share entitlement ratio. In terms of the Scheme, the pledge subsisting over the equity shares of 
the Demerged Company stood automatically extended to, and was correspondingly created over, the equity 
shares of the Resulting Company so allotted. 
The Borrower has since fully repaid the facility sanctioned by the Lender. Consequently, the pledge created 
over the aforesaid 31,80,433 equity shares of the Company has been released by the Share Pledge Trustee, 
and the said shares now stand free of encumbrance. 
Kindly take the same on record. 
Thanking You,
