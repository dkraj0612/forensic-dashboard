Navratna  CPSE  signs  term  loan  agreement  with  Damodar  Valley  Corporation  for

Financing  strengthens  lRFC's  role  as  a  long-term   partner  for  rallway-linked  clean

by 2030
New Delhi, September 28, 2026:  Indian Railway Finance Corporation  Limited  (lRFC),
a  Navratna  Central  Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Railways,  has taken
another  significant  step  in  its  strategic  expansion  and  diversification  with  the  signing
of a  Rs 4,200 crore Term  Loan Agreement with  Damodar Valley Corporation  (DVC) to
finance  Its  renewable energy projects across Jharkhand  and West Bengal.
The transaction  marks an  important milestone  in  lRFC's evolving  role  as a  diversified
Infrastructure  financier,  extending   its  established   long-term  financing  capabilities  to
the clean  energy ecosystem  while  remaining  closely  aligned  with  the  broader railway
ecosystem.  The  loan  agreement  was  signed  in  New  Delhi  in  the  presence  of senior
officials of lRFC and  DVC,  led  from  DVC  by Manish  Kumar,  Member (Finance),  DVC,
and  Manoj  Kumar Dubey,  Chairman  &  Managing  Director,  lRFC.
The  financing  will  support  DVC's  portfolio  of  floating  solar,  ground-mounted   solar,
rooftop  solar  and   Battery   Energy  Storage  System   (BESS)   projects,   leveraging   its
existing  land,  reservoirs  and transmission  infrastructure.
IF`FC, C,MD, Dubey, sa.id, "Renewable energy is no longer peripheral to the Railways;
it  is  at  the  core  of  Indian  Railways' journey towards  Net  Zero  Carbon  Emissions  by
2030.  Through  this  Rs 4,200  crore financing  to  DVC,  lRFC  is extending  its  long-term
financing  capability  to  the  clean  energy  infrastructure  that  will  power  lndia's  future.
This is IRFC 2.0 in  action -strategic diversification that remains firmly connected to
the Rallways ecosystem and to the natlon's larger development priorities."
"This  partnership  with  DVC  demonstrates  how  lRFC  can  bring  long-term  capital  to
renewable    energy    infrastructure    that    supports   the    Railways'    growing    energy
requirements  while  contributing  to  a   greener,   more  sustainable   and  future-ready
India," Dubey added.
The    transaction    thus    extends    lRFC's    financing    capabilities    into    clean    energy
infrastructure  that  complements  Its  core  railway financing  mandate,  while  supporting
the broader transition towards a sustainable energy ecosystem.
The  DVC transaction  marks another important step  in  lRFC's calibrated diversification
into  strategic railway-linked  infrastructure sectors,  building  on  its established  strength
in  providing  long-term  financing.
