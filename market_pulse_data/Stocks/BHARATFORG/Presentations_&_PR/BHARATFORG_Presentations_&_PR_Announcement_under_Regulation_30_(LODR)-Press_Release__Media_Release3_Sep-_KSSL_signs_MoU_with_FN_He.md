Kalyani Strategic Systems Partners with FN Herstal to Build  
Small Arms and Counter-UAS Capability in India 
 
- Memorandum of Understanding opens the path to a dedicated production center in India, technology 
cooperation and a potential joint venture in support of Make in India – 
 
New Delhi, India | September 3, 2026: Kalyani Strategic Systems Ltd. (KSSL), a wholly owned subsidiary of 
Bharat Forge Limited, and FN Herstal, a wholly owned subsidiary of FN Browning Group, today signed a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to pursue local manufacturing and industrial cooperation in support 
of India's defence requirements. The partnership brings together six decades of Indian advanced engineering 
and technology-led manufacturing at scale with more than 135 years of global leadership in small arms and 
integrated weapon systems. 
 
Under the MoU, KSSL and FN Herstal will evaluate the establishment of a dedicated production center in India 
and a Strategic Alliance / Joint Venture covering a range of FN Herstal defence solutions for the Indian market, 
spanning small arms, integrated weapon systems and counter-unmanned aerial system (C-UAS) solutions. The 
initiative is aligned with the Government of India's Make in India policy. It reflects a shared intent to build 
capability inside the country through local manufacturing, technology cooperation and value creation, 
strengthening India's defence industrial base and deepening domestic participation in defence procurement 
programmes. For the Indian armed forces, this translates into long-term security of supply and greater 
strategic autonomy in a category of systems that is central to operational readiness.  
 
Mr. Amit Kalyani, Vice Chairman and Joint Managing Director, Bharat Forge Ltd., said: 
"Kalyani Strategic Systems (KSSL) is proud to partner with FN Herstal to bring world-class technology, products, 
and manufacturing expertise to India. By combining FN Herstal's globally respected portfolio and decades of 
experience with KSSL's engineering excellence and industrial scale, we are creating a strong platform to deliver 
advanced defence solutions from India - for India and global markets. Together, we aim to support India's 
defence modernisation through locally manufactured, globally leading products while strengthening 
indigenous capabilities and advancing the vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat." 
 
Mr. Julien Compere, Chief Executive Officer, FN Browning Group, said: “India is a strategic partner for FN 
Herstal, and we are proud to take this important step with Kalyani Strategic Systems. This agreement reflects 
a shared ambition: to combine FN Herstal’s proven defence solutions, trusted by the world’s most demanding 
armed forces, with KSSL’s outstanding industrial capabilities to help build long-term sovereign capacity for the 
Indian Armed Forces. Together, we are committed to supporting the objectives of Make in India through local 
manufacturing, technology cooperation and the development of advanced defence capabilities, creating 
lasting value for India, its armed forces and our two companies.” 
 
Under the terms of the MoU, the parties will jointly explore and assess the feasibility of potential cooperation 
projects aimed primarily at meeting the requirements of the Indian market.

About Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited 
Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited (KSSL), a wholly owned subsidiary of Bharat Forge Limited, drives the 
defence business of the Kalyani Group, one of the world's foremost advanced engineering and technology-led 
manufacturing enterprises. With more than six decades of expertise in design, engineering, metallurgy and 
advanced manufacturing, KSSL delivers sovereign defence capability across the land, marine and unmanned 
domains, spanning artillery systems, armoured and protected vehicles, small arms, ammunition, marine 
systems, and unmanned platforms. Alongside battle-ready systems, KSSL enables partner nations to build their 
own defence industrial base through technology partnership, local industrialisation and assured through-life 
support, backed by proven production at scale and security of supply. Trusted by Indian and global armed 
forces and by governments seeking resilient, self-reliant capability, KSSL is a global defence partner of choice. 
 
About FN Herstal 
FN Herstal, headquartered in Herstal, Belgium, is the defence and security arm of FN Browning Group and a 
world leader in the design, development and manufacture of small calibre weapons, ammunition and 
integrated weapon systems for armed forces and law enforcement agencies. Founded in 1889, FN has 
equipped users in a large number of countries across more than 135 years, and today its solutions extend from 
individual small arms to crew-served and remotely operated weapon stations, precision systems and counter-
unmanned aerial system capability. FN Browning Group operates production sites across NATO and EU 
countries and reported revenue exceeding EUR 1 billion in 2025.
