Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
wish to inform you that Mr. Krishnakumar Vaidyanathan has tendered his resignation from the position of Executive 
Director & Chief Operating Officer (COO) of the Company.  
 
He has decided to step down to pursue professional opportunities outside the organization. The resignation has 
been accepted by the Chairman. Consequently, the Company has decided to relieve him from his duties with effect 
from the close of business hours on September 30, 2026.  
 
The Company places on record its sincere appreciation for the valuable contributions, leadership, and dedication 
demonstrated by Mr. Krishnakumar Vaidyanathan during his tenure with the organization.  
 
In accordance with requirement of regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III and SEBI Master 
Circular HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, details attached herein shall be 
taken on record. 
 
You are requested to take the above information on your record. 
  
Thanking You,
