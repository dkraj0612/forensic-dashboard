Headline: DreamFolks strengthens its Southeast Asia presence, showcasing 
Travel & Lifestyle experiences at Singapore FinTech Festival 2026 
As a Gold Sponsor and Exhibitor, DreamFolks showcased its technology-led beneﬁts platform, 
enabling banks, card networks, and ﬁntechs to deliver personalised privileges across customer 
journeys. 
Singapore, 19 November 2026: Dreamfolks Services Limited, India's leading travel and lifestyle 
experiences company, has strengthened its presence in Southeast Asia, with Singapore as its regional 
base. The company took part in the Singapore FinTech Festival (SFF) 2026 as a Gold Sponsor and 
Exhibitor, held from 18 to 20 November at Singapore EXPO. At the festival, DreamFolks showcased its 
comprehensive Total Banking Privileges solutions - via its API-ﬁrst tech platform that runs the entire 
beneﬁt lifecycle and enables ﬁnancial institutions to recognise and reward customers across payments, 
deposits, ﬁnancing and wealth, and not just on a credit /debit card. 
DreamFolks now runs its regional business from its Singapore oﬃce, which has a dedicated 
partnerships team. This gives banks, card networks, ﬁntechs, and wallets across Southeast Asia a local 
partner for everything from programme design and onboarding to account management, customer 
engagement and round-the-clock support. Through this model, DreamFolks positions itself as a long-
term partner rather than a one-time beneﬁts provider. 
Commenting on DreamFolks' participation at the festival, Liberatha Kallat, Founder, Chairperson and 
Managing Director, Dreamfolks Services Limited, said, “Southeast Asia is a key focus market for 
DreamFolks, with its Singapore presence serving as a strategic hub for strengthening partnerships and 
expanding its regional footprint. With our Banking Privileges across travel and lifestyle experiences, 
powered by our technology, global experience network and ﬂexible partnership model, DreamFolks 
enables institutions to turn privileges into a lasting driver of customer value and business growth.” 
At the event, DreamFolks demonstrated its API-ﬁrst beneﬁts platform, which runs the entire beneﬁt 
lifecycle: design, eligibility, delivery and analytics. The tech platform features an AI-powered beneﬁts 
designer, real-time eligibility and entitlement management, built-in abuse prevention, seamless 
delivery through a single integration and cost optimisation for clients.  DreamFolks’ Total Banking 
Privileges proposition enables banks, card networks, and ﬁntechs, to link personalised beneﬁts to 
customer behaviours, from card spending and savings milestones, to ﬁnancing and wealth 
relationships. 
The company also showcased its cross-border beneﬁts model, which enables eligibility veriﬁcation and 
beneﬁt redemption while allowing issuers to retain ownership of customer data. This is supported by 
DreamFolks’ network of 6,000+ experience touchpoints across 100+ countries and 500+ cities, and 
more than one million transactions a month. 
As part of the conference programme, Balaji Srinivasan, Executive Director & CTPO at DreamFolks 
addressed the Frontier Stage on 19 November in a session titled “Beyond Rates and Rewards: Finance 
Enters the Experience Economy.”  He explained how the experience economy is emerging as the next 
frontier of diﬀerentiation for banks, card networks and ﬁntechs, highlighting the evolving role of 
technology and strategic partnerships in shaping this shift.
