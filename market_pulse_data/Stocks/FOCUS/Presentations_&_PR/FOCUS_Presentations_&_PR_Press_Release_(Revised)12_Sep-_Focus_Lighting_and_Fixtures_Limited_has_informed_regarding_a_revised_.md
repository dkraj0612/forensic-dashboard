FOCUS LIGHTING STRENGTHENS COMMERCIAL PORTFOLIO FROM 
INGKA CENTRES INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED 
 
Mumbai | September 12, 2026 
Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited, a leading manufacturer in the lighting solutions and fixtures industry, has 
secured a significant order from Ingka Centres India Private Limited, valued at approximately Rs 5-10 crores. 
The order encompasses the manufacture, supply and delivery of LED lights and fixtures in accordance with the 
specifications and requirements of the customer. This order further reinforces Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited's 
capabilities in delivering high-quality, energy-efficient and innovative lighting solutions for large-scale projects. 
The Company believes that this order is a significant addition to its order book and further strengthens its relationship 
with reputed institutional customers. It also reflects the confidence placed in the Company's product quality, 
manufacturing capabilities and execution expertise. 
 
About Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited  
Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited (NSE – FOCUS) The Company was established in 2005 and is engaged in the 
business of manufacturing LED lights and Fixtures. The Company provides wide range of products in different 
verticals in field of lighting which includes Retail, Home, Railway and Outdoor – landscape. The Company is further 
expanding with patented technology in outdoor facade lighting. Its comprehension of lighting technology, the 
influence it can have on the viewer and the techno-aesthetic requirement of the market has enticed it to develop 
lighting solutions where the criteria are efficiency, colour rendition and trouble-free service life to achieve optimum 
lighting parameters such as luminous intensity, maintenance-friendliness and freedom from glare. The new range of 
luminaires have been designed and developed by the finest product designers from Germany, proficient in the field 
of lighting design; supported by a proficient team of Indian Professionals. The company is having its manufacturing 
unit at Sanad Gujarat.
