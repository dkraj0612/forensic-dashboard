FOCUS LIGHTING AND FIXTURES LIMITED SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETES LANDMARK LIGHTING 
PROJECT AT KANKARIA LAKE, AHMEDABAD 
 
Mumbai | August 17, 2026 
 
Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited, a leading manufacturer of innovative lighting solutions, is pleased to announce 
the successful completion of its prestigious lighting project at Kankaria Lake, one of the city's most iconic and largest 
lake in Ahmedabad and historically significant landmarks. 
 
The project, awarded by the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC), was originally announced by the Company 
on November 27, 2025, as a prestigious lighting project for the iconic Kankaria Lake. The successful completion of 
the project reflects the Company’s capabilities in executing large-scale, high-visibility lighting projects for prominent 
public infrastructure and civic landmarks. It further reinforces Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited’s commitment to 
delivering innovative and sustainable lighting solutions that combine design excellence with practical functionality. 
 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Amit Vinod Sheth, Managing Director of Focus Lighting And Fixtures 
Limited said, “The successful completion of the Kankaria Lake lighting project is a proud milestone for Focus 
Lighting And Fixtures Limited. Kankaria Lake is an integral part of Ahmedabad’s heritage, culture and identity, and 
being entrusted with enhancing its visual appeal through our lighting solutions has been both a privilege and a 
responsibility. We are delighted to have delivered a solution that brings together aesthetics, functionality and energy 
efficiency, while complementing the character of this iconic public space. The successful execution of this project 
reflects our team’s technical capabilities, execution strength and commitment to delivering quality lighting solutions 
at scale.” 
 
The Company continues to focus on expanding its presence across architectural, infrastructure, commercial and urban 
lighting applications, with an emphasis on innovative technology, energy-efficient solutions and execution excellence.  
 
With the successful completion of the Kankaria Lake project, Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited continues to 
illuminate spaces that brings together technology, sustainability and design to create lasting visual experiences. 
About Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited 
Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited (NSE – FOCUS) The Company was established in 2005 and is engaged in the 
business of manufacturing LED lights and Fixtures. The Company provides wide range of products in different verticals 
in field of lighting which includes Retail, Home, Railway and Outdoor – landscape. The Company is further expanding 
with patented technology in outdoor facade lighting. Its comprehension of lighting technology, the influence it can have 
on the viewer and the techno-aesthetic requirement of the market has enticed it to develop lighting solutions where the 
criteria are efficiency, colour rendition and trouble-free service life to achieve optimum lighting parameters such as 
luminous intensity, maintenance-friendliness and freedom from glare. The new range of luminaires have been designed 
and developed by the finest product designers from Germany, proficient in the field of lighting design; supported by a 
proficient team of Indian Professionals. The company is having its manufacturing unit at Sanad Gujarat.
