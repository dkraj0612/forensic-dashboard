Focus Lighting and Fixtures Limited Inaugurates Experience Centre in the United Kingdom,  
Marking a Significant Milestone in Its Global Expansion Journey 
 
Mumbai | November 18, 2026 
 
Focus Lighting and Fixtures Limited (“the Company”), a leading player in the lighting and fixtures industry, is pleased 
to announce the inauguration of its Experience Centre in the Islington, London, United Kingdom, marking a significant 
milestone in the Company's continued journey towards expanding its international presence and strengthening its 
footprint in key overseas markets. 
The inauguration of the Experience Centre represents an important step in the Company's global growth strategy and 
reflects its commitment to taking its expertise in innovative lighting solutions to international markets. The initiative is 
aimed at establishing closer engagement with customers, architects, interior designers, lighting consultants, developers 
and other industry professionals while creating greater awareness of the Company's product capabilities and design-led 
lighting solutions. 
The newly inaugurated Experience Centre has been conceptualised as a dedicated space to showcase the Company's 
comprehensive portfolio of lighting solutions across indoor and outdoor applications, with a strong emphasis on 
precision lighting, visual comfort, glare control and performance-driven design. The Centre will provide visitors 
with an opportunity to experience the Company's diverse range of lighting products, understand their technical and 
aesthetic features and explore solutions designed to meet varied architectural, commercial, residential and outdoor 
lighting requirements. 
At the core of the Company's lighting philosophy is the objective of delivering precise illumination, enhanced visual 
comfort and effective glare management without compromising on aesthetics and functionality. Through its range of 
thoughtfully engineered lighting fixtures, the Company seeks to create lighting environments that enhance the visual 
experience of a space while addressing the practical and technical requirements of modern architecture and design. 
The Experience Centre will enable visitors to experience firsthand how carefully designed lighting solutions can 
transform spaces through controlled illumination, anti-glare technology, appropriate light distribution and seamless 
integration with architectural elements. The display will also highlight the Company's capabilities in delivering lighting 
solutions for diverse indoor and outdoor environments, catering to the evolving requirements of contemporary lighting 
design. 
The initiative is expected to enhance the Company's visibility in the international lighting industry, facilitate engagement 
with the local design and architectural community and create opportunities to develop relationships with potential 
customers, distributors, channel partners and other stakeholders. The Company believes that establishing a physical 
presence in the United Kingdom will provide a platform to better understand regional market requirements, respond to 
evolving customer preferences and explore potential business opportunities in the overseas market. 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Amit Vinod Sheth, Managing Director of Focus Lighting And Fixtures 
Limited said, "The inauguration of our Experience Centre in the United Kingdom marks an important milestone in 
our journey towards expanding our international presence and a strategic step forward in our global growth journey. 
We believe that lighting goes beyond illumination and plays a significant role in defining spaces, enhancing 
experiences and bringing architectural concepts to life. Through this UK Flagship Experience Centre, we aim to bring 
our products, design capabilities and lighting solutions closer to customers and industry professionals in the United 
Kingdom. This initiative reflects our commitment to exploring new markets, building meaningful relationships and 
creating sustainable growth opportunities for the Company. Having a dedicated space here allows us to engage 
directly with the international design community, demonstrate our engineering capabilities in real-world applications, 
and deliver tailored lighting solutions to our overseas partners. We look forward to strengthening our presence in 
international markets and taking our brand to a wider global audience."

About Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited 
Focus Lighting And Fixtures Limited (NSE – FOCUS) The Company established in 2005, is engaged in the business 
of manufacturing LED lights and Fixtures. The Company offers a comprehensive range of lighting products and solutions 
catering to diverse requirements across various applications. With an emphasis on innovation, design excellence, product 
quality and technological advancement, the Company strives to deliver lighting solutions that combine aesthetics, 
functionality and performance. 
Over the years, the Company has continued to strengthen its capabilities and expand its product portfolio to address the 
evolving requirements of the lighting industry. Through its focus on customer engagement, product development and 
market expansion, the Company aims to further strengthen its position in the lighting industry and explore emerging 
opportunities in domestic and international markets.
