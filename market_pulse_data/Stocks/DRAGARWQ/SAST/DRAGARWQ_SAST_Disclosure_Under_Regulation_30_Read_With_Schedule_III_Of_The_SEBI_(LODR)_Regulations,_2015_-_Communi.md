Dear Shareholder, 
 
Subject:  Advance Intimation – Fixation of Record Date for Scheme of Amalgamation between 
Dr. Agarwal’s Eye Hospital Limited (“Transferor Company”) and Dr. Agarwal’s Health Care 
Limited (“Transferee Company”) and their respective shareholders & creditors  
 
We are pleased to inform you that the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Chennai 
Bench, vide its order dated September 30, 2026, has sanctioned the Scheme of Amalgamation under 
Sections 230 to 232 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, amongst Dr. 
Agarwal’s Eye Hospital Limited (“Transferor Company” or “AEHL”) and Dr. Agarwal’s Health 
Care Limited (“Transferee Company” or “AHCL”) and their respective shareholders and creditors 
(“Scheme”). 
 
1. Fixation of Record Date 
Notice is hereby given that the Board of Directors of the Company, in consultation with the Board of 
the Transferee Company, has fixed Wednesday, November 18, 2026, as the Record Date for 
determining the eligible equity shareholders of the Transferor Company who shall be entitled to 
receive fully paid-up equity shares of the Transferee Company pursuant to the Scheme. 
 
2. Share Exchange Ratio  
Pursuant to Clause 18.1 of the Scheme, the share exchange ratio is: 
 
Eligible shareholders of Dr. Agarwal’s Eye Hospital Limited will receive 23 Equity Shares of face 
value ₹1/- each fully paid-up in Dr. Agarwal’s Health Care Limited for every 2 Equity Shares of 
face value ₹10/- each fully paid-up held in the Dr. Agarwal’s Eye Hospital Limited as on the Record 
Date (i.e., November 18, 2026). 
 
 3. Treatment of Fractional Entitlements 
 
No fractional equity shares will be issued directly to any shareholder. Fractional entitlements arising 
out of allotment of Equity Shares (if any) will be consolidated and allotted to the Trustee nominated 
by the Board, who shall hold these Shares in trust on behalf of the eligible Shareholders of the 
Company (Trustee 1).  
 
The Trustee 1 will sell these consolidated shares on the Stock Exchanges at market price within 90 
(ninety) days from the date of allotment and distribute the net proceeds (after deducting applicable 
costs, expenses, and withholding taxes) to eligible shareholders in proportion to their respective 
fractional entitlements.
