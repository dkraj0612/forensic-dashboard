Press Release – Bajaj Consumer Care Limited appoints Mr. Jitendra Mahajan as Independent 
Director.  
 
Mumbai, October 16, 2026: Bajaj Consumer Care Limited has announced the appointment of Mr. 
Jitendra Mahajan (DIN: 02602356) as an Independent Director on its Board effective from October 14, 
2026.  
 
About Mr. Jitendra Mahajan 
Mr. Jitendra Mahajan is a seasoned FMCG business leader with over three decades of experience 
across leading consumer companies including Marico, Britannia and Diageo India. As a long-standing 
Executive Committee member, he brings a strong combination of enterprise strategy, P&L orientation, 
governance, transformation and operational leadership, with experience spanning businesses of over 
US$1 billion in India and international markets. His leadership experience covers supply chain, 
manufacturing, procurement, digital & technology, innovation, sustainability, risk management and 
business operations, providing him with a broad, end-to-end perspective on value creation in complex 
consumer businesses. 
 
He brings expertise in driving sustainable profitable growth, large-scale business transformation, cost 
and productivity improvement, digital enablement, ESG & sustainability, risk and resilience, and 
building future-ready organizations and leadership talent. Having helped businesses scale, transform 
operating models, integrate acquisitions and strengthen governance and execution disciplines, he can 
bring a pragmatic combination of strategic challenges, operational insight, financial and commercial 
orientation, governance oversight and long-term value creation. 
 
Mr. Kushagra Bajaj, Chairman Bajaj Consumer Care Limited, said: “I welcome Mr. Jitendra Mahajan 
to the Board. His expertise in supply chain, logistics and business operations will be of immense benefit 
to the Company”. 
 
Welcoming Mr. Mahajan’s appointment, Mr. Naveen Pandey, Managing Director Bajaj Consumer 
Care Limited, said: “We are delighted to welcome Mr. Mahajan to the Bajaj Consumer Care Board as 
an Independent Director. Mr. Mahajan’s leadership and deep expertise in FMCG sector will be helpful 
in guiding our transformational journey”. 
 
Mr. Jitendra Mahajan said: “I am grateful to the Board for the opportunity to serve Bajaj Consumer 
Care Limited. I look forward to contributing to its governance, strategy and long-term value creation 
for all the stakeholders”. 
 
About Bajaj Consumer Care: Bajaj Consumer Care Limited, is a leading FMCG company under the $2.5 
billion conglomerate Bajaj Group, led by Chairman Kushagra Bajaj with interests in sugar, ethanol, 
power and consumer care businesses. Bajaj Consumer Care specializes in personal care products. With 
a legacy of trust and excellence, Bajaj Consumer Care continues to expand its footprint in domestic 
and global markets with innovative, consumer-centric offerings.
