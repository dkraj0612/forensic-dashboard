Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) and in continuation of our earlier 
intimations dated 11th September, 2025 for acquisition of brownfield Rail Siding in Kudathini, Ballari, 
Karnataka through JSW Port Logistics Private Limited, wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, with the 
object of developing Multi-Modal Logistics Park, including a fully equipped Inland Container Depot (“ICD”), 
and dated 10th March, 2026 for commencement of interim operations at the aforesaid rail siding, we further 
wish to inform you that JSW Port Logistics Private Limited has received Letter of Intent (“LOI”) from the 
Inter-Ministerial Committee, Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue, Central Board of Indirect Taxes 
and Customs, for setting up of an ICD at Village Kudathini, Ballari District, Karnataka, which is the first ICD 
in the Ballari region, for handling of Import / Export cargo subject to the terms and conditions as mentioned 
in the LOI. 
 
The necessary details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read the SEBI 
Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January, 2026 has been 
attached herewith as an Annexure A. 
 
The Company was informed about the grant of the LOI through email on 10th September, 2026 at around 
8:00 p.m. 
 
The above is for your information and record.
