### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Arihant Foundations & Housing Limited reported no deviation in the utilization of the Rs. 108.60 crore raised through a preferential issue of equity shares and warrants for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, a confirmation reiterated by their monitoring agency, ICRA Limited. Approximately Rs. 84.87 crore has been utilized for stated objectives, primarily land purchase and related expenses, with the unutilized balance of Rs. 23.73 crore securely held in fixed deposits and bank accounts. While environmental clearance for a subsidiary project is still in process, there are no material impacts or red flags concerning fund misuse identified in this report.

preferential issue of 13,65,624 Equity Shares and, in the first tranche, 25% (Rs. 10.76

in the offer document. 
 


fixed deposit with ICICI Bank (Rs. 20.00 Cr) and is held as a balance in the Preferential 
Issue account with ICICI Bank (Rs. 3.88 Cr, inclusive of interest income of Rs. 0.15 Cr 
earned on the fixed deposit). 
 
 
The entire proceeds of the preferential issue, aggregating to Rs. 108.60 Cr, have been 
received as on June 30, 2026; there is no consideration receivable against the Share 
Warrants as on that date.
