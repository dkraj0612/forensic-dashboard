and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)

In continuation to our previous disclosures and following the receipt of the In-principle 
approval from the Stock Exchange, we hereby inform you that, pursuant to Regulation 
29(1)(d) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, a meeting of the Rights Issue Committee of the 
Board of Directors is scheduled to be held on Thursday, October 22, 2026.  
 
The Committee will, inter alia, consider, discuss, and decide various matters in connection 
with the Rights Issue, including the specific terms of the Rights Issue, such as the 
determination of the Rights Issue price and related payment mechanism, rights entitlement 
ratio, the record date, timing of the Rights Issue and other terms and conditions with respect 
to the Rights Issue.
