Anant Raj Cloud, Orange Business Singapore Sign MoU with Haryana Govt to Take the State’s 
Cloud Services to Global Markets 
Collaboration aims to attract overseas data workloads and strengthen Haryana’s position as a hub 
for data centres, cloud and AI infrastructure 
New Delhi, October 7, 2026: Anant Raj Cloud Singapore Pte. Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of 
Anant Raj Limited, and Orange Business Services Singapore Pte. Ltd, a Singapore-based digital 
technology and network services company, have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with 
the Government of Haryana, through the Department of Industries & Commerce. The collaboration 
aims to take Haryana-based cloud and data centre services to global markets and facilitate the hosting 
of overseas data in the state. 
The latest MoU builds on Anant Raj Limited’s commitment to Haryana’s digital infrastructure. In June 
2026, the company had signed an MoU with the Government of Haryana under the Make in Haryana 
event, committing ₹25,000 crore towards the development of data centre infrastructure across the state. 
Under the new MoU, Anant Raj Cloud Singapore will promote Haryana as a preferred global hub for 
data centres, cloud and AI infrastructure. It will undertake global marketing of Ashok Cloud, Anant 
Raj’s cloud platform, and data centre services hosted in Haryana, while facilitating the onboarding of 
overseas customers looking to host their data in the state. 
The collaboration also reaffirms Anant Raj Limited’s commitment to investments in data centre and 
cloud services in Haryana. The company, through its group entities, has committed to developing 
approximately 307 MW of IT Load across its Manesar, Panchkula and Rai campuses, with further 
investments planned in phases based on demand, commercial viability and requisite approvals.

The Government of Haryana has also introduced a dedicated Data Centre Policy 2026, offering a range 
of incentives to support the development of data centre infrastructure in the state. At the national level, 
India offers a competitive cost environment, with relatively lower Capex and Opex (Capital and 
operating costs), further strengthening its proposition for attracting global data workloads. 
Orange Business will explore opportunities to work as a technology partner for the establishment of 
data centres and support cloud services, including those designed for AI workloads and high-
performance computing. Its role will include providing technology architecture, cloud and AI hardware 
and technical expertise, while also supporting Anant Raj Cloud Singapore’s go-to-market initiatives in 
international markets. 
Amit Sarin, Managing Director, Anant Raj Limited, said, “This MoU marks an important step in 
taking Haryana’s digital infrastructure capabilities to a wider global market. Our focus is not only on 
building data centre capacity in the state but also on creating an ecosystem that can serve customers 
beyond India. With Ashok Cloud, our expanding data centre footprint and the support of Orange 
Business, we see a strong opportunity to bring global data workloads to Haryana and strengthen the 
state’s position in the digital infrastructure landscape.” 
Mr. Christophe OZER - Head of Digital Infrastructure & Partners APAC, from Orange Business 
said, “We are pleased to strengthen our collaboration with Anant Raj Cloud and support their plans to 
take Haryana-based cloud services to international markets. As a technology partner, we bring 
expertise in cloud architecture, AI infrastructure and managed digital services to support the 
development of secure and AI-ready environments. This collaboration can serve both Indian and global 
customers.” 
Anant Raj Limited currently operates data centre facilities at its Manesar and Panchkula campuses, with 
capacity expansion underway. The company is working towards its planned capacity of approximately 
307 MW IT Load across Manesar, Panchkula and Rai, with the Rai campus in Sonipat having potential 
capacity of around 200 MW IT Load. 
The latest collaboration also builds on the relationship between Anant Raj and Orange Business, which 
began in 2024 with a partnership to deliver managed cloud services in India, and now expands that 
relationship towards global cloud services and digital infrastructure. 
About Ashok Cloud Limited 
Ashok Cloud Limited, a subsidiary of Anant Raj Limited is a dedicated digital infrastructure and cloud 
services company, providing advanced data centres, co-location services, sovereign & public cloud 
offerings, secured Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) solutions, AI infrastructure and disaster recovery 
services in India and to foreign companies through Anant Raj Cloud Singapore Pte Ltd.  
About Anant Raj Limited 
Anant Raj Limited is a leading real estate developer with its presence in the State of Delhi, Haryana, 
Andhra Pradesh, Rajasthan and NCR is known for its diversified business portfolio.  With decades of 
experience, the company has developed landmark projects across residential, commercial, hospitality, 
industrial asset classes and IT parks. In addition to its real estate ventures, Anant Raj has expanded into 
high-tech infrastructure, including the development of cutting-edge data center facilities including
