Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at their respective meetings held

September 30, 2026, in accordance with Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations

conclusion on these unaudited consolidated financial results.

activities, which constitute a single reportable segment: 'Real Estate Development'.
