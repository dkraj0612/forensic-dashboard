Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Subject: Change in Senior Management 
 
Pursuant to the Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”),    
we would like to inform you that the Board of Directors at its Meeting held earlier today,          
i.e., September 28, 2026, has, inter alia:  
 
1.​ Noted the resignation of Mr. Priyan Pillay as the Executive Vice President - Legal of the 
Company effective close of business on October 23, 2026 to pursue an opportunity outside 
Colgate-Palmolive back in his home country. 
 
2.​ Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, 
approved the appointment of Mr. Abhijit Yadav, currently Legal Director as the Executive 
Vice President - Legal of the Company effective October 24, 2026, in succession to           
Mr. Priyan Pillay.  
 
Details with respect to aforementioned change, as required under Regulation 30(6) read with 
SEBI Circular No SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 is enclosed 
herewith as Annexure A.
