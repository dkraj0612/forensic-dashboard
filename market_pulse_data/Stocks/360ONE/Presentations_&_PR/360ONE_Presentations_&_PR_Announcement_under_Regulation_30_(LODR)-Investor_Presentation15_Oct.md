> **Executive Summary:**  360 ONE WAM Limited reported robust business expansion in Q2 FY27, with Assets Under Management (AUM) growing 31.0% YoY and Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) up 27.5% YoY. While operational revenue streams are strong, overall profitability was compressed, leading to a drop in Return on Tangible Equity.

## Page 3

Mumbai, October 15, 2026: 360 ONE WAM Limited, one of India’s largest wealth and alternates asset managers, 
announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 
Business Performance 
• Assets under Management for 360 ONE stood at Rs 8,99,481 Crs, consisting of ARR AUM of Rs 3,53,382 Crs and 
Transactional / Brokerage / syndicated AUM of Rs 5,46,099 Crs 
o 
Wealth Management: ARR AUM rose to Rs 2,52,627 Crs (+24.3% YoY) supported by net flows across segments. 
360 ONE Plus proposition saw growth of 37.8% YoY, while Distribution grew by 15.0% YoY. Overall, it manages 
assets for 9,000+ families and corporates 
o 
Asset Management: ARR AUM increased to Rs 1,00,754 Crs (+9.4% YoY) driven by growth across Private Equity, 
Credit and Real Assets segments 
Financial Performance for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 
• Total Revenue increased by 4.8% YoY to Rs 853 Crs mainly driven by strong growth in ARR AUM 
• Revenue from Operations was Rs 896 Crs - an increase of 17.5% YoY 
• ARR revenue was Rs 706 Crs - an increase of 27.5% YoY 
• Combined ARR retention at 79 basis points (bps), with Wealth Management and Asset Management retentions at 
79 bps each 
• Consolidated Profit After Tax was Rs 302 Crs as against Rs 316 Crs for Q2 FY26 
• Tangible net worth stood at Rs 7,268 Crs. Return on Tangible Equity was at 16.9% 
• Our core wealth and asset management businesses continued to deliver strong performance amid heightened 
macroeconomic volatility, underscoring the depth of our client relationships and the trust families place in us. Q2 
results include impact of the prevailing market conditions, which we view as transitory. Our fundamentals endure - 
the strength of the 360 ONE brand, a robust platform, a client-first model and our continued ability to attract 
exceptional talent. Anchored in disciplined risk management and the highest standards of governance, we remain 
well positioned for sustained, long-term growth
