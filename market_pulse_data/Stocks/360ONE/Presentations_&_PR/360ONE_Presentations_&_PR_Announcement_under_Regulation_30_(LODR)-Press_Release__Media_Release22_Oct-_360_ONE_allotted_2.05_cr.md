PRESS RELEASE 
 
UBS acquires 4.8% stake in 360 ONE WAM through warrant exercise 
Warrant exercise marks the latest milestone in the leading institutions’ strategic collaboration 
Mumbai, India, October 22, 2026: 360 ONE WAM Ltd (“360 ONE”), one of India's largest independent wealth 
and asset management firms, announced today that UBS AG, one of the world’s leading wealth managers and 
universal banking firms headquartered in Switzerland, has exercised all the warrants allotted to it by 360 ONE. 
Pursuant to this, 360 ONE has allotted 20,502,939 equity shares to UBS AG, representing approximately a 4.8% 
equity stake in 360 ONE WAM Ltd.  
UBS AG had subscribed to 20,502,939 warrants on a preferential basis pursuant to an agreement entered into 
with 360 ONE. The warrants were allotted on July 21, 2025, with 25% of the exercise price paid at the time of 
allotment. UBS AG has now exercised all such warrants and paid the remaining 75% of the exercise price. 
About 360 ONE 
360 ONE WAM is among India’s leading wealth and asset management firms, managing assets of more than ~₹9 
lakh crore (USD 94.1 billion) and serving more than 9,000 families and corporates. 360 ONE Wealth specialises 
in empowering ultra-high-net-worth and high-net-worth families to manage, grow and optimise their wealth, 
including legacy-planning solutions. 360 ONE Asset offers investment solutions to clients worldwide looking to 
invest in Indian markets across asset classes that span public equities, private equity, private credit, real assets 
and renewables. Headquartered in Mumbai, 360 ONE has a presence in 32 major domestic and global locations. 
Website:  www.360.one 
About UBS 
UBS is a leading and truly global wealth manager and the leading universal bank in Switzerland. It also provides 
diversified asset management solutions and focused investment banking capabilities. UBS manages 7.3 trillion 
dollars of invested assets as per the second quarter 2026. UBS helps clients achieve their financial goals through 
personalized advice, solutions, and products. Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland, the firm is operating in more 
than 50 markets around the globe. UBS Group shares on the Six Swiss Exchange and the New York Stock 
Exchange (NYSE.
