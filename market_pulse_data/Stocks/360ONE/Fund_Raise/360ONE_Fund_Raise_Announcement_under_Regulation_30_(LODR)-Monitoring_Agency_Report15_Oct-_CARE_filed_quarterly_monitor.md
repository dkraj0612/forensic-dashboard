### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  This monitoring agency report for 360 ONE WAM Limited's ₹2,111.80 crore preferential issue confirms no deviation in the utilization of the ₹527.95 crore raised and deployed prior to the quarter ended September 30, 2026, with no additional funds called or utilized during the period. A material impact and potential red flag is the approved extension of the utilization timeline for a significant ₹1,000 crore investment in 360 ONE Prime Ltd., now deferred from FY 2025-26 to FY 2027-28, indicating slower-than-planned deployment for a major strategic objective. This leaves ₹1,583.85 crore of the preferential issue uncalled, suggesting substantial unexercised capital-raising capacity.
