Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Subject: Allotment of Equity Shares upon conversion of warrants by Mr. Saahil Murarka 
 
This is further to our intimation dated May 29, 2025, in relation to allotment of 33,33,333 warrants to 
Mr. Saahil Murarka (“Allottee”) on preferential basis.  
 
We wish to inform that pursuant to conversion of all the warrants held by the Allottee and upon 
receipt of Rs. 293,68,99,706.31/- i.e. the balance 75% of the warrant exercise price from the Allottee, 
the Stakeholders Relationship Committee of the Board of Directors of the Company vide resolution 
passed by circulation on November 25, 2026, has allotted 33,33,333 equity shares of face value of Re. 
1/- each to Mr. Saahil Murarka at an issue price of Rs. 1,174.76/- per equity share. The equity shares 
allotted on conversion shall rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the Company in all 
respects. 
 
Consequent to the aforesaid allotment, the issued, subscribed and paid-up share capital of the 
Company has increased from Rs. 42,88,78,523/- comprising of 42,88,78,523 equity shares of Re. 1/- 
each, to Rs. 43,22,11,856/- comprising of 43,22,11,856 equity shares of Re. 1/- each. 
 
Further, we have enclosed the details pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with point A(2) of annexure 18 of SEBI Master 
Circular dated January 30, 2026, with respect to issue of equity shares, as ‘Annexure 1’.
