Press Release 
 
360 ONE strengthens group leadership with the appointment of Aashish Agarwal as Chief Executive 
Officer of 360 ONE WAM Group 
 
Mumbai, India, September 22, 2026: 360 ONE today announced that Aashish Agarwal is joining as 
Chief Executive Officer of the 360 ONE WAM Group, partnering with the firm's founders to further 
grow and strengthen the business. Karan Bhagat, currently Managing Director & CEO, will be 
appointed as Vice Chairman and continue as Managing Director of the Group, driving group strategy, 
capital allocation and key client & institutional relationships. Yatin Shah, Co-Founder, 360 ONE, will 
continue to drive 360 ONE’s decade-long leadership in wealth management.  
 
With this appointment, 360 ONE is well positioned to build across all three of its verticals: Wealth 
Management, Asset Management and Capital Markets. Specifically, the structure is designed to bring 
the full 360 ONE ecosystem to every client – the Group's combined capabilities working together for 
families and institutions alike, so that clients experience the firm as one platform.  
 
Additionally, it positions 360 ONE to build for the scale India's next chapter of growth will demand. As 
the Indian economy advances on its journey towards USD 5 trillion and over a period of time to a USD 
15 trillion economy, that growth will require large, sophisticated domestic financial institutions of the 
kind every major economy has built. 360 ONE intends to be at the forefront of that shift, as the leading 
Indian institution serving the country's most sophisticated capital allocators. 
 
Commenting on the announcement, Karan Bhagat said, “I am extremely excited to continue to build 
and accelerate the 360 ONE platform, especially at this stage of our country’s journey. Over the last 
18 years, we have built 360 ONE into a trusted partner for India’s wealth creators. This structure lets 
each of our three businesses be led with sharper focus while we operate as one Group. Aashish brings 
exceptional financial services depth and an owner’s mindset to building businesses, and along with 
the founders, will drive the Group’s next phase of growth.” 
 
Aashish Agarwal said, “I am delighted to be joining 360 ONE. Karan and Yatin have built an exceptional 
franchise over 18 years, and I look forward to working closely with them to build an Indian financial 
institution of enduring scale and global standing. India's wealth creation story is still early, and as 
clients and family offices grow more sophisticated, their needs increasingly span investing, capital 
raising and strategic advice. Bringing Wealth Management, Asset Management and Capital Markets 
together allows us to serve those needs end to end, as one Group.” 
 
About Aashish Agarwal 
Aashish Agarwal is a veteran investment banker with deep institutional and investor relationships, 
combined with a strong professional entrepreneurial instinct. He brings in-depth experience of capital 
markets, investment banking and asset management.
