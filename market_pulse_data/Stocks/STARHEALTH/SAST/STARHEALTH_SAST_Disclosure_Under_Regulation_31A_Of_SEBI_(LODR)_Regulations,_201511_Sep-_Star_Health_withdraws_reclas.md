Industrial Glass Fiber Industries and Interics: 
 
The business of Industrial Glass Fiber Industries and Interics, stand wound up with effect from March 31, 2024 
and hence the entities have ceased to exist. In view of the same and pursuant to Regulation 31A of SEBI LODR 
they automatically cease to form part of the promoter group of the Company.  
 
IDC Electronics Limited: 
 
The Company has received request from the promoters for withdrawal of reclassification application filed with 
respect to IDC Electronics Limited. 
 
The Company shall make necessary steps for processing the withdrawal of reclassification application filed with 
the stock exchanges with respect to the aforesaid entities subject to the approval of the Board of Directors of the 
Company.
