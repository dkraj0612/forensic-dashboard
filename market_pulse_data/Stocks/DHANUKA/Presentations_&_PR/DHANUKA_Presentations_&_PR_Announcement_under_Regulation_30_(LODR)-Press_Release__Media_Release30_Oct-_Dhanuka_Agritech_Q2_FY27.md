Dhanuka Agritech's Q2 Net Profit Surges 16.23% to ₹109.22 Crore; 
Revenue Grows 9.91% to ₹657.51 Crore 
 
Profit growth outpaces revenue growth as the agrochemical major reports improved profitability in 
Q2 FY27 
Gurugram, October 30, 2026: Dhanuka Agritech Limited, one of India's leading agrochemical 
companies, has reported a strong improvement in its second-quarter profitability for FY27, with its 
Profit After Tax (PAT) growing 16.23% year-on-year to ₹109.22 crore. 
Revenue from operations for the quarter increased 9.91% to ₹657.51 crore, against ₹598.25 crore in 
Q2 FY26. Profit Before Tax (PBT) expanded 16.27% to ₹146.47 crore, compared to ₹125.97 crore in 
the year-ago quarter, reflecting improved operational traction. 
Q2 FY27 Financial Highlights (YoY): 
 
Revenue from Operations: ₹657.51 crore (Up 9.91%) 
 
Gross Profit: ₹270.77 crore (Up 6.62%) 
 
Gross Profit Margin: 41.18% (compared to 42.45% in Q2 FY26) 
 
EBITDA: ₹152.97 crore (Up 11.88%) 
 
PBT: ₹146.47 crore (Up 16.27%) 
 
PAT: ₹109.22 crore (Up 16.23%) 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Rahul Dhanuka, Managing Director, Dhanuka Agritech 
Limited said: “Our Q2 profitability expansion was supported by healthy channel inventory 
liquidation and steady demand during the autumn farming cycle. We remain focused on building a 
sustainable and innovation-led growth platform for Dhanuka Agritech.The Indian agricultural sector 
continues to present significant opportunities, particularly in the areas of crop productivity, advanced 
crop protection solutions and the adoption of innovative agricultural technologies. Our focus remains 
on strengthening our product portfolio, deepening farmer engagement, and expanding our market 
presence. We believe that continued investment in innovation and farmer-centric solutions will remain 
central to our long-term growth journey." 
The unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, were 
reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 
October 30, 2026. 
About Dhanuka Agritech 
Dhanuka Agritech Limited is a leading player in India’s crop protection and crop care sector. Dhanuka is 
renowned for its innovative crop protection solutions and commitment to agricultural excellence. With over 46 
years of experience, Dhanuka delivers high-quality products designed to enhance crop yields and support 
sustainable farming. Dhanuka’s commitment to the abundance of farmers and farming and customer satisfaction 
makes it a trusted partner in driving agricultural success and farm prosperity.
