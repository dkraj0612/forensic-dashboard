Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting – financial results  
 
In reference to our letter dated September 25, 2026, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors at 
their meeting held today i.e., November 02, 2026, have, inter-alia,  
 
1. Approved Unaudited (Standalone and Consolidated) Financial Results of the Company for the 
quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(SEBI Listing Regulations) we enclose herewith a copy of the Unaudited (Standalone and Consolidated) 
Financial Results of the Company for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, accompanied 
by Limited Review Report thereon issued by the Joint Statutory Auditors of the Company. 
 
2. Declaration of Interim Dividend for the Financial Year 2026-27 and Record Date  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and 43 of SEBI Listing Regulations, the Board of Directors have declared an 
interim dividend of Rs. 4.30/- per equity share (representing 430%) of Re.1/- each fully paid up for the 
financial year 2026-27 and the said interim dividend will be paid on or before December 01, 2026.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 42 of SEBI Listing Regulations, the Board of Directors have fixed Friday, November 
06, 2026, as the “Record Date” for the purpose of payment of Interim Dividend for the Financial Year 2026-
27. Accordingly, members whose name appear as on close of business hours as on November 06, 2026 
i.e. (a) as beneficial owners as per the details furnished by the Depositories in respect of shares held in 
electronic form and (b) as members in the Register of Members would be entitled for the said interim 
dividend pay-out. 
 
3. Noted the Capex approvals granted by Board of Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies Limited 
(ARACT) the Company’s Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS) 
 
The Board of ARACT, WOS, has approved a capex of Rs. 2,320 crores for increasing the Pack 
Manufacturing capacity from 4 Gwh to 9 Gwh and setting up of LFP Cell Manufacturing Plant at the Amara 
Raja Giga Corridor in Telangana. The said plants will be established within next 30 months. The capex for 
these facilities will be financed through a mix of equity and debt. This investment re-affirms and is in line 
with the Company's 'Energy & Mobility' roadmap and its Giga Corridor initiative aimed at establishing an 
integrated ecosystem in India for emerging Energy and Mobility technologies.
