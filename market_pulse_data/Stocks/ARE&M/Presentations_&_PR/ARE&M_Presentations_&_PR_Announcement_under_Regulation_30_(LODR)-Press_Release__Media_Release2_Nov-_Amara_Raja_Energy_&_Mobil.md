Amara Raja Energy & Mobility reports 13% increase in Q2 FY27 
revenue over same quarter previous year  
Hyderabad/Tirupati: Amara Raja Energy and Mobility Limited (ARE&M), a comprehensive 
solutions provider in the Energy & Mobility space, (BSE: 500008 & NSE Code: ARE&M) today, 
reported profit before tax of 250.71 Cr for the Q2 of FY27. 
The Company reported an Earnings Per Share (EPS) of ₹10.29 for Q2 FY27, along with an 
interim dividend of ₹4.30 per equity share.

Performance was driven by strong double-digit growth in aftermarket and OEM in 
automotive batteries, and the Home Energy business. The new energy business also 
witnessed good growth in stationary telecom battery pack demand. 
Speaking on the results, Mr. Harshavardhana Gourineni, Executive Director- Automotive 
and Industrial, said, "Robust OEM demand across 4W and 2W, together with healthy 
aftermarket volumes, drove domestic revenue growth of over 10%, while Industrial UPS 
grew over 7%. International demand remains strong, but geopolitical shipping disruptions 
have temporarily limited our ability to fulfil it. As logistics normalize, we are confident of 
accelerating growth." 
Mr. Vikramadithya Gourineni, Executive Director – New Energy Business, said, “We 
continue to see strong volume growth, led by the stationary segment, while our Giga 
Factory-1 in Telangana remains on track. With the Telecom sector rapidly shifting to lithium, 
we are confident of sustaining our market leadership through this technology transition. 
Our BESS plant is also expected to come online next month, further strengthening our 
capabilities across the energy storage value chain.” 
Mr. Jayadev Galla, Chairman and Managing Director, said, “We delivered a solid 
performance this quarter. Our Lead Acid Business continued to perform strongly, supported 
by broad-based traction across segments, while the New Energy Business maintained its 
growth momentum. The progress on our Giga Factory remains on track, reinforcing our 
confidence in building a strong, future-ready energy platform.”

About Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Limited  
Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Limited (ARE&M) encompasses a diverse range of solutions 
and products, which includes energy storage solutions, Lithium-ion cell manufacturing, wide 
range of EV chargers, Li-ion battery pack assembly, automotive and industrial lubricants, 
and exploration of new chemistries, among others. ARE&M is also one of the largest 
manufacturers of energy storage products for both industrial and automotive applications in 
India. Amara Raja is the preferred supplier to major telecom service providers, telecom 
equipment manufacturers, the UPS sector (OEM & Replacement), Indian Railways, and the 
Power, Oil & Gas industry segments. Amara Raja’s industrial battery brands comprise of 
PowerStack®, AmaronVolt® and Quanta®. The company also manufactures India’s leading 
automotive battery brands Amaron® and Powerzone, which are distributed through a large 
pan-India sales & service retail network. The company supplies automotive batteries under 
OE relationships to Ashok Leyland, Ford India, Honda, Hyundai, Mahindra & Mahindra, 
Maruti Suzuki, and Tata Motors amongst others. Amara Raja’s Industrial and Automotive 
Batteries are exported to over 70 countries around the world.
