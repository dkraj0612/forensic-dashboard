12. Lock in and transfer restrictions 
The securities issued under the preferential offer shall be subject to the lock-in and transfer restrictions 
prescribed under Regulations 167 and 168 of the SEBI ICDR Regulations, 2018, as amended: 
1. Promoters/Promoter Group: Equity shares, including shares allotted upon conversion of 
warrants, shall be locked in for 18 months from trading approval, subject to the prescribed 20% 
limit on total capital. Shares allotted beyond this limit shall be locked in for six months. 
2. Non-promoter allottees: Equity shares, including shares allotted upon conversion of warrants, 
shall be locked in for six months from trading approval. 
3. Unlisted warrants/convertible securities: These shall be locked in for one year from allotment. 
4. Existing shareholding: The allottees’ entire pre-preferential shareholding, if any, shall be locked in 
from the relevant date until 90 trading days from trading approval; for unlisted 
warrants/convertible securities, until 90 trading days from allotment. 
5. Transfer restrictions: Transfers shall comply with Regulation 168, including obtaining trading 
approval from all relevant stock exchanges. Permitted promoter transfers shall remain subject to 
takeover regulations and continuation of the remaining lock-in with the transferee.
