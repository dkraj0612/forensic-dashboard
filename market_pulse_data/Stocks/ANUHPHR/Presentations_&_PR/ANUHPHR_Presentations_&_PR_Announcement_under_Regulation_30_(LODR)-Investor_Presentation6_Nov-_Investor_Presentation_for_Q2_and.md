### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Anuh Pharma Ltd. reported robust H1 FY27 profitability, with PAT surging 42.25% and EBITDA margins significantly expanding, driven by a strategic focus on higher-margin products and domestic demand despite modest 1.80% revenue growth. The recent commissioning of a new Tarapur unit, increasing total capacity to 2,975 MTPA, provides a material impact for future expansion, with management aiming to rebuild softer exports and achieve 15-20% annual growth. While the shift from export-driven revenue and a slight sequential dip in Q2 performance warrant monitoring, the overall profitability improvement and capacity addition are key positive takeaways.
