PRESS RELEASE   
Bajel Projects Reports Highest Ever Unexecuted Orderbook at  
~Rs. 5000 Cr with Steady Q2 EBITDA Margins at 4.8% 
 
Mumbai, November 6th, 2026: Bajel Projects Ltd, a Bajaj Group company, has declared its results for the 
quarter (Q2) and half-year (H1) ended 30th September 2026. 
Bajel Projects has achieved its highest ever Unexecuted Order Book (UEOB) of ~Rs. 5000 Cr at the end of 
Q2 FY27 representing a growth of 48% when compared to end of Q2 FY26. 
For Q2 FY27, the Company recorded modest Revenue from Operations of ~Rs. 514 Cr due to delayed start 
of projects resulting from war in the middle east and domestic RoW issues. For H1 FY27, revenue from 
operations stood at ~Rs. 1,081 Cr.  
EBITDA for Q2 FY27 stood at ~Rs. 25 Cr, while maintaining an EBITDA margin of 4.8% when compared to 
the same period last year. The EBITDA margin remained steady despite high commodity prices and other 
cost pressures. For H1 FY27, EBITDA stood at ~Rs. 49 Cr with EBITDA margin at 4.4%. 
Proﬁt before tax (PBT) for Q2 FY27 stood at ~Rs. 8 Cr and proﬁt after tax (PAT) at ~Rs. 6 Cr driven by 
substantial reduction in interest costs of 31% when compared to the same period last year. For H1 FY27, 
PBT stood at ~Rs. 15 Cr and PAT at ~Rs. 10 Cr, compared to ~Rs. 12 Cr and ~Rs. 9 Cr respectively.  
During the quarter, Bajel formalized a landmark 500 kV transmission line agreement with Egypt’s Egyptian 
Electricity Transmission Company (EETC) worth Rs. 400 Cr+ (incl. GST) and secured ultra-mega and mega 
orders under the WR-ER Inter-Regional Network Expansion Scheme, valued at ~Rs. 1,600 Cr (incl. GST) 
In October, the Company also won two orders for substation works in Mumbai from Tata Power valued at 
~Rs. 200 Cr+ each (incl. GST). Further strengthening its order book, Bajel secured orders valued at ~ Rs. 
1,500 Cr (incl. GST) for 765 kV transmission lines and substations in Gujarat, marking the Company’s largest 
order win in the power transmission segment.  
Commenting on the results, Mr. Rajesh Ganesh, MD & CEO, Bajel Projects Ltd., 
"We have achieved our highest ever unexecuted order book (UEOB) of ~Rs. 5000 Cr at the end 
of Q2 FY27 and ~ Rs. 6500 Cr, as of date, reﬂecting healthy revenue visibility for the future. Our 
performance in Q2 FY27 reﬂects our focus on margin discipline and building a high-quality order 
book. While revenue from operations for the quarter was modest, we held our Q2 EBITDA 
margin steady at 4.8% despite high commodity prices and other cost pressures. 
As India continues to strengthen its power transmission network to meet growing energy 
demand and enable the country's clean energy ambitions, we remain focused on seamless 
project delivery, while pursuing opportunities that are aligned with our capabilities and return

About Bajel Projects Limited 
Bajel Projects Limited (Bajel) is a leading Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) and 
manufacturing solutions company with a strong domestic and international presence across the Power 
Transmission and New Energies (formerly Power Distribution) sectors. As part of the Bajaj Group, Bajel is 
recognized for its project management capabilities and operational excellence, making it a preferred 
partner to leading state, national and international utilities, and private infrastructure players. Formerly 
the EPC arm of Bajaj Electricals Limited, Bajel continues to be guided by the same values and beliefs that 
have driven its growth for over two decades at Bajaj Electricals Ltd.
