Bajel Projects Secures a Major Order worth INR 200 Cr+ for 220kV GIS Substations in Mumbai
Mumbai, 9th October, 2026 — Bajel Projects Limited, a leading EPC player in power transmission infrastructure, has been
awarded an EPC order for the supply and installation of 220kV Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS) substations at Salsette and
Powai in Mumbai. Per the Company's project classification policy (inclusive of GST), the order qualifies as a Major* order.
Scope of Work
1. Package A – 220kV GIS at Salsette Receiving Station
The scope covers the supply and installation of a 220kV GIS at the Salsette receiving station.
2. Package B – 220kV Outdoor GIS Substation at Powai Receiving Station
The scope covers the supply and installation of a 220kV Outdoor GIS substation at the Powai receiving station.
GIS technology needs far lesser space than conventional air-insulated substations. That makes it the preferred choice for
dense urban load centres like Mumbai, where land is scarce and demand keeps rising. Together, the two substations will add
capacity and reliability to the city's transmission network.
Commenting on the win, Rajesh Ganesh, Managing Director & CEO, Bajel Projects Limited, said:
“We are proud to contribute to strengthening Mumbai's power infrastructure. Building high-voltage GIS substations inside a
working metropolis calls for precise engineering, tight site planning and uncompromising safety, and this order reflects the
confidence our clients place in Bajel's execution capabilities. Mumbai city's growing power needs demand a grid that is
resilient and future-ready, and we are committed to delivering both projects to the highest standards of quality.”
