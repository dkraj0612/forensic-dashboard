Bajel Projects Wins an Ultra-mega Order Worth INR 600+ Crore for 765kV Transmission Line Package 
(TL05) under WR-ER Inter-Regional Network Expansion Scheme 
 
Mumbai, 11 August 2026: Bajel Projects Limited, a Bajaj Group Power EPC company, has been awarded 
a transmission line package (TL05) in the ultra-mega band*, under the WR-ER Inter-Regional Network 
Expansion Scheme – Part A. 
 
The scope covers construction of 765kV double-circuit transmission lines connecting Raigarh (Tamnar), 
Raigarh (Kotra) and Dharamjaygarh in Chhattisgarh, reinforcing critical inter-regional power evacuation 
capacity between the Western and Eastern regions of the national grid. 
 
This latest win adds to Bajel's growing order book in high-voltage transmission infrastructure and 
reinforces the company's position as a preferred EPC partner for strategic grid-strengthening projects 
across India. 
 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Rajesh Ganesh, MD & CEO, Bajel Projects Limited, said: 
"This order strengthens our presence in high-capacity 765kV transmission infrastructure and reflects the 
continued trust placed in Bajel's execution capabilities. As India's power grid scales up to meet rising 
demand, we remain focused on delivering projects that are critical to the nation's energy security and 
inter-regional connectivity."
