### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Aries Agro Limited has received a confirmed demand of Rs. 3.96 crores, including interest and penalties, from the GST Appellate Authority concerning disallowed input tax credit from fiscal year 2018-19. This represents a material financial liability that could impact the company's cash flow and profitability. The demand stems from input tax credit claims linked to vendors whose GST registrations were retrospectively cancelled, highlighting potential supply chain or vendor due diligence risks, and while the company intends to appeal to the Goods and Services Tax Appellate Tribunal, the current order confirms the significant liability.
