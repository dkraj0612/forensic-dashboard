CSM Technologies Strengthens Digital Identity Capabilities with MOSIP Adoption 
Expands DPI Playbook for Global Markets 
 
Bhubaneswar - August 24, 2026 - CSM Technologies Limited (NSE: CSM | BSE: 544806), a 
multinational GovTech company delivering digital transformation solutions for governments 
and public institutions, has entered into an engagement to adopt the Modular Open Source 
Identity Platform (MOSIP) framework for designing and implementing foundational digital 
identity solutions. 
The engagement marks a strategic expansion of CSM Technologies’ Digital Public 
Infrastructure (DPI) capabilities, adding foundational digital identity to its growing portfolio 
of Government Technology (GovTech) solutions. It also strengthens the company’s ability to 
pursue emerging opportunities in international markets where governments are looking to 
build secure, scalable and interoperable digital foundations for public service delivery. 
MOSIP is an open-source technology framework that enables countries to build and own 
customised foundational digital identity systems. Its modular and vendor-neutral architecture 
allows governments to configure identity ecosystems around their specific policy, 
governance, technology and service-delivery requirements. 
For CSM Technologies, the adoption of MOSIP represents an important capability-building 
initiative. The company’s technical teams have undergone comprehensive training on the 
platform, building specialised expertise required to design, integrate, customise and 
implement MOSIP-based identity solutions. 
Priyadarshi Pany, Managing Director & CEO, CSM Technologies Limited, said: "Digital 
identity is increasingly becoming a foundational layer of modern DPI. Our engagement with 
MOSIP strengthens our ability to help governments build identity systems that are secure, 
scalable, interoperable and aligned to their own institutional and citizen needs. For CSM, this 
is not simply an addition to our technology portfolio; it is a significant capability that 
complements our experience of building mission-critical digital platforms for governments 
across emerging economies." 
The move comes at a time when foundational digital identity is gaining importance as a core 
building block of DPI. A trusted identity layer can enable governments to connect citizens with 
a wider range of public services and digital ecosystems, including social protection, 
healthcare, education, financial inclusion, taxation and other citizen-facing services.

MOSIP’s modular architecture enables countries to develop customised identity systems 
while retaining greater ownership and flexibility over their digital identity infrastructure. The 
platform is designed to support integration with existing national systems and wider 
technology ecosystems, allowing governments to build identity solutions suited to their 
specific requirements. 
For CSM Technologies, this architecture creates strong synergies with its existing capabilities 
across e-Governance, citizen services, social protection, agriculture, healthcare, public 
finance, GIS, analytics and enterprise digital transformation. The company’s experience in 
implementing large-scale government platforms provides a complementary foundation for 
delivering integrated DPI ecosystems rather than standalone technology deployments. 
The engagement also strengthens CSM Technologies’ international GovTech proposition, 
particularly in emerging markets across Africa and Latin America, where governments are 
investing in digital infrastructure to improve public service delivery, inclusion and 
administrative efficiency. 
According to the World Bank’s 2026 Global Digital Public Infrastructure Program, 2.9 billion 
people living primarily in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia still lack digital IDs that can be 
used for online transactions, highlighting a substantial global gap in trusted digital identity 
infrastructure.  
Rather than approaching digital identity as an isolated technology layer, CSM Technologies 
will leverage its broader GovTech capabilities to support the integration of identity with 
government registries, service-delivery platforms, beneficiary ecosystems and other DPI 
components, subject to the requirements of individual countries and projects. 
The development further reinforces CSM Technologies’ evolution from an e-Governance 
technology provider into a broader DPI and GovTech solutions partner for governments and 
public institutions. 
About CSM Technologies Limited 
CSM Technologies Limited is a multinational GovTech company headquartered in 
Bhubaneswar, India. The company delivers Digital Public Infrastructure, e-Governance, 
Artificial Intelligence, Analytics, Cloud, Cybersecurity, GIS, Smart Cities, Mining, Agriculture, 
Logistics, Public Finance, Utilities and Enterprise Digital Transformation solutions for 
governments and enterprises. 
With operations across 20 cities in 14 countries, CSM Technologies works with governments 
and institutions to build secure, scalable and technology-driven digital ecosystems that 
enhance governance, operational efficiency and citizen outcomes.
