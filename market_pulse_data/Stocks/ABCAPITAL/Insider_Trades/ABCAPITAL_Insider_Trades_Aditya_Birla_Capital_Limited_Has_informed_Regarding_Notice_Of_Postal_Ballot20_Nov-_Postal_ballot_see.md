requirements.
Pursuant to Regulation 23(4) of the Listing Regulations, as amended, all material related party transactions (“RPTs”) and subsequent 
material modifications require prior approval of the shareholders through ordinary resolution.
As per the prescribed thresholds under Schedule XII of the Listing Regulations, where the annual consolidated turnover of the 
Company exceeds ` 40,000 Crore, a RPT is considered material if the transaction(s) to be entered into individually or taken together 
with previous transactions during a financial year exceeds ` 3,000 Crore plus 2.5% of the annual consolidated turnover of the 
listed entity above ` 40,000 Crore, or ` 5,000 Crore, whichever is lower. Accordingly, the materiality threshold for the financial year 
2026-27 for seeking shareholders’ approval for material RPTs of the Company is ` 3,137 Crore. The said limits are applicable even 
if the transactions are in the ordinary course of business and at an arm’s length basis.
Since, the aggregate value of the transactions proposed to be entered into between the Company and Grasim exceeds the 
above-mentioned threshold, such transactions qualify as material RPTs and approval of the Members is being sought by way of 
an Ordinary Resolution.
The Company is inter-alia engaged in the business of lending, factoring, assignment of receivables (AOR) and other financing 
activities. The proposed arrangement enables ABCL to provide receivables financing and working capital facilities to Grasim 
through AOR, factoring, reverse factoring and related financing arrangements. Under these arrangements, ABCL may acquire 
or finance trade receivables arising from the sale of goods or services by Grasim and provide liquidity against such receivables, 
thereby generating financing income and expanding ABCL’s trade finance and receivables financing portfolio. These facilities are 
without any recourse to Grasim.

the proposed transactions form part of their ordinary course of business. Based on current estimates and business requirements, 
approval is sought for a cumulative transaction limit of up to ` 4,950 Crore for the financial year 2026-27 (“RPT Period”). The 
transactions are proposed to be undertaken on an arm’s length basis and in ordinary course of business, in accordance with ABCL’s 
credit evaluation, underwriting and risk management frameworks, and on terms comparable to similar transactions undertaken 
with unrelated parties.
