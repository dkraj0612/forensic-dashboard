### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> **Executive Summary:**  Anand Rathi Wealth Limited shareholders have approved two key resolutions: a strategic amendment to its Memorandum of Association to enable acting as an Asset Management Company and Trustee for various investment funds, and the authorization of significant related party transactions (RPTs). The RPTs involve the purchase and/or sale of debentures/securities with Aligned Investment Private Limited, a related party, for an aggregate value up to Rs. 1,200 Crores by March 31, 2027. While the MoA change reflects a strategic growth initiative, the substantial volume of RPTs with a related entity, even if declared arm's length, represents a material financial commitment that warrants careful oversight due to its size and nature.

Resolution No. 1: Special Resolution of approval for Alteration of the Objects Clause of 
the Memorandum of Association (MoA) of the Company 
“RESOLVED THAT pursuant to the provisions of Section 13 and all other applicable 
provisions, if any, of the Companies Act, 2013 (including any statutory modification or 
re-enactment thereof for the time being in force), and the rules framed thereunder, 
and subject to necessary approvals, registrations, consents, and sanctions, if any, and 
any other appropriate regulatory or statutory authorities, approval of the Members of 
the Company be and is hereby accorded for the insertion/addition of a new clause 
8 in Clause III (A) of the Memorandum of Association of the Company, in the following 
manner: 
 
“8. To act, whether in India or abroad, either by itself or through its subsidiaries, joint 
ventures or associates, as a Sponsor, Asset Management Company, and/or Trustee 
for any type of investment funds, mutual funds, pooled investment vehicles, REITs, 
InvITs, alternative investment funds (AIFs), and set-up, operate financial/securities-
related business such as portfolio manager, investment advisory, research analyst, 
fund management entity, capital market intermediary and/or any other regulated 
entity(ies), and for that purpose to set up, promote, settle, sponsor and execute 
trusts, investment vehicles including REITs, INviTS, devise and manage various 
schemes for raising funds and to deploy such funds, seek appropriate 
licences/permissions, whether in India or abroad; to carry on, transact, and 
manage the business of an Sponsor, Asset Management Company, and/or Trustee 
company, and to engage in such other activities as may be permitted as per 
applicable laws; provided that the Company shall not carry on any business or 
activity which requires a registration, licence or approval under any law for the time 
being in force, including the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, the 
Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 
and the rules and regulations made thereunder, except upon obtaining such 
registration, licence or approval, if any.” 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Board of Directors of the Company (hereinafter referred 
to as ‘Board’ which term shall be deemed to include any duly constituted committee 
empowered to exercise its powers including powers conferred under this resolution) 
be and is hereby authorized to take all necessary steps and to execute all such 
documents, applications, filings, and declarations, and to do all such acts, deeds, and 
things as may be deemed necessary, proper, or expedient to give effect to this 
resolution, including filing of necessary e-forms with the Registrar of Companies 
(including e-Form MGT-14 within thirty days of the passing of this resolution, as required 
under Section 117 of the Act) and accepting such modifications in the said clause as 
may be required by the Registrar of Companies."
