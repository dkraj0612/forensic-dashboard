H1 FY27 Standalone PAT grows by 25% and AUM rises by 18% Y-o-Y 
Announces Interim Dividend of ₹ 4 per share 
 
Mumbai, 9th October 2026 - Anand Rathi Wealth Limited announced its financial results for 
the quarter ending September 2026 (Q2 FY27) and for the half year ending September 2026 
(H1 FY27). 
 
For July – September 2026 (Q2 FY27), the company posted a standalone business net profit of 
₹ 121 crores, registering a Y-o-Y increase of 25%.  Total revenue during this period rose by 
17% to ₹ 345 crores.  
For April – September 2026 (H1 FY27), the company posted a standalone net profit of ₹ 237 
crores, registering a Y-o-Y increase of 25%.  Total revenue during this period rose by 18% Y-
o-Y to ₹ 671 crores. 
 
For July – September 2026 (Q2 FY27), the company reported a consolidated net profit of ₹ 122 
crores, registering a Y-o-Y increase of 22%.  Total revenue during this period rose by 16% to ₹ 
357 crores. For April – September 2026 (H1 FY27), the company posted a consolidated net 
profit of ₹ 238 crores, registering a Y-o-Y increase of 23%.  Total revenue during this period 
rose by 17% to ₹ 693 crores.

Commenting on the market trends and results, Mr. Rakesh Rawal, CEO, and Mr. Feroze 
Azeez, Joint CEO, said: "We delivered another quarter of consistent and market-agnostic 
performance in a challenging quarter for Indian equities. Global factors such as ongoing 
conflict involving the US, Israel, & Iran, higher US bond yields, a strong US dollar, higher 
crude oil prices and continued foreign portfolio outflows weighed on market sentiment. In 
this environment, our performance reflects the strength of our uncomplicated wealth 
solutions for our clients and the collective efforts of our team. 
Excluding the impact of fair value gains on investments, ESOP expenses, and related tax 
effects, consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) for the first half of FY27, grew 23% year-on-year 
to ₹ 238 crores, while revenue increased 17% year-on-year to ₹ 693 crores. In the first six 
months, we have achieved 52% of our full-year PAT guidance of ₹ 460 crores and 49% of our 
full-year revenue guidance of ₹ 1,415 crores. 
Our Assets Under Management (AUM) stood at ₹ 1,08,377 crores as on 30th September 2026, 
an increase of 18% year-on-year, compared with a 8% decline in Nifty over the same period.  
Short-term volatility does not change the long-term opportunity. While foreign investors 
remained cautious, domestic investors continued to show strong conviction. Market 
corrections also create opportunities for disciplined investors, and our clients’ portfolios are 
positioned to benefit from these opportunities over time. This confidence was reflected in our 
inflows, with Q2 FY27 delivering our highest-ever quarterly Equity Mutual Fund net inflows 
of ₹ 2,867 crores and total net inflows of ₹4,186 crores, with both growing 39% year-on-year. 
A meaningful share of these inflows came from existing clients deepening their relationship 
with us. 
We added 1,500 plus client families on a net basis over the last twelve months, taking our total 
client base to 14,300 plus families. Our clients are becoming more informed and data-driven 
and understand our uncomplicated approach to achieving their wealth objectives. This is

About Anand Rathi Wealth Limited  
Anand Rathi Wealth Limited is among India’s leading wealth solutions firms, serving high 
and ultra-high-net-worth individuals through an uncomplicated, data-driven and research-
based approach that helps clients make informed decisions to achieve their wealth objectives. 
The Company operates across 19 cities in India, with an international presence in Dubai and 
London, UK. It has also received the requisite regulatory approvals to commence operations 
in GIFT City, India.
