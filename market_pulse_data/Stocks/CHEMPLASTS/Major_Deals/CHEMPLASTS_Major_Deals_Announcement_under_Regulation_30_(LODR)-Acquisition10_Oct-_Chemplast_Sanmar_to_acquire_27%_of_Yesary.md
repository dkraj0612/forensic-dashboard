Proposed Investment in /Acquisition of Equity Shares of Yesaryes Energy Private Limited

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 read with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 
January 30, 2026, we wish to inform that Chemplast Sanmar Limited (the “Company”) is entering  
into a Power Purchase Agreement with Yesaryes Energy Private Limited (“Power Producer”) and a 
Share Purchase Agreement with Veebee Yarnntex Private Limited (“Seller”) and Yesaryes Energy 
Private Limited in terms of which the Company will be acquiring equity shares of Yesaryes Energy 
Private Limited, details of which are contained in Annexure 1 below. This would enable the Company 
to source green energy (solar power) from the Power Producer under Group Captive Power Plant 
Scheme.  
  
Yesaryes Energy Private Limited is a Company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 and it is 
operating Solar energy Power Plant in Tamil Nadu having Capacity of 37.5 MW (the “Plant”). 
 
This would enable the Company to source environment friendly Green Energy (Solar power) from 
this Plant of the Power Producer, for captive consumption and would help in reducing the energy 
cost of the Company. 
 
Date & Time of occurrence of the event/information: 10th October, 2026 & 11.30 AM (IST) 
 
 
This is for your information and records.
