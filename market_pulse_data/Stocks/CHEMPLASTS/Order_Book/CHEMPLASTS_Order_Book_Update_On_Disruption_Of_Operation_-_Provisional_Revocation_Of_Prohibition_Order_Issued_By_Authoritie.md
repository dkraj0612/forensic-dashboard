Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Update on Disruption of 
operation – Provisional Revocation of Prohibition Order issued by 
Authorities 
 
Ref: 
i) Intimation dated July 18, 2026 regarding the disruption of operation 
at one of the Company’s plants namely Ethylene-Di-Chloride (EDC) 
manufacturing plant at Karaikal - Puducherry 
 
ii) Intimation dated July 20, 2026 regarding prohibition order issued 
by the Inspector of Factories, Puducherry 
iii) Intimation dated July 23, 2026 regarding Directions issued by the 
Puducherry Pollution Control Committee 
 
Further to the above intimations, upon satisfactory implementation of all 
necessary corrective measures to restore operations at the EDC plant, the 
Company has received the provisional revocation of the Prohibition Order issued 
by the Inspector of Factories, Puducherry and Puducherry Pollution Control 
Committee (PPCC), Puducherry in connection with the incident, to facilitate the 
recommencement of operations by adhering to certain conditions. Accordingly, the 
EDC plant re-commenced the operations today. 
 
The recommencement of operations will be reviewed by the PPCC before issuing 
the final revocation order. 
 
Date & Time of occurrence of the information: September 25, 2026 at &
