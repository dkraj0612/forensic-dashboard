As recommended by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of 
Directors at its meeting held today have approved the appointment of                         
Mr Prashanth Vasu (DIN: 11278134) as Non-Executive and Non-Independent 
Director of the Company with effect from 9th October, 2026 in the casual vacancy 
arising out of the resignation of Mr V S Radhakrishnan (DIN: 08064705) from the 
Board of Directors of the Company. 
 
Mr Prashanth Vasu is not related to any Director of the Company and is not 
debarred from holding the Office of Director by virtue of any order passed by SEBI 
or any other such authority. 
 
After the appointment of Mr Prashanth Vasu as Non - Executive and                        
Non- Independent Director of the Company, the  composition of Board of Directors 
of the Company will be 5 (five) Independent Directors and 3 (three) Non – 
Independent Directors. 
 
Details with respect to the appointment of Mr Prashanth Vasu as required under 
Regulation 30(6) read with Schedule III Part A Para A (7) of the Listing Regulations 
and SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026
