We are pleased to inform that Axita Cotton Limited a leading Cotton Bales Manufacturer, has on August 21, 2026 
submitted Expression of Interest for participation in the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) of Varidhi 
Cotspin Private Limited. 
 
Varidhi Cotspin Private Limited (Varidhi) is a manufacturer of cotton yarn located in Dholka, Ahmedabad, Gujarat and 
having its Manufacturing Unit established in 2017. Varidhi is having a spinning facility housing 29,184 spindles 
featuring a spinning unit capacity of roughly 4,442 metric tonnes per annum (MTPA). Its produce Ring Spun 100% 
Cotton, Compact, Combed, Carded Yarn for hosiery and weaving industries. 
 
Axita Cotton Limited is keen to acquire Varidhi Cotspin Private Limited through the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 
(IBC) process, which is expected to complement the Company’s existing operations and support its long-term business 
expansion strategy by strengthening its presence across the cotton value chain. 
 
Axita Cotton Limited has established itself as a quality-focused cotton manufacturer and exporter, with a strong 
emphasis on quality, timely delivery, customer satisfaction, innovation and sustainability. The Company’s established 
presence in the cotton industry provides a strong foundation to explore opportunities in complementary segments of 
the textile value chain. 
 
The proposed participation in the CIRP of Varidhi represents a strategic opportunity for Axita Cotton Limited to 
expand its business operations and explore potential synergies in cotton and yarn manufacturing.
