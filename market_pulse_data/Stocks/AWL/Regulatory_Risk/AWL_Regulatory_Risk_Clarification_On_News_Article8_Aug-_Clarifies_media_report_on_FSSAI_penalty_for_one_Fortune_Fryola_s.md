Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Sub: Clarification/Confirmation on news item pertaining to AWL Agri Business 
Limited (“the Company”) appearing in “Media/ Publication”.  
 
This is in relation to the article appeared on www.thehindubusinessline.com dated August 
7 2026 titled “FSSAI imposes penalty, adjudication order on AWL Agri Business Ltd”. 
 
In this regard, we wish to state that it has come to the notice of the Company through 
various media reports and social media posts referring to a penalty purportedly imposed 
by the Ld. Adjudicating District Magistrate, Goa, under the provisions of the Food Safety 
and Standards Act, 2006 and rules thereunder in relation to a single sample of Fortune 
Fryola sunflower oil, a niche variant of Fortune Sunflower Oil. The Company has not yet 
received a certified copy of the adjudication order. Based on the information available to 
the Company, the matter relates to declared levels of Vitamins A and D in that specific 
sample and does not relate to the safety of the product. It may be noted that added 
vitamins are inherently sensitive to storage conditions, light, and time. 
 
At AWL Agri Business Limited, product safety and quality remain our highest priorities. 
Our sourcing, manufacturing, refining, packaging, and quality-control processes comply 
with stringent food safety standards, and we are committed to deliver products 
confirming all applicable FSSAI requirements.  
 
The Company will review the adjudication order upon receipt and pursue such legal 
remedies as may be considered appropriate. The article has no material impact on the 
financial position or business operations of the Company.
