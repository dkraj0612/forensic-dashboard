> **Executive Summary: Atlantaa Limited Credit Rating Reaffirmation**  Atlantaa Limited's credit ratings were reaffirmed at IVR BB+/Stable (Long Term) and IVR A4+ (Short Term), despite significant financial deterioration in FY26, including negative EBITDA and interest coverage, and highly volatile operating income. A major red flag is the nascent stage of its key Atlantaa Enclave II real estate project, which is only 19% complete with a low cash flow adequacy ratio of 24.55%, indicating substantial execution and market risk. This financial fragility and project risk are compounded by a 5-day delay in disclosing the reaffirmed ratings to the exchanges, attributed to an internal error.

Experienced Promoters 
Promoters of the company namely Rickiin Barot and Rajhoo Barot have more than two to four 
decades of experience in the infrastructure industry. Promoters of the firm are supported by an 
experienced second line of management. The extensive experience of promoters in the industry 
has enabled the promoters to understand the complexity of the business and develop its 
reputation in the domestic market. 
Long track record of operations 
The company has been in operation for more than four decades since its inception in 1984. It has 
an extensive experience in construction and maintenance infrastructure projects for various 
government and local authorities. The company also has experience in development of real estate 
projects for both residential and commercial purposes. This long-standing experience has helped 
the company to build strong relationships with its suppliers and customers. 
Weaknesses 
Volatility in scale of operations 
The company’s scale of operations has remained volatile, with total operating income increasing 
sharply from Rs. 14.06 crore in FY23 to Rs. 88.52 crore in FY24, before declining to Rs. 29.49 crore 
in FY25 and further to Rs. 27.23 crore in FY26. The sharp increase in FY24 was primarily driven 
by higher revenue from the EPC segment; however, EPC income subsequently moderated from 
Rs. 87.92 crore in FY24 to Rs. 29.49 crore in FY25 and Rs. 12.57 crore in FY26. The decline was 
partly offset by revenue of Rs. 14.30 crore from the real estate segment in FY26 following the 
commencement of the Atlantaa Enclave II project during Q4FY26. The company’s revenue profile 
remains susceptible to the timing of project awards, pace of execution, receipt of statutory 
approvals, availability of work fronts and milestone-based revenue recognition inherent in the 
EPC and real estate businesses. 
Nascent stage of construction and project-execution risk: 
As of June 30, 2026, Atlantaa Enclave II had incurred Rs.91.41 crore against an estimated project 
cost of Rs.475.07 crore, translating into construction progress of approximately 19.24%.

Accordingly, with less than one-fifth of the estimated project cost incurred and substantial 
construction remaining until the RERA completion date of December 31, 2028, the project is 
considered to be at a nascent stage. Cash flow adequacy ratio (defined as committed receivables 
from sold units divided by balance construction cost and debt outstanding) is 24.55% for this 
project as of June 30, 2026 reflecting the significant execution risk linked to this project. . Out of 
total 805 units, the company had achieved 203 bookings as of September 18, 2026, reflecting 
approximately 25.00% of bookings. The project therefore remains exposed to market and 
collection risks as the projectis funded through Promoters contribution and customer advances. 
The overall cash-flow coverage ratio is approximately 1.29x FY27-FY29. Dependence on the 
timely conversion of bookings into collections expose the project to execution delays, cost 
overruns and pressure on cash flows. The company’s ability to accelerate construction, improve 
bookings and collections, and adhere to the milestones stipulated under the RERA schedule will 
remain key rating monitorable. 
Further, the company has undertaken a redevelopment project in Borivali, which is presently at 
the approval stage and is expected to be launched in November 2026. Any delay in obtaining the 
requisite approvals or executing the projects, coupled with slower-than-anticipated bookings and 
collections or escalation in project costs, could lead to continued volatility in revenue and 
profitability and exert pressure on the company’s cash flows and overall financial risk profile. 
Exposure to cyclicality and regulatory risks in the real estate sector 
Atlantaa Limited remains exposed to the cyclical real estate sector, with demand and cash flows 
sensitive to macroeconomic conditions, interest rates and consumer affordability. Delays in 
obtaining regulatory approvals may affect project launches, sales and collections. 
Liquidity ~Adequate 
The liquidity position of the company is expected to remain adequate over the near term. The 
current ratio remained comfortable at 12.02x as on March 31, 2026. Cash and Cash Equivalents 
as on March 31, 2026, Rs.11.42 crore. Further, the absence of scheduled debt repayment 
obligations provides additional financial flexibility. The company has not availed any term loans 
from financial institutions for funding either of its two on-going real estate development projects.
