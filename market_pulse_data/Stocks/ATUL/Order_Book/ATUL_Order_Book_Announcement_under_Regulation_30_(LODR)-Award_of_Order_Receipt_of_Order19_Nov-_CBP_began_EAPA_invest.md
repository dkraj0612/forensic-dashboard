Pursuant to the Regulation 30 of the Regulations, we inform you that Atul USA Inc, a wholly-owned 
subsidiary entity of the Company has received a notice from U.S. Customs and Border Protection Office 
under the Trade Facilitation and Trade Enforcement Act of 2015, commonly referred as the Enforcement 
and Protect Act (EAPA) for alleged evasion of antidumping duty (AD) and countervailing duty (CVD) on 
imports by it. The details as required under the Regulations are as under: 
 
Name of the authority 
U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP)
Nature and details of the action(s) taken, 
initiated or order(s) passed; 
CBP has initiated an investigation based on the suspicion 
that Chinese 2,4-Dichlorophenoxyacetic Acid (2,4-D) and 
its derivatives were transhipped through India to the USA 
warranting higher AD and CVD. The investigation is 
initiated against Atul USA Inc to ascertain alleged evasion 
of AD and CVD on 2,4-D and its derivatives.  As an interim 
measure, CBP has suspended the liquidation of each 
unliquidated entry of such covered merchandise that 
entered into the USA after August 11, 2026 or as permitted 
by law. CBP may also take additional measures necessary 
to protect the revenue of the United States.
