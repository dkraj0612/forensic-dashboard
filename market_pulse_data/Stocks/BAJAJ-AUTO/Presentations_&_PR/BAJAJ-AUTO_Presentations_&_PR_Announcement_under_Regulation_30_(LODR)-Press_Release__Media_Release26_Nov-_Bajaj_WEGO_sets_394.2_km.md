Bajaj WEGO sets a record of 394.2 km on a single charge and becomes the first 
electric three-wheeler to complete the Kashmir-to-Kanyakumari journey 
 
• 
Bajaj WEGO covers 394.2 km on a single charge, setting a new record for electric 3-wheelers 
• 
Becomes the first electric three-wheeler to complete the iconic Kashmir to Kanyakumari 
journey, covering almost 4,023 km in nine days 
• 
Both records have been recognized with the India Book of Records and Asia Book of Records 
 
Pune, 26th November 2026: Bajaj Auto Ltd., the world’s most valuable two-wheeler and three-
wheeler company, today announced two records set by Bajaj WEGO, its electric three-wheeler brand. 
Bajaj WEGO set the record for the longest distance covered by an electric three-wheeler on a single 
charge, travelling 394.2 km between Namakkal and Tirunelveli in Tamil Nadu. It also became the first 
electric three-wheeler to travel from Kashmir to Kanyakumari, covering a total distance of 4,023 km in 
nine days. Both records have been recognised by the India Book of Records and Asia Book of Records, 
with certificates to be jointly issued by the two organisations. 
Engineered for ‘Maha-range’ 
Bajaj WEGO is engineered to maximise the range requirements of electric three-wheeler customers. 
Its 394.2 km record, achieved on a single charge between Namakkal and Tirunelveli in Tamil Nadu, 
demonstrates its range capability in real-world conditions across varied Indian terrains and road 
conditions. For operators, this means covering longer distances with fewer charging interruptions and 
managing more trips through the day. 
A cross-country first for electric three-wheelers 
Bajaj WEGO’s 4,023 km journey began at Lal Chowk, Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, on 27 October 2026 
and concluded in Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu, on 5 November 2026, covering the distance over nine 
days. 
Mr. Narvijay Yadav, Adjudicator with the India Book of Records and Asia Book of Records, joined the 
expedition at Srinagar. Mr. Sanjay Bhola, also an Adjudicator with both organisations, was present 
during the final leg of the journey in Kanyakumari. 
Recognised by independent record organisations 
The India Book of Records has been engaged in record-making for over 19 years and is registered with 
the Government of India. It is affiliated with the Asia Book of Records and follows the Asian Protocol 
of Records (APRs), based on a consensus reached among the chief editors of national record books at 
a meeting in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. The Asia Book of Records brings together record holders from 
major national record books, including those of India, Vietnam, Indo-China, Laos and Nepal, providing 
a platform for them to compare achievements and seek recognition as Asia Book of Records holders. 
It maintains a database of 40,000 entries. 
Together, the two independently registered records validate Bajaj WEGO's ‘Maha-range’ proposition, 
demonstrating both its single-charge range and sustained endurance.
