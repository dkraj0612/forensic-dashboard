### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aeroflex Enterprises Limited reported exceptionally strong Q1 FY27 results, with Total Income up 134.55% and PAT surging 615.76% YoY, primarily driven by a significant ₹227.42 Crore one-time gain from the divestment of its 68% stake in MRO. Operationally, the company expanded its "critical engineering ecosystem" with an investment in Stilonn Valves and strategically entered the liquid cooling solutions market for data centers via a major long-term agreement. While the reported profits are substantial, the significant portion attributable to the non-recurring MRO divestment gain means underlying operational EBITDA growth was a more modest 35.63% YoY, which investors should consider.
