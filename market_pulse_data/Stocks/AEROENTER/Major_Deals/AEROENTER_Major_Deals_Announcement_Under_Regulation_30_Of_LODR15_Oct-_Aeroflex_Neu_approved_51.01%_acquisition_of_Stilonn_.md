### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aeroflex Neu Limited, a material subsidiary of Aeroflex Enterprises Limited, has approved the acquisition of an additional 31.43% equity stake in Stilonn Valves and Controls Private Limited for ₹16.00 crore, bringing its total ownership to 51.01% and making Stilonn a subsidiary. This strategic move aims to expand Aeroflex Neu's presence in high-growth AI infrastructure, data center, and critical healthcare sectors through Stilonn's precision engineering products. While the transaction is identified as a related party acquisition, the company affirms it is being undertaken on an arm's length basis.

Subject: Update – Approval of Acquisition of Equity Shares of Stilonn Valves and 
Controls Private Limited by Aeroflex Neu Limited, a Material Subsidiary of the

Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to 
inform you that the Board of Directors of Aeroflex Neu Limited, the Material Subsidiary of 
the Company, at its meeting held on October 15, 2026, has, inter alia, approved acquisition 
of 7,27,040 equity shares of Stilonn Valves and Controls Private Limited ("Stilonn"), 
Company engaged in the business of manufacturing of precision engineering products in the 
high-growth AI Infra and Data Centre space along with critical pharmaceutical and 
healthcare sector, including valves, sanitary fittings and allied goods, for an aggregate 
consideration of ₹16,00,21,504/- (Rupees Sixteen Crore Twenty-One Thousand Five 
Hundred and Four Only). The acquisition shall be undertaken in one or more tranches 
through a combination of purchase of equity shares from the existing shareholders of Stilonn 
and subscription to additional equity shares proposed to be issued by Stilonn, pursuant to 
the execution of a Share Purchase Agreement, Share Subscription and Shareholders' 
Agreement and such other ancillary Transaction Documents as may be necessary. 
Consequent to the completion of the proposed acquisition, the shareholding of Aeroflex Neu 
Limited in Stilonn shall stand at 51.01% of its issued and paid-up equity share capital, 
thereby resulting in Stilonn becoming a Subsidiary of Aeroflex Neu Limited. 
 
This acquisition forms part of Aeroflex Neu Limited’s initiative in the AI Infra and Data 
Centre sector and in establishing an integrated platform for high-tech and critical 
engineering solutions.
