Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India

the Board of Directors of Aeroflex Neu Limited (“Aeroflex Neu”), the Material Subsidiary of the 
Company at its meeting held today, i.e., Friday, October 30, 2026, inter alia, considered and 
approved to enter into wire and cable manufacturing and exporting business by acquiring 1,10,000 
Equity Shares of Aerolex Cables Private Limited (“Aerolex”), representing 55% of its issued and 
paid-up share capital, for ₹ 3100 lakhs. The Board of Aeroflex Neu also approved the draft Share 
Purchase and Shareholders Agreements and ancillary transaction documents. Upon completion, 
Aerolex will become subsidiary of Aeroflex Neu Limited. 
 
Aeroflex Neu shall acquire additional Aerolex shares in subsequent tranche(s), subject to definitive 
agreements to be signed by the parties. 
The acquisition supports Aeroflex Neu’s growth and diversification plans by enabling it to enter and 
expand into the manufacturing of wire and wire-related products business. It will broaden its product 
range and strengthen its position in the AI Infra and Data Centre sector to establish an integrated 
platform for high-tech and critical engineering solutions. 
Aeroflex Neu Limited has given the requisite Disclosure for the aforesaid to the stock exchanges.
