Limited, the Material Subsidiary of the Company 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that 
the Board of Directors of Aeroflex Industries Limited, the Material Subsidiary of the Company, at 
its meeting held today, i.e., Wednesday, October 14, 2026, inter alia, noted and approved the capacity 
enhancements and expansion plans as follows: 
 
1. Noted completion of SS Hose & Braiding Division capacity enhancement to 20 million 
meters per annum. 
 
2. Noted completion of Liquid Cooling SFN Skid Assemblies Division capacity enhancement 
to 12,000 pieces per annum; the remaining 3,000 pieces per annum is expected by December 
2026. 
 
3. Approved enhancement of SS Hose & Braiding Division capacity from 20 million to 30 
million meters per annum and a setting up of a new State of the Art Assemblies Plant to 
convert 70%–75% of hoses and braids into assemblies, at an estimated investment of ₹124 
crores which is expected to be completed in phased manner by March 2028. 
 
4. Approved enhancement of Liquid Cooling SFN Skid Assemblies Division capacity from 
15,000 to 30,000 pieces per annum and setting up of an additional facility, at an estimated 
investment of ₹78 crores which is expected to be completed in phased manner by March 
2028. 
 
The aggregate investment of ₹202 crore for SS Hose & Braiding and liquid cooling SFN 
Skid Assemblies expansions will be financed through Internal Accruals, Equity and Bridge 
Loan Financing.
