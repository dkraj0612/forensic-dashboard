Subject:  Update on Acquisition of Equity Stake in Stilonn Valves and Controls Private 
Limited ("Stilonn") by Aeroflex Neu Limited, Material Subsidiary of the Company 
With reference to our earlier intimation dated October 15, 2026 regarding the proposed acquisition 
of equity stake in Stilonn Valves and Controls Private Limited ("Stilonn") by Aeroflex Neu Limited, 
material subsidiary of the Company, we wish to inform you that Aeroflex Neu Limited has completed 
the acquisition of an aggregate 7,27,040 equity shares of Stilonn through a combination of purchase 
of equity shares from existing shareholders pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement and 
subscription to additional equity shares of Stilonn pursuant to the Share Subscription and 
Shareholders' Agreement and ancillary Transaction Documents. 
 
Consequent to completion of the aforesaid acquisition, the aggregate shareholding of Aeroflex Neu 
Limited in Stilonn stands at 51.01% of its issued and paid-up equity share capital and, accordingly, 
Stilonn has become a subsidiary of Aeroflex Neu Limited. 
 
The acquisition forms part of the Aeroflex Neu Limited’s initiative in the AI Infra and Data Centre 
sector to establish an integrated platform for high-tech and critical engineering solutions. 
 
It is further informed that, Aeroflex Neu Limited has made the requisite disclosure regarding the 
acquisition of equity stake to the stock exchanges which is enclosed herewith. 
Request you to kindly take the same on record.
