SHRI MANOJ KUMAR SHARMA ASSUMES CHARGE AS  
DIRECTOR (PROJECTS) OF GAIL (INDIA) LIMITED 
 
 
New Delhi, September 19, 2026: Shri Manoj Kumar Sharma today assumed the charge as Director 
(Projects) of GAIL (India) Limited, India’s integrated energy major. 
  
Prior to assuming charge as Director (Projects), GAIL (India) Limited, Shri Sharma served as 
Executive Director and Regional Head, Eastern Region Pipelines, at Indian Oil Corporation Limited 
(IOCL). He was responsible for operations and maintenance, construction and City Gas Distribution 
(CGD) businesses across five states, overseeing a pipeline network spanning more than 5,700 km. 
  
With a Master’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering (Production & Industrial Engineering) from Delhi 
College of Engineering (DCE) and a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Management from Xavier 
Institute of Management, Bhubaneswar (XIMB), Shri Sharma brings extensive experience of over 
three decades across the entire value chain of hydrocarbon infrastructure, encompassing design and 
engineering, project management, procurement, construction management, and operations and 
maintenance of crude oil, petroleum products, LPG, and natural gas pipeline networks, as well as 
strategically significant crude oil terminal projects. 
  
Earlier, Shri Sharma has also served as Executive Director (Projects), Pipelines Division, IOCL, where 
he was responsible for engineering, procurement and construction management of major oil, LPG and 
natural gas pipeline projects in India and overseas. 
  
He has served on the Boards of GSPL India Transco Limited (GITL), GSPL India Gasnet Limited 
(GIGL), and Kochi Salem Pipeline Private Limited (KSPPL) as Nominee Director representing IOCL, 
contributing to strategic governance and the development of energy infrastructure. 
  
With extensive leadership experience and a proven track record in board-level governance, project 
execution, business development, operational excellence and cost optimisation, Shri Sharma brings a 
comprehensive understanding of the energy infrastructure sector along with his focus on innovation 
and emerging energy initiatives that will contribute to GAIL’s continued growth and transformation 
in the evolving energy landscape.
