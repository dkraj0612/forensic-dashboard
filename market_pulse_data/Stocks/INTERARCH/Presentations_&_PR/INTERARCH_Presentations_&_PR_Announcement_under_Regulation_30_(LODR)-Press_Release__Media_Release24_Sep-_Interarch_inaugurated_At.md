Press Release 
Interarch Inaugurates Advanced Heavy Structural Steel Manufacturing Facility in Attivaram, 
Andhra Pradesh 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
24 September 2026: Interarch Building Solutions Limited (BSE: 544232 | NSE: INTERARCH), a 
leading provider of turnkey steel construction solutions, today inaugurated its new heavy 
structural steel manufacturing facility at Attivaram, Andhra Pradesh, marking a significant 
expansion of its capabilities in the design and manufacture of complex steel structures. 
The facility has been developed to address the increasing requirement for heavy and high-
performance structural steel in projects where scale, precision and engineering complexity 
are critical. It will enable Interarch to manufacture structural components for applications 
ranging from high-rise buildings and data centres to semiconductor and electronics facilities, 
renewable energy projects, EV infrastructure and large industrial developments. 
Built across approximately 20 acres, the Phase 1 utilises around 25,000 sq. m. of built-up 
area and has an initial manufacturing capacity of 24,000 MT per annum. This plant is 
equipped with specialised equipment sourced from leading technology providers in Europe 
and India, enabling high-precision fabrication and the manufacturing of complex heavy 
structural members. The balance land is earmarked for the development of Phases 2 and 3, 
of which the civil works for Phase 2 currently underway. 
A key feature of the facility is its ability to undertake demanding fabrication requirements, 
including large-sized hot-rolled sections and plate girder structural members, designed for 
projects with stringent engineering and dimensional specifications. This facility expands 
Interarch’s manufacturing portfolio beyond conventional PEB applications and strengthens its 
ability to participate in projects requiring sophisticated and heavy structural steel solutions.

Located in Andhra Pradesh, the facility also provides a strategic manufacturing base for 
servicing customers across South and West India. Its proximity to industrial clusters, ports 
and major transportation networks is expected to support efficient movement of materials 
and finished structures to project locations. 
The new facility complements Interarch’s existing manufacturing ecosystem in Attivaram. 
The company had earlier commissioned Phase 2 of its PEB facility at the location, with an 
installed capacity of 24,000 MT per annum and an investment of approximately ₹32 crore, 
making Attivaram an increasingly important manufacturing hub for Interarch’s steel 
construction operations. 
Mr. Arvind Nanda, Managing Director, Interarch Building Solutions Ltd, said, “The 
inauguration of our Attivaram facility marks a significant milestone in Interarch’s evolution as 
a comprehensive steel construction solutions provider. India is entering a phase of accelerated 
industrial and infrastructure development, creating demand for steel structures that are 
larger, more sophisticated and engineered for greater performance. This facility strengthens 
our ability to participate in that opportunity with enhanced manufacturing depth and a 
broader portfolio of capabilities. Our focus is not simply on adding capacity, but on building 
the technological and execution capabilities required to support the next generation of 
industrial and infrastructure projects.” 
Mr. Gautam Suri, Whole-Time Director, Interarch Building Solutions Ltd, said, “Attivaram 
represents a significant advancement in our heavy structural steel manufacturing capabilities. 
The facility brings together advanced machinery, precision fabrication and specialised 
manufacturing processes to address structural requirements that demand a high degree of

assemblies, our objective is to deliver consistent quality at scale while meeting the 
increasingly demanding specifications of modern construction. This capability will enable us 
to take on more complex projects and translate innovative structural designs into efficiently 
manufactured, high-quality steel solutions.”

About Interarch Building Solutions Limited 
Interarch Pre- Pre-Engineered Buildings 
Interarch Building Solutions Limited commenced its operations in 1983. Today, 40 years later, 
Interarch is one of the leading turnkey pre-engineered steel construction solution providers 
in India with integrated facilities for design and engineering, manufacturing, and on-site 
project management capabilities for the installation and erection of pre-engineered steel 
buildings. 
TRACDEK® Metal Roofing & Cladding Systems 
We manufacture world-class pre-engineered roofing and cladding systems designed to meet 
custom requirements. Our extensive portfolio includes the Hi-Rib Roofing & Cladding System, 
Klippon Roofing & Cladding System, and SS-2000 Standing Seam Roofing System. Each of 
these solutions is engineered to provide exceptional performance and durability, ensuring 
that we meet a wide range of needs with precision and reliability. 
Trac® Ceilings 
Our TRAC® range of metal ceilings are manufactured from fully recyclable materials and are 
pre- painted and prefabricated in our factory. The ceiling systems are friendly to handle and 
will not promote the growth of bacteria and fungi. TRAC® ceilings exhibit excellent corrosion 
resistance. They can withstand very high humidity (up to 100 %) and are suitable for use in 
outdoor applications. 
Interarch Life: Non-Industrial Buildings 
We offer a state-of-the-art solution for non-industrial buildings through our innovative load-
bearing wall framing systems, crafted to support lightweight structures. Embracing a hassle-
free drywall construction approach, these systems guarantee swift and efficient assembly, 
simplifying the construction process for our esteemed clients. With a focus on durability and 
safety, our load- bearing wall framing systems are engineered to be earthquake and termite-
proof, providing robust protection for the structure and its occupants. 
Moreover, these systems offer the flexibility of dismantling if required, providing adaptability 
for future changes or modifications. These structures can be custom-made according to the 
specific plans, accommodating varying shapes, sizes, and designs to meet the exact needs of 
customers.
