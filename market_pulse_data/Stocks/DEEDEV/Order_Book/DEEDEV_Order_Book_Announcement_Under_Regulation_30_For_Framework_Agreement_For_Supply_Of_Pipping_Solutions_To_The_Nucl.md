Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) – Framework Agreement for 
the Nuclear Power Sector 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations, we wish to inform that the Company 
has entered into a Framework Agreement with a domestic EPC contractor, for jointly pursuing 
business opportunities in the nuclear power sector. Under the Framework Agreement, the 
Company will act as a strategic partner to the EPC contractor and will provide pre-bid 
support, design and engineering, manufacturing, supply and sourcing of piping products, 
and related documentation. 
 
The details as required under SEBI Listing Regulations read with Master Circular no. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are enclosed as Annexure I.
