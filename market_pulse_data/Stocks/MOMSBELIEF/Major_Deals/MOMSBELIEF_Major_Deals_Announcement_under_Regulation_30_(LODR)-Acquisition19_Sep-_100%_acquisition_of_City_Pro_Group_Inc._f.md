Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read

Belief US Inc. and, accordingly, a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company with effect from 
September 18, 2026.
