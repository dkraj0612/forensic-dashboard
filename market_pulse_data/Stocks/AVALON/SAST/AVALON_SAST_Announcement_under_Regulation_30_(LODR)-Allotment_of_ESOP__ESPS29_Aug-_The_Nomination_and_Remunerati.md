Pursuant to the above-mentioned SEBI Regulations, we would like to inform you that on Saturday, 
August 29, 2026, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, through Circular Resolution, allotted

from INR 13,36,23,510 comprising of 6,68,11,755 equity shares to INR 13,37,64,600 comprising of     
6,68,82,300 equity shares of Face Value Rs. 2/- each.
