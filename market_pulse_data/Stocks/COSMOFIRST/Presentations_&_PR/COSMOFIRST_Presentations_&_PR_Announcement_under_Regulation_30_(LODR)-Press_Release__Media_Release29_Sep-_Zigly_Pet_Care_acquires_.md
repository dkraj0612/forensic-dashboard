Zigly Pet Care strengthens veterinary care footprint with acquisition of leading 
speciality veterinary clinic in Mumbai 
 
Mumbai, September 29, 2026: Zigly Pet Care, India’s ﬁrst omnichannel pet care brand, today announced 
the acquisition of Prolife Speciality Vet Clinic, managed by Dr. S. M. Gadge in Malad, Mumbai, further 
expanding its veterinary healthcare network and fast-tracking its strategy to build one of the most 
comprehensive pet care ecosystems in India. This is Zigly Pet Care’s third strategic acquisition as the 
company continues to expand its veterinary care network with existing practices and is expected to be 
operational 24x7 in the next nine to twelve months. 
 
Operational since year 2000, the Prolife Speciality Vet Clinic has built a reputation for clinical excellence, 
advanced surgical capabilities and trusted veterinary care, treating close to 20K pets and reporting an ARR 
of INR 4.36 crore. Currently, the clinic is oﬀering outpatient consultations, diagnostics, surgery, endoscopy, 
pathology and pharmacy services and will continue to operate with its existing team of veterinarians, a 
centre manager and experienced support staﬀ, ensuring continuity of care for existing pet parents.  
 
Commenting on the acquisition, Saurabh Jain, Chief Executive Oﬃcer, Zigly Pet Care, said, “India’s pet 
care industry is evolving rapidly, with pet parents increasingly seeking specialised healthcare backed by 
clinical expertise and round the clock support. Prolife Speciality Vet Clinic has earned the trust of Mumbai’s 
pet parent community over more than two decades, making it an ideal partner in our growth journey. As 
we plan to transform this into a 24x7 facility and integrating it with the Zigly Pet Care ecosystem, we are 
expanding access to advanced veterinary care while strengthening trusted our ability to deliver a seamless 
pet parenting experience. This acquisition also reﬂects our long-term strategy of scaling high-quality 
veterinary services through strategic partnerships with respected clinicians across the country.” 
 
The addition of the Mumbai clinic will further expand Zigly Pet Care’s veterinary footprint in the city and 
strengthen its access to quality, specialised pet healthcare. This follows Zigly Pet Care’s earlier acquisitions 
of Small Animal Clinic & Surgical Centre in Khar West and Dr. Santa’s Pet Clinic, reﬂecting the company’s 
focus on expanding its network through partnerships with established veterinary practices. These 
acquisitions enable Zigly Pet Care to build a wider network of experienced veterinary professionals and 
bring specialised clinical expertise closer to pet parents across neighbourhoods. The expanded footprint 
will also help improve access to timely consultations, diagnostics and treatment, particularly for pets 
requiring ongoing or specialised care. By bringing trusted neighbourhood clinics into its ecosystem, the 
company aims to make quality and pro-active veterinary care more accessible while strengthening its 
presence in key markets. 
 
As pet ownership in India continues to grow and the demand shifts towards specialised and accessible 
veterinary care, Zigly Pet Care is focused on expanding its healthcare footprint through a mix of greenﬁeld 
expansion and strategic acquisitions. The decision is aligned with the brand’s commitment to strengthen 
veterinary infrastructure, support seasoned clinicians and make world-class pet healthcare more 
accessible to pet families nationwide.
