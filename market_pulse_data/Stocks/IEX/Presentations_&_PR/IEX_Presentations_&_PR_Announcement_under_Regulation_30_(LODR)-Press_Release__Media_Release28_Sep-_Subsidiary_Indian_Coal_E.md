IEX EXPANDS ITS ENERGY MARKETS FOOTPRINT: SUBSIDIARY INDIAN COAL EXCHANGE LTD
APPLIES FOR LICENCE WITH COAL CONTROLLER ORGANISATION
NEW DELHI, MONDAY, 28TH SEPTEMBER 2026: Indian Energy Exchange (IEX), India’s premier
electricity exchange, today announced that its wholly owned subsidiary, Indian Coal Exchange
Limited, has applied for a licence with the Coal Controller Organisation (CCO), the regulatory
authority for coal exchanges.
IEX has incorporated Indian Coal Exchange Limited on June 1, 2026, with an authorised share
capital of Rs 100 crore. Indian Coal Exchange will be a physical delivery-based coal trading
exchange, providing an organised, transparent and technology-enabled marketplace for coal
trading in accordance with the Coal Exchange Rules, 2026. By bringing buyers and sellers
together on a single, neutral platform, Indian Coal Exchange will facilitate trades at designated
delivery points, enabling efficient price discovery and wider market access.
Backed by IEX’s 18 years of experience in building transparent, technology-driven and market-
based trading platforms, the Indian Coal Exchange will mark the Group’s entry into the coal
market. Complementing IEX’s electricity market business, the Indian Gas Exchange (IGX),
India’s leading natural gas trading platform, and its role in facilitating I-REC issuance in India,
Indian Coal Exchange will further broaden the group’s energy market offerings across the
energy value chain.
ABOUT IEX
IEX is India’s premier electricity exchange providing a nationwide, automated trading platform for physical
delivery of electricity, renewable power, renewable energy certificates and energy saving certificates. The
exchange platform enables efficient price discovery and increases the accessibility and transparency of the power
market in India while also enhancing the speed and efficiency of trade execution. The Exchange is ISO Certified
for quality management, Information security management and environment management since August 2016.
The Exchange is a publicly listed company with NSE and BSE since October 2017 and is approved and regulated
by Central Electricity Regulatory Commission since 27 June 2008.
