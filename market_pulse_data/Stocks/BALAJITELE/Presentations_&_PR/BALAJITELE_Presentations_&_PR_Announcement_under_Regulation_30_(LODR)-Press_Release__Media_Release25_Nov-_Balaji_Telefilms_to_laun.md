A New Match, A New Format: Balaji Telefilms Brings Relationship-Led Entertainment to 
YouTube with ‘It's A Meal’ 
 
Featuring popular television personalities Mannara Chopra, Nyrraa M Banerji, Aman Gandhi, 
Akanksha Chamola and others, and hosted by Urvashi Dholakia, the show brings together 
entertainment, relationships and unexpected connections 
 
Mumbai, November 25, 2026: What happens when different personalities come together in the 
hope of finding that one special connection? Bringing a refreshing twist to the world of relationships 
and matchmaking, Balaji Telefilms is set to launch It's A Meal, an upcoming digital entertainment 
show premiering exclusively from 19th December on the Balaji Telefilms YouTube channel. Blending 
familiar faces, spontaneous conversations and the possibility of finding love, the show promises an 
entertaining journey filled with chemistry, surprises and plenty of unexpected moments. 
 
Featuring some of the most popular personalities from the television industry, including Mannara 
Chopra, Nyrraa M Banerji, Aman Gandhi, Akanksha Chamola and others, It's A Meal brings together 
singles from different backgrounds and personalities as they navigate the exciting, and sometimes 
unpredictable, journey of making connections. Hosted by Urvashi Dholakia, the show adds another 
layer of entertainment with her distinctive charm and energy, making the search for a perfect match 
all the more engaging. 
 
For the participants, the experience is as much about stepping outside their comfort zones as it is 
about discovering new connections. Speaking about being part of the show, actress Akanksha 
Chamola said, “This show is sure to get people out of their comfort zones to find their favourite 
person. I believe every person has a different story, and sometimes all it takes is one conversation to 
discover a connection. I'm looking forward to the fun, the surprises and everything this experience 
has in store.” 
 
Sharing her excitement, Nyrraa M Banerji added, “Love, chemistry and relationships, these are 
always interesting because everyone has a different way of looking at them. What I find exciting 
about this show is that I'll get to meet people coming from different walks of life and see how things 
go with them. It's going to be challenging at times for sure but also very surprising.” 
 
The Format was created by Adhiksha Gupta & Mohammed Nagaman Lateef. Beyond its 
entertainment proposition, It's A Meal also reflects Balaji Telefilms' continued efforts to explore 
fresh concepts and diversify its digital content portfolio. As viewing preferences evolve and digital 
platforms create opportunities for new storytelling formats, the company is looking at content 
propositions that combine relatable themes with personality-driven entertainment to engage 
audiences across platforms.

Commenting on the show's launch, Sanjay Dwivedi, Group CEO & CFO, Balaji Telefilms, said, “At 
Balaji Telefilms, our focus has always been on identifying evolving audience preferences and 
translating them into engaging entertainment experiences. As the digital entertainment landscape 
continues to grow, we see immense opportunities to experiment with fresh concepts, distinctive 
formats and stories that resonate with audiences across platforms. With It's A Meal, we are bringing 
an interesting take on relationships and matchmaking, packaged in an entertaining format. This is 
part of our larger vision to continuously innovate, expand our content offerings and strengthen our 
presence in the digital entertainment space.” 
 
Balaji Telefilms talent division, Hoonur’s Head of talent, Mohammed Nagaman Lateef said, “The idea 
behind It's A Meal was to of course to make the journey of finding a connection entertaining and 
engaging. Relationships and matchmaking are subjects that naturally spark curiosity, conversations 
and emotions, and we wanted to explore this with a fresh, contemporary perspective. Bringing 
together all the distinct personalities creates an interesting dynamic that can surely lead to 
something unexpected. Ultimately, we hope the show leaves audiences entertained and rooting for 
their favourite matches.” 
 
With It's A Meal, Balaji Telefilms brings together the universal appeal of relationships and the 
entertainment value of familiar faces in a format designed for digital audiences. Get ready for 
conversations, chemistry, surprises and perhaps even a few happily-ever-afters. After all, the next 
perfect match could just be one meal away!
