Dear Sir/Madam, 
Sub: Disclosure nuder Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("LODR Regulations") - Update on Divestment 
from captive wind power project 
This has reference to earlier letter dated l 0th April 2023 informing the stock exchanges 
regarding the Company's equity investment of 39.08% in Ulric Renewables Private Limited 
("Ulric"), a special purpose vehicle promoted and incorporated by Cleantech Solar India OA 
2 Pte. Ltd. ("CIOA2") for development of captive wind power projects aggregating 18.9 MW 
for supplying renewable power to the Company's manufacturing facilities in Tamil Nadu and 
Maharashtra. 
As a further update, we wish to inform you that captive wind power project having a 
contracted capacity of 10.5 MW at Jath, Maharashtra for the Maharashtra factories could not 
be commissioned within the scheduled commencement date. Accordingly, pursuant to 
mutually agreed terms between Ulric, the Company and CIOA2, including amendment and 
settlement documentation executed by the parties, Exide has diluted its shareholding in Ulric 
from 39.08% to to 26.07%. 
The captive wind power project for the Company's Hosur facility, Tamil Nadu, continues to 
remain operational under the existing arrangement, and Ulric shall continue to supply 
electricity from the wind power project having a contracted capacity of 8.4 MW to the Hosur 
facility.
