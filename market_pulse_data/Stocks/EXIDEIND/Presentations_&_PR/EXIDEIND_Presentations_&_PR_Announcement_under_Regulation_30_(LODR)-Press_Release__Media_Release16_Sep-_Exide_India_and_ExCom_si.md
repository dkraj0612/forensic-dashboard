Exide, India and ExCom, Germany Enter into Strategic Cooperation to Strengthen 
Industrial Battery Business Across the European Economic Area 
India / Germany, 16 September 2026: Exide Industries Limited ("Exide India"), one of India's 
leading Lead Acid battery and energy storage solutions companies, and ExCom Energy 
Solutions GmbH ("ExCom GmbH"), Germany, have entered into a long-term strategic 
cooperation agreement for the industrial battery and energy storage systems  across the 
European Economic Area (EEA). Products oﬀered under this cooperation will be marketed and 
sold under the ExCom brand. 
The agreement, signed on 15 September 2026, establishes a close commercial and technical 
collaboration between the two companies. The cooperation will cover the EEA market.  ExCom 
GmbH will act as the central commercial and technical interface for customers in the region, 
leading customer development, distribution, technical project support, and after-sales 
coordination. 
The partnership will initially focus on traction and motive-power battery solutions serving 
material-handling equipment, logistics operations, and other industrial mobility applications, 
as well as stationery battery systems for industrial, infrastructure and energy storage 
applications. 
By combining Exide India's extensive manufacturing capabilities, broad technology portfolio, 
and decades of expertise in industrial battery solutions with ExCom GmbH's market presence, 
application engineering capabilities, and local customer support, infrastructure, the 
collaboration aims to deliver enhanced value to customers across the EEA. 
The cooperation is designed to provide customers with a comprehensive portfolio of solutions 
encompassing battery systems, charging technologies, system integration, application 
engineering, and lifecycle services. Products and solutions will be tailored to meet the speciﬁc 
technical, regulatory, and operational requirements of customers throughout the EEA. 
Commenting on the partnership, Mr. Avik Roy, Managing Director & Chief Executive Oﬃcer, 
Exide Industries Limited, said: 
The European Economic Area represents a strategically important market for Exide India’s 
industrial energy storage business, with growing demand for dependable, eﬃcient, and 
sustainable energy solutions. ExCom GmbH brings strong market access, deep application 
expertise, and proven local execution capabilities.

About Exide India Industries Limited 
Exide Industries Limited is one of India's leading Lead Acid battery and energy storage 
solutions companies. With a legacy spanning more than seven decades, the Company oﬀers a 
comprehensive portfolio of automotive, industrial, traction, and stationary battery solutions. 
Exide India operates an extensive manufacturing and distribution network and continues to 
invest in research, product development, and advanced manufacturing technologies. Through 
its wholly owned subsidiary, Exide Energy Solutions Limited, the Company is also developing 
advanced chemistry cells, modules, and battery packs for mobility and stationary energy 
storage applications.

About ExCom Energy Solutions GmbH 
ExCom Energy Solutions GmbH is a Germany-based energy solutions company specializing in 
industrial battery systems, motive power application, charging technologies, advanced 
chemistry solutions, and stationary energy storage systems.
