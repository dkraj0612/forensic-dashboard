Management Commentary:
Commenting on the Company’s Performance, Mr. Phanindranath Kakarla, Managing
Director & Chief Executive Officer of the Company said,
“Q1 FY27 has been an outstanding start to the year, setting a strong foundation for our journey as a listed
company. Total income grew 115% year-on-year to Rs. 276.1 crore. Profit before tax more than doubled to
Rs. 192.4 crore, while profit after tax rose 107% to Rs. 143.1 crore, translating into a PAT margin of about
52% of total income.
This performance was driven by a sharp pick-up in recoveries, which rose 151% to Rs. 1,066.0 crore from
Rs. 425.0 crore in Q1 FY26. Fees and other income doubled to Rs. 118.0 crore. We also strengthened our
balance sheet, with net worth rising to Rs. 3,222.3 crore and borrowings declining to Rs. 1,100.3 crore
from Rs. 1,205.5 crore as of March 2026.
Our corporate book led the performance, with recoveries of Rs. 671.0 crore, more than three times the
level recorded last year. Retail recoveries grew 88% to Rs. 351.0 crore, demonstrating the strength of the
collections framework we have built for this segment. Recoveries from SME and other segments grew 47%
to Rs. 44.0 crore. Our cumulative SR redemption ratio improved to 52.5% as of June 2026.
The operating environment for our industry remains constructive. India's retail credit market continues to
deepen, while stress across lending segments is expected to rise. Recovery rates for ARCs have improved
steadily, and regulatory initiatives such as SEBI's Special Situation Funds and the proposed RBI framework
for the securitisation of stressed assets are expected to broaden the toolkit available for stress resolution.
We believe ARCs are well positioned to remain one of the most effective channels for banks and financial
institutions to resolve stressed assets.
As the largest private ARC in India by AUM and profitability, we will continue to grow our corporate book
by focusing on creditworthy, mid-sized assets. We also intend to increase the share of retail and SME
assets, expand our collection-as-a-service offering for banks, and explore opportunities in stressed assets
from microfinance institutions.
We will continue to invest in technology and data analytics to improve recovery efficiency. With a robust
acquisition pipeline and a growing capital base, we are confident in our ability to deliver sustained and
profitable growth in the coming years.
Our listing on the NSE and BSE in September 2026 marks a defining milestone in our two-decade journey.
It brings greater visibility, access to the capital markets and a wider shareholder base, while the continued
backing of State Bank of India and Avenue Capital reinforces confidence in our franchise. On behalf of the
Board and management team, I thank our lenders, investors, employees and new shareholders for their
trust and support. We remain committed to disciplined growth and long-term value creation.”

About Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited - ARCIL 
Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited (“ARCIL”) is an asset reconstruction company operating across
India, engaged in acquiring stressed assets from banks and financial institutions and implementing resolution
strategies to maximize recovery and optimize the value of those assets.
ARCIL was incorporated in 2002 and received RBI registration to commence securitisation and asset
reconstruction business on 29 August 2003 under the SARFAESI Act. It completed its first stressed-asset
acquisition in December 2003 and has therefore operated for more than two decades. The Company operate
across three business verticals - Corporate loans, SME and Other loans and Retail loans and classify the
stressed assets that they acquire based on the resolution mechanism they employ which are subject to their
internal assessments. They bid for stressed assets and deploy a mix of resolution and collection strategies,
based on the nature of the underlying loan portfolio or the single credit stressed asset.
