I think you may have noticed the image on the cover of our presentation. Kintsugi is the Japanese 
art of repairing broken pottery with gold so that the restored piece carries more value than before. 
That is a fair picture of our work. We take assets that are broken and restore their value, guided 
by the same approach. Buy with discipline, resolve with urgency and deploy capital with a clear 
focus on returns.  
This half year also marked a milestone for us. In September, ARCIL became India's first listed 
ARC. Since then, we have multiple investors and more than 1 lakh shareholders and we take 
seriously the transparency that comes with being a listed company, including being clear about 
how our business should be read.  
Given our recent listing, it has only been a few weeks since our first call so I will keep the context 
brief and spend most of my time on what this half year tells us. Pramod will then take you 
through the numbers in detail. The scale of the opportunity has not changed, in fact, likely to 
grow. 
CRISIL estimates total stress across banks and NBFCs at about INR23.7 lakh crores in FY26. 
About INR15.9 lakh crores of it, two-thirds is NPAs and written-off loans, which is what stressed 
lenders actually sell. The remaining 7.8 lakh crores are special mention accounts. Over the last 
couple of years, we have seen that banks and NBFCs have also started selling special mention 
accounts.  
In fact, in the retail book across the industry, close to over 25% of the accounts being sold by 
number of accounts is of special mention accounts. Corporate written-off loans alone account 
for about INR6.4 lakh crores and retail and MSME for the remaining about INR9.5 lakh crores.  
This pool has been growing by around INR1 lakh crores a year while ARCs buy below 10% of 
it, which is our own estimate, internal estimate, each year as per our estimates. That gap and the 
growing stressed asset market is the industry's runway. As India's first and largest private ARC, 
we are well equipped to use that runway. We price with discipline.
