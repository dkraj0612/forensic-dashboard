Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited
Mumbai, Maharashtra, October 29, 2026:
Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited (NSE: ARCIL, BSE: 544921), India’s first and largest private
asset reconstruction by AUM, Profitability and Market Share, focused on acquiring and resolving non-
performing assets (NPAs) and stressed loans from banks and financial institutions announced its
unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026. The financial
numbers are based on Ind-AS.
Given the inherent nature of the Asset Reconstruction business, which involves long-tenure
resolution cycles and few lumpy recoveries in corporate, ARCIL’s financial performance should
be evaluated over a longer-term horizon rather than on a quarter-to-quarter basis. Quarterly
fluctuations may not fully reflect the underlying strength of the company and sustainable earnings
trajectory of the Company. This release therefore focuses on half-yearly results; quarterly figures
are available in the results filed with the stock exchanges.

Management Commentary:
Commenting on the Company’s Performance, Mr. Phanindranath Kakarla, Managing
Director & Chief Executive Officer of the Company said,
2
“We have delivered a strong performance in the first half of FY27, reflecting the resilience of our business
model, disciplined execution and sustained focus on profitable growth. Total income grew by 48% year-on-
year to Rs. 516.4 crore, while profit after tax increased by 54% to Rs. 290.0 crore. Our PAT margin
expanded to approximately 56% from 54% in the corresponding H1 FY26, underscoring the strength of our
earnings profile.
A key highlight of the H1 FY27 was the significant acceleration in our asset acquisition activity. This
momentum took our total acquisitions for H1 FY27 to Rs. 1,707.0 crore, an increase of 18% year-on-year.
ARCIL's own investment in H1 FY27 acquisitions stood at Rs. 717.8 crore. Our assets under management
grew by 21% year-on-year to Rs. 20,274 crore as of September 30, 2026, with ARCIL's investment in AUM
at Rs. 4,442 crore. This performance reflects our ability to identify and capitalize on attractive acquisition
opportunities while maintaining a disciplined approach to capital deployment.
Our recovery performance remained healthy, with Q2 recoveries of Rs. 1,069.0 crore, marking the second
consecutive quarter of recoveries exceeding Rs. 1,000 crore. Total recoveries for H1 FY27 increased by 21%
year-on-year to Rs. 2,135.1 crore. During H1, corporate recoveries stood at Rs. 1,226.5 crore, retail
recoveries contributed Rs. 764.4 crore, while SME and other recoveries contributed Rs. 144.2 crore. Our
cumulative Security Receipt (SR) redemption ratio remained at 52% as of September 2026, reflecting our
continued focus on resolution and value realization.
Banks and financial institutions continue to recognize the important role played by Asset Reconstruction
Companies in facilitating efficient resolution across corporate, retail and SME segments. The continued
expansion of India's credit market, particularly in retail lending is creating a broader and more diversified
opportunity landscape for the ARC industry.
As we enter the second half of the financial year, which has historically witnessed a higher share of
business activity including acquisition and resolution, we are encouraged by a healthy acquisition pipeline
and the opportunities ahead. Our strategic priorities remain focused on expanding our corporate portfolio,
particularly in mid-sized assets with sound underlying value; strengthening our presence in retail and SME
segments; scaling our CaaS (collection-as-a-service) offering for banks; and selectively evaluating
opportunities in stressed microfinance portfolios. Alongside these initiatives, we will continue to
strengthen our technology and data analytics capabilities to drive greater operational efficiency, improve
recovery outcomes and enhance scalability.
With a strong balance sheet, a diversified business platform and a clear strategic roadmap, we believe
ARCIL is well-positioned to capitalize on emerging opportunities in the stressed asset market. We remain
committed to disciplined growth, sustainable profitability and creating long-term value for all our
stakeholders.”

About Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited - ARCIL 
Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited (“ARCIL”) is an asset reconstruction company operating across
India, engaged in acquiring stressed assets from banks and financial institutions and implementing resolution
strategies to maximize recovery and optimize the value of those assets.
ARCIL was incorporated in 2002 and received RBI registration to commence securitisation and asset
reconstruction business on 29 August 2003 under the SARFAESI Act. It completed its first stressed-asset
acquisition in December 2003 and has therefore operated for more than two decades. The Company operate
across three business verticals - Corporate loans, SME and Other loans and Retail loans and classify the
stressed assets by the resolution route it expects to use. It bids for stressed assets and deploys a mix of
resolution and collection strategies, based on the nature of the underlying loan portfolio or the single credit
stressed asset.
