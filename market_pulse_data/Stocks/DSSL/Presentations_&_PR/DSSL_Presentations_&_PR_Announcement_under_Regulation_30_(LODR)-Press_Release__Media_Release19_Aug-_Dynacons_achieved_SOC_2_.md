Dynacons Achieves SOC 2 Type II Certification, Reinforcing Its Commitment to 
Security, Reliability and Trust 
Mumbai, India- August 19, 2026 
Dynacons Systems & Solutions Limited, a leading provider of digital infrastructure, cloud, 
cybersecurity, and managed services solutions, today announced that it has successfully 
achieved SOC 2 Type II Certification, a globally recognized benchmark for information security, 
operational excellence, and customer trust. 
The certification validates the effectiveness of Dynacons' controls and processes related to 
Security, Availability and Confidentiality over an extended audit period. SOC 2 Type II is one of 
the most respected independent assurance standards for service organizations and demonstrates 
an organization's ability to securely manage customer data and maintain reliable service delivery. 
The assessment confirms that Dynacons has established and consistently operated a robust 
framework of policies, procedures, and controls designed to protect information assets and support 
the evolving security and compliance requirements of enterprise customers. 
Significance of the Achievement 
The successful completion of the SOC 2 Type II audit further strengthens Dynacons' position as a 
trusted technology partner for enterprises, financial institutions, government organizations, and 
regulated industries that require the highest standards of data protection and operational 
resilience. 
The certification: 
 
Validates the operational effectiveness of security controls over a sustained period. 
 
Reinforces customer confidence in Dynacons' cloud, managed services, cybersecurity, and 
digital transformation offerings. 
 
Aligns Dynacons with globally recognized best practices for governance, risk management, 
and information security. 
 
Supports customers in meeting their own compliance, audit, and regulatory requirements. 
Management Commentary 
Mr. Shirish Anjaria, Chairman cum Managing Director, Dynacons Systems & Solutions 
Limited, said: 
"Achieving SOC 2 Type II Certification represents a significant milestone in Dynacons' continuous 
commitment to security, reliability, and operational excellence. As organizations increasingly 
entrust critical workloads and sensitive data to technology partners, independent validation of our 
controls becomes even more important. This certification reinforces the trust our customers place 
in us and reflects the dedication of our teams to maintaining the highest standards of service 
delivery and data protection."

About SOC 2 Type II 
SOC 2 (Service Organization Control 2) is an internationally recognized auditing framework 
developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). A SOC 2 Type II 
report evaluates both the design and operating effectiveness of controls over a defined review 
period, providing assurance that security and operational controls are functioning effectively on an 
ongoing basis.
