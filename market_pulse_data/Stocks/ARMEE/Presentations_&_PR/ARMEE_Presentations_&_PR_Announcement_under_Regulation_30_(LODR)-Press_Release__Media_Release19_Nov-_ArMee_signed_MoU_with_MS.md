ArMee Infotech Signs MoU with MSEDCL for 135 MW Solar 
Power Project in Maharashtra; Aggregate Order Value of Rs 
1,629 Crore 
Ahmedabad, November 19, 2026: ArMee Infotech Limited (BSE: 544951; NSE: ARMEE), an infrastructure 
solutions company, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on November 17, 2026 with Maharashtra 
State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL) for a 135 MW solar power project in Maharashtra. 
The project carries an aggregate order value of Rs 1,629.03 crore. 
The project will be executed through ArMee MH ONE Renewable Energy Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Company, which will enter into a 25-year Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with MSEDCL 
to set up the 135 MW plant and supply power to the utility. 
The MoU is a significant step for ArMee's renewable energy business, which the Company has been building 
alongside its established IT infrastructure and managed services operations. The project will add to 
Maharashtra's solar generation capacity and support the state's clean energy goals. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Ridhish Patel, Chairman & Managing Director, ArMee Infotech 
Limited, said, "This MoU with MSEDCL is a milestone for ArMee and a clear signal of our commitment to 
renewable energy. A 135 MW project with one of India's largest power distribution utilities reflects confidence 
in our ability to execute at scale. We will bring the same delivery discipline that has defined our infrastructure 
business for over two decades to build a strong renewable energy portfolio, one that creates long-term value for 
our shareholders and all our stakeholders." 
About ArMee Infotech Limited 
ArMee Infotech Limited is an infrastructure solutions company with operations spanning IT infrastructure, IT 
managed services and renewable energy. Established in 2003, the Company has experience in executing 
complex, large-scale projects across government, PSU, BFSI and private sector clients in multiple Indian states. 
Its IT infrastructure business provides integration services for hardware and software solutions, including 
computers, servers, interactive panels, smart classrooms and ICT laboratories. Its IT managed services business 
focuses on technology maintenance, technical manpower and operational support. The Company is expanding 
its presence in renewable energy through solar EPC, long-term power purchase agreements and battery energy 
storage systems, leveraging its project execution and system integration capabilities.
