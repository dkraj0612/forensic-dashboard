Ahmedabad, October 17, 2026: ArMee Infotech Limited (ARMEE), an infrastructure solutions company operating 
across IT infrastructure, managed services and renewable energy, today announced its unaudited financial results for 
the quarter ended June 30, 2026. 
The Company reported improved profitability in Q1 FY27 despite modest revenue growth. Standalone revenue from 
operations rose to ₹273.27 crore from ₹270.29 crore a year earlier. EBITDA grew 64.61% to ₹7.72 crore, with 
EBITDA margin expanding to 2.83% from 1.74%. Standalone PAT increased 191.58% to ₹2.77 crore. 
On a consolidated basis, revenue from operations increased 1.66% to ₹275.79 crore. EBITDA rose 128.62% to ₹6.95 
crore and EBITDA margin improved to 2.52% from 1.12%. Consolidated PAT was ₹1.82 crore, compared with a loss 
of ₹0.35 crore in Q1 FY26. 
The performance is encouraging in the context of the Company’s exposure to government, PSU and institutional 
procurement cycles, where execution and revenue recognition may be weighted towards later quarters of the financial 
year.

Management Commentary 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Ridhish Patel, Chairman & Managing Director, ArMee Infotech Limited, said: 
“Our Q1 FY27 performance reflects the strength of our operating fundamentals and continued focus on disciplined 
execution. Despite the traditionally subdued first quarter associated with government and institutional business cycles, 
we delivered a meaningful improvement in profitability and operating margins. 
The successful completion of our ₹300 crore IPO marks an important milestone in ArMee’s journey, reinforcing our 
commitment to building a stronger, future-ready organisation. 
Our expansion into renewable energy, particularly battery energy storage and solar infrastructure, represents a strategic 
extension of our established project execution capabilities. The recent award of the 250 MW / 500 MWh BESS project 
and formation of a dedicated renewable energy subsidiary further strengthen this direction. 
As we move forward, our priorities remain strengthening our core businesses, maintaining financial discipline, 
expanding selectively into infrastructure opportunities and creating sustainable long-term value for stakeholders.” 
Strategic Developments Subsequent to the Quarter 
Battery energy storage: ArMee Infotech secured a 250 MW / 500 MWh Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project 
from Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL). Awarded through tariff-based 
competitive bidding under the Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme, the project involves a 15-year Battery Energy 
Storage Purchase Agreement (BESPA). It represents the Company’s largest BESS project to date. 
Renewable energy subsidiary: On October 15, 2026, the Company incorporated ArMee MH One Renewable Energy 
Private Limited as a wholly owned subsidiary. The special purpose vehicle will develop, own, operate and maintain 
solar power projects and associated infrastructure. 
IPO milestone: The successful completion of the Company’s ₹300 crore Initial Public Offering marks a significant 
corporate milestone supporting its next phase of growth and institutional development. 
Outlook 
ArMee Infotech remains focused on strengthening its established IT infrastructure and managed services businesses 
while developing its renewable energy portfolio across solar and battery energy storage. Its priorities include execution 
discipline, operational efficiency, prudent capital allocation and sustainable stakeholder value creation. 
About ArMee Infotech Limited 
ArMee Infotech Limited (INE0TWE01016), established in 2003, provides infrastructure integration, project execution 
and managed services across government, PSU, BFSI and private sector clients in India. Its portfolio includes 
computing infrastructure, servers, smart classrooms, ICT laboratories, technology maintenance and operational support. 
The Company is expanding into renewable energy through solar EPC, long-term power purchase arrangements and 
battery energy storage projects.
