POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, we wish to inform you of the termination / non-execution 
of the Bondada EPC Project awarded by Bondada Engineering Limited (“BEL” or “Awarding 
Entity”), which formed part of the Company’s order book disclosed in its Red Herring Prospectus 
dated 16 September 2026 and Prospectus dated 28 September 2026 (“Offer Documents”).  
The Company had initially accepted the Letters of Award (LOAs) dated June 26, 2026 issued by BEL 
for the said project. Subsequently, there was a substantial escalation in raw material costs, materially 
affecting the commercial viability of the project under the originally agreed terms. 
The Company had communicated these cost escalations to BEL through various correspondences and 
sought appropriate revisions to the commercial terms. 
In view of the continued cost escalation and the resulting commercial constraints, the Company has 
communicated its inability to proceed with the project under the existing terms. 
The decision has been taken after careful commercial evaluation, keeping in view financial prudence, 
project viability and protection of stakeholders’ interests. 
The Company remains committed to maintaining financial discipline and pursuing sustainable, 
commercially viable business opportunities. 
The financial implications and consequential adjustments to the Company’s outstanding order book, as 
applicable, shall be appropriately reflected in its subsequent disclosures. The details required under the 
aforesaid Master Circular are enclosed as Annexure-A to this letter 
This intimation is also being made available on the website of the Company at 
https://armeeinfotech.com/investors/ in terms of Regulation 30(8) and Regulation 46 of the Listing 
Regulations.
