Press Release 
Ganesh Consumer Products concludes sale of movable and immovable assets of 
Hyderabad unit for ₹ 32.50 crore; sharpens focus on Eastern India 
 
Kolkata, October 1, 2026: Ganesh Consumer Products Limited ("the Company"), a leading packaged foods 
company in Eastern India, today announced that it has concluded the sale of the movable and immovable assets 
of the Company’s Hyderabad unit to Megacity Flour Mills Private Limited for a total consideration of ₹ 32.50 
Crores. 
The unit is located at EPIP Industrial Park, Pashamylaram, Patancheru, Sangareddy District (Formerly part of 
Medak district), Telangana, on land measuring 10,220 square metres. It was commissioned in 2015 and has a 
capacity of 312 tonnes per day to manufacture whole wheat flour and wheat-based value-added flour. 
Rationale 
The Hyderabad unit, commissioned in 2015 to serve the Southern Indian market, is non-core to the Company's 
current strategy. This transaction will enable the Company to redeploy the capital and management bandwidth 
towards its expansion and capital expenditure plans in Eastern India. 
Transaction highlights 
• Consideration of ₹ 32.50 crore, about 2.7 times the unit's net block of ₹ 11.98 crore as at August 31, 2026 
(unaudited). 
• The unit recorded revenue of ₹ 12.53 crore in FY26, which is about 1.4% of the Company's revenue from 
operations. 
• The sale is expected to result in a one-time gain, subject to closing adjustments and taxes. 
• The buyer is not a related party of the Company. 
Management commentary 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Manish Mimani, Managing Director, said: "The sale of movable and 
immovable assets of our Hyderabad unit allows us to channelise the capital and management focus into the 
expansion strategies in Eastern India, where our brand is the strongest. We will continue to invest in capacity, 
distribution and creation of new categories across the region." 
About Ganesh Consumer Products Limited 
Established in 1936 and headquartered in Kolkata, Ganesh Consumer Products Limited markets packaged staples, 
value-added flours, spices and emerging food products under its flagship brand, Ganesh. It is the third-largest 
packaged whole wheat flour (atta) brand and the largest brand in wheat-based derivatives (maida, sooji and dalia) 
in East India (FY25). Following this transaction, the Company will operate 7 manufacturing facilities across West 
Bengal and Uttar Pradesh.
