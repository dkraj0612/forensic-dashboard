SUBJECT:   
INTIMATION OF WITHDRAWAL OF REVISED RECORD DATE  
 
    
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We refer to our earlier intimation dated August 13, 2026 wherein it was informed that the Record Date 
has been revised from Friday 14 August, 2026 to Wednesday 02 September, 2026 for the purpose of 
determining the entitlement of members to the Final Dividend for the Financial Year 2025-26, if declared 
by the members at the forthcoming AGM. 
 
In this regard, we wish to inform you that the Company has decided to continue the original record date 
i.e. Friday, August 14, 2026 for the purpose of determining the entitlement of members to the Final 
Dividend for the Financial Year 2025-26, if declared by the members at the forthcoming AGM. 
 
Thus, the revised record date i.e. Wednesday, September 02, 2026 stands cancelled. Further, the intimation 
dated August 13, 2026 pertaining to the revision of record date for determining the entitlement of members 
to the Final Dividend for the Financial Year 2025-26 is hereby withdrawn. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 42 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), Regulations, 2015 
(as amended from time to time) (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), the Record Date, for determining the 
entitlement of the members to the Final Dividend for the Financial Year 2025-26, if declared by the 
members at the forthcoming AGM will remain Friday, August 14, 2026 (already informed vide our 
intimation dated August 04, 2026). 
 
Kindly take the same on your record.
