Brief profile of Ms. Avanthi Rao 
 
 
 
 
Ms. Avanthi Rao currently heads the Cummins Components and Software (CCS) Business Unit 
for a Cummins Group Company. She joined Cummins in 2010 as a campus hire upon completion 
of her MBA and has since held leadership roles in the Cummins Turbo Technologies (CTT) and 
Cummins Engine Components (CEC) businesses in India. Prior to joining Cummins, she was 
associated with Denso India Pvt. Ltd. 
 
Ms. Rao holds an MBA in Finance and a Bachelor of Technology degree in Mechanical 
Engineering. 
 
She is recognized for her analytical and structured approach and has demonstrated a strong 
focus on mentoring talent and developing high-performing, diverse and inclusive teams. She has 
also been actively associated with community initiatives focused on empowering women in rural 
communities, supporting higher technical education through scholarships, and creating 
opportunities for underprivileged sections of society.
