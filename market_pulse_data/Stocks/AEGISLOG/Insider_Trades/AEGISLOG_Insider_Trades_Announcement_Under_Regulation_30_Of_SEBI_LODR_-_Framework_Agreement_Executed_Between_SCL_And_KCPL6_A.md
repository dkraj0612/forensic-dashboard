### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aegis Logistics' wholly-owned subsidiary, Sea Lord Containers Limited (SCL), has secured a significant INR 37.17 Crore construction contract from Konkan Storage Systems (Kochi) Private Limited (KCPL), a related entity. This agreement entails SCL building 49,577 cbm liquid storage tanks and associated facilities, representing a material revenue intake for Aegis Logistics, with the entire sum payable upon execution. While this is a related party transaction, the company declares it has been conducted on an 'arm's length basis,' mitigating potential red flags.

for liquid products with an aggregate storage capacity of 49,577 cbm, along with associated 
facilities, including truck loading bays for petroleum products, a tanker loading gantry and the 
installation of a carbon steel (CS) pipeline.
