### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aegis Logistics Ltd. reported an exceptionally strong Q1 FY27, with significant growth in revenue (+37%), Normalised EBITDA (+184%), and Profit After Tax (+212%), primarily driven by a remarkable 296% increase in the Gas Division's EBITDA from record distribution volumes. The company demonstrates strong financial health and a clear strategy for expanding its terminal network, underpinned by a growing asset base and substantial cash reserves. A potential red flag is the significant increase in current borrowings from FY25 to FY26, which, despite strong liquidity, warrants further review regarding its impact on the company's capital structure and short-term liabilities.
