Aegis signs definitive agreement for its proposed acquisition of a majority stake in Tristar 
Aegis Logistics Limited via its step-down 100% subsidiary, Aegis Logistics International Pte Ltd (collectively 
“Aegis”), has entered into a definitive agreement under which Aegis proposes to acquire c.89.8% of Tristar Holdings 
Limited (“Tristar”) from Agility Tristar Holdings Limited, Gulf Investment Corporation and Diamond Holdings 
Limited for an equity consideration of US$ 809mn (including contingent and interest for deferred consideration).  
 
Established in 1998, Tristar is an integrated liquid logistics solutions provider offering end-to-end liquid logistics 
services including maritime logistics, road transport, fuel tank storage, fuel distribution and aviation fueling. Tristar 
is incorporated in Dubai and has operations in 30+ countries globally. For fiscal year ended Dec 31, 2025, Tristar 
generated c.US$ 1.4bn of revenue, c.US$ 257mn of EBITDA and c.US$ 82mn of net profit on a consolidated basis. 
 
Mr. Raj Chandaria, Chairman and Managing Director of Aegis Logistics, said: “The acquisition of Tristar will 
kick-off Aegis’ transformation from a domestically focused operator into an international energy logistics platform 
with significant global reach and expanded service offerings. The transaction will allow Aegis to benefit from greater 
scale, as well as business and geographic diversification. Furthermore, the acquisition will grant Aegis with access to 
an extensive network of blue-chip customers and premier industry partners within the Tristar ecosystem. The 
combination of Aegis and Tristar’s complementary capabilities is expected to unlock material synergies across the 
integrated business. This acquisition is expected to create an immediate contribution to overall profitability and also 
provides important long-term strategic benefits for Aegis." 
 
Mr. Eugene Mayne, Founder and Group CEO Tristar Group said: “We are delighted to welcome Aegis Logistics 
as our new majority shareholder, marking an exciting new chapter in Tristar’s growth journey. This partnership brings 
together complementary strengths, capabilities and a shared vision for the future, creating significant opportunities to 
expand our reach and strengthen our position in the energy logistics sector. Together, we look forward to building a 
more competitive and globally connected business, while upholding our unwavering commitment to safety, 
operational excellence and delivering long-term value to our customers.” 
 
Mr. Tarek Sultan, Chairman of Agility Global PLC, said: “Over 23 years of Agility’s ownership, Tristar’s strong 
leadership and operational excellence, backed by our patient capital and active management, helped scale the company 
from US$ 13.5mn in revenue into a global integrated energy logistics platform generating US$ 1.4bn in revenue across 
30 countries. This disciplined exit crystallizes value for our shareholders while enabling Agility to concentrate on its 
growth priorities. Aegis, with its strategic capital, deep sector expertise, and platform scale, combined with Tristar’s 
world-class capabilities and extensive customer base, are well-positioned together. We wish Aegis and Tristar every 
success as they embark on the next chapter of growth.” 
 
The proposed acquisition is subject receipt of regulatory approvals under the applicable competition and anti-trust 
laws and satisfaction of customary closing conditions. The acquisition is expected to be completed in Q4 FY27. 
 
Advisors 
BNP Paribas is acting as exclusive financial advisor to Aegis, with Norton Rose Fulbright and Veritas Legal acting as 
legal counsel. 
 
About Aegis Logistics Limited (BSE: 500003, NSE: AEGISLOG) 
Incorporated in 1956, Aegis Logistics is a listed Indian company providing sourcing, storage, distribution, storage and 
third-party logistics services in the oil, gas, and chemicals sector. Aegis' vision is to support India’s transition towards 
a more sustainable future and with a mission to store and distribute bulk liquids and gases in a safe, sustainable manner.
