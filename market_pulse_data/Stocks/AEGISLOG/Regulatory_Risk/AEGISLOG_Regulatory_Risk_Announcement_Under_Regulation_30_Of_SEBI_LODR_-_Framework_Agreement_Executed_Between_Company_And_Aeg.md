Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements), Regulations, 2015 as amended, we hereby notify that Aegis Logistics 
Limited (“the Company”) and Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited(“AVTL”), its Subsidiary Company has 
executed today i.e. August 06, 2026, a Framework Agreement wherein the Company will construct 
and develop an additional refrigerated double steel wall, full containment, insulated with suspended 
deck storage tank for propane with capacity of 51,998 MT tank and allied facilities at its terminal 
located at Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority (“JNPA”) tank farm area.
