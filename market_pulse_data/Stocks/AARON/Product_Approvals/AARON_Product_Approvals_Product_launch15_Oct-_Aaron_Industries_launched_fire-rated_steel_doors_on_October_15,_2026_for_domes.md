Subject: Intimation Regarding Launch of New Product – Fire-Rated Steel Doors under the Commercial

Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform you that Aaron Industries Limited 
has introduced Fire-Rated Steel Doors as a new product under the Commercial and Industrial Fire 
Door Systems category, effective October 15, 2026. 
 
The Company's Swing Metal Fire Doors have received a Fire Certificate from Gujarat Test House, 
Ahmedabad, with a fire-resistance rating of 120 minutes in accordance with IS 3614:2021. The product is 
designed for use in passageways, corridors and other designated fire-safety applications in 
commercial, industrial and institutional buildings, subject to applicable building codes and certified 
product specifications. 
 
The introduction of this product represents an expansion of the Company's existing product portfolio and 
reflects its continued focus on product development, manufacturing capabilities and the introduction of 
value-added products to address evolving customer requirements. 
 
The requisite details pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and the SEBI Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are enclosed herewith as 
Annexure-A. 
 
This is for your information and records.
