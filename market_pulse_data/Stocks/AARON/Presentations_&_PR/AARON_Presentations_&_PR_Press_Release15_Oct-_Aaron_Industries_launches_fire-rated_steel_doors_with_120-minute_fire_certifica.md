Press Release 
 
“AARON INDUSTRIES LIMITED INTRODUCES FIRE-RATED STEEL DOORS FOR COMMERCIAL AND 
INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS” 
 
Surat, October 15, 2026: Aaron Industries Limited (NSE:AARON), is pleased to announce the 
introduction of Fire-Rated Steel Doors as a new product offering under the Commercial and 
Industrial Fire Door Systems category, marking a further expansion of the Company's product 
portfolio into fire-safety solutions for the building and infrastructure industry. 
 
The Company's Swing Metal Fire Doors have received a Fire Certificate from Gujarat Test House, 
Ahmedabad, with a fire-resistance rating of 120 minutes in accordance with IS 3614:2021. This 
development represents an important milestone in the Company's product expansion initiatives and 
reinforces its focus on quality, safety and engineering capabilities. 
 
Designed for applications such as building passageways, corridors, fire exits and other designated 
fire-safety installations, the Fire-Rated Steel Doors are intended to address the requirements of 
commercial and industrial infrastructure. The product is also suited to relevant applications in 
hospitals, hotels, residential projects and other buildings, subject to applicable building codes and 
certified product specifications. 
 
Developed with an emphasis on safety, durability and precision engineering, the new product broadens 
Aaron Industries Limited's offerings beyond its established portfolio of elevator cabins, automatic door 
systems, EVOQ360 Home Lifts, cabin safety frames and other elevator components. The introduction 
reflects the Company's continued efforts to leverage its manufacturing capabilities, develop new 
products and address evolving customer requirements across the construction, infrastructure and 
allied industries. 
 
With this addition, Aaron Industries Limited aims to expand its presence in the Commercial and 
Industrial Fire Door Systems category, creating opportunities to serve a wider customer base through 
its growing range of engineered products. The Company remains focused on product innovation, 
manufacturing excellence and the development of value-added solutions to support its long-term 
growth objectives. 
 
About Aaron Industries Limited 
Aaron Industries Limited is a trusted name in the elevator industry, offering a comprehensive range of 
high-quality elevator products, components, EVOQ360 Home Lifts, and stainless steel polishing 
services. The Company specializes in end-to-end elevator solutions, designing and manufacturing 
elevator cabins, automatic door systems, and cabin safety frames that seamlessly blend functionality 
with artistic elegance for residential and commercial projects. To enhance its supply chain efficiency 
and ensure in-house quality control, Aaron Industries established a state-of-the-art stainless steel 
sheet polishing division, which plays a key role in its backward integration strategy by providing 
polished stainless steel for elevator doors and cabins. With a strong commitment to innovation, quality, 
and customer satisfaction, the Company continues to expand its footprint across India, solidifying its 
position as a leader in elevator solutions and stainless steel processing. 
 
For more details, please visit www.aaronindustries.net.
