Order for Home Lifts
Aaron Industries Limited has received an order from Gulf Elevators
Private Limited, a domestic entity, to supply up to 30 units of
EVOQ360 Home Lifts, with an estimated contract value of ₹2
crore (excluding GST). The order is scheduled to be executed on
or before September 30, 2027, reflecting continued business
opportunities for the EVOQ360 Home Lift segment. This is not
included in the confirmed orders stated above.
Exclusive Territory Partnerships in Pipeline
Five distributors onboarded for EVOQ360 Home Lifts, with
strong multi-region partnership interest and additional
exclusive distributorships in the pipeline to expand market
reach.
Strong Market Traction
EVOQ360 Home Lifts have been supplied to 44+ projects across
India since launch, including 19 units dispatched in Q2 FY27. With
an additional 60 units in confirmed advance orders from 18 parties,
the Company continues to strengthen market presence and
customer demand. The orders are expected to be dispatched in a
phased manner up to December 2026, subject to site readiness
and other customer-specific installation requirements.
