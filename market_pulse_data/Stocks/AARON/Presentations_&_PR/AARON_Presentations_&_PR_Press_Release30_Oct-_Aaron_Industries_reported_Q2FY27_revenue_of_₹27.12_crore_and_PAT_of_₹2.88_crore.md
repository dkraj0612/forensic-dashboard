Management Commentary 
 
"We are pleased to report a strong performance during the second quarter and the first half of FY27. The Company 
has maintained its growth momentum, with revenue from operations increasing by 21.93% in Q2FY27 and 
24.29% in H1FY27 on a year-on-year basis. More importantly, the improvement in EBITDA and profitability 
reflects the progress being made in strengthening our operational performance. 
 
Both our business divisions have contributed to the overall growth. While the Elevator Division continues to 
expand its business across various elevator components, our Stainless Steel Polishing Division has recorded 
particularly encouraging growth during the first half of the year. Our integrated manufacturing capabilities and 
diversified product portfolio remain important strengths of the business. 
 
We remain focused on improving capacity utilisation, strengthening our distribution network, expanding our 
customer base and enhancing our product offerings. With the investments made in manufacturing infrastructure 
and capacity expansion, we are working towards leveraging our installed capacities and improving operational 
efficiencies.

Outlook 
 
Aaron Industries Limited remains focused on leveraging its expanded manufacturing infrastructure, improving 
capacity utilisation and strengthening its market presence across both business divisions. 
 
The Company intends to continue investing in product development, distribution network expansion and 
operational efficiencies, while capitalising on emerging opportunities in the elevator components, EVOQ360 
home lift and stainless steel processing markets. 
 
With a diversified product portfolio, integrated manufacturing capabilities and an expanding customer and 
distribution network, the Company remains committed to delivering sustainable business growth and enhancing 
shareholder value. 
 
About Aaron Industries Limited 
 
Aaron Industries Limited is a trusted name in the elevator industry, offering a comprehensive range of high-
quality elevator products, components, EVOQ360 Home Lifts, and stainless steel polishing services. The 
Company specializes in end-to-end elevator solutions, designing and manufacturing elevator cabins, automatic 
door systems, and cabin safety frames that seamlessly blend functionality with artistic elegance for residential 
and commercial projects. To enhance its supply chain efficiency and ensure in-house quality control, Aaron 
Industries established a state-of-the-art stainless steel sheet polishing division, which plays a key role in its 
backward integration strategy by providing polished stainless steel for elevator doors and cabins. With a strong 
commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction, the Company continues to expand its footprint 
across India, solidifying its position as a leader in elevator solutions and stainless steel processing.
