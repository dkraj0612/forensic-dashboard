(India) Private Limited (“A1 Labs”), thus making it a wholly owned subsidiary of Apitoria and a step down 
subsidiary of the Company. 
 
In continuation to the above and in compliance with Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of the SEBI 
LODR Regulations, this is to inform that as part of the terms agreed under the definitive agreements entered 
into by Apitoria as part of the acquisition of A1 Biochem group, Dr. Rajendra Gadikota, former promoter of A1 
Biochem group has subscribed to 89,94,500 equity shares and 2,41,05,500 Compulsory Convertibles 
Debentures (hereinafter together referred to as ‘Securities’) of A1 Labs equaling to 20% stake (on a fully 
diluted basis). 
 
Pursuant to the completion of the allotment of Securities, Dr. Rajendra Gadikota is holding 20% of the stake 
A1 Labs on a fully diluted basis and A1 Labs accordingly ceases to be a wholly-owned subsidiary. A1 Labs 
continues to remain a subsidiary of Apitoria and a step down subsidiary of the Company in terms of the 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI Listing Regulations. 
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations read along with applicable SEBI Master
