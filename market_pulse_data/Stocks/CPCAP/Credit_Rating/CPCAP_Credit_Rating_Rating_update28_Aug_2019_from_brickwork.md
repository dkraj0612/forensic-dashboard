About the Company 
Kota-based (Rajasthan) CPL was started as a tutorial centre to provide tuitions for competitive exams like
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
AIEEE, IIT-JEE, PMT, etc. in 1993 by Mr Pramod Maheshwari, an IIT-Delhi alumnus, as a
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
proprietorship firm and was reconstituted as a public limited company in March 2000; which
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
subsequently got listed in October 2010 on the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock

has become an integrated education services provider in segments such as play schools, test-preparations,
