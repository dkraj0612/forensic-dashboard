2. Executive Summary 
CP Capital Limited posted strong growth across revenue, earnings, and profitability in Q1 FY27 compared to Q4 
FY26, alongside a sharp improvement in asset quality during the quarter. On a standalone basis, PAT grew 102% 
quarter-on-quarter to ₹11.5 Cr and EBITDA grew 65% to ₹18.0 Cr. Gross NPA fell from ₹79.90 Cr to ₹35.21 Cr, 
with 99.90% of NPAs being secured and having 2.5x security coverage.
