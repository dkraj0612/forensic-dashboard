Press Release 
Alkem launches Renocia Exo™, India’s first patented Ginsenoside 
Exosome-based hair growth serum 
• Inspired by regenerative dermatology, Renocia Exo™ offers a new multi-
targeted approach to hair loss management by supporting the biology of 
the hair follicle-not just hair growth.  
• The formulation integrates Ginsenoside phyto-exosomes, biomimetic 
peptides, copper peptides and stem cell-supportive actives to support 
follicular vitality, scalp health and the natural hair growth cycle.  
Mumbai, 8th September 2026: Alkem Laboratories Ltd. (BSE: 539523, NSE: ALKEM, 
“Alkem” and its subsidiaries) today announced the launch of Renocia Exo™, a next-generation 
hair regeneration therapy developed to support the health and vitality of the hair follicle through 
a comprehensive scientific approach. 
Hair loss has emerged as one of India's fastest-growing dermatological concerns, affecting not 
only ageing adults but also an increasing number of younger individuals due to genetics, 
lifestyle changes, environmental stressors and evolving health patterns. While treatment options 
have expanded over the years, many continue to focus on a limited aspect of the hair growth 
cycle, even though hair loss is now understood to be driven by a complex interplay of biological 
mechanisms. 
Commenting on the launch, Sandeep Singh, Managing Director, Alkem, said, “The science 
of hair restoration has evolved significantly over the last decade, and so has our understanding 
of what drives progressive hair thinning. It is influenced by multiple biological processes, 
including follicular miniaturization, altered cellular signalling, oxidative stress, inflammation, 
and changes within the follicular microenvironment. This evolving understanding calls for 
equally evolved therapeutic solutions. With Renocia Exo™, we bring together multiple 
regenerative technologies to support the biology of the hair follicle in a single daily therapy.” 
Unlike conventional topical approaches that primarily focus on stimulating visible hair growth, 
Renocia Exo™ has been developed to support several interconnected biological processes 
associated with healthy hair follicle function. 
The formulation also focuses on supporting multiple signalling pathways involved in hair 
follicle activity while promoting a favourable environment for healthier, stronger hair over time. 
Its lightweight, cosmetically elegant composition is free from alcohol, sulphates, silicones 
parabens and mineral oil, making it suitable for regular use as part of dermatologist-guided hair 
loss management.
