Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read with the 
applicable SEBI Circular, we wish to inform you that Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited ("the Company") had executed a 15-year 
onsite industrial gas supply agreement on 14th February, 2025 with Jai Balaji Industries Limited. Under the agreement, the 
Company was to establish, own and operate a 325 Tons per day (TPD) Air Separation Unit (ASU) at the customer's manufacturing 
facility located at Durgapur, West Bengal.  
 
We are pleased to announce that the plant has now been successfully commissioned today i.e. 25th September, 2026 and 
revenues from the plant shall therefore commence from September 2026. The agreement provides long-term recurring revenue 
visibility through a take or pay type contractual structure. It strengthens the Company’s presence in Eastern India and its long-term 
onsite business portfolio. 
 
The details as required under Schedule Ill Part A of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, are enclosed herewith as Annexure A. 
 
The same has also been disseminated on the website of the Company at https://ellenbarrie.com/ 
 
We request you to kindly take the above on record.
