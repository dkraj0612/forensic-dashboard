FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE 
Ellenbarrie secures ₹481 Crore Air Separation Unit Contract from Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd. for its Coal to Ammonium Nitrate project 
at Bandhabal, Jharsuguda District, Odisha.  With this strategic project, Ellenbarrie marks its entry into one of India's largest long-term 
industrial gas opportunities under the National Coal Gasification Mission  
Kolkata, India: Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited ("Ellenbarrie") today announced that it has secured a ₹481 crore contract for design, 
engineering, supply, erection, testing, pre-commissioning, commissioning, trial runs operation, operator training, demonstration of performance 
guarantees, supply of spares and handing over, all on turnkey basis of a 1200 TPD cryogenic Air Separation Unit (ASU) from BHEL for its Coal to 
Ammonium Nitrate (2000TPD) Project. 
The project will be executed over eight quarters and is expected be commissioned in FY29. The contract is on a Build and Transfer (B&T) basis 
and does not involve long-term ownership or operation of the plant.  
The project forms part of India's broader push towards coal gasification, a strategic national initiative aimed at reducing import dependence while 
creating domestic production capacity for chemicals, fertilisers and clean industrial feedstocks. The Government of India has outlined an ambitious 
vision of gasifying 100 million tonnes of coal annually by 2030, with coal gasification expected to support domestic production of ammonia, urea, 
methanol, hydrogen and other downstream chemicals. The National Coal Gasification Mission identifies these projects as a key pillar of India's 
resource security and industrial self-reliance strategy.  
Coal gasification plants require large quantities of oxygen and nitrogen supply as a critical process input, making cryogenic Air Separation Units 
an integral part of every commercial-scale facility. As India progresses towards its coal gasification targets, substantial investment will be required 
in new ASUs, creating a multi-year opportunity for industrial gas companies with proven engineering, execution and operating capabilities. The 
Mission also envisages phased development of multiple commercial-scale projects supported by public and private sector investment, alongside 
policy incentives to accelerate deployment.  
Commenting on the development, Varun Agarwal, Joint Managing Director of Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases, said: 
"This order represents an important strategic milestone for Ellenbarrie as we strengthen our participation in India's emerging coal gasification 
ecosystem. While this is a Build and Transfer project, it recognises our capability to deliver large-scale cryogenic Air Separation Units for complex 
industrial applications. As India's coal gasification programme gathers momentum over the coming years, we believe our engineering expertise, 
execution track record and deep experience in industrial gases position us well to participate in this long-term growth opportunity. This will be the 
largest plant built by Ellenbarrie till date and takes us into an exclusive list of companies having the capability of building plants of more than 1000 
TPD. 
The award further expands Ellenbarrie's engineering capabilities beyond its traditional merchant and onsite industrial gas business and reinforces 
its position as one of India's leading domestic industrial gas companies capable of executing large, technologically sophisticated ASU projects. 
About Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited 
Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited is one of India's leading industrial gases companies, manufacturing and supplying oxygen, nitrogen, argon, 
hydrogen and specialty gases to customers across steel, pharmaceuticals, healthcare, engineering, chemicals and other industrial sectors. The 
company operates a diversified network of merchant and onsite plants across India and continues to expand its production footprint to serve the 
country's growing industrial demand.
