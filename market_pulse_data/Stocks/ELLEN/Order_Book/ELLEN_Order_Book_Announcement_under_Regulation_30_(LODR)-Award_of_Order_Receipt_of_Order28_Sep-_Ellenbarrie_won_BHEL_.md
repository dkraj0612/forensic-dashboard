Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”), read with the applicable SEBI Circular, we wish to inform you that Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited (the 
“Company”) has been awarded a contract by Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), for the supply and erection of a “Cryogenic Air Separation 
Unit (ASU) for the proposed Coal to Ammonium Nitrate (2000 TPD) Project at Bandhabal, Jharsuguda district, Odisha (India).” 
 
The Capacity of the ASU is 1200 TPD and the date of award 28th September, 2026. 
 
The details required under Schedule III, Part A of the SEBI Listing Regulations, read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, are enclosed herewith as Annexure A.

designated persons and their immediate relatives, connected persons, fiduciaries and intermediaries shall be closed till 48 hours from the date the 
said information generally made available with the public. 
 
The same has also been disseminated on the website of the Company at https://ellenbarrie.com/
