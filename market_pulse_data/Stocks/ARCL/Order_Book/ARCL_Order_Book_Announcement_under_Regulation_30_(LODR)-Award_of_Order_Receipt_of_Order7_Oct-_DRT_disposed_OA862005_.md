Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform that the Company has received the Final Order dated 06 
October 2026 passed by the Debts Recovery Tribunal-I, Kolkata in OA/86/2005 – Stressed 
Assets Stabilisation Fund vs. Allied Resins and Chemicals Ltd. 
The Tribunal has recorded that, pursuant to the settlement arrangement entered between the 
Company and its secured creditors, the settlement amount of ₹8,22,94,500/- has been paid by 
the Company along with payment of ₹21,95,394/- towards interest. 
Accordingly, the Hon’ble Tribunal, in terms of Section 19(20A) of the Recovery of Debts and 
Bankruptcy Act, 1993, has ordered that OA/86/2005 stands disposed of as the claim of the 
Applicant Bank has been satisfied in terms of the settlement agreement. 
The Company considers the matter to have been resolved pursuant to the settlement and 
payments recorded by the Tribunal. 
 
This is for your information and record.
