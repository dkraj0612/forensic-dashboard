> Anlon Healthcare Limited's 13th Annual General Meeting approved significant strategic moves, including a substantial increase in authorized share capital and the preferential issuance of over 85 million equity shares for non-cash consideration to acquire Apiqo Organics Private Limited and Bizotic Lifescience Private Limited.
