Established in 2013, Anlon Healthcare is a pharmaceutical company engaged in the manufacturing of 
APIs and pharmaceutical intermediates. 
•
Anlon has built a strong presence in niche and specialty APIs, particularly NSAIDs, with its products 
serving diverse applications across pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, veterinary healthcare and personal 
care.
•
Over the years, Anlon has strengthened its backward-integrated API manufacturing capabilities, 
building a presence in niche and specialty products. 
•
Recent acquisitions of Apiqo, Bizotic and Anlon Medicos have expanded its manufacturing base, 
backward integration and entry into formulations. 
•
Anlon has further incorporated two new subsidiaries, Anlon Biologics and Anlon Medicare extending 
the platform into peptides, biosimilars and biological compositions, and into surgical implants for 
trauma, spine and joints.
•
Anlon is now evolving from an API-focused manufacturer into an integrated pharmaceutical platform, 
with growing presence across Industrial & Fine Chemicals → Intermediates → APIs → Formulations →
