Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals Ltd.  
announces its results for Q2 FY27 
 
Mumbai, 16th October 2026: Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals Ltd. (‘Company’), India’s leading 
Consumer Durables player, reported its standalone & consolidated financials for the quarter and half year 
ended 30th September 2026. 
 
Q2 FY27 Financial (Consolidated) Highlights: 
 
Revenue grew 18.2% YoY to Rs. 2,263 Cr, driven by double-digit growth in general trade, highest ever 
quarterly sales in international business 
 
EBITDA increased 24.1% YoY to Rs. 205 Cr, delivering 9.0% margin, driven by disciplined price increases, 
cost efficiencies and operating leverage amidst inflationary environments 
 
PBT excluding exceptional items is at Rs. 167 Cr, growth of 36.2% YoY 
 
Q2 FY27 Segment Performance Highlights: 
ECD accelerated growth momentum with 19.2% YoY, with highest quarterly growth in last 9 quarters, 
driven by strong performance in core categories and new businesses gaining early traction  
 
Highest quarterly growth in last 9 quarters led by robust growth in fans, conventional pumps, LDA and 
SDA 
 
BLDC growth continues with significant sequential expansion in BLDC reach and continued momentum in 
new portfolio launches 
 
Pumps growth led by double-digit growth in the Non-Solar Business 
 
LDA registered strong growth capitalizing on water heater momentum, with continued market share gains 
and emerging as one of the strongest players in trade channel  
 
Kitchen appliances growth led by strong performance in general trade, backed by improved product mix 
 
Solar rooftop installations pertaining to AP tender progressing well with ~20,000 units installed so far, 
covering 50% of order book 
 
Lighting continued its robust growth trajectory, delivering strong double-digit revenue growth of 
14.3% YoY 
 
Delivered healthy growth across both B2C and B2B segments 
 
EBIT margin of 12.9%, impacted by B2B order at pre-contracted prices. However, EBIT margin improved 
sequentially by 90 bps from previous quarter amidst inflationary environments 
Consolidated revenue grew by 18.2% YoY;   
Strong double-digit growth across segments for third consecutive quarter; Profits 
significantly ahead of revenue growth 
Butterfly continues double-digit revenue growth



Commenting on the CGCEL’s performance, Promeet Ghosh, MD & CEO, said, “We are delighted that this is 
Crompton’s third consecutive quarter of strong double-digit growth across segments, with market share gains 
in key categories. ECD, with revenue growth of 19.4% YoY, delivered a robust performance driven by broad 
based growth across categories. The new categories in this segment continue to gain early traction. Lighting 
business, growing at 14.3% YoY, continues its healthy double-digit growth trajectory with sustainable profit 
margin. Butterfly, with 13.1% YoY growth, continued its momentum with robust performance across key 
categories.  
 
This performance demonstrates the impact of the transformative initiatives undertaken as part of Crompton 
2.0 strategy. Our focused investments in brand building, consumer centric innovation and successful 
turnaround of Lighting and Butterfly businesses have strengthened our growth engines, which are now 
beginning to progressively yield results.   
 
During the quarter, we have unveiled a refreshed brand identity for Crompton, renewing our 85 year-old legacy 
and beginning a bold new chapter. We remain committed to investing in brand “Crompton”, accelerating 
consumer-led innovation while moving towards sustainable and profitable growth.”

products - Fans, Lights, Pumps and Appliances including Kitchen Appliances. The Company has strong dealer 
base across the country and wide service network offering robust after sales service to its customers.
