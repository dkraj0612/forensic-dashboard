Management Commentary
https://cdn4.iconfinder.com/data
Shrimp Feed
In Q2FY27, Shrimp feed revenues grew by 19.0% YoY from Rs.11,582 Mn to Rs.13,782 Mn due to increase in Sale price.
PBT in Q2FY27 came in at Rs 945 Mn from Rs.1,741 Mn YOY with margins at 4.2% due to increasing Raw Material prices.
Shrimp Processing & Export
Shrimp processing revenue decreased by 17% YoY to Rs. 3,744 Mn in Q2FY27 from 4,515 Mn in Q2FY26, primarily driven by 20% 
decline in sales volume. This was mainly attributable to an increase in stock-in-transit during Q2FY27 compared to the 
corresponding quarter of previous year. The impact was partially offset by favourable foreign exchange movements.
EBITDA margin declined to 7.3% in Q2FY27 from 13.5% in Q2FY26, primarily due to provision for differential Anti-Dumping Duty 
(ADD) liability arising from the administrative review 19, conducted by the United States Department of Commerce. This 
followed the revision in ADD rate from 1.35% to 3.76% as per the final determination. The decline in EBITDA margin was further 
impacted by an increase in Ocean fright rates during the quarter under review.
