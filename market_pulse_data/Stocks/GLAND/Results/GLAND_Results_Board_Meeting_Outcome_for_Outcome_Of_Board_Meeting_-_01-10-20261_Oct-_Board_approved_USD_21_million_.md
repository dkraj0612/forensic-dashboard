Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you 
that the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Gland Pharma Limited (the “Company”), at its meeting held 
today, i.e., Thursday, October 01, 2026, has, inter-alia, considered and approved: 
 
I. 
Investment of USD 21 million by the Company in Gland Pharma USA Inc. (“Gland USA”), proposed 
to be a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company (as informed to the Stock Exchanges on 13th 
September, 2026). Gland USA proposes to utilize the funds to subscribe to 60% of the equity share 
capital of Novelstar Pharma Inc. (“Novelstar” and such investment “Novelstar Investment”).  
 
The proposed investment is intended to facilitate expansion of the Company’s business in the United 
States of America. Upon completion of the Novelstar Investment by Gland USA, Novelstar will 
become a subsidiary of Gland USA and a step-down subsidiary of the Company.  
 
The details relating to the investment in Gland USA and Novelstar Investment, as required under 
Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-
CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are set out in Annexure A and Annexure B, 
respectively. 
 
II. 
Mr. Wenjie Zhang (DIN: 10727581), Non-Executive & Non-Independent Director, has tendered 
resignation to his office as a Director due to his pre-occupations. The resignation was accepted with 
immediate effect. 
 
The details relating to Mr. Wenjie Zhang, as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations 
read with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026, along with a copy of his resignation letter with detailed reasons thereof are set out in Annexure 
C and Annexure D respectively. 
 
III. 
Appointment of Mr. Xingli Wang (DIN: 11899926), as an Additional Director (Non-Executive & 
Non-Independent Director) of the Company with effect from October 01, 2026.  
 
The approval of the members as required under Regulation 17(1C) of Listing Regulations for the 
aforesaid appointment will be obtained within the stipulated timelines, through a postal ballot and e-
voting.

Brief profile 
Mr. Xingli Wang, aged 63, is currently the President of Fosun 
Pharma, and holds positions including the Chief Executive Officer 
of the Global R&D Center and Chief Executive Officer of 
innovative medicine division, Fosun Pharma and Non-Executive 
Director of Shanghai Henlius (stock code: 02696), a subsidiary 
listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.  
 
Mr. Wang joined the Fosun Group in January, 2023 and served as 
the Executive President of Fosun Pharma from January, 2023 to 
June, 2025. Prior to joining the Fosun Group, Mr. Wang served as 
a Senior lecturer in Cardiovascular Medicine at The University of 
New South Wales (“UNSW”), Australia, and as a cardiologist and 
professor with tenure at Baylor College of Medicine, USA, 
Medical Director of Schering-Plough Corporation, a company 
formerly listed on the New York Stock Exchange (stock code: 
SGP) (merged into Merck & Co., Inc. in 2009). He also worked in 
Novartis AG (stock code: NVS), a company listed on the New 
York Stock Exchange, where he successively served as Project 
Supervisor, Global Project Clinical Head, Head of Novartis Global 
Drug R&D (China) and General Manager of Biomedical Research 
Institute (China).  
 
Mr. Wang obtained a Bachelor’s degree in Medicine from 
Shandong Medical College (merged into Shandong University in 
2000) and a Doctorate degree in Cardiovascular Science from the 
UNSW. Mr. Wang also holds a license to practice Medicine in 
Australia.
