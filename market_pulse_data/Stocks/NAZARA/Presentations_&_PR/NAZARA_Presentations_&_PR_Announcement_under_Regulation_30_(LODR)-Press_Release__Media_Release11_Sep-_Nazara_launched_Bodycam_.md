NAZARA LAUNCHES BODYCAM IN FORTNITE 
THROUGH AN OFFICIAL UEFN ADAPTATION 
The tactical shooter's signature body-camera perspective, close-quarters combat, and high-pressure multiplayer 
arrive in Fortnite; developed by Virstate and published by Nazara Technologies under agreement with Reissad 
Studio. 
 
 
 
Mumbai, India, September 11, 2026. Nazara Technologies and Virstate today announced that 
Bodycam UEFN, the official Bodycam game adaptation for Fortnite* developed under agreement 
with Reissad Studio is now available to players worldwide starting today. 
 
Built by Virstate in Unreal Editor for Fortnite (UEFN), and published by Nazara Technologies, 
Bodycam UEFN, the game brings the franchise’s signature body-camera perspective, intense close-
quarters combat, realistic atmosphere, and high-pressure multiplayer gameplay into Fortnite. 
 
Players can jump in now by searching for Bodycam in Fortnite Discover or by entering island code: 
9784-3946-5466  
 
Built as an official expansion of the Bodycam universe, Bodycam UEFN is designed to introduce a 
new generation of players to the franchise while giving existing fans a new way to experience its 
tension, realism, and tactical combat. The project is not a replacement for the original Bodycam PC 
experience, but a free-to-play gateway into its world, created specifically for Fortnite’s ecosystem.

Nitish Mittersain, MD & CEO, Nazara Technologies, said, “Bodycam UEFN is Nazara’s first build 
inside Fortnite, and it is just the beginning. Fortnite is now one of the largest discovery platforms in 
the world, and we intend to build there at scale. Reissad has raised the bar with an experience that 
is raw, immersive, and visually stunning. Bringing Bodycam to Fortnite proves that UEFN is ready for 
premium-quality FPS experiences. It becomes the template for the IPs, original experiences, and 
partnerships we bring to this ecosystem next.” 
 
At launch, Bodycam UEFN features Russian Building, one of the most recognizable environments 
from the original game, recreated for Fortnite with a focus on authenticity, atmosphere, visual 
fidelity, and fast tactical encounters. The map is designed for high-pressure close-quarters combat 
where positioning, reflexes, sound awareness, and precision determine the outcome of every fight. 
 
Bodycam UEFN launches with three multiplayer modes: 
 
● Free For All - Every player fights alone in fast-paced matches where survival depends on 
awareness, accuracy, and reaction time. 
● Team Deathmatch - Two teams compete to reach the score limit first in squad-based 
combat inspired by classic competitive shooters. 
● Gun Game - Players progress through an evolving arsenal by securing eliminations, racing to 
reach the final weapon and claim victory. 
 
The experience also includes long-term progression systems, allowing players to climb ranks, 
unlock weapons, earn rewards, and pursue competitive goals through regular play. Bodycam UEFN 
is planned as a live experience, with future updates expected to introduce additional maps inspired 
by the original Bodycam, new weapons and equipment, new rewards and unlockables, seasonal 
updates, community events, and additional gameplay modes. 
 
With Bodycam UEFN, Nazara Technologies aims to raise expectations of what players can 
experience inside Fortnite and demonstrate how major gaming IPs can use UEFN as a serious 
platform for franchise expansion. 
 
“Bodycam has always been built with the ambition to stay ahead of technology rather than wait for 
the industry to move first,” said Luca Dassier, Studio Director at Reissad. He also added, 
“Partnering with Nazara Technologies and Virstate is what makes this expansion possible. They lead 
it, they own it, and they bring the publishing and development strength to build Bodycam a genuine 
home on Fortnite. For us, this partnership is about preparing for the future of Unreal Engine 6 by 
gaining hands on experience with Verse, the programming language expected to play a major role in
