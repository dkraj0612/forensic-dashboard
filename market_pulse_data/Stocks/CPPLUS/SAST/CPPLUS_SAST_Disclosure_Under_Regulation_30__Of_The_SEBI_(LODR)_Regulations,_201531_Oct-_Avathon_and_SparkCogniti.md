Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is in continuation of our earlier intimation dated July 8, 2026, wherein it was informed that Avathon, Inc. 
and SparkCognition India Private Limited, (collectively, the “Claimants”) through their counsel, submitted a 
request for Arbitration before the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) for initiating arbitration 
proceedings against the Company in relation to a dispute arises out of an agreement entered into between the 
Company and the Claimants. 
 
In furtherance of above, we wish to inform that the aforesaid Arbitration proceedings has been withdrawn by 
the Claimants basis the mutual discussion between the parties and therefore, no arbitration proceedings in this 
regards remain pending against the Company. 
 
The withdrawal of the aforesaid arbitration proceedings does not have any material adverse impact on the 
financial, operational or other activities of the Company. 
 
The detailed disclosure with respect to the above intimation as required under the SEBI Circular 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 is enclosed herewith as Annexure. 
 
This disclosure will also be hosted on the Company's website viz. https://www.adityagroup.com/
