Resignation as Internal Auditors of M/s. BLB Limited 
Dear Sir / Ma’am, 
We, Sarat Jain & Associates, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration Number: 014793C), 
were engaged as the Internal Auditors of M/s. BLB Limited (“the Company”) for carrying out 
the Internal Audit assignments for the Financial Year 2025-26. 
In this regard, we hereby confirm that we have completed the Internal Audit assignments for 
the Financial Year 2025-26 and have submitted the Internal Audit Reports in respect of the 
half-years ended 30" September, 2025 and 31“ March, 2026. 
We further confirm that the aforesaid Internal Audit Reports constitute our final deliverables 
in respect of the Internal Audit Engagement for FY 2025-26 and that, as on the date hereof, 
there are no Internal Audit Reports, audit observations or unresolved matters pending from our 
end in relation to the said engagement. 
In view of the completion of the Internal Audit assignments for FY 2025-26 and submission of 
the aforesaid Internal Audit Reports, we hereby tender our resignation as the Internal Auditors 
of the Company with effect from 1“ October, 2026. 
We further confirm that there are no other material reasons for our resignation other than those 
stated in this letter. 
We place on record our appreciation for the cooperation, assistance and support extended by 
the Board, management and officials of the Company during the course of our engagement.
