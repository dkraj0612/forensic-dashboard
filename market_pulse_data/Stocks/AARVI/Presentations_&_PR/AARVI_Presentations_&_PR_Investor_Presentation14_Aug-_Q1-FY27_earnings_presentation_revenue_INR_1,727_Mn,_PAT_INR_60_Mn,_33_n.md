### 🤖 AI Intelligence Summary
> Aarvi Encon reported a solid 14.1% year-on-year growth in operational income for Q1-FY27 and a 42.9% increase in Profit After Tax (PAT), boosted significantly by a surge in "Other Income" and a notably lower tax expense. However, this net profit growth masks a material decline in core operational profitability, with EBITDA margins contracting sharply by 63 basis points year-on-year and 134 basis points quarter-on-quarter to 2.61%. The reliance on non-operational factors to drive PAT, amidst a weakening core EBITDA margin, presents a red flag regarding the underlying health of the company's core staffing services.
