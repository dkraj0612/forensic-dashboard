About Mahanagar Gas Limited 
 
Mahanagar Gas Limited (MGL) is one of India's leading City Gas Distribution companies, supplying 
Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) and Piped Natural Gas (PNG) across Mumbai and adjoining areas. 
MGL has established a strong presence in the natural gas distribution sector and serves a wide range 
of customers across the residential, commercial, industrial and transportation segments. 
 
About Aarvi Encon Limited 
 
Aarvi Encon Limited is a leading technical manpower and engineering services company with a 
strong presence across the energy, oil & gas, petrochemical, power, infrastructure, renewable energy 
and other industrial sectors. 
 
The Company provides technical manpower, engineering, operations and maintenance services to 
leading organisations across India and international markets. Aarvi Encon continues to strengthen its 
capabilities and geographical presence while focusing on delivering high-quality, reliable and cost-
effective manpower solutions to its clients.
