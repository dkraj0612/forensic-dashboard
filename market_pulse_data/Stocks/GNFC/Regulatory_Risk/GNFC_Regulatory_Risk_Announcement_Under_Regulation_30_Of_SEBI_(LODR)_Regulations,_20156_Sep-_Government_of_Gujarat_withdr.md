    Sub.:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30(2) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time, we hereby inform that the 
Government of Gujarat (GoG), vide its Notification No. AIS/35.2026/37/Ser(I) dated 
September 05, 2026, has withdrawn the nomination of Shri Rajkumar Beniwal, IAS           
(DIN No: 07195658) from the Managing Director of the Company. 
 
Further, vide the aforesaid Notification, the Government of Gujarat has nominated            
Shri Sanjeev Kumar, IAS (DIN No: 03600655), for holding additional charge of Managing 
Director on the Board of the Company. Shri Sanjeev Kumar, IAS (DIN No: 03600655), will 
be formally appointed as Managing Director of the Company at the ensuing meeting of 
Board of Directors. 
 
 
The necessary formalities relating to handing over and taking over of charge, including 
completion of all statutory procedures and compliances, for the induction of Shri Sanjeev 
Kumar, IAS (DIN No: 03600655), and the relinquishment of Shri Rajkumar Beniwal, IAS 
(DIN No: 07195658) as Managing Director and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company, 
shall be completed in due course. Accordingly, the exact date of relinquishment of charge 
and assumption of charge shall be intimated to the Stock Exchange(s) separately in due 
course.
