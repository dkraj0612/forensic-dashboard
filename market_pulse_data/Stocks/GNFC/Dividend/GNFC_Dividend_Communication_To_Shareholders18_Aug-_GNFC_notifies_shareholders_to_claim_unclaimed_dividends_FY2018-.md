unclaimed for seven consecutive years are required to be transferred to the Investor Education and 
Protection Fund (“IEPF”), established by the Government of India under Section 125 of the Act. 
The above requirement is subject to there being no order of any Court, Tribunal or Statutory Authority 
restraining such transfer and provided that the shares are not pledged or hypothecated under the 
provisions of the Depositories Act, 1996.
