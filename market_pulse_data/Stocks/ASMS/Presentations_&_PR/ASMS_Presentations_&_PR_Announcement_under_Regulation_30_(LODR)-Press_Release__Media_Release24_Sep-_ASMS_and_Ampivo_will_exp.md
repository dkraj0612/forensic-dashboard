ANNEXURE-I 
 
ASMS and Ampivo to Expand Digital Agriculture and Farmer Services 
Across Rural India 
National, 24th September 2026:  
Avio Smart Market Stack Limited (ASMS), formerly Bartronics India Limited, and 
Ampivo Smart Technologies are set to deepen their collaboration following the MoU 
between the companies, with plans to introduce Ampivo’s digital agriculture platform 
“Agri Kisan Setu” across identified villages within ASMS’ rural network. 
Ampivo has independently developed and launched its agriculture application, Agri Kisan 
Setu, available on the Google Play Store in seven languages. The platform is already being 
used in villages where Ampivo is conducting farmer trainings and pilot programmes, 
enabling the company to gather on-ground feedback and continuously refine its technology, 
content and service workflows. 
As part of its broader expansion strategy, Ampivo is working with FPOs, NGOs, institutions 
and other ecosystem partners across India to extend its digital agriculture platform to a larger 
farmer base. The proposed collaboration with ASMS is an important part of this expansion, 
providing an opportunity to introduce the platform across ASMS’ established rural network. 
Ampivo’s platform supports agri enterprises with farmers, agri-entrepreneurs, and rural 
stakeholders by providing access to modern farm tools, farm management assistance, 
agricultural commodity-wise market information, and bringing together key agricultural 
services through a unified digital interface, including market price discovery, buyer and seller 
connectivity, agricultural knowledge and tutorials, government-scheme information, and 
specialised services such as drone services to improve farm productivity, decision-making, 
supply chain efficiency, and streamline farm operations, reduce costs, and enhance farm 
income.  
The platform is also being developed to support carbon credits and digital Measurement, 
Reporting and Verification (DMRV), with the objective of enabling farmers to participate in 
eligible sustainability and carbon reduction-linked programmes. Ampivo is also developing 
digital workflows to help farmers find government schemes and entitlements relevant to their 
location, profile, and agricultural activities. 
N. Vidhya Sagar Reddy, Managing Director, Avio Smart Market Stack Limited, said: 
“ASMS is focused on building an ecosystem that connects rural communities with markets, 
technology, financial services and new economic opportunities. Ampivo has developed and 
deployed a live digital agriculture platform, and we see an opportunity to extend these 
capabilities across our network villages. Together, ASMS’ rural reach and Ampivo’s 
technology can provide farmers with easier access to agricultural information, services, 
markets and emerging sustainability opportunities.”

Anupam Shah, Chief Operating Officer, Ampivo Smart Technologies, said: “Ampivo has 
progressed from technology development to live deployment, with our application available 
on the Google Play Store and being used through our pilot programmes. Our multilingual and 
configurable platform is designed to adapt to different geographies, crops and farmer 
requirements. We are now expanding through partnerships with FPOs, NGOs and institutions 
across India, and ASMS provides an important opportunity to take Ampivo to a much larger 
rural ecosystem.” 
 
The collaboration is expected to initially focus on introducing Ampivo across identified 
ASMS network villages and on-boarding farmers to the platform’s existing agricultural, 
knowledge and marketplace services. The companies will subsequently evaluate deeper 
integration with ASMS’ agriculture initiatives, including digital advisory, service delivery 
and eligible sustainability and carbon-credit programmes. 
About Avio Smart Market Stack Limited 
Avio Smart Market Stack Limited (ASMS) is a diversified platform company focused on building scalable businesses across 
Financial Inclusion & Digital Services, Agri Supply Chain & Technologies, and Diagnostics & Healthcare. Through its 
Financial Inclusion division, the Company has established a strong presence across rural India, creating a trusted distribution 
network. Building on this foundation, ASMS is developing a technology-enabled agri ecosystem while leveraging its 
existing reach. The Company has also entered into a strategic partnership with a cutting-edge molecular diagnostics 
company to diversify into the high-growth healthcare and diagnostics sector. 
About Ampivo Smart Technologies 
Ampivo Smart Technologies is a technology company developing digital platforms that connect farmers with agricultural 
information, markets, services and institutional ecosystems. Its multilingual agriculture application is live on the Google 
Play Store and is being piloted across rural markets. Ampivo is expanding its farmer ecosystem through collaborations with 
FPOs, NGOs, institutions and other partners across India.
