Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“Listing Regulations”), we 
wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of Avio Smart Market Stack Limited 
(formerly known as Bartronics India Limited) (“Company”), at its meeting held on Friday, 
October 23, 2026, has, inter alia, considered and approved the following matters: 
 
 
1. Raising of funds through a Rights Issue 
 
The Board considered and approved the raising of funds for an amount not exceeding Rs. 
49,00,00,000/- (Rupees Forty-Nine Crore only) by way of an offer and issuance of equity 
shares of face value of Rs. 1/- each (“Equity Shares”) to the eligible equity shareholders of 
the Company as on the record date to be determined and notified subsequently, in accordance 
with applicable laws, including the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2018, as amended (“SEBI ICDR Regulations”), and subject to receipt of 
requisite statutory and regulatory approvals, wherever applicable. 
 
The Board has delegated authority to Rights issue Committee to  consider and approve the 
Draft Letter of Offer in connection with the proposed for rights issue and undertake such 
other act, and things as may be necessary in relation thereto.  
 
The detailed terms and conditions of the Rights Issue, including the issue price, rights 
entitlement ratio, record date, issue period, timing, terms of payment and other related 
matters, shall be determined by the Board or a duly authorised committee thereof, as 
applicable, in accordance with the applicable laws and regulatory requirements. 
 
The requisite disclosures under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, read with Part A of 
Schedule III thereto and SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 
November 11, 2024, are enclosed as Annexure I.

The proposed investment is expected to facilitate farmer onboarding, expand the platform’s 
reach, strengthen digital engagement and develop Ampivo’s in-house technology capabilities, 
including artificial intelligence, data intelligence and platform engineering. 
 
The investment is expected to strengthen the Company’s presence in the AgriTech sector and

4. Execution of Share Purchase and Subscription Agreement with Ampivo Smart 
Technologies Private Limited 
 
The Board considered and approved the execution of the Share Purchase and Subscription 
Agreement with Ampivo Smart Technologies Private Limited in connection with the 
Company’s proposed investment of Rs. 1,400.00 lakh (Rupees Fourteen Crore only) by way 
of subscription to its equity shares, subject to finalisation of the definitive agreement and 
compliance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements. 
 
The agreement sets out the terms and conditions governing the proposed investment and the 
respective rights and obligations of the parties. In terms of the agreement, the Company is 
entitled to receive 70% of the revenues generated from customers onboarded or facilitated by 
the Company, subject to the terms and conditions specified therein. 
 
The disclosures relating to the agreement, as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing 
Regulations, read with Part A of Schedule III thereto and applicable SEBI circulars, are set 
out in Annexure II. 
 
5. Withdrawal of Application for In-Principal Approval for Preferential Issue and 
Adjustment of Processing Fees Against Fresh Application 
 
The Board was apprised that the Company had filed an application under Regulation 28(1) of 
the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, seeking in-
principal approval for the proposed preferential allotment of 11,13,02,372 equity shares of 
the Company, bearing Application Reference No. 57363. 
 
The Board noted that, in view of non-compliance with Regulation 159(1) of the SEBI ICDR 
Regulations, 2018, the Company had submitted withdrawal applications to BSE Limited and 
the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”), seeking withdrawal of the aforesaid 
application and proposing to submit a fresh application after carrying out the necessary 
rectifications and ensuring compliance with the applicable regulatory requirements.

The Board further noted that, in response to the withdrawal application submitted by the 
Company, NSE had requested the Company to submit a copy of the Board Resolution 
approving the withdrawal of the aforesaid application. Accordingly, the Board considered 
and approved the withdrawal of Application Reference No. 57363 and authorised the 
submission of a certified true copy of the relevant Board Resolution to NSE and BSE 
Limited, as may be required. 
 
The Board noted that, in the withdrawal applications submitted to BSE Limited and NSE, the 
Company had also requested that the processing fees already paid be carried forward and 
adjusted against the processing fees payable for the fresh application relating to the same 
preferential issue, subject to the applicable rules and procedures of the respective Stock 
Exchanges. 
 
The Board authorised the appropriate officials of the Company to submit the certified true 
copy of the Board Resolution to the respective Stock Exchanges, follow up on the request for 
adjustment of processing fees, and undertake all necessary acts, deeds and things in 
connection with the withdrawal of the existing application and submission of a fresh 
application after completing the necessary rectifications and complying with the applicable 
regulatory requirements. 
 
The meeting of the Board commenced at 4:00 P.M. (IST) and concluded at 5:30 P.M. (IST).
