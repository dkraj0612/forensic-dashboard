The Voice That Answers Every Call: Blue Cloud Launches BluVaani, One 
of India's First Sovereign, On-Premises AI Call Dispatchers for Police 
Helplines 
After a successful pilot that handled more than 8,500 calls in ten days, BluVaani goes commercial. It 
runs entirely on the department's own premises, with no Internet needed, so the data never leaves its 
hands. Every decision stays with the officer. 
Hyderabad, 20 November 2026: In cyber fraud, minutes decide whether money can be traced or is 
lost for good. Today, Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited (BSE: 539607) announced the 
commercial launch of BluVaani (The Sovereign AI Call Dispatcher), an India-built, complete 
sovereign edge AI solution that runs entirely on the customer's own premises with no Internet needed, 
making it one of India's first sovereign, on-premises AI call dispatchers for police helplines. Built 
around an AI voice agent that works in all Indian vernacular languages, it answers the citizen's call at 
the very first minute, understands the complaint, opens the case and puts it in front of an officer. 
BluVaani is part of Blue Cloud's Access Genie platform and is now available to police departments, 
government agencies and public service helplines. 
 
The launch follows a successfully completed pilot project on a state police cyber-crime helpline. In 
ten operating days, BluVaani handled more than 8,500 calls and flagged more than 1,500 possible 
fraud cases for officer review. These are results from a live public helpline, not from a laboratory. 
 
The problem every helpline faces 
 
A helpline is only as strong as its first minute. When call volumes surge, citizens wait, details are 
taken in a hurry, repeat callers are treated as strangers, and officers lose hours to paperwork that 
should have taken seconds. BluVaani was built to remove that bottleneck without removing the 
officer from the decision. 
 
What BluVaani does 
 
• 
Runs entirely on the department's own premises, with no Internet needed. 
• 
Works in all Indian vernacular languages, so every citizen can report a complaint in the 
language they speak. 
• 
Answers every call and understands the complaint in a natural conversation. 
• 
Extracts the key details and creates the case record, so that no detail is lost to a rushed note. 
• 
Recognises repeat callers and links them to the existing file, so that the officer sees the full 
picture. 
• 
Flags possible fraud cases and places them at the top of the officer's desk for review. 
• 
Keeps the citizen informed with status updates on WhatsApp, so that no one is left 
wondering.

Sovereign by design 
 
BluVaani runs on a BlueEdge server deployed on the customer's own premises, as part of Blue 
Cloud's Access Genie platform. It needs no Internet connection: calls are processed at the edge, inside 
the department's own four walls, and nothing is sent to an outside cloud. At the close of the pilot, all 
call transcripts and audio recordings were formally handed over to the department, which took 
custody of them, and none were retained by Blue Cloud or its implementation partner. The department 
then cleared the removal of the deployed server. For police and government agencies, this means the 
data, the infrastructure and the decisions stay in their own hands. 
 
Officers decide. Always. 
 
BluVaani handles first contact and paperwork. Officers review and file every case. Investigation and 
every decision remain with the police. The technology widens what an officer can handle; it does not 
replace the officer's judgement.

India's public helplines carry the weight of a digital economy that is growing faster than the systems

sovereign, India-built platforms for public safety.

About BluVaani and Access Genie 
 
BluVaani, The Sovereign AI Call Dispatcher within Access Genie, is a complete sovereign edge AI 
solution on Blue Cloud's AI platform for police and public safety operations. It sits alongside 
CopWriter, which turns spoken statements into case records, and Complaint Memo, which turns daily 
status reports into routed official memos. It runs on a BlueEdge server on the customer's own 
premises with no Internet needed, and the department's data stays under its control. 
 
About Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited 
 
Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited, incorporated in 1991 and listed on BSE and NSE, is a 
Hyderabad-based AI-first technology group. Its businesses cover AI and sovereign platforms for 
government and public safety, telecom and 5G fixed wireless access, healthcare technology and
