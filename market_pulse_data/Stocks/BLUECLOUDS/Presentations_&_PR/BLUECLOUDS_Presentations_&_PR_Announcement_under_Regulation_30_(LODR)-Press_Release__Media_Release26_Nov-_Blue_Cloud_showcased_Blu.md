PRESS RELEASE 
For Immediate Release | Mumbai, 26 November 2026 
Blue Cloud Showcases Sovereign AI Healthcare and Civic 
Technology to Mayors from Over 100 Countries at the 3rd World 
Mayors’ Forum, Mumbai 
 
BluHealth kiosks and BioSter draw strong interest from city leaders; Dr. Kiran Velpula 
addresses mayors and ministers on AI and the future of cities 
KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• Delegates from over 100 countries, mostly mayors and senior city leaders, with ministers in 
attendance 
• BluHealth kiosks and BioSter showcased, drawing strong interest from mayors 
• Dr. Kiran Velpula, Deputy Mayor of Peoria, addressed the forum on AI in cities 
• Blue Cloud team took part in several forum sessions and engaged dignitaries from across 
states and countries 
 
Mumbai, 26 November 2026: Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited (BSE: 539607 | NSE: 
BLUECLOUDS), an AI-first technology company headquartered in Hyderabad, is participating in the 
3rd World Mayors’ Forum and 7th Asia Cities Summit 2026 at the Fairmont Mumbai, being held on 
25 and 26 November 2026. The forum is organised by the All-India Institute of Local Self-
Government (AIILSG) as part of its centenary year, with the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation as 
Host City Partner. 
The two-day forum has brought together delegates from over 100 countries, most of them mayors and 
senior city leaders, along with the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Maharashtra and ministers from several 
states. The agenda covers the future of Indian cities, city-to-city cooperation, artificial intelligence in 
urban governance, public safety, waste, climate and energy. 
Healthcare technology showcased to city leaders 
Blue Cloud presented two solutions at the forum, both designed for the everyday needs of cities and 
their citizens: 
• BluHealth kiosks: AI-integrated, non-invasive health screening that delivers vital-sign readings 
through a camera in minutes, with no invasive equipment, so that preventive screening can 
reach more people at public places. 
• BioSter: a solution that helps keep air and surfaces in hospitals, schools and public spaces safer 
from pathogens.

Address on AI and the future of cities 
Dr. Kiran Velpula, President of Blue Cloud USA and Deputy Mayor of the City of Peoria, Illinois, 
USA, addressed the forum’s discussion on “Artificial Intelligence, Real Cities: Governing the Tech-
Driven Urban Future”, before an audience of mayors, ministers and senior city leaders. Drawing on 
his experience in municipal governance, he shared Peoria’s approach to city leadership and set out 
why Blue Cloud believes cities need technology they can own and run. He described Blue Cloud’s 
approach in three parts: 
• AI-first, not AI-added: working products and platforms that cities can operate in the field, 
rather than reports that sit on a shelf. 
• Sovereign by design: Blue Cloud’s Access Genie platform and BluEdge sovereign AI keep 
intelligence and citizen data within the country and under the customer’s control, with 
cybersecurity built in from day one. 
• Outcome-driven: BluHealth for preventive health screening, BioSter for safer public spaces, 
Blura SAGA for early threat detection and citizen-grievance listening, and BluVaani, an AI 
voice agent for police and civic helplines.

Engagement with dignitaries and city leaders 
The delegation included Dr. Murthy Devarabotla, Director of Blue Cloud and President of The

staying within the customer’s own country.
