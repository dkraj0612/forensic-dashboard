PRESS RELEASE 
For Immediate Release | Hyderabad, 19 November 2026 
Blue Cloud Softech Solutions and Mahesh Babu Foundation Sign 
Collaboration Agreement for Technology-Led Social Impact in 
Healthcare, Education and Skill Development 
Five-year partnership builds on the GMB Life preventive wellness app and opens the way for 
CSR-funded and grant-funded community initiatives 
Hyderabad, 19 November 2026: Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited (BSE: 539607 | NSE: 
BLUECLOUDS), an AI-first technology company, today announced that it has signed a Collaboration 
and Implementation Agreement with the Mahesh Babu Foundation. The five-year agreement 
formalises and expands the partnership between the two organisations to design and deliver 
sustainable social impact initiatives for communities across healthcare, education and skill 
development. 
 
Under the agreement, Blue Cloud will bring its artificial intelligence platforms and digital health 
technology, together with its access to funders and the CSR and grant ecosystem. The Mahesh Babu 
Foundation will act as the implementing partner, bringing its community outreach, credibility and on-
ground network. Individual initiatives will be taken up through Project Orders, each setting out the 
scope, responsibilities, funding, reporting and data protection arrangements, so that every programme 
is structured, accountable and compliant with applicable law, including the CSR provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. 
The collaboration covers healthcare and preventive wellness, digital health and health awareness, and 
education and skill development, and can extend to other sectors the two organisations agree on. 
 
The agreement builds on the work already under way between the two organisations. GMB Life, a 
preventive screening and wellness app powered by Blue Cloud's BluHealth platform, is available on 
the App Store and Google Play and connects users to the Foundation's health camps and wellness 
initiatives. It will continue without interruption. The next phase planned for GMB Life is BluHealth-
enabled healthcare kiosks at selected partner locations, operated with local doctors, which will 
proceed under a separate Project Order. 
 
Commenting on the agreement, Tejesh Kumar Kodali, Group Chairman, Blue Cloud Softech 
Solutions Limited, said: 
 
“Technology creates its greatest value when it reaches the people who need it most. Our work 
with the Foundation has already put preventive wellness in the hands of everyday users through 
GMB Life. This agreement gives that work a lasting structure, so that we can combine the 
Foundation's community reach with Blue Cloud's AI platforms and take them to many more 
communities.” 
– Tejesh Kumar Kodali, Group Chairman, Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited
