## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) has concluded FY25 with a record-breaking fourth quarter and full year, demonstrating robust financial performance and resilience. Standalone total income reached INR 3,420 crores for FY25, marking the highest-ever yearly sales and profits. The company achieved a growth of 14.47%, outpacing the industry in its core sectors. EBITDA margins expanded by 148 basis points YoY to 17.52% for FY25, driven by better realization, a favorable product mix, and efficient cost control. PBT and PAT also saw significant growth of 25% and 23%, respectively. Q4 FY25 standalone total income was INR 967.55 crores, up 12.98% YoY, with sustained EBITDA margins at 17.7%. A significant development was securing the company's single largest order to date: 1,121 rough terrain forklifts and telehandlers for the Indian armed forces valued at INR 420 crores, expected to contribute to 4% of revenue in FY26 and 5% or more in FY27. ACE maintains a debt-free balance sheet with ample liquidity. The Board has recommended a final dividend of 100%. The company is navigating challenges like CEV-5 (BS-V) emission norm implementation, which has led to temporary demand sluggishness and price adjustments, but anticipates normalization within the current quarter.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Leadership:** ACE maintains its strong position in the crane industry, with over 75% market share in Hydra cranes and 51% in new-generation cranes.
*   **Defense Sector Entry:** Secured a significant order for 1,121 rough terrain forklifts/telehandlers (RTFLT) from the Indian armed forces (INR 420 crores), a pivotal step for indigenous manufacturing and modernization. Defence is expected to contribute ~4% of revenue in FY26, rising to 5%+ in FY27.
*   **Export & Defense Synergy:** Combined contribution from exports and defense is targeted at 9-10% in FY26, with a medium-term goal of 10-15%.
*   **CEV-5 (BS-V) Transition:** Products are upgraded to meet new emission norms, entailing price increases (5-6% for 40% of products, 12-13% for 60%). This is expected to be a temporary phenomenon, with normalization anticipated within the current quarter.
*   **Sectoral Contribution:** Manufacturing and logistics (~45%), infrastructure & construction (~35%), real estate (~12-13%), and agriculture (~7-8%) form a balanced revenue base, enhanced by strategic diversification.
*   **Capacity and Modernization:** Current capacity stands at 13,200 units for cranes, 2,700 for material handling, and 1,800 for construction equipment. Capex of INR 300-350 crores in FY26 is allocated to modernization, automation, and capacity expansion for specific crane types.
*   **Kato Works JV:** On track for operationalization by Q3 FY26, targeting an annual opportunity of INR 300-400 crores. Initial revenue contribution of INR 100+ crores expected in FY26.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on Chinese Imports:** Awaiting final order on investigation concerning truck and crawler cranes (>100 metric tons), with hopes for implementation in Q2 FY26. This could address predatory pricing by Chinese manufacturers and revitalize the domestic larger crane industry.
*   **Electric Cranes:** Ready with products and awaiting final MBR registration approvals, expected within a month.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative

*   **FY25 Revenue Growth & Doubling Target:** While the previous Q3 call reaffirmed the target of doubling the FY23 top-line by FY26, management now acknowledges a potential shortfall, adjusting the FY26 top-line growth guidance to 14-15% from previous expectations of ~20%+. They are confident of achieving over double FY23 revenue by FY27.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** The reported FY25 EBITDA margin of 17.52% (standalone) and Q4 FY25 margin of 17.7% are consistent with the stated goal of maintaining a stable margin profile between 17% and 18%. Management is optimistic about potential expansion due to operating leverage in H2 FY26.
*   **Kato Works JV Timeline:** The JV remains on target for operationalization by Q3 FY26, with initial revenue contribution expected in H2 FY26, aligning with previous timelines.
*   **CEV-5 (BS-5) Transition Impact:** Management acknowledges the temporary impact of CEV-5 norms on demand and pricing, attributing the current sluggishness in inquiries and orders partly to this. This aligns with their expectation of a short-term adjustment period.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Capex plans are proceeding as stated, with a focus on modernization and specific capacity enhancements rather than broad-based expansion beyond the existing INR 5,000+ crore capacity.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines

*   **FY26 Revenue Growth Guidance:** Targeted at 14-15% top-line growth. Projections will be revisited by the end of Q2 FY26.
*   **EBITDA Margins Guidance:** Expected to remain stable between 17% and 18% for FY26, with potential for slight expansion in H2 FY26 due to operating leverage.
*   **Kato Works JV:** Expected to be functional by Q3 FY26. Annual opportunity of INR 300-400 crores. Projected revenue contribution of INR 100+ crores in FY26 (top-line contribution will be on the bottom line for ACE as it's a 50-50 JV).
*   **Defense & Export Contribution:** Defense expected to contribute ~4% of revenue in FY26, rising to 5%+ in FY27. Combined defense and exports targeted at 9-10% in FY26, with a medium-term goal of 10-15%.
*   **CEV-5 (BS-V) Normalization:** Demand and pricing are expected to normalize within Q1 FY26.
*   **Electric Cranes:** Approvals for registration expected within the next month, enabling commercial sales.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD):** Final order expected in June, with potential implementation in Q2 FY26. Calculations suggest a duty of ~40%.
*   **FY27 Doubling Target:** Reaffirmed that the FY23 top-line will be doubled by FY27, exceeding the original FY26 target.
*   **Capex FY26:** INR 300-350 crores, including INR 100 crores for modernization, INR 100 crores for new plant/capacity expansion (total INR 250 crores), and INR 150 crores for land acquisition.
*   **Demand Outlook:** Acknowledged a slight slowdown in inquiries over the last 15-20 days, primarily impacting the hiring and rental segment due to price increases. No significant slowdown observed from government capex, infra, or real estate sectors.
