## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported a significantly challenging Q2 FY2020, with revenue declining **8.6% quarter-on-quarter and 22% year-on-year**, reaching ₹268 Crores. This performance is attributed to a broad economic slowdown and extended monsoons, impacting both industrial and infrastructure sectors. Despite the revenue de-growth, ACE managed to maintain **EBITDA margins at 8.2%**, a slight improvement from Q1 FY2020 (8.1%) and a notable increase from Q2 FY2019 (7.2%). For the first half of FY2020, revenue degrew by 17.5%, with EBITDA margins at 8.1% (vs. 7.9% in H1 FY2019). Management has **revised the full-year FY2020 revenue guidance to a negative growth of 15% to 20%**, down from the previous flattish outlook. They aim to maintain EBITDA margins at around 8% for FY2020. The company anticipates a slow market for the next two to three months, with hopes for improvement in the subsequent quarters. Medium to long-term prospects remain positive, contingent on economic activity picking up.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACE's strategy continues to navigate a challenging market while preparing for a future recovery. Key initiatives include:

*   **Product Portfolio Management:** Acknowledging dismal performance in the agri segment due to market shrinkage and aggressive pricing by larger players.
*   **Export Focus:** Increased export revenue share to 7.5% in H1 FY2020 (from 5.5-6% in FY2019), targeting 9-10% for FY2020. A significant export order of ₹70 Crores from a neighboring country is expected to be executed in Q3/Q4 FY2020.
*   **Cost & Margin Management:** Efforts to manage margins despite de-growth, supported by a mix of steel price cooling and the full impact of prior price increases. Acknowledging that further price cuts are not logical in the current market.
*   **New Product Development:** Development of a new, high-margin crane product (likely the NX series) is progressing, with positive customer feedback and potential approval for Delhi Metro deployment, which could be a "jackpot." Subsequent, more cost-effective models are planned within 4-6 months.
*   **Working Capital Improvement:** Plans to bring inventory back to normal levels (65-70 days) by March 2020. Short-term borrowings have increased compared to last year, but management expects to normalize by March 2020.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Aiming to utilize 90-100% of the ₹34.25 Crores allocated for the share buyback, continuing the current fashion of purchasing.
*   **Defense Segment:** Sales in the defense segment are expected to remain stagnant in FY2020, similar to last year, despite ongoing project work and approvals for test trials.
*   **Contract Manufacturing:** Exploring contract manufacturing opportunities, with one proposal being actively worked on, potentially materializing in the next 3-4 months.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:**

*   **FY2020 Revenue Guidance:** Management has significantly revised their FY2020 revenue guidance downwards from "flattish" to a **negative growth of 15% to 20%**. This is a clear deviation from the previously revised outlook, indicating a more severe than anticipated market slowdown.
*   **FY2020 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** The target for FY2020 EBITDA margin remains around **8%**, consistent with the lower end of the previous guidance (8% to 8.5%).
*   **Q2 FY2020 Performance:** The reported revenue decline of 22% YoY and 8.6% QoQ is substantially worse than the "flattish" outlook for the year and highlights the immediate impact of the slowdown.
*   **Agri Segment Performance:** Management admits to "miserable" performance in the agri segment, attributing it to factors "beyond our control" and increased aggression from larger players. This contradicts a general sentiment of market stability from previous quarters.
*   **Steel Price Impact:** While steel prices have cooled, management notes that the benefit is partially offset by lower sales volumes. They state there have been no price reductions to customers, only a slight 1% hike in August, as they cannot afford to cut prices.
*   **Export Revenue:** The company is performing better than anticipated on exports, achieving 7.5% in H1 FY2020 and targeting 9-10% for FY2020, which aligns with previous positive commentary.
*   **Working Capital:** The transcript confirms an increase in short-term borrowings by approximately ₹70 Crores from the March quarter, primarily due to inventory buildup. Management aims to normalize this by March 2020.
*   **Management Tone:** The tone has shifted from acknowledging "short-term pain" to a more defensive stance, admitting to "miserable" performance in certain segments and stating they are currently focused on "how to save our skins." There is a lack of clear visibility on the exact cause of the drastic slowdown, with management admitting to being unable to pinpoint a single reason.
