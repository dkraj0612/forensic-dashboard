## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported a resilient Q1 FY2022 performance, overcoming disruptions from the second COVID-19 wave and regional lockdowns. The company achieved its highest ever Q1 EBITDA and Net Profit margins, attributed to cost efficiencies, a favorable product mix, and strategic price increases on select equipment. Operational revenues saw a significant year-on-year increase to ₹324 Crores. Export sales also demonstrated strong growth, contributing 9% of overall revenues. Management expressed confidence in strong macroeconomic factors driving future demand, while acknowledging pandemic-related disruptions as an ongoing concern. ACE successfully upgraded its crane and construction equipment models to BSIV CEV emission norms, enhancing fuel efficiency and environmental friendliness, and opening potential for additional international markets. The company is targeting 15%-20% revenue growth for FY2022, an upgrade from previous guidance.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Resilience Amidst COVID-19:** Achieved highest ever Q1 EBITDA and PAT margins by leveraging cost efficiencies, higher operating leverage, a better product mix, and price increases, despite initial Q1 impacts from lockdowns.
*   **Product Upgradation:** Successfully upgraded Crane and Construction Equipment models to BSIV CEV emission norms, improving fuel efficiency and environmental friendliness.
*   **Export Growth:** Export sales grew 47% QoQ and 260% YoY, contributing 9% of overall revenues, driven by new products and a significant order from Bangladesh.
*   **Industry Tailwinds:** Positioned to benefit from government initiatives, revival in industrial capex, and growth in real estate, infrastructure, railways, and logistics.
*   **Diversified Product Portfolio:** Strong market leadership in Pick & Carry Cranes (63% share) and domestic Tower Cranes (>60% share), alongside offerings in Crawler Cranes, Truck Mounted Cranes, Lorry Loaders, Backhoe Loaders, Rollers, Graders, Forklifts, Tractors & Harvesters.
*   **Manufacturing Capacity:** State-of-the-art facilities with substantial annual capacities.
*   **Customer-Centric Approach:** Focus on customer relationships, market intelligence, product robustness, value, service, and customization.
*   **R&D and Training:** In-house R&D for product development and a dedicated training center.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Low leverage, reflected by an AA- (Stable)/A1+ credit rating from ICRA.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **Q1 FY2022 Performance Commentary:** Management accurately described Q1 FY2022 as being impacted by COVID-19 lockdowns, but highlighted a strong rebound in demand as restrictions eased. The claim of achieving the "highest ever EBITDA and PAT margins" for Q1 was confirmed by the presented figures and management's clarification that this was in both absolute and percentage terms for the Q1 period.
*   **COVID-19 Impact Quantification:** Management quantified the COVID-19 impact on the topline for the first two months of the fiscal, stating a loss of approximately 30-40% of revenue in April and half of the revenue in May. This led to an estimated loss of ₹80-100 Crores in topline for the quarter.
*   **Price Increases:** Management confirmed multiple price increases implemented over the last 6-8 months (October, January, April, and end of June) to offset rising input costs, particularly steel. They noted customer resistance but eventual acceptance.
*   **BSIV Upgrade:** Management confirmed the successful upgrade of cranes and construction equipment to BSIV CEV norms.
*   **Employee Vaccination:** Management reported achieving 100% vaccination for employees and dealer personnel.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **FY2022 Revenue Guidance:** Upgraded guidance for FY2022 revenue growth to 15%-20% (from 10%-15%), citing strong macroeconomic factors but acknowledging pandemic disruptions and monsoon impacts. Management indicated this could be a revenue guidance.
*   **Segment-Specific Growth Projections (FY2022):**
    *   Crane Segment: 15% growth.
    *   Other Segments (Construction Equipment, Material Handling, Agri): 25% growth.
*   **EBITDA Margins (FY2022):** Expectation to maintain sustained EBITDA margins broadly in the existing range, potentially between 11% and 12%, dynamically managing pressures from steel prices and inflation.
*   **Construction Equipment Segment Profitability:** Management projects the Construction Equipment segment, currently with lower margins, to reach 11-12% EBITDA margins within approximately two quarters (Q2 or latest by Q3 FY2023) as capacity utilization increases and operating leverage kicks in.
*   **Agri Business:** Contemplating subsidiarization of the Agri business to provide more focus and capture its growth potential.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Actively seeking inorganic growth opportunities, with a focus on opportunities that will benefit the bottomline, rather than primarily topline. Management expects an acquisition to fructify within the current year.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** For Q1 FY2022, capacity utilization was reported as: Cranes (45-50%), Construction Equipment (30%), Material Handling (70-75%), and Agriculture (35-40%).
*   **Debt Reduction/Leverage:** Management reiterated a debt-averse approach and indicated that funds raised via QIP would not be used for debt, preferring a clean balance sheet. Current short-term borrowings were ₹124 Crores.
*   **Employee Costs:** Expected to stabilize between 4.5%-5% of revenue for the company overall, and 3-4% for the crane business, with no significant increases anticipated due to existing key positions being filled.
*   **Automation/Telematics:** Management views GPS/telematics as low-margin services, integrated into the sale of equipment, and does not expect substantial margin gains from these.
*   **QIP/Equity Raise:** Seeking approval for an equity raise of up to ₹175 Crores for inorganic growth opportunities and general corporate purposes, to be exercised opportunistically over the next year.
