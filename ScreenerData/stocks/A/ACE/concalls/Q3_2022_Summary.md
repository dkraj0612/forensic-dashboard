## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) delivered its best-ever Q3 revenue in FY2022, reaching ₹437 Crores, representing a 9% year-on-year and 21% quarter-on-quarter growth. Despite unabated inflationary pressures, a lackluster festive season, and the Omicron scare, the company maintained an EBITDA margin of 10.20% and a PAT margin of 6.3%. For the nine months ended FY2022, operational revenue surged by 45% YoY to ₹1,119 Crores, with EBITDA growing 48% and PAT 69% YoY.

ACE demonstrated strong market resilience, growing in all four segments (Cranes, Construction Equipment, Material Handling, Agri), even as the broader construction equipment industry de-grew by 23% in the quarter. Management attributed this outperformance to a focus on product superiority, competitive pricing, and strong customer support. The company secured new defense orders for special mobile cranes and truck-mounted multi-function cranes, leveraging the "Atmanirbhar Bharat" initiative. The Union Budget FY2023's focus on infrastructure and capital expenditure is expected to further strengthen demand. Management raised its FY2022 revenue growth guidance to "at least 23-24%" and remains confident in achieving its long-term targets, including doubling business to ₹2,500 Crores by FY2024.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Share Gains:** Maintained dominant leadership in Cranes (62-63% market share), grew in Construction Equipment and Agri despite industry de-growth. Acknowledged smaller base in CE and Agri allowed for growth despite overall market slowness. Aiming for 65-66% in cranes and 28-30% in material handling in next 1-2 years.
*   **Operational Resilience & Cost Management:** Dynamically managed business to deliver consistent margins (10.20% EBITDA) amidst unprecedented commodity inflation (steel ~60% of input cost, crude, rubber). Implemented multiple price hikes (~20-26% in aggregate over past year) to partially offset rising input costs and BS4 transition. No further price hikes planned for Q4 unless inflation persists.
*   **Strategic Inorganic Growth:** Actively working on "two smaller options" for market consolidation, with a basic understanding in place, aiming for an announcement within Q3 FY2022.
*   **Diversified Growth Vectors:**
    *   **Defence Sector Expansion:** Secured new orders for special mobile cranes for DRDO and special truck-mounted multi-function cranes for an electricity department, aligning with "Atmanirbhar Bharat."
    *   **Product Innovation:** Multi-activity cranes (NX360, NXT models) picked up well, inducted into leading manufacturing companies and Metro projects (Delhi, Mumbai, Kanpur, Patna, Bangalore).
    *   **Export Market Challenges:** Export initiative taken up well, but severely affected by container freight costs doubling or tripling, causing delays in shipments and fresh order negotiations.
*   **Balance Sheet & Working Capital:** Improved working capital days sequentially from 76 (Q2) to 62 (Q3), but noted an increase YoY (from 44 days in Q3 FY2021) due to higher inventory (₹317 Cr vs ₹220 Cr YoY). Expects inventory to rationalize by March end, bringing WC days to ~45. Cash on books stood at ₹185 Crores.
*   **Union Budget Impact:** Capital expenditure outlay stepped up by 35.4% to ₹7.5 lakh Crores, with effective capital expenditure at 4.1% of GDP for FY23. This focus on infrastructure and PM Gati Shakti is expected to significantly strengthen demand in the medium to long term.
*   **Capacity Utilization & Operating Leverage:** Current utilization levels (Cranes 50-60%, CE 35%, Material Handling 80%, Agri 35%) offer ample room for growth without significant new capex, with operating leverage expected to boost margins across segments as utilization improves. Material Handling capacity expansion is being planned.
*   **Rental Segment Dynamics:** Industry slowdown attributed to the rental segment's difficulty in adjusting to significantly increased machine costs (due to input prices and BS4 transition) vis-à-vis rental rates. Demand at the end-user level remains robust.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **FY2022 Revenue Guidance:** **Upgraded.** Management had reiterated 15%-20% growth for FY2022 in Q2, with potential upside. In Q3, they explicitly **raised this guidance to "at least 23-24%."** This shows confidence in stronger-than-expected performance in the final quarter.
*   **FY2022 EBITDA Margins:** **Consistent but challenging.** Q2 guidance was 10.5%-11% for FY2022. Q3 reported 10.20% EBITDA margin. Management acknowledged "unabated and unprecedented inflationary pressures" and expects "some pressure on margin profile in the near term" but aims to manage a "healthy range," indicating that achieving the higher end of the 10.5-11% might be difficult. The narrative highlights the continued struggle to fully pass on 1.5-2% of input cost increases.
*   **Segment-Specific Growth Projections (FY2022):**
    *   **Crane Segment:** Q2 projected 15%-20% growth for the full year. Q3 saw 4.5-5% YoY and 11.5-12% QoQ growth, which management described as "not blockbuster" but still growth. The narrative is consistent with cautious optimism given H2 FY2021's strong base.
    *   **Construction Equipment Segment:** Q2 projected 25%-30% growth for the year and 40%-50% in H2. Q3 showed 22% YoY growth, despite the overall industry de-growing by 23%. While slightly below the aggressive 25% target mentioned, the outperformance relative to the industry is highlighted as a positive, indicating progress towards the goal despite market headwinds.
    *   **Agri Business:** Q2 noted a 6% YoY deterioration. In Q3, Agri registered ₹77 Crores in revenue, "maintaining margins," despite the overall agri segment in the country de-growing. This shows improved resilience or market share gains given its small base.
*   **Construction Equipment Segment Profitability:** Q2 aimed for margin stabilization. Q3 saw this segment clock 5.45% EBITDA, still lower than other segments. Management acknowledged scope for improvement and targets 10-12% in the "next year" (FY2023) with operating leverage.
*   **Agri Business Subsidiarization:** No direct update on the subsidiarization process mentioned in Q2.
*   **Inorganic Growth Timeline:** **Consistent with Q2's revised timeline.** Q2 mentioned potential delay but hoped to finish "in this quarter (Q3 FY2022)." Q3 reiterated they are "working on two smaller options" with "basic understanding in place" and "hopefully within this quarter, we should be able to inform."
*   **Capacity Utilization:** **Slightly higher across the board.** Q2 reported Cranes (45-50%), Construction Equipment (30%). Q3 reported Cranes (50-60%), Construction Equipment (35%), Material Handling (80%), Agri (35%). Material Handling is now approaching full capacity, triggering expansion plans.
*   **Debt Reduction/Leverage:** Q2 aimed for near-zero or ₹15-20 Crores by year-end. Q3 did not provide an updated debt figure but reported ₹185 Crores cash on book, suggesting financial health to achieve debt reduction goals.
*   **Price Increases:** **Consistent.** Q2 noted multiple hikes and a planned 5-6% for Q3. Q3 confirmed multiple price hikes (totaling 20-26% over the last year) covering November, January, March, June, and November.
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** **Improved sequentially but worsened YoY.** Q2 expected reduction towards 45 days. Q3 reported an improvement from 76 days (Q2) to 62 days (Q3), but noted Q3 FY2021 was 44 days. Inventory levels were higher YoY (₹317 Cr vs ₹220 Cr). Management is confident of rationalizing inventory and reaching ~45 days by March end (Q4 FY2022), consistent with the Q2 objective.
*   **Defence Orders Contribution:** Q2 mentioned initial batches in Q4 FY2022, but 80-90% revenue to FY2023. Q3 mentioned new orders received for special mobile cranes and truck-mounted multi-function cranes, aligning with the earlier narrative.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **FY2022 Revenue Guidance:** Raised to "at least 23-24%" (from previous 15%-20%).
*   **Q4 FY2022 Outlook:** Expects Q4 to be "similar or better than our last year numbers" (Q4 FY2021 was ₹457 Crores) and "definitely better than our Q3 performance" (Q3 FY2022 was ₹437 Crores).
*   **FY2023 Sales Growth:** Management suggests "15%, 20% should be easy," but could "definitely be much faster than that," potentially "around 25%-30%." Final guidance expected by mid-end March or early April.
*   **Agri Business Margins:** Expects to bring agri division margins above 10% into 12-13% levels in the "next year" (FY2023), driven by increasing volumes and operating leverage.
*   **Construction Equipment Segment Long-Term Target:** Reaffirmed target of ₹500 Crores sales by FY2024 end (with +/- 10% margin), despite Q3's hiccup.
*   **Defence Orders Contribution:** Expects to contribute "anywhere between 100 to 150 crores in defense if not more" in FY2023. Further bigger inquiries expected to mature in H1 FY2023, with some in H2 FY2023.
*   **Price Hikes:** No intention for another round of price increases in Q4 FY2022 unless inflation continues "unabated."
*   **Cash on Book:** ₹185 Crores at the end of Q3 FY2022.
*   **Capacity Utilization & Expansion:** Material Handling capacity is at ~80% and planning for quick expansion to cater to next year's demand.
*   **Overall Margins with Utilization:** With increased utilization across segments (e.g., CE/Agri reaching 50-55%), overall EBITDA margins could reach "around 11.5%, 12%."
*   **Long-Term Revenue & Margin Target:** On track to double business to ₹2500 Crores by FY2024 (not FY2025 as an investor suggested), with targeted margins of 13-14% (or possibly 14-15%).
*   **Capex:** A world-class paint shop is under planning and execution; some part in Q4, but most will go into the next year (FY2023). Significant capex for capacity addition is anticipated only in FY2024, once on track to the ₹2500 Crore revenue target.
*   **Market Share Targets:** Aims for 28-30% market share in Material Handling and 65-66% in Mobile Cranes within the next 1-2 years.
