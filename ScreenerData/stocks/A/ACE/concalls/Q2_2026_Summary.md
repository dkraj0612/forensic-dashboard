## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) presented its Q2 and H1 FY2026 results, showcasing resilience amidst challenging market conditions. While H1 FY2026 total income saw a slight decline of 4.9% YoY to INR 2,823 Mn (consolidated), largely attributed to the transition to CEV-5 emission norms and prolonged monsoons, the company demonstrated significant margin expansion. Consolidated EBITDA grew by 13.6% YoY to INR 1,383 Mn for Q2 FY2026 and by 11.945% to INR 2,823 Mn for H1 FY2026, with EBITDA margins expanding by 180 basis points to 19.12% on a consolidated H1 basis. This margin improvement was driven by calibrated pricing, favorable product mix, cost efficiencies, and softening commodity prices. Management indicated that the most challenging phase related to emission norms and monsoons appears to be behind them, with an expectation of recovery driven by government infrastructure spending, easing interest rates, and improved liquidity. The proposed anti-dumping duty on Chinese cranes is highlighted as a significant structural positive, expected to benefit ACE's heavy crane segment.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **CEV-5 (BS-V) Transition and Market Normalization:** The company experienced a dip in H1 FY2026 total income due to the CEV Stage V emission norms and extended monsoons, which also led to price adjustments. Management believes the most challenging period is over, and Q2 results show stabilization with early signs of recovery.
*   **Government Infrastructure Push:** The presentation reiterates the significant government focus on infrastructure development, citing substantial capital spending budgets for FY2026 across roads, railways, urban infrastructure, and housing. Initiatives like PM Gati Shakti and PLI schemes are expected to drive demand.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on Chinese Imports:** Management views the proposed ADD on Chinese crane imports as a significant long-term strategic advantage, promoting fair trade and bolstering the domestic manufacturing ecosystem. This is expected to strengthen ACE's leadership in the Heavy Crane segment. Implementation is anticipated by December.
*   **Kato Works JV:** The JV progress is not explicitly detailed in this specific transcript, but previous information indicated operations were expected in Q3 FY2026.
*   **Defense Sector Order:** ACE has received a significant order for 1,121 Rough Terrain Forklifts (Telehandlers) from the Ministry of Defence, valued at ₹420 Crores. Partial execution is expected in the current fiscal year, contributing to "Make in India" and "AatmaNirbhar Bharat" initiatives.
*   **Backhoe Loader Segment Focus:** While specific growth numbers for the backhoe loader segment are not provided in this transcript, the company's strategy to strengthen its position is implied by its overall diversified product portfolio.
*   **Export Growth & Global Presence:** ACE maintains a global presence in over 37 countries and continues to offer a wide range of construction equipment.
*   **Capital Allocation:** The company is focused on growth and investment, with emphasis on manufacturing capabilities, R&D, and customer service. Land acquisition and modernization capex are ongoing.
