## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported a strong Q4 FY23, achieving its highest-ever quarterly sales and profits. For the full fiscal year FY23, operational revenue reached ₹2,150 Crores, marking a significant 33% year-on-year (YoY) growth, exceeding market growth. EBITDA for FY23 expanded to 11.4% (from 10% in FY22), growing 51.5% to ₹247 Crores. PBT and PAT also saw robust growth of 58.4% to ₹220 Crores and 52.2% to ₹161 Crores, respectively. Q4 FY23 standalone operational revenue stood at ₹613 Crores, up 20% YoY and 10.4% sequentially, with EBITDA margins expanding to 13.2% (PBT 12%, PAT 8.5%). This strong performance was attributed to a better product mix, improved price realizations, efficient cost control, and softening commodity prices.

All segments contributed to growth: Cranes revenue grew 38.4% to ₹1,525 Crores, maintaining market leadership with margins expanding to 12.77%. The Construction Equipment (CE) segment was a standout, growing 42% to approximately ₹250 Crores, with full-year margins at 9.5% and Q4 margins reaching 13.9%. Material Handling grew 11.2% to ₹170 Crores with stable margins, while the Agri division saw 7% growth but faced margin pressure. Export sales surged 90% YoY, now contributing 6.7% of total revenue, with a target of 9-10% in FY24. Management expressed optimism for India's economic outlook, driven by infrastructure and manufacturing, and projects continued strong demand. ACE remains debt-free with ample liquidity, with a dividend of 50% (₹1 per share) recommended for FY23. The company is actively pursuing capacity expansion (₹80-90 Crores CapEx) to support future revenue targets and is focusing on product innovation, including electric cranes and higher tonnage models.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Incurring ₹80-90 Crores capital expenditure to enhance capacity, particularly for higher tonnage cranes. This expansion is expected to be operational from Q2 FY24 and aims to propel turnover to ₹3,800-4,000 Crores.
*   **Product Innovation & Portfolio Expansion:** Focused on commercializing India's first fully electric crane (expected Q2 FY24). Multi-activity NX series cranes showing good traction, with expectations to grow from 3-4% to 10-20% of the pick-and-carry portfolio in 2-3 years, driven by better margins. Expanding into higher tonnage crawler and truck cranes to compete with imports.
*   **International Expansion:** Export sales contribution increased to 6.7% in FY23 (from 4.7% in FY22), targeting 9-10% for FY24 and a medium-term goal of 15-20%. Focus on product strengths like pick-and-carry cranes, backhoe loaders, and higher horsepower tractors.
*   **Defense Sector Focus:** Defense revenue contributed 2.5-3% in FY23, with an internal target to increase this to 8-10% going forward, citing typical and tailor-made products with reasonable margins.
*   **Debt-Free Status & Liquidity:** Maintains a debt-free balance sheet with ₹135 Crores from QIP and generated profits (totaling ~₹400 Crores) held in liquid investments, ready for strategic acquisitions.
*   **Competitive Strategy:** For larger cranes, plans to compete with Chinese imports by offering competitive pricing, secured credit terms (via bank tie-ups for bill discounting), and leveraging a 7-8% logistics advantage plus a 7.5% customs duty. Engaging MHI and DGFT regarding potential dumping practices.
*   **M&A Activities:** A specific backward integration target (NCLT company) was lost to existing promoters due to MSME rules. A forward integration target is on hold due to high valuation expectations. Exploring other related opportunities.
*   **Working Capital & Accounting:** Implemented new provisioning for impairment losses of financial assets (expected credit loss) for overdues beyond 6 months, driven by new auditors (KPMG) and revised accounting standards, though most are deemed recoverable.
