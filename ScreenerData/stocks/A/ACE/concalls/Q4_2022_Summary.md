## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) concluded FY2022 with its highest-ever annual sales, crossing the ₹1,600 Crores turnover mark, marking a robust 33% year-on-year growth. This significantly outpaced the broader market, demonstrating strong market share gains across segments. For Q4 FY2022, operational revenue grew 11.8% YoY to ₹512 Crores, also a record high for a quarter. Despite unprecedented inflationary pressures from global events and commodity price hikes, ACE maintained its EBITDA margin at 10.3% for the full year and 10.4% for Q4, showcasing effective balancing of growth and profitability.

Profit Before Tax (PBT) increased by 27.5% to ₹138 Crores, and Profit After Tax (PAT) by 32% to ₹106 Crores for FY2022. The company achieved debt-free status as of March 31, 2022, leveraging its QIP proceeds and strong operational cash flows. While the operating environment remains challenging in the near term, management anticipates moderation in inflationary pressures in H2 FY2023 due to government actions, which should aid margin expansion. ACE remains focused on its strategic growth agenda, product portfolio expansion (including larger cranes for infrastructure projects and defense orders), and market consolidation efforts.

**Segmental Performance (FY2022):**

*   **Cranes:** Revenue growth of 40%, increasing units from 4,558 (FY21) to 5,328 (FY22). Margins sustained at ~12%. Market share in pick & carry cranes is ~65%, tower cranes >65%.
*   **Construction Equipment (CE):** Revenue growth of 32% to ₹172 Crores. Margins sustained at 5%-6%.
*   **Material Handling:** Revenue growth of 48% to ₹152 Crores. Margins at 12%.
*   **Agri Division:** Revenue of ₹198 Crores (flattish YoY). Margins at 6.5%. Tractor industry subdued in H2 FY22.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Leadership & Share Gains:** Maintained dominant market share in Cranes (65% in pick & carry and tower cranes), despite industry headwinds, and recorded strong growth in Construction Equipment and Material Handling.
*   **Operational Resilience & Cost Management:** Successfully sustained EBITDA margins at ~10.3% amidst unprecedented commodity inflation by driving cost savings and implementing calibrated pricing actions, partially offsetting raw material cost increases. Management expects margin expansion in H2 FY23 with cooling commodity prices.
*   **Strategic Inorganic Growth:** Actively pursuing two smaller acquisitions aimed at consolidating the lower end of the market to enhance pricing power in regional markets. Expects to announce these within 15-30 days. Also, eyeing a potentially larger acquisition (product or backward integration) in the next 2-4 months.
*   **Diversified Growth Vectors:**
    *   **Defence Sector Expansion:** Started executing a pilot order for 4x4 multipurpose tractors with special attachments for the Indian Army. Has an order book of ₹110-120 Crores for FY2023 execution, with a pipeline of additional projects.
    *   **Product Portfolio Expansion:** Investing ₹30-35 Crores in FY2023 capex to expand facilities for manufacturing larger cranes (100-ton and 160-ton crawler cranes, 80-ton truck cranes) to cater to growing infrastructure demand (e.g., bullet train, metro projects), reducing reliance on imports.
    *   **Agri Business Revival:** Despite the tractor industry being subdued, the company has strengthened teams and financial tie-ups, expecting significant growth in FY2023, buoyed by good crop prices and monsoon predictions.
*   **Balance Sheet & Working Capital:** Achieved debt-free status as of March 31, 2022. The QIP proceeds (₹130 Crores) and operating profits are strategically parked in short and long-term investments (~₹200 Crores total) to control working capital, fund future capex, and acquisitions. Acknowledged carrying excess inventory (₹50-80 Crores) due to supply chain issues but is working to normalize it. Actively engaging with NBFCs to improve debtor payment timelines.
*   **Capacity Utilization & Capex:** Cranes utilized at 70%-75%, Material Handling at 75%, Construction Equipment and Agri at 30%-35%. New capex of ~₹50 Crores for FY2023 (including ongoing paint shop and new plant for larger cranes) will increase capacity and add capabilities, targeting ₹300-400 Crores revenue potential from this new capacity, with an expected ₹100 Crores addition in FY2023.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Noted a slight shift towards end-users buying directly versus rental companies, as rental rates have not fully absorbed the increased machine costs due to BS-IV transition and commodity price hikes. Exports grew 18% to ₹76 Crores in FY2022, with focus on African and Middle Eastern markets.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **FY2022 Revenue Guidance:** **Exceeded.** Q3 guidance was "at least 23-24%" growth for FY2022. ACE reported 33% growth in FY2022, significantly outperforming its own upgraded guidance.
*   **FY2022 EBITDA Margins:** **Achieved within range.** Q3 noted "some pressure" but aimed for a "healthy range" (around 10.20%). ACE reported 10.3% for FY2022 and 10.4% for Q4, consistent with maintaining a healthy margin despite challenges.
*   **Q4 FY2022 Outlook:** **Exceeded.** Q3 expected Q4 to be "similar or better than our last year numbers" (Q4 FY2021: ₹457 Crores) and "definitely better than our Q3 performance" (Q3 FY2022: ₹437 Crores). Q4 FY2022 revenue of ₹512 Crores surpassed both Q4 FY2021 and Q3 FY2022, demonstrating strong execution.
*   **Inorganic Growth Timeline:** **Delayed.** Q3 aimed for an announcement within Q3 FY2022, or "within this quarter (Q3 FY2022)." The Q4 transcript states it "has trickled into this quarter" (Q1 FY2023) but expects to close and announce within "next 15-20 days, 30 days."
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** **Mixed results.** Q3 aimed to rationalize inventory and reach ~45 days by March end (Q4 FY2022). Q4 indicated carrying "a little extra inventory" (₹50-80 Crores excess) due to supply chain issues, implying the 45-day target for inventory wasn't fully met. However, debtors are "well in control," and efforts are ongoing to improve financier payment delays. Creditors were consciously managed lower to support vendors.
*   **Debt Reduction/Leverage:** **Achieved.** Q3 aimed for near-zero or ₹15-20 Crores debt by year-end. ACE reported becoming a "debt free Company as on 31st March."
*   **Defence Orders Contribution (FY2023):** **Consistent.** Q3 expected ₹100-150 Crores contribution in FY2023. Q4 reported an order book of ₹110-120 Crores for FY2023 execution and anticipates an additional ₹50-100 Crores of fresh business.
*   **Long-Term Revenue Target:** **Slightly softened timeline.** Q3 stated doubling business to ₹2,500 Crores by FY2024. Q4 reiterated targeting ₹2,400-₹2,500 Crores by "FY24 if not FY25" with "possibility that we can reach there in the next 2 years," implying a slight extension possibility.
*   **Overall Margins with Utilization:** **Consistent with positive outlook.** Q3 suggested 11.5%-12% EBITDA with increased utilization. Q4 discussed a potential 2%-3% margin increase from cooling commodity prices plus another 2%-3% (or more) from operating leverage, suggesting an overall potential for higher margins if conditions improve.
*   **Capex:** **Consistent.** Q3 mentioned paint shop partially in Q4 FY2022, most in FY2023. Q4 confirmed FY2022 capex was ~₹40 Crores (including paint shop and cabin manufacturing) and projected ~₹50 Crores for FY2023 for ongoing paint shop work and a new plant for bigger cranes.
*   **Agri Business Subsidiarization:** No update provided in Q4, consistent with Q3.
