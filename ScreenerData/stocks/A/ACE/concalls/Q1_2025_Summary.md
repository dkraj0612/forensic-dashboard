## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) delivered a resilient performance in Q1 FY25, achieving its best-ever first-quarter results despite the impact of general elections. The company reported standalone operational revenues of INR 734 crores, a 12.82% year-on-year growth. EBITDA increased by over 28% to INR 125.50 crores, with EBITDA margins expanding by 212 basis points to 17.11%. Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by 24.46% to INR 83.71 crores. Management attributes the margin expansion to operating leverage, a better product mix, improved price realizations, efficient cost controls, and favorable commodity prices. The company has merged its crane, construction equipment, and material handling segments for accounting and communication purposes due to their similar margins and fungible operations. Looking ahead, ACE reiterates its FY25 guidance of 15-20% consolidated revenue growth with sustained margins, expecting a potential revision by the end of Q2 FY25. The company also announced an in-principle agreement for a 50-50 joint venture with Japanese manufacturer Kato Works Limited to produce medium and large-sized cranes for the Indian market and for export.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Leadership:** Continues to leverage its dominant position in the Indian crane market, particularly in Pick & Carry Cranes.
*   **Segment Consolidation:** Merged Crane, Construction Equipment, and Material Handling segments due to fungible operations and similar financial profiles.
*   **Joint Venture with Kato Works:** In-principle agreement for a 50-50 JV to produce medium and large-sized cranes (truck cranes, crawler cranes, rough terrain cranes) for the Indian and export markets. This aims to establish a strong foundation in larger crane segments.
*   **Infrastructure Focus:** Benefits from government initiatives and capital expenditure on infrastructure, expecting demand to gain momentum post-monsoon.
*   **Product Development:** Introducing new models and technologies, including advanced NX series (multi-activity cranes) and progressing with EV cranes.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Ongoing capex to increase crane capacity to 15,000-18,000 units by Q3 FY25.
*   **Defense Sector:** Medium-to-long-term target of 5-10% revenue contribution from defense. This year's target is 3.5-4%, potentially higher if larger orders materialize.
*   **Export Growth:** Aims for 15-20% export contribution in the next 3-4 years. Currently around 8-8.5% on an increased base.
*   **Emission Norms (BS-V):** Anticipates pre-buying in Q3 FY25 due to the transition to BS-V norms for construction equipment, which will increase costs by 2-5% for 60% of the portfolio and 12-15% for 40% of the portfolio.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative

*   **FY25 Revenue Guidance (15-20% consolidated growth):**
    *   **Previous Stance (Q4 FY24):** "reiterated the 15-20% growth projection for FY25... anticipate exceeding this... acknowledge near-term headwinds from elections and monsoons impacting Q2 FY25."
    *   **Q1 FY25 Transcript Analysis:** Management "reiterate[d] our earlier guidance and expect a growth of 15% to 20% on a consolidated basis with sustained margins." They are hopeful of revising projections by end of Q2 FY25 based on the macroeconomic scenario.
    *   **Conclusion: Consistent.** The guidance remains the same. Management acknowledges headwinds but expresses confidence in achieving targets.

*   **EBITDA Margins (Sustain 16%+ and expand):**
    *   **Previous Stance (Q4 FY24):** "aims to maintain 16%+ levels and achieve further expansion... confident in sustaining and further expanding them."
    *   **Q1 FY25 Transcript Analysis:** Reported Q1 FY25 standalone EBITDA margin of 17.11%, an expansion of 212 bps YoY. Management is "expecting good sales in quarter 3 on account of prebuy... and we are looking forward to growing in this year with a 16% to 17% EBITDA margin, which looks really sustainable."
    *   **Conclusion: Exceeded/On Track.** Q1 margins are strong and management is confident in sustaining them in the 16-17% range. A slight sequential dip was explained by lower volumes due to elections and increased material/admin costs, which are considered temporary.

*   **New Product Rollouts (Electric Cranes, AWP, Telehandlers):**
    *   **Previous Stance (Q4 FY24):** "Electric Cranes are ready but regulatory approvals delayed... targeting sales from July onwards. AWPs are productionized, with deliveries expected to commence in June... Reach Stackers, and specialized export-focused Backhoe Loaders and Telehandlers are planned for July/August."
    *   **Q1 FY25 Transcript Analysis:** **EV Cranes:** "everything is ready. We have sold some units, but the government CMVR approvals are still awaited. We should be getting them any time... waiting for the CMVR go ahead." **AWP/Telehandlers:** Not explicitly mentioned, but focus is on NX series (multi-activity cranes) and the Kato JV.
    *   **Conclusion: Delayed but Progressing.** EV cranes are awaiting regulatory approval. Management is actively working on advanced products like NX series and the Kato JV.

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Previous Stance (Q4 FY24):** "annual crane capacity increased to 13,200 units and plans are underway to expand this beyond 18,000 units within FY25 (targeting Q3 FY25)..."
    *   **Q1 FY25 Transcript Analysis:** "in the last quarter, we were at about 65% utilization in the cranes sector. ... the capex which is also currently happening, by quarter 3, our cranes capacity will increase to anywhere between 15,000 to 18,000 cranes."
    *   **Conclusion: On Track.** Capacity expansion plans are proceeding as stated.

*   **Defense Sector Contribution:**
    *   **Previous Stance (Q4 FY24):** "Last year our contribution from defense team at 2.5% of revenue. And this year, we are hopeful that it might be in excess of 3.5% at least. It could be much more than that. It totally depends on a slightly bigger order we are expecting... within quarter 2 or quarter 3."
    *   **Q1 FY25 Transcript Analysis:** "medium- to long-term target is 5% to 10% revenue generation contribution from defense. So this year, hopefully, from 1.5%, we should be able to be somewhere between 3.5% to 4% in the current period."
    *   **Conclusion: Consistent.** Target for the current year is reiterated, dependent on order finalization.

*   **Joint Venture/Acquisitions:**
    *   **Previous Stance (Q4 FY24):** "advanced stages for a potential JV/partnership, with announcement delayed for strategic reasons." "exploring acquisitions in India and abroad for product enhancement and export market penetration."
    *   **Q1 FY25 Transcript Analysis:** "in-principle agreement has been reached with Kato Works Limited... to establish a 50-50 joint venture in India." Work on overseas acquisitions is progressing, with potential news in the next 1-2 quarters.
    *   **Conclusion: Progressing.** JV with Kato is finalized in principle. Acquisition exploration continues.
