## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) delivered its highest-ever Q2 performance in terms of revenue, EBITDA, PBT, and PAT, demonstrating resilience despite challenging market conditions and persistent inflation. Standalone operational revenue grew 36% year-on-year (YoY) to ₹491 Crores, while EBITDA surged 45% YoY to ₹52.56 Crores, expanding margins to 10.7% (vs. 10.1% YoY and 9.3% QoQ). PBT and PAT also saw strong growth of 56% and 55% respectively. For the first half of FY2023, revenues climbed 45% to ₹990 Crores, with EBITDA growing 43.9% to ₹98.3 Crores, maintaining a 9.9% margin.

Segmental performance remained robust, with Cranes leading the way with 48% YoY revenue growth and 12.81% margins, driven by volume, price increases, and a shift towards bigger models. Construction Equipment grew 36%. While Material Handling saw a 5% revenue dip QoQ/YoY due to order booking and production issues, management expects a strong recovery. The company successfully navigated commodity volatility, with mild steel prices correcting, though other commodities remain elevated. Management anticipates further margin expansion from Q3 FY22-23 due to cooling commodity prices and operating leverage.

Strategically, ACE is expanding its manufacturing footprint by setting up a new facility for bigger cranes (40+ tonnes), expected to commence production by March 2023, quadrupling capacity and unlocking space for other segments. The Ghana assembly plant project is on track, with advances expected in Q3 FY22-23, reinforcing export ambitions. The real estate sector's heightened activity is driving strong demand for tower cranes, with ACE developing new, higher-capacity models. Management has revised its full-year FY2023 revenue growth guidance upwards to "at least 25%+" for the overall topline, reflecting confidence in sustained demand and strategic initiatives.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion for Bigger Cranes:** Initiated setup of a new manufacturing facility for truck cranes, crawler cranes, and rough terrain cranes (above 40 tonnes). This plant, with an estimated capex of ₹30-35 Crores, will quadruple capacity (from 40-50 to 180-200 units annually) and generate ₹300-400 Crores in revenue (expandable to ₹500-600 Crores). Expected to commence production by March 2023.
*   **Unlocking Existing Capacity:** The new bigger crane plant will simultaneously free up 40-50% of capacity in existing plants for forklift and tower crane businesses, supporting growth in these segments.
*   **Defense Sector Engagement:** Continues smooth execution of orders for missile handling systems and specialized vehicles directly with the Ministry of Defense, despite some margin pressure from earlier quoted prices due to commodity inflation.
*   **International Expansion (Ghana Project):** The US$24.98 million Ghana assembly plant project is on track, with advances expected in November 2022 (Q3 FY23). Manufacturing facility setup is planned for completion within FY2024, enabling recurring revenue from CKD/SKD supplies from FY2025 onwards.
*   **Real Estate & Tower Crane Market:** Observing heightened activity in the real estate sector, especially vertical construction in Tier 2/3 cities. ACE has seen 45-50% growth in tower cranes in H1 FY23. Developing 20-25 tonne tower crane model for precast construction (expected H1 FY24) and exploring new self-erecting models (40-42m height) next year.
*   **Cost Management & Margin Recovery:** Despite persistent inflation, EBITDA margins expanded in Q2 due to cost efficiencies. Mild steel prices have corrected significantly, though long steel and alloys have seen lesser corrections. Management expects an additional 1-1.5% margin expansion from cooling commodities in Q3, coupled with operating leverage from increased volumes.
*   **Strategic Pricing:** Plans to implement a price increase across products from January 1, 2023, to offset continued inflationary pressures.
*   **Inorganic Growth Prospects:** Actively exploring a backward integration opportunity with a company currently in liquidation, with ACE planning to be an active bidder. Other larger M&A opportunities are stalled due to valuation disagreements.
*   **Improved Working Capital Management:** Targeted reduction of working capital days from current 56 days to 39-40 days by year-end, primarily by normalizing inventory levels which were consciously built up for anticipated festive season sales.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **FY2023 Topline Growth (15-20% overall):** **Revised Upwards.** Management explicitly revised guidance to "at least 25% increase in our topline for the current year."
*   **FY2023 Segment-Specific Growth Projections:**
    *   **Cranes (15%):** **Revised Upwards.** New guidance is "25% plus in crane segment."
    *   **Construction Equipment (30-35%):** **Consistent.** Reaffirmed target.
    *   **Material Handling (20-25%):** **Revised Downwards.** New guidance is "around 15%." (Q2 saw a 5% degrowth).
    *   **Agri (15-20%):** **Revised Downwards.** New guidance is "around 15%." (Q2 saw 6% growth).
*   **FY2023 EBITDA Margins (Expansion in H2 FY23, double-digit, 1.5-2%+ expansion):** **Consistent.** Q2 EBITDA margin hit 10.7%, up from 9.3% in Q1. Management expects "1% to 1.5% for the margin expansion is possible" in Q3/Q4 from commodity cooling, slightly lower than the previously stated "1.5-2% (or more)".
*   **Commodity Price Outlook (Steel 10-15% correction):** **Partially Achieved.** Management stated "mild steel... has seen some meaningful corrections", but "long steel has not corrected the way it increased. It has only corrected half of what it had increased" and "alloy steel and castings have reduced lesser". The narrative is more nuanced than previously.
*   **Profitability Impact from Steel Cooling (2-2.5% increase):** **Slightly Lowered.** Management now expects "1% to 1.5%" margin expansion from cooling commodities, compared to "1.5-2% (or more)" previously.
*   **M&A Timeline (Larger acquisition within 2-4 months or "at least one in FY23"):** **Delayed/Challenged.** Management stated they are "stuck on valuation with respect to one of the opportunities" and "another one we gave up". A backward integration opportunity is now with a company in liquidation, which will "take its own time."
*   **Defense Orders Contribution (₹110-120 Crores for FY23 execution + ₹50-100 Crores fresh):** **Consistent.** Management stated they have "started executing that order" and "do not see any commercial problems," though margins are "a little squeezed."
*   **Seasonality (Q1/Q2 revenues below ₹500 Crores):** **Mixed.** Q1 FY23 revenue was ₹501 Crores (exceeded). Q2 FY23 revenue was ₹491 Crores (met the "below ₹500 Cr" expectation). Narrative around Q2 being "pretty subdued because of monsoons" is consistent.
*   **Long-Term Revenue Target (₹2,500 Crores by FY24):** **Consistent.** While not explicitly re-stated, the increased FY23 growth guidance ("at least 25%+") and robust outlook for backhoe loaders (700+ units for FY23, 250-300 units/quarter from next year) suggests confidence in achieving ambitious long-term targets.
*   **CE (Backhoe) FY24 Target (₹500 Crores):** **Consistent (Lowered).** Management reiterated that the overall backhoe loader market has been subdued. While projecting 700+ units for FY23 and 250-300 units/quarter for FY24, this volume, at current prices, would be well below the ₹500 Crores target, aligning with previous acknowledgements that the target might not be met.
*   **Ghana Project Revenue (Initial: ₹200 Crores FY23/24; Post: ₹150-400 Crores from FY25):** **Consistent.** Management expects "advances in the month of November itself and hopefully some activity will start on that in the next quarter" and "planned to finish it within FY2024."
*   **Exports (FY2023 > ₹100 Crores):** **Consistent.** Management stated "Export was lacking in the first half... we should be able to cross Rs.100 Crores in revenue at least in exports as well."
*   **Auditor Appointment (KPMG):** **Achieved.** KPMG formally appointed and Q2 FY23 is their first reviewed quarter.
