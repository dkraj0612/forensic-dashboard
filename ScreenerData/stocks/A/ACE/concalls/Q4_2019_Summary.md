## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported results for Q4 FY2019, highlighting a challenging year marked by unexpected macroeconomic headwinds. Despite a 24% revenue growth to ₹1343 Crores for the full fiscal year FY2019, Q4 FY2019 saw an 18% year-on-year and quarter-on-quarter decline in revenue. Management attributed this sharp Q4 downturn to factors such as abnormal steel prices, input cost escalations, and a general "wait and watch" sentiment among customers following state elections. For FY2020, ACE projects a conservative revenue growth of 10%, targeting an EBITDA margin of 9% to 9.5% and a PAT margin of around 5%. The company also announced an approved share buyback scheme of up to ₹34.25 Crores. ACE is also focusing on increasing its export revenue share to 9%-10% in FY2020 and is confident in the long-term prospects of the company.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACE's strategy remains focused on leveraging its market leadership in cranes and expanding its product portfolio. Key drivers highlighted include:

*   **Infrastructure Development:** Continued government focus on infrastructure projects remains a core driver.
*   **Mechanization Growth:** Increasing adoption of mechanization in construction and manufacturing.
*   **Product Innovation:** Introduction of new multi-activity cranes with higher margins and plans for new truck cranes and larger crawler cranes.
*   **Export Growth:** Targeting 9%-10% revenue from exports in FY2020 by expanding into Africa, South America, and South East Asia.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Focus on reducing working capital cycles and maintaining inventory control, including a target of 3-5 day delivery for pick and carry cranes.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Existing capacities are sufficient to handle projected growth with no significant capex planned for the next 1-1.5 years.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:**

*   **Revenue Growth:** The company achieved 24% revenue growth for FY2019 (₹1343 Cr vs ₹1087 Cr), which is a significant increase compared to the previous year. However, the stated expectation of consistent growth was significantly impacted in Q4 FY2019, with an 18% YoY decline attributed to unforeseen economic and political factors.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** For FY2019, the EBITDA margin was 8%, a decrease from 9.2% in FY2018. The Q4 margin improved to 8.6% QoQ, indicating some recovery. The management acknowledges the challenging environment impacted margins.
*   **PAT Margin:** The PAT margin for FY2019 was 4.2%, down from 4.8% in FY2018.
*   **Export Revenue:** ACE successfully increased its export revenue share to 6% in FY2019, up from 4% in FY2018, aligning with its strategic focus.

The narrative consistently points to external factors (steel prices, election uncertainty) as the primary cause for the Q4 downturn, rather than internal operational failures. Management's tone remains cautiously optimistic about medium to long-term prospects, but acknowledges short-term turbulence.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **FY2020 Revenue Growth:** Conservative growth rate of 10%. Guidance may be revised upwards in Q2 if a strong political mandate is achieved.
*   **FY2020 EBITDA Margin:** Targeting 9% to 9.5%.
*   **FY2020 PAT Margin:** Targeting around 5%.
*   **Export Revenue Target (FY2020):** 9% to 10% of revenue.
*   **Share Buyback:** Approved scheme to buy back shares worth up to ₹34.25 Crores.
*   **Steel Price Pass-Through:** Policy to pass on steel price increases of over 3%-4% to customers in the following month.
*   **New Product Launch Impact:** Expectation that new multi-activity cranes will command higher margins and capture 15%-20% of the Indian crane market in 2-3 years. Introduction of new series truck cranes and larger crawler cranes (100-150 ton capacity) in FY2020.
*   **Tractor Segment Improvement:** Expectation of normalized Segment EBIT of ₹4-5 Crores with the potential to reach 7%-8% EBIT margin with increased sales volume.
*   **Capex:** Limited significant capex anticipated for the next 1-1.5 years, with FY2020 capex projected at ₹15-20 Crores (including ₹8 Crores maintenance capex).
*   **Working Capital Reduction:** Aim to further reduce working capital days and squeeze the cycle over the next two quarters.
*   **Dividend Policy:** Intention to distribute 25%-30% of free cash as dividends, with a slightly lower distribution in the current year to focus on becoming debt-free.
*   **Delivery Period Improvement:** Target of 3-5 day delivery for pick and carry cranes is in place and working.
*   **Agri-equipment Margins:** Expectation to stabilize at 7%-8% EBIT level with sales close to 5000 units, and potentially 10%-11% with operating leverage.
*   **Rental Business Revenue:** FY2019 revenue was ₹13 Crores, down from FY2018's ₹19-20 Crores, due to caution regarding payments in a slow construction market.
*   **Q1 FY2020 Outlook:** Hopeful to match or exceed Q4 FY2019 sales, with a potential range of +5% to -10% depending on election outcomes.
