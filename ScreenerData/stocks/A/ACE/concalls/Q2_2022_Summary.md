## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) delivered a strong Q2 FY2022 performance, achieving its highest ever Q2 revenue, EBITDA, and PAT margins, despite facing prolonged monsoons and significant inflationary pressures. Operational revenues surged by 35% year-on-year to ₹360 Crores, driven by robust growth across all segments except Agri. EBITDA grew by 42% to ₹37 Crores (10.2% margin), PBT by 57%, and PAT by 58% to ₹23 Crores (6.4% margin).

The company successfully completed a QIP of ₹135 Crores, attracting diverse marquee investors, and further expanded into the defence sector with breakthrough orders for specialized tractors and cranes from the Ministry of Defence. While management expressed confidence in the continued economic recovery and a strong second half, they acknowledged persistent challenges from elevated commodity prices (especially steel, which increased 8-10% in October), global supply chain disruptions, and customer resistance to necessary price increases. ACE reiterated its FY2022 revenue growth guidance of 15%-20% with potential upside, while aiming to maintain EBITDA margins between 10.5% and 11% amidst cost pressures.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Sustained Market Leadership:** Deteriorated dominant market leadership in the Crane segment (241 Crores revenue, 45% YoY growth), aiming for number one position in the harvester business.
*   **Operational Resilience & Cost Management:** Achieved highest-ever Q2 margins through sustained focus on cost efficiencies and a customer-centric approach, despite challenging operating environment and inflationary trends.
*   **Strategic Price Hikes:** Implemented multiple price increases (10% in Q3/Q4 FY2021, 10% in Q2 FY2022, 5-6% planned for Q3 FY2022) to partially offset drastic input cost increases, particularly steel.
*   **Inorganic Growth Focus:** Actively exploring acquisitions at both component and complete machinery levels, primarily focused on bottom-line improvement and market consolidation to gain pricing power.
*   **Diversified Growth Vectors:**
    *   **Defence Sector Entry:** Secured breakthrough orders for multipurpose 4x4 tractors and special metal boom cranes from the Ministry of Defence.
    *   **Product Innovation:** Already offers CNG/LPG forklifts, working on adopting these to cranes and backhoe loaders. First in India to indigenously design and manufacture lithium-ion technology-based electric forklifts.
    *   **Export Market Expansion:** Looking to expand to Central Asia, Eastern Europe, Russia (with secured payments), and South America.
*   **Balance Sheet Strength:** Strong cash flows and balance sheet post-QIP, with borrowings reduced to ₹57 Crores, aiming for near-zero by year-end. QIP funds allocated for long-term sustainable growth initiatives, market expansion, consolidation, and general corporate purposes.
*   **Long-Term Vision:** Targeting ₹3000-₹3500 Crores topline for ACE, on track to double business to ₹2500 Crores in three years. Construction Equipment segment identified as a key growth driver, potentially surpassing cranes.
*   **Operational Improvement:** Continuous process of improvement and upgradation, including plans for semi-robotics in weld/paint shops and a world-class new paint shop for better aesthetics.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **FY2022 Revenue Guidance:** Management reiterated its upgraded Q1 FY2022 guidance of **15%-20% revenue growth for FY2022**, consistent with the previous quarter, noting "possible upside." The internal target for the year, however, is significantly higher at **25%-30% volume growth**, combined with 8%-10% price growth, implying a potential 20%-25% total revenue growth.
*   **Segment-Specific Growth Projections (FY2022):**
    *   **Crane Segment:** Q1 projected 15% growth. Q2 FY2022 saw a robust 45% YoY growth. Management's current narrative suggests a "little slower" growth projection of **15%-20%** for cranes for the full year, attributing it to a strong pick-up in H2 of the previous year.
    *   **Other Segments (Construction Equipment, Material Handling, Agri):** Q1 projected 25% growth. In Q2 FY2022, Construction Equipment grew 30% YoY, Material Handling grew 42% YoY, but Agri deteriorated by 6% due to erratic monsoons. Management maintains a projection of **25%-30% growth for Construction Equipment** for the current year, with hopes of reaching **40%-50% levels in H2 FY2022**.
*   **EBITDA Margins (FY2022):** Q1 FY2022 guidance was to maintain sustained EBITDA margins broadly in the 11%-12% range. Q2 FY2022 saw a margin of 10.2%. The current guidance for FY2022 has been adjusted to **10.5%-11%**, reflecting ongoing challenges from high input costs (steel increased another 8-10% in October) and increased customer resistance to price hikes, making margin expansion difficult. This indicates a slightly more conservative outlook on margin expansion compared to Q1.
*   **Construction Equipment Segment Profitability:** Q1 FY2022 projected 11%-12% EBITDA margins by Q2 or Q3 FY2023. Q2 FY2022 transcript mentioned "stabilization of EBITDA margins" for the construction equipment segment, indicating progress towards the stated goal.
*   **Agri Business Subsidiarization:** Q1 FY2022 mentioned contemplating subsidiarization. Q2 states they are "in a process of doing it" to bring more focus and drive profitability/topline, but it has not been completed yet.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Q1 FY2022 expected an acquisition to fructify within the current year. Q2 states, "we are expecting that we may not be able to something within this quarter, at least one of the possibilities. So, let us see if you are able to finish it in this quarter." This indicates a potential delay or a more cautious tone regarding the timing of an acquisition.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Consistent. Q1 FY2022 reported Cranes (45-50%) and Construction Equipment (30%). Q2 FY2022 also stated they are "grossly underutilized" with Cranes at **45%-50%** and Construction Equipment at **30%**.
*   **Debt Reduction/Leverage:** Consistent. Q1 reiterated a debt-averse approach. Q2 stated borrowings are down to **₹57 Crores**, with a goal of being close to zero or ₹15-20 Crores by the year-end.
*   **Price Increases:** Consistent. Q1 confirmed multiple price increases. Q2 detailed these, noting ~10% in Q3/Q4 FY2021, ~10% in Q2 FY2022, and another 5%-6% being pushed in Q3 FY2022 to compensate for rising input costs.
*   **BSIV Upgrade:** Consistent. Q1 confirmed successful upgrade. Q2 noted "substantial price increase on an account of the BSIV" for Construction Equipment, indicating the market impact of the regulatory change.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **FY2022 Revenue Guidance:** Reiterated **15%-20% growth** for the full year, with a "possible upside." Management's internal target for volume growth is **25%-30%**, with total revenue growth potentially around **20%-25%** after accounting for price increases.
*   **FY2022 EBITDA Margins:** Expectation to maintain EBITDA margins between **10.5% and 11%** for the year. Challenging to expand due to high input costs and customer resistance, aiming for "flattish" margins year-on-year.
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** Expectation for inventory levels to normalize and working capital days to reduce (from 76 days back towards 45 days) as the second half of the year typically sees higher business volumes. Receivables, excluding export exposure, are expected to return to normalcy by **Q4 FY2022 or Q1 FY2023** as COVID-related payment delays subside.
*   **Inorganic Growth Timeline:** Expecting "at least one of the possibilities" (acquisition) to fructify within "this quarter" (Q3 FY2022), though acknowledging it "may not be able to something within this quarter."
*   **QIP Fund Utilization:** Funds to be used as per the utilization objective document: general corporate purposes, inorganic and organic growth, and potential debt reduction.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** No new capex is needed to reach the **₹2500 Crores** revenue target, as existing capacity is grossly underutilized.
*   **Construction Equipment Segment Growth:** Projecting **25%-30% growth** for the current year, with hopes of reaching **40%-50%** levels in H2 FY2022. The three-year target of **₹500 Crores** sales from Construction Equipment is on track (with a +/- 10% margin).
*   **Agri Business Subsidiarization:** "In a process of doing it" to bring more focus and drive profitability/topline. Not currently planning to sell, but open to ideas.
*   **Long-Term Revenue Target:** On track to double business to **₹2500 Crores in three years**.
*   **Defence Orders:** Initial batches of supply for the **₹75 Crores and ₹25 Crores** orders are expected in **Q4 FY2022**, but 80%-90% of the revenue contribution is likely to shift to the **next financial year**. More orders are expected, potentially coming into effect in **Q2 or Q3 FY2023**.
*   **Geographic Export Expansion:** Planning to expand exports to **Central Asia, Eastern Europe, and Russia** (with secured payments), followed by **South America**, before considering Mainland Europe or America.
*   **Operating Leverage:** For every additional **₹100 Crores** of revenue beyond a **₹1200-₹1300 Crores** turnover level, the company expects to add approximately **₹17-₹18 Crores to its EBITDA** (implying an incremental EBITDA margin of 17%-18%), assuming input costs stabilize.
*   **Chip Shortage Impact:** While most supplies are unaffected, there's an acute problem with chips for engines >130-150 HP (sourced from Ashok Leyland), expected to be resolved by **December**. This has been mitigated by overstocking engines.
