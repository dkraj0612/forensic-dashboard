## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported a subdued Q1 FY2026, with total income declining by 7.63% YoY to ₹703 crores. This was primarily attributed to the implementation of new CEV Stage V emission norms and enhanced safety certification requirements, leading to a 7-12% price increase across most product categories. Customer sentiment was further impacted by geopolitical uncertainties and pre-buying in the previous fiscal year, alongside the early onset of the monsoon. Despite these challenges, ACE demonstrated resilience by expanding its EBITDA margin by approximately 300 basis points to 20.28% and increasing EBITDA by 13.6% to ₹142.55 crores. PBT and PAT also saw significant growth of 13.66% and 15.67%, respectively, with expanded PBT and PAT margins. The company anticipates market normalization from Q2 FY2026 onwards. The defense sector order from the Indian armed forces (₹420 crores) is slated for execution starting in Q3 FY2026, with partial contribution expected within FY2026. While the Ghana project remains in limbo, ACE is actively pursuing inorganic growth opportunities and potential white-labeling arrangements. The Kato Works JV is expected to be finalized and operational by Q3 FY2026, targeting an initial revenue contribution in FY2026. Management expects demand and pricing to stabilize with the receding monsoon and the festive season.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **CEV-5 (BS-V) Transition Impact:** The primary driver for the Q1 FY2026 slowdown was the implementation of new emission norms (CEV Stage V) and safety certifications, leading to price increases of 7-12% and initial customer skepticism, particularly for engines below 50 horsepower.
*   **Defense Sector Order Execution:** Execution of the ₹420 crore defense order is set to commence in Q3 FY2026, with an estimated ₹50-70 crores revenue contribution in FY2026 and the majority in FY2027. Additional orders for Ashok Leyland Defence and BRO are in the pipeline.
*   **Export Growth:** Exports are projected to increase to 6-7% of total revenue in FY2026, up from 4% in FY2025. Combined with defense, this segment is expected to contribute around 10% in FY2026, with a medium-term target of 10-15%. Export margins are slightly higher than domestic margins.
*   **Kato Works JV:** The JV agreement is expected to be finalized within the current or early next quarter, with operations targeting commencement in Q3 FY2026. An initial revenue contribution of ₹100+ crores is anticipated in FY2026.
*   **Government Infrastructure Push:** Significant government focus on infrastructure projects, with substantial order releases planned, is expected to drive demand in the construction equipment and road machinery segments, potentially growing by 30-40% this year.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on Chinese Imports:** Management is optimistic about potential ADD on heavier cranes and Chinese construction equipment imports, which could significantly reduce competition and improve realizations.
*   **Capacity and Capex:** The company possesses a revenue capacity of over ₹5,000 crores. Current capex of over ₹100 crores in FY2026 is for modernization and upgrades. Further capacity expansion of ₹250-300 crores is planned for FY2027 and FY2028.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative

*   **FY26 Revenue Growth:** The company had previously guided for ~20%+ growth, aiming to double FY23 revenue by FY26. In the current call, management acknowledges a slowdown due to emission norms and pre-buying. They now defer giving specific revenue guidance for FY26, stating they will provide a more realistic picture by the end of Q2 FY26, after market normalization. They reiterate the confidence of doubling FY23 revenue by FY27 and tripling by FY29.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management achieved an EBITDA margin of 20.28% in Q1 FY2026, surpassing the previous year's Q1 margin and indicating successful cost efficiencies and price adjustments. They believe margins should stabilize around 16-17% overall, with export margins being slightly higher.
*   **CEV-5 (BS-V) Transition Impact:** Management accurately predicted the temporary demand sluggishness and price increases due to CEV-5 norms. They now report stabilization in customer acceptance and expect demand to normalize from Q2 FY2026 onwards.
*   **Kato Works JV Timeline:** The JV is still progressing, with expected finalization this quarter or early next, targeting Q3 FY2026 for operations, aligning with previous guidance.
*   **Defense Order Execution:** The execution of the ₹420 crore defense order is confirmed to start in Q3 FY2026, with partial realization in FY2026, as previously indicated.
*   **Ghana Project:** The status remains unchanged, with the project in limbo due to funding issues.
