## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) demonstrated a remarkable recovery in Q2 FY2021, achieving revenue parity with Q2 FY2020 despite a challenging start to the year. Management reported "pleasant surprise" with revenue at ₹268 Crores, similar to the prior year's ₹268 Crores. Profitability significantly improved, with EBITDA margins expanding to 9.7% (vs. 8.2% YoY) and PBT margins to 7.1% (vs. 5.8% YoY). EBITDA grew 18% to ₹26 Crores, and PBT increased to ₹19 Crores. Management has revived full-year revenue guidance, now expecting to be "in touching distance" of last year's sales (approximately ₹1,150 Crores), with a potential small deficit of 5-6%, and anticipates double-digit EBITDA margins. This turnaround is driven by a revival in demand, improved sales in construction equipment, material handling, and agri segments, and a proactive approach to overcoming supply chain constraints.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACE's strategy in Q2 FY2021 focused on capitalizing on a recovering market and improving operational efficiency:

*   **Demand Revival:** Significant improvement in demand seen since August, with October sales exceeding September levels.
*   **Segmental Growth:** Strong performance in construction equipment (YoY growth), material handling (up 30-31%), and agri (up 55%), offsetting a slightly laggard crane segment due to supply chain issues.
*   **Supply Chain Mitigation:** Proactive measures taken to overcome supply chain constraints, with management confident of resolution by mid-December.
*   **Cost Control & Efficiency:** Continued focus on liquidity preservation, cost control, and improved operational efficiency, leading to reduced employee costs and improved margins.
*   **Market Share Gains:** Expectation of increased market share in the pick and carry crane segment by 2-3%.
*   **New Product Development:** On track to launch a new machine in February-March, with sales impact expected from FY2022.
*   **Debt Reduction:** Aiming to reduce debt to ₹50-60 Crores by the end of FY2021.
*   **Export Resumption:** Exports are resuming, with a target of 7-8% revenue contribution in the current year.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **Q2 FY2021 Revenue:** Management achieved the "similar revenues on year-on-year basis" stated, reaching ₹268 Crores, a significant turnaround from the previously discussed severe degrowth.
*   **EBITDA & PBT Margins:** The reported EBITDA margins of 9.7% and PBT margins of 7.1% are well above Q2 FY2020 levels (8.2% and 5.8% respectively), aligning with the narrative of improved profitability.
*   **Full Year Revenue Guidance:** Management has "revived" their revenue guidance from a 30-35% degrowth (as initially feared) to being "in touching distance of last year sales," a substantial positive shift.
*   **Full Year EBITDA Margin:** The guidance for EBITDA margins has been raised to "double digit" from the previous 6-7% target.
*   **Supply Chain Issues:** While supply chain constraints were a significant headwind, preventing full capitalization of revenue potential in Q2, management has addressed this by finding workarounds and expects resolution, demonstrating adaptability.
*   **Crane Segment Performance:** Management acknowledges cranes were "a little laggard" due to supply chain issues but expects them to be back on track.
*   **Construction Equipment Margin:** Management acknowledged a temporary dip in construction equipment EBIT due to inventory write-offs for new model introductions but expressed confidence in reaching 7-10% EBITDA for the segment from Q3 onwards.
*   **Agri Segment:** The tractor and harvester segments showed strong growth (55% for agri), indicating a successful strategy in this area.
*   **Market Share in Cranes:** The narrative of gaining 2-3% market share in the pick and carry segment is consistent with previous expectations.
