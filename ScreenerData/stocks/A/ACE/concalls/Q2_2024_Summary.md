## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) delivered another strong performance in Q2 FY25, achieving their best-ever Q2 results in terms of revenue and profit, despite a challenging operating environment marked by extended monsoons and election-related sentiment shifts. The company sustained its growth momentum, with operational revenues growing 12.2% YoY to INR 754.34 crores, and EBITDA margins expanding by 268 basis points to 18.04% (INR 142 crores). This represents sequential growth as well, with EBITDA up 13.3% QoQ and margins improving by 150 bps. The first half of FY25 also saw record EBITDA, PBT, and PAT margins. ACE is strategically investing in capacity expansion, with total capacity set to reach over INR 5,100 crores by Q4 FY25, and is strengthening its presence in the defense sector with significant orders anticipated.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Diversified Portfolio & Segment Growth:** ACE continues to benefit from demand across its core segments: Cranes, Material Handling, and Construction Equipment, which contributed 92% of total revenue in Q2 FY25. The Agri segment contributed 8% of revenue.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** The company is on track to increase its revenue capacity to over INR 5,100 crores by Q4 FY25, with further potential to reach INR 6,500 crores on existing land. Additional land parcels have been acquired for future growth.
*   **Defense Sector Focus:** ACE secured significant orders, including a largest-ever single order of over INR 400 crores from the Ministry of Defense. This is expected to drive defense revenue contribution beyond 5% in FY26 and FY27.
*   **Product Innovation:** Development of Electric Cranes and Aerial Work Platforms (AWPs) is progressing, with commercial rollouts expected from Q4 FY24/Q1 FY25, pending regulatory notifications.
*   **JV with Kato Works:** A 50-50 joint venture is being finalized with Kato of Japan to manufacture bigger capacity cranes (crawler, truck, rough terrain) leveraging both companies' technologies. This JV aims to upgrade ACE machines with Kato technology for the Indian market and utilize Kato technology for international markets.
*   **Infrastructure & Government Support:** The government's continued focus on infrastructure spending (INR 11.11 lakh crores for FY25) and manufacturing growth bodes well for sustained demand.
*   **Emission Norms Compliance:** ACE is preparing for the transition to BS-V emission norms for engines from January 1, 2025, which will lead to a cost increase for some machines (11-15% for BS III to BS V, 5-6% for BS IV to BS V). The company is engaging in pre-buying strategies with customers.
*   **Export Market Expansion:** The company is actively working on upgrading machine aesthetics and technology to target developed markets in Europe and America in the next 2-3 years. The Kato JV will also contribute to export market entry.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative

*   **Q2 FY24 Stated Narrative:** "Another quarter of exceptional performance," "best-ever quarterly revenue and profit," sustained growth, improved profitability, balance sheet strength, progress with capacity expansion for higher-capacity cranes, and poised for future growth.
*   **Q2 FY25 Transcript Analysis:** Management reiterated this positive trajectory, stating, "yet another quarter of strong performance...wherein we have been able to increase our top line with expanded margin profile," achieving "best ever quarter 2 performance in terms of revenues and best ever margin profile and profits." Standalone operational revenues grew 12.2% YoY, and EBITDA margin reached a record 18.04%. The narrative strongly confirms and builds upon the previous quarter's success.
    *   **Conclusion: Exceeded.** Management has not only met but exceeded the "best ever" performance previously indicated, demonstrating consistent execution and further margin expansion.

*   **Segment Growth:**
    *   **Q2 FY24 Transcript:** Cranes (~30% YoY), CE (~100% YoY), Material Handling (26% YoY), Agri (33% YoY).
    *   **Q2 FY25 Transcript:** Cranes & forklifts were primary drivers of the 9% consolidated volume growth. Construction equipment segment faced headwinds from rains in H1, with a flatter performance expected for FY25. Agri segment faced a YoY dip due to a large export order in H1 FY24 and discontinuation of rotavators, but saw sequential QoQ growth of 42%.
    *   **Conclusion: On Track & Evolving.** While core segments continue to perform well, the CE segment experienced short-term headwinds. The Agri segment's YoY decline is explained by specific factors, with management confident in achieving full-year growth targets.

*   **Margin Improvement:**
    *   **Q2 FY24 Transcript:** Standalone EBITDA margin of 15.73%, an expansion of 75 bps sequentially. Drivers: operating leverage, product mix, cost control, commodity prices.
    *   **Q2 FY25 Transcript:** Standalone EBITDA margin of 18.04%, an expansion of 268 bps YoY and 150 bps QoQ. Drivers identified: product mix (higher tonnage cranes, multi-activity cranes), softening commodity prices (steel), and operational efficiencies.
    *   **Conclusion: On Track & Expanded Significantly.** Margins have expanded substantially beyond the previous quarter's levels, driven by the stated factors and improved operational leverage.

*   **Capacity Expansion & New Products:**
    *   **Q2 FY24 Transcript:** Brownfield capex nearing completion (Q3/Q4 FY24), targeting INR 4,000 crore revenue potential. Electric cranes and AWPs to start deliveries from Q4 FY24.
    *   **Q2 FY25 Transcript:** Capacity set to reach over INR 5,100 crores by Q4 FY25. New facilities partially operational in Q3 FY25. Electric cranes & AWPs expected to gain traction from late December/January, pending regulatory approvals.
    *   **Conclusion: On Track.** Capacity expansion is progressing as planned, and new product launches are aligned with previous timelines, subject to regulatory clearances.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines (from Q2 FY25 Transcript)

*   **FY25 Revenue Guidance:** Reiterate guidance of 15% plus growth overall. Potential to reach up to 20% growth. Management acknowledges a slower first half due to elections and monsoons but expects the second half (historically 55% of revenue) to compensate.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Total revenue capacity to reach over INR 5,100 crores by Q4 FY25. Further nominal capex can increase this by another INR 600 crores. Existing land can support capacity up to ~INR 6,500 crores, with additional land acquired for future growth.
*   **New Product Rollouts:**
    *   **Electric Cranes & AWPs:** Expected to gain traction from late December 2024 or January 2025, pending CMVR regulations notification.
    *   **Kato JV:** Definitive agreements being negotiated, aiming for JV setup within Q4 FY25 or early Q1 FY26. Production to commence 6-8 months post-JV setup, with revenue expected in FY26 and full steam in FY27.
*   **Defense Segment:** Expecting INR 400+ crores (largest single order) and another INR 20-25 crores order in November. Aim to execute the INR 400 crore order within FY26-FY27, adding INR 200 crores revenue annually and pushing defense contribution to 5-7% of overall revenue.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Aiming to maintain current EBITDA margins at current levels (~18%) and achieve 2-3-4% expansion if market conditions permit.
*   **Emission Norms (BS-V Transition):** Effective January 1, 2025.
    *   **Cost Impact:** BS III to BS V transition to increase machine costs by 11-15% (avg. 12%). BS IV to BS V transition by 5-6%. Approximately 60% of machines are in the BS III to BS V category.
    *   **Pre-buying:** Expecting 15-20% additional sales in Q3 FY25 due to pre-buying before the price hike.
*   **M&A Activity:** International acquisition plan put on hold for a "better opportunity" which could be announced in Q4 FY25 or Q1 FY26.
*   **Rental Income:** Small revenue stream (INR 8-9 crores) from a small fleet of crawler cranes.
*   **Spare Parts Revenue:** Target to increase spare parts contribution to total revenue by 50-100% from current levels (~4% of turnover).
*   **Medium to Long-Term Revenue Target:** Aim to triple revenue from FY23 levels (INR 2,200 crores) to INR 4,400 crores by FY26. Current focus is on FY23-FY26. Future capex plans are being considered for potential growth beyond this target.
*   **Developed Market Exports:** Targeting entry into developed markets (Europe, America) in the next 2-3 years, supported by technology upgrades and aesthetic improvements.
