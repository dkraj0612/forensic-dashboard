## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported its best-ever quarterly performance in Q3 FY2021, with significant year-on-year growth across key financial metrics. Operational revenue surged by 38% to ₹400 Crores, while EBITDA grew by an impressive 102% to ₹45 Crores, leading to an EBITDA margin of 11.29% (excluding other income). Profit before tax increased by 154% to ₹42.7 Crores, and net profit saw a 143% jump to ₹31 Crores, with a PAT margin of 7.72%. For the first nine months of FY2021, revenue stood at ₹770 Crores, with an operating EBITDA of ₹69 Crores and EBITDA margins of 8.99%. Management highlighted that supply chain constraints prevented even better performance in the December quarter, but expressed optimism for continued strong demand driven by the revival in construction, infrastructure, and industrial/logistics segments. The company also achieved a significant milestone by being the first indigenous manufacturer to develop lithium-ion electric forklifts.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACE's Q3 FY2021 strategy and performance drivers included:

*   **Demand Revival:** Strong recovery in construction, infrastructure, industrial, and logistics sectors.
*   **Segmental Performance:** Significant growth in agri equipment (driven by realignment and cost control), construction equipment showing profitability for the first time, and strong performance in cranes.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Continued focus on cost control measures and improved operational efficiency.
*   **Supply Chain Mitigation:** Proactive management of supply chain constraints, with ongoing efforts to diversify vendors and resolve issues.
*   **New Product Development:** Successful development of lithium-ion electric forklifts, marking a first for indigenous manufacturing.
*   **Market Share Expansion:** Gained approximately 2% market share in the pick-and-carry crane segment.
*   **Employee Motivation:** Approval of an employee stock option scheme to motivate and attract talent.
*   **Government Initiatives:** Anticipation of stronger economic recovery aided by government focus on urban infra and rural development.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **Q3 FY2021 Performance:** Management reported "the best quarterly performance in the history of our company so far," exceeding previous historical highs and demonstrating a strong turnaround.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved 38% YoY revenue growth, aligning with the positive momentum noted in the previous quarter.
*   **EBITDA & Margins:** Reported 102% YoY EBITDA growth and 11.29% EBITDA margins, significantly improving from Q2 FY2021 and demonstrating successful margin expansion.
*   **PBT & Net Profit Growth:** Substantial growth in PBT (154%) and Net Profit (143%) also signifies a strong execution.
*   **Full Year FY2021 Guidance:** Reaffirmed the guidance of being "in touching distance of our last year’s revenues with better margins by the end of current financial year," indicating sustained optimism.
*   **Supply Chain Constraints:** Acknowledged that supply chain constraints prevented fully capitalizing on revenue potential in December, impacting top-line and profit margins slightly. This aligns with the Q2 narrative but highlights continued challenges.
*   **Steel Price Increase:** Successfully passed on steel price increases to customers without compromising margins, a change in strategy compared to historical difficulties.
*   **Agri & Construction Equipment Segments:** Demonstrated successful realignment and focus on these segments, leading to strong growth and profitability, as planned.
