## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) delivered a robust Q2 FY25, reporting its best-ever second quarter with significant year-on-year growth in revenue and EBITDA margins. Standalone operational revenues grew by 12.2% YoY, driven by a 9% increase in volumes for Cranes, Material Handling, and Construction Equipment. EBITDA margins expanded impressively by 268 basis points to 18.04%, attributed to operating leverage, a favorable product mix, and stringent cost controls. The company's performance is supported by the government's continued focus on infrastructure development, manufacturing, and capital expenditure. ACE is strategically positioned to capitalize on these growth drivers, leveraging its diversified product portfolio and strong market presence across various sectors. The company is also upgrading its products to new emission norms (CEV-5/BS-5), enhancing its competitiveness in both domestic and international markets.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Leadership:** Continues to leverage its dominant position in India's Pick & Carry Crane (63%+ market share) and Tower Crane (60%+ market share) segments.
*   **Diversified Portfolio:** Serves Construction, Infrastructure, Manufacturing, Logistics, Defence, and Agri sectors with a broad range of equipment.
*   **Global Expansion:** Expanding its presence in over 37 countries, with a strategic focus on increasing export contribution. New products like Forma Tractors, Telehandlers, and Phantom 4x4 Backhoe Loader are designed for international markets.
*   **Product Development & Emission Norms:** Upgraded products to CEV-5 (BS-5) emission norms, aligning with European standards and opening new international market opportunities. Introduction of Next-Gen Cranes (35 Tons 4X4) and progress in Crawler Cranes, Aerial Work Platforms, and Telehandlers.
*   **Infrastructure Focus:** Benefits from government initiatives, including significant capex in roads, railways, housing (PMAY), and urban infrastructure, as well as the National Infra Pipeline.
*   **Logistics & Warehousing Growth:** Capitalizing on the projected growth in the logistics market and government support for logistics parks and warehousing.
*   **Agri Equipment Growth:** Supported by increased budgetary allocation to Rural Development and Irrigation, and expected growth in the tractor market.
*   **Defense Sector:** Remains a target for medium-to-long-term growth, with current year targets and increasing capital outlay in the defense budget.
*   **Customer Centricity:** Focus on flexible, quick adaptation, and providing customized solutions through strong manufacturing and R&D capabilities.
*   **Service Network:** Maintains a wide sales and service network across 125+ locations in India.
