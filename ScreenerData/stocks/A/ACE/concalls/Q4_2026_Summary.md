## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported its Q4 FY2026 and full-year FY2026 standalone results amidst stable demand conditions, albeit with some moderation in March due to geopolitical headwinds in West Asia, leading to supply disruptions, inflationary pressures, and rupee depreciation. Despite these challenges, ACE delivered its best-ever Q4 revenue performance while sustaining operating margins.

For the full year FY2026, total income remained largely flat at INR 3,395 crores. However, the company demonstrated strong profitability growth, with EBITDA expanding by 81 basis points to 18.33% (INR 622.36 crores, up 4% YoY), PBT up 4.3% to INR 566 crores, and PAT growing 5.4% to INR 425 crores. Q4 FY2026 saw a 5.58% YoY increase in total income to INR 1,021 crores, with an EBITDA margin of 16%. The company remains debt-free with ample liquidity and has recommended a final dividend of 100% (INR 2 per share).

A significant development is the finalization of a 50-50 joint venture with KATO Works Company, Japan, which will focus on truck, crawler, and rough terrain cranes. This JV is expected to accelerate technology upgradation, localization, and expand export opportunities, particularly in the heavy crane segment. Management acknowledges the evolving macroeconomic environment and input cost volatility, emphasizing calibrated pricing actions and cost efficiencies to maintain profitability and market share.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **KATO Works Joint Venture (JV):** The 50-50 JV with KATO Works Company, Japan, has been finalized. This partnership aims to strengthen ACE's presence in the heavy crane segment (truck, crawler, and rough terrain cranes) through technology upgradation, deeper localization, and expanded export opportunities. The JV also includes ACE exporting components to KATO Japan.
*   **Defense Sector Expansion:** ACE holds an INR 575 crore defense order book. Management expects defense to contribute 5-6% of total revenue (upwards of INR 200-220 crores) in FY2027. Approvals for the Rough Terrain Forklifts (Telehandler) order have been secured, with execution set to begin in Q1 FY2027. The company is also developing prototypes for Quick Response Surface to Air Missile (QRSAM) material handling systems.
*   **Export Growth:** Combined defense and export revenue reached approximately 9% in FY2026. The target for FY2027 is 6-7% from exports alone, leading to a combined 11-13% for defense and exports, with a long-term goal of 10-15%.
*   **Capacity and Capex Plans:** Current blended capacity utilization for cranes, material handling, and construction equipment stands at approximately 60%, providing headroom for growth. For FY2027, planned capex is around INR 200 crores (includes INR 130-135 crores for contracted land parcels and INR 40-50 crores for a new plant dedicated to defense equipment and new products). Additionally, a new tower crane factory (upwards of INR 400 crores) is intended, with construction potentially starting in FY2027 based on demand, taking 12-18 months.
*   **Pricing Strategy & Cost Management:** To counter 20-22% increase in steel prices (a major input cost), ACE has implemented multiple price hikes: 1-1.5% in January, 4% on May 1st, and another 5% on June 1st, totaling 9-10%. Management anticipates a total price increase of 11-14% eventually to offset the rising input costs. Competitors are also reported to be increasing prices.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Demand for higher tonnage cranes continues to rise. The "Hydra type" (old generation) cranes, which faced skepticism post CEV-5 emission norm transition, have seen demand return in recent months. Backhoe loaders and construction equipment are expected to show decent growth in FY2027. The company is also making progress on a backhoe loader product improvement, with a proof of concept expected by June/July.
*   **Long-Term Vision:** ACE remains confident in its medium to long-term growth path, reiterating its target of INR 6,000-6,200 crores in revenue by FY2029 or FY2030, supported by new product initiatives, defense orders, and export growth.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative

*   **FY26 Revenue Guidance:** Management successfully met its "flat to positive" revenue guidance for FY2026, reporting a largely flat total income of INR 3,395 crores.
*   **EBITDA Margin Trajectory:** Q4 FY2026 standalone EBITDA margin was 16.0%. For the full year, it was 18.33%. Management reiterates the goal to "sustain our margin profile, EBITDA without other income in the range of around 15% to 16%," consistent with previous guidance.
*   **Kato Works JV Timeline:** The target of announcing "definitive agreements shortly" from Q3 FY2026 was met with the finalization of the 50-50 joint venture.
*   **Defense Order Execution (₹420 Cr):** The Q3 target of INR 30-40 crores execution in FY2026 for the specific INR 420 crore Rough Terrain Forklifts order was not fully met, as execution is now slated to begin in Q1 FY2027. However, total defense revenue in FY2026 (approx. INR 102 crores) exceeded this specific order's guidance, indicating other defense orders were executed.
*   **Defense Revenue Share:** The Q3 expectation of 4-5% revenue from defense in FY2027, rising to 5-6% going forward, is consistent with Q4 commentary projecting 5-6% for FY2027 (upwards of INR 200-220 crores).
*   **Export Revenue Share:** Q3 targeted 6-7% exports for FY2026 and >10% for FY2027. Q4 mentions combined defense and export at ~9% for FY2026, implying exports were around 6%. For FY2027, the target is presented as 6-7% for exports and 5-6% for defense, totaling 11-13% combined, broadly aligning with the "upwards of 10%" combined target.
*   **Construction & Infrastructure Equipment (CIE) Scheme:** The Q3 expectation for the scheme to be announced within 2-4 months was not mentioned in the Q4 transcript, suggesting no update or delay.
*   **Electric Cranes Commercial Sale:** The Q3 expectation for electric cranes to be ready for commercial sale in Q4 FY2026 was not mentioned, indicating it might not have materialized as planned or is no longer a key focus for immediate reporting.
*   **Backhoe Loader Growth:** Management confirmed progress on the JCB backhoe segment improvement, with a "proof of concept" expected in June/July, supporting the long-term growth projection.
*   **Tower Crane Capacity Expansion:** Q3 planned for ~1,100 units next year. Q4 stated current capacity at 950-1,000 cranes, with an intent for a new factory, broadly consistent on intent but refining immediate capacity figures.
*   **Land Acquisition:** Progress on land acquisition was confirmed with planned capex of INR 130-135 crores to complete contracted land parcels, consistent with the Q3 timeline.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD):** Management confirmed the proposed ADD on Chinese cranes was "very unfortunately" not notified by the Ministry of Finance by the December deadline, and its future fate is uncertain. This marks a shift from Q3's "awaiting notification" to outright uncertainty regarding implementation.
*   **Q4 FY2026 Outlook:** Q3 indicated "continued positive growth trajectory and improving demand." Q4 saw "best ever revenue performance" but acknowledged a slowdown in March due to geopolitical issues, indicating positive growth but with unexpected headwinds.
