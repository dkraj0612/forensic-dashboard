## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported its Q3 FY2026 results, indicating a stabilization and early signs of recovery after the temporary slowdown attributed to CEV-5 (BS-V) emission norms and monsoon impacts. The company's standalone performance showed flattish total income year-on-year at INR 888 crores, with a significant EBITDA margin expansion of 74 basis points to 18.5%. For the nine months ended December 31, 2025, total income declined by 3.21% to INR 2,373 crores, yet EBITDA grew by 7.15% to INR 458 crores with a substantial EBITDA margin expansion of 186 basis points to 19.32%. Profit after tax for the nine months grew by 11% to INR 316 crores. Management highlighted the introduction of new-generation technology-powered equipment and expressed optimism about the impact of government infrastructure spending, favorable trade deals (India-EU, US), and the proposed anti-dumping duty on Chinese cranes, which is awaiting notification. The company also confirmed a significant INR 420 crore order for Rough Terrain Forklifts from the Ministry of Defence, with partial execution expected in FY2026 and further in FY2027.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product Innovation:** ACE has launched an extensive suite of new-generation equipment featuring intelligent tower cranes, AI-assisted pick and carry cranes, advanced aerial work platforms, high-performance telehandlers, and material handling systems. These are designed for higher productivity, enhanced safety, and reduced total cost of ownership.
*   **Market Recovery & Demand Drivers:** Management sees demand stabilizing in Q2 and returning to normalcy in Q3 FY2026, with expectations of a favorable operating environment driven by timely project awards, improved on-ground execution, supportive policy measures, and higher government capital expenditure.
*   **Government Infrastructure Focus:** The Union Budget 2026 reinforces a growth-oriented roadmap with sustained thrust on capital expenditure, projecting a CAGR of 19.4% for infra capex from FY22-FY27 and an outlay of INR 12.21 lakh crores for FY27. Initiatives like the enhancement of the Construction and Infrastructure Equipment (CIE) scheme, risk guarantee funds, and development of corridors are expected to fuel demand.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) & CIE Scheme:** While the proposed ADD on Chinese cranes is still awaiting notification by the Finance Ministry, management views it as a significant long-term strategic advantage. The new CIE scheme, focusing on import substitution for equipment like crawler and truck cranes, is also anticipated to be a major boost, expected within 2-4 months.
*   **Defense Sector Growth:** The company has INR 500 crores of defense orders pending execution, with plans to execute INR 30-40 crores in FY2026, carrying forward INR 460-470 crores to FY2027. An order for 150 Heavy Recovery Vehicles (HRVs) is also expected to be announced shortly.
*   **Export Expansion:** ACE is targeting 6-7% of revenue from exports in FY2026, aiming for upwards of 10% in FY2027, focusing on markets in the Middle East, Africa, and South America.
*   **Capacity and Future Expansion:** Current standalone capacity is upwards of INR 5,000-5,500 crores, with revenue at INR 3,300-3,400 crores. The company plans to reach INR 6,000-7,000 crores by FY2029-FY2030. Expansion plans include a new tower crane plant in Palwal and potential rationalization of logistics costs by setting up a facility in Indore.
*   **Kato Works JV:** The company expects to announce definitive agreements for the Kato joint venture shortly, which will help utilize existing capacity.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative

*   **FY26 Revenue Guidance:** The transcript confirms the expectation of "flat to positive" revenue for the current year (FY2026) in value terms, acknowledging a decline in volume terms. This aligns with the narrative of recovery in H2 and a revised, more conservative outlook from previous aggressive growth expectations.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management has successfully delivered on margin expansion. The standalone Q3 FY2026 EBITDA margin of 18.5% and 9M FY2026 margin of 19.32% significantly exceed previous stabilization targets, driven by calibrated pricing, product mix, and cost efficiencies.
*   **CEV-5 (BS-V) Transition Impact:** Management reiterates that the initial softness due to CEV-5 transition was temporary, with demand stabilizing in Q2 and normalizing in Q3. The price acceptability for new norms has now been achieved.
*   **Defense Order Execution:** While the INR 420 crore defense order is confirmed, management now states partial execution of INR 30-40 crores in FY2026, carrying forward a larger portion to FY2027, which is consistent with previous commentary about execution timelines.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD):** Management's positive stance on the proposed ADD is reiterated, but the key shift is the confirmation that it has not been notified by the Finance Ministry as expected in December. However, the new CIE scheme is presented as a significant positive development to support domestic manufacturing.
*   **Kato Works JV Timeline:** No specific timeline update is provided, but definitive agreements are expected to be announced shortly.
