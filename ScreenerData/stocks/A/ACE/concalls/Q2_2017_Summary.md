## Executive Overview

ACE, India's leading material handling and construction equipment manufacturing company, presented a robust outlook in its Q2 2017 earnings transcript. The company boasts a diverse product portfolio, including mobile cranes, rollers, tractor-mounted cranes, crawler cranes, forklifts, and other construction equipment, holding a commanding over 63% market share in the mobile cranes segment. ACE operates state-of-the-art production facilities supported by a strong R&D backbone, with a stated philosophy to be the leading company in the cranes and construction equipment sector with a global focus. The company is strategically positioned to capitalize on India's increased focus on infrastructure development, growing mechanization, and opening of FDI. Key strengths highlighted include a strong brand name, a wide range of products, value-for-money propositions, and a commitment to R&D, with its R&D department recognized by the DSIR, Government of India.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACE's strategy is centered on Market Penetration, Market Development, and Product Development. The company aims to leverage several key drivers for growth:

*   **Infrastructure Push:** Increased government focus on infrastructure projects (Railways, Roads, Metro, Defence, Smart Cities) and rising infrastructure spending as a percentage of GDP.
*   **Private Sector Participation & FDI:** Growing private sector involvement and the opening of FDI in the sector.
*   **Mechanization Growth:** Increasing adoption of mechanization in construction and manufacturing sectors.
*   **Finance Availability:** Ease of access to finance for material handling and construction equipment.
*   **Product Diversification & Innovation:** Establishing new product categories and continuous R&D investment to match international quality standards and develop new models.
*   **Market Share Expansion:** Focused efforts to increase market share across various segments, including aiming for 70% in mobile cranes and leveraging brand equity in crawler cranes.
*   **Network Expansion:** Strengthening the sales and service network across India with dedicated spare parts divisions.
