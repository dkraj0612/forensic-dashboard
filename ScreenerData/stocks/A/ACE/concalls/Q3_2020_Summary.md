## Executive Overview

Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) reported a Q3 FY2020 marked by a slight sequential improvement, with revenue increasing by 8% quarter-on-quarter to ₹290 Crores (estimated based on 20% YoY decline from Q3 FY2019 reported ₹344 Crores). However, on a year-on-year basis, revenue declined by 20%. The company attributes this sequential improvement to green shoots observed since December 2019, with a notable increase in order flow and execution continuing into January 2020. Despite the YoY revenue decline, ACE managed to improve EBITDA margins to 8.3% in Q3 FY2020, an improvement from 7.9% in Q3 FY2019. Management has revised the full-year FY2020 revenue guidance to a negative growth of 12% to 15%, an upward revision from the previous negative 15% to 20% guidance. They aim for EBITDA margins slightly better than 8% and anticipate an 8% to 10% improvement in EPS, partly due to a successful share buyback. Management expresses optimism about overcoming short-term turbulence and a return to normalized revenue and bottomline in the medium to long term, contingent on economic recovery.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACE's strategy is focused on navigating the improving market conditions and capitalizing on future growth:

*   **Market Bottoming Out:** Management perceives the economic slowdown as having bottomed out in December 2019, with green shoots and increased order flow observed.
*   **Export Focus:** Continued efforts to increase export revenue share, targeting 10-12% for FY2020 and aiming for 20-25% in the next 2-3 years. Expansion into CIS, Middle East, and South East Asia is planned.
*   **Cost & Margin Management:** Successful price increases of 2-5% implemented in January 2020 to counter rising steel prices. Aiming for 10-11% EBITDA margins in the next fiscal year.
*   **New Product Development:** New crane models (25, 45, 30, 55, 60 ton truck cranes launched, 80 ton to launch in March/April) are entering the market to regain market share from Chinese manufacturers. Patents are applied for new, potentially market-leading products.
*   **Working Capital Improvement:** Targeting to reduce net debt to ₹50-55 Crores by end of March 2020 through inventory reduction. Receivable days improved to 33 days.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Current utilization is 40-60%, with capacity to more than double business without significant capex in the next 1-2 years.
*   **Emission Norms:** Preparation for BS-IV engine upgrade for construction equipment from October 2020, anticipating a price increase of ₹1 lakh to ₹3 lakhs per machine.
*   **Agri Business Stabilization:** Agri business stabilized in Q3 FY2020 and is expected to grow from next quarter.
*   **Contract Manufacturing:** Negotiations ongoing for contract manufacturing opportunities with foreign manufacturers.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Narrative:

*   **FY2020 Revenue Guidance:** Management revised FY2020 revenue guidance upwards to **negative 12% to 15%** from negative 15% to 20%. This signifies a positive adjustment, indicating a less severe downturn than previously anticipated.
*   **FY2020 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** The target for FY2020 EBITDA margin remains slightly better than **8%**, consistent with previous expectations.
*   **Q3 FY2020 Performance:** The reported QoQ revenue increase of 8% and YoY decline of 20% is broadly in line with the sentiment of bottoming out. The improvement in EBITDA margin to 8.3% YoY is a positive indicator.
*   **Market Bottoming Out:** The narrative shifted from anticipating a slow market for a few more months to expressing confidence that the slump bottomed out in December 2019, supported by observed green shoots and increased order flow.
*   **Steel Price Impact:** The company has proactively implemented price increases (2-5% in January) to offset rising steel costs, a move supported by firming steel prices and increased demand. This contrasts with previous concerns about margin erosion.
*   **Export Revenue:** Progress on exports is consistent with previous discussions, with efforts to increase the share to 10-12% for FY2020 and higher in subsequent years.
*   **Working Capital:** Debt levels are reported at ₹100 Crores with a clear plan to reduce them significantly by the end of FY2020. Receivable days have also seen improvement.
*   **Agri Segment Performance:** Management acknowledges the "miserable" performance in H1 FY2020 but reports stabilization in Q3, with hopes for growth in the upcoming quarters.
*   **New Crane Products:** The launch of new truck crane models addresses the gap previously dominated by Chinese manufacturers, with a stated aim to regain market share.
