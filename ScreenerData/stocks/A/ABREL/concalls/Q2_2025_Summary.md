## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited (ABREL) reported its Q2 FY26 earnings, demonstrating strong sales momentum driven by its real estate segment. The company highlighted the supportive macroeconomic environment in India, with GDP growth and easing inflation contributing to positive real estate market sentiment. ABREL emphasized its strategic transformation into a focused real estate platform and its commitment to sustainability and innovation. The company achieved strong bookings of INR 890 crores in Q2 FY26, a significant increase of 111% over the previous quarter, driven by flagship projects in Mumbai and Bengaluru. ABREL reiterates its confidence in achieving its launch pipeline for the year, with a robust Gross Development Value (GDV) of INR 13,900 crores. The company also received accolades for its commitment to health, safety, and ESG standards.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q2 FY26 Bookings:** INR 890 crores (+111% QoQ)
*   **Launched Area Sold (as of Sep 30, 2025):** 80% across India
*   **Launch Pipeline (Rest of FY26):** GDV of INR 13,900 crores
*   **Total GDV:** INR 70,000 crores
*   **Project Development Cost (H1 FY26):** INR 1,030 crores (approx.)
    *   Construction Cost: INR 350 crores
    *   Design, Liaison, Brokerage: INR 200 crores
    *   Land Purchase: INR 150 crores
    *   Operational Costs: INR 130 crores
    *   Interest Cost (Capitalized): INR 140 crores
*   **Collections (H1 FY26):** INR 900 crores (excluding JV with Mitsubishi)
*   **Net Debt Increase (QoQ):** INR 2.8 billion (attributed to FSI premiums and timing mismatches)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Real Estate Focus:** Continued strategic transformation to become a leading real estate platform. Emphasis on premium and luxury segments, sustainability, and innovation in product development.
*   **Sales Momentum:** Driving sales in existing portfolio and preparing for a robust launch pipeline.
*   **Construction Execution:** Ensuring strong execution across all project sites.
*   **Sustainability & ESG:** Commitment to green building practices, energy-efficient design, and industry-leading ESG standards, as recognized by the British Safety Council and GRESB Real Estate Assessment.
*   **Business Development:** Actively pursuing new projects with an aim to finalize INR 10,000-15,000 crores of GDV by year-end, through a mix of joint ventures and outright acquisitions.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Real Estate - Consistency & Target Achievement:**
    *   **Project Launches:** Management's intention to launch projects remains consistent, though some RERA approval timelines have caused minor delays (e.g., Pune projects rectified after initial RERA in previous owner's name). The Thane project's environmental clearance and RERA are now expected this quarter for a Q4 launch, overcoming previous NGT ruling hurdles.
    *   **Sales Momentum:** The strong booking numbers in Q2 FY26 (INR 890 crores, +111% QoQ) validate management's confidence in sustained customer interest and brand appeal, despite the absence of new launches in the quarter.

Management assures that significant collections are expected in October and November (approx. INR 600 crores), mitigating these gaps and projecting solid cash flow collection of INR 2,000 crores cumulatively by December. Land payments for Thane are being managed through SPV funding post IFC infusion.
