## Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited (ABREL) - Q4 FY26 Earnings Call - Executive Summary

### Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited (ABREL) delivered a strong Q4 FY26, marking an exceptional close to the fiscal year. The company reported significant presales of INR 4,288 crores, a 69% quarter-on-quarter increase, and collections of INR 994 crores. For the full fiscal year FY26, ABREL achieved presales of INR 8,136 crores, collections of INR 3,341 crores, and sold 5.5 million square feet. Key drivers for Q4 included successful new project launches and strong performance from existing inventory, notably Birla Arika Phase 2 (NCR) which sold 97% of its launch inventory for INR 1,600 crores, Birla Taranya (MMR) with INR 952 crores in sales, and Birla Trimaya Phase 4 (Bengaluru) contributing INR 649 crores. The company also announced its maiden redevelopment project in Khar, Mumbai, with a GDV potential of INR 1,700 crores. Operational highlights include receiving the British Safety Council's "Sword of Honour" and recognition as one of the Best Organizations for Women 2026.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Premiumization & Outperformance:** Continued focus on premium and luxury segments, which continue to outperform affordable and mid-income segments in Tier 1 cities.
*   **Calibrated Launches & Pricing:** Industry response to evolving demand through disciplined pricing, flexible payment plans, and strategic launch phasing.
*   **Project Execution & Brand Strength:** Emphasis on execution, brand strength, and product differentiation as key industry success factors.
*   **Commercial Portfolio Growth:** Strong leasing activity in the commercial segment, driven by GCC demand for Grade A assets, leading to tightening vacancy and a rental upcycle.
*   **Redevelopment Expansion:** Actively pursuing redevelopment opportunities, with the Khar project being the first.
*   **Sustainability & Safety:** Ongoing commitment to rigorous safety practices and ESG leadership, as evidenced by awards and certifications.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **FY26 Presales Target Achievement:** Management stated they would exceed last year's presales target for FY26, and the reported INR 8,136 crores successfully achieved this, exceeding the previous year's performance despite some launch delays.
*   **Q4 Performance Narrative:** The strong Q4 performance, with 69% QoQ growth in presales, validates management's expectation of a robust close to the fiscal year. The commentary highlights the success of new launches and ongoing inventory sales.
*   **Project Launch Timelines:** Management acknowledged delays in certain launches (e.g., Niyaara Tower C, potentially spilling into Q3 FY27) due to approval processes. However, they have been proactive in lining up other launches and generating significant sales from available inventory. The narrative around prioritizing "right deals" and navigating approvals remains consistent.
*   **Business Development (BD) Pipeline:** While not providing specific guidance on conversion percentages for the INR 60,000 crores BD pipeline, management reiterated their cautious yet active approach. They previously aimed to conclude deals worth INR 10,000 crores by March 31st, and the current BD discussions suggest a continued focus on significant future opportunities. The company's ambition to be among the top real estate players is reiterated, emphasizing both size and reputation.
*   **Paper Business Divestment:** Progress is confirmed, with CCI approval obtained. State-level approvals are pending, and the conclusion of the transaction is expected within the current quarter (Q1 FY27), with cash inflow anticipated soon after.
