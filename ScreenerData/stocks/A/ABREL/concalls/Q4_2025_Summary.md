## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited (ABREL) held its Q4 FY25 earnings call, highlighting a significant strategic shift with the approved divestment of its Pulp and Paper business to ITC for INR 3,498 crores, expected to conclude by Q2 FY26. This move sharpens the company's focus on its core real estate segment, which demonstrated robust growth in FY25. The real estate business achieved a landmark year, with booking values more than doubling to INR 8,087 crores and collections increasing by 100% to INR 2,706 crores. The company added projects with a Gross Development Value (GDV) of over INR 25,000 crores, bringing its total GDV to nearly INR 70,000 crores. Management expressed strong confidence in the real estate sector's resilience, driven by urbanization, lifestyle aspirations, and a growing emphasis on sustainability. ABREL is also strategically expanding into redevelopment and commercial real estate to build an annuity income stream.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Real Estate Focus:** Continued emphasis on premium and luxury residential segments, with a strong pipeline of new project launches and phased expansions. Strategic expansion into redevelopment and commercial real estate for annuity income.
*   **Growth Ambition:** Aiming to reach INR 15,000 crores in annual pre-sales within three years.
*   **Brand Enhancement:** Leveraging brand visibility, including sponsorship of Royal Challengers Bengaluru in IPL 2025.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Secured USD 33 million in equity participation from Mitsubishi Estate Company for Birla Evara, Bengaluru.
*   **Sustainability:** Integration of green building practices and energy-efficient designs into the business model.
*   **Business Development:** Aiming to add INR 15,000 - 20,000 crores of new GDV in FY26, focusing on key markets like Bengaluru, NCR, Mumbai, and Pune.
*   **Divestment of Pulp & Paper:** Strategic move to sharpen focus and enhance balance sheet for real estate growth.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Real Estate - Consistency & Target Achievement:**
    *   **Project Launches:** Management confirmed robust launches in Q4 FY25 with stellar sales at three new projects and two new phases, aligning with previous expectations of a strong pipeline. The company is planning for INR 14,000 crores of GDV launches in FY26, with a majority skewed towards Q3 and Q4.
    *   **Sales Momentum:** FY25 presales significantly exceeded guidance, achieving INR 8,087 crores against a previous guidance of INR 7,000-8,000 crores. Q4 FY25 bookings of INR 5,738 crores doubled YoY, validating strong customer interest. Management expressed confidence in achieving the three-year target of INR 15,000 crores in annual sales.
    *   **Debt & Cash Flow:** Current consolidated debt is INR 3,575 crores with a net worth of ~INR 3,900 crores (Debt-to-Equity of 0.92x). Post-paper business divestment, approximately INR 2,000 crores of debt will be repaid, significantly reducing leverage. Future cash flows from collections are expected to cover construction costs, with excess funds earmarked for growth or fresh borrowings if needed. Construction spend for FY25 was ~INR 1,600 crores.
    *   **Business Development (BD):** Management added projects with over INR 25,000 crores GDV in FY25 and aims to add INR 15,000 - 20,000 crores in FY26. This demonstrates consistent focus and execution on expanding the project pipeline.
    *   **Worli West Land Litigation:** Management provided clarity on the Supreme Court order, confirming the impact on ~6.5 acres of leased land from BMC, leading to a GDV reduction of INR 5,000 crores and a loss of 6.8 lakh sq ft FSI potential. They are exploring legal options and have allocated an investment of ~INR 1,200 crores for the 10-acre Worli land acquisition, distinct from the BMC land.
    *   **Execution Capability:** Management highlighted strong execution, with three projects (Birla Vanya, Birla Alokya, Birla Navya Phase 1) delivered within RERA timelines and with strong NPS scores. The Golden Peacock National Quality Award reinforces their commitment to quality and execution. While no major project *completions* were scheduled for FY26, focus remains on execution, with significant deliveries expected from FY28 onwards (e.g., Worli).

*   **Pulp & Paper Segment Challenges:**
    *   **Divestment Confirmation:** Management confirmed the Board's approval for the divestment of the Pulp and Paper business via slump sale to ITC for INR 3,498 crores, with an expected closure by Q2 FY26. This aligns with previous discussions about addressing challenges in this segment.
    *   **Market Conditions:** While not directly discussed in terms of recent performance (as it's being divested), the buyer (ITC) will now inherit these market dynamics. Previous concerns about weak domestic demand and rising input costs are implicitly addressed by the sale.
