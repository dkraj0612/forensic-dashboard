## Executive Overview

Century Textiles & Industries Ltd (CTIL) presented its Q2 FY21 and H1 FY21 results, indicating a gradual recovery amidst an "Abnormal Business Environment" due to the pandemic. While consolidated net sales for Q2 FY21 were Rs. 596 crores with an EBITDA of Rs. 66 crores (11.1% margin) and a net loss of Rs. 10 crores, H1 FY21 reported Rs. 990 crores in net sales, Rs. 97 crores in EBITDA (9.8% margin), and a net loss of Rs. 47 crores. The company highlighted improved operational performance across its diversified businesses: Real Estate, Pulp & Paper, and Textiles. Management expressed optimism for the upcoming festive season and continued focus on leveraging brand equity and resilience.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Overall Strategy:** CTIL continues to navigate the "turbulent near term business environment" with a focus on recovery and growth. The company emphasizes its historical resilience, customer-centricity, and sustainability. Digitalization and proactive stakeholder engagement are key enablers.
*   **Real Estate (Birla Estates):**
    *   **Market Traction:** Strong quarterly sales (Rs. 96.1 Cr in Q2) and collections across launched projects (Birla Vanya, Birla Alokya, Birla Navya). Positive outlook driven by low home loan interest rates, stamp duty reductions, and NRI interest.
    *   **Project Development:** Focus on developing group-owned land parcels and pursuing JDA/strategic tie-ups. New project launches are planned.
    *   **Commercial Assets:** High occupancy maintained at Birla Aurora and Birla Centurion, with continued lease income. Management anticipates potential pressure on renewals due to WFH trends but aims to hold and eventually REIT these assets.
*   **Pulp & Paper:**
    *   **Demand Dynamics:** Overall demand remains low, though retail tissue saw a slight increase, and positive sentiments for board and tissue are observed.
    *   **Strategic Initiatives:** Proactive participation in government tenders to maintain volume and capacity utilization. The New Education Policy is viewed as a long-term positive.
    *   **Expansion:** A new tissue plant has been commissioned in Gujarat. Future expansion in tissue and packaging board is being considered.
*   **Textiles:**
    *   **Adaptation:** Focus on the "Birla Care" brand for health and hygiene products, collaborating with HeiQ Switzerland and DuPont. Supply orders for antiviral sheets have been received from DRDO.
    *   **Operational Recovery:** Labor shortage issues improved by September, leading to increased capacity utilization. Export orders for bed linen improved, but domestic apparel fabric demand remains subdued.
    *   **R&D Focus:** Increased R&D for health and hygiene products is a priority.
    *   **Future Outlook:** Market normalization is expected by mid-FY22.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Context Audit:**
    *   **Cement Demerger:** The absence of cement operations and significantly reduced debt confirm the completion of the demerger with UltraTech Cement, aligning with the strategy for a debt-light entity.
    *   **Debt Reduction:** Consolidated net debt of ~Rs. 1,330 crores (gross) represents a substantial reduction from pre-demerger levels, though higher than the projected ~Rs. 813 crores post-demerger. Management clarifies that approximately Rs. 1,000 Cr of the current debt is attributable to the real estate business, indicating a strategic capital allocation shift.
    *   **Real Estate - Growth & Deal Making:** The narrative has evolved from achieving significant player status in 7-10 years with 2-3 deals annually to focusing on developing existing land parcels and JDA opportunities, with specific project launches planned. While specific deal numbers were not provided, the emphasis is on execution and pipeline expansion, with the aspiration for Real Estate to become the largest business reiterated.
    *   **Pulp & Paper Capex:** Progress has been made on the previously mentioned capex for tissue doubling/modernization with the commissioning of a new tissue plant.
    *   **Textiles Capex:** No specific capex updates were provided; the current strategy prioritizes R&D for health and hygiene products and utilization of existing capacities.
    *   **Management's Tone on Headwinds:** The acknowledgment of the "Abnormal Business Environment" due to the pandemic remains consistent. However, the tone has shifted from solely mitigation to a more proactive "recovery and growth" phase, particularly for Real Estate and Textiles (with new product lines). Management explicitly stated that "every quarter is going to be better than the previous one."

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, or Timelines for Tracking:**
    *   **Real Estate - Worli Project Launch:** October 2021 (Diwali next year), with the first phase comprising ~6.5 lakh sq ft residential development. Overall development plans include ~3 million sq ft residential, ~1 million sq ft commercial, and ~150,000 sq ft retail. Commercial assets are to be retained for leasing, with a long-term plan for a REIT.
    *   **Real Estate - Kalyan Phase II Launch:** Before the end of FY21, with Phase 2 size of ~3.5 to 4 lakh sq ft.
    *   **Real Estate - Gurugram JV:** Sales acceleration is planned during Diwali. Continued investment of ~Rs. 30-34 crores is anticipated, pending milestones.
    *   **Real Estate - Bangalore JV:** An advance of Rs. 50 Cr has been paid, with another ~Rs. 40-50 Cr for the balance peak investment.
    *   **Real Estate - Total Investment (Next 1-2 Years):** Approximately Rs. 1,000 crores of additional investment is expected, to be largely funded by sales cash flows. This could lead to a peak debt of around Rs. 2,500 crores in two years.
    *   **Pulp & Paper - Tissue Plant:** The new tissue plant has been commissioned and is expected to commence production in November (capacity of 3,000 tons/month).
    *   **Textiles - Future Outlook:** Market normalization is expected by mid-FY22.
    *   **Capacity Utilization:** Pulp & Paper is operating at 74-75% and gradually increasing. Textiles capacity utilization was ~74% in September.
    *   **Price Increases (Paper):** Management is monitoring the situation with excess inventory and will consider price increases at an appropriate time, anticipating demand growth during the festive season.
    *   **Commercial Property Renewals:** Some pressure on lease renewals is expected due to WFH trends, but current tenant engagement is positive.
    *   **Re-rating Potential:** Management acknowledges suggestions for a stock re-rating if it becomes a "pure property play" but emphasizes the current business model's role in generating cash flows and the strategic timing required for asset sales.
