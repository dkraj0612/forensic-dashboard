## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited (ABREL) has reported its Q1 FY26 earnings, marked by a continued strategic focus on its core real estate business and progress on the divestment of its paper division. The company achieved a booking value of INR 423 crores and collections of INR 545 crores in Q1 FY26, demonstrating a 61% year-on-year growth in booking value despite a planned consolidation phase with no new project launches. A significant development was the INR 420 crores ($50 million) investment from the International Finance Corporation (IFC) in its Pune (Manjri) and Thane projects, signaling strong investor confidence and bolstering ESG credentials. Management remains committed to its long-term growth ambition, leveraging its brand strength and expanding its project pipeline, with a strong emphasis on premium and luxury segments. The divestment of the paper business to ITC is on track for completion by the end of CY2025.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q1 FY26 Real Estate Bookings Value:** INR 423 crores (+61% YoY)
*   **Q1 FY26 Real Estate Collections:** INR 545 crores (+12% YoY)
*   **Q1 FY26 Area Sold:** 0.3 million sq ft (+327% YoY)
*   **Total Portfolio GDV Potential:** ~INR 70,000 crores
*   **IFC Investment (in Manjri & Thane projects):** $50 million (~INR 420 crores)
*   **Paper Business Divestment Completion:** Expected by end of CY2025
*   **Real Estate EBITDA Margin (Q4 FY25 for reference):** 33% (project dependent)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Real Estate Focus:** Continued emphasis on premium and luxury residential segments in key metros (Mumbai, NCR, Bengaluru, Pune). Strategic expansion into redevelopment in Mumbai, leveraging brand trust.
*   **Growth Ambition:** Aiming to reach INR 15,000 crores in annual pre-sales within three years.
*   **Brand Enhancement:** Increased marketing investment, including principal sponsorship of Royal Challengers Bangalore (IPL 2025), significantly boosting brand recall.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Securing equity investment from IFC and continued partnership with Mitsubishi Estate Company to manage capital concentration, share risks, and leverage global expertise.
*   **Sustainability:** Achieved Bureau of Energy Efficiency certification for Birla Aurora and Birla Centurion, underscoring commitment to ESG. IFC's investment categorized under green projects.
*   **Business Development (BD):** Targeting adding new projects to maintain pipeline momentum, with a focus on right deals, returns, and market positioning.
*   **Divestment of Pulp & Paper:** Strategic move to sharpen focus on real estate and improve balance sheet strength.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Real Estate - Consistency & Target Achievement:**
    *   **Sales Momentum (Q1 FY26):** Booking value of INR 423 crores and collections of INR 545 crores represent growth, but a planned consolidation quarter with no new launches led to lower absolute figures compared to the high base of Q4 FY25 (which saw INR 5,738 crores in bookings). Management attributes the Q1 performance to a lack of fresh inventory post-Q4 absorption and a planned approach to inventory management, rather than a market slowdown. They remain confident in their long-term sales targets.
    *   **Project Pipeline:** The company plans launches with an estimated GDV of over INR 13,900 crores for the remainder of FY26, with a strong skew towards Q3 and Q4, aligning with previous guidance on launch timing.
    *   **Debt & Cash Flow:** Management indicates comfortable cash flow positions with sufficient borrowing capacity and operating cash surplus. The paper business divestment proceeds are expected to further reduce debt.
    *   **Business Development (BD) Pipeline:** While BD achievement in FY25 was strong (over INR 25,000 crores GDV added), management is focused on selective, high-return deals for FY26, aiming for substantial additions without specifying an exact target figure, but on track.
    *   **Worli Project (Birla Niyaara):** Management acknowledges some cancellations in Q1 FY26 but attributes this to specific customer issues and a planned inventory management strategy (selling closer to possession). They are confident in the demand and pricing for subsequent phases, with Phase 3 launch planned for late Q3 or early Q4 FY26.
    *   **Execution Capability:** Management reiterates their focus on timely project delivery with safety, quality, and budget adherence. All projects are stated to be on track with timelines.

*   **Pulp & Paper Segment Challenges:**
    *   **Divestment Progress:** Management confirmed the divestment of the paper business to ITC is on track for completion by the end of CY2025, aligning with previous timelines.
