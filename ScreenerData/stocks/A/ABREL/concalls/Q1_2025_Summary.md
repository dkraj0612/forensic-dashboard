## Executive Overview

Century Textiles and Industries Limited (ABREL) demonstrated resilience in Q1 FY25, with consolidated turnover from continuing operations growing by a robust 28% year-on-year to INR 1,140 crores. This growth was primarily propelled by the Real Estate segment, which saw an impressive 800% year-on-year revenue increase to INR 338 crores, driven by project deliveries and strategic land acquisitions. The company secured two significant land parcels in Gurugram (GDV INR 5,300 crores) and Manjri, Pune (GDV INR 2,500 crores). While the Real Estate segment showed strong booking growth of 27% year-on-year to INR 262 crores, the Pulp & Paper segment faced headwinds, with average net sales realization (NSR) down 14% year-on-year, impacting EBITDA to INR 65 crores on INR 786 crores of sales. Consolidated EBITDA for continuing operations declined 15% year-on-year to INR 125 crores, with an EBITDA margin of 11%. The company continues its strategic focus on premium and luxury real estate and cost-optimization in the Pulp & Paper segment.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Real Estate:** Leverage strong market demand, particularly in premium and luxury segments. Focus on customer centricity, execution, and aggressive land acquisitions to build a robust GDV pipeline. Aim to launch approximately INR 12,000 crores of GDV in FY25 and add INR 15,000-20,000 crores annually. Expand presence in key markets like Gurugram, Pune, Bengaluru, and NCR.
*   **Pulp & Paper:** Navigate ongoing downturn in paper, board, and tissue prices by focusing on cost reduction initiatives (bamboo, veneer chips, fiber plant pulp) and increasing domestic market share. Address subdued demand and potential oversupply through marketing and branding.
*   **Textile:** Continue with the strategy of exiting loss-making operations. The joint venture with Grasim in textiles is in its initial stages and expected to show profits from FY26.
