## Executive Overview

Century Textiles and Industries Limited (ABREL) delivered a strong Q4 FY24 performance, building on its turnaround strategy driven by robust growth in its Real Estate and Pulp & Paper segments. Consolidated turnover for continuing operations surged by over 50% YoY and QoQ in Q4 FY24, with EBITDA growing 81% YoY and 26% QoQ. The company highlighted the exceptional success of its 'Silas' tower at Birla Niyaara, which achieved nearly INR 2,400 crores in bookings at launch, marking it as one of Mumbai's fastest-selling uber-luxury projects. Revenue recognition and handovers commenced for Birla Vanya (Kalyan) and Birla Navya (Gurugram). The Pulp & Paper segment, despite facing raw material cost pressures and supply chain disruptions, saw increased production and sales volumes. The company reiterated its strategy to exit loss-making operations within the Textile division, with a decision to discontinue operations at the Birla Century Bharuch unit, except for minor manufacturing activities supporting a joint venture. Total debt outstanding stood at approximately INR 2,482 crores as of March 31, 2024.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Real Estate:** Capitalize on surging demand and strong consumption in the residential real estate market, particularly in premium and luxury housing. Leverage urbanization trends, a growing rental market, and consistent property value appreciation. Focus on customer centricity, operational excellence, and sustainability.
*   **Pulp & Paper:** Navigate raw material cost pressures (imported pulp, wood availability) and supply chain disruptions. Implement cost reduction initiatives in pulp, recovery, and power. Prioritize domestic market for tissue and board, given higher domestic realizations. Focus on marketing, branding, and cost-reduction initiatives to address market oversupply.
*   **Textile:** Complete discontinuation of operations at the Birla Century Bharuch unit, except for minor manufacturing and allied activities supporting a joint venture. This aligns with the strategy to exit unsatisfactory performance and continued operational losses.
