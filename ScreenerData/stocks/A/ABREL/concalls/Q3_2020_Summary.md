## Executive Overview

Century Textiles & Industries Ltd. (CTIL) presented its Q3 FY20 results, indicating performance across its diversified business segments: Real Estate, Pulp & Paper, and Textiles. While overall financial figures for the quarter show a decrease in EBITDA compared to the previous year, driven by lower realizations in Pulp & Paper and a one-time income reversal in Textiles, the company highlighted positive operational developments and strategic initiatives across its businesses. Management provided updates on project launches and development timelines in Real Estate, capacity utilization and expansion in Pulp & Paper, and sustainability initiatives in Textiles.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Pulp & Paper EBITDA (Q3 FY20):** Rs. 132 crores (down 20% YoY due to lower realization and sales, partially offset by RM cost reduction and efficiency gains).
*   **Textiles EBITDA (Q3 FY20):** Rs. 10 crores (down from Rs. 12 crores YoY, impacted by a Rs. 3 crore reversal due to MEIS license withdrawal).
*   **Total Net Debt Outstanding (as of Dec 31, 2019):** ~Rs. 1,405 crores.
*   **Average Interest Rate (9M ending Dec 2019):** 7.55% (down from 7.75% in the prior period).
*   **Revenue Pie (Q3 FY20):** Pulp & Paper (88%), Textiles (8%), Other (4%).
*   **Revenue Pie (9M FY20):** Pulp & Paper (88%), Textiles (8%), Other (4%).
*   **EBITDA (9 Months FY20):** Rs. 564 crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Real Estate (Birla Estates):**
    *   **Project Launches & Development:** Two projects launched in Q3 FY20 (Birla Vanya, Birla Alokya) with another planned for Q4 FY20 (AnantRaj JDA, Phs1). Key approvals received for the Worli project, expected launch in Q1 FY22. Definitive agreement signed for Magadi Road project, expected launch in Q4 FY21.
    *   **Portfolio:** Focus on residential development, particularly in premium/mid-income segments, using a capital-efficient, asset-light model. Active development across multiple owned land parcels and joint ventures, with significant revenue potential. Commercial assets (Birla Aurora, Birla Centurion) continue to generate stable leasing revenue.
    *   **Market Context:** Acknowledged muted growth and stagnant sales in the broader real estate market due to economic slowdown, but highlighted branded developers' continued growth and increased office absorption.

*   **Pulp & Paper:**
    *   **Operational Performance:** Capacity utilization at 104% in Q3 FY20 (vs. 106% in Q3 FY19). Sales down 13% YoY with an approximate 8-10% drop in realization, partially compensated by RM cost reduction (8-9%) and improved operational efficiency.
    *   **Expansion & Demand:** New tissue plant commissioned in Gujarat, expected to start production in November (3,000 tons/month). Anticipates demand growth during the festive season and plans to consider price increases. Growth drivers include increased government spending on education, rising literacy and hygiene awareness, better packaging for FMCG/e-commerce, and substitution of plastics.

*   **Textiles:**
    *   **Operational Performance:** Capacity utilization at 95% in Q3 FY20 (up 2% YoY). Sales up 3% YoY. EBITDA impacted by retrospective MEIS license withdrawal.
    *   **Strategic Initiatives:** Focus on sustainability, holding LEED V4 certification, and global brand approvals. Plans to increase exports, procure sustainable wood, and optimize working capital. Investment of Rs. 500 crore over three years planned for growth, modernization, and upgradation.

This is in line with the previous quarter's emphasis on navigating a challenging environment.
