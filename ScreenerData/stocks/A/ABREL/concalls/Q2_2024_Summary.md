## Executive Overview

Century Textiles and Industries Limited (CTIL), operating as ABREL, presented its Q2 FY24 earnings, showcasing a mixed performance across its three core segments. The Real Estate division, driven by Birla Estates, continues to be the primary growth engine, demonstrating robust bookings and strategic land acquisitions. The Pulp & Paper segment faced margin pressures due to declining realizations, though management anticipates a recovery. The Textile division is undergoing restructuring with a focus on an outsourced model. Consolidated turnover for Q2 FY24 was INR 1,087 Crores, with an EBITDA of INR 46 Crores and a Net Loss of INR 33 Crores. The first half of FY24 saw a consolidated turnover of INR 2,193 Crores, EBITDA of INR 181 Crores, and a Net Loss of INR 40 Crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Overall Company Strategy:** Leverage legacy for national development, focusing on quality, innovation, agility, and customer centricity. Optimize performance across divisions amidst global economic challenges.
*   **Birla Estates Strategy:** Focus on premium/luxury segments, expand in MMR, NCR, Pune, Bengaluru. Acquire strategically located land. Develop a diversified portfolio. Deliver projects with high-quality standards and on-time.
*   **Pulp & Paper Strategy:** Navigate price pressures through cost management and operational efficiency. Aim for increased capacity utilization. Capitalize on seasonal demand for writing/printing paper and board segments.
*   **Textile Strategy:** Restructure operations by closing spinning/weaving units and moving to an outsourced model for grey fabric. Focus on process house operations and optimizing cash flow. Develop sustainable products.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Context Audit:**
    *   **Real Estate:** Management's narrative of strong demand and a favorable market remains consistent. The proactive acquisition of significant land parcels (Thane and Mathura Road) with substantial GDV aligns with the stated strategy of strengthening presence and project pipeline. The successful launch and rapid booking of Birla Trimaya, Bengaluru, validates the execution of their project launch strategy. Delivery of key projects (Birla Alokya, Birla Vanya) is on track, reinforcing management's commitment to timely execution.
    *   **Pulp & Paper:** The observed price pressure and decline in realizations are consistent with prior commentary. Management's anticipation of prices bottoming out around mid-September and an expected uptick, supported by international pulp price increases, has begun to materialize. The explanation for volume decline (tableware plant disruptions due to floods) provides a specific, albeit temporary, reason for the YoY dip. The focus on cost management to maintain operating profitability, despite lower realizations, remains consistent.
    *   **Textile:** The ongoing restructuring, including the move towards an outsourced model for grey fabric, directly addresses the previous observation of grey fabric no longer being a USP and the objective of enhancing profitability through fixed cost savings. The acknowledgement of potential continued losses in the short term due to fixed costs and demand-supply imbalance is a realistic assessment.
    *   **Macro Environment:** Management continues to acknowledge the challenging global macroeconomic environment, consistent with previous calls.
