## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited (ABREL) reported strong Q3 FY26 performance, driven by robust sales and new project launches. The company achieved presales of INR2,536 crores, a significant 276% year-on-year increase, and collections of INR1,290 crores, up 157% year-on-year. For the nine months of FY26, presales reached INR3,848 crores (+64% YoY), with collections at INR2,347 crores (+44% YoY). Key highlights include the successful and rapid sell-out of Birla Pravaah in Gurugram for INR1,850 crores within 24 hours and the launch of Birla Evam in Manjri, Pune. Management expressed optimism for the upcoming quarter with multiple launches planned. The company also achieved significant operational milestones, including approximately 58 million safe man-hours and safety awards, alongside BREEAM certification for Birla Niyaara. The divestment of the paper business to ITC is on track for completion by calendar year-end 2025.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Focus on Premium & Luxury:** Continued emphasis on premium, luxury, and ultra-luxury segments in key metros like Mumbai, NCR, Bengaluru, and Pune.
*   **Project Launches:** Strategic launches in Gurugram, Pune, and strengthening presence in core markets. Plans for multiple launches in Q4 FY26.
*   **Business Development (BD):** Active pursuit of new projects with a focus on quality deals, prudent pricing, and robust due diligence. Targeting INR10,000 crores GDV by March 31st.
*   **Brand Enhancement:** Principal sponsorship of Gujarat Titans in IPL to further boost brand visibility.
*   **Sustainability & Safety:** Highlighting safety achievements (58 million safe man-hours) and sustainability certifications (BREEAM for Birla Niyaara).
*   **Commercial Portfolio Expansion:** Aiming to develop approximately 1 million sq ft of commercial space at the Niyaara site and seeking partners for commercial ventures. Targeting INR1,000 crores in rental income in the next 4-5 years.
*   **Redevelopment:** Exploring opportunities in redevelopment, particularly in Mumbai.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Sales Momentum (Q3 FY26):** Management has demonstrated exceptional performance in Q3 FY26, significantly exceeding prior year sales and collections. The narrative around Q1 FY26's "consolidation phase" and "planned approach to inventory management" appears validated by the strong Q3 rebound. The earlier delay in Niyaara Phase 3 launch to FY27 was acknowledged but mitigated by strong performance in other launches and ongoing inventory. Management's confidence in exceeding last year's presales target for FY26 remains high.

*   **Project Pipeline & Launches:**
    *   The Q3 FY26 performance aligns with the strategy of a back-ended launch schedule, with significant launches anticipated in Q4 FY26.
    *   The delay in Birla Niyaara Tower C was attributed to unanticipated Supreme Court orders and subsequent approval processes, not a market issue. This aligns with their stated consistency in focusing on "right deals" and navigating approvals.
    *   The increased GDV for the Thane project from INR1,700 crores to INR2,700 crores is explained by strong market demand and prospects.
    *   The scaled-down GDV for Birla Arika and Birla Punya was explained as a strategy to maximize value through redesign and phased launches, not due to demand pressure. This reflects a strategic approach to inventory management rather than a faltering pipeline.

*   **Business Development (BD) Pipeline:** Management reiterated their cautious but active BD approach. While acknowledging the difficulty in confirming exact timings for deals, they remain confident in concluding deals worth at least INR10,000 crores before the end of March. The previous year's success in finalizing INR25,000 crores of projects underscores their capability. The target GDV of INR150 billion by FY28 is still deemed achievable.

*   **Paper Business Divestment:** Progress is confirmed, with CCI approval obtained and awaiting MoEF approval and lease transfer for plant land. Management is hopeful for closure within the next two months, expecting cash inflow before the end of the calendar year 2025.
