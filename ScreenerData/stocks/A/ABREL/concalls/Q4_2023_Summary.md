## Executive Overview

Century Textiles and Industries Limited (CTIL), operating as ABREL, presented an investor presentation focused on its real estate arm, Birla Estates, highlighting its significant growth trajectory and strategic market positioning. The presentation detailed the company's legacy and diversified business model, emphasizing the strength of Birla Estates as a key growth engine. Birla Estates showcased record sales achievements and a robust pipeline of projects across major Indian cities, indicating strong execution capabilities and ambitious expansion plans. The presentation did not cover the performance of the Pulp & Paper or Textile segments in detail but underscored the overall strength and strategic intent of the group, particularly in real estate.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Overall Company Strategy:** CTIL's 125-year legacy is built on national development, quality, innovation, agility, and customer centricity, positioning it to capitalize on market opportunities.
*   **Birla Estates Strategy:**
    *   Aim to be among the top 5 real estate companies in India.
    *   Focus on delivering exceptional and premium home and office spaces.
    *   Establish a strong presence in 4 key macro markets: MMR, NCR, Pune & Bengaluru.
    *   Develop a diversified portfolio of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties.
    *   Build a portfolio with a developable potential in excess of 20 Mn SFT.
    *   Continue establishing a commercial real estate portfolio.
    *   Maintain a robust project pipeline of owned and joint development land parcels.
    *   Actively pursue land acquisitions in chosen markets.
