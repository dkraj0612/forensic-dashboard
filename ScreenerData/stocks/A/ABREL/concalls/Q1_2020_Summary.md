## Executive Overview

Century Textiles & Industries Ltd (CTIL) reported a challenging Q1 FY21, primarily impacted by the "Abnormal Business Environment" due to the nationwide COVID-19 lockdown. The company's operations were severely curtailed, leading to significant drops in capacity utilization, turnover, and EBITDA across its continuing businesses (Real Estate, Pulp & Paper, Textiles). Despite the unprecedented headwinds, CTIL prudently managed cash flows, optimized costs, and actively pursued collections.

The **Real Estate** segment demonstrated resilience, securing INR 42 Crs in leasing collections and INR 4.5 Crs from residential sales (with INR 33 Crs worth of inventory sold). Notably, the segment saw a "strong uptick in demand enquiries and conversions" at launched projects and positive pre-launch responses for the Gurugram project (Birla Navya), indicating underlying market demand. Construction activities restarted in May 2020, and the company is embracing digitalization for sales.

**Pulp & Paper** faced drastically reduced demand, with capacity utilization at 57% and sales revenue halved. However, the business successfully bid for two state government tenders, and its board segment secured "good amount of institutional order flow" from the Pharma and FMCG sectors, partially offsetting the decline in "Away From Home" tissue consumption.

The **Textiles** business was the most severely impacted, with capacity utilization plummeting to 14% and resulting in an EBITDA loss. Idle capacity was partially utilized for R&D, and the company launched "Masks" under the BIRLA-CARE brand, receiving positive market response.

The previously announced cement demerger and associated debt reduction appear to have largely materialized, with consolidated net debt significantly lower than pre-demerger levels, positioning CTIL as a debt-light entity to navigate the current environment.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Overall Strategy:**
    *   **COVID-19 Response:** Acknowledged "Abnormal Business Environment," focused on managing cash flows, optimizing costs, and active collections to mitigate the impact of the nationwide lockdown.
    *   **Digitalization:** Expanding digital sales experience and channel partner network.
*   **Real Estate (Birla Estates):**
    *   **Market Resilience:** Leveraging strong demand enquiries and conversions at launched projects despite lockdown.
    *   **Operational Recovery:** Resumed construction activities in May 2020.
    *   **Sales & Marketing:** Enhanced focus on digital sales and expanding channel partners.
*   **Pulp & Paper:**
    *   **Diversification & Resilience:** Successfully bid for state government tenders to maintain sales volume amidst low demand.
    *   **Product Focus:** Capitalizing on institutional demand for board (Pharma, FMCG) to offset "Away From Home" tissue segment weakness.
*   **Textiles:**
    *   **Innovation & Adaptation:** Partially utilized unused capacity for R&D work and launched "Masks" under the BIRLA-CARE brand as a new product line to adapt to changing market needs.
    *   **Preparedness:** Maintaining capacity in a state of readiness for market stabilization.
