## Executive Overview

Century Textiles & Industries Ltd (CTIL) presented a transformative strategic shift for Q4 2018, primarily driven by the planned demerger of its Cement business. This move is anticipated to significantly reduce the company's debt by Rs. 3,000 Cr, positioning CTIL as a "debt-light" entity. The company intends to leverage this reduced financial burden to expand its core growth businesses: Real Estate (under Birla Estates), Pulp & Paper, and Textiles. A key focus is unlocking the substantial value from its vast owned land parcels, estimated at over Rs. 7,500 Cr, offering significant development potential. Birla Estates is embarking on a capital-efficient, asset-light model through joint development agreements to capitalize on India's booming real estate market. The Pulp & Paper and Textiles segments are slated for modernization and capacity expansion to drive future profitability and growth.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Overall Strategy:**
    *   **Debt Reduction:** Cement business to be demerged with UltraTech Cement (UTCL) in H1 FY2019-20, transferring Rs. 3,000 Crore of debt. This will transform CTIL into a debt-light company, enhancing financial leverage for future expansion, particularly in Real Estate.
    *   **Focus on Core Businesses:** Prioritizing and expanding Real Estate, Pulp & Paper, and Textiles segments.

*   **Real Estate (Birla Estates):**
    *   **Value Unlocking:** Monetizing owned land parcels at premium locations with significant development potential (> Rs. 7,500 Cr value, 13 Mn.Sq.Ft FSI).
    *   **Market Focus:** Concentrating on high-growth markets: Mumbai, NCR, Bangalore, and Pune.
    *   **Model:** Pursuing a capital-efficient, asset-light Joint Development Agreement (JDA) route.
    *   **Product Mix:** Primarily premium/mid-income residential development, with an opportunistic approach to commercial projects.
    *   **Key Drivers:** Rapid urbanization, shrinking household sizes, rising per capita income, government thrust on affordable housing, tax sops, GST rate reduction, and structural changes driven by RERA and GST implementation favoring organized players.

*   **Pulp & Paper:**
    *   **Profitability & Growth:** A profitable business with a current EBITDA Margin of ~22%.
    *   **Expansion & Modernization:** Plans to double tissue capacity and modernize paper plant machines to improve product mix and enhance asset life.
    *   **Key Drivers:** India's low per capita paper consumption (~1/4th of global average), increasing urbanization, government thrust on education, hygiene awareness, and growth in the hospitality sector.

*   **Textiles:**
    *   **Modernization:** Maintaining current capacities while undertaking normal modernization and upgradation of facilities to ensure EBITDA growth.
    *   **Strengths:** State-of-the-Art Vertically Integrated Plant, legacy of over 100 years, strong presence in fine spinning.
    *   **Key Drivers:** Strong industry outlook, increased penetration of organized retail, favorable demographics, rising income levels, abundant raw material (cotton), growing export demand, 100% FDI allowance, and supportive government initiatives (SITP, new textile policy aiming for US$ 300 billion trade by 2025).
