## Executive Overview

Adani Transmission Limited (ATL), a leading infrastructure and utility company, presented its investor update, highlighting its robust operational performance, strategic growth initiatives, and commitment to financial discipline and sustainability. The presentation outlines ATL's dual-pronged approach to growth, leveraging its strong transmission network while expanding its integrated utility operations through Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited (AEML). Key themes include capex-driven growth in its Regulated Asset Base (RAB), enhancing network reliability and affordability, increasing renewable energy procurement, and maintaining a stable regulatory framework. The company emphasized its repeatable, robust, and proven transformative investment model and its commitment to ESG principles.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Total Regulated Asset Base (RAB) (Fully built):** USD 4.3 Bn (INR 300 Bn)
*   **AEML RAB (H1 FY21):** USD 1.7 Bn (INR 117 Bn)
*   **Transmission Network:** 14,837 ckt kms (operational and under-construction as of Sept'20)
*   **EBITDA:** Not explicitly quantified in USD mn for Q3 2020, but the presentation highlights significant EBITDA growth (~24% CAGR FY16-20) and aims for continued expansion. FY20 EBITDA was INR 343 Bn.
*   **Debt Profile:** Average debt maturity of 10.3 years (as of Sept 2020), with a weighted average cost of debt of 9.2%.
*   **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 4.3x (as of Sept 2020).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capex-driven Growth in RAB:** ATL plans significant capex to expand its RAB, particularly at AEML (INR 95 Bn over 5 years, FY20-25) and in transmission projects.
*   **Focus on Reliability and Affordability:** AEML aims for 99.99% supply reliability and affordable tariffs, supported by network strengthening and optimum power purchasing.
*   **Sustainability and Renewable Energy:** AEML is committed to increasing renewable power procurement from 3% to 30% by 2023 and 50% by 2025, supported by a 700 MW hybrid PPA. ATL also focuses on waste management and carbon footprint reduction.
*   **Technology Adoption:** Leveraging advanced technologies like SCADA, LiDAR, drones, and an Energy Network Operating Center (ENOC) for efficient operations, predictive maintenance, and fault detection.
*   **Consumer Centricity:** AEML continues to enhance customer experience with multiple payment options, self-help services, and a focus on 24/7 consumer care.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** QIA's 25.1% equity stake in AEML for INR 32 Bn strengthens governance and provides growth capital.
*   **Regulatory Framework:** Operates within a stable and predictable regulatory framework (EA 2003, CERC, SERC) ensuring tariff determination based on Aggregate Revenue Requirement (ARR) and providing for cost pass-throughs.
*   **Transmission Growth:** ATL aims to reach 20,000 Ckt kms by FY2022 through a robust under-construction pipeline and TBCB bids, capitalizing on India's significant transmission investment opportunity.
