## Executive Overview

Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) delivered a solid Q2 FY2026 performance, with strong execution across its transmission, distribution, and smart metering segments. The company has a substantial INR 60,000 crore transmission order pipeline and a smart metering order book with a revenue potential of INR 29,000 crores (2.46 crore meters). Significant capex deployment is underway, with INR 5,976 crores invested in H1 FY2026, and an annual target of at least INR 10,000-11,000 crores, primarily in the transmission and smart metering businesses. The distribution segment, particularly AEML, continues to generate surplus, enabling debt reduction and bond buybacks. The C&I Power Solution business is expanding its reach, servicing 717 MW of aggregate load demand.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transmission Network Expansion:** Continued focus on winning bids from the identified INR 80,000-90,000 crores pipeline over the next 1-1.5 years, aiming to maintain a 25% market share. Commissioning of key projects like Khavda Phase-II, KPS-1, and Sangod in H1 FY2026, with more expected in H2 FY2026.
*   **Smart Metering Growth:** Rapid deployment and billing of smart meters, targeting 1 crore meter installations by end of FY2026. Ongoing bidding opportunities for a significant portion of the 10 crore smart meter market.
*   **Distribution Business Enhancement:** Pursuing parallel licensing opportunities in Navi Mumbai and Mundra, and Ghaziabad/Jewar. Focus on enhancing AEML's performance through deleveraging and operational efficiency, maintaining low distribution losses.
*   **C&I Power Solution Business:** Expanding service offerings to external consumers, targeting 717 MW of aggregate load demand. Developing district cooling solutions with a capacity of 88,000 tons of refrigeration.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Maintaining high transmission availability and minimizing distribution losses. Investing in technology and training to improve execution and overcome manpower shortages.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context:** Previous summaries indicated a strong focus on transmission expansion, smart metering growth, disciplined capital allocation, and operational execution. Management consistently communicated progress on its pipeline and strategic priorities.

**Current Transcript (Q2 FY2026):** Management's narrative remains consistent with previous communications, emphasizing strong operational execution, transmission pipeline development, and contributions from smart metering. The company has accelerated its capex deployment, particularly in the second half of FY2026, with plans to commission significant transmission projects. The substantial smart metering order book and ongoing deployment demonstrate continued strategic focus. The QIP raise from the previous period is enabling this expansion. The C&I business and cooling solutions are developing as planned. Management effectively addressed headwinds like prolonged monsoons impacting installation rates and right-of-way challenges by highlighting proactive measures like in-house training and a hybrid approach to ROW acquisition. The EBITDA growth trajectory is expected to be second-half weighted due to project commissioning. No significant shift in tone regarding headwinds; instead, the focus remains on proactive project execution and strategic growth initiatives. The EBITDA growth trajectory is expected to exceed 15% and potentially reach over 20%.
