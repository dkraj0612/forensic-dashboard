## Executive Overview

Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) concluded FY2026 with a strong Q4, demonstrating robust operational execution and significant growth across its business segments. The company successfully commissioned the crucial Mumbai HVDC project within FY2026 and surpassed its smart meter installation target, achieving 83 lakh installations against a projected 70 lakh. Capex has significantly increased, nearing INR 15,000 crores for FY2026, with projections for FY2027 and FY2028 also indicating substantial investments. Management highlighted improvements in credit ratings and a reduction in interest costs despite scaling up capex, underscoring financial discipline. The C&I business is emerging as a key growth driver, with substantial contracted capacity and potential in sectors like data centers.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transmission Network Expansion:** Continued aggressive bidding and execution, with a strong identified pipeline of INR 150,000 crores. Focus on both ISTS and increasing intrastate opportunities.
*   **Smart Metering Acceleration:** Aiming to install another 1 crore meters in FY2027, leveraging strong execution capabilities and a large addressable market. Exploring securitization of receivables for funding.
*   **Distribution Business Diversification:** Continued asset hardening and RAB additions at AEML, maintaining service levels and customer tariffs. Tracking UP DISCOM privatization opportunities.
*   **New Business Verticals (C&I and Cooling):** C&I business is positioned as a significant growth driver, offering integrated power solutions including round-the-clock power with renewable and BESS capabilities. District cooling opportunities are being actively pursued in new clusters.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Maintaining high transmission line availability and further reducing distribution losses.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Focus on maintaining healthy credit ratings (AAA+ / AAA) and reducing interest costs through disciplined capital management and refinancing. Funding capex through internal accruals, debt, and securitization.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context:** Previous summaries highlighted a strong transmission order pipeline, rapid smart meter deployment, disciplined capex, and focus on operational efficiency. Management consistently communicated progress on strategic priorities and pipeline development, supported by QIP funding.

**Current Transcript (Q4 FY2026):** Management's narrative remains highly consistent with its previously stated strategies and growth trajectory, with significant progress reported on key execution fronts.

*   **Smart Meter Execution:** Management confirmed exceeding the FY2026 smart meter installation target (83 lakh vs. 70 lakh projected), emphasizing its scale and global comparability. They indicated confidence in installing another 1 crore meters in FY2027.
*   **Transmission Project Execution:** The Mumbai HVDC project was confirmed as fully commissioned in FY2026, aligning with timelines. Discussions on new HVDC projects (Khavda-Olpad, Bhadla-Fatehpur) indicate progress and planned capex deployment in FY2027-2028. Delays in specific projects were not prominently highlighted in this call as in the previous one, suggesting forward momentum.
*   **Capex Deployment:** FY2026 capex was reported at INR 14,431 crores. Significant capex guidance was provided for FY2027 (INR 21,000-22,000 crores) and FY2028 (INR 23,000-25,000 crores), reflecting ongoing commitment to expansion. Capitalization figures for FY2027 and FY2028 were also provided, with significant increases expected from FY2029 onwards due to HVDC projects.
*   **Financial Performance:** Improved credit ratings (AAA+ / AAA) and reduced interest costs were highlighted as key achievements despite increased capex. The company is comfortable with its leverage ratio, aiming to maintain it between 4.5x to 4.7x.
*   **New Business Initiatives:** The C&I business was emphasized as a major growth driver for the coming year, with significant contracted capacity and potential for data centers. Management plans a detailed presentation on C&I in the next fiscal year. District cooling opportunities were also reiterated.
*   **Headwinds Mitigation:** Management noted that supply-related issues for equipment have eased significantly, with new manufacturing capacities being added in India. They also highlighted proactive procurement strategies that have prevented project delays due to equipment delivery. ROW challenges were acknowledged, with discussion on government policies and committee interventions to resolve them.
