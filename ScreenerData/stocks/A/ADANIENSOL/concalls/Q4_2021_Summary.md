## Executive Overview

Adani Transmission Limited (ATL) delivered a robust Q4 FY21 presentation, underscoring its established "Grid-to-Switch" integrated utility platform and leadership in India's transmission and distribution (T&D) sector. The company highlighted its sustained growth in transmission network assets, efficient capital management through low-cost funding and elongated debt maturities, and a significant expansion of its Regulated Asset Base (RAB). ATL emphasized its deep commitment to operational excellence driven by advanced technology adoption (ENOC, SCADA, drones) and a strong focus on ESG principles, aiming for enhanced value creation and sustainable growth. The strategic partnership with QIA in AEML further strengthened its governance and capital structure, positioning ATL to capitalize on substantial future growth opportunities in the T&D space.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Transmission Network (as of Dec 2020):** 15,487 ckt km (operational and under-construction).
*   **Regulated Asset Base (RAB) (Fully-built basis, as of Dec 2020):** INR 314 Bn / US$ 4.3 Bn (excluding HVDC project).
*   **AEML Supply Reliability:** 99.99%.
*   **AEML Network Availability:** ~99.87%.
*   **Distribution EBITDA Margin (9MFY21):** 28%.
*   **Operational EBITDA Margin (Transmission):** 92% (FY20).
*   **O&M Cost:** Rs. 0.19 million per ckt km (one of the lowest globally).
*   **Equity Invested Creation:** Every INR 1 billion of equity invested allows for the creation of INR 2.25 billion of equity employed.
*   **Debt Profile (as of Sep 2020):** Average debt maturity of 10.3 years. Weighted average cost of debt reduced to 9.2% (H1FY21) from 10.9% (Mar 2016).
*   **Liquidity Cover:** Maintained at 1.2x-2x as a policy for all listed entities.
*   **Market Share (TBCB FY20):** 37%.
*   **Average Residual Concession Life:** 31 years (Transmission) / 17 years (Distribution).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated "Grid-to-Switch" Platform:** Leveraging transmission network and AEML's distribution for end-to-end service, covering 3 million+ consumers in Mumbai.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology Adoption:** Achieving high reliability (near 100% line availability), efficiency, and low O&M costs through advanced O&M practices (ENOC, SCADA, predictive maintenance, drone inspection, ERS tool).
*   **Sustainability and ESG Integration:** Deeply embedded ESG framework for value creation, focusing on climate strategy (carbon neutrality, low-carbon tech, renewable energy), circular economy (zero waste to landfill, 100% fly ash utilization), resource management (water neutrality, afforestation), and robust governance.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Focus on self-funded growth, re-designing capital structure through low-cost capital, elongated debt maturity, efficient capital churn, and strategic partnerships (QIA in AEML) for de-risking and value creation.
*   **Growth & Expansion:** Robust under-construction pipeline (Transmission and HVDC), significant capex plan for AEML to grow RAB, and aggressive pursuit of greenfield, acquisition, franchise, and PPP opportunities in the T&D space.
*   **Predictable Regulatory Framework:** Operates within mature and stable regulatory environments (CERC, SERC) with payment pooling mechanisms to mitigate counterparty risk.
