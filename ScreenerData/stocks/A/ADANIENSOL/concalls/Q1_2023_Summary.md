## Executive Overview

Adani Transmission Limited (ATL) continues its robust growth trajectory, showcasing strong financial and operational performance in Q4 FY23 and FY23. The company reinforced its integrated "Grid-to-Switch" utility model, demonstrating significant expansion in its transmission network, improved distribution efficiency, and a strengthened financial profile. ATL's commitment to operational excellence, technological adoption, and ESG principles remains a cornerstone of its strategy, positioning it for continued leadership in the Indian T&D sector. Strategic capital management, including debt diversification and elongation, alongside successful equity infusions, further solidifies its growth outlook.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated "Grid-to-Switch" Platform:** Continued focus on end-to-end power solutions, serving over 12 million consumers in Mumbai and Mundra SEZ.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Maintaining high transmission availability (>99.7%) and distribution reliability (>99.9%) with a focus on low O&M costs.
*   **Sustainability and ESG Integration:** Commitment to Net Zero by 2050, achieving 30% RE procurement, Zero Waste to Landfill, Single-Use Plastic Free sites, and Net Water Positive status. Enhanced ESG ratings from MSCI and FTSE.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Robust capital raising (US$ 700 mn revolving facility, US$ 2 bn GMTN program for AEML), debt diversification, elongation of maturity profile, and securing marquee equity partners (QIA, IHC, GQG).
*   **Growth & Expansion:** Expanding transmission network with a robust under-construction pipeline (INR 220 bn), including smart metering projects and exploring new geographies for distribution through parallel licenses.
*   **Predictable Regulatory Framework:** Operating within stable regulatory environments with proactive engagement, as evidenced by the MERC orders for AEML and MEGPTCL.
