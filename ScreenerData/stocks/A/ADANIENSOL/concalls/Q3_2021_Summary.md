## Executive Overview

This Q3 FY21 earnings presentation for Adani Transmission Limited (ATL) showcases a strong focus on operational excellence and technological advancement within its integrated "Grid-to-Switch" platform. The company highlights its robust transmission network and the performance of its Mumbai electricity distribution utility, AEML. Key themes include the expansion of its transmission network, the strategic adoption of technology for enhanced efficiency and reliability, and a deep commitment to ESG principles. ATL reiterates its role as a leading player in India's T&D sector, emphasizing reliability, affordability, and sustainability, while continuing to grow its asset base and customer reach.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Transmission Network (September 2021):** 18,801 ckt km (operational, under-construction, and LOI assets).
*   **Regulated Asset Base (RAB) (Fully-built basis, September 2021):** INR 459 Bn / US$ 6.3 Bn.
*   **AEML Supply Reliability:** 99.99%.
*   **AEML Network Availability:** 99.87%.
*   **O&M Cost per ckm:** One of the lowest globally, with employee expense per ckm also being competitive.
*   **Incentives Earned:**
    *   HVDC: Average availability is 4.65% above normative over 3 years.
    *   HVAC: Average availability is 1.32% above normative over 3 years.
*   **Electricity Expense Reduction (FY2021):** 43% reduction through solar plant integration for auxiliary consumption.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated "Grid-to-Switch" Platform:** Leverages transmission network and AEML's distribution for end-to-end service.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology Adoption:** Focus on reliability, efficiency, and cost optimization through advanced O&M practices like Predictive and Condition-Based Maintenance (CBM), ENOC (Energy Network Operation Center), and drone surveillance.
*   **Sustainability and Renewable Energy:** Transitioning to green energy for auxiliary requirements, with solar plants commissioned at multiple substations, reducing CO2 emissions. Exploration of micro-grids.
*   **ESG Integration:** Embedded ESG framework for value creation, mapped to UN Sustainability Goals. Initiatives include water conservation, waste management, green initiatives (tree plantation), and support for education and community safety.
*   **Capital Management:** Redesigning capital structure through low-cost capital and elongated debt maturity. Focus on value creation through ROE optimization and efficient O&M.
*   **Growth & Expansion:** Continuing to expand transmission network presence across India and enhancing operational capabilities.
