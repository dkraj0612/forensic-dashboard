## Executive Overview

This investor presentation from Adani Transmission Limited (ATL), dated March 8, 2021, focuses on Adani Electricity Mumbai Ltd. (AEML) and its operational excellence, highlighting a transformative business model driving scale and growth. The presentation underscores AEML's unique position in Mumbai, a complex operating environment, and its strategies to overcome challenges through reliability, responsiveness, and sustainability. Key themes include technological adoption for enhanced operations, consumer-centric service delivery, a strong ESG focus, and financial prudence. The presentation reinforces ATL's strategy of capital expenditure-driven growth in the Regulated Asset Base (RAB) and its commitment to value creation for stakeholders, supported by the onboarding of marquee investors like Qatar Investment Authority (QIA).

*   **Total Regulated Asset Base (RAB) (Estimate):** Not explicitly quantified in this transcript, but implied to be growing with capex.
*   **AEML RAB (Estimate):** Implied to be substantial given the ~INR 95 Bn capex plan for FY20-25 and its role in powering Mumbai.
*   **EBITDA:** FY20 EBITDA is presented in Rs. Crs, though the specific figure is not detailed in the provided text.
*   **Debt Profile:** All listed entities maintain a liquidity cover of 1.2x-2x as a policy. Seven international bonds totaling ~USD 4 Bn were issued in FY20. USD 1.80 billion ECB settlement on a single day was achieved. A USD 400 Mn. CAPEX revolver facility from 8 international banks is in place.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capex-driven Growth:** AEML has a capex plan of Rs. 95 Bn for FY20-25.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Focus on reliability (99.99% supply reliability target implied through case studies of grid disturbance management) and affordability (overall tariff to fall in real terms despite capex).
*   **Sustainability and Renewable Energy:** Target to procure up to 60% renewable power by 2025, achieving COP21 targets 5 years ahead of schedule. Use of environment-friendly dry and ester oil transformers.
*   **Technology Adoption:** Extensive use of SCADA, DMS, GIS, OMS, SAP, AI for forecasting, IoT, chatbots, mobile apps, and digital payments to enhance operational efficiency, customer service, and reliability. "Digital Twin" concept utilized.
*   **Consumer Centricity:** AEML serves 12 million+ consumers in Mumbai, employing initiatives like kiosks, chatbots, digital payments, and a mobile app for enhanced customer experience and grievance redressal. Aims to be a "supplier of choice."
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** QIA acquired a 25% stake in AEML in 2020, with a further 25.1% stake for Rs. 32 Bn mentioned in the historical context, strengthening governance and value creation.
*   **Regulatory Framework:** Operates under a stable and evolved regulatory framework (MERC) based on a rate of return approach on RAB, with cost pass-through and efficiency-linked incentives.
*   **Transmission Growth:** ATL's transmission network stands at 14,837 ckm (as per a comparative slide, though specific target for this transcript is not reiterated from prior context).
*   **Loss Reduction:** Orbital Shift strategy for distribution loss reduction and smart metering deployment (12 million consumers targeted).
*   **Privatization & Competition:** AEML operates in a competitive environment and has successfully managed complex challenges like the largest slum in Mumbai with low distribution losses.
