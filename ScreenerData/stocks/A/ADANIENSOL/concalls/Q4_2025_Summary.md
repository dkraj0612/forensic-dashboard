## Executive Overview

Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) reported a strong performance for FY2025, marked by significant growth in capex, order book expansion, and operational efficiencies across its transmission, distribution, and smart metering businesses. The company doubled its capex in FY2025 to Rs 11,444 crores, with projections for FY2026 to be between Rs 16,000 to Rs 18,000 crores. The transmission order book has reached approximately Rs 60,000 crores, with a healthy pipeline expected to continue due to increasing renewable energy integration needs. The smart metering business has gained considerable traction, with 32 lakh meters installed by the end of FY2025 and a target of 70 lakh meters in FY2026, aiming for a cumulative 1 crore meters. Operational metrics remain robust, with transmission system availability at 99.7% and distribution losses in AEML reduced to 4.7%. The company demonstrated strong EBITDA growth of 23% in FY2025 and anticipates sustained high growth over the next 4-5 years.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transmission Network Expansion:** Focus on green power evacuation projects, maintaining a strong order book of Rs 60,000 crores for execution over the next 4-5 years. Significant opportunities are anticipated at the state level, alongside ISTS projects.
*   **Smart Metering Growth:** Accelerating deployment with a target of 70 lakh meters in FY2026, aiming for a cumulative 1 crore meters by FY2026 end. Positioned to participate in upcoming state bids (Tamil Nadu, MP, Telangana, Karnataka, AP).
*   **Distribution Business Enhancement:** Exploring privatization opportunities in UP, with a transaction advisor appointed and bidding documents expected soon. AEML aims to increase its Regulated Asset Base (RAB).
*   **Operational Excellence:** Maintaining high transmission availability and further reducing distribution losses. Integrating smart metering solutions effectively.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Disciplined bidding to ensure attractive returns and maintain financial metrics. Leveraging internal accruals and debt financing for capex.
