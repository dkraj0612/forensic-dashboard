## Executive Overview

Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) demonstrated continued growth momentum in Q3 FY2025 across financial, operational, and deployment metrics. A significant win was the HVDC Bhadla-Fatehpur transmission project, valued at Rs 25,000 crores capex, which substantially bolstered the transmission order pipeline to Rs 55,000 crores. The company also secured the Khavda Phase IV Part-D (Pune-Boisar) project. Capex deployment accelerated, with Rs 3,000 crores in Q3 FY2025 for transmission alone, bringing the 9-month total to Rs 7,500 crores. Smart meter deployment has gained traction, with 18 lakh meters deployed to date and a target of 40 lakh by year-end FY2025. Operational performance remained strong, with transmission system availability at 99.7% and a reduction in distribution T&D losses to 4.66%. Financially, revenue grew by 15%, operational EBITDA by 9%, and PAT by 80% YoY for the quarter. AESL is strategically selective in future bidding to maintain financial ratios and returns.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transmission Network Expansion:** Aggressively participating in bidding opportunities, aiming for INR 60,000 crores in the next year while remaining selective to preserve financial ratios. Focus on efficient execution and timely commissioning of secured projects.
*   **Smart Metering Growth:** Accelerating deployment with a target of 40 lakh meters by FY2025 end and 70 lakh in FY2026. Leveraging experience to overcome on-ground challenges and outperform competitors. Targeting 20-25% of the remaining 10 crore smart meter market.
*   **Distribution Business Enhancement:** Exploring privatization opportunities in UP. Focused on increasing the Regulated Asset Base (RAB) in AEML, with ~Rs 2,000 crores expected in FY2026. The Mumbai HVDC project remains on track for Q3 FY2026 commissioning.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Maintaining high transmission system availability and further reducing distribution T&D losses. Integrating smart meter solutions effectively with distribution company systems.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Strategic participation in bids, aiming to maintain market share around 20% in transmission. Focusing on efficient capex deployment and prudent debt management.
