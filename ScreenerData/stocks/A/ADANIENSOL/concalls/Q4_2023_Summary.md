## Executive Overview

Adani Transmission Limited (ATL) presented its Q4 and FY23 earnings, highlighting a strong financial and operational performance. The company continues to emphasize its integrated "Grid-to-Switch" utility model, demonstrating robust expansion in its transmission network and improved distribution efficiency. ATL's strategy remains focused on operational excellence, technological adoption, and ESG principles, reinforcing its leadership in the Indian T&D sector. Strategic capital management, including debt diversification and elongation, alongside successful equity infusions, positions the company for sustained growth. This presentation also details the specifics of a new senior secured notes issuance, underscoring prudent financial management and strategic debt optimization.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenue (FY23):** INR 12,149 Cr (19% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated Operational EBITDA (FY23):** INR 5,341 Cr (15% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (FY23):** INR 6,101 Cr (11% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated PAT (FY23):** INR 1,281 Cr (4% YoY growth, including one-time regulatory income)
*   **Consolidated Cash Profit (FY23):** INR 3,411 Cr (12% YoY growth)
*   **Net Debt to EBITDA (FY23):** 4x
*   **Transmission Network Length (FY23):** 19,779 ckt km (operational and under-construction), with 1,704 ckms added in FY23.
*   **Transmission System Availability (FY23):** 99.7%
*   **AEML Supply Reliability (ASAI) (FY23):** >99.9%
*   **AEML Distribution Losses (FY23):** 5.93% (down from 6.55% in FY22)
*   **AEML Collection Efficiency (FY23):** >100%
*   **AEML Energy Demand (Units Sold) (FY23):** 9,062 million units (13.7% YoY growth)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated "Grid-to-Switch" Platform:** Continued focus on end-to-end power solutions, serving over 12 million consumers in Mumbai and Mundra SEZ.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Maintaining high transmission availability (>99.7%) and distribution reliability (>99.9%) with a focus on low O&M costs.
*   **Sustainability and ESG Integration:** Commitment to Net Zero by 2050, achieving 30% RE procurement, Zero Waste to Landfill, Single-Use Plastic Free sites, and Net Water Positive status. Enhanced ESG ratings from MSCI and FTSE.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Robust capital raising (US$ 700 mn revolving facility, US$ 2 bn GMTN program for AEML), debt diversification, elongation of maturity profile, and securing marquee equity partners (QIA, IHC, GQG). Successful issuance of USD senior secured notes for refinancing and general corporate purposes.
*   **Growth & Expansion:** Expanding transmission network with a robust under-construction pipeline (INR 220 bn), including smart metering projects and exploring new geographies for distribution through parallel licenses.
*   **Predictable Regulatory Framework:** Operating within stable regulatory environments with proactive engagement, as evidenced by MERC orders for AEML and MEGPTCL.
