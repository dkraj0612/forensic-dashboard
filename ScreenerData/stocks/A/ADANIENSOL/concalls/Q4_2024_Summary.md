## Executive Overview

Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL) reported a robust Q4 FY2024, characterized by significant operational achievements and a strong pipeline for future growth. The company achieved a key milestone of operationalizing over 20,000 circuit kilometers in its transmission network, including the completion of the critical 400 KV Kharghar-Vikhroli line aimed at reducing Mumbai's congestion and the 765 KV Khavda-Bhuj line for renewable power evacuation. In its distribution segment, Adani Electricity Mumbai (AEML) further reduced distribution losses to a historic low of 5.29% and was recognized as the top utility in India for the second consecutive time. The smart metering business has seen substantial order wins, totaling 22.8 million projects with a contract value of INR 27,000 crores, with implementation already underway. Financially, AESL reported a 17% revenue growth to over INR 14,200 crore for FY24 and a 7% increase in operational EBITDA to INR 5,695 crore. Management expressed optimism about the expanding transmission bidding pipeline, now estimated at INR 1.1 lakh crores, with particular emphasis on HVDC projects and those linked to the Khavda renewable hub.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transmission Network Expansion:** Continued focus on executing the under-construction pipeline and aggressively bidding for new projects, particularly HVDC lines and those supporting the Khavda renewable energy zone. Management is targeting a 20-25% market share in the INR 1.1 lakh crore bidding pipeline.
*   **Smart Metering Growth:** Aggressive deployment of secured smart meter orders (22.8 million) with a total contract value of INR 27,000 crores, expected to be recognized over 9-10 years, with capex incurred over the next 25-30 months. Partnerships for key components and infrastructure are in place.
*   **Distribution Business Enhancement:** Ongoing efforts to improve operational efficiency in AEML, evidenced by historically low distribution losses. Parallel license applications in Navi Mumbai and Gautam Buddh Nagar are under evaluation. Mundra SEZ distribution business is being expanded to cover the entire district.
*   **Strategic Asset Development:** Progressing on the HVDC Mumbai project with an expected completion in Q1/Q2 FY26. Management aims for attractive IRRs (14-15%) on transmission and smart meter projects, prioritizing returns over market share in competitive bids.
*   **Supply Chain Management:** Proactive long-term tie-ups with key equipment suppliers (transformers, substations) to mitigate potential global and domestic supply chain constraints due to high demand.
