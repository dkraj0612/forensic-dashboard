## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported its Q3 FY'24 results, showcasing a period of moderate growth and strategic initiatives. Standalone revenue saw an 11.8% year-on-year increase to INR 285.2 crores for the nine months ending December 31, 2023, with EBITDA growing by 44.6% to INR 70.2 crores and PAT rising by 36.8% to INR 54.3 crores. For Q3 FY'24 specifically, standalone revenue grew by 3.5% YoY to INR 103.2 crores, while EBITDA increased by 2.7% YoY to INR 26.4 crores, and PAT saw a 5.4% YoY increase to INR 20.3 crores. Consolidated revenue for the nine months grew 12.1% YoY to INR 366.7 crores, with EBITDA up 30.4% to INR 70.6 crores, and PAT up 22.6% to INR 48.8 crores. Q3 consolidated revenue increased 5.2% YoY to INR 129.7 crores, though EBITDA saw a marginal decrease of 0.4% YoY to INR 27 crores, while PAT grew 3% YoY to INR 19.1 crores. The company attributed the slower YoY growth in Q3 to the Red Sea crisis impacting sales shipments. Key strategic moves include Board approval for investment in Telluric Foods, aiming for synergies and efficient capital utilization, and the proposed merger of subsidiaries ADF Foods India Limited and Telluric Foods Limited. The company also launched new products under the Ashoka brand and is focused on brand building and capacity expansion for long-term growth and margin enhancement.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Building & Product Innovation:**
    *   Launched new products under flagship brand Ashoka (frozen desserts, canned sweets, Indo-Chinese sauces).
    *   Focus on premiumization and targeting urban, upper-middle-class consumers in India for the ADF Soul brand.
    *   Ashoka brand continues to be the primary revenue driver, contributing 55-60% of standalone business.
*   **India Market Focus (ADF Soul):**
    *   Targeting INR 100 crores revenue within three years.
    *   Currently selling through e-commerce (own website, Amazon, Flipkart, BigBasket, Swiggy).
    *   Plans to expand into modern and general trade in a phased manner.
    *   Monthly revenue run rate at INR 40-45 lakhs, approx. INR 5 crores annually.
*   **International Expansion:**
    *   "Truly Indian" brand: Work in progress for Costco and Walmart listings for next fiscal.
    *   Tesco listing secured in 700 UK outlets for 3 SKUs, aiming for at least $1 million in revenue in FY'25.
    *   US market remains the top international market, with ADF Foods considered a top 3 Indian food brand.
*   **Distribution & Agency Business:**
    *   Supply chain issues with Ekaterra are resolving, with confidence in streamlining for FY'25.
    *   Discussions underway with a couple of new companies for distribution.
    *   Leveraging distribution business to enhance retailer influence and potentially grow own brands.
*   **Capacity Expansion & Operational Efficiency:**
    *   Debottlenecking and cold storage investments nearing completion.
    *   Greenfield project in Surat for ADF Soul brand to commence with INR 60 crores CAPEX for Phase 1 over 15 months.
    *   Existing plants have capacity utilization of 60-80%, with current capacity sufficient for the next 1.5 years before the greenfield plant is operational.
*   **Corporate Restructuring:**
    *   Board approval for investment in Telluric Foods and proposed merger of subsidiaries (ADF Foods India Limited and Telluric Foods Limited) to achieve synergies, efficient capital utilization, and operational flexibility.
*   **Debt-Free Status:** Company continues to maintain a debt-free balance sheet.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q3 FY'24 vs. Previous Guidance):**

*   **Revenue Growth:** The projected 15-20% YoY growth for FY'24 appears to be at risk based on nine-month standalone revenue growth of 11.8%. Management acknowledges losing significant sales in the last 15 days of December due to container availability issues stemming from the Red Sea crisis but expects these orders to materialize in Q4, giving them confidence to still meet their targets.
*   **Distribution Business:** Management acknowledges ongoing supply chain issues with Ekaterra (tea business handover from Unilever) but states that a lot of these issues have been resolved in Q3 and expects good growth over Q2 numbers. They are also in talks with other companies to expand the distribution business, indicating a continued commitment.
*   **Ashoka Brand Growth:** Ashoka brand continues its strong performance, growing at a CAGR upwards of 30%. For FY'24, it is expected to contribute INR 260-270 crores, representing approximately 25% YoY growth, slightly below the previously implied 30% target if calculated from Q2 FY'24 context.
*   **"Truly Indian" Brand & US Market:** Discussions for Costco and Walmart listings under "Truly Indian" are ongoing for the next fiscal year, aligning with previous timelines. The UK listing in Tesco (700 outlets) is a significant positive development.
*   **Nates and PJ's:** No progress update provided. While legal cases and fixed costs have reduced, the entity still incurs legal costs which are expected to cease in the next financial year.
*   **Greenfield Project (Surat):** Remains on track, with Phase 1 CAPEX of INR 60 crores over 15 months starting from April '25.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Standalone EBITDA margins remain strong and are expected to be maintained. Consolidated EBITDA margins saw a slight YoY dip in Q3 FY'24, but management expects improvement as supply chain issues normalize and brand building investments in Telluric yield results. The company aims to maintain current margin levels (standalone 25.6%, consolidated 20.8% in Q3).
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Current plant capacity utilization is between 60-80%, with existing facilities expected to suffice for the next 1.5 years.
