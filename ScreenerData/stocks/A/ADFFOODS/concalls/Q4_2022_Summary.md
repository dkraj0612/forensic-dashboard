## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited (ADFFOODS) concluded FY2022 with a record consolidated revenue of Rs. 421 Crores, marking a 14% year-on-year growth. The fourth quarter (Q4 FY2022) saw a 5% increase in revenue to Rs. 108 Crores, with gross profit rising by 25% to Rs. 57 Crores. Despite facing significant global supply chain disruptions and increased freight and input costs, the company managed to maintain healthy profitability, with EBITDA at Rs. 15 Crores and PAT at Rs. 11 Crores in Q4 FY22. For the full year, EBITDA stood at Rs. 67 Crores, PBT at Rs. 65 Crores, and PAT at Rs. 48.5 Crores, a slight decrease of 3% YoY, resulting in a PAT margin of 11.5%. The company continues to be net debt-free with strong return ratios (ROCE of 19%, ROE of 23%). Management highlighted continued focus on capacity expansion (Greenfield and Brownfield), distribution network strengthening, and growing the agency distribution business. New initiatives include the launch of a new brand "Khansaama," incorporation of an e-commerce subsidiary "Telluric Foods," and an exclusive distribution alliance with Patanjali Ayurveda for the UK and Western Europe.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Revenue Growth:** Driven by robust volume offtake, introduction of new products, and entry into new geographies.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Ongoing investments in Greenfield and Brownfield projects, including acquiring land for cold storage expansion near the Nadiad factory, to unlock further capacity.
*   **Distribution Network:** Strengthening direct distribution through investment in distribution centers and warehouses to enhance supply chain control and retailer reach.
*   **New Brand Launch:** Introduction of "Khansaama" brand, featuring kababs, curries, breads, and pastes, aimed at expanding product offerings.
*   **E-commerce Expansion:** Incorporation of a new subsidiary, "Telluric Foods," to manage buying, selling, and distribution of food products via e-commerce and other channels in India. This platform will initially launch ADF's own products under the "Soul" brand and later include imported and other Indian products.
*   **Patanjali Alliance:** Exclusive distribution of Patanjali products in the UK and Western European markets, commencing in FY23, to cater to the growing Indian diaspora.
*   **QSR & Cloud Kitchen Vertical:** Actively pursuing opportunities in this segment, having secured the first order with a domestic chain. ADF possesses the necessary infrastructure to cater to these needs.
*   **Plant-Based Foods:** US subsidiary is actively producing plant-based products (e.g., meatless meatballs). Discussions are underway for expanding the product range into plant-based foods for the international market, with potential sourcing from India.
*   **Product Development & Innovation:** Continuous focus on launching new and innovative products while maintaining high-quality standards and customer centricity.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Actively evaluating and in discussions for potential inorganic acquisition opportunities.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (FY2022 vs. Previous Guidance):**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved consolidated revenue of Rs. 421 Crores (14% YoY growth), meeting the expectation of continuing growth from previous periods and surpassing the Rs. 100 Crore quarterly revenue target consistently. Full year revenue growth was strong.
    *   **Profitability:** The gross margin ratio improved significantly to 50% from 42% in FY21, effectively mitigating the impact of freight costs and raw material inflation through price action and better product mix. However, consolidated EBITDA and PAT margins saw a slight decrease in FY22 compared to FY21, which management attributed to global supply chain disruptions, freight rate increases, and input cost inflation. Despite this, absolute profitability was maintained or improved in some areas.
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Management reiterated the importance of capacity expansion through Greenfield and Brownfield investments and reported progress in securing land for cold storage expansion. The focus on debottlenecking and modernization continues.
    *   **Distribution Network:** The company emphasized its investments in distribution centers and warehouses for direct distribution, which has helped gain supply chain control. The Patanjali alliance was executed for UK and Europe, with the business commencing in FY23.
    *   **Agency/Distribution Business:** This segment grew by 12% YoY, contributing to overall growth. The management previously indicated a focus on maintaining the balance between own brands and distribution business.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines (from Q4 FY2022 transcript):**
    *   **Patanjali Distribution (FY23):** Expected revenue of £1-2 million (approximately Rs. 10-20 Crores) in the first year. This is a pure distribution arrangement where ADF's UK entity will buy products from Patanjali and distribute them in ethnic Indian stores in the UK and Europe.
    *   **PLI Scheme Incentive:** Received eligibility of approximately Rs. 8 Crores for FY22. Actual booking of PLI revenue was around Rs. 7.5 Crores for FY22, to be received in the current year (FY23). The company has been granted Rs. 62 Crores under the PLI scheme for marketing over five years (until FY26), which will support increased branding and promotional spend.
    *   **EBITDA Margins (FY23 Outlook):** Management acknowledged the volatility of commodity prices and continued cost pressures. While committed to improving margins and aiming to return to previous levels, they could not provide a specific range (18-20%) due to ongoing volatility, emphasizing continued efforts for price increases and cost-cutting.
    *   **Inorganic Acquisitions:** Actively evaluating opportunities and in discussions with potential targets. Aiming to execute something in the first quarter of FY23.
    *   **Promoter Share Pledge:** The pledge of 10.25 lakh shares was for paying off warrants. Management hopes to clear this off in the next 1-2 years. Promoter holding increased to 36.5% from 31.5%.
    *   **Cash Balance and Investment Returns:** Approximately Rs. 80 Crores in cash is invested in bank fixed deposits and mutual funds, yielding 4-5% return. This cash is earmarked for acquisitions and internal growth/CAPEX.
    *   **Raw Material & Logistics Prices:** Situation remains volatile, especially for commodity prices. Management is implementing price increases and leveraging their position as large buyers and exporters for preferential rates.
    *   **QSR & Cloud Kitchen Vertical:** Actively pursuing this segment. Secured the first order with a domestic chain last week and expects continued growth in this space.
    *   **Plant-Based Foods:** US subsidiary is active in plant-based products. Discussions are ongoing for expanding the range for the international market.
    *   **Telluric Foods (E-commerce Subsidiary):** Road map includes launching own "Soul" brand products initially, followed by other imported and Indian products. Targeting Rs. 5 Crores in revenue in the first year and Rs. 50-60 Crores within 4-5 years. Initial years may be in investment mode without immediate profits, but healthy margins are expected thereafter.
    *   **Africa Market Exploration:** Management will explore opportunities in African markets, potentially leveraging the Patanjali relationship.
    *   **Dividend Policy:** Intention to maintain dividend payments while retaining cash for expansion plans.
    *   **Outlook for FY23:** Expectation to maintain the growth trajectory, contingent on avoiding COVID or supply chain disruptions.
