## Executive Overview

ADF Foods reported Q1 FY'26 consolidated revenues of INR 132.9 crores, a 10% YoY increase. Consolidated EBITDA stood at INR 23.5 crores with a margin of 17.7%. The company navigated global economic uncertainties, tariffs, and seasonal fluctuations through disciplined cost management and operational efficiencies. Key developments include a brand refresh for "Truly Indian" with new packaging rolling out in Q3 FY'26, and positive early outcomes from sales team reorganizations in the US and Australia. The Surat greenfield facility remains on track for H2 FY'26 commissioning. Management remains cautiously optimistic about sustaining growth amidst ongoing uncertainties.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Refresh & Expansion:** "Truly Indian" brand refreshed with new identity and packaging rollout in Q3 FY'26. New listings secured for "Ashoka" and "Truly Indian" in major retail chains including Costco (Australia & US) and Tesco (UK). "Truly Indian" now in 1,600 US stores.
*   **Sales & Distribution Reorganization:** Strategic reorganization of sales teams in the US and the establishment of a new team in Australia are showing positive results in new listings.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Surat Greenfield facility on track for Q3 FY'26 commissioning, with a capacity of 10,000 metric tons (Phase 1) for frozen products. Brownfield expansion at Nadiad and Nasik facilities also ongoing.
*   **Product Development:** Focus on new frozen product lines at Surat facility. Expanded SKU offerings and store access for Tesco in the UK.
*   **Cost Management & Efficiency:** Disciplined cost management and operational efficiencies implemented to mitigate rising input costs and brand investments.
*   **Distribution Business:** Margins in the distribution business have slightly improved due to marketing incentives and integration into pricing. Expected steady-state margin of 12-14%.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth (Q1 FY'26 Consolidated):** Achieved 9.3% YoY revenue growth, aligning with the "cautiously optimistic" outlook for sustaining the growth trajectory, though down QoQ due to seasonal factors.
*   **EBITDA Margins (Q1 FY'26 Consolidated):** Demonstrated a healthy 17.7% margin, increasing 160 bps YoY, indicating successful mitigation of rising input costs and brand investment pressures through cost management.
*   **Brand Investment Impact:** Management continues to invest in brands ("Truly Indian" and SOUL) and marketing spend remains at 7-8% of top line, a consistent strategy from prior periods.
*   **ADF SOUL Performance:** Management acknowledged that SOUL has "not gone as we had planned earlier" and has revised the 3-year forecast downwards to INR 50-75 crores. This is a factual shift from previous optimism.
*   **"Truly Indian" Brand (US):** Showing positive momentum with brand refresh and new listings; management reiterates confidence in its growth. EBITDA break-even is now a focus, with potential impact from US tariffs being monitored.
*   **Ashoka Brand Performance:** Growth in FY'25 was affected by distributor and sales team reorganization, and temporary production issues at Nadiad due to a GPCB notice. Management is confident of returning to growth in coming quarters.
*   **Surat Greenfield Project:** Commissioning timeline shifted slightly to Q3 FY'26 from H2 FY'26, but remains on track.
*   **Distribution Business (Lipton US):** Full US distribution rights were secured from the last quarter, with growth expected to materialize from Q2 onwards.
*   **Tariffs (US):** Management is actively monitoring the dynamic tariff situation. They reiterate the strategy to pass on a portion of any increased tariffs (estimated 10-15% at MRP) across the value chain rather than absorbing the full amount. Clarity on final tariffs is awaited.
*   **Nadiad Facility:** Production was impacted in April due to a GPCB notice but has normalized from May.
*   **UK FTA:** Zero duty is a positive and expected to result in cost savings.
