## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited (ADFFOODS) reported Q1 FY23 consolidated revenues of INR 97.2 Crores, marking a 12.5% year-on-year growth. Despite this growth, profitability was significantly impacted, with EBITDA at INR 9.1 Crores (9.3% margin) and PAT at INR 7.6 Crores (7.9% margin). The company faced persistent challenges from high raw material inflation, increased freight costs (reaching 16% of revenue), and supply chain disruptions in its U.S. subsidiary. To mitigate these pressures, ADF implemented a selective price hike in June, with the full impact expected in subsequent quarters, and is observing a softening trend in raw material and freight costs.

Strategically, ADF continues to strengthen its product portfolio with new ready-to-eat/cook and food accompaniment launches under the flagship "Ashoka" brand. A significant step into the QSR segment was announced with an in-principle approval to invest $1 million for a minority stake in NaanSense operating LLC, a US-based Indian QSR startup, positioning ADF as a preferred supplier. The e-commerce subsidiary, Telluric Foods, is set to launch products under the "Soul" brand in the Indian market from Q2 FY23. Capacity expansion remains a focus, with land acquired for a new Greenfield plant, targeting commissioning by H2 FY24. While demand in the US saw a temporary slowdown due to holiday travel, management anticipates normalization from August and is confident in executing its growth strategies.

## Key Financial Metrics

**Q1 FY23 Highlights:**
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 97.2 Crores (12.5% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated Gross Profit:** INR 47.9 Crores (26.9% YoY growth)
*   **Gross Margin Ratio:** 49.2% (up from 42% in FY21, comparable to 50% in FY22)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 9.1 Crores (9.3% margin)
*   **Consolidated PAT:** INR 7.6 Crores (7.9% margin)
*   **Processed & Preserved Food Revenue:** INR 78.6 Crores (12.8% YoY growth)
*   **Agency Distribution Revenue:** INR 18.6 Crores (10.7% YoY growth)
*   **Freight Cost:** ~16% of top line (up from ~14% in FY22)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **New Product Development:** Launched new products under the "Ashoka" brand in ready-to-eat, ready-to-cook, and food accompaniments.
*   **E-commerce Expansion (India):** "Soul" brand products (pickles, sauces, chutneys) are ready for launch in the Indian market from Q2 FY23 via the Telluric Foods e-commerce subsidiary. Telluric Foods will provide backend support for Indian operations.
*   **QSR & Cloud Kitchen Vertical:**
    *   Secured second order with a domestic chain in India.
    *   In-principle board approval for a $1 million strategic investment in NaanSense operating LLC, a US-based startup in ethnic Indian QSR/fast casual dining, with ADF becoming a preferred supplier.
*   **Patanjali Alliance (UK/Europe):** First shipment received in May; Q1 FY23 revenues of GBP 35,000-38,000.
*   **Agency Distribution Expansion:** Signed a new distribution agreement for a strong brand of frozen products from Malaysia; first shipment arrived last month.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Brownfield:** Consolidation of previous year's CapEx for streamlined processes.
    *   **Greenfield:** Land (approx. 15,000 sq m) acquired in a Mega Food Park (150km from flagship plant). Application for Ministry of Food Processing grant in process, expecting in a couple of months. Target commissioning by H2 FY24.
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Implemented selective price hikes of 8-10% on some products (3-5% overall impact) in June to offset cost inflation.
*   **Forex Impact:** Favorable USD trajectory helps top line, partially offset by GBP depreciation.
*   **Distribution Network:** Investing in warehouses (two taken on lease in US, 100,000 sq ft) for direct distribution, aiming to add 4 more DCs over the next two years in the US to improve margins.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q1 FY23 vs. Previous Guidance):**
    *   **Revenue Growth (FY22 guidance: maintain growth trajectory):** Achieved 12.5% YoY growth in Q1 FY23, indicating consistency in maintaining a growth trajectory.
    *   **Profitability/EBITDA Margins (FY22 guidance: committed to improving margins, aiming for 18-20% but with volatility):** Q1 FY23 EBITDA margin was 9.3%, falling significantly short of the 18-20% range. Management acknowledged impact from high raw materials, freight, and US subsidiary supply chain issues. The gross margin, however, improved to 49.2%, suggesting effectiveness in price action and product mix, but operating costs remain a challenge. The narrative is consistent with acknowledging volatility but actual performance is below the aspirational target.
    *   **Patanjali Distribution (FY22 guidance: £1-2 million revenue in FY23):** Actual Q1 FY23 revenue was only £35,000-38,000. Management *significantly lowered* the full-year FY23 expectation to at least £0.5 million, indicating a slower-than-anticipated ramp-up.
    *   **PLI Scheme Incentive (FY22 guidance: ~Rs. 7.5 Crores for FY22 to be received/booked in FY23):** Management stated MOFPI pushed PLI incentive applicability by one year (FY23-FY27), and as a conservative position, no PLI income was booked in Q1 FY23. This represents a *delay and deviation* from previous booking expectations.
    *   **Inorganic Acquisitions (FY22 guidance: aiming to execute something in Q1 FY23):** Instead of a full acquisition, ADF announced a strategic *investment* ($1M, minority stake) in NaanSense LLC, a QSR startup. This fulfills the spirit of exploring inorganic growth but with a different scope than a full acquisition.
    *   **QSR & Cloud Kitchen Vertical (FY22 guidance: secured first order):** Q1 FY23 confirmed supplying to one domestic chain and being on the second order, further demonstrating progress by investing in NaanSense LLC. This shows *consistent progress*.
    *   **E-commerce (Telluric Foods) (FY22 guidance: launching "Soul" brand, targeting Rs. 5 Cr revenue in first year, Rs. 50-60 Cr in 4-5 years):** Q1 FY23 confirmed products are ready for launch in Q2 FY23 under the "Soul" brand, showing *consistent progress* on the timeline.
    *   **Capacity Expansion (FY22 guidance: ongoing investments in Greenfield/Brownfield):** Q1 FY23 provided detailed updates on the Greenfield plant (land acquired, grant application, H2 FY24 target), demonstrating *consistent progress*.
