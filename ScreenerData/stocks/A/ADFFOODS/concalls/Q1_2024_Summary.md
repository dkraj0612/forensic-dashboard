## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported robust Q1 FY'24 results, demonstrating significant year-on-year growth. Standalone revenue increased by 17.2% to INR 84.6 crores, with EBITDA nearly tripling to INR 21.1 crores, resulting in an improved EBITDA margin of 24.9%. Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by 121% to INR 16.4 crores with a PAT margin of 19.3%. On a consolidated basis, revenue grew by 15.7% to INR 112.4 crores, with EBITDA and PAT increasing by 141.4% and 92.7% respectively, leading to improved consolidated margins of 19.5% for EBITDA and 13.1% for PAT. The company attributes this performance to operational efficiencies, softening input and freight costs, and successful product launches.

## Key Financial Metrics

**Q1 FY'24 Highlights (Standalone):**
*   **Revenue:** INR 84.6 Crores (17.2% YoY growth)
*   **EBITDA:** INR 21.1 Crores (190.4% YoY growth)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 24.9% (up 14.8 ppts YoY)
*   **PAT:** INR 16.4 Crores (121.0% YoY growth)
*   **PAT Margin:** 19.3% (up 9.1 ppts YoY)

**Q1 FY'24 Highlights (Consolidated):**
*   **Revenue:** INR 112.4 Crores (15.7% YoY growth)
*   **EBITDA:** INR 21.9 Crores (141.4% YoY growth)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 19.5% (up 10.2 ppts YoY)
*   **PAT:** INR 14.7 Crores (92.7% YoY growth)
*   **PAT Margin:** 13.1% (up 5.2 ppts YoY)

**Cost Trends:**
*   **Freight Costs:** Standalone freight as a percentage of sales is stable at approximately 7% (consolidated also around 7%). Management expects this to remain stable for the year.
*   **Other Expenses:** Increased due to investments in the "Truly Indian" brand launch and test marketing.
*   **Distribution Business Margins:** Lower due to product mix, with management expecting EBITDA margins in the 9-10% range.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product Innovation:** Launched two new product lines under the Ashoka Brand: Frozen Curries (Palak Paneer, Punjabi Choley, Dal Makhani) for foodservice and Pure Butter Ghee in various pack sizes.
*   **International Expansion:**
    *   Secured listings for three Ashoka SKUs with a discounter and 15 SKUs (ambient and frozen) with a supermarket chain in the UK.
    *   Walmart Canada continues to be a key market with 12 SKUs across ambient and frozen categories.
    *   "Truly Indian" brand is being launched in frozen format and pitched to US retailers.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   New large cold storage capacity built in New Jersey warehouse to enhance product fulfillment and support future frozen product expansion.
    *   Greenfield project in Surat remains on track for groundbreaking in Q2 FY'24, with commercial production expected within 18 months. Total CAPEX of INR 80 crores is planned for this project, with an expected 3x revenue return (approximately INR 250 crores).
    *   Additional freezer capacity being set up at the Nadiad plant.
*   **Brand Focus:** Ashoka brand continues to grow at a high rate, having crossed INR 200 crores in FY'23 with a 33% CAGR.
*   **Distribution:** Distribution business saw significant sequential and YoY growth (28.6% and 40.4% respectively) due to streamlined supply chain post-Unilever to Ekaterra transition and the addition of complementary products. Distribution strategy leverages cross-selling opportunities with own brands.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q1 FY'24 vs. Previous Guidance):**

*   **Q1 Performance:** Management's expectation of consistently improving Q1 results was met, with the bottom line more than doubling year-on-year.
*   **Margin Improvement:** The predicted margin increase due to operational efficiency and softening input/freight costs materialized, leading to substantial EBITDA margin expansion.
*   **New Product Launches:** The launch of frozen curries and ghee aligns with the stated focus on innovation.
*   **US Warehouse Capacity:** The new cold storage in New Jersey is operational, fulfilling a strategic objective for enhanced logistics.
*   **Ashoka Brand Growth:** Continued high growth for the Ashoka brand was reported, consistent with previous commentary.
*   **UK Listings:** Successful listings in the UK are a positive development against stated international expansion goals.
*   **Greenfield Project (Surat):** Remains on track with groundbreaking expected in Q2 FY'24, aligning with prior timelines.
*   **Distribution Business Performance:** Growth and margin expectations (9-10% EBITDA) are being managed, with product mix identified as the key driver for current margin levels.
*   **Nate's and PJ's Business (ADF Foods USA):** While samples have been submitted, final supermarket acceptance is pending. Management is focused on reducing fixed costs in this entity and expects improved performance in FY'24, though a specific recovery timeline from the previous year's losses was not explicitly reiterated.
*   **Unilever/Ekaterra Transition:** Supply chain issues have been streamlined, contributing to distribution business growth.
*   **PLI Scheme:** INR 1.5 crores was booked in this quarter, continuing the trend of benefit realization.
*   **B2B/Private Label Margins:** Management confirmed that margins in the B2B and large supermarket chain supply segments are comparable to their own brands.
*   **Promoter Share Pledge:** Management confirmed no additional pledging of shares, with existing pledges related to last year's preferential warrant funding.
