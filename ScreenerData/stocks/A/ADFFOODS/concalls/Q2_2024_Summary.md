## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported a solid Q2 FY'24, demonstrating continued year-on-year growth across key metrics. Standalone revenue increased by 17% to INR 97.3 crores, with EBITDA rising by 46.2% to INR 22.7 crores, resulting in an improved EBITDA margin of 23.3%. Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by 35.4% to INR 17.7 crores, with a PAT margin of 18.2%. On a consolidated basis, revenue grew by 16.8% to INR 124.6 crores, with EBITDA increasing by 20.8% to INR 21.8 crores, and PAT growing by 9.8% to INR 14.9 crores. The company attributed the standalone performance to operational efficiencies and product mix, while acknowledging that consolidated margins and profit were impacted by investments in brand building (Telluric subsidiary) and ongoing supply chain challenges within the distribution business. The company also announced a special dividend of INR 4 per share to reward shareholders.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Human Capital Enhancement:** Hired seasoned industry veterans: Arjuun Guuha (Director of Operations, Whole-Time Director) to oversee operations, planning, and greenfield expansion; Balark Banerjea (President, India domestic business) to drive ADF Soul growth; and Neetu Gursahani (Head of Marketing and Branding) to build brands.
*   **Brand Focus:**
    *   **ADF Soul:** Plans to introduce frozen products next year (Q2 FY'25). Currently focusing on shelf-stable products. A INR 5 crore investment is committed for FY'24 to promote the brand in India. The aim is to reach INR 50 crores top-line within three years.
    *   **Truly Indian:** Showcasing products to US supermarkets, with listings expected from FY'25. Investment of approximately $0.5 million planned for FY'25 in the US, primarily for listing fees. International expansion is aggressive, targeting the mainstream market.
    *   **Ashoka:** Continues to be the flagship brand, sold in over 55 countries, catering to the ethnic diaspora.
    *   **"Camel" and "Aeroplane" Brands:** Growing at approximately 20% YoY. Plans for a range of hot sauces under the "Camel" brand.
*   **Product Development:** Developing frozen Indian desserts, plant-based products, and street food products.
*   **Distribution & Agency Business:**
    *   **Ekaterra (Tea Brands):** Significant decline due to ongoing supply chain issues faced by ekaterra. Expected improvement in supply from Q4 FY'24 and normalization from FY'25.
    *   **Patanjali:** Limited market in the UK due to regulatory/labeling compliance issues; no growth expected for FY'24. Discussions ongoing for compliance.
    *   **Unilever:** Launched Knorr Soups (pre-made in Canada) last month. In discussions for other portfolio products, including potential ice cream distribution pending Unilever's back-end development.
    *   **New Client:** In discussions for a new beverage client, with a potential launch in Summer and sales commencing from Q1 FY'25.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Brownfield and debottlenecking investments at Nadiad and Nashik facilities completed.
    *   Lease facility at Surat transferred to Nadiad, saving lease overheads and improving efficiency.
    *   New cold storage capacity in New Jersey, USA, is operational to enhance fulfillment and support frozen product expansion.
    *   **Greenfield Project (Surat):** Groundbreaking expected in Q2 FY'24. Commercial production within 18 months. Phase 1 CAPEX of INR 50 crores planned for FY'24/FY'25, Phase 2 CAPEX of INR 30 crores thereafter. Expected INR 250 crores revenue contribution.
*   **International Business:** UK listings (Morrison's) are contributing positively, with GBP 1 million expected from these listings next year. Canada and UK together contribute around 25% of revenue. Demand in US and UK markets remains good, with continued portfolio expansion and distribution growth.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q2 FY'24 vs. Previous Guidance):**

*   **Q2 Performance:** Management's expectation of continued steady growth was met, with both standalone revenue and profit showing strong year-on-year increases. The interim announcement of improved performance in Q2 was validated by the financial results.
*   **Margin Improvement:** Standalone EBITDA and PAT margins expanded year-on-year, aligning with the strategy of operational efficiencies and product mix. However, consolidated EBITDA and PAT margins saw a slight decrease or marginal increase, attributed to investments in brand building for the Telluric subsidiary and the impact of the struggling distribution business.
*   **New Product Launches:** Launches under the Ashoka brand have shown encouraging response, though it's early days. The "Truly Indian" brand is in the presentation stage with US retailers, with listings expected from FY'25, aligning with the phased launch strategy.
*   **US Warehouse Capacity:** Operational, as previously stated, supporting future frozen product expansion and distribution.
*   **Ashoka Brand Growth:** Continues to be the dominant brand, as expected.
*   **UK Listings:** Morrison's has listed products and they are performing well, contributing to international revenue growth.
*   **Greenfield Project (Surat):** Remains on track, with groundbreaking expected in Q2 FY'24 and commercial production within 18 months, consistent with prior timelines.
*   **Distribution Business Performance:** The commentary confirms significant headwinds for the distribution/agency business, particularly with Ekaterra. While management expects improvement from Q4 FY'24 and normalization from FY'25, the current performance is below expectations and has impacted consolidated margins. The earlier stated strategy of leveraging distribution for own brand penetration is facing challenges due to issues with partners like Ekaterra and Patanjali.
*   **Nate's and PJ's Business (ADF Foods USA):** No positive update, with samples still under review and no feedback received, indicating a stalled progress on this front.
*   **Unilever/Ekaterra Transition:** The anticipated smooth transition has not materialized, causing supply chain issues for the agency business. Management remains optimistic about a recovery.
*   **PLI Scheme:** INR 1.5 crores was booked in Q1, and a further INR 4 crores in export incentives were received in Q2 FY'24 (included in revenue). Annual PLI application is underway.
*   **B2B/Private Label Margins:** Not explicitly discussed in this transcript, but the focus on own brands like Soul and Truly Indian suggests a strategic shift or continued emphasis on higher-margin offerings.
*   **Promoter Share Pledge:** Not discussed in this transcript.
*   **Dividend:** A special dividend of INR 4 per share was announced, which the company confirmed is funded by sufficient cash reserves.
