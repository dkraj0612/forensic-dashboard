## Executive Overview

ADF Foods reported a solid Q3 FY'25 with consolidated revenues growing 13.8% YoY to INR 147.5 crores, driven by consistent demand across its brands. The company managed to sustain EBITDA margins in the high teens despite investments in brand development, increased raw material and freight costs, which were partially offset by cost control measures and rupee depreciation. The India-focused ADF SOUL brand is expanding into quick commerce and select modern trade outlets (Nature's Basket, Reliance Fresh, Haiko, Food Square, Dorabjee's, DMart in Mumbai and Pune). The Nadiad cold storage facility is now operational, enhancing supply chain capabilities. The Surat greenfield facility is on track for a second half of FY'26 commissioning. Management reiterates confidence in future growth driven by investments in ADF SOUL and the "Truly Indian" brand, as well as the private label business.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Expansion:** Continued focus on core brands with investments in ADF SOUL (India) and "Truly Indian" (US).
*   **Channel Development (SOUL):** Expansion into quick commerce and select modern trade outlets in Mumbai and Pune. Future strategy targets 40% modern trade, 40% quick commerce, 20% e-commerce for FY'26.
*   **International Market Presence:** "Truly Indian" brand at 1,300 stores in the US. Ashoka brand continues to perform well.
*   **Private Label Business:** Growing alongside "Truly Indian" brand penetration, with expected good growth in FY'25.
*   **Capacity Enhancement:** Nadiad cold storage operational. Surat greenfield project progressing towards H2 FY'26 commissioning.
*   **Investment Phase:** ADF SOUL and "Truly Indian" brands are in an investment phase, with marketing and team-building efforts.
*   **Product Diversification:** SOUL brand launching frozen foods including breads (parathas, kulchas, naans) and snacks (paneer pakoras, samosas) with a plan for ~15 SKUs in the next three months.
*   **Distribution Business:** Management views it as strategically important for brand strengthening and operating cost reduction. Expects positive returns once supply chain issues are resolved.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth (Q3 FY'25):** Consolidated revenue grew 13.8% YoY to INR 147.5 crores. This performance is a step-up from Q1 FY'25's 8.1% growth. Management attributes the Q3 sequential dip to the Diwali stocking effect in Q2 and carry-forward from earlier container availability issues. This narrative is consistent with previous explanations of volatility in the distribution segment.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Consolidated EBITDA margin at 17.9% saw a YoY decline of 290 bps. Management attributes this to increased raw material prices, freight costs, and investments in brand development. This is consistent with their stated strategy of investing in growth brands, even if it temporarily impacts margins. They reiterate the goal of achieving high-teen EBITDA margins on a yearly basis.
*   **ADF SOUL Growth:** The brand has entered modern trade in Mumbai and Pune, with plans to expand. The monthly run rate is around INR 70 lakhs. Management expects it to take another 12 months to break even at the EBITDA level. This timeline is more detailed than the previous Q1 FY'25 update, which projected break-even by Q2 FY'26.
*   **"Truly Indian" Brand & US Market:** The brand remains at 1,300 stores in the US, with early signs of good secondary sales from promotions. Management expects contributions to start from Q1/Q2 FY'26 (a slight shift from the Q3 FY'25 expectation in the prior call). They expect it to break even at EBITDA level in 6-8 months. The investment in the US business via ADF UK Limited for working capital and "Truly Indian" brand support is confirmed.
*   **Greenfield Project (Surat):** Commissioning is now expected in H2 FY'26, a slight delay from the September 2025 target mentioned previously.
*   **Distribution Business:** Management continues to emphasize its strategic importance, despite volatility. They project steady-state annual revenue between USD 12-14 million once supply chain issues are resolved.
*   **Margins Impacted:** Management quantified the Q3 margin dip, stating freight costs had a 3% impact, marketing spend around 1.5%, and raw material costs around 1.5%.
*   **Investment in Talent:** Management confirmed building dedicated teams for "Truly Indian" and private labels in the US, and a new team for the SOUL business in India, with further hires planned.
