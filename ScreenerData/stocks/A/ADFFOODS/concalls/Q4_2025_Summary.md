## Executive Overview

ADF Foods reported Q4 FY'25 results with consolidated revenues reaching INR 590 crores for the full year, a 13.3% YoY increase. Consolidated EBITDA stood at INR 98.3 crores, with a margin of 16.7%. The company experienced headwinds from rising raw material, labor, and freight costs, impacting EBITDA margins. Strategic investments in brand development for ADF SOUL (India) and "Truly Indian" (US) are ongoing. The company secured nationwide distribution rights for Lipton teas in the US and expects this vertical to show steady growth. The Surat greenfield facility is on track for H2 FY'26 commissioning. Management remains committed to achieving strong financial growth, with a FY'27 revenue target of INR 1,000 crores and high-teen EBITDA margins.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Investment:** Continued significant investment in "Truly Indian" (US) and ADF SOUL (India) brands.
*   **"Truly Indian" Expansion:** Brand refresh with new visual identity. Presence in over 1,400 US stores, with growth expected to accelerate. Fourfold revenue increase in FY'25 (on a small base).
*   **ADF SOUL (India):** Launched frozen food range (parathas, kulchas, naans, snacks) complemented by existing dips and pickles. Products available via quick commerce and select modern trade. Reorganized SOUL team with dedicated investments.
*   **Ashoka Brand:** Steady growth in most markets, though experienced flat growth in the US in FY'25 due to strategic adjustments. Management is optimistic about regaining mid-teen growth in the US. New team in Australia to strengthen narrative.
*   **Distribution Business:** Secured nationwide distribution rights for Lipton teas in the US. Transition issues from Unilever to Ekaterra are resolved, expecting improved supply and steady growth.
*   **Capacity Enhancement:** Surat greenfield facility on track for H2 FY'26 commissioning to support frozen product lines.
*   **Private Label/B2B:** Constitutes ~30% of revenue, operating on healthy gross margins (30%+). Expects to remain at similar levels.
*   **International Markets:** Besides US, focusing on growth in Australia (targeting 40-50% growth), Germany, UK, and the Middle East.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth (Full Year FY'25):** Consolidated revenue grew 13.3% YoY to INR 589.6 crores. This is broadly consistent with prior year growth and management's stated goals.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Consolidated EBITDA margin at 16.7% saw a YoY decline of 350 bps, exceeding the previous quarter's concern of 290 bps YoY decline. Management attributes this to rising raw material, labor, and freight costs (each impacting standalone margins by 1.25%, 1%, and 1.4% respectively), as well as increased brand and marketing expenditures. This explanation aligns with previous calls about investing in growth brands and navigating cost pressures.
*   **Brand Investment Impact:** Management quantified the investment in "Truly Indian" and SOUL brands at INR 15 crores for FY'25, impacting the bottom line. They stated that without this investment, the bottom line would have been INR 15 crores higher. This reiterates their strategic decision to invest for future growth, even if it impacts near-term profitability.
*   **ADF SOUL Performance:** Management acknowledges that ADF SOUL has underperformed expectations, achieving INR 6 crores top-line and necessitating a cautious approach to reduce cash burn, with a planned investment of another INR 9 crores. This is a shift from previous confidence in SOUL's growth momentum. The guidance for SOUL for the next 3 years is now INR 50-75 crores.
*   **"Truly Indian" Brand (US):** Achieved a fourfold increase in top line in FY'25 and is present in 1,400 stores. Management continues to invest in the brand, expecting it to grow upwards of 100% in FY'26. EBITDA break-even for this business is now targeted in 6-8 months.
*   **Surat Greenfield Project:** Commissioning remains on schedule for H2 FY'26, a consistent target.
*   **Distribution Business (Lipton US):** Management confirmed securing nationwide rights for Lipton teas in the US and resolved supply side issues from Unilever transition. They anticipate better numbers in the next financial year.
*   **Nadiad Facility:** The facility is operational with a 3-month extension from GPCB to comply with requirements. No shutdown occurred.
*   **Tariffs (US):** Management is monitoring the 10% tariff to be effective end of May. They plan to absorb part of it through cost controls and pass on the remainder through the value chain, impacting distributors, retailers, and minimally consumers. No significant pushback or demand impact is seen yet.
*   **UK FTA:** Zero duty is a welcome change, expected to result in cost savings rather than price reductions. UK contributes ~20% to standalone revenue.
