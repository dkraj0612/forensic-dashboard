## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported record-breaking results for FY '23, achieving the highest-ever standalone and consolidated revenue and Profit After Tax (PAT) in the company's history. This strong performance underscores sustained demand for its products and the positive impact of strategic initiatives in product portfolio enhancement, new product introductions, and marketing investments. Standalone revenue for FY '23 reached INR 353.3 crores (17% YoY growth), with EBITDA of INR 76.8 crores and PAT of INR 60 crores. Consolidated revenue for the year was INR 450.3 crores (6.9% YoY growth), with EBITDA of INR 80.6 crores and PAT of INR 55.9 crores. The company highlighted a softening trend in freight costs, which positively impacted margins, while acknowledging ongoing inflationary pressures on certain raw materials, which are being managed through cost optimization and operational efficiencies. Key developments include the launch of 35 new products under the flagship Ashoka brand, expansion into plant-based meats and frozen desserts, and a strategic investment in Telluric Foods India to develop the "Soul" brand. Operational enhancements through debottlenecking at existing plants and a planned greenfield expansion in Surat are underway to drive future growth.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Focus:** Ashoka brand crossed INR 200 crores in revenue, growing at a CAGR of 33.2% over the last two years. "ADF Soul" is positioned for India, with a D2C listing strategy and urban-centric focus, planned to move into supermarkets and general trade within 5 years. Truly Indian brand is targeting mainstream clientele outside the South Asian diaspora, currently in Germany and the US.
*   **New Product Development:** Launched 35 new products under Ashoka, entering plant-based meats and frozen desserts categories.
*   **International Expansion:** ADF U.S.A. subsidiary focused on distribution business growth and increasing Ashoka brand presence. Patanjali distribution in the UK generated GBP 1 million in revenue and is expected to double this year, with expansion into Europe planned by H2 FY'24. Discussions for a new distribution agency agreement are in final stages.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Debottlenecking at Nadiad and Nashik plants completed, yielding INR 30 crores potential revenue from a INR 5 crores capex. Greenfield expansion in Surat planned to break ground in Q2 FY'24, with commercial production expected within 18 months. Total capex for Surat is INR 80 crores (INR 50 crores Phase 1, INR 30 crores Phase 2), expecting a 3x return (~INR 250 crores revenue).
*   **Marketing & Distribution:** Increased marketing spend on a 360-degree approach (digital, TV, sampling, in-store). Focus on expanding distribution in the U.S. across general trade (South Asian Diaspora) and modern trade/club stores (Truly Indian brand). Food service channel development for college campuses and convenience stores also initiated.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (FY '23 vs. Previous Guidance):**
    *   **Record Revenue & PAT:** Management achieved its objective of record standalone and consolidated revenue and PAT for FY '23, exceeding historical highs.
    *   **Freight Costs:** The stated expectation of softening freight costs materialized and positively impacted margins, as anticipated.
    *   **Raw Material Inflation:** Management acknowledged ongoing inflationary pressures, consistent with prior commentary, and highlighted mitigation strategies.
    *   **ADF Soul Brand Investment:** Management confirmed the "ADF Soul" brand in India is in an investment mode for the next 2 years, aligning with the strategy of building awareness before moving to supermarket penetration. The INR 2 crore loss is attributed to this brand-building investment.
    *   **Greenfield Project:** The Surat greenfield project remains on track, with groundbreaking anticipated in Q2 FY'24, within the previously communicated timeframe.
    *   **U.S. Operations (ADF Foods USA - PJ's and Nate):** The previously identified supply chain issues due to supplier litigation were a significant headwind, causing a INR 7 crore adverse effect on profitability (as per Shardul Doshi's statement on consolidated PAT reduction, and INR 5 crores loss attributed to supply chain disruption in FY'23). Management's prior outlook for resolution within 3-6 months has shifted, with hopes for resolution by September FY'24 due to ongoing litigation and the need to find alternate suppliers. The prior EBITDA margin expectation of 12-15% for this business is on hold pending resolution.
    *   **Distribution Business (Unilever/Ekaterra Transition):** The previously noted "lower sales" due to transition have stabilized. Management expects growth from the Ekaterra business and Patanjali.
    *   **PLI Scheme:** The company booked INR 8 crores of PLI income in FY'23, reflecting ongoing benefits.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines (from Q4 FY'23 transcript):**
    *   **Standalone Business Growth Target:** Endeavor to double standalone revenue every 3 years, targeting ~25% YoY growth.
    *   **Sustainable Margins:** Aim to maintain standalone EBITDA margins in the high 20s and consolidated margins in the low to mid-20s. While Q4 standalone margins were 28.8%, management aims to maintain annualized margins close to these levels, acknowledging some benefits will be passed to consumers.
    *   **Ashoka Brand Growth:** Expects continued aggressive growth.
    *   **Truly Indian Brand Growth:** Expects growth in the US market as product portfolio expands.
    *   **U.S. Business Outlook (ADF Foods USA):** Hopes to resolve litigation and find alternate suppliers by September FY'24. Expects positive contribution and improved performance (better than last year's INR 5 crore loss) from this entity in FY'24, with potential for revenue generation.
    *   **Patanjali Growth:** Expecting at least 100% growth in FY'24 for the UK business, and planning to start Europe business in H2 FY'24.
    *   **Greenfield Project (Surat):** Groundbreaking in Q2 FY'24, with commercial production within 18 months post-groundbreaking. Phase 1 capex INR 50 crores, Phase 2 capex INR 30 crores, expecting minimum 3x revenue contribution (~INR 250 crores).
    *   **New Distribution Agreements:** Hopeful to finalize at least one new agency distribution agreement in the U.S. within the next 2-3 months, with supplies expected by September.
    *   **Distribution Business Growth:** Expecting 10-15% growth from existing distribution (Ekaterra, Patanjali) and 15-20% growth including the new agency.
    *   **Core Brand Growth:** Targeting ~25% growth for own brands.
    *   **PLI Scheme Earnings:** Total potential incentive of INR 61.35 crores over FY'23-FY'27, based on marketing and brand promotion spending. Expected to book increasing amounts year-on-year, potentially up to INR 15-16 crores in the final year. Booked as income from operations.
    *   **Acquisition Strategy:** Actively evaluating acquisition proposals, particularly in international markets (especially the U.S.) for complementary products, brands, and distribution, due to higher value propositions compared to India.
    *   **Capacity Support:** Debottlenecking in existing plants has created INR 100 crores revenue potential. The leased plant has INR 30 crores revenue potential. Total current capacity supports ~INR 130 crores of additional top-line growth.
    *   **ADF Soul India:** In investment mode for the next 2 years, with a INR 2 crore loss attributed to brand building.
