## Executive Overview

ADF Foods reported a strong Q4 FY'26, achieving consolidated revenues of INR 196.7 crores, a 23.7% YoY increase, and hitting an all-time high. Standalone revenues grew 11.6% YoY to INR 150.3 crores. Consolidated EBITDA reached INR 34.3 crores with a 17.4% margin, while standalone EBITDA surged 24.8% to INR 36.5 crores. The company successfully commenced operations at its Surat greenfield facility in Q4 FY'26, with Phase 1 now operational. Management expressed cautious optimism despite ongoing geopolitical challenges and supply chain issues, attributing growth to strong brand momentum for Ashoka and accelerating traction for Truly Indian.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Momentum:** "Ashoka" brand continues strong diaspora demand. "Truly Indian" shows accelerated growth with new listings in major US chains (Costco, Raley's, Safeway-Albertsons, Whole Foods) reaching ~3,000 stores. Received NEXTY and Freezies Awards for Truly Indian products.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Surat greenfield facility Phase 1 operational since March 2026. Phase 2 slated for Q3 FY'27.
*   **Market Penetration:** Deepening penetration in existing markets and exploring new ones for both Ashoka and Truly Indian.
*   **Geopolitical Challenges:** West Asia conflict impacted GCC sales significantly (~80-85% reduction in March/April). Shipping availability remains a major challenge for GCC. Other markets have longer transit times and slightly increased freight rates.
*   **Product Innovation:** Introduction of pizza bases at Surat facility (Phase 2).
*   **Brand Investment:** "Truly Indian" remains in an investment mode.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth (Q4 FY'26 Consolidated):** Achieved 23.7% YoY growth, aligning with a strong performance narrative. Management attributed this to new listings and brand penetration. Volume growth accounted for 60-65% of Q4 FY'26 revenue growth, with some benefit from rupee depreciation and product mix.
*   **EBITDA Margins (Q4 FY'26 Consolidated):** Consolidated EBITDA margin at 17.4% (190 bps YoY expansion) indicates effective cost management and improved product mix, as stated. Standalone EBITDA margins expanded to 24.3% (260 bps YoY).
*   **Tariff Situation (US):** Application for refund of over $1.5 million made; awaiting outcome in FY'27.
*   **Capacity Constraint Narrative:** Surat plant operationalization addresses future capacity needs. Existing facilities saw growth through debottlenecking and capacity additions.
*   **Surat Greenfield Project:** Phase 1 operational as targeted in March 2026. Full ramp-up to ~INR 200-250 crores top-line potential is expected within 3 years.
*   **Brand Investment:** "Truly Indian" continues to be in an investment mode, projected to reach INR 75-80 crores in FY'27. Break-even at subsidiary level in ~3 years.
*   **Forex Impact:** Rupee depreciation provided some benefit, alongside volume growth and product mix, contributing to the revenue growth.
*   **Middle East Impact:** Management factually stated an 80-85% impact on GCC business due to shipping unavailability, resulting in insignificant sales in March/April and a significant risk to the revenue guidance if sustained. GCC business accounts for under 15% of overall revenues.
*   **PLI Incentive:** Received INR 16 crores for FY'26. This is the last year of the current scheme. Expectation of similar range for FY'27.
*   **Distribution Business:** Growth driven by adding new complementary brands and increasing SKUs (from 440 to ~600).
