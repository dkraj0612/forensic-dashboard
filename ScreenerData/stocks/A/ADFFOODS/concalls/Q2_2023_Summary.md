## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported Q2 FY23 with consolidated revenues of INR 106.7 Crores, a 12.7% increase year-on-year, and H1 FY23 consolidated revenues of INR 203.9 Crores. Standalone revenues for Q2 FY23 were INR 83.2 Crores (12.8% YoY growth) and for H1 FY23 were INR 155.3 Crores (17.3% YoY growth). The company demonstrated resilience amidst ongoing inflationary pressures on raw materials, though freight costs have shown a declining trend. Profitability improved sequentially, with standalone Q2 FY23 PAT at INR 13.1 Crores (15.7% margin) and consolidated Q2 FY23 PAT at INR 13.6 Crores (12.7% margin). A significant development was the launch of the "ADF Soul" brand in India via e-commerce channels, offering healthy and tasty premium products, with initial traction being encouraging. The US subsidiary, ADF Foods USA, continues to face supply chain disruptions, significantly impacting its contribution to revenue and profitability, with a reported loss of INR 3 Crores and a topline impact of INR 25 Crores in H1 FY23 compared to INR 3 Crores profit in H1 FY22. Management expressed confidence that the worst for the US subsidiary is behind them and anticipates improvement. The company is also advancing its distribution business, with strategies to expand its reach and product portfolio.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **New Product Development:** Continues to launch new products in ready-to-eat, meal accompaniments (pastes, pickles) under existing brands.
*   **E-commerce Expansion (India):** Launched "ADF Soul" brand in India via own e-commerce website and platforms like Amazon. Focus on healthy, tasty, premium products targeted at millennials. Plans to add more international gourmet products.
*   **Distribution Network:**
    *   **US Subsidiary (ADF Foods USA):** Continues to face supply chain disruptions, impacting sales and profitability. Management is optimistic that the worst is behind them and expects improvements.
    *   **Agency Distribution:** Transition period due to Unilever selling its tea brands to a PE firm caused flattish sales in Q2 FY23 for this segment (impacted ~7-8% of sales or INR 16 Cr in Q2). Expects recovery from January.
    *   **Patanjali:** Launched in the UK, on track to achieve the GBP 0.5 million target for FY23. Expanding to other European countries.
    *   **New Agency Brands:** In discussions with two more companies for distribution agreements, with one expected to finalize by year-end.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Greenfield Project:** Total CAPEX of INR 60 Crores planned, primarily for frozen foods (replicating Nadiad lines) and later ambient products. Target commissioning by H2 FY24. Estimated revenue potential of INR 170-180 Crores at full capacity.
    *   **Brownfield Expansions:** Additional CAPEX of INR 10 Crores planned for existing plants (Surat, Nasik, Nadiad).
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Selective price increases were taken in previous quarters to mitigate raw material inflation. Standalone PAT margin improved sequentially due to cost control, operational savings, and prudent price increases.
*   **Marketing:** Significant investment planned for the "ADF Soul" brand in India, focusing on social media campaigns starting from November FY23. Approximately INR 3 Crores allocated for marketing spend in the next 5 months (remaining FY23). Breakeven for "ADF Soul" is targeted for FY24.
*   **QSR Investment:** The $1 million strategic investment in NaanSense LLC is currently on hold pending further discussions and clarity, with a decision expected before the end of the fiscal year.
