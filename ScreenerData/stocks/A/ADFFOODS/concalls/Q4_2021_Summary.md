## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited (ADFFOODS) reported strong Q4 FY21 and full-year FY21 results, driven by record revenues and robust profitability. The company achieved its highest ever revenues in FY21 at Rs. 370 crores, a nearly 30% increase YoY. This growth was attributed to increased demand for convenient and healthy ready-to-eat foods due to global stay-at-home orders. Management highlighted a three-pronged growth strategy: increasing capacities, strengthening distribution, and growing the agency business, with significant progress made across all fronts. The company maintains a strong balance sheet with zero debt and a healthy cash position, indicating a focus on profitable growth and shareholder returns.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Revenue Growth Drivers:** Increased demand for convenient and healthy ready-to-eat foods, product portfolio expansion across categories and geographies.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Commercial production started at a leased facility in Surat (1,950 metric tonnes capacity).
    *   Initiated Greenfield expansion on 7 acres in South Gujarat for Ready-to-Eat, Frozen Food, and Condiment categories, targeting ~9,000 metric tonnes additional capacity. Phase 1 estimated at Rs. 60 crores CAPEX, expected by beginning of FY2024.
*   **Distribution Network Strengthening:** Leased 66,000 sq ft warehouse space in the US as a distribution center. Increased product line listings with supermarket chains in Canada and the US.
*   **Agency Business:** Steady offtake in US and UK markets with additional products added for distribution.
*   **Product Innovation:** Launched several product variants in frozen snacks, vegetable, and ambient ranges. Plans to launch new Indian meat product brands in the US and UK.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Strong balance sheet with zero total debt and significant cash reserves. Focus on judicious investment for superior shareholder returns.
*   **Indian Market Entry:** Phased approach starting with E-commerce, then modern trade, and general trade over three years, aiming for 10-15% of overall sales.
