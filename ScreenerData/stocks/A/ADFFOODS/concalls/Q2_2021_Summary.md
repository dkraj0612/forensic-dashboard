## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited (ADFFOODS) presented its Q2 FY22 results, indicating a period of growth despite significant global supply chain challenges. The company reported a 16% YoY increase in revenue for both the quarter and the half-year, driven by volume offtake and strong demand. However, margin compression was a notable headwind, primarily attributed to global container shortages and soaring freight costs. Despite these challenges, management highlighted efforts in cost optimization, price adjustments, and a favorable sales mix that helped sustain profitability. The company continues to focus on product innovation and expanding its distribution network.

## Key Financial Metrics

**Q2 FY22 Highlights:**
*   **Revenue from Operations:** 16% YoY growth
*   **EBITDA & Margin:** 14% YoY growth
*   **Profitability:** Margin compression noted due to increased freight costs (from 3% of revenue in Q2 FY21 to 11% in Q2 FY22).

**Half-Year FY22 Highlights:**
*   **Revenue from Operations:** 16% YoY growth
*   **EBITDA & Margin:** 17% YoY growth

**FY21 Consolidated Financial Performance (as a comparison point):**
*   **Total Income:** 19% 3-year CAGR
*   **EBITDA & EBITDA Margin:** 23% 3-year CAGR
*   **PBT & PBT Margin:** 28% 3-year CAGR
*   **PAT & PAT Margin:** 41% 3-year CAGR

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Revenue Growth Drivers:** Volume offtake and strong demand contributed to the 16% YoY revenue growth.
*   **Product Innovation:** Dedicated R&D team is developing innovative products and variants, with recent launches in Sauces and Frozen Snacks.
*   **Market Focus:** Continued emphasis on export markets with a strong presence in the US and growing presence in the UK. Distribution strategy involves moving up the value chain and improving supply chain management.
*   **Operational Strengths:** Operates three state-of-the-art manufacturing units with various accreditations (USDA Organic, Bureau Veritas, UKAS, BRC). Undertaking brownfield and debottlenecking efforts to unlock additional capacities (~20%).
*   **Distribution Network:** Robust global distribution network with country managers for key regions. Established a step-down subsidiary in New Jersey, USA, and leased a second warehouse.
*   **Brand Portfolio:** Diverse product range across categories including frozen snacks, RTE, meal accompaniments, pastes, and sauces. Operates distinct brands targeting different consumer segments and geographies (e.g., ethnic stores vs. mainstream).
*   **Distribution Business:** Distributing synergistic products for a Fortune 500 FMCG company to offer a wider product portfolio to retailers.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Focus on improving cash generation, maintaining a robust balance sheet, and generating strong returns for shareholders. Returned Rs 57.1 crores to shareholders over the last 5 years.
