## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported its Q4 FY'24 results, showcasing a strong performance with record-breaking revenue and profitability. Consolidated revenues surpassed INR 500 crores for FY'24, a significant milestone. The company achieved consolidated EBITDA margins of 20.2%, exceeding its target of high teens. On a standalone basis, EBITDA and PAT have nearly doubled over the past two years, indicating effective operating leverage. Q4 FY'24 consolidated revenues saw a robust 24.8% YoY increase to INR 153.6 crores, driven by demand-led growth across all brands. The flagship Ashoka brand continues to expand with new products and market entries, while the India-focused Soul brand shows promising initial response with plans for modern trade launch in FY'25 and a target of INR 100 crores revenue within 3-4 years. The "Truly Indian" brand has launched in the U.S. with several supermarket listings, though it remains in an investment phase. The agency distribution business faced supply chain challenges but is expected to improve in FY'25. Management remains optimistic about FY'25, projecting revenue growth upwards of 20% and high-teen consolidated EBITDA margins. Significant investment is planned for the Soul and "Truly Indian" brands, alongside a INR 75 crores commitment for Phase 1 expansion of the Surat greenfield project.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Focus and Innovation:** Continued emphasis on brand building for Ashoka (flagship, targeting diaspora) and "Truly Indian" (mainstream US audience). Launch of new products under Ashoka and expansion of Soul brand with new categories (frozen foods, sauces, dips) planned for FY'25.
*   **India Market Expansion (Soul Brand):** Targeting INR 100 crores revenue within 3-4 years. Initial base for Soul around INR 3 crores, with a current run rate of INR 6 crores. Aiming for INR 30 crores revenue in FY'25. Modern trade launch planned for Q4 FY'25. Expected to break even in Year 3.
*   **International Market Growth ("Truly Indian" Brand):** Launched in the U.S. with listings in 2 large chains (approx. 350 outlets). Aiming for broader supermarket penetration. Also present in over 1,500 stores in Germany and 500-600 stores in the UK across major chains. Canned and frozen sweets currently for international markets.
*   **Agency Distribution Business:** Hampered by supply chain issues from principal (Lipton Tea Company/Ekaterra) but expect normalization and good growth in FY'25. Exploring discussions with 1-2 new companies for selective distribution partnerships. B2B/private label business accounts for 23% of standalone revenue, with potential to grow to 30% with selective private label business.
*   **Capacity Expansion & Operational Efficiency:** Greenfield project in Surat (Gujarat Agro Food Park) underway, expected to be ready in 15 months. Existing facilities (Nashik, Nadiad) operating at 70%+ capacity with ongoing brownfield expansions.
*   **Investment Mode for New Brands:** Soul and "Truly Indian" brands are in an investment phase for the next 2-3 years, with significant marketing and operational investments.
