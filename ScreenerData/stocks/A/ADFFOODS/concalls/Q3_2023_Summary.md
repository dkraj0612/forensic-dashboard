## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported a strong Q3 FY23, achieving the highest-ever quarterly revenue and PAT on both consolidated and standalone bases. Standalone Q3 FY23 revenue reached INR 99.8 crores (14.5% YoY growth), with EBITDA of INR 25.7 crores and PAT of INR 19.2 crores. Consolidated Q3 FY23 revenue was INR 123.2 crores (5.2% YoY growth), with EBITDA of INR 27.1 crores and PAT of INR 18.5 crores. The company demonstrated resilience amidst ongoing raw material inflationary pressures, benefiting from a softening trend in freight costs. Key strategic initiatives include the expansion of the "ADF Soul" brand with new international gourmet products and new ready-to-eat/cook products under existing brands, including plant-based meat options. The company also received a significant INR 10 crores grant for its proposed Greenfield project in Surat.

## Key Financial Metrics

**Q3 FY23 Highlights (Consolidated):**
*   **Revenue:** INR 123.2 Crores (5.2% YoY growth, 15.4% QoQ growth)
*   **EBITDA:** INR 27.1 Crores (37.5% YoY growth, 50.2% QoQ growth)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 22% (520 bps YoY improvement, 510 bps QoQ improvement)
*   **PAT:** INR 18.5 Crores (15% margin, 340 bps YoY improvement, 230 bps QoQ improvement)

**Q3 FY23 Highlights (Standalone):**
*   **Revenue:** INR 99.8 Crores (14.5% YoY growth, 19.9% QoQ growth)
*   **EBITDA:** INR 25.7 Crores (79.1% YoY growth, 66% QoQ growth)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 25.8% (930 bps YoY improvement, 720 bps QoQ improvement)
*   **PAT:** INR 19.2 Crores

**Cost Trends:**
*   **Freight Cost:** Declined significantly to ~12% of standalone topline in Q3 FY23 (down from a peak of ~20%).
*   **Raw Material Costs:** Continued to face inflationary pressures.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **New Product Development:** Launched new international gourmet products (dips, sauces, curries) under "ADF Soul," and ready-to-eat/cook products, including plant-based meat options under Ashoka and Khansaama brands.
*   **E-commerce Expansion (India):** "ADF Soul" brand response is encouraging, with plans to expand to more e-commerce platforms and target INR 1 crore monthly sales by April.
*   **Distribution Business:** Transition issues related to Unilever's tea business sale have stabilized, with expected improved performance from Q4 FY23 onwards. Patanjali distribution is progressing well in the UK, with expansion into Holland planned. Discussions for new agency distribution agreements are in final stages.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Received an INR 10 crore grant for the proposed Greenfield project at Surat, Gujarat. Debottlenecking efforts are underway at the Nadiad facility to improve capacity utilization.
*   **Marketing:** Continues aggressive marketing spend, including listing fees, sampling, social media, and television advertising, with PLI scheme expected to boost these initiatives.
*   **Market Focus:** Prioritizing key markets (North America, Europe, GCC) which constitute ~80% of revenue, while maintaining presence in smaller markets without significant additional effort. Efforts are ongoing to increase presence in mainstream channels in the US and UK.

*   **Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q3 FY23 vs. Previous Guidance):**
    *   **Nates and PJ's Business:** Management acknowledged significant negative impact. In H1 FY23, this business incurred a loss of INR 4 crores, a deterioration from a INR 3 crores profit in the prior year's comparable period. While previous guidance suggested a resolution within 3-6 months, the current transcript indicates an endeavor to resolve issues by September FY24. The business contributed INR 8.5 crores to revenue and INR 3 crores to profit in Q3 FY22. The like-to-like growth for other businesses was approximately 17%.
    *   **EBITDA Margins:** The company achieved consolidated EBITDA margins of 22% in Q3 FY23, significantly exceeding the previous guidance range of 15-18% for FY23 and demonstrating strong operational performance and benefits from freight cost reduction. Standalone margins also hit record levels.
    *   **Freight Costs:** Management's expectation of a softening trend in freight costs has materialized, with costs down to 12% of standalone topline in Q3 FY23.
    *   **Distribution Business (Unilever Transition):** The "flattish" sales noted previously due to the Unilever transition have now stabilized, with management expecting improvement from Q4 FY23, aligning with earlier anticipation of recovery.
    *   **Patanjali Revenue (FY23):** While not explicitly restated for FY23, progress in the UK and expansion to Holland indicates ongoing strategy execution.
    *   **Greenfield Plant Commissioning:** The INR 10 crore grant for the Surat project is a positive development, with capex commitment for the project now effectively INR 50 crores after the grant, remaining on track for execution.
