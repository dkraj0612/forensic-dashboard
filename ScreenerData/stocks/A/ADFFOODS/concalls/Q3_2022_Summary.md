## Executive Summary: ADF Foods Q3 FY2022 Earnings Call

ADF Foods Limited (ADFFOODS) demonstrated robust performance in Q3 FY2022, reporting a consolidated revenue growth of 19% YoY to Rs. 119 Crores, with a significant standalone revenue increase of 28% YoY to Rs. 87 Crores. This growth was primarily driven by strong volume offtake, particularly during the festive season, coupled with effective price hikes, an improved sales mix, and manufacturing optimizations. These strategic measures successfully mitigated the impact of rising freight and input costs, leading to a substantial improvement in gross margins. The distribution business also experienced a strong quarter, with revenues growing 67% YoY to Rs. 26 Crores, driven by demand in both the US and UK markets.

## Key Financial Metrics

**Q3 FY2022 Highlights:**
*   **Total Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 119 Crores (19% YoY growth)
*   **Standalone Business Revenue:** Rs. 87 Crores (28% YoY growth)
*   **Distribution Business Revenue:** Rs. 26 Crores (67% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated Gross Profit:** Rs. 65 Crores (53% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated Gross Margin:** 54.5% (up from 42.5% in Q3 FY21)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 20 Crores (Margin of 16.9%)
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs. 14 Crores (Margin of 11.5%)

**9 Months FY2022 Highlights:**
*   **Total Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 319 Crores (18% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated Gross Profit Margin:** 50%
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 17.4%
*   **Consolidated PAT Margin:** 11.7%
*   **Net Cash Position:** Rs. 48.4 Crores

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Revenue Growth:** Driven by strong volume in core export brands, supported by price increases and an improved product mix.
*   **Distribution Business Expansion:** Strategic alliance with Patanjali Ayurved Limited for exclusive distribution in UK and Western European markets, aiming to expand product basket and engage the Indian diaspora.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Ongoing debottlenecking and modernization of existing units to unlock ~20% additional capacity. Brownfield expansions are also underway.
*   **Distribution Network Enhancement:** Continued investment in distribution centers and warehouses for direct distribution, gaining supply chain control and retailer reach. Plans to open additional warehouses in the US.
*   **Product Mix Focus:** Emphasis on higher-margin segments like ready-to-eat and frozen foods, which are growing faster.
*   **Indian Market Entry:** Phased approach starting with e-commerce, followed by modern and general trade, with a long-term goal of 10-20% contribution to overall sales.
*   **US Market Growth:** Continued investment in distribution for brands like PJ's and Nate's, focusing on expanding distribution and launching new products, targeting 15-20% annual growth for the US business.
*   **New Verticals:** Focused effort to cater to the Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) and cloud kitchen segment, establishing a dedicated vertical.

The business mix remains skewed towards own brands (79%) vs. distribution (21%), with a focus on maintaining this emphasis.
