## Executive Overview

ADF Foods Limited reported a mixed Q1 FY'25, with consolidated revenues growing 8.1% YoY to INR 121.6 crores, a performance deemed "decent" despite lost sales in June due to container unavailability. The company highlighted continued strong demand across its brands. The flagship Ashoka brand saw product additions and market penetration, while the India-focused Soul brand is progressing as planned and gearing up for strategic expansion into quick commerce and modern trade channels in H2 FY'25, aiming for INR 100 crores revenue within 3-4 years. The US expansion of the "Truly Indian" brand is on schedule, with new supermarket listings and an Amazon online presence, with contributions expected from Q3 FY'25. The agency distribution business saw a decrease due to prior supply chain issues, but optimism remains for good growth in FY'25 as these resolve. Management reiterates a positive outlook for FY'25, projecting revenue growth upwards of 20% and consolidated EBITDA margins in the high teens, despite planned investments in the Soul and "Truly Indian" brands. The Surat Greenfield Phase 1 project is on track.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Building and Expansion:** Continued focus on Ashoka's growth through new products and market penetration. Soul brand expansion in India is on track, targeting INR 100 crores in 3-4 years, with plans for modern trade launch in Q4 FY'25 and break-even in Year 3. "Truly Indian" brand in the US is expanding with new supermarket listings and Amazon presence, with contributions expected from Q3 FY'25.
*   **Channel Strategy (Soul Brand):** For FY'26, projected sales mix: 40% modern trade, 40% quick commerce, 20% e-commerce. Currently, e-commerce is the dominant channel.
*   **International Market Presence:** Ashoka brand present in Germany (approx. INR 10-12 crores annual revenue) and UK for several years, contributing significantly to revenue. "Truly Indian" listed in regional supermarkets on the West Coast US and Amazon. Walmart Canada listing in approx. 200 stores. Tesco UK listing in approx. 700 stores with a limited shelf-stable range.
*   **Agency Distribution Business:** Faced headwinds from supply chain issues but expects normalization and good growth in FY'25. Exploring partnerships with 1-2 new companies.
*   **Capacity Enhancement:** Greenfield project in Surat on track for September 2025 commissioning. Brownfield expansions and debottlenecking at Nadiad and Nashik are ongoing.
*   **Investment Phase:** Soul and "Truly Indian" brands are in an investment phase for the next 2-3 years, with significant marketing and team-building efforts.
*   **Product Diversification:** Expanding Soul brand into categories like frozen foods, sauces, dips, and Italian sauces. Exploring Indo-Chinese and Indo-Thai fusion products and Momos.
*   **B2B/Private Label:** Estimated 20-25% growth anticipated for FY'25, with expansion into US customers. These products offer gross margins upwards of 40%.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth (Q1 FY'25):** Consolidated revenue grew 8.1% YoY to INR 121.6 crores. Management described this as "decent" and attributed the lower-than-expected growth partly to lost sales (estimated USD 1 million) due to container unavailability. This aligns with the previous guidance of upwards of 20% revenue growth for FY'25, though Q1's performance indicates potential challenges in achieving this target without strong H2 growth.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Consolidated EBITDA margin declined to 16.1% (down 340 bps YoY), impacted by lost sales, higher freight costs, and continued investment in Soul and "Truly Indian" brands. This is consistent with the previous guidance of high-teen margins for FY'25, acknowledging that Q1 is seasonally lower and margins are expected to ramp up. Management confirmed that yearly consolidated EBITDA margins are expected to be in the high teens.
*   **Soul Brand Growth:** The INR 100 crore revenue target in 3-4 years remains a stated goal. The brand is currently at a run rate of INR 7-8 crores annually, with a monthly run rate of INR 60 lakhs in Q1. Break-even is targeted for Q2 FY'26, with EBITDA profitability expected in FY'26.
*   **"Truly Indian" Brand & US Market:** New supermarket listings and Amazon presence are in place, with contributions expected from Q3 FY'25. Management reiterated confidence in the brand's long-term potential, similar to Ashoka. Investment in a new US team for this brand and private labels is underway.
*   **Greenfield Project (Surat):** Construction has commenced, with commissioning expected by September 2025, consistent with previous announcements.
*   **Distribution Business:** Management acknowledges the decrease due to supply chain issues but is optimistic for good growth in FY'25 as these normalize, projecting 15-20% growth once resolved.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Overall capacity utilization is around 70%, with specific lines at 80-85%. Current capacity is expected to meet demand until the Greenfield project becomes operational.
*   **Meat Products:** Management clarified that they offer plant-based meat products, not actual meat. The plan to launch meat products under the "Khansaama" brand was put on hold due to the inability to find a reliable contract manufacturer for export.
*   **Inventory:** Inventory days have slightly increased to approx. 90 days due to container shortages and a build-up post-mango and vegetable season, with a target to normalize to 70-odd days.
