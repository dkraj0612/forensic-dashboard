## Executive Overview

ADF Foods reported strong Q3 FY'26 results with consolidated revenues reaching an all-time high of INR 191 crores, a 29.5% YoY increase. Standalone revenues grew 13.3% YoY to INR 137.2 crores. Consolidated EBITDA also hit a record INR 37.1 crores with a margin of 19.4%, driven by improved product mix and cost optimization. Standalone EBITDA surged 35.1% YoY to INR 34.4 crores. The company reported a consolidated PAT (excluding exceptional items) of INR 29.2 crores, up 55.7% YoY. The Surat greenfield facility's Phase 1 is on track for operationalization by Q4 FY'26. Management remains cautiously optimistic about sustaining the growth trajectory.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Momentum:** Flagship "Ashoka" brand continues to strengthen market presence. "Truly Indian" is showing accelerated growth, driven by listings in major supermarket chains like Whole Foods, Costco, and Albertsons-Safeway, now available in over 2,000 stores in the US.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Surat greenfield plant's Phase 1 is nearing completion and expected to commence operations in March 2026, introducing two new product lines and expanding existing capacity.
*   **Market Penetration:** Continued focus on deeper market penetration and expanding brand reach across key markets, including Europe.
*   **Tariff Impact & Resolution:** Management remains optimistic about demand robustly continuing despite previous tariff challenges. The anticipated clearing of tariffs is expected to enable aggressive pitching of new products to supermarkets.
*   **Product Innovation:** Introduction of new frozen products, including frozen breads, and expansion of "Truly Indian" offerings in Europe, including frozen foods in Germany.
*   **Investment Mode:** Continued investment in brands and capabilities, particularly for the "Truly Indian" brand, which is expected to remain in an investment mode for the next three years.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth (Q3 FY'26 Consolidated):** Achieved a record 29.5% YoY growth, exceeding previous quarter's growth. Management attributed this to traction from new listings and brand penetration.
*   **EBITDA Margins (Q3 FY'26 Consolidated):** Consolidated EBITDA margin was 19.4%, a decrease of 260 bps QoQ but an increase of 150 bps YoY. Management explained the QoQ decrease was due to the sales mix from subsidiaries (distribution and "Truly Indian" business), while standalone margins improved.
*   **Tariff Situation (US):** Management reiterated that price increases due to tariffs were absorbed by distributors and passed to consumers. They anticipate that the resolution of tariff issues will allow for aggressive new product introductions. No demand reduction was observed despite price increases.
*   **Capacity Constraint Narrative:** Management clarified that capacity constraints were specific to certain product lines and have been addressed through debottlenecking. The new Surat plant will further alleviate capacity concerns and introduce new product lines.
*   **Surat Greenfield Project:** Remains on track for Q4 FY'26 operationalization, with Phase 1 coming online in March 2026. Full capacity ramp-up is expected in 3-4 quarters post-commissioning.
*   **Brand Investment:** Management confirmed that "Truly Indian" will continue to be in an investment mode for the next three years, with elevated marketing spends contributing to its growth trajectory.
*   **Forex Impact:** Srinivas Ayyagari noted that the dollar has balanced out in Q3, leading to lower forex gains compared to Q2. They anticipate minimal forex gains in coming quarters due to rupee strengthening. The company does not actively hedge.
*   **Volume Growth:** Volume growth accounted for approximately 70% of the total revenue growth in Q3 FY'26, with the balance from price growth.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **FY'27 Revenue Target:** Reaffirmed confidence in achieving INR 925 crores to INR 1,000 crores, contingent on a revised domestic market strategy by Q2 FY'27.
*   **FY'27 Blended EBITDA Margin Target:** Expectation to maintain margins in the high teens.
*   **Surat Plant New Products:** Two new frozen product lines to be launched from the Surat plant, one in March 2026 (Phase 1) and the second in Q2 FY'27 (Phase 2).
*   **"Truly Indian" Brand Development:** Expected to remain in an investment mode for the next three years. Break-even at the subsidiary level is projected in approximately 3 years, and combined EBITDA break-even in about 18 months.
*   **European Market Expansion:** Continuous introduction of new products and expansion into Eastern and Western European markets. New supermarkets added in the Netherlands and Germany. Launch of frozen foods under "Truly Indian" brand in Germany this quarter.
*   **ADF SOUL Brand (India):** Currently in Bombay markets with a monthly run rate of INR 50 lakhs. A revised business plan and guidance will be provided in Q1 FY'27.
*   **PLI Incentive:** Approximately INR 7 crores received for Year 1. Year 2 and remaining Year 1 incentives are expected by September 2026.
*   **Distribution Business Margin:** Steady-state margins expected in the 12% to 14% range (previously stated 14-15% in historical summary), with higher margins in recent quarters due to promotional support.
*   **White Labeling/B2B:** Accounts for approximately 20% of overall revenues; margins are lower than branded products but still considered healthy due to reduced marketing spend.
*   **New SKUs:** Average of 15-25 new SKUs added annually across brands. Expectation of 10-12 additional products from the Surat plant in FY'27.
*   **Frozen Food Segment:** Accounts for upwards of 40% of total revenues and remains the fastest-growing category. No capacity constraints on frozen products.
