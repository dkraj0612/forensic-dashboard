## Executive Overview

ADF Foods reported Q2 FY'26 with strong standalone revenue growth of 11.5% YoY to INR 140.1 crore, and consolidated revenue of INR 162.6 crore (0.8% YoY growth). Standalone EBITDA saw a significant 36.1% YoY increase to INR 37.7 crore, with a margin of 26.9%, driven by improved product mix, cost optimization, and favorable foreign exchange. Consolidated EBITDA grew 29.1% YoY to INR 35.8 crore with a margin of 22%. PAT also showed robust growth, with standalone PAT up 39.1% to INR 29.7 crore and consolidated PAT up 34.2% to INR 26.4 crore. Key developments include brand refreshes for "Ashoka" and "Truly Indian", expansion of store penetration to over 2,000 stores including Costco, and the nearing completion of the Surat greenfield facility, slated for H2 FY'26 commissioning. Management is optimistic about sustaining growth despite ongoing tariff uncertainties.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Brand Refresh & Expansion:** "Ashoka" and "Truly Indian" brands have undergone refreshes. "Truly Indian" is now available in over 2,000 stores, including Costco in the US and Australia.
*   **Market Penetration & Retail Expansion:** Increased store penetration and geographic expansion driving growth. New listings secured in key retail outlets like Costco (US and Australia) and Safeway Albertsons (US).
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Surat Greenfield facility for frozen products nearing completion and expected to commence operations in H2 FY'26.
*   **Product Mix Improvement:** Focus on higher-margin frozen products is contributing to better gross and EBITDA margins.
*   **Cost Management & Efficiency:** Prudent cost optimization and operational efficiencies are enhancing margins.
*   **Distribution Business:** Steady-state EBITDA margins expected in the 14-15% range for the current year.
*   **UK-India FTA:** Anticipated zero duty on imports to the UK is expected to positively impact demand by reducing end retail prices.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth (Q2 FY'26 Consolidated):** Achieved 0.8% YoY growth. While lower than anticipated, management attributed this to the lag in realization of new listings and noted strong standalone growth. Standalone revenue growth of 11.5% YoY was robust.
*   **EBITDA Margins (Q2 FY'26 Consolidated):** Demonstrated a significant 22% margin, increasing 480 bps YoY. This was driven by improved product mix (higher proportion of frozen foods), cost optimization, and favorable forex, aligning with past commentary on margin drivers.
*   **Tariff Situation (US):** Management continues to monitor the dynamic tariff situation. They have maintained prices to distributors, expecting distributors to pass on increased costs to consumers, with an estimated 20% increase on the final consumer price for a 50% tariff. They are hopeful of a resolution to the US-India trade deal soon, which would prevent higher consumer prices.
*   **Brand Refresh Impact:** Brand refreshes for "Ashoka" and "Truly Indian" have been completed, with "Truly Indian" expanding its store count significantly.
*   **Cost Structure:** Management noted that raw material costs are not substantially lower, but improved sales mix and cost optimization are contributing to better COGS as a percentage of sales.
*   **Surat Greenfield Project:** Remains on track for H2 FY'26 commissioning. Management expects ramp-up to full capacity to take 3-4 quarters post-commissioning.
*   **Distribution Business:** Steady-state EBITDA margins are projected to be in the 14-15% range for the current year.
*   **Volume Growth:** Management indicated volume growth constituted approximately 70% of the total growth in Q2 FY'26.
