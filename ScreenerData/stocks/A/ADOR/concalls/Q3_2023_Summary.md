## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) held an Analyst/Institutional Investor Meet on February 8, 2023, to discuss its Q3 FY23 performance. The company reported continued robust demand, supported by government CAPEX proposals. Management highlighted a strong focus on margin expansion, which was evident in the Q3 results. Key updates included progress on the amalgamation with Ador Fontech (ADFL) and a detailed Q&A session covering segment performance, export strategy, and the outlook for various business divisions.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q3 FY23 Performance:**
    *   Sales: While specific Q3 FY23 consolidated sales figures were not explicitly stated, the transcript indicates strong performance and growth. Consumables division performance was noted as robust.
    *   PBT Margin (Q3 FY23): Management confirmed a focus on margin expansion, with specific mention of improvements in consumables margins, which were described as "significantly higher than historical margins" and not a one-off event. The company aims for these higher levels to be sustainable.
    *   Working Capital: Management emphasized continued tracking and management of working capital, especially with the growth across various business verticals.
    *   Volume Growth (Q3 FY23): Consumables division saw volume growth in the region of 7% in Q3 FY23 versus the prior year. Later corrected by management to 10-11% for the last three months. Nine-month volume growth was described as "flattish" due to a dip in Q1.
    *   Export Revenue (Q3 FY23): Export revenue was INR 18 Crores for the quarter, representing approximately 85% welding consumables and 10-15% welding equipment. The company expects to end the year with approximately 60% growth in exports.
    *   Automation Business (AI sales): Not explicitly detailed for Q3 FY23 in this transcript, but the overall equipment segment is a focus for margin improvement.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product Innovation & "Make in India":** Continued focus on product development and certifications.
*   **Export Market Expansion:** Aggressive entry into key markets, with expectations of 40-50% growth on an already growing base in the next fiscal year.
*   **Flares & Process Equipment Division (FPED):** A significant ONGC order of approximately INR 120-130 Crores was secured, with execution spread over 18-24 months starting from July 2022. Bulk of this will be recognized over the next financial year. Management emphasized a strategic shift back to core engineering-focused work, moving away from previous EPC model bloat. Smaller orders in the INR 10-30 Crores range are also being pursued.
*   **Merger with Ador Fontech (ADFL):** Application filed with NCLT. Management is hopeful of closing the merger within the calendar year 2023.
*   **Demand Drivers:** Robust general demand, further supported by government CAPEX proposals. Key sectors include general infrastructure, heavy engineering, railways, oil & gas, and cement expansion.
*   **Equipment Segment:** Focus on strengthening the mid-segment of the technology pyramid and increasing manufacturing capabilities. Acknowledged high import penetration in this segment (approx. 80%), with a long-term view on increasing local manufacturing preference.
*   **Consumables Segment:** Aiming to outperform market growth through strong distribution, product approvals, and leveraging market trends. Margins are expected to be sustainable at higher levels.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Previous Targets:**
    *   **Margins:** Management confirmed that the higher margins seen in the consumables division are not a one-off and are sustainable, reflecting successful efforts in pricing and operating leverage, aligning with previous discussions about margin improvement. The FPED division is also expected to see margin improvement.
    *   **Working Capital & Debt:** Continued focus on working capital management was reiterated. The move away from the previous EPC model in FPED was explicitly to avoid working capital bloat.
    *   **Product Mix & Growth:** Volume growth in consumables was confirmed, though initial figures were refined. The focus remains on outperforming market growth.
    *   **Headwinds:** Steel price volatility and supply chain issues were mentioned as factors that require monitoring, but the overall tone was positive, indicating these are being managed. The previous "inventory hangover" in Q2 was noted as having "evened itself out."

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter (Q4 FY23 onwards):**
    *   **Consumable Margins:** Expected to remain at sustainable higher levels.
    *   **Equipment Margins:** Focus on improvement, dependent on the CAPEX cycle.
    *   **Exports:** Expected to grow 40-50% on the current year's base, with an expected 60% growth for the full FY23.
    *   **Volume Growth (FY2024):** Expectation of volume growth seen in Q3 to be a baseline for going forward, with an ambition to outperform the market.
    *   **FPED Revenue (FY2024):** Expected to contribute INR 120-130 Crores (though management indicated this might be slightly higher than initial indications).
    *   **Merger Completion:** Hopeful to close within the calendar year 2023.
    *   **Flares Division Margin:** Q3 margin of 14% is seen as a good base to build upon, with expectations of improvement. Gross margins around 20% were alluded to.
    *   **Industry Growth (FY2024):** Expected to be in line with Q3 performance, with some potential upside.
    *   **Automation Business Scale:** No specific guidance, but the segment is identified as a focus for margin improvement.
