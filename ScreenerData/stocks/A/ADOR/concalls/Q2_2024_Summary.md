## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) held an analyst and institutional investor meet to discuss its Q2 FY25 performance. The company has expanded its international footprint, entering the U.S. market with two distributors and an employee, and strengthening its presence in Australia. While the first half (H1) of FY25 saw a slight decline in consolidated sales (-5%) and a reduction in PBT (INR 46 crores vs. INR 57 crores in H1 FY24), this was significantly impacted by an exceptional item of INR 41.7 crores. This exceptional item comprised an impairment charge on a subsidiary acquired through the Ador Fontech (ADFL) merger, merger-related expenses, reward to non-service employees, and provision on obsolete inventory, partially offset by a gain on property sale. The ADFL merger is progressing, with expected value creation in manufacturing and supply chains. AWL is also focusing on new product development, including a higher range of welding equipment and a battery-operated welder, alongside strengthening its market presence in Saudi Arabia. Management acknowledged subdued Q2 margins across segments due to higher input costs and product mix but anticipates a return to steady-state levels and upward margin expansion in H2 FY25. The company is also strategically shifting its focus in the Flares & Process Equipment Division (FPED) towards projects with minimal site work and higher margins, moving away from large site-intensive projects.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **H1 FY25 Performance:**
    *   **Consolidated Sales:** INR 265.1 Crores (down 5% YoY).
    *   **PBT (excluding exceptional items):** INR 46 Crores (down from INR 57 Crores in H1 FY24).
    *   **Exceptional Items:** INR 41.7 Crores (impairment on subsidiary, merger expenses, reward, inventory provision, gain on property sale).
    *   **Consolidated Net Impact:** INR 26 Crores (compared to INR 42 Crores in H1 FY24).
    *   **EBITDA Margins:** Declined slightly due to increased fixed costs.
    *   **PBT Margins (H1 FY25, excluding exceptional items):** 9% (down from 11% in H1 FY24).
    *   **ROCE:** Declined from 21% to 19% (excluding exceptional items).
    *   **Debt-to-Equity:** Negligible at 0.8% as of September.

*   **Segmental Performance (H1 FY25):**
    *   **Products Division:** ~78% of total business. Margins at 10% for Q2.
    *   **Services Division:** ~8% of total business. Negative 4% margin in Q2.
    *   **M&R (erstwhile Fontech) Division:** ~14% of total business. Margins at 15% for Q2.

*   **Working Capital:**
    *   Operative working capital increased by nine days.
    *   Inventory slightly increased from March.
    *   Receivables and payables optimized.

*   **Exports:**
    *   **FY24 Exports:** INR 120 Crores (doubled YoY).
    *   **H1 FY25 Growth:** ~20%.
    *   **FY25 Growth Expectation:** 25% to 30%.
    *   **New Markets:** Entered U.S. market.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **International Expansion:** Focus on U.S. and Australia, building on strong performance in the Middle East.
*   **Product Development:** Launching higher-range welding equipment to compete with imports, and a new battery-operated welder with CE approval and ongoing trials.
*   **ADFL Merger Synergies:** Focusing on manufacturing and supply chain integration, cost structure synergies, and product development.
*   **FPED Strategy Shift:** Moving away from large, site-intensive projects to smaller projects with higher margins and minimal site work, focusing on manufacturing control within plants.
*   **Market Share:** Management expresses no concern regarding market share in core businesses.
*   **Demand Drivers:** Softness in core sector growth observed in recent months, with tentative signs of improvement in H2 FY25.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Previous Targets:**
    *   **Consumables Volume Growth (FY24):** Previous guidance was ~10%. The current transcript indicates H1 FY25 volumes were "pretty much a little bit 2% down" on an H1 basis, and Q2 volumes were "slightly lower than that" compared to H1. This represents a deviation from the previously targeted growth.
    *   **Equipment Growth (FY24):** Expected to be similar to consumables volumes. This is also not materializing as volumes are down.
    *   **Equipment Margins (FY24):** Aiming closer to 14-15% EBIT. Q2 margins were subdued at 10% for products, with management stating it was an "aberration" and expecting a return to steady-state and upward movement in H2 FY25.
    *   **FPED Revenue (H2 FY24):** Majority expected in H2 FY24. Management stated the project is progressing on a "much, much stronger basis" and while they have been conservative on accounting, there is confidence in execution and potential "back-end benefit." The project is now expected to spill over into FY26, with roughly 15% of billing going into the next financial year.
    *   **Exports (FY24):** Expected to grow at least 20%. Management reports FY24 exports at INR 120 Crores (doubled YoY), and H1 FY25 growth at 20%, with an expectation to close FY25 at 25-30% growth. This is consistent or exceeding previous guidance.
    *   **Merger Completion:** Expected by FY2024. The merger with Ador Fontech is underway, with an impairment charge on a subsidiary indicating progress and strategic re-evaluation.
    *   **Inventory Management (FY24):** Efforts to reduce high inventory levels. The transcript notes an increase in inventory in Q2 FY25, but also optimized receivables and payables.
    *   **Capex (FY24):** Budgeted around INR 30 crores. No specific mention in this transcript.
    *   **Blended Margins (1-2 years post-merger):** Working towards 13-15%. Management discusses achieving 2% "low hanging fruit" for improvement in the next year and another 2% over 2-3 years, indicating a longer-term margin expansion strategy.
    *   **Export Share (5-6 years):** Potential for exports to become more than 20% of sales. No update on this long-term target.
