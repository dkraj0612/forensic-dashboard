## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Business Strength:** Ador Welding positions itself as a leader in the welding industry with a rich history and extensive product and service portfolio.
*   **Market Reach:** The company emphasizes its strong domestic and international network, including sales offices, manufacturing plants, field sales professionals, distributors, and an International Business Division (IBD) handling business in over 70 countries.
*   **Product Diversification:** Beyond consumables and equipment, Ador Welding is actively involved in Project Engineering Business (PEB), Welding Automation Products and Services (WAPS), and has a dedicated Welding Training Division to foster skilled professionals.
*   **Quality & Certifications:** The company highlights its adherence to international quality standards, evidenced by CE marking, OHSAS 18001, ISO 9001:2015, and ISO 14001:2015 certifications for its plants and products.
*   **Customer Base:** Ador Welding showcases a strong clientele of major Indian and international corporations across various sectors, including Oil & Gas, Infrastructure, Automotive, Defense, and heavy industries, demonstrating trust and reliability.
*   **Strategic Growth Pillars:**
    *   **Consumables:** Largest production capacities in India, catering to all welding processes, with an annual capacity exceeding 70,000 tons.
    *   **Equipment:** Manufacturing plants in Pune with advanced capabilities.
    *   **Project Engineering Business (PEB):** Design and project management services since 1980, with a state-of-the-art fabrication facility.
    *   **Welding Automation Products and Services (WAPS):** Customized welding solutions and technology centers.
    *   **Welding Training Division:** A subsidiary focused on skill development and creating a talent pool for the welding industry.
