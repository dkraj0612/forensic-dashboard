## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited held an Analyst/Institutional Investor Meet on November 15, 2022, to provide an update on its performance and strategic initiatives. The company highlighted progress on several fronts, including extending its "Make in India" product range for welding equipment, opening a new office in the Jebel Ali Free-Trade Zone to boost exports, and securing a significant INR 145 crore order for its Flares and Process Equipment division (FPED). Management acknowledged the impact of steel price volatility and supply chain disruptions but expressed optimism regarding demand stabilization and recovery. The proposed merger with Ador Fontech is progressing, with the company awaiting NCLT approval.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product Innovation & "Make in India":** Continued focus on expanding the range of "Make in India" welding equipment and reducing reliance on imports. A new product line requiring capex is under development.
*   **Export Market Expansion:** The Jebel Ali Free-Trade Zone office is operational to drive export growth. The company targets 50-60% export growth in FY23, aiming for INR 100 crores by FY25, driven by enhanced distribution, warehousing, and addressing weak spots in markets like Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
*   **Flares & Process Equipment Division (FPED):** Strategy re-emphasized on high-value engineered projects. Secured a substantial INR 145 crore ONGC order, with execution expected to commence in Q4 FY23, contributing ~10% of revenue, followed by ~55-60% in FY24 and the balance in FY25. FPED targets revenue of INR 75 crores for FY23 and aims for 10-12% margins and 18-20% ROC in the long term, with potential to scale to INR 100 crores.
*   **Merger with Ador Fontech:** The NOC from stock exchanges has been received, and the company is in the process of filing with the NCLT. Management is hopeful for timely approval, although NCLT processes are acknowledged as potentially lengthy (estimated one year).
*   **Demand Drivers:** General infrastructure, railways, heavy engineering, and oil & gas sectors are showing strong demand.
*   **Distribution Network:** Approximately 65% of sales occur through ~200 distributors, with the remaining 30-35% through direct sales.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Management is balancing capital allocation between the highly capital-efficient consumables business and the project business, prioritizing areas where engineering value addition is highest.
*   **Automation Division:** Partnership signed with Indotec (Scandinavia) for welding and cutting automation solutions, though it's in early stages.
