## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) reported its best-ever quarterly performance in Q4 FY23, with strong sales and improved profitability. Management highlighted a robust market demand driven by resilient economic growth in key sectors. The company is actively focusing on internal initiatives such as capacity enhancement, product mix optimization, and international market expansion. Progress on the merger with Ador Fontech (ADFL) continues, with completion anticipated by FY24. While the consumables segment maintained strong margins, the equipment division faced some supply chain challenges impacting margins, though volume growth was strong. Working capital management remains a focus, particularly in reducing inventory levels.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q4 FY23 Performance:**
    *   **Sales:** INR 285 Crores.
    *   **PBT Margin:** 12.4% (up from 10.2% in the prior year, marking best-ever quarterly performance).
*   **Segmental Performance (FY23 & Q4 FY23):**
    *   **Consumables:**
        *   Q4 FY23 Volume Growth: 4-5% vs. Q4 FY22.
        *   FY23 Overall Volume Growth: Approximately flat.
        *   FY24 Volume Growth Expectation: ~10% (conservative estimate).
        *   Margins: Expected to be in line with HY2 FY23 levels, sustained.
    *   **Equipment:**
        *   FY23 Volume Growth: Almost 30%.
        *   Margins: Remained subdued due to supply chain issues; room for improvement over FY24, aiming closer to 14-15% EBIT but not expected to hit this year.
        *   Outlook: Similar level of growth expected as consumables in FY24.
    *   **Flares & Process Equipment Division (FPED):**
        *   Q4 FY23 Performance: Done "quite well."
        *   Margins: Gross margin expectation of 18-20%.
        *   Uran Project Billing: Majority expected in H2 FY24. FY24 revenue for flares business will be mostly from the ONGC project.
*   **Working Capital & Capex:**
    *   **Receivables:** Increased from INR 93 crores to INR 125 crores, considered "more normalized" for the end of the year, driven by end-user, international, and flares business.
    *   **Inventory:** Increased from INR 90 crores to INR 116 crores, acknowledged as "certain high levels" with efforts planned for reduction in FY24.
    *   **ROCE & Borrowing:** Expected to be flat for the year.
    *   **Debt-to-Equity:** Expected to move slightly with projects.
    *   **Capex:**
        *   FY23: INR 15 crores (flat, with spill-over).
        *   FY24 Budget: Approximately INR 30 crores (pushing towards this, including spill-over).
        *   Next 2 Years: Approximately INR 40-45 crores.
*   **Exports:**
    *   FY23 Revenue: Closed at INR 61 crores.
    *   FY24 Growth Expectation: At least 20%.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market & Product Focus:**
    *   **Domestic:** Capacity enhancement in specific product lines, optimizing product mix, and seeking better realizations and volumes.
    *   **"Make in India":** Continued strong commitment in the equipment segment, despite slightly lower margins compared to import-heavy competitors.
    *   **Automation Business:** Identified as a key area with a large product gap, focusing on capability to execute larger and more sophisticated projects.
*   **International Expansion:** Exploring new markets, particularly in the Middle East, Americas, and Africa, leveraging global factors and Ador's brand strength. Expects these regions to drive future export growth, with less immediate focus on Europe.
*   **Digitization:** Internal programs focused on B2B and distributor supply chains.
*   **Merger with Ador Fontech (ADFL):** Application submitted to NCLT, shareholder meetings convened for August 2023. Expects completion by FY2024. Rationale is consolidation of manufacturing and cost benefits, while maintaining separate front-end sales. Ador Fontech is viewed as a "value growth" oriented business with strong application potential.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Implemented measures including stopping thermocol use, reducing plastic consumption by 30% in one plant, and reducing water usage by 50% in one plant and 20% in others.
*   **Demand Drivers:** Strong demand from railways, general heavy engineering (EPC projects), and cement expansion. Oil & gas is mixed. Shipbuilding is identified as an interesting play for the coming cycle.
*   **Qualitative & Structural Changes:** Implementation of a variable pay structure (10-35% variable, 15-40% linked to company profit) across all employee levels to foster greater ownership and alignment with company profitability. Investment in technical and business development expertise. Focus on driving brand growth and future investments.
*   **Industry Transition:** Acknowledges the global transition in welding processes from stick electrodes to continuous welding (GMAW, SAWs) due to labor costs and productivity. India is moving from 70-30% (non-continuous to continuous) to an expected 65-35% or 60-40% by the end of the current capex cycle.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Previous Targets:**
    *   **Margins (Consumables):** Management consistently reiterated "sustainable higher margins" in Q3 FY23. In Q4 FY23, they stated margins were "in line with what was in HY2," suggesting sustainability was met.
    *   **Working Capital & Debt:** Q3 FY23 emphasized "continued tracking and management of working capital" and moving away from EPC model bloat. Q4 FY23 noted increased inventory levels ("certain high levels") but stated efforts are underway to reduce them, while receivables were considered "more normalized." This indicates a mixed outcome, with inventory requiring specific attention.
    *   **Product Mix & Growth (Consumables Volume):** Q3 FY23 reported "flattish" 9-month volume growth, aiming to "outperform market growth." Q4 FY23 confirmed FY23 overall volume was "pretty much almost flat," with Q4 FY23 growth at 4-5%. This implies the ambition to outperform the market was not fully realized for FY23 volumes.
    *   **Exports:** Q3 FY23 expected to "end the year with approximately 60% growth in exports" and grow 40-50% on that base in the next fiscal year. Q4 FY23 reported FY23 exports at INR 61 crores but *revised down* the FY24 growth expectation to "at least 20%." This is a significant deviation from the previous high growth guidance.
    *   **FPED Revenue (ONGC Order):** Q3 FY23 noted the INR 120-130 Crores order with "bulk of this will be recognized over the next financial year (FY24)." Q4 FY23 guidance aligns, stating the "Uran project in the second half of the year will be the larger part of the billing," confirming execution timeline consistency.
    *   **Merger Completion:** Q3 FY23 was "hopeful of closing the merger within the calendar year 2023." Q4 FY23 updated this to "expected to be completed by the financial year 2024," which is a slight shift from calendar year to financial year, extending the timeline slightly.
    *   **Automation Business Scale:** No specific guidance was given in Q3 FY23. Q4 FY23 identified it as a "large product gap" related to capability to execute sophisticated projects, indicating a continued focus on development rather than immediate scale.
    *   **Headwinds:** Q3 FY23 noted "steel price volatility" and "supply chain issues." Q4 FY23 mentioned supply chain issues affecting equipment margins but noted supply chain talks "seemed to be normalized a lot now," suggesting some improvement. Steel prices were mentioned in context of Q4 FY22 volume push.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter (Q4 FY23 onwards):**
    *   **Consumables Volume Growth (FY24):** Expected to be in the region of 10% (conservative estimate, with ambition for more).
    *   **Consumables Margins (FY24):** Expected to be in line with HY2 FY23.
    *   **Equipment Growth (FY24):** Expected to be similar to consumables volumes.
    *   **Equipment Margins (FY24):** Improvement over the year, aiming closer to 14-15% EBIT, but not expected to hit that number this year.
    *   **FPED Gross Margin:** Expected at 18-20%.
    *   **FPED Revenue (FY24):** Mostly from the ONGC project, with bulk billing in H2 FY24.
    *   **Exports (FY24):** Expected to grow at least 20% (on FY23 base of INR 61 crores).
    *   **Merger Completion:** Expected by FY2024.
    *   **Inventory Management:** Efforts to reduce high inventory levels in FY24.
    *   **Capex (FY24):** Budgeted around INR 30 crores, primarily for new product lines in the welding business.
    *   **Capex (Next 2 Years):** Total approximately INR 40-45 crores.
    *   **Blended Margins:** With merger, working towards 13-15% blended margins in a year or two.
    *   **Export Share:** Potential for exports to become more than 20% of sales over the next five to six years.
    *   **Automation:** Focus on enhancing capability to execute larger and more sophisticated projects.
