## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) held an analyst and institutional investor meet on May 2nd, 2024, to discuss its recent performance and outlook. The company reported gross revenues of INR 884 crores, with key segments including Consumables (INR 678 crores), Equipment & Automation (INR 172 crores), and Flares & Process Equipment (INR 35 crores). While Consumables margins showed improvement, Equipment margins dipped due to automation-related issues, and the Flares division experienced a hit from project execution delays. Management highlighted a focus on high-end, 'Make in India' welding equipment, particularly in plasma cutting, and emphasized critical project execution for the year. The merger with Ador Fontech is progressing, with an NCLT meeting expected soon.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **FY24 Gross Revenue:** INR 884 Crores
    *   **Consumables:** INR 678 Crores
    *   **Equipment & Automation:** INR 172 Crores
    *   **Flares & Process Equipment:** INR 35 Crores
*   **Segmental Performance Commentary:**
    *   **Consumables:** Margins improved slightly; volume growth in Q4 FY24 was 1-2% YoY and 14% QoQ. Full-year volume growth for FY24 is estimated around 10-11%. Channel inventory is perceived as slightly low.
    *   **Equipment & Automation:** Margins dipped due to automation issues and a less favorable product mix. Revenue grew by 25-30% YoY. Automation contributes approximately 12-13% to equipment revenue, with plans to increase this to 25-30% of revenue this year. Steady-state margins are expected to be closer to 7-9%.
    *   **Flares & Process Equipment:** Experienced a hit due to delayed ONGC project execution. A portion of INR 30-35 crores revenue was deferred from FY24. The business is expected to scale to 2.5x-3x of this year's revenue, with margins slightly lower than initially planned due to cost overruns. Management is reassessing the long-term play for this division after project completion.
*   **Exports (Welding Business):** Doubled YoY to approximately INR 120 Crores in FY24. Exports consist of 70% consumables and 30% equipment. Expectation for FY25 export growth is 35-40%.
*   **Merger with Ador Fontech:** NCLT meeting is imminent, with dividend payouts by both entities being processed beforehand.
*   **Working Capital:** Generally in line with expectations.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product Development & High-End Offerings:** Focus on 'Make in India' high-end welding equipment, including plasma cutting technology, to compete with imports and offer a wider range.
*   **Project Execution:** Ensuring correct execution of ongoing projects, particularly the ONGC project, remains critical.
*   **International Expansion:** Targeting 2-3 new geographies in FY25, building on existing international presence.
*   **Automation Integration:** Increasing the contribution of automation to equipment revenue and aiming for profitable growth in this segment.
*   **Flares & Process Equipment (FPED) Strategy:** Reassessing the long-term viability and strategic fit of this division post-ONGC project execution.
*   **Synergies from ADFL Merger:** Expected benefits in back-end services, management, production, and import benefits from scaled-up production.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Previous Targets:**
    *   **Consumables Volume Growth (FY24):** Previously guided around 10%. Management now states FY24 volume growth is approximately 10-11%, with Q4 FY24 showing 1-2% YoY growth. This aligns with earlier expectations, though the commentary on subdued recent months suggests a potential moderation.
    *   **Equipment Growth (FY24):** Previously expected similar to consumables. This transcript indicates 25-30% YoY revenue growth in equipment, a positive deviation.
    *   **Equipment Margins (FY24):** Aiming for 14-15% EBIT. Q2 margins were subdued at 10% for products. Management acknowledges current margins are lower (4.6% for FY24, 2.5% in Q4) due to automation issues and product mix, but expects steady-state margins of 7-9% and potential for improvement.
    *   **FPED Revenue (H2 FY24):** Majority expected in H2 FY24. Management admits the ONGC project execution is late, with INR 30-35 crores deferred to the current fiscal year. The project is now expected to spill over into FY26 with approximately 10-15% of billing.
    *   **Exports (FY24):** Expected to grow at least 20%. Management reported doubling exports to INR 120 Crores in FY24, exceeding prior guidance.
    *   **Merger Completion:** Expected soon with NCLT. Dividend payouts are being processed.
    *   **Inventory Management:** Channel inventory is considered slightly low due to recent steel price movements.
    *   **Blended Margins (1-2 years post-merger):** Working towards higher margins. Management indicates scope for improvement, with an EBITDA margin target that is halfway to the 14% mark (excluding Flares impact). Peers have EBITDA margins above 18%.
*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter (H2 FY25 onwards):**
    *   **Consumables Volume Growth (FY25):** Expectation of double-digit growth, barring election impacts.
    *   **Consumables Margins:** Aiming for improvements through product mix and better selling of offerings. Product offering-led growth for domestic consumables.
    *   **Equipment Revenue Growth (FY25):** Fundamentals in place for another good year, though margin improvement is key.
    *   **Equipment Steady-State Margins:** Expected to be closer to 7-9%, with scope for further increase.
    *   **Automation Contribution (FY25):** Expected to increase to 25-30% of welding equipment revenue, contributing positively to net margins.
    *   **Flares & Process Equipment (FPED) Revenue (FY25 onwards):** Expected to be 2.5x-3x of this year's revenue. A portion of ONGC project billing (10-15%) will spill over into FY26. Margins will be slightly lower than initially planned due to cost overruns.
    *   **Flares & Process Equipment (FPED) Margins:** Management is reassessing the long-term play and fit for this division.
    *   **Exports (FY25):** Expectation of 35-40% growth.
    *   **Ador Fontech Outlook:** Maintenance & Repair (M&R) products to grow in line with IIP. M&R services have potential for exponential growth if executed correctly. Capital equipment will integrate with welding, driving its pickup.
    *   **3D Printing Technologies Subsidiary:** Losses have been significantly reduced. A decision on its future will be made in the next 6-8 months based on market changes. No synergy with core operations.
    *   **Dividend Payout:** Plan to maintain the current payout ratio benchmark, with potential to go above it.
    *   **Capex:** Approximately 70% of last year's capex plan will continue this year, with new lines and refurbishments planned. Capex is expected to continue for at least another 18 months.
    *   **EBITDA Margins:** Aiming to move closer to the 14% mark, with current FY24 margin (excluding Flares) being halfway there. Peers have EBITDA margins above 18%.
    *   **Plasma Cutting:** Focus area for significant advancement in the next 6-8 months.
    *   **Welding Consumables Imports:** Not a major concern in the middle-down segment; imports are more prominent in exotic metals and higher-end steel work.
    *   **Reclamation Welding Market:** EWAC is the leader, followed by Ador Fontech, with 2-3 other smaller domestic and import players.
