## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) held an Analysts / Institutional Investors Meet on May 12th, 2025, to present its progress and financial updates. The company reported a 5% revenue growth for FY25 over FY24 and highlighted strong operating cash flow of approximately INR 139 crores. Key achievements include the completion of the merger with Ador Fontech (effective September 25th, 2024) and expansion into new international markets, specifically the USA and Australia, with international business growing by over 25%. Management emphasized a focus on turning around the Flares & Process Equipment division through timely project execution, cost optimization, and securing orders with improved margins. The company is also concentrating on high-end welding consumables like Flux Cored Wire and Stainless-Steel products, with market trials for 'Rhino-E' underway. Strategic pillars include volume growth, improved pricing, fostering a performance culture, enhancing brand value, strengthening the distribution network, and leveraging merger synergies across manufacturing and supply chain.
