## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited held an Analyst/Institutional Investor Meet on November 9, 2023, to discuss Q2 FY24 results and business updates. The company reported its highest-ever second-quarter sales at INR 229 crores, a 23% increase. Management highlighted margin expansion and strong demand across various sectors. Key business updates included the launch of India's first battery-powered welder, a successful participation in the SN Trade Show in Germany, and ongoing progress on the merger with Ador Fontech, with shareholder votes in favor of the amalgamation.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product Innovation & "Make in India":** Successfully launched India's first battery-powered welder, with official readiness expected in Jan-Feb. Continued focus on R&D, with 5% of profits allocated to the technical team.
*   **Export Market Expansion:** Strong performance in H1 FY24, already matching FY23's export figures. Middle East remains a key market (80% of exports) driven by oil & gas expenditure and brand recognition. Efforts are underway to enter other markets in the Americas.
*   **Flares & Process Equipment Division (FPED):** The ONGC order of INR 125 crores (net of GST) is entering its "hot billing phase" from December/January onwards, with 80-90% of the balance expected to be executed between January and July. Management aims for 20-35% growth in this division for FY25.
*   **Merger with Ador Fontech:** Shareholder votes in favor of amalgamation have been completed; the process is ongoing with the NCLT.
*   **Demand Drivers:** Strong demand from general infrastructure, railways, auto, shipbuilding, power plants, and cement sectors.
*   **Automation Division:** Identified as a key driver for margin improvement in the equipment segment.
*   **Consumable Business:** Focus on product mix improvement, sourcing advantages, and value selling to sustain margins. Capacity expansion of 6,000-10,000 tons planned in the next six months.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Previous Targets:**
    *   **Margins:** Management reported Q2 FY24 PBIT margins of 16.5% on consumables, which they consider one of the highest in four to five years, indicating a strong improvement from previous quarters and a move towards stated goals. The previous target of improving consumable margins to ~15% appears to have been met or exceeded in Q2. Equipment margins are still lagging but are expected to improve as automation scales.
    *   **Working Capital & Debt:** Borrows have reduced significantly to INR 32 crore, and the debt-to-equity ratio remains very low at 0.1%. Working capital is described as "under 9.8%", implying continued focus on efficiency.
    *   **Product Mix & Growth:** The 22% volume growth in consumables and 35% in equipment in Q2 FY24 significantly exceeds historical industry growth rates, suggesting market share gains or exceptionally buoyant demand. Export revenue in H1 FY24 has already matched FY23's total, indicating strong progress towards ambitious international growth targets.
    *   **Headwinds:** Management acknowledges that steel price volatility poses a risk, but states that with rate contracts and the ability to pass on costs with a one-month lag, the risk is manageable. Supply chain issues for equipment have largely eased.
    *   **ONGC Order Execution:** The previous transcript indicated execution starting in Q4 FY23. The current transcript clarifies that the "hot billing phase" begins from December/January onwards, with 80-90% of the balance execution between Jan and July, indicating a slight shift in the timeline for full revenue recognition.
