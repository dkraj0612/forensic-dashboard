## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited's (Ador) investor presentation on June 7, 2022, provided an update on its performance, strategic initiatives, and a significant upcoming merger with Ador Fontech. The company highlighted efforts to improve operational efficiency, reduce debt, and strengthen its market position. Key financial improvements include a reduction in working capital days to 69 and the complete elimination of borrowings by March 2022, bringing the debt-to-equity ratio down to 0.12. The company is focusing on enhancing its product mix, expanding its international presence, and leveraging technology for better customer experience and operational efficiency. A major development discussed was the proposed amalgamation of Ador Welding and Ador Fontech, aimed at creating synergies and enhancing shareholder value. The company also touched upon its project engineering division, which has been refocused on flares and process equipment.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **FY '22 Performance Highlights:**
    *   **Welding Consumables Volume Growth:** ~30%
    *   **Welding Consumables Value Growth:** ~45-48%
    *   **Export Revenue (FY '22):** ₹33 crore
    *   **Flares & Process Equipment Division (FPED) Revenue (FY '22):** ₹27 crore (near breakeven)
*   **Balance Sheet & Ratios (as of March '22):**
    *   **Working Capital Days:** Reduced to 69 days.
    *   **Borrowings:** Reduced from ~₹120 crore (Oct/Nov 2020) to ₹0 crore by March '22.
    *   **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** Reduced to 0.12.
*   **Capacity Utilization (April '22 / Q4 FY22):**
    *   **Wires:** ~90%
    *   **Electrodes:** 60-75%
    *   **Equipment:** 60-70%

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Operational Improvements:** Focus on reducing working capital, eliminating debt, and enhancing margins, particularly in the welding consumables segment.
*   **Product Mix Enhancement:** Continuous effort to improve product mix to drive profitability, acknowledging a ~10-15% gap with competitors in certain areas.
*   **Market Expansion & Distribution:** Strengthening distribution network, including focus on Eastern and Southern geographies. Increasing online sales traction on a B2B level. Aggressive growth targets for Ador International, aiming for 35-40% year-on-year growth, primarily in the Middle East and Africa, with potential European expansion.
*   **Technology Upgradation & CAPEX:** Significant investment planned in upgrading manufacturing technologies, particularly for welding consumables, to improve efficiency and quality. CAPEX will be re-evaluated post-merger, focusing on manufacturing rejig and technology adoption.
*   **Project Engineering Division (FPED):** Refocused strategy on flares and process equipment, emphasizing selection of high-value engineered projects with better return on capital and margins. Expecting stronger performance and gradual improvement.
*   **Merger with Ador Fontech:** Aiming to create synergies in welding, cutting, and complementary businesses. Expected benefits include leveraging the Ador brand, combined distribution, manpower, infrastructure, and financial capabilities. Value creation is seen as synergistic rather than just cost-cutting.
*   **"Make in India" & BIS Standards:** Continued focus on enhancing product quality and obtaining Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) approvals.
*   **Clientele:** General infrastructure, railways, shipbuilding, defense, automotive, and oil & gas sectors remain key focus areas.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Previous Targets:**
    *   The previous historical summary from June 2021 highlighted a target of reducing working capital days and borrowings. The current transcript confirms significant progress: working capital days reduced to 69 (from a previously stated goal of being "along the lines of where we want to be") and borrowings were reduced to zero (from a stated goal of bringing them down).
    *   The previous transcript also mentioned a focus on stable revenue growth, increased margins, better cash flows, and cost reduction. While quantitative margin targets were not provided then, the current transcript acknowledges the ongoing challenge of improving consumable margins back to historical levels (around 15%) due to raw material price volatility.
    *   The previous narrative did not explicitly discuss headwinds. This transcript, while optimistic, acknowledges recent demand lulls (May '22) and the impact of steel price volatility, indicating a more nuanced view of current market conditions.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter (Q1 FY23 onwards):**
    *   **Consumable Margins:** Management is working towards improving consumable margins to ~15%, but cannot provide a specific date due to raw material volatility. Acknowledges month-on-month improvements and sees slight improvement, but volatility (especially steel price fluctuations) makes precise timing difficult.
    *   **Exports:** Expects 35-40% year-on-year growth in exports for a few years, driven by new talent and strategy, expanding beyond the Middle East to parts of Europe.
    *   **Demand Scenario:** Demand was good in April, weak in May, but showing signs of recovery in early June. Expects demand to be "fairly decent" but not with the same exuberance as Q4 FY22 due to stocking levels. Equipment demand remains good.
    *   **CAPEX:** CAPEX is being re-evaluated post-merger, focusing on manufacturing rejig and technology upgrades. Expected to increase but the net impact on the balance sheet might not be significant due to land assets. New technology investments have an estimated 2.5-3 year ROI.
    *   **Dividend Payout:** Aims to establish a consistent dividend payout ratio as a new base, expecting it to be sustainable irrespective of CAPEX.
    *   **Factory Consolidation:** Plans are on hold pending a holistic review due to the merger, expected to take a few months.
    *   **Flares & Process Equipment (FPED):** Targeting ₹50 crore revenue in FY '23, with potential to scale to ₹100 crore with 10-12% margins and 18-20% ROC.
    *   **Equipment Business:** Expected to scale to ₹150-170 crore in 2-3 years. Steady-state margins are expected to improve slightly, potentially moving towards mid-teens, but not necessarily at the same level as consumables.
    *   **Merger Synergies:** Expected to be significant over 2-3 years, focusing on value creation and synergistic benefits. Specific quantitative benefits are not yet disclosed.
    *   **International Business:** Targets 35-40% year-on-year growth, with potential contribution from online sales.
    *   **Market Share:** Estimates 15-20% share in the organized welding consumables market and 5-6% in welding equipment.
    *   **Kuwait Receivable:** Hopeful for recovery of ~₹15 crore in receivables within 12-18 months.
    *   **Ador Fontech Margins:** Acknowledged to be higher (14-16%) due to specialized product mix and applications, which explains part of the valuation differential with welding consumables business.
    *   **Combined Entity Growth:** Aims to grow at least 4-5 percentage points above IIP over the next 3-5 years.
    *   **Future Gaps:** Acknowledges gaps in welding automation and certain imported product segments, open to inorganic growth to fill these.
    *   **No specific revenue or EBITDA guidance is provided for FY23.** Guidance is qualitative and focuses on strategic intent and operational improvements.
