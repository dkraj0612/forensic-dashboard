## Executive Overview

This document synthesizes information from Ador Welding Limited's (Ador) presentation held on June 10, 2021, for an Investors Meet. The presentation, held virtually, provided updates on the company's progress and financials for FY 2020-21, addressing analysts' and investors' queries. Ador Welding, an established industrial group with over 113 years of history, highlighted its FY21 group revenues of approximately ₹700 crore. The company also touched upon new ventures, including e-Vehicle Chargers and an eCom subsidiary (1908 e-Ventures). The Indian welding market is projected to grow from ₹4,500 crore to ₹6,700 crore by 2025, with Ador positioning itself to capitalize on this growth through its focus on infrastructure, railways, defence, oil & gas, steel, and automotive sectors. Key strategic actions include strengthening distribution, reconfiguring manufacturing systems, advanced product development, enhancing application-oriented selling, and improving order strike ratios. The company aims for sustained business operations, focusing on stable revenue growth, increased margins, better cash flows, and cost reduction.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Growth:** The Indian welding market is projected to grow at a 9% CAGR to ₹6,700 crore by 2025. Ador targets capturing a significant share of this growth.
*   **Key Growth Sectors:**
    *   **Infrastructure:** Driven by the Government's National Infrastructure Pipeline (₹111 lakh crore by 2025).
    *   **Railways & Defence:** Supported by "Make in India" and import embargoes on defense items.
    *   **Core Sectors (Oil & Gas, Steel, Power, Auto):** Significant government outlay in PLI schemes, introduction of vehicle scrappage policy, and substantial investment in oil and gas infrastructure.
    *   **Steel Consumption:** Expected to grow at a 10% CAGR to 140 Million Metric Ton by FY22.
*   **Research & Development:** Ador possesses an R&D center for equipment and consumables, recognized by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research, with 35 researchers and an ISO 17025 accredited laboratory.
*   **Strategic Actions:**
    *   Strengthen distribution and market presence.
    *   Reconfigure manufacturing systems and focus on advanced product development.
    *   Enhance application-oriented selling and improve order strike ratios.
    *   Utilize IT systems for efficiency.
*   **Forward-Looking Objectives:**
    *   Sustained revenue growth, increased margins, better cash flows, and cost reduction.
    *   Consolidate business offerings and build on the Ador brand.
    *   Reduce legacy costs.
    *   Enhance customer experience through technology innovation and process alignment.
*   **New Business Ventures:** Exploration of e-Vehicle Chargers and establishment of an eCom subsidiary (1908 e-Ventures).
