## Ador Welding Limited (AWL) - Q4 FY26 Earnings Call Summary

**Date of Call:** April 30, 2026
**Transcript Reference:** Q4_2026

---

## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) held its Analyst/Institutional Investor Meet on April 30, 2026, to discuss its Q4 FY26 and full-year FY26 performance. The company reported FY26 standalone revenue growth of approximately 2%, driven by a 3% increase in Q4 FY26 revenue. EBITDA for Q4 FY26 was INR 38 crores, with a 200 bps increase in EBITDA margin. Gross margins stood at 36% for Q4 and 38% for the full year, up 250 bps YoY. The company successfully recovered INR 14 crores from the old Kuwait project and reported PBT of approximately INR 130 crores, excluding the impact of the Uran project. Management reiterated its commitment to product innovation, market expansion, and operational efficiency, highlighting a partnership with Miller for specialized welding equipment and strengthening its "Made in India" offerings in robotics and specialized consumables. The company is also focusing on building its automation capabilities and expanding into sectors like shipbuilding and defense. AWL is celebrating its 75th year and aims to continue its focus on creating the best welding experience.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Focus:** Delivering the best welding experience and aiming to be India's No. 1 Welding Company.
*   **Product Innovation:**
    *   Partnership with Miller for submerged arc welding applications in power, structural, and shipbuilding.
    *   Strengthening "Made in India" robotic line products.
    *   Adding nuclear-approved consumables and consumables for the wind manufacturing industry.
    *   Focus on high-end nickels and special critical welding applications.
    *   Developing battery-powered welding equipment ("Rhino-E"), recognized by CII.
*   **Automation & Robotics:** Significant push in automation, robotics, cobots, and laser welding, with new products and initial orders secured.
*   **Market Penetration:** Aggressive focus on shipbuilding, defense, thermal, and nuclear sectors. Expansion into new international markets (USA, Australia).
*   **Operational Efficiency & Margin Improvement:** Continued efforts to improve margins incrementally, aiming for 100-200 bps improvement over coming periods.
*   **Flares and Process Equipment (FPED) Division Restructuring:** Realigned into a product management-driven function within the larger business, focusing on INR 20-30 crores product lines with profitability. Expects breakeven from this product line.
*   **Brand Positioning:** Initiatives to align marketing and branding with its position as a Tier 1 player not part of a global MNC.
*   **Acquisition Strategy:** Open to targeted acquisitions, focusing on technology rather than solely market share.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Audit of Previous Targets:**
    *   **FY25 Revenue Growth (5%):** The current transcript reports FY26 standalone revenue growth of ~2% and Q4 FY26 growth of ~3%. This indicates a deceleration from the reported FY25 growth.
    *   **International Business Growth (over 25%):** The transcript indicates FY26 exports were "fairly flattish" compared to the previous year, a significant shift from the strong growth reported in FY25. The Middle East market, particularly Saudi Arabia, saw some softness but remains a core market.
    *   **Ador Fontech Integration:** While not explicitly stated as complete, the focus has shifted from integration to addressing past baggage (Uran, Kuwait). The business is now described as purely welding.
    *   **Services Business (FPED) Turnaround:** The FPED division has been restructured into a product line. Management expects the "Flares and Process Equipment" product line to breakeven, indicating a continued effort towards profitability in this area, but the separate division is no longer.
    *   **Product Mix & Premiumization:** Consistent focus is evident through the Miller partnership, nuclear approvals, and expansion in specialized consumables.
    *   **New Markets (USA, Australia):** Management confirms traction in the US market in the last two months despite earlier tariff issues.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter (FY25 onwards):**
    *   **FY27 Capex:** Expected to be INR 30-35 crores, possibly stretching to INR 40 crores over the next two years, primarily for new welding consumables lines.
    *   **Margin Improvement:** Expects 100-200 bps improvement on EBITDA margins over the next few quarters, with some contribution from gross margins.
    *   **FPED Product Line Breakeven:** Expects the restructured "Flares and Process Equipment" product line to break even on an approximate INR 20-30 crores revenue base.
    *   **Domestic Volume Growth:** Currently tracking steel consumption and IIP data (4-5% growth). Aims for 6-7% or potentially 10% growth through increased capex, automation, and international expansion.
    *   **International Market Outlook:** Exports were "flattish" in FY26. Expects improved demand in Middle East and traction in the US. Interest in Europe post-FTA is noted.
    *   **Welding Automation:** Best order book ever seen for welding automation, with significant scope for growth.
    *   **Acquisitions:** Open to targeted acquisitions, focusing on technology and adjacency opportunities within fabrication, joining, or cutting of steel.
    *   **Shipbuilding Sector:** A big focus area; holds ~75-80% of required approvals. Potential for significant incremental revenue, though a target of INR 1,000 crores over 5-7 years from this sector is deemed a "very large number."
    *   **Revenue Growth Target (FY29):** While not explicitly reaffirming INR 2,000 crores by FY29, management reiterates the principle of tripling earnings over a similar period and is working towards achieving significant growth through multiple drivers.
    *   **Middle East Exposure:** Remains high and secure through distributors; demand is expected to be good.
    *   **Uran Project:** Accounting losses are complete. The project is at 96-97% closure with minor delays in final commissioning, but no major further exposures or losses are anticipated.

    *   **Income Tax Demand:** Appeal filed last week; management is hopeful it will come in their favor.
    *   **Consumables vs. Equipment Mix:** Automation is expected to increase, but the current base is small, so no significant margin impact is foreseen. Focus on growing this segment and plugging gaps.
    *   **Market Share:** Estimated domestic market share is 17-18%.
    *   **Capex for Volume Doubling (5-7 years):** Estimated INR 100-150 crores (excluding land).
    *   **Geopolitical Issues:** Management is comfortable with the current position after addressing merger and loss contracts, indicating readiness for the future.
    *   **Steel Cycle:** Acknowledged as being in a brighter phase, presenting some challenges but generally manageable.
    *   **Shareholder Value/Market Cap:** Management believes the market understands the company; historical issues were holding back perception, and this is now being resolved.
