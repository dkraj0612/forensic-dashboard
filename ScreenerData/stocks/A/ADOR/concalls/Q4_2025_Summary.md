## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) presented its FY25 performance and future outlook at an Investor Presentation on May 12th, 2025. The company reported a 5% revenue growth for FY25, driven by over 25% growth in its international business. Domestic volumes remained flat, though there were signs of a pick-up in the equipment segment from March onwards, likely due to increasing CAPEX spending. The integration of Ador Fontech is nearing completion (98%), with expected synergies to materialize in the next 6-18 months. Management reiterated its long-term vision to transform AWL into a mid-cap entity in terms of valuation, processes, and structures, aiming to double revenue in four years. The strategy emphasizes product innovation (flux-cored wire, stainless steel, EV welders), premiumization, and expansion into high-growth sectors like defense, thermal, and nuclear, as well as new international markets (US, Australia).

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **FY25 Revenue Growth:** 5%
*   **FY25 International Business Growth (Value):** Over 25%
*   **FY25 Domestic Volume:** Flat (equipment segment showing pick-up from March)
*   **FY25 Operating Cash Flow:** INR 139 crore
*   **Working Capital Improvement:** 18 days reduction in working capital cycle.
*   **Debt Status:** Implied debt-free (from previous context).
*   **Ador Fontech Integration:** Approximately 98% completed.
*   **Services Business (FPED) Performance:** Continued focus on turnaround, cost optimization, and improving margins. Specific financial metrics for FY25 are not detailed in this transcript, but the previous context indicated an operating loss.
*   **M&R Business (formerly Ador Fontech):** Focus on in-house products (Flares, Process Equipment, Heat Exchangers) with improved margins.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Vision:** Creating the best welding experience.
*   **Mission:** To be India's No. 1 Welding Company.
*   **Goal:** To be a Mid-Cap Company by 2029.
*   **Product Innovation & Premiumization:** Continued focus on flux-cored wire, stainless-steel products, and high-end welding consumables. Market trials for "Rhino-E" (EV welder) have begun.
*   **Market Penetration:** Aggressive focus on defense, thermal, and nuclear sectors. Expansion into new international markets, specifically the USA and Australia.
*   **Operational Excellence & Efficiency:** Driving efficiency, cost optimization, and manufacturing improvements.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Volume growth and improved pricing across all product segments. Emphasis on performance culture and time-to-market for new products.
*   **Merger Synergies:** Realizing value creation through integration of Ador Fontech, with synergies across manufacturing and supply chain.
*   **Distribution Network:** Strengthening the distribution network and customer engagement.
