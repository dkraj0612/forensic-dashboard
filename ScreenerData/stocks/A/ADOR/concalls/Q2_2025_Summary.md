## Executive Overview

Ador Welding Limited (AWL) held an Analyst / Institutional Investor Call on October 15th, 2025, for the Half Year ended September 2025. The company reported a 5% quarterly sales growth and highlighted improvements in gross margins (32.7% in Q2 FY25) and EBITDA margins (12.5% in Q2 FY25), driven by disciplined pricing, a better product mix, and improved operational efficiency. For the half-year, revenues were softer, with gross margins at 31% and EBITDA at 11%. Working capital improved, with inventory days at 47, and ROCE is back to 23%. The company is focusing on launching new, higher-end products across welding equipment and consumables, including those for wind applications and improved stainless steel products. The welding automation division is showing signs of a turnaround with increased order flow and new robotic solutions. Management expressed confidence in the sustainability of current margin levels and the positive impact of the Ador Fontech merger integration. New market penetration is ongoing, with efforts in shipbuilding and defense sectors, while international growth faces headwinds in the US (tariffs) and Saudi Arabia.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product Innovation & Premiumization:** Introduction of advanced welding equipment (data reading/mapping, solar complementary product to Rhino E) and consumables (fluxes for wind, drum packaging for MIG, new stainless-steel products). Focus on high-margin products.
*   **Welding Automation Turnaround:** Increased order base for existing products, addition of cohort and robotic solutions, with early order wins.
*   **Market Expansion (Specific Segments):** Aggressive efforts in defense and shipbuilding sectors with ongoing approvals and order generation.
*   **Brand Initiatives:** Participation in international exhibitions (e.g., Germany) to enhance brand placement and penetrate select international markets.
*   **Merger Synergies (Ador Fontech):** Integration is progressing well, with benefits starting to be seen in management structures, distribution, and cost efficiencies.
*   **Focus on Core Business:** Strict control over FPED, avoiding large exposure projects and focusing on smaller, profitable ones (up to INR 10-15 Crores).
*   **Distribution Network Optimization:** Leveraging the existing network for new product introductions and market reach.
