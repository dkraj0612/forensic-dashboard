## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) reported Q2 FY26 results with total revenue of INR 2,395.6 million (consolidated), a decline of 8% year-on-year on a standalone basis for Q2 FY26 revenue at INR 2,317.8 million. H1 FY26 consolidated revenue grew 10% year-on-year to INR 5,401.5 million. The company experienced production challenges in elemental bromine due to technical issues and lower efficiencies, impacting volumes despite robust demand and healthy pricing. Industrial salt volumes were below target due to a prolonged monsoon but demand visibility remains strong. ACI is progressing with strategic diversification, notably receiving approval for its SiCSem compound semiconductor project under the Indian Semiconductor Mission. The company also experienced a search and seizure operation by the Income Tax department.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI's strategy remains focused on leveraging its core strengths in marine specialty chemicals while aggressively pursuing diversification into high-potential new-age sectors.

*   **Core Business:**
    *   **Elemental Bromine:** Demand is robust with healthy pricing, but production volumes were impacted by plant inefficiencies and technical issues, exacerbated by extended monsoons. Management is implementing corrective measures to improve efficiency and expects performance to improve. Long-term contracts mitigate spot price volatility.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** Volumes in Q2 were below guidance due to prolonged monsoon impacting production and transportation, but demand remains strong with long-term contracts. Management is confident in meeting the FY26 guidance of 4.5 million tons.
    *   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Pilot trials have been successful. Plant modifications are underway for plant-scale trials in Q4 FY26. Commercial production and sales are expected post-monsoon FY27, with potential for high margins.
*   **Downstream Integration & Diversification:**
    *   **Bromine Derivatives (Acume Chemicals):** Operating at 30-35% capacity utilization, with expected improvement to 50% by year-end as customer qualifications and approvals for Clear Brine Fluids (CBF) are secured.
    *   **Oren Hydrocarbon (now Idealis Mudchemie):** Progress in reviving and refurbishing units has been slower than anticipated due to challenges from NCLT acquisition and lower global crude prices. Samples have been sent to customers. Meaningful revenue contribution is now expected from FY27.
    *   **Semiconductor Initiative (SiCSem Private Limited):** Received approval from the Union Cabinet under the Indian Semiconductor Mission. Ground-breaking ceremony held, marking the commencement of commissioning. Phase 1 investment of INR 2,067 crores is planned, with a funding mix involving government support and company contribution. The project has a timeline of approximately 30 months for completion. Collaboration with Clas-SiC (UK) for technology access and training.
    *   **Energy Storage Initiative (Offgrid Energy Labs Inc., US):** Investment of USD 12 million for a 21% stake completed. Offgrid plans a 10 MWh demo manufacturing facility in the UK, taking 12-18 months. Investment proceeds will fund the pilot plant's capex and opex for two years.
    *   **Flame Retardant (TBBA - Phase 2):** Actively being pursued, with updates expected in coming months.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit against Historical Context (Q2 FY25 Transcript Guidance):**

*   **Logistics & Monsoon Impact:** **Consistent observation, with impacts still significant.** The Q2 FY26 transcript reiterates the prolonged and erratic monsoon as a significant factor impacting production efficiency for bromine and volumes for industrial salt, consistent with historical observations but highlighting ongoing challenges.
*   **Bromine Volume Guidance (FY25 - now FY26):** **Guidance for FY26 is being re-evaluated due to production issues.** The Q2 FY25 transcript projected "in excess of 20,000 metric tons for the year" (FY25). In Q2 FY26, management indicates they aim to "meet last year's production volumes" (approx. 18,000 tons) despite production challenges, and that FY27 volumes are expected to be progressively ramped up. The previous FY25 guidance of 20,000-25,000 tons for FY26 is not directly reaffirmed, but rather the focus is on improving H2 FY26 performance over H1.
*   **Industrial Salt Volume Guidance (FY25 - now FY26):** **Guidance of 4.5 million tons maintained.** The Q2 FY25 transcript aimed for 4.5 million tons for FY25. In Q2 FY26, management is confident in meeting the guidance of 4.5 million tons for FY26, despite Q2 volumes being below target.
*   **SOP Contribution:** **Timeline shifted to FY27.** The Q2 FY25 transcript expected meaningful contributions in FY26. The Q2 FY26 transcript now indicates commercial production and sales are expected post-monsoon FY27.
*   **Oren Hydrocarbon Commencement:** **Significant delays encountered.** The Q2 FY25 transcript anticipated meaningful contributions from Q4 FY25. The Q2 FY26 transcript admits to "disappointingly slow" progress due to NCLT acquisition challenges and now expects meaningful revenue from FY27.
*   **Bromine Derivatives Ramp-up:** **Slower than anticipated.** The Q2 FY25 transcript noted satisfactory ramp-up. In Q2 FY26, utilization is still at 30-35%, with expectations to reach 50% by year-end. Revenue for H1 FY26 was INR 43 crores.
