## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) experienced a challenging Q1 FY25, primarily due to significant logistics-related disruptions impacting dispatches, especially for its dominant Industrial Salt export business. Total revenue for the quarter stood at INR 2,234 million, a 37% year-on-year decline, with Industrial Salt contributing 55% and Bromine 44% of the revenue. EBITDA was INR 849 million, maintaining a healthy 38% margin, while Net Profit After Tax was INR 485 million. Despite the revenue dip, management anticipates a recovery in the coming quarters as logistical challenges ease. The company continues to advance its downstream integration strategy, with its Bromine derivatives subsidiary, Acume Chemicals, showing encouraging client response, and the Oren Hydrocarbon acquisition receiving NCLT approval, expected to commence operations in H2 FY25.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI is navigating near-term logistical headwinds while pressing forward with its long-term growth initiatives:

*   **Core Business Resilience:**
    *   **Bromine:** Steady domestic demand and an encouraging pickup in export markets from Agro Chemicals, Pharmaceuticals, and Flame-Retardant manufacturers. Management has set a target of 22,000 to 25,000 metric tons for Bromine production in FY25, including captive requirements. Pricing is stable, with contracts continuing.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** The business experienced dispatch delays due to truck shortages and port berthing issues, impacting Q1 volumes. However, demand remains firm, and customer contracts are in place, with an expectation of recovery in FY25 contingent on easing external challenges. The focus is on improving processes, cost efficiencies, and logistics.
    *   **SOP:** Pilot plant trials have shown progress. The company is contemplating a second-grade SOP for domestic and global markets, with sales expected to commence in Q3 FY25 and meaningful contributions anticipated in H2 FY25.
*   **Downstream Integration & Diversification:**
    *   **Bromine Derivatives (Acume Chemicals):** Clear Brine Fluids (CBF) and PTA Synthesis products are receiving encouraging responses, with approximately 10 clients approved and discussions ongoing with over a third of the client base. Sales are expected to start ramping up from September and accelerate in H2 FY25.
    *   **Oren Hydrocarbon Acquisition:** NCLT approval was received on July 9, 2024. The acquisition is synergistic, aiming to offer specialty mud chemicals and CBF products to the Oil & Gas and drilling industries. Refurbishment is underway, with business expected to commence in H2 FY25, focusing on two key plants initially.
    *   **New Growth Platform (Sicsem Private Limited):** A subsidiary has been incorporated for operations in the Power Electronics segment, with initial R&D work on silicon carbide crystal growth in collaboration with IIT Bhubaneshwar. Further details will be provided as development progresses.
    *   **Flame Retardant (FR) Products (TBBA - Phase 2):** Management is progressing with obtaining permissions but will proceed in a measured manner, with commissioning unlikely before March 2025, prioritizing the ramp-up of Phase 1 derivatives.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit against Historical Context (Q4 FY24 Guidance for Q1 FY25 / FY25):**

*   **Logistics Challenges:** **Consistent with concerns, more impactful than anticipated.** While logistics challenges were flagged in the previous quarter, the impact on Q1 FY25 dispatches, particularly for Industrial Salt, proved more significant, leading to a revenue decline. Management acknowledged this as the primary reason for the lower volumes.
*   **Bromine Volume Guidance (FY25):** **Slightly conservative adjustment.** The previous guidance for FY25 was around 25,000-28,000 MT. The current opening remarks mention 22,000-25,000 MT, with management clarifying this as a conservative stance due to the ramp-up of the derivative plant's captive consumption and not reflecting any demand issues.
*   **SOP Contribution:** **Consistent with expectation of H2 FY25 impact.** The expectation for meaningful contributions in H2 FY25 and initial sales in Q3 FY25 remains.
*   **Oren Hydrocarbon Synergies & Revenue:** **Synergies confirmed, revenue projection recalibrated.** The rationale for acquisition remains strong. However, the revenue projection for the first year has been adjusted downwards from INR 100-250 crores to a more conservative "200 plus mark" due to the delay in NCLT approval and subsequent startup in H2 FY25.
*   **Bromine Pricing:** **Stable as anticipated.** Management reiterated that current pricing is stable around INR 220-225 per kilo and sees no reason for it to drop, despite fluctuations in spot prices.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** **Strong and improving.** Q1 FY25 EBITDA margin of 38% is consistent with and slightly above the targeted range, aided by operational efficiencies and a shift towards grid power, reducing costs and carbon footprint.
