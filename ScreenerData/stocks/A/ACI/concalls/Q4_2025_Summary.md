## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) reported Q4 FY25 and full-year FY25 results, demonstrating resilience in a challenging global chemical market. The company highlighted strong demand for its core products, particularly Bromine and Industrial Salt, and made progress on strategic diversification initiatives. Management expressed cautious optimism regarding a global market recovery, citing geopolitical uncertainties but also noting signs of improving demand and stable supply chains. ACI's Q4 FY25 standalone revenue reached INR 3,333.3 million, with Industrial Salt accounting for 76% and Bromine 24%. For the full year FY25, standalone revenue was INR 10,634.5 million, with Bromine at 35% and Industrial Salt at 65% of the mix. The company remains net debt-free with a strong balance sheet.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI continues to focus on strengthening its core marine chemical operations while advancing strategic growth areas.

*   **Core Business:**
    *   **Elemental Bromine:** Remains a key strength, with ACI being India's largest exporter and manufacturer. Contracted volumes were broadly in line with the prior year, though Q4 FY25 volumes were impacted by an extended winter causing subdued evaporation rates and lower brine concentration. Demand consistently exceeds supply. ACI is focused on improving brine efficiency and has infrastructure capacity exceeding 30,000 tons, with potential for enhancement.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** ACI is India's largest exporter. Q4 FY25 saw a strong recovery with volumes reaching 1.3 million tons. Operational challenges, primarily logistics, have been addressed by adding a dedicated fleet. With a new washery and logistics investments, annual capacity now exceeds 5 million tons. Long-term customer relationships and contracts provide stability.
    *   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Trials are progressing, with plant-scale demonstrations planned for the next quarter. Meaningful contributions are still anticipated from the second half of FY26.
*   **Downstream Integration & Diversification:**
    *   **Bromine Derivatives:** Operations are scaling up, with current utilization between 20-30%. Clear brine fluids and catalysts for PTA synthesis contributed to Q4 FY25 performance. Exports have commenced. The company aims to reach over 50% utilization in the near term and 60-70% in FY27. Investments to date total INR 160-170 crores.
    *   **Oren Hydrocarbon (Idealis Mudchemie):** Progress in reviving operations, with two facilities in Andhra Pradesh ready. Remaining units are expected to commence operations in the latter part of FY26. Conservative revenue contribution of INR 150 crores is expected in FY26.
    *   **Semiconductor Initiative (SiCSem Private Limited):** Land acquisition is complete. Application submitted to the Indian Semiconductor Mission, awaiting approval. State-level approval in Orissa is secured.
    *   **Energy Storage Initiative (Offgrid Energy Labs Inc., US):** Investment progressing well, with the US-based innovator advancing site identification and vendor selection for a pilot plant in the UK.
    *   **Flame Retardant Bromine Project:** Actively being pursued. The project is part of the overall Bromine Derivatives business budget.
