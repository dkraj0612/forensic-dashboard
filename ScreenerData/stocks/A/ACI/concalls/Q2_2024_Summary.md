## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) reported Q2 FY24 results amidst industry-wide headwinds, including destocking and geopolitical uncertainties. While overall revenue saw a marginal YoY drop of 3% to INR 3,020 million, the company maintained an EBITDA margin of 35% and reported a net profit of INR 663 million, a 4% YoY increase. Management indicated a positive outlook for the second half of FY24, expecting demand to improve from end-customers as inventory levels normalize. The company continues to focus on downstream integration, with its Bromine derivative plant construction on track for commercialization between Q4 FY24 and Q1 FY25. Discussions with potential clients for these derivatives are progressing, and the company sees opportunities arising from the geopolitical situation. ACI also reported traction in its Sulphate of Potash (SOP) business and a consistent volume run rate for Industrial Salt.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI's strategy remains focused on value chain enhancement and downstream integration, particularly in Bromine derivatives, while maintaining market leadership in its core products.

*   **Bromine Derivatives:** The plant construction is on track, with commercialization expected between Q4 FY24 and Q1 FY25. Initial sampling and R&D efforts are underway, with trials for PTA Synthesis and Clear Brine Fluids progressing. Flame Retardants are part of Phase 2, with sampling expected a few months away.
*   **Bromine Business:** Management acknowledges current headwinds like destocking and temporary blips in demand. However, the company anticipates improved demand in H2 FY24 and sees opportunities from customers seeking alternative sources due to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East. ACI maintains a prudent approach, prioritizing margins over chasing volume at lower prices.
*   **Industrial Salt:** Consistent quarterly volume run rate of 1 million tons is maintained, serving chlorine and chlorine-derivative manufacturers with firm demand driven by long-term contracts.
*   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Traction from clients is observed, with meaningful business done in Q2 FY24. Multiple trials are ongoing for product diversification, and the domestic market is showing healthy demand.
*   **Client Base Expansion:** ACI has expanded its client base with a balanced mix of global and domestic customers, entering into longer-term contracts with larger clients.
*   **Geopolitical Opportunity:** The company is experiencing more meaningful dialogue with new prospects for both elemental Bromine and derivatives, leveraging the "Israel Plus One" strategy.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Target Attainment & Narrative Consistency:**

*   **Bromine Derivative Plant Commissioning & Sampling:** Management confirms the Bromine derivative plant construction is on track for commercialization within the previously indicated timeframe (Q4 FY24 - Q1 FY25). Sampling for PTA Synthesis and Clear Brine Fluids has commenced, with trials in R&D near completion for customer dispatches starting in the new year. This aligns with prior guidance.
*   **Bromine Market Headwinds:** Management consistently acknowledges the temporary blip in bromine demand due to industry-wide destocking and a "slowing down of consumption" in China impacting raw material sourcing. This narrative is consistent with the previous quarter's report.
*   **SOP Business Traction:** The company reiterates seeing traction and meaningful business in SOP, consistent with previous statements.
*   **Geopolitical Impact:** Management's stance on the Middle East tensions has evolved slightly. While previously stating no significant impact, they now acknowledge potential opportunities as customers seek "plan B" alternate sources. This is a nuanced shift, acknowledging an opportunity rather than a direct supply disruption impact.
*   **Pricing and Margins:** Management reiterates its commitment to maintaining EBITDA margins in the 37%-40% range, even amidst lower bromine realization prices. They are not willing to sell below desired price levels, indicating a focus on margin protection.

**New Forward-Looking Promises & Guidance:**

*   **Bromine Derivative Commercialization:** Expected between Q4 FY24 and Q1 FY25.
*   **Bromine Derivative Sampling:** Trials and test samples are ready; customer dispatches expected to start in the new year.
*   **Bromine Volumes (FY25):** Management does not dispute the assumption of healthy volume growth in FY25 due to restocking and customers de-risking geographical concentration.
*   **SOP Technology Partner Trials:** Last batch trials with the technology partner are expected in Q4 FY24, potentially leading to infrastructure changes with minimal capex.
*   **Bromine Consumption (In-house):** At peak utilization, ACI plans to consume 13,000-14,000 tons per annum of Bromine in-house for derivatives.
*   **Bromine Derivative Spread:** On market prices of Bromine, a margin of 20%-25% is expected for derivatives.
*   **Flame Retardant Consumption:** Approximately 40% of the 13,000-14,000 tons of captive Bromine consumption will be for flame retardants.
*   **FY25 Volume Outlook:** Management is not revising the yearly volume outlook, indicating an expectation to meet targets, with Q3 and Q4 typically being the strongest quarters.
*   **Cost Savings:** Targeting cost savings primarily on power and fuel, with a switch to gridline power expected this quarter, leading to significant reduction in power costs.
*   **Battery Opportunity:** Progress is being made in the battery space/energy storage, with details to be shared upon delivery.
*   **Specialty Chemical Subsidies:** New subsidies incorporated in October are intended for opportunities in the specialty chemical space, with dedicated vehicles being considered.
