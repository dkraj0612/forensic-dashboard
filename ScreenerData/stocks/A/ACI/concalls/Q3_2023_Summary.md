## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) held its first-ever earnings call for Q3 FY2023, marking a significant milestone post-IPO. The company reported robust financial performance, with total revenue growing 19% year-over-year to Rs. 3,723 million, driven primarily by exports (77% of revenue). EBITDA saw a substantial 36% YoY increase, reaching Rs. 1,677 million, translating to a healthy 45% EBITDA margin. Net profit after tax more than doubled to Rs. 983 million. For the nine-month period, revenue grew 42% YoY to Rs. 10,943 million, with EBITDA up 51% YoY to Rs. 4,755 million at a 43.5% margin. Net profit after tax surged 131% YoY to Rs. 2,468 million. The company utilized IPO proceeds to redeem NCDs, resulting in a net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.1x. ROE and ROCE remain strong, exceeding 40%.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI's strategy centers on expanding its core product offerings (bromine, industrial salt, sulphate of potash) and moving into value-added downstream derivatives.

*   **Core Product Expansion:**
    *   **Bromine:** Capacity expansion from 28,500 TPA to over 42,000 TPA is underway, with an additional 14,000 TPA earmarked for downstream derivative production.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** Capacity expansion through an additional washing line is planned, increasing throughput. The company is operating at over 100% utilization.
    *   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Efforts are focused on resolving raw material preparation challenges to improve production numbers.
*   **Downstream Derivatives:** A greenfield project in Jhagadia, Gujarat, will produce bromine derivatives like flame retardants, clear brine fluids, and bromine catalysts. Production is expected to commence by the end of FY2024. This project involves an capex of Rs. 250 Crores and is funded by a mix of equity and debt (1/3rd own funds, 2/3rd borrowing).
*   **Market Focus:** Exports remain a significant revenue driver, particularly in the Far East and Middle East, with an increasing exposure to Europe for SOP.
*   **Customer Base:** A diversified client base of 52 clients, with 27 global and 25 domestic, including large conglomerates and global trading companies.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Target Attainment & Narrative Consistency:**

*   **Bromine Sales Decline (QoQ/YoY):** Management attributed the Q3 YoY decline in bromine sales primarily to COVID-related logistical disruptions and shipment staggering in China, a major importer. They characterized this as an "aberration" expected to correct as the situation in China improves. They also highlighted the successful diversion of volume to the robust domestic market.
*   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Management acknowledged past challenges in raw material preparation for the SOP plant but stated that solutions have been found, expecting improved production in upcoming periods. The production of 9,000 tonnes has been achieved, and sales are being negotiated for booking within FY2023.
*   **Derivatives Plant Commissioning:** Initial understanding suggested Q2 FY2024 for commissioning, but management clarified that commercial production and billing are targeted to start *before* the end of FY2024, indicating no delay, but rather an ambition to potentially bring forward the timeline for both phases of the plant.

**New Forward-Looking Promises & Guidance:**

*   **Bromine Capacity:** Expansion to over 42,000 TPA, with an additional 14,000 TPA for derivatives.
*   **Derivatives Plant:**
    *   Commence production by end of FY2024.
    *   Target revenue of approximately three times asset turn on full ramp-up (implying around Rs. 750 Cr. revenue on Rs. 250 Cr. capex).
    *   Target EBITDA margins in the early 30s for derivatives.
    *   Initial internal consumption of 5,000-6,000 tonnes of bromine, scaling up to 13,000 tonnes.
*   **Industrial Salt Capacity:** Additional washing line expected in the second half of the current calendar year.
*   **Employee Cost:** New normal employee cost expected to be around Rs. 40 Crores per annum.
*   **Funding for Derivatives Plant:** 1/3rd from own funds, 2/3rd from borrowing.
*   **SOP Production:** Expectation of improved production numbers in the months and years to come.
*   **Salt Realization:** Realization growth of approximately 30% for nine months, sustainable at current levels for the next year.
*   **Bromine Internal Consumption:** Anticipation of 5,000-6,000 tonnes in the first year, scaling up to 13,000 tonnes for the downstream plant.
*   **Bromine Capacity Utilization:** Aiming to move utilization up from 75%, with capacity expansion also planned.
*   **Bromine Pricing:** Contracts are on a rolling basis, with new contracts reflecting current market prices over time. Endeavor to maintain current margins.
