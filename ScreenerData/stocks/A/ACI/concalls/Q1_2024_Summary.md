## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) has released its Q1 FY25 investor presentation, highlighting significant strategic progress and a substantial financial event. The company announced a key acquisition of Oren Hydrocarbons Private Limited for approximately Rs. 77 crores, aiming to enhance its offerings in specialized mud chemicals for the Oil & Gas sector. Operationally, Phase 1 of the Jhagadia, Gujarat, derivatives plant has been commissioned, producing Clear Brine Fluids and PTA Synthesis catalysts. Phase 2, focused on flame retardant products, is under re-evaluation. While specific financial figures for Q1 FY25 are not detailed in this presentation, the report emphasizes ACI's established leadership in Indian Bromine merchant sales and its position as a leading manufacturer of specialty marine chemicals, including industrial salt and SOP. The company continues to focus on moving up the value chain through downstream integration and capacity augmentation.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI's strategy is centered on value chain enhancement, downstream integration, and strategic acquisitions:

*   **Downstream Derivatives:**
    *   **Phase 1 (Clear Brine Fluids, PTA Synthesis Catalyst):** Commissioned at Jhagadia, Gujarat, via subsidiary Acume Chemicals Private Limited. This marks a key step in expanding Bromine derivative product offerings. Part 2 of Phase 1 will add more Bromine derivative products.
    *   **Phase 2 (Flame Retardants):** The company is re-evaluating this phase of capacity expansion.
*   **Strategic Acquisition:**
    *   A wholly-owned subsidiary was declared the successful bidder for Oren Hydrocarbons Private Limited for ~Rs. 77 crores. This acquisition targets the Oil & Gas sector, providing high-quality specialized mud chemicals (lubricants and drilling fluids).
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Bromine Derivatives:** Expanded downstream Bromine derivative performance products.
    *   **Bromine & Industrial Salt:** Expanded capacities through debottlenecking.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** Intends to add one additional washery (250 tons per hour capacity) for production.
    *   **Bromine Derivatives Capacity:** Added 14,500 MT per annum capacity in FY23 for the greenfield project.
*   **Market Position:**
    *   Maintains a leadership position in Indian Bromine merchant sales.
    *   Only manufacturer of SOP from natural sea brine in India.
    *   100% export business for Industrial Salt.
    *   Strong clientele with 40 global and 32 domestic customers.
*   **Product Diversification:**
    *   Focus on Bromine, Industrial Salt, and Sulphate of Potash (SOP).
    *   SOP production was impacted by higher NaCl content in KTMS (kainite type mixed salt) historically, with efforts to improve harvesting and conversion ratios ongoing.
