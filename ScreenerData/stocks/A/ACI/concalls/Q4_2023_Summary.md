## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) reported strong financial results for Q4 FY2023 and the full fiscal year. Revenue for FY2023 grew 30% YoY to Rs. 14,852 million (Rs. 1,485 crores), driven by a robust performance in both bromine and industrial salt segments. EBITDA for the full year increased by 41% YoY to Rs. 6,785 million, with a healthy EBITDA margin of 46%. Net profit after tax more than doubled, growing 103% YoY to Rs. 3,837 million, significantly aided by a reduction in interest costs following the redemption of NCDs. The company achieved a net debt-to-equity ratio of zero and reported strong returns with ROE at 45% and ROCE at 39% for FY2023.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI's strategy continues to focus on expanding its core marine chemicals portfolio and moving into value-added downstream derivatives. The company aims to leverage its integrated production capabilities.

*   **Core Product Focus:**
    *   **Bromine:** Leadership position maintained in India for merchant sales and exports. Demand in the first half of FY23 was strong, with some impact from staggered shipments in the second half. The company successfully diverted volume to the domestic market.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** Strong performance driven by higher volumes and improved contracted prices. Capacity utilization exceeded 100%, and the company added new customers.
    *   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Efforts are ongoing to increase production. While production of 8,840 tons was achieved in FY23, sales are being negotiated, with improved production expected in coming periods.
*   **Downstream Derivatives:** The greenfield project in Jhagadia, Gujarat, for bromine derivatives (flame retardants, clear brine fluids, bromine catalysts) is on track for production commencement by the end of FY2024. This project involves Rs. 250 Crores in capex.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Industrial Salt:** Additional washing line capacity of 250 tons per hour expected in the latter part of FY2024.
    *   **Bromine:** Continued investment in capacity expansion and capability.
*   **Market Focus:** Exports remain significant (73% of FY23 revenue), with key geographies including Japan, China, South Korea, Qatar, Belgium, and the Netherlands. The domestic market is also a growing focus, particularly for bromine.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Target Attainment & Narrative Consistency:**

*   **Bromine Sales & Pricing:** Management acknowledged the "aberration" in bromine prices and volatile spot prices, attributing them to destocking in the overseas market, particularly China. They reiterated their strategy of focusing on maintaining EBITDA margins through contract-based business and diverting volume to a firm domestic market. The Q4 FY23 transcript indicates that while spot prices might be under pressure, the company's contract-based business and domestic focus have helped maintain realizations. The previous mention of staggering shipments in China was consistent with this call's explanation of destocking.
*   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** The transcript confirms that efforts to resolve raw material preparation challenges are ongoing. The production of 8,840 MT in FY23 is significantly higher than the prior year, and management is actively negotiating sales for existing stock and planning for increased production in the future.
*   **Derivatives Plant Commissioning:** The timeline of commencement of production by the end of FY2024 remains consistent with previous guidance. Management stated that serious marketing efforts would begin towards September-October 2023, providing sufficient time before commercial production.

**New Forward-Looking Promises & Guidance:**

*   **Bromine Pricing:** Expectation of average realization in line with previous investor presentation indications, with a belief that pricing will sustain within a single-digit discount range on spot contracts.
*   **Bromine Volume:** Anticipate growth in FY24, with potential for more than high single-digit growth on a full-year basis.
*   **Industrial Salt Pricing:** Confidence that pricing will remain at current levels, supported by longer-term contracts.
*   **Industrial Salt Volume:** Expect growth of more than single digits in FY24.
*   **SOP Volume:** Expectation to sell around 8,000 MT in FY24, with a target of at least slightly higher than that.
*   **Derivatives Plant (Jhagadia):**
    *   Commence production by the end of FY2024.
    *   Target EBITDA margins of 25-30% on bromine input cost.
    *   Asset turnover expected to be around 3x.
    *   Projected sales of Rs. 600-700 crores.
    *   Captive consumption of ~40% of bromine production at peak capacity.
*   **Employee Cost:** Recurring employee cost (including ESOP provisions) is estimated to be around Rs. 75 crores per annum for the next couple of years.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Aim to maintain EBITDA margins in the 40-45% range for the company overall. Expect OPMs to not decline and potentially expand with the derivatives plant coming online.
*   **Overall Top Line Growth:** Aiming for 2x overall top-line growth in the next two to three years.
*   **SOP Pricing:** Safe assumption of $450 per ton.
*   **Lease Renewal (Rann of Kutch):** Process is underway.
