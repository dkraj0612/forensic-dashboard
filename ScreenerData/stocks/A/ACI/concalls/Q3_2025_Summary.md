## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) reported Q3 FY26 results, showcasing a mixed performance driven by operational challenges and strategic advancements. Standalone revenue saw a 12% year-on-year growth for Q3 FY26, reaching INR 2,608.1 million, with consolidated revenue up 10% YoY. The nine-month period (H1 FY26) for standalone revenue grew 7.4% YoY to INR 7,840 million. While demand for elemental bromine remains strong with favorable pricing, production volumes were constrained by operational issues stemming from weather-induced brine quality changes and plant adjustments, impacting throughput in early Q3. Industrial salt experienced strong year-on-year volume growth in Q3, with stable pricing, and a robust demand outlook. The company is progressing with its strategic diversification, particularly the SiCSem semiconductor project, which has seen groundbreaking and is awaiting finalization of fiscal support. However, delays persist in projects like Oren Hydrocarbon (Idealis Mudchemie) and Sulphate of Potash (SOP) due to technical complexities and ecosystem challenges, respectively. A new Managing Director, Mr. Rampraveen Swaminathan, has been appointed, bringing extensive experience in business leadership and transformations.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI's strategy continues to balance core marine chemical operations with a strong push into advanced materials and new-age sectors.

*   **Core Business:**
    *   **Elemental Bromine:** Demand remains robust with favorable pricing, supported by a healthy order book and internal consumption from derivatives. However, performance was constrained by operational challenges and disruptions from weather-related brine quality changes, impacting throughput. Improvement projects are in progress to restore steady-state operations and aim for a higher run rate in FY27.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** Q3 saw strong year-on-year volume growth, reverting to a quarterly run rate of over 1 million tons. Pricing remained stable sequentially, with robust demand visibility and good long-term contracts. Continued scaling and seasonal uptick are expected in Q4.
    *   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Pilot trials are complete, and plant-scale trials are imminent. Commercial production and sales are now expected in the latter half of FY27. Challenges related to feedstock brine quality are being addressed through plant modifications.
*   **Downstream Integration & Diversification:**
    *   **Bromine Derivatives (Acume):** Operations are at 30-40% utilization, with a focus on expanding the product portfolio to 15 products and improving penetration. The goal is to reach 50-60% utilization by year-end FY26 and 60-70% in FY27.
    *   **Oren Hydrocarbon (Idealis Mudchemie):** Progress has been slow due to NCLT acquisition complexities and ecosystem challenges. Three plants are operational, and customer trials are underway, with meaningful revenue contribution now expected from FY27.
    *   **Semiconductor Initiative (SiCSem Private Limited):** Groundbreaking has occurred, and land preparation is complete. The finalization of the fiscal support agreement with the Indian Semiconductor Mission is pending. The project remains on target with internal schedules.
    *   **Energy Storage Initiative (Offgrid Energy Labs Inc., US):** The company has acquired 18.14% stake. Offgrid plans a 10 MWh demo manufacturing facility in the UK.
    *   **Flame Retardant (TBBA - Phase 2):** Project feasibility is being evaluated with detailed planning. The project is active and not on hold.
