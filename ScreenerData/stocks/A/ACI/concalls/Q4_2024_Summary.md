## Executive Overview

Archean Chemical Industries (ACI) demonstrated resilience in Q4 FY24 despite prevailing macroeconomic challenges and an uneven global chemical market. The company reported total revenue of INR 2,972 million for the quarter, with Bromine contributing 36% and Industrial Salt 64%. Full-year FY24 revenue reached INR 13,763 million. EBITDA for Q4 FY24 stood at INR 1,019 million (34% margin), and Net Profit After Tax was INR 596 million. For the full year, EBITDA was INR 5,110 million (37% margin), and Net Profit After Tax was INR 3,224 million. The company announced a final dividend of INR 1 per equity share, bringing the total FY24 dividend payout to INR 37 crores.

ACI's strategic focus remains on its core bromine and industrial salt businesses while advancing downstream integration. The first phase of the bromine derivatives plant at Jhagadia has been commissioned, with initial sales of Clear Brine Fluids commencing. The acquisition of Oren Hydrocarbon is pending NCLT approval, with management optimistic about its synergies with the bromine derivatives business. Sulphate of Potash (SOP) is progressing, with encouraging pilot plant trial results and the introduction of a second-grade SOP for the domestic market. The company anticipates significant growth from its derivative products in FY25.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ACI is executing a multi-pronged strategy focused on core business strength and strategic diversification:

*   **Core Business Momentum:**
    *   **Bromine:** Demand is normalizing, with a full order book for FY25. Domestic and export markets are showing recovery. Long-term contracts provide stability, with pricing around INR 220-225 per kilo. Management is confident in achieving FY25 volume targets.
    *   **Industrial Salt:** The company continues to be a dominant player in the Asian market, with efforts to improve volumes and cost efficiencies. FY24 volumes crossed 4 million tons, and sustained growth is expected in FY25.
    *   **Sulphate of Potash (SOP):** Encouraging results from pilot plant trials are reported, indicating progress towards commercialization in H2 FY25. A second-grade SOP is being developed for the domestic market, with some sales expected in FY25.
*   **Downstream Integration & Diversification:**
    *   **Bromine Derivatives Facility (Jhagadia):** Phase 1 has been commissioned, with initial sales of Clear Brine Fluids commenced through its subsidiary Acume Chemicals Private Limited. Further product portfolio expansion within Phase 1 is underway, with testing and approval from over 60 clients and approval from 10 clients. Phase 1 is expected to consume 10,000-15,000 tons of bromine.
    *   **Oren Hydrocarbon Acquisition:** NCLT approval is pending. Management is optimistic about synergies with the bromine derivatives business, particularly in the oil and gas sector. A capex of INR 20-30 crores is anticipated for refurbishment. Revenue potential of INR 100-250 crores is projected in the first year post-acquisition.
    *   **New Growth Platforms (Sicsem Private Limited):** A new subsidiary has been incorporated for operations in the Power Electronics segment; further details will be provided as development progresses.
    *   **Flame Retardant (FR) Products (TBBA):** Management has taken a pause on committing further capex for Phase 2 (FR related projects) to re-evaluate market timing and pricing. They will continue to monitor the TBBA market and re-evaluate capex deployment. Integrated manufacturing provides a competitive advantage for FR products.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Cost-saving initiatives are ongoing.
