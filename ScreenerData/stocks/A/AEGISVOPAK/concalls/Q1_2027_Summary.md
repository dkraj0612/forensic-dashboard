## Executive Overview

Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited (AVTL) has reported strong full-year FY26 results, its first since listing, demonstrating robust growth in revenue and profitability. The company reaffirmed its strategic vision through "Project GATI" (Gateway Access to India), which focuses on scaling infrastructure ahead of demand and diversifying its portfolio. Since the joint venture's formation in November 2021, AVTL has significantly expanded its liquid storage capacity by 3.75x and LPG static capacity by 4.5x.

Key operational advancements include the successful acquisition of a 75% stake in Hindustan Aegis LPG Limited (HALPG), solidifying its East Coast presence. The major expansion at JNPT is progressing, with the first phase of new liquid capacity expected to be operational in Q1 FY27. AVTL is making strong strides in new product development, notably with India's first independent ammonia terminal at Pipavav nearing commissioning (expected July 2026), supported by a long-term take-or-pay agreement. The company continues to invest heavily in its network, with a CAPEX target of USD 1.2 billion by FY27 and a pipeline of USD 5 billion by 2030, funded by a disciplined approach aiming for a 0.6x gearing ratio.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

AVTL's "Project GATI" remains central to its strategy, focusing on infrastructure scaling, portfolio diversification, and disciplined financial management.

*   **Network Expansion:**
    *   **Haldia:** Completed acquisition of 75% stake in HALPG (25,000 MT LPG), secured long-term (until 2038) exclusive terminaling agreement with HPCL. Acquired 3 acres for future liquid expansion.
    *   **JNPT:** Major expansion (318,100 CBM liquid, 77,236 MT LPG, LPG bottling plant) with total capex ~INR 1,675 crores. First phase of new liquid capacity expected operational in Q1 FY27. Evaluating further cryogenic gas tank (36,000-50,000 MT).
    *   **Kochi:** Plans to add 60,000 CBM liquid capacity on newly allotted land.
    *   **Kandla:** Received VLGC in December (VLGC-compliant terminal). Jamnagar-Loni LPG pipeline complete. Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG pipeline connection expected H1 FY27. CRL4 liquid terminal (94,148 CBM) on track for commissioning in FY27. Non-binding MoU with L&T for potential ammonia terminals.
    *   **Pipavav:** Commissioned cryogenic LPG terminal (48,000 MT) in June 2025. New VLGC-compliant jetty expected within calendar year 2026. Secured 15-year take-or-pay agreement for petroleum product handling (0.5 million MT/year), operations to commence by year-end 2026. Strong progress on India’s first independent ammonia terminal (36,000 MT), backed by 15-year take-or-pay with Hindustan Zinc, expected commissioning July 2026. ITOCHU Corporation acquired initial 10% stake in ammonia subsidiary.
    *   **Mangalore:** Commissioned cryogenic LPG terminal (82,000 MT) in June 2025. LPG rail gantry under active construction. Added 75,000 CBM liquid capacity, fully operational. Secured additional land for 60,000 CBM liquid capacity.
    *   **Vadhavan Port:** Non-binding MoU signed for potential development of liquid and gas handling facilities (~INR 20,000 crores outlay).
    *   Intends to expand from 7 ports to 12 ports by 2030.
*   **New Product Development:** Strong focus on ammonia (first independent terminal at Pipavav, potential for more, vertical integration in sourcing/storing/distributing), exploring Ethane, Propylene, and natural gas infrastructure.
*   **Infrastructure Enhancement:** JLPL complete; KGPL expected H1 FY27. VLGC berthing at Kandla commenced; Pipavav VLGC-compliant jetty expected mid-FY27. LPG rail gantry at Mangalore and liquid rail gantry at Pipavav under construction. Mangalore-Hassan-Cherlapalli pipeline expected operative in FY28.
*   **Key Drivers:** India's growing energy consumption and industrial activity, AVTL's strong position as India's largest independent third-party storage provider, synergies from Aegis Logistics and Royal Vopak, strategic port locations, multi-modal connectivity, focus on energy transition and new energy products. In-house construction capabilities and Vopak's global operational standards are key differentiators.
