## Executive Overview

Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited (AVTL) reported a strong Q3 FY26, with revenue from operations growing 22.3% year-on-year to INR 197.5 crores. This performance was bolstered by a significant 37% year-on-year increase in liquid terminalling revenue to INR 116.5 crores, driven by capacity additions and an improved product mix. Gas terminaling revenue saw a more modest 6% increase to INR 81 crores, with gas throughput at 0.67 million metric tons. Profit After Tax (PAT) showed substantial growth, surging 62.7% year-on-year to INR 61.5 crores. For the nine-month period of FY26, revenue from operations increased by 18.3% to INR 549.1 crores, with PAT up an impressive 90% to INR 163.2 crores. The company has successfully completed the acquisition of a 75% stake in Hindustan Aegis LPG Limited (HALPG), adding 25,000 MT of LPG capacity and marking entry into the East Coast market at Haldia. Significant progress was noted across multiple ports, including the ongoing expansion at JNPT for liquid and LPG storage, the commencement of VLGC berthing at Kandla, and new liquid capacity development at Kochi and Mangalore. AVTL remains on track with its ambitious CAPEX plans, targeting INR 10,000 crores (USD 1.2 billion) by FY27. The company also continues to progress on India's first independent ammonia terminal at Pipavav, expected before Q1 FY27.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

AVTL's strategic roadmap, "Project GATI" (Gateway Access to India), continues to drive expansion, efficiency, and diversification.

*   **Network Expansion & Consolidation:**
    *   Pipavav and Mangalore LPG terminals are operational and contributing to revenue.
    *   Acquisition of HALPG (75% stake) completed, adding 25,000 MT LPG capacity and establishing an East Coast presence in Haldia.
    *   Significant expansion underway at JNPT, adding 318,100 cbm liquid capacity and 77,236 MT LPG capacity, with the first phase of liquid capacity expected in Q1 FY27.
    *   Development of new liquid terminals at Kandla (94,148 cbm) is progressing for commissioning next year.
    *   Expansion underway at Kochi and Mangalore, adding 60,000 cbm liquid capacity each.
    *   Acquired additional land at Haldia for future expansion.
    *   Non-binding MoU signed for investment in Vadhavan Port.
*   **New Product Development:**
    *   Construction of India's first independent Ammonia Terminal (36,000 MT) at Pipavav progressing, expected before Q1 FY27.
    *   Non-binding MoU with Larsen & Toubro for Ammonia Terminals at Kandla to support green ammonia facilities.
    *   Exploring presence in other gases like LNG.
*   **Infrastructure Enhancement:**
    *   Jamnagar Loni LPG Pipeline (JLPL) expected operational by February end 2026.
    *   Kandla Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline (KGPL) scheduled for connection by June 2026.
    *   VLGC berthing commenced at Kandla in Q3 FY26.
    *   Rail gantry at Mangalore expected to be operational by September 2026.
*   **Inorganic Growth:**
    *   HALPG acquisition completed.
    *   Continuous evaluation of acquisition opportunities.
*   **Financial Strengthening:**
    *   Commitment to disciplined capital deployment and prudent leverage.
    *   Future CAPEX funded by equity, internal accruals, and debt.

**Key Drivers:**

*   Robust demand growth for LPG, liquids, and ammonia in India.
*   India's increasing reliance on imports for key energy products.
*   AVTL's position as India's largest independent third-party storage terminal operator.
*   Synergies from Aegis Logistics and Vopak.
*   Strategic port locations with multimodal connectivity.
*   Focus on the energy transition and new energy products.
