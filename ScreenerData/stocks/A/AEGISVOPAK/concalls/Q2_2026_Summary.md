## Executive Overview

Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited (AVTL) demonstrated strong growth in Q2 FY26, with revenue from operations increasing by 26.2% year-on-year to INR 187.6 crores. This performance was driven by increased volumes from recently commissioned LPG terminals at Pipavav and Mangalore, which are now revenue accretive. Management announced the proposed acquisition of a 75% stake in Hindustan Aegis LPG Ltd (HALPG), which will consolidate an additional 25,000 MT of LPG capacity and mark AVTL's entry into the East Coast market at Haldia. Significant progress was highlighted across multiple ports, including expansions at JNPA for liquid and LPG storage, enhanced connectivity and VLGC berthing at Kandla, and new liquid capacity development at Kochi and Mangalore. AVTL remains on track with its ambitious CAPEX plans, targeting USD 1.2 billion by FY27 and USD 5 billion by 2030, while maintaining prudent leverage. The company is also progressing on India's first independent ammonia terminal at Pipavav, expected before Q1 FY27.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

AVTL's strategic roadmap, "Project GATI" (Gateway Access to India), continues to focus on expanding storage footprint, enhancing throughput efficiency, and diversifying product and geographical presence.

*   **Network Expansion & Consolidation:**
    *   Pipavav and Mangalore LPG terminals are operational and contributing to revenue.
    *   Proposed acquisition of HALPG to add 25,000 MT LPG capacity and establish an East Coast presence in Haldia.
    *   Significant expansion underway at JNPA including liquid capacity (318,100 cbm), LPG storage (77,286 MT), and an LPG bottling plant.
    *   Development of new liquid terminals at Kandla (94,148 cbm) and expansion at Kochi and Mangalore (60,000 cbm each).
    *   Secured additional land at Haldia for future expansion.
    *   Non-binding MoU signed for investment in Vadhavan Port.
*   **New Product Development:**
    *   Construction of India's first independent Ammonia Terminal (36,000 MT) at Pipavav, expected before Q1 FY27.
    *   Non-binding MoU with Larsen & Toubro for Ammonia Terminals at Kandla to support green ammonia facilities.
    *   Exploring presence in other gases like natural gas and ethane.
*   **Infrastructure Enhancement:**
    *   JLPL pipeline connectivity expected operational by Q3 FY26, and KGPL by Q4 FY26 to boost Kandla volumes.
    *   VLGC berthing commencement at Kandla in Q3 FY26.
    *   Development of rail gantries for liquids at Pipavav.
    *   LPG Rail Gantry and Bottling Plant under construction at Mangalore.
*   **Inorganic Growth:**
    *   HALPG acquisition subject to shareholder approval.
    *   Continuous evaluation of acquisition opportunities.
*   **Financial Strengthening:**
    *   Commitment to disciplined capital deployment and prudent leverage.
    *   Future CAPEX to be funded by equity, internal accruals, and debt.

**Key Drivers:**

*   Robust demand growth for LPG, liquids, and ammonia in India.
*   India's increasing reliance on imports for key energy products.
*   AVTL's position as India's largest third-party storage terminal operator.
*   Synergies and global expertise from Aegis Logistics and Royal Vopak.
*   Strategic port-based locations with multimodal connectivity.
*   Focus on the energy transition and new energy products like ammonia.
