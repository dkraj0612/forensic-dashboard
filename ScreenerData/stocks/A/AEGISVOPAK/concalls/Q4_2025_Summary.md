## Business Strategy & Drivers

AVTL's core strategy revolves around expanding its terminal network, both organically and inorganically. Key strategic pillars include:

*   **Strategic Expansion of Network:** Increasing capacity at existing locations to grow market share and leverage economies of scale.
*   **Entry into New Locations:** Evaluating opportunities in emerging ports to manage flows of liquids, gases, and future energy transition products like ammonia and hydrogen.
*   **Establishment of Industrial Terminals:** Building storage infrastructure integrated with manufacturing clusters, leveraging Vopak's global expertise.
*   **Investment in Alternative Energies:** Developing capacities for feedstock and ammonia terminals, repurposing existing infrastructure, and building new facilities for cleaner fuels.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Evaluating acquisition opportunities to expand at existing locations and enter new markets.

**Key Drivers:**

*   **India's growing demand for LPG:** Driven by government initiatives like PMUY and increasing industrial consumption.
*   **Demand for chemicals and petroleum products:** Supported by initiatives like 'Make in India' and PCPIRs.
*   **Import dependency for LPG and chemicals:** Creating a sustained need for storage and handling infrastructure.
*   **Strategic JV with Royal Vopak:** Providing global technical expertise, best practices, and access to new product storage.
*   **Multimodal Evacuation Infrastructure:** Pipelines, rail, and road connectivity enhance logistical efficiency.
