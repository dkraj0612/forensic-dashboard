## Executive Overview

Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited (AVTL) continues its aggressive expansion and consolidation strategy, as evidenced by the Q1 FY26 transcript. The company has successfully commissioned its new LPG terminals at Pipavav (48,000 MT) and Mangalore (82,000 MT), making them revenue accretive. A significant strategic move is the approved acquisition of a 75% stake in Hindustan Aegis LPG Ltd (HALPG), adding 25,000 MT of LPG capacity and marking an entry into the East Coast market via Haldia. Furthermore, substantial CAPEX is underway at JNPA, including new liquid and LPG terminals and a bottling plant. AVTL is also progressing on India's first independent ammonia terminal at Pipavav, with completion expected before Q1 FY27. Management reiterated ambitious CAPEX plans, targeting $1.2 billion by FY27 and $5 billion by 2030, while maintaining prudent leverage. The company highlighted its strong financial performance, driven by increasing LPG and liquid demand, and its strategic advantages stemming from its joint venture structure with Aegis Logistics and Royal Vopak.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

AVTL's strategy remains focused on aggressive expansion, diversification, and leveraging promoter strengths:

*   **Network Expansion & Consolidation:**
    *   Commissioned LPG terminals at Pipavav and Mangalore are now revenue accretive.
    *   Acquisition of HALPG adds East Coast presence at Haldia and consolidates Mumbai-based LPG capacity.
    *   Major expansion underway at JNPA for liquid, LPG, and bottling facilities.
    *   Additional liquid capacity planned for Kochi and Mangalore.
    *   Secured land at Haldia for scalable infrastructure.
*   **New Product Development:**
    *   India's first independent ammonia terminal (36,000 MT) under construction at Pipavav, with completion expected before Q1 FY27.
    *   Continued focus on new energy products.
*   **Infrastructure Enhancement:**
    *   Pipavav and Kandla terminals to benefit from enhanced pipeline connectivity and VLGC berthing capabilities by Q3 FY26.
    *   Development of multimodal evacuation infrastructure (pipelines, rail gantries).
*   **Inorganic Growth:**
    *   Proactive evaluation of acquisition opportunities for expansion and entry into new locations.
*   **Financial Strengthening:**
    *   Commitment to maintaining prudent leverage ratios.
    *   Future CAPEX to be funded by a mix of internal accruals and debt.

**Key Drivers:**

*   Strong demand growth for LPG and liquid products in India, supported by government initiatives.
*   India's increasing reliance on imports for LPG and ammonia to meet domestic demand.
*   AVTL's unique positioning as India's largest third-party liquid and gas storage terminal operator.
*   Synergies and global expertise derived from its joint venture with Aegis Logistics and Royal Vopak.
*   Strategic advantage of port-based terminals with multimodal evacuation capabilities.
*   Focus on energy transition products like ammonia.
