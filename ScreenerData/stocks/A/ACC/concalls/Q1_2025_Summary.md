## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cement, along with its listed subsidiary ACC and Sanghi, demonstrated a resilient performance in Q1 FY25 amidst subdued industry conditions. The Adani Group's cement business reported consolidated revenue of INR 8,311 crores, driven by micro-market management and an increased share of blended cement (86%) and premium products (24% of trade volume). EBITDA stood at INR 1,280 crores, with a margin of 15.4%, and EBITDA per ton at INR 807. The company continues to strengthen its market share, targeting 20% by FY28, with current capacity at 89 million tons (including Penna acquisition). Significant progress is being made on capacity expansion, with 140 million tons targeted by FY28, supported by ongoing organic and inorganic growth initiatives. Cost optimization remains a key focus, with notable reductions in energy and transportation costs, driven by initiatives like increased AFR and Waste Heat Recovery System (WHRS) utilization. The company maintains a debt-free status with INR 18,299 crores in cash and cash equivalents as of June 30, 2025. Strategic focus includes integrating acquisitions, expanding capacity, and further driving cost leadership through digitization and ESG initiatives.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Current Capacity: 89 MnTPA (including Penna acquisition).
    *   Target: 140 MnTPA by FY'28.
    *   Key commissioning milestones: 4 MnT clinker unit at Bhatapara and associated grinding units at Sankrail, Farakka, and Sindri by end of FY25. Grinding unit at Salai Banwa (UP) in Q1 FY26. Brownfield expansions at Bathinda (Punjab) and Marwar Mundwa (Rajasthan) in Q2 FY26. Target of 100 MnTPA by Q2 FY26, and 112 MnTPA by FY26 with further additions.
    *   14 additional grinding unit projects identified for future expansion.
    *   Secured 275 million tons of new limestone reserves in Q1 FY25.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Focus on achieving cost of production below INR 3,650 by FY28.
    *   **Energy Costs:** Increased AFR share to 9.3% and WHRS share to 15.1%. Targeting 186 MW of WHRS capacity by March 2025 (currently 165 MW), aiming for ~300 MW by FY28. 1,000 MW of RE capacity (wind and solar) expected by Q1 FY26, targeting 60% green power to reduce cost by INR 90/ton by FY28.
    *   **Freight Costs:** Reduced lead distance and increased direct dispatches (55% of sales). Ordered 11 GPWIS rakes and 26 BCFC rakes for efficient clinker and fly ash transport. Introduction of EV trucks planned from Q2 FY25.
    *   **Raw Material Costs:** Bidding for captive coal mines and leveraging imported pet coke.
*   **Market Leadership & Solutions Provider:**
    *   Internal target of 20% market share by FY28.
    *   Increased premium products share to 24% of trade volume.
    *   Utilizing Ambuja, ACC, and Penna brands in the South India market post-integration.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Acquisition of Penna Cement nearing completion (expected full closing in a fortnight).
    *   Synergies from Adani Group's diverse businesses for raw material procurement, power, and logistics.
    *   Approved amalgamation of Adani Cementation Limited with Ambuja Cements Limited (NCLT approval on July 18, 2025).
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Committed to Net Zero by 2050 with SBTi-validated near-term targets.
    *   Targeting 60% green power by FY28.
    *   Ambuja is 11x water positive, 8x plastic negative.
    *   Use of over 5.7 million tons of waste-derived resources in Q1 FY25.
    *   Pledged to plant 8.3 million trees by 2030.
    *   Created societal value for over 4.6 million people.
    *   Optimizing Sanghi's infrastructure for efficient marine transport.
