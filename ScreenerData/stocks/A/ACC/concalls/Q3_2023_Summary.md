## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cements (consolidated with ACC), operating under the Adani Group, delivered a robust Q3 FY2024 performance, marked by significant profitability improvement and aggressive progress on strategic initiatives. The company reported a 3% YoY sales volume growth, driven by enhanced ground network and micro-market focus. Consolidated EBITDA (excluding other income) surged by 69% YoY, with EBITDA per ton jumping 64% to INR 1,204, and EBITDA margins expanding by 8.4 percentage points to 21.7%. This exceptional growth was primarily attributed to sustained cost reduction in power & fuel (down INR 349/ton) and efficiency improvements, leveraging Adani Group synergies.

Key strategic milestones achieved include the successful acquisition of Sanghi Industries in Q3 FY2024, contributing to a total of 9.9 MTPA capacity added in the last 12 months. The company is actively implementing 32 MTPA of additional capacity, taking its total to 110 MTPA, representing 80% of its ambitious 140 MTPA target by FY2028. Significant investments in renewable energy (RE) and waste heat recovery systems (WHRS) are underway, with INR 10,000 crores earmarked for green power initiatives, targeting a 60% green power share by FY2028 and projected annual savings of INR 2,000 crores. Ambuja Cements remains debt-free, with its net worth at an all-time high of INR 42,824 crores and robust cash reserves.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Operational Efficiency & Cost Leadership:**
    *   Continued focus on leveraging Adani Group synergies for cost leadership, leading to significant reductions in power & fuel costs.
    *   Optimization of fuel mix, increased captive fuel and alternative fuel consumption.
    *   Expansion of Waste Heat Recovery System (WHRS) capacity, with mix increasing to 12.7%.
    *   Logistics cost optimization initiatives, including reverse bidding, transporter renegotiation, and digitization for tracking.
    *   Long-term tie-ups for key raw materials secured for future cost reductions.
*   **Capacity Expansion & Growth:**
    *   Successful acquisition of Sanghi Industries (54.5% stake) completed in Q3 FY2024.
    *   9.9 MTPA (15%) capacity added in the last 12 months.
    *   32 MTPA additional cement capacity currently under implementation, contributing to a total of 110 MTPA (80% of the 140 MTPA target by FY2028).
    *   Secured 600 Mn MT of new limestone reserves, supplementing the 1 Bn MT from Sanghi acquisition.
    *   Board approval for 2.25 MTPA clinker unit in Mundra and 12 MTPA cement grinding units across five new locations.
*   **Product Portfolio & Market Focus:**
    *   Premium products now constitute 22% of trade sales volume, showing a rising trend.
    *   Trade sales, catering to the profitable Individual House Builder (IHB) segment, represent ~75% of total sales, noted as the highest amongst peers.
    *   Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) identified as a new growth engine.
    *   Emphasis on digitization across sales and marketing.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Strong commitment to Net Zero by 2050, aligned with SBTi and GCCA.
    *   Major investments in Renewable Energy (RE) and WHRS, with INR 10,000 crores earmarked for green power projects.
    *   Focus on circular economy initiatives (Geoclean, EcomaxX, AFR, alternate raw materials) and water positivity.
    *   Management expresses confidence in achieving ESG targets ahead of schedule.
