## Executive Overview

ACC Limited (ACC) and Ambuja Cements, operating under the Adani Group umbrella, reported a strong Q4 2022 (December Quarter) performance characterized by significant operational efficiencies and a substantial jump in financial performance quarter-on-quarter. This marks the first full quarter under new management, showcasing a transformational journey. The company experienced a healthy 11% increase in net revenue and a notable 7% growth in cement volumes, driven by increased CAPEX in housing and infrastructure, supported by a positive government budget. Crucially, cost optimization efforts, particularly in fuel and logistics, led to an impressive expansion of EBITDA margins, which rose from 6.1% to 14.4% on a consolidated basis. The integration and synergy realization between ACC, Ambuja, and the broader Adani Group are key drivers of this improvement. Looking ahead, the company is focused on doubling its capacity to 140 million tons within five years, enhancing operational performance, and leveraging group adjacencies for cost leadership.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q4 2022 Performance (Consolidated):**
    *   **Net Revenue:** Rs. 8,036 Crores (Sequential up 11%, YoY up 4%)
    *   **Cement Volume:** 7% growth (YoY)
    *   **EBITDA:** Rs. 1,138 Crores (Sequential up 161%)
    *   **EBITDA Margin:** 14.4% (Expanded by 8% points sequentially from 6.1%)
    *   **Ambuja Cements Standalone:**
        *   **EBITDA:** Rs. 715 Crores (Sequential up 103%)
        *   **Net Revenue:** Rs. 4,218 Crores (Sequential up 13%, YoY up 11%)
        *   **Volume:** Up 11% (Sequential)
        *   **EBITDA Margin:** 17.5%
    *   **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs. 9,454 Crores
    *   **Cost Per Ton (Metric Ton):** Overall cost reduced by Rs. 283 per ton.
        *   **Kiln Fuel Cost:** Reduced by 14% (from Rs. 2.84 to Rs. 2.45 per 1,000 kilocalories).
        *   **Logistics Costs:** Reduced by 6% to Rs. 1,339 per ton.
        *   **Raw Material Cost:** Reduced by 5% (from Rs. 703 to Rs. 667 per ton).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Operational Efficiency & Synergies:**
    *   First full quarter under Adani Group, focusing on operational efficiencies and synergies between ACC, Ambuja, and other group businesses.
    *   Emphasis on cost optimization driven by fuel cost reduction and other operational efficiencies.
    *   Leveraging group adjacencies for cost leadership, including potential for own wagons, terminals, and grinding stations.
    *   Implementing a unified management team to run the entire cement business end-to-end.
    *   Strengthening logistics through a hub-and-spoke model and exploring own wagons and rail schemes.
    *   Focus on improving clinker factor and asset sweating.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Target to double capacity from 67.5 million tons to 140 million tons in five years.
    *   Ametha new capacity (3.3 MTPA clinker, 1 MTPA grinding) targeting commissioning by March 2023, commercial production in Q2 FY23.
    *   Ongoing focus on releasing clinker from debottlenecking existing kilns.
    *   Commitment to increase Waste Heat Recovery System (WHRS) capacity to 190 Megawatts by March 2025 (from ~65 MW in Dec 2022).
*   **Product Portfolio & Market Focus:**
    *   Strong brand legacy of ACC and Ambuja, with a focus on premium products.
    *   Expansion of Ready Mix Concrete (RMC) business, with plans to grow from 78+ plants to over 250 in the mid-term, targeting the large buyer and infrastructure market.
    *   Launching ECOMaxX Concrete for green solutions in RMC.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Rebranded "Geocycle" to "Geoclean" to accelerate alternate fuel usage.
    *   Targeting 30% thermal substitution rate for alternate fuels by 2027.
    *   WHRS projects contributing to 40% of power being green power.
    *   Ambuja's water positivity at 8 times.
    *   Focus on circular economy and strengthening CSR initiatives.
    *   Relaunching green initiatives under Geoclean, aiming for higher consumption of waste material.
*   **Financial Prudence:**
    *   Ambuja Cements remains debt-free with significant cash reserves.
    *   Focus on working capital improvement, with DSO reduced from 10 to 8 days.
