## Executive Overview

ACC Limited (ACC) and Ambuja Cements, operating under the Adani Group, delivered a strong Q2 FY2024 performance characterized by robust volume growth, significant cost optimization, and sustained EBITDA expansion. The consolidated entity reported a 4.1% revenue increase to INR 7,424 crores, driven by improved realization and micro-market strategies. Operational costs per ton saw a substantial decline of 11.8% year-on-year, primarily due to aggressive cost reduction in energy (down 33%) and logistics. This translated to a remarkable 298% YoY jump in consolidated EBITDA to INR 1,302 crores, with EBITDA per ton soaring by 291% to INR 995, and EBITDA margins expanding by 12.9% to 17.5%. Ambuja Cements standalone also reported significant growth, with revenue up 8% and EBITDA up 147% YoY. The company continues to prioritize capacity expansion, with the Ametha integrated unit commissioned and significant progress on new clinker and grinding facilities. The acquisition of Sanghi Industries is on track for closure in Q3 FY2024, expected to accelerate growth targets. ACC remains debt-free with substantial cash reserves of INR 11,721 crores as of September 30, 2023.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Operational Efficiency & Cost Leadership:** Continued focus on leveraging Adani Group's synergies to become the lowest cost producer. Significant reductions achieved in energy, transportation, and other expenses through initiatives like:
    *   **Energy Cost:** Improved fuel management (kiln fuel cost down 38%), increased waste heat recovery (WHRS) capacity to 90 MW.
    *   **Logistics:** Footprint optimization, increased direct dispatches to 49%, improved rail coefficient to 28%, and introduction of 25 bulk rakes for fly ash transport.
    *   **Other Expenses:** Streamlined operations as a single entity with a unified executive team and reduced redundancies.
    *   **Raw Material Security:** Secured bids for 10 new limestone mines (584 million tons) and two coal mines (Gare Pelma and Dahegaon Gowari), aiming for 40-50% coal self-sufficiency.

*   **Capacity Expansion & Growth:**
    *   **New Capacities:** Ametha integrated unit (3.3 MTPA clinker) commissioned. New clinker capacity of 4 MTPA at Bhatapara (completion Q2 FY26) and 4 MTPA at Maratha (completion Q4 FY26). New grinding units commissioned at Tikaria (1.5 MTPA), with others at Sankaren, Farakka, Jalgaon, Amravati, and Salai Banwa (expected Q3 FY25 to Q1 FY26).
    *   **Sanghi Industries Acquisition:** Expected to close in Q3 FY24, valued at INR 5,000 crores (fully funded by internal accruals). Expected to accelerate the 140 MTPA target and reinforce market leadership.

*   **Product Portfolio & Market Focus:**
    *   **Brands:** Ambuja and ACC remain the primary brands post-acquisition.
    *   **Premium Products:** Share of premium products in trade sales increased to 23.4%.
    *   **RMC:** Identified as a growth engine, with plans to leverage ECOMaxX green concrete.

*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Continued focus on increasing Alternate Fuels and Raw Materials (AFR) usage, renewable energy (200 MW captive solar under construction), and WHRS capacity.
    *   Received multiple awards for energy management and safety.
