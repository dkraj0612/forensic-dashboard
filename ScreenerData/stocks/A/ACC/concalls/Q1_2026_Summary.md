## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cements delivered a strong Q1 FY'26, marking its highest-ever revenue and EBITDA for a quarter. The company achieved its highest sales volume at 18.4 million tonnes, a 20% YoY increase, boosting market share to 15.5%. Revenue crossed INR 10,000 crores, reaching INR 10,289 crores, up 23% YoY, driven by a 4% price gain and a 43% YoY increase in premium product share to 33%. Costs improved by INR 119 per metric tonne YoY, contributing to a record quarterly EBITDA of INR 1,961 crores, with an EBITDA per metric tonne of INR 1,069 and a margin of 19.1%. The company remains debt-free with a net worth of INR 66,436 crores. Management has increased its demand estimates for the fiscal year to 7-8% and is actively integrating acquired entities, particularly Orient Cement, which was consolidated from April 2025.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Current Cement Capacity: 104.5 MTPA.
    *   Target: 118 MTPA by FY'26 exit, 140 MTPA by FY'28.
    *   Commissioning progress: 5 MTPA grinding capacity commissioned in the last 3 months; target of 13 MTPA additional capacity this fiscal year.
    *   Key projects in advanced stages: Bhattapada, Salai Banwa, Dahej, Marwad, Kalamboli, Krishnapatnam, Bhatinda, Jodhpur, and Warishali Ganj.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Target EBITDA: INR 1,500 PMT by 2028.
    *   Cost Reduction: Achieved INR 119 per MT YoY improvement. Previously targeted INR 530 per ton reduction over the journey, approximately 35-40% (INR 175-200 per ton) achieved.
    *   Fuel Cost: Reduced from INR 1.73 to INR 1.59 per 1000 kCal. Focus on improving heat consumption by 35-40 kCal/kg.
    *   Green Power: Share increased to 28.1% (YoY +9.7%), targeting 60% by FY'28. Renewable power commissioned at 473 MW (out of 1000 MW target). WHRS capacity at 228 MW.
    *   Logistics: Primary lead distance reduced by 8 km to 269 km, targeting further reduction by 50 km by FY'28. Focus on rail and sea logistics.
*   **Market Leadership & Solutions Provider:**
    *   Premium Products: Targeting 35% share in trade sales for FY'26 (currently 33%).
    *   Brand Equity: Recognized as most trusted cement brand for the fourth consecutive year. Launched "Nirmaan Anotsav" with CREDAI.
    *   Digitalization: First in industry to adopt Digipin. Cements Network Operating Centre (CiNOC) enhances predictive analysis and decision-making.
    *   Customer Engagement: Implemented Adani's certified technology ACP at over 21,000 customer sites. Conducted 325+ building workshops empowering 9,000+ contractors. CEO Club and Dhan Varsha Grah Lakshmi Saubhagya Awards for channel partners.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Acquisitions: Seamless integration of Sanghi, Asian, Tuticorin, Penna, and Orient Cement, with synergies realized ahead of schedule. Orient Cement consolidated from April 22, 2025.
    *   Adani Ecosystem: Leveraging group synergies for procurement, power, and logistics.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Net Zero: First in India with SBTi-validated net-zero and near-term targets.
    *   Renewable Energy: 473 MW of renewable energy commissioned out of 1000 MW target.
    *   Water & Plastic Positivity: 12x water positive, 11x plastic negative.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Management is on track with capacity expansion, having commissioned 5 MTPA recently and aiming for 13 MTPA more in FY26. The overall target of 118 MTPA by FY'26 and 140 MTPA by FY'28 remains the focus.
    *   **Cost Reduction & Efficiency:** The company achieved a significant YoY cost reduction of INR 119 per MT. While sequential costs showed an increase, management attributed this to the integration of Orient Cement and clarified that fuel costs have reduced YoY. The targeted INR 530 per ton cost saving journey is progressing, with approximately 35-40% achieved.
    *   **Financial Strength:** The debt-free status is maintained with a strong net worth and solid cash flow generation supporting capex.
    *   **Merger & Integration:** Integration of acquired entities, particularly Orient Cement, is progressing well and ahead of schedule, with brand migration to Ambuja and ACC being successful.
    *   **ESG Commitment:** Continued emphasis on ESG, with progress on renewable energy targets and SBTi validation.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift:** The management's tone remains confident and upbeat, highlighting record financial performance and successful integration of acquisitions. The narrative emphasizes value creation through a combination of volume growth, price improvements, cost efficiencies, and enhanced channel engagement, amplified by the Adani portfolio synergies. There is a clear focus on execution and delivering on stated targets.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter:**
    *   **Demand Outlook:** Increased FY'26 demand estimate to 7-8% (from 6-7%).
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Target to commission an additional 13 MTPA this fiscal year. Expectation of seeing "some of these capacities" commissioned this quarter and "most capacities" by December, with full 118 MTPA target by FY'26 end.
    *   **Orient Cement Integration:** Effective date for consolidation was April 22, 2025. Brand penetration and migration to Ambuja and ACC are progressing very well.
    *   **Penna Capacities:** Clinker capacity expected by end of September (Q2 FY'26). Balance payments for Penna are included within the overall CAPEX program.

    *   **Cost Savings:** The INR 530 per ton cost saving target remains and continues to be a focus for both existing and acquired assets.

    *   **Other Expenses:** Improvements expected in "other expenses" due to non-recurring nature of new asset startup costs and one-time gains from the previous year.
    *   **ACC Efficiency:** Primary efforts focused on investments and efficiency gains for ACC to bridge the EBITDA gap with peers.
    *   **ACC Brand:** ACC Gold is highlighted as a "blockbuster product" in the premium segment.
    *   **Next Phase Expansion (21 MTPA):** Groundwork for FY'27-28 expansion is largely done, including land, approvals, and vendor negotiations. Preparatory civil work has commenced at some sites.
    *   **Geographical Focus for Expansion:** Balance is sought across all five regions (North, West, Central, East, South), with a particular focus on increasing capacity in the Central region (currently 8% of cement capacity).
