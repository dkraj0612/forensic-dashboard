## Executive Overview

ACC Limited's Q2 2021 investor presentation highlights a strong rebound in sales volumes and financial performance, with notable year-over-year growth in Net Sales, EBITDA, and Profit After Tax. The company is demonstrating resilience despite the challenges posed by the second wave of COVID-19, driven by cost efficiencies under its 'Parvat' project and improved realisations from premium product positioning. Strategic priorities include capacity expansion, cost optimization through various efficiency projects, and the continued rollout of environment-friendly products like ECOPact. Digitization efforts are also a key focus for bringing efficiencies across operations. ACC continues to embed its Sustainable Development (SD) 2030 Plan, with progress noted in waste-derived resource utilization and CO2 emissions reduction, though these metrics are still below their 2030 targets. Community engagement and health infrastructure support remain a high priority, as evidenced by extensive COVID-19 relief efforts.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Capacity expansion project at Ametha and associated grinding units have commenced. Kymore (14MW) project on track for Q2 2022 commissioning.
*   **Cost Efficiency & Optimization:** 'Parvat' project driving margin expansion. Focus on clinker factor optimization, reduced energy consumption, lead distance reduction, and leveraging Master Supply Agreement. Projects like Geo Mix and network optimization are underway to mitigate fuel cost headwinds.
*   **Premiumization & Product Innovation:** Strengthening brand positioning through enhanced consumer connect and premium products (e.g., Gold Water Shield, F2R, Superfast).
    *   **ECOPact:** Rolled out Pan India, showing excellent volume ramp-up and appreciation from green-conscious customers.
    *   **Admixture & Special Products:** Development of cost-effective concrete admixtures and special additives (Cool walls).
    *   **Environment-Friendly Products:** Under development, targeting >50% water saving, >80% CO2 footprint reduction, and Zero Clinker Binder.
*   **Digitization:** Implementation of PACT (Performance & Collaboration Tool) across plants, pilot projects for drones, cement fineness prediction, and VRM failure prediction. TAC (Transport Analytic Center) for secondary lead analysis and direct dispatch. Digitizing S&OP from EPOD to transport invoicing. Digital assets like Dealer Connect and Contractor Loyalty are being leveraged.
*   **Circular Economy & Sustainability (SD 2030 Goals):**
    *   **Waste-Derived Resources:** Consumed 6 million tonnes in H1'21 (Target 30 million tonnes/year).
    *   **CO2 Emissions:** Net specific CO2 emissions at 496 kg/t cementitious materials in H1'21 (Target 400 kg/t).
    *   **Water Positive:** Index <1 in H1'21 (Target Water Positive Index – 5).
    *   **Geocycle Projects:** Chanda commissioned, Jamul & Kymore feasibility in progress.
    *   **Thermal Substitution Rate (TSR):** Continuous focus on Municipal Solid Waste and Refuse Derived fuel.
*   **Community Development:** 0.83 million people touched through community development projects in 2020; on track for H1. Extensive COVID-19 relief efforts including mask production, food distribution, tele-counselling, disinfection drives, and oxygen support.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context:** The previous Q2 2020 analysis noted ACC's rebound and focus on cost management and operational efficiency. The Q2 2021 presentation confirms a continued strong execution on these fronts. The prior historical summary (from June 2021) emphasized sustainability and long-term growth with the "Sustainability Plan 2030" and initiatives like ECOPact.

**Audit:**

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Financial Recovery:** The Q2 2021 results significantly exceed the rebound observed in Q2 2020, demonstrating robust recovery and growth in sales volumes and profitability, largely driven by economic recovery and the company's operational efficiencies.
    *   **Sustainability Targets:** While the company continues to invest and make progress in its SD 2030 goals (e.g., increased waste-derived resource consumption, efforts in CO2 reduction), the reported H1'21 metrics (496 kg/t CO2 emissions, 6 million tonnes waste consumption) are still some distance from the ambitious 2030 targets (400 kg/t CO2, 30 million tonnes waste annually). The Water Positive Index is also below its target. This indicates an ongoing journey rather than achieved targets for the current period.
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Projects like Ametha have commenced, and Kymore is on track, aligning with previously stated expansion plans.
*   **Narrative/Tone Shift:** The narrative has strengthened its focus on delivering results through optimization and premiumization. While sustainability remains a core strategic priority (SD 2030 Goals are core priority), the immediate focus of this presentation is on the financial and operational resilience shown in Q2 2021, highlighting "Strong performance delivery in a challenging quarter." The emphasis on cost efficiencies under 'Parvat' and improved realisations from premium products indicates a clear strategy for margin expansion in the near term.
*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter:**
    *   **Guidance/Outlook:** The company expects continued strong performance driven by economic recovery and its strategic initiatives. Specific volume or revenue guidance is not explicitly provided for the next quarter, but the trend is positive.

While specific margin targets for the next quarter are not given, the performance in Q2 and H1, along with ongoing initiatives, suggests a sustained effort to improve profitability.
