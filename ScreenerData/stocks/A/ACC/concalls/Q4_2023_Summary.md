## Executive Overview

ACC Limited and Ambuja Cements (consolidated) reported Q3 FY24 earnings, demonstrating robust operational and financial performance. Revenue increased by 3% YoY to INR 8,129 crores, driven by micro-market strategies and dealer network expansion. Operational costs per tonne declined by 10% YoY to INR 4,526, primarily due to a 21% drop in energy costs (kiln fuel down 25% to INR 1.84 per 1,000 kcal) and a 1% reduction in transportation costs. Direct dispatches increased to 52%, with a rail coefficient of 26%. These efficiencies led to a significant 70% YoY jump in consolidated EBITDA to INR 1,732 crores, with EBITDA per tonne up 65% to INR 1,225, and EBITDA margins expanding by 8.4 percentage points to 21.3%. The acquisition of Sanghi Industries, completed in the quarter, resulted in a cash outflow of INR 3,801 crores, reducing consolidated cash and cash equivalents to INR 8,591 crores as of December 31, 2023. The company is actively executing its long-term strategy of capacity expansion and cost leadership.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Targeting to double capacity to 140 million tons by FY28.
    *   Ametha integrated unit commissioned (3.3 million tons clinker, 1 million tons cement).
    *   Sanghi acquisition added 6.6 million tons clinker and 6.1 million tons cement capacity.
    *   Total clinker capacity now 51 million tons; grinding capacity at 77.4 million tons.
    *   Plans for 35 new grinding units.
    *   New clinker lines (Bhatapara, Chandrapur) under construction; expected commissioning in FY25-26.
    *   Multiple grinding units planned across states, expected commissioning by FY26-27.
*   **Cost Reduction:**
    *   Aiming for over INR 400 per ton cost reduction.
    *   Waste Heat Recovery (WHR) capacity increased to 119 MW (from 40 MW in Sep 2022), targeting 186 MW by March 2025.
    *   Securing captive coal mines (Gare Palma, Dahegaon-Gowari) to meet 40-50% of coal requirements.
    *   Increased share of Alternative Fuel & Raw Materials (AFR) to 9.1% (from 7.1% at acquisition).
    *   Logistics cost reduction through lead distance optimization (284 km vs 291 km), direct dispatches (52%), and rail/road mix optimization.
    *   Acquired 11 General Purpose Wagon Investment Scheme rakes and 26 BCFC rakes for efficient transportation.
    *   Secured bids for 10 new limestone mines with significant reserves.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Targeting 200 MW Renewable Energy (RE) capacity by FY24 end, aiming for 60% power from green sources by FY26.
    *   Estimated INR 90 per ton power cost reduction on expanded capacity by FY28 from RE.
*   **Synergies & Integration:**
    *   Master Supply Agreements (MSA) between Ambuja, ACC, and Sanghi to optimize operations and leverage group brands.
    *   Sanghi plant to increase utilization from 25-30% to 75-80% via MSA.
    *   Arrangement with Adani Cement's Dahej plant for toll manufacturing.
