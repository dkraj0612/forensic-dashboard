## Executive Overview

ACC Limited, in its Q3 FY'26 investor presentation, highlighted a robust performance driven by strategic consolidations and operational efficiencies. The company reported normalized consolidated revenue growth of 20% and EBITDA growth of 20% YoY for Q3 FY'26. This performance was bolstered by a 2% increase in cement sales volume, significantly outpacing industry averages. The company remains debt-free with a net worth of INR 69,854 Cr and boasts a strong credit rating of AAA (Stable) / A1+ from CRISIL and CARE. Key strategic initiatives include the ongoing amalgamation of Orient Cement and Sanghi Industries with Ambuja Cements, creating a unified "One Cement Platform," and the significant expansion of renewable energy capacity. Management reiterated a commitment to cost leadership and achieving an EBITDA of INR 1,500 PMT by March 2028, supported by ongoing digitization and AI-driven operational improvements.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Current Capacity: ~109 MTPA, with 2.4 MTPA Marwar Grinding Unit operational.
    *   Target: 155 MTPA by March 2028 exit.
    *   Planned Additions: 115 MTPA by March 2026, including 5.6 MTPA debottlenecking by FY'27 and 9.4 MTPA in FY'28.
    *   Commissioned: 4 MTPA brownfield Clinker Unit in Bhatapara, Chhattisgarh.
    *   Ongoing Investments: Growth capex of ~INR 8,000 Cr and efficiency capex of ~INR 2,000 Cr.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Target: Cost of production below INR 3,650 PMT by March 2028.
    *   Green Power: Target of 60% by March 2028 exit; current share at 36.9% (9MFY'26).
    *   Energy Efficiency: Kiln fuel lower by 10P @ 165P/’000 kCal. Maximization of cheaper AFR material.
    *   Logistics: Strategic modal shift with waterways to go up to ~5% by FY'28. Accelerated automation and digitization.
    *   Raw Material: Long-term arrangements for major raw materials, infrastructure developments for raw material handling.
*   **Market Leadership & Solutions Provider:**
    *   Market Share: 16.6%.
    *   Premium Products: Sustained at 35% of trade sales; volume growth of premium cement at 31% YoY.
    *   Customer Engagement: CiNOC (Cement Intelligent Network Operating Centre) launched with AI integration. FutureX initiative engaging 750+ institutions. Digital sales platform and ePOD system implemented.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Amalgamations: ACC and Orient with Ambuja Cements creating a unified "One Cement Platform." Sanghi Industries and Penna Cement mergers expected to be completed by March 2026.
    *   Adani Ecosystem Synergies: Supply chain, off-take, raw material procurement, power, and logistics leveraging Adani Group's diverse businesses.
    *   Brand Strategy: Ambuja, ACC, and Penna brands to be used in South India.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   S&P Global CSA: Ambuja and ACC scored 90 and 89 respectively (highest in sector).
    *   Renewable Energy: 898 MW commissioned, on track for 1,122 MW by FY27.
    *   Circular Economy: 100% waste diversion, zero landfill. ~36,000+ tons diverted across last FY and ongoing FY.
    *   Water Positive: 16.9x currently.
    *   Green Power: Share increased to 36.9% (9MFY'26).
