## ACC Limited - Q4 2021 Earnings Call Summary

### Executive Overview

ACC Limited's Q4 2021 earnings call highlights a strategic focus on accelerated growth, driven by capacity expansions and product innovation, while reinforcing its commitment to ESG principles. Despite facing cost inflation, particularly in fuel prices, the company demonstrated resilience through initiatives like "Project Parvat" and optimized logistics. Management emphasized the company's long-term vision of becoming a leading sustainable building material provider, with significant investments planned in capacity, ESG, and efficiency. Growth in premium products and the Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) business are key strategic priorities.

### Key Financial Metrics

*   **Full Year 2021 Performance:**
    *   **Cement Sales Volume:** 29 million tons (+13% YoY)
    *   **RMX Sales Volume:** 2.81 million cubic meters (+24% YoY)
    *   **Net Sales:** INR 15,814 Crores (+17% YoY)
    *   **EBITDA:** ~ INR 3,000 Crores (+27% YoY)
    *   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.2% (+250 bps YoY)
    *   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 1,863 Crores (+30% YoY)
*   **Q4 2021 Performance:**
    *   **Cement Volume:** Down 3% YoY
    *   **Net Sales:** Up 2% YoY (driven by price increases)
    *   **EBITDA:** INR 556 Crores (-3% YoY)
    *   **PAT:** INR 281 Crores (-40% YoY) - *Note: Prior year Q4 included a one-time gain.*
*   **Cement EBITDA per Tonne (Full Year 2021):** INR 988/tonne (+INR 113/tonne YoY)

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Sindri expansion is operational.
    *   Tikaria expansion is nearing completion, ahead of schedule.
    *   Ametha integrated unit on track for clinker and cement production by September/October 2022.
    *   Salai Banwa project ahead of schedule.
    *   **Target:** To reach 45-50 million tons from current 34-35 million tons over the next two to three years.
*   **Product Portfolio & Innovation:**
    *   **Premium Products:** Robust growth of nearly 20% YoY, contributing over 27% of trade sales. Offers a price premium of Rs. 30-50 per bag and a significant EBITDA margin benefit.
    *   **ECOPact:** Reached 15% of the concrete business within a year of launch.
    *   **RMC Business:** Plans to double the number of plants over the next three years, expanding presence from top 15 to next 50 cities. High return business with low capital intensity per plant.
    *   **Products & Solutions:** Doubled turnover in FY2022, focusing on waterproofing, plasters, and admixtures, with intent to make this a meaningful vertical.
*   **Cost Efficiency & Optimization:**
    *   **Project Parvat:** Continues as a flagship cost efficiency initiative, delivering upwards of Rs. 300/tonne in P&L efficiency over the last two years. Further Rs. 100-150/tonne expected in coming semesters through various initiatives (clinker factor reduction, fuel flexibility, TSR, raw material mix, geo-mix, MSA leverage).
    *   **MSA with Ambuja:** Leveraged for cost and revenue management.
    *   **Fuel Cost Management:** Plants have flexibility in fuel usage; back-to-back buying strategy employed.
*   **Digitalization:**
    *   "Plants of Tomorrow" journey to drive productivity and safety.
    *   Digitalization in logistics and sourcing for market nimbleness and cost/process efficiencies.
    *   Commercial digitalization focused on seamless customer connection and enhanced consumer experience.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   **Net Zero Pledge:** First Indian company to sign the pledge, with action plans validated by SBTi.
    *   **Waste-Derived Fuels (TSR):** Current TSR at 7%, targeting 15% by 2025. Consumed 11 million tons of waste in 2021, planning a 50% increase in three years.
    *   **River Cleaning Initiative ("Bubble"):** Leveraging plastic waste from water bodies (e.g., Agra canal) as alternative fuel; expanding to other locations. Primarily an environmental initiative, with incidental fuel benefit.
    *   **Waste Management:** Piloting plastic waste collection from cricket stadiums for alternative fuel.
    *   **Green Power:** Targeting 16% of power from clean and green sources (WHRS & renewables) by 2022, increasing to over 40% by 2025.
    *   **Waste Heat Recovery Systems (WHRS):** Immediate milestone of 45 MW by 2022, targeting 85-90 MW by 2025, representing ~25% of total power consumption.
    *   **Water Positivity:** Aiming to move from 1.1 times to 5 times by 2030.
*   **Community Development:** Continued focus on livelihood, education, and WASH programs.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context:** The Q3 2021 analysis indicated a focus on resilience amidst cost inflation, driven by "Project Parvat" and premium pricing strategies. Capacity expansion and sustainability goals were highlighted as ongoing priorities.

**Audit:**

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Full Year 2021 Financials:** ACC has shown strong year-on-year growth across key metrics like sales volume, net sales, EBITDA, and PAT, exceeding previous year's performance despite a challenging cost environment, aligning with its growth narrative.
    *   **Q4 2021 Financials:** While overall volumes declined and PAT saw a significant drop (partially due to a one-time gain in the prior year), net sales saw a marginal increase due to price rises. Management acknowledges Q4 was a tough quarter.
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Projects like Sindri are operational, Tikaria is nearing completion, and Ametha is on track, demonstrating consistent progress on stated expansion plans. The broader capacity target of 45-50 million tons over the next two to three years has been clearly articulated.
    *   **Project Parvat:** Management has quantified realized benefits (upwards of Rs. 300/tonne) and outlined future potential, indicating consistent execution and realization of this flagship cost efficiency program.
    *   **Sustainability:** While concrete steps and targets are being communicated (e.g., TSR, WHRS, Net Zero pledge), specific metrics like CO2 emissions and water positivity from previous periods were not re-stated in detail for Q4, but the commitment remains strong. The river cleaning initiative is a new, tangible ESG effort.
*   **Narrative/Tone Shift:** The tone remains consistent with a focus on growth and long-term strategy. Management explicitly acknowledges the "tough quarter" for Q4 and the "volatility in the fuel space," noting that price increases in Q4 were "muted and grossly insufficient" to offset fuel cost inflation. This confirms the significant cost headwinds faced, but the underlying narrative emphasizes proactive management through Project Parvat, MSA, and plant flexibility. The company also highlights a discipline in making announcements, ensuring they are backed by internal approvals and execution capability.
*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter:**
    *   **Capacity Expansion Timelines:**
        *   Ametha integrated and grinding units: Operational by September/October 2022.
        *   Salai Banwa: Operational towards the end of Q1 or early Q2 next year (2023).
        *   **Target:** 45-50 million tons capacity by 2024-2025.
        *   **Near-term Capacity Expansion:** ~40 million tons over the next 14-15 months (from current projects).
    *   **Project Parvat Future Benefits:** Further Rs. 100-150/tonne efficiency expected in coming semesters.
    *   **RMC Business Expansion:** Double the number of plants in three years; expand to next 50 cities.
    *   **TSR Target:** 15% by 2025.
    *   **Waste Consumption:** 50% increase planned over the next three years.
    *   **WHRS:** 45 MW by 2022; 85-90 MW by 2025.
    *   **Green Power Mix:** 16% by 2022; >40% by 2025.
    *   **New Products:** Continued development of sustainable and green products.
    *   **Oxygen Plants:** Installation of 5 Oxygen Plants underway (mentioned in Q3 context, likely nearing completion).
    *   **No Explicit Financial Guidance:** Management declined to provide specific forecasts for fuel prices or Q4 2021 vs. Q1 2022 cost comparisons due to volatility.
    *   **Margins:** While Q4 margins faced pressure, the full-year EBITDA margin showed expansion. Management continues to focus on Project Parvat and premium products to support margin improvement. The gap with leading players is acknowledged, with a stated intent to bridge it.
