## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cements Limited (Adani Cement) demonstrated resilience and strategic execution in Q3 FY'25 amidst a tepid industry demand in H1 FY'25, attributing it to elections and heavy rains, while anticipating an 8-9% growth in H2 FY'25. The company continues its aggressive growth trajectory towards a 140 MTPA target by FY'28, with significant progress in both organic and inorganic expansion. Key strategic pillars of cost leadership, operational excellence, ESG commitment, and leveraging group synergies remain central. The integration of recent acquisitions like Penna and Sanghi, along with the advanced stage of the Orient Cement acquisition, are expected to bolster market position and profitability. The company highlighted a robust balance sheet, maintaining a debt-free status and healthy cash reserves, enabling continued capital expenditure.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Target: 140 MTPA by FY'28.
    *   Progress: Orient Cement acquisition at an advanced stage; total capacity to reach 104 MTPA by Q4 FY'25 post Orient acquisition.
    *   Commissioning Pipeline: Bhatapara clinker and Sankrail, Farakka, Sindri grinding units by end of FY'25; Salai Banwa grinding by Q1 FY'26; Bathinda, Marwar grinding by Q2 FY'26; Kalamboli, Dahej, Jodhpur Penna, Krishnapatnam grinding by Q3 FY'26; Maratha clinker and Warsaliganj grinding by end of FY'26, reaching 118 MTPA. 14 additional grinding units planned to reach 140 MTPA by FY'28.
    *   Milestone: 100 Ready-Mix plants commissioned in Q3 FY'25.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Target: Achieve INR 3,650-3,700 per ton cost by FY'28.
    *   Initiatives: Lowering energy costs through increased WHRS capacity (197 MW currently, targeting 218 MW by Mar'25) and 1,000 MW RE commissioning by FY'26 (200 MW solar commissioned in Q3 FY'25). Aiming for 60% green power for planned 140 MTPA capacity and 83% for clinkerisation. Expected power cost reduction of INR 100 per ton by FY'28.
    *   Freight Optimization: Reduced lead distance by 4 km to 285 km (primary) and 46 km (secondary); increased direct dispatch to 57%; ordered 11 GPWIS rakes and 26 BCFC rakes.
    *   Raw Material Security: Secured bids for 2 mines with 631 million tons of reserves in MP and Karnataka.
    *   Alternate Fuels & Resources (AFR): Partnership with Coolbrook for zero-carbon heating technology; increased utilization of waste-derived resources.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Focus on ramping up utilization of Penna and Sanghi assets, aiming for 70%+ utilization in FY'26.
    *   Transitioning acquired assets to Ambuja and ACC brands, requiring market investments.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Net-Zero target by 2050.
    *   Partnership with Coolbrook for zero-carbon heating technology.
    *   Utilized 4.8 million tons of waste-derived resources in Q3 FY'25.
    *   Water positive operations.
    *   Improved ESG ratings (DJSI, CDP, Sustainalytics, MSCI).
*   **Market Penetration:**
    *   Strong focus on micro-market management and dealer network expansion.
    *   Blended cements at 82%; premium products increased to 26% of trade sales volume.
    *   Direct dispatch increased to 57%.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Management is actively progressing towards the 140 MTPA target. The Orient Cement acquisition is nearing completion, and the commissioning pipeline for new units is on track, with several slated for completion by end of FY'25 and FY'26. The overall capacity is projected to reach 118 MTPA by end of FY'26.
    *   **Cost Reduction & Efficiency:** Management continues to drive cost optimization initiatives. Operational costs per ton saw a YoY decline in Q3 FY'25, driven by lower energy and transportation expenses. While consolidated costs were impacted by lower utilization and higher cost structures of newly acquired assets (Penna & Sanghi), the company is actively implementing initiatives to bring these in line with Ambuja's standards. The end-state target of INR 3,650-3,700 per ton by FY'28 remains.
    *   **Financial Strength:** The company maintains its debt-free status and a strong cash position, supporting its aggressive capex plans. Net worth has seen a significant increase.
