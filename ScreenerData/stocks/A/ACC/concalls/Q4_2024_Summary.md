## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cement (Adani Cement) demonstrated robust performance in Q4 FY'24, marked by significant revenue and EBITDA growth, driven by increased volumes, strategic acquisitions, and cost optimization initiatives. The company is aggressively pursuing its 140 MTPA capacity target by FY'28 through a combination of organic and inorganic expansion, with several new units slated for commissioning in FY'25 and FY'26. Cost leadership remains a key focus, with substantial reductions achieved in energy and transportation costs, supported by investments in renewable energy, Waste Heat Recovery Systems (WHRS), and optimized logistics. The company maintains a strong balance sheet, bolstered by promoter fund infusions and a debt-free status, enabling continued capital expenditure. The integration of Sanghi Industries is progressing well, with plans for capacity enhancement and operational efficiency improvements. The management is confident in the positive industry outlook, driven by government initiatives and expected demand growth exceeding capacity expansion.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Target: 140 MTPA by FY'28.
    *   Current Capacity: 78.9 MTPA (post Tuticorin acquisition).
    *   FY'25 Target: 86 MTPA exiting the fiscal year.
    *   Commissioning Pipeline:
        *   Bhatapara clinker (4 MTPA) expected by Q4 FY'25.
        *   Sankarel & Farakka grinding units by Q3 FY'25.
        *   Maratha clinker (4 MTPA) by Q2 FY'26.
        *   Salai Banwa, Sindri, Marwar, Mundra grinding units by end of FY'26.
        *   Hoshiarpur & Pune grinding units by FY'27.
    *   Clinker Expansion: Targeting 82 MTPA of clinker by FY'28, with 80% brownfield and 20% greenfield expansions.
    *   Sanghi: Plan to refurbish existing kilns for 100% utilization in H2 FY'24, with future plans for two additional 4 MTPA kilns. Targeting 5 MTPA clinker production in FY'25.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Target: INR 3,650-3,700 per ton cost by FY'28 (INR 530 per ton further reduction from current base).
    *   Energy Costs: 13% YoY decline in Q4 FY'24 driven by better fuel management, increased AFR utilization (10.6% from 8.7%), and WHRS capacity (134 MW currently, targeting 186 MW by Mar'25). Power cost target of INR 4.50/unit by FY'28 from current INR 6.75/unit.
    *   Freight Optimization: 8% YoY decline in Q4 FY'24 logistics cost. Targeting primary lead distance reduction to ~100 km. Procurement of 11 GPWIS rakes and 26 DCFC rakes for efficient clinker and fly ash transportation.
    *   Raw Material Security: Secured bids for 2 new coal mines (Dahegaon Gowari, Lama Tola) adding 4 MTPA capacity, aiming for 80-90% captive coal supply within 12 months. Total limestone reserves at 7.8 billion tons.
    *   AFR Utilization: Increased to 10.6% of fuel mix.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Sanghi: Refurbishment of kilns, aiming for 5 MTPA clinker in FY'25. ICD repayment structure to improve financial leverage. Expanded jetty draft to 4.5 meters for improved evacuation.
    *   Master Supply Agreements (MSAs): Operating effectively, with lowest cost plants serving markets and highest EBITDA routes prioritized.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Commitment to Net-Zero by 2050.
    *   Targeting 60% green power sourcing by FY'28. Commissioning 200 MW solar in Q1 FY'25, part of 1000 MW RE investment.
    *   Water positive operations (Ambuja 11x).
    *   8x plastic negativity (Ambuja).
    *   Used over 21 million tons of waste-derived resources in FY'24.
    *   Pledged to plant 8.3 million trees by 2030.
*   **Market Penetration:**
    *   Micro-market management strategy and dealer network expansion.
    *   Blended cement at 86% of sales; premium products at 24% of trade sales.
    *   Direct dispatch maintained at 54%.
    *   Aggressive growth plan to achieve 20% market share by FY'28.
