## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cements (ACC) delivered another quarter of industry-leading performance in Q3 FY'26, marked by robust volume growth and strategic advancements, despite a temporary uptick in average operating costs. The company achieved its highest-ever quarterly sales volume at 18.9 million tonnes, a 17% YoY increase, significantly outpacing industry growth and maintaining its market share at 16.6%. Consolidated revenue grew 20% YoY to INR 10,277 crores, bolstered by a INR 5 per bag improvement in realizations, driven by a strong focus on premium products (35% of trade sales) and a shift towards trade channels.

A defining development this quarter is the proposed amalgamation of ACC and Orient Cement with Ambuja Cements, aiming to create a unified 'One Cement Platform' for accelerated growth, EBITDA expansion, and capital efficiency. Early operational success is evident in acquired assets, with utilization improving meaningfully to 58% (Q3 average) and 65% (December exit).

While average Q3 costs increased to INR 4,500 per tonne, management attributed this to specific one-off expenses (brand transition, asset overhauling, equipment failures) and emphasized that the December exit cost was already below INR 4,000 per tonne. Cost leadership remains a strategic priority, with reductions in kiln fuel and power costs, and an increase in green power share to 37%. Capacity expansion is on track, with total capacity now at 109 MTPA and a slight adjustment to the FY'26 exit target to 115 MTPA (from 118 MTPA). The company remains debt-free with strong ratings.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Debottlenecking:**
    *   Current Cement Capacity: 109 MTPA.
    *   Revised Target: 155 MTPA by FY'28 (unchanged).
    *   FY'26 Exit Target: 115 MTPA (revised from 118 MTPA due to Warisaliganj delay and 2 MTPA mothballing).
    *   Debottlenecking Initiatives: Adding 15 MTPA to cement capacity at low capex cost continues.
    *   Greenfield/Brownfield Progress: 2.4 MTPA Penna Marwar grinding unit commissioned ahead of schedule. Bhatapara clinker Line 3 commissioned. Penna clinker expected in February. Maratha clinker expected Q1/Q2 FY'27. Agreement signed for a new 4 MTPA greenfield clinker line in Assam.
    *   Mothballing: 2 MTPA capacity (Sindri, Jamul) mothballed due to economic unviability.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Cost Reduction Targets: Reiterated target of ~INR 4,000 per tonne by March '26 exit, INR 3,800 by March '27, and INR 3,650 by March '28.
    *   Q3 Cost Drivers: Kiln fuel cost declined by 6% YoY, power cost reduced by 15% YoY, logistics cost reduced by 1% YoY.
    *   Green Power: Share increased to 37% in Q3 FY'26 (up from 33%). Current renewable capacity at 900 MW, targeting 1,122 MW by FY'27. Expect significant cost benefits when consumed captively.
    *   Logistics: Focused on reducing lead distances and leveraging rail (BCFC rakes, CONCOR tie-up).
*   **Market Leadership & Solutions Provider:**
    *   Premium Products: Volumes increased 31% YoY, maintaining 35% share of trade sales.
    *   Trade Channel Focus: Aiming for 70%-30% (trade/non-trade) mix by January exit, moving towards 75%-25%.
    *   Digitalization: CiNOC (Cement Intelligent Network Operations Center) launched for operational efficiency and productivity.
    *   Customer Engagement: Expanded engagement with industry platforms (CREDAI, BAI, NAREDCO). "Adani Certified Technology site" (ACT) network growing (36,000 sites).
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Acquisition Integration: Proposed amalgamation of ACC and Orient Cement with Ambuja. Acquired assets (Penna, Orient, Sanghi) show early operational success and improved capacity utilization (58% Q3 average, 65% Dec exit). Sanghi clinker utilization reached 80% in December.
    *   Adani Ecosystem: Continued leveraging for procurement, power, and logistics.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Net Zero: Progressing on SBTi-validated targets. MOU with Coolbrook for kiln electrification progressing (Boyareddypalli plant).
    *   Carbon Capture: Indo-Swedish pilot project with IIT Bombay and Government of Sweden.
    *   Disclosures: First Indian cement company to adopt TNFD framework.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Management's capacity expansion plan remains consistent with the Q2 target of 155 MTPA by FY'28. However, the FY'26 exit target was slightly revised downwards from 118 MTPA to 115 MTPA, attributed to a 3-month delay in Warisaliganj commissioning and the mothballing of 2 MTPA of unviable assets. The debottlenecking initiatives are on track, adding 15 MTPA.
    *   **Cost Reduction & Efficiency:** This quarter showed a deviation in the Q3 average performance. While Q2 reported a significant YoY cost reduction and targeted ~INR 4,000 per tonne by March '26, the Q3 average cost increased to INR 4,500 per tonne. Management provided detailed explanations, attributing this to one-off expenses (brand transition, asset overhauling, equipment failures at Tandur and Jamul) and affirmed that the exit cost for December was already below INR 4,000 per tonne. They reiterated the long-term cost targets of INR 3,800 by March '27 and INR 3,650 by March '28, citing ongoing benefits from green power, WHRS, and new efficient capacities.
    *   **Financial Strength:** The company maintained its debt-free status and strong net worth. The decline in cash quarter-on-quarter was attributed to planned capital expenditure, consistent with the aggressive growth strategy.
    *   **Merger & Integration:** The Q2 narrative of seamless integration of acquired entities (Orient, Penna) and improved profitability continued into Q3, with management highlighting "early operational success" and significantly improved capacity utilization for these assets. The turnaround of Sanghi is specifically emphasized, with December exit figures showing strong clinker utilization.
    *   **ESG Commitment:** Management demonstrated consistent commitment to ESG, reporting increased renewable energy capacity (900 MW, up from 673 MW in Q2), progress on strategic decarbonization pathways (Coolbrook, carbon capture), and adoption of new frameworks like TNFD.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift:** The management's tone remains confidently bullish, consistently highlighting market outperformance in volumes and strategic execution. While acknowledging the Q3 increase in average operating costs, they framed it as a temporary aberration due to one-off events and stressed the positive cost trajectory from December onwards. The focus has sharpened on balancing volume growth with value creation through premiumization and trade channel penetration. The proposed amalgamation of ACC and Orient Cement is a new, highly positive strategic development.
