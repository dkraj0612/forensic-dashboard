## Executive Overview

ACC Limited's Q3 2021 results showcase resilience amidst challenging cost environments, with "Project Parvat" continuing to be a key driver of performance. While cement sales volumes saw a slight year-over-year dip to 6.6 million tonnes, the company achieved improved price realizations through commercial margin levers, partially offsetting raw material cost inflation. Despite significant headwinds from global fuel costs, ACC managed to mitigate the impact through network optimization and geo-mix improvements. The company reinforced its commitment to sustainability, highlighted by its "Net Zero Future" pledge and progress in circular economy initiatives. Digitalization efforts are ongoing across operations, and community development programs continue to expand.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Capacity expansion projects at Ametha (Clinker 2.7 MTPA, cement grinding 1.0 MTPA) and split cement grinding units (3.8 MTPA) in Madhya Pradesh are proceeding.
*   **Cost Efficiency & Optimization:** "Project Parvat" continues to be a flagship program for cost consciousness. Initiatives include leveraging premium products for price premium, innovative TV campaigns, on-site trials, digital marketing, and implementing cost measures like Best Cost Country (BCC) sourcing and target best costing. Network optimization, lead, and geo-mix improvements are key to mitigating fuel price increases.
*   **Premiumization & Product Innovation:**
    *   **ECOPact:** Gained wider acceptance and demonstrated accelerated volume share.
    *   **Differentiated Positioning:** Strengthened with further penetration into key customers and weather branding amplification.
    *   **New Products:** Innovative and Green cement & concrete range under development. Value-added dry mix range launched.
*   **Digitization:**
    *   **Operations:** TIS & PACT implemented at all plants. Pilot projects for Drone, VRM failure prediction, and MoT initiated. Technical Information System (TIS) and Performance & Collaboration Tool (PACT) adopted.
    *   **Logistics & Sourcing:** Transport Analytics Centre (TAC) integration with BlueYonder is in progress. EPOD pilot to be rolled out soon. Procurement Dashboard in progress.
    *   **Commercial:** Dealer adoption of total order bookings is encouraging. RMX business app customization in progress for better customer engagement.
*   **Circular Economy & Sustainability (SD 2030 Goals):**
    *   **Waste-Derived Resources:** Consumed 7.6 million tonnes in 9M'21 (Target 30 million tonnes/year).
    *   **CO2 Emissions:** Net specific CO2 emissions at 495 kg/t cementitious materials in 9M'21 (Target 400 kg/t).
    *   **Water Positive:** Index >1 in 9M'21 (Target Water Positive Index – 5).
    *   **Geocycle Projects:** Chanda operational, Jamul and Kymore under execution.
    *   **Environment Conservation:** Reducing freshwater withdrawal, enhancing water harvesting, biodiversity assessment, and implementing management plans. Bubble barriers being set up to prevent plastic leakage into rivers.
*   **Community Development:** 45,278 new beneficiaries reached through CSR efforts in 9M'21. Significant support provided for housing, education, health (including COVID-19 related initiatives), and environmental projects.
*   **Community Health Infrastructure Support:** 3 Oxygen Plants installed, with 5 more underway. Partnering with government for vaccination drives and supporting COVID-care centers.
