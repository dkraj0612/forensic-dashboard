## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cements (ACC) delivered a strong FY'26, exceeding industry growth with a 16% YoY volume increase to 73.7 million tonnes. Normalized annual EBITDA grew 31% YoY to INR 6,539 crores (INR 887 per tonne), with PAT seeing a 17% rise to INR 2,647 crores. The company maintains a debt-free status and high credit ratings. The amalgamation of ACC and Orient Cement with Ambuja is progressing, while Sanghi Industries and Penna Cement integrations are complete. Key FY'27 priorities include operational streamlining, margin expansion, a focus on trade and premium sales, improved utilization at acquired assets, and cost leadership, with a target cost reduction of Rs. 150-200 PMT.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Integration:**
    *   Current Cement Capacity: 109 MTPA. Target to reach 119 MTPA by end of FY'27 through commissioning of several grinding and clinker capacities.
    *   Focus shifting towards stabilizing newly commissioned capacities and improving utilization across the existing base, targeting 85% overall utilization.
    *   Acquired Asset Integration: Sanghi utilization improved to 57% in Q4 FY'26; targeting 65-70% for FY'27.
    *   Strategic Recalibration: Moving grinding units closer to markets to reduce logistics costs, with Sanghi focusing on clinker production for coastal Gujarat.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Cost Reduction Initiatives: Fuel mix optimization, higher renewable energy usage, reduced logistics costs (rail/sea), disciplined production/inventory management, and maximizing low-cost AFR.
    *   FY'27 Cost Reduction Target: Expected to reduce total cement cost by Rs. 150-200 PMT in FY'27 from the current level of ~Rs. 4,400, excluding geopolitical impacts.
    *   Cost Drivers: Q4 FY'26 impacted by fuel cost inflation, packaging supply constraints, and labor migration. Mitigation measures are actively being strengthened.
*   **Market Leadership & Solutions Provider:**
    *   Trade Channel Focus: Premium cement sustained at 35% of trade sales, with 22% YoY volume growth in trade.
    *   Digital Transformation: CiNOC (Cement Intelligent Network Operations Centre) launched for AI integration. Digital sales platform and ePOD system enhance efficiency.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Proposed Amalgamation: ACC and Orient Cement with Ambuja Cements to create a unified ‘One Cement Platform’. Awaiting SEBI NOC; expected completion over FY'27.
    *   Acquired Assets: Mergers of Sanghi and Penna are effective. Focus on improving machine reliability and overall asset utilization.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   High ESG Scores: Ambuja and ACC scored 90 and 89 respectively in the 2025 S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment.
    *   Environmental Commitments: Water positive, zero liquid discharge, and significant tree plantation drive. Pioneering Coolbrook’s RotoDynamic Heater technology for decarbonization.
    *   Social Impact: Community development projects benefiting millions.
