## Executive Overview

ACC Limited's Q1 2021 results demonstrate a significant rebound from the COVID-19 impacted Q2 2020. The company reported strong year-on-year growth in net sales, EBITDA, and Profit After Tax, driven by robust demand, favorable pricing, and effective cost management initiatives. Key achievements include the commissioning of the Sindri grinding unit, the launch of the "ECOPact" range of green concretes, and continued progress on expansion projects. The company maintained a focus on health and safety, with a significant portion of its employees vaccinated. ACC expresses a cautiously positive outlook for cement demand, underpinned by government infrastructure spending.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cost Efficiency:** Project 'Parvat' is on track, driving efficiency across cost line items. Mitigation actions for rising fuel and input costs include leveraging alternate fuels (Geocycle), energy efficiency improvements, and optimizing input mix. Focus on direct dispatches, network optimization, and procurement savings also mitigated rising diesel costs. Higher manpower productivity and volume absorption further reduced costs per ton.
*   **Growth & Expansion:** Sindri expansion (1.4 MTPA) commissioned in record time. Greenfield expansion in Ametha and associated grinding units are progressing as per plan.
*   **Innovation & Sustainability:** Launched "ECOPact," the industry's broadest range of green concretes, including ECOPact+, PRIME, and ECOPact Max in metros. Pioneered low-carbon concrete in India with significant carbon footprint reduction. Ongoing projects focus on low CO2 binder, low dust, and reduced curing. Waste Heat Recovery (WHR) projects are on track.
*   **Digitisation:** Embedding TIS (Technical Information System) and PACT (Performance & Collaboration Tool) in plants. Leveraging TAC (Transport Analytics Center) and Blue Yonder for logistics efficiencies. High usage of the 'Dealer Connect Portal'. Predictive maintenance pilots are underway.
*   **Health & Safety:** Prioritizing employee and community well-being. Facilitating employee vaccination (approx. 70% of eligible employees vaccinated). Strict adherence to government guidelines across plants and offices with adequate safety infrastructure.
*   **Community Development:** Continued focus on WASH (Water, Sanitation, Health & Hygiene), Malnutrition Mitigation, and skilling programs. Reached new beneficiaries and created community infrastructure.
