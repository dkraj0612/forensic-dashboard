## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cements (ACC) delivered a strong Q2 FY'26, demonstrating robust year-on-year growth in volumes, revenue, and EBITDA. The company achieved its highest-ever sales volume for a quarter at 16.6 million tonnes, a 20% YoY increase, outperforming industry growth and leading to a 1% market share gain to 16.6%. Revenue rose 21% YoY to INR 9,174 crores, supported by a 3% price gain and a 35% share of premium products in total trade sales, which translated to a 28% YoY growth in premium cement sales.

A significant driver of this performance was improved cost efficiencies. Total costs reduced by 5% YoY, with kiln fuel cost (including AFR) at INR 1.60 per 1,000 kcal being the lowest among peers. Management targets an exit cost of ~INR 4,000 per metric tonne by March '26, aiming for INR 3,650 by March '28. Consolidated EBITDA reached INR 1,761 crores, up 58% YoY, with EBITDA per tonne at INR 1,060, a 32% YoY increase, and an EBITDA margin of 19.2% (up 4.5% YoY). The company remains debt-free with a net worth of INR 69,493 crores.

Strategic initiatives, including debottlenecking of plants and logistics, integration of acquired entities (Orient, Penna, Sanghi), and digital transformation via CiNOC, are contributing to enhanced operational efficiency and future growth.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Debottlenecking:**
    *   Current Cement Capacity: 107 MTPA.
    *   Revised Target: 155 MTPA by FY'28 (up from 140 MTPA).
    *   Debottlenecking Initiatives: Adding 15 MTPA to cement capacity at a low capex of $48 per ton (integrated basis). This includes grinding capacity expansion at 13 locations.
    *   Clinker Capacity Expansion: Target revised to 96 MTPA by FY'28 (from 84 MTPA), with additions of three new lines.
    *   Greenfield/Brownfield Progress: Projects like Salai Banwa, Penna Marwar, Dahej, Kalamboli, Bathinda, Jodhpur, and Warisaliganj progressing well, with some commissioning in Q3 FY'26 and remainder by FY'26 end. Bhatapara clinker line (4 MTPA) trial runs started.
    *   Logistics Debottlenecking: Expected to improve current capacity utilization by 3% (3 million tons) over 24 months.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Cost Reduction Targets: Target ~INR 4,000 per tonne by March '26 (5% reduction from ~INR 4,200 in Sept '26), INR 3,800 by March '27, and INR 3,650 by March '28.
    *   Fuel Cost: Kiln fuel cost (excluding AFR) at INR 1.65 per 1,000 kcal is lowest among peers. Including AFR, it's INR 1.60.
    *   Green Power: Share increased to 33% in Q2 FY'26. Target of 60% by FY'28. Renewable energy capacity at 673 MW (targeting 900 MW by FY'26 end and 1,122 MW by FY'27).
    *   Logistics Cost: Down 7% YoY to INR 1,224. Primary lead distance reduced by 2 km to 265 km.
*   **Market Leadership & Solutions Provider:**
    *   Premium Products: Aiming for 35% share in trade sales for FY'26.
    *   Brand Integration: Adani brand now integrated into Ambuja and ACC, showing positive results.
    *   Digitalization: Launch of CiNOC for operational efficiency and stakeholder engagement.
    *   Customer Engagement: Continued initiatives like FutureX, Samvaad, Dhanvarsha, and CEO Club. Adani certified technology sites using premium cement growing.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Acquisition Integration: Seamless integration of Penna and Orient Cement, with sales under Ambuja and ACC brands showing improved profitability.
    *   Adani Ecosystem: Leveraging group synergies for procurement, power, and logistics.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Net Zero: SBTi-validated net-zero and near-term targets. MOU with Coolbrook of Finland progressing.
    *   Renewable Energy: Capacity at 673 MW, targeting 1,122 MW by FY'27.
    *   Positive Impact: Water positive (12x), plastic negative. Expectation of INR 200-225 crores annual income from carbon credits.
