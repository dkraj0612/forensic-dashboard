## Executive Overview

This revised investor presentation from ACC Limited, dated April 12, 2024, reiterates the Adani Cement business's (ACC, Ambuja Cements, Sanghi Industries) strategic focus on integrated operations, aggressive capacity expansion, and cost leadership, all underpinned by robust ESG commitments. The presentation reaffirms the group's ambition to achieve 20% market share by FY'28 with a capacity of 140 MTPA, leveraging group synergies across logistics, procurement, and shared services. Ambuja Cements' strong financial position, characterized by its debt-free status and a substantial increase in cash and cash equivalents due to warrant money receipts, is highlighted as a key enabler for its accelerated capex program. The presentation also emphasizes Adani Cement's differentiating factors, including premium product focus, higher trade sales, significant fly ash absorption, and green power initiatives.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Aggressive capex program to double capacity to 140 MTPA by FY'28, utilizing existing capacity, new projects (Ametha, Bhatapara, Chandrapur), and acquisitions. Focus on increasing both clinker and grinding capacity.
*   **Cost Leadership:** Aiming for 8-10% cost optimization through efficiency and productivity improvements, targeting to reduce the cost gap with the world's lowest cost manufacturers by approximately Rs 1000 PMT. This involves securing assured raw material supplies competitively and leveraging group synergies for procurement (coal, fly ash), logistics (APSEZ MMLPs), and shared services. Increased Thermal Substitution Rate (TSR) and Fly Ash absorption are key components.
*   **Synergies and Integration:** Full utilization of group synergies across logistics, procurement, shared services (ABEX, Digital), and expertise in mining (AEL/ANR), project execution (AIIL), and operational excellence (APL). Integration of Sanghi Industries aims to enhance utilization through Ambuja and ACC brands.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):** Significant investments in Renewable Energy (Solar, Wind, WHRS) to reach a 60% green power share by FY'28. Commitment to water positivity (10x target), circular economy (waste-derived resources), and reducing Net specific CO2 emissions. Ambuja Cements recognized for water positivity and plastic waste co-processing. Social initiatives focus on health, livelihoods, social inclusion, management, and agriculture.
*   **Market Penetration:** Targeting 20% market share by FY'28, with a focus on increasing trade sales (75% target) and premium product volume (23% target).
