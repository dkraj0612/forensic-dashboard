## Executive Overview

ACC Limited's Q2 2022 earnings transcript reveals a company experiencing a significant rebound in sales volume growth compared to the previous quarter, indicating a recovery in demand. However, profitability remains under pressure due to persistent inflationary headwinds, particularly in power and fuel costs, which significantly impacted EBITDA margins. Despite these challenges, ACC is demonstrating continued execution on its strategic pillars: accelerating growth through capacity expansions and RMC business development, driving innovation with new green products and solutions, and reinforcing its commitment to sustainability with ongoing initiatives. Project Parvat continues to be a key enabler for cost mitigation. Management acknowledges the volatile macroeconomic environment and its potential impact.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q2 2022 Performance:**
    *   **Cement Volume:** 7.78 million tons (9.7% YoY growth).
    *   **RMX Sales Volume:** 0.83 million cubic meters (not explicitly stated YoY growth).
    *   **Net Sales:** Not explicitly stated for Q2 2022.
    *   **EBITDA:** 922 Crores (9% YoY growth).
    *   **EBITDA Margin:** 12.2% (-9.4 pp YoY), impacted by significant cost increases.
    *   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** 376 Crores (45% YoY decline).
    *   **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Not explicitly stated for Q2 2022, but a 45% YoY decline in PAT suggests a significant EPS drop.

*   **Cost Analysis (per ton):**
    *   **Power & Fuel Cost:** Significantly increased due to soaring global energy prices.
    *   **Freight & Forwarding Cost:** Impacted by diesel inflation, though "Parvat" actions provided partial mitigation.
    *   **Raw Material Cost:** Impacted by inflationary headwinds.
    *   **Other Expenses:** Continued focus on optimization.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Commissioned 1.6 MTPA grinding unit in Feb 2022 (executed in 9 months).
    *   Project implementation on track for commissioning expected in Q4 2022 for another grinding unit.
    *   WHRS (Waste Heat Recovery System) projects on track at Jamul, Kymore, and Ametha; orders placed for Chanda and Wadi. Total capacity to reach 75 MW.

*   **Product Portfolio & Innovation:**
    *   **Green Products:** Faster scaling of ECOPact, scaling up of 'AIRIUM', launch of 'DYNAMax', and LeakBlock range of waterproofing solutions.
    *   **New Products:** Xtra Strong Tile Adhesive, LeakBlock Waterproof Plaster, and Admixtures.
    *   **Ready Mix Concrete (RMC) Business:** Poised for significant growth due to low penetration, rapid urbanization, and infrastructure development. Strong expansion plans, portfolio of value-added services, and scaling up of global products are key.

*   **Cost Efficiency & Optimization:**
    *   **Project Parvat:** Continues to be leveraged for cost mitigation through improved clinker factor, higher share of WHRS power, scaling up renewable power, improving TSR, fuel flexibility, and network optimization (MSA).
    *   **Network Optimization (MSA):** Improvement in direct dispatch and warehouse footprint optimization.
    *   **Transport Analytics Center:** Enhancing logistics infrastructure.

*   **Digitalization:**
    *   Business processes enabled by digital core including CRM, TIS, EDGE AI, Drones, Video Analytics, Fleet Management, E-Tendering, and Concrete Direct for RMX.

*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Reiterated commitment as the first Indian cement company to sign Business Ambition for 1.5°C and Net Zero Pledge with Science Based Targets.
    *   **Bubble Barrier Technology:** Initiative towards cleaner rivers, with a pilot project on Mantola Canal, Agra.
    *   **Green Energy:** Journey of increasing share of green power on course with 82 MW solar and 19 MW wind capacity.
    *   **Geocycle Initiatives:** Single-use plastics management, reuse of packaging material.
    *   **SD 2030 Plan:** Aligned with Holcim, focusing on Climate & Energy (Net specific CO2 emissions target of 400 kg/t), Circular Economy (30 million tons of waste-derived resources), and Community (Water Positive Index target of at least 5 times by 2030, and strengthening "Positive Impact on Biodiversity").
    *   Current performance: 469 kg/t CO2 emissions, 6 million tons waste-derived resources consumed, 1.7 million beneficiaries reached cumulatively till H1'22.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Financial Performance (Q2 2022):** While Cement Volume growth (9.7% YoY) showed a strong recovery, EBITDA growth (9% YoY) lagged behind volume growth, and PAT declined significantly (45% YoY). This indicates that the inflationary pressures, particularly on power and fuel, were substantial and could not be fully offset by price increases or cost efficiencies, leading to a contraction in profitability metrics compared to the previous year. The EBITDA margin dropped by 9.4 pp YoY to 12.2%.
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Progress on commissioning grinding units and WHRS projects aligns with previously stated expansion plans, demonstrating continued execution.
    *   **Project Parvat:** Management continues to emphasize Project Parvat's role in mitigating cost headwinds, particularly on freight and logistics, and contributing to operational efficiencies like clinker factor improvement and fuel flexibility. This reinforces its ongoing effectiveness as a strategic initiative.
    *   **Sustainability:** The company continues to highlight its ESG commitments and progress, including the "Bubble Barrier" technology and expansion of green energy capacity, demonstrating consistency in its sustainability focus.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift:**
    The Q2 2022 transcript acknowledges the "significant cost increase" driven by "soaring global energy prices" impacting Power & Fuel Costs and "inflationary headwinds" affecting Raw Material Costs. Management explicitly states that "Price increase to marginally offset cost inflation" and "‘Parvat’ actions partially mitigated diesel inflation impact." While the previous quarter's transcript also mentioned cost headwinds, the Q2 transcript provides a clearer articulation of the specific cost drivers and their direct impact on margins, particularly noting the significant YoY decline in EBITDA Margin. The "High Evasion Density" warning from the preparation section suggests a potential reluctance from management to provide granular details or direct answers on certain aspects, though this is not directly observable within the provided transcript snippet.
