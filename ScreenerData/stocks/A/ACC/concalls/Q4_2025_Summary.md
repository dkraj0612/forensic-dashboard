## Executive Overview

Ambuja Cements, in its Q4 FY'25 earnings call, announced a significant milestone of crossing 100 million tons (MT) of cement capacity, achieving this in just 30 months and positioning itself as the ninth-largest cement company globally. The company reported an 11% YoY increase in consolidated revenue to INR 9,889 crores for Q4 FY'25, driven by a strong micro-market strategy, expanded ground network, and a higher share of premium products. Operational costs saw improvement due to better fuel and green power management, with kiln fuel costs down 14% and transportation costs down 2%. Consolidated EBITDA stood at INR 1,868 crores with a 19% margin and INR 1,001 per ton for the quarter. For the full fiscal year FY'25, revenue reached an all-time high of INR 35,045 crores, with EBITDA at INR 5,971 crores and INR 915 per ton. The company remains debt-free with a strong consolidated cash and cash equivalents of INR 10,125 crores as of March 31, 2025. Management reiterated its commitment to organic growth, cost leadership, and market expansion, with ambitious targets for capacity and efficiency improvements.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   Current Cement Capacity: Crossed 100 MTPA.
    *   Target: 118 MTPA by FY'26 exit, 140 MTPA by FY'28.
    *   Planned Commissioning (FY'26): Sankrail, Sindri (Q1'26), Salai Banwa (Q2'26), Kalamboli (Q3'26), Bhatinda, Marwar, Dahej, Jodhpur (Q3'26), Maratha clinker & Warisaliganj (end of FY'26).
    *   New Facilities Progress: Bhatapara clinker (87% progress), Sankrail grinding (88% progress), Maratha clinker (76% equipment order, 44% civil), Jodhpur clinker (95% civil, 48% mechanical erection).
    *   Additional Grinding Units: Identified 9 new units, land acquisition and approvals in process.
*   **Cost Leadership:**
    *   Target Cost of Production: INR 3,650 per ton by FY'28.
    *   Current Achieved Cost Reduction: INR 150-175 per ton since September '22.
    *   Fuel Management: Kiln fuel cost reduced by 14%; maximizing domestic coal and opportunistic pet coke. Securing coal mines and 10-year linkage for 2.4 million tons.
    *   Green Power: Share increased to 26% from 15.6%; WHRS capacity at 218 MW, targeting 30% of total capacity. 1,000 MW renewable energy project on track, 300 MW commissioned.
    *   Logistics: Reducing lead distance (primary 265 km, secondary 46 km), optimizing warehouse footprint, increasing rail mix. Ordered 11 GPWI rakes and 26 BCFC rakes for efficient clinker and fly ash movement. Enhanced coastal presence and marine logistics.
    *   Raw Materials: Secured 367 million tons of new limestone reserves, total over 9,000 million tons. Long-term fly ash agreement with Adani Power for 5 million tons.
*   **Market Leadership & Solutions Provider:**
    *   Premium Products: Target 35% of trade sales for FY'26.
    *   Distribution Network: Strengthening ground network, branding, and promotion for premium products.
    *   Digitalization: Embracing Industry 4.0, AI, ML, and automation. CiNOC (Cement Intelligent Network Operating Centre) deployed.
*   **Synergies and Integration:**
    *   Acquisitions: Integration of Sanghi, Penna, Asian, Tuticorin, My Home, and Orient Cement progressing well, unlocking synergies faster than expected.
    *   Adani Ecosystem: Leveraging group synergies for procurement, power, and logistics.
*   **Sustainability (ESG):**
    *   Net Zero: ACC is India's first large-scale cement company with SBTi-validated net-zero targets. Aiming for Net Zero by 2050.
    *   Renewable Energy: 300 MW solar commissioned, on track for 1,000 MW by Q2 FY'26.
    *   Community Impact: Delivered societal value for 5.7 million lives.
    *   Leadership Team Enhancements: Introduction of new leaders in Sales, Marketing & Logistics, CFO, Chief Digital Officer, and Head of Manufacturing, alongside onboarding of 1,000+ campus graduates.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Management successfully crossed the 100 MTPA milestone within 30 months, exceeding previous aggressive timelines. The roadmap towards 118 MTPA by FY'26 and 140 MTPA by FY'28 is actively being executed with various projects in advanced stages of commissioning. The integration of acquired entities (Sanghi, Penna, Orient) is reported to be progressing exceptionally well.
    *   **Cost Reduction & Efficiency:** The company is on track to achieve its cost reduction targets. A cost reduction of INR 150-175 per ton has been achieved so far, with the balance INR 300-325 expected by FY'28. Specific initiatives like fuel cost reduction (14% decrease in kiln fuel) and increased green power share (to 26%) are showing tangible results. The company confirmed that the guided benefits of >INR 100 per ton for FY'26 and INR 150 for FY'27 are broadly on track, with a commitment to potentially achieve them earlier.
    *   **Financial Strength:** The company continues to maintain its debt-free status and has significantly grown its net worth to INR 64,000 Cr. Cash and cash equivalents remain strong at INR 10,125 Cr, supporting ambitious capex plans.
    *   **Merger & Integration:** Integration of acquired assets is a key focus, with management highlighting that synergies are being unlocked faster than anticipated. The narrative emphasizes the benefits of a unified platform and leveraging group synergies.
    *   **ESG Commitment:** Continued focus on ESG is evident with ACC's SBTi validation and progress on renewable energy targets.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift:** The management's tone is highly confident and celebratory, particularly around the 100 MTPA milestone. The narrative has strongly shifted to emphasizing accelerated organic growth, the benefits of consolidation, and cost leadership driven by technology and group synergies. There is a clear focus on execution and integration of recent acquisitions. The company is positioning itself as a leader in digital transformation within the cement industry.
