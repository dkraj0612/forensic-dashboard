## Executive Overview

ACC Limited's Q2 2020 results reflect the profound impact of the COVID-19 pandemic and subsequent nationwide lockdowns. The company experienced significant declines in both Cement and Ready-Mix Concrete (RMX) volumes, directly attributable to operational disruptions. Despite these volume and revenue pressures, ACC maintained a strong EBITDA margin through rigorous cost control measures, focusing on optimizing raw material and energy expenses, alongside rationalizing freight costs. The company also highlighted substantial community support initiatives through ACC Trust and its CSR activities, alongside recognition for its sustainability efforts.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Cement Volume:** 4.8 million tons (41% YoY decline)
*   **RMX Volume:** 0.15 million cubic meters (Greater impact than Cement volumes due to urban concentration)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 33% (with a 1.6 percentage point decline YoY)
*   **EBITDA:** Rs. 26 Crores (significant drop implied due to volume impact)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cost Optimization:** Focused on raw material cost reduction through source mix optimization and improved negotiation, along with lower power & fuel costs via better fuel mix (higher proportion of low-cost coal) and thermal energy efficiency.
*   **Freight & Warehousing Rationalization:** Achieved through better primary rail freight planning, secondary freight, and warehousing cost rationalization.
*   **Fixed Cost Reduction:** Implemented stricter cost controls on discretionary expenses, negotiation-led savings, and rationalization of various cost items despite negative leverage from lower sales volumes.
*   **Community & CSR Engagement:** Significant focus on aiding communities impacted by COVID-19 through sanitization, social distancing education, food distribution, and mask production. Continued support for education, livelihood programs, and health initiatives through CSR.
*   **Sustainability Focus:** Recognized with 'Outstanding Accomplishment' at the CII-ITC Sustainability Awards 2019 for integrating sustainable business practices into corporate governance and strategy.
