## Executive Overview

Accelya Solutions reported a strong Q4 FY21 with consolidated operating income of Rs. 857.25 million, a significant increase from Rs. 741.82 million in Q4 FY21 (March 2021) and Rs. 628.34 million in Q4 FY20 (June 2020). Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) also saw substantial growth, reaching Rs. 183.59 million in Q4 FY21, up from Rs. 108.87 million in Q4 FY21 (March 2021) and Rs. 35.57 million in Q4 FY20 (June 2020). This performance, attributed to the recovery in the airline industry and effective cost rationalization measures, has enabled the company to recommend a final dividend of Rs. 17 per share, bringing the total dividend payout for FY21 to Rs. 52 per share. The company's financial year end is June 30.
