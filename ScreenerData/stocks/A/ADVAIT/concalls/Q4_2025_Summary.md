## Executive Overview

Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL) presented its Q4 FY2025 results on May 13, 2025, highlighting a year of significant growth and strategic expansion. The company reported record revenue and EBITDA for FY2025, with notable progress in its Power Transmission Solutions (PTS) and New and Renewable Energy (NRE) divisions. Key developments include the successful supply of OPGW cables and stringing services, entry into the DISCOM EPC business, and the supply of its first indigenously manufactured Emergency Restoration System (ERS). AETL is actively expanding its manufacturing capabilities, particularly in Green Hydrogen (GH2) electrolyzers and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), and is strategically positioning itself for the growing carbon markets. The company's vision remains focused on spearheading energy transition through innovative solutions and import substitution under the "Make in India" initiative.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Strategy:** Focus on import substitution under "Make in India" and "Atmanirbhar Bharat," offering EPC services for these manufactured products.
*   **Power Transmission Solutions (PTS):**
    *   **Key Products/Services:** OPGW cables, stringing tools (achieved major market share), Emergency Restoration Systems (ERS - first supply to PGCIL under Make in India), HTLS projects, ACS, and DISCOM EPC business (leveraging RDSS and SIS schemes).
    *   **Order Book Contribution:** Previously cited as ~84%, this transcript focuses on overall order book figures.
    *   **Manufacturing:** Upgraded OPGW manufacturing facility, expanding stringing tools business with new product lines (Aerial Roller, Pulleys, Turn Table, Sag Plate, Kitto Clamp, Die Sets).
*   **New and Renewable Energy (NRE):**
    *   **Green Hydrogen (GH2):**
        *   Developing 300 MW electrolyzer manufacturing plant.
        *   Completed 300 kW GH2 Plant (THDC India Ltd.) in Rishikesh (Jan 2024) with 300 kWh Alkaline Electrolyser, 70 kWh PEM Fuel Cell, and 50 kg/day GH2 production.
        *   Two new GH2 projects expected commissioning in June 2025 (300 kW with dual applications) and May 2025 (IPP Developer mode project by GSECL with Sun Tracking System).
        *   Supplying hydrogen equipment for the hydrogen ecosystem.
    *   **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS):**
        *   First BESS project awarded from GUVNL for 50MW / 100 MWh on BOO basis.
        *   Awarded a 100 MW EPC project by Adani Green Energy Six Ltd. in Khavda, including civil works, electrical installations, module mounting structure, and robotic cleaning system.
        *   Eyeing 1 GW of BESS Projects over the next 5 years.
    *   **Solar EPC:** Completed first solar project (30 MW) in Khavda (previously mentioned). Awarded 100 MW EPC project by Adani Green Energy Six Ltd. in Khavda.
*   **Manufacturing Expansion:** Setting up a new manufacturing facility for stringing tools, and a fuel cell manufacturing unit in collaboration with TECO Group, Norway & AVL Group, Austria.
*   **Carbon Markets & Sustainability Services:** AGPL Carbon Markets aims to increase managed inventory from 1.4 million credits to over 8 million credits by 2030.
