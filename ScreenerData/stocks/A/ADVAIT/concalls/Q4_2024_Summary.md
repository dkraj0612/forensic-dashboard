## Executive Overview

Advait Infratech Limited (AIL), now operating as Advait Energy Transitions Limited, presented an investor presentation on December 4, 2024, showcasing strong financial performance and ambitious growth plans across its core infrastructure and emerging green energy segments. The company reported significant year-on-year increases in consolidated revenue and EBITDA, alongside robust EBITDA margins and Return on Capital Employed (ROCE). AIL highlighted its established position in power transmission products and EPC services, while aggressively expanding its footprint in the green hydrogen ecosystem, including electrolyzer manufacturing and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). The company also emphasized its commitment to sustainability services, including carbon consultancy and REC trading, and its vision to be a world-class leader contributing to "Atmanirbhar Bharat" and India's energy transition.

*   **Consolidated EBITDA (FY24):** Grew by 145.29% YoY.

*   **Market Capitalisation:** Not explicitly stated in the transcript, but the historical context mentioned INR 82.3 Cr fundraise at INR 1388/share.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Power Transmission & Infrastructure:** Continues to manufacture and supply products like Stringing Tools, OPGW, OFC cables, ACS, ERS, and OPGW joint boxes. The company notes significant government investment in transmission infrastructure, with DPRs prepared for extensive OPGW network expansion and a government roadmap for INR 2.44 trillion investment by 2030. AIL is identified as a key player alongside Sterlite, Apar Industries, and LS Cable.
*   **Emergency Restoration Systems (ERS):** AIL offers indigenous ERS technology for transmission lines (33kV to 800kV). While global sales are approximately INR 300 Cr, Advait expects to capture INR 21 Cr of the Indian market size in 2024-25. Tenders have been floated, with expected orders in Q4-24 and Q1, Q2-25. The company aims to deploy ERS to address disruptions caused by tower failures, mitigating significant costs for utilities.
*   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem:**
    *   **Electrolyzer Manufacturing:** Advait has a temporary setup with a 50 MW/year assembly capacity and plans to establish a 300 MW facility by 2027, expanding to 1 GW by 2030. The company was allocated 100 MW capacity by SECI under Tranche-II of the PLI scheme for electrolyzer manufacturing. They are also researching and planning for a Fuel Cell (FC) manufacturing unit in India with a technology partner.
    *   **Green Hydrogen EPC Solutions:** Advait has completed a 300 kW GH2 Plant (THDC India Ltd. in Rishikesh) including a 300 kWh Alkaline Electrolyser and 70 kWh PEM Fuel Cell, producing 50 kg/day of GH2. This project is highlighted as a pioneering microgrid system. Further, a project for THDCIL and KP Group in Uttarakhand and Gujarat is scheduled for commissioning in Feb 2025, involving Hydrogen and Oxygen cascade filling at 200 bar pressure.
    *   **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS):** Advait is eyeing 1 GW of BESS projects over the next 5 years, focusing on grid stabilization, EV charging, and ancillary services.
*   **Solar EPC:** Advait secured a 30 MWp Solar EPC Project at Khavda, Gujarat, worth INR 59 Cr, expected to be commissioned by Jan 2025. The company aims to execute 100 MW (EPC and IPP) by the end of 2024-2025 and 250 MW by 2025-2026.
*   **Sustainability Services & Carbon Consultancy:** Advait provides consultancy for emission reduction, carbon neutrality, and REC trading. They have onboarded 40+ projects (200 MW equivalent) under carbon registries like Verra and Cercabono, managing a carbon credits inventory of 4 million credits and targeting 80 million+ by 2027.
