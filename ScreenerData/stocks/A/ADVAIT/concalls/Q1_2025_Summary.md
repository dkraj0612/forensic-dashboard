## Executive Overview

Advait Infratech Limited (AIL), in an investor presentation dated September 30, 2024, reinforced its strategic focus on the energy transition, highlighting advancements in its Green Hydrogen Ecosystem, Power Transmission, and Sustainability Services divisions. The company detailed significant collaborations and partnerships, including an MOU with TECO 2030 for fuel cell manufacturing and technology transfer with GuoFu for electrolyzer production. AIL emphasized its commitment to indigenous manufacturing under the "Make in India" initiative, securing a substantial 300 MW capacity allocation under the MNRE's SECI PLI for electrolyzer production through its partnership with GuoFu. The presentation also touched upon the Emergency Restoration System (ERS) market and the growing opportunities in Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) and carbon consultancy.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Power Transmission & Infrastructure:** The company continues to leverage government initiatives like the Rs 2.44 trillion investment roadmap for transmission infrastructure by 2030. Key players in this segment include Sterlite Ltd., Advait Infratech Ltd., Apar Industries, and LS Cable.
*   **Emergency Restoration Systems (ERS):** AIL is positioned to capitalize on the need for rapid restoration of power transmission lines (33kV to 800kV) following tower failures. The Indian ERS market is estimated at INR 21 Cr for 2024-25, with expected orders in Q4-24 and Q1, Q2-25. The global ERS market is approximately INR 300 Cr.
*   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem:**
    *   **Electrolyzer Manufacturing:** Through a technology transfer (ToT) partnership with GuoFu, AIL aims to manufacture and assemble electrolyzers for the Indian market. This collaboration has led to a secured 300 MW capacity allocation under MNRE's SECI PLI. The manufacturing facility in Kadi, Gujarat, has a current capacity of 300 MW/year, with plans to expand to 1000 MW/year by 2030.
    *   **Fuel Cell Manufacturing:** A significant development is the MOU with TECO 2030, a Norway-based clean-tech company. This MOU outlines AIL's intent to invest in TECO 2030 and establish a joint venture (JV) for fuel cell technology manufacturing in India and SAARC countries, with AIL holding majority shareholding. TECO 2030 is undergoing extensive fuel cell testing at AVL in Austria.
    *   **Green Hydrogen EPC Solutions:** Advait Greenergy offers comprehensive EPC solutions for green hydrogen projects, including supply and installation of skids for separation, purification, storage, transformers, rectifiers, and control systems.
*   **Sustainability Services & Carbon Consultancy:** The company offers services for emission reduction, carbon neutrality, and Renewable Energy Certificate (REC) trading. The global carbon market is valued at $2 billion and projected to reach over $50 billion by 2030. India issued over 106 million I-RECs in the first half of the year, valued at $187.5 million for 2024.
