## Executive Overview

Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL) reported its Q1 FY2026 results on August 5, 2025, showcasing significant year-over-year (YoY) growth across both standalone and consolidated segments. The company highlighted continued momentum in its Power Transmission Solutions (PTS) division, with notable project completions and new orders, particularly in the DISCOM EPC business. The New and Renewable Energy (NRE) division is showing strong potential, with advancements in Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and Green Hydrogen (GH2) initiatives. AETL received recognition for its sustainability practices, underscoring its commitment to energy transition and import substitution under the "Make in India" initiative.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Standalone Revenue (Q1 FY2026):** INR 75.99 crore (30% YoY growth)

*   **Consolidated PAT (Q1 FY2026):** Not explicitly stated, but implied significant growth.

    *   PTS Division: 66% of the order book.
    *   NRE Division: 34% of the order book.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Strategy:** Focus on energy transition through innovative solutions in Power Transmission and Renewable Energy. Emphasis on import substitution and EPC solutions, leveraging global technology partnerships for manufacturing in India.
*   **Power Transmission Solutions (PTS):**
    *   **Key Achievements:** Completed 300Kms of OPGW live line stringing for Punjab and Haryana; successfully completed GETCO HTLS conductor stringing 3 months ahead of schedule; achieved major market share in India for stringing tools.
    *   **DISCOM EPC Business:** Completed Rs. 44 Cr of DISCOM EPC projects (leveraging RDSS) and won new orders of Rs. 167 Cr under the SIS scheme in Gujarat.
    *   **Product Expansion:** Enhanced manufacturing capacity for stringing tools and ERS.
*   **New and Renewable Energy (NRE):**
    *   **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS):** Secured first BESS project from GUVNL (50MW / 100 MWh) on BOO basis; awarded a 100 MW EPC project by Adani Green Energy Six Ltd. in Khavda (includes civil works, electrical installations, module mounting structure, and robotic cleaning system).
    *   **Green Hydrogen (GH2):** Developing a greenfield manufacturing facility with 300 MW capacity for indigenous electrolysers. Two GH2 projects are expected commissioning in June 2025 (300 kW, dual applications) and May 2025 (IPP Developer mode by GSECL with Sun Tracking System). Completed a 300 kW GH2 Plant for THDC India Ltd. (Jan 2024).
    *   **Fuel Cell Manufacturing:** Setting up an assembly line for advanced fuel cell technology in collaboration with TECO Group and AVL Group.
    *   **Solar EPC:** Awarded a 100 MW EPC project by Adani Green Energy Six Ltd. in Khavda.
*   **Carbon Markets:** AGPL Carbon Markets aims to increase managed inventory from 1.4 million credits to over 8 million credits by 2030. Recognized for Excellence in Sustainability practices at CNBC - TV18 SME Champion Awards.
