## Executive Overview

Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL) held its Q3 FY2026 post-results conference call on February 12, 2026. The company reported strong performance for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025, marked by a significant milestone: listing on the NSE Main Board on January 20, 2026, alongside its existing BSE listing. The order book has crossed the INR 1,000 crore mark, growing 132% YoY, with the Power Transmission Solutions (PTS) division contributing 84% and the New and Renewable Energy (NRE) division contributing 16%. Management projects continued robust growth, aiming for 40-45% revenue growth in FY2026. A strategic move includes establishing a multi-integrated Giga-factory complex for the NRE division and PTS capacity expansion near Ahmedabad, targeted for commercialization by mid-2028.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Strategy:** Focus on identifying and manufacturing import-substituted products under "Make in India" and "Atmanirbhar Bharat" initiatives. Provides EPC services for these products.
*   **Power Transmission Solutions (PTS):** Key growth drivers include DISCOM EPC business (46% revenue contribution in 9MFY26), ERS, stringing tools, ACS, and OPGW. Aims for high indigenization (>95%) in the ERS segment. Planning assembly operations for glass insulators and specialized composite post insulators.
*   **New and Renewable Energy (NRE):** Diversifying into Solar EPC, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) manufacturing and EPC, and Green Hydrogen (GH2) EPC.
    *   **Green Hydrogen:** Developing 30 MW electrolyzer assembly and manufacturing unit by March 2026. Plans for a 300 MW manufacturing plant. Completed first GH2 projects (1MW and 300kW). Technology transfer for fuel cells secured with a European company.
    *   **Solar EPC:** Successfully commissioned first solar project in Khavda (30 MW). Executing a block of 12.5 MW in the 100 MW Adani project. Anticipating good opportunities in Q4.
    *   **BESS:** Targeting 2.5 GWh manufacturing capacity by Q3 FY2027. Developing EMS in-house and collaborating with channel partners for PCS.
*   **Manufacturing Expansion:** Establishing a multi-integrated Giga-factory complex for NRE division and PTS capacity expansion near Ahmedabad, targeting mid-2028 for full commercialization.
*   **Carbon Markets & Sustainability Services:** Focus remains on building carbon credit inventory and providing consultancy services for ESG policies.
