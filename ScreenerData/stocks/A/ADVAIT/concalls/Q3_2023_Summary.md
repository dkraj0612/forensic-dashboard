## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Infrastructure Business:** Continues to focus on power transmission, substation, and telecommunication infrastructure, manufacturing and supplying stringing tools, OPGW, OFC cables, ACS, ERS, and OPGW joint boxes.
*   **Green Energy Transition:**
    *   **Green Hydrogen:** Progressing with Green Hydrogen production technologies and end-to-end services. The company has commenced site preparation and installation for a Green Hydrogen-based micro-grid project from THDC in Uttarakhand.
    *   **Fuel Cells & Electrolyzers:** Expanding manufacturing facilities for the assembly and supply of Fuel Cells and Electrolyzers.
*   **Climate Services:** Active in the Carbon Credit market, offering consultancy services and end-to-end carbon neutrality and net-zero solutions, as well as sustainability and decarbonisation consultancy.
*   **Manufacturing & Infrastructure Enhancements:**
    *   Continuing expansion of manufacturing facilities for ERS, Fuel cells, and Electrolyzers.
    *   Establishing an in-house testing station for the Emergency Restoration System.
    *   Establishing an in-house Training Centre for live line installation.
*   **Market Expansion:**
    *   Initiated manufacturing and supply of stranded ACS in the Middle East market.
    *   Opened a product line for the manufacturing of Steel tube type OPGW.
*   **Technology Partnerships:** Continues to leverage technology partnerships, including with "HuaDe" and "Guolu" (as per historical context).
*   **Corporate Actions:** Successful migration to the BSE Mainboard (as per historical context) continues to be a driver for enhanced shareholder value.
