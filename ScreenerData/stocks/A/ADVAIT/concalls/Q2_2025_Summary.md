## Executive Overview

Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL) held its post-results conference call on November 14, 2025, to discuss its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2025 (Q2 FY2026). The company reported robust year-on-year growth, driven primarily by its Power Transmission Systems (PTS) division, which contributed 76% to the order book, and its New and Renewable Energy (NRE) division, contributing 24%. AETL's order book surpassed INR 1,000 crores, a significant 177% year-on-year increase. The company highlighted successful order wins, including a substantial ERS order from Power Grid and a large EPC order from DGVCL. Operational highlights included challenging stringing of OPGW cables in Leh and Ladakh and the execution of its first High-Tension Low-Sag conductor project. AETL is also strategically investing in expanding its manufacturing capabilities, including a new integrated manufacturing facility near Ahmedabad, with planned completion of expansion for PTS by Q3 FY2027 and electrolyzer manufacturing (10 MW by January 2026, 300 MW by Q3-Q4 2026). The company reiterated its commitment to the Green Hydrogen ecosystem and its ongoing involvement in the carbon credit business.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Power Transmission Systems (PTS):** Continues to be a core business, with strong order book growth driven by government investments. Key areas include manufacturing of ACS and OPGW cables (securing approvals with Indian utilities and first Level-N project in Malaysia), power transmission stringing tools (record sales and supplies to over five countries), Emergency Restoration Systems (ERS) (record order booking and new manufacturing facility being set up), and EPC projects. Expansion in stringing tools capacity is planned.
*   **New and Renewable Energy (NRE):** A critical part of India's energy roadmap. AETL is committed to the Green Hydrogen ecosystem, having completed India's first green hydrogen EPC project and expecting to deliver another 1 MW project. Plans include a 300 MW electrolyzer manufacturing plant and BESS system projects. First 10 MW of electrolyzer capacity is expected by January 2026, with 300 MW planned by Q3-Q4 2026.
*   **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS):** First 100 MWh project expected by June 2026. The company is bidding for more projects but remains capital and margin-sensitive. Plans to set up a manufacturing facility for BESS DC blocks by 2026.
*   **Solar EPC:** Offering turnkey solutions and actively seeking large EPC orders for solar projects.
*   **Overseas Expansion:** Malaysia order (INR 12-15 crores for OPGW LiveLine installation) is a stepping stone, with expressions of interest in Ethiopia and Kazakhstan for OPGW LiveLine business. Existing footprint in overseas markets through stringing tool supplies.
*   **Sustainability Services:** Generating sustained revenue from the carbon credit business.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Previous Forward-Looking Promises:**

*   **ERS Orders:** The transcript confirms securing a significant ERS order for 765 KV, 400 KV from Power Grid, amounting to INR 90 crores, which is a substantial achievement in this segment. The mention of a new ERS manufacturing facility being set up also aligns with previous strategic expansions.
*   **Electrolyzer Manufacturing Capacity:** The 300 MW manufacturing capacity plan is actively being pursued. The first 10 MW of electrolyzer capacity is targeted for January 2026, with the full 300 MW capacity planned for completion by Q3-Q4 2026. This reinforces the previous commitment. The technology transfer with GuoFu is implicitly continued through the investment in Advait Greenergy Private Limited (AGPL) for building up electrolyzer and fuel cell technologies.
*   **SECI PLI Allocation:** While not directly mentioned, the company states it is "perfectly on time" to meet PLI scheme timelines for electrolyzers, indicating continued alignment. Benefits are expected from 2027 to 2031.
*   **Green Hydrogen Project (THDC & KP Group):** The company has completed India's first green hydrogen EPC project and expects to deliver another 1 MW project in Bharuch in the current quarter (Q2 FY2026). This is an update on their Green Hydrogen EPC capabilities.
*   **Carbon Market Achievements:** The company is generating "sustained revenue" from its carbon credit business, which is an update on the broader market opportunity focus. Specific credit management figures were not reiterated in this transcript.
*   **Fuel Cell Manufacturing:** The company has "entered an agreement that for our Asia-Pacific region, we will be only holding those technologies and which is patented" related to fuel cells, indicating progress in securing intellectual property for this segment, although the previous MOU with TECO 2030 was not explicitly referenced.
*   **BESS Projects:** Revenue from BESS is expected to commence in March 2026 (INR 50-60 crores) with the entire order to be completed by June 2026. The first 100 MWh project is expected to be completed by June 2026.
