## Executive Overview

Advait Infratech's Q4 FY2023 earnings release indicates a strategic diversification into green energy and climate services, complementing its established infrastructure business. The company is advancing its Green Hydrogen projects, including a micro-grid project from THDC, and is actively engaging in the Carbon Credit market. Furthermore, Advait is expanding its manufacturing capabilities for critical components like Emergency Restoration Systems (ERS) and Aluminium Clad Steel Wire (ACS), with a specific focus on increasing ACS production capacity and entering the Middle East market. The company also highlights its entry into the solar EPC business with targeted capacity and revenue projections.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Infrastructure Business:** Continues to focus on power transmission, substation, and telecommunication infrastructure, manufacturing and supplying stringing tools, OPGW, OFC cables, ACS, ERS, and OPGW joint boxes.
*   **Green Energy Transition:**
    *   **Green Hydrogen:** Progressing with Green Hydrogen production technologies and end-to-end services. Received an order for a Green Hydrogen-based micro-grid project from THDC in Uttarakhand, India, expected to generate over INR 80 million in revenue and projected for completion by the end of 2023.
    *   **Solar EPC:** Strategically positioning as a mid-size solar EPC company targeting Independent Power Producers (IPPs) and small-scale industrial clients. Targets a capacity of 4-5 MW per year, anticipating INR 100-120 million in annual revenue from this segment.
*   **Climate Services:** Foraying into the Carbon Credit Market, offering consultancy services, end-to-end carbon neutrality, net-zero solutions, and cleantech tools.
*   **Manufacturing & Infrastructure Enhancements:**
    *   Added one more Wire Drawing Line to the ACS manufacturing facility, increasing capacity to approximately 4,000 tons per annum. Further augmentation of ACS production capacity is planned for FY2023-2024.
    *   Development of an indigenous and cost-effective Emergency Restoration System (ERS) in collaboration with CSIR-SERC, Chennai, suitable for transmission lines from 33kV to 800kV. Advait is now qualified with Power Grid for ERS.
*   **Market Expansion:**
    *   Initiated manufacturing and supply of stranded ACS in the Middle East market. (Note: This was a stated intention in previous historical context and is confirmed as commenced).
*   **Corporate Actions:** Incorporated Advait Greenergy Private Limited, a fully owned subsidiary. Corporate Guarantee exposure for TG Advait India Private Limited has been removed.
