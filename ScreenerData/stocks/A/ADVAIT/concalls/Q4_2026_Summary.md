## Advait Energy Transitions Limited - Q4 FY2026 Earnings Call Summary

### Executive Overview

Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL) reported strong performance for Q4 FY2026 and the full fiscal year ended March 31, 2026. The company demonstrated significant year-over-year growth in both revenue and profitability, driven by its Power Transmission Solutions (PTS) and New and Renewable Energy (NRE) segments. AETL's order book reached an all-time high, indicating robust future growth prospects. Strategic investments in manufacturing capabilities, particularly for BESS and electrolyzers, alongside the establishment of dedicated subsidiaries, underscore the company's commitment to India's energy transition and its Vision 2030.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Focus:** Energy transition, leveraging India's drive for energy independence and sustainability. Emphasis on import substitution and indigenous manufacturing.
*   **Power Transmission Solutions (PTS):**
    *   Secured INR 70 crores for ERS supply to MNRE.
    *   Won first EPC order in Uttarakhand (INR 33 crores) and an EPC order from GETCO (INR 27 crores).
    *   Secured INR 22 crores order for stringing tools and capital tools.
    *   Received NABL laboratory certification and approval from three new utility boards for OPGW products.
    *   Plan to continue 40-50% growth for the next 5 years for the PTS division.
*   **New and Renewable Energy (NRE):**
    *   Commissioned 75 MW of Adani's solar project at Khavda.
    *   Approximately 67.5 MW project at Khavda expected completion in Q1 FY27.
    *   Advait Green Energy Private Limited established for Green Hydrogen and Solar EPC.
    *   Advait Battery Ecosystems Private Limited established for BESS manufacturing and C&I solutions; potential expansion into battery recycling.
    *   Akara established for carbon solutions delivery and trading.
    *   Aura (Advait Unified Resource) established for asset-based business.
*   **Manufacturing Expansion:**
    *   Developing a multi-integrated manufacturing facility near Dholera, Phase 1 operational by Q4 FY27.
    *   Phase 1 for electrolyzer manufacturing (100 MW) and BESS manufacturing (2.5 GWh).
*   **Green Hydrogen (GH2) & Fuel Cells:**
    *   Believes the GH2 market will see exponential demand in the next 3-5 years.
    *   Developing manufacturing capabilities for electrolyzers and advanced fuel cells (MoU with AVL and TECO for Ahmedabad plant).
    *   Fuel cell business expected to contribute to revenue from FY28-FY29 onwards, with potential for 500 MW market by FY28-FY29.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Previous Forward-Looking Promises (from Q1 FY2026 context/Q4 FY2025 transcript):**

*   **Revenue Growth Outlook (40-45% for FY2026):** The company achieved an 80% YoY revenue growth in FY2026 (INR 714.52 crores vs. INR 397.66 crores in FY25), significantly exceeding its previous guidance and demonstrating strong execution.
*   **NRE Division Contribution Growth:** The NRE division's contribution to the order book has increased to 36% (from 34% in Q1 FY2026 context), aligning with management's strategy for NRE to surpass PTS in the future.
*   **Electrolyzer Manufacturing Capacity (300 MW target):** Development of a greenfield manufacturing facility with 100 MW electrolyzer capacity (Phase 1) is underway near Dholera, progressing towards the initial target.
*   **BESS Projects (Eyeing 1 GW over 5 years):** While no new BESS projects were explicitly won in this quarter, the establishment of Advait Battery Ecosystems and plans for BESS manufacturing (2.5 GWh) indicate continued strategic focus.

**New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance:**

*   **Revenue Growth Outlook:** Management expressed confidence in continuing "sustained revenue growth, which may be 40% plus" for FY2027, supported by a strong order book and tender pipeline. They also acknowledged their previous year's 80% growth and suggested the 40% guidance might be conservative.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** Management aims for "improving the margins by one point for the next year" (FY2027), targeting approximately 12-13%.
*   **Order Book Target (FY2027):** Targeting an order book of INR 1,600 to INR 1,650 crores by the end of FY2027.
*   **NRE vs. PTS Order Book Mix:** Expecting the NRE share to increase to 35% in FY2027 (from ~25-27% currently).
*   **Electrolyzer Manufacturing Margins:** Margins are expected to be 5-10% initially, potentially increasing to 20% in subsequent years (FY2028 onwards) once the supply chain matures.
*   **Fuel Cell Business Revenue:** Expects revenue contribution from FY2028-FY2029, with potential market size of 500 MW by FY2028-FY2029.
*   **BESS Manufacturing Facility:** The 2.5 GWh plant will be operational by September-October FY2027, with INR 100-200 crores of business expected in the first two months of operation. Full utilization leading to INR 1,000 crores+ revenue from this plant is expected from FY2028 onwards.
*   **Capex:**
    *   FY2026 Capex (PTS division): Approx. INR 100 crores (from internal funds).
    *   FY2027 Capex (excluding IPP): INR 137 crores.
    *   FY2027 Capex (IPP and subsidiary investments for BESS, electrolyzers): INR 75 crores.
    *   Total Capex for FY2027: INR 300-350 crores.
*   **Solar EPC:** Capacity to handle 100-200 MW per year. Moving towards pure-play solar EPC and expect to secure orders including modules within the current year.
*   **Stringing Tools Business:** Management aims to double revenue through new product portfolios.
*   **Carbon Markets:** Target by 2030: 8+ million credits under management.
*   **Debt Funding for Operations:** Management indicated that working capital investment has impacted current cash flow, and future realization will improve operational cash flow. Funding for daily operations will be a mix of debt and equity.
*   **UHV Transmission System:** The company will benefit from the upcoming ultra-high voltage transmission system by providing allied products such as high ampacity conductors, OPGW, stringing tools, and ERS, but will not be an EPC contractor for the transmission lines themselves.
