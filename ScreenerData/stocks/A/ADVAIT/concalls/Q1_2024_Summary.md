## Executive Overview

Advait Infratech Limited (AIL) presented an investor update on September 30, 2024, highlighting significant progress and new strategic initiatives across its core infrastructure and emerging energy transition businesses. The company is solidifying its position in power transmission infrastructure while aggressively expanding into the green energy sector, particularly in Green Hydrogen and Fuel Cell technologies, supported by strategic partnerships and manufacturing capabilities. AIL is also reinforcing its commitment to sustainability services and has provided updated guidance for its various business units for FY2023-24.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Power Transmission & Infrastructure:** Continues to be a core business, offering solutions up to 400KV transmission lines. AIL is positioned as a key player in the India Electric Wire and Cable Market, with a focus on High Temperature Low Sag (HTLS) conductors to enhance efficiency and capacity.
*   **Energy Transition & New & Renewable Energy:**
    *   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem:** Advait Greenergy Private Limited (AGPL) and Advait Greenergy (A&G) are focused on electrolyzer assembly and manufacturing, fuel cell supply, and carbon consultancy. The Kadi, Gujarat facility has a current capacity of 300 MW/year, with plans to expand to 1000 MW/year by 2030. AGPL has secured 300 MW capacity under the MNRE’s SECI PLI for Electrolyser Production through a technology transfer (ToT) with GuoFuHee.
    *   **Fuel Cell Technology:** A significant development is the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with TECO 2030 (Norway) for potential joint venture (JV) manufacturing of fuel cells in India and SAARC countries, with AIL intending to hold majority shareholding. This JV will be authorized to develop, manufacture, and commercialize TECO 2030 Fuel Cell Technology in these markets.
    *   **Electrolyzer Manufacturing:** AIL, through collaboration with GuoFuHee, is manufacturing and/or assembling electrolyzers and hydrogen pressure vessels for the Indian market. The NRE division's electrolyzer systems are designed for compact footprint and modular configurations.
*   **Sustainability Services:**
    *   **Carbon Consultancy:** Offering strategies for carbon emission reduction, achieving carbon neutrality, and end-to-end support for carbon neutrality and net-zero solutions.
    *   **Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs):** Providing consultancy for REC trading, with a focus on International RECs (I-RECs).
*   **Emergency Restoration Systems (ERS):** AIL is offering indigenous ERS technology for transmission lines from 33kV to 800kV, positioning itself with a 7% expected share of the Indian ERS market projected at INR 21 Cr for 2024-25. The company notes that utilities are planning procurements, with expected orders in Q4-24 and Q1, Q2-25.
*   **Manufacturing & Capacity Expansion:**
    *   ACS manufacturing capacity is being augmented.
    *   ERS technology is developed indigenously and suitable for various voltage levels.
