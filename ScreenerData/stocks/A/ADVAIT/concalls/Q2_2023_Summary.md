## Executive Overview

Advait Infratech reported its Q2 FY2024 results, highlighting a significant transformation driven by its expansion into green energy and climate services. The company has successfully migrated to the BSE Mainboard, a move aimed at enhancing shareholder value. Key strategic initiatives include the establishment of India's first in-house Emergency Restoration System (ERS) manufacturing unit, diversification into Green Hydrogen and Carbon Credit solutions through its subsidiary Advait Greenergy Private Limited (AGPL), and international market expansion with the manufacturing and supply of stranded ACS in the Middle East. The company is also investing in its manufacturing facilities for Fuel Cells and Electrolyzers, and plans to establish an in-house training center for live line installation.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Infrastructure Business:** Continued focus on power transmission, substation, and telecommunication infrastructure. Manufacturing and supply of stringing tools, OPGW, OFC cables, ACS, ERS, and OPGW joint boxes.
*   **Green Energy Transition:**
    *   **Green Hydrogen:** Ventured into Green Hydrogen production technologies and end-to-end services. Commenced site preparation and installation of a Green Hydrogen-based micro-grid project from THDC in Uttarakhand.
    *   **Fuel Cells & Electrolyzers:** Expanding manufacturing facilities for assembly and supply of Fuel Cells and Electrolyzers for Hydrogen generation.
*   **Climate Services:**
    *   **Carbon Credits:** Forayed into the Carbon Credit market offering consultancy services and end-to-end carbon neutrality and net-zero solutions.
    *   **Sustainability & Decarbonisation:** Providing sustainability consultancy and decarbonisation consultancy services.
*   **Manufacturing & Infrastructure Enhancements:**
    *   Established India's first in-house ERS manufacturing unit with in-house testing capabilities per IEEE 1070.
    *   Expanding present manufacturing facility for ERS, Fuel cells, and Electrolyzers.
    *   Establishing an in-house testing station for ERS.
    *   Establishing an in-house Training Centre for live line installation.
*   **Market Expansion:**
    *   Beginning manufacturing and supplying stranded ACS in the Middle East market.
    *   Opening a product line for manufacturing of Steel tube type OPGW.
*   **Corporate Action:** Successful migration from BSE SME Platform to BSE Mainboard, enhancing shareholder value.
*   **Technology Partnerships:** Entered into technology partnerships with "HuaDe" and "Guolu".
