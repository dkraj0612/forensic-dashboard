## Executive Overview

Aarvi Encon, a long-standing Indian technical staffing and project management solutions provider with 36 years of operation, continues to focus on strategic growth and market expansion. The company maintains a robust deployed workforce exceeding 4,500 professionals and serves a diverse, blue-chip client base including Larsen & Toubro and Reliance Industries. Aarvi highlights its deep sector expertise, strong in-house talent acquisition capabilities (over 800,000 resumes), and a focus on increasing client wallet share, acquiring new clients, and expanding its international footprint. The Operations & Maintenance (O&M) segment remains a significant contributor, accounting for 26% of business and generating higher margins. The company was recognized with the "Times Exemplary Leaders Award 2023" for Excellence in Human Capital Management.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

Aarvi Encon's strategy remains focused on leveraging its market leadership and expanding its service portfolio. Key strategic drivers include:

*   **Client Wallet Share Expansion:** Increasing business from existing clients through additional manpower supply and identifying cross/up-selling opportunities.
*   **New Client Acquisition:** Securing new clients within established industry verticals.
*   **International Market Penetration:** A dedicated international sales team is focused on global manpower deputation to strengthen international presence.
*   **Talent Acquisition & Retention:** Continuous efforts to attract and retain professionals, leveraging a substantial in-house database (over 800,000 resumes) and a robust referral program.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Maintaining quality standards, compliance, and enhancing domain knowledge for operational efficiency.
*   **Digitalization:** Implementing digitalization for contactless operations management.
*   **Vertical Diversification:** Expanding into new industry verticals such as Ports, Defence, and Healthcare.
*   **Service Offering Diversification:** Broadening expertise and service offerings across the engineering value chain.
*   **O&M Segment Growth:** Positioned as a leading O&M provider in India for Solar Energy, Pipeline & Oil & Gas. O&M activities currently account for 26% of business and generate higher margins.
