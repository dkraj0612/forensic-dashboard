## Business Strategy & Drivers

Aarvi Encon's strategic direction is geared towards sustained growth and market leadership:

*   **Client Engagement:** Increasing wallet share from existing clients by supplying additional manpower and identifying cross-selling opportunities.
*   **Client Acquisition:** Providing manpower services to new clients in existing verticals.
*   **International Expansion:** A dedicated international sales team is focusing on international deputation to develop a stronger global presence.
*   **Talent Management:** Continuous efforts to improve talent acquisition to attract and retain skilled professionals, leveraging a database of over 800,000 resumes and a strong referral program.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Enhancing domain knowledge and achieving operational excellence to meet quality standards and compliance.
*   **Digitalization:** Implementing digitalization across various processes to enable contactless operations management.
*   **Sector Diversification:** Venturing into new industry verticals such as Ports, Defence, and Healthcare, and diversifying expertise across the engineering value chain.
*   **O&M Segment Growth:** 26% of current business comes from Operations and Maintenance (O&M) activities, which are noted to garner higher margins.
