## Executive Overview

Aarvi Encon, a prominent Indian technical staffing and project management solutions provider, presented its Q3 FY25 investor update, highlighting a 31% year-over-year (YoY) sales increase driven by new client additions. While revenue remained consistent quarter-over-quarter, the company experienced a decline in EBITDA margins, attributing it to a non-cash expense from ESOP issuance and the impact of low-margin contracts currently in progress. Aarvi continues to leverage its extensive resume database and referral program, maintaining a deployed workforce of over 4,500 professionals across diverse sectors. The company has signed 10 new clients during the period.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **EBITDA (INR Mn):** INR 24 Mn (9M-FY25)
*   **EBITDA Margin (%):** 1.82% (9M-FY25)
*   **Net Profit Margin (%):** 1.94% (9M-FY25)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

Aarvi Encon's strategic focus remains on expanding its service offerings and market reach:

*   **Client Acquisition:** Secured 10 new clients during the period, contributing to YoY revenue growth.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved a 31% YoY increase in sales.
*   **Service Diversification:** Continues to offer services across the project lifecycle, from conceptualization to Operations & Maintenance (O&M).
*   **Talent Management:** Maintains a large in-house resume database (>800,000) and a strong referral program for talent acquisition.
*   **Market Targeting:** Focuses on sectors including oil & gas, power, LNG, infrastructure, ports & terminals, and IT, among others.
*   **Shutdown Mobilization:** Possesses the capability to mobilize large workforces for short-to-medium term shutdown assignments.
