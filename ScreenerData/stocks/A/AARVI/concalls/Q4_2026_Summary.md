## Executive Overview

Aarvi Encon, a seasoned Indian technical staffing and project management firm, reported its Q4 FY26 results, showcasing robust international growth and strategic new client acquisitions. The company continues to leverage its substantial workforce of over 5,000 professionals and an extensive resume database of over 800,000. Q4 FY26 was marked by a significant 73% YoY increase in international sales and 179% YoY growth in international PAT. Domestically, Aarvi secured over INR 200 crore in new orders and added more than 10 new clients across diversified sectors, maintaining a strong client retention rate of 97%. Operational highlights include the establishment of new engineering offices in Chennai and Malaysia, expansion of manpower for O&M activities in solar projects, and securing major manpower and engineering contracts in UAE and Indonesia. The company continues to focus on increasing the share of higher-margin Operation & Maintenance services.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

Aarvi Encon's strategy for Q4 FY26 continued to focus on:

*   **International Expansion:** Strong momentum in international business with significant YoY growth in sales and PAT. Expansion efforts include new offices in Malaysia and strengthening Qatar operations, alongside securing contracts in UAE and Indonesia.
*   **Client Acquisition & Retention:** Addition of over 10 new clients in Q4 across diversified sectors and maintaining a strong client retention rate of 97%.
*   **Domestic Order Acquisition:** Securing major domestic orders aggregating over INR 200 crore, enhancing revenue visibility.
*   **Sector Diversification & Service Enhancement:** Continued deployment for O&M activities in solar module and cell manufacturing. Focus on increasing the share of higher-margin O&M services in the business mix.
*   **Operational Enhancement:** Establishment of a new engineering office in Chennai to bolster engineering and project execution capabilities.
*   **Talent & Database Leverage:** Continues to rely on its extensive resume database (>800,000) and strong referral program for talent acquisition.
*   **Manpower Mobilization:** Proven capability to mobilize large manpower for short-term shutdown assignments.
