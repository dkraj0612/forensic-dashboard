## Executive Overview

Aarvi Encon, an established Indian technical staffing and project management solutions provider with 36 years of experience, is strategically navigating its growth path. The company maintains a substantial deployed workforce exceeding 4,500 professionals, serving a distinguished client roster that includes Larsen & Toubro and Reliance Industries. Aarvi's core strengths lie in its deep sector expertise, robust in-house talent acquisition capabilities (over 800,000 resumes), and a clear focus on increasing client wallet share, acquiring new clients, and expanding its international presence. The Operations & Maintenance (O&M) segment continues to be a key contributor, representing 26% of the business and delivering higher margins. The company's commitment to talent management was recognized with the "Times Exemplary Leaders Award 2023" for Excellence in Human Capital Management.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

Aarvi Encon's strategy is centered on reinforcing its market leadership and diversifying its service offerings. Key strategic drivers include:

*   **Client Wallet Share Expansion:** Increasing business from existing clients through additional manpower supply and identifying cross-selling/up-selling opportunities.
*   **New Client Acquisition:** Securing new clients within established industry verticals.
*   **International Market Penetration:** Establishing operations in Saudi Arabia and expanding its presence in Indonesia, with a focus on local operations and recruitment. The company has exited its UK operations due to cash flow issues.
*   **Talent Acquisition & Retention:** Leveraging its large in-house database (over 800,000 resumes) and a strong referral program to attract and retain talent. Recognition as "Great Place to Work" reinforces this focus.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Maintaining quality standards, compliance, and enhancing domain knowledge for efficiency.
*   **Digitalization:** Implementing digitalization for contactless operations management.
*   **Vertical Diversification:** Targeting new industry verticals such as Ports, Defence, and Healthcare.
*   **Service Offering Diversification:** Broadening expertise and service offerings across the engineering value chain.
*   **O&M Segment Growth:** Strengthening its position in the O&M segment with new contracts and a strategic focus on higher-margin O&M and renewable project orders.
