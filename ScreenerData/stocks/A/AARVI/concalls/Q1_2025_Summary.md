## Executive Overview

Aarvi Encon, a prominent Indian technical staffing and project management solutions provider with over three decades of experience, continues to navigate its growth trajectory, emphasizing its robust deployed workforce exceeding 4,500 professionals. The company serves a distinguished client roster, including major players like Larsen & Toubro and Reliance Industries. Aarvi's operational strengths are rooted in its extensive in-house talent acquisition capabilities, boasting a database of over 800,000 resumes, and a strategic focus on expanding client wallet share, acquiring new clients, and enhancing its international presence. The O&M segment remains a key contributor, with a significant 7-year maintenance contract highlighted. The company has also expanded its workforce, with over 5,000 engineers/technical personnel on payroll.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

Aarvi Encon's strategy remains focused on solidifying its market leadership and diversifying its service offerings. Key strategic drivers include:

*   **Client Acquisition & Wallet Share Growth:** Continuing to increase business from existing clients by supplying additional manpower and identifying cross-selling and up-selling opportunities. Providing manpower services to new clients in existing verticals is also a focus.
*   **International Expansion:** Developing a stronger international presence through a dedicated international sales team focusing on manpower deputation abroad. Specific past mentions of UAE and UK operations are present.
*   **Talent Management:** Maintaining a consistent effort to improve talent acquisition to attract and retain skilled professionals, leveraging its extensive resume database and referral program.
*   **O&M Segment Growth:** Aarvi is positioned as a leading O&M company in India for Solar Energy, Pipeline, and Oil & Gas industries, with 26% of its business currently coming from O&M activities, which garners higher margins.
*   **Diversification & New Verticals:** Venturing into new industry verticals such as Ports, Defence, and Healthcare, and diversifying expertise and service offerings across the engineering value chain.
*   **Operational Excellence & Digitalization:** Enhancing domain knowledge, achieving operational excellence, and implementing digitalization of various processes for contactless operations management.
