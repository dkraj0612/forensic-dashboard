## Executive Overview

Aarvi Encon, a prominent Indian technical staffing and project management solutions provider with over 37 years of experience, delivered a Q3 FY26 performance marked by robust YoY growth in sales and PAT. The company continues to leverage its substantial workforce of over 4,500 professionals and an extensive resume database of over 800,000. Key sectors remain oil & gas and power, with notable expansion into new verticals such as Solar PV Module/Cell Manufacturing and Ports. Q3 FY26 saw significant YoY growth in Sales (+27.2%) and PAT (+66.7%). Operationally, the company signed 13 new clients and received 33 new orders, reinforcing its business development efforts. Expansion of Chennai operations by relocating to a larger office facility was a key operational highlight to support growing teams and enhance client support.
