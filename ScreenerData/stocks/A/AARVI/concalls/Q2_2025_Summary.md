## Business Strategy & Drivers

Aarvi Encon's strategic direction is centered around sustainable growth and market expansion:

*   **Client Penetration:** Increasing wallet share from existing clients through additional manpower supply and cross-selling/up-selling opportunities.
*   **New Client Acquisition:** Providing manpower services to new clients within existing verticals.
*   **International Expansion:** Dedicated international sales team focused on expanding global deputation of manpower.
*   **Talent Management:** Continuous efforts to enhance talent acquisition and retention of professionals.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Improving efficiencies via enhanced domain knowledge and achieving operational excellence.
*   **Digitalization:** Implementing digitalization for contactless operations management.
*   **Service Diversification:** Venturing into new industry verticals such as Ports, Defence, and Healthcare.
*   **Service Offering Expansion:** Diversifying expertise and service offerings across the engineering value chain.
*   **O&M Focus:** Leading O&M services for Solar Energy, Pipeline & Oil & Gas, with 26% of business currently from this higher-margin segment.
