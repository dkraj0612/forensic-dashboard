## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported its Q3 FY20 unaudited financial results, demonstrating robust consolidated profit growth driven by a diversified business model. The company highlighted a 17% year-on-year increase in consolidated PAT for Q3 FY20 and a 27% increase year-to-date. ABCL continues to emphasize value-accretive growth across its segments, with particular strength in its lending, asset management, and insurance businesses. Strategic initiatives include expanding its branch network, leveraging technology for enhanced customer and distributor experience, and maintaining a disciplined approach to risk management, evidenced by a well-diversified borrowing mix and comfortable capital adequacy.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy continues to focus on profitable and value-accretive growth across its diversified businesses. Key strategic thrusts include:

*   **Lending Business Optimization:** Shifting loan book composition towards higher-margin retail and SME segments, with strong retail momentum (up 30% y-o-y) and SME secured term loans/WCDL (up 17% y-o-y). Focus on reducing ticket sizes and building granularity, with a significant emphasis on secured lending and cash-flow based underwriting. Plans to expand branch network by 150-200 branches over 18-24 months.
*   **Asset Management Expansion:** Increasing equity AAUM mix to 37%, growing retail market share, and expanding penetration in B-30 cities. Leveraging digital channels and an IFA network for sourcing. Focus on enhancing profitability through asset mix and operational efficiencies.
*   **Life Insurance Value Enhancement:** Driving growth in individual FYP, improving Net VNB margins, and increasing the protection mix (YTD at 11%). Continuing to focus on quality of business through improved persistency and reduced surrender ratios.
*   **Health Insurance Scale-up & Break-even Path:** Accelerating GWP growth, especially in the retail segment, and managing the combined ratio. The focus remains on achieving break-even by FY21-22.
*   **Technology & Digital Integration:** Launching new investor portals, micro ticket size products, and simplifying SIP registration through digital mandates. Enhancing distributor experience and onboarding digital ecosystem partners.
*   **Capital Management & Risk Mitigation:** Broadening the investor profile, diversifying the borrowing mix, and maintaining comfortable capital adequacy ratios (CRAR at 19.7% for NBFC and 17.1% for HFC). Optimizing ALM for liquidity and cost.
