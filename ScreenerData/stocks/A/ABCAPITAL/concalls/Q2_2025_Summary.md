## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported a strong Q2 FY25, demonstrating resilience and growth driven by its diversified business model and focus on digital transformation. The company saw significant year-on-year growth in consolidated revenue and profit after tax. Key businesses, including NBFC, HFC, AMC, Life Insurance, and Health Insurance, all exhibited robust performance with AUM growth, improved asset quality, and strategic advancements. ABCL continues to leverage its omnichannel architecture and customer-centric approach to deliver holistic financial solutions. The proposed amalgamation of Aditya Birla Finance Limited (ABFL) into ABCL is progressing well, with regulatory approvals in hand and expected completion within six months.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy remains focused on leveraging India's economic growth, digitization, and a customer-centric, omnichannel approach. Key drivers include:

*   **Digital and Omnichannel Expansion:** Continued enhancement of the ABCD (D2C) platform, which has seen 2.5 million customer acquisitions, and the Udyog Plus (B2B) platform, with over 1.6 million registrations and INR 2,900 Cr AUM. A revamped servicing app is planned within 3-6 months.
*   **NBFC Portfolio Calibration:** Proactive tightening of underwriting norms in unsecured personal and consumer loans, with increased focus on SMEs and secured business loans. Direct sourcing through branches and the ABCD app is being prioritized.
*   **HFC Growth Acceleration:** Significant investments in digital, technology, and distribution have led to a monthly disbursement run rate of INR 1,400 crore, supporting 51% YoY AUM growth.
*   **AMC Market Share Improvement:** Initiatives to strengthen investment and retail sales teams are showing results, with a 47% increase in monthly SIP flows and a 39% YoY growth in equity AUM. Focus on Alternate Assets and Passive business continues.
*   **Life Insurance Revival:** Strong growth in individual FYP (33% YoY in H1 FY25), driven by new banca tie-ups (Bank of Maharashtra, IDFC First Bank, commencing with Axis Bank) and robust proprietary channels.
*   **Health Insurance Leadership:** Maintaining its position as the fastest-growing standalone health insurer, driven by the "Health First" model and data-enabled customer acquisition. Market share increased to 11.9%.
*   **Corporate Structure Simplification:** The amalgamation of ABFL into ABCL has received no objection from the RBI and is expected to be completed within six months.
*   **Brand & Ecosystem Leverage:** Continued integration with the Aditya Birla Group (ABG) ecosystem, with Udyog Plus sourcing approximately 45% of disbursements from ABG.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **NBFC RoA:** Achieved 2.34% in Q2 FY25, tracking towards the historical 2.5%-3% guidance.
    *   **NBFC Credit Cost:** Improved to 1.25% in Q2 FY25, below the stated guidance of 1.5% and representing a best-in-class performance.
    *   **Life Insurance VNB Margin:** Reported 7.4% in H1 FY25. While management aims to reach 17-18% by year-end, this is a significant uplift from the H1 figure and requires detailed monitoring against historical performance and new surrender regulations.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** Improved to 113% in H1 FY25, on track towards the goal of reaching 100% by FY26.
    *   **NBFC Direct Sourcing:** Strong progress indicated by 2.5 million customer acquisitions on ABCD and 1.6 million registrations on Udyog Plus, supporting the shift towards internal sourcing.

*   **Narrative/Tone Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   The overall tone is optimistic, highlighting India's economic resilience and the company's strategic positioning for growth.
    *   Specific headwinds are addressed proactively. The NBFC business has tightened underwriting in smaller unsecured loans, focusing on SMEs and secured lending to improve asset quality.
    *   The upcoming new surrender guidelines in life insurance are acknowledged, with proactive measures taken regarding commission structures and product re-alignment.
    *   Regulatory communication regarding aggressive lending in housing finance is stated as not having been received.

*   **Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter/Year:**
    *   **Amalgamation Timeline:** Expected completion in the next six months.
    *   **NBFC Portfolio Growth:** Target to grow the overall NBFC portfolio by a CAGR of 25% over the next two to three years.
    *   **NBFC NIM:** To offset yield compression from secured business growth, focus on increasing personal/consumer loans via own channels and business loans via Udyog Plus is expected to mitigate margin decline.
    *   **NBFC Credit Cost:** Expected to remain range-bound around 1.5%, with current levels of 1.25% expected to be sustained.
    *   **Life Insurance Individual FYP Growth:** Aim to grow by a CAGR of 20% over the next two to three years.
    *   **Life Insurance VNB Margin:** Endeavor to maintain a VNB margin of 17%-18% for the full year FY25.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** Target to achieve 100% by FY26.
    *   **HFC AUM Growth:** Guidance to double the book in the next 18 to 24 months.
    *   **ABIBL Stake Sale:** Expected to complete in Q2 FY25.
    *   **Digital Platform Growth:** Aim to acquire 30 million customers on ABCD over three years.
