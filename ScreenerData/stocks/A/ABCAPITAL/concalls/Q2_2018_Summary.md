## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital (ABCL) presented its Q2 FY18 results, highlighting a unified brand strategy and continued integration of its diverse financial services businesses under the "ONE" umbrella. The company emphasized a holistic approach to customer financial needs, encompassing protecting, investing, and financing solutions. Key milestones achieved include the launch of the unified brand, significant growth in Assets Under Management (AUM) for its asset management arm, and credit rating upgrades for its lending businesses. ABCL also received in-principle approval to establish an Asset Reconstruction Company. The presentation detailed performance across its lending, asset management, insurance (life and health), and other financial services segments, underscoring strategic initiatives for growth, customer acquisition, and product diversification.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's overarching strategy revolves around its "ONE" brand initiative, aiming to provide a holistic suite of financial solutions (Protecting, Investing, Financing) through a unified platform, single login, and single view of money. Key drivers include:

*   **Brand Integration:** Consolidating all financial services under the Aditya Birla Capital brand for simplicity, convenience, and empowerment.
*   **Digital Adoption:** Enhancing customer engagement and service delivery through digital channels, evident in digital issuance for health insurance and online volume focus in general insurance advisory.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Penetrating Tier 2 & Tier 3 geographies through branch expansion for lending businesses (Aditya Birla Finance and Housing Finance).
*   **Product Diversification:** Launching new products like Affordable Housing by ABHFL and alternate asset funds by ABSLAMC.
*   **Distribution Enhancement:** Strengthening distribution networks across various channels, including agency, banca, and non-agency partnerships, for insurance businesses.
*   **Customer Centricity:** Focusing on a "cradle to grave" approach to meet all customer money needs.
*   **Strategic Approvals:** Pursuing an Asset Reconstruction Company license to expand capabilities.
