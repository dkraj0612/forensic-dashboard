## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported a strong Q4 FY25, marking the successful completion of its amalgamation with Aditya Birla Finance Limited (ABFL). Consolidated revenue grew by 13% YoY to INR 14,138 Crore, and consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT), excluding one-off items, increased by 6% YoY to INR 865 Crore. For the full fiscal year FY25, consolidated revenue rose by 20% to INR 47,369 Crore, with PAT (excluding one-offs) up 8% YoY to INR 3,142 Crore. The company attributes its performance to a continued focus on quality and profitable growth, leveraging data, digital, and technology, alongside prudent risk management. The simplified corporate structure post-amalgamation is expected to provide better access to capital and drive synergies. The NBFC business saw robust AUM growth and improved credit quality, while the Housing Finance (HFC) business demonstrated accelerated disbursement rates and AUM growth. Asset Management Company (AMC) reported a market share improvement, and both Life and Health Insurance businesses exhibited strong growth and market share gains, with Health Insurance achieving break-even for the fiscal year. ABCL is actively advancing its omnichannel strategy with strong traction across its ABCD app and Udyog Plus platform.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy focuses on driving quality and profitable growth through a customer-centric approach, leveraging data, digital, and technology. Key strategic pillars include:

*   **NBFC Portfolio Optimization:** Continued emphasis on strengthening internal sourcing channels for personal and unsecured MSME loans via branches and the ABCD app. The company is also exploring collaborations with marquee digital platforms for sourcing, while retaining underwriting and fulfillment control. Growth is expected to be driven by increased AUM, margin expansion, productivity improvements, and a favorable product mix shift.
*   **HFC Growth Acceleration:** Sustaining strong growth momentum with investments in distribution, data, digital, and emerging technologies. Focus on achieving operating leverage and significantly improving RoA.
*   **Omnichannel Distribution:** Enhancing customer engagement and loyalty through seamless interaction across digital platforms, branches, and VRMs. The ABCD D2C platform has acquired 5.5 million customers and contributed significantly to personal loan disbursements. Udyog Plus continues to scale, serving 2.3 million MSMEs.
*   **Insurance Business Expansion:** Life Insurance aims for consistent growth in Individual FYP and VNB margin expansion, supported by expanding banca partnerships and digital capabilities. Health Insurance continues to be the fastest-growing standalone player, focusing on its differentiated "Health First" model and market share gains, with the aim of achieving a 100% Combined Ratio.
*   **Corporate Structure Simplification:** The successful amalgamation of ABFL into ABCL creates a larger, unified operating entity with better access to capital and enhanced agility.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **NBFC Credit Cost:** Achieved 1.21% in Q4 FY25, an improvement from the previous year and sequentially, well within the guidance.
    *   **NBFC RoA:** Achieved 2.25% in Q4 FY25, up sequentially, with a full-year RoA of 2.27%. The management reiterated confidence in growing this further.
    *   **Life Insurance VNB Margin:** Achieved 18.0% in FY25, meeting the target and demonstrating the effectiveness of product and commission adjustments.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** Achieved break-even for FY25 with a Combined Ratio of 102% (old accounting), a significant improvement and on track for future targets.
    *   **NBFC Portfolio Mix (Personal & Consumer):** While reduced to 12-13% from ~20%, the company has stabilized this segment and is targeting a calibrated growth back towards 20% over the next 3 years.
    *   **HFC Growth:** Achieved 69% YoY AUM growth and surpassed disbursement run rate targets, demonstrating sustained momentum.
    *   **Amalgamation Completion:** Successfully completed by the target date of April 1, 2025 (effective date of April 1, 2024).

*   **Narrative/Tone Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   Management acknowledges the volatile economic environment due to tariff measures and geopolitical tensions but notes positive signs like softening bond yields, falling crude oil prices, cooled CPI inflation, and accommodative RBI stance.
    *   The cautious approach taken in the NBFC personal and unsecured MSME segments due to early warning signals and macro challenges is highlighted as a prudent decision that has protected asset quality.
    *   The management expressed confidence that the operating environment has stabilized.
