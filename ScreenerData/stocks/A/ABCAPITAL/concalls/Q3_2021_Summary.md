## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported robust Q3 FY22 results, demonstrating strong profit delivery and on-track progress towards its FY24 targets. Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) surged by 46% year-on-year (y-o-y) to ₹577 Crore, with Q3 FY22 marking the highest ever quarterly profits. The company is on track to deliver ahead of its FY24 targets, aspiring to be among the top listed companies in terms of profitability. Key segments showcased significant growth and improved profitability: the NBFC segment achieved a PAT increase of 1.5x y-o-y with improved NIMs, while the Housing Finance (HFC) segment saw a 39% y-o-y PAT growth and an increasing affordable book mix. The Asset Management Company (AMC) reported strong growth in Assets Under Management (AUM), with equity AUM up 39% y-o-y and PBT/AAUM at 33 bps. Life Insurance (LI) saw a 21% y-o-y increase in total premium and a significant improvement in Net VNB margin to 11.2%, while Health Insurance (HI) GWP grew by 31% y-o-y and is on track for break-even (excluding COVID impact). ABCL continues to leverage its digital-first approach, integrated platform ("One ABC"), and expanded partner ecosystems to drive granular retail growth, operational efficiency, and customer experience.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy continues to revolve around leveraging its integrated financial services platform, digital capabilities, and extensive partner ecosystem to drive profitable growth. Key strategic pillars include:

*   **Digital Transformation & Ecosystem Integration:** Aggressive expansion of digital onboarding (e.g., 95%+ customers onboarded digitally across businesses), API integrations (1300+ APIs), and AI/ML-based tools for customer acquisition, service, and risk management. Ecosystem partners are crucial for customer acquisition at scale across all business lines.
*   **Granular Retail & Affordable Segment Growth:** Continued focus on increasing the Retail + SME mix in NBFC (targeting 60%+) and the affordable book mix in HFC (targeting 35%+). Expansion of branch networks into Tier 3/4 cities and increasing penetration in these markets are key.
*   **Product Innovation & Value Accretion:** Introduction of new products across segments, including wealth management and alternate assets in AMC, and value-accretive savings plans in LI. A focus on repricing products to protect margins in LI and managing NIMs in lending businesses.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Cost Optimization:** Ongoing efforts to reduce operational costs through automation (460+ RPA robots), co-located branches (targeting annualised opex savings of ₹50-60 Cr), and digital service interactions.
*   **Risk Management & Quality of Book:** Maintaining strong collection efficiencies (98.8% in NBFC, 96.3% in HFC), robust provisioning, and a focus on secured loan books. Utilizing ML risk scorecards and analytics for enhanced risk assessment.
*   **"One ABC" Platform Advantage:** Maximizing coverage and market access through co-located branches, facilitating cross-selling, and achieving cost benefits. The unified brand and single interface aim to enhance customer experience and leverage data for deepening relationships.
