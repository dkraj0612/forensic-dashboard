## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) presented its Q2 FY20 unaudited financial results, showcasing robust performance across its diversified business segments amidst a challenging economic environment. The company reported significant year-on-year growth in revenue and profitability, driven by strong contributions from its lending, asset management, and insurance businesses. ABCL continues to emphasize its integrated financial services platform, leveraging technology for improved customer experience, operational efficiency, and scalable business models. The transcript highlights disciplined risk management, strategic capital raising, and a focus on quality over growth, particularly in its lending operations.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy remains focused on delivering value across its diversified portfolio through disciplined growth, operational efficiency, and technological integration. Key drivers include:

*   **Lending Business Focus:** Continued emphasis on retail growth, segment diversification towards higher-margin SME and Retail, and maintaining robust asset quality with Stage 3 assets (ex-IL&FS) at 1.39%. Emphasis on cash-flow based underwriting and secured lending.
*   **Asset Management Expansion:** Deepening retail penetration, particularly in B-30 cities, growing the SIP book, and expanding distribution through IFA networks and bank tie-ups.
*   **Life Insurance Value Enhancement:** Focusing on value-accretive products, increasing the protection mix, and leveraging a balanced distribution strategy, with partnerships playing a significant role.
*   **Health Insurance Scale-up:** Driving GWP growth, particularly in the retail segment, expanding distribution through banca tie-ups, and focusing on customer value proposition and product diversification.
*   **Technology & Digital Adoption:** Leveraging AI/ML/NLP for improved customer service, automating processes, enhancing risk management, and building a scalable hybrid cloud infrastructure.
*   **Capital Management & Risk Mitigation:** Securing long-term capital, optimizing borrowing costs, maintaining comfortable capital adequacy, and strategically managing risk through prudent underwriting and reduced ticket sizes.
*   **Portfolio Optimization:** Aligning other businesses for future potential and contribution to overall RoE.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q2 FY20 Context):**
    *   **Lending Business Quality & Growth:** Management's focus on quality over growth and segment diversification is evident. Stage 3 assets for NBFC remained low at 1.39% (ex-IL&FS), and HFC maintained strong asset quality with Gross Stage 3 at 0.85%. The retail loan book in HFC grew 22% y-o-y, aligning with the focus on retail growth. NIM expansion in NBFC (5.28%) and HFC (3.01% Net Interest Margin) also met expectations.
    *   **Life Insurance VNB Margin & FYP Growth:** The H1 FY20 Net VNB margin improved to 0.2% (up ~250 bps y-o-y), and Q2 FY20 Net VNB was 5.7%, indicating progress towards improving value-accretive business. Individual FYP growth of 20% also outpaced industry growth.
    *   **Health Insurance Break-even:** The Q2 FY20 PBT loss of `70 Crore is an improvement from the peak loss of `73 Crore in Q2 FY19, supporting the stated path to break-even by FY21-22. GWP growth of 70% is also significant.
    *   **Asset Management PBT Margin:** The PBT to AAUM margin was maintained at 27 bps, meeting previous performance levels.
    *   **Other Businesses Contribution:** The ARC platform turning profitable in its first year aligns with the strategy for other businesses to contribute to RoE.
