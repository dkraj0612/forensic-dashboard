## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported a challenging Q3 FY25 with a 10% YoY consolidated revenue growth to INR 10,949 Crore, but a slight decline in consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) to INR 708 Crore from INR 736 Crore in Q3 FY24. This performance is attributed to macro-economic headwinds including moderating urban demand, tight liquidity, and capital market volatility. ABCL has strategically recalibrated its NBFC portfolio by reducing exposure to unsecured personal loans and increasing secured business loans, which has maintained strong asset quality, albeit impacting NIMs. The Housing Finance (HFC) business demonstrated robust growth, exceeding a monthly disbursement run rate of INR 1,500 Crore. Asset Management Company (AMC) saw a 23% YoY growth in average AUM. Life Insurance delivered strong individual first-year premium growth and improved VNB margins, while Health Insurance continued its rapid expansion as the fastest-growing standalone health insurer. The company is advancing its omnichannel strategy with the successful launch of the ABCD app and a new servicing app, and the scaling of the Udyog Plus platform. The amalgamation of Aditya Birla Finance Limited (ABFL) into ABCL is progressing and expected to complete by March 31, 2025.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy centers on navigating a challenging macro environment by focusing on quality and profitable growth, leveraging data, digital, and technology. Key initiatives include:

*   **NBFC Portfolio Calibration:** Continued focus on reducing unsecured personal loans and increasing secured business loans to SMEs, alongside growing the corporate and mid-market portfolio.
*   **HFC Growth Acceleration:** Significant investments in digital, technology, and distribution have fueled a substantial increase in disbursements and AUM growth.
*   **Omnichannel Distribution Enhancement:** The ABCD D2C platform has acquired over 4.1 million customers, with strong traction in payments. A revamped servicing app has launched, and the Udyog Plus B2B platform now serves over 2.2 million MSMEs with INR 3,300 Crore AUM, contributing 25% of unsecured business loan disbursements.
*   **Life Insurance Focus:** Continued growth in individual FYP, supported by new banca tie-ups (Axis Bank, Bank of Maharashtra, IDFC First Bank), and efforts to mitigate the impact of new surrender guidelines through product and commission adjustments, leading to improved VNB margins.
*   **Health Insurance Leadership:** Maintaining its position as the fastest-growing standalone health insurer, driven by its "Health First" model and data-enabled customer acquisition strategies, leading to market share gains.
*   **Corporate Structure Simplification:** The amalgamation of ABFL into ABCL has received shareholder approval, with completion expected by March 31, 2025.
*   **Digital and Ecosystem Integration:** Udyog Plus now sources 50% of disbursements from the ABG ecosystem, and the Stellar platform for channel partners has launched.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **NBFC Credit Cost:** Achieved at 1.36% in Q3 FY25, below the stated normalized threshold of 1.5%.
    *   **NBFC RoA:** Reported at 2.10% in Q3 FY25. While down from previous quarters, management aims to improve this from the current 2.1% towards 2.5% in 18-24 months through margin expansion and controlled credit costs.
    *   **Life Insurance VNB Margin:** Achieved 10.8% in 9M FY25, with an aim to reach 17-18% for FY25. Significant margin expansion is expected in Q4 due to timing factors and product repricing.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio (CoR):** Improved to 114% in 9M FY25 (reported), with an underlying figure of 110% if accounting norms were unchanged. The company reiterates its goal to reach 100% by FY26.
    *   **NBFC Portfolio Mix:** Secured business loans have increased to 74% from 69% YoY, meeting the strategy of de-risking the portfolio. The personal and consumer loan segment, which had reduced to 13% from 20% YoY, is planned to be grown back to 18-20% in the medium term.
    *   **HFC Growth:** Achieved 62% YoY AUM growth and exceeded the monthly disbursement run rate target, with a target to double the book in 18-24 months.

*   **Narrative/Tone Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   The tone remains cautiously optimistic, acknowledging macro-economic challenges and proactive measures taken in response.
    *   Management explicitly addresses the impact of the macro environment on GDP growth and inflation.
    *   Proactive portfolio calibration in NBFC due to early warning signals and tight liquidity is highlighted.
    *   Life insurance management has taken steps to mitigate the impact of new surrender guidelines on product pricing and commission structures, indicating preparedness.
    *   The management of the HFC business acknowledges the need to balance growth with quality and has focused on digital reinvention and analytics.

*   **Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter/Year:**
