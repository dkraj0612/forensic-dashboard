## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported its Q3 FY23 results, highlighting continued strong performance across its diversified financial services platform. The company emphasized its strategic focus on a "One ABC, One P&L" approach, aiming to provide holistic solutions to customers and their ecosystems. Key developments include the launch of a unified B2B platform for the MSME ecosystem and the approval for a dedicated omni-channel Direct-to-Customer (D2C) subsidiary. The company demonstrated robust growth in its lending book, with a particular focus on granularization towards retail and MSME segments. Digital adoption remains a core driver, evident in high digital penetration across various business functions and customer interactions. Management remains confident in delivering ahead of FY24 guidance for several key metrics.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy is centered around its "One ABC, One P&L" philosophy, emphasizing customer-centricity and ecosystem solutions. Key strategic pillars include:

*   **Customer-Centric Ecosystem:** Launch of a unified B2B platform for MSMEs offering lending and value-added services (VAS), and the formation of a dedicated D2C subsidiary to distribute PIFA (Protecting, Investing, Financing, Advising) products through an omni-channel experience. This aims to serve existing customers, acquire new ones, and provide a seamless experience across all touchpoints.
*   **Granular Growth & Digitalization:** Continued focus on increasing the mix of Retail, SME, and HNI customers in the NBFC loan book. Significant emphasis on digital sourcing for personal loans (56% sourced digitally), digital customer service interactions (84% for NBFC), and digital policy renewals in life insurance (78%).
*   **Product Innovation:** Development and integration of payment stacks and VAS to enhance customer experience and brand recall, particularly through the D2C and MSME platforms.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Risk Management:** Leveraging data analytics for customer onboarding, underwriting, and servicing. Improvement in asset quality for NBFC, with a focus on risk-calibrated loan growth. Implementation of AI/ML for fraud detection and underwriting automation in health insurance.
*   **Scale and Market Share Expansion:** Continued growth in Gross Written Premium (GWP) for health insurance and expansion of distribution networks for asset management and life insurance.

    *   **Consolidated PAT Growth (excl. exceptional items):** *Guidance:* Continued PAT growth. *Delivered:* PAT grew 27% y-o-y (significantly impacted by fair value gain in ABHI). (**Met, with significant non-operational impact**)
    *   **Customer Base Growth:** *Guidance:* Continued customer acquisition. *Delivered:* Added ~1.4 million customers in Q3 FY23, reaching ~43 million. (**Met**)
    *   **Lending Book Growth (NBFC + HFC):** *Guidance:* Accelerated growth in lending book. *Delivered:* Lending book grew 40% y-o-y to ₹85,869 Crore. (**Met**)
    *   **NBFC Loan Book Growth:** *Guidance:* Strong growth in NBFC loan book. *Delivered:* Loan book grew 47% y-o-y to ₹72,994 Crore. (**Exceeded**)
    *   **NBFC Retail, SME, HNI Mix:** *Guidance:* Maintaining focus on this segment. *Delivered:* This mix constitutes 66% of the loan book. (**Met**)
    *   **NBFC NIM:** *Guidance:* Continued NIM expansion. *Delivered:* NIM expanded by 76 bps y-o-y to 7.00%. (**Exceeded**)
    *   **NBFC Asset Quality:** *Guidance:* Improvement in asset quality. *Delivered:* Stage 2 & 3 assets declined by 491 bps y-o-y to 6.49%. (**Exceeded**)
    *   **Life Insurance FYP Growth:** *Guidance:* Outperforming industry growth. *Delivered:* Individual FYP grew 25% y-o-y in 9M FY23, ahead of industry growth. (**Exceeded**)
    *   **Health Insurance GWP Growth:** *Guidance:* Strong GWP growth. *Delivered:* GWP grew 59% y-o-y in 9M FY23. (**Met**)
    *   **Digital Penetration:** *Guidance:* Continued digital adoption. *Delivered:* High digital penetration across various business functions (e.g., 56% personal loan disbursements, 84% NBFC customer service). (**Met/Exceeded**)
