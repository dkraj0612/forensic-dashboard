## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported its highest ever consolidated quarterly profit in Q3 FY21, underscoring a robust recovery and strong performance across its diversified business segments. Consolidated revenue increased by 16% year-on-year (y-o-y) to Rs. 5,346 Crore, while Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by 15% y-o-y to Rs. 289 Crore. The company's strategy remains focused on leveraging technology, digital transformation, and strategic partnerships to enhance customer experience, drive operational efficiency, and capitalize on cross-selling opportunities within its extensive customer base.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy in Q3 FY21 continued to revolve around its diversified platform and digital initiatives:

*   **Digital Transformation:** Enhanced customer experience and operational efficiency through digital onboarding, paperless processes, and AI-powered tools (chatbots, voice bots).
*   **Lending Business Growth:** Strong revival in gross disbursements, exceeding pre-COVID levels, with a focus on granular retail and SME segments. Expansion into semi-urban locations with new branches.
*   **Asset Management Revival:** Sustained AUM growth driven by a strong retail franchise, SIP registrations, and B-30 market penetration. Emphasis on digital transactions and distributor engagement.
*   **Insurance Segment Strength:** Life Insurance outperformed private players in Individual FYP and Group NBP, showing improved persistency and cost ratios. Health Insurance maintained its fastest-growing status, expanding its customer base and improving its combined ratio.
*   **Cross-selling and Upselling:** Initiatives to maximize value from the 20 million customer base through targeted programs and data analytics.
*   **Partnerships:** Strategic collaborations with Fintechs to drive customer acquisition and leverage specialized expertise.
