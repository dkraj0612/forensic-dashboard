## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) has released a presentation detailing its operational performance and strategic direction, emphasizing the strong foundation of the Indian economy and its positive impact on the financial services sector. The company highlights its continued focus on digital transformation, customer-centricity, and an omnichannel architecture to deliver holistic financial solutions. Key business segments including NBFC, HFC, AMC, Life Insurance, and Health Insurance are showcased with their growth trajectories and strategic initiatives. Significant emphasis is placed on the ongoing simplification of its corporate structure through the proposed amalgamation of Aditya Birla Finance Limited (ABFL) into ABCL, which is progressing with regulatory approvals. ABCL reiterates its commitment to quality and profitable growth, leveraging its strong brand, diversified offerings, and robust digital platforms.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

The overarching strategy for ABCL is built on leveraging the "Strong growth foundation of the Indian economy" and its "tectonic shift" towards digitization and financial innovation. Key drivers and strategies include:

*   **Omnichannel Architecture & Digital Stack:** Expanding robust and agile digital platforms (ABCD-D2C, Udyog Plus-B2B, B2D) to provide seamless customer experiences and intuitive journeys across digital, virtual, and branch channels.
*   **Customer-Centricity & 360o Solutions:** Building a deep understanding of customer needs (retail, SME, mid & large corporates) to offer customized, simple, and holistic financial solutions through an "ONE EXPERIENCE" approach.
*   **NBFC Focus:** Continuing as a top diversified NBFC with a specific focus on the SME segment (approx. 53% of AUM). Emphasis on secured lending (70%+ of portfolio) and prudent risk management, aiming for sustainable RoA between 2.5%-3% across cycles.
*   **HFC Expansion:** Maintaining robust loan book growth (23% CAGR over two years) with a balanced portfolio (prime, affordable, construction finance) and a focus on quality origination.
*   **AMC Diversification & Growth:** Driving AUM growth (21% y-o-y) and strengthening the retail franchise, with a focus on equity AUM and SIP flows. Expanding in passive AUM and alternate assets.
*   **Life Insurance Quality & Growth:** Aiming for continued individual FYP growth (CAGR of 25%+) and maintaining VNB margins (18%+), supported by new banca tie-ups and improved persistency rates.
*   **Health Insurance Innovation & Profitability:** Continuing to be the fastest-growing standalone health insurer, driven by the unique "Health First" model and incentivizing wellness. Targeting a combined ratio of 100% by FY26.
*   **Corporate Structure Simplification:** Executing the proposed amalgamation of ABFL with ABCL to create a unified NBFC, enhance financial strength, achieve operational efficiencies, and potentially eliminate holdco discount.
*   **Brand & Ecosystem Leverage:** Utilizing the strong Aditya Birla Group brand and parentage, and leveraging the wider ABG and ABC ecosystem for growth opportunities and access to capital.
