## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) presented its audited financial results for the quarter and financial year ended March 31, 2023. The presentation highlighted strong performance across its diversified financial services platform, emphasizing its "One ABC, One P&L" philosophy focused on customer centricity, digital integration, and ecosystem synergies. The company reported a consolidated revenue of ₹27,416 crore for FY23, a 27% year-on-year increase. Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) for FY23 stood at ₹2,057 crore, a 33% year-on-year increase, though this figure includes a significant fair value gain of ₹2,739 crore from Aditya Birla Health Insurance (ABHI) becoming a joint venture. Underlying operational performance showed robust growth in lending books, improved Net Interest Margins (NIMs) in NBFC and Housing Finance, strong premium growth and VNB margin expansion in Life Insurance, and significant market share gains in Health Insurance. The company is actively investing in digital capabilities and expanding its MSME offerings through the "Udyog Plus" platform.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy continues to revolve around its "One ABC, One P&L" model, aiming to drive quality and profitable growth. Key strategic drivers include:

*   **Customer Centricity & Ecosystem:** Deepening customer understanding and maximizing lifetime value through comprehensive "Protecting, Investing, Financing and Advising" (PIFA) solutions. Enhancing the "One Experience" via an omnichannel architecture.
*   **Digital Reinvention:** Continued investment in digital capabilities for seamless customer onboarding, servicing, and operational efficiency. This includes launching a comprehensive B2B platform for MSMEs ("Udyog Plus") and integrating with ONDC & OCEN. A new subsidiary, "ABC Digital," has been incorporated to distribute financial products.
*   **MSME & Retail Focus:** Scaling up "Udyog Plus" for MSMEs with cash flow-backed lending and value-added services. Expanding personal and consumer loans through digital sourcing and new product launches.
*   **Distribution Network Expansion:** Increasing presence in Tier III/IV cities with a focus on co-located branches at "One ABC" locations. Building direct-to-customer channels and leveraging ABG/ABC ecosystem synergies.
*   **Product Innovation:** Launching new products and enhancing existing ones, with a focus on protection, value-added services, and diversified offerings across all businesses.
*   **Leveraging Analytics:** Extensive use of analytics across sourcing, underwriting, and collections for better customer selection, risk-based pricing, and operational efficiency.
*   **Partnerships & Innovation:** Engaging with startups through hackathons and innovation challenges to foster an innovation-led culture and ensure a competitive edge.
