# Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) - Q4 FY21 Earnings Summary

## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) delivered its **highest ever quarterly consolidated profit** in Q4 FY21, reporting a Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹375 Crore, a significant 2.6x increase year-on-year (y-o-y) and 30% quarter-on-quarter (q-o-q) growth. For the full financial year FY21, consolidated PAT grew by 22% y-o-y. This strong performance was underpinned by robust growth across all key businesses, driven by a strategic focus on granular retail expansion, digital transformation, and enhanced operational efficiencies. ABCL's active customer base expanded by 22% y-o-y to 24 Million.

Key segment highlights include:
*   **NBFC (Aditya Birla Finance Limited):** Achieved its highest ever Retail + SME mix in disbursements at 64% and reported its highest Q4 NIM at 5.98% (↑98 bps y-o-y). PAT surged 1.9x y-o-y to ₹254 Crore. Asset quality improved with Gross Stage 3 reducing by 93 bps y-o-y to 2.68%.
*   **Housing Finance (ABHFL):** Recorded strong Q4 gross disbursals (↑47% y-o-y) with 52% in the affordable segment, leading to its highest Q4 NIM at 3.85% (↑77 bps y-o-y). PAT grew 71% y-o-y to ₹36 Crore.
*   **Asset Management (ABSLAMC):** Delivered its highest ever quarterly profit, with Equity AAUM growing 11% y-o-y and equity mix expanding to 36%. FY21 RoE stood at 35%.
*   **Life Insurance (ABSLI):** Achieved its highest ever Net VNB Margin at 10.6% for FY21 (↑370 bps y-o-y) and strong EV growth of 24.2% with a RoEV of 13.7%. Total Gross Premium increased 22% y-o-y.
*   **Health Insurance (ABHI):** Remained the fastest growing health insurer with FY21 GWP up 49% y-o-y (vs. industry at 16%). Q4 Combined Ratio reached its lowest ever at 107%.

ABCL continues to leverage its "One ABC" integrated platform, extensive digital capabilities (e.g., 95% digital onboarding in LI, 88% in HFC), and partner ecosystems to drive sustainable, profitable growth while maintaining strong asset quality and cost discipline. The company also filed a DRHP for the AMC listing in April 2021, signaling progress on value unlocking.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL’s strategy for sustained profitable growth is built on an integrated digital-first approach and granular market penetration:

*   **Accelerated Digital Transformation:** Aggressive adoption of APIs, AI/ML models, audio-visual voice bots, and self-service portals to streamline customer onboarding (95% digital in LI, 88% in HFC), servicing, and risk management. Key initiatives include the "ABC ONE APP," "abc.com," and "Select Advisor Portal."
*   **Granular Retail & Affordable Growth:** Continued focus on expanding the Retail + SME + HNI mix in NBFC, increasing affordable housing disbursals in HFC, and driving SIPs and B-30 penetration in AMC. Expansion into Tier II-IV cities is a key part of this strategy.
*   **Enhanced Operational Efficiency:** Ongoing efforts to optimize costs through automation (RPA), co-located branches, and digital channels, contributing to significant cost savings (~₹300 Crore in FY21) and improving efficiency ratios across businesses.
*   **Robust Risk Management:** Maintaining strong asset quality through granular portfolio mix, high collection efficiencies (98.7% NBFC, 95.2% HFC), enhanced provisioning coverage, and leveraging data analytics for proactive risk identification and fraud detection.
*   **Integrated Platform Advantage ("One ABC"):** Leveraging the diversified financial services platform to maximize cross-sell opportunities, enhance customer experience, and achieve economies of scale and synergy benefits.
*   **Product Innovation & Value Accretion:** Launching new, value-accretive products across segments (e.g., protection and savings plans in LI) and continuously improving product mix (e.g., higher equity mix in AMC).
*   **Strategic Value Unlocking:** Progressing with the planned listing of the Asset Management business, with DRHP filed in April 2021.
