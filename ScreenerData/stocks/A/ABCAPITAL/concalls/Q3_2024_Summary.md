## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported its unaudited financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2023. The company demonstrated strong consolidated revenue growth of 29% year-on-year (YoY) to ₹9,997 crore for Q3 FY24 and 39% YoY growth in consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) to ₹736 crore. This performance was driven by robust growth across its diversified financial services platform, including lending books, asset management, life insurance, and health insurance. The company continues to emphasize its "One ABC, One P&L" philosophy, focusing on customer centricity, digital innovation, and ecosystem synergies. Key highlights include significant growth in loan books for NBFC and HFC, strong premium growth and VNB margin expansion in Life Insurance, and market share gains in Health Insurance. ABCL is actively investing in digital capabilities, expanding its MSME offerings through the "Udyog Plus" platform, and enhancing its distribution network.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy remains focused on profitable growth through its integrated financial services model, emphasizing:

*   **Customer Centricity & Ecosystem Integration:** Deepening customer relationships and leveraging the "One ABC" ecosystem for cross-selling and up-selling opportunities. Expanding digital channels for customer onboarding and servicing.
*   **Digital Reinvention:** Continued investment in digital capabilities, including AI/ML for underwriting, collections, and customer engagement. Enhancing the "Udyog Plus" platform for MSMEs with cash flow-backed lending and value-added services.
*   **MSME & Retail Focus:** Scaling "Udyog Plus" with differentiated offerings and digitally assisted income programs. Expanding personal and consumer loans through digital sourcing and new product launches like Education Loans and 2W Loans.
*   **Distribution Network Expansion:** Increasing branch presence in Tier III/IV cities and leveraging ABG/ABC ecosystem synergies. Building direct-to-customer channels and strengthening partner networks.
*   **Product Innovation:** Launching new products across all verticals, with a focus on protection (Life and Health), value-added services, and diversified offerings. Examples include "ACTIV ONE" in health insurance and new AIFs in asset management.
*   **Data Analytics & Automation:** Utilizing data analytics for customer selection, risk-based pricing, and enhanced customer experience. Implementing automation in onboarding, servicing, and collections.
*   **Risk Management:** Strengthening underwriting through scorecard usage and alternate data, improving asset quality, and maintaining healthy provisioning and PCR.
