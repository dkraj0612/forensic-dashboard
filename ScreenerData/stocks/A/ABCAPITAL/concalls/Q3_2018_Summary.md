## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) presented its Q3 FY19 unaudited financial results, showcasing continued growth across its diverse financial services portfolio under the unified "ONE" brand strategy. The company highlighted strong performance in its lending businesses, with a significant increase in its lending book and improved Net Interest Margins (NIMs). The asset management arm demonstrated robust equity AAUM growth and an expanding SIP book. Life insurance reported impressive individual First Year Premium (FYP) growth and an improving Value of New Business (VNB) margin, while health insurance continued its journey towards break-even. The presentation emphasized a diversified portfolio, focus on value-accretive growth, digital adoption, and a customer-centric approach.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy continues to be driven by the "ONE" brand initiative, focusing on a holistic customer lifecycle approach. Key drivers highlighted include:

*   **Brand Integration:** Reinforcing the unified Aditya Birla Capital brand for a seamless customer experience across Protect, Invest, and Finance.
*   **Digital Adoption:** Enhancing customer engagement and service delivery through digital platforms, evident in digital transactions for SIPs and the development of robo-advisory and distributor mobile apps.
*   **Geographic Expansion & Granularity:** Strengthening distribution networks, particularly in Tier 2/3 cities and B-30 locations, to increase retail granularity. This includes expanding branch presence and leveraging partnerships.
*   **Product Diversification:** Offering a comprehensive suite of products, with a focus on value-accretive segments such as affordable housing, SME lending, and alternative asset funds.
*   **Partnership Growth:** Significantly scaling up collaborations with banks, such as the HDFC Bank partnership, contributing substantially to distribution and business growth.
*   **Customer Centricity:** Meeting a wide range of customer financial needs from "cradle to grave."
*   **Value Accretive Growth:** Shifting the loan book towards higher-margin segments and focusing on profitable scale.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Improving cost-income ratios through scale and efficiency gains.
