## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) presented its H1 FY19 unaudited financial results, highlighting continued robust growth across its diversified financial services portfolio, adhering to the "ONE" brand strategy. The company transitioned to Ind AS reporting effective April 1, 2018, with H1 FY19 and H1 FY18 financials now Ind AS compliant. Key performance drivers included significant expansion in its lending businesses, strong equity AUM growth in asset management, and impressive individual APE growth in life insurance. While the health insurance segment continued its journey towards break-even with peak quarterly losses, ABCL emphasized its diversified portfolio, focus on value-accretive growth, digital adoption, and a customer-centric approach. Profitability for H1 FY19 was noted to be impacted by market conditions affecting fair value of investments, investment in HDFC banca ramp-up, and brand/interest expenses at the standalone level.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL continues to execute its strategy focusing on diversified growth and customer-centricity:

*   **Integrated Financial Services:** Leveraging the "ONE" brand to offer solutions across the customer lifecycle (Protect, Invest, Finance) with over 10 Mn active customers.
*   **Lending Business Focus:** Shifting towards higher-margin Retail+SME+HNI mix (49% as of Sep 30, 2018), with affordable housing and unsecured retail lending segments showing strong growth and profitability. Maintaining strong asset quality with AAA ratings for NBFC/HFC and robust risk management.
*   **Asset Management Retail Expansion:** Deepening penetration in B-30 cities, significantly growing the SIP book (over `1,000 Crore monthly), and expanding the IFA network. Digitalization of transactions is a key focus.
*   **Life Insurance Value Creation:** Concentrating on value-accretive products, increasing protection mix, and leveraging strong partnerships (e.g., HDFC Bank) alongside proprietary channels for distribution.
*   **Health Insurance Scale-up:** Rapidly building distribution capacity with multiple Banca tie-ups (8 within 18 months) and expanding provider networks across cities. Driving value through geographical and product diversification, and increasing retail GWP mix.
*   **Digital Adoption:** Building scale across digital assets, including robo-advisory websites (SIPNOW) and dedicated mobile applications for distributors and customers.
*   **Capital Management:** Maintaining comfortable capital adequacy (CRAR at 17.2% for NBFC, 17.09% for HFC) and optimising ALM for liquidity and cost through diversified borrowing mix.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q3 FY19 Context):**
    *   **Lending Book Growth:** Management's focus on growth and shifting towards value-accretive segments remains consistent. The H1 FY19 data shows the lending book growing at a 33% CAGR (FY15-FY18) and the HFC book growing ~5x over 2.5 years, aligning with the previous emphasis on expanding the lending book and SME + Retail mix.
    *   **NIM Improvement:** The previous context noted NIM expansion/stability. In H1 FY19, NBFC NIM was maintained at 4.6%-5.0% and HFC NIMs were maintained across interest rate cycles, demonstrating consistent ability to manage margins as stated.
    *   **Life Insurance FYP Growth:** Q3 FY19 reported 79% y-o-y individual FYP growth. H1 FY19 continues this trend with a 60% y-o-y growth in Individual APE, significantly outperforming industry growth and consistent with plans to strengthen distribution.
    *   **Health Insurance Break-even:** The objective of reaching break-even was previously stated, and H1 FY19 reiterates this, confirming "Health insurance at peak quarterly loss in Q2 FY19 and expected to break-even in ~3 years." This shows consistency in the long-term outlook.
    *   **Asset Management Retail/Alternate Assets & IFA Network:** The target for IFA network expansion was 275+ locations by FY19 with B-30 focus. H1 FY19 confirms "Continued focus on retail expansion" and "Target to reach 275+ locations by FY19," indicating consistency in strategy execution.
    *   **Asset Reconstruction Company:** The status of the JV with Värde Partners remains "subject to regulatory approvals" in H1 FY19, consistent with the prior update of having "in-principle" approval.

*   **Narrative/Tone Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   While the Q3 FY19 context highlighted a generally positive tone with acknowledgment of broking margin compression, the H1 FY19 transcript is more explicit about factors impacting overall profitability. It notes that "Profitability in H1 FY19 impacted by: Change in Fair Value of investments due to market conditions; Life Insurance profit ex fair value changes impacted mainly on account of investment in HDFC banca ramp-up; Brand and interest expenses incurred at ABCL standalone." This represents a slight shift towards greater transparency regarding broader market and investment-related impacts on overall profitability, beyond just the known regulatory changes in broking. However, the overall narrative remains focused on strategic progress and growth across segments.
