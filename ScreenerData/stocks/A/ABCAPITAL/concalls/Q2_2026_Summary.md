## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported a solid Q2 FY26, demonstrating continued growth across its diversified businesses with a resilient Indian economy as a backdrop. Consolidated profit after tax (PAT) saw a modest 3% year-on-year increase to INR 855 Crore, while consolidated revenue grew 4% YoY to INR 12,481 Crore. The NBFC segment exhibited robust AUM growth of 22% YoY to INR 1.4 Trillion, with disbursements up 39% sequentially, driven by a stabilizing credit environment in personal and consumer loans. The Housing Finance (HFC) business maintained strong growth with disbursements up 44% YoY and AUM growing 65% YoY, alongside improving operational efficiencies. Asset Management Company (AMC) achieved an average AUM of INR 4.25 Trillion, a 11% YoY increase, and reported a 13% YoY growth in operating profit. The Life Insurance business continued its strong growth as the fastest-growing private insurer with individual first-year premium growth of 19% YoY in H1 FY26, and a significant improvement in Net VNB margin. The Health Insurance business also showed strong momentum, growing Gross Written Premium (GWP) by 31% YoY (on a 1/n basis) and increasing its market share. ABCL's omnichannel strategy, particularly through its ABCD app and Udyog Plus platform, continues to scale, enhancing customer engagement and distribution reach.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL continues to focus on driving quality and profitable growth through a customer-centric, data-driven, and digitally enabled approach. Key strategic priorities include:

*   **NBFC Portfolio Augmentation:** Continued focus on personal and consumer loans and unsecured business loans, with observed stabilization and sequential growth. Maintaining a cautious approach in smaller ticket unsecured segments while secured SME and corporate/mid-market segments demonstrate steady growth.
*   **HFC Business Expansion:** Sustaining strong growth momentum by focusing on both prime and affordable segments, leveraging investments in distribution, data, and digital capabilities for operating leverage.
*   **Omnichannel Distribution Network:** Expanding reach and customer engagement through the ABCD D2C platform and the Udyog Plus B2B platform for MSMEs, complemented by a growing branch network, with a focus on tier 3 and tier 4 towns.
*   **Insurance Business Growth:** Life Insurance aims for sustained growth in individual premiums and VNB margins, while Health Insurance focuses on market share gains and profitability improvement through its differentiated "Health First" model.
*   **Capital & Risk Management:** Maintaining prudent risk management practices to protect capital and deliver risk-calibrated returns, supported by strong capital adequacy ratios.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **NBFC Personal & Consumer Loans Growth:** Management noted stabilization and positive growth momentum. Actual disbursements grew 52% YoY and 26% QoQ, aligning with this outlook.
    *   **NBFC Unsecured Business Loans:** Management observed stabilization and sequential growth. Actual disbursements grew 32% YoY and 37% QoQ, indicating a positive trend.
    *   **NBFC Credit Cost:** Management expects credit costs to be in the range of 1.2% to 1.3% for FY26. The reported Q2 FY26 credit cost was 1.16%, within the expected range.
    *   **HFC RoA Improvement:** Management reiterated guidance for 2% to 2.2% RoA over the next 6-8 quarters. The reported Q2 FY26 RoA was 1.82%, showing sequential improvement and progress towards the target.
    *   **Life Insurance Net VNB Margin:** Management maintained guidance for Net VNB margins of over 18% for FY26. The reported H1 FY26 margin was 11.6%, with management expecting it to reach the target by year-end, despite the GST impact.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** Management aimed to improve the combined ratio from 105% in the previous year. The reported H1 FY26 combined ratio was 108%, indicating a sequential improvement from the previous year's comparable figure (113%).
    *   **NBFC NIM Improvement:** Management anticipated NIMs to improve as the higher-yielding unsecured loan portfolio grows. While headline yields saw some compression, NIMs improved by 9 bps QoQ, and management expects further yield improvement in Q3/Q4 FY26.
    *   **NBFC Opex to AUM Reduction:** The company mentioned that H1 FY26 Opex to AUM was 1.9%, and guided for future performance in this range. Q2 FY26 saw a sequential increase in opex to AUM but this was attributed to a one-off event, with the underlying trend expected to remain within the guided range.

*   **Narrative/Tone Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   Management acknowledged global macro-economic uncertainties but highlighted the resilience of the Indian economy.
    *   The narrative regarding the personal and consumer loans segment shifted to a more optimistic tone, emphasizing stabilization and growth resumption.
    *   The GST exemption on insurance products was viewed positively for long-term demand and penetration, though short-term margin pressures due to loss of input tax credits were acknowledged, with mitigation strategies being explored.

*   **Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter/Year:**
    *   **NBFC Yields and Margins:** Expected to improve in Q3 and Q4 FY26 due to the growth in personal and consumer loans and unsecured business loans.
    *   **NBFC Credit Cost:** Expected to remain in the range of 1.2% to 1.3% at the company level for FY26.
    *   **NBFC NIM:** Expected to improve in Q4 FY26, and further in Q1 FY27.
    *   **HFC ROA:** On track to achieve 2% to 2.2% over the next 5-6 quarters.
    *   **Life Insurance Net VNB Margin:** Guidance maintained at over 18% for FY26, despite potential short-term impact from GST exemption.
    *   **Life Insurance Individual FYP Growth:** Guidance maintained to grow at a CAGR of 20%+ for the next 3 years.
    *   **Life Insurance Net VNB Growth:** Guidance maintained to double Net VNB in the next 3 years.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** To improve in the current year from 105% in the previous year (old accounting).
    *   **NBFC Opex to AUM:** Expected to stabilize around 1.9% going forward.
    *   **Life Insurance VNB Margin Impact from GST:** Potential impact could range between 200-250 basis points, with efforts underway to mitigate this.
    *   **NBFC NBFC Cost of Funds:** While competitive, management expects yield improvements to drive margin expansion.
    *   **NBFC ROA:** Expected to be closer to 2.4% by the end of Q4 FY26, with a medium-term aspiration of 2.5%.
    *   **HFC Net Interest Income (NII):** Expected to settle in the range of 4.75% to 4.80% by the end of the year, compensated by operating leverage.
