## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported strong results for FY26, demonstrating significant year-on-year growth across its diversified financial services businesses. The company achieved a consolidated profit after tax (PAT) of INR 3,797 Crore, a robust 21% increase YoY, on a consolidated revenue of INR 53,871 Crore, up 14% YoY. This performance was driven by broad-based growth, particularly in the NBFC and Health Insurance segments. The NBFC business saw its total lending portfolio grow by 32% YoY to INR 2,07,368 Crore, with a strong RoA of 2.25%. The Housing Finance (HFC) segment also performed well, with AUM growing by 16% YoY to INR 5,91,343 Cr. The Life Insurance business continued its upward trajectory with individual first-year premium growth of 15% YoY, while the Health Insurance business reported a significant 39% YoY increase in Gross Written Premium (GWP) to INR 6,855 Crore. ABCL's strategic emphasis on an omnichannel approach, powered by digital platforms like ABCD and Udyog Plus, and the integration of AI across its operations, continues to be a key enabler of growth and efficiency.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy continues to focus on integrated growth, leveraging its diverse business lines and digital capabilities. Key drivers include:

*   **NBFC Growth & Quality:** Continued expansion in retail, SME, and HNI loans, with a significant focus on secured lending (72% of loan book). Emphasis on digital acquisition, AI-powered collections, and scorecard utilization for improved customer selection and underwriting.
*   **HFC Expansion:** Accelerating growth in prime and affordable segments, supported by the ABG ecosystem and digital capabilities for seamless onboarding and servicing.
*   **AMC Diversification:** Scaling the retail franchise, expanding product offerings in mutual funds and alternative assets (AIF, PMS, Real Estate), and strengthening its pan-India distribution network, with over 80% sourcing from B30 cities.
*   **Life Insurance Focus:** Driving individual FYP growth at 20%+ CAGR for the next three years, with a target of expanding VNB margins above 18%. Focus on digital adoption, underwriting improvements using AI/ML, and strengthening proprietary and bancassurance channels.
*   **Health Insurance Scale & Profitability:** Continuing to scale the "Health First" model with a focus on superior customer experience and profitability. Expansion of distribution footprint, tech-led transformation, and leveraging data analytics for customer risk stratification and personalized interventions.
*   **Digital & AI Integration:** Pervasive adoption of AI and digital technologies across all business verticals for enhanced customer engagement, operational efficiency, risk management, underwriting, and sales productivity.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **NBFC RoA:** Management guided for an RoA of 2.25% by end of FY26. The reported standalone NBFC RoA for FY26 was 2.25%, meeting the target.
    *   **NBFC Stage 2 & 3 Loans:** Management had guided for a significant reduction in Stage 2 & 3 loans. The reported figure for FY26 was 0.76%, down 63 bps YoY, indicating successful execution.
    *   **NBFC NIM Improvement:** Management had anticipated NIM improvements. The reported Net Interest Income (NII) for the NBFC business in FY26 was INR 8,807 Crore, showing robust growth.
    *   **HFC ROA Improvement:** While a specific target wasn't reiterated from prior periods, the HFC segment demonstrated strong operational metrics.
    *   **Life Insurance Net VNB Margin:** Management had guided for VNB margins above 18% for FY26. The VNB margin for FY26 was not explicitly stated, but VNB growth was strong at 29% YoY. However, the reported RoEV was 13.2%, suggesting the margin target might not have been fully met or needs further clarification on how it's calculated.
    *   **Life Insurance Individual FYP Growth:** Management's guidance was for 20%+ CAGR over the next 3 years. The reported FY26 growth was 15% YoY, showing positive momentum but slightly below the pace needed for a strict 20% CAGR.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** Management aimed to improve the combined ratio. The reported FY26 combined ratio (without 1/n) was 102%, indicating a need for further improvement from the previous year's level (implied from prior context).
    *   **NBFC Opex to AUM Reduction:** Management had expected Opex to AUM to stabilize around 1.9%. The reported Opex / Avg. Lending book for FY26 was 31.50% which is presented as a percentage of the lending book, not AUM, requiring clarification on the exact metric and comparison.

*   **Narrative/Tone Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   Management acknowledged the impact of "Industry Headwinds" on the health insurance sector but highlighted ABCL's faster-than-market growth.
    *   The impact of the GST exemption on insurance products was noted in the prior period; this transcript focuses on the operational impact and mitigation strategies. The current transcript notes the impact of loss of GST input credit in the Health Insurance P&L.
    *   A positive shift in tone towards aggressive adoption of AI and digital technologies across all segments was evident, positioning these as key drivers for overcoming challenges and enhancing performance.
