## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) delivered a strong Q2 FY23, reporting its **highest ever quarterly consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT)** of ₹488 Crore, a 30% year-on-year (y-o-y) increase. This performance was driven by accelerated growth across its key businesses, a rising active customer base (41 Million), and improved profitability metrics. The company's diversified financial services platform continues to leverage its integrated "One ABC" strategy, digital capabilities, and focus on customer-centricity to achieve sustainable growth and value accretion.

Key segment highlights include:
*   **NBFC (Aditya Birla Finance Limited):** Achieved its highest ever NIM of 5.13% (↑89 bps y-o-y) and robust loan book growth of 36% y-o-y, with a continued improvement in the Retail & SME mix (now 65%). Profit Before Tax (PBT) surged 26% y-o-y.
*   **Housing Finance (ABHFL):** Recorded its highest ever quarterly profit with a Return on Assets (RoA) of 2.4%, and 47% growth in disbursals y-o-y, with a focus on quality origination (92%+ disbursement at CIBIL >700 & NTC).
*   **Asset Management (ABSLAMC):** Delivered strong profit growth, with PBT/AAUM increasing to 34 bps y-o-y and AUM growing to ₹2,65,800 Crore. The company is focusing on increasing its passive and alternate asset segments and expanding its distribution network.
*   **Life Insurance (ABSLI):** Achieved its highest ever Net VNB Margin at 10.6% for FY21 (re-stated from previous summary but relevant context) and saw strong Q2 FY23 growth with Individual First Year Premium (FYP) up 16% y-o-y, ahead of private players. Embedded Value (EV) grew to ₹7,780 Crore with a RoEV of 15.7%.
*   **Health Insurance (ABHI):** Received a strategic investment of ~₹665 Crore from ADIA for a 9.99% stake, valuing the company at ~₹6,650 Crore, highlighting strong growth prospects. Gross premium grew 66% y-o-y in H1 FY23, with a focus on a differentiated, data-driven, and digitally enabled approach.

ABCL is actively enhancing its digital capabilities, expanding its distribution reach, and focusing on product innovation to meet diverse customer needs and drive future growth.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy focuses on achieving profitable growth through its diversified financial services platform, emphasizing:

*   **Digital Transformation & Customer Acquisition:** Continued investment in digital platforms (e.g., 97% of customers onboarded digitally, 87% of customer interactions on digital channels) to enhance customer experience, drive acquisition (e.g., 1.8 Mn customers via ecosystem partnerships in lending), and improve operational efficiency (e.g., 55% straight-through processing for service journeys).
*   **Granular Retail & SME Growth:** Expanding the Retail & SME loan book mix, increasing focus on the affordable housing segment, and driving SIPs and B-30 penetration in Asset Management. Branch network expansion in Tier 3/4 cities continues.
*   **Operational Efficiency and Cost Optimization:** Leveraging automation (550+ RPA robots), digital channels, and process improvements to drive cost efficiencies and improve operating metrics.
*   **Robust Risk Management and Portfolio Quality:** Maintaining strong asset quality through granular portfolio mix, high collection efficiencies (99.2% in NBFC), focus on quality origination (92%+ disbursement at CIBIL >700 & NTC), and utilizing data analytics for proactive risk identification.
*   **Integrated Platform Advantage ("One ABC"):** Maximizing cross-selling opportunities, enhancing customer lifetime value, and leveraging group synergies through a unified platform.
*   **Product Innovation:** Developing and launching new products (e.g., new Fixed Maturity product, 'Akshaya Plan' in LI, 'ACTIVFIT' in HI) to cater to specific customer segments and market demands.
*   **Value Accretion:** Progressing with strategic initiatives, including the significant investment from ADIA in the Health Insurance business.
