## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) announced on March 11, 2024, a significant strategic move: the proposed amalgamation of its wholly-owned subsidiary, Aditya Birla Finance Limited (ABFL), into the parent company. This transaction, subject to regulatory and other approvals and expected to take 9-12 months, aims to transform ABCL from a Non-Banking Financial Company - Core Investment Company (NBFC-CIC) into an NBFC - ICC (operating company). The amalgamation is driven by the objective of consolidating the business, realizing operational synergies, enhancing financial stability and flexibility through a unified, larger entity, and simplifying the corporate structure. Pro forma financials indicate an approximate 150 basis point increase in the total Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) for the amalgamated entity. This restructuring is also a proactive measure to comply with RBI's Scale Based Regulations, which mandate the listing of ABFL by September 30, 2025. An investor call was held on the same day to brief stakeholders on the Scheme of Amalgamation.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

The central strategic initiative discussed in this transcript is the amalgamation of ABFL into ABCL. The key motivations and anticipated outcomes are:

*   **Consolidation and Synergy:** Unifying ABFL's operations with ABCL to achieve operational efficiencies, cost reductions, and enhanced growth prospects.
*   **Improved Financial Strength:** Creating a larger, more financially robust entity with improved access to capital and greater overall financial stability.
*   **Structural Simplification:** Streamlining the group's structure by reducing the number of legal entities and simplifying compliance processes.
*   **Regulatory Compliance:** Proactively addressing RBI's Scale Based Regulations, which necessitate the listing of ABFL.
*   **Stakeholder Value Enhancement:** Fostering long-term sustainable growth and improved stakeholder value through a more integrated and efficient business model.
*   **Transition to Operating Company:** ABCL will shift from being a holding company to an operating company, allowing for direct business operations.
