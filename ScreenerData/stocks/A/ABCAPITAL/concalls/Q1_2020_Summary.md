## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported its Q1 FY21 results, demonstrating resilience across its diversified businesses despite the COVID-19 lockdown. The company achieved a consolidated PAT of Rs. 198 Crore, a 1.4x quarter-on-quarter increase, showcasing a recovery from the previous quarter. Key segments showed positive momentum: Health Insurance (HI) GWP surged 72% year-on-year, Life Insurance (LI) Individual First Year Premium (FYP) grew 5% year-on-year against an industry decline, and the NBFC and HFC businesses showed PBT growth (ex-COVID provision) driven by cost optimization and improving sub-segment mixes. ABCL emphasized the successful deployment of technology across its platform to enhance customer experience and operational efficiency.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy in Q1 FY21 continued to focus on resilience, technology deployment, and value-accretive growth:

*   **Digital Transformation:** Accelerated deployment of technology across all businesses for customer onboarding, servicing, collections, and distributor engagement. This included Paperless Onboarding, Video KYC, Chatbots, WhatsApp, and Voice Bots.
*   **Lending Business Optimization:** Cautious restart of lending with a gradual ramp-up, prioritizing retail and SME segments. Focus on digital onboarding (75% in NBFC) and rebalancing the portfolio towards target segments. Emphasis on granular loan books and secured lending.
*   **Health Insurance Expansion:** Continued aggressive growth in GWP (72% YoY), driven by a strong retail mix (73%) and the unique "Health First Model" focusing on incentivized wellness and personalized engagement. The aim is to reach break-even by Q4 FY21-22.
*   **Life Insurance Value Enhancement:** Focus on growing Individual FYP (5% YoY, outperforming industry) through digital sourcing (96%) and improving product mix with higher protection and controlled ULIPs. Emphasis on persistency and cost management.
*   **Asset Management Growth:** Driving AUM growth (8% QoQ) with a strong rebound in Equity AUM (19% QoQ), retail and B-30 traction, and increased SIP market share. Continued focus on digital transactions and customer acquisition.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Ongoing opex reduction across businesses, with a target of 8% reduction over annualized FY20 cost base and 10% reduction in consolidated non-vol-linked opex (ex-ABHI) q-o-q.
