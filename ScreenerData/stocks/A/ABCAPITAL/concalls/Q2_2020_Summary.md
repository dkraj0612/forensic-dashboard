## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) showcased a strong recovery in Q2 FY21, with consolidated revenue increasing by 14% quarter-on-quarter (q-o-q) to Rs. 4,879 Crore and Profit After Tax (PAT) growing by 33% q-o-q to Rs. 264 Crore. This performance reflects a significant rebound as the economy reopens. The company highlighted robust technological deployment across its platform to enhance customer experience and operational efficiency. Key business segments demonstrated positive momentum: NBFC saw expanded Net Interest Margins (NIM) and increased Pre-Provision Operating Profit (PPOP), while Life Insurance (LI) Individual First Year Premium (FYP) grew 7% year-on-year (y-o-y), significantly outperforming private players. Health Insurance (HI) continued its rapid growth, and Asset Management (AMC) experienced a strong revival in Assets Under Management (AUM), particularly in equity and retail segments. The company also emphasized its focus on cost optimization, with aggregate non-volatility linked opex decreasing by 8% y-o-y.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL's strategy in Q2 FY21 continued to focus on leveraging the economic reopening and its diversified platform:

*   **Technology Deployment:** Accelerated implementation of digital initiatives across all businesses, including paperless onboarding, video KYC, chatbots, and WhatsApp services, to improve customer experience and operational efficiency.
*   **Lending Business Revival:** Witnessed a strong revival in disbursements, with September 2020 levels returning to pre-COVID norms. The focus remained on growing retail and SME segments, with increased digitalization in sourcing and underwriting.
*   **Health Insurance Expansion:** Continued to be the fastest-growing health insurer, with GWP growth of 75% y-o-y, driven by a strong retail mix and the "Health First Model."
*   **Life Insurance Value Enhancement:** Achieved positive y-o-y growth in Individual FYP, significantly outperforming the industry, and saw a substantial increase in Group NBP. The focus remained on digital sourcing, improving persistency, and managing expenses.
*   **Asset Management Growth:** Experienced a strong rebound in AUM, driven by retail, SIP, and B-30 growth, supported by robust investment performance and a growing retail franchise. Digital transactions and investor engagement were key priorities.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Continued focus on opex reduction, with aggregate non-volatility linked and excl. ABHI opex down 8% y-o-y.
