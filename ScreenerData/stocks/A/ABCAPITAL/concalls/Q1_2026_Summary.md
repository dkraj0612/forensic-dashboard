## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) reported a solid Q1 FY26, demonstrating continued growth across its diversified businesses despite a fluctuating global economic environment. Consolidated profit after tax (PAT) grew by 10% year-on-year to INR 835 Crore, with consolidated revenue also rising 10% YoY to INR 11,333 Crore. The NBFC business saw robust AUM growth of 22% YoY to INR 1.31 Trillion, driven by strong disbursements in personal and consumer loans (up 65% YoY), while maintaining a cautious approach in the smaller ticket unsecured MSME segment. The Housing Finance (HFC) business continued its high growth trajectory with disbursements up 76% YoY and AUM growing 70% YoY. Asset Management Company (AMC) achieved an average AUM of INR 4.03 Trillion, a 14% YoY increase, and the Life Insurance business posted an impressive individual first-year premium growth of 23% YoY. The Health Insurance business also showed strong momentum with a 40% YoY growth in Gross Written Premium (GWP) (excluding multi-year guidelines). ABCL's omnichannel strategy, particularly through its ABCD app and Udyog Plus platform, continues to scale, enhancing customer engagement and distribution reach.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL continues to execute its strategy of driving quality and profitable growth through a customer-centric approach, leveraging data, digital, and technology. Key strategic enablers include:

*   **NBFC Portfolio Management:** Continued focus on strengthening internal sourcing for personal and consumer loans, with stabilization observed and sequential growth resuming. A cautious approach remains for the smaller ticket unsecured MSME segment, while secured SME and corporate/mid-market segments show steady growth.
*   **HFC Business Expansion:** Maintaining strong growth momentum through investments in distribution, data, and digital capabilities, aiming for operating leverage and improved RoA.
*   **Omnichannel Distribution:** Expanding reach and customer engagement through the ABCD D2C platform and the Udyog Plus B2B platform for MSMEs, alongside a growing branch network.
*   **Insurance Business Growth:** Life Insurance aims for sustained growth in individual premiums and VNB margins. Health Insurance focuses on market share gains and improving its combined ratio, driven by its differentiated "Health First" model.
*   **Capital and Risk Management:** Prudent risk management remains a bedrock, protecting capital and ensuring risk-calibrated returns.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **NBFC Personal & Consumer Loans Growth:** Management stated the environment seemed to have settled and building blocks were in place. Actual disbursements grew by 65% YoY and 28% QoQ, aligning with this positive outlook.
    *   **NBFC Small Ticket Unsecured MSME Segment:** Management reiterated its cautious approach and tightened underwriting norms. Actual disbursements in this segment declined 8% sequentially.
    *   **NBFC Credit Cost:** Management expects credit costs to remain in a similar range for FY26. The reported Q1 FY26 credit cost was 1.30%, which is within the expected range.
    *   **HFC RoA Improvement:** Management reiterated its guidance to achieve a RoA of 2% to 2.2% over the next eight quarters. The reported Q1 FY26 RoA was 1.59%, showing sequential improvement.
    *   **Life Insurance VNB Margin Guidance:** Management maintained guidance to expand Net VNB margins to over 18% for the year and double Net VNB in three years. The reported Q1 FY26 VNB margin was 7.5%, with management indicating margin expansion drivers at play.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** Management aims to strive for a 100% Combined Ratio as per old accounting regulations in the current year. The reported Q1 FY26 Combined Ratio was 107%.
    *   **HFC Opex to AUM Reduction:** Management projected a reduction of 100-130 basis points over the next eight quarters. Q1 FY26 saw a 32 bps sequential improvement in Opex to Assets.

*   **Narrative/Tone Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   Management acknowledged global macroeconomic uncertainty due to tariff negotiations and geopolitical tensions but highlighted the strength and stability of the Indian economy.
    *   The cautious approach in the unsecured MSME segment was explicitly attributed to ongoing uncertainties and tightened underwriting norms, which is consistent with previous commentary.
    *   The narrative around the personal and consumer loans segment shifted from cautious management to a more optimistic tone regarding stabilization and growth resumption.

*   **Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines for Next Quarter/Year:**
    *   **NBFC NIM Improvement:** Management expects NIMs to start improving in the next few quarters as the higher-yielding unsecured loan portfolio (personal, consumer, and business loans to SMEs) grows.
    *   **NBFC Personal & Consumer Loans Growth:** The company will continue to track this portfolio closely as it grows, given the stabilization and improved credit quality indicators.
    *   **NBFC Capital Adequacy:** Sufficiently funded for the next 9 to 12 months; capital raise to be considered post that period.
    *   **HFC RoA:** Well on track to achieve 2% to 2.2% over the next eight quarters.
    *   **Life Insurance Net VNB Margin:** Maintain guidance to expand Net VNB margins to over 18% for FY26.
    *   **Life Insurance Individual FYP Growth:** Maintain guidance to grow Individual FYP at a CAGR of 20%+ for the next 3 years.
    *   **Life Insurance Net VNB Growth:** Maintain guidance to double Net VNB in the next 3 years.
    *   **Health Insurance Combined Ratio:** Strive to achieve a 100% Combined Ratio as per old accounting regulations in the current year.
    *   **NBFC Small Ticket Unsecured Loan Segment:** Continue to watch performance closely and maintain a cautious approach.
    *   **HFC Opex to AUM Reduction:** Expect further operating leverage to kick in, targeting a reduction of 130 basis points over the next eight quarters.
    *   **Credit Cost (NBFC):** Expected to remain in a similar range for FY26.
