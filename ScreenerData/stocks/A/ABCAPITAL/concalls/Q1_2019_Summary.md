## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL) presented its Q1 FY20 unaudited financial results, demonstrating robust all-round performance across its diversified businesses. The company reported strong revenue growth and improved profitability, driven by consistent performance in its lending, asset management, and insurance segments. The "ONE" brand strategy continues to be central, emphasizing an integrated financial services platform. The transcript highlights continued investments in technology and expansion of distribution networks, particularly in smaller cities and through partnerships. Profitability was generally strong, with specific segment improvements noted, though some areas like Health Insurance still operate at a loss while scaling up.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABCL continues to focus on a diversified portfolio and value-accretive growth, leveraging the "ABC platform" and broader ABG ecosystem. Key strategic drivers include:

*   **Lending Business Diversification:** Shifting towards higher-margin segments like SME and Retail, which grew by 25% y-o-y. Focus on cash-flow based underwriting and maintaining robust asset quality with Stage 3 assets at 1.24% (ex-IL&FS).
*   **Asset Management Retail Expansion:** Deepening penetration in B-30 cities (34% of retail AUM) and growing the SIP book and IFA network. Expanding presence through PSU and Co-operative Bank tie-ups.
*   **Life Insurance Value Creation:** Focusing on value-accretive products, increasing the protection mix, and leveraging a balanced distribution strategy with partnerships contributing 50% of individual FYP.
*   **Health Insurance Scale-up:** Rapidly building distribution capacity (10 Banca tie-ups) and expanding provider networks (5,800+ hospitals). Focusing on customer value proposition and product diversification.
*   **Digital Adoption & Technology Investment:** Automating customer journeys, strengthening collections infrastructure, and enhancing underwriting capabilities.
*   **Capital Management:** Maintaining comfortable capital adequacy (CRAR at 17.6% for NBFC and 17.2% for HFC) and optimizing ALM with a diversified borrowing mix.

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q1 FY20 Context):**
    *   **Lending Book Growth & Mix Shift:** Management's stated intention to focus on SME + Retail and higher-margin segments is evident. SME + Retail grew by 25% y-o-y. The NBFC NIM expanded by 50 bps to 5.39%, and the HFC book grew 29% y-o-y, aligning with growth objectives.
    *   **Life Insurance FYP Growth:** The target for strong rebound and individual FYP growth is reflected in the 30% y-o-y growth, significantly outperforming the industry.
    *   **Health Insurance Break-even:** The target to break-even in FY21-22 is supported by the reduction in PBT loss to `65 Crore from a peak of `73 Crore in Q2 FY19. The combined ratio improved to 146% from 190%.
    *   **Asset Management Retail Expansion & IFA Network:** The continued focus on retail expansion and B-30 cities is evident with B-30 contributing 34% of retail AUM. The IFA network has grown by 7,400 in one year.
