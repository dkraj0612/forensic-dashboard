## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL), formed through a demerger and acquisition strategy, is positioned as India's largest branded apparel player. The company, previously part of Aditya Birla Nuvo Limited (ABNL), has consolidated its fashion and lifestyle businesses, including Madura Fashion and Pantaloons Fashion & Retail Ltd. The Q1 2015 transcript highlights a strategic shift towards modern retail, focusing on building scale, enhancing brand portfolio, and leveraging multi-channel distribution. India's favorable demographic profile and growing discretionary spending are identified as key growth drivers. ABFRL aims to capitalize on opportunities in various apparel segments, from premium to value fashion, with a strong emphasis on building consumer loyalty and driving retail excellence.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Consolidation:** Demerger of Madura Fashion into Pantaloons Fashion & Retail Ltd. to create a unified branded apparel entity.
*   **Brand Portfolio Expansion:** Strengthening existing brands (Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, Allen Solly, Peter England) and introducing new ones across various segments (super-premium, value, fast fashion, kids).
*   **Retail Network Expansion:** Rapidly expanding the retail footprint through new store launches and refreshes, aiming for a new store every two weeks in FY15 for Pantaloons.
*   **Channel Strategy:** Transitioning from wholesale to retail, with a focus on multi-channel distribution and increasing modern retail's share.
*   **Consumer Engagement & Loyalty:** Building consumer loyalty through initiatives like a large loyalty program (60% of sales) and understanding consumer needs via social media.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Investing in IT systems (SAP rollout), supply chain efficiency, and in-house design capabilities.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Favorable Indian macroeconomics including GDP growth, young population, rising discretionary spending, urbanization, and changing consumer habits.
*   **Strategic Focus Areas:** Menswear (led by Madura), Womenswear (led by Pantaloons), luxury/super-premium, fast fashion, and kids' wear.
