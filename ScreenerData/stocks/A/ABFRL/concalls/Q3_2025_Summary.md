## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) reported Q3 FY'25 results, indicating a continued subdued consumption environment despite robust festive and wedding season demand. The company achieved consolidated revenue of INR 4,305 crores, a 3% YoY increase, with EBITDA at INR 683 crores (15.9% margin), a 13% YoY growth. This performance saw EBITDA margin expansion across all businesses. The company is progressing with the demerger of ABLBL, expected within 2-3 months, and has successfully raised USD 490 million in equity capital for the demerged ABFRL entity, making it debt-free with ample cash for growth. Key businesses like Lifestyle Brands showed strong LTL growth, while Pantaloons and Ethnic businesses also demonstrated improved profitability and revenue growth, albeit with strategic network rationalizations in some segments. The net debt for consolidated ABFRL is approximately INR 1,800 crores post-QIP, with further reduction expected post-preferential issue, aiming for ABLBL to start FY'26 with INR 700 crores debt and demerged ABFRL with INR 1,300 crores cash.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Portfolio Optimization:** Continued focus on rationalizing less productive channels and stores across businesses (Lifestyle, Pantaloons, TCNS, Forever 21 offline) to improve overall profitability and operational efficiency.
*   **Premiumization & Value Segmentation:** Pantaloons is strategically shifting towards a mid-market player with a focus on premiumization and exiting value retailing. Style Up is being scaled as a distinct value segment offering.
*   **Ethnic Business Consolidation & Growth:** Strengthening the largest ethnic portfolio in India, with designer brands showing robust growth. Tasva demonstrated positive EBITDA and accelerated store expansion plans. TCNS is undergoing optimization for profitable growth.
*   **Digital & Emerging Business Focus:** TMRW continues to show strong organic growth, with plans for external fundraising. Emerging growth segments within ABLBL are seeing incremental revenue and margin improvement, despite the impact of phasing out Forever 21 offline.
*   **Network Expansion & Re-acceleration:** After a period of consolidation and optimization, ABFRL is poised for aggressive retail expansion, particularly in Lifestyle Brands and Style Up.
*   **Financial Strength:** Successful equity raise for the demerged ABFRL entity significantly strengthens its balance sheet, making it debt-free and cash-rich to fund future growth.
