## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) reported Q2 FY'25 results on November 8, 2024, indicating continued subdued demand but with positive festive and wedding season indicators. The company achieved consolidated revenue of INR 3,644 crores, a 13% YoY increase, with EBITDA at INR 410 crores (11.2% margin), a 11% YoY growth. This performance includes the full consolidation of TCNS following its amalgamation on September 1, 2024. The company is progressing with the demerger of ABLBL and ABFRL, with an expected completion by the end of the financial year. Profitability for established businesses remained robust, while high-growth segments like Ethnic and TMRW saw significant revenue increases with margin improvements. Exceptional items included a INR 98 crore impairment for Forever 21 and a INR 121 crore mark-to-market gain on increased stake in Tarun Tahiliani. Consolidated PAT was negative INR 215 crores, impacted by higher depreciation, amortization, and interest costs due to TCNS inclusion. The store network stands at 4,538 stores covering 12 million sq. ft.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Portfolio Integration & Demerger:** Successfully completed the amalgamation of TCNS and is progressing with the demerger of ABLBL and ABFRL, aiming for completion by year-end.
*   **Focus on Profitable Growth:** Emphasis on improving margins through better planning, product enhancements, stringent markdown management, and deprioritizing poor-quality channels.
*   **Established Business Strength:** ABLBL segment continues to deliver robust margins, with Lifestyle brands focusing on product innovation, casualization, and elevated retail experiences.
*   **High-Growth Segment Expansion:** Demerged ABFRL segment, including Pantaloons, Ethnic, and TMRW, demonstrated significant revenue growth and margin improvement.
*   **Pantaloons Turnaround:** Improved profitability driven by business model enhancements, focus on key markets, refined product propositions, and better planning. Style Up format scaling well.
*   **Ethnic Business Consolidation:** Integration of TCNS and Tarun Tahiliani couture business strengthens the portfolio. Designer brands showing strong growth.
*   **Digital Portfolio Growth (TMRW):** Scaling rapidly with a focus on Gen-Z and Millennials, demonstrating significant organic growth.
*   **Luxury Retail Stability:** The Collective and mono brands maintained profitable double-digit LTL growth.
*   **Working Capital Efficiency:** Tight inventory control and improved planning led to better capital efficiency.
