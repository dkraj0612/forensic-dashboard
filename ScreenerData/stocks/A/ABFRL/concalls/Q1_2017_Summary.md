## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) is solidifying its position as India's largest pure-play fashion lifestyle company, aiming for significant scale and market leadership across menswear and womenswear. The company is leveraging a diversified brand portfolio, including strong established brands like Van Heusen and Allen Solly, alongside strategic acquisitions such as Pantaloons and Forever 21. ABFRL is aggressively expanding its retail footprint, emphasizing value fashion and a multi-channel distribution strategy. The company's focus on digital transformation through an omni-channel program and investment in brand building is intended to drive sustainable, future-ready growth, despite short-term impacts from strategic adjustments and market headwinds.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Leadership:** Aiming to be India's first billion-dollar pure fashion player and the #1 player across men's and women's wear segments.
*   **Brand Portfolio Expansion:** Strengthening iconic brands, growing through extensions and distribution. Acquired Forever 21 India rights for women's fast fashion and finalized partnership with Simon Carter, London. Exploring other partnerships for white spaces. Entry into men's innerwear and active-wear planned.
*   **Retail Network Optimization:** Rationalized retail network by shutting down 76 stores to improve network health. Added 12 new stores to the Pantaloons network, now with 4 franchisee stores. Piloted franchisee model for Pantaloons. Transitioning Pantaloons to a value fashion retailer. Introduced concept stores for kids and women.
*   **Channel Strategy:** Driving digital transformation through an omni-channel program. Key omni-channel functionalities rolled out across 100 stores, with plans for 400 by Q2 FY17. Leveraging e-commerce through partnerships and omni-channel execution.
*   **Consumer Engagement:** Maintaining focus on loyal customers (~55-75% of sales).
*   **Financial Strategy:** Focused on building a healthy, sustainable business. Calibrated discounts to maintain 'intrinsic' brand premium, investing in brand building, rationalizing cost structure. This strategy led to lower short-term sales and profits in Q1 FY17.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Casuals & Womenswear categories growing faster than the market. Value fashion growing rapidly as consumers upgrade. Women's fast fashion growth driven by international brands. Formalwear segment experiencing slower growth.
*   **Product Mix:** ~63% of Pantaloons revenue from exclusive brands. Madura menswear portfolio ~INR 4,300 Cr; womenswear ~INR 1,150 Cr.
