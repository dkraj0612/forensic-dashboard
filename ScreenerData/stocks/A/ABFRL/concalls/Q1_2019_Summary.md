## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) continues its strategic expansion and consolidation, reporting growth across key financial metrics in Q1 FY20 despite a challenging market. The company emphasizes its commitment to a calibrated business model, focusing on brand building, aggressive retail expansion, and a ramp-up in e-commerce. ABFRL is actively diversifying its portfolio, with significant investments in the ethnic wear segment and new growth areas like innerwear and global brands. The focus remains on enhancing profitability and building a sustainable, future-ready business through strategic acquisitions, channel expansion, and consistent brand investment, particularly targeting growth in Tier 2-4 towns and leveraging the influence of Indian design.

*   **Growth Strategy:** Focus on revenue growth (8% overall, 6% in Q1 FY20), EBITDA growth (28% in Q1 FY20), and enhanced brand visibility. Aggressive retail expansion and e-commerce ramp-up are key.
*   **Portfolio Expansion:**
