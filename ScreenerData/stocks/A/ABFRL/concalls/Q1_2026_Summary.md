## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) has reported its Q1 FY'26 results, demonstrating continued profitable growth amidst a cautious market environment. The company's focus remains on disciplined execution and operational efficiency, with all businesses maintaining or improving margins. Growth was primarily driven by newer, high-potential segments, which now contribute 44% to overall revenue, up from 37% last year. Key developments include ServiceNow Ventures' INR 437 crore investment in TMRW, leveraging AI for enhanced speed to market and customer experience. The demerger process is complete, with Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) successfully listing on June 23, 2025. ABFRL's Q1 FY'26 revenue grew by 9% year-on-year to INR 1,831 crores, with EBITDA rising 38% year-on-year to INR 169 crores. The company holds INR 2,070 crores in gross cash as of June 2025.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q1 FY'26 Revenue:** INR 1,831 crores (9% YoY growth)
*   **Q1 FY'26 EBITDA:** INR 169 crores (38% YoY growth)
*   **Q1 FY'26 EBITDA (Excluding TMRW):** INR 245 crores (49% YoY growth)
*   **Q1 FY'26 Ethnic Business Revenue:** INR 436 crores (25% YoY growth)
*   **Q1 FY'26 Ethnic Business EBITDA Margin:** Improved significantly to positive EBITDA (vs. loss last year), with 1600 bps expansion.

*   **Q1 FY'26 Pantaloons Revenue:** INR 1,094 crores (4% adjusted growth YoY, LTL flat)
*   **Q1 FY'26 Pantaloons EBITDA Margin:** 17.1% (marginally lower due to lower sales, but consistently above 18% on EBITDA level)

*   **Q1 FY'26 Tasva Sales Growth:** 72% YoY growth (39% LTL growth)
*   **Q1 FY'26 TCNS Revenue:** Growth over last year, with 4% retail LTL growth post-April.
*   **Q1 FY'26 ABFRL Gross Cash:** INR 2,070 crores (as of June 2025)
*   **Q1 FY'26 TMRW Investment:** INR 437 crores from ServiceNow Ventures
*   **Q1 FY'26 ABFRL Investment in TMRW (Cumulative):** INR 770 crores

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Focus on Profitable Growth:** Continued emphasis on disciplined execution and operational efficiency to drive profitability across all businesses.
*   **Growth in Newer Segments:** Accelerating momentum in high-potential segments like ethnic wear and digital-first brands (TMRW) which are becoming key growth engines.
*   **Portfolio Optimization:** Ongoing efforts to refine and optimize existing portfolios, such as TCNS and Pantaloons, for profitable scale.
*   **Network Expansion:** Strategic store additions and upgrades, particularly for Pantaloons, Style Up, Tasva, and TMRW's offline presence.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Leveraging technology, including AI with ServiceNow Ventures' investment in TMRW, to enhance speed to market and customer experience.
*   **Brand Strengthening:** Continued focus on building differentiated product offerings, sharper brand positioning, and stronger consumer connect.
