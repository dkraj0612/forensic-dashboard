## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) concluded FY'25 with a transformative Q4, marked by the successful demerger of its Western wear brands into Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) and the continued operation of the remaining businesses as ABFRL. Both entities are now positioned for distinct growth trajectories. ABLBL is set to list by the end of June, while ABFRL has secured INR 2,350 crores in cash post-demerger, strengthening its balance sheet for aggressive growth. Despite prevailing macro headwinds impacting consumer discretionary spending, both entities demonstrated resilient performance with robust profitability. ABLBL achieved EBITDA margin expansion of 200 basis points, and the demerged ABFRL more than doubled its EBITDA. The company's strategic focus remains on profitable scale-up, portfolio optimization, and disciplined execution across its diverse business segments, including ethnic wear, value retail, luxury, and digital-first brands.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Demerger Completion & Independent Growth:** The successful demerger of ABLBL and ABFRL establishes two focused entities to pursue independent value creation journeys with strengthened balance sheets.
*   **Portfolio Optimization & Profitability Focus:** Continued emphasis on rationalizing underperforming channels and stores (e.g., Forever 21 offline) and enhancing distribution quality to drive profitable growth.
*   **Brand Portfolio Development:** Strengthening core brands and expanding high-potential segments like Ethnic Wear (designer-led and premium) and Value Retail (Style Up).
*   **Network Expansion:** Aggressive retail rollout plans for both ABLBL (300+ new stores in 12 months) and ABFRL (significant store additions for Pantaloons, Style Up, and Tasva).
*   **Financial Fortification:** Significant cash infusion for demerged ABFRL to fund multi-year growth initiatives across its platforms.
*   **Digital & Emerging Businesses:** Continued focus on TMRW for scalable growth and potential external fundraising.
