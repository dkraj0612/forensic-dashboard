## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) is solidifying its position as India's largest branded apparel player, following the strategic acquisition of Madura Garments and the ongoing integration with Pantaloons Fashion & Retail. The company leverages a robust portfolio of established brands across various segments, from premium (Louis Philippe, Van Heusen) to value (People) and super-premium (The Collective). ABFRL is aggressively expanding its retail footprint, aiming for rapid store openings and a significant increase in modern retail penetration. The company emphasizes building consumer loyalty through a large loyalty program and enhancing operational efficiency via IT investments like SAP. ABFRL is well-positioned to capitalize on India's favorable demographics, rising discretionary spending, and the growing organized apparel market.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Consolidation & Brand Portfolio:** Unified apparel business under ABFRL. Strengthening power brands like Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, and expanding into new segments with launches like "People" (value) and "The Collective" (super-premium). JV with Hackett established.
*   **Retail Network Expansion:** Aggressive store expansion for Pantaloons, targeting a new store every two weeks in FY15. Enhanced store network for Pantaloons with 14 new stores and 22 refreshes in FY14.
*   **Channel Strategy:** Transitioning to a retail-led model with a focus on multi-channel distribution. Wholesale contributes ~25% of Madura revenue, and Direct Selling (DS) contributes ~20%.
*   **Consumer Engagement & Loyalty:** Strong loyalty program (4.5 million members, 60% of sales). Focus on consumer engagement and building loyalty. Utilizing social media analytics ("EPIC Centre").
*   **Operational Excellence:** SAP rollout completed in all stores (130+). Four RDCs (Regional Distribution Centers) expected to be operational in the next 6 months. Investments in IT for differentiation.
*   **Growth Drivers:** India's healthy GDP growth, young demographic (54% below 25), rising discretionary spending, increasing per capita apparel consumption, and low modern trade penetration creating opportunities in organized retail.
*   **Strategic Focus Areas:** Diversified product categories including Men's Casual, Women's Western wear, Men's Formals, Kids & Accessories. ABFRL aims to be the largest menswear and womenswear retailer in India.
