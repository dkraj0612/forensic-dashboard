## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) reported a Q4 FY'26 with 16% YoY revenue growth to INR 1,990 crores, driven by strong performance across segments. Pantaloons was a highlight, achieving 19% YoY growth. TMRW also showed robust momentum with 45% YoY growth. The company continued its disciplined store expansion, adding over 180 stores in the year. While reported net loss stood at INR 164 crores (including exceptional items), the underlying operating performance showed improvement with a reduced loss of INR 235 crores (adjusted for one-offs) compared to INR 289 crores in the prior year. Full-year revenue reached INR 8,177 crores, an 11% YoY increase, with adjusted EBITDA growing 23% to INR 903 crores and margins improving to 11%. ABFRL maintained a strong liquidity position with INR 1,545 crores in gross cash at the consolidated level as of March 2026.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pantaloons Revitalization:** Continued focus on a strategic shift towards a stronger customer proposition, elevated in-store experience, and sharpened execution, with positive early results seen in merchandising, store design, and online business.
*   **Ethnic Business Growth:** Scaling the ethnic portfolio, comprising designer-led and premium ethnic wear, with continued strong LTL growth and margin expansion.
*   **New Business Investments:** Disciplined expansion of newer formats like OWND!, Tasva, and TMRW, focusing on building scale and long-term growth platforms.
*   **Digital & Omnichannel Expansion:** Growing TMRW's digital-first brands and expanding their omnichannel presence, including a growing store footprint.
*   **Luxury Market Penetration:** Encouraging early traction for Galeries Lafayette, focusing on curated luxury experiences and driving consumer engagement.
*   **Network Expansion & Optimization:** Calibrated store additions, focusing on larger format stores for Pantaloons and disciplined scaling for new businesses.
*   **Financial Discipline:** Maintaining operational leverage, managing costs, and securing funding for growth initiatives across the portfolio.
