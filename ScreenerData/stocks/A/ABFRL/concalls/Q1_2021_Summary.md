## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL) reported significant year-on-year revenue growth in Q1 FY22, recovering to approximately 40% of its pre-COVID Q1 FY20 base. This recovery was driven by a sharp increase in operational days, strong e-commerce performance, and a robust rebound in physical store footfalls as lockdown restrictions eased. While the company posted an EBITDA loss for the quarter, this was attributed to factors like reduced fixed cost rebates and increased variable costs in line with sales recovery, alongside normalized employee benefit expenses. ABFRL is strategically navigating the post-COVID landscape by prioritizing employee and consumer safety, accelerating digitization, and enhancing its product offerings with a focus on casualization and premiumization. The company is expanding its omni-channel capabilities, investing in digital transformation, and scaling its diverse brand portfolio across multiple segments, including apparel, innerwear, athleisure, and ethnic wear, with a clear vision to build a comprehensive fashion powerhouse.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q1 FY22 Consolidated Revenue:** INR 812 Cr (150% higher than Q1 FY21)
*   **Q1 FY22 Consolidated EBITDA:** INR (145) Cr
*   **Q1 FY22 Revenue vs. Q1 FY20 (Pre-COVID Base):** ~40% recovery
*   **E-commerce Sales Growth:** More than 2.5x
*   **Loyal Customers:** 45% of revenues

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Revenue Growth & Recovery:** Driven by increased operational days, aggressive e-commerce growth, and recovery in physical retail as lockdowns eased.
*   **E-commerce & Omni-channel:** Continued rapid growth across own brand websites and third-party marketplaces. Strengthened omni-channel coverage with over 1,300 omni-enabled stores. Focus on hyperlocal capabilities for same-day delivery.
*   **Portfolio Diversification & Brand Building:**
    *   **Lifestyle Brands:** Revenue grew 2.3x YoY to INR 435 Cr. Retail LTL growth at 187%. Strong performance in casual wear (59% share) and kids wear. Expansion in small-town formats (PE Red ~350 stores, AS Prime 17 stores operational).
    *   **Pantaloons:** Revenue grew 2.7x YoY to INR 220 Cr. Retail L2L at 156%. Expansion of categories like loungewear and infant wear. E-commerce sales grew ~3x.
    *   **Innerwear & Athleisure:** Revenue 2x YoY, ahead of pre-COVID levels. Strong demand for athleisure and active wear. Women's category identified as a strong growth pillar. Focus on distribution expansion.
    *   **Youth Western Wear:** Revenue grew ~4x YoY. Profitable model established with new terms. Focus on increasing local sourcing.
    *   **Super Premium Brands:** Revenue grew over 5x YoY. Collective.in establishing as a premier luxury portal. Marketplaces model initiated for scale.
    *   **Ethnic Wear:** Revenue doubled YoY. New men's ethnic brand planned for festive season. Jewelry and Home categories growing well (~2.5x).
    *   **Sabyasachi:** Revenue grew ~4x YoY. Opened first international outlet in Dubai.
*   **Cost Management:** Swift control of costs in line with sales impact. Significant reduction in rental expenses (Rs. 75 Cr. vs. Rs. 159 Cr. in LY) and discretionary expenses.
*   **Product Innovation:** Enhanced pivot towards relevant product categories like casual wear and loungewear. Piloting new categories and segments.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Continued investment in technology, omni-channel operations, digital marketing, and analytics.
