## Executive Overview

Ami Organics (AMI) reported robust Q4 FY24 results, achieving an all-time high quarterly revenue of INR 225.0 Cr, a 20.7% YoY and 35.2% QoQ increase. This performance was driven by strong growth in both Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates (18% YoY, 47% QoQ) and Specialty Chemicals (36% YoY). EBITDA stood at INR 43.2 Cr, up 5.9% YoY and 62.8% QoQ, with an EBITDA margin of 19.2%. Key developments include the commencement of commercial operations for Ami Organics Electrolytes Private Limited, the acquisition of three process patents, and a Gold Rating from EcoVadis for sustainability. Management expressed confidence in sustaining growth, targeting 20-25% revenue growth for FY25.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates:** Strong growth driven by advanced intermediates for regulated and generic APIs and NCEs. Focus on chronic therapy areas with a wide basket of ~500 molecules and significant global market share in key molecules. CDMO contracts are being aggressively pursued, with an existing project expected to ramp up from H2 FY25.
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Robust YoY growth, boosted by the acquisition of Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC). Organic specialty chemical business grew by 18% in Q4 FY24. Expansion into battery chemicals and semiconductor chemicals is a key focus. BFC integration has begun, with steady organic growth expected, and potential for step-up jumps as new clients and products are onboarded.
*   **Electrolyte Additives (Battery Chemicals):** Commercial operations have commenced with firm orders. Supplies are expected to start from Q2 FY25, with an anticipated 100% growth in FY26. Capacity expansion plans are in place.
*   **Electrolyte Solutions (CDMO):** This segment is in its nascent stage, with a collaboration with a global MNC player. Further details are expected as the project progresses.
*   **Sustainability & Innovation:** Achieved Gold Rating accreditation from EcoVadis. Secured three process patents during the quarter, with a focus on developing indigenous, cost-effective, and environmentally friendly processes.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** Ankleshwar Unit 2 was inaugurated and is operational, focusing on advanced pharmaceutical intermediates. The company continues to invest in R&D and advanced technologies.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**

*   **FY24 Revenue Growth:** The reported 16.3% revenue growth for FY24 aligns with or slightly exceeds the previously guided 15-18% range, demonstrating consistency.
*   **Q4 FY24 EBITDA Margin:** The reported 19.2% EBITDA margin met the guided sequential improvement to 18-20%. However, it remains below the Q4 FY23 margin of 21.9% and the medium-term target of 23-26%. Management attributes the YoY decline to unfavorable product mix, higher depreciation, and finance costs, while sequential improvement was driven by operating leverage and lower freight costs.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Growth:** The 36% YoY growth in specialty chemicals for Q4 FY24 is a strong performance, with organic growth of 18% excluding BFC.
*   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization:** Commercial operations have commenced as guided, with initial supplies expected from Q2 FY25.
*   **Ankleshwar Facility:** The facility has been inaugurated and one production block is operational. The ramp-up of other blocks is planned over the next 3 years.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals Integration:** Integration has started, and the business is expected to see steady organic growth. The transcript clarifies that the sales decline was due to the transition and customer qualification process, with full conversion expected in Q1/Q2 FY25.
*   **Pricing Environment:** Management perceives prices from China to have stabilized with a potential upward bias, aligning with previous guidance.
*   **Working Capital:** The company maintained tight control on working capital, leading to strong cash flow generation from operations.

**New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**

*   **FY25 Revenue Growth:** Targeting 20-25% revenue growth for FY25.
*   **FY25 Revenue Split:** Expectation of a 40:60 revenue split between H1 and H2 FY25, driven by CDMO contract commercialization in H2.
*   **FY25 Capex:** Approximately INR 250 crores, including INR 70 crores for Ankleshwar Unit 2 balance, INR 100 crores for electrolyte additive infrastructure (Unit 3), INR 50 crores for captive solar power, and INR 30-40 crores for maintenance. Similar capex expected for FY26 for maintenance.
*   **EBITDA Margin Outlook:** Targeting to return to historical margins of 21% and then see steady-state growth. Medium-term target remains 23-26%.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Margins:** Specific margin targets were not explicitly re-stated in this call, but management anticipates steady-state recovery in gross margins.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals:** Full conversion to JV expected in Q1 or Q2 FY25. Expectation of normalization and approvals to complete in Q1 and Q2 FY25.
*   **Fermion Contract:** Commercial supply ramp-up expected towards the end of FY25 (consistent with prior guidance).
*   **Electrolyte Additives:** Commercial supply to customers expected by Q2 FY25. Capacity expansion for larger plant being decided after 2.5 years. Targeting ~10 additives now.
*   **Electrolyte CDMO Business:** Finalizing capex and JV structure for the project with a global MNC player. An announcement is expected soon.
*   **Ankleshwar Facility (Unit 2):** One production block commercialized. Targeting 30-35% utilization for breakeven for individual blocks. Expecting other 2 blocks to fill up over the next 3 years, reaching >60-65% utilization.
*   **Unit 3 (Specialty Chemicals):** Currently at ~50% utilization for FY24. Expected to ramp up significantly over the next 2-3 years. Dedicated to specialty chemicals business (excluding semiconductor).
*   **Pricing Environment:** Prices are stabilized, with potential upward bias in H2.
*   **Working Capital:** Positive cash flow generation from operations maintained.
*   **Debt Prepayment:** Intention to raise equity to deleverage the balance sheet and prepay existing loans. Debt ceiling increase is primarily for statutory compliance.
*   **Asset Turn:** Current asset turn around 2.8x. Expecting 2.5x to 3x on a net block basis in 2-2.5 years.
*   **Apixaban:** Strong product with good visibility; ramp-up expected from H2 FY25, peaking in FY26-FY27. All 5 intermediates for the originator product are in place.
*   **Lumateperone:** Patent protection secured for a niche and complex process. Expected to be a larger volume product from 2029.
*   **Darolutamide:** Not overly dependent in FY24. FY25 is a qualification year, with ramp-up from FY26. Originator expects significant growth, and AMI has all 5 intermediates.
*   **CDMO Business:** Mix of early-stage (NCE programs) and late-stage molecules. Focus on incremental growth, with CDMO expected to be a sizable portion of the portfolio. Margins on NCE programs are good.
*   **Tax Rate:** Standalone tax rate is 25.16%. Baba Fine Chemicals has a tax rate of 35%.
*   **Export/Domestic Split:** FY24 Exports at 56%; Domestic at 44%.
