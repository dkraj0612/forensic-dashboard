## Acutaas Chemicals (Formerly Ami Organics Ltd.) - Q1 FY26 Earnings Call - Executive Summary

**Date:** July 30, 2025

### Executive Overview

Acutaas Chemicals reported a robust Q1 FY26, with revenue growth of 17.3% YoY to INR 207.2 crores. This growth was primarily driven by the advanced pharmaceutical intermediates segment, while the specialty chemicals segment remained steady. The company achieved significant margin expansion, with Gross Margin improving by 1,117 bps YoY to 53.2% and EBITDA Margin up by 785 bps YoY to 24.6%. PAT grew substantially by 3x YoY to INR 44 crores. Management reaffirmed its commitment to delivering 25% revenue growth for FY26 and expects further margin improvements, driven by a favorable product mix and cost optimization. Key strategic initiatives include the progress in battery chemicals, semiconductor chemicals, and pharmaceutical CDMO segments.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates & CDMO:** The pharmaceutical intermediates segment delivered INR 165.8 crores in Q1 FY26, a 23.3% YoY growth, driven by both core advanced intermediates and the CDMO segment. Management expects CDMO projects to start contributing to the top line from Q4 FY26 onwards. Three additional CDMO projects, each with a potential revenue of INR 50-100 crores, are expected to commercialize by Q4 FY26, dependent on regulatory approvals. The non-CDMO pharma intermediate business is showing strong visibility with a robust R&D pipeline up to API patent expiry beyond 2040.
*   **Specialty Chemicals (SpecChem):** This segment reported flattish revenue of INR 41.4 crores in Q1 FY26. While commodity chemicals saw healthy volume growth, performance was offset by softer sales from Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC). Management expects BFC to pick up in the next financial year with new customers and improved demand.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** Capex for the electrolyte additive project at Jhagadia is on track for completion by Q3 FY26. The plant will have a capacity of 2,000 metric tons each for VC and FEC, with commercial supply expected to commence by Q4 FY26. Initial utilization is for export markets, not dependent on domestic EV project timelines. New product introductions are expected to translate into commercial contracts starting FY27, requiring an additional capex of INR 40-50 crores for infrastructure and utilities.
*   **Semiconductor Chemicals (BFC & JV):** BFC's demand has been soft, but management is aggressively expanding into Korea, Japan, and Taiwan. The company has entered into a joint venture in South Korea named Indichem, investing approximately KRW 30 billion for a 75% stake. This venture will manufacture specialty chemicals for chip production, with strong visibility on product mix and customers. The plant execution is underway, targeting completion in H2 calendar year 2026, with commercial production beginning late 2026 or early 2027. This JV's products are for worldwide markets, not exclusive to Korea and Taiwan. Existing BFC products are not being used for forward integration; the JV is for entirely new, advanced products.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** Total capex for FY26 is expected to be around INR 250 crores. The Ankleshwar site capex is completed, with the last pending block commercialized in Q1 FY26. A total of 15.8 MW solar power plants have been commissioned, expected to offset a majority of electricity costs from Q3 FY26. Capex for a new pilot plant at the Sachin unit has commenced, expected to be completed within a year. Total capex for Unit 2 at Ankleshwar is INR 310 crores.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**
    *   **FY25 Revenue Growth (25%):** **Met/Exceeded.** The company reported 40.3% YoY growth for FY25 in the previous earnings call. For Q1 FY26, they delivered 17.3% YoY growth. Management reaffirmed confidence in delivering 25% revenue growth for FY26.
    *   **FY25 CAPEX (INR 195 Cr):** **Met.** Full year capex was INR 195 crores. For FY26, capex is projected at INR 250 crores, including INR 180 crores for electrolyte additives (total INR 177 crores announced earlier, with incremental additions), INR 30 crores for pilot plant, and INR 40 crores for maintenance.
    *   **EBITDA Margin Improvement:** **Achieved/Exceeded.** Q1 FY26 EBITDA margin was 24.6%, a significant improvement over Q1 FY25 (16.7%) and Q4 FY25 (27.5%). Management expects further margin improvements for FY26.
    *   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization (H2 FY26):** **On Track.** Production expected to commence by Q4 FY26.
    *   **CDMO Business Growth (Target INR 1,000 Cr by FY28):** **Progressing.** New CDMO projects are expected to commence revenue contribution from Q4 FY26. The size of these new projects (INR 50-100 crores each) is smaller than the previously alluded "blockbuster" product but contributes to overall CDMO growth.
    *   **Ankleshwar Unit 2 Completion:** **Completed.** Block 1 capitalized in Q4 FY25, and the last pending block in Unit II was commercialized in Q1 FY26.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**
    *   **FY26 Revenue Growth:** Confidence in delivering 25% revenue growth.
    *   **FY26 EBITDA Margins:** Expectation of further improvements and sequential increase, with full-year margins expected to be higher than FY25. Scope for 50-100 bps improvement due to operating leverage.
    *   **CDMO Business Contribution:** Three new projects with revenue potential of INR 50-100 crores each are expected to commercialize by Q4 FY26, contingent on regulatory approvals.
    *   **Electrolyte Additive Production:** To commence by Q4 FY26, initially for export markets. Commercial contracts expected from FY27 onwards.
    *   **BFC Ramp-up:** Expected to pick up in the next financial year (FY27) with new customers and improving demand.
    *   **Semiconductor JV (Indichem) Commercialization:** Production expected by late 2026 or early 2027. Products are for worldwide markets.
    *   **Capex for FY26:** Approximately INR 250 crores (INR 180 Cr for electrolyte additives, INR 30 Cr for pilot plant, INR 40 Cr for maintenance).
    *   **Gross Margin Sustainability:** Favorable product mix and cost optimization measures are sustainable. Expectation of gross margins remaining north of 50% in coming periods, dependent on product mix.
    *   **Spec Chem Margins:** Expected to remain in double digits at EBITDA level. Electrolyte additive business will improve overall SpecChem margins once it picks up from FY27.
    *   **Pharma Intermediate Margins:** CDMO business offers better margins than non-CDMO pharma intermediates. Oncology remains a dominant therapy for CDMO. Non-CDMO covers 17 therapeutic segments.
    *   **Darolutamide Label Expansion:** Approved in Europe, US, and Japan, expected to add to demand for the customer, with Acutaas maintaining its customer demand projections.
    *   **Export Markets:** For semiconductor JV, products are for worldwide markets (excluding India). For electrolyte additives, customers are in Japan and Taiwan, with current supply predominantly from China.
    *   **Working Capital:** Around 128 days in Q1 FY26, expected to be around 110 days for the full year.
    *   **Other Income:** INR 16 crores in Q1 FY26, including INR 12.5 crores of foreign exchange gain.
