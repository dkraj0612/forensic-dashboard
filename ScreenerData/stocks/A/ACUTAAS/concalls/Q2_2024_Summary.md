## Executive Overview

Ami Organics (AMI) reported Q2 FY24 with revenue of INR 172 crores, a 17.3% year-on-year growth, but below expectations due to pricing pressures and a customer product launch delay. The company experienced a significant EBITDA margin compression to 14.4% (vs. 19.1% in Q2 FY23) primarily due to high-cost inventory, unfavorable product mix (higher domestic sales, increased contribution from lower-margin specialty chemicals), and increased employee costs. A notable development was the full impairment of the JV with Ami Oncotheranostics, resulting in a reported PAT of negative INR 17 crores, with an adjusted PAT of INR 14.7 crores. The company has revised its full-year revenue growth target downwards from 20-25% to 18-22%, citing top-line erosion due to price pressures. Despite these headwinds, AMI anticipates a stronger H2 FY24 driven by a clear order book and expected stabilization in raw material prices. The acquisition of Baba Fine Chemicals was completed during the quarter, with integration impacting operations in H1 FY24. Significant CAPEX is underway for the Ankleshwar facility and captive solar power projects.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates:** Experienced pricing pressure (10-30%) impacting margins, with an 8% YoY growth. A key product launch delay by a customer is expected to defer revenue to Q4 FY24/Q1 FY25. Domestic sales mix is higher (46% vs. 54% export), contributing to lower gross margins by 2-3%. Management aims to recover positioning and margins in the coming quarters.
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Despite pricing pressure, this segment delivered strong 72% YoY growth. A new UV absorber product for the automotive paint industry has been launched and is expected to contribute from Q3 FY24. Management targets 18-19% margins for this segment.
*   **Electrolyte Additives:** Production is set to begin in Q4 FY24 after customer audits and greenlight for production. Raw material orders are placed, and supply is expected from Q4 FY24.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals Acquisition:** Acquisition of controlling partnership completed. Integration in H1 FY24 impacted operations, but the company is poised for growth in FY25. The acquired entity contributed INR 9 crores to Q2 revenue and INR 14 crores to H1 net profit.
*   **Fermion Contract:** Partnership expanded to include a third advanced intermediate, significantly increasing potential revenue. Shipping of previously signed intermediates to commence from Ankleshwar facility in Q4 FY24, with commercial supply and ramp-up expected in H2 FY25.
*   **Ankleshwar Facility:** Block 1 to be operational by early Q4 FY24, with full facility operational by FY25. Currently, 33% capacity is allocated to Fermion, with the remaining 66% available for future growth and discussions with new clients. The facility is dedicated to pharma intermediates.
*   **Cost Optimization & CAPEX:** Completed acquisition of Baba Fine Chemicals (investment value INR 68 crores, goodwill INR 54 crores). Approved a 16 MW captive solar power plant (CAPEX INR 65-70 crores), in addition to a 5 MW plant already under installation. Total CAPEX for H1 FY24 was INR 105 crores, with another INR 100-110 crores planned for the remainder of the year. Inventory levels increased due to strategic buildup for the Fermion contract.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**

*   **FY24 Revenue Growth:** Management has revised the full-year revenue growth target downwards from 20-25% to 18-22%. This is attributed to "top-line erosion" due to price erosion, rather than a decline in volume growth, which is expected to remain at 20-25%. This is a clear deviation from the previously stated target.
*   **EBITDA Margin Improvement:** The Q2 FY24 EBITDA margin of 14.4% is significantly lower than the Q2 FY23 margin of 19.1% and a stark contrast to the previously stated confidence in achieving margins above 21% for FY24. Management attributes the current quarter's margin compression to specific factors like high-cost inventory and product mix, expecting recovery from Q3 onwards.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Growth:** The segment continues to show strong growth (72% YoY in Q2 FY24), aligning with previous positive commentary, although the previously stated target of 25-30% for FY24 was for the entire segment, and the current commentary focuses on robust growth and margin expansion to 18-19%.
*   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization:** Previously expected to start invoicing in Q4 FY23 or Q1 FY24, the company now states production will begin in Q4 FY24, with supply starting from Q4 FY24 after customer audits. Initial orders are confirmed but contingent on raw material receipt.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals Integration:** The transcript acknowledges that H1 FY24 was dedicated to acquisition and integration, which impacted operations. The previous expectation of substantial growth in FY25 remains, but FY24 is described as a "consolidation and integration year" with muted performance.
*   **Fermion Contract:** Commercial supply is confirmed to start from Q4 FY24, with ramp-up in H2 FY25, aligning with previous guidance. The expansion to three intermediates is a positive development.

**New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**

*   **FY24 Revenue Growth:** Revised to 18% to 22% from the previous 20-25% range, due to price erosion. Volume growth is expected to remain strong at 20-25%.
*   **FY24 EBITDA Margin:** Management anticipates a sequential recovery from Q3 FY24, with stabilization of raw material prices and improvement in gross margins. Specific full-year target is not reiterated, but the Q2 performance indicates a significant miss on prior expectations.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Margins:** Targeting 18-19% margins for this segment. UV absorber product expected to contribute from Q3 FY24, with a potential business size of 600-1000 metric tons.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals Growth:** FY24 is a consolidation year. Substantial growth is expected in FY25, aiming to reach INR 200-250 crores in 4-5 years.
*   **Fermion Contract:** Commercial supply to commence from Q4 FY24, with full ramp-up expected in H2 FY25. Three advanced intermediates are now part of the contract.
*   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization:** Production to begin in Q4 FY24, with supply starting from Q4 FY24.
*   **Ankleshwar Facility:** Block 1 operational by early Q4 FY24, full facility by FY25. Management targets full capacity operation by FY27.
*   **Pricing Environment:** Expects raw material prices to stabilize and anticipates recovering market positioning and margins. Acknowledges that local competitors use export price data to negotiate with customers.
*   **Working Capital:** Net working capital days improved to 100 days in H1 FY24 (down 16 days from March 2023), driven by improved debtor days and payable cycles. However, inventory days increased to 145 days due to strategic stocking for the Fermion contract.
*   **Solar Power CAPEX:** A 16 MW captive solar power plant project approved, with CAPEX of INR 65-70 crores.
*   **Write-off:** Ami Organics JV with Ami Oncotheranostics fully impaired (INR 31.7 crores) due to focus on core chemistry business.
