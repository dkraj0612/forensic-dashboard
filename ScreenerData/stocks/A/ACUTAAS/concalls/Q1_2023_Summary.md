## Executive Overview

Ami Organics (AMI) demonstrated resilient performance in Q1 FY23, achieving revenue growth of 15.8% YoY to INR 131 crore amidst a challenging global economic landscape. The company highlighted strong performance driven by its export market and innovator-driven business. Gross margins saw a significant improvement of 572 bps YoY to 48.8%, attributed to an improved product mix, cost optimization, process enhancements, and successful cost pass-through to customers. However, EBITDA margins were flat sequentially at 18.1%, impacted by higher energy and freight costs, as well as the lower EBITDA margins of the specialty chemicals business. Management expressed confidence in margin improvement throughout the year. The company is actively progressing with its electrolyte additive business, with samples in advanced stages of customer approval and commercialization anticipated by Q4 FY23. Furthermore, AMI plans to launch two new agrochemical products under its import substitute vertical in H2 FY23.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharma Intermediates Growth:** Continued strong demand, especially from the export and innovator-driven segments. Management notes strong order visibility and a diversified customer base with long-term relationships, including a significant portion of revenue from chronic therapy products (91%).
*   **Specialty Chemicals Expansion:** Focus on new product development and operational efficiency. The acquisition of Gujarat Organics is contributing to diversification, with plans to improve operational efficiencies and develop new molecules for accelerated growth.
*   **Electrolyte Additives:** AMI has developed a core electrolyte for energy storage devices, positioning itself as the first Indian and global company outside China to achieve this. Samples are undergoing customer approval, with commercialization anticipated by Q4 FY23.
*   **Import Substitution:** Planning to launch two new agrochemical products in H2 FY23. The company is also transitioning existing products to continuous flow technology for improved efficiency.
*   **Capacity Expansion & Technology:** The new Ankleshwar plant for advanced pharmaceutical intermediates is under construction, with civil work started and machinery ordered. The plant is expected to be operational by Q4 FY24. The company is also leveraging continuous flow reactor technology for increased efficiency and sustainability, having successfully commercialized two products and piloting three more.
*   **Cost Management & Energy Transition:** Management acknowledges the impact of higher energy and freight costs on EBITDA margins but anticipates improvement throughout the year. The shift to coal from furnace oil at the GOL Jhagadia plant is expected to yield significant cost reductions once commissioned.
*   **Customer & Product Diversification:** The company has a diversified customer base with 54% of revenue from Top 10 customers in FY22 and a significant portion of customers associated for over 5-10 years. AMI aims to grow its specialty chemicals segment at a faster rate than its core pharma intermediate business.

*   **Revenue Growth:** Management's guidance of ~25% CAGR for FY23 appears on track. Q1 FY23 revenue growth of 15.8% YoY, while lower than the full-year target, is seasonally the weakest quarter, and management expressed confidence based on the order book.
*   **Gross Margin Improvement:** Management's strategy to improve gross margins through product mix, cost optimization, and technology adoption has been validated with a significant YoY increase of 572 bps to 48.8%.

Management attributes this to elevated energy and freight costs and lower EBITDA margins from the specialty chemical business, a consistent narrative from previous periods regarding cost pressures. Management reiterated confidence in improving margins over the year.
