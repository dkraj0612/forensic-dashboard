## Executive Overview

Acutaas Chemicals closed FY26 with a strong fourth quarter, achieving Rs. 432.8 Crores in revenue, a 40.4% year-on-year increase. The company reported a record Profit After Tax (PAT) margin of 31.0% for the quarter, contributing to an annual PAT of Rs. 356.4 Crores. Management reiterated its clear strategy of building a diversified chemicals business across three high-growth verticals: Battery Chemicals, Semiconductors, and Pharmaceutical CDMO. The company is confident in de-risking its revenue base and creating multiple engines of compounding growth. Acutaas enters FY27 from a position of strength and is confident in delivering 25% revenue growth in the upcoming fiscal year.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates & CDMO:** The CDMO business continues its strong growth momentum, supported by a steady non-CDMO business. This segment showed solid growth in FY26.
*   **Specialty Chemicals (SpecChem):** The Semiconductor Business (BFC) saw a strong recovery, driven by a steady commodity chemicals business. Cost efficiencies and product mix improvements led to strong EBITDA expansion.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** First phase of capex completed. Commercial operations have commenced, with meaningful revenue contribution expected in FY27. Capacity for VC and FEC is fully contracted for the next three years.
*   **Semiconductor Chemicals (BFC & JV):** BFC is showing encouraging traction and recovery. The South Korean joint venture, Indichem, has invested INR 190 crores in FY26. Capex for Indichem is expected to complete in the second half of CY26, with commercialization and revenue generation anticipated in CY27. The peak revenue from the Indichem plant is expected to be around 1x the capex.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** Capex for FY26 was INR 195 crores. A significant expansion of the R&D center is planned, featuring a 10x capacity increase to support all verticals.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**
    *   **FY26 Revenue Growth (Targeted ~30%):** **Achieved.** The company reported FY26 revenues of INR 1,339.4 crores, reflecting 33.0% YoY growth, exceeding the guided approximately 30%.
    *   **FY26 CAPEX (Revised to INR 220 Cr):** **Achieved (Slightly Below).** Actual FY26 capex was INR 195 crores. A spillover of approximately INR 50 crores is planned for FY27.
    *   **EBITDA Margin Improvement (Target 32-35% for FY26):** **Exceeded.** FY26 consolidated EBITDA margin was 35.9%, surpassing the upgraded guidance. Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin was exceptionally strong at 42.4%.
    *   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization (End of Q4 FY26 / Q1 FY27):** **Achieved.** Commercial operations have started, with meaningful contribution expected in FY27.
    *   **Semiconductor JV (Indichem) Commercialization (Late 2026/Early 2027):** **On Track/Ahead.** Capex completion in H2 CY26, earlier than initially guided.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**
    *   **FY27 Revenue Growth:** Guided at 25%. Management is open to revising upwards if potential exceeds this.
    *   **FY27 EBITDA Margins:** Expected to be maintained at a similar level as FY26 (around 35-36%).
    *   **CDMO Business:** Four additional validated products to contribute meaningfully in FY27, with peak revenue potential of INR 50-100 crores each. INR 1,000 crores revenue target by FY28 remains.
    *   **Battery Chemicals Contribution:** Meaningful revenue contribution in FY27, ramping up sequentially.
    *   **Semiconductor Business (BFC & Indichem):** Continued ramp-up for BFC. Indichem to commence commercial production in H2 CY26 and contribute revenue from CY27.
    *   **Capex for FY27:** Approximately INR 50 crores spillover (electrolyte additive and pilot plant) plus INR 40 crores maintenance capex. R&D center capex to be finalized.
    *   **Gross Margin Sustainability:** Management indicated gross margins are sustainable above 55%, with Q4 FY26 reaching 62.0%.
    *   **Pharma Segment EBITDA Margins:** 44% for Q4 FY26.
    *   **Specialty Chemicals Segment EBITDA Margins:** 29% for Q4 FY26.
    *   **Working Capital:** Increased to 120 days for FY26 from 114 days in FY25.
    *   **R&D Centre Expansion:** Ambitious 10x capacity expansion planned.
    *   **Indichem Partner:** Proprietary firm with 30+ years of experience in the semiconductor ecosystem.
    *   **Sodium-Ion Batteries:** Developing molecules applicable to various battery technologies.
    *   **Fermion Contract:** Primary supplier benefiting from Bayer/Orion's projected growth.
    *   **Goodwill:** INR 104 crores goodwill is considered recoverable based on future projections.
