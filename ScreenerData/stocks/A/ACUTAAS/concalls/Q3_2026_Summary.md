## Acutaas Chemicals (Formerly Ami Organics Ltd.) - Q3 FY26 Earnings Call - Executive Summary

**Date:** January 28, 2026

### Executive Overview

Acutaas Chemicals reported a strong Q3 FY26, with revenues approaching INR 400 crores and achieving their highest-ever margins, leading to a Profit After Tax (PAT) exceeding INR 100 crores. The company revised its full-year FY26 revenue growth guidance upwards to approximately 30%. Key developments include the inauguration of their battery chemicals facility at Jhagadia and continued progress in the semiconductor chemicals JV (Indichem) in South Korea. Management reaffirmed their strategic vision of developing three independent, self-sustaining growth engines by FY28 across pharmaceutical intermediates, battery chemicals, and semiconductor chemicals.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates & CDMO:** The Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates segment delivered INR 351.1 crores in Q3 FY26, a 47.0% YoY growth, driven by the CDMO business and steady contributions from core advanced intermediates. Four validated CDMO products are expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue from FY27 onwards. Management is strategically reviewing its product portfolio, focusing on quality growth and exiting low-margin products.
*   **Specialty Chemicals (SpecChem):** This segment reported INR 42.1 crores in Q3 FY26, a 16.5% YoY growth, supported by consistent commodity chemical growth and a recovery in the Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) business.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** The first phase of the electrolyte additive block at Jhagadia was inaugurated on January 19, 2026. Trial production and validation are underway, with commercial operations expected to ramp up from Q1 FY27. The second phase of capex is ongoing and targeted for completion by Q1 FY27. Initial orders for VC and FEC have been secured with revenue visibility for FY27. Two additional products are expected to contribute from mid-FY27.
*   **Semiconductor Chemicals (BFC & JV):** BFC is showing encouraging traction, particularly with its Japanese customer beyond Heraeus products, indicating a turnaround. The South Korean joint venture, Indichem, has invested approximately INR 130 crores to date, with capex progressing as planned. Commercialization is anticipated by late 2026 or early 2027. Products are for worldwide markets.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** Total capex for FY26 is projected at INR 220 crores, lower than the previous INR 250 crores guidance due to spill-overs in battery chemicals and pilot plant projects. This includes INR 170 crores for electrolyte additives for FY26. The Ankleshwar plant utilization was 40% for the quarter.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**
    *   **FY26 Revenue Growth (25%):** **Upgraded.** Management has revised the FY26 revenue guidance upwards to approximately 30% growth, citing strong order book and performance.
    *   **FY26 CAPEX (INR 250 Cr):** **Revised Downwards.** Total capex for FY26 is now projected at INR 220 crores due to project spill-overs, with INR 170 crores specifically for electrolyte additives within FY26.
    *   **EBITDA Margin Improvement:** **Achieved/Exceeded.** Q3 FY26 EBITDA margin was 38.3%, significantly exceeding previous levels and supporting the upgraded full-year guidance. Management has also upgraded the full-year EBITDA margin guidance from 28-30% to 32-35%.
    *   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization (H2 FY26):** **Slightly Delayed.** Inauguration occurred in January 2026, but commercialization is now expected by the end of Q4 FY26 (Q1 FY27).
    *   **CDMO Business Growth (Target INR 1,000 Cr by FY28):** **Progressing Well.** Four validated products are expected to contribute from FY27, and management reiterated confidence in reaching the INR 1,000 crores milestone by FY28, driven by a cumulative basket of CDMO projects, not solely anticancer.
    *   **Ankleshwar Unit 2 Completion:** **Completed.** Commercialization of the last pending block occurred in Q1 FY26.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**
    *   **FY26 Revenue Growth:** Revised upwards to ~30%.
    *   **FY26 EBITDA Margins:** Upgraded guidance from 28-30% to 32-35%.
    *   **CDMO Business Contribution:** Four validated products to start contributing from FY27; INR 1,000 crores target by FY28 for the cumulative CDMO business.
    *   **Electrolyte Additive Production:** Commercial operations expected by end of Q4 FY26 (Q1 FY27). Two additional products to contribute from mid-FY27.
    *   **BFC Ramp-up:** Started turning around this quarter, with expectations of further traction.

    *   **Battery Chemicals Segment Margins:** Expected to be below traditional Pharma Intermediates and above existing commodity chemical business margins. Initial margins will be lower, improving with scale and operational efficiencies.

    *   **Chinese Competition:** Management highlighted their strength in multiple chemistries at scale as a USP against Chinese competition, leading to market leadership in many products.
    *   **US Market Exposure:** Small direct export exposure to the US, primarily through BFC, with minimal impact from US tariffs.
