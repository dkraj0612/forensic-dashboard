## Executive Overview

Acutaas Chemicals (formerly Ami Organics Ltd.) reported a strong Q2 FY26 performance with a 24.1% YoY revenue growth to INR 306.2 Cr. This growth was predominantly driven by the Pharmaceutical Intermediates segment, which saw a 27.1% YoY increase, fueled by the CDMO business and core advanced intermediates. The Specialty Chemicals segment grew a steady 7.3% YoY, though Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) continued to perform softly. Significant margin expansion was a key highlight, with EBITDA doubling YoY to INR 95.3 Cr and EBITDA margins improving to 31.1%, driven by a better product mix and operational efficiencies, including contributions from the solar power plant. Management revised its FY26 revenue growth guidance upwards to 25% and provided a new EBITDA margin outlook of 28%-30% for FY26, citing a higher contribution from CDMO and improved profitability. New business verticals like battery chemicals and semiconductor chemicals are progressing, with battery chemical production expected to commence in Q4 FY26 and the Korean JV (Indichem) to contribute from H2 FY27.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates & CDMO:** Strong 27.1% YoY growth driven by CDMO business and core advanced intermediates. CDMO projects are progressing, with validation batches dispatched and commercialization estimated by end of FY26. Management sees CDMO as a key growth driver with healthy customer inquiries and new molecule additions.
*   **Specialty Chemicals (SpecChem):** Steady 7.3% YoY growth, with commodity chemicals showing strong performance. Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) continues to face subdued demand in the semiconductor industry, but engagement with new customers in Korea, Japan, and Taiwan is progressing for newer products, though contributions are expected to be small initially.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** Capex for electrolyte additive projects (VC and FEC) at Jaghadia is progressing well, expected completion by Q4 FY26. Production commencement is anticipated soon after, with full year FY27 expected to see meaningful contribution. Firm contracts are in place with export customers.
*   **Semiconductor Chemicals:** The JV in Korea, Indichem, marks a major milestone for international expansion. Groundbreaking ceremony completed, capex underway, and commercial contributions expected from H2 FY27. This and battery chemicals are positioned as important growth pillars alongside CDMO.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** The pilot plant capex at Sachin site is proceeding as per plan, expected completion by Q3 FY26. Management plans to introduce systems, KMPs, and equipment as needed for each segment's growth.
*   **Sustainability:** The 16 MW solar power project is fully commissioned, with the 5MW portion contributing to margin expansion in Q2 FY26.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**
    *   **FY25 Revenue Growth Target (Revised to 30%):** **Not directly audited as this is for FY26.** The Q2 FY26 performance (24.1% YoY growth) and H1 FY26 performance (21.3% YoY growth) are on track to meet the revised FY26 revenue growth guidance of 25%.
    *   **FY25 Capex (~INR 250 crores):** **Met/On Track.** For FY26, management reaffirmed CAPEX of approximately INR 250 crores. INR 141 crores was spent in H1 FY26, primarily on the Jaghadia site for electrolyte additives and the Sachin pilot plant.
    *   **EBITDA Margin Outlook (FY26: 28%-30%):** **New Guidance.** Management revised its FY26 EBITDA margin guidance upwards to 28%-30%, a significant increase from previous expectations. This is attributed to a better product mix, higher CDMO contribution, and operational efficiencies.
    *   **Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) Recovery (Expected FY26):** **Delayed/Gradual.** BFC's performance remains soft due to the semiconductor industry slowdown. While engagement with new customers is ongoing, initial contributions are expected to be small. Full recovery or meaningful contribution is not yet evident in Q2 FY26.
    *   **Enchem AMI Organics JV (Status quo):** **Remains Status Quo.** The JV for electrolyte solutions manufacturing remains unchanged.
    *   **Ankleshwar Unit 2 Completion (Blocks 2 & 3):** **Completed/Operational.** All capex at Ankleshwar Unit 2 is complete. Block 3, dedicated to a contract, has been operational since Q4 FY24. Capacity utilization will take time to reach optimum levels over the next 2-3 years.
    *   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization (Sales from H2 FY25):** **Timeline Shifted/FY26 Focus.** Commercial production is now expected to commence in Q4 FY26, with meaningful contribution expected in FY27.
    *   **Apixaban/Rivaroxaban Peak Sales (Expected FY26):** **New CDMO Contribution.** While not explicitly named, management stated that ongoing CDMO projects are expected to start contributing by end of FY26, subject to regulatory approvals, with validation batches already dispatched.
    *   **Solar CAPEX Completion (End Q3 FY25):** **Completed.** The solar project is fully commissioned, with a portion contributing to Q2 FY26 margins.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**
    *   **FY26 Revenue Growth:** Confirmed at 25% YoY.
    *   **FY26 CAPEX:** Approximately INR 250 crores (INR 40 crores maintenance, INR 180 crores for electrolyte additives, INR 30 crores for pilot plant).
