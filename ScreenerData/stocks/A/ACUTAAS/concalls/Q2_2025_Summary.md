## Ami Organics Ltd. - Q2 FY2025 Earnings Summary

### Executive Overview

Ami Organics delivered a robust performance in Q2 FY25, reporting a 43.2% YoY revenue growth to INR 246.7 Cr, driven by a strong 53% growth in its Pharmaceutical Intermediates business, including an earlier-than-anticipated ramp-up in CDMO contracts. The Specialty Chemicals segment also contributed with 7.6% YoY growth, although it was impacted by subdued performance from Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC). The company achieved significant margin expansion, with EBITDA doubling YoY to INR 48.9 Cr and EBITDA margins improving to 19.8%. A key milestone was the official GMP compliance declaration by PMDA Japan for its Sachin facility. Despite a generally slower-than-expected chemical sector recovery, industry-wide deferrals in EV investments, and continued subdued demand in the semiconductor industry affecting BFC, management expressed confidence by revising its FY25 revenue growth guidance upwards from 25% to 30%. The transcript also noted "High Evasion Density" in management's communication during the call.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates:** Achieved strong 53% YoY growth, driven by key molecules and an accelerated ramp-up of CDMO business. Key therapeutic areas include anti-cancer, anti-depressant, seizure disorder, anti-psychotic, Parkinson’s disease, anticoagulants, and cardiovascular. The PMDA Japan GMP compliance for the Sachin facility enables sales to the Japanese market for originator CDMO products.
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Grew 7.6% YoY, with ex-Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) growing over 25%. BFC's subpar performance continues due to subdued demand in the semiconductor industry and integration challenges. New product promotion and client outreach in Korea and Japan are underway for BFC.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** CAPEX for 2,000 MT each of VC and FEC capacity has begun, with machinery orders placed. Project completion is now expected in H1 CY25, impacted by EV market deferrals. Firm contracts are in hand.
*   **Electrolyte Solutions (CDMO):** The Enchem AMI Organics JV for electrolyte manufacturing in India remains in "status quo," with JV terms still under discussion. This segment is seen as an additional arm, not critical to core growth.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** One block of Ankleshwar Unit 2 is operational, with the remaining two blocks now expected to be completed in Q3 FY25 (delayed due to heavy rains). Commercial supplies from Block 2 are anticipated from Q4 FY25. The full Ankleshwar unit capacity is 442 KL, currently at 20% utilization, targeting full utilization in three years.
*   **Sustainability & Innovation:** The 16 MW solar CAPEX project is now expected to be completed by Q3 FY25 (delayed due to monsoon), with energy savings contributing to EBITDA from Q4 FY25.
*   **CDMO Pipeline:** Gradual shipments for Apixaban have started and are slowly ramping up, with FY26 expected to see full utilization across regulated markets. The company has a strong CDMO pipeline, with double-digit molecules in Phase 2 and a single-digit in Phase 3. A large NCE CDMO contract is in an advanced stage, with validation batches sent and supply expected from Q4 FY25/Q1 FY26.

    *   **FY25 Revenue Split (40:60 H1:H2):** The Q1 expectation for H1 was 40% of full-year revenue. With H1 FY25 revenue at INR 423.4 Cr (up 29.8% YoY), the company is well on track to meet or exceed its full-year growth target, suggesting the H1 performance is robust.
    *   **FY25 Capex (Approximately INR 250 crores):** **On Track.** Management reaffirmed FY25 CAPEX at approximately INR 250 crores.
    *   **EBITDA Margin Outlook (>20% in FY25, long-term 23-26%):** **Tracking.** Q2 FY25 EBITDA margin was 19.8%, and H1 FY25 was 18.5%. Management expects sequential improvement, targeting FY21 levels (23%) and long-term 25-30% in the next three years.
