## Executive Overview

Ami Organics (AMI) reported Q3 FY24 with revenue of INR 166.5 crores, a 9.2% year-on-year growth. This growth was primarily driven by a significant 25% increase in volume, while pricing pressures continued to impact realizations. The company's EBITDA margin for the quarter was 15.9%, a decrease from 20.2% in Q3 FY23, attributed to an unfavorable product mix and higher employee expenses. For the nine-month period (9MFY24), revenue grew by 14.4% to INR 493 crores, with EBITDA at INR 85.3 crores, up 4.3% year-on-year, and an EBITDA margin of 17.3%. Key developments include the inauguration of the Ankleshwar plant, expansion of the Fermion contract with two additional advanced intermediates, and the commencement of commercial operations for electrolyte additives. The company also signed an MOU with the Government of Gujarat for a INR 300 crore electrolyte manufacturing facility.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates:** Volume growth of 25% in Q3 FY24 despite pricing pressures. Apixaban sales have begun with a ramp-up expected in Q1/Q2 FY25. The Ankleshwar plant is operational, with Block 1 dedicated to Fermion (33% capacity) and the remaining 66% in discussions with other pharma clients for CMO business.
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Strong volume growth reported. UV absorber product has supplied validation quantities and will ramp up from Q1 FY25. The specialty chemical plant capacity utilization is projected to reach full utilization by FY26. Baba Fine Chemicals, now part of Specialty Chemicals, is undergoing integration and customer qualification, with expected ramp-up in 2-3 years.
*   **Electrolyte Additives:** Commercial operations have commenced with long-term supply agreements. A capacity of 500 metric tons each for two products (VC and FVC) is established, with plans to add 2,000 metric tons each for upcoming demands. Revenue contribution is expected from this quarter or Q1 FY25.
*   **Electrolytes (CDMO):** An MOU has been signed with a global manufacturer for CDMO services for electrolyte solutions, with a potential INR 300 crore investment from the Gujarat government. This is a separate business from electrolyte additives and is expected to be CAPEX intensive with a secure, long-term revenue stream.
*   **Fermion Contract:** Validation batches are underway in Q3 FY24, with commercial supply ramp-up expected towards the end of FY25. The contract now includes three advanced intermediates.
*   **Cost Management & CAPEX:** EBITDA margins are targeted to improve sequentially to 18-20% in Q4 FY24 and then towards 23% from FY25. Debt funding has been utilized for CAPEX, with a projected Debt to Equity ratio of around 37% equity.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**

*   **FY24 Revenue Growth:** Management revised the guidance for FY24 from 15-18% (previously revised from 20-25%) due to continued top-line erosion from price pressure. This indicates a shortfall from initial expectations.
*   **EBITDA Margin Improvement:** While Q3 FY24 EBITDA margins were 15.9%, down from 20.2% in Q3 FY23, management stated that sequential improvement is expected, targeting 18-20% in Q4 FY24 and towards 23% in the medium term. This suggests a recovery trajectory, though current performance is below prior year levels.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Growth & Margins:** While volume growth is strong, the previously mentioned target of INR 200-250 crores for the specialty chemical business may be impacted by pricing corrections and longer-than-expected qualification times for Baba Fine Chemicals. However, management expresses confidence that volume growth will eventually drive desired revenue.
*   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization:** Commercial production and supply have commenced, aligning with the revised timelines.
*   **Ankleshwar Facility:** The plant was inaugurated in December 2023. Ramp-up for Fermion is expected towards the end of FY25.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals Integration:** Integration is underway, with Q3 FY24 revenue included. Management acknowledges this year is a consolidation year, with ramp-up expected in 2-3 years, a shift from immediate growth expectations.

**New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**

*   **FY24 Revenue Growth:** Guidance remains at 15% to 18% due to ongoing price erosion.
*   **FY25 Revenue Growth:** Projected to be between 17% and 22%.
*   **FY24 EBITDA Margin:** Targeting a sequential increase to 18-20% in Q4 FY24, with a medium-term aim of 23-26%.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Margins:** Targeting 18-19% for the segment.
