## Executive Overview

Ami Organics (AMI) reported Q3 FY23 results with a reported revenue growth of 7.9% year-on-year to INR 152 crore. Management attributed the slower reported growth to deferred shipments, estimating an adjusted growth of 15% for the quarter. The pharmaceutical intermediates segment continued to perform strongly, driven by exports, while the specialty chemicals segment remained subdued due to lower sales of a key product. Key developments include a multi-year, multi-ton contract with Fermion for a patented product, with commercial supplies expected to commence in Q4 FY24 after a validation period. The company also reported progress in import substitute products, with two commercialized products showing strong traction and two new products receiving trial orders. Efforts to improve the specialty chemicals business include a project to switch fuel at the Jhagadia facility to coal and the conversion of Methyl Salicylate to flow technology. Management reiterated its full-year revenue growth guidance of 25% for FY23 and expressed confidence in achieving similar growth in FY24, citing a strong order book and new contracts.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharma Intermediates Growth:** Strong performance driven by exports. Signed a multi-year, multi-ton, multi-million-euro contract with Fermion for a patented product, with commercialization expected from Q4 FY24. Management is focusing on higher-level intermediates from European companies outsourcing production.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Revitalization:** Segment was subdued due to lower sales of a top product. However, management expects improvements in FY24 driven by cost optimization at Jhagadia (switch to coal boilers) and the commercialization of Methyl Salicylate using flow technology. 39 new customers were added in this segment during Q4.
*   **Electrolyte Additives:** Samples have been sent to nine customers, with one requesting a trial order. Management is confident of receiving commercial orders in FY24, with commercialization expected in Q1 or Q2 FY24, gradually ramping up thereafter. Potential for a sizable business is seen.
*   **Import Substitution & New Products:** Two existing products show strong traction, and two new products developed this year have received trial orders. A healthy product pipeline for the future is maintained.
*   **Flow Technology Adoption:** Methyl Salicylate has been converted to flow technology with installed flow reactors. Management believes continuous production via flow reactors enhances operational efficiency, quality, and safety, leading to better margins.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** The Ankleshwar plant construction is progressing as planned and will be supported by Unit 1 in Surat initially for the Fermion contract before transferring.
*   **Cost Management & Efficiency:** Efforts are underway to improve profitability through fuel switching at Jhagadia and optimization of specialty chemical production. Management aims to improve EBITDA margins sequentially by 1-1.5% per quarter.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**

*   **Revenue Growth:** Management reiterated its 25% revenue growth guidance for FY23. The reported 7.9% YoY growth in Q3 FY23, with an adjusted 15% accounting for deferred shipments, indicates a potential miss on the higher end of the target, requiring a very strong Q4. The management's commentary on Q4 being seasonally higher than Q3 provides some optimism.
*   **Gross Margin Improvement:** Gross margins declined sequentially and YoY to 46.0% in Q3 FY23, attributed to high-cost inventory and a change in product mix. Management stated that most of the high-cost inventory has been consumed, with a minimal impact expected in Q4 and Q1 FY24 being a "clean quarter."
*   **EBITDA Margin Improvement:** EBITDA margins improved sequentially to 20.2% from 19.1% in Q2 FY23, aligning with management's expectation of continuous improvement. This improvement was driven by factors like lower freight and utility costs and cost optimization programs. Pharma business EBITDA margins crossed 22%, while specialty chemical margins remained around 10%. Management aims to reach FY21 EBITDA margins and improve by 1-1.5% quarterly.
*   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization:** The timeline for commercialization has shifted slightly, with management now expecting commercial orders in FY24 (Q1 or Q2) rather than before the end of FY23 as previously indicated.
*   **Import Substitute Products:** Progress continues with trial orders for new products.

**New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**

*   **FY23 Revenue Growth:** Reiteration of 25% growth guidance for FY23, acknowledging a strong Q4 will be necessary, potentially exceeding 35-45% growth for the quarter.
*   **FY24 Revenue Growth:** Expectation of CAGR of 25% for FY24, supported by forward contracts, new customers, and originators increasing purchases.
*   **Sequential EBITDA Margin Improvement:** Management expects continuous improvement of 1-1.5% per quarter in EBITDA margins, aiming to reach FY21 levels (23-24%) for pharma and gradually improving specialty chemical margins.
*   **Electrolyte Product Commercialization:** Commercialization expected in Q1 or Q2 FY24, gradually ramping up. Capacity expansion planning for this segment will commence in FY24.
*   **Fermion Contract:** Commercial supply expected to start in Q4 FY24 after a validation period. Validation supplies will occur in Q4 FY23. While not expected to yield very high margins, it will benefit from operating leverage at scale and be a "multi-million euro" revenue contributor.
*   **Specialty Chemicals Growth:** Management expects this segment to improve in FY24 with cost optimization and flow technology adoption.
*   **Working Capital:** Aim to bring working capital days down to 100 from 123, with a long-term target of 90-100 days.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** CAPEX of INR 200 crore planned for FY23-FY24 (INR 100 crore per year), funded by internal accruals, IPO funds, and potentially debt. CAPEX for electrolyte capacity is not yet finalized but will require 6-9 months for plant construction.
*   **Pharma Intermediate Pipeline:** Over 480 products are invoiced, with patent expiry until 2039-40. 15-20 new products commercialize annually.
*   **Key Product Revenue Contribution:** Top 5 products contribute 22% (product #1), 10%, 8%, 5%, and 3%. Specific product names were not disclosed due to competitive concerns.
*   **Salicylic Acid Competition:** Management views increased salicylic acid production by others as beneficial for raw material sourcing leverage, but foresees potential forward integration competition in methyl salicylate and benzyl salicylate.
