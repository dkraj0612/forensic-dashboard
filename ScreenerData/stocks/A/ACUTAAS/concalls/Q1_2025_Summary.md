## Ami Organics Ltd. - Q1 FY2025 Earnings Summary

### Executive Overview

Ami Organics reported a 15% YoY revenue growth to INR 176.7 Cr in Q1 FY25, with a 16.6% increase in Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates and a 10% growth in Specialty Chemicals, the latter being partially offset by subdued performance from Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC). Despite global industry headwinds including geopolitical instability, extended delivery times, and competitive pressure from China, Ami Organics successfully navigated these challenges, driven by strong performance in its core pharmaceutical business and operational improvements. The company achieved a significant milestone with a no-observation GMP inspection by the PMDA in Japan, opening new opportunities. Management expressed confidence in achieving their 25% revenue growth guidance for FY25, anticipating sequential improvement from Q1, which is historically their lowest quarter.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates:** Demonstrated strong 16.6% YoY growth, driven by both CDMO and core product performance. Focus remains on chronic therapy areas where technology and process are key differentiators, mitigating oversupply concerns from China.
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Saw 10% growth, primarily from commodity chemicals, but partially offset by the subdued performance of Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC). BFC is undergoing integration and marketing efforts for new products, targeting a recovery in the latter half of the year.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** Commercial operations and CAPEX for electrolyte additives are on track for completion by end of FY25. Long-term contracts are signed, with supplies expected from H2 FY25. Capacity expansion for VC and FEC to 2,000 metric tonnes is underway, alongside development of 8 new additives.
*   **Electrolyte Solutions (CDMO):** Enchem AMI Organics Private Limited, a step-down subsidiary, is being established to focus on electrolyte manufacturing in India. JV terms with a Korean parent are under finalization.
*   **Sustainability & Innovation:** Initiated a 16 MW solar CAPEX project, expected to contribute to EBITDA improvement from Q3 FY25 by meeting most energy needs.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** Two remaining blocks of the Ankleshwar Unit 2 are on track for completion by Q2 FY25. The company has secured three process patents and continues to invest in R&D and advanced technologies like continuous flow chemistry. The PMDA inspection in Japan was successfully completed with no critical or major observations.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**

*   **FY25 Revenue Growth Target (20-25%):** Management reiterates confidence in meeting the 25% revenue growth target for FY25, citing strong order book visibility and sequential growth expectations from Q1.
*   **Q1 FY25 Revenue Growth (15% YoY):** Achieved 15% YoY revenue growth, aligning with or slightly below the previous guidance of 20-25% for the full year, but expected to be a low point.
*   **EBITDA Margin Outlook:** Management acknowledges the lower Q1 FY25 EBITDA margin (16.7%) compared to Q1 FY24 (22.1%), attributing it to lower gross margins (though sequentially improved), higher employee costs due to increments and new hires for Ankleshwar, and seasonal low revenue. The previous guidance for EBITDA margin improvement to historical levels of 21% and then to 23-26% remains the long-term goal.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) Performance:** BFC's subdued performance continues, operating around Rs. 5 Cr revenue per quarter, due to integration and client-side demand issues, impacting specialty chemical segment growth. Recovery is expected in H2 FY25. Management is actively marketing new products and engaging with customers in Korea, Japan, and Taiwan.
*   **Ankleshwar Unit 2:** One block is operational, with the remaining two blocks on track for Q2 FY25 completion. Detailed utilization and revenue targets for these blocks are premature but expected to contribute significantly over the next 3 years.
*   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization:** Sales for electrolyte additives are expected to start from H2 FY25, with long-term contracts signed. Previous guidance of commencement from Q2 FY25 has shifted slightly.
*   **Apixaban/Rivaroxaban Sales:** Gradual shipments for Apixaban have started, with a significant ramp-up expected in FY26. Rivaroxaban is also being moderately sold but not yet at peak. Management is prepared for price erosion at the intermediate level upon patent expiry.
*   **Solar CAPEX:** The 16 MW solar CAPEX project is on track for completion by end of Q2 FY25, with energy savings expected from Q3 FY25.

**New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**

*   **FY25 Revenue Growth:** Commitment to deliver >25% revenue growth for the full year.
*   **FY25 Revenue Split:** Expectation of 40:60 revenue split between H1 and H2 FY25, driven by CDMO contract commercialization in H2.
*   **FY25 Capex:** Approximately INR 250 crores, including balance for Ankleshwar Unit 2, electrolyte additive infrastructure, solar power, and maintenance.
*   **EBITDA Margin Outlook:** Targeting operational leverage improvement and gross margin recovery to achieve >20% EBITDA margin in FY25, potentially in Q3/Q4. Long-term target remains 23-26%.
*   **Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) Recovery:** Recovery expected to begin in the second half of the year. Improvement projected over the next 1-1.5 years from new customers and applications.
*   **Enchem AMI Organics JV:** JV terms for electrolyte solutions manufacturing in India are under finalization with a Korean partner. Announcement expected soon.
*   **Ankleshwar Unit 2 Utilization:** Blocks 2 and 3 are expected to be commissioned by Q2 FY25. Peak utilization targets for these blocks are premature but expected to ramp up over the next 3 years. Asset turn for Ankleshwar unit is expected to be >3x, potentially >4x.
*   **Apixaban/Rivaroxaban Peak Sales:** Expected in FY26.
*   **Tax Rate:** Standalone tax rate is 25.16%, but combined rate will be slightly higher than 25% due to BFC.
*   **Current Net Cash:** Approximately INR 385 crores as of June.
*   **Product Mix:** Originator business expected to be higher than 50% in pharma. Current mix is ~50% originator, 50% generic in pharma; 80% pharma, 20% commodity in specialty chemicals.
*   **Logistics & Freight:** While costs are incremental, strategic planning and early bookings are mitigating significant sales impact from container availability and longer lead times.
*   **Segment Margins:** Advanced Intermediate margin at 17.1%, Specialty Chemicals at 15.44%.
*   **Ankleshwar Unit 1 Capitalization:** INR 110 crores capitalized, with ~INR 125 crores in WIP. Full quarter impact of depreciation seen in Q1 FY25.
*   **Sachin & Jhagadia Utilization:** Sachin at ~70% (reduced from 72%), Jhagadia at ~50-55% (increased from <50%). Optimal utilization for fungible plants up to 85%.
