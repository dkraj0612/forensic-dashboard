## Executive Overview

Acutaas Chemicals (formerly Ami Organics Ltd.) reported a strong Q3 FY25 with revenue from operations increasing by 65.2% YoY to INR 275 crores, and 11.5% sequentially. This growth was driven by a significant ramp-up in the CDMO business and steady performance in Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates. Management has revised its FY25 revenue growth guidance upwards from 30% to 35%. EBITDA margins improved to 25%, a significant YoY increase of 904 bps, driven by better product mix and operating leverage. The company is now projecting that its overall CDMO business could reach approximately INR 1,000 crores by FY28. New initiatives in battery chemicals and semiconductor chemicals are progressing, with the electrolyte additive CAPEX on track for H1 FY26 completion and BFC expecting a ramp-up from FY27.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates & CDMO:** Strong 85.7% YoY growth driven by CDMO ramp-up and Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates. Management anticipates CDMO revenue to reach approximately INR 1,000 crores by FY28. Several CDMO projects are progressing through clinical trial stages (Phase II/III to approval), expected to drive future growth. Discussions with innovator companies are encouraging, with successful execution of current projects boosting confidence.
*   **Specialty Chemicals (SpecChem):** Reported revenue of INR 36 crores, a slight YoY decline and a sequential drop of 11%, primarily due to lower volumes in commodity chemicals. Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) remained flat YoY but showed sequential growth, with Q2 FY25 seen as the bottom for BFC revenue, expecting consistent growth thereafter, and a ramp-up from FY27. Specialty chemicals also saw some deferred orders and selective order withdrawal based on pricing.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** CAPEX is on track for completion by H1 FY26. Management is adopting a cautious approach for the 'Solution' business (Korean JV) due to market uncertainties, maintaining status quo. However, for electrolyte additives, long-term contracts provide visibility, and the business is expected to ramp up from FY27.
*   **Semiconductor Chemicals (BFC):** Discussions with Japanese and Korean clients are encouraging. While BFC's significant ramp-up is expected from FY27, current initiatives are in validation and sample shipment stages. Management sees significant long-term potential with margins higher than Pharma and CDMO, ranging from 40% to 65% for high-purity molecules.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** Block 2 at Ankleshwar site has commercialized, and Block 1 is expected to commercialize by end of Q4 FY25. This capacity is expected to largely cater to requirements for the next 2-3 years. Additional land is available at Sachin GIDC for future needs.
*   **Sustainability:** Not explicitly mentioned in this transcript, but previously commissioned solar projects are likely contributing to operational efficiencies.
