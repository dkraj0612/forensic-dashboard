## Acutaas Chemicals (Formerly Ami Organics Ltd.) - Q4 FY25 Earnings Call - Executive Summary

**Date:** May 2, 2025

### Executive Overview

Acutaas Chemicals (formerly Ami Organics Ltd.) concluded FY25 with a landmark achievement, surpassing INR 1,000 crores in revenue for the first time. The company reported strong Q4 FY25 performance with revenue from operations reaching INR 308.5 crores, a 37.1% YoY increase. For the full year FY25, revenue grew by 40.3% YoY to INR 1,006.9 crores. EBITDA margins saw significant improvement in Q4 FY25, reaching 27.5%, and for the full year, EBITDA stood at INR 232.1 crores, up 80.6% YoY. The company also announced its rebranding to Acutaas Chemicals Limited, reflecting its expanded vision to become a diversified specialty chemicals company. Management reaffirmed its commitment to delivering 25% revenue growth in FY26 and expects further margin improvements.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Pharmaceutical Intermediates & CDMO:** Pharma Intermediates segment delivered INR 273 crores in Q4 FY25 (+44% YoY) and INR 854 crores for FY25 (+50% YoY), driven by CDMO growth and steady Pharma Intermediates performance. The CDMO business is on track to reach INR 1,000 crores by FY28. Management is confident in securing new CDMO contracts, with some in the qualification stage expected to contribute revenue in H2 FY26. Continuous flow chemistry capabilities have been scaled, with several chemistries successfully implemented and operated commercially.
*   **Specialty Chemicals (SpecChem):** The SpecChem segment reported flattish revenue of INR 36 crores in Q4 FY25 and INR 153 crores for FY25 (+2% YoY). This was primarily due to a degrowth in Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC), offset by strong volume growth (>25%) in commodity chemicals. Other Spec Chem business (excluding BFC) grew well, with parabens and methyl salicylate showing strong volume growth.
*   **Battery Chemicals (Electrolyte Additives):** Capex for the electrolyte additive plant is on track for completion by H1 FY26, with production expected to commence in H2 FY26. The plant will have a capacity of 2,000 metric tons per annum for VC and 2,000 metric tons for FEC. Initial utilization will be low, ramping up over three years. Pricing will be market-driven, with long-term contracts providing visibility and good margins.
*   **Semiconductor Chemicals (BFC):** BFC is seeing steady progress with seeding activities and expansion into Taiwan, Korea, and Japan. New customers have been added, and samples have been submitted. A significant ramp-up is anticipated from FY27 onwards. Management sees long-term potential with high margins. The business is expected to scale up in the next financial year, with new customers and picking up demand.
*   **Manufacturing & R&D:** Capex for FY25 was INR 195 crores, primarily for Ankleshwar site, solar, and electrolyte additive projects. Block 1 at Ankleshwar is expected to be capitalized in Q4 FY25. A 10.8 MW solar plant has been commissioned, projecting annual savings of INR 16-18 crores at peak capacity (16 MW). Another 5 MW solar plant is under development. A new pilot plant facility at Sachin, Surat, is being established to expedite scaling of new products, high-potent chemicals, and CRAMS model.
*   **Rebranding:** The company has officially rebranded to Acutaas Chemicals Limited to reflect its diversified growth strategy.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Targets vs. Current Performance:**
    *   **FY25 Revenue Growth Target (Revised to 35%):** **Met/Exceeded.** The company reported 40.3% YoY growth for FY25, surpassing the revised guidance.
    *   **FY25 CAPEX (~INR 118 crores spent in 9M FY25):** **Met/Exceeded.** Full year capex was INR 195 crores, with INR 130 crores in FY26 to be spillover for electrolyte additives and other projects.
    *   **EBITDA Margin Outlook (FY25: Improved Margins):** **Achieved/Exceeded.** Full year FY25 EBITDA margin was 23.1%, a significant increase. Q4 margins at 27.5% also reflect strong performance.
    *   **Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) Recovery:** **Gradual Improvement.** BFC saw degrowth impacting SpecChem's overall performance, but management expects it to scale up in the next financial year with new customers and picking up demand.
    *   **Electrolyte Additives Commercialization (Completion H1 FY26):** **On Track.** Production expected in H2 FY26.
    *   **CDMO Business Growth (Target INR 1,000 Cr by FY28):** **Strong Progress.** The business is on track, with new contracts in qualification stage expected to contribute revenue in H2 FY26.
    *   **Ankleshwar Unit 2 Completion (Blocks 1 & 2):** **Commercialization Underway.** Block 1 expected to be capitalized in Q4 FY25. Block 2 and 1 are fungible multipurpose facilities. Block 3 is dedicated to a marquee customer.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Tracking:**
    *   **FY26 Revenue Growth:** Confidence in delivering 25% revenue growth.
    *   **FY26 EBITDA Margins:** Expectation of further improvements and sequential increase, with full-year margins expected to be higher than FY25 (23.1%).
    *   **CDMO Business Revenue:** Target of approximately INR 1,000 crores by FY28 remains intact.
    *   **Electrolyte Additive Production:** To commence in H2 FY26, with optimum capacity utilization expected within 3 years.
    *   **BFC Ramp-up:** Targeting scaling up in the next financial year with new customers.
    *   **Specialty Chemicals Growth:** Expected to grow in FY26 due to ramp-up from electrolyte additives and BFC. Commodity chemicals expected to grow at a similar pace to FY25 (>25% volume CAGR).
    *   **Capex for FY26:** Approximately INR 200 crores, including INR 130 crores spillover for electrolyte additives (total INR 170crs for additives), and INR 70 crores for maintenance and pilot plant at Sachin.
    *   **Solar Power Savings:** INR 16-18 crores per annum at peak capacity (16 MW).
    *   **Semiconductor Chemicals Margins:** Potential for high margins, with slow scaling up expected in FY26 and significant ramp-up from FY27.
    *   **Spec Chem Margins:** Expected to remain in the 14-15% range initially, with potential to ramp up to 16-17% by Q4 FY26 or early next financial year. Electrolyte and semiconductor businesses are expected to contribute better margins.

    *   **CDMO Revenue Contribution:** New CDMO contracts are on track for revenue commencement in H2 FY26.
    *   **Generic Portfolio:** Prices are stable, and raw material prices are also stable. Expecting further growth in FY26 due to patent expiries.
    *   **Ankleshwar Site Unit 2 Capex:** Total capex for Unit 2 is INR 310 crores.
    *   **PMDA Approval:** Two manufacturing sites approved by PMDA will help promote intermediates to Japanese customers and make the company a preferred vendor.
    *   **Export vs. Domestic Market:** Both are focus markets with a diversification policy.
