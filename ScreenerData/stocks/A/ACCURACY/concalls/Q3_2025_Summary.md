## Executive Overview

Accuracy Shipping Limited (ASL) reported Q3 FY26 results amidst a volatile global logistics landscape characterized by tariff uncertainties, geopolitical developments, and moderating freight rates. Despite these headwinds, ASL demonstrated resilience, driven by its strong Clearing & Forwarding (C&F) franchise. Revenue for Q3 FY26 stood at INR 157 Crs, and for the nine months ended December 31, 2025 (9MFY26), it reached INR 509 Crs. The company reported a PAT of INR 0.69 Cr for Q3 FY26 and INR 1.9 Crs for 9MFY26. The company's strategic diversification continues to show progress, with the Commercial Vehicles (CV) segment contributing 25% to 9MFY26 revenue and generating EBITDA of INR 3.5 Crs for the quarter and nine months. The Petroleum & Petroleum Products segment contributed 6% to the revenue. ASL is focusing on strengthening core operations, expanding transportation and last-mile capabilities, improving its margin mix, and building scalable verticals.
