## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **End-to-End Logistics:** Providing comprehensive logistics solutions from origin to destination under a single roof, covering the entire value chain.
*   **Revenue Diversification:** Creating new revenue streams through backward and forward integration, adding multiple services, and exploring new verticals.
*   **Pan India Presence with Global Network:** Operates 14 offices across India and has over 60 agency agreements globally.
*   **Industry Diversification:** Catering to a diversified range of industries to accelerate revenue and profit growth. Historically focused on Marble & Granite, now expanding to Paper, Textile, Glass, and other sectors.
*   **Commercial Vehicle (CV) Dealership:** Partnership with Ashok Leyland for the sale and service of Heavy Commercial Vehicles (HCVs) and spare parts across three locations in Kutch. This also serves to service their own fleet, creating cost savings and synergies.
*   **Petroleum Products:** Offers a complete range of lubricants from Shell and Servo, with significant storage capacity in the Kutch region.
*   **Large Marquee Customer Base:** Over 1900 customers across industries, providing revenue sustainability.
*   **Custom Broking Services:** Offers comprehensive customs clearing services for both air and sea, handling a significant volume of TEUs monthly and providing timely EXIM advice.
*   **Warehousing (CFS):** Significant exclusive warehouse space and tie-ups for empty parks for container storage.
*   **Transportation:** Owns a fleet of 372 operational trucks and has exclusive tie-ups for additional vehicles, supported by a 24-hour working team.
*   **Project Cargo:** Specializes in tailor-made logistics solutions for over-dimensional, heavy, and bulky cargo.
