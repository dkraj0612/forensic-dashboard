## Executive Overview

Accuracy Shipping Limited (ASL) hosted its Q4 FY24 earnings call, providing an update on its financial performance and strategic initiatives. The company continues to emphasize its role as an end-to-end logistics solutions provider with a diversified business model encompassing customs clearing, warehousing, transportation, project cargo, petroleum products, and the Ashok Leyland HCV dealership. Management highlighted India's economic resilience amidst global uncertainty, projecting strong GDP growth for FY24. However, the logistics sector, particularly freight forwarding, faced continued headwinds from global demand and geopolitical issues like the Red Sea crisis, impacting revenue and freight rates. Despite these challenges, ASL reported improved EBITDA for Q4 FY24, driven by increased freight rates and improved procurement efficiencies. The company is focused on leveraging its diversified segments and expanding its customer base for future growth.

*   **Petroleum Business Revenue (FY24):** INR 69 Crs

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **End-to-End Logistics Solutions:** Continuing to offer integrated services across the supply chain.
*   **Industry Diversification:** Strategically diversifying across various sectors including marble, granite, ceramics, paper, tiles, machinery, and expanding into rubber and tires to reduce reliance on specific industries. Marble and granite's revenue share decreased from 43% in FY23 to 38% in FY24.
*   **Revenue Diversification:** Offering multiple services such as clearing and forwarding, last mile delivery, warehousing, fuel sales, and HCV sales/servicing.
*   **Customer Acquisition & Wallet Share:** Focus on increasing business from existing customers and onboarding new ones across all services.
*   **Commercial Vehicle (CV) Dealership:** Ashok Leyland dealership for sale & service of HCVs and spare parts. This segment contributed ~28% to FY24 revenue. Management sees this as supporting the core logistics business.
*   **Petroleum Products:** Operating fuel stations in the Kutch region, leveraging large storage capacity. This segment contributes ~4% to FY24 revenue.
*   **Fleet Expansion:** Addition of 11 trucks (5 for Mumbai, 6 for Chennai) to enhance service delivery to MNCs and large clients, costing INR 4.27 Crs.
*   **New Client Acquisitions:** Signed contracts with Borosil and BKT Tires, each expected to contribute around 2% of total turnover.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue & Profitability:** The Q4 FY24 transcript confirms a decline in overall annual revenue for FY24 (INR 708.3 Crs vs. INR 877.3 Crs in FY23) and a significant drop in FY24 EBITDA (INR 24 Crs vs. INR 34.7 Crs in FY23), directly attributed to "subdued global demand" and the "Red Sea crisis." This aligns with management's previously stated headwinds impacting the logistics segment. However, Q4 FY24 EBITDA saw a year-on-year improvement (INR 14.14 Crs vs. INR 12.0 Crs), which management attributes to "improved procurement efficiency and other cost-saving measures" and a significant increase in freight rates, enhancing negotiation capacity.
*   **Diversification Strategy:** Management continues to strongly advocate for its diversification strategy. The transcript highlights the reduced reliance on marble and granite, with their share of revenue decreasing. The Commercial Vehicle (CV) segment's contribution of 28% to FY24 revenue is a key indicator of this strategy. Management reiterates that the CV and petroleum segments are "providing support to the main business" (logistics).
*   **CV Vertical Performance:** The CV segment contributed INR 204 Crs to FY24 revenue. While previously stated as profitable, the current transcript notes that the CV segment is currently at "break-even." Management anticipates further margin improvement in this segment.
*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines to Track:**
    *   **Improved Performance Outlook:** Management is "anticipating for this logistics business to cross around INR700 crores this year" (FY25) and expects a 10% to 15% growth in the logistics industry for FY25.
    *   **CV Segment Targets:** Internal target for the sale of commercial vehicles is INR 300 Crs for FY25.
    *   **Petroleum Segment Targets:** Anticipated revenue of INR 35 Crs for FY25.
    *   **EBITDA Margin Guidance:**
        *   FY25: Anticipating EBITDA margin of around 4%.
        *   FY26: Targeting EBITDA margin of around 5%.
    *   **Debt Reduction:** Current debt level is INR 109 Crs (INR 76 Crs working capital, INR 33 Crs term loan). Repayment of INR 16 Crs is planned for this year, reducing debt by 50%.
    *   **No Divestment:** Management explicitly states they are "not looking for a diversification of business" and the other segments support the core logistics.
    *   **Geopolitical Impact Mitigation:** Management notes the market has "settled by the Cape of Good Hope," implying reduced immediate impact from Red Sea crisis, though open sea routes would be better.
    *   **Clientele Diversification:** No single customer contributes more than 2% of revenue, with a total clientele of 2,500+.
    *   **Fleet Optimization:** New truck purchases are strategic to improve service for MNCs and gain competitive advantage.
