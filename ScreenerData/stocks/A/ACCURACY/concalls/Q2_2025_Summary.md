## Executive Overview

Accuracy Shipping Limited (ASL) experienced a challenging Q2 FY26, marked by a 25% year-on-year decline in revenue to INR 190 Crs. This downturn is attributed to a slowdown in the logistics industry, impacted by the US tariff situation and geopolitical uncertainties, which led to a dip in volumes in core sectors like Marble & Granite and Ceramic Tiles. Despite these headwinds, the company demonstrated sequential QoQ revenue growth of 18%, signaling potential stabilization. H1 FY26 revenue stood at INR 352 Crs. The company's strategic diversification efforts are yielding results, with non-core sectors (Rubber, Paper, Textile, Glass, etc.) contributing approximately 37% of H1 FY26 revenues, a significant shift from historical reliance on Marble & Granite. EBITDA for Q2 FY26 was INR 6.8 Crs, with a margin of 3.6%, showing a YoY improvement of 94 basis points. H1 FY26 EBITDA was INR 12 Crs with a margin of 3.4%. Profit After Tax (PAT) for Q2 FY26 was INR 0.60 Crs (a 37% YoY increase), and INR 1.0 Cr for H1 FY26. Management expressed cautious optimism for the remainder of FY26, focusing on continued industry diversification, operational efficiencies, and improving container volumes.

*   **Sequential Stabilization:** QoQ revenue growth of 18% in Q2 FY26 indicates early signs of market stabilization.
*   **Container Volume Improvement:** Witnessing improved momentum in container volumes supported by strengthening demand.
*   **Freight Rate Management:** Average realizations for H1 FY26 stood at ₹47,539, reflecting an improved value mix and pricing efficiency, despite general freight rate pressure.
*   **Operational Efficiencies:** Ongoing focus on enhancing operational efficiencies to support steady and sustainable growth.
