## Executive Overview

Accuracy Shipping Limited (ASL) presented its Q3 FY24 investor presentation, highlighting operational performance and strategic initiatives. The company continues to position itself as an end-to-end logistics solutions provider with a global network, emphasizing its pan-India presence and diverse customer base. While the Q3 FY24 results indicate revenue and profitability pressures due to macroeconomic headwinds, management remains optimistic about future growth driven by its diversification strategies. The presentation also details various business verticals, including customs clearing, warehousing, transportation, project cargo, petroleum products, and the significant Ashok Leyland HCV dealership.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q3 FY24 Revenue:** Rs. 187.5 Crs (down from Rs. 212.8 Crs in Q3 FY23)
*   **9MFY24 Revenue:** Rs. 537 Crs (down from Rs. 699.8 Crs in 9MFY23)
*   **Q3 FY24 Gross Profit:** Rs. 10.6 Crs (down from Rs. 13.6 Crs in Q3 FY23)
*   **9MFY24 Gross Profit:** Rs. 30.6 Crs (down from Rs. 36.1 Crs in 9MFY23, calculation based on Gross Margins provided)
*   **Q3 FY24 Gross Margin:** 5.6%
*   **9MFY24 Gross Margin:** 5.7%
*   **Q3 FY24 EBITDA:** Rs. 4.4 Crs (down from Rs. 7.7 Crs in Q3 FY23)
*   **9MFY24 EBITDA:** Rs. 9.6 Crs (down from Rs. 22.6 Crs in 9MFY23)
*   **Q3 FY24 EBITDA Margin:** 1.8%
*   **9MFY24 EBITDA Margin:** 1.8%
*   **Commercial Vehicle (CV) Segment EBITDA (9MFY24):** Rs. 2.2 Crs

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **End-to-End Logistics Solutions:** Offering integrated services from origin to destination.
*   **Revenue Diversification:** Adding new revenue streams through backward and forward integration, and expanding service offerings.
*   **Pan India Presence with Global Network:** 14 offices in India and 64 agency agreements globally.
*   **Industry Diversification:** Reducing dependency on core sectors (Marble & Granite, Ceramic Tiles) by catering to sectors like Rubber (21% of 9MFY24 revenue), Paper, Textile, and Glass, which cumulatively contribute ~35% of revenue for 9MFY24.
*   **Commercial Vehicle (CV) Dealership:** Ashok Leyland dealership for sale and service of HCVs and spare parts across three locations in Kutch. This segment contributed ~30% to revenue in 9MFY24.
*   **Petroleum Products:** Offering Shell and Servo lubricants with significant storage capacity in the Kutch region.
*   **Large Marquee Customer Base:** Over 1900 customers providing revenue sustainability.
*   **Custom Broking Services:** Handling approximately 6,000 TEUs monthly, offering comprehensive import/export customs clearance.
*   **Warehousing (CFS):** ~1,80,000 sq. ft exclusive warehouse space and ~6,00,000 sq. ft of exclusive tie-ups for empty parks.
*   **Transportation:** Operating 371 trucks and having exclusive tie-ups for additional vehicles.
*   **Project Cargo:** Specialized solutions for over-dimensional, heavy, and bulky cargo.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue & Profitability:** The Q3 FY24 transcript confirms a decline in revenue and profitability compared to Q3 FY23 and 9MFY23. Management attributes this directly to "weak global demand and containers availability" and "weak global demand amid high inflationary pressures and container availability" for Q3 FY24 and 9MFY24 respectively. This acknowledges macroeconomic headwinds, a shift from the historical context of strong growth driven by favorable conditions.

*   **Diversification Strategy:** Management reiterates its commitment to the diversification strategy, highlighting the increasing contribution of the Commercial Vehicles (CV) segment (~30% of 9MFY24 revenue, ~2.2 Crs EBITDA for 9MFY24) and other sectors (~35% of 9MFY24 revenue) as evidence of successful diversification efforts. This aligns with previous statements about adding meaningful revenue from diversified services.

*   **CV Vertical Performance:** The transcript notes that the CV vertical "continues to be profitable and proves beneficial to our strategy of revenue diversification," and management anticipates "gradual improvement in margins and anticipate the same to improve further." This indicates a positive outlook on this segment despite overall pressure.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines to Track:**
    *   **Optimism for Improved Performance:** Management remains "optimistic of delivering improved performance backed by our strategic emphasis on Revenue Diversification and Industry Diversification and increase wallet share from existing customers."
    *   **Focus on Revenue Growth and Operating Leverage:** "Going forward we continue to focus on improving our revenues and operating leverage to expand our EBITDA."
    *   **CV Segment Margin Improvement:** Anticipation of "gradual improvement in margins" within the commercial vehicle segment, leading to enhanced overall EBITDA.
    *   **Continued Commitment to End-to-End Logistics:** The core strategy of providing comprehensive logistics solutions under one roof is reinforced.
    *   **No specific revenue or EBITDA targets provided for upcoming quarters.**
