## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics Limited reported strong Q3 FY16 earnings, driven by significant growth in its Gas division, particularly in LPG throughput volumes. The company highlighted four key announcements expected to boost future earnings: securing terminal agreements with Indian Oil for Pipavav and Mumbai LPG terminals leading to an anticipated 30-40% increase in throughput volumes, a record provisional order book for its LPG sourcing subsidiary (AGI), the commissioning of a second chemical berth at Mumbai Port significantly enhancing competitiveness, and a strategic agreement with ESSAR Oil to sell branded petrol and diesel at Aegis's Auto Gas stations. Management expressed confidence in sustained growth due to India's booming LPG demand and the company's expanding infrastructure and strategic partnerships.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** Q3 FY16 revenue was Rs. 528.8 crores, compared to Rs. 954.7 crores in the prior year. Management attributed this year-on-year revenue decrease to the sharp fall in international gas and oil prices, which are passed on to customers.
*   **Segment EBITDA:** Total segment EBITDA for Q3 FY16 was Rs. 53.8 crores, a 19% increase year-on-year (Rs. 45.2 crores).
    *   Liquid Terminals Division EBITDA: Rs. 26.4 crores (up 14.3% YoY).
    *   Gas Terminals Division EBITDA: Rs. 27.4 crores (up 24% YoY), an all-time quarterly high for the division.
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs. 36 crores in Q3 FY16, a 40% rise year-on-year (Rs. 25.6 crores), excluding a Rs. 31 crore pre-tax gain on the sale of AGI stake to ITOCHU in the prior year.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 32.8 crores in Q3 FY16, a 68.6% rise year-on-year (Rs. 19.5 crores), excluding a Rs. 23 crore after-tax gain from the ITOCHU stake sale in the prior year.
*   **Dividend:** Board approved an interim dividend of 30 paisa per share for Q3 FY16.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **LPG Terminal Agreements:** Signed terminal agreements with Indian Oil for Pipavav and Mumbai LPG terminals, expected to boost incremental throughput volumes by 30-40% (300,000-400,000 MT annualized) over FY14-15 levels, potentially reaching close to 1 million tons throughput for the first time in FY15-16.
*   **LPG Sourcing (AGI):** Provisional order book for AGI for the next four quarters (including Q4 FY16) is the highest in its history, indicating a significant jump from the typical 700,000 MT annual volume.
*   **Mumbai Port Competitiveness:** Commissioning of the second chemical berth at Mumbai Port is a significant development, enabling handling of larger ships (VLGCs) and increasing total port capacity by 2.5 million MT per year. This is expected to boost competitiveness and throughput volumes for Aegis's Mumbai terminals.
*   **Retail LPG (Auto Gas) Expansion:** Agreement with ESSAR Oil to convert 55-60 existing Auto Gas stations to sell ESSAR branded diesel and petrol, alongside Aegis Auto LPG. This partnership is expected to generate an additional Rs. 4-5 crores in pre-tax profits in FY16-17 and boost Auto Gas volumes by 9,000-10,000 MT per year by FY17-18.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:** Ongoing projects include Pipavav LPG expansion (2,700 MT spheres by March/April 2016), and planned commissioning of new capacity at Kandla (100,000 KL) and Haldia (25,000 KL) liquid terminals in Q3 and Q4 FY16-17, respectively.
*   **Market Opportunity:** Driven by India's growing LPG import reliance, constrained domestic supply, inadequate infrastructure, and high logistics costs, coupled with a booming LPG consumption driven by increased penetration and historically low international prices.
