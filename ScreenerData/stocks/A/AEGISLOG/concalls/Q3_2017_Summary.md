## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Vertical Integration:** Creating a "Leading LPG Sourcing Player in India" through strategic alliances, exemplified by the JV with ITOCHU in Singapore for Aegis Group International Pte. Ltd. (AGI).
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:** Building a "necklace of terminals around the coastline of India" at deep-water ports to accommodate VLGCs. Key projects include:
    *   Haldia: Greenfield capacity expansion (2 x 12,500 MT tanks, 24 carousel bottling plant) costing Rs. 250 Cr for LPG terminal and Rs. 25 Cr for bottling plant.
    *   Mumbai: Debottlenecking of LPG terminals to add 400,000 MT incremental capacity (total 1,100,000 MT).
    *   Pipavav: Brownfield capacity expansion aiming for ~800,000 MT at full utilization, with throughput volumes expected to grow through existing and new customer relationships.
    *   Kandla: Greenfield Liquid Terminal Expansion (Rs. 75 Cr).
    *   Mangalore: Greenfield Liquid Terminal Expansion (25,000 KL, Rs. 18 Cr).
    *   Haldia Port: Brownfield Liquid Terminal Expansion (35,000 KL, Rs. 15 Cr).
*   **LPG Sourcing & Supply:** Capitalizing on India's LPG demand-supply gap and import needs, aiming for cost leadership through AGI.
*   **Auto Gas Retailing:** Distributing LPG as auto fuel through a network of 107 Auto Gas Stations across 7 states, alongside Bulk Industrial Distribution and Packed Cylinder distribution.
*   **Fee-Based Revenue Model:** Primarily a fee-based model for Gas Logistics, with revenue streams from handling and other service charges (O&M fees), Auto Gas Retailing, and Packed LPG Cylinders for the Commercial segment.
*   **Market Opportunity:** Addressing the demand-supply gap in LPG in India where incremental demand is met through imports, while terminal capacity has a shortfall, leading to high demurrage costs.
