## Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) - Q2 FY'26 Earnings Call - One-Page Executive Summary

### Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) delivered a robust Q2 FY'26, showcasing strong growth momentum with consolidated revenue up 31% YoY to INR 2,294 crores, normalized EBITDA rising 46% YoY to INR 347 crores, and Profit After Tax (PAT) surging 61% YoY to INR 244 crores. This performance was driven by increased volume expansion and operational efficiencies across its Liquid and LPG segments, coupled with improved terminal utilization. The company continues to progress on its ambitious infrastructure development pipeline, with significant capacity expansions underway across key ports like JNPT, Mumbai, Kandla, and Kochi, alongside strategic moves into the ammonia sector. The Vadhavan Port opportunity and the integration of Haldia operations into Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited (AVTL) are key strategic highlights. Management expresses confidence in sustaining this growth trajectory and exceeding previous long-term CAGR guidance.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & New Terminals:**
    *   **Mumbai Port:** Additional 64,000 KL liquid capacity expected operational by Q1 FY'27 (INR 125 Cr CAPEX).
    *   **JNPT:** 318,100 CBM liquid capacity, 77,286 MT LPG capacity, and 35,000 MTPA LPG bottling plant under development (INR 1,675 Cr CAPEX). Partial liquid capacity expected by end of FY'26. Exploring additional 36,000 MT cryogenic GAS tank.
    *   **Kandla Port:** New 94,148 CBM liquid terminal planned (operational next year). VLGC berthing to commence Q3 FY'26. PNGRB approval received for Kandla-Gorakhpur (KGPL) pipeline connection.
    *   **Kochi Port:** Additional 60,000 CBM liquid capacity planned.
    *   **Pipavav Port:** India's first 36,000 MT ammonia terminal nearing completion (operational Q1 FY'27). KGPL pipeline connection expected Q4 FY'26. Rail gantry for liquid product evacuation underway.
    *   **Haldia Port:** Acquired 3 acres for ~60,000-100,000 CBM liquid capacity expansion. Proposed consolidation of Hindustan Aegis under AVTL.
    *   **Vadhavan Port:** Non-binding MOU signed for potential INR 20,000 Cr project; awaiting land allotment and approvals.
*   **Green Energy & Sustainability:**
    *   **Ammonia Terminals:** Pipavav ammonia terminal to be operational in Q1 FY'27. Kandla ammonia terminal planned pending binding agreement with Larsen & Toubro. Exploring vertical integration in ammonia business.
*   **Infrastructure Development:**
    *   **KGPL Pipeline:** Expected commissioning by Q4 FY'26.
    *   **JLPL Pipeline:** Connectivity expected in Q3 FY'26.
*   **Financial Growth:**
    *   Record Q2 FY'26 revenue and EBITDA in both Liquid and Gas segments.
    *   Ambitious CAPEX program with AVTL aiming for $1.2 billion aggregate outlay by next year, with a long-term plan to reach $5 billion by 2030. Maintaining conservative debt gearing.
    *   Haldia consolidation expected to be positive for EPS.
