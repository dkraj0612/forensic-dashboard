## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) has reported its H1 2022-23 results, demonstrating robust year-on-year growth driven by significant increases in revenue and EBITDA. The company highlights the successful commencement of its partnership with Royal Vopak, the launch of the Kandla LPG terminal, and the acquisition of the Friends terminal as key achievements solidifying its market leadership in various ports across India for both liquid and gas logistics. Management reiterated its vision to be India's leading oil, gas, and chemical logistics provider, emphasizing quality, reliability, and service. Environmental sustainability is now integrated into terminal design. The company anticipates a strong second half of the fiscal year, building on the performance of the first half. A special interim dividend of Rs. 2 per share was declared, reflecting the strong cash position post the joint venture transaction.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** Increased to Rs. 4,386 crores in H1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 1,313 crores YoY), primarily due to higher sourcing volumes.
*   **Normalized EBITDA:** Grew by 33% YoY to Rs. 347 crores in H1 FY23, marking a lifetime high.
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rose by 21% YoY to Rs. 259 crores in H1 FY23.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Increased by 21% YoY to Rs. 209 crores in H1 FY23.
*   **Liquid Terminal Division:**
    *   Revenue: Rs. 188 crores in H1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 130 crores YoY), a 44% increase.
    *   EBITDA: Rs. 124 crores in H1 FY23 (a 31% increase).
*   **Gas Terminal Division:**
    *   Revenue: Rs. 4,198 crores in H1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 1,183 crores YoY).
    *   EBITDA: Rs. 223 crores in H1 FY23 (a 34% increase).
*   **LPG Throughput Volumes (Mumbai, Haldia, Pipavav):** 1.47 million metric tons in H1 FY23 (vs. 1.305 million tonnes YoY), a 13% increase.
*   **Bulk Industrial Distribution Volume:** 173,990 metric tons in H1 FY23 (vs. 45,816 metric tons YoY), a 379% increase.
*   **Commercial & Domestic Cylinder Distribution:** 17,310 metric tons in H1 FY23 (vs. 11,352 metric tons YoY), a 52% increase.
*   **Autogas Sales:** 10,248 metric tons in H1 FY23 (vs. 9,554 metric tons YoY), a 7% increase.
*   **Sourcing Business Volume:** 457,960 metric tons in H1 FY23 (vs. 159,633 metric tons YoY).
*   **Special Interim Dividend:** Rs. 2 per share.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **"Necklace of Terminals" Strategy:** Continues to be a cornerstone, with market leadership established in Kandla, Pipavav, Mumbai, Mangalore, Kochi, and Haldia for both liquids and LPG. New locations are targeted for similar market leadership.
*   **LPG & Gas Logistics:** Focus on increasing throughput and distribution volumes. Development of multi-modal connectivity and VLGC compliance at jetties (Pipavav, Kandla Oil Jetty #7) to enhance competitiveness and position terminals as gateways for North and Central India LPG pipelines.
*   **Distribution Business Growth:** Expected to flourish, contributing significantly to base throughput. This includes bulk industrial, commercial, domestic, and autogas segments.
*   **Vopak Joint Venture:** Achieved successful closure and is performing in line with expectations, actively evaluating new business opportunities.
*   **Environmental Sustainability:** Incorporated into the design philosophy of new terminals and retrofitting of older facilities.
*   **Market Drivers:** Continued demand for LPG as a clean fuel, driven by price sensitivity and India's unique market positioning favoring LPG over natural gas.
