## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) reported a strong Q3 FY22, demonstrating a significant recovery and return to growth following a weaker Q1 FY22 impacted by the Delta wave. The company achieved a lifetime high in EBITDA for the quarter and posted its highest ever profit before tax, signaling a robust performance and reinforcing the positive trend observed in Q2 FY22. Management declared an interim dividend of Rs. 2 per share, reflecting increased confidence in business conditions and future prospects. The Vopak joint venture is progressing towards financial closure by March 31, 2022, and remains a key strategic focus for future growth.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** Rs. 1,214 crores in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 635 crores in Q2 FY22), driven by higher sourcing volumes.
*   **Group EBITDA:** Rs. 159.5 crores in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 147.6 crores in Q2 FY22 and Rs. 147 crores in Q3 FY21), a lifetime high, representing an 8% increase over the previous quarter.
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs. 132 crores in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 124 crores in Q2 FY22 and adjusted Rs. 120 crores in Q3 FY21), a 6% increase over Q2 FY22 and 10% over Q3 FY21, marking the highest PBT of any previous quarter.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) attributable to common shareholders:** Rs. 102 crores in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 94 crores in Q2 FY22 and Rs. 85 crores in Q3 FY21), a 9% increase over Q2 FY22 and 20% over Q3 FY21.
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Rs. 2.90 for Q3 FY22, cumulative Rs. 7.49 for the first 9 months of FY21-22.
*   **Liquid Terminal Division:**
    *   Revenue: Rs. 68 crores in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 64 crores in Q2 FY22), a 5% increase QoQ and 19% YoY.
    *   EBITDA: Rs. 47 crores (estimated at ~70% margin) in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 39 crores in Q3 FY21), a 18% YoY increase, reflecting capacity additions.
*   **Gas Terminal Segment:**
    *   Revenue: Rs. 1,146 crores in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 571 crores in Q2 FY22).
    *   EBITDA: Rs. 113 crores in Q3 FY22 (vs. Rs. 101 crores in Q2 FY22 and Rs. 108 crores in Q3 FY21), an 11% increase QoQ and 5% YoY.
*   **LPG Throughput Volumes (Mumbai, Haldia, Pipavav):** 752,759 MT in Q3 FY22 (vs. 737,770 MT in Q2 FY22), showing a rebound.
*   **Bulk Industrial Segment Volume:** 29,662 MT in Q3 FY22 (vs. 23,545 MT in Q2 FY22), a 26% growth QoQ and 28% YoY.
*   **Commercial & Domestic Cylinder Segment Volume:** 6,414 MT in Q3 FY22 (vs. 6,313 MT in Q2 FY22), steady performance.
*   **Autogas Sales Volume:** 5,961 MT in Q3 FY22 (vs. 5,987 MT in Q2 FY22), slightly lower QoQ and YoY.
*   **Sourcing Business Volume:** 125,858 MT in Q3 FY22 (vs. 59,581 MT in Q2 FY22).
*   **Interim Dividend:** Rs. 2 per share.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **LPG Business Recovery:** The company is seeing a strong rebound in its LPG business, with significant volume recovery at Haldia and Pipavav, driven by organic growth and improving infrastructure.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** Progress on VLGC compliance at Pipavav and Kandla oil jetty #7 is crucial for enhancing competitiveness. The central India LPG pipeline (KGPL) Phase I completion is also a key driver.
*   **Distribution Network Expansion:** Commissioning of new LPG bottling plants at Wada and Jamnagar supports the retail network growth. Continued expansion of the auto LPG station network is planned.
*   **Vopak Joint Venture:** Progressing towards financial closure by March 31, 2022, this JV is expected to be a significant growth driver.
*   **Focus on Margins:** Management highlighted improved margins in the distribution segments (packed cylinders and auto LPG) as a key contributor to the rise in EBITDA, alongside stable throughput margins.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Previous Promises vs. Current Transcript:**

*   **Q1 FY22 Performance:** Management acknowledged a "weaker start" in Q1 FY22 due to the Delta wave, consistent with expectations. The recovery in Q2 and reinforcement in Q3 aligns with their forecast of returning to normal performance.
*   **Vopak Joint Venture:** The commitment to complete the financial closure by March 31, 2022, remains. Progress is described as "smoothly" and "on track."
*   **Kandla LPG Terminal Commissioning:** Despite prior indications of a March 31st commissioning, management now states that welding is ongoing and commissioning is expected by March 31st, indicating some potential for minor delays or near-term completion. Regulatory permissions are confirmed.
*   **Pipavav VLGC Compliance:** Previously expected by June 2022, management confirms the jetty will be VLGC compliant by end of June 2022, aligning with prior guidance.
*   **Kandla Oil Jetty #7 VLGC Compliance:** Expected completion by September 2022 remains consistent with prior timelines.
*   **KGPL Pipeline (Phase-I):** Expected completion by December 2022 is confirmed.
*   **Distribution Business Growth:** Management expressed disappointment with the flat auto LPG volumes despite new station additions, citing COVID-related jitters (Omicron scare in December) and the ramp-up time for new stations. However, they reiterated confidence in the segment's margin and future growth. The packed cylinder segment growth is steady.
*   **Financial Performance:** The reported Q3 FY22 results show significant improvement, achieving lifetime highs in EBITDA and PBT, fulfilling management's expectation of a good overall performance for FY22.
*   **Liquid Terminal Margins:** Management clarified that the ~70% EBITDA margin in the liquid segment is healthy and within the guided range of 65-75%, with variations dependent on product mix. They noted this is a sustainable margin.
*   **LPG Throughput Utilization:** Management addressed concerns about utilization, explaining that the abnormal Q3 FY20 throughput was due to specific one-off events (refinery issues). They emphasized a gradual capacity utilization growth over 5-7 years and projected a 4 million tonne run rate next year (FY23) post-Kandla commissioning. They acknowledged that Pipavav and Haldia volumes are on a recovery path and will improve over the next 8-12 quarters.
