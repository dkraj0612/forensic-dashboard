## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) commenced FY'25 with a robust Q1 performance, marking the highest-ever figures for volumes, EBITDA, Profit Before Tax, and Profit After Tax across both its gas and liquid divisions. The company achieved an EBITDA of INR 250 crores for the quarter, with the liquid division contributing INR 108 crores and the LPG division INR 142 crores. Profit after tax grew by 19% year-over-year to INR 158 crores. This strong start is attributed to continued volume growth at the Kandla terminal, new capacity additions in the liquid segment, and full utilization of newly commissioned facilities. Management expressed high confidence in sustaining this momentum, reiterating their guidance of achieving a 25% CAGR over the next three years, fueled by ongoing Greenfield and Brownfield expansions. The company remains committed to supporting India's transition to cleaner fuels, with plans to commence construction of its first ammonia terminal in Gujarat this fiscal year and potentially build more in the future. Nearly 50% of the INR 4,500 crore capital expenditure program (by FY'27) is already completed or in progress, with Pipavav and Mangalore LPG terminals expected to be commissioned on schedule and budget, generating revenue from Q1 FY'26.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Capex:**
    *   **Liquid Division:** JNPT terminal (110,000 kl) on track for commissioning this year. Mangalore expansion (71,000 kl) expected within 12-15 months. Kandla capacity increased to 970,000 kl, with an additional 25,000 kl planned. Kochi expansion of 25,000 kl underway. Haldia expansion (50,000 kl) commissioned last year and will reflect in FY'25 results. New Mumbai terminal (150,000 kl) approved by the board, with an estimated cost of INR 250 crores.
    *   **Gas Division:** Pipavav adding two cryogenic LPG tanks (45,000 MT total capacity), expected commissioning in Q1 FY'26. Mangalore cryogenic LPG project (85,000 MT capacity) progressing on schedule.
    *   **JV Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd (AVTL):** INR 4,500 crores capex plan by FY'27 is progressing well, with nearly 50% completed or underway. The pace of capital spending is expected to continue beyond FY'27.
*   **Green Energy Focus:** Construction of the first ammonia terminal in Gujarat to commence this fiscal year after regulatory approvals. The company aims to become vertically integrated in this business similar to its LPG operations.
*   **Distribution & Retail Business Growth:** Distribution volumes were impacted in Q1 but EBITDA realizations were strong and sustainable. Management expects growth to accelerate with new terminals coming online and a reversal of previous pricing pressures.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** Emphasis on port jetty capabilities for VLGC berthing, cryogenic tank capacities, and multi-modal evacuation (rail, road, pipeline) to enhance efficiency and reduce logistics costs for customers.
