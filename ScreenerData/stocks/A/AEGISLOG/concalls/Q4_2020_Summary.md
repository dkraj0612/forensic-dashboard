## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics announces a transformative Joint Venture (JV) with Royal Vopak, aiming to significantly bolster its LPG and chemical storage and handling business in India. The JV, named Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd (AVTL), will be 51% owned by Aegis and 49% by Vopak. This strategic alliance leverages Aegis's Indian market leadership and execution capabilities with Vopak's global expertise, standards, and customer base. The combined entity will initially possess 960,000 cbm of storage capacity. Aegis will retain full ownership of its Mumbai liquid and LPG terminals and its retail business. The deal signifies a major step in Aegis's "necklace of terminals" strategy, accelerating growth plans and diversifying into new product storage, including potential renewable energy projects.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Transaction Value:**
    *   Total pre-tax cash consideration to Aegis Logistics Ltd. for stake sale of 49% in Aegis terminals and 24% in HALPG business (after purchase of CRL Terminals): **Rs. 2,568 Crs**
    *   Potential additional payment to Aegis by Vopak based on revenue projections: **Up to Rs. 198 Crs** (paid in Year 3)
*   **JV Structure:**
    *   Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd (AVTL) will be 51% owned by Aegis, 49% by Vopak.
    *   AVTL will acquire 100% of Kandla Logistics Ltd, KSSPL, and CRL Terminals Ltd.
    *   Vopak will acquire 49% of AVTL and 24% of Hindustan Aegis LPG (HALPG).
*   **Combined Initial Capacity:** 960,000 cbm.
*   **Debt:** AVTL will assume external debt with conservative leverage ratios. Future capex for AVTL will be funded by free cash flows, additional project debt, and shareholder injections.
*   **Taxes:** Aegis to pay applicable taxes on the transaction.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Strategy:** Execution of the "necklace of terminals" strategy, amplified by the JV with Vopak to drive accelerated growth and expand capabilities.
*   **Joint Venture (AVTL):**
    *   **Objective:** To capitalize on India's growing LPG, chemical, and other gas storage and handling opportunities.
    *   **Synergies:** Aegis brings Indian market knowledge and assets; Vopak brings global expertise, standards, and new product handling capabilities.
    *   **Growth Acceleration:** Plans to execute strategic connectivity projects over the next 3-5 years (FY2023-2027) to achieve full capacity utilization of LPG terminals.
*   **Market Drivers:**
    *   **India's Energy Demand:** Expected tripling of fossil fuel imports over the next two decades due to rising energy demand and decommissioning of old refineries.
    *   **LPG Import Growth:** Significant increase in LPG imports projected over 20 years, driving demand for import terminals, capacity, and throughput.
    *   **Clean Fuel Transition:** Large segment of the Indian population still relies on solid biomass for cooking, creating a substantial market for LPG as a clean alternative.
*   **Infrastructure Development:**
    *   **VLGC Compliant Jetties:** To enable larger vessels and reduce shipping costs.
    *   **Terminal Expansions:** Further expansion of liquid tanks capacity at existing terminals.
    *   **Multi-modal Transport:** Integration of rail, barge, and pipeline connectivity for LPG to reduce delivered costs.
    *   **Inland Depots:** To improve logistics for LPG.
    *   **New Greenfield Liquid Terminals:** To serve dedicated industrial clients.
    *   **Other Gases & Renewables:** Exploration of storage for other gases and renewable energy projects.
*   **Retail Business:** Focus on scaling up the retail business, building brand awareness ("Puregas," "Aegis Autogas"), and expanding the national distribution network.
*   **Customer Focus:** Serving national oil companies, MNCs, specialty chemical companies, and traders with a pan-India footprint and access to global best practices.
