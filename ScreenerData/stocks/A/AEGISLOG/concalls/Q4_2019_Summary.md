## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics' Q4 FY2019 earnings presentation continues to emphasize its "necklace of terminals" strategy, focusing on robust growth in LPG throughput and retail, alongside ongoing expansion in liquid terminals. While explicit Q4 financial results are not directly comparable to prior periods in this transcript, the company highlights significant volume increases across key segments. Investments in new capacities and infrastructure are being executed, notably at Haldia for LPG and across several locations for liquid terminals. Management's tone indicates confidence in operationalizing these new assets and capitalizing on India's growing LPG demand, which is increasingly met by imports. The presentation also touches upon the company's revenue model and reinforces its strategic positioning within the Indian oil, gas, and chemical logistics sector.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Strategy:** Aegis continues to execute its "necklace of terminals" strategy, building a contiguous network of deep-water port-based logistics and supply chain infrastructure for oil, gas, and chemicals along India's coastline.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:**
    *   **Liquid Terminals:** Significant brownfield and greenfield expansions are noted at Kandla (Rs. 75 crs, ~811,000 KL total capacity), Mangalore (Rs. 18 crs, 25,000 KL), and Haldia (Rs. 35 crs, 35,000 KL).
    *   **Gas Terminals (LPG):** Major greenfield expansion at Haldia (Rs. 250 crs for terminal, Rs. 25 crs for bottling plant) with 25,000 MT static capacity and 2.5M MT throughput potential. Debottlenecking at Mumbai (Rs. 15 crs) adds 400,000 MT throughput. Focus on leveraging pipeline connectivity (JLPL, KGPL at Kandla; Uran-Chakan at Mumbai).
*   **Market Drivers:**
    *   **LPG Demand-Supply Gap:** The presentation highlights India's growing LPG consumption, with incremental demand increasingly met through imports, directly driving the need for terminal capacity.
    *   **Vertical Integration:** The strategy to create a "Leading LPG Sourcing Player in India" through a JV with Itochu is reiterated.
    *   **Retail Expansion:** Growth in Auto Gas Retailing (114 stations) and Commercial LPG Distribution (155 distributors) are key revenue stream drivers.
*   **Revenue Streams:** Primarily fee-based revenue for gas logistics throughput, handling charges, O&M fees. Margins from Auto Gas Retailing and distribution of packed LPG cylinders for the commercial segment also contribute.
*   **Operational Strengths:** Strategic port locations, deep-water access for VLGCs, diverse tank types, road/rail/pipeline connectivity, and a strong infrastructure network.
