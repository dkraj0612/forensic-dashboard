## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) reported a strong FY'24 with record consolidated normalized EBITDA exceeding INR 1,000 crores. The company achieved record revenues and EBITDA in its Liquid Division and record volumes in its Logistics and Distribution segments. Full-year Profit After Tax grew by 32% to INR 672 crores, with EPS reaching INR 16.22, a 23% increase year-over-year. Management attributes this performance to continued volume growth in Kandla, expansion of the distribution business, and new liquid capacity utilization. The company also announced a final dividend of INR 2 per share, bringing the total for FY'24 to INR 6.50 per share. Aegis Logistics remains focused on its strategy of capacity expansion, M&A, distribution network growth, and exploring opportunities in sustainable fuels, notably announcing the commencement of construction for its first ammonia terminal in Gujarat. Management is confident in sustaining the positive momentum into FY'25 and beyond, driven by ongoing capex and project commissioning.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & New Terminals:**
    *   **JNPT:** 110,000 KL terminal progressing well, on time and budget, for commissioning later in the year.
    *   **Pipavav:** Increased volumes due to VLGC handling capabilities. Adding 2 cryogenic tanks (45,000 MT total) expected by Q1 FY'26. LPG bottling plant commissioned. New liquid berth announced by the port.
    *   **Mangalore:** Acquired 44,168 KL specialized storage terminal. Additional capacity under construction, expected by end of FY'25. Total liquid capacity to reach 154,000 KL. Additional 71,000 KL liquid expansion in the next 12-15 months. 85,000-ton cryogenic LPG project on schedule.
    *   **Kandla:** Commissioned 35,000 KL liquid tanks in Q4 FY'24, bringing total to ~970,000 KL. Additional 25,000 KL tankage next year. LPG bottling plant commissioned.
    *   **Kochi:** Acquired 16,000 KL liquid storage terminal, performing well. Expanding capacity by another 25,000 KL.
    *   **Haldia:** Expansion of 50,000 KL liquid tankage completed.
    *   **Overall:** Total storage capacity ~2 million KL. Plan to construct additional 300,000 KL in FY'25.
*   **Distribution Network Growth:**
    *   Continued focus on expanding footprint, signing up more distributors.
    *   Anticipate step-up in distribution volumes with Pipavav and Mangalore cryogenic projects in FY'26.
*   **Infrastructure Development:**
    *   **KGPL Pipeline:** Progressing well, expected to come online timed with cryogenic projects (end of FY'25/Q1 FY'26).
*   **Green Energy & Sustainability:**
    *   **Ammonia Terminal:** Definitive announcement of commencing construction in Gujarat this fiscal year (post-permits). Initial capacity of 36,000 MT, with potential for ~1.2 million tons throughput annually. Capex estimated at INR 500 crores. Expectation to become vertically integrated in ammonia, similar to LPG.
*   **Financial Growth:**
    *   Record consolidated EBITDA, Liquid and Gas EBITDA.
    *   Strong PAT and EPS growth.
    *   Robust financial position with low debt and strong cash flow.
    *   Major expansion program of INR 4,500 crores by FY'27 progressing on time and budget.
