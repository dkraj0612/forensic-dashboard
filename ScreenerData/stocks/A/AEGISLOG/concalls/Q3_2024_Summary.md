## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) delivered a strong Q3 FY'24 performance, with nine-month cumulative figures showing record highs in EBITDA, Liquid revenues, Liquid EBITDA, and Gas EBITDA. The company is progressing well on its INR 1,750 crore expansion program, with several projects commissioned or nearing completion across Pipavav, Kandla, and JNPT. Management expressed confidence in sustained momentum into Q4 FY'24 and a solid base for FY'25, driven by ongoing capacity expansions and strategic initiatives. The company is also actively exploring opportunities in sustainable fuels and new energy.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Normalized EBITDA (9M FY'24):** INR 675 Cr (Record High)
*   **Consolidated Profit Before Tax (9M FY'24):** INR 554 Cr (+28% YoY)
*   **Consolidated Profit After Tax (9M FY'24):** INR 435 Cr (+24% YoY)
*   **Consolidated EPS (9M FY'24):** INR 10.62 (+16% YoY)
*   **Liquid Revenues (9M FY'24):** INR 356 Cr (+18% YoY)
*   **Liquid EBITDA (9M FY'24):** INR 243 Cr (+21% YoY) (Record High)
*   **Gas EBITDA (9M FY'24):** INR 431 Cr (+12% YoY) (Record High Profitability)
*   **LPG Throughput Volumes (9M FY'24):** 3 million MT (+22% YoY) (Record High)
*   **Distribution Volumes (9M FY'24):** 433,280 MT (+21% YoY)
*   **Cash on Hand:** INR 1,700 Cr
*   **Bank Debts:** INR 1,200 Cr

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & New Terminals:**
    *   Pipavav: Jetty equipped for VLGCs, LPG bottling plant commissioned, two additional gas spheres commissioned (adding 3,700 MT).
    *   Kandla: 35,000 KL liquid tankage to be commissioned in Q4 FY'24, LPG bottling plant commissioned.
    *   JNPT: 110,000 KL liquid terminal progressing, ~20,000 KL expected in Q4 FY'24, balance by June 2024.
    *   Mangalore: 76,000 KL liquid expansion progressing, partial operations expected by end of FY'24, balance in Q1 FY'25.
    *   Kochi: Acquired additional 16,000 KL storage tank terminal.
    *   Haldia: Expansion of 50,000 KL liquid tankage completed.
*   **Distribution Network Growth:**
    *   Focus on industrial clusters in Gujarat, Mumbai, and developing markets in Haldia.
    *   Anticipate growth in the South with Mangalore commissioning.
    *   Exploring opportunities for LPG-powered generator sets and boilers, with technical challenges for larger gensets but strong traction for industrial heat applications due to emission mandates.
*   **Infrastructure Development:**
    *   Pipavav rail gantry operational.
    *   Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline (KGPL) expected commissioning by December 2024. NOC has reserved 1.5 million tons for Aegis.
*   **Green Energy & Sustainability:**
    *   Active projects in ammonia, with advanced stages and expectation of concrete announcements when terms are finalized.
    *   Focus on supporting India's transition to a sustainable future.
*   **Financial Growth:**
    *   Record nine-month cumulative EBITDA, Liquid and Gas EBITDA.
    *   Strong growth in Profit Before Tax and Profit After Tax.
    *   Robust financial position with low debt and strong cash flows.
    *   Aggressive CAPEX program of INR 4,500 crores expected by 2027.
