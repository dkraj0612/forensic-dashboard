## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics Limited's Q1 FY2019 earnings call highlights a robust operational performance, driven primarily by significant growth in the Gas Logistics division. The company reported a 19% increase in total revenues to Rs. 1017 Crores and a substantial 38% rise in consolidated segment EBITDA to Rs. 91 Crores. While Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a 17% year-on-year increase to Rs. 51.46 Crores, the growth was moderated by a higher tax rate due to the implementation of new Indian Accounting Standards. The Liquid Terminal division showed steady performance, with incremental revenues and profits expected from Q3 FY2019 onwards due to the commissioning of expansion projects in Kandla, Mangalore, and Haldia. The company reiterated its commitment to long-term growth through strategic infrastructure development and new project pipeline.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Strategy:** Continued focus on the "necklace of terminals" strategy, enhancing India's oil, gas, and chemical logistics infrastructure.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:**
    *   **Liquid Terminals:** Kandla 100,000 KL project operational, expected to contribute from Q3 FY2019. Mangalore 25,000 KL and Haldia-4 35,000 KL projects expected to complete in Q2 FY2019, delivering revenues from Q3 FY2019.
    *   **Gas Terminals:** Strong performance from existing LPG terminals, particularly Haldia, exceeding budget significantly. Focus on future LPG terminal development on the West Coast.
*   **Market Drivers:**
    *   **LPG Demand:** Continued growth in LPG consumption, driven by government schemes like PMUY. Strong demand in the Eastern region, particularly impacting Haldia volumes.
    *   **Import Dependence:** Aegis's infrastructure supports India's growing reliance on imported LPG.
*   **Service Offerings:** Primarily fee-based revenue model within Gas Logistics, with a focus on throughput volumes. Diversified offerings including Auto Gas and Packed LPG distribution.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Emphasis on high utilization rates for operational terminals. Pipavav liquid terminal utilization remains low (20-25%) due to its remote location and higher inland transport costs. Focus shifting to LPG growth in Pipavav with plans for a railway gantry.
