## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) held its Q1 FY'26 earnings call on August 8, 2025, highlighting strong operational performance and continued strategic expansion. The company announced the successful listing of its subsidiary, Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited (AVTL), in June 2025, which remains consolidated under Aegis Logistics. Management reported an upgrade in their ESG rating from MSCI to AA. Significant progress was noted across various port locations, including capacity expansions, new terminal developments for liquids and LPG, and strategic alliances. The distribution segment is also a key focus, leveraging existing infrastructure to reach end customers with improved earnings potential per ton. Management expressed confidence in sustaining performance and exceeding previous long-term CAGR guidance.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Normalized EBITDA (Q1 FY'26):** INR 256 Cr (2% YoY increase)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & New Terminals:**
    *   **Mumbai Port:** 50% of 125,000 KL upcoming liquid capacity expected operational in Q2 FY'26, balance by end of FY'26. INR 250 Cr project.
    *   **JNPT Port:** Additional 30 acres allotted for INR 1,675 Cr project encompassing liquids, LPG, and an LPG bottling plant.
    *   **Kandla Port:** TRL4 liquid terminal (94,148 cubic meters) expected operational in FY'27.
    *   **Pipavav Port:** 48,000 MT cryogenic LPG capacity added (total 70,800 MT). Rail gantry for liquid product evacuation planned. Ammonia terminal (36,000 MT) expected completion before Q1 FY'27 with 15-year take-or-pay contract for Hindustan Zinc.
    *   **Mangalore Port:** 82,000 MT cryogenic LPG storage terminal operational. Further liquid capacity planned on allotted land.
    *   **Haldia Port:** Liquid capacity operating at higher utilization; LPG terminal utilization increasing.
    *   **Seventh Port:** Seeking land for additional liquid capacity development.
    *   **Vadhavan Port:** Long-term opportunity, development over approximately 5 years.
*   **Green Energy & Sustainability:**
    *   **Ammonia Terminal:** Development underway at Pipavav. Non-binding MOU with L&T for an ammonia terminal at Kandla for their green ammonia facility.
*   **Infrastructure Development:**
    *   **KGPL & JLPL Pipelines:** Expected operationalization in Q2 FY'26, connecting to Kandla Port.
*   **Financial Growth & Capital Allocation:**
    *   **AVTL Listing:** Successful listing in June 2025. Aegis Logistics retains 44.71% equity. AVTL financials remain consolidated.
    *   **Capital Expenditure:** Aegis Vopak Terminals (AVTL) to reach US$ 1.2 billion CAPEX by end of FY'26 and US$ 5 billion by 2030, funded by accruals and debt (gearing capped at 3.5x EBITDA). Phase-II equity infusion legally required within 3 years.
    *   **Dividend:** Interim dividend of INR 2.00 per share for FY'26 declared.
*   **Distribution Business:**
    *   Focus on leveraging existing terminal facilities to reach end customers, with higher earnings per ton.
    *   Cross-selling fuel agreement signed with Jio BP.
    *   Expansion plans for domestic LPG distribution in Tier 1, 2, & 3 cities.
    *   Serving industrial clusters from four LPG terminals.
