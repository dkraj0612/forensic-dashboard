## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) has demonstrated a year of record performance, marked by significant capacity expansion and strategic partnerships. The company achieved its highest-ever normalized EBITDA in Q4 FY22, driven by a strong recovery in sourcing volumes and increased logistics volumes in the Gas division. The Liquid division also performed robustly, benefiting from new capacities at Kandla, Mangalore, and Haldia. Key strategic milestones include the successful completion of the Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd (AVTL) joint venture and the acquisition of Friends Group terminals at Kandla Port, alongside 100% acquisition of CRL Terminals Ltd. Management is optimistic about future growth, with a robust capex plan underway for the AVTL JV and expansion in its retail LPG business.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Normalized EBITDA (FY21-22):** Rs. 586 crore (10% YoY increase), with Q4 FY21-22 achieving a record Rs. 164 crore.
*   **Normalized EBITDA (Liquid Division, FY21-22):** Rs. 194 crore (13% YoY increase).
*   **Normalized EBITDA (Gas Division, FY21-22):** Rs. 390 crore (8% YoY increase).
*   **Revenue (Gas Division Distribution, Q4 FY22):** Rs. 106 crore (13% YoY increase).
*   **Acquired Friends Group Terminals Capacity:** ~500,000 KL at Kandla Port.
*   **Acquired CRL Terminals Ltd Capacity:** 250,000 KL at Kandla.
*   **AVTL JV Capex Plan:** Rs. 1,250 crores for liquid and gas terminalling facilities at Pipavav, Haldia, Mangalore, and Kochi, adding 175,000 KL liquid and 100,000 MT gas storage.
*   **Profit After Tax (FY21-22):** Rs. 385 crore (11% increase YoY).
*   **Dividend Paid (FY21-22):** Rs. 2.50 per share (25% YoY increase).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Infrastructure Development:** Continued focus on increasing storage and handling capacities across key ports (Kandla, Mangalore, Haldia, Pipavav, Kochi) to meet growing demand. Projects include VLGC compliance for jetties and pipeline connectivity.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** The successful completion of the Aegis Vopak JV (AVTL) is a cornerstone, pooling resources for diversified growth in terminals and exploring new product verticals like natural gas and ammonia. The sourcing JV with Itochu aims for cost leadership in LPG imports.
*   **Retail Business Expansion:** Aggressive plans to scale up the LPG retailing network with more auto gas stations and distributors, targeting expansion into new states and product segments (e.g., Aegis puregas, Magna, Aegis Chota Cikander brands).
*   **Diversification:** Leveraging the AVTL JV to diversify into other types of gas and product storage, beyond LPG, and exploring new growth opportunities.
*   **Integrated Logistics Services:** Providing end-to-end solutions from sourcing to distribution, enhancing customer value.
*   **Leveraging Pipelines:** Strategic positioning to benefit from major LPG pipeline infrastructure like KGPL, enhancing terminal utilization and customer cost savings.
