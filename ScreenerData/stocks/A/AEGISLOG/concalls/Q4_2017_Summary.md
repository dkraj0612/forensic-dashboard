## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics Limited's Q4 2017 earnings call signals continued strategic expansion and vertical integration within its Gas Logistics segment, with a clear focus on capitalizing on India's growing LPG demand. Management reiterates its "fee-based revenue model" and the ongoing execution of its strategy to build a "necklace of terminals around the coastline of India." The transcript highlights the critical role of the ITOCHU joint venture (AGI) for LPG sourcing, aiming for cost leadership in the import market. Significant infrastructure expansion projects are underway and planned, including capacity enhancements at Haldia and Mumbai, and liquid terminal expansions at Kandla, Mangalore, and Haldia Port. The Auto Gas business, along with Bulk Industrial Distribution and Packed Cylinder distribution, remains a key component of their diversified revenue streams. The company is actively pursuing its mission to build an "unrivalled national port infrastructure and distribution network."

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Vertical Integration & LPG Sourcing:** The JV with ITOCHU Petroleum Co. (Singapore) Pte Ltd. for Aegis Group International Pte. Ltd. (AGI) is emphasized, with Aegis having sold 40% equity for $5.85 million. The objective is to become a "leading LPG sourcing player in India" and achieve "Cost Leadership" by lowering delivered prices.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:**
    *   **Haldia LPG Terminal:** Greenfield capacity expansion with 2 x 12,500 MT tanks and a 24 carousel bottling plant (Rs. 250 Cr for terminal, Rs. 25 Cr for bottling plant). Total static capacity targets 2,500,000 MT at full utilization. A 20-year MOU has been signed with a large PSU as an anchor customer.
    *   **Mumbai LPG Terminals:** Debottlenecking to add 400,000 MT incremental capacity, bringing total static capacity to 1,100,000 MT (Rs. 15 Cr). Internal pipeline connectivity (Uran-Chakan/Shikrapur grid) is completed.
    *   **Pipavav LPG Terminal:** Brownfield capacity expansion with static capacity targeting ~800,000 MT at full utilization. Throughput volumes are expected to grow through existing and new customer relationships.
    *   **Liquid Terminal Expansions:**
        *   Kandla: Greenfield Liquid Terminal Expansion (Rs. 75 Cr).
        *   Mangalore Port: Greenfield Liquid Terminal Expansion (25,000 KL, Rs. 18 Cr).
        *   Haldia Port: Brownfield Liquid Terminal Expansion (35,000 KL, Rs. 35 Cr).
    *   **Total Liquid Terminal Capacity:** Post-expansion at Kandla & Haldia is expected to be ~689,000 KL.
*   **LPG Demand-Supply Gap:** The company aims to capitalize on the LPG demand-supply gap in India, where incremental demand is met through imports, necessitating increased terminal capacity.
*   **Fee-Based Revenue Model:** Primarily a "fee-based revenue model" for Gas Logistics, with revenue streams from "Handling and Other Service Charges" (O&M fees).
*   **Diversified Distribution:**
    *   **Auto Gas Retailing:** Operates a network of 107 Auto Gas Stations across 7 states.
    *   **Bulk Industrial Distribution:** Supplies LPG via road tankers to various industries.
    *   **Packed Cylinders:** Distributes packed cylinders for commercial and industrial users through 102 Commercial Distributors across 45 Cities in 8 states.
*   **Mission:** To build an "unrivalled national port infrastructure and distribution network" in the Oil and Gas sector.
*   **Connectivity:** Emphasis on well-developed infrastructure, including deep-water ports for VLGCs and rail connectivity at Pipavav (and planned at other ports except Mumbai).
