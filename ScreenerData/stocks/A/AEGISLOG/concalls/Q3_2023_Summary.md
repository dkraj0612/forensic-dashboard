## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) demonstrated a strong performance in Q3 FY23, building on its robust Q3 FY23 results. The company highlighted continued growth in its liquid and gas terminal divisions, with significant contributions from capacity expansions and increased throughput volumes. The distribution business also saw substantial growth, driven by the increasing adoption of propane as a clean industrial fuel. Management reaffirmed confidence in the company's strategic direction, emphasizing ongoing capex plans for new facilities and exploration into green energy opportunities. The transcript suggests a positive trajectory for the remainder of FY23 and beyond, with key infrastructure projects progressing and new business opportunities being actively pursued.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Half Yearly Normalized EBITDA:** Highest ever, with a 33% YoY increase.
*   **Q2 FY23 Revenue:** 33% YoY increase (Note: This is likely a typo and should refer to Q3 FY23 based on the transcript context. The previous summary for Q3 FY23 showed revenue of INR 2,087 crores).
*   **Liquid Division Revenue:** Continues to perform strongly with new capacities at Kandla, Mangalore, Haldia, and JV capacities (Friends+CRL).
*   **Gas Division Growth:** Driven by distribution volumes and increasing logistics volumes.
*   **LPG Bottling Plants Commissioned:** 5 plants at Udupi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Wada, and Jamnagar.
*   **New Distributors:** 12 added, with presence in 14 states.
*   **New Gas Stations:** 10 added in FY23, with more on the way.
*   **LPG Retailing EBITDA Margins:** Increased to an average of Rs. 10,000 per MT.
*   **Dividend:** Board approved 3rd Interim Dividend of 200% (Rs. 2/- per equity share).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Capex:**
    *   Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd (AVTL) JV plans to add 175,000 kiloliters of liquid storage and 100,000 MT of gas storage across Pipavav, Haldia, Mangalore, and Kochi.
    *   Envisaged capital expenditure for these projects is approximately Rs. 1,250 crores.
    *   Environmental permits, license applications, and engineering drawings are underway.
    *   Aegis secured land parcels in Haldia (H5 - 2.5 acres) and Mangalore (21 acres).
    *   AVTL achieved financial closure and secured international sourcing tenders for LPG (800,000 metric tons).
*   **Infrastructure Development & Connectivity:**
    *   Pipavav Port's LPG jetty is now VLGC compliant, awaiting commissioning permits.
    *   Preliminary work has started to connect Pipavav to the KGPL pipeline.
    *   Haldia Terminal commissioned an additional Jetty LPG Pipeline, increasing unloading rates.
    *   HPCL received PNGRB permission for a pipeline from Aegis Haldia Terminal to its Panagarh Bottling plant.
    *   The Paradip to Durgapur cross-country pipeline is operational and will support evacuation for HPCL.
    *   Mumbai Terminal continues to operate at full capacity with major oil companies.
    *   Uran-Chakan pipeline is being extensively used by HPCL.
    *   Kandla Terminal commenced LPG handling and is supplying LPG to the Morbi industrial hub, generating healthy distribution margins.
    *   KGPL pipeline (Phase 1 expected mid-FY24) is world's longest LPG pipeline (2800 km, 8.25 MMT capacity), with preliminary work started for pump house and manifold at Pipavav to connect to Aegis terminal.
    *   Kandla Oil Jetty #7 VLGC compliance work is ongoing.
    *   Barge loading of LPG for Inland Waterways trial run (RORO) completed at Haldia.
*   **Distribution & Retail Business Growth:**
    *   Strong growth in distribution volumes is expected, especially with LPG/propane as a clean industrial fuel.
    *   Launched 425 KG Magna Cylinder in the East zone and entered East for fuel retailing.
    *   Expansion plans for the retail LPG business include reaching 200 stations across 20 states and expanding into a national distribution network for hotels, restaurants, and industry under "Aegis puregas" and "Magna" brands.
    *   Plans to operate up to 37 LPG bottling plants nationally.
    *   Expansion in Tier 1, 2, & 3 cities with distributors and POS under "Aegis Chota Cikander" brand.
*   **Sourcing Business:**
    *   LPG Sourcing JV with ITOCHU Corporation aims for cost leadership in the LPG import market.
*   **Liquid Division:**
    *   Handles diversified products including chemicals, POL, and vegetable oils.
    *   Offers import, export, storage, and logistics services.
    *   Possesses MS, SS, Epoxy Coated, and IFR tanks with pipeline connectivity to major clients.
*   **Green Energy:** (No new specific updates in this transcript, but historical context remains relevant).
