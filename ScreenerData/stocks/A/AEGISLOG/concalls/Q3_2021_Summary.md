## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **"Necklace of Terminals" Strategy:** The core strategy remains to build a comprehensive network of port-based oil and gas storage terminals around India's coastline. All ports are intended to be deep-water ports accommodating VLGCs.
*   **LPG Growth & Infrastructure:**
    *   Focus on increasing LPG throughput volumes through new customer relationships and infrastructure development (e.g., Pipavav, Kandla).
    *   Planned incremental volumes of 0.5 Mn MT/year from Pipavav projects and 1 Mn MT/year from the new Kandla terminal.
    *   Strategic vision to increase LPG capacity by 2020-2025 through expansions and new terminals.
    *   Developing multi-modal connectivity (pipelines, rail) at all ports except Mumbai to enhance customer reach.
*   **Liquid Terminals:** Continued expansion of liquid tank capacity at existing terminals and development of new greenfield liquid terminals.
*   **Retail Business Expansion:**
    *   **Autogas Stations:** Plan to grow from 118 stations to 200 stations over 20 states.
    *   **Commercial LPG:** Expansion into a national distribution network for hotels, restaurants, and industries under the "Aegis Puregas" brand.
    *   **Domestic LPG:** Expansion in Tier 1, 2, & 3 cities with distributors and POS under the "Aegis Chota Cikander" brand.
    *   **LPG Bottling Plants:** Aiming for up to 37 LPG bottling plants (owned and third-party contracts).
*   **Market Drivers:**
    *   **LPG Demand-Supply Gap:** India faces a significant demand-supply gap for LPG, met through increasing imports.
    *   **Increasing LPG Imports:** This necessitates significant terminal capacity for handling imported LPG.
    *   **Clean Fuel Transition:** LPG is positioned as a clean alternative fuel, driving demand.
