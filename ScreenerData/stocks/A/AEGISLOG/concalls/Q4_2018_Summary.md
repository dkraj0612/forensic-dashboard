## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics Limited's latest earnings transcript (Q4 2018, though the transcript itself seems to refer to FY18 performance) showcases a company steadfastly executing its "necklace of terminals" strategy, aiming to become a dominant player in India's burgeoning oil, gas, and chemical logistics sector. The narrative centers on significant infrastructure expansion, particularly in LPG terminals, to capitalize on India's growing demand and import dependence. The company highlights its diversified business verticals, including 3PL services, and a broad customer base. While detailed financial figures for Q4 are not explicitly presented in a standalone format, the provided "Financial Performance" section offers a comparative view of 9MFY17 vs. 9MFY18, indicating growth in EBITDA and Profit After Tax. The transcript also reiterates the company's mission to build an unrivalled national network and emphasizes its fee-based revenue model within Gas Logistics.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Vision & Mission:**
    *   Purpose: To be the leading provider of logistics and supply chain services to India’s oil, gas and chemical industry.
    *   Strategy: Build an unrivalled national network of port-based tank terminals, pipelines and multi-modal transportation facilities – "a necklace of terminals."
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:**
    *   **Terminals:** Focus on port-based tank terminals with multiple tank sizes (Coated, Stainless Steel, Heated), including a new "2nd Mumbai Liquids" terminal. Mention of Refrigerated Gas Terminals in Mumbai and Haldia, and a Pressurized Gas Terminal in Pipavav.
    *   **Pipelines:** Propane pipeline to RIL ethylene cracker.
    *   **Connectivity:** Road, Rail, and Coastal Shipping connectivity are key.
*   **Service Offerings:**
    *   **3PL Services:** For over fifty large oil companies and chemical industries.
    *   **Contract Types:** Long Term, Spot, and Throughput Contracts.
    *   **Services:** Unloading, transfer through jetty pipelines, storage, distribution, customs bonding, inventory management, just-in-time delivery, and on-site product quality testing.
*   **Diversified Network:**
    *   **Auto Gas:** Network of 108 Autogas stations in 7 states.
    *   **Packed LPG Distributors:** Network of packed LPG distributors.
*   **Market Opportunity:**
    *   **LPG Consumption Growth:** India's LPG consumption is growing, with significant import growth indicated.
    *   **Demand/Supply Balance:** Visuals suggest a demand-supply gap being met by imports.
    *   **Government Schemes:** The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is identified as a driver for LPG consumption growth, targeting 80 million households by 2020. Eastern region noted for high growth.
*   **Acquisition:** Mention of "Acquisition of Shell India LPG business."
