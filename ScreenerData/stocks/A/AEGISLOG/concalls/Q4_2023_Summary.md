## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) reported a record-breaking fiscal year 2023 (FY23), driven by strong performance across both its liquid and gas divisions. The company achieved its highest ever consolidated revenues of INR 8,627 crores, a 86% YoY increase, and a record EBITDA of INR 804 crores, up 37% YoY. This robust performance was attributed to strategic initiatives including a successful joint venture with Vopak, acquisition of significant liquid capacity, the ramp-up of the new Kandla LPG terminal, and substantial growth in the distribution business. Management expressed confidence in sustaining this positive momentum into FY24 and beyond, supported by ongoing large-scale capital expenditure projects. A new vision and mission statement emphasizing support for India's transition to cleaner fuels was also introduced.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenue (FY23):** INR 8,627 crores (86% YoY increase)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (FY23):** INR 804 crores (37% YoY increase, highest ever)
*   **Consolidated Profit After Tax (FY23):** INR 511 crores (33% YoY increase)
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS) (FY23):** INR 13.19 (29% YoY increase)
*   **Liquid Division Revenue (FY23):** INR 417.9 crores (55% YoY increase)
*   **Liquid Division EBITDA (FY23):** INR 271.5 crores (39% YoY increase)
*   **Gas Division Revenue (FY23):** INR 8,209.2 crores (88% YoY increase)
*   **Gas Division EBITDA (FY23):** INR 532.2 crores (36% YoY increase)
*   **Throughput Volumes (LPG) (FY23):** 33.3 lakh metric tons (17% YoY increase)
*   **Commercial & Industrial Bulk Volumes (FY23):** 4.74 lakh metric tons (243% YoY increase)
*   **Sourcing Volumes (FY23):** 8.95 lakh metric tons (61% YoY increase)
*   **Final Dividend Recommended (FY23):** INR 1.25 per share (bringing full-year dividend to INR 5.75 per share, or 575% of face value).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Capex:**
    *   **Liquid Division:** 50,000 kiloliters (kl) Haldia expansion commissioned. Additional 550,000 kl acquired from Friends Group available for full FY24. Kochi (50,000 kl) and Mangalore (70,000 kl) expansions expected by end of FY24. Commenced building 110,000 kl liquid tanks at JNPT, expected mid-2024. Total 230,000 kl under construction for FY25 availability.
    *   **Gas Division:** Pipavav port's LPG jetty now VLGC compliant (awaiting permit). Progress on connecting Pipavav and Kandla to Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG pipeline and Jamnagar pipeline for Kandla. Additional 3,000 metric tons (MT) spheres at Pipavav expected Q2. New cryogenic LPG terminal at Pipavav (48,000 MT) underway. Construction of India's largest cryogenic LPG terminal at Mangalore (80,000 MT) underway. Total 131,000 MT static LPG terminal capacity under construction.
    *   **JV Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd (AVTL):** Achieved financial closure. Joint business plan for INR 4,500 crores capex over 5 years (2023-2027). Specific projects like Pipavav and Mangalore cryo terminals, and liquid expansions are part of this. Approximately 40% of this capex (INR 1,500-1,700 crores) is currently underway.
*   **Distribution & Retail Business Growth:** Continued growth expected in distribution business (commercial, industrial, Autogas). Kandla terminal stabilization expected to drive future distribution growth. Expansion of fuel stations and cylinder filling plants continues.
*   **Sourcing Business:** Sourcing JV with ITOCHU Corporation continues to be integral to the overall strategy of integrated LPG supply, providing market insights and competitive tendering capabilities.
*   **Green Energy Focus:** Active exploration of green initiatives, specifically ammonia terminals, with a commitment to provide concrete updates within 6 months.
