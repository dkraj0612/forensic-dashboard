## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) has reported a strong Q4 FY'25, marking a new milestone with normalized EBITDA crossing INR 1100 Cr for the full fiscal year. The company achieved its highest-ever PAT, logistics volumes, and liquids revenue. Q4 FY'25 saw the full commissioning of the JNPT liquid storage terminal, contributing to record revenues and EBITDA for the Gas Division. Key strategic achievements include the successful listing of Aegis Vopak Terminals Limited (AVTL) and the commencement of development for a 36k MT ammonia storage terminal at Pipavav. Management expressed confidence in sustaining performance and exceeding previous long-term CAGR guidance.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & New Terminals:**
    *   **JNPT Port:** Full commissioning of 101,900 KL Liquids Terminal. Additional land allotted.
    *   **Pipavav Port:** Started development of a 36k MT ammonia storage terminal. LPG pipelines connectivity secured and progressing well.
    *   **Kandla Port:** Expansion completed. LPG pipelines connectivity secured and progressing well. Additional land allotted.
