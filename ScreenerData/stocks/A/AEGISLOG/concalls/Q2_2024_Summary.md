## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) delivered a strong Q2 FY'24, marked by a significant milestone: throughput volumes of 1 million metric tons of LPG in a single quarter, a testament to its expanding infrastructure and market penetration. This robust operational performance translated into a 48% year-over-year increase in Profit After Tax (PAT) to INR 150 crores and a 26% rise in EBITDA to INR 231 crores. The company's growth projects, including expansions at Pipavav and the commissioning of the LPG bottling plant at Kandla, are progressing well and are expected to further bolster performance. Management expressed confidence in sustaining this positive momentum, driven by ongoing investments and a strategic focus on cleaner energy solutions.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY'24):** INR 231 crores (26% YoY increase)
*   **Consolidated Profit After Tax (Q2 FY'24):** INR 150 crores (48% YoY increase)
*   **Consolidated Earnings Per Share (Q2 FY'24):** INR 3.62 (36% YoY increase)
*   **Liquid Division Revenue (Q2 FY'24):** INR 117 crores (10% YoY increase)
*   **Liquid Division EBITDA (Q2 FY'24):** INR 79.5 crores (15% YoY increase)
*   **Gas Division EBITDA (Q2 FY'24):** INR 151.2 crores (32% YoY increase)
*   **LPG Logistics Throughput Volumes (Q2 FY'24):** 1.02 million metric tons (23% YoY increase)
*   **Distribution Volumes (Q2 FY'24):** 131,000 metric tons (13% YoY increase)
*   **Sourcing Volumes (Q2 FY'24):** 174,000 metric tons (decreased YoY, low impact on profits)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Capex:**
    *   **Pipavav:** Two additional LPG spheres (3,700 MT static capacity) commissioned in Q2 FY'24, ready for Q3 business. The 45,000 MT cryogenic LPG project is on budget and on time.
    *   **Kandla:** LPG bottling plant completed this quarter. New liquid terminal at Kandla is operational and delivering as expected.
    *   **JNPT:** 110,000 kl liquid terminal progressing well, expected operational by end of FY'24.
    *   **Mangalore & Kochi:** New liquid capacity expansions are underway and expected operational by end of FY'24. The 85,000 MT cryogenic LPG project at Mangalore is on budget and on time.
    *   **Investment Program:** Progressing on the INR 1,750 crores investment program announced in June 2023.
*   **Green Energy Focus:** Continuing to seek opportunities in sustainable fuels and new energy. Ammonia terminal plans are still under development.
*   **Distribution & Retail Business Growth:** Stable growth in distribution volumes (13% YoY), with improved margins and a focus on industrial applications as an alternative fuel to natural gas. Kandla LPG terminal stabilization is expected to drive further steady growth.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** Pipavav port upgrading jetty to handle VLGCs, concurrent development of KGPL pipeline, VLGC jetty, and Aegis's cryogenic facility, supported by a high-performing LPG rail gantry.
