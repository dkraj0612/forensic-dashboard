## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) reported a strong Q1 FY24, demonstrating robust growth in EBITDA (up 30% YoY) and Profit After Tax (up 23% YoY), achieving its highest-ever Q1 profit before tax and profit after tax. The company highlighted its commitment to safety and sustainability with no environmental or safety incidents reported. Growth momentum from the previous year continued across both liquid and gas divisions, reinforcing customer trust and providing confidence in delivering a consistent annual performance. Key growth projects announced previously are progressing well, on budget, and on time. The Board declared an interim dividend of INR 2.50 per share, underscoring financial stability and commitment to shareholder returns.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q1 FY24 Normalized EBITDA:** Increased by 30% YoY.
*   **Q1 FY24 Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 133 crores, a 23% YoY increase, marking a lifetime high for Q1.
*   **Q1 FY24 Earnings Per Share (EPS):** INR 3.30 (compared to INR 2.95 in FY22 - *note: Q1 FY23 EPS not explicitly stated for comparison, but YoY growth implies positive movement from prior period*).
*   **Liquid Division Q1 FY24 Revenue:** INR 115 crores (42% YoY increase).
*   **Liquid Division Q1 FY24 EBITDA:** INR 78.5 crores (43% YoY increase), a quarterly record.
*   **Gas Division Q1 FY24 Revenue:** INR 1,986 crores (8% YoY decrease, attributed to lower sourcing volumes and LPG prices).
*   **Gas Division Q1 FY24 EBITDA:** INR 133.5 crores (23% YoY increase).
*   **Throughput Volumes (LPG) Q1 FY24:** 8.81 lakh metric tons (vs. 6.37 lakh metric tons in Q1 FY23, a significant increase).
*   **Distribution Volumes (LPG) Q1 FY24:** 1.59 lakh metric tons (86% YoY increase, a lifetime record).
*   **Sourcing Volumes Q1 FY24:** 2.26 lakh metric tons (marginal decrease from 2.3 lakh metric tons in Q1 FY23).
*   **Interim Dividend Declared:** INR 2.50 per share.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Projects at JNPT (liquid terminal, expected FY24), Mangalore & Kochi (liquid capacity, expected FY24), and Pipavav & Mangalore (LPG capacity, expected FY25) are progressing. Construction of an LPG bottling plant at Kandla has commenced (expected FY24).
*   **Distribution Business Growth:** Focus on bulk LPG/propane as an industrial fuel, alongside traditional cooking gas and auto LPG, has driven significant volume increases. This is seen as a competitive and sustainable alternative to traditional fuels.
*   **Terminal Operations:** Pipavav jetty is now VLGC compliant, awaiting its first berthing. Haldia operations are back to normal. Mumbai terminal continues to operate at full capacity. Kandla terminal is operational and meeting expectations.
*   **Retail Expansion:** Continued focus on expanding the LPG retailing network.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** The company continues to leverage its partnership with Vopak for exploring new opportunities, although the transcript clarifies that Aegis's overall growth projection (25% EPS CAGR) accounts for its entire business, not solely the JV.
*   **High-Margin Segments:** The distribution business is highlighted as a higher-margin, less capex-intensive segment that significantly improves ROCE.
