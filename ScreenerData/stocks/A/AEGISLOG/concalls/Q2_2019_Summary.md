## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics Limited reported a mixed Q2 FY2019, with strong growth in the Gas Terminal division significantly offsetting a temporary decline in the Liquid Terminal division. Consolidated revenues grew 15% year-on-year to Rs. 1425.9 Crores, with consolidated segment EBITDA rising 31% to Rs. 100.57 Crores. However, Profit After Tax (PAT) growth was moderated to 3% year-on-year, reaching Rs. 57.5 Crores, partly due to uncommissioned projects in the Liquid Terminal division incurring costs without revenue.

The Gas Terminal division delivered stellar performance, with EBITDA surging 58% on the back of 50% higher LPG throughput volumes, primarily driven by record volumes at the Haldia terminal. The retail LPG business (packed cylinders, auto gas) also achieved record sales volumes, marking initial contributions from the domestic LPG segment. Conversely, the Liquid Terminal division's EBITDA dropped 21%, attributed to higher lease costs for the Kandla expansion project before its Q3 commissioning, alongside some one-off charges in Mumbai.

Management expressed confidence that the Liquid Terminal division's profitability will "bounce back" in Q3 and Q4, as the Kandla (100,000 KL) and Haldia-4 (35,000 KL) projects are now fully operational. Efforts continue to close transformational LPG terminal expansion deals on the West Coast, with a target timeline of 2-3 quarters. The sourcing business secured a record 1.5 million tonnes tender from IOC for 2019, though at potentially lower margins. Aegis reiterates its long-term focus on the "necklace of terminals" strategy, with the Gas division expected to be the primary growth driver for years to come.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Strategy:** Continued commitment to the "necklace of terminals" strategy, focusing on expanding India's oil, gas, and chemical logistics infrastructure.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:**
    *   **Liquid Terminals:** Kandla 100,000 KL and Haldia-4 35,000 KL projects are now fully operational in Q3, having incurred costs in Q2 without revenue. Mangalore 25,000 KL project is mechanically complete, awaiting final operating permits. Future plans for Mangalore include an additional 60,000-70,000 KL capacity.
    *   **Gas Terminals:** Haldia LPG terminal is a star performer, achieving record volumes and driving significant growth in the division. Intensive work continues on multiple transformational LPG terminal deals and projects on the West Coast.
*   **Market Drivers:**
    *   **LPG Demand:** Continued strong demand for LPG, especially in the Northeast region, benefiting the Haldia terminal. Government initiatives like PMUY continue to support overall LPG consumption.
    *   **Retail LPG:** Growth in the packed cylinder business, with successful entry into the domestic LPG sector (Chhota SIKANDER brand) in urban Western India cities. Expansion planned for the Northeast.
*   **Service Offerings:**
    *   **Throughput Model:** Strong fee-based revenue from LPG logistics, primarily driven by Haldia.
    *   **Distribution:** Expanding Puregas commercial LPG (hotels, restaurants, small industry) with 112 distributors. Growing Auto Gas with plans for new "unified sites" (Auto LPG + Essar petrol/diesel).
    *   **Sourcing:** AGI (JV with Itochu) won a record 1.5 million tonnes tender from IOC for 2019, making it one of IOC's largest suppliers. Awaiting HPCL and BPCL tenders for 2019.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Challenges:**
    *   **Haldia LPG:** High utilization and profitability, expected to become the most profitable Aegis LPG terminal due to lower port costs.
    *   **Pipavav Liquid:** Remains low (25-30% utilization) due to lack of railway connectivity for liquids.
    *   **Pipavav LPG:** Volumes static, but efforts are focused on securing rail connectivity for LPG to significantly increase throughput (potentially hundreds of thousands of tonnes). Consideration for future storage expansion post rail connectivity.
    *   **Mumbai LPG:** Volumes stable, awaiting HPCL's completion of the Uran-Chakan pipeline (last 25km remaining, estimated 6-9 months delay) to unlock significant growth.
