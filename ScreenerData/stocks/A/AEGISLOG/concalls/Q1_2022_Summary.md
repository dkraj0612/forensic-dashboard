## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) reported a strong Q1 FY23, building on the previous fiscal year's performance with significant year-on-year growth in revenue and EBITDA, reaching lifetime highs. Key drivers include the successful stabilization of the Kandla LPG terminal and continued growth in the distribution business, particularly in the bulk industrial segment. The company reiterated its vision to be India's leading logistics provider for the oil, gas, and chemical industries, emphasizing reliability and service. The Aegis-Vopak joint venture is performing as expected and actively exploring new opportunities. Management declared an interim dividend of Rs. 1.50 per share, reflecting confidence in future performance. The company anticipates robust profit growth in the fiscal year, supported by expansion projects and favorable market conditions, especially for LPG.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** Rs. 678 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 399 crores YoY), primarily due to higher sourcing volumes.
*   **Normalized EBITDA:** Rs. 164 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 114 crores YoY), a 43% increase, marking a lifetime high.
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs. 131 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 90 crores YoY), a 46% increase.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 107 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 72 crores YoY), a 49% increase.
*   **Liquid Terminal Division:**
    *   Revenue: Rs. 81 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 66 crores YoY), a 23% increase, a lifetime high.
    *   EBITDA: Rs. 55 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 49 crores YoY), a 12% increase.
*   **Gas Terminal Division:**
    *   Revenue: Rs. 2,155 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 612 crores YoY).
    *   EBITDA: Rs. 109 crores in Q1 FY23 (vs. Rs. 65 crores YoY), a 68% increase.
*   **LPG Throughput Volumes (Mumbai, Haldia, Pipavav):** 6.37 lakh MT in Q1 FY23 (vs. 5.67 lakh MT YoY), a 13% increase, despite a 10% dip in Haldia volumes due to jetty upgrades.
*   **Bulk Industrial Distribution Volume:** 85,300 MT in Q1 FY23 (vs. 30,900 MT YoY), a 176% increase, a lifetime high.
*   **Commercial & Domestic Cylinder Distribution:** 5,973 MT in Q1 FY23 (vs. 5,039 MT YoY), a 19% increase.
*   **Autogas Sales:** 5,148 MT in Q1 FY23 (vs. 3,567 MT YoY), a 44% increase.
*   **Sourcing Business Volume:** 2,29,992 MT in Q1 FY23 (vs. 1,00,052 MT YoY).
*   **Interim Dividend:** Rs. 1.50 per share.
*   **Cash Position:** Rs. 1,500 crores.
*   **Debt Position:** Rs. 900 crores (consolidated).
*   **Vopak JV Cash Inflow:** Rs. 2,050 crores received by Aegis.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **"Necklace of Terminals" Strategy:** Continues to be central, with focus on market leadership in key ports for liquids and LPG.
*   **LPG & Gas Logistics:** Emphasis on increasing throughput and distribution volumes, with VLGC compliance projects at Pipavav and Kandla progressing to enhance competitiveness and gateway functions for North and Central India.
*   **Distribution Business Growth:** Driven by stabilization and utilization of the Kandla LPG terminal as a distribution hub, and strong performance in bulk industrial, commercial, domestic, and autogas segments.
*   **Aegis-Vopak Joint Venture:** Successfully closed and performing in line with expectations, actively evaluating new business opportunities.
*   **Market Drivers:** Continued strong demand for LPG due to price sensitivity and India's energy landscape, with management confident in the sustained high prices and supply crunch of natural gas.
*   **Acquisitions:** Integration of CRL terminals and French group terminals to bolster liquid business, with efforts underway to improve their profitability.
