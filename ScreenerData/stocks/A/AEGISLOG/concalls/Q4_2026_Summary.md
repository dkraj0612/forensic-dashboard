## Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) reported an exceptional FY'26, marking a "breakout year" with record financial performance. Consolidated revenue surged 23% year-on-year to INR 8,333 Cr, while normalized EBITDA grew a substantial 36% to INR 1,599 Cr. Profit after tax (PAT) saw a significant 41% increase, crossing the INR 1,000 Cr milestone for the first time at INR 1,107 Cr. The fourth quarter of FY'26 was particularly strong, with revenue up 52%, EBITDA up 54%, and PAT up 43% year-on-year. Cash and investments on the balance sheet reached approximately INR 6,000 Cr, reflecting strong financial discipline and growth funding capacity. The company's diversified operations and disciplined execution were highlighted as key drivers. Management recommended a final dividend of INR 6.70 per share, bringing the total for FY'26 to INR 8.7 per share. The company has a clear roadmap for continued growth, funded by a balanced mix of equity, internal accruals, and debt, targeting a conservative gearing ratio.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Infrastructure Expansion & Development:**
    *   **Mumbai Port:** Additional 64,000 KL liquid storage under development (INR 125 Cr), targeted for commissioning in H1 FY'27.
    *   **JNPT Port:** Major expansion underway with ~318,100 CBM additional liquid storage, ~77,236 MT LPG capacity, and a 35,000 MT LPG bottling plant (INR 1,675 Cr). First phase of liquid capacity expected in H1 FY'27. Evaluating further cryogenic gas tank (36,000-50,000 MT).
    *   **Haldia Port:** Acquired 75% stake in Hindustan Aegis LPG Limited, adding ~25,000 MT LPG storage and marking entry into East Coast market. Acquired additional 3 acres for liquid expansion.
    *   **Kandla Port:** Handled first VLGC vessel in Dec '25, making it VLGC-compliant. Jamnagar-Loni pipeline complete. Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG pipeline expected H1 FY'27. CRL 4 liquid terminal (~94,148 CBM) targeted for next year commissioning. MOU with L&T for potential ammonia terminals.
    *   **Pipavav Port:** Commissioned 48,000 MT cryogenic LPG terminal in June '25. New VLGC-compliant jetty expected within CY'26; KGPL pipeline connection in Q2 FY'27. Building additional liquid rail gantry and secured 15-year take-or-pay for petroleum products (>0.5 million MT/year). Advancing India's first independent ammonia terminal (36,000 MT) with commissioning in H1 FY'27. Itochu Corporation acquired 10% stake in Aegis Terminal Pipavav Limited.
    *   **Kochi Port:** Operating 82,545 CBM liquid storage; evaluating addition of 60,000 CBM.
    *   **Mangalore Port:** Commissioned 82,000 MT cryogenic LPG terminal in June '25. Progressing on LPG rail gantry. 75,000 CBM liquid capacity operational, total liquid storage 193 CBM. Evaluating further 60,000 CBM liquid storage.
    *   **Vadhavan Port:** Signed non-binding MOU for potential INR 20,000 Cr investment, subject to approvals and land allocation.
*   **Green Energy & Sustainability:**
    *   Developing India's first independent ammonia terminal at Pipavav.
    *   MOU with L&T for potential ammonia terminal development at Kandla.
*   **Logistics & Distribution:**
    *   LPG distribution volumes surged 45% YoY, driven by operational expansion across multiple terminals and increased demand.
    *   Expected to commence ammonia distribution, with plans to include it within overall gas distribution volumes.
*   **Financial Strategy:**
    *   Aggregate gross capex of USD 1.2 billion by March '27, with a long-term pipeline of USD 5 billion through 2030.
    *   Disciplined funding through balanced mix of equity, internal accruals, and debt, targeting gearing ratio of ~0.6x.
    *   Strong cash and investments position (~INR 6,000 Cr) to fund growth.
