## Aegis Logistics - Q3 FY'26 Earnings Transcript Analysis

### Executive Overview

Aegis Logistics (AEGISLOG) reported a robust Q3 FY'26 performance, driven by strong volume growth and enhanced operating metrics across both its Liquid and Gas divisions. For the 9 months of FY'26 (ending December 31, 2025), the company achieved record revenue and EBITDA in the Liquids division and record EBITDA in the Gas division, fueled by the highest-ever volumes in its Logistics and Distribution business. Consolidated revenue grew 13% year-on-year to INR 5,739 Cr, and normalized EBITDA surged 26% to INR 929 Cr. Profit after tax (PAT) saw a significant 39% increase, reaching INR 652 Cr. The third quarter of FY'26 specifically showed a strong 29% rise in normalized EBITDA to INR 326 Cr and a 45% jump in PAT to INR 233 Cr. Management reiterated their confidence in continued growth, aided by operational leverage, cost optimization, and ongoing infrastructure development across key ports. The company has also effectively utilized IPO proceeds to pay down debt.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Infrastructure Expansion & Development:**
    *   **Mumbai Port:** Additional 64,000 KL liquid capacity under development (INR 125 Cr), targeted for Q1 FY'27 commissioning.
    *   **JNPT Port:** INR 1,675 Cr project progressing, including new liquid capacity (318,100 cubic meters), LPG capacity (77,286 metric tons), and an LPG bottling plant (35,000 metric tons). First phase of new liquid capacity expected in Q1 FY'27. Evaluating additional 36,000 MT cryogenic gas tank.
    *   **Kandla Port:** VLGC berth operational since Q3 FY'26, received first VLGC vessel on December 31, 2025. 94,148 cubic meter liquid terminal at CRL-4 plot expected operational in FY'27. Non-binding MOU with L&T for an ammonia terminal for their green ammonia project.
    *   **Kochi Port:** Additional 60,000 cubic meters of liquid capacity development underway.
    *   **Pipavav Port:** 15-year take-or-pay contract signed with a large conglomerate for handling petroleum products, managing over 0.5 million MT annually by end of calendar year. Construction of India's first independent ammonia terminal (36,000 MT capacity) progressing, expected completion before Q1 FY'27. New VLGC-compliant jetty under construction, expected within calendar year.
    *   **Mangalore Port:** 82,000 MT LPG terminal commissioned in June '25. Plans to add further 60,000 cubic meters of liquids capacity.
    *   **Haldia Port:** AVTL acquired 75% stake in Hindustan Aegis LPG Limited, adding 25,000 MT LPG storage capacity. Additional 3 acres acquired for liquid expansion.
    *   **Vadhavan Port:** Non-binding MOU for potential investment of INR 20,000 Cr, awaiting land development and approvals for gas and liquid complex.
*   **Green Energy & Sustainability:**
    *   Developing India's first independent ammonia terminal at Pipavav.
    *   MOU with L&T for an ammonia terminal at Kandla for green ammonia project.
    *   Evaluating opportunities in hydrogen and other new energies.
*   **Logistics & Distribution:**
    *   KGPL and JLPL pipelines expected to be operational by June 2026, significantly driving volumes at Kandla.
    *   Distribution volumes (LPG) are growing rapidly, driven by industrial demand and shift from alternative fuels.
    *   On the verge of starting ammonia distribution.
*   **Financial Strategy:**
    *   Aggregate gross capex outlay of USD 1.2 billion (INR 10,000 Cr) by FY'27, with a long-term plan of USD 5 billion by 2030.
    *   Funding through internal accruals and prudent debt, maintaining a conservative debt gearing ratio of 0.6x and overall leverage capped at 3.5x EBITDA.
    *   IPO proceeds used to pay down outstanding debt.
    *   Expected EBITDA from new assets upon maturity: ~25% of asset value.

While the initial target of March/April '26 was missed, the June 2026 timeline has been reconfirmed with reasonable confidence, with most of the pipeline complete and some gas fill having occurred.
