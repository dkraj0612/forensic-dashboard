## Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) reported a robust Q2 FY26, with consolidated revenues reaching ₹234 crore, a 15% year-over-year increase. This marks the highest quarterly revenue in the company's history and demonstrates sustained growth momentum. Profitability also saw a significant improvement, with PBT growing 32% YoY to ₹21 crore and PAT increasing 33% YoY to ₹15 crore. Growth was broad-based across business verticals and geographies, with Rest of World (ROW) operations growing 17% YoY driven by U.S. business traction and strong contributions from Europe and the Middle East. India operations continued their double-digit growth with a 12% YoY increase, fueled by enterprise and government segments, particularly smart city projects. The Services business grew 17% YoY, while the Solutions revenue increased by 8% YoY. Enterprise customer revenue outpaced government segment growth, indicating a revival in digital transformation plans. The company secured a substantial order intake of approximately ₹698 crore, significantly bolstering its order book. A key achievement was the successful completion and handover of the new Command and Control center, "Drishti," for the Pune Smart City project, valued at ₹510 crore. Management acknowledges continued pricing pressures but remains focused on operational efficiencies and strategic value-added solutions to improve margins.

## Key Financial Metrics

**For Q2 FY26 (Consolidated):**
*   **Revenue from Operations:** ₹234 crore, a 15% YoY increase.
*   **Quarterly Revenue:** Highest ever quarterly revenue achieved.
*   **Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) Revenue:** ~₹878 crore (up from ~₹850 crore in Q1 FY26 and ₹807 crore for FY25).
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** ₹21 crore, a 32% YoY increase.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** ₹15 crore, a 33% YoY increase.
*   **EBITDA:** ₹28 crore, a 23% YoY increase.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Stable despite persistent pricing pressure, supported by operational efficiencies.
*   **India Business Revenue:** 12% YoY growth.
*   **Services Business Revenue:** 17% YoY growth.
*   **Solutions Business Revenue:** 8% YoY growth.
*   **Non-Government Customer Revenue:** 18% YoY growth.

**Order Wins:**
*   **Total Order Intake (Q2 FY26):** ~₹698 crore (includes new wins and renewals).
*   **Pune Smart City Project (Drishti):** Initial order ₹510 crore (combined with follow-up order), Command and Control center completed and handed over in August 2025.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Sustained Revenue Growth:** Targeting ₹250 crore quarterly revenue run rate by Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27 to achieve the ₹1,000 crore annualized revenue milestone.
*   **International Expansion:** Strengthening U.S. direct sales with a new Chief Revenue Officer and targeting Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). Continued traction in Europe and the Middle East. Hiring approximately 120-130 people in Europe (Germany and Switzerland).
*   **Value-Added Solutions & Services:** Focusing on higher-margin offerings, including application support and AI-based technology management, through partners and direct enterprise clients.
*   **Smart City & Government Focus:** Continued execution on smart city projects, with future growth engines identified in ongoing services and Phase 2 projects. Noida RFP is out, and bidding is underway. Mumbai project renewal (₹2,100 crore) being strategically evaluated for partnership.
*   **AI and Automation Integration:** Leveraging AI for intelligent service management and operational efficiency.
*   **Margin Improvement Strategy:** Targeting 12-13% EBITDA margins within 3-4 quarters, driven by optimization in the second or third year of large deals, transition to Operations & Maintenance (O&M) phases of projects, and a strategic shift towards higher-margin solutions and services.
*   **Financial Resilience:** Maintaining a strong cash position and diversified banking relationships to support ongoing projects and new wins.
*   **Acquisition Strategy:** Actively exploring acquisitions in Cybersecurity and Cloud (as per previous context).
