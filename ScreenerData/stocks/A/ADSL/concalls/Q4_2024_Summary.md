## Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) reported robust Q4 FY24 results, showcasing significant year-on-year improvements and demonstrating progress on its strategic initiatives. The company declared its highest-ever dividend of 30%, marking its 40th anniversary. Consolidated revenues grew by 6% YoY to ₹177 crore, with EBITDA and PAT also showing substantial YoY increases to ₹24 crore and ₹17 crore, respectively. EBITDA margins improved to 14% from 11% in Q4 FY23. The Indian domestic business continued its strong performance, with standalone FY24 revenues up 36% YoY to ₹287 crore, and EBITDA margins reaching 15%. Management reaffirmed the ₹1,000 crore revenue target for FY26 and anticipates margin improvement, aiming for mid-teens by FY26. The company is actively expanding its global presence, establishing a subsidiary in Dubai, and focusing on higher-margin services like cybersecurity and the rebranded "Digital Desk" platform (formerly ADiTaaS). While acknowledging some deal closures are taking longer due to the global macro environment, management expressed confidence in its pipeline and future growth trajectory.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ADSL's strategy is centered on leveraging its Indian market strength, expanding its global footprint, and focusing on higher-margin services. Key drivers include:

*   **Domestic Market Focus:** Continued strong performance in India, with significant YoY growth in revenue and profitability.
*   **Global Expansion:** Establishment of a subsidiary in Dubai to tap into Middle East opportunities.
*   **Smart City Projects:** Active participation and execution in smart city initiatives, with completed and ongoing projects contributing significantly to revenue. The company sees a large opportunity in the smart city space, estimating a market size of ₹50,000 crore over the next five years.
*   **"Digital Desk" Platform:** Rebranding of ADiTaaS to "Digital Desk" with enhanced AI and automation features to drive adoption and improve customer productivity.
*   **Cybersecurity and Cloud:** Active pursuit of opportunities in these high-growth areas, including potential inorganic growth through acquisitions.
*   **Direct Sales & Marketing:** Refinement of direct sales channels to expand business.
*   **Focus on Higher Margins:** Strategic selection of deals, particularly emphasizing the Operation & Maintenance (O&M) phase of projects, which offer better margins (25-35% gross, mid-teens EBITDA).
*   **Annuitization of Revenue:** Emphasis on annuity-based business and services, contributing to financial stability.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Actively exploring acquisitions in Cybersecurity and Cloud sectors.
