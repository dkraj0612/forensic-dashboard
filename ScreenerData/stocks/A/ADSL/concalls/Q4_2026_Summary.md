## Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) delivered a strong Q4 FY26, concluding fiscal year 2026 with record annual revenues of ₹968 crore, a 20% year-over-year (YoY) increase. The company's Q4 run rate pushed its annualized revenue past the ₹1,000 crore mark, demonstrating resilience in a challenging operating environment. Strategic imperatives, including AI-led digital transformation, global customer engagement, and expansion into cloud, cybersecurity, and managed services, continue to be the core drivers for future growth. The Board of Directors has recommended a dividend of 30% for FY26.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **AI Integration:** ADSL is heavily focused on AI implementation, showcasing numerous case studies including AI-driven L1 deflection, Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption, AI-augmented SOC triage, and an AI-powered computer vision platform. The company highlights AI's role in productivity gains, cost reduction, and enhanced operational efficiency.
*   **Managed Workplace Services:** Continued expansion of global managed workplace services, including multilingual service desks and remote support. The company is also exploring opportunities in managed services for AI data centers and data sovereignty solutions.
*   **Smart Governance:** Secured a significant city-wide Integrated Command & Control Centre (CCC) deployment, reinforcing their expertise in large-scale smart city initiatives.
*   **Enterprise & Global Expansion:** Strong focus on enterprise growth and client retention through renewals of long-standing engagements. Multi-region workplace services and AI adoption in the US are key drivers for international business.
*   **Digital Engineering Services:** Emphasis on delivering continuous, long-term support for clients.
