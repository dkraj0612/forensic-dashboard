## Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) reported a strong Q1 FY26, with consolidated revenues growing by 22% year-on-year to ₹219 crore. This marks the fourth consecutive quarter exceeding ₹200 crore in revenue, with trailing twelve months (TTM) revenue reaching approximately ₹850 crore. The growth was broad-based, driven by both India and international operations. India revenues surged by 27% YoY, fueled by enterprise and government segments, particularly smart city projects like Pune. International business showed encouraging signs of recovery, with renewed client engagement in the U.S. and growing interest from Europe and the Middle East. Segment-wise, Services revenue grew 20% YoY, and Solutions revenue increased by 32% YoY. The company secured ₹185 crore in order intake this quarter, alongside a significant announcement of a ₹421 crore, 5-year multi-year deal with a leading pharmaceutical company in Europe. Management acknowledged persistent pricing pressure from customers seeking cost control, necessitating a more flexible pricing strategy, but expects margins to improve as contracts mature and through technology adoption, particularly AI. The company is actively exploring acquisitions in Cybersecurity and Cloud spaces.

## Key Financial Metrics

**For Q1 FY26 (Consolidated):**
*   **Revenue from Operations:** ₹219 crore, a 22% YoY increase.

**Order Wins:**

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Sustained Growth Trajectory:** Aiming to maintain the growth momentum seen over the last three quarters, supported by a healthy deal pipeline and increased win rates.
*   **Smart City & Government Focus:** Continued execution in smart cities (e.g., Pune) and government projects, with an expectation of larger ticket sizes and ongoing opportunities in Tier 2/3/4 towns and Phase 2 projects.
*   **International Expansion:** Renewed client engagement in the U.S. and growing interest from Europe and the Middle East, with efforts to build direct sales capabilities in these regions.
*   **Solutions & Services Integration:** Leveraging the Solutions segment as a pipeline for recurring revenue Services business, with a focus on technology integration, including AI.
*   **AI Integration:** Accelerating AI adoption across operations, including Agentic AI for NOC operations and AI-based managed services, aiming to improve efficiency and margins.
*   **Cybersecurity & Cloud Acquisitions:** Actively exploring acquisitions in the Cybersecurity and Cloud domains to strengthen offerings.
*   **Talent Development & Sales Team Expansion:** Investing in upskilling and expanding the sales team, particularly in the U.S., to drive direct customer acquisition and diversify partnerships.
*   **Data Center Services:** Growing traction in data center development and management, both within smart city projects and for enterprise clients, including cloud migration and upgrades.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Cost Management:** Responding to customer pricing pressure with operational flexibility and a strategic approach to pricing, while focusing on efficiency improvements to maintain resilient margins.
