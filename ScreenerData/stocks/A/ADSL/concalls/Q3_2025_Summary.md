## Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) reported a resilient Q3 FY26 (ended December 31, 2025) performance, with consolidated revenues reaching ₹247 crore, a 12% year-over-year increase. This marks the highest quarterly revenue in the company's history and brings the YTD revenue to ₹700 crore (16% YoY growth). EBITDA increased by 11% YoY to ₹26 crore, and PBT saw a 13% YoY rise to ₹22 crore. The company is seeing increased demand for integrated, outcome-driven solutions, playing to its strengths as a Master System Integrator. International operations showed strong momentum, with the "Rest of World" segment growing 26% YoY, while the US market sees improved activity. India operations experienced a 5% YoY de-growth due to a lack of significant project milestones in Q3, but renewed activity is expected in Q4. The company's strategy remains focused on execution excellence, prudent capital allocation, and building a diversified order book, positioning it well amidst evolving global dynamics and policy support for digital infrastructure.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **AI Integration:** Embracing an "AI-first" strategy, integrating AI into all services, and developing proprietary Agentic AI platforms and leveraging commercial platforms (Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini). AI Ops are being used in the NOC for complex solutions and issue resolution.
*   **Integrated Solutions:** Customers increasingly prioritize integrated, outcome-driven solutions over fragmented deployments, aligning with ADSL's Master System Integrator role.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Continued momentum in international operations (26% YoY growth in Rest of World), with US Enterprise clients showing decisiveness in network modernization, digital workplace, and cybersecurity. Middle East offers opportunities driven by digital transformation and government investments.
*   **Data Centre Capabilities:** Expertise in architecting, designing, and implementing data centres for corporate clients, including build, operate, and manage models, rather than competing with large hosting providers. This is a core competence, particularly for government and smart city projects.
*   **Smart Cities & Public Infrastructure:** Sustained stellar performance in Smart City business. Anticipating growth in public place technologies beyond Smart Cities, including airports, ports, and railway stations, leveraging learnings from Smart City projects.
*   **Diversified Order Book:** Healthy order flows supporting revenue visibility and long-term growth. Focus on larger deal sizes, longer tenures, and broader scopes.
*   **Enterprise Segment Growth:** Renewed enterprise sentiment leading to higher revenue growth from non-government customers (13% YoY) compared to government segments (12% YoY).
*   **Talent & Solutions Development:** Ongoing investment in upskilling the workforce and developing future-ready solutions, particularly in AI and cybersecurity.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Revenue Growth & ₹1,000 Crore Target (Annualized by end of FY26/early FY27):**
    *   **Consistency:** Consistent. Q3 FY26 revenue of ₹247 crore is the highest ever quarterly revenue, bringing YTD to ₹700 crore. Management states they are progressing towards ₹1,000 crore annualized revenue, and expect to hit a ₹250 crore quarterly run rate from next quarter onwards, a quarter ahead of schedule.

*   **Quarterly Revenue Target (₹250 crore quarterly run rate by Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27):**
    *   **Consistency:** Ahead of schedule. Q3 FY26 revenue of ₹247 crore is close to the ₹250 crore target, indicating they are on track to achieve this run rate from Q4 FY26.

*   **EBITDA Margins (Target of 12-13% within 3-4 quarters; expectation of continued margin pressure for next 3-4 quarters):**
    *   **Shift in Narrative (Mixed):** Management reported improved margins in Q3 FY26 over Q2 FY26, and stated their aim is to maintain the current 12% EBITDA margin and try to improve from there. However, the Q3 FY26 EBITDA margin (which is approximately 10.5% based on ₹26 crore EBITDA on ₹247 crore revenue) is below the previously targeted 11-12%. They acknowledge that while gross margins are improving, investments in large contract acquisition and compliance for multi-country contracts can put pressure on EBITDA. They also noted that as large Smart City projects move into O&M, margins are expected to improve.

*   **Order Wins & Pipeline (Robust pipeline; ₹698 crore target for Q2 FY26 was a prior quarter's metric):**
    *   **Consistency:** Consistent. Order intake in Q3 FY26 was over ₹250 crore. Management highlights an improvement in the quality of the pipeline with larger deal sizes and longer tenures, and a healthy pipeline for government orders, especially in Maharashtra, Western Railways, and Metros.

*   **Smart City Focus & Project Milestones:**
    *   **Consistency:** Consistent. Smart City business continues to perform well. Delays in Q3 FY26 billings were attributed to elections and subsequent permission issues, impacting the Pune project's milestones. These are expected to be completed by March 31st, driving renewed billing in Q4 FY26.

*   **International Business & U.S. Sales:**
    *   **Consistency:** Consistent. US enterprise clients are showing more decisive activity. International operations, particularly "Rest of World," are demonstrating improving momentum (26% YoY growth in Q3 FY26).

*   **Acquisition Strategy (Actively exploring Cybersecurity and Cloud acquisitions):**
    *   **Consistency:** Not explicitly mentioned in this transcript. However, the strong emphasis on AI, cybersecurity, and digital transformation services suggests a continued strategic focus on these domains, which could be pursued through organic development or strategic partnerships/acquisitions.

*   **Auditor's Observations:**
    *   **Significant Development (Negative):** The transcript *does* contain references to audit qualifications regarding physical verification of fixed assets and inventory, aged debtors (>3 years old), and unbilled revenue. Management acknowledges these are not new issues and are actively working towards rectification by March 31, 2026. They aim to convert the loan to equity to resolve an outstanding qualification related to their US subsidiary. This is a notable departure from the previous quarter's summary and requires close monitoring.
