## Allied Digital Services Limited: Q3 FY25 Executive Summary

### Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) reported a strong Q3 FY25, with consolidated revenues reaching ₹220 crore, marking a significant 29% year-over-year increase. This is the second consecutive quarter where revenues have exceeded the ₹200 crore threshold, demonstrating sustained business momentum despite a modestly challenging macroeconomic environment. Profitability also saw a sharp sequential improvement in margins, with EBITDA now at approximately 12%. India operations were a key driver, with standalone revenues growing 45% YoY, while Rest of World (ROW) operations increased 16% YoY, driven by improved traction in the U.S. The Services segment grew 22% YoY, and the Solutions segment showed robust growth of 59% YoY, with Solutions activity serving as a pipeline for future Services revenue. The company secured new orders and contract renewals totaling over ₹200 crore, diversifying its portfolio and enhancing long-term growth visibility across enterprise and government sectors. Management expressed optimism, citing signs of recovery in discretionary spending and a strong pipeline of opportunities. Key wins included a U.S.-based multinational investment bank, a major U.S. eye care provider, a leading non-profit, the world's largest vaccine manufacturer (for OT security), a multinational automotive brand, and a public sector bank for data center solutions.

### Key Financial Metrics

**For Q3 FY25 (Consolidated):**
*   **Revenue from Operations:** ₹220 crore, a 29% YoY increase.
*   **Quarterly Revenue:** Second consecutive quarter exceeding ₹200 crore.
*   **India Standalone Revenue:** 45% YoY growth.
*   **Rest of World (ROW) Revenue:** 16% YoY growth, with improved U.S. traction.
*   **Services Business Revenue:** 22% YoY growth.
*   **Solutions Business Revenue:** 59% YoY growth.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Approximately 12%, with sharp sequential improvement.
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT) & Profit After Tax (PAT):** Strong growth reported, though specific figures for Q3 FY25 not detailed beyond "sharp improvement."
*   **Order Intake (Q3 FY25):** Over ₹200 crore (new orders and contract renewals).
*   **Employee Cost:** ₹37.5 crore, an 11% YoY increase (impacted by reclassification, wage hikes, and reduced notional ESOP loss).
*   **Cash on Books:** Approximately ₹180 crore.
*   **Tax Rate:** ~12% in Q3 FY25 due to deferred tax adjustments.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Diversified Growth:** Continued focus on a multi-pronged strategy encompassing geographic diversity (U.S., India, potential European expansion), customer base (direct, partnership, enterprise, government), and solution mix (core services, innovation, solutions).
*   **Margin Expansion through Value-Add:** Prioritizing opportunities with core service components, innovation, and strategic solutioning over mere revenue driving. Leveraging technology and AI to enhance productivity, reduce costs, and improve profitability.
*   **AI and Automation Integration:** Integrating AI across solutions (e.g., driver monitoring, surveillance, incident management) and collaborating with partners to develop new tools. Anticipating increased AI pervasiveness due to cost reductions from advancements like DeepSeek.
*   **Managed Services Model:** Employing an asset-based billing model for global clients, providing flexibility in manpower management and long-term margin leverage.
*   **Smart City Expertise:** Reinforcing position as an end-to-end IT services integrator and strategic player in large-scale system integration projects in India.
*   **Financial Discipline:** Maintaining a strong cash position for potential investments in customer acquisition and strategic acquisitions, while actively working to reduce Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) to 60-65 days.
