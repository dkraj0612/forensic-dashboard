## Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) reported a solid Q3 FY26, demonstrating continued growth and resilience in a dynamic global environment. Consolidated revenues reached ₹247 crore, marking a 12% year-over-year increase and extending the streak of exceeding ₹200 crore in quarterly revenue to six consecutive quarters. EBITDA grew 4% year-over-year to ₹26 crore, though margins reflect challenging operating conditions. Profit Before Tax (PBT) saw a healthy 13% year-over-year increase. The company highlighted a growing customer demand for integrated, AI-powered transformations across Data, Cloud, and Cybersecurity. ADSL secured several new contracts, including AI chatbot solutions for healthcare, AI/ML video analytics for energy, and end-user support services in the US. The company also received contract renewals and awards, recognizing its marketing and brand impact. Management emphasized investments in next-generation technologies, talent, and delivery capacity to support digital and AI journeys.
