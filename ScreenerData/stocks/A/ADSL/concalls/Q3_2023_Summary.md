## Executive Overview

Allied Digital Services Limited (ADSL) presented its investor relations material for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2023. The presentation highlights a strong year-on-year (YoY) growth in standalone revenues for Q3 FY24, up 36%, driven by disciplined order book execution. This translated into a significant YoY increase in EBITDA by 110% and Profit After Tax (PAT) by 115% for the standalone entity. Management notes an augmentation of assignments and expansion of scope from existing enterprise and government clients, alongside steady renewals, despite an elongated new business cycle. ADSL is strategically investing in its direct sales engine to capitalize on accelerating digitization and believes deferred contracts are now materializing. The company continues to emphasize its digital revenue leveraging next-gen technology and maintains a robust order pipeline. Key business developments include securing the Master System Integrator role for the Ayodhya Smart City Project's CCTV integration and winning a new order for the Lucknow Safe City Project. Several contract renewals and expansions were also announced, including with a global FMCG corporation, an American home appliance brand, and a restaurant chain, collectively boosting revenue and signaling strong client satisfaction. The company also highlighted its commitment to ESG principles and its "Great Place to Work" certification.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ADSL's strategy continues to focus on leveraging technology for digital transformation and expanding its service offerings. Key drivers include:

*   **Digital Revenue Focus:** Emphasizing revenue streams that utilize next-generation technologies like AI, Cloud, and Cybersecurity.
*   **Order Book Execution:** Disciplined execution of existing orders as a core driver of revenue and profitability.
*   **Client Engagement & Expansion:** Augmenting assignments, expanding scope with existing enterprise and government clients, and securing steady contract renewals.
*   **Direct Sales Investment:** Strategically increasing investment in the sales engine for direct outreach to capture opportunities in the accelerating digitization market.
*   **Smart City & Safe City Projects:** Continued involvement and leadership in government initiatives like the Ayodhya Smart City and Lucknow Safe City projects, showcasing expertise in integrated surveillance and control systems.
*   **Global Managed IT Services:** Strengthening partnerships and securing contract renewals with global corporations across various sectors (FMCG, Appliances, QSR) for workplace, field, and end-user management services.
*   **Technological Depth:** Continuing to develop and offer solutions around ADiTaaS and other proprietary platforms.
*   **Operational Progress:** Making operational improvements and strengthening business fundamentals.
