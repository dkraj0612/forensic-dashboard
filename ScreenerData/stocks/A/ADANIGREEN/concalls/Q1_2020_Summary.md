## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported strong Q1 FY21 results, demonstrating resilience amidst the COVID-19 pandemic. The company maintained "must-run" status for its renewable plants, ensuring continuous electricity off-take with no material curtailment. AGEL was recognized as the world's largest solar power developer, having secured an 8 GW solar development bid from SECI, the largest single solar development bid globally. This award brings their total capacity (operational, awarded, and under-implementation) to 14.2 GW, moving closer to their 25 GW by 2025 target. A strategic partnership with TOTAL S.A. was finalized, with TOTAL acquiring a 50% stake in 2,148 MW of operational solar capacity for Rs. 3,707 Cr, unlocking cash for future growth. Operationally, Net Export increased by 24% YoY to 1,382 million units, driven by new capacity additions and improved solar and wind CUFs. Financially, Revenue from Power Supply rose 10% YoY to Rs. 609 Cr, and EBITDA from Power Supply increased by 12% YoY to Rs. 555 Cr. The company turned profitable, reporting a Net Profit of Rs. 22 Cr in Q1 FY21, a significant improvement from a Net Loss of Rs. 97 Cr in Q1 FY20, attributed to higher EBITDA, increased Other Income, and a change in depreciation method. Cash Profit grew 9% YoY to Rs. 232 Cr. Construction activities have resumed in a phased manner with negligible impact. AGEL is also planning a wholly-owned subsidiary for a 2000 MW solar module manufacturing facility, expected to commence construction by January-February 2021 and commission by December 2021.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 25 GW by 2025, with 14.2 GW already secured through operational, awarded, and under-implementation projects.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Monetizing operational assets through strategic divestments (e.g., TOTAL SA JV) to fund future growth and adopt global best practices.
*   **Manufacturing Facility:** Establishing a 2000 MW solar module manufacturing unit to enhance domestic capabilities and leverage "Make in India" initiatives.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Diversified financing sources and a focus on refinancing operational assets through international bond markets.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Leveraging Adani Energy Network Operation Centre (Adani-ENOC) for robust O&M practices to ensure high plant availability and CUFs.
*   **Securing Large Bids:** Actively participating in and winning significant solar development bids to fuel future growth.
*   **Hybrid Projects:** Executing large hybrid projects, with an expectation of commissioning ahead of schedule.
