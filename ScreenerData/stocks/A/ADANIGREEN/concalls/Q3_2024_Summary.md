## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) presented a strong Q3 FY24 earnings call, highlighting robust operational performance and strategic advancements. The company reported a significant 59% increase in energy sales to 16,293 million units for the first nine months of FY24, driven by a 16% year-on-year growth in operational capacity to 8.4 GW. Revenue from power supply surged by 57% to INR 5,794 crores, with EBITDA also rising 52% to INR 5,412 crores, maintaining an industry-leading EBITDA margin of 92%. Cash profit saw a substantial 61% increase to INR 2,944 crores. AGEL has secured Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) for its entire 8 GW manufacturing-linked solar tender, bringing its total PPA-backed portfolio to 19.8 GW and a locked-in growth portfolio of 20.8 GW, including a 1.01 GW merchant portfolio. The company reiterated its commitment to the 45 GW target by 2030, emphasizing a disciplined capital management approach and strong governance, evidenced by top ESG rankings.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 45 GW renewable energy capacity by 2030.
*   **Khavda Project:** World's largest renewable power plant, serving as a blueprint for mega-scale projects. Transmission readiness is improving, with initial lines ready for 3 GW of capacity addition.
*   **PPA & Merchant Portfolio:** Total PPA-backed portfolio of 19.8 GW. Locked-in growth portfolio at 20.8 GW, including 1.01 GW merchant capacity.
*   **Merchant Portfolio Growth:** Expectation to grow to "lower teens" of the total portfolio by the end of the decade, with strategic investments in this area.
*   **Group Captive Potential:** North of 2 GW over the next few years, outside the 45 GW target.
*   **Technology & Integration:** Continued focus on optimizing returns through a balanced portfolio mix.
*   **Pumped Storage Hydro (PSP):** Part of the long-term FY30 strategy, with multiple MOUs signed and detailed feasibility studies underway. Expecting to commence PSP implementation from FY26, with one project in advanced stages (environmental clearance, land acquisition, financial closure).
*   **Digitalization:** Emphasis on advanced analytics and AI for project development and operations.
*   **Sustainability & Governance:** Ranked #1 in Asia and top 3 globally in RE sector by ISS ESG. Ranked first in the power sector by CRISIL ESG.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Historical Context Audit

*   **Capacity Addition Target (FY25):** Management did not provide specific FY25 targets in this transcript. However, they discussed their execution capacity of 2.5 GW currently and their aim to grow it to over 5 GW from next year, implying a ramp-up from previous fiscal year targets. They explicitly stated they are synchronizing PPA execution with this growing capacity.
*   **Transmission Readiness:** Management confirmed that transmission readiness is now in place for new capacity additions, specifically mentioning the first evacuation lines for Khavda are ready for 3 GW. This addresses a potential previous concern regarding transmission bottlenecks.
*   **Merchant Strategy:** Management acknowledged the increase in merchant capacity to approximately 5% of the portfolio, and indicated a strategic intention to grow this to "lower teens" by the end of the decade, aligning with previous commentary on seeking balanced portfolios and tapping into C&I customers.
*   **TotalEnergies JV:** The transcript confirms the completion of a new Joint Venture (JV) with TotalEnergies for a 1,050 MW portfolio, which was fully consolidated. Previous stapled instruments were converted to compulsory convertible debentures, treated as equity-like instruments for accounting.
*   **Capital Management:** The issuance of equity warrants to promoters and the upscaling of debt funding under a construction financing framework demonstrate continued execution of their capital management philosophy. Refinancing of operational assets at competitive rates also aligns with this strategy.
