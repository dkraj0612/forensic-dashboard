## Executive Overview

This executive summary synthesizes the key information presented in Adani Green Energy Limited's (AGEL) investor presentation dated September 21, 2021. AGEL continues to position itself as a leading global solar power developer and a key player within the Adani Group's integrated infrastructure portfolio. The presentation emphasizes AGEL's robust, repeatable, and transformative business model focused on aggressive capacity expansion, risk mitigation, operational excellence powered by its Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC), and strong ESG commitments. AGEL highlights its de-risking strategy through a growing operational portfolio and access to diversified, sophisticated funding sources including international banks and green bonds. The company reiterates its commitment to achieving industry-leading margins and stable cash flows through its fully contracted portfolio and long-term PPAs with predominantly sovereign-rated counterparties. A strategic alliance with TotalEnergies SE is reinforced as a crucial element for growth and capital access.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Total Renewable Capacity (MW):** 19,794 MW (as of presentation date, including 5,370 MW operational, 5,424 MW under execution, and pipeline including SB Energy & Inox acquisitions)
*   **Operational Capacity (MW):** 5,370 MW (as of presentation date)
*   **Contracted Portfolio:** 100%
*   **Counterparty Mix (by capacity for fully built portfolio):** 87% Sovereign/sovereign equivalent rated
*   **Average Portfolio Tariff:** INR 3.13/unit (for locked-in growth)
*   **EBITDA Margin (Power Supply):** 91% (FY21)
*   **Revolving Construction Facility:** USD 1.35 billion (Framework Agreement with 12 international banks, expanded to 16)
*   **HoldCo Green Bonds:** USD 750 million (20 & 10-year dual tranche)
*   **TotalEnergies Investment:** Up to USD 2.5 billion (strategic alliance)
*   **Capacity Target:** 25,000 MW by 2025 (reiterated)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transformative Investment Philosophy:** AGEL continues to leverage the Adani Group's proven model of scaling, growth, and free cash flow generation across its infrastructure and utility verticals.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Reiteration of the 25 GW by 2025 target. The presentation includes significant capacity under acquisition (SB Energy 5 GW, Inox 150 MW wind), indicating active pursuit of growth beyond existing operational and under-execution projects.
*   **Risk Mitigation:** Focus on securing resource-rich sites (~31 GW potential across 200,000+ acres) and ensuring connectivity. The portfolio remains 100% contracted with a high proportion of sovereign-rated counterparties, and a strategic shift towards a majority operational portfolio by 2022 is highlighted to reduce development risk.
*   **Operational Excellence:** ENOC is central to technology-enabled, analytics-driven O&M using AI for predictive maintenance, aiming to maximize generation and EBITDA per MW through high asset availability (~100% solar target) and low operating costs.
*   **Capital Management:** Diversified funding includes the USD 1.35 Bn revolving construction facility, USD 750 Mn HoldCo green bonds, and access to international markets (144A, REG S, REG D). The debt structure is noted to be shifting from PSU banks to bonds, with a focus on securing Investment Grade (IG) ratings.
*   **ESG Integration:** AGEL emphasizes its commitment to global ESG leadership. Specific targets for Net Water Neutrality, Single-Use-Plastic-Free (SuPF) operations, and Zero-Waste-to-Landfill (ZWL) are outlined, alongside strong governance with the establishment of an ESG committee composed of independent directors.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** The long-term partnership and significant investment from TotalEnergies SE are central to AGEL's growth and capital strategy.
