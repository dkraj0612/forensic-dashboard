## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported robust H1 FY26 financial and operational highlights, demonstrating continued growth and operational excellence. The company's operational capacity expanded by 49% YoY to 16.7 GW, with a significant addition of 5.5 GW over the past year, including 2.4 GW of greenfield capacity in H1 FY26, representing 74% of the FY25 annual addition. Revenue from power supply saw a 26% YoY increase to INR 6,088 crore, while EBITDA grew by 25% YoY to INR 5,651 crore, surpassing FY23's annual EBITDA. The company maintained an industry-leading EBITDA margin of 91.8%. AGEL's commitment to sustainable growth is underscored by its strong ESG rankings, being the #1 in India and #7 globally in the RE sector by Sustainalytics. The company continues to focus on large-scale renewable energy development, particularly at its Khavda project, and emphasizes operational efficiency through digitalization and AI-driven O&M.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** AGEL is on track to achieve its target of 50 GW by 2030, with a strategic focus on resource-rich states like Gujarat and Rajasthan.
*   **Khavda Project:** The world's largest single-location renewable energy project continues to be a key growth driver, with significant capacity operationalized and planned for phased development through 2029.
*   **Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP):** AGEL has secured over 5 GW of PSP sites and is integrating storage solutions for grid stability and enhanced returns.
*   **Merchant, C&I, and Hybrid Opportunities:** Increased focus on merchant sales, Commercial & Industrial (C&I) customers, Contract for Difference (CFD), and mid-duration hybrid power purchase agreements to diversify revenue streams and capture higher returns.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Leveraging digitalization, AI/ML-driven analytics via the Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC), and advanced O&M practices to ensure high plant availability, improve CUF, and reduce operational costs.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Commitment to maintaining top-tier ESG ratings and implementing sustainable practices, including water positivity, zero waste to landfill, and no net loss of biodiversity.
*   **Capital Management:** Prudent debt management, access to international markets, and diversified funding sources ensure growth is well-funded and de-risked. Construction readiness and robust transmission infrastructure planning are critical enablers.
