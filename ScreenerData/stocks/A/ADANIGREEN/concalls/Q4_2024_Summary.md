## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported a strong Q4 FY24, achieving 10.9 GW of operating capacity, making it India's fastest renewable player to reach this milestone. The company added 2,848 MW of greenfield capacity in FY24, representing a 35% year-on-year increase and over 15% of India's total renewable capacity addition for the fiscal year. AGEL is developing the world's largest single-location renewable energy plant in Khavda, with 2 GW already operationalized within 12 months of groundbreaking. Financially, revenue from power supply grew 33% YoY to INR 7,735 crores, and EBITDA rose 30% YoY to INR 7,222 crores, with a run-rate EBITDA of INR 10,462 crores. Net debt to run-rate EBITDA improved to 4x from 5.4x. The company maintains an industry-leading EBITDA margin of 92%. AGEL has revised its 2030 renewable energy capacity target upwards from 45 GW to 50 GW, with 100% of this capacity fully funded. The company also plans to add at least 5 GW of pumped hydro storage capacity by 2030, with construction underway for a 500 MW project.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Revised target of 50 GW renewable energy capacity by 2030 (up from 45 GW).
*   **Khavda Project:** World's largest single-location renewable plant (30,000 MW), with 2 GW operationalized and full completion targeted by 2029.
*   **Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP):** Targeting at least 5 GW by 2030. Construction commenced for the first 500 MW project in Andhra Pradesh; FY27 targeted for commissioning.
*   **Merchant & C&I Portfolio:** Actively pursuing C&I customers and Adani Group entities for decarbonization. Targeting "lower teens" for merchant/C&I within the total portfolio mix (currently ~5% fungible merchant capacity).
*   **Execution Capacity:** Aiming to add 6,000 MW of greenfield capacity in FY25 and 6,000-8,000 MW annually in subsequent years.
*   **Technology & Integration:** Strategic use of pumped hydro storage as a cheaper alternative to batteries for grid stability and meeting demand.
*   **Sustainability & Governance:** Ranked #1 in Asia and top 5 globally in RE sector by ISS ESG; CDP rated 'A' for Supplier Engagement and 'A-' for Climate Change.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Historical Context Audit

*   **Capacity Addition Target (FY25):** Management has now provided a target of at least 6,000 MW for FY25, aligning with their previously indicated execution capacity ramp-up.
*   **Transmission Readiness:** Management explicitly stated that transmission readiness, including for Khavda, is factored into their capacity addition plans and will not be a constraint, directly addressing potential concerns.
*   **Merchant Strategy:** The company is actively pursuing C&I customers and indicates a strategic shift towards a larger merchant/C&I component in their portfolio, aiming for "lower teens" by the decade's end, aligning with earlier discussions about seeking balanced portfolios.
*   **TotalEnergies JV:** Not explicitly mentioned in this Q4 FY24 transcript, but previous consolidations were noted.
*   **Capital Management:** The oversubscribed bond issuance and the clear statement of 100% funding for the 50 GW target (through internal accruals and promoter warrants) reinforce their disciplined capital management.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, or Timelines

*   **Capacity Addition (FY25):** At least 6,000 MW of greenfield capacity.
*   **Capacity Addition (FY26 onwards):** 6,000-8,000 MW annually on a run-rate basis.
*   **Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP) Commissioning:** First 500 MW project targeted for FY27. Standard PSP projects have a 3-3.5 year commissioning timeline.
*   **PSP Capital Cost:** INR 4.5 crores to INR 5 crores per MW.
*   **Merchant/C&I Portfolio Target:** Guidance of about 10% for the mix of merchant and C&I sales together.
*   **Solar Module Prices (India):** Domestic module prices are around $0.17 - $0.175 (factoring in BCD) or ~$0.24 for recent SECI/NTPC tenders. Expectation of module prices inching towards a downward trajectory due to increased domestic manufacturing.
*   **Solar Project Capital Cost:** Approximately INR 5 crores per MW at a 1.35-odd AC-DC ratio, inclusive of BCD and domestic modules.
*   **Wind Project Capital Cost:** Approximately INR 6.5 crores per MW.
*   **Green Hydrogen:** AGEL's focus remains on utility-scale projects for government counterparties, C&I, and group companies' decarbonization; no current plan to allocate significant capacity for green hydrogen.
*   **Infirm Power Sale:** No infirm power sales in Q4 FY24; P&L reflects revenue upon plant commissioning.
*   **CTU Connectivity & Land:** 100% of the pipeline has sufficient CTU connectivity and land.
