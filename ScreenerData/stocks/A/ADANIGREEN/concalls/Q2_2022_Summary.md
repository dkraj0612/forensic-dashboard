## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **"Resource as Value Approach":** Strategic acquisition of vast land banks (~200,000 acres) in resource-rich locations to secure future generation potential (~40 GW).
*   **De-risking Strategy:** Comprehensive approach to mitigate risks from site acquisition and financing to construction and operations.
*   **Project Management Excellence:** Utilizing the Project Management & Assurance Group (PMAG) for end-to-end project integration, control, and timely execution.
*   **Technology-Enabled O&M:** Employing the Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC) with advanced analytics, predictive maintenance, and AI/ML for cost-efficient operations and high performance.
*   **Capital Management:** Disciplined approach to capital structure, including debt capital market refinancing, green bond issuances, and a robust construction financing framework.
*   **Partnership with TotalEnergies:** Strategic alliance strengthening renewable energy footprint, governance, and best practices. TotalEnergies owns 19.7% in AGEL and 50% in Adani Green Energy Twenty-Three Limited.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Commitment to ambitious sustainability goals, including water neutrality, zero waste to landfill, single-use plastic reduction, and biodiversity management.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 45 GW of renewable energy capacity by 2030, with a significant portion already locked-in (20.4 GW).
