## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported a strong Q1 FY '26, surpassing 15 GW of operational renewable energy capacity. The company added 1.6 GW of greenfield capacity in the quarter, contributing to a 4.9 GW addition over the past year, a record for India's renewable sector. Operational capacity now stands at 15.8 GW, with Khavda project contributing significantly to this growth, reaching 5.6 GW of solar, wind, and hybrid portfolios. Financially, AGEL saw a 31% year-on-year increase in revenue from power supply to INR 3,312 crores and a 31% rise in EBITDA to INR 3,108 crores, maintaining an industry-best EBITDA margin of 92.8%. Energy sales grew by 42% to 10.5 billion units. Management reiterated their commitment to the 50 GW by 2030 target, emphasizing execution at scale, operational excellence through advanced technologies, and strong governance. AGEL also highlighted its ESG leadership, receiving top rankings from FTSE Russell, Reuters, NSE, and CRISIL.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Capacity Expansion:** Maintaining commitment to 50 GW by 2030 target, with a focus on strategic selection of tenders for predictable and better returns.
*   **Khavda Project Development:** Continued focus on Khavda as a key growth hub, with 5.6 GW operationalized. Evacuation capacity upgrades are being monitored closely.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Emphasis on AI-driven decision-making, automation, and advanced analytics. Recognition through CII Performance Excellence Awards.
*   **Revenue Diversification & Flexibility:** Strategic use of merchant sales for early-commissioned projects and exploration of C&I opportunities. Increasing focus on wind in the merchant portfolio. Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are under active evaluation.
*   **Data Center Demand:** Identifying data centers as a significant opportunity, with a target of 25% of capacities for such markets by FY30. Long-term contracts are preferred, but spot market participation is also planned.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Growth funded through a comprehensive capital management framework, with ongoing evaluation of refinancing to reduce borrowing costs.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Continued commitment to sustainability, with top global ESG rankings maintained.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Historical Context Audit

*   **Capacity Expansion (50 GW by 2030):** Management reiterated they are "firmly on track" and added 4.9 GW in the past year, demonstrating strong execution. The 5 GW target for FY26 remains a key focus.
*   **Khavda Project Progress:** Consistent emphasis on Khavda as a major growth driver, with 5.6 GW now operational. The target of 30 GW by 2029 remains.
*   **Operational Efficiency (CUF, Technology):** Solar CUF in Q1 FY26 was reported at 28%, which is lower than previous high-performing Khavda figures (over 32%). Management attributes this to the proportion of Khavda in the overall portfolio being one-third and seasonal impacts. Wind CUF at 42.3% was strong, noted as adjusted for a six-month maintenance schedule. Management confirmed they are better prepared for monsoon impacts this year compared to the previous year.
*   **Merchant/C&I Focus:** The strategy to use early commissioning for merchant sales continues, generating "infirm revenue" and "pure-play merchant" income. Merchant solar prices were reported at INR 2.20 in Q1 FY26, lower than Q4 FY25 (INR 3.00), attributed to early monsoons and grid curtailment. Wind merchant prices were INR 5.70, which is higher than Q4 FY25 (INR 4.70).
*   **Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP):** Management clarified that the cancellation of specific PSP projects in Andhra Pradesh does not impact the announced 5 GW PSP addition target by FY30-31, as these are de-risked projects.
*   **Funding and Capital Management:** Gross debt increased to INR 78,000 Cr. The company is actively looking at refinancing to reduce borrowing costs but expects gross debt to remain stable due to ongoing new capacity additions.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Consistent reinforcement of ESG commitments with top global rankings.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, or Timelines

*   **Capacity Addition (FY26 Projection):** Management is "on track" for the 5 GW capacity addition target for the fiscal year.
*   **Khavda Evacuation Capacity:** Management is closely monitoring and expecting improvements, with "enough evacuation for us to evacuate everything" within the fiscal year. Challenges are described as minor, impacting less than 5% of EBITDA.
*   **Solar CUF (FY26 Projection):** Expected to be in a similar range or better than the last quarter's performance (i.e., >28%), with an increasing proportion of Khavda capacity improving overall CUF.
*   **Wind CUF (FY26 Projection):** Management expects improvements driven by the inclusion of larger 5.2 MW turbines and better wind resources, aiming for CUF to remain best-in-class.
*   **Merchant Sales Realizations:**
    *   Solar: INR 2.20 per unit (Q1 FY26). Management advised looking at full-year trends rather than quarterly swings due to seasonality.
    *   Wind: INR 5.70 per unit (Q1 FY26).
*   **ISTS Waiver Impact:** Strategy is to adhere to government policies; 75% of the waiver remains, and management does not foresee a major immediate change in market pricing.
*   **Chitravathi PSP Execution Timeline:** No specific new timeline provided, but previous report indicated focus on early execution.
*   **Maharashtra 5 GW Project Timeline:** No new update provided in this call.
*   **Capex (FY26):** INR 6,500 Cr was spent in Q1 FY26. 95% of capex for the 5 GW capacity addition is under sanctions with debt tie-ups.
*   **Hybrid Project Under Implementation:** No new specific project mentioned, but overall strategy includes hybrid projects.
*   **Capacity with Pending PPAs:** Total of 36.5 GW of capacity is accounted for; 31.5 GW is under PPA, with the remainder for merchant/C&I segments.
*   **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS):** Actively evaluating technologies and developing a strategy for BESS, which will be announced at an appropriate time. This is *not* included in the current 50 GW target.
*   **Data Centers:** 25% of capacities targeted for data center markets by FY30, with a focus on long-term contracts.
*   **Adani-Total JV Pipeline:** Approximately 500 MW is in the pipeline for commissioning within the next six months to one year.
*   **Labour Availability:** Management claims no significant labor issues, citing effective infrastructure and facilities at Khavda to retain labor for long-term projects.
*   **DCR Cell Efficiencies:** Currently, AGEL does not have DCR cells in its portfolio; management expressed confidence in the quality of DCR products if adopted.
