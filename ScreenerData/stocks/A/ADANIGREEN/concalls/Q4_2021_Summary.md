## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) presented an investor update on December 16, 2021, underscoring its ambition to become the world's largest renewable energy company. The company reiterated its robust growth trajectory, targeting 25 GW by 2025 and an ambitious 45 GW of renewable energy capacity by 2030, a significant expansion of its previous long-term goal. AGEL highlighted its fully funded growth pipeline, leveraging the USD 1.35 billion revolving project finance facility and access to international bond markets.

The update emphasized a disciplined yet transformative capital management approach, strong project execution capabilities, and analytics-driven Operations & Maintenance (O&M) through its Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC), which ensures high plant availability and industry-leading EBITDA margins. AGEL also detailed the strategic alliance with TotalEnergies, which includes a USD 2.5 billion investment and a 50:50 joint venture, reinforcing governance and best practices. A heightened focus on ESG commitments was evident, with new aggressive targets for becoming single-use plastic-free, zero-waste-to-landfill, and net water neutral by FY25, alongside a revised governance framework increasing independent director representation. The company positioned itself as a compelling investment case, capitalizing on India's vast renewable potential and its own proven transformative business model.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aggressive Capacity Expansion:** Commitment to 25 GW by 2025, and a new target of 45 GW by 2030, supported by resource-rich sites with ~31 GW potential and secured land for 25 GW, with an additional 10 GW identified.
*   **Fully Funded Growth:** Strategic capital management, including the revolving construction facility and international bond placements, to de-risk and optimize finance costs, ensuring continuous funding.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Leveraging ENOC for analytics-driven O&M, maximizing plant availability (nearly 100% for solar) and energy generation, leading to optimized costs and high EBITDA margins.
*   **Strategic Alliances:** Deepening partnership with TotalEnergies for unlocking value, bringing in global best practices, and ensuring strong governance.
*   **Robust Supply Chain:** Strong vendor relationships (20,000+) and strategic alliances with Tier-1 suppliers for effective and timely project execution.
*   **Integrated Project Management:** Centralized coordination through Project Management & Assurance Group (PMAG) and standardized development processes (SOPs) for timely and cost-effective delivery.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Commitment to being a global top 10 company in ESG benchmarking, with concrete targets for carbon emission reduction, water neutrality, waste management, and social impact.
*   **New Technology Integration:** Focus on integrating battery storage and other new technologies, alongside GW-scale development to optimize costs.
*   **De-risked Portfolio:** 100% contracted portfolio with 25-year PPAs, 88% with sovereign/sovereign equivalent counterparties, minimizing counterparty risk.
