## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported a strong Q1 FY2022, marked by significant operational capacity expansion and strategic progress. The company commissioned 200 MW of greenfield projects (150 MW wind in Gujarat and 50 MW solar in UP), bringing its installed capacity to 3,520 MW, with further additions expected through acquisitions. Consolidation of the SB Energy acquisition, expected by August end, will significantly boost operational capacity to approximately 5,400 MW. AGEL reaffirms its 25 GW by 2025 target, with a projected 9,000 MW operational by FY2022, a substantial shift from its previous developer-centric model to a mature asset operator. Financial performance showed robust growth, with Revenue from Power Supply up 39% YoY to ₹848 Cr and EBITDA from Power Supply up 41% YoY to ₹789 Cr. Cash Profit also saw a significant 35% increase to ₹460 Cr. Operational efficiency, driven by the Energy Network Operation Center (ENOC), led to improved solar and wind Capacity Utilization Factors (CUFs) and a high EBITDA margin of 92.5%. The company highlighted strong ESG performance, including water positivity at its Kamuthi plant and zero fatalities.

*   **Total Portfolio (Operational & Under Construction):** ~20 GW (excluding Andhra Pradesh tender)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aggressive Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 25 GW by 2025, with a near-term goal of 9,000 MW operational by FY2022, supported by both greenfield commissioning and strategic acquisitions (SB Energy, Inox).
*   **Transition to Mature Asset Operator:** Shifting focus from development to operating a significantly larger asset base, aiming for substantial free cash flow generation.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Leveraging ENOC for enhanced plant availability, improved CUFs, and strong EBITDA margins.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Completing the SB Energy acquisition and other potential inorganic opportunities to accelerate capacity growth.
*   **Manufacturing Linkage:** Progressing on manufacturing bids, with initial headway made for 500 MW, integrating with the broader SECI manufacturing-linked projects.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Continuing to benefit from the partnership with TotalEnergies for improved practices and governance.
*   **Disciplined Bidding Strategy:** Focusing on projects that meet internal hurdle rates (targeting ~16% equity IRR) and maintaining financial discipline, including investment-grade ratings.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Strong commitment to ESG principles, aiming for top global benchmarking and continuous improvement in sustainability metrics.
*   **Securing Land Bank:** Strategic acquisition of land, including significant holdings in Khavda (70,000 acres for ~15 GW), to de-risk future development.
