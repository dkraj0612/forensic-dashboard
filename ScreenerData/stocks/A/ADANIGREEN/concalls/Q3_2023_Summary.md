## Executive Overview

This presentation, dated November 18, 2023, for a Credit Conference, reiterates Adani Green Energy Limited's (AGEL) established business philosophy and investment rationale, with a strong, continued emphasis on its ESG initiatives. AGEL highlights its integrated, end-to-end project development model, leveraging its substantial land bank and group synergies. The presentation underscores strategic partnerships and AGEL's role within the broader Adani Group's infrastructure portfolio. Capital management remains a key focus, with a continued emphasis on diversified funding sources and de-risking strategies throughout the project lifecycle. The company reaffirms its commitment to ambitious renewable capacity targets, aligned with India's national climate goals. The overall tone suggests a continuation of a focused approach on operational excellence, strategic execution, and ESG performance, with updated operational and under-execution capacity figures.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **"Resource as Value Approach":** Strategic aggregation of ~40 GW generation potential across ~200,000 acres, with completed geotechnical and resource analysis, and detailed design planning.
*   **Integrated Project Development:** Continual emphasis on a centralized Project Management & Assurance Group (PMAG) for end-to-end project oversight, with a team of 120 professionals.
*   **GW-Scale Hybrid Cluster Development:** Highlighted operationalization of 2,140 MW Hybrid cluster in Rajasthan, noted as India's first and world's largest solar-wind hybrid cluster.
*   **Technology Adoption & O&M Excellence:** Centralized Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC) for continuous monitoring, predictive analytics, and high availability (consistently >90% EBITDA margins).
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Implied continuation of alliances within the Adani Portfolio.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Fully funded growth through a USD 1.64 Bn revolving construction facility (expanding to USD 3.04 Bn) and takeout financing. Diversified funding includes USD 750 Mn Green Bond issuance and a focus on SPV-level project financing.
*   **Group Synergies:** Leveraging Adani Group's strengths in infrastructure and utilities is a recurring theme.
*   **ESG Integration:** Strong focus on ESG as a core business tenet, with detailed case studies and certifications (Water Positive, Single-Use-Plastic Free, Zero Waste to Landfill) for operational sites.
