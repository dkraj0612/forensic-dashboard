## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **GW Scale Hybrid Cluster Development:** Focus on developing large-scale hybrid projects within dedicated clusters to achieve significant scale efficiencies. The Rajasthan Hybrid Cluster serves as a model.
*   **Integrated Project Development:** Emphasizing a systematic, end-to-end development process from site origination (3 years of advance resource estimation) to execution, coordinated by a centralized Project Management & Assurance Group (PMAG).
*   **Technology Adoption:** Implementing advanced technologies like bifacial modules and horizontal single-axis tracking systems for solar, and enhanced turbine designs for wind, to maximize Capacity Utilization Factor (CUF).
*   **Resource-Rich Site Strategy:** Leveraging extensive land banks (~200,000 acres) with assessed solar and wind resources for future development.
*   **ESG Integration:** Embedding ESG practices across all projects, with specific initiatives in the Rajasthan cluster including community development, infrastructure improvements, and water resource management. The company reiterates its commitment to sustainability goals.
*   **Partnerships:** Continuing the strategic alliance with TotalEnergies, with TotalEnergies holding a 19.7% stake in AGEL and 50% in Adani Green Energy Twenty-Three Limited.
