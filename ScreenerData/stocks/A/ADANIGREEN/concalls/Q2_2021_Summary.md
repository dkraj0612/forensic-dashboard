## Executive Overview

This transcript details Adani Green Energy Limited's (AGEL) H1 FY22 results conference call for the half-year ended September 30, 2021. The call highlights significant capacity expansion, primarily driven by the successful acquisition of SB Energy's 5 GW renewable portfolio for USD 3.5 billion. This acquisition boosted AGEL's operational capacity to 5.4 GW and its total portfolio to 20.3 GW, representing a 4x locked-in growth. Management also reported progress on the SECI manufacturing-linked solar projects with PPA signings for 867 MW and contract agreements for a 2 GW solar PV manufacturing plant. Furthermore, AGEL was awarded 450 MW of wind projects from SECI. The company reiterated its commitment to ESG initiatives, including its sponsorship of a climate change gallery at the Science Museum in London and its founding membership in the ‘Global Alliance on Sustainable Energy’. On the corporate governance front, AGEL approved revisions to its board charter, enhancing independent board committees, including an audit committee and a corporate responsibility committee comprised solely of independent directors. Financially, the company reported strong performance with a 54% increase in sale of energy to 3,954 million units and a 48% increase in revenue from power supply to INR 1,682 crore. EBITDA from power supply grew by 50% to INR 1,577 crore, with a margin improvement to 93%. Management expressed confidence in achieving their 25 GW renewable capacity target ahead of 2025.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aggressive Capacity Expansion:** Driven by strategic acquisitions (SB Energy) and organic growth to achieve targets beyond 25 GW.
*   **Hybrid Projects:** All three hybrid projects are expected to be completed by end of FY22.
*   **ESG Integration:** Continued commitment to global ESG benchmarks, becoming single-use-plastic-free, zero-waste-to-landfill, and water positive. Sponsorship of the Science Museum's 'Adani Green Energy Gallery'. Founding member of the ‘Global Alliance on Sustainable Energy’ and signed UN Energy Compact for SDG-7.
*   **Operational Excellence:** High plant availability (~100% for solar) and improved CUFs for both solar (23.2%) and wind (40.7%) attributed to O&M, technology advancements, and improved wind speeds.
*   **Counterparty Risk Mitigation:** ~100% of SB Energy's portfolio comprises sovereign-rated counterparties. Overall portfolio counterparty mix is 88% sovereign-rated.
*   **Supply Chain Management:** Modules for FY22 projects are ordered and LCs opened. Contracts for ongoing projects (7,500-8,000 MW) were locked in 5-7 months prior at varying price points.
*   **Energy Transition Focus:** Exploring opportunities across the energy transition spectrum, including battery storage and potentially green hydrogen as part of the broader Adani Group strategy.
*   **Wind Sector Focus:** AGEL remains committed to wind energy as part of its diversified portfolio, recognizing its importance alongside solar.
*   **Regulatory Engagement:** Active engagement with government initiatives promoting renewable energy for both solar and wind.
*   **Manufacturing & PLI Scheme:** Adani Group is serious about solar manufacturing, with existing capacity and expansion plans, and has applied for the 4 GW PLI scheme. However, manufacturing is a separate business line from AGEL.
*   **RTC Tender Participation:** AGEL bid for a 2.5 GW round-the-clock tender, indicating continued interest in 24x7 renewable solutions with a minimum 51% renewable component.
