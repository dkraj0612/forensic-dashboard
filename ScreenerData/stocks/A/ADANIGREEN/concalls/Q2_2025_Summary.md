## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported robust operational and financial performance for H1 FY26. The company's renewable energy capacity expanded by 49% year-on-year to 16.7 GW, with 2.4 GW of greenfield capacity added in H1 FY26, positioning AGEL as India's largest and fastest-growing pure-play renewable energy company. Energy sales saw a significant 39% YoY increase to 19.6 billion units. Financially, revenue from power supply grew 26% YoY to INR 6,088 crore, and EBITDA increased by 25% to INR 5,651 crore, maintaining an industry-leading EBITDA margin of 92%. Cash profit surged 17% YoY to INR 3,094 crore. AGEL continues to strengthen its sustainability credentials, ranking first in India and seventh globally in renewable energy by Sustainalytics. The company is making steady progress on its 50 GW by 2030 target, with particular focus on the Khavda project.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Renewable Energy Capacity (H1 FY26):** 16.7 GW (49% YoY increase)
*   **Greenfield Capacity Added (H1 FY26):** 2.4 GW (74% of total FY25 capacity addition)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Capacity Expansion:** Management reiterated commitment to the 50 GW by 2030 target, with strong execution in H1 FY26.
*   **Khavda Project as Growth Hub:** Continued development of the 30 GW Khavda project as a key growth driver.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Focus on AI/ML and data analytics for O&M, aiming for high plant availability and industry-leading margins.
*   **Revenue Diversification:** Increasing focus on merchant, C&I, and infirm power sales to enhance returns, especially through early execution.
*   **Energy Storage Integration:** Strategic development of Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP) and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). AGEL is developing BESS at an unprecedented scale in India, with strategy to be announced shortly.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Growth strategy funded through revolving credit facilities and promoter equity. Leverage is expected to remain between 4-5x Net Debt to Run-Rate EBITDA for the next 2-3 years, with a sharp decline expected post-2029.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Continued commitment to sustainability with top global ESG rankings.
