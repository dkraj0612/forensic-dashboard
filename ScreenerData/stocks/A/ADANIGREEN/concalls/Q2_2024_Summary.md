## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported strong H1 FY25 performance with a significant increase in revenue and EBITDA, driven by robust capacity additions and operational efficiency. The company added 2.9 GW of greenfield capacity in the last year, bringing its total operational capacity to 11.2 GW. Key strategic developments include the ongoing construction of the Khavda project, the world's largest renewable energy plant, and securing a 5 GW solar power LOA from Maharashtra State Discom. AGEL also marked its entry into the C&I segment with a deal to power a Google data center. The company reiterated its commitment to its 50 GW renewable energy target by 2030 and emphasized disciplined capital management, including the redemption of a USD 750 million bond and a new JV with TotalEnergies. AGEL continues to focus on ESG integration and technological advancements, including exploring battery storage solutions.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 50 GW renewable energy capacity by 2030. Current locked-in portfolio (operational + LOA + under construction) is significant and growing.
*   **Khavda Project:** World's largest renewable energy plant (30 GW) under development, with 2 GW solar capacity operational within 12 months. Deployment of 5.2 MW WTGs (largest onshore) at Khavda showing ~44% CUF in stabilization phase.
*   **Project Execution:** Focus on detailed planning and execution for 6 GW capacity addition in FY25, including managing monsoon impact and ensuring timely delivery.
*   **Merchant & C&I Focus:** Increased focus on merchant wind capacity (80% of new wind in FY25) for accretive returns and diversification. First C&I deal signed with Google for 61 MW, targeting data center decarbonization.
*   **Technology Adoption:** Utilizing advanced technologies like 5.2 MW WTGs and exploring battery storage solutions due to falling prices and improved chemistry.
*   **Capital Management:** Redemption of USD 750 million Holdco bond, strengthening partnership with TotalEnergies via a new JV (1.15 GW portfolio).
*   **ESG Integration:** AGEL is the first Indian renewable energy company to join the Utilities for Net Zero Alliance and maintains top ESG rankings.
*   **Pumped Storage Hydro (PSP):** Integration of PSP as a key strategy for round-the-clock renewable energy and grid stability, with over 20 GW of PSP sites available.
*   **Manufacturing Initiatives:** Group's wind turbine manufacturing ready for commercial launch, awaiting MNRE clearance. 4 GW module manufacturing capacity operational.
