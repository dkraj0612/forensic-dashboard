## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) presented its Q1 FY24 earnings, highlighting strong operational and financial performance driven by continued capacity expansion and operational excellence. The company reiterated its commitment to its 45 GW renewable energy target by 2030, leveraging its integrated project development model and "Resource as Value Approach." Key financial metrics showed significant year-on-year growth in operational capacity, sale of energy, revenue, EBITDA, and cash profit. AGEL emphasized its industry-leading EBITDA margins, high plant availability, and robust ESG integration, including certifications for water positivity and zero waste to landfill. The company also discussed its disciplined capital management, sourcing strategies, and advancements in its manufacturing initiatives. While reaffirming its strategic direction, management displayed a high density of deflection terminology, focusing on long-term strategy and operational achievements.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Operational Capacity:** 8,316 MW (as of June 2023)
    *   Year-on-Year Growth: 43%
    *   New Capacity Added in Q1 FY24: 2,516 MW (Hybrid 1,750 MW, Solar 212 MW, Wind 554 MW)
*   **Sale of Energy:** 6,023 million units (YoY increase of 70%)
*   **Revenue from Power Supply:** INR 2,059 crores (YoY increase of 55%)
*   **EBITDA from Power Supply:** INR 1,938 crores (YoY increase of 53%)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 92.5%
*   **Cash Profit:** INR 1,051 crores (YoY increase of 55%)
*   **Run-rate EBITDA (8,316 MW capacity):** INR 7,645 crores
*   **Net Debt (as of June 2023):** INR 40,800 crores
*   **Net Debt to Run-rate EBITDA:** 5.3x (decreased from 6.53x in March 2022)
*   **Receivables:** No overdue receivables; 87% sovereign portfolio.
*   **Plant Availability (Solar):** Exceeded 99%
*   **CUF:** Solar 26.9%, Wind 38.7%, Hybrid 47.2%

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 45 GW renewable energy capacity by 2030. Current locked-in portfolio (operational + signed-in + letters of award) exceeds 20 GW.
*   **"Resource as Value Approach":** Aggregation of generation potential across vast land banks.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Driven by AI and digitization through the Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC), ensuring high plant availability and industry-leading EBITDA margins.
*   **ESG Integration:** Focus on decarbonization, water positivity, single-use plastic-free, and zero waste to landfill. Commitment to supplier decarbonization by FY26. Socioeconomic development in operating regions remains a priority.
*   **Technology Adoption:** Emphasis on advanced technologies like trackers and bifacial modules, and deployment of larger capacity wind turbines (5+ MW).
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Primarily funded through the revolving construction facility, with plans for future debt capital market issuances and potential QIP.
*   **Supply Chain Localization:** Ongoing efforts to localize procurement of critical components, including trackers, solar modules, and wind turbines. Group's manufacturing initiatives for modules and wind turbines are progressing.
*   **C&I Segment Focus:** Exploring opportunities in the Commercial & Industrial (C&I) segment for renewable energy solutions.
*   **Pumped Storage Hydro:** Integrating pumped storage as a key part of the future strategy to provide round-the-clock renewable energy.
