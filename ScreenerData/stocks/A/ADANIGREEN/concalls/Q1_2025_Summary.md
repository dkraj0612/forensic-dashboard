## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported a strong Q1 FY25, showcasing significant operational expansion and robust financial growth, reaffirming its path towards the ambitious 50 GW by 2030 target. The company's total operational capacity reached 11.2 GW, with a notable 2.6 GW added over the past year. This quarter saw the operationalization of 250 MW of wind power at Khavda, utilizing India's largest 5.2 MW onshore wind turbine generators, bringing Khavda's total operational capacity to 2.25 GW.

Financially, AGEL demonstrated excellent performance with a 24% YoY increase in revenue from power supply to INR 2,528 crore and a 23% YoY rise in EBITDA from power supply to INR 2,374 crore, maintaining an industry-leading EBITDA margin of 92.6%. Cash profit surged by 32% to INR 1,390 crore. Energy sales grew 22% YoY to 7,356 million units, with generation consistently exceeding PPA commitments (11% in FY24, 31% of annual in Q1 FY25). The company's credit profile was strengthened by an upgrade to ‘AA-’ by India Ratings & Research. AGEL also continued to achieve top-tier ESG rankings, including 3rd in FTSE Russell ESG assessment for alternative electricity.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Capacity Expansion:** AGEL is firmly on track to achieve its 50 GW capacity target by 2030, which includes at least 5 GW of energy storage through pumped hydro. Execution is being accelerated through advanced robotics, robust local supply chains, and sustained human resource mobilization in key project sites like Khavda and Rajasthan.
*   **Khavda Project as a Growth Hub:** The development of the 30 GW Khavda project, the world's largest single-location renewable energy plant, continues to be a central pillar of growth. Strategic deployment of 5.2 MW WTGs leverages Khavda’s superior wind resources (8m/s average).
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Continued focus on tech-enabled operations and maintenance, with ongoing integration of AI and ML to further enhance efficiency, plant availability (targeting 98-99% for new wind), and improve already industry-leading EBITDA margins.
*   **Revenue Diversification & De-risking:** Increasing focus on merchant sales, particularly for high-CUF wind (projected 35% CUF at Khavda) and strategic solar deployments (projected 33% CUF). Diversification extends to Commercial & Industrial (C&I) customers, bilaterals, and hybrid power purchase agreements to capture higher returns. Pre-COD generation, enabled by early commissioning, provides significant value kicker (1.5-2 years benefit) and merchant exposure.
*   **Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP) Integration:** Secured over 5 GW of PSP sites with an estimated capex of INR 4.5 crore/MW. These solutions are key for grid stability, enabling arbitrage between daytime and evening power prices, and providing firm, dispatchable power for C&I customers. PSPs are strategically located for low capex and minimal environmental impact.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Maintaining a prudent financial strategy with access to promoter warrants and strong internal cash generation to fund growth without additional equity dilution. Borrowing costs are decreasing due to strong market appetite, and net debt to run-rate EBITDA is expected to remain stable.
*   **Leveraging Carbon Markets:** AGEL is well-positioned to benefit from increasing demand for green electrons from C&I customers (e.g., data centers, hard-to-abate sectors), sales of carbon credits from Gold Standard/Verra registered projects, and Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) as RPO enforcement strengthens.
