## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) presented its Q1 FY25 results, highlighting continued robust operational and financial performance. The company's total operational capacity reached 11.2 GW, with a significant 2.6 GW added in the last year. The operationalization of 250 MW of wind power at Khavda in July 2024, utilizing India's largest 5.2 MW onshore wind turbines, brings Khavda's total operational capacity to 2.25 GW. Financially, AGEL reported a 24% YoY increase in revenue from power supply to INR 2,528 crore and a 23% YoY rise in EBITDA from power supply to INR 2,374 crore, maintaining an industry-leading EBITDA margin of 92.6%. Cash profit surged by 32% to INR 1,390 crore. Energy sales grew 22% YoY to 7,356 million units. AGEL's credit profile was strengthened by an upgrade to ‘AA-’ by India Ratings & Research. The company also continues to achieve top-tier ESG rankings, including 3rd in FTSE Russell ESG assessment for the Alternative Electricity Subsector.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Capacity Expansion:** AGEL is on track for its 50 GW by 2030 target, driven by accelerated greenfield additions and innovative execution using robotics and digitalization.
*   **Khavda Project as a Growth Hub:** The 30 GW Khavda project is progressing as the world's largest single-location renewable energy plant, with strategic deployment of advanced WTGs and customized design planning.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Continued focus on tech-enabled O&M via the Energy Network Operations Center (ENOC) and AI/ML integration, aiming for high plant availability (98-99% for new wind projects) and maintaining industry-leading EBITDA margins.
*   **Revenue Diversification:** Increasing focus on merchant and C&I opportunities to maximize value, alongside 25-year fixed tariff PPAs for predictable cash flows.
*   **Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP) Integration:** Over 5 GW of PSP sites secured, with a stated capital cost of INR 4.5 crore/MW, crucial for grid stability and arbitrage.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Fully funded growth strategy leveraging a USD 3.4 bn Revolving Construction Facility and promoter equity infusions, with growth risk delinked from credit performance via ring-fenced structures.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Commitment to sustainable practices with top-tier ESG rankings and ambitious targets for water positivity, zero waste, and biodiversity.
