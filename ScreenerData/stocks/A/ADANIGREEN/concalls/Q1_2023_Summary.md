## Executive Overview

This presentation from Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) for an investor/analyst ESG conference on June 21, 2023, reiterates the company's core business philosophy and investment rationale, with a significant emphasis on its Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) initiatives. AGEL highlights its integrated, end-to-end project development model, leveraging a substantial land bank and group synergies. The presentation underscores strategic partnerships, particularly with TotalEnergies, and AGEL's role within the broader Adani Group's infrastructure portfolio. Capital management remains a key focus, with a continued emphasis on diversified funding sources and de-risking strategies throughout the project lifecycle. The company reaffirms its commitment to ambitious renewable capacity targets, aligned with India's national climate goals. The overall tone and content suggest a continuation of the previous trend of **High Evasion Density**, with a strong focus on process, strategy, and ESG performance over granular, up-to-the-minute financial updates.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Operational Capacity (as of Presentation Date - June 21, 2023):** 8,216 MW
*   **Under Execution / Near Construction Capacity:** 10,319 MW (Includes capacity where PPA is signed, and capacity awarded pending PPA execution)
*   **Total Locked-in Portfolio (as of Presentation Date):** 20,434 MW (includes Operational and Under Execution/Near Construction)
*   **Total Capacity Potential (Resource Rich Sites):** ~40 GW from ~200,000 acres
*   **Average Portfolio Tariff:** INR 2.97/unit
*   **Revolving Construction Facility:** USD 1.64 Bn (fully funds project pipeline)
*   **HoldCo Issuance:** USD 750 Mn (20 & 10 year dual tranche bond)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **"Resource as Value Approach":** Strategic aggregation of ~40 GW generation potential across ~200,000 acres, with completed geotechnical and resource analysis, and detailed design planning.
*   **Integrated Project Development:** Continual emphasis on a centralized Project Management & Assurance Group (PMAG) for end-to-end project oversight.
*   **GW-Scale Hybrid Cluster Development:** Operationalization of 2,140 MW Hybrid cluster in Rajasthan, noted as India's first and world's largest solar-wind hybrid cluster.
*   **Technology Adoption & O&M Excellence:** Centralized Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC) for continuous monitoring, predictive analytics, and high availability (consistently >90% EBITDA margins).
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Continuation of the alliance with TotalEnergies.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Fully funded growth through a USD 1.64 Bn revolving construction facility and takeout of construction debt post-commissioning. Diversified funding includes USD 750 Mn Green Bond issuance.
*   **Group Synergies:** Leveraging Adani Group's strengths in infrastructure, ports, and logistics.
*   **ESG Integration:** Strong focus on ESG as a core business tenet, with ambitious targets and certifications in Water Positive, Single-Use-Plastic Free, and Zero Waste to Landfill.
