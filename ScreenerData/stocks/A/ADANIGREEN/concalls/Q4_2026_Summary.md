## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported a strong fiscal year 2026, marked by significant growth in energy sales and operational capacity. The company added 5.1 GW of greenfield capacity, making it the highest annual capacity addition globally by any company outside China. AGEL's operational portfolio now stands at 19.3 GW. The Khavda project continues its rapid expansion, with 9.4 GW of solar, wind, and hybrid assets operational, alongside 1.4 GWh of battery storage commissioned. The company also made progress on its pumped hydro storage projects, with the maiden 500 MW Chitravathi project slated for completion in FY27. Financially, revenue from power supply increased by 22% year-on-year to INR 11,602 crores, and EBITDA grew by 23% to INR 10,865 crores, maintaining a high EBITDA margin of 91.2%. AGEL received an inaugural BBB+ rating from Japan Credit Agency and an ESG 1+ rating from CareEdge, underscoring its financial discipline and sustainability commitment.

## Key Financial Metrics

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Capacity Expansion:** Firmly on track for the 50 GW by 2030 target, with continued strong greenfield additions and a focus on executing at Khavda.
*   **Khavda Project Development:** Remains a central growth engine, expanding solar, wind, hybrid, and now battery storage capacity.
*   **Technology and Operational Excellence:** Continual adoption of advanced technologies, digitization, and data analytics for predictive maintenance and superior performance.
*   **Integrated Storage Solutions:** Significant expansion in battery energy storage systems (BESS) to complement renewable generation. Pumped hydro storage is also progressing.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Secured favorable credit ratings (JCR BBB+), reflecting financial discipline and ability to sustain growth.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Consistent global and domestic recognition for sustainability and responsible business practices.
