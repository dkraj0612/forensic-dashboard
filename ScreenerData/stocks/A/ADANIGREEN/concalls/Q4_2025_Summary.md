## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported a record fiscal year 2025, characterized by significant capacity additions and strong financial performance. The company added 3.3 GW of new renewable energy capacity, representing the highest ever by any renewable company in India and contributing 16% to India's utility-scale solar and 14% to wind power installations in FY25. Operational progress at the Khavda project in Gujarat is substantial, with over 4 GW operationalized in two years and a target of 30 GW by 2029. Financially, AGEL surpassed $1 billion in EBITDA, with energy sales increasing by 28% to 28 billion units, revenue from power supply growing by 23% to INR 9,495 crores, and EBITDA rising by 22% to INR 8,818 crores. The company also successfully refinanced a $1.06 billion construction facility with a long-term, rated AA+ facility. AGEL continues to emphasize its ESG commitments, achieving water positivity ahead of schedule and maintaining top global ESG rankings. The company is on track to add another 5 GW of capacity in the current fiscal year.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Capacity Expansion:** Management reiterated commitment to the 50 GW by 2030 target, with a focus on continuous execution and a targeted 5 GW capacity addition in FY26.
*   **Khavda Project Development:** Continued focus on Khavda as a key growth hub, leveraging advanced technologies like bifacial N-type modules, single-axis trackers, and robotic cleaning systems to maximize generation and achieve 30 GW by 2029.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Emphasis on AI-driven decision-making, automation, and advanced analytics for optimized operations, resource management, and best-in-class safety and governance.
*   **Revenue Diversification & Flexibility:** Strategic use of merchant sales for early-commissioned projects and exploration of C&I and storage opportunities to enhance returns. Increasing focus on wind in the merchant portfolio.
*   **Energy Storage Integration:** Active monitoring and development of battery storage solutions, with a strategy to be announced.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Growth funded through a comprehensive capital management framework, aiming to maintain leverage between 4-5x Net Debt to Run-Rate EBITDA for the next 2-3 years, with a sharp decline expected post-2029.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Continued commitment to sustainability, with all operations now water positive and top global ESG rankings maintained.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Historical Context Audit

*   **Capacity Expansion (50 GW by 2030):** Management stated they are "firmly on track" and reiterated commitment to 50 GW by 2030. The 3.3 GW addition in FY25 demonstrates strong execution.
*   **Khavda Project Progress:** The narrative around Khavda as a major growth driver and its rapid operationalization (4 GW in two years) is consistent. The target of 30 GW by 2029 remains.
*   **Operational Efficiency (CUF, Technology):** Management highlighted technological advancements (bifacial modules, trackers, robotics) driving generation, particularly at Khavda, with Q4 solar CUF exceeding 32%. The previous H1 FY26 report mentioned solar CUF impacted by monsoons, and the current report indicates similar or better performance expected for FY26, with stabilization periods shortening.
*   **Merchant/C&I Focus:** The strategy to utilize early commissioning for merchant sales is confirmed, with a significant portion of FY25 sales involving PPA-linked capacity sold on the merchant market due to early commissioning.
*   **Pumped Hydro Storage (PSP):** The Chitravathi PSP project is progressing well, with a focus on early execution.
*   **Funding and Capital Management:** The successful refinancing of a large construction facility and the stated leverage targets (4-5x for 2-3 years, decline post-2029) are consistent with previous disclosures.
*   **ESG Leadership:** The achievement of water positivity ahead of schedule reinforces the company's sustained commitment to ESG principles.
*   **FY25 Capacity Addition Target:** The previous report's context implied a target for FY25. The current transcript indicates that 3.3 GW was added in FY25 against a target of 5 GW (as inferred from the question about slippages and the previous report's context). Management attributed the shortfall to environmental factors (rainfall impact) primarily at Khavda, which required re-mobilization.
