## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) reported a strong performance for the first 9 months of FY '26, characterized by significant year-on-year growth in energy sales (+37%) and operational capacity (+48% to 17.2 GW). The company added 5.6 GW of greenfield capacity in calendar year '25, representing a substantial portion of national renewable capacity additions. The Khavda project, now at 7.7 GW operational capacity, continues its accelerated development, including plans for a large battery energy storage system. Financially, revenue from power supply increased by 25% to INR 8,508 crores, and EBITDA grew by 24% to INR 7,921 crores, maintaining a high EBITDA margin of 91.5%. Management highlighted the use of advanced technologies, data analytics, and AI for operational efficiency and reaffirmed their commitment to the 50 GW by 2030 target. AGEL also continues to be recognized for its ESG leadership, maintaining top industry rankings.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Capacity Expansion:** Firmly on track for the 50 GW by 2030 target, with significant greenfield additions and strong execution at the Khavda site.
*   **Khavda Project Development:** Continues as a key growth hub with substantial operational capacity and plans for one of the world's largest single-location battery energy storage projects.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Leveraging AI, machine learning, and data analytics for real-time monitoring and superior performance.
*   **Storage Solutions:** Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and Pumped Hydro Storage (PHS) are strategically integrated to enhance grid reliability, de-risk operations, and capture peak power pricing.
*   **Merchant & Hybrid Portfolio:** Maintaining flexibility in merchant capacity, leveraging it for arbitrage with storage solutions, while also evaluating PPA opportunities for optimal shareholder value.
*   **Disciplined Capital Management:** Ongoing debt raising to fund significant capex pipeline, with debt to EBITDA levels expected to remain stable.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Continued recognition as a top global green utility and sustainability performer.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Historical Context Audit

*   **Capacity Expansion (50 GW by 2030):** Management reiterated being "firmly on course" and highlighted 5.6 GW added in CY25, reinforcing the scale of execution. The previous Q1 FY26 summary mentioned adding 1.6 GW in Q1 FY26 and targeting 5 GW for FY26; this transcript indicates 5.6 GW added in CY25 and commitment to FY26 expansion.
*   **Khavda Project Progress:** The narrative remains consistent with Khavda as a major growth engine. Operational capacity has increased from 5.6 GW (as per Q1 FY26 summary) to 7.7 GW in this transcript. The previously stated target of 30 GW by 2029 remains implicitly on track with this scale of development.
*   **Operational Efficiency (CUF/PLF):** Management acknowledged lower solar CUF (around 28% in Q1 FY26) in the previous summary due to Khavda's proportion and seasonality. This transcript confirms lower wind PLFs/CUFs in Q3 FY26 were "absolutely wind speed" related, not grid curtailment, indicating a shift in the primary driver for reduced PLF compared to the previous quarter's solar CUF discussion. Management stated improved performance in H1 of the current year.
*   **Merchant/C&I Focus:** The strategy to utilize merchant power for arbitrage, especially with storage, is reinforced. Previous Q1 FY26 summary noted merchant solar prices at INR 2.20 in Q1 FY26. This transcript reports solar merchant realization at INR 2.20 per unit in Q3 FY26, and wind at INR 3.5 per unit, with a clarification that REC is on top. The previous report mentioned wind merchant prices at INR 5.70 in Q1 FY26.
*   **Grid Availability & Curtailment:** This remains a significant theme. Management confirmed grid availability issues impacting generation, specifically citing delays in grid augmentation in Rajasthan and Khavda. While Q1 FY26 summary indicated management was "closely monitoring" Khavda evacuation capacity, this transcript details specific delays and upcoming augmentations (1 GW in Rajasthan, 2-3 GW expected at Khavda in Q4 FY26, and another 1 GW by end of March). Management states no curtailment on long-term GNAs.
*   **Funding and Capital Management:** Gross debt was INR 78,000 Cr in Q1 FY26. This transcript indicates a net debt of INR 76,000 Cr and a debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 4.6x (operating) and 5.6x (overall), suggesting manageable leverage within the current growth phase.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Consistent reinforcement of ESG commitments with top global rankings.
