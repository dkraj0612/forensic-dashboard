## Executive Overview

Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) presented an investor update on September 13, 2022, detailing its business philosophy, operational capabilities, and capital management strategy. The presentation emphasized AGEL's "Resource as Value Approach," leveraging extensive land banks and strategic site acquisitions for future growth. AGEL highlighted its robust project execution framework, exemplified by the Project Management & Assurance Group (PMAG), and its technology-enabled Operations & Maintenance (O&M) capabilities through the Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC). The company underscored its commitment to a de-risking strategy across the project lifecycle and its strong capital management, including significant debt refinancing and green bond issuances. Furthermore, AGEL reiterated its partnership with TotalEnergies and showcased its substantial progress on ESG commitments, including water neutrality, single-use plastic reduction, and biodiversity management. The presentation aimed to convey AGEL's well-positioned status for industry-leading growth and its disciplined, transformative business model.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **"Resource as Value Approach":** Strategic acquisition of vast land banks (~200,000 acres) in resource-rich locations to secure future generation potential (~40 GW).
*   **De-risking Strategy:** Implementing a comprehensive approach to mitigate risks at every stage of the project lifecycle, from site acquisition and financing to construction and operations.
*   **Project Management Excellence:** Leveraging the Project Management & Assurance Group (PMAG) for end-to-end project integration, control, and timely execution.
*   **Technology-Enabled O&M:** Utilizing the Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC) with advanced analytics, predictive maintenance, and AI/ML for cost-efficient operations and high performance.
*   **Capital Management:** Disciplined approach to capital structure, including debt capital market refinancing, green bond issuances, and a robust construction financing framework.
*   **Partnership with TotalEnergies:** Strengthening the renewable energy footprint through a strategic alliance, enhancing governance and best practices.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Commitment to ambitious sustainability goals, including water neutrality, zero waste to landfill, single-use plastic reduction, and biodiversity management, supported by strong ESG assurance frameworks.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 45 GW of renewable energy capacity by 2030, with a significant portion already locked-in (20.4 GW).
