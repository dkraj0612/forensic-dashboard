## Executive Overview

ABB India concluded FY2022 with a strong Q4 performance, crossing the INR 10,000 crore revenue milestone, a significant achievement given past divestments. The company demonstrated robust growth across orders, order backlog, revenue, and profitability, with all business divisions contributing evenly. Management highlighted strong traction in both traditional and new market segments, including robust demand in Tier II/III/IV cities and strategic wins in data centers and electric vehicle (EV) components. ABB's commitment to ESG was reinforced with 100% renewable energy usage across all campuses and a significant reduction in GHG emissions. The company maintains a strong cash position with a portion earmarked for strategic inorganic acquisitions.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue (FY2022):** Crossed INR 10,000 crore, representing a 14% CAGR since 2018.
*   **Orders (Q4 2022):** INR 2,600 crores (primarily base orders, no exceptionally large orders). Total orders for FY2022 grew 35% on a base order basis and 31% including large orders.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q4 2022):** INR 6,468 crores, up 32% YoY, providing strong revenue visibility for 2023. Process Automation accounts for a significant portion due to its business nature.
*   **Profitability (Q4 2022):**
    *   PBT: 16.5% (without exceptional items).
    *   PAT: Not explicitly detailed for Q4 '22 but showed direct correlation to PBT due to absence of exceptional items.
*   **Cash Balance (End of Q4 2022):** INR 3,600 crores, with INR 2,000 crores set aside for inorganic opportunities.
*   **Material Cost:** Favorable in Q4 '22 compared to previous quarters due to mix, volumes, and price increases.
*   **Exchange Gain (Q4 2022):** INR 30.6 crores.
*   **Export Revenue:** 12% of orders, with actual export growth at 14-15%, indicating faster domestic demand growth.
*   **Tier II/III/IV Market Contribution:** Increased to 48% of order intake (vs. 43% previous year), primarily in EL, MO, and PA segments.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Revenue Growth & Milestone Achievement:** Focus on sustaining INR 10,000+ crore revenue, building on FY2022's achievement.
*   **Market Penetration:** Continued emphasis on expanding reach into Tier II, III, and IV cities through enhanced channel networks, segment-specific seminars, and closer customer proximity.
*   **ESG Leadership:** Commitment to Green Factory certifications, 100% renewable energy, significant GHG emission reduction (82% in 2022), single-use plastic-free factories, and water positivity initiatives. Engaging suppliers in green initiatives.
*   **Capacity Expansion & Optimization:** Incremental capacity expansion for foreseen volumes. Prioritizing optimization of existing facilities through automation before new brick-and-mortar expansions.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Exploring bolt-on, small-to-medium-sized acquisitions in digitalization, energy sector, energy efficiency, and EV charging to complement the core portfolio.
*   **Data Center & EV Growth:** Leveraging global and local capabilities for data center opportunities. Positioning for growth in the EV segment with specialized IE3 motors for various vehicle types.
*   **Service Business Focus:** Elevating services as a distinct division to capitalize on the installed base, customer upgrades, and demand for digital and reliable solutions.
*   **Localization & Global Supply Chain:** Continuing efforts to localize products and leverage Indian manufacturing capabilities for global supply, emphasizing ESG standards and productivity gains.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Revenue Milestone:** Management's aspiration to reach INR 10,000 crore revenue has been achieved in FY2022. This is a significant validation of their long-term strategy following divestments.
*   **Order Inflows & Backlog:** The narrative of strong order inflows is consistent with the reported 32% YoY increase in backlog to INR 6,468 crore. While Q4 '22 had fewer large orders, the base order growth was strong (21% YoY), and management expressed confidence in the order funnel for Process Automation.
*   **Profitability:** Management achieved robust PBT margins in Q4 '22 (16.5% excluding exceptional items), exceeding previous targets and demonstrating the impact of revenue growth, capacity utilization, favorable mix, and value chain improvements. The goal of returning to 10% PAT levels is still a pursuit.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Management continues to drive and highlight significant progress on ESG, including 100% renewable energy, Green Factory certifications, and GHG emission reductions, aligning with previous communications.
*   **Supply Chain & Commodity Impact:** Management acknowledged commodity prices and forex as moving elements but reported that material costs were favorable in Q4 '22 due to effective price increases and volume/mix. Supply chain disruptions were managed well.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Revenue Trajectory:** Aim to sustain revenues above INR 10,000 crore annually, with a focus on converting the strong order backlog into revenue.
*   **Profitability:** Chase towards achieving 10% PAT levels. Q4 '22 PBT margin of 16.5% is to be used as a benchmark for ongoing performance.
*   **Inorganic Acquisitions:** Developing a robust pipeline for bolt-on, small-to-medium-sized acquisitions globally and locally, focusing on digitalization, energy, energy efficiency, and EV charging. No specific timeline mentioned, but prioritizing "right deals" for stable platforms.
*   **EV Segment:** Expect continued momentum and contribution from the EV motor segment as the market expands and OEMs localize supply chains. The group's global EV charging business strategy will influence India's approach.
*   **Services Growth:** Expect services to move beyond current 20-21% levels to 30%+ in the next three to four years, driven by a dedicated division and focus on the installed base and customer upgrades.
*   **Export Growth:** Continued focus on improving operational competitiveness and ESG standards to drive export growth, leveraging ABB's global supply chain strategy.
*   **Robotics & Discrete Automation Margins:** Expectation to maintain improved margins driven by services and offerings in electronics and logistics sectors, though Q4 '22 had exceptionally high margins due to capacity utilization.
*   **Group vs. India Order Reconciliation:** The disparity between group and India order growth is attributed to direct orders placed by Indian customers with ABB global units, which are not booked through ABB India's books. Management will continue to monitor these direct transactions.
*   **Future Quarters:** Anticipate Q1 results (April/May) to be as interesting as Q4.
