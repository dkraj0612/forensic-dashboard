## Executive Overview

ABB India reported a satisfactory Q2 2015, demonstrating operational execution and performance improvements that led to top-line growth and enhanced profitability. Despite a challenging market characterized by subdued industry investment and stressed customer balance sheets, the company managed to improve cash generation sequentially and year-on-year. Order intake was impacted by a lower number of large orders, although some significant orders were secured. Growth was notable in renewables (solar and wind), and the services segment continued its double-digit growth trajectory. Management highlighted that a sustained recovery in industrial production and credit to industry is needed before a significant revival can be expected. A significant portion of the Q2 results was influenced by the company's "cash over revenue" policy, leading to a deferral of approximately INR 150 crores in sales due to unfulfilled payment conditions.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** Delivered growth compared to the previous year, with product divisions showing firm performance.
*   **Profitability:** Improved across most divisions, with the exception of Power Systems due to revenue shortfall. Expenses were largely under control.
*   **Depreciation:** Higher due to the adoption of new rates as per the Companies Act 2013, adding approximately INR 8.5 crores per quarter.
*   **Cash Flow:** Improved sequentially and year-on-year, though overall cash flow was impacted by increased borrowings.
*   **Working Capital:** Increased by approximately INR 300 crores, attributed to a higher inventory of INR 150 crores pending customer clearances, increased receivables due to additional sales and tight liquidity, and stock for short-cycle solar projects.
*   **Receivables:** Over six months stood at INR 260 crores, an increase from INR 234 crores at the end of the previous year.
*   **Exports:** Contributed 15% to orders and 13-14% to revenue on a half-yearly basis.
*   **Forex Loss:** INR 11 crores for the quarter.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Focus on Operational Efficiency and Product Competitiveness:** Continued efforts led to improved profitability.
*   **"Cash Over Revenue" Policy:** Strict adherence to this policy, leading to deferred sales of INR 150 crores in Q2 due to unfulfilled payment conditions.
*   **Renewables Growth:** Solar and wind sectors are showing robust development and significant growth potential, with solar becoming a substantial segment for ABB India.
*   **Services Growth:** Double-digit growth in the services segment, maintaining strong customer connect.
*   **Railways Investment:** Capitalizing on ongoing investments in the railway sector.
*   **Transmission & Distribution (T&D):** Performing better than core industries, with traction in substations, grid connections, and balance of systems.
*   **"Make in India" and Domestic Manufacturing:** While domestic manufacturing has been a long-standing practice for ABB India, the company expects increased traction on domestic manufacturing clauses in tenders in subsequent quarters.
*   **Tariff-Based Bidding in Transmission:** ABB India works with developers, focusing on system optimization and long-term maintenance to provide value beyond just tender price.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Management stated they were able to deliver some growth in the top-line, which aligns with the reported revenue performance that was better than previous quarters.
    *   **Profitability Improvement:** Management indicated an improvement in profitability due to operational efficiency, which is confirmed by the reporting of better profitability across divisions (excluding Power Systems).
    *   **Cash Generation:** Management claimed better cash generation sequentially and year-on-year. The financial report confirms cash generation was higher than the sequential quarter and same time last year, though the overall cash flow was impacted by increased borrowing, as pointed out by an analyst.
    *   **Order Intake:** Management acknowledged order intake was below the previous year due to fewer large orders. This narrative is consistent.
    *   **Market Challenges:** Management consistently described the market as challenging, with subdued industry investment and stressed customer balance sheets. This tone remains consistent with previous discussions if this were not the oldest transcript.
    *   **Deferred Sales (INR 150 crores):** This was a recurring theme. Management confirmed deferred sales of INR 150 crores in this quarter, similar to the previous quarter. They clarified these were not from the same customers.
    *   **Export Performance:** Management stated exports were down this quarter due to delays in large projects. This is consistent with the explanation that some large orders were not closed on time.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Recovery Timeline:** Management indicated that a revival in industrial production and credit to industry is necessary, and they are "a few quarters away" from seeing an industrial revival.
    *   **Solar Market Growth:** Expectation that the solar market would touch anywhere between 3000 megawatt coming up for ordering in the coming quarters.
    *   **Renewable Business Size:** Solar is moving from being a "small business" to a "good sized segment."
    *   **Domestic Manufacturing Clause Impact:** Expectation that more domestic manufacturing clauses will appear in tender conditions in subsequent quarters.
    *   **Power Systems Outlook:** Fairly confident in seeing traction in the next coming quarters, with renewables growing, and T&D starting to grow.
    *   **Sustainable RM-to-Sales:** Expected to remain in the range of 66% to 68% depending on the product mix.
    *   **NCD Issuance:** INR 600 crores NCD is under process to replace high-cost debt with cheaper debt.
    *   **Gross Block/CAPEX:** No significant CAPEX for the last six months, consolidating CAPEX, more or less in line with the previous year's end.
    *   **Employee Count:** Over 6,000 people.
