## Executive Overview

ABB India delivered a strong Q2 2022, showcasing significant year-over-year growth. Orders surged by 64% and revenues by 44%, with Profit After Tax (PAT) increasing by 115%. This performance was driven by operational efficiencies, strong customer engagement, and effective price realization across most business segments. The company continues to leverage its diverse portfolio and customer-centric solutions, particularly in energy efficiency and digitalization. ABB India also reported strong progress on sustainability initiatives, including achieving its RE100 goal and advancing water positivity and waste management targets. Despite ongoing supply chain challenges and commodity price volatility, management expressed confidence in solid backlog execution and strategic market segment focus, while acknowledging potential future headwinds.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders (Q2 2022):** INR 2,767 crores, up 64% YoY.
*   **Revenue (Q2 2022):** INR 2,053 crores, up 44% YoY.
*   **Profitability (Q2 2022):**
    *   PBT: 9.5% (Target of minimum 10% to be caught up in coming quarters).
    *   PAT: Up 115% YoY (sequential quarter PAT impacted by turbocharger divestment).
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q2 2022):** ~INR 6,000 crores.
*   **Cash Balance (End of Q2 2022):** INR 2,800 crores (Total ~INR 3,100 crores with investments).
*   **Divisional Performance (Orders / Revenue / PBIT %):**
    *   **Electrification:** PBIT at 96 crores (11.5% - higher by 2% if Forex impact is added back).
    *   **Motion:** PBIT at 74 crores (9% - would be 13% if abnormal impacts are added back).
    *   **Process Automation:** Bouncing back with improved margins due to service revenue.
    *   **Robotics and Discrete Automation:** Prospects good, but deliveries impacted by semiconductor issues; expected to bounce back.
*   **Services Revenue (Q2 2022):** Growth in Process Automation contributing to margin expansion.
*   **Export Revenue (Q2 2022):** INR 246 crores, up 43% YoY (from INR 171 crores in Q2 2021).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Segment Focus:** Continued focus on 23 market segments and 20 divisions, driving penetration and product portfolio expansion. Growth observed across short-cycle, discrete, and process industries.
*   **Digitalization & Sustainability:** Emphasis on industrial digitalization and de-carbonization projects. Offering smart building and power equipment for renewable projects.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** Proven through successful implementation of drives for HVAC systems, delivering significant energy savings (e.g., 30% in Amanbagh project).
*   **Channel & Customer Connect:** Robust customer connect and geographical presence, including deeper penetration into tier 4 and 5 cities through an expanded partner network, driving momentum and price realization.
*   **Product Portfolio & Solutions:** Diverse, customer-centric solutions. Key projects include compact substations, power handling systems, traction converters, and continuous emission monitoring.
*   **Sustainability Initiatives:**
    *   Achieved RE100 goal (100% renewable energy) from Q1 2022.
    *   Moving towards water positive/neutral premises and single-use plastic-free premises.
    *   Committed to zero waste to landfill goal at manufacturing units.
    *   Waste recyclability at 96%.
    *   All factories certified as green factories (Platinum/Gold).
    *   Strong ESG performer rating by CRISIL.
    *   Social programs focusing on community development (scholarships, infrastructure upgrades).
*   **Robotics & Discrete Automation:** Pipeline and backlog are strong, with confidence in a market bounce back despite current semiconductor headwinds.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Order & Revenue Growth:** Management's narrative of robust growth was validated by a strong 64% order growth and 44% revenue growth YoY, significantly exceeding previous quarters' performance.
*   **Profitability:** Management aims to stabilize and consistently achieve double-digit PBT margins. The reported 9.5% PBT was slightly below the stated target, with management promising to catch up in upcoming quarters. The significant YoY PAT growth was noted as being influenced by the Q1 divestment. Operational EBITDA margins showed expansion, excluding one-time and mark-to-market impacts.
*   **Cash Position:** A strong and strengthening cash position was reiterated, with INR 2,800 crores on the balance sheet.
*   **Sustainability Initiatives:** Progress on sustainability was highlighted, with the RE100 goal achieved and further targets for water positivity and waste management actively being pursued.
*   **Material Cost & Supply Chain:** While price realization and operational efficiencies mitigated some impact, supply chain headwinds and commodity price volatility were explicitly mentioned as ongoing concerns impacting margins, particularly for Robotics and Discrete Automation. Management stated they are passing on price increases to customers.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Market Execution:** Expects continued demand and focus on flawless execution of the order backlog.
*   **Headwinds:** Acknowledges ongoing potential headwinds from commodity price volatility, inflation, interest rate hikes, and geopolitical impacts. Will monitor these closely.
*   **Margin Target:** Aim to stabilize and consistently achieve a PBT corridor of 10%.
*   **Cash Management:** Continue to consolidate and build cash position.
*   **Sustainability Timelines:**
    *   Achieve water positive/neutral premises.
    *   Achieve single-use plastic-free premises beyond compliance.
    *   Achieve zero waste to landfill goal at manufacturing units.
    *   Enhance waste recyclability (already at 96%).
    *   Green factory certifications for all manufacturing units (3 in 2022).
*   **Employee Cost:** Aim to maintain employee expenses within 8% to 9% of sales.
*   **Process Automation Business:** To be viewed over a three or four-quarter rolling average.
*   **Specific Capex Announcements:** New capacity investments are in progress at Maneja, Faridabad, Nelamangala, and Nashik plants; expect announcements in the next one to two quarters.
*   **Forex Hedging:** Policy remains to hedge all Forex exposures immediately to protect tendered margins, though mark-to-market impacts may appear in results. Conscious of the gap between exports and imports with a strengthening USD.
*   **Profitability Drivers:** Expects continued profitability ramp-up driven by increased factory utilization, strong backlog execution, and a higher value-add product mix.
*   **Unallocable Expenses:** Normalized run rate expected between 32-35 crores per quarter.
