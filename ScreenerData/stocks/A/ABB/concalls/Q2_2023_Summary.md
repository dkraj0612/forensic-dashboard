## Executive Overview

ABB India demonstrated a strong Q2 2023 performance, mirroring the positive market sentiment and robust demand seen in Q1. The company is benefiting from a manufacturing sector rebound and a customer preference shift towards reliable suppliers offering comprehensive solutions, with a growing emphasis on sustainability and energy efficiency. ABB India is experiencing broad-based demand across its 18 business lines and 23 market segments, with particular strength in robotics and automation. A robust order backlog of INR 7,700 crores provides excellent revenue visibility. The company continues to pursue both organic growth through productivity enhancements and inorganic opportunities via bolt-on acquisitions. Emerging market segments, identified previously, are now significant contributors, and ABB is actively identifying new growth areas for the next 1-3 years.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Order Inflow Growth (Q2 2023):** 10% YoY (against a high base). Cumulative half-year order growth of 22%.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q2 2023):** INR 7,729 crores, up 29% YoY.
*   **Revenue Growth (Q2 2023):** 22% YoY, reaching INR 2,500 crores quarterly.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) Growth (Q2 2023):** Over 100% YoY, reaching INR 296 crores.
*   **PAT Margin (Q2 2023):** 11.8% for the quarter, 11% for the half-year.
*   **Material Costs:** Maintained at 63.5% of revenue, aided by product mix and commodity price softening.
*   **Personnel Expenses:** INR 168 crores, reflecting annual increments.
*   **Exports:** Approximately 11-12% of revenue. Domestic demand is growing faster than export demand.
*   **Product vs. Services vs. Projects:** Products dominate offerings (77%), followed by services (13%) and projects (10%).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Diversification:** Expanding beyond "SUSTAIN" core segments into "ENHANCE" and "FOCUS" segments, now covering 23 market segments.
*   **Sectoral Focus:** Deep dive into the Food & Beverage segment, highlighting its growth potential and ABB's relevant product portfolio.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** Continued focus on energy-efficient products and solutions as a key market differentiator and customer value proposition.
*   **Sustainability:** Reduced GHG Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 85% (vs. 2019 baseline), with 2 plants water-positive.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Strong engagement across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 markets, including new customer acquisition.
*   **Productivity & Localization:** Investments in automation and robotics for shop floor productivity; continued localization efforts.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Actively exploring bolt-on acquisitions globally and locally to enhance customer propositions.
*   **Emerging Segments:** Strong growth in automotive (including EV), electronics, F&B, data centers, and process industries.
*   **Digitalization:** Initiative to digitize investor relations and meeting requests for a more user-friendly experience.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow Growth:** Management aimed for 12-15% growth. Q2 delivered 10% YoY against a high base, with cumulative half-year growth at a strong 22%. This aligns with stated objectives, acknowledging quarterly fluctuations and normalization.
*   **PAT Margin:** Management previously aimed for stabilized double-digit PBT and has consistently achieved this. The current target of 10-12% PAT for the next 4-5 quarters is reiterated, supported by a strong order backlog, demonstrating consistency.
*   **Material Cost Corridor:** Management's commitment to the 63.5%-64.5% corridor remains, achieved through product mix and judicious market selection.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Continued commitment to ESG as an ingrained practice was reiterated.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Order Inflow:** Expectation to maintain double-digit order intake, targeting 12-15% going forward, with deeper penetration in markets and geographies.
*   **PAT Margin:** Credible ambition to achieve and stabilize double-digit PAT (10-12%) for the next 4-5 quarters.
*   **Material Cost:** Aim to maintain material costs within the 63-64% corridor through a favorable product mix, commanding better prices, and judicious market selection.
*   **Employee Costs:** Target of 8.5-9% of revenue, with expected revenue growth of 12-15%.
*   **Manufacturing Sophistication:** Continued investment in domestic manufacturing, focusing on productivity arbitrage.
*   **Domestic Market Growth:** Anticipate domestic market growth to be stronger than exports, with exports oscillating around 12-15%.
*   **New Segments:** Expectation that emerging segments will continue to be significant contributors, with new segments contributing significantly in 1-3 years.
*   **Technology Adoption:** Emphasis on client solutions, particularly in robotics, and setting up customer experience centers.
*   **Ecosystem Development:** Focus on building ecosystems around manufacturing locations.
*   **Market Outlook:** Positive outlook with all 18 divisions and 23 market segments showing positive inclination. Anticipates 15%+ growth in new segments, 10-15% in mid-mature segments, and 6-8% in core segments.
*   **Energy Transition:** Significant demand driven by energy efficiency, EV, and renewable energy integration.
*   **Data Centers & City Power Distribution:** Identified as significant growth areas for ABB's medium voltage and ELDS portfolio.
*   **Building Segment Growth:** Expected to continue strong growth.
*   **Long-Term Profitability Drivers:** Shift towards product and Engineered-to-Order (ETO) business, productivity measures, and a strong service business.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Viewed as manageable, focusing on customer propositions where ABB's offering is highly valued.
*   **PLI Scheme Impact:** Indirect benefit, encouraging customer capacity expansion, driving demand for ABB's solutions.
*   **Railways:** ABB is participating in the market for propulsion systems for railways and metro projects.
*   **Supply Chain:** Eased out in most areas; no significant supply chain strains currently visible.
*   **Margin Expansion Levers:** Focus on intelligent management of capacities, costs, and offering to customers, maintaining credibility rather than solely chasing higher percentages.
*   **Direct Sales:** Increase in direct sales attributed to large orders and system orders, considered quarter-sensitive but indicative of strategic engagement.
*   **Exports:** Early stages of development as global teams adjust footprints, but domestic market growth is faster.
*   **Energy-Efficient Drives:** Significant growth potential in India due to demand for energy savings and carbon neutrality initiatives. ABB holds a large share in i3, i4 range of energy-efficient motors.
*   **Government Schemes (RDSS):** While not directly involved in smart metering, there is a good opportunity in the medium voltage breaker side for the modernization of the distribution network (11 kV and 33 kV).
