## Executive Overview

ABB India commenced calendar year 2025 with a Q1 performance characterized by modest growth in orders, revenues, and profit after tax, all registering around 3-4% year-on-year. This follows a period of robust growth in FY24, with Q1 FY25 orders bolstered by a significant INR 200 crore large order, alongside a healthy 10% rise in base orders. The order backlog remains strong, up 11% year-on-year to INR 9,958 crores, providing good revenue visibility for the fiscal year, with an estimated two-thirds to be executed in the coming quarters. Profitability was supported by a favorable revenue mix, operational efficiencies, and a positive Forex impact, although operational EBITDA was slightly impacted by lower volumes compared to a strong Q4 FY24. The company continues to emphasize its diversified business model across four key areas: Electrification, Motion, Process Automation, and Robotics, with a continued focus on India's growth drivers, sustainability, and expanding its local manufacturing footprint.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Operational Structure:** Operates across four business areas: Electrification (distributed solutions, installation products), Motion (drives, motors, generators, traction, service), Process Automation (energy & process industries, marine, ports, measurement, analytics), and Robotics & Discrete Automation (automotive, electronics, manufacturing).
*   **Manufacturing & Reach:** 75 years of manufacturing in India with five plants, 25 shop floors, 28 sales offices, and over 750 partners for deeper market penetration.
*   **Product Portfolio Expansion & Localization:** Continuous expansion and localization of product portfolios to meet market demand and competitive pricing, with specific mention of low voltage flameproof motors for higher frame sizes (IE2, IE3, IE4 efficiency) and modular LIORA switches for smart buildings.
*   **Sustainability Focus:** Reduced Scope 1 & 2 GHG emissions by 87% vs. 2019 baseline. Nelamangala campus achieved "Mission to Zero" status. Recognized for Sustainability and CSR practices.
*   **Market Segmentation:** Operates across 23 market segments, balancing high-growth, moderate-growth, and low-growth segments to maintain resilience. Emphasizes deep customer engagement.
*   **Emerging Segments:** Continues to identify and engage with emerging segments like Data Centers, Transport (Railways, Metros), Renewable Energy, and Food & Beverage.
*   **Deep Dive - Cement Industry:** Highlighting opportunities in India's large cement sector with solutions for energy efficiency and process automation.
*   **Tier 2/3 City Penetration:** Continued efforts to reach Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, showing substantial improvement in order inflow from these regions.
*   **Exports:** Growing traction in export markets, leveraging world-class facilities and productivity.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** While Q1 FY25 total orders showed a modest 4% YoY growth, this was supported by a large INR 200 crore order, which was anticipated to move from Q4 FY24. Crucially, base order growth was a healthy 10%, indicating underlying demand strength despite macroeconomic uncertainties. This aligns with the previous year's narrative of managing order fluctuations due to large project cycles.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved 3% YoY revenue growth, which is lower than the robust FY24 performance. Management attributes this to customer preference for scheduling supplies based on market volatility and a slightly lower revenue base compared to the strong Q4 FY24. This is framed as a consequence of market conditions rather than an inability to execute.
*   **Profitability:** Maintained a strong PBT margin of 20% and stable operational EBITDA of 16.4%. Favorable revenue mix, operational efficiencies, and Forex impact are cited as key drivers, consistent with previous performance drivers. The slightly lower operational EBITDA compared to Q1 FY24 is directly linked to lower volumes, reinforcing the impact of operating leverage.
*   **Order Backlog:** The order backlog continues to grow robustly (11% YoY), providing good visibility and supporting the narrative of future revenue execution.
*   **Sustainability:** Continued to demonstrate progress on sustainability goals, including GHG emission reduction and achieving "Mission to Zero" status, reinforcing past commitments.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Market Outlook:** Management expresses optimism about the underlying Indian economy's spending capacity, anticipating a pickup once current uncertainties clear. They are focusing on opportunities rather than macroeconomic concerns.
*   **Order Inflow Guidance:** While not providing specific numbers for the full year due to market uncertainties (Amban situation mentioned), the intent remains to aim for double-digit growth in base orders. The speed of recovery in Q2 and Q3 will be key indicators.
*   **Revenue Execution:** Two-thirds of the current order backlog is expected to be executed in the coming quarters, with the balance carrying over to the next year.
*   **Profitability Corridor:** The PAT margin target of 12% to 15% remains, supported by good priced orders secured previously and capacity leverage.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Plans include expanding capacities organically, increasing dividends to shareholders, and evaluating inorganic opportunities. There is no rush, with a focus on the right opportunities at the right price.
*   **Process Automation:** This segment is dependent on large CAPEX decisions from private and government sectors. While large projects are currently facing sluggish decision-making due to global and domestic uncertainties, these projects remain in the pipeline, and recovery is anticipated as market confidence improves. The base order business for Process Automation continues to perform well.
*   **Motor Segment (IE3/IE4):** Management declined to provide specific market share or CY23 penetration numbers, deeming them highly sensitive business information.
*   **Electrification Segment:** Strong order inflows (71% growth YoY in Q1'25 compared to Q4'24) and a significant increase in order backlog (36%) provide good visibility and support profitability.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Plans for capacity expansion are being considered in Process Automation, Electrification, and Motion segments, particularly in units whose volumes have grown significantly and require larger space or expanded localization capabilities. Specific announcements will be made when ready.
*   **Service Business:** Aims to increase the service mix to 15% from the current 12-13%. Exports are also a focus, with a healthy 40% YoY growth in Q1'25 orders.
*   **Sophistication of Products & Margins:** Higher sophistication drives margins in faster-growing, technology-differentiating segments. Moderate growth segments show a mix of value-based pricing and competitive tendering. High-volume, low-growth segments derive margin accretion from capacity leverage and delivery efficiency. A balance across these segments is key to maintaining the 12-15% PAT margin.
*   **Global Trade Friction:** While global trade tensions exist, India's trade system appears net positive. Export orders have seen strong growth, and no dampening effect is currently observed. Resolving global conflicts could positively impact the broader economic environment.
