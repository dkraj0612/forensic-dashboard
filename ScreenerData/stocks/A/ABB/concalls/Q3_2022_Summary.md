## Executive Overview

ABB India demonstrated another quarter of robust performance in Q3 2022, maintaining positive momentum in orders, revenue, and profitability. The company reported strong order inflows, with backlog reaching an all-time high of INR 6,500 crore, providing significant future revenue visibility. Profitability saw improvement, with PBT reaching 10.8% (accumulated PBT at 10.1%), driven by increased revenue and volume/mix expansions. The company continues to emphasize its ESG initiatives, with four of its six units now Green Factory certified and all campuses running on 100% renewable energy. ABB is also seeing strong performance in key growth segments like data centers and is committed to further localization of products. Management expressed confidence in continued momentum, strong backlog execution, and controlled expenses, while acknowledging ongoing supply chain challenges, particularly in Robotics and Discrete Automation, and potential impacts from global commodity and forex volatility.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders (Q3 2022):** INR 2,634 crore (primarily base orders, no exceptionally large orders).
*   **Revenue (Q3 2022):** Up 19% YoY.
*   **Profitability (Q3 2022):**
    *   PBT: 10.8%.
    *   PBT (before exceptional items): Accumulated PBT reached 10.1%.
    *   PAT Margin: 9.6% (includes a one-time gain of INR 45 crore from turbo business divestment).
    *   Operational EBITDA: 12% (compared to 11% sequentially and 9.4% in Q3 FY21).
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q3 2022):** INR 6,500 crore (all-time high).
*   **Cash Balance (End of Q3 2022):** INR 3,200 crore.
*   **Material Cost:** 65.2% (compared to 63.8% in Q2 2022 and 65.5% in Q3 2021), attributed to revenue mix.
*   **Other Income:** Increased due to higher cash reserves and interest accrual (INR 10-12 crore) and a one-time tax refund (INR 3-4 crore).
*   **Divisional Performance Highlights:**
    *   **Electrification:** Consistent order intake of over INR 900 crore for the third consecutive quarter, 36% YoY growth, 18% backlog growth, improving profitability due to volumes, capacity, and price realization.
    *   **Motion:** Strong order backlog, including major orders for system drives and traction converters with longer delivery durations. Profitability remains strong but impacted by Forex and commodities.
    *   **Process Automation:** Consistently growing with a significant increase in order backlog (INR 1,400 crore to INR 2,500 crore), reflecting core sector investments. Turnaround in market presence and profitability.
    *   **Robotics & Discrete Automation:** Fast-growing but facing execution headwinds from supply chain disruptions (semiconductor issues).
*   **Revenue by Offering:** Project and Services ~23%; Product business increasing.
*   **Export Revenue:** 12% to 15% of orders.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Focus & Diversification:** Continued strength across traditional and emerging sectors, with a specific deep dive into the data center market. ABB's diverse portfolio across 20 market segments provides stability.
*   **Data Center Growth:** India's data center market is identified as a significant growth opportunity (projected 22% CAGR, ~$1 billion opportunity), driven by global players, 5G, data protection laws, and cloud adoption. ABB's global presence and high-quality offerings provide a competitive edge.
*   **ESG & Sustainability:** Significant progress on ESG strategy:
    *   4 out of 6 units are Green Factory certified.
    *   All campuses run on 100% renewable energy.
    *   Focus on Green Factory Buildings, Green Manufacturing Processes, and Green Products.
    *   CRISIL and IGBC recognition for water management initiatives.
    *   Plans to involve suppliers in green initiatives.
    *   Diversity and inclusion focus.
*   **Capacity Expansion & Localization:** Capacity expansion for smart products factory focused on domestic growth, with potential for global supply network leverage. Ongoing expansion plans, domestic and foreign, are underway. Active efforts to localize products not previously manufactured in India to meet sophisticated domestic demand for high-quality products.
*   **Green Energy Offerings:** ABB's products and solutions inherently drive energy efficiency for customers. Its own green practices and products serve as a "showcase" to customers, driving adoption of energy-efficient solutions and reducing customer GHG emissions.
*   **Channel & Customer Engagement:** Deeper penetration into Tier 2/3 cities and enhanced customer engagement are driving growth.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Order Inflows & Backlog:** Management's narrative of strong order inflows is validated by booking INR 2,634 crore, primarily from base orders, and achieving an all-time high backlog of INR 6,500 crore. This aligns with the positive momentum described.
*   **Profitability:** Management's target of achieving double-digit PBT margins has been met on an accumulated basis (10.1%) and in the current quarter (10.8% PBT). The PAT margin (9.6%) was impacted by a one-time gain, but the underlying operational performance and PBT trajectory suggest a move towards consistent double-digit PAT in the future. The narrative around consistent margin improvement and operational EBITDA expansion remains consistent.
*   **Cash Position:** The strong cash reserves of INR 3,200 crore were reiterated, consistent with previous periods.
*   **Sustainability Initiatives:** Management continues to drive and highlight progress on its ESG agenda, with specific achievements like 100% renewable energy adoption and Green Factory certifications. The strategy of focusing on Green Factory Buildings, Manufacturing Processes, and Products remains central.
*   **Supply Chain & Commodity Impact:** Management acknowledges ongoing supply chain disruptions, particularly for Robotics and Discrete Automation due to semiconductor issues. While price realization and operational efficiencies have mitigated impacts, the transcript notes that commodity prices are stabilizing, and the company will monitor demand for pricing trends.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Profitability Trajectory:** Aim to stabilize and remain steady in double-digit PBT margins, with the eventual goal of achieving double-digit PAT.
*   **Capacity Management:** Priority is to optimize existing facilities through automation before embarking on new brick-and-mortar expansions. New capacity expansions are underway and will be announced.
*   **Domestic Market Focus:** Primary focus for Indian capacity expansion is to serve the domestic market, with global divisions leveraging Indian footprint for exports.
*   **Data Center Opportunity:** ABB expects to leverage its global relationships and local capabilities to secure significant share in the growing Indian data center market, particularly serving customers demanding high reliability and availability.
*   **Green Initiatives Integration:** Continued focus on leveraging ABB's own green practices and products as a showcase for customers. Phase 1 and Phase 2 will focus on energy efficiency solutions for customers.
*   **Process Automation Business:** To be viewed over a three or four-quarter rolling average, reflecting project milestones.
*   **New Product Localization:** Expect announcements regarding the localization of new products not previously manufactured in India.
*   **Inorganic Opportunities:** Management is exploring inorganic opportunities, and any decisions will be announced.
*   **Future Outlook:** Management expressed confidence in continued momentum, with expectations of a strong domestic market, especially in low-voltage products for railways and data centers. They do not foresee capacity as a constraint due to multi-year planning and sub-supplier engagement.
*   **Q4 2022 and FY2022:** Management anticipates more interesting year-end results and will provide updates in February.
