## Executive Overview

ABB India reported a landmark year in 2016, driven by a substantial 54% year-on-year order growth, reaching INR 12,466 crores. This surge was primarily fueled by high-technology orders, reflecting India's progress in power, modern industries, and transportation. Operational excellence initiatives contributed to sustainable profitability improvements. The company remains optimistic about India's potential as a fast-growing economy, leveraging its diversified portfolio to tap into emerging sectors like renewables, data centers, food and beverage, and railways. Q4 2016 saw robust order growth in base orders and significant revenue increases in services (25%) and exports. Key strategic wins included the UHVDC contract (RB800) for the Raigarh-Pugalur transmission link and the launch of a containerized micro-grid solution, with ABB India positioned as a global center of excellence. The company also noted continued investment in digitalization and new technologies like IoT. While market conditions presented challenges, including short-term impacts from demonetization, ABB India demonstrated resilience and a strong cash position, ending the year debt-free with INR 600 crores in net cash.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Order Intake:**
    *   Full Year 2016: INR 12,466 crores, a 54% increase YoY.
    *   Q4 2016: INR 5,628 crores, a 170% increase YoY (largely due to large orders).
    *   Base Orders (excl. large orders) in Q4 2016: Flat but positive, with a short-term impact from demonetization.
    *   Exports: 15-16% of sales for the year.
    *   Services: Revenue grew 20% YoY, contributing 13% of revenue.
*   **Revenue:**
    *   Full Year 2016: INR 8,648 crores, a 6% increase YoY.
    *   Q4 2016: INR 2,492 crores, a 3% increase YoY.
*   **Profitability:**
    *   Profit Before Tax (PBT) - Full Year 2016: INR 576 crores, a 21% increase YoY.
    *   PBT Margin - Full Year 2016: 6.7% (improved from 2.7% in 2012).
    *   PBT - Q4 2016: INR 224 crores, a 7% increase YoY.
    *   PBT Margin - Q4 2016: 9% (improved from 1.3% in Q4 2012).
    *   Profit After Tax (PAT) - Full Year 2016: INR 3,076 crores, a 25% increase YoY.
    *   PAT Margin - Full Year 2016: 4.3% (improved from 1.8% in 2012).
    *   PAT - Q4 2016: INR 147 crores, a 13% increase YoY.
    *   PAT Margin - Q4 2016: 5.9% (improved from 1% in Q4 2012).
    *   Segmental Performance (Q4 2016):
        *   DM: Revenue up, substantial profitability improvement.
        *   EP: Revenue and profits grown.
        *   PA: Sluggish revenue but better profitability.
        *   PG: Flat revenue, influenced by conservative accounting for new standards.
*   **Order Backlog:** INR 11,821 crores, up from INR 7,946 crores last year.
*   **Cash Flow & Balance Sheet:**
    *   Cash from Operations: INR 1,072 crores, a significant improvement from INR 578 crores last year.
    *   Net Cash: Approximately INR 600 crores.
    *   Debt-Free.
*   **Expenses:**
    *   Material Cost: Stable at ~65%.
    *   Personnel Expenses: 8.9% for the full year (down from 9.2% last year).
    *   Other Expenses: In line with last year.
*   **Net Working Capital:** DSO improved to ~130 days from ~150 days in 2015.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **High-Technology Orders Focus:** Prioritizing orders with advanced technology to align with India's development in power, industry, and infrastructure.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Continued initiatives for operational efficiency contributing to sustainable profitability.
*   **Leveraging Emerging Sectors:** Capitalizing on growth in renewables, data centers, food & beverage, and railways.
*   **Export Hub Development:** Utilizing ABB India as a base for exports, with specific focus on contractors working in emerging markets and direct exports from feeder factories.
*   **Digitalization and IoT:** Leading in digitalization solutions, with pilots in IoT (IoTSP), motor sensors, and integrated control rooms.
*   **New Product Introductions:** Launching innovative solutions like containerized micro-grids (ABB India as global COE) and compact substations.
*   **Infrastructure & Transportation Focus:** Significant participation in large infrastructure projects (e.g., HVDC link) and modernization of railways, ports, and urban mobility.
*   **Service Business Growth:** Re-energizing service offerings to drive profitability and customer engagement.
*   **Cost Management:** Maintaining stable material costs and optimizing personnel and other expenses.
*   **Cash Over Revenue Mantra:** Disciplined execution leading to strong cash generation and a debt-free position.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Order Growth:** Management achieved a landmark 54% order growth for the full year, significantly exceeding previous expectations and marking a significant turnaround from flatter growth in prior periods. The narrative around "high technology" orders was consistently emphasized.
    *   **Profitability Improvement:** The consistent double-digit growth in PBT and PAT (21% and 25% YoY respectively for the full year) validates management's focus on operational excellence and profitability. The improvement in PBT margins from 2.7% to 6.7% over five years (2012-2016) demonstrates sustained progress.
    *   **Cash Flow:** The reported phenomenal cash improvement and a net cash of INR 600 crores strongly align with the "cash over revenue" mantra and previous emphasis on disciplined execution.
    *   **Services Growth:** The 20% YoY growth in services and its increased contribution to revenue (13%) align with management's stated focus and re-energizing efforts in this segment.
    *   **Exports:** The reported 15-16% contribution of exports to sales indicates continued focus and execution, with management highlighting new areas of growth.
    *   **Market Narrative:** Management continued to acknowledge pockets of concern but maintained an overall positive outlook on India's economy and opportunities, citing government actions and investments in emerging sectors. The short-term impact of demonetization was acknowledged.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **HVDC Order Execution:** The Raigarh-Pugalur HVDC contract (INR 3,600 crores for ABB India's scope) has an estimated 36-month execution period. Management stated that the bidding models incorporate strong risk and return estimations, and the contract was signed in early January, mitigating the impact of past delays on profitability. Margins are expected to be in line with ABB's bandwidth.
    *   **Power Grids (PG) Segment Growth:** Management expects PG to perform decently and to make efforts for a "decent performance as what we had in 2015," acknowledging that large projects have long gestation periods. They do not foresee significant headwinds on current backlog execution.
    *   **Uday Scheme Traction:** While acknowledging the scheme's intent to improve utility balance sheets, management sees opportunities primarily in digitalization and new technology content rather than traditional hard infrastructure investment. Incremental orders continue to be received.
    *   **Cash Utilization & CAPEX:** ABB India has a debt-free balance sheet with ~INR 600 crores in net cash. This cash will be used for working capital, planned CAPEX (~$100 million annually, subject to market pickup), and repayment of the NCB structure maturing in Q3 2018. Buybacks are not currently planned.
    *   **Railways Segment:** This segment currently exceeds 5% of sales. Management sees substantial potential due to ongoing investments in metros and Indian Railways, with opportunities across turbochargers, propulsion converters, transformers, and electrification through the Power Grid division. They are also participating in traction motor supplies.
    *   **Port Modernization:** Significant interest is seen in port automation, electrification, and onshore power solutions for ships, with pilot projects underway. Management anticipates a wave of adoption following initial implementations.
    *   **Renewable Contribution:** Renewables continue to be strong, contributing 15-16% of revenue, with wind being particularly strong in 2016. Capacity for solar inverters was doubled, and micro-grid solutions are seen as a growing area for both domestic and export markets.
    *   **Gross Margins:** Management is satisfied with the current gross margin position and expects material costs to remain stable, aiming for the 64%-66% range observed over the last 8-12 quarters. The proportion of projects in the mix will increase with the HVDC order.
    *   **Order Inflows by Segment:** Detailed segment-wise order inflows for the full year were not provided in the call but will be available in the Annual Report.
