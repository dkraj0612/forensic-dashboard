## Executive Overview

ABB India kicked off 2023 with a robust Q1 performance, reporting its highest-ever order growth in a first quarter over the last five years, with orders up 36% and revenues up 22%. This strong start was driven by consistent growth across most business areas, particularly in short-cycle businesses, reflecting broad-based demand from Tier-I to Tier-IV cities. The company highlighted significant growth in its services segment (orders up 37%, revenues up 33%) and strong traction in the automotive (including EV) and electronics sectors. Management expressed confidence in the company's robust order backlog, which grew 37% year-on-year to INR 7,170 crore, providing good revenue and margin visibility for future execution. ABB also continued its commitment to sustainability, maintaining 100% renewable energy utilization and further reducing CO2 emissions.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders (Q1 2023):** Up 36% YoY, highest ever Q1 order growth in 5 years.
*   **Revenue (Q1 2023):** Up 22% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q1 2023):** Improved to 11.4%, up 290 basis points YoY.
*   **PBT (before exceptional items) (Q1 2023):** Up 66% YoY.
*   **PAT (like-to-like) (Q1 2023):** Up 65% YoY.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q1 2023):** INR 7,170 crores, up 37% YoY.
*   **Cash Balance (End of Q1 2023):** Approximately INR 4,000 crores (INR 3,942 crores).
*   **Material Costs:** Holding stable between 63.5% - 64.5%.
*   **Services Revenue:** Up 33% YoY.
*   **Other Expenses:** Increased to INR 402 crores from INR 313 crores, primarily due to higher volumes and increased travel expenses.
*   **Exceptional Provision (Q1 2023):** INR 23 crores for a traction motor issue.
*   **Foreign Currency Expenditure (2022):** Increased to INR 4,460 crores from INR 3,060 crores in 2021, driven by imports for backlog execution from third-party vendors.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Penetration:** Continued focus on short-cycle businesses and expansion across Tier-I to Tier-IV cities.
*   **Sectoral Growth:** Leveraging growth in automotive (especially EV), electronics, metals, cement, oil & gas, and transport (railways and metro).
*   **Services Expansion:** Elevating the services business, aiming for significant growth in its contribution.
*   **Electronics Theme:** Capitalizing on the projected 30% CAGR growth of the Indian electronics market, supporting new capacities in high-end cellphones to industrial electronics.
*   **Sustainability:** Maintaining 100% renewable energy usage, progress in waste and water recyclability, and focus on CSR in education, diversity, inclusion, communities, and environment.
*   **Order Backlog Conversion:** Focus on planned execution of the strong order backlog to drive revenue and margin growth.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Optimizing existing plants through automation and potentially increasing shifts, with a distributed capacity utilization across 25 manufacturing plants.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Actively exploring M&A opportunities for expansion, with a pipeline for both global and local targets.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Order Growth:** Management's narrative of strong order inflows is validated by the 36% YoY growth in Q1 2023, which they highlighted as the highest ever for a Q1 in five years.
*   **Revenue Growth:** The 22% YoY revenue growth in Q1 2023 aligns with the positive trajectory expected from the strong order backlog.
*   **Profitability:** EBITDA margin improvement to 11.4% and PBT growth of 66% demonstrate progress. The stated goal of achieving 10%+ PAT is being pursued, with Q1 showing a strong like-to-like PAT growth of 65%. Management acknowledged a sequential dip in profitability due to a provision for a traction motor issue and favorable forex in the previous quarter.
*   **Cash Position:** The cash balance of approximately INR 4,000 crores reinforces management's ability to fund expansions and pursue acquisitions.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Continued commitment and progress on 100% renewable energy, CO2 emission reduction, and waste/water management, aligning with previous communications.
*   **Market Segments:** The broad-based growth across multiple segments (Tier-I to Tier-IV cities, automotive, electronics, transport) aligns with management's stated strategy of diversified market penetration.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Order Inflow Growth:** Management expects a positive trajectory and aims for a 4-quarter rolling average growth of 12-15%, while acknowledging that higher growth (20-30%) depends on customer order patterns and project funding.
*   **PAT Margin:** Management aims to maintain PAT margins of 10%+ for the next few quarters.
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Management indicated that margins depend on the mix and industry cater to, but expects the backlog to sustain consistent margin earning capacity. They believe margins can be maintained by converting the backlog with good capacity utilization.
*   **Inorganic Acquisitions:** Management is actively pursuing inorganic expansion opportunities with a pipeline of global and local targets, but emphasized that these deals take time to materialize. No specific timeline was provided.
*   **Electronics Market:** ABB is ready with its product offerings to support the high-growth Indian electronics market and is eagerly awaiting the materialization of investments in this segment.
*   **Services Business:** While services revenue grew 33%, management acknowledged the percentage contribution might appear muted due to faster growth in product businesses but expects it to increase over time. They clarified that services are 12-13% of revenue, not 6-8%.
*   **Supply Chain:** While easing compared to previous periods, management remains cautious and maintains high inventory levels due to specific component handling pressures.
*   **Process Automation (PA):** Despite sequential decline in order flows, management highlighted a strong pipeline for project orders in petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, city gas distribution, and PSUs like ONGC, Indian Oil, Oil India, and NTPC.
*   **New Energy/Decarbonization:** ABB's core portfolio addresses energy efficiency, and they see opportunities in EV, energy-efficient motors (IE3 and higher), building management systems, and robotics/automation for EV manufacturing.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Management views competitive intensity as a continuous, lifelong factor that varies across product lines and market segments. Their preparedness over the last 3-4 years provides them with current competitiveness.
*   **Tier-II/III/IV City Growth:** Management sees strong aspiration for high-quality products in these cities, driven by OEM suppliers to top-tier end-users and evolving real estate standards.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Capacity utilization is distributed across 25 plants, with some running single shift and others two shifts, with room for further increases through additional shifts and automation.
*   **Focus Areas (High-Growth Segments):** Management declined to share specific market share or order backlog percentage for focus areas due to competitive sensitivities.
*   **Commodity Prices:** Management indicated that pricing is determined by inflation and supply-demand dynamics, with fair competition in the market. They are not the sole price determiners.
