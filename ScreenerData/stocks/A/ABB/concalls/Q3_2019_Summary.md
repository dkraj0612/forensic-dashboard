## Executive Overview

ABB India reported a "resilient and consistent performance" for Q3 2019, despite mixed macroeconomic signals and subdued demand in capital goods. The company demonstrated growth in orders (5% YoY) and revenues (17% YoY) for the quarter, with year-to-date (9-month) figures showing 11% growth in orders and 13% in revenues. Profitability saw improvement, with PBT and PAT showing positive trends compared to previous periods. Management attributes this performance to a diversified order book, strong execution by business teams, improved product mix, and operational efficiencies. Key growth drivers highlighted include the Motion and Electrification divisions, while Industrial Automation and Robotics faced headwinds due to sector-specific downturns. The company is actively pursuing customer connect programs in tier-2/tier-3 cities and leveraging digital tools for sales and service. The ongoing transformation, including the PG demerger, continues to be a strategic focus.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Diversified Portfolio & Market Penetration:** Leveraging a diversified order book across multiple product lines and segments to mitigate macroeconomic vagaries. Focus on market share expansion in under-penetrated areas and new geographies.
*   **Digitalization & Smart Technology:** Continued emphasis on digital solutions for customers. Motion division's connected services enable remote monitoring and faster fault resolution, improving customer productivity and reliability.
*   **Sectoral Focus & Growth Areas:**
    *   **Electrification:** Growth driven by data centers, buildings, power distribution, and metro projects.
    *   **Motion:** Strong momentum in product and service business, with good penetration in tier-2/tier-3 cities. Transportation sector, especially traction equipment, remains a key growth driver.
    *   **Industrial Automation (IA):** Facing headwinds due to subdued core sector capex, but focusing on Opex spending and digitalization initiatives. Gaining traction in pharma, steel, and thermal power plants.
    *   **Robotics & Discrete Automation:** Robotics experiencing impact from the automobile segment downturn, but actively diversifying into non-automobile sectors. Long-term investment planned for robotics.
    *   **Transportation:** Significant growth driver, with railways and metros being key areas. Contribution to total business profile around 7-8% of orders/revenues.
*   **Service Business Expansion:** Growing importance of services, contributing 18% of orders/revenues, supporting profitability and customer connect. Install base penetration has increased to over 30%.
*   **Export Focus:** Continuing to expand the export business by leveraging India's competitive supply base and meeting global standards.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Implementing customer connect programs in tier-2/tier-3 cities and utilizing web marketing for specialized customer interactions.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Resilient and Consistent Performance:** Management's narrative of a "consistent performance" is supported by 5% order growth and 17% revenue growth in Q3 YoY, along with positive year-to-date trends. This aligns with the previous quarter's emphasis on resilience.
*   **Revenue and Order Growth:** The reported revenue growth of 17% YoY and 11% YoY for orders (9-month) demonstrates continued momentum, consistent with management's focus on drawing reasonable growth through market share and new segment expansion.
*   **Profitability Improvement:** While specific PBT/PAT figures weren't detailed for Q3, the transcript indicates "margins and profitability saw a very good growth compared to the previous quarters" and improved Operational EBITA margins over FY17. This addresses the prior emphasis on improving profitability through product mix and production efficiency.
*   **Digitalization Drive:** The proactive use of connected services in the Motion division and continued customer engagement programs align with the ongoing digitalization strategy.
*   **Exports and Services Growth:** The increase in services to 18% of orders/revenues and continued emphasis on export expansion are consistent with previous drivers of accelerated growth.
*   **PG Demerger:** Mentioned as a "discontinued business" in the context of reporting Power Grid performance, indicating its separation is proceeding.
*   **Market Conditions & Headwinds:** Management acknowledges the "mixed" market, "subdued" demand for capital goods, and specific sector downturns (automobiles for Robotics, core sectors for IA). However, the company's ability to achieve growth through diversification and focus on resilient segments demonstrates adaptability and effective navigation of these challenges, as opposed to simply stating the headwinds. The tone is not one of succumbing to headwinds but of strategizing around them.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Future Business Prospects:** "The best of ABB portfolio in India is yet to come."
*   **Growth Drivers:** Continued focus on Electrification and Motion as major divisions post-PG demerger, expected to constitute 65-68% of orders/revenues. Industrial Automation to represent around 20%.
*   **Robotics Investment:** New investments planned in the robotics area for the upcoming year, aimed at long-term positioning.
*   **Export Market Expansion:** Gradual and structured opening of new export markets, with increasing product lines becoming available for export.
*   **Transportation Sector:** Continued growth expected, with further potential driven by metro expansion and government policies. Contribution to total business profile is 7-8% of orders/revenues.
*   **Service Business Growth:** Long-term potential for service business expansion, aiming to reach global best-in-class penetration rates of 65-70% from the current 30-32%.
*   **Margin Profile:** Focus on improving margins in Industrial Automation and Robotics as utilization rates increase and export mix diversifies. The gap in Motion division margins (vs. parent) is expected to bridge as export profile increases and pricing becomes more globally competitive.
*   **Tax Rate Impact:** The effective tax rate of 30% is due to the reevaluation of deferred tax assets over two quarters, impacting the financial year ending December 2019.
*   **FDI and Manufacturing Footprint:** The recent tax rate reduction is expected to make India a more competitive location for FDI, which will be factored into global corporate budgeting. This could potentially lead to an increase in manufacturing footprint in India, contingent on demand pickup.
*   **Demand Side Precondition:** For significant FDI in manufacturing for domestic consumption, the demand side in India must pick up to provide confidence for more investments.
*   **Sustainability of Growth:** Management believes the current order backlog provides good visibility for future revenues, especially in Electrification and Motion divisions. They anticipate a slow and steady bridging of the gap in Industrial Automation as market conditions improve.
