## Executive Overview

ABB India's Q2 2017 results show a significant uptick in both orders and revenues, a positive shift from the steady revenues in the previous quarter. Order intake grew by 8% quarter-on-quarter to ₹2,040 crores, and consolidated orders for H1 2017 rose by 3% to ₹3,870 crores. Revenue saw a robust 9% increase year-on-year to ₹2,102 crores in Q2, driven by a strong execution of technology-driven, short-cycle orders and improved cash flow. Profitability also improved, with Profit Before Tax (PBT) up 36% and Profit After Tax (PAT) up 35% year-on-year for the quarter. Operational EBITA grew 11% for H1. The company is leveraging government investments in railways, transmission and distribution, and renewables, while also seeing growth in exports and services. Traditional industrial segments remain sluggish, with management noting consolidation and a wait-and-watch approach for private sector CAPEX. The company is actively developing its offerings in high-end technologies like GIS, HVDC, and VAMS, and is positioning itself to benefit from the burgeoning EV charging infrastructure and the Internet of Things (IoT) through its "Internet of Things, Services and People" (IoTSP) strategy. The order backlog stands at ₹7,752 crores, providing good revenue visibility.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Order Intake (Q2 2017):** ₹2,040 crores (8% Q-o-Q growth).
*   **Order Intake (H1 2017):** ₹3,870 crores (3% Y-o-Y growth).
*   **Revenue (Q2 2017):** ₹2,102 crores (9% Y-o-Y growth).
*   **Revenue (H1 2017):** ₹4,102 crores (9% Y-o-Y growth).
*   **Profit Before Tax (Q2 2017):** ₹121 crores (36% Y-o-Y growth).
*   **Profit After Tax (Q2 2017):** ₹77 crores (35% Y-o-Y growth).
*   **Operational EBITA (Q2 2017):** ₹148 crores.
*   **Operational EBITA (H1 2017):** ₹255 crores (11% Y-o-Y growth).
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q2 2017):** ₹7,752 crores.
*   **Services Growth (Q2 2017):** Up 32% Y-o-Y.
*   **Exports Contribution:** Orders at 14-15%, Revenues at 16-17%.
*   **Renewables Revenue Contribution:** Approximately 15% of overall revenues.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Sector Focus:** Continued emphasis on government-led sectors like railways, transmission & distribution, and renewables.
*   **Technology Adoption:** Increasing market adoption of high-end technologies such as Gas Insulated Switchgears (GIS), High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC), VAMS, and Statcon for better power quality.
*   **New Markets & Products:** Actively pursuing export opportunities, particularly in Africa, with an order for hybrid switchgear. Launching new generation Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCCBs) like Tmax XT.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Focus on revenue growth through short-cycle orders, relentless pursuit of cash, timely project execution, and streamlining operations.
*   **Digitalization & IoT:** Significant focus on IoTSP (Internet of Things, Services and People), leveraging the large installed base for data analysis, productivity enhancements, and new service offerings across all divisions.
*   **EV Ecosystem:** Engaging with OEMs for EV charging network development and co-creation of software services.
*   **Services Growth:** Continued strong performance driven by lifecycle extension, debottlenecking, upgrades, and retrofits on the existing installed base, augmented by new value-add solutions.
*   **Export Growth:** Continued efforts to expand export business, with a strong pipeline in traditional markets like Bangladesh and Sri Lanka.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Order Growth:** The Q2 result of 8% Q-o-Q order growth is a positive step after a mixed Q1, aligning with the emphasis on base orders. H1 growth of 3% is slower than the previous year, but the current quarter's performance shows renewed momentum.
    *   **Revenue Growth:** The 9% Y-o-Y revenue growth in Q2 is a significant improvement from revenues being "held steady" in the previous comparable period, indicating better execution and absorption of the earlier GST disruptions.
    *   **Profitability:** PBT and PAT saw strong Y-o-Y growth (36% and 35% respectively), indicating improved operational performance and potentially better project execution margins compared to the previous year. Operational EBITA also showed an 11% growth for H1.
    *   **Services Growth:** The 32% Y-o-Y growth in services is consistent with management's stated focus and the previous quarter's strong performance, highlighting its importance as a revenue driver.
    *   **Exports Growth:** The 14-15% order contribution and 16-17% revenue contribution from exports show continued progress in this strategic area, aligning with the narrative of leveraging India as an export hub.
    *   **Market Narrative:** Management acknowledges the sluggishness in traditional industrial segments and private sector CAPEX due to NPAs and excess capacity, a consistent observation. However, the tone has shifted to more optimism regarding government-led initiatives and improved order execution, particularly in the current quarter.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Revenue Outlook:** Management expects continued revenue growth driven by government spending, renewables, and a robust order backlog. While no specific percentage guidance was given, the focus is on leveraging opportunities.
    *   **GST Impact:** Initial customer caution from June has seemingly subsided, and the company is seeing order growth, suggesting stabilization. The medium-term view on GST remains positive.
    *   **ECL Provisions:** The Q2 transcript does not mention specific ECL provisions, but the historical context noted that IndAS impact, including ECL provisions, was expected to continue for a couple of years (2016 and 2017).
    *   **HVDC Projects:** The Northeast Agra project is nearing energization and testing, expected for handover early next year. No other HVDC orders have been booked and are not discussed until booked.
    *   **Inverter Technology:** New, higher efficiency inverter technology will be launched in India, with a roadmap for localization as the market develops. Expects considerable traction by 2017-2018.
    *   **Solar Sector Outlook:** Expects up to 5 GW of solar projects to be bid out this year, reflecting positively on their pipeline. Booked 300-350 MW of inverter orders per quarter in Q1 and Q2.
    *   **Transmission & Distribution CAPEX:** Expects continued focus and strong play in T&D projects led by Power Grid, with potential for tariff-based bidding by private players.
    *   **Process Automation Uptick:** Difficult to predict a specific uptick in 9-12 months, as market consolidation continues. Green shoots in cement and steel are visible, but significant new asset construction is not yet evident.
    *   **Discrete Automation Margins:** Margins are impacted by foreign exchange movements and the mix of products vs. projects. Execution timelines influence margins.
    *   **Power Grid Margins:** Improved due to higher exports and significant service contribution in Q2.
    *   **765kV GIS Market:** ABB is a major player and competes well. The market is redefining itself in terms of voltage levels, with state electricity boards moving towards GIS. ABB's standing will become clearer in the next 1-2 quarters.
    *   **CAPEX Plan:** Approximately ₹20 crores spent on CAPEX in H1, with a similar amount expected in H2. Focus is on consolidating existing investments, with future plans contingent on new product/technology needs.
    *   **IoTSP Opportunity:** A significant focus across all divisions (Process Automation, DM, EP, Power Grid) and services. It's a key criterion in customer discussions.
    *   **Future HVDC Orders:** Extension of recent HVDC orders to Kerala is under bidding, with potential finalization by year-end or Q1 next year.
