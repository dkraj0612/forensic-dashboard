## Executive Overview

ABB India concluded Fiscal Year 2024 with a robust Q4 performance, demonstrating significant year-on-year growth in revenues and profits, driven by strong operational efficiencies and favorable business mix. While Q4 order intake saw a dip of 14% due to the absence of a large order from the previous year, the full-year order growth remained healthy at 6%. The company reported record profitability with PBT margins expanding significantly and a substantial increase in PAT. ABB India continues to leverage its diversified portfolio across 18 divisions and 23 market segments, with particular strength in Renewables, Data Centers, Transportation, and Food & Beverage. The company remains committed to sustainability initiatives and is actively contributing to India's development narrative through its product offerings and CSR programs. Management expressed confidence in the long-term India growth story, emphasizing a positive outlook despite transient market fluctuations.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders Received (Q4 2024):** INR 3,052 crores (down 14% YoY, primarily due to a large order in Q4 FY23).
*   **Orders Received (FY 2024):** INR 11,888 crores (up 6% YoY).
*   **Order Backlog (End of FY 2024):** INR 9,400 crores (up 12% YoY), with an estimated 65-70% expected to be executed in FY25.
*   **Revenue Growth (Q4 2024):** Up 22% YoY.
*   **Revenue Growth (FY 2024):** Up 17% YoY.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) Growth (Q4 2024):** Up 54% YoY.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) (FY 2024):** INR 2,659 crores (up 50% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin (Q4 2024):** 15.8%.
*   **PAT Margin (FY 2024):** 15.4%.
*   **Operational EBITDA (FY 2024):** 20%.
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS) (FY 2024):** 25.1% growth YoY.
*   **Cash Balance (End of FY 2024):** INR 5,390 crores.
*   **Dividend:** Approved a final dividend 51% higher year-on-year.
*   **Material Costs (FY 2024):** Reported as 58% of revenue, described as an "all-time record favorable material cost position" due to good revenue mix and booking orders at low commodity prices.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Focus:** Continues to operate across 18 divisions and 23 market segments, aligning with India's growth drivers. Key growth areas identified include Data Centers, Railways, Metros, Renewable Energy, and the Food & Beverage sector.
*   **Emerging Segments:** Actively seeks and engages with new and emerging market segments, referencing Data Centers as a prime example of past successful identification.
*   **Geographical Expansion:** Focus on deeper penetration into Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities to capture rising aspirations for high-quality products.
*   **Sustainability:** Reduced GHG emission intensity by 86% (vs. 2019 baseline). 40% of suppliers are enrolled in the ESG awareness and assessment program. Three of six plants are zero-waste-to-landfill units. Recognized as a best-in-class company for sustainability practices.
*   **Localization & Portfolio Expansion:** Continuous expansion and localization of the product portfolio to remain competitive and meet the Indian market's demand for sophisticated, premium products. Expansion of Faridabad IEC Low Voltage Motor Manufacturing Unit.
*   **Innovation & New Products:** Launched new products in Analyzers. Secured projects in advanced monitoring of PV and hybrid PV wind projects.
*   **Energy Transition:** Supporting renewable energy, EV charging, and green hydrogen projects with motors and drives.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Continuous engagement with customers to understand their needs, which informs decisions on bringing and localizing ABB technologies.
*   **Infrastructure & Development:** Contribution to infrastructure, process industries, and transportation (especially railways and metros) through product offerings and automation solutions. Robotics seeing significant uptake in electronics and automotive sectors, with warehousing identified as an emerging growth area.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** Full-year order growth of 6% was achieved, falling slightly short of the robust growth seen in prior years. The Q4 dip was attributed to the non-recurrence of a large order, which management describes as transient and not a concern for long-term growth. The base order run rate remains healthy.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Delivered strong 17% YoY revenue growth for the full year, exceeding historical performance. This was aided by good backlog execution and increased capacity utilization.
*   **PAT Growth & Margins:** Achieved substantial PAT growth of 50% YoY for FY24 and reported record PAT margins. Management highlighted operational efficiency, favorable revenue mix, and capacity utilization as key drivers, consistent with previous periods.
*   **Sustainability Initiatives:** Continued to demonstrate progress with specific achievements in GHG emission reduction, zero-waste-to-landfill units, and supplier ESG programs, aligning with stated commitments.
*   **Order Backlog:** Maintained a strong and growing order backlog (INR 9,400 crores, up 12%), providing good revenue visibility.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Market Outlook:** Remains very positive and "long on India," anticipating strong future growth cycles. Sees transient factors impacting short-term order growth but believes the underlying demand and planned investments will drive future expansion.
*   **Order Inflow Guidance:** While not providing explicit numbers, management believes the current run rate is healthy and does not rule out potential step-up in quarterly order intake to around INR 4,000 crores long-term, given strong pipelines and capacity expansion.
*   **Revenue Execution:** Expects 65-70% of the current order backlog to be executed in FY25, with the balance carrying over to FY26.
*   **Profitability Corridor:** Aims for a PAT margin corridor of 12-15% moving forward, acknowledging that current high margins are supported by favorable past commodity pricing and operational efficiencies, which may ease over time.
*   **Cash Utilization:** Plans to utilize cash for increased dividends to shareholders, organic expansion (details to be revealed in coming months), and carefully evaluated inorganic opportunities. Cash will also support working capital needs for growth.
*   **Motion Segment:** Expects delays in private CAPEX investments to shift to the next quarter. Sees potential for motor pricing erosion to stabilize.
*   **Process Automation:** Pipeline remains strong with opportunities in oil & gas, petrochemicals, and pharma. Focus on value-added selling and operational efficiencies.
*   **Robotics & Automation:** Sees continued strong momentum in electronics and automotive, with potential for equal consumption in automotive and electronics in the near future. Warehousing technology identified as an emerging segment expected to boom in 2-3 years.
*   **Electrification Segment:** Continued growth traction in Renewables, Buildings, and Power Distribution. Data Centers and Transportation (metros, railways) are significant growth opportunities.
*   **Exports:** Sees ongoing tailwinds for India's participation in the global market due to world-class operations, productivity, and labor arbitrage. Global divisions will drive export market expansion.
*   **Competition:** Management believes ABB is well-protected from Chinese competition due to localization, high quality, reliability, sustainability focus, and strong customer confidence. In robotics, the advantage lies in application engineering. In Process Automation, while some large CAPEX projects may see competitive bidding from Chinese players, this is currently a minority occurrence.
*   **Sustainability:** Continues to focus on energy consumption, water management, waste reduction, and sharing best practices with suppliers.
*   **Investments:** Sees the Union Budget's focus on infrastructure, investment, manufacturing, green energy, and R&D as beneficial. All businesses are aligning strategies to leverage these.
*   **Premiumization:** Anticipates continued demand for high-quality, premium products post-COVID, which is favorable for ABB's gross margins.
*   **Divisional Growth:**
    *   **Electrification:** Strong traction in Renewables, Buildings, and Power Distribution. Data Centers and Transportation remain key growth drivers.
    *   **Motion:** Seeing potential in Bioethanol, Hydrogen, Data Centers (HVAC), and Transport (traction converters/motors). Light industries (Pharma, Food & Beverage) also show momentum. Process industry segments (Cement, Metal, Oil & Gas) are experiencing transient headwinds but customers remain bullish.
    *   **Process Automation:** Steady revenue and order volumes, with improving margins through value-added selling and services.
    *   **Robotics:** Fastest growing division, prevalent in electronics and automotive. Food & Beverages also showing strong growth. Metals industry showing early signs of automation adoption. Warehousing identified as a future boom segment.
