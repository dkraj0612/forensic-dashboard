## Executive Overview

ABB India concluded 2015 with a Q4 that demonstrated resilience amidst a challenging market. While the company reported a decline in overall order intake for the quarter (-17% YoY), this was attributed to the absence of very large orders seen in the prior year's comparable quarter. Crucially, "base orders," representing the core business, showed a healthy 10% growth for the quarter and 10% for the full year, indicating stable underlying demand. Revenue saw a modest overall increase of 2% for the year, driven by product divisions, though Power Systems experienced a significant dip. Profitability improved year-over-year, supported by better operational performance, localization, cost control, and favorable forex movements. The Services segment continued its strong growth trajectory, exceeding INR 1,000 crores for the first time and becoming a significant contributor to profitability. Management expressed cautious optimism for 2016, citing a strong order backlog of INR 8,000 crores for revenue visibility, but acknowledged ongoing market caution and the need for reforms to materialize.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:**
    *   Full Year 2015: Increased by 2% YoY.
    *   Q4 2015: Saw a 14% increase in Process Automation, though full-year PA was flat. Power Products grew 12%, Discrete Automation & Motion grew 9%, and Low Voltage Products grew 8%. Power Systems experienced a 13% dip for the full year.
*   **Order Intake:**
    *   Q4 2015: Down 17% YoY due to the absence of large orders.
    *   Full Year 2015: Roughly flat YoY overall.
    *   Base Orders: Grew 10% in Q4 and 10% for the full year.
*   **Profitability:**
    *   Improved YoY for the full year and Q4.
    *   Driven by material cost savings, increased localization, competitive product introductions, higher volumes, favorable forex, and strong service segment performance.
    *   Products generally outperformed systems in terms of profitability.
*   **Depreciation:**
    *   Adoption of new depreciation rates under the Companies Act 2013 impacted the year.
*   **Exports:**
    *   Contribution to revenues was 12% for the full year.
    *   Order inflow contribution was around 11-12% for the full year.
    *   Export performance was softer YoY due to global market uncertainties and delayed decision-making in neighboring markets.
*   **Forex Impact:**
    *   Full Year 2015: Net loss of INR 34 crores (vs. INR 43 crores loss last year).
    *   Q4 2015: Net loss of INR 13.5 crores.
    *   Benefit derived from lower import costs in Euros.
*   **Debt Management:**
    *   Issued Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) resulting in interest savings of over INR 5 crores for the quarter.
*   **Order Backlog:**
    *   Strong backlog of approximately INR 8,000 crores, providing good revenue visibility for about one year.
    *   No non-moving order backlog reported.
*   **Services Segment:**
    *   Crossed INR 1,000 crores in revenue for the first time.
    *   Grew approximately 10-12% (orders and revenue).
    *   Became a significant contributor to profitability.
*   **Employee Count:** 5,850 at year-end.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Focus on Base Orders and Services:** Continued emphasis on growing "base orders" and the Services business, which offers stable revenue and profitability.
*   **Renewables Growth:** Strong performance in solar and wind, with solar inverters business seeing a twofold increase and surpassing 1.3 GW of orders. ABB equipment in over 50% of India's solar capacity.
*   **Railways and Transportation:** Capitalizing on increased investment, contributing to propulsion converters, transformers, and turbochargers. Railways contribute about 4% to overall business.
*   **"Make in India" and Localization:** Continued focus on localization as a driver of competitiveness and cost-efficiency across various product lines (drives, motors, switchgears, etc.). India also serves as a significant R&D and engineering hub for the Group.
*   **Smart Solutions and Digitalization:** While not explicitly detailed in Q4, the previous focus on microgrids, EV charging, and IoT is implicitly part of the company's long-term strategy.
*   **T&D Market Engagement:** Expectation of similar or slightly higher opportunities in the T&D market, with interest from Power Grid and state utilities, alongside traction in metros and traction converters.
*   **Cautious Project Selection:** Disciplined approach to project orders, prioritizing profitability and ensuring payment security before revenue recognition.
*   **Operational Efficiency and Cost Control:** Ongoing efforts to reduce costs, bring in competitive products, and optimize the cost base.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Full year revenue growth of 2% was modest, with mixed performance across divisions. Management's earlier statement about even small revenue growth leading to better profitability due to cost control holds true.
    *   **Profitability Improvement:** Management's assertion of improved profitability due to operational performance, localization, and forex tailwinds is validated by the results.
    *   **Order Intake:** The narrative of a dip in Q4 order intake due to large order absence was accurate. However, the strong 10% growth in "base orders" for the quarter and full year aligns with their strategy of focusing on core business.
    *   **Market Challenges:** Management consistently highlighted market caution, low capacity utilization in core sectors, and delays in reforms (GST, labor, Electricity Act amendment). This narrative remains consistent, with a cautious optimism for 2016 contingent on these factors.
    *   **Deferred Sales ("Cash Over Revenue"):** The emphasis on prioritizing profitability over revenue recognition for projects where payment security is a concern (due to contractual conditions and customer liquidity) is consistent. This policy impacts project revenue recognition.
    *   **Export Performance:** Management's acknowledgment of a softer export performance YoY due to global markets and decision-making delays is consistent with the reported figures.
    *   **Services Growth:** The strong performance and crossing of INR 1,000 crores in services aligns with management's focus on this segment.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Industrial Revival Timeline:** Management reiterates that significant industrial revival is contingent on government reforms taking action on the ground, and it remains a "wait and watch" scenario for customers in core sectors.
    *   **Order Backlog Conversion:** Confident in converting the INR 8,000 crores backlog into revenue over the next couple of quarters, with products having shorter cycles and projects potentially spread over two years.
    *   **2016 Outlook:** Cautiously optimistic for 2016, expecting revenue visibility from the strong backlog. Expectation of good conversions in Q1 and Q2 for T&D projects.
    *   **Solar Market Outlook:** Healthy pursuit list for solar inverters, with 2017 also anticipated to be strong.
    *   **T&D Market Outlook:** Expecting similar or slightly higher opportunities in T&D in 2016 compared to 2015, with large projects from Power Grid and state utilities.
    *   **Transportation Sector Outlook:** Positive upward movement in railway projects, with continued business in propulsion converters, transformers, and turbochargers.
    *   **Export Market Prospects for 2016:** Confidence stems from potential new markets for ABB India products and the expected conversion of existing orders facing decision-making bottlenecks.
    *   **Margins:** Management declined to provide specific guidance on margin sustainability or potential decrease, citing its sensitivity.
    *   **Service Business Expansion:** Commitment to further expand the Services business in 2016.
    *   **Renewables Market Growth (2016):** Anticipation of similar annual GW numbers for solar and wind in 2016 as seen in 2015, driven by government push.
