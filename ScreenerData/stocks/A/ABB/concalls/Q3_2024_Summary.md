## Executive Overview

ABB India reported a solid Q3 2024 performance, marked by sustained order growth and resilient execution despite some segment-specific headwinds. Orders received grew by 11%, driven by a strategic increase in large orders, complementing robust base order inflows. Revenues saw a 5% year-on-year increase, reflecting the execution of existing backlog, with management noting that larger, longer-gestation orders are yet to significantly contribute to revenue conversion. Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a notable 22% increase, attributed to a favorable revenue mix, improved margin capture on executed orders, and efficient capacity utilization. Sustainability remains a key focus, with the Nelamangala campus achieving zero CO2 emissions (Scope 1 & 2) and the Nashik Plant-2 receiving Platinum rating for green factory building. The company maintains a positive outlook on the Indian market, highlighting continued strength in segments like data centers, railways, and the energy sector, while acknowledging cyclicality in others.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders Received (Q3 2024):** INR 3,000 crores, up 11% YoY.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q3 2024):** ~INR 10,000 crores. This comprises approximately 25% large orders and 75% base orders.
*   **Revenue Growth (Q3 2024):** Up 5% YoY.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) Growth (Q3 2024):** Up 22% YoY.
*   **PAT Margin (Q3 2024):** 15%.
*   **Material Costs:** Stable, influenced by revenue mix and positive price impact. System provision adjustment noted.
*   **Other Expenses:** Increased due to specific warranty costs moved from material costs. Ongoing expenses expected in the 470-480 crore range.
*   **Cash Position:** Strong, with collections outpacing revenues. Inventory built up for backlog execution.
*   **Exports:** 12% of revenues.
*   **Electrification PBIT Margin:** 20-21%.
*   **Motion PBIT Margin:** 23%+.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Alignment:** Operates across 23 market segments with 18 divisions, aligning with India's growth drivers.
*   **High-Growth Segments:** Continued strong performance and pipeline in Data Centers, Railways, Metros, Renewable Energy (10 GW milestone reached), and the energy sector.
*   **Geographical Reach:** Ongoing focus on deeper penetration into Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets via channel partners.
*   **Sustainability & ESG:** Nelamangala campus CO2 compliant (Scope 1 & 2), Nashik Plant-2 Green Factory rating, recognized in sustainability rankings. Continued focus on emission reduction.
*   **Product Portfolio & Innovation:** Launched ABB-free@home smart home portfolio. Focus on energy efficiency (IE3/IE4 motors).
*   **Premiumization:** Customers increasingly prioritize quality and reliability.
*   **Energy Transition:** Active participation in renewable energy, EV charging, and green hydrogen projects.
*   **Services Growth:** Consistent growth in services contributing to profitability.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** Achieved 11% YoY growth, aligning with management's expectation of large orders starting to pick up, complementing base orders. The narrative reflects a shift from consistent base order commentary to acknowledging the increasing contribution of large contracts.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Delivered 5% YoY growth. Management acknowledged that the conversion of large orders, which have longer gestation periods, is slower than base orders, impacting the velocity of revenue growth compared to past periods.
*   **PAT Growth:** Achieved 22% YoY growth, consistent with management's focus on operational excellence, revenue mix, and capacity utilization.
*   **Margin Capture:** Management highlighted successful margin capture on orders, both pre- and post-execution, contributing to PAT growth.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Management noted good capacity leverage and ability to cater to market demand with incremental investment, consistent with previous commentary.
*   **Sustainability Initiatives:** Demonstrated continued progress with specific achievements like the Nelamangala campus certification, indicating consistency with stated ESG commitments.
*   **Order Backlog:** Maintained a strong backlog (~INR 10,000 crores), providing good revenue visibility, with a significant portion being base orders.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Market Outlook:** Positive outlook on the Indian market, with continued base strength. Expects improvement in segments currently showing lower uptake over time. Data centers and transport remain key growth areas.
*   **Process Automation Pipeline:** Pipeline remains strong and vibrant, with opportunities in oil & gas, petrochemicals, and pharma expected to convert to firm orders in coming quarters. Focus on steady, risk-mitigated growth in this segment.
*   **Motion Segment:** Expects LT motor pricing erosion to stop, with a hopeful upturn in demand due to new investments. Drive business continues to perform well due to sustainability focus.
*   **Electrification Capacity:** Continued capacity expansion in line with market view and business cases. Focus on productivity improvements in existing plants and deeper localization. Plans for future investment with a 10-year visibility.
*   **Data Center Growth:** Expects continued significant expansions in the data center segment from both global and local investors.
*   **Railways & Metros:** These segments are expected to continue expanding, contributing to electrification growth.
*   **Packaging & Cold Storage:** These are identified as high-growth segments within the food industry.
*   **Instrumentation Business:** Expanding portfolio and localization, expecting market share gains in water monitoring and analytics. Potential announcements in Q2 next year.
*   **Export Potential:** Room for catering to Middle East and African markets in energy and process industries, but primary focus remains on India and South Asia.
*   **Large Deal Conversions:** Positive conversion rate on large deals, particularly in transportation and mobility (metro lines, railway projects), with a good lineup expected.
*   **Order Inflow Run Rate:** While not providing specific numbers, management believes the current 2,500-3,000 crore run rate is healthy. They are not ruling out a potential step jump in quarterly order intake to closer to 4,000 crores in the longer term, citing strong pipelines across divisions and incremental capacity expansion capabilities. However, acknowledges it's difficult to predict the exact timing.
*   **Motor Business Profitability:** Management highlights that while motors are a significant part of the Motion segment, other growing businesses like drives and traction converters, along with services, are contributing to strong overall margins. They emphasize the value customers place on ABB's energy-efficient and premium products, making price less of a differentiator in these segments.
*   **Margin Stability:** Management aims for stable profitability moving forward, supported by higher capacity utilization, better cost ratios, and continued focus on services and localization. The advantage from past raw material prices is expected to thin down.
