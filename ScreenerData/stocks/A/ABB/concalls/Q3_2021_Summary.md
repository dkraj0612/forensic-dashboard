## Executive Overview

ABB India delivered a robust Q3 2021 performance, demonstrating a strong recovery that not only surpassed pre-COVID levels but also showcased significant year-over-year growth across all business areas. Orders surged by 41% and revenue by 6% compared to Q3 2020, with a notable 10% increase compared to Q3 2019. This growth was broad-based, driven by healthy demand, favorable product mix, and improved operational efficiencies, which collectively led to a strong Operational EBITA margin. The company also maintained a healthy cash position and actively expanded manufacturing capacity for its Low Voltage (LV) motors. Management highlighted strong customer engagement and successful execution of backlog, leading to positive revenue momentum. Emerging government initiatives in infrastructure, battery manufacturing, and the hydrogen mission are expected to be significant future growth drivers. The company is also making strides in its ESG strategy, focusing on sustainable business models and renewable energy utilization.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Demand & Growth:** Capitalizing on high levels of demand with double-digit growth in all business areas. Focus on broad-based order growth across segments including automotive, oil & gas, and power generation.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Value:** Leveraging improved value offerings and operational efficiencies to achieve high Operational EBITA margins. Emphasis on price realization through channel and segment focus.
*   **Digitalization & Innovation:** Continued adoption of digital products and technologies (e.g., ABB eMart, Emax2 by SMEs). Industry first collaborative robotics on ABB shopfloor. Focus on smart products for better margins.
*   **Manufacturing Expansion:** Expanding manufacturing capacity for Low Voltage (LV) motors at the Faridabad facility, powered by renewable energy.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Maintaining strong customer connect and focusing on executing the order backlog effectively.
*   **Government Initiatives:** Aligning with government growth drivers such as AMRUT 2.0, GatiShakti, battery manufacturing incentives, and the National Hydrogen Mission.
*   **ESG Strategy:** Driving sustainability through a focus on renewable energy utilization (RE100), net-zero carbon goals, and waste reduction.
