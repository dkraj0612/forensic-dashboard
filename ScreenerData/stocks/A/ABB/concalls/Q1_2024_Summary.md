## Executive Overview

ABB India commenced 2024 with a robust first quarter, demonstrating strong growth across orders and revenues. The company achieved several financial milestones, including crossing INR 3,000 crores in revenue for the first time and surpassing INR 5,000 crores in cash. This performance was driven by operational excellence, capacity utilization, favorable material costs due to localization and an improved business mix, and strong execution across its four business areas: Electrification, Motion, Process Automation, and Robotics & Discrete Automation. Key growth drivers include the accelerating domestic market, with significant contributions from high-growth segments like data centers, railways, and metros, alongside continued penetration into Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. The company also highlighted its unwavering commitment to sustainability, with further progress in reducing GHG emissions and increasing water positivity. The substantial order backlog provides strong revenue visibility for the upcoming quarters.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders Received (Q1 2024):** INR 3,607 crores, up 15% YoY and sequentially. Data center orders contributed INR 373 crores.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q1 2024):** INR 8,900+ crores, up 25% YoY, providing strong revenue visibility.
*   **Revenue Growth (Q1 2024):** 28% YoY, crossing INR 3,000 crores for the first time.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) Growth (Q1 2024):** 87% YoY, attributed to good capacity leverage and cost optimization.
*   **Material Costs (Q1 2024):** 59.8% of revenue, a historical low, driven by higher services and exports, localization, SEM savings, and favorable pricing from older high-cost commodity orders.
*   **Cash Position (End of Q1 2024):** INR 5,036 crores.
*   **Exports:** 10% of revenue, with a 23% YoY growth, though domestic growth outpaced exports significantly (92% of revenue from India).
*   **EBITDA Margins (Services):** Northwards of 25%.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Alignment:** ABB India's portfolio remains well-aligned with India's economic drivers, including infrastructure development, industrialization, digitization, and manufacturing expansion. The company serves 23 market segments through 19 divisions, ensuring fundamental resilience.
*   **High-Growth Segments:** Continued strong traction in Data Centers, Railways, Metros, and Electronics. Data center business is accelerating, with global majors increasing investments. Transportation sector demand for propulsion technology in Railways and electrification in Metros remains a positive driver.
*   **Geographical Reach:** Deep penetration into Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets is an ongoing focus, with a significant journey ahead for further expansion.
*   **Sustainability & ESG:** Commitment to sustainability is a core value. Scope 1 & 2 GHG emissions reduced by 88% (vs. 2019 baseline). More than 50% of campuses are water positive, with a target to improve water recyclability to 45% in 2024. Two units are targeted for "Zero Waste to Landfill" certification in 2024.
*   **Product Portfolio Expansion:** Introduction of new products, such as the ACH180 compact drive for the HVACR sector, and continued localization efforts. Technology and AI are shaping industry needs.
*   **Premiumization:** Customers are increasingly prioritizing quality, reliability, and advanced technology, leading to better price realization and a shift towards companies like ABB due to demonstrated support capabilities.
*   **Focus Theme (Q1 2024):** Deep dive into Buildings and Infrastructure, a $1.4 trillion market, highlighting the synergy with Electrification and Motion portfolios.
*   **Channel Partners:** 41% of business comes through channel partners, integrators, and OEMs, supporting reach into diverse geographies.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** Exceeded expectations with 15% YoY growth, demonstrating strong underlying demand and effective market penetration, building on previous strong quarters.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Delivered a significant 28% YoY growth, surpassing previous high growth rates and crossing the INR 3,000 crore revenue milestone, indicating strong execution supported by backlog.
*   **PAT Growth:** Achieved a substantial 87% YoY growth, driven by capacity leverage and cost optimization, consistent with management's focus on operational excellence.
*   **Material Costs:** Achieved a historic low of 59.8%, surpassing the previously mentioned "comfortable range" and attributed to a combination of factors including services, exports, localization, and favorable pricing from older orders, consistent with management's emphasis on cost control.
*   **Cash Position:** Surpassed INR 5,000 crores, demonstrating strong cash generation and financial health.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Continued to report tangible progress and met targets for GHG emission reduction and water positivity, aligning with stated commitments.
*   **Order Backlog:** Maintained strong growth with a 25% YoY increase, providing excellent revenue visibility.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Ongoing investment in incremental expansion of capacities for both people and physical resources, with approximately INR 180-200 crores invested in the last 2 years for expansion. This will continue as demand expands.
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Endeavor to maintain profitability at or above 12% (last year's level), with efforts to improve. While the existing backlog from periods of high commodity prices aided Q1 margins, future book-and-bill revenues will incorporate current pricing adjustments.
*   **Drives Portfolio:** Expectation for continued growth in the drives market, significantly faster than market growth, driven by customer focus on sustainability and energy efficiency. i3/i4 motor standards will not dampen drive demand as penetration is still low.
*   **Data Center Opportunity:** The data center market is "accelerating" with increasing investment by global players. While not yet 10-15% of revenue, the trend is positive and expected to continue. Products for data centers are "highly localized."
*   **Tier-2/Tier-3 City Penetration:** This remains a journey with a "long way to go," indicating continued focus and investment in these markets. The opportunity is considered on the "lower side of the curve" rather than mature.
*   **Electrification (EL) Portfolio:** Significant expansion is underway across all business areas, including Electrification. The company feels "fairly complete" for ELDS and ELSP technologies for data centers.
*   **Motion Segment:** While decision-making for some system orders has been postponed to Q2, the backlog is steady, and the segment is expected to navigate price pressures through revenue mix and price advantages.
*   **Process Automation (PA):** Seen as a "turnaround segment" with high revenue execution in services, especially in oil & gas and metals & mining. Project execution and order composition are improving margins.
*   **Robotics Segment:** Expected to see order catch-up in coming quarters from relevant industries. Revenue growth is solid, and higher revenue execution will translate to the bottom line.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Consistent policy: 30-35% for working capital expansion, balance for inorganic/organic options (strategic business expansion), and shareholder returns (e.g., dividends).
*   **Energy Transition:** Motion division sees opportunities beyond energy efficiency in hydrogen and ammonia production (powering electrolyzers) and transportation of these fuels. Traction motor business for railways and metros continues to be a strong contributor.
*   **Market Shift:** Acknowledgment of a shift from price sensitivity to reliability, availability, and support, especially post-COVID, benefiting companies like ABB. This is contributing to premiumization and market expansion.
*   **Services:** Highest concentration in Process Automation (40% of PA business), followed by Motion (32%) and Electrification (22%). Services generally achieve EBITDA margins north of 25%.
