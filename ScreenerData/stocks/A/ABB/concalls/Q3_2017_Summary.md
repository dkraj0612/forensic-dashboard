## Executive Overview

ABB India's Q3 2017 results indicate a mixed performance with strong order growth driven by traction transformers and other portfolio contributions, alongside revenue growth across all divisions. Process Automation saw a turnaround with improved revenue and profitability, while Power Grids faced headwinds and forex impact, leading to a conservative approach in cost recognition. The company expanded its solar inverter factory capacity, introduced higher efficiency products, and executed innovative projects like shore-to-ship power supply and a 1200 kV UHV Transformer. Management remains cautiously optimistic, emphasizing the company's strong installed base for services and its strategic focus on energy and digitalization trends, with a global organizational restructuring set to take effect in early 2018.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Focus:** Leveraging government initiatives (Make in India, Startup India, ease of doing business) and investments in emerging areas like renewables, railways, ports, and data centers.
*   **Divisional Performance:** Turnaround in Process Automation (led by metals and services); Revenue growth across all divisions.
*   **Product & Technology Innovation:**
    *   Expanded solar inverter factory capacity in Bangalore.
    *   Introduction of higher efficiency solar inverters.
    *   Execution of India's first shore-to-ship power supply for a commercial port.
    *   Delivery of string inverters to 190 government schools in West Bengal.
    *   Energized the world's first 1200 kV Ultra High Voltage Power Transformer.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Intensive customer engagement through factory visits and technology experience.
*   **Global Strategy Alignment:** Embracing the III Stage of ABB Group's next level strategy focusing on energy transformation and digitalization. ABB India is the Center of Excellence for Micro Grid technology globally.
*   **Organizational Restructuring (effective Jan 1, 2018):**
    *   New "Robotics and Motion" division.
    *   Process Automation to be renamed "Industrial Automation".
    *   Electrification Products division to expand, incorporating EV charging, solar, and power quality businesses.
*   **Service Growth:** Continued strong performance driven by OPEX spending from the large installed base, focusing on upgrades, retrofits, energy efficiency, and optimization.
*   **Digitalization:** Positioned to be a key player in digital transformation, leveraging India's R&D and labs. Strategic partnership with Microsoft announced globally.
*   **Project Selection:** Selective project booking with careful due diligence on credit availability and risk.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Order Growth:** The Q3 transcript indicates a "good order from Traction Transformers and plus other portfolio mix" leading to "well-rounded growth" and a "good mix of growth all over." This aligns with the previous quarter's focus on base orders and shows renewed momentum, a positive shift from the slower H1 growth mentioned in the prior summary. The comment that "base orders are holding steady" also signifies consistency.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Revenue grew across all four divisions in Q3. While the Q-o-Q growth was noted as "slightly less than running rate of previous two quarters," it was still positive. This is consistent with the narrative of leveraging market opportunities.
*   **Profitability:** Q-o-Q profit growth (PBT +30%, PAT +38%) is strong, indicating improved operational performance. The turnaround in Process Automation and continued strong services and exports revenue contribute to this. However, Power Grids faced headwinds and forex impact, requiring a conservative approach to cost recognition.
*   **Services Growth:** The reported 27% revenue growth in services for Q3 is a significant acceleration from the 32% Y-o-Y growth noted in Q2, demonstrating continued strong performance and alignment with management's strategic emphasis on leveraging the installed base for OPEX spending.
*   **Exports Growth:** Exports revenue grew by 28% in Q3, continuing the positive trend mentioned in Q2 (16-17% revenue contribution). This confirms ongoing progress in leveraging India as an export hub.
*   **Market Narrative:** Management acknowledges the "sluggishness which is reflected in declining IIP" and muted industry CAPEX. This consistency in identifying market challenges remains. However, the tone has become more optimistic regarding government initiatives, emerging market segments, and the benefits of their technological offerings and organizational restructuring.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Market Outlook:** Expects continued steady GDP growth driven by services and government initiatives. Acknowledges sluggishness in industry CAPEX. Anticipates further impact from government initiatives in the future.
*   **Process Automation Revival:** "Very early signs of revival" and "willingness from customers to spend money" are noted, but primarily on the OPEX side. Large CAPEX tenders are under negotiation but not yet finalized. It is "a bit early to comment about a revival."
*   **Power Grids Strategy:** Facing headwinds due to time elongation in infrastructure projects. Adopting a conservative cost recognition policy for delayed projects. Management noted that "this could be a momentary swing" and they will "turn it on when we have the final closing of the projects." Execution of Bangladesh projects is proceeding "very smoothly."
*   **Other Expenses:** Increased other expenses are attributed to investments in high-end IT solutions (cyber security, digitalization) and volume-based expenses accompanying revenue growth.
*   **Asset Sale:** ₹31 crores realized from the sale of a "small piece of factory place," part of footprint rationalization initiatives. Not a recurring event.
*   **Order Inflow Drivers:** Significant traction in Utilities, Transportation, and Infrastructure segments. Industry segment is muted, with focus on OPEX for the installed base.
*   **Order Backlog Breakup:** Majority of the order backlog is expected to come from Power Grid and Systems orders due to their longer shelf life.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Increasing, with more global players attracted to India's growth. ABB relies on its 60-year local manufacturing, engineering, and know-how capabilities.
*   **Margins:** Management declined to comment on future margin levels, citing a policy against forward-looking statements. Emphasized focus on cost review, portfolio alignment, and emerging trends in energy and digitalization.
*   **Solar Business:** Steady at 15-16% of revenue. Capacity expansion in Bangalore.
*   **Energy & Digital India Opportunities:** Expect large orders in the energy space (wind, solar, hydro, pumped hydro storage, battery storage, remote monitoring, EV charging, Smart Grid technologies) and digitalization in the next 6-12 months.
*   **HVDC Orders:** No HVDC orders booked in Q3. Management states they only talk about orders when booked and announced.
*   **Mergers & Acquisitions:** India is a "fertile ground" for M&A to acquire technologies and strengthen the portfolio. Active interest and a pipeline of assets are being monitored.
*   **EV Charging:** Evolving on technology and business fronts, with significant interest from automotive OEMs and government. Expected to play out "within next 2-3-years in a very big way." Business models involve multiple players (operators, utilities, government, OEM associations).
*   **Railway Business:** Strong traction transformer and turbocharger business. Also supplying propulsion converters, auxiliary converters, and power electronic converter panels for locomotives and trains. Evaluating DC wayside rectifier business for metro rail. Considering traction motors as an area of interest under evaluation.
*   **Raw Material Costs:** Moving within the 65-66% band, dependent on order mix. Operational excellence initiatives (localization, cost reduction) continue.
*   **CAPEX Plan (per Q2 transcript):** Approximately ₹20 crores in H1, similar amount expected in H2. Focus on consolidating existing investments.
