## Executive Overview

ABB India demonstrated a strong start to 2016 in Q1, reporting a significant revenue increase of 10% year-on-year, marking its best Q1 revenue in five years. This growth was achieved despite a subdued overall market, driven by strong performance in renewables and specific orders in next-generation substations. A key highlight was achieving a cash-positive quarter for the first time in several quarters, a testament to disciplined execution and cash collection efforts. Profitability saw a substantial 30% year-on-year increase in both PBT and PAT, attributed to operational efficiencies, improved project management, and localization efforts, particularly in Electrification Products and Discrete Automation & Motion. The Power Grids division, while seeing a slight revenue dip, significantly improved its PBIT. Management highlighted a disciplined approach to project selection, prioritizing profitability and cash over revenue. Exports also showed robust double-digit growth, indicating a strategic push to utilize ABB India as a hub for serving neighboring markets. The company is well-positioned to capitalize on a potential market revival and India's growth plans.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:**
    *   Q1 2016: Increased by 10% YoY, the best Q1 revenue in five years.
    *   Discrete Automation and Motion (DM) and Electrification Products (EP) were major contributors to revenue growth.
    *   Process Automation (PA) systems business remained static, with an expectation of pickup in subsequent quarters.
*   **Order Intake:**
    *   Not explicitly detailed for Q1 2016 as a whole, but three of the four new divisions witnessed order growth.
    *   Exports reported double-digit growth, contributing 13-15% to sales and orders, an improvement from 11-12%.
*   **Profitability:**
    *   PBT and PAT up 30% YoY.
    *   Electrification Products (EP) saw significant improvement due to localization, new markets, and customers.
    *   Discrete Automation and Motion (DM) also showed profitability improvement.
    *   Power Grids PBIT improved significantly due to operational efficiencies and project management.
    *   Process Automation profitability was impacted by volume de-growth.
*   **Expenses:**
    *   Material cost: Stable at 64.2%.
    *   Personnel expenses: Slight increase to 9.3% due to wage revisions.
    *   Other expenses: Increased due to new product introductions (higher warranty provisioning), intensified training, mark-to-market Forex impact (approx. INR 8 crore loss), and investment in setting up a global shared accounting services center in Bangalore (one-time expense for a couple of quarters).
*   **Cash Flow:**
    *   Achieved a cash-positive quarter for the first time in several quarters, largely driven by Power Grids division's collection efforts.
*   **Order Backlog:**
    *   Maintained a steady backlog providing good visibility for approximately one year.
*   **Services Segment:**
    *   Remained a constant 11-12% of revenue.
    *   Focus remains on install base penetration rather than a specific revenue percentage.
    *   Positive outlook and a major support area for profitability.
*   **Renewables:**
    *   Continues to be a major contributor, forming 15-16% of the business.
    *   Provided a strong offset to the muted market environment.
    *   Double-digit quarter-on-quarter growth for renewables overall.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Focus on Operational Efficiency and Profitability:** Emphasis on disciplined project selection, cash collection, and cost control measures to drive profitability.
*   **Leveraging Renewables Growth:** Renewables continue to be a significant growth driver, offsetting weakness in traditional sectors. The company is well-positioned to capitalize on this trend.
*   **Strengthening Export Hub Role:** Utilizing ABB India as a base to serve growth markets in Africa and Southeast Asia, with exports showing double-digit growth.
*   **Investments in Future Capabilities:** Setting up a global shared accounting services center in Bangalore to drive cost savings and efficiencies for the group.
*   **New Division Structure:** The recently implemented four-division structure is showing positive momentum, improving customer connectivity and ease of doing business.
*   **Localization and "Make in India":** Continued efforts in localization across product lines are driving competitiveness.
*   **Digitalization and New Technologies:** Continued engagement with new era technologies like IoT (IoTSP) and microgrids, with partnerships like the one with IIT Madras for microgrids and battery energy storage.
*   **Market Focus:** Continued engagement with government spending, particularly with major customers like PGCL, and capitalizing on opportunities in railways, urban mobility, smart cities, and industrial energy efficiency.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved 10% YoY revenue growth in Q1 2016, significantly exceeding the modest 2% growth reported for FY15, and marking the best Q1 in five years. This contrasts with the flat full-year revenue in FY15.
    *   **Profitability Improvement:** The 30% YoY increase in PBT and PAT aligns with management's narrative of improved profitability driven by operational efficiencies and localization.
    *   **Order Intake:** While specific overall Q1 order intake figures were not detailed, the reported order growth in three of the four new divisions and strong export order growth are positive, contrasting with the Q4 2015 decline attributed to large orders.
    *   **Market Challenges:** Management continues to acknowledge a subdued market and the dependence of significant industrial revival on government reforms. The commentary on FII interest in India and India being a "rising star" provides a more positive macro outlook than previously emphasized.
    *   **Cash Flow:** Management's focus on "cash over revenue" and disciplined execution is validated by achieving a cash-positive quarter for the first time in several quarters.
    *   **Services Growth:** The Services segment continues to be a focus, maintaining its revenue share and contributing to profitability, aligning with previous emphasis.
    *   **New Division Structure:** Management reported that the transition to the new four-division structure went well and is already showing positive effects, as anticipated.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Global Shared Services Center:** The one-time expenses related to setting up the global shared accounting services center in Bangalore will recur for "a couple more quarters." This center will lead to significant cost savings in the future.
    *   **Warranty Provisioning:** Higher warranty expenses on new products are expected to be reviewed on a quarter-on-quarter basis. A specific timeline for reduction cannot be projected.
    *   **Export Growth:** Management is confident in continued double-digit growth in exports and utilizing ABB India as an export hub.
    *   **Solar Inverter Market:** Ambition to maintain a leadership position in the solar inverter market and sufficient internal scalability to meet anticipated growth targets (6-8 GW for the year).
    *   **Power Grids (PG) Performance:** Confident that the Power Grids division will "catch up" in Q2 after a slight dip in orders and revenue in Q1.
    *   **HVDC Projects:** Active bidding for projects like Raigarh-Pugalur and Pugalur-Thrissur. Timelines for awards are dependent on the customer's decision-making. Collaboration with BHEL may continue for future projects.
    *   **Policy Reforms as Drivers:** Key policy reform areas identified as future drivers include renewables, railways, urban mobility, smart cities, water projects, and industrial energy efficiency.
    *   **ABB India's Role in Global R&D:** Royalty expenses will remain between 3.5% to 4.5% of revenue as a contribution for group R&D and new technologies.
    *   **2016 Outlook:** Positive outlook for Q1 and Q2 conversions in T&D projects. Renewables are expected to maintain strong annual GW numbers. Management views the market positively but avoids specific growth predictions.
    *   **Margins:** Management declined to provide specific guidance on margin sustainability or potential decrease, citing sensitivity.
