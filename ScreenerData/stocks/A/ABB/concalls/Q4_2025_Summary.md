## Executive Overview

ABB India delivered a strong Q4 FY25, marked by a significant 52% YoY order growth, primarily driven by the resolution of delayed large orders and robust base business momentum (up 27% YoY). This performance comes after a period of market consolidation. Revenue saw a modest 6% YoY growth to INR 3,557 crores, reflecting good execution from a strong order backlog. Profitability metrics showed resilience, with EBITDA at 15.4% and PBT at 16.2%, although PAT margin stood at 12.2%. The company ended the year with a strong order backlog of INR 10,471 crores, providing good revenue visibility. Management expressed cautious optimism for CY26, highlighting positive trends in emerging industries, infrastructure, and a revival in core industries, while continuing to monitor global uncertainties. Strategic focus remains on innovation, sustainability, and leveraging India's growth trajectory.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Focus:** Electrification and Automation, aligned with India's development trends of "electrifying everything and automating everything."
*   **Innovation & Investment:** Expanding product portfolio, including new lines for energy-efficient drives and next-generation machinery drives, with a focus on localization.
*   **Sustainability:** Achieved AWS Gold certification for water stewardship; continued reduction in GHG emissions; ESG initiatives covering 51% of suppliers. Products contribute to customer energy efficiency.
*   **Market Segments:** Strong momentum in renewables, electronics, data centers, infrastructure, and transport. Revival seen in core industries (metals, chemical, oil & gas).
*   **Customer Engagement:** Continuous engagement with existing and new customers across geographies and market segments to expand market penetration and introduce new applications.
*   **Data Centers:** High optimism for the data center market, driven by hyperscale, co-location, and Indian enterprise investments. ABB is securing significant orders in this segment.
*   **Global Initiatives:** Leveraging global technology advancements, including potential integration of AI data center solutions with NVIDIA.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** The strong 52% YoY order growth in Q4 FY25, driven by large orders, validates management's prior commentary that these were delayed, not lost. The 27% YoY base order growth also aligns with positive signals. The overall narrative of market re-acceleration is consistent with previous quarters.
*   **Revenue Growth:** The 6% YoY revenue growth in Q4 FY25, while lower than order growth, is deemed a "good catch-up" post festival season and reflects solid execution from the backlog.
*   **Profitability:** While PBT margin (16.2%) remained stable sequentially and improved from Q3 FY25, it was lower than the prior year (20.5%). Management attributes this to higher material costs (QCO strategy, forex, metal prices) and a stabilizing pricing environment post-COVID demand surge. The PAT margin of 12.2% is at the lower end of the historical range.
*   **Order Backlog:** Remains strong at INR 10,471 crores, providing good revenue visibility, consistent with previous reporting.
*   **Sustainability:** Continued strong performance and recognition in sustainability initiatives, meeting or exceeding targets, demonstrating consistent commitment.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Market Outlook for CY26:** Generally positive, with demand building up after a breather in early 2025. Encouraged by emerging markets, infrastructure, transport, and a revival in core industries. Will continue to monitor Q1-Q4 trends for sustainability of the recovery.
*   **Margin Outlook:** The **12%-15% PAT margin target remains sustainable**. Improvement is contingent on managing forex and metal prices, and the eventual resolution of QCO-related material cost impacts (expected material consumption over the next 2 quarters, with order execution potentially resolving over 3-4 quarters). Management suggests that with increased volumes beyond the current 6-7% growth rate, further margin accretion is possible.
*   **QCO Impact:** The strategic decision to use imported material to address QCO concerns has led to higher material costs and will be consumed over the next 2 quarters. The government has **enhanced testing timelines, not rolled back QCO orders**, indicating its continued implementation.
*   **Large Orders Pipeline:** Opportunities exist across automation, ETO, ELDS, and Robotics, particularly in energy (power generation, refining) and process industries (metals, mineral, mining). Greenfield and modernization projects are driving opportunities.
*   **Data Centers:** Very strong demand and inquiry pipeline. ABB is securing significant orders, with data centers representing approximately **10-11% of the current INR 10,471 crore backlog**. Expectation of further growth driven by AI, hyperscale, co-location, and Indian enterprise plans. Tax holidays up to 2047 are expected to make India attractive for data center investments.
*   **Revenue Growth Ambition:** Aiming for **double-digit revenue growth** in the future, supported by the robust backlog and order booking momentum.
*   **IE2 to IE3 Motor Standard:** A potential upgrade to IE3 and IE4 minimum efficiency standards in India is seen as a welcome move, supporting exports and sustainability goals. ABB already has over 50% of its own production at IE3/IE4 levels and has introduced IE5 technology.
*   **EU-India FTA:** Expected to take 5-6 months for ratification, with potential positive impacts on ABB India's ability to deploy more portfolio and integrate supply chains. This is factored in for the future.
*   **Indian Union Budget 2026-27:** Seen as supportive for emerging industries, infrastructure, transport, and core industries, providing confidence for capacity and capability investments.
*   **Robotics Business:** The global divestment is complete. For India, the Board will evaluate potential separation into a new company.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Primarily domestic from established players. No direct impact yet from Chinese competition, though some large projects might prefer Chinese equipment. European competitors are expanding capacity as the market grows. ABB is focused on maintaining its #1 or #2 position.
*   **Process Automation Outlook:** Performed in line with the industry's capex environment and management's selective approach to high-quality, value-added projects. Pipeline is building up, with opportunities in power generation, refining, and heavy industries.
*   **Data Center Technology in India:** ABB technologies, including power-efficient solutions like SSDs and transformers, are seamlessly available in India as per customer demand and design criteria. Discussions are ongoing with domestic players for incorporation into future designs.
