## Executive Overview

ABB India delivered a Q3 FY25 performance characterized by strong 13% YoY growth in base orders, though overall order growth was a modest -3% YoY due to the absence of large orders compared to the prior year. Revenue saw a healthy 14% YoY increase, showcasing robust execution. Profitability, however, remained under pressure, with PAT down 7% YoY, primarily impacted by ongoing challenges from QCO compliance, forex volatility, and market dynamics leading to increased competition and a less favorable product mix. Management highlighted a continued strong cash position of approximately INR 5,000 crores. The company remains focused on its core growth drivers of electrification, digitalization, and sustainability, with a particular emphasis on emerging segments like data centers and renewable energy. While acknowledging near-term margin pressures and delayed decision-making for large projects, management expressed cautious optimism for a recovery in the medium term, anticipating the resolution of QCO impacts and a gradual improvement in market conditions over the next few quarters.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders Received (Q3 2025):**
    *   Base Orders: +13% YoY
    *   Total Orders: -3% YoY (impacted by absence of large orders from prior year)
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q3 2025):** INR 9,895 crores (approx. 30% large orders, 70% smaller orders)
*   **Revenue Growth (Q3 2025):** +14% YoY
*   **Profitability:**
    *   PBT Margin: 16.4% (vs. 14.9% sequentially, 20.5% YoY)
    *   PAT: -7% YoY
*   **Cash Balance (End of Q3 2025):** Approx. INR 4,500 crores (strategic inventory stocking for QCO compliance impacted this)
*   **Exports:** Constitute a minor portion of business, with no significant impact expected from US tariffs due to "local for local" strategy.
*   **Material Costs:** Impacted by QCO compliance, forex volatility, unfavorable product mix, and intensified competition.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Business Areas:** Process Automation, Motion, Robotics, and Electrification continue to be key segments.
*   **Localization & Product Portfolio Expansion:** Focus on introducing premium products like IE5 ultra-premium efficiency LV motors and expanding into new market segments and geographies.
*   **Sustainability Focus:** On track with GHG emission reduction targets (achieved 87.05% of 87% target). Four motion factories certified as zero waste to landfill, with three of four water-positive units achieved.
*   **Market Segments:** High engagement in data centers, electronics, and renewables; moderate growth in other segments. Deep dive into the textile sector, highlighting ABB's offerings.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Continued customer connect programs across various market segments and geographies.
*   **Emerging Segments:** Continued focus on renewables, buildings & infrastructure, data centers, and green hydrogen.
*   **Service Business:** Ongoing focus on increasing service mix.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** Management reiterated strong base order growth (+13% YoY), aligning with previous commentary on a robust base. However, the narrative around the *absence* of large orders and delayed decision-making is consistent with prior quarters, leading to negative overall order growth (-3% YoY). The cyclical nature of large orders remains a key theme.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved 14% YoY revenue growth, exceeding the historical Q2 performance and demonstrating strong execution from the backlog.
*   **Profitability:** PBT margin at 16.4% was an improvement sequentially but below the prior year's 20.5%. PAT declined 7% YoY. Management attributed this to a combination of factors (mix, competition, QCO, forex), a narrative consistent with previous discussions. The 12%-15% PAT margin guidance is still considered achievable in the long term, but near-term pressures remain.
*   **Order Backlog:** Remains strong at INR 9,895 crores, providing good revenue visibility. Management confirmed no slow-moving or non-moving orders.
*   **Sustainability:** Progress on sustainability goals continues to be highlighted, with targets being met or on track, demonstrating consistent commitment.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Market Outlook:** Cautious optimism persists. Private consumption and government CAPEX are seen as tailwinds, while trade uncertainty, geopolitical tensions, and forex volatility are headwinds. Expects gradual recovery in large project investments.
*   **Second-Half/Near-Term Outlook:** Expects market levels to remain similar for the next one to two quarters, with potential positive shifts thereafter. A recovery in growth is anticipated.
*   **QCO Impact:** This impact is expected to persist for "another three to four quarters" as the company navigates the certification process and potential reliance on imports. The government is extending deadlines, but the underlying capacity issue for BIS certification persists.
*   **Profitability Margin:** The 12%-15% PAT margin target remains. Improvement is contingent on resolving QCO impacts (estimated 3-4 quarters) and market development. Management acknowledges that the current margin levels are at the lower end of their desired band and that current pressures are not one-off but cyclical engineering industry impacts.
*   **Large Orders Pipeline:** Opportunities exist, but decisions are delayed. Specific quantification of future large orders is not provided due to policy. Conversions are anticipated in Q3 and Q4, particularly in traction and rail.
*   **Competition:** Acknowledged intensified competition, particularly in Motion and some Process Automation segments. ABB aims to selectively participate in orders and avoid "toxic orders."
*   **Robotics Business:** The divestment of the global robotics business to SoftBank has been completed. For India, assets will be evaluated by the Board for potential separation into a new company, subject to ABB India Board approval and due process.
*   **NVIDIA Engagement:** ABB parent is working with NVIDIA for AI data centers. ABB India will naturally have access to these technologies locally if there are no geographical restrictions, potentially increasing TAM in the data center market. Specific timelines for product availability in India are not yet detailed.
*   **Revenue Growth (CY2026):** Ambition remains to be in the double-digit corridor. While acknowledging delays in mobility orders and the pace of other segments, management expects the robust backlog and engineered-to-order (ETO) business in Electrification and Motion to drive revenue growth. Process Automation is acknowledged as cyclical.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** ABB is actively looking for large ticket inorganic options globally and locally to complement existing portfolios and enter new market segments.
