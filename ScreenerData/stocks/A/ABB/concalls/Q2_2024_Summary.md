## Executive Overview

ABB India delivered a strong Q2 2024, demonstrating sustained growth and operational excellence across its four business segments: Electrification, Motion, Process Automation, and Robotics & Discrete Automation. Orders and revenues continued their upward trajectory, driven by robust demand in emerging sectors and underlying strength in the Indian economy. The company reported a significant expansion in Profit After Tax (PAT) due to a favorable revenue mix and superior order execution. ABB India's strategic focus on localization, product portfolio enhancement, and deep market penetration into Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities continues to yield positive results, supported by a strong order backlog providing excellent revenue visibility. The company also reaffirmed its commitment to sustainability, making progress in emission reduction and water positivity initiatives.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders Received (Q2 2024):** INR 3,400 crores, up 13% YoY.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q2 2024):** INR 9,500 crores, up 23% YoY. This backlog comprises approximately 40-45% long-gestation projects and 55-60% short-cycle orders.
*   **Revenue Growth (Q2 2024):** INR 2,800 crores, up 13% YoY. The company noted a potential postponement of INR 200 crores due to election and budget timing.
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT) Growth (Q2 2024):** INR 594 crores, up 51% YoY.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) Growth (Q2 2024):** Up 50% YoY.
*   **Material Costs (Q2 2024):** 57% of revenue, a significant improvement from previous periods (63% and 59.8%).
*   **Cash Position (Q2 2024):** INR 4,872 crores, after dividend payout.
*   **Exports (Q2 2024):** Orders grew by 39% YoY, contributing 12% of total revenue.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Operational EBITDA up 64% for the quarter. PBIT margin stood at 21%.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Alignment:** ABB India's portfolio is strategically aligned with India's economic growth drivers, including infrastructure, industrialization, digitization, and manufacturing. The company operates across 23 market segments through 18 business divisions.
*   **High-Growth Segments:** Continued strong momentum in Data Centers, Railways, Metros, and the energy sector. The transportation sector, driven by railways and metros, remains a key contributor to order intake.
*   **Geographical Reach:** Ongoing focus on deeper penetration into Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets, leveraging channel partners.
*   **Sustainability & ESG:** Continued focus on Scope 1 & 2 GHG emissions reduction, with progress on water positivity and a target for two "Zero Waste to Landfill" units by 2024. Supplier sustainability training is also in progress.
*   **Product Portfolio & Innovation:** Introduction of IE4 cast iron super premium efficiency motors, IE3 aluminum motors, and the AquaMaster electromagnetic flowmeter. Partnership with Witt India for tunnel ventilation solutions.
*   **Premiumization & Customer Mindset Shift:** Post-COVID, customers are increasingly prioritizing quality, reliability, and support over price, benefiting ABB's offerings.
*   **Energy Transition:** ABB is actively participating in renewable energy generation, EV charging infrastructure, and green hydrogen projects through its diverse product offerings.
*   **Services Growth:** Consistent growth in service revenues is a key contributor to profitability, with dedicated service business units across divisions.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** Achieved 13% YoY growth, demonstrating strong underlying demand and effective market penetration, aligning with stated positive market momentum.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Delivered 13% YoY growth. While strong, the noted INR 200 crore postponement due to elections and budget timing indicates a minor deviation from potentially higher expected revenue, though management characterized it as a temporary shift.
*   **PAT Growth:** Substantial 50% YoY growth, consistent with management's focus on operational excellence and revenue mix.
*   **Material Costs:** Achieved a significant reduction to 57% of revenue, demonstrating successful cost management and optimization efforts, well beyond previous levels. This was attributed to a combination of external (revenue mix, price push) and internal (operational efficiency, localization) factors.
*   **Cash Position:** Maintained a strong cash position of INR 4,872 crores post-dividend.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Reported continued progress on sustainability goals, indicating consistency with stated commitments.
*   **Order Backlog:** Maintained strong growth with a 23% YoY increase, providing excellent revenue visibility.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Manufacturing expansion will continue to be incremental, focusing on productivity improvements within existing spaces and sectional expansion. New, more sophisticated facilities are being planned for units with rapid growth (e.g., Bangalore plant), with mandates for both domestic and export markets. Specific plans are in place for Electrification (Nashik, ELDS, ELSP, ELSB), Motion (railways, metro segment motors), Process Automation (new location), and Robotics (continued investment). Land banks in Baroda and Faridabad are available for future capacity additions.
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Management aims for stable profitability moving forward, supported by higher capacity utilization, better cost ratios, and continued focus on services and localization. The benefits from past raw material price advantages are expected to thin down as prices stabilize.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Strong conviction in steady growth across heavy industry, light industry, transport, and infrastructure segments, despite potential cycles and global impacts. Tier 2 and Tier 3 city growth is expected to positively impact base orders. Robotics market expected to see constant growth.
*   **Order Backlog Mix:** Order backlog has 40-45% long-cycle projects (15-18 month execution) and 55-60% short-cycle orders (6-9 month execution).
*   **New Management Mandate:** The new Group CEO, Morten Wierod, has a mandate for growth while maintaining profitability, building on the previous focus on stability and profitability.
*   **Motor Efficiency:** Over 52-53% of ABB India's motor production is IE3 and IE4, significantly exceeding current Indian norms (IE2).
*   **ABB India's Value Proposition:** Management believes ABB India is 20-25 years behind China's growth trajectory from 20-25 years ago, indicating a substantial runway for growth and demand expansion, with industry willing to pay for world-class quality and reliability.
*   **Service Revenue Focus:** Continued growth in service revenues is expected to be a significant contributor to profitability.
*   **Price Variation in Backlog:** Long-cycle orders (40-45%) typically have price variation mechanisms. Short-cycle orders (55-60%) are predominantly fixed-price, with some exceptions.
*   **Commodity/Currency Impact:** Current margin expansion reflects the advantage of higher booking prices due to past raw material costs on fixed-price contracts. This advantage is expected to diminish as prices stabilize. Currency stability also plays a role in mitigating deflationary pressures.
