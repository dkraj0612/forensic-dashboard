## Executive Overview

ABB India demonstrated strong Q1 2022 performance, with orders up 26% and revenues up 21% year-over-year. Management highlighted robust order growth across all divisions, driven by a broad market segment focus and increased penetration in Tier II/III cities. The company continues to benefit from its energy efficiency solutions and digitalization initiatives. ABB India also voluntarily published its Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) as an early adopter. Despite facing material cost headwinds and supply chain disruptions, the company maintained a strong cash position and operational efficiencies, leading to double-digit PBT percentage before exceptional items. Strategic investments in manufacturing capacity and a continued focus on sustainability underscore ABB India's commitment to long-term growth.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders (Q1 2022):** INR 2,300 crores, representing the strongest first quarter order intake in calendar year history, driven by base orders and customer connect.
*   **Revenue (Q1 2022):** Up 21% year-over-year, though sequentially down 6% due to execution scheduling.
*   **Profitability (Q1 2022):** Double-digit PBT percentage before exceptional items achieved, driven by capacity utilization and price pass-on strategies.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q1 2022):** INR 5,230 crores, executable in the coming quarters.
*   **Cash Position (End of Q1 2022):** Strong and strengthening cash position, with DSOs reduced compared to Q4 2021. Inventory increased consciously to manage supply disruptions.
*   **Divisional Performance:**
    *   **Electrification:** Robust growth in orders and revenues, with improved profitability due to product business advantages and price increases.
    *   **Motion:** Strong, consistent performance in orders, revenue, and profitability, supported by a solid backlog, channel contribution, and export growth.
    *   **Process Automation:** Base volumes maintained with a good mix of services and orders, despite being a project-dependent business facing cyclicality in core sectors.
    *   **Robotics and Automation:** Kick-up in orders, with continued fast growth expected in this small but significant business.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Segment Focus:** Participation across 23 market segments, with a focus on growth areas and leveraging strong customer connect in Tier II/III cities.
*   **Export Growth:** Strong traction in exports (+33%), with increasing market allocations from global group for products manufactured in India.
*   **Services Backbone:** Continued strong support from services, leveraging the installed base for reliability and availability of customer assets.
*   **Process Industries & Optimization:** Seeing an uptick in demand from steel, cement, chemical, and oil & gas sectors, with solid projects forming.
*   **Digitalization & ESG:** Commitment to Sustainability in Practice with a 10-point agenda. Achieved 100% renewable energy in all campuses in 2022. Green certifications (Platinum/Gold) obtained for multiple locations. Nelamangala declared water positive. Focus on waste recycling and water conservation.
*   **Social Impact:** Initiatives focused on community development in areas of presence, including infrastructure improvement, health benefits through mobile vans, and scholarship programs for meritorious girls.
*   **Channel Expansion:** Strategic focus on expanding reach in Tier II/III cities through channel partners, empowering them with technical training and new product portfolios.
*   **Product Portfolio:** Continued expansion of product offerings, particularly in LV motors, and development of new products like flameproof motors.
*   **New Factory:** Announced new expanded digital substation products and system factory in Baroda.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Order Growth:** Management's expectation of strong order growth was met with a 26% YoY increase in Q1 2022, the strongest first quarter in history.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved 21% YoY revenue growth, aligning with the positive outlook.
*   **Profitability:** Double-digit PBT percentage before exceptional items was achieved, demonstrating effective management of cost headwinds through price realization and capacity utilization. The stated goal of maintaining credible double-digit PBT margins was met.
*   **Cash Position:** Management's emphasis on cash as a focus area is reflected in the continued solid and strengthening cash position and reduced DSOs.
*   **Sustainability Initiatives:** The proactive approach to sustainability and ESG was evident with the publication of the BRSR report and achieving 100% renewable energy across campuses. Green certifications further validate these efforts.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** While specific capex numbers for the next two years are not yet disclosed, management indicated ongoing organic capacity expansion and exploration of inorganic opportunities, aligning with the strategy of utilizing cash for growth.
*   **Turbocharger Business Divestment:** The sale of the turbocharger business was executed, with an exceptional item of INR 293 crores reported.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **2022 Outlook:** Expects continued demand for energy efficiency solutions and a focus on flawless execution of the order backlog. Acknowledges potential headwinds from supply chain disruptions, geopolitical impacts, and commodity price volatility. Will monitor interest cost and inflation.
*   **Margin Focus:** Aim to stabilize and consistently achieve double-digit PBT margins. Vision to reach double-digit PAT margins in the medium term, contingent on potential government corporate tax relaxations.
*   **Cash Management:** Consolidate and build cash position to fuel growth and maintain agility in volatile markets.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Ongoing organic expansion and evaluation of inorganic opportunities. Specific capex announcements planned for October.
*   **Sustainability Timelines:** Committed to achieving 100% renewable energy in all campuses in 2022. Working towards water positivity at other locations and zero waste to landfill.
*   **New Business Areas:** Energy efficiency and digitalization continue to be key focus areas. Robotics is identified as a high-growth segment with significant potential for expansion.
*   **E-Mobility Division:** The parent company's e-mobility division listing is a global initiative with no direct impact on ABB India at this time.
*   **Employee Cost:** Aim to maintain employee expenses within 8% to 9% of sales, balancing headcount with automation and robotization.
*   **Process Automation Business:** To be viewed over a three or four-quarter rolling average due to its cyclical and project-dependent nature.
*   **Market Segment Contribution:** Will share revenue contribution breakdown for high, moderate, and low growth segments at a later stage.
*   **FOREX Gain:** Management will provide a note on Q1 FOREX gains.
