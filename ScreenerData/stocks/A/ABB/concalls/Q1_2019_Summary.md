## Executive Overview

ABB India reported a "resilient and consistent performance" for Q1 2019, driven by strong revenue growth of 18% year-over-year (YoY) to INR 1,850 crore, primarily fueled by execution from its order backlog. Profitability saw a significant uplift, with Profit Before Tax (PBT) and Profit After Tax (PAT) surging by 98% and 95% YoY, respectively. Operational EBITA also demonstrated robust growth of 111% YoY to INR 125 crore, leading to an improved margin of 6.8%. The company continues to emphasize its commitment to digitalization through ABB Ability™ solutions, evidenced by expanding its digital portfolio and customer engagements. Strategic partnerships and key order wins, particularly in exports and services, are highlighted as drivers of accelerated growth. Management's focus on operational execution and managing the ongoing transformation, including the PG demerger, remains central to its strategy.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Digitalization Focus:** ABB Ability™ portfolio expansion and increased customer digital solution engagements (tripled). Leverages digital technology for resurgent sectors and smart factories, including AI, ML, and digital twins.
*   **Portfolio Transformation:** PG demerger on track, with four leading businesses operational from April 1, 2019.
*   **Market Penetration:** Growth across divisions and majority business lines, with continued growth in base orders (up 17%). Attractive growth and stronger margins are key objectives.
*   **Sectoral Focus:**
    *   **Power:** Digitization of power distribution technologies, compact substations, and RMUs. Power plant digital automation.
    *   **Production:** Advanced applications for O&G, digital solutions for safety and productivity, drives and soft starters for edible oil.
    *   **Work:** Technologies for skill building and workspace uplift, end-to-end automation for data centers, building automation for hospitality, switching/power safety for critical installations, automation for retail.
    *   **Live:** Backbone for smarter and sustainable installations, customized traction solutions, power distribution panels, GIS for metro rail, marine customer day.
    *   **Move:** Sustainable mobility solutions, traction solutions for railways.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Continued customer interactions, partner workshops, and big bang events at the Ability Center. Focus on Industry 4.0 in tier-2 cities.
*   **Growth in Exports & Services:** Significant growth in export orders (38% YoY) and service revenue (21% YoY) are identified as key growth accelerators.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Resilient and Consistent Performance:** The Q1 2019 results, characterized by robust revenue growth and significant profit improvement, align with management's description of a "resilient and consistent performance."
*   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved 18% YoY revenue growth, exceeding the previous quarter's narrative which suggested revenue growth was "slightly less than running rate of previous two quarters." This indicates a strong rebound in execution.
*   **Profitability Improvement:** PBT and PAT surged by 98% and 95% respectively, with Operational EBITA growing by 111% and margins improving. This directly addresses the previous quarter's focus on improving profitability and reflects "capacity utilization and revenue mix provided a major step-up in profitability."
*   **Digitalization Drive:** The transcript consistently emphasizes the expansion of ABB Ability™ and increased digital solution engagements, fulfilling the strategic imperative from the prior period.
*   **Exports and Services Growth:** The stated 38% YoY growth in export orders and 21% YoY growth in service revenue directly support management's comments about accelerating growth in these areas.
*   **PG Demerger:** The statement that the "PG demerger on track" and "Four leading businesses operational April 1, 2019" confirms progress on this key strategic initiative.
*   **Market Conditions:** While management acknowledges market challenges ("effects of revenue mix, adverse forex & market price challenges in solar," "Delayed decision in system orders metals & Oil & Gas Sector"), the strong execution and growth in key segments demonstrate an ability to navigate these conditions effectively, shifting focus to opportunities.
