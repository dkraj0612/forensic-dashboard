## Executive Overview

ABB India concluded 2021 with a robust Q4 performance, demonstrating strong year-over-year growth across all key financial metrics. The company secured significant orders, reflecting broad-based demand and successful execution of its backlog. Management highlighted increased productivity through smart factory initiatives and a growing focus on energy efficiency, positioning ABB India to capitalize on government-led growth drivers. The divestment of the turbocharger business was approved, aligning with global group strategy. While facing material cost headwinds, particularly in the Electrification and Motion divisions, the company maintained profitability through improved price realization, capacity utilization, and a favorable product mix. Strategic investments in manufacturing capacity, sustainability initiatives, and digital solutions like e-mart underscore ABB India's commitment to long-term growth and operational excellence.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders (Q4 2021):** INR 2,243 crore (highest in several quarters, includes a ~INR 400 crore order from an oil customer and steel customers).
    *   Significant growth compared to Q4 2020 and sequential growth from Q3 2021.
*   **Revenue (Full Year 2021 vs. 2020):** Strong year-over-year growth across all parameters.
    *   Benefited from systems and services comeback, stable oil & gas and power generation activity, and traction in transport infrastructure, buildings, renewables, data centers, and railways.
*   **Profitability (Full Year 2021):**
    *   Profit Before Tax (PBT): 20% growth compared to the previous quarter; substantially higher year-on-year.
    *   Profit After Tax (PAT): Higher year-on-year due to exceptional items in the previous quarter (business sale, plant monetization).
    *   Operational Margins: Focus on clear margins from business lines, though impacted by commodity and FOREX volatility.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q4 2021):** Strong order backlog, covering approximately seven months of revenue, with project revenues to be spread over the project schedule.
*   **Cash Position (End of Q4 2021):** Strong cash balance, reflecting profits earned during the year, available for fueling future growth. DSOs substantially reduced year-on-year.
*   **Divisional Performance:**
    *   **Electrification:** Strong performance in orders and revenues, with a backlog of INR 1,316 crore executable in the coming months. Benefited from product mix and improved price realization.
    *   **Motion:** Strong growth in the first three quarters, with revenues picking up significantly. Faced material cost headwinds but managed through global supply chain integration. Good mix of services, exports, and system orders contributed to strong profitability.
    *   **Process Automation:** Turnaround year with improved orders, revenues, and profitability. Strong cash generation reflected in results.
    *   **Robotics and Automation:** Strong growth in Q1 and Q2, making up for a muted previous year. Good backlog of INR 130 crore, expected to be augmented.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Productivity and Smart Factories:** Continued investment in smart factory initiatives and manufacturing excellence, leading to substantial productivity gains (e.g., 30% increased manufacturing in the same space at Nelamangala factory). Awarded for Smart Factory and Manufacturing Excellence by Frost and Sullivan.
*   **Energy Efficiency Focus:** Flagged energy efficiency movement as a "sweet spot," driven by global demand for energy-efficient assets and infrastructure.
*   **Digitalization and Services:** Growing uptake of short-cycle orders, systems, and services. ABB e-mart shows strong growth, enabling broader reach into Tier II/III cities.
*   **Infrastructure and Government Initiatives:** Partnered with Indore Smart City for continuous power supply and provides solutions for clean water supply (e.g., Surat). Anticipating CAPEX push from the government in data centers, food & beverage, water & wastewater, transport, warehouse & logistics, electronics, pharma & healthcare, power distribution, building & infrastructure, marine & port, cement, metals & mining.
*   **Manufacturing Capacity & Exports:** Expanded capacity for low voltage (LV) motors at Faridabad (powered by renewable energy). Growing export mandate for several divisions, serving markets from North America to Southeast Asia. Baroda factory expansion planned for large machines for export.
*   **Sustainability & ESG:**
    *   **Green Buildings:** Committed to gold or platinum standard green factory certifications (Nelamangala: platinum, Peenya: gold).
    *   **Water Positive:** Nelamangala plant declared water positive (index 1.24).
    *   **Green Power:** Aiming for 70-85% green power by end of 2022.
    *   **Waste Recycling:** At 95% waste recycling.
    *   **Net Zero:** Committed to net zero discharge from plants by 2025-2026.
    *   **Safety & Health:** Weekly and daily focus on safety, with a 98% hazard resolution rate. Health and well-being prioritized.
*   **CSR Initiatives:** Sponsoring education for meritorious girls from poor families, with mentorship and internship opportunities.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Productivity Gains & Smart Factory:** Management's emphasis on smart factory investments and productivity gains is directly supported by the Frost & Sullivan award and the specific example of the Nelamangala factory's performance improvement.
*   **Energy Efficiency Momentum:** The "sweet spot" positioning for energy efficiency is validated by the positive response and growing demand mentioned.
*   **Revenue and Order Growth:** The strong Q4 results and full-year performance demonstrate a "strong growth" narrative, with specific mentions of systems and services comeback, and traction in key sectors.
*   **Profitability and Margin Management:** While acknowledging material cost headwinds, management successfully offset them with improved price realization, capacity utilization, and favorable product mix, leading to "strong profitability" in divisions like Motion and Electrification. The focus on "clear margins from business lines" is evident.
*   **Sustainability Investments:** Investments in green factories and renewable energy are yielding results with certifications and increased green power utilization targets, validating the commitment to ESG.
*   **Cash Position:** The statement of a "strong cash position" is backed by the robust cash balance and reduced DSOs, indicating efficient working capital management.
*   **Divestment of Turbocharger Business:** The board's approval of the divestment to a wholly-owned subsidiary aligns with the global group's strategy and is a concrete action taken.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **2022 Outlook:** Expects less COVID disturbances, agility to manage them, and a slow but steady growth momentum. Focus on flawless execution of the strong backlog.
*   **Margin Focus:** Continue to focus sharply on margins and stabilize them. Aiming for operating margins from business lines, clear of extraordinary income. Will combat commodity and FOREX volatility through supply chain actions and hedging.
*   **Order Mix:** Aim for a good mix of orders from services, exports, and fast-growing segments with better margins.
*   **Cash Management:** Consolidate cash position, maintain focus on cash collections, and maintain the "cash over revenue" mantra.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Ample land adjacent to existing plants for doubling capacity. Brownfield expansions planned for Nashik (heavy localization of products with strong market position), with similar investments for robotics and expansion in Maneja and Faridabad.
*   **Green Initiatives Timeline:** Aiming for 70-85% green power by the end of 2022. Committed to net zero discharge from plants by 2025-2026.
*   **New Business Areas:**
    *   **Warehouse Automation:** Global acquisition to be integrated locally; seeing good traction in America and Europe.
    *   **Building Automation:** Seen as a "sweet spot" for portfolio, expected to gain traction due to ESG and energy efficiency movements.
*   **Robotics and Automation Market:** Projected to expand significantly (multiplier effect) due to low current base and increasing adoption in automotive, electronics manufacturing, food & beverage, warehousing, and battery manufacturing.
*   **EV Charging:** Demand picking up in India as EV availability increases, though not at the same level as Europe and America.
*   **Turbocharger Business Sale:** Process of forming a wholly-owned subsidiary is underway, expected to complete in the next two to three months. More color expected in Q1 2022 results.
*   **E-Mart Sales:** Continue to evolve the e-mart platform to improve reach and customer selection, while managing behavior change and ensuring proper product fit through configurators.
*   **Competitiveness:** Acknowledges increasing competition with market expansion, but focus remains on customer proposition and control.
