## Executive Overview

ABB India delivered a Q2 FY25 performance marked by 5% YoY growth in base orders, though total order growth was subdued due to the absence of large orders seen in the prior year. Revenue reached an all-time high for a second quarter at INR 3,175 crores, a 12% increase YoY, demonstrating strong execution capability. However, profitability was impacted by several one-off factors, including compliance with QCO guidelines leading to higher import content, forex volatility, and specific project-related costs in the Electrification segment. The company celebrated 30 years of listing on the NSE, highlighting its long-term shareholder value creation. Despite current market softness and challenges, management remains cautiously optimistic, emphasizing a strong order backlog of INR 10,064 crores and a focus on long-term growth drivers like electrification, digitalization, and sustainability.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Orders Received (Q2 2025):** Base orders grew 5% YoY. Total order growth was impacted by the absence of large orders from the prior year.
*   **Order Backlog (End of Q2 2025):** INR 10,064 crores.
*   **Revenue Growth (Q2 2025):** Up 12% YoY to INR 3,175 crores (all-time high for Q2).
*   **EBITDA (Q2 2025):** 13% (lower than the previous quarter due to specific impacts).
*   **Cash Balance (End of Q2 2025):** INR 5,054 crores (after dividend distribution).
*   **Profitability (PBT Margin):** 21% (similar to the previous year's quarter, but profitability was impacted by one-off items detailed below).
*   **Dividend:** Interim dividend of INR 9.77 per equity share declared.
*   **Exports:** Constitute 12% of revenue, a slight increase from previous periods.
*   **Material Costs:** Impacted by higher import content due to QCO compliance and forex volatility.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Business Areas:** Continues to operate across Process Automation, Motion, Robotics, and Electrification, catering to diverse industries.
*   **Localization & Local-for-Local Strategy:** Emphasis on deepening local supply chains and manufacturing to serve the domestic market, a strategy that has proved beneficial.
*   **Sustainability Focus:** On track with GHG emission reduction targets (87.5% reduction vs. 2019 baseline), aiming for "zero waste to landfill" campuses and water positivity.
*   **Market Penetration:** Continued focus on Tier-II and Tier-III cities, showing improved market share in these regions.
*   **Emerging Segments:** Identifying and engaging with high-growth segments like transport, buildings & infrastructure, data centers, and renewable energy.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Deepening connect with customers across various market segments, including niche and emerging areas.
*   **Product Portfolio:** Introduction of new products tailored for the Indian market and continuous efforts to enhance energy efficiency and technological offerings.
*   **Service Business:** Aiming to increase the service mix to 15% from the current 12-13%.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Order Inflow:** While base orders showed a positive 5% YoY growth, the absence of large orders from the previous year led to a more moderate overall order intake. Management reiterates the cyclical nature of large orders and maintains a positive outlook on converting the existing pipeline. The narrative around "softness in large orders" continues, aligning with previous quarters.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Achieved a record Q2 revenue, demonstrating strong execution capability (12% YoY growth), which is a positive deviation from the more modest revenue growth in Q1.
*   **Profitability:** PBT margin remained at 21%, similar to the prior year's quarter. However, operational profitability was significantly impacted by specific, non-recurring events (QCO compliance, forex, one-off costs). Management transparently disclosed these impacts and is working on mitigation strategies. The guidance for PAT margin (12%-15%) remains, but the achievability in the near term is subject to resolving these operational challenges.
*   **Order Backlog:** The order backlog continues to grow robustly (implied by INR 10,064 crores), providing good revenue visibility for the next 18-24 months.
*   **Sustainability:** Progress on sustainability goals continues to be highlighted, demonstrating consistent commitment.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Market Outlook:** Management expresses cautious optimism, expecting a gradual recovery in large project investments. Government CAPEX is picking up, but private CAPEX remains cautious due to global uncertainties. Emerging trends like energy transition, digitalization, and data centers are seen as future growth drivers.
*   **Second-Half Outlook:** Expects the market to take time to fully recover; mid-term (next year onwards) momentum is anticipated to return.
*   **QCO Impact:** The impact of QCO guidelines on higher import content and inventory levels is expected to linger for the "next couple of quarters" as the company manages a judicious mix of imported and localized components while meeting customer commitments.
*   **Profitability Margin Target:** The 12%-15% PAT margin target remains, but near-term achievement is contingent on resolving QCO impacts and managing forex volatility. Management indicated it will be a "mixed bag" in the next quarter.
*   **Price Realization:** While inflationary pressures exist, management indicates the market should absorb price increases, particularly for longer-cycle orders. Volatility in forex may pose a challenge for short-cycle product orders.
*   **Large Orders Pipeline:** While not fully dried out, the pipeline for large orders is considered reasonable, with conversions anticipated in Q3 and Q4, particularly in traction and rail segments.
*   **Competition:** Acknowledges increased participation from Chinese manufacturers, especially in heavy equipment and Process Automation segments, and price competition. ABB will selectively participate, avoiding "toxic orders." No significant competition from Chinese imports has been seen in Electrification and Motion segments.
*   **Electrification Segment Impact:** The specific hit taken was due to corrections on installed equipment to comply with local standards, a typical engineering challenge that has been addressed.
*   **Exports:** No significant impact expected from US tariffs, as exports to the US are a minor portion of the business, and the company's strategy is "local for local."
*   **Base Orders vs. Large Orders:** Management clarifies that base orders (MTS, MTO) are robust and driven by channelization and product localization, while large system orders are cyclic and dependent on CAPEX decisions. The current trend reflects a stronger base order performance contrasted with delayed large project decisions.
*   **Recovery Timeline:** Business leaders indicated delays rather than reductions in prospect pipelines. A pick-up is hoped for towards the end of the year.
