## Executive Overview

ABB India delivered a robust Q4 and full-year 2018 performance, demonstrating significant growth across key financial metrics, primarily driven by a strategic focus on digitalization and strong execution across its business divisions. Orders surged by 16% YoY to INR 6,729 crore for the full year, with notable strength in services (up 27% YoY) and exports (up 50% YoY) in Q4. Revenue also saw healthy growth of 10% YoY to INR 6,690 crore for the full year, supported by quick conversion of its strong order backlog which stood at INR 5,077 crore. Profitability improved significantly, with PBT growing 67% YoY and PAT by 58% YoY in Q4, reflecting improved operational EBITA margins. The company continues to leverage ABB Ability™ digital solutions as a key differentiator across its offerings, fostering collaborations and partnerships to drive innovation and digitalization in India. ABB India's strategic positioning as a global engineering and digitalization hub, coupled with its commitment to localization and investment, underpins its confidence in future growth prospects.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Digitalization Focus:** ABB Ability™ digital solutions are a key differentiator across all businesses, enabling enhanced productivity, growth, and operational efficiency. This is supported by initiatives in AI, ML, digital twins, and remote monitoring.
*   **Portfolio Transformation:** The company is actively managing its portfolio, with a stated focus on digitalization and the divestment of Power Grids. Simplification of the business model and structure is also a priority.
*   **Collaborations & Partnerships:** Strategic alliances with institutions like IIT Roorkee and NITI Aayog, alongside thought leadership in AI policy and EV initiatives, are driving innovation and market engagement.
*   **Market Penetration:** Growth is being achieved across divisions and business lines, supported by increased orders in both base and large project categories.
*   **Global Hub for India:** ABB India is positioned as a global engineering and digitalization hub, contributing to global projects and leveraging its business services for worldwide efficiency and innovation.
*   **Sectoral Focus:** Significant opportunities are being pursued in power distribution modernization (IPDS, UDAY), smart cities, industrial automation for safety and productivity, green buildings, sustainable mobility (EV charging, rail), and data center management.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Extensive customer outreach through technology days, round tables, and showcasing capabilities across infrastructure, utilities, industry, and transportation sectors.
