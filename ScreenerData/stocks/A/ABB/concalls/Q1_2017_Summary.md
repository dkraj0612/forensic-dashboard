## Executive Overview

ABB India's Q1 2017 performance showcased robust order growth of 28% YoY, significantly exceeding expectations and marking a strong start to the year. This growth was primarily driven by government initiatives and spending across various sub-sectors, complemented by selective industry investments. Key areas contributing to this performance included Indian Railways, Renewables (solar and wind), Transmission & Distribution, and emerging opportunities in Oil & Gas (Euro-VI compliance) and Cement sectors. The company also highlighted increased customer engagement in digitalization and a 40% YoY growth in its Services segment, alongside a substantial 120% increase in Exports, bolstered by a large systems order from Bangladesh. Despite the acknowledged short-term impacts of demonetization (which proved unexpectedly positive for cash realization) and the upcoming GST implementation posing potential initial trade disruptions, ABB India maintains a positive outlook, driven by strong underlying themes and a growing order backlog of ₹12,000 crores, providing good revenue visibility. The company is actively preparing for the transformative GST regime and the global integration of the B&R acquisition.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Order Intake:**
    *   Q1 2017: ₹2,342 crores, a 28% increase YoY.
    *   Base Orders: Grew by 17%.
    *   Large Orders: Increased five-fold.
    *   Services: Revenue grew by approximately 40% YoY.
    *   Exports: Increased by 120% YoY, contributing to 15% of revenue.
*   **Revenue:**
    *   Q1 2017: Reported 8% growth.
*   **Profitability:**
    *   Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by 3% YoY.
    *   Operational EBITDA: Showed 8% growth, though the reported EBITDA margin was flat at 6.1%.
    *   Segmental Performance (New Accounting Standards - IndAS impact noted):
        *   Robotics and Motion: Net Sales ₹461 crores.
        *   Electrification Products (EP): Net Sales ₹644 crores.
        *   Industry Automation (formerly Process Automation): Net Sales ₹291 crores.
        *   Power Grids (PG): Net Sales ₹820 crores.
*   **Order Backlog:**
    *   Q1 2017: ₹12,023 crores, a 50% increase YoY.
    *   Breakdown: Approximately 50% from large orders (including HVDC order of ₹3,500 crores) and the balance from short and medium-cycle orders.
*   **Cash Flow & Balance Sheet:**
    *   DSO: Improved to 126 days from the previous quarter.
    *   Net Cash Position: Strong, with collections on RP800 contract advance.
    *   Debt-Free Status Maintained.
*   **Expenses:**
    *   Material Cost: Stable at 63.8%.
    *   Personnel Expenses: ₹193 crores, slightly higher due to IndAS actuarial valuation impact.
    *   Other Expenses: Impacted by Q4 accruals and one-time ECL impact from IndAS.
*   **New Accounting Standards (IndAS):**
    *   Significant transition changes noted, including Expected Credit Loss (ECL), embedded derivatives, revenue recognition, and fair valuation of provisions. These impacts are expected to continue for four quarters.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Government Initiatives as Key Drivers:** Continued reliance on government spending and initiatives across power, infrastructure, and transportation sectors.
*   **Digitalization Focus:** Increased customer engagement and offering of digital solutions for enhanced productivity.
*   **Sectoral Growth Focus:**
    *   **Power & Renewables:** Addressing thermal power plant retirement, expanding renewable capacity, and investing in grid modernization.
    *   **Indian Railways:** Leveraging significant investment in electric coaches and solar power at stations.
    *   **Oil & Gas:** Capitalizing on investments for Euro-VI compliance.
    *   **Cement:** Benefiting from housing and infrastructure projects driving sector investment.
    *   **Ports:** Modernization and adoption of new technologies in ports.
    *   **Food & Beverage:** Positive outlook post-demonetization impact.
*   **Export Hub Leverage:** Continued focus on leveraging India as an export base, with strong performance driven by a large systems order.
*   **Services Growth:** Maintaining strong growth in the services segment.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Continued focus on localization and cost management to maintain material costs.
*   **GST Preparedness:** Proactive preparation for the Goods and Services Tax implementation to ensure a smooth transition for customers and suppliers.
*   **B&R Acquisition Integration:** Anticipating the completion of the B&R acquisition and future integration plans.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Order Growth:** Management achieved a strong 28% order growth in Q1 2017, significantly exceeding previous periods and aligning with the positive momentum discussed at the end of 2016. The emphasis on government initiatives and "high-technology" orders remains consistent.
    *   **Profitability Improvement:** While PAT growth was moderate at 3%, the commentary highlighted operational EBITDA growth of 8% and a stable material cost. The IndAS transition created some noise, particularly on reported margins, but the underlying operational performance appears robust. Management has consistently emphasized operational excellence and cost management.
    *   **Services Growth:** The reported 40% YoY growth in services aligns with management's stated focus on re-energizing this segment.
    *   **Exports Growth:** The significant 120% YoY growth in exports aligns with management's strategy to leverage India as an export hub and is a testament to their execution.
    *   **Market Narrative:** Management's commentary remains positive, acknowledging ongoing government support and sector-specific growth drivers. They also acknowledge potential short-term disruptions from GST but view it positively in the medium term. The previously noted concerns about private sector debt and NPA issues were reiterated as factors needing resolution for increased private investment traction.
*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Revenue Growth Outlook:** Expectation of revenue uptick due to the mixed order book (short, medium, and long cycle), with 8-10% growth seen as steady, and potential peaks from large project executions.
    *   **GST Implementation:** Anticipated initial trade disruption around July-August, but a medium-term positive impact for the nation and the company. ABB is establishing strong project teams for smooth transition.
    *   **HVDC Order Execution (RP800):** Execution and billing will be aligned with customer requirements and project schedule. The contract is for 36 months. Specific billing break-up not disclosed.
    *   **Power Grids (PG) Segment:** Expected to perform decently, with efforts to achieve performance similar to 2015. No significant headwinds on current backlog execution.
    *   **Railways Segment:** Contribution expected to be between 5% to 7% of revenue. Positive outlook due to modernization drive and adoption of new technologies.
    *   **Cement Sector:** Positive momentum seen, with definite inquiries for expansion and increased focus on productivity and efficiency from well-consolidated players.
    *   **Renewables Pricing:** While tariffs are falling, management believes customers prioritize ABB's high quality and reliability over lowest price, especially given the long lifecycle of solar farms. Pricing pressure exists, and ABB will focus on innovation in technology and cost reduction.
    *   **Solar Inverter Capacity:** Current factor capacity is 4.5 GW. ABB believes it holds approximately 45% of the installed base in India.
    *   **Euro-VI Compliance Opportunity:** Estimated $6 billion investment opportunity in the Oil & Gas sector, with a positive impact on ABB's electrical, process automation, and other portfolios. Specific order quantum not yet translated.
    *   **Export Trajectory:** Continued strong participation and engagement in global markets. Management views this as the "start of the story" for exports from India, without setting specific percentage targets, focusing on market engagement and sustainability.
    *   **IndAS Impact:** Expected to continue for the next four quarters. Specific quantification of the ECL impact is not being revealed at this point, but net profit reconciliations for previous periods are available.
    *   **Capacity Utilization:** 75% to 80%.
    *   **B&R Acquisition:** Expected to be completed by the second half of the year, with further integration information to be announced later.
