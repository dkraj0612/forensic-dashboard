## Executive Overview

ABB India reported a satisfactory Q3 2015, driven by a significant rebound in order intake. Despite ongoing challenges in the industrial production and credit environment, the company demonstrated operational resilience and strategic execution. Order growth of over 60% for the quarter was a key highlight, attributed to a focused product strategy and the emergence of delayed project decisions. Solar energy continued its strong growth trajectory, crossing the 2-gigawatt milestone. The services segment maintained its double-digit growth, indicating increased market activity in maintenance and operations. Management acknowledged that substantial revival in industrial investments is still some quarters away, contingent on capacity utilization and improved credit flow. The "cash over revenue" policy remained a factor, influencing revenue recognition in project-based businesses.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** Increased by 7% for the quarter, with most divisions showing growth except for Process Automation (PA) and Power Systems (PS), the latter due to project execution timing.
*   **Order Intake:** Grew by over 60% for the quarter and 11% year-to-date, a significant improvement driven by both product segments and project orders.
*   **Profitability:** Positively impacted by revenue growth and cost control, with material costs stable at 65%. Solar and Services segments contributed to improved profitability due to higher margins. Favorable Euro-USD exchange rates also provided a tailwind.
*   **Depreciation:** Higher by approximately INR 8 crores per quarter due to the adoption of new depreciation rates under the Companies Act 2013.
*   **Exports:** Contributed 12% to order inflow for the quarter, down from 15% in the previous year, reflecting a subdued global market.
*   **Forex Impact:** Minimal impact during the quarter due to favorable exchange rates.
*   **Debt Management:** The company is in the process of issuing INR 600 crores in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) to replace higher-cost debt with cheaper financing.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Product and Project Focus:** Continued emphasis on driving product sales while selectively pursuing attractive projects with disciplined order-taking.
*   **Solar Energy Growth:** Significant expansion in the solar sector, with the company crossing the 2 GW milestone and positioning itself to capitalize on government targets. ABB's equipment is present in over 50% of installed solar capacity in India.
*   **Services Segment:** Maintained double-digit order growth, indicating increased customer focus on maintenance and operational efficiency.
*   **Transmission & Distribution (T&D):** Remains an active sector with good growth potential, with a focus on increasing automation and intelligence in the distribution segment.
*   **"Make in India" and Domestic Manufacturing:** Continued commitment to local manufacturing, with expectations of increased traction on domestic manufacturing clauses in tenders.
*   **Smart Solutions:** Developing a portfolio of smart solutions including microgrids, EV charging, and IoT, with expectations of substantial business contribution in the next 4-5 years.
*   **Railways and Transportation:** Capitalizing on growth opportunities in the transportation sector, including upgrades to train systems and metros.
*   **Oil & Gas Sector:** Well-positioned to benefit from potential investments as the sector improves, though lower oil prices present a balancing factor.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Focus on continuous cost reduction and improving cost-effectiveness of solutions, aiming to bring down the cost curve faster than market prices.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Management achieved 7% revenue growth for the quarter, aligning with their expectation that even small revenue growth can lead to better profitability due to cost control.
    *   **Profitability Improvement:** Management's assertion of improved profitability due to cost control, indigenization, favorable forex, and growth in solar and services is supported by the financial results.
    *   **Order Intake:** The narrative of order intake rebounding was accurate, with a significant 60%+ growth reported for the quarter, driven by products and delayed projects. This is a positive shift from the previous quarter's focus on subdued order intake.
    *   **Market Challenges:** Management consistently described the industrial production and credit environment as sluggish, which remains an ongoing headwind. They reiterated that a substantial revival hinges on capacity utilization and credit flow.
    *   **Deferred Sales ("Cash Over Revenue"):** Management continues to adhere to the "cash over revenue" policy, noting its impact on revenue recognition in project businesses due to contractual conditions and payment confidence. This narrative is consistent.
    *   **Export Performance:** Management acknowledged subdued export order performance in the first half and noted a rebound in Q3 with improved project visibility. This aligns with the reported 12% contribution to orders.
    *   **Solar Growth:** Management's emphasis on solar growth was validated by crossing the 2 GW milestone and its contribution to profitability.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Industrial Revival Timeline:** Management reiterated that a substantial industrial revival is "a few quarters away," dependent on improved industrial production and credit to industry.
    *   **Solar Market Growth:** Expectation that the solar market will continue to be a fast-growing sector, with significant government targets for 2020.
    *   **Smart Solutions Contribution:** Anticipation that smart solutions will contribute substantially to the business over the next four to five years.
    *   **T&D Sector Growth:** Expectation of continued growth in the T&D sector, with a focus on increasing automation and intelligence in distribution.
    *   **HVDC Growth:** Expectation of growth in HVDC business driven by increasing renewable energy integration and green energy corridors.
    *   **Discom CAPEX Potential:** Significant potential for investment in the distribution segment once reforms and CAPEX reforms take hold, mirroring global trends where distribution accounts for the largest share of investment.
    *   **Cost Management:** Continuous effort to reduce costs and develop more cost-effective solutions, aiming to drive down the cost curve faster than market prices.
    *   **Export Market Prospects:** Seeing better prospects for export growth in surrounding Asian countries.
    *   **Global Strategy:** India is a cornerstone of ABB's global strategy, with increasing ownership of products, global support, and manufacturing in India.
    *   **Power Grids Division:** Reorganization into four divisions, with a new "Power Grids" division combining power and automation for grids.
