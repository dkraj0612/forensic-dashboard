## Executive Overview

ACME Solar Holdings Limited concluded FY26 with robust growth in both generation and battery storage capacity, driven by strong sector tailwinds and strategic execution. The company commissioned approximately 2.3 GW of Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) capacity to date, contributing significantly to daily revenue. Operational generation contracted capacity now stands at 2,990 MW. The under-construction portfolio expanded to 5.1 GW of peak power FDRE projects, with a total portfolio reaching 8,071 MW, requiring an estimated 17 GWh of BESS. Financially, Q4 FY26 saw total revenue of INR 705 crores (+31% YoY) and FY26 revenue of INR 2,507 crores (+59% YoY). EBITDA margins remained strong at over 90% for both the quarter and full year. PAT for FY26 was INR 498 crores with a margin of 19.9%. The company secured INR 15,000 crores in financing for under-construction projects and refinanced INR 3,300 crores, reducing interest rates by 150 basis points. The weighted average cost of debt for operational projects is 8.4%. ACME Solar is strategically focusing on central offtakers and BESS integration to enhance revenue, optimize working capital, and navigate evolving grid requirements.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aggressive BESS Integration:** Continued focus on commissioning large-scale BESS capacity (targeting 10 GWh total operational BESS) to utilize transmission infrastructure, capture tariff arbitrage, and provide peak power solutions. BESS is running on merchant and short-term contracts, delivering significant daily realization.
*   **Shift to Central offtakers & FDRE Projects:** Prioritizing projects with central counterparties (CTU connected) for improved payment security and reduced working capital cycles (14 days DSO). Increasing focus on FDRE projects incorporating battery storage to meet demand for dispatchable and peak power.
*   **Merchant Operations & Arbitrage:** Leveraging BESS for merchant sales and short-term contracts, capitalizing on price differentials between peak and non-peak hours. The company is also strategically using batteries to charge from the grid using conventional power (as per MNRE clarification) and sell at peak times, deferring solar capex until LTOA is operational to improve returns.
*   **Debt Optimization & Capital Deployment:** Proactive financing and refinancing efforts to reduce the cost of debt. Secured substantial financing for under-construction projects and achieved a 150 bps reduction in interest rates for refinanced debt.
*   **Strategic Portfolio Management:** Consciously building a portfolio with central offtakers to mitigate risks associated with state DISCOM payments. Focusing on projects with better transmission connectivity and earlier commissioning timelines to enhance attractiveness for PPA signings.
*   **Diversified Revenue Streams:** While focusing on long-term contracts, actively participating in short- and medium-term BESS opportunities to capitalize on market dynamics.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Technology Adoption:** Maintaining high plant availability and CUF, while exploring advanced solutions like BESS for grid stability and optimized revenue.
