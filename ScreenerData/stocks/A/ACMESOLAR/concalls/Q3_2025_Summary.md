## Executive Overview

ACME Solar Holdings Limited delivered a robust Q3 FY26, marked by continued capacity expansion, significant BESS ramp-up, and strong financial performance. The company commissioned 72 MW of wind capacity during the quarter, bringing its total operational portfolio to 2,962 MW and staying on track for its 450 MW FY26 target. Strategic foresight in procurement has allowed the company to secure 1.7 GW of modules and a large quantity of BESS for FY27 projects, realizing over 10% CAPEX savings despite global policy shifts like China's VAT export rebate withdrawal.

Financially, Q3 FY26 saw adjusted revenue increase by 54% YoY to INR 617 crores, with EBITDA margins improving to 91.5% from 89.6% last year. PAT stood at INR 114 crores. The company significantly optimized its debt profile, reducing the weighted average cost of debt for its operational portfolio to 8.45% (down over 100 basis points YoY), with overall debt at 8.6%. Balance sheet metrics improved further, with net operational debt to EBITDA at 4.2x.

Key developments include substantial progress in PPA signings, adding 450 MW this quarter (including 250 MW FDRE with NHPC at INR 4.33/unit and a 130 MW Round-the-Clock project with REMC at INR 4.35/unit), expanding the total PPA signed portfolio to 2.7 GW and overall contracted portfolio to 7,770 MW (including 16.0 GWh BESS). Management also upgraded its BESS commissioning guidance, now expecting 2 GWh to be operational in Q4 FY26 and an additional 2 GWh in Q1 FY27, with modular early deployment at operational sites. A one-time curtailment loss of INR 17.5 crores from the Sikar project due to temporary grid infrastructure issues was noted, but this has since been resolved.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated BESS Deployment:** Fast-tracking modular BESS installation at existing operational sites to unlock early value, optimize transmission infrastructure, avoid CAPEX, and ensure early cash flow while seamlessly integrating with FDRE projects.
*   **Proactive Procurement & CAPEX Optimization:** Leveraged market conditions to procure modules and BESS for FY27 capacity, achieving over 10% CAPEX savings and mitigating impacts from policy shifts like China's VAT export rebate withdrawal.
*   **PPA Diversification & Growth:** Continued focus on securing FDRE and RTC contracts, expanding the PPA signed portfolio with recent wins and strong near-term visibility. This is driven by the expiring ISTS waiver, encouraging states to procure before 2028.
*   **Debt Management & Credit Enhancement:** Sustained efforts in refinancing and credit rating upgrades led to a significant reduction in the weighted average cost of debt, enhancing financial flexibility and balance sheet strength.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Grid Stability:** Maintained high CUF and plant availability. Addressed project-specific curtailment issues (Sikar project resolved) and stated no revenue impact from grid-stability-related curtailments due to GNA regulations.
*   **Future-Ready Infrastructure:** Secured 7.5 GW of connectivity inventory for future bids, supporting long-term operational capacity targets (10 GW by 2030).
*   **Innovation & Technology Focus:** Actively exploring and investing in next-generation technologies (Perovskite cells), automation (robotic installation), and proprietary R&D for optimizing battery, solar, and wind integration to deliver cheaper baseload power.
