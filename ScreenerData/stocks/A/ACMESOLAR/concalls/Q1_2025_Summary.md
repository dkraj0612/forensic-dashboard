## Executive Overview

ACME Solar Holdings Limited (ACME Solar) reported a strong Q1 FY26, marked by significant capacity additions and a strategic pivot into battery energy storage systems (BESS). The company commissioned 350 MW of new projects, including its first 50 MW wind project, bringing its operational IPP portfolio to 2,890 MW. A key milestone was securing its first standalone BESS projects of 550 MWh with NHPC and signing PPAs for 550 MW of FDRE and solar projects. This expands their under-construction pipeline to 4,080 MW plus 550 MWh of storage. Financially, ACME Solar demonstrated robust growth with a 72% year-on-year increase in total revenue to INR 584 crores and a 76% rise in EBITDA to INR 531 crores, maintaining a healthy 91% EBITDA margin. The company also highlighted significant progress in debt refinancing, securing a loan of INR 1,070 crores at 8.5% fixed for 5 years, and achieving a CRISIL AA- stable rating for its 1,200 MW SECI assets. Management reiterated its focus on disciplined growth, profitability, cost optimization, and technological innovation, particularly in FDRE and BESS solutions.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Utility Model:** Continued focus on the full value chain for optimal execution and cost management.
*   **Diversification into BESS:** Winning first standalone BESS projects (550 MWh) and integrating BESS into FDRE projects to address grid stability and peak demand.
*   **FDRE & Hybrid Solutions:** Strategic focus on Firm and Dispatchable Renewable Energy and hybrid projects to meet evolving energy needs.
*   **Early Commissioning Strategy:** Aiming for early commissioning of BESS components of FDRE projects to potentially capture merchant market prices before PPA commencement.
*   **Technology and Innovation:** Continued investment in R&D, including testing of perovskite solar cells and advanced FDRE configurations to reduce land requirements and improve efficiency.
*   **Debt Management and Optimization:** Proactive debt refinancing to reduce interest costs, extend tenors, and diversify lender base. Targeting AA family ratings for projects and aiming for A+ with a positive outlook for the holding company.
*   **Capital Market Access:** Exploring capital markets for financing diversification, supported by improved credit ratings.
*   **Strategic Site Selection:** Focusing on high GHI zones and diversifying across states (Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Bihar, UP, MP, Chhattisgarh) rather than concentrating in Rajasthan/Gujarat to avoid evacuation delays.
*   **PPA Focus:** Emphasis on securing long-term PPAs while strategically utilizing merchant opportunities for early-commissioned BESS.
