## Executive Overview

ACME Solar Holdings Limited (ACME Solar) reported a solid Q2 FY26, demonstrating robust year-on-year growth in revenue and EBITDA, while advancing its strategic diversification into Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and Firm and Dispatchable Renewable Energy (FDRE) projects. The company commissioned 378 MW of renewable energy capacity, with the remaining 72 MW on track for completion by early January FY26. A significant development is the plan to operate approximately 1 GWh of BESS on a merchant basis from Q4 FY26, projecting an annual EBITDA of INR 170 crores, assuming a INR 5/unit price difference. ACME Solar's operational portfolio now stands at 2,918 MW, generating an estimated INR 2,025-2,075 crores in steady-state project-level EBITDA with a 14-15% EBITDA yield. The company secured PPAs for 50 MW of FDRE with Tata Power and 550 MWh of standalone BESS with NHPC. New project wins aggregated 720 MW, including 50 MW FDRE and 670 MW solar plus BESS, expanding the under-construction portfolio to 4.5 GW. Financially, Q2 FY26 saw total revenue of INR 601 crores (104% YoY increase) and EBITDA of INR 534 crores (108% YoY increase), with an EBITDA margin of 89%. Net operational debt to EBITDA was 4.3x, and net debt to net worth was 1.9x. The company achieved significant capital optimization, reducing interest rates by 75 bps on INR 2,080 crores of existing debt and refinancing INR 1,100 crores at 8.4%. ACME Solar's credit rating was upgraded to AA- by CRISIL and ICRA.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Utility Model:** Continuing to manage the full value chain.
*   **Diversification into BESS & FDRE:** Strategic focus on these solutions to meet grid stability and peak demand needs. Winning standalone BESS projects and integrating BESS into FDRE projects.
*   **Merchant BESS Operations:** Planning to operate ~1 GWh of BESS on a merchant basis from Q4 FY26, projecting significant EBITDA upside.
*   **Technology and Innovation:** Commissioned pilot BESS (10 MWh) for efficiency testing and optimization. Progressing with perovskite solar cell testing and aiming to reduce land requirements.
*   **Debt Management and Optimization:** Proactive debt refinancing and securing favorable credit ratings (AA- across the board). Aiming for further interest rate reduction.
*   **Strategic Site Selection & PPA Focus:** Diversifying sites to mitigate delays and securing long-term PPAs. New bids increasingly come with pre-demand locked in.
*   **Reduced GST Impact:** Benefiting from the reduction in GST on solar and wind equipment, which enhances project viability and demand.
