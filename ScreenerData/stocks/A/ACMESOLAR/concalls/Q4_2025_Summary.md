## Executive Overview

ACME Solar Holdings Limited concluded FY25 with a landmark Q4, showcasing disciplined execution, robust capacity growth, and financial prudence. The company added 1,200 MW of solar capacity during FY25, reaching an operational portfolio of 2,700 MW, poised to touch 3 GW soon. Strategic bid wins totaled 1,900 MW across FDRE, hybrid, and solar technologies, expanding the total project portfolio to approximately 7 GW.

Key commissioning highlights include 165 MW of the 300 MW Sikar plant in Rajasthan, with an additional 112.5 MW commissioned recently and the remaining 135 MW expected within 30 days. This plant is initially targeting the merchant market due to favorable pricing (INR 3.1/unit), expected to generate INR 230-240 crores annually when fully operational. The 50 MW wind project is also nearing full commissioning.

Financially, Q4 FY25 saw adjusted revenue increase by 73% YoY to INR 539 crores, with EBITDA growing 119% YoY to INR 488 crores. For the full FY25, revenue was INR 1,575 crores (+32% YoY) and EBITDA INR 1,400 crores (+43% YoY), maintaining an EBITDA margin of over 89%. PAT for FY25 surged 290% to INR 251 crores. The company significantly strengthened its balance sheet, reducing net operational debt to EBITDA to 4.4x (well within the 5.5x guidance) and net debt to net worth to 1.7x. Refinancing of INR 7,700 crores debt at 8.8% reduced interest rates by 75 basis points for these projects. Credit ratings improved, with Crisil upgrading the company to A+ (positive outlook) and 750 MW of operational projects receiving an AA- rating. Proactive procurement of batteries and other long-lead items for FY27 projects has locked in prices below budget, complemented by currency hedges.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aggressive Capacity Expansion & Commissioning:** Rapid operationalization of 1,200 MW solar in FY25, with continued focus on commissioning the Sikar and wind projects, targeting 3 GW operational capacity soon.
*   **Strategic Shift to FDRE, Hybrid & BESS:** Prioritizing dispatchable and flexible power solutions with integrated battery storage to cater to evolving grid requirements and capitalize on evening/morning peak power demand.
*   **Proactive CAPEX & Debt Optimization:** Locking in battery prices below budget and implementing currency hedges to mitigate cost volatility, alongside securing substantial construction and refinancing debt at improving rates due to enhanced creditworthiness.
*   **Monetizing Merchant Opportunities:** Intentionally operating newly commissioned assets, such as the Sikar plant, in the merchant market to capture high short-term tariffs before transitioning to long-term PPAs.
*   **Robust Land & Connectivity Management:** Ensuring comprehensive connectivity for current projects and securing surplus for future bids, complemented by strategic land acquisition and applications for government land to support long-term growth.
*   **Disciplined Bidding & Capital Allocation:** Adopting a conservative bidding approach, prioritizing execution capabilities, and staggering commissioning timelines to manage capex without requiring additional equity, all aimed at achieving a balanced 10 GW target.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Technological Adoption:** Maintaining high plant availability and CUF, while exploring new solutions like Gas-Insulated Substations (GIS) to enhance reliability and sustainability.
