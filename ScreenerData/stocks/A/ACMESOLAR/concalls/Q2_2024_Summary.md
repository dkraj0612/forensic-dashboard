## Executive Overview

ACME Solar Holdings Limited (ACME Solar) reported its Q2 and H1 FY25 results in their first earnings call post-listing. The company highlighted its fully integrated utility model, encompassing design, construction, engineering, and 25-year operation of renewable energy plants, primarily solar. A significant development during the quarter was the successful energization of a 1,200 MW ISTS plant, comprising four PPAs at INR 2.44 per kWh, with daily revenues of approximately INR 1.5 crores as of November 20th, and targeting peak revenue shortly. This energization increases their operational capacity to 2.5 GW (DC). The company also announced the upcoming commissioning of its first wind plant and the Bikaner 2 plant, the latter to operate on a merchant basis for 6-12 months before seeking a long-term PPA. ACME Solar emphasized its focus on long-term PPAs, in-house EPC capabilities, and a strategic shift towards round-the-clock (RTC) power and Firm and Dispatchable Renewable Energy (FDRE) solutions. Financing for upcoming projects will be sourced from EPC cash flow, power sale free cash flow to equity, and refinancing, supplemented by IPO proceeds for deleveraging.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Fully Integrated Model:** Capturing the entire value chain from design to operation, optimizing technology, time, and tariffs for competitiveness.
*   **Long-Term PPAs:** Focus on 25-year PPAs for revenue visibility and predictable cash flows.
*   **100% Solar Focus (Historically):** Transitioning towards round-the-clock (RTC) power and FDRE solutions to address peak power demand.
*   **Strategic Shift to FDRE & Hybrid:** Targeting mid-teen returns and solving peak power issues for utilities, with a unique solution increasing solar component and reducing wind to enhance revenue predictability and minimize risk.
*   **Execution Capability:** Emphasis on controlling time and cost through integrated value chain, including pre-bid activities like securing connectivity and land acquisition in optimal locations (Rajasthan, Gujarat).
*   **Financing Strategy:** Leveraging long-term partners for debt financing, with greenfield financing, refinancing, and IPO proceeds as key funding sources. Refinancing aims to reduce interest rates, extend loan tenors, and increase free cash flow.
*   **Capex Management:** Targeting a total capex of INR 40-42,000 crores for the remaining 4 GW of pipeline, funded by 75% debt and 25% equity.
