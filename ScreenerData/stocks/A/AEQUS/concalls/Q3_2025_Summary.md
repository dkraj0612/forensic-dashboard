## Executive Overview

Aequs Limited, a vertically integrated precision manufacturer, reported its Q3 FY2025-26 earnings, marking its first call as a publicly listed company. The company showcased strong year-on-year revenue growth, particularly in its Aerospace segment, driven by increasing wallet share and new customer wins in the consumer segment. The IPO was well-received, indicating strong investor confidence. Management emphasized its commitment to deepening capabilities, expanding integrated ecosystems, and leveraging its diversified manufacturing prowess across both aerospace and consumer verticals.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aerospace Segment:** Continued focus on increasing wallet share with existing OEMs (Airbus, Boeing), leveraging a strong order book of USD $814 million (expected to be delivered over the next five years). Expanding into higher value-added components like landing gear and engine systems, utilizing its vertically integrated capabilities.
*   **Consumer Segment:** Scaling up production and driving capacity utilization in consumer electronics and toys. Onboarding new customers like Mattel, and scaling shipments for existing programs (e.g., Hasbro, Tramontina). Focus on improving profitability through increased utilization.
*   **Vertical Integration & Ecosystem:** Leveraging its end-to-end manufacturing capabilities (forging, machining, molding, stamping, special processes) across its clusters in India and international facilities to offer a unique value proposition.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Capitalizing on India's growing manufacturing capabilities, cost advantages, skilled talent, and the "China+1" supply chain diversification trend. Strategic partnerships remain a key element.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Analysis of Stated Goals vs. Performance:**
Management's narrative and performance in this Q3 FY26 call largely align with the strategic objectives and positive outlook presented in the prior historical context.

*   **Revenue Growth:** The company delivered exceptionally strong revenue growth in Q3 FY26 (51% YoY), exceeding expectations and highlighting the successful ramp-up in both segments. Nine-month revenue growth of 28% YoY also demonstrates consistent top-line expansion.
*   **Margin Improvement:** EBITDA margins have shown a significant improvement, reaching 14% for the nine months ended December 31, 2025, up from 10% in the previous year. This indicates successful cost management and a favorable product mix. The aerospace segment EBITDA margin for the nine months was 24%.
*   **Consumer Segment Scale-up:** The transcript consistently reiterates the focus on scaling the consumer segment, with management acknowledging the early investment phase and the impact of underutilization on current profitability. The progress in industrializing consumer electronics programs and onboarding new customers like Mattel aligns with strategic goals.
*   **Aerospace Orderbook:** The strong aerospace order book of USD $814 million provides revenue visibility and supports management's claims of sustained growth in this segment.

**Narrative Consistency:**
The management's tone remained optimistic, emphasizing disciplined execution, operational reliability, and the strategic advantage of their integrated ecosystem. There was no significant shift in narrative regarding headwinds; instead, the focus was on capitalizing on opportunities, managing the scaling of the consumer segment, and the positive impact of customer demand on future capacity planning.

**New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance for Next Quarter:**

*   **Aerospace Order Book:** Expected to be delivered over the next five years (up to 2031). Noted as a TCV (Total Contract Value).
*   **Consumer Electronics Demand:** Capacity built is fully committed by customers; focus is on scaling ramp-up to meet demand. No "order book" in the traditional sense, but rather capacity built based on projected demand.
*   **Consumer Profitability Timeline:** Management stated they need more time to provide a precise timeline for the consumer segment to break even and turn profitable due to evolving utilization rates and customer requests for capacity expansion. This is a slight delay in providing specific guidance compared to the previous historical context's implied expectation of PAT positivity by FY27.
*   **CapEx:** Most CapEx for the current year is already done, with some capitalization expected in Q4 FY26 for consumer electronics. No significant new CapEx planned for the remainder of FY26.
*   **Aerospace Segment Utilization:** Guidance to reach approximately 75% utilization. Current utilization in India is around 71%.
*   **Aerospace New Programs:** Expanding into landing gear and engine components, engaging with customers, and seeing opportunities for margin improvement due to vertical integration.
*   **Aerospace CapEx:** Continuous, non-lumpy CapEx expected to happen in aerospace, with planning 18-24 months out based on order book and machine lead times.
*   **Consumer Margins:** Long-term target for consumer segment (toys, electronics, durables) at optimal utilization is expected to be broadly in line with aerospace, around 18-20% EBITDA margin.
*   **Aerospace Margins:** Expectation to continue in the 20%+ range, with management comfortable at this level given their scale and integrated capabilities.
*   **Aerospace Sole Supplier Status:** Over 90% of parts are supplied to OEMs/Tier-1s where Aequs holds sole supplier status.
*   **Aerospace Client Mix:** Revenue is ultimately for Airbus and Boeing, whether delivered directly or through Tier-1/Tier-2 suppliers. No client-wise revenue breakup provided.
*   **Aerospace to Defense:** Desire to be a system-level contributor, evidenced by the UAV JV.
*   **Consumer Electronics Segment (ECMS):** Approved for mechanical enclosures under the ECMS scheme.
*   **Aerospace Wallet Share:** Qualitative statement that Airbus A321 and the A320 family represent significant content, with narrow-body aircraft being the largest revenue share.
*   **Long-term Growth:** Aerospace expected to grow north of 20% CAGR. Consumer segment has the potential to grow much faster. Goal to balance consumer and aerospace business in the long run.
*   **PAT Positivity Timeline:** Management needs more time to re-evaluate and confirm timelines for achieving overall PAT positivity due to positive dynamics in the consumer segment requiring capacity expansion decisions. This is viewed as a positive challenge rather than a negative development.

**Guidance on Margins:**
No explicit forward guidance for EBITDA or PAT margins for the upcoming quarter was provided. However, management reaffirmed the long-term target for consumer segment margins to align with aerospace margins (18-20% EBITDA) at optimal utilization. Aerospace segment margins are expected to remain in the 20%+ range.
