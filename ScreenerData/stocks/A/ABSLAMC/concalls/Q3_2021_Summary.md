## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited (ABSLAMC) presented its unaudited financial results for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2021, showcasing continued growth and strategic advancements. The company's Mutual Fund Quarterly Average Assets Under Management (QAAUM) reached INR 2,988 billion, a year-on-year increase of 17%, while maintaining a market share of 8.69%. The retail franchise remains strong with 7.6 million investor folios. Digital transformation efforts are evident, with 83% of overall transactions and 76% of new folios created digitally. Revenue grew by 10% year-on-year to INR 3,530 million, and Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a significant increase of 27% year-on-year to INR 1,862 million. ABSLAMC continues to focus on expanding its passive fund offerings, growing its Alternate Assets business, and strengthening its distribution network, with over 90% of distributors onboarded digitally.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy continues to focus on:

*   **Strengthening Retail Franchise:** Maintaining a strong retail investor base and growing folios, supported by a robust network of Mutual Fund Distributors (MFDs).
*   **Digital Transformation:** Enhancing digital capabilities for both investors and distributors, with a high percentage of transactions and new folios being digital.
*   **Product Innovation and Diversification:** Expanding the passive fund segment with new launches and a robust pipeline, alongside growing the Alternate Assets business.
*   **Distribution Network Enhancement:** Continuously expanding the MFD network, with a focus on digital onboarding and deepening relationships through loyalty programs and targeted education.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Leveraging technology and automation for streamlined processes, improved customer service, and data-driven decision-making.
