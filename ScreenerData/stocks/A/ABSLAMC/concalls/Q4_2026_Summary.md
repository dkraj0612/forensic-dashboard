## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC (ABSLAMC) concluded FY'26 with a strong Q4 performance characterized by robust AUM growth, strategic expansion in alternate assets, and a positive outlook despite global macroeconomic uncertainties. The company reported a 17% YoY growth in Overall Average AUM to ₹4.74 lakh crores and a 14% YoY increase in Mutual Fund AUM to ₹4.36 lakh crores. Equity Mutual Fund AUM showed significant growth, up 17% YoY to ₹1.97 lakh crores. The Indian economy demonstrated resilience, with an expected GDP growth of 6.75%, positioning it as a leading global growth engine. The mutual fund industry saw a substantial 21% YoY growth in quarterly average AUM to ₹81.5 lakh crores, driven by record SIP contributions of ₹32,000 crores in March '26. ABSLAMC's SIP book grew to ₹1,204 crores in March '26, with a 16% QoQ increase in new SIP registrations. The investor folio count reached 1.1 crore. The alternate business segment experienced extraordinary growth, with PMS/AIF assets surging nearly threefold YoY to ₹32,570 crores, significantly boosted by the ESIC mandate. Passive assets crossed ₹40,000 crores, growing 25% YoY. Financially, for FY'26, ABSLAMC reported a 6% YoY increase in Profit After Tax (PAT) to ₹975 crores. Operating Revenue grew 9.5% YoY to ₹1,845 crores, and Operating Profit Before Tax (PBT) increased 7.5% YoY to ₹1,015 crores. The board proposed a dividend of ₹25.5 per share for FY'26.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy continues to focus on diversified AUM growth, enhanced product offerings, deepening distribution reach, and leveraging technology. Key drivers include:

*   **AUM Growth and Diversification:** Aggressive expansion in PMS/AIF (especially Credit Funds and Real Estate Funds), Offshore, and Passive businesses. The ESIC mandate remains a significant contributor to PMS/AIF growth.
*   **Strengthening Core Equity & Hybrid Offerings:** Focus on a portfolio of "focused funds" (Flexi Cap, Multi Asset Allocation, Multi-Cap, Balanced Advantage, Small & Midcap, GenNext) and improving fund performance to drive inflows.
*   **Expanding Alternate Business:** Scaling PMS and AIF solutions for HNIs and family offices, including planned launches in AIF Credit and Real Estate. Targeting ₹5,000 crores in AIF Credit Funds over three years and ₹5,000 crores in Real Estate Funds within 1.5 years. The aim is to increase the alternate revenue contribution from ~7-8% to 14-15% of total revenue.
*   **GIFT City Operations:** Establishing a wholly-owned subsidiary, Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC International IFSC Limited, to enhance its presence and launch products for inward and outward remittances with smaller ticket sizes. Actively fundraising for various GIFT City-based funds.
*   **Boosting Passive Business:** Continued expansion of its passive product suite (now 54 offerings) with a long-term target of ₹1 lakh crore AUM.
*   **Product Innovation:** Planned launches include an Innovation Fund (PMS/AIF), ABSL Global Emerging Market Fund Series II (GIFT City), lifecycle funds, and potential REITs/InvITs offerings, subject to regulatory approval.
*   **Distribution & Sales Enhancement:** Deepening presence across India (19,000+ pin codes) with plans to add new locations in FY'27. Investing in the direct sales team and strengthening engagement with MFDs through value-added programs. Leveraging technology to enhance investor and partner app experiences.
*   **SIP Focus:** Continued emphasis on the 'Har Ghar Me SIP' campaign to drive SIP adoption and maintain market share gains, despite industry-wide volatility impacting cancellations.
*   **Regulatory Adaptation:** Proactive approach to TER revisions, aiming for a neutral to positive impact on AMC profitability and minimal impact on distributor commissions.
