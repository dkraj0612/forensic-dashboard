## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC (ABSLAMC) concluded FY'25 with a Q4 performance demonstrating resilience and strategic execution amidst a dynamic market. The company reported a 17% YoY growth in Overall Average Assets Under Management (AUM), reaching ₹4.06 lakh crores. Mutual Fund AUM grew by 15% YoY to ₹3.82 lakh crores, with Equity AUM showing an 11% YoY increase to ₹1.69 lakh crores. While the Indian economy remains robust with a projected 6.5% GDP growth, global macroeconomic conditions present moderate but uneven growth prospects with persistent risks like geopolitical tensions and trade restrictions. The Indian Mutual Fund industry saw a 25% YoY growth in average AUM to ₹67.42 lakh crores. Q4 FY'25 witnessed a slowdown in equity net sales for the industry due to market volatility, with ABSLAMC's SIP book standing at ₹1,316 crores in March 2025. The company's investor folio count crossed 1.06 Crore, with 27 lakh new folios added in FY'25. The alternative business segment experienced significant growth, with PMS/AIF assets jumping 268% YoY to ₹11,330 crores, driven by the ESIC mandate and growth in existing offerings. Offshore assets grew 14% YoY to ₹12,070 crores, and Passive assets reached ₹34,700 crores. Financially, ABSLAMC reported a 19% YoY increase in Profit After Tax (PAT) to ₹931 crores for FY'25. Operating Revenue for FY'25 was up 25% YoY to ₹1,685 crores, and Operating Profit Before Tax (PBT) increased 31% YoY to ₹944 crores. The board proposed a dividend of ₹24 per share for FY'25.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy remains focused on AUM growth, enhanced distribution, product innovation, and profitability:

*   **AUM Growth & Diversification:** Continued focus on expanding PMS, AIF (Equity, Fixed Income/Credit), Offshore, and Passive businesses to drive overall AUM growth. The ESIC mandate significantly boosted PMS/AIF AUM.
*   **Strengthening Core Equity & Hybrid Offerings:** Leveraging improved fund performance to drive traction in equity net sales. Emphasis on five "focused funds" (Frontline Equity, Flexicap, Multi Cap, Balance Advantage, and Gennext thematic fund) and other thematic products. Promoting hybrid funds (multi-asset, balanced advantage, equity savings) for revenue contribution.
*   **Expanding Alternate Business:** Enhancing PMS and AIF solutions for HNIs and family offices, including the planned launch of an ABSL India Equity Innovation Fund in PMS. The ESIC mandate is a key development in expanding debt portfolio management.
*   **GIFT City Operations Development:** Continuing to attract offshore investors with India-dedicated funds and LRS schemes, with active fundraising for new offerings.
*   **Boosting Passive Business:** Expanding the diverse product portfolio (currently 53 offerings) to meet investor demand and improve tracking.
*   **Product Innovation:** Planning the launch of an Innovation Fund (PMS/AIF) and continuing to expand the passive product suite.
*   **Distribution & Sales Enhancement:** Investing in the direct sales team (aiming for ~100 people) and leveraging existing models. Strategic engagement in key locations and MFD empowerment programs are ongoing.
*   **SIP Focus:** Maintaining a strong emphasis on SIPs for new customer acquisition and increasing average ticket size, despite short-term market volatility impacting registrations and cancellations.
*   **Expense Management:** Optimizing existing teams for higher productivity rather than significant headcount expansion. Prudent management of marketing and distribution expenses.
