## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited (ABSLAMC) reported strong performance for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2021. The company demonstrated significant growth across key metrics, driven by an expanding asset base, a robust retail franchise, and strategic investments in technology and distribution. Mutual Fund Quarterly Average Assets Under Management (QAAUM) crossed INR 3 trillion, reaching INR 3,003 billion, a notable increase of 26% year-on-year. The company maintained its market share at 9.15%, underscoring its leadership position. The focus on digital transformation is yielding results, with 84% of overall transactions and 77% of new folios created digitally. Revenue grew by 30% year-on-year to INR 3,320 million, and Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a substantial increase of 38% year-on-year to INR 1,731 million. ABSLAMC continues to expand its product offerings, particularly in the passive segment and alternative assets, and is actively strengthening its distribution network, with a significant focus on Tier 30 cities.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy revolves around several key pillars:

*   **Strengthening Retail Franchise:** Continued focus on growing the retail investor base, evidenced by the increase in folios and new folio additions. Emphasis on building strong relationships through a vast network of Mutual Fund Distributors (MFDs).
*   **Digital Transformation:** Accelerating digital initiatives for both customer and distributor engagement. This includes enhancing own digital capabilities, forging partnerships with fintechs, and leveraging data analytics for personalized offerings. 84% of transactions and 77% of new folios are now digital.
*   **Product Innovation and Diversification:** Expanding the product suite, with a focus on the passive segment through ETFs, FoFs, and Index Funds, and growing the Alternate Assets business. New product launches are strategically aligned with market trends.
*   **Distribution Network Enhancement:** Broadening the reach of the distribution network, particularly in Tier 30 cities, through MFDs, banks, and national distributors. Strategic partnerships with fintechs are also a key component.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Driving operational efficiency through technology and process automation, as demonstrated by increased digital transaction volumes and streamlined internal processes.
