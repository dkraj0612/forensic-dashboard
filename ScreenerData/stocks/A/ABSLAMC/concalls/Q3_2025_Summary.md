## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC (ABSLAMC) delivered a strong performance in Q3 FY'25 (ended December 31, 2024), marked by significant AUM growth and robust financial results amidst a dynamic market environment. The company's Overall Average Assets Under Management (AUM), including alternate assets, surpassed ₹4 lakh crores, reflecting a 23% year-on-year (YoY) increase. Mutual Fund Average AUM similarly grew 23% YoY to ₹3.84 lakh crores, with Equity AUM notably surging 32% YoY to ₹1.79 lakh crores.

The Indian economy remains a bright spot, projected for 6.5% GDP growth in 2025, although global markets are experiencing volatility which management expects to subside in Q1 CY2025. The Mutual Fund industry itself witnessed record growth, with average AUM reaching ₹68 lakh crores (up 39% YoY) and SIP inflows increasing 50% YoY to ₹26,500 crores in December 2024.

ABSLAMC's SIP book demonstrated solid growth, up 38% YoY to ₹1,382 crores in December 2024, with 6.70 lakh new SIPs added during the quarter (a 3x YoY increase). The company's investor folio count crossed 1.05 Crore, adding 24 lakh new folios in 9M FY'25. Improved equity fund performance and market perception have translated into traction in equity net sales.

The alternate business, including PMS/AIF equity, grew 44% YoY to ₹3,853 crores, while Offshore AUM increased 28% YoY to ₹12,686 crores. The passive business also maintained its momentum, with total assets at ₹31,600 crores. Strategic product launches like the ABSL Conglomerate Fund (₹1,375 crores) and the industry's first 3-6 month index fund (₹715 crores) contributed to growth.

Financially, ABSLAMC recorded strong results for Q3 FY'25, with Revenue from Operations rising 30% YoY to ₹445 crores. Operating Profit showed even more significant growth, surging 42% YoY to ₹262 crores, underscoring effective operational management. Management attributes success to sustained focus on fund performance, diversified product offerings, and enhanced distribution engagement through specific programs like "Yashaswi," "Fulcrum," and "Legacy Leap."

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategic focus revolves around diversified AUM growth, enhanced distribution, and product innovation across all segments:

*   **Diversified AUM Growth:** Continued investment in PMS, AIF (equity, credit, real estate), offshore, and passive businesses, aiming for alternate segment growth to outpace the mutual fund business.
*   **Strengthening Core Equity Offerings:** Drive "focused funds" (five diversified and three thematic funds) across the country, capitalizing on improved fund performance and market perception to gain traction in net sales.
*   **Expanding Alternate Business:** Enhance PMS and AIF solutions for HNIs and family offices. Progress AIF credit funds (targeting ₹750-1000 crores for the first issue) and real estate funds (already managing ₹750 crores).
*   **GIFT City Operations Development:** Actively attracting offshore investors to India-dedicated funds (Europe, UCITS platforms, GIFT City). Already seeing inflows and planning new fund launches (global blue chip fund, Fund of Funds).
*   **Boosting Passive Business:** Expand a diverse product portfolio (currently 52 offerings) with additional planned launches to meet investor demand and improve tracking performance.
*   **Product Innovation:** Successfully launched the ABSL Conglomerate Fund and the industry's first 3-6 month index fund. An Innovation Fund has received approval and is slated for future launch. A robust pipeline exists for passive offerings across various asset classes.
*   **Distribution & Sales Enhancement:**
    *   **Leveraging Existing Models:** Expand the VRM (Virtual RM) model for IFAs and increase the contribution from the "Service-to-Sales" operational team.
    *   **Emerging Market Penetration:** Maintain and selectively expand physical presence in smaller locations (currently 95 locations) to increase market share.
    *   **Targeted Engagement:** Focus on 25 high-volume locations contributing 80% of industry sales, implementing intensive ground-level engagement and "voyage events" with key partners.
    *   **MFD Empowerment Programs:** Continue initiatives like "Yashaswi" (empowering women MFDs), "Fulcrum" (equipping MFDs to build high-performing teams), and "Legacy Leap" (cultivating next-generation MFDs) to foster stronger relationships and capabilities within the distribution network.
*   **SIP Focus:** Actively promoting ₹250/₹100 SIPs to drive new customer acquisition, alongside strategies to incrementally increase the average ticket size of contributions.
*   **Expense Management:** Strategic realignment of marketing and distribution expenses based on market conditions, and a disciplined approach to headcount growth with budgeted recruitment for the current fiscal year largely completed at operating levels.
