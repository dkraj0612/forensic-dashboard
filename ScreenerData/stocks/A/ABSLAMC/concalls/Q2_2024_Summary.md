## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited (ABSLAMC) reported its Q2 FY'24 earnings, showcasing a mixed performance with continued growth in certain segments despite broader industry challenges. The company's overall average Assets Under Management (AUM) reached ₹3.24 lakh crores, a 10% YoY increase. Mutual Fund AUM crossed ₹3 lakh crores, with Equity AUM reaching ₹1.3 lakh crores. The Indian mutual fund industry saw AUM grow by 20% YoY to ₹46.98 lakh crores, with strong net equity sales of ₹90,000 crores in the quarter. SIP flows grew 24% YoY to ₹16,042 crores in September 2023, and the investor base expanded to 15.9 crore folios. ABSLAMC's own SIP book grew to ₹968 crores from ₹931 crores YoY. Financially, revenue grew 8% YoY to ₹335 crores, and operating profit increased 5% YoY to ₹181 crores for Q2 FY'24. The management highlighted a turnaround in equity fund performance, with a higher percentage now beating benchmarks and peer averages, which is expected to aid in reversing market share losses. The passive business saw significant growth (68% YoY to ₹28,400 crores), and an offshore equity fund was launched in GIFT City.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy is focused on:

*   **Addressing Market Share Loss:** Implementing initiatives to arrest and reverse market share declines, particularly in equity segments, by leveraging improved fund performance.
*   **Strengthening Investment Performance:** Enhancing equity fund performance through team augmentation (Co-CIO Equity hired) and internal talent development, with a target of beating benchmarks and peer averages by wider margins.
*   **Product Diversification & Innovation:** Launching new products, including a US Treasury ETF fund-of-fund, a Transport and Logistics Fund, structured opportunity and index-linked AIFs, and exploring quant-based funds. Expansion in fixed income, particularly with higher yields, and thematic categories remains a priority.
*   **Channel Engagement:** Activating and engaging with distribution partners, with a focus on digital platforms and direct channels for incremental growth. Enhancing engagement with MFDs and national distributors.
*   **Digital & Direct Channel Focus:** Leveraging digital platforms (e.g., Groww, Paytm, PhonePe) for SIP growth and customer acquisition. Building a dedicated direct sales team to drive direct business.
*   **Alternate & Passive Business Growth:** Continuing to expand the passive fund offerings and growing the AIF and PMS segments, with a focus on profitability.
*   **GIFT City Expansion:** Establishing a presence in GIFT City to offer global investment opportunities to Indian investors, including emerging market equity funds.
*   **SIP Book Growth:** Implementing strategies to improve SIP registrations, increase ticket sizes (e.g., Sampoorna SIP), and re-engage with online platforms based on improved performance. Targeting SIP book to cross ₹1,000 crores.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context:** In previous quarters, ABSLAMC management had acknowledged lagging equity fund performance and market share erosion, outlining strategies to improve fund manager capabilities, enhance product offerings, and strengthen distribution engagement, particularly with IFAs and digital channels. They also emphasized the growth of passive and alternate assets.

**Current Transcript - Audit of Stated Promises & New Forward-Looking Guidance:**

**Targets Met/Narrative Consistent:**

*   **Economic Outlook:** Management maintained a positive outlook on India's economic growth (7.8% GDP in Q2 FY'24), robust macro indicators (GST, PMI), and favourable conditions for consumption and market sentiment, consistent with previous commentary. The inclusion in JPMorgan Emerging Market Bond Index was noted positively.
*   **Equity Performance Turnaround:** Management reported significant improvement in equity fund performance, with most funds beating benchmarks and peer averages on a 1-year basis, addressing previous concerns. This is seen as a key driver for future flows.
*   **Passive & Alternate Assets Growth:** Passive offerings grew by 68% to ₹28,400 crores. AIF fundraising is underway, and the first GIFT City fund garnered $11.2 million.
*   **SIP Book Growth:** ABSLAMC's SIP book saw a modest YoY increase to ₹968 crores, with efforts focused on increasing the ticket size and overall registrations. The management highlighted strategies to reach ₹1,000 crores.
*   **Financial Performance:** Revenue growth of 8% YoY and operating profit growth of 5% YoY indicate a stable financial trajectory, though growth rates moderated compared to previous periods.
*   **Team Strengthening:** A Co-CIO Equity has been hired, and internal talent is being given greater responsibility, aligning with previous commentary on strengthening the investment team.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, and Timelines for Future Tracking:**

*   **Product Launches & Pipeline:**
    *   Launch of a US Bond Yield Fund (open-ended) with a total industry limit of $800 million ($200 million for ABSLAMC).
    *   Launch of a Transport and Logistics Fund, closing on November 10th.
    *   Pipeline includes Structured Opportunity Fund (Category II AIF) and Index Linked Fund (Category III AIF).
    *   Plan to launch a quant-based fund, pending SEBI clearance, driven by the new Head of Equity.
    *   Continuous focus on thematic categories and identifying new fund opportunities within TER guidelines.
*   **Distribution & Channels:**
    *   SIP strategy focus on increasing ticket size through products like Sampoorna SIP.
    *   Engaging with online platforms (Groww, Paytm, PhonePe, Kuvera) based on improved equity scheme performance.
    *   Increased engagement with MFD partners and national distributors for SIP growth.
    *   Digital channels are the largest contributor to incremental growth, followed by direct channels. Traditional channels (IFA, Bank, ND) are maintaining their position, with digital and direct channels expected to see higher year-on-year growth.
    *   A separate team of ~30 people is driving direct business.
*   **Financial Projections/Targets:**
    *   **Yields:** Equity yield expected to be around 70 bps (blended) and 55-60 bps (incremental). Debt yield around 24-25 bps. Yields remain stable due to the increasing mix of equity assets.
    *   **OPEX:** Slight increase in expenses due to increments on the people side and AIF commissions, expected to remain in a similar range going forward, with potential minor increases due to hiring.
    *   **Profitability:** Continued growth expected from passive and AIF businesses.
*   **Market Share:** Aiming to improve flow market share, especially in equity, by leveraging improved performance. Gross flow market share in focused schemes improved by 3-4% on average over the last quarter.
*   **SIP Book Dynamics:** Targeting SIP book to cross ₹1,000 crores. SIP discontinuance is expected, particularly from younger investors on online platforms, but management believes they will return.
*   **Regulatory Landscape (TER changes):** Operating within existing TER guidelines. No new specific guidance on potential SEBI changes.
*   **Investment Team Changes:** Co-CIO Equity hired, with further strengthening of the investment team in credit opportunities and offshore business ongoing. Impact of investment team changes is expected to reflect over 3-6 months.
*   **Gross to Net Flows:** Gross sales improving in focused schemes. Focus on improving net flows through SIP growth and customer retention.
*   **Channel Contribution:** Digital channels are the largest incremental contributors to AUM growth. MFDs remain the dominant traditional channel, followed by national distributors and banks. RIAs will be classified under direct.
*   **NFOs:** NFOs provide incremental AUM, new customers, and new SIPs. Commission payout for NFOs is slightly higher than for existing funds to drive traction.
*   **Fund Performance:** Most equity funds beating benchmarks and peer averages on a 1-year basis. Around 45-50% outperforming on a 3-year basis.
*   **Millennial Investor Behavior:** Millennials on online platforms may exhibit higher churn but are expected to become long-term investors over time. Digital channels contribute approximately 37-39% of flows, largely driven by this demographic.
*   **High-Risk Funds:** Management has not given significant weightage to recent articles about high-risk funds in the mutual fund space, noting that AIFs are already designed for accredited investors.
*   **Channel Mix Change:** Expectation of continued dominance by MFDs and national distributors, with incremental wallet share growth from digital and direct channels. This shift is not expected to come at the cost of other channels, but rather contribute to overall industry growth.
