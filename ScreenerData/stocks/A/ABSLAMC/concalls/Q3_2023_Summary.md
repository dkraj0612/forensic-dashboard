## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited (ABSLAMC) reported Q3 FY'24 results, demonstrating a robust performance against a backdrop of receding global economic concerns and a strong Indian growth trajectory. The company's overall average Assets Under Management (AUM), including alternate assets, reached ₹3.25 lakh crores, an 11% year-on-year increase. The Indian mutual fund industry experienced significant growth, with quarterly average AUM reaching ₹49.21 lakh crores (22% YoY growth), driven by strong SIP inflows and increased investor participation. ABSLAMC's equity AUM grew to ₹1.36 lakh crores, and the company added approximately 4.6 lakh new folios over nine months, servicing around 80 lakh folios. Retail investor contribution to AUM increased to 52%, and B-30 cities accounted for 17.8% of total AUM. Management expressed confidence in continued momentum, highlighting strengthened investment teams and strategic initiatives in alternate assets and digital distribution. Financials showed a healthy YoY increase in revenue (16%) and Profit After Tax (26%) for Q3 FY'24.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy continues to focus on:

*   **Strengthening Retail Franchise:** Enhancing customer acquisition and engagement, with retail investors now accounting for 52% of AUM. Continued focus on B30 cities, contributing 17.8% of AUM.
*   **Product Diversification & Innovation:** Expanding offerings in passive products (43 products, AUM of ₹29,300 Cr), alternate assets (PMS, AIFs), and GIFT City initiatives.
*   **Distribution Network Enhancement:** Leveraging a multi-pronged approach including MFDs, NDs, banking channels, direct channels, and digital platforms. Emphasis on activating MFDs and expanding reach in emerging markets.
*   **Investment Performance Focus:** Strengthening the investment team with a new Co-CIO Equity and a Head of Credit for AIFs. Continued improvement in equity fund performance is a key objective.
*   **Growth in Alternate Assets:** Structural changes in the alternate assets vertical to build robust franchises in AIF and PMS, with a pipeline of credit-oriented funds and structured opportunity funds.
*   **GIFT City Initiatives:** Launching and promoting products like the ABSL Index Linked Fund in GIFT City.
*   **Digital Adoption:** Increased traction from online channels and digital platforms, contributing to purchase volumes.
