## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited (ABSLAMC) reported its Q4 FY'23 and full-year results, reflecting a period of stable AUM, muted industry growth, and strategic adjustments. The company's overall average Assets Under Management (AUM), including alternate assets, stood at ₹2.86 lakh crores for Q4 FY'23, with mutual fund AUM at ₹2.75 lakh crores. The Indian mutual fund industry experienced flattish growth in the quarter, with AUM reaching ₹40.49 lakh crores, and a 16% YoY growth in the SIP book to ₹14,275 crores by March 2023. ABSLAMC's equity AUM remained flat at ₹1.16 lakh crores, representing 42% of its mutual fund AUM. The company added approximately 6.7 lakh new folios in FY'23, bringing the total to 80.3 lakh folios. Retail investors constituted 52% of ABSLAMC's AUM, and B30 cities contributed 17% of total AUM. Financials showed a decline in revenue from ₹314 Cr in Q3 FY'23 to ₹297 Cr in Q4 FY'23, and operating profit before tax decreased from ₹174 Cr to ₹149 Cr. For the full year FY'23, revenue was ₹1,227 Cr, down from ₹1,293 Cr in FY'22, and operating profit before tax was ₹667 Cr, down from ₹753 Cr in FY'22. The board proposed a final dividend of ₹5.25 per share, bringing the total dividend for FY'23 to ₹10.25 per share.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy is focused on:

*   **Halting Market Share Erosion & Improving Momentum:** Efforts to stop "leakages" and improve overall market share, particularly in segments where participation has been lower.
*   **Strengthening Retail Franchise:** Continued focus on increasing retail investor AUM, with emphasis on both B30 and T30 markets.
*   **Product Diversification & Innovation:** Launching and growing offerings in passive products, and expanding into PMS and AIF categories. Exploring GIFT City initiatives for offshore business.
*   **Distribution Network Enhancement:** Activating distributors through models like Virtual Relationship Manager (VRM) and Sampark. Increasing direct customer connect and focusing on emerging markets.
*   **Investment Performance Improvement:** Addressing performance issues in specific equity categories (ELSS, small/mid-cap, large & mid-cap) through fund management changes, with an expectation of stabilization and return to benchmark/peer group performance.
*   **Alternate Assets Growth:** Planning AIF product launches in equity and fixed income, and developing real estate credit funds.
*   **GIFT City Initiatives:** Seeking approval for and planning to launch funds domiciled in GIFT City.
*   **Digital Adoption & Direct Connect:** Stepping up engagement with direct customers and leveraging online channels.
