## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited (ABSLAMC) reported a strong Q4 FY'24 and full fiscal year FY'24 performance, marked by significant financial growth and an optimistic outlook for the Indian economy and mutual fund industry. The company achieved its highest-ever profitability in FY'24, with Profit Before Tax (PBT) up 27% YoY to ₹1,008 Cr. and Profit After Tax (PAT) up 31% YoY to ₹780 Cr. Total revenue for FY'24 increased by 21% YoY to ₹1,641 Cr. Q4 FY'24 saw a 34% YoY increase in total revenue to ₹440 Cr., with PBT up 48% YoY to ₹268 Cr. and PAT up 54% YoY to ₹208 Cr. The board proposed a dividend of ₹13.5 per share for FY'24.

The company experienced a significant uptick in AUM, with overall average AUM (including alternate assets) reaching ₹3.46 lakh Cr. in Q4 FY'24, a 21% YoY growth. Mutual Fund AUM stood at ₹3.32 lakh Cr., with Equity AUM at ₹1.52 lakh Cr. This growth was attributed to improved equity fund performance, a strong fixed-income franchise, a robust sales engine, and a growing digital business. SIP flows saw a notable increase, reaching ₹19,271 Cr. in March 2024 for the industry (up 35% YoY) and ₹1,252 Cr. for ABSLAMC in March 2024 (a 25% QoQ increase from December 2023). ABSLAMC added approximately 11.5 lakh folios in FY'24, bringing the total serviced folios to around 86 lakhs.

Management highlighted the positive impact of strategic talent acquisition, including a new Co-CIO Equity, and strengthened offerings in PMS and AIF segments. Expansion in GIFT City and a focus on passive business growth were also key themes. The company remains committed to its long-term vision of achieving market leadership in SIPs and leveraging its diversified product suite.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABSLAMC's strategy is centered on:

*   **Leveraging Improved Performance:** Capitalizing on the turnaround in equity fund performance to drive AUM growth and attract new investors.
*   **Strengthening Sales & Distribution:** Enhancing sales productivity through a revamped sales excellence model, sharper focus on high-potential locations and partners, and robust engagement with MFDs, digital platforms, and direct channels.
*   **Product Diversification and Innovation:** Continuing to launch new products across various categories, including quant-based funds, thematic funds, and expanding offerings in PMS and AIF segments.
*   **Digital & Direct Channel Focus:** Building out a dedicated direct HNI team (CARE initiative) and leveraging digital platforms for customer acquisition and engagement.
*   **SIP Book Growth:** Renewed focus on increasing SIP registrations and market share, aiming for 8-9% SIP market share, by emphasizing goal-based investing and consistent communication.
*   **Alternate & Passive Business Expansion:** Strengthening PMS and AIF offerings with a focus on equity and fixed-income credit opportunities, and building capabilities in the passive space.
*   **GIFT City Operations:** Expanding offerings under the LRS scheme and launching feeder funds.
*   **Talent Development:** Continuing to attract and retain key talent, particularly in investment and sales functions.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context:** In previous quarters, ABSLAMC management had acknowledged challenges with equity fund performance and market share, outlining strategies to improve investment capabilities, diversify products, and strengthen distribution channels, with a particular emphasis on digital and IFA engagement. Growth in passive and alternate assets was also a focus.

**Current Transcript - Audit of Stated Promises & New Forward-Looking Guidance:**

**Targets Met/Narrative Consistent:**

*   **Economic & Industry Outlook:** Management maintained a consistently optimistic view on India's economic growth (projected over 7% in FY'25) and the mutual fund industry's potential. The positive sentiment towards the Indian equity market, despite recent volatility due to regulatory initiatives in the small and mid-cap segment, remains consistent.
*   **Financial Performance:** The reported highest-ever profitability for FY'24 and strong YoY growth in revenue and profits for both the full year and Q4 FY'24 align with expectations of a strong financial year.
*   **Equity Performance Turnaround:** The narrative around improved equity fund performance, with most funds now beating benchmarks and peer averages on a 1-year basis, is consistent with previous commentary and presented as a key driver for future flows.
*   **SIP Book Growth:** The significant jump in monthly SIP registrations (to 2.2-2.3 lakhs) and the increase in ABSLAMC's SIP book to ₹1,252 Cr. in March 2024 demonstrate progress towards the ₹1,000 Cr. target mentioned previously, and the ambitious goal of 8-9% market share.
*   **Talent Acquisition & Team Strengthening:** The hiring of a Co-CIO Equity and other senior investment professionals for passive and credit opportunities aligns with the strategy to strengthen the investment and management teams.
*   **Passive and Alternate Asset Growth:** While specific AUM figures for passive assets showed modest growth, the management highlighted continued focus and investment in this area, along with traction in PMS and AIF products, particularly the ABSL India Special Opportunities Fund.
*   **Channel Engagement:** The emphasis on improving sales productivity, engaging with MFDs, and leveraging digital channels is consistent. The reported increase in MFD channel SIP registrations is a positive outcome.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, and Timelines for Future Tracking:**

*   **Product Pipeline:**
    *   Launch of a Quant Fund planned for post-election, before June 30th, to be managed by the Co-CIO and Head of Equity.
    *   Further product development is in the drawing board, with a focus on thematic categories.
    *   Plans to strengthen Fixed Income credit opportunity AIFs and build on the success of the Real Estate AIF.
*   **Distribution & Channels:**
    *   Targeting an 8-9% SIP market share.
    *   Aiming for renewed participation from existing channels and continued new customer additions.
    *   Expansion of the direct HNI team (CARE) to 40-50 members by the end of the year to enhance customer service and wallet share.
    *   Integration of certain siloed support channels into the retail sales team for greater synergy.
    *   Continued engagement with digital platforms (Groww, Zerodha, ETMONEY, Kuvera, Angel) for customer acquisition, with a focus on the top 3-4 platforms.
*   **Financial Projections/Targets:**
    *   **Yields:** Equity yield expected to be around 68 bps, with a slight drop due to telescopic pricing as AUM increases. Debt yields around 23-25 bps. Potential for improved debt yields if longer duration instruments are utilized.
    *   **OPEX:** People costs expected to increase due to inflation and potential recruitment in sales, passive, and alternate businesses. Other expenses may see a recurring cost associated with annual events like "Voyage," likely in Q4.
    *   **Revenue:** Continued growth expected from passive and AIF businesses. PMS and alternate asset revenue expected to be around ₹100 Cr. for FY'24.
*   **Market Share:** Aiming to stabilize and then increase market share, particularly in SIPs, and recover to at least 5% flow market share. Gross equity sales are improving quarter-on-quarter.
*   **SIP Book Dynamics:** SIP market share has improved from 5.7% in December '23 to 6.5% in March '24. The goal is to reach 8-9% market share. The monthly run rate for SIP registrations is around 1.95-2.2 lakhs.
*   **Regulatory Landscape:** No specific guidance on potential SEBI changes. Operating within existing TER guidelines.
*   **Investment Team Changes:** Impact of investment team changes expected to reflect over 3-6 months.
*   **Channel Mix Change:** Digital channels contribute approximately 40-45% of SIP flows (on an account basis), with traditional channels (primarily MFDs) contributing 55-60%.
*   **NFOs:** NFOs provide incremental AUM, new customers, and new SIPs. Commission payout for NFOs is slightly higher to drive traction.
*   **Fund Performance:** Continued emphasis on improved equity fund performance as a key driver for inflows.
*   **Millennial Investor Behavior:** Acknowledged higher churn from younger investors on online platforms but expect them to become long-term investors.
*   **EPFO Mandate:** ABSLAMC participated in the bidding process and is awaiting a decision on winning a mandate for EPFO investments. No clarity on the number of companies to be selected or the frequency of such invitations.
*   **Passive vs. Active:** Management views both passive and active funds as important. While active funds have outperformed passive in the last year, they will continue to build capabilities in the passive space, focusing on profitable products. Active management will remain a focus area for several more years.
