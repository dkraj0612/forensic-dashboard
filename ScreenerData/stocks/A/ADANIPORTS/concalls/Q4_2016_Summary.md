## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported strong results for Q4 FY16, demonstrating resilience amidst a challenging economic environment. The company achieved robust cargo volume growth, driven by a diversified cargo base and significant container volume increases. APSEZ highlighted improvements in operational efficiency and continued strategic development of its port network and SEZs. Management expressed confidence in sustained growth and profitability, emphasizing their ability to handle increasing cargo volumes and their focus on debt reduction.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Increased by 19% to ₹6,152 crores.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Grew by 24% to ₹2,867 crores.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** Improved to 64% from 63% in the previous year.
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Increased by 18% to ₹725 crores.
*   **Gross Debt:** Approximately ₹21,000 crores.
*   **Net Debt:** Approximately ₹17,000 crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Handled 157 million tons for the full year, with a consolidated cargo growth of 5% YoY. Container volumes showed strong growth of 24% YoY (3.35 million TEUs in Q4).
*   **Port Network Expansion:** Continued development and operational improvements across existing ports (Mundra, Dhamra, Hazira, Kattupalli, Vizag). Emphasis on the twin-port strategy and enhancing coastal shipping capabilities.
*   **SEZ Development:** Progress in developing SEZs, with a focus on integrated ecosystems. SEZ revenue for the quarter was approximately ₹340 crores.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Continued focus on leveraging technology, optimizing operations, and improving profitability through efficiency gains.
*   **Logistics and Infrastructure:** Expanding logistics business, including a focus on container logistics and building integrated solutions for customers.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Continued engagement with partners and customers, including new agreements for cargo handling.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Context:** This is the first earnings call transcript available, serving as the baseline for performance and guidance.
*   **Management Narrative:** Management acknowledged a generally weak economic scenario and a decline in coal imports. However, they emphasized that overall performance remained robust due to a diversified cargo base and strong container growth. They highlighted outperformance in container volumes and Western coast ports compared to peers. The narrative remained consistent with the previous quarter's positive outlook on operational efficiency and strategic growth.
*   **New Forward-Looking Guidance:**
    *   **Cargo Volume Guidance:** Full-year consolidated cargo for FY17 is expected to grow by 15% to 20%. Coastal shipping cargo is targeted at 25-30 million tons by 2020.
    *   **CAPEX:** Continued CAPEX spending planned for FY17, with an estimated outlay of approximately ₹900 crores for port expansion and ₹150-200 crores for the LNG terminal at Dhamra.
    *   **Debt Reduction:** Management is actively looking to reduce debt through operational cash flow and potentially strategic restructuring.
    *   **Profitability:** Expectation of continued improvement in operating efficiencies and profit margins, with a focus on increasing port revenue and SEZ revenue.
    *   **Timelines:** CT4 terminal is expected to be commissioned by the end of Q4 FY17 or early Q1 FY18. Continued expansion of Dhamra port is also anticipated.
    *   **SEZ Revenue:** Approximately ₹340 crores for Q4 FY16, with continued growth expected.
