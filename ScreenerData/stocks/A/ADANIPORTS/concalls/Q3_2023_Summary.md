## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported strong Q3 FY23 results, demonstrating robust growth and reaffirming their FY24 guidance. The company achieved its highest-ever nine-month performance, with revenue and EBITDA tracking towards the upper end of their guided ranges. Key drivers included strong port business performance with increased revenue per ton due to a favorable shift in customer mix and growth outside JV terminals, and significant logistics business expansion with improved EBITDA margins. APSEZ highlighted its continued focus on deleveraging, targeting a Net Debt to EBITDA ratio of around 2.5x by FY24. The company also provided updates on strategic initiatives, including progress on major acquisitions and international projects, while reiterating a clear policy on capital allocation and shareholder returns.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q3 FY23 Operational Highlights:**
    *   Total Revenue from Operations: Rs. 4,786 Crores (+18% YoY).
    *   Port Business Revenue: Rs. 3,936 Crores.
    *   Logistics Business Revenue: Rs. 490 Crores.
    *   EBITDA: Rs. 3,011 Crores (+15% YoY).
    *   Port Business EBITDA: Rs. 2,737 Crores.
    *   Logistics Business EBITDA: Rs. 142 Crores (+80% YoY).
    *   Port Revenue per Ton: Increased by Rs. 58 YoY (+13%), with Rs. 15 attributed to INR depreciation and Rs. 43 to higher-paying customers and non-JV terminal growth.
    *   Logistics EBITDA Margin: Expanded by 400 bps to 29.3%.
    *   Port EBITDA Margin: Maintained at 70%.

*   **Financial Outlook & Targets:**
    *   Expected Net Debt by March 2023: Rs. 44,000 Crores (includes Haifa Port payment, Tumb ICD, and ongoing Capex).
    *   FY2024 EBITDA Guidance: Rs. 14,000 - Rs. 15,000 Crores.
    *   FY2024 Capex Guidance: Rs. 4,000 - Rs. 4,500 Crores.
    *   Planned Debt Repayment/Prepayment in FY24: Rs. 5,000 Crores.
    *   Target Net Debt to EBITDA Ratio (FY24): ~2.5x.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transport Utility Model:** Continued integration of port and logistics services to provide end-to-end solutions.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Focus on driving efficiencies and improving EBITDA margins through asset utilization and digitalization.
*   **Cargo Diversification:** Expanding into new cargo types and balancing cargo across East and West Coasts.
*   **Geographical Diversification:** Growth in non-Mundra ports and international expansion (Haifa, Tanzania, Sri Lanka).
*   **Logistics Integration:** Deepening integration of rail, MMLPs, and warehousing for a single-window customer service.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Ongoing expansions and development of new terminals and logistics infrastructure.
*   **Capital Management:** Commitment to deleveraging to ~2.5x Net Debt to EBITDA and a dividend policy of 20-25% of PAT.
*   **Acquisition Strategy:** Proven track record of acquiring and enhancing asset performance (e.g., Karaikal Port bid status).

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance:**
    *   **Revenue & EBITDA:** Management is confident of achieving FY24 revenue and EBITDA guidance, likely closing the year at the upper end of the guided ranges. This indicates strong performance against initial expectations.
    *   **Net Debt to EBITDA:** The target of ~2.5x for FY24 is reaffirmed, with planned debt repayment of Rs. 5,000 Crores in FY24 contributing to this. This shows consistent financial management.
    *   **Logistics Growth:** Logistics revenue increased by 64% YoY, and EBITDA grew by 80% with margin expansion, demonstrating consistent execution in this segment.
    *   **Capex:** FY24 Capex guidance of Rs. 4,000-4,500 Crores is presented as a tail-end completion of existing projects rather than a reduction in growth strategy.

*   **Consistency of Narrative:** The narrative remains consistent with previous communications, emphasizing the "Transport Utility" model, integrated logistics and port development, organic growth, diversification, strategic partnerships, and digital transformation. The commitment to deleveraging and operational efficiency is a recurring theme.
