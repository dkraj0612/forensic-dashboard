## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) delivered a strong Q3 FY18, demonstrating significant growth across key operational and financial metrics. Driven by robust cargo volumes and expanding market share, the company saw a substantial increase in revenue and EBITDA. Container volumes continued their impressive surge, with APSEZ solidifying its position as a leading container port operator in India. The company also provided optimistic guidance for the remainder of FY18 and beyond, emphasizing continued growth in container and bulk cargo segments, along with strategic expansion projects and a focus on operational efficiency and deleveraging.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Diversification & Growth:** Continued focus on handling all types of cargo. Strong growth in other bulk (8%), container (22%), and agri (17%) segments, offsetting de-growth in coal at certain ports.
*   **Container Market Leadership:** APSEZ is on track to become the largest container port operator in India in FY18. Aggressive expansion in container volumes driven by new liner services and terminal capacity (CT4).
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Aiming for 30-35% revenue growth in logistics with a target steady-state EBITDA margin of 12%.
*   **SEZ Development:** Focused on land sales and development of SEZ infrastructure, including liquid terminals and bulk handling facilities.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Management is focused on achieving at least 100 basis points increase in port EBITDA margins for FY18, targeting 73-74%.
*   **Strategic Port Development:** Continued investment in expanding capacity and efficiency at key ports like Mundra, Hazira, Kattupalli, and Dhamra, with ongoing projects like CT3 extension and CT4 commissioning.
*   **Deleveraging:** Plans to use generated free cash flow for debt reduction, aiming to improve the Net Debt to EBITDA ratio.

**Audit of Q3 FY18 Performance and Narrative:**

*   **Headwinds (HMEL, Dhamra):** Management's narrative remains consistent. They acknowledged past headwinds impacting Q1/Q2 performance. The current Q3 results show a strong rebound, with overall cargo volumes growing 3.6% for the nine months and significant acceleration in Q3. The promise of H2 recovery is being fulfilled.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management is exceeding previous targets. Port EBITDA margin for the nine months reached 68%, up 200 bps YoY, already surpassing the initial FY18 target of 70% and indicating continued margin expansion. The consolidated EBITDA margin also improved to 68% in Q3 FY18. Management is now targeting at least 100 basis points increase for the full year, implying further gains.
*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Management reaffirmed their **guided cargo volume growth of 12% to 14% for FY18**. The current 9-month volume of 135 million tons is on track to meet this guidance. The strong Q3 performance validates the expectation of a robust H2.
*   **SEZ Income Recognition (CT4):** The narrative has shifted. While the H1 FY18 summary stated CT4 income was fully recognized, the current transcript clarifies that CT4 income was "partially booked in this quarter" (Q1 FY18) and "a similar amount of at least revenues and maybe larger amount of profits" are expected in subsequent quarters. This is a significant change in the timing of full recognition compared to the prior understanding.
*   **Kattupalli Acquisition Timeline:** The timeline has been **accelerated** further. The H1 FY18 context indicated completion by January 2018, and the Q1 FY18 call mentioned September 2017. In this Q3 call, management stated the sale is expected by September 2017, indicating consistent acceleration.
*   **CAPEX Guidance:** Q3 CAPEX spending is tracking proportionally to the **FY18 guidance of INR 2,500-2,800 crores**. Management has indicated they might save INR 100-200 crores, showing prudent capital allocation.
*   **Net Debt to EBITDA:** Management stated they are "well on course" with debt reduction targets, implying continued improvement from the H1 FY18 ratio of 2.64x. The strategy to use free cash flow for debt repayment remains a priority.
*   **Vizag Impairment:** A significant impairment of INR 72 crores was recognized for Vizag Port due to accounting policies and a loan impact on the consolidated level. This was explained as a necessary step following losses.
