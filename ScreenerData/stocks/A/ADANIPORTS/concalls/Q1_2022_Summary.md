## Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) - Q1 FY2022 Earnings Call - One-Page Executive Summary

**Date:** October 26, 2023

---

### Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a robust Q1 FY2022, showcasing significant growth across its port and logistics operations. The company attributed its strong performance to efficient port operations, seamless multimodal integration, and its proprietary cargo evacuation network. APSEZ has further solidified its market leadership, with Mundra port emerging as India's largest container handling port, surpassing JNPT. The acquisition of Gangavaram Port is progressing, with integration and consolidation expected soon, further strengthening APSEZ's East Coast presence. The company is actively expanding its logistics vertical, acquiring rail track assets and developing multimodal logistics parks, reinforcing its transition towards a comprehensive transport utility. Management reiterated its confidence in achieving long-term targets, including 500 MMT cargo throughput and doubling EBITDA by FY2025.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Logistics and Transport Utility:** Continued focus on evolving into a comprehensive logistics service provider, moving beyond port operations to direct customer delivery.
*   **Inorganic Growth & Consolidation:** Progressing with the acquisition of Gangavaram Port and consolidation of rail track assets under Sarguja Rail Corporation (SRCPL).
*   **Market Share Expansion:** Leveraging operational efficiencies and capacity additions to gain market share in both overall cargo and container segments.
*   **Cargo Diversification:** Expanding cargo basket to include new types like sulphur, dolomite, LPG, and LNG, catering to government's push for a gas-based economy.
*   **Strategic Port Development:** Focus on enhancing capabilities at flagship Mundra port and developing the East Coast portfolio (Krishnapatnam, Gangavaram, Dhamra).
*   **Logistics Network Expansion:** Building multimodal logistics parks, warehousing, and expanding the rail distribution network, including acquisition of strategic assets on DFC corridors.
*   **Capacity Augmentation:** Investing in new services and strategically shifting container volumes between Kattupalli and Ennore to optimize customer solutions.
