## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a strong 9-month period ending December 2016, demonstrating continued outperformance against pan-India port growth. Cargo volumes grew by 11% YoY for 9MFY17 (126 MMT) and 8% YoY for Q3FY17 (41 MMT), significantly exceeding the all-India growth of 7% and 8% respectively. Revenue and EBITDA saw substantial growth of 20% and 22% respectively for 9MFY17, with PAT increasing by 38%. Q3FY17 performance was even more robust, with revenue up 32%, EBITDA up 30%, and PAT up 23%. The company's strategic shift towards higher-margin cargo, particularly containers, is evident, with container volumes growing by 28% in 9MFY17, contributing 36% of the total cargo mix. The logistics and SEZ businesses also showed positive momentum, and the acquisition of Abbot Point Bulkcoal in Australia began contributing to the results from Q3FY17. Management reaffirmed its commitment to debt reduction and highlighted improved operational efficiencies and cost rationalization, including a strategic demerger of the marine services business.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Continued outperformance against India's port growth, driven by a diversified cargo mix and strong performance at Dhamra (47% growth) and Hazira (24% growth).
*   **Container Focus:** Significant 28% YoY growth in container volumes for 9MFY17, with a target of becoming India's highest container volume handler. Container contribution to cargo mix increased to 36%. Hyundai added a new service at Kattupalli, expecting ~15,000 TEUs annual volume.
*   **Logistics Business:** Adani Logistics Limited achieved terminal volumes of 2.21 lakh TEUs (24% YoY growth) and rail volumes increased by 52% in 9MFY17. Rail capacity increased by 33% in Q3FY17.
*   **SEZ Development:** Momentum in enquiries from diverse sectors (packaging, battery inverters, solar panels) expected to drive EXIM cargo growth at Mundra.
*   **Marine Services Demerger:** Adani Harbour Services Pvt Ltd (formerly TMHSPL) formed to consolidate tugging business, aiming for cost rationalization, better asset utilization, and specialized focus, with benefits from tonnage taxation.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** Container Terminal 4 (CT4) commissioning expected by end of Q4 FY17 or early Q1 FY18.
*   **Australian Operations:** Abbot Point Bulk Coal acquisition completed and results consolidated from Q3 FY17.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Historical Context:** The Q3 FY17 earnings call continues the company's narrative of strong operational and financial performance, driven by strategic cargo diversification and operational efficiencies. Management's tone remains confident.

*   **Management Narrative & Target Attainment:**
    *   **Related Party Loan Reduction:** Management reaffirmed commitment to reduce related party loans, targeting complete reduction by March 2017. Progress on this front is expected to be finalized by the end of FY17.
    *   **Cargo Volume Guidance:** The full-year guidance was refined, with management now expecting APSEZ's growth to be at least 50% higher than pan-India port growth in FY17, narrowing the earlier guidance of 10-15%. This is an "observation" rather than a deviation, reflecting consistent outperformance.
    *   **CAPEX Guidance:** FY17 CAPEX is confirmed to be lower than INR 3,400 crores, indicating effective cost control. FY18 CAPEX is expected to be lower than FY17.
    *   **SEZ Revenue:** SEZ income reported as consistent, with no exceptional land sales in Q3 FY17, contrary to previous quarters which sometimes included such sales.
    *   **Container Handling:** On track to handle significant container volumes, with expectations of minimum 1 million TEUs per quarter going forward.
    *   **Marine Business Demerger:** Acquisition of TMHSPL completed, and business demerged into Adani Harbour Services to leverage tonnage taxation benefits, estimated at INR 150 crores annually.
    *   **Dhamra Port Operations:** Container handling to commence from Q4 FY17.

*   **New Forward-Looking Guidance & Promises:**
    *   **Container Volumes:** Aim to be India's highest container volume handler by FY17. Expecting minimum of 1 million TEUs per quarter going forward.
    *   **Dhamra Port:** Container handling to commence from Q4 FY17. Expected to handle liquids, fertilizers, and agri products from FY18 onwards. Consistent YoY growth of 25-30% expected.
    *   **Kattupalli Acquisition:** Expected to be completed by Q4 FY17 or Q1 FY18.
    *   **Vizhinjam Port:** Jetty contract to be awarded in the next 2-3 weeks. Project completion expected by mid to end of 2018. Initial capacity of 1.2 million TEUs.
    *   **Bhavanapadu Port:** No update from the government on the bid submission.
    *   **SEZ Recurring Revenue:** Consistent income from SEZ clusters expected quarter-on-quarter.
    *   **CAPEX Trend:** Capital expenditure is expected to peak by mid-FY18 and then trend downwards.
    *   **Tonnage Tax Benefit:** Annual tax savings of approximately INR 150 crores from the marine services demerger.
    *   **Net Interest Cost:** Approximately 2.5% on the debt portfolio.
    *   **Overseas Opportunities:** Exploring Greenfield port development opportunities in Southeast Asia, Africa, Bangladesh, or Myanmar, but no concrete plans currently.
    *   **FX Reclassification:** Reclassification of certain FX gains/losses from EBITDA to finance costs due to IND AS implementation.
    *   **Kattupalli Payment:** Payment to L&T for Kattupalli acquisition will be a one-time payment, expected to be completed in Q4 FY17, removing future EBITDA impact.
    *   **Maersk Business:** While growth of Maersk line business may face challenges due to consolidation, no risk of them shifting their entire volume out of Mundra is anticipated.
    *   **MAT Credit:** MAT credit for Hazira and Dhamra was not taken in Q3 FY17 due to a 10-year lapse period. This is being re-evaluated with tax experts following recent finance bill changes extending the period to 15 years, potentially making it a one-off impact.
