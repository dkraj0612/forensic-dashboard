## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported H1 FY18 results showing a recovery and improved operational momentum. While overall cargo volumes grew by a modest 2.2% YoY to 87 MMT (excluding Australia's 13 MMT coal), container volumes surged by 19% YoY, outperforming pan-India growth and increasing APSEZ's market share to 33%. This growth was driven by new liner additions and strong performance at Kattupalli, Mundra, and Hazira. Temporary headwinds from HMEL's maintenance shutdown and Dhamra port's mechanization impacted H1 volumes, but management anticipates a stronger H2 FY18, projecting growth to be 1.5 times India's cargo growth. Financially, H1 FY18 saw a 36% YoY increase in operating income to INR 5,451 crores and a 31% YoY rise in EBITDA to INR 3,493 crores, with EBITDA margins improving by 200 bps to 69%. The company generated INR 690 crores in free cash flow and reduced net debt by INR 737 crores, bringing the net debt to EBITDA ratio down to 2.64x. APSEZ is progressing on its logistics expansion and expects to reward shareholders by March 2018, contingent on unwinding certain debt positions.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Targeting 1.5x pan-India volume growth in H2 FY18. Outperformance expected to continue, driven by diversified cargo mix.
*   **Container Focus:** Continued strong growth in container volumes (19% in H1 FY18), aiming to be India's highest container handler. Focus on increasing market share.
*   **Logistics Business Expansion:** Land acquisition completed for ICDs in Bangalore and Tiroda; construction to commence soon. Order for 15 more rail rakes to be placed. CAPEX allocation of INR 125-150 crores for logistics in FY18.
*   **SEZ Development:** Ongoing development and recognition of SEZ income (e.g., CT4 completed, CTC extension, LPG, LNG, Thane LNG in progress) expected to drive future revenue.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Kattupalli acquisition progressing, with transaction expected to complete by January 2018.
*   **New Agreements:** Signed 7 MMT take-or-pay contract with SAIL at Dhamra for the next three years. Seeing an uptick in volumes from Reliance Industries at Dahej and Hazira.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** CT3 expansion nearing completion. Vizhinjam port expected to be ready by end of CY2020 with a 2 MMT capacity, aiming for 1.25-1.5 MMT in its first year.
