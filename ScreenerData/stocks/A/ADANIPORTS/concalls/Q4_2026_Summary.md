## Executive Overview

Adani Ports & Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported strong results for FY26, exceeding guidance for revenue, EBITDA, and capex. The company achieved significant YoY growth across key segments, handling a record 500.8 MMT of cargo. Domestic ports saw revenue growth and an increase in market share, with improved ROCE. International ports experienced substantial revenue growth driven by acquisitions and ramp-ups. The Logistics business delivered robust revenue growth, achieving double-digit ROCE ahead of schedule, fueled by asset-light and asset-zero services. The Marine business also saw impressive revenue growth due to fleet expansion. APSEZ maintained financial discipline with a Net Debt to EBITDA ratio of 1.9x and a consolidated ROCE of 16%. Management acknowledged geopolitical volatility and global tariff uncertainties but emphasized the resilience of their integrated transport utility model and disciplined execution.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility:** Continued focus on the "shore-to-door" model, integrating port operations, rail, multi-modal logistics parks, warehousing, and road delivery.
*   **Segmental Growth & Diversification:** Acceleration in asset-light and asset-zero services (Trucking, International Freight Network) alongside efficient asset-heavy operations. Marine business expansion and international port development are key growth enablers.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Emphasis on digital platforms, AI, and automation to enhance efficiency, productivity, and asset utilization.
*   **Capital Efficiency:** Driving double-digit ROCE across segments, with a consolidated target of 20% ROCE.
*   **Strategic CAPEX:** Accelerated investments in key ports (Mundra, Dhamra, Hazira, Vizhinjam) and infrastructure to support future capacity and growth.
*   **Sustainability:** Commitment to Net Zero by 2040, with investments in decarbonization, renewable energy, electric vehicles, and biodiversity initiatives, all integrated with economic benefits.
