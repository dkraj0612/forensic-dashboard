## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) has announced an agreement to acquire a significant stake in Indian Oiltanking Limited (IOTL) and its subsidiary, IOT Utkal Energy Services Ltd. This strategic acquisition positions APSEZ as India's largest third-party liquid storage player, a crucial step in its transformation into the largest transport utility. The deal is expected to diversify APSEZ's cargo mix, enhance realization and margins, and solidify its partnership with Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. (IOCL). IOTL, an established player with strategically located assets and a proven track record in handling complex projects, brings end-to-end terminalling solutions and possesses substantial land parcels for future expansion. This move aligns with APSEZ's long-term growth strategy, capitalizing on India's projected doubling of crude and POL demand over the next two decades.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **IOTL Acquisition Deal Terms:**
    *   Acquisition of Oiltanking India GmbH’s 49.38% equity stake in Indian Oiltanking Ltd (IOTL).
    *   Acquisition of Oiltanking GmbH’s 10% equity stake in IOT Utkal Energy Services Ltd.
    *   EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 8x for the acquisition, noted as lower than comparable transactions.

*   **IOTL Key Capacities & Assets:**
    *   Total Storage Capacity: Approximately 2.4 Mn KL across six facilities in six states.
    *   Land Parcels: ~90 acres in Maharashtra, Jamshedpur, and Punjab, plus commercial real estate in Mumbai and Kolkata.

    *   Biogas Plant: Compressed Biogas (CBG) plant at Namakkal, Tamil Nadu.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Acquisition of IOTL:** This is the primary strategic driver highlighted. APSEZ aims to become India's largest third-party liquid storage player, enhancing its transport utility model.
*   **Cargo Mix Diversification:** The acquisition will significantly diversify APSEZ's cargo portfolio beyond traditional dry and container cargo, adding a substantial liquid storage segment.
*   **Strengthening IOCL Partnership:** The acquisition deepens the relationship with IOCL, India's largest refiner, creating synergistic opportunities.
*   **Geographic and Asset Expansion:** IOTL's existing facilities and land banks provide immediate scale and future expansion potential. APSEZ intends to leverage IOTL's capabilities in building and operating liquid terminals, including jetties and pipelines.
*   **Growth in Liquid Cargo Demand:** The strategy is underpinned by the expectation of India's crude and POL demand more than doubling in the next two decades.
*   **Focus on End-to-End Solutions:** APSEZ plans to leverage IOTL's expertise in providing end-to-end terminalling solutions, from concept to commissioning.
*   **Company Governance:** APSEZ will appoint the Managing Director of IOTL, while IOCL will nominate the Finance Director, ensuring strategic alignment.
