## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported its strongest ever full-year performance for FY23, exceeding revenue and EBITDA guidance. The company achieved a cargo throughput of 339 million metric tons, a 9% year-on-year increase, and a record 8.8 million TEUs in container volumes. Logistics business EBITDA margins improved to 28%. APSEZ made significant investments, including six acquisitions totaling INR 18,000 crores and INR 9,000 crores in organic capex. The company reaffirmed its target of a Net Debt to EBITDA ratio of around 2.5x by March 2024, supported by scheduled and unscheduled debt repayments, including a bond buyback program. Strategic initiatives include expanding logistics infrastructure and new cargo types at ports.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **FY23 Operational Highlights:**
    *   Total Operating Revenue: INR 20,852 Crores (+22% YoY).
    *   EBITDA: INR 12,833 Crores (+22% YoY).
    *   Logistics Business EBITDA Margin: 28% (+150 bps YoY).
    *   Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 5,393 Crores (+9% YoY), after a write-off of INR 1,273 crores for Myanmar asset sale.
    *   Cargo Throughput: 339 million metric tons (+9% YoY).
    *   Highest Ever Container Volume: 8.8 million TEUs.
    *   Total Investment in FY23: INR 27,000 Crores (INR 18,000 Crores on acquisitions, INR 9,000 Crores on organic capex).
    *   Gross Debt to Fixed Asset Ratio: Declined to 60% from 80% in FY19.
    *   Net Debt to EBITDA Ratio: 3.1x (within guided range of 3x to 3.5x).
    *   Recommended Dividend: INR 5 per share (payout of INR 1,080 Crores).

*   **FY24 Guidance:**
    *   Cargo Volume: 370 to 390 million metric tons.
    *   Revenue: INR 24,000 to INR 25,000 Crores.
    *   EBITDA: INR 14,500 to INR 15,000 Crores.
    *   Capex: INR 4,000 to INR 4,500 Crores (INR 3,000 Crores for ports, INR 1,000-1,500 Crores for logistics).
    *   Target Net Debt to EBITDA Ratio (March 2024): ~2.5x.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transport Utility Model:** Continued integration of port and logistics services for end-to-end solutions.
*   **Acquisitions & Organic Growth:** Completed six key acquisitions (Haifa Port, Indian Oil Tanking, Ocean Sparkles, ICD-Tumb, Gangavaram Port, Karaikal Port) and invested in organic capex.
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Commissioning of MMLPs, addition of container and bulk trains, and expansion of agri-silo capacity.
*   **Cargo Diversification:** Addition of new cargo types at various ports (e.g., soya bean, sulphur, sugar, rice, wheat).
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Commissioning of terminals and storage facilities, with ongoing expansions underway.
*   **Deleveraging:** Commitment to reducing Net Debt to EBITDA to 2.5x by March 2024.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Reaffirmed dividend policy of 20-25% of PAT.
*   **ESG Focus:** Ranked first in the transport and logistics sector by Moody's; top 10 globally by S&P.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance:**
    *   **Revenue & EBITDA:** APSEZ exceeded its FY23 revenue and EBITDA guidance, achieving INR 20,852 Crores and INR 12,833 Crores respectively, indicating strong execution. The FY24 guidance for revenue (INR 24,000-25,000 Crores) and EBITDA (INR 14,500-15,000 Crores) was also provided with confidence.
    *   **Net Debt to EBITDA:** The Net Debt to EBITDA ratio stood at 3.1x at the end of FY23, which is within the guided range of 3x to 3.5x. Management reaffirmed the target of around 2.5x by March 2024, stating that recent bond buybacks are within the already factored repayment plans.
    *   **Logistics Growth:** The logistics EBITDA margin improved to 28%, higher than most listed peers, demonstrating consistent performance in this segment.
    *   **Capex:** The FY24 capex guidance of INR 4,000-4,500 Crores is planned for port expansions and logistics business development.

*   **Consistency of Narrative:** The management maintained a consistent narrative emphasizing the "Transport Utility" model, integrated logistics and port development, organic growth, diversification, strategic acquisitions, and digital transformation. The focus on deleveraging and operational efficiency remains a core theme.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Myanmar Asset Sale:** Sale consideration of $30 million received; actual entry will happen in Q1 FY24.
    *   **Karaikal Port:** Included in FY24 volume guidance (8-12 million tons); EBITDA contribution expected between INR 300-350 Crores, consolidated from FY24 beginning.
    *   **Tariff Hikes:** 3-4% per ton hike implemented or under negotiation, expected to be in place by June.

    *   **Colombo Terminal:** Reclamation complete, jetty construction commenced; Phase I commissioning by December 2024.

    *   **Logistics Container Trains:** Order for 14 bulk trains and 24 container trains; to arrive during FY24. Total train count to increase to 93 (43 container, 40 bulk).
    *   **MMLPs:** Targeting to commission three more in FY24, taking total to 9.
    *   **Agri-Silo Capacity:** Will surpass guided ambition of 2.5 million metric tons by FY26 with recent contracts at 70 locations.
    *   **Haifa Port Real Estate:** Unlocking value from real estate is a medium-to-long term plan, with potential realizations materializing from year 4-5.

    *   **Share Pledges:** Reduced from 17% to 4% as of March 31, 2023; moving towards zero in about a year.
