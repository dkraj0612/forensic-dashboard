## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) delivered its strongest ever half-yearly results in H1 FY2024, marked by record cargo volumes, revenue, and EBITDA. The company reported a robust 26% YoY increase in operating revenue to INR 12,894 crores and a 49% YoY growth in EBITDA to INR 7,429 crores. Cargo throughput reached 203 million metric tonnes, a 14% YoY increase, with significant growth across dry bulk, liquids, and containers. APSEZ is on track to achieve its full-year revenue and EBITDA guidance, likely at the higher end, supported by strong October volumes. The company successfully repaid INR 5,000 crores of loans and completed bond buybacks, leading to a reduction in net debt to INR 38,696 crores and an improved net debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 2.8x. Key acquisitions and ongoing strategic investments in logistics infrastructure continue to drive growth and market share gains.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **H1 FY2024 Operational & Financial Highlights:**
    *   **Operating Revenue:** INR 12,894 Crores (+26% YoY)
    *   **EBITDA (incl. forex):** INR 7,429 Crores (+49% YoY)
    *   **Ports EBITDA Margin:** 72% (+220 bps YoY)
    *   **Logistics EBITDA Margin:** 29% (best in domestic peer group)
    *   **Cargo Throughput:** 203 million metric tonnes (+14% YoY)
    *   **Dry Bulk Cargo:** +10% YoY
    *   **Liquids Cargo:** +21% YoY
    *   **Container Cargo:** +18% YoY
    *   **Mundra Container Volume:** 3.6 million TEUs (+15% vs. closest competitor)
    *   **Logistics Rail Volumes:** 279,177 TEUs (+25% YoY)
    *   **Logistics Bulk Cargo (GPWIS):** 8.92 million metric tonnes (+42% YoY)
    *   **Total Logistics Rakes:** 104 (as of September end)
    *   **Net Debt:** INR 38,696 Crores (down from INR 39,989 Crores on March 31, '23)
    *   **Net Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio:** 2.8x (down from 3.1x on March 31, '23)

*   **FY2024 Guidance Reaffirmed:**
    *   **Cargo Volume:** Aiming for the higher end of 370-390 million metric tons (achieved 240 million MT in first 7 months).
    *   **Capex:** INR 4,000 to INR 4,500 Crores (equity contribution for Sri Lanka port included in this).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transport Utility Model:** Continued focus on integrated port and logistics solutions for end-to-end services.
*   **Logistics Network Expansion:** Commissioning of Loni ICD (10th MMLP), addition of warehouses, and expansion of the rail fleet to 104 rakes.
*   **Asset Integration & Utilization:** Enhanced utilization of existing logistics assets leading to significant growth in rail and bulk volumes.
*   **International Expansion:** Successful ramp-up of Haifa Port in Israel, with potential to benefit from increased traffic due to regional conflicts. Funding secured for Colombo Port in Sri Lanka.
*   **Cargo Diversification:** Growth observed across all major cargo categories, indicating a balanced portfolio.
*   **Deleveraging:** Proactive debt repayment and bond buybacks demonstrating commitment to financial discipline.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance:**
    *   **H1 FY2024 Performance:** APSEZ significantly outperformed initial half-year expectations, achieving record volumes, revenue, and EBITDA. The company's cargo throughput of 203 million MT positions it strongly to meet or exceed the upper end of its full-year guidance.
    *   **Debt Reduction & Leverage:** Management has actively pursued its stated intention to repay loans and execute bond buybacks, leading to a tangible reduction in net debt and improvement in the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio, aligning with previous financial targets.
    *   **Logistics Growth:** The logistics business continues to demonstrate strong growth and margin improvement (29% EBITDA margin), consistent with management's focus on this segment.
    *   **Haifa Port Operations:** Management accurately reported initial performance and provided clear insights into operational status amidst regional conflicts, noting increased traffic due to diversion from other ports.

*   **Consistency of Narrative:** The management maintained a consistent narrative emphasizing the integrated transport utility model, the strength of its logistics business, and its commitment to deleveraging. The strategic focus on expanding its port and logistics network, both domestically and internationally, remains a core theme.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Colombo Port (Sri Lanka):** Phase 1 commissioning and operationalization expected by December 2024. Capex for this project is included in the existing guidance.
    *   **West Bengal Greenfield (Tajpur) Port:** Waiting for Letter of Award (LOA). Construction is realistically 18-24 months away post-LOA, including environmental clearances.
    *   **Haifa Port (Israel):** Expected to handle 1.1 million metric tonnes in October, with continued strong volumes anticipated due to traffic diversion from Ashdod port. Management is negotiating with unions for margin improvement, with settlement expected by December.
    *   **Vizhinjam Port:** First vessel expected by October 2023. Phase I commissioning by March 2024, full commissioning by May 2024. Scalable commercial operations are expected in the second half of FY2025.
    *   **Logistics Rakes:** Plan to reach 128 rakes by the end of FY2024 (adding 24 from 104 currently). Further additions are planned for FY2025, with details to be confirmed.
    *   **MMLPs:** 10 MMLPs commissioned as of H1 FY2024.
    *   **Warehousing Capacity:** Increased to 2.4 million square feet.
    *   **FY2025 Outlook:** Management is on track to achieve its previously stated guidance of 500 million tonnes by 2025. Specific FY2025 volume guidance was not provided but implied strong growth.
    *   **Tax Regime Change:** A subsidiary is moving to a 25% tax regime from 35%, foregoing INR 455 crores of net MAT credit (a non-cash accounting write-off) for long-term tax savings.
    *   **Gujarat Port Concession Policy:** Awaiting policy formulation from the Gujarat government for private ports.
    *   **Dighi Port (Maharashtra):** Expected to stabilize and become a significant alternative gateway to JNPT in 4-5 years, currently undergoing development including liquid operations, railway line, and industrial park.
    *   **International Strategy:** Focus remains on high-growth countries, brownfield expansions, privatization opportunities, and local partnerships, with continuous evaluation of opportunities in countries like Vietnam, Bangladesh, Eastern Africa, and the Middle East.
    *   **IMEC Impact:** The India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) is seen as a strategic positive for Haifa, potentially reducing transit times and costs.
    *   **Container Import/Export Mix:** 60% import, 40% export.
    *   **Haifa Port Revenue Currency:** Revenues are primarily in local currency (NIS), which has historically been stable against the USD. Hedging is in place for near-term exposures.
    *   **Other Financial Assets:** Increase attributed to advances for acquisitions (now completed) and short-term investments. Advances for capex have decreased.
    *   **JV Profitability:** The lower consolidated JV associate income reported is due to losses from Dhamra LNG, which is in a ramp-up stage.
    *   **ICDs:** No planned deployment or realization of funds in ICDs going forward; current balance is zero.
    *   **Medium-Term Financial Policy:** Target of 2.5x net debt-to-EBITDA ratio remains for the medium to long term, with flexibility for short-term increases for strategic growth opportunities.
    *   **Inorganic Growth:** APSEZ will continue to evaluate inorganic opportunities but expects significant growth to come from brownfield expansions and organic capacity additions at existing ports, offering a lower cost base than greenfield projects.
    *   **Port Privatization:** Management is cautious about returns from government privatizations but will consider strategically located assets if they fill portfolio gaps.
