## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a challenging Q1 FY21, with cargo volumes declining by 27% year-on-year due to lockdown measures. Despite this, the company demonstrated resilience, with consolidated revenue declining by a lesser 18% and maintaining healthy port EBITDA margins of 70%. This was achieved through a strategic focus on container business diversification and cost optimization initiatives, including moving operating contracts from fixed to variable costs and reskilling manpower. Mundra Port emerged as the largest container port in India, surpassing JNPT. The logistics business saw a 10% revenue growth. Management indicated that the worst is likely behind them, with July showing a 6% year-on-year cargo volume growth and a significant shift towards exports. Key acquisitions like Krishnapatnam and Dighi are on track for completion. APSEZ also strengthened its ESG credentials and board independence.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q1 FY21 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 2,293 Crores (18% YoY decline)
*   **Q1 FY21 Consolidated EBITDA:** Not explicitly stated, but implied to be lower than Q1 FY20 due to revenue decline and favorable FX impact on consolidated figures.
*   **Q1 FY21 Port Revenue:** Rs. 1,904 Crores (21% YoY decline)
*   **Q1 FY21 Port EBITDA Margin:** 70% (Maintained)
*   **Q1 FY21 Logistics Revenue:** Rs. 200 Crores (10% YoY growth)
*   **Q1 FY21 Cargo Volume:** 41.5 MMT (27% YoY decline)
*   **Q1 FY21 FX Gain (Mark-to-market):** Rs. 37 Crores (vs. Rs. 3 Crores gain in Q1 FY20)
*   **Q1 FY21 Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs. 943 Crores
*   **Q1 FY21 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 758 Crores
*   **Q1 FY21 COVID Contribution:** Rs. 80 Crores (Rs. 60 Cr from APSEZ, Rs. 20 Cr from Adani Harbor)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Resilience:** Despite overall decline, focus on container business and cargo mix diversification (43% container, 28% coal, 10% crude, 19% other bulk) cushioned the impact.
*   **Market Share Expansion:** Container market share increased from 36% to 38%.
*   **Mundra Port Leadership:** Became the largest container port in India, handling 0.97 million TEUs, surpassing JNPT.
*   **Logistics Growth:** Adani Logistics Limited (ALL) saw rail volume growth of 37% and revenue growth of 10%, operating 60 rakes. Adani Agri-Logistics Limited (AALL) managed procurement and movement of 0.61 million metric tonnes.
*   **Acquisition Progress:** Krishnapatnam Port (KPCL) acquisition approval received, expected completion in Q2 FY21. Dighi Port resolution plan approved, expected completion by Q3 FY21.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Strategy to move operating contracts from fixed to variable, negotiate price reductions with vendors, and re-optimize resource utilization (equipment, manpower) to maintain margins.
*   **ESG Focus:** Improved CSR hub ranking to high, reduced risk rating by Sustainalytics, commitment to science-based targets and carbon neutrality by 2025. Board strengthened with a higher proportion of independent directors.
*   **International Projects:** Myanmar Phase-1 on schedule for December 2020 commissioning.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Cargo Volume Decline:** Management acknowledged the 27% decline due to lockdowns but emphasized their strategy to cushion the impact, which proved largely successful with revenue declining by a lesser 18%.
*   **Market Share:** The narrative of increasing container market share was validated, with it rising to 38%.
*   **Port EBITDA Margins:** Management successfully maintained port EBITDA margins at 70% despite lower volumes, attributing this to cost optimization and moving fixed costs to variable. This aligns with their strategy to focus on operational efficiency.
*   **Logistics Performance:** The positive narrative for logistics was supported by 10% revenue growth and 37% rail volume increase.
*   **Acquisitions:** Progress on Krishnapatnam and Dighi acquisitions remains on track as per previous indications. The Snowman deal was mutually terminated with Adani Logistics retaining a minority stake.
*   **Pricing:** Management confirmed a 2.5% price hike per tonne, along with benefits from FX movement, contributing to realization per tonne improvement.
*   **Evacuation Issues:** Management stated that evacuation bottlenecks (rail and road) have been resolved.
*   **Guidance on FY21 Volumes:** Management is refraining from giving specific FY21 cargo volume guidance until August and September data is analyzed, opting for a cautious approach due to ongoing uncertainties. This is a shift from providing guidance in previous periods, reflecting the volatile macro environment.

**New Forward-Looking Guidance & Promises (from Q1 FY21 Transcript):**

*   **FY21 Cargo Volume Guidance:** To be provided in October 2020, after assessing August and September performance. Management believes the worst is behind them and does not foresee further degrowth.
*   **FY21 SEZ/Port Led Development Income:** Expected to remain in the range of Rs. 800 Crores to Rs. 1000 Crores.
*   **Mundra Port Connectivity:** DFC connectivity expected to be completed by November 2020, and definitely by end of the calendar year.
*   **Dighi Port Operations:** Expected to become operational by end December 2020. Initial CAPEX of Rs. 150 Crores expected for making it operational. Future CAPEX will be evaluated after 6-12 months of operations.
*   **Container Volume Mix:** Expected to remain around 40-41% of the cargo mix for the current and next fiscal year.
*   **Dhamra Port Growth:** Difficult to provide guidance for the whole year; guidance to be provided in October. July volumes showed a 4-5% YoY increase.
*   **Capex:** Focused on maintaining investment grade rating, with CAPEX in FY21 expected to be Rs. 1800 to Rs. 2000 Crores.
*   **Logistics Business:** ALL to operate 60+ rakes. Developing logistics park in Nagpur and warehouses at Kattupalli, Taloja, and Mundra for H2 FY21.
*   **Myanmar Project:** Phase-1 commissioning expected by December 2020 or early January 2021. Phase-2 construction will commence when Phase-1 capacity utilization reaches ~70%.
*   **Vizhinjam Port:** Phase-1 commissioning expected by end of calendar year 2022.
*   **Kattupalli Port:** Commissioned liquid tank farms for Bitumen with a take-or-pay contract for 5 years with Puma Energy.
*   **Mundra Transshipment:** Increase to 24% from 18% is a structural change and expected to continue, predominantly at Mundra port.
*   **Hazira Port Railway Connectivity:** Agreement with G-Ride (Gujarat Govt. company) needed, followed by 24 months for line construction. Already has railway and environment approvals. Not currently constraining container volumes.
*   **Promoter Pledge:** Currently 33.17% of promoters' holding (21% of total APSEZ stock), on track to be reduced over 18-20 months.
*   **Peak Net Debt for FY21:** To be provided with more detail at end of September, considering KPCL acquisition debt (approx. Rs. 6200 Crores) and Dighi funding.
