## Executive Overview

Adani Ports (APSEZ) demonstrated resilience in Q3 FY16, reporting strong operational performance despite a challenging economic scenario. Total cargo volumes grew by 14% YoY, driven by a diversified cargo base and strong container growth. The company is focusing on improving operational efficiency and expanding its port network, including strategic investments in new SEZs and leveraging national waterways. APSEZ is confident in its growth trajectory and ability to handle increasing cargo volumes.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Total Revenue:** Increased by 19% YoY to ₹5,309 crores.
*   **EBITDA:** Grew by 18% YoY to ₹1,954 crores.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Increased to 64% from 62% in the previous year.
*   **PAT:** Grew by 22% YoY to ₹3,421 crores.
*   **Gross Debt:** ₹17,000 crores.
*   **Net Debt:** ₹7,000 crores (implies cash and liquid investments of ₹10,000 crores).
*   **Operating Income per Tonne:** Increased by 8% YoY.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Diversified Cargo Growth:** Strong performance in containers (14% growth) and crude oil volumes (42% growth) offsetting weakness in coal imports.
*   **Port Network Expansion:** Continued development of existing ports and exploration of new growth areas.
*   **SEZ Development:** Focus on creating ecosystems for industries within SEZs (e.g., Mundra Solar Park, Kattupalli SEZ) to drive integrated growth.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Emphasis on leveraging technology and improving top-line efficiency.
*   **National Waterways and Coastal Shipping:** Exploring opportunities to leverage national waterways and coastal shipping for cargo movement.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Continued focus on the twin-port strategy and strengthening partnership models.
