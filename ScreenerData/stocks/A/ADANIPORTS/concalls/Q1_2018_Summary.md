## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a mixed Q1 FY18, with consolidated operating revenue growing an impressive 50% YoY to INR 2,745 crores and EBITDA increasing by 37% to INR 1,598 crores. Profit Before Tax (PBT) also saw robust growth of 24% to INR 1,092 crores. However, Profit After Tax (PAT) declined to INR 702 crores due to Mundra port entering the full taxation regime.

Operational highlights for APSEZ included handling 44 million tons of cargo (plus 6 MMT from Australia's ABPO business). Cargo volumes were significantly impacted by a scheduled 60-day maintenance shutdown at HMEL, a key customer at Mundra, resulting in a 2 MMT reduction in crude volumes. A 23-day berth shutdown at Dhamra for expansion also affected its growth. Despite these temporary headwinds, container volumes surged by 21% YoY, with APSEZ's container market share increasing from 31% to 33%. Mundra port achieved a milestone by handling over 1 million TEUs for the first time in a quarter. The logistics business also performed strongly, with revenue growth of 28% and rail volumes up 69%.

Management expressed confidence in recovering the temporary shortfalls in the coming quarters, reaffirming their full-year cargo volume guidance. The company continues to focus on cargo diversification, container growth, logistics expansion, and strategic port development projects like CT4 and CT3 extension. Port EBITDA margins improved by 100 basis points to 72%, indicating strong operational efficiency.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Diversification:** Continued focus on diversifying cargo mix to reduce dependence on single commodities, with container and other bulk cargo growing faster than coal.
*   **Container Growth:** Aggressive strategy to increase container market share, driven by new liner additions (IMEX, IMAD, Misawa), commissioning CT4 at Mundra, and strong performance across Mundra, Hazira, and Kattupalli. Trial handling of containers at Dhamra is planned.
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Pursuing an asset-light model for hinterland connectivity, with new ICDs driving rail volume growth. New services and diversion of North India cargo from JNPT to Mundra also boosting logistics.
*   **Long-Term Contracts:** Recently signed agreements with JSW Cement (1.2 MMT clinker at Dhamra), HPCL (increased throughput from 5 MMT to 8 MMT at Mundra), and Tata Steel (steel coil at Dhamra) to propel other bulk cargo volumes. Existing long-term take-or-pay contract with Reliance (2 MMT at Hazira and Dahej) continues.
*   **SEZ Development & Income Recognition:** Recognizing income from port development projects (e.g., CT4 partially in Q1, CT3 extension in Q2) and lease rentals. Plans for land sales to SEZ customers and definitive agreements for LNG terminal at Mundra are in progress.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Continuous focus on technology initiatives and cost efficiencies to drive port EBITDA margin expansion.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Kattupalli operations already taken over, with formal sale expected by September 2017. Evaluating new acquisition opportunities at a sensible valuation.

---

**Audit of Q1 FY18 Performance and Narrative:**

*   **Headwinds (HMEL, Dhamra):** Management's narrative remains consistent. They accurately attributed a **~2 MMT crude volume impact from HMEL's 60-day shutdown** and a **23-day berth shutdown at Dhamra** as reasons for lower Q1 growth. They maintained these were temporary and would be covered in subsequent quarters, aligning with the H1 FY18 outlook for a stronger H2.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management is exceeding previous targets. Port EBITDA margin reached **72% in Q1 FY18**, already higher than the 70% reported for H1 FY18 and the 70% target set for the full FY18. They now expect "at least 100 basis points increase" for the full year, indicating further margin expansion confidence.
*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Management reaffirmed their **guided cargo volume growth of 12% to 14% for FY18**, despite the Q1 headwinds. This shows consistency with the H1 FY18 outlook for strong H2 growth.
*   **SEZ Income Recognition (CT4):** There is a **direct contradiction** in the narrative regarding CT4 income. The H1 FY18 context stated that "CT4 port development income has been fully recognized in H1 FY18." However, in the Q1 FY18 transcript, management explicitly states that **CT4 income (INR 661 crores revenue, INR 270 crores PBT) was "only partially booked in this quarter"** (Q1 FY18) and that a "similar amount of at least revenues and maybe larger amount of profits" are expected in the next quarter or thereafter. This discrepancy in the timing of full recognition needs clarification.
*   **Kattupalli Acquisition Timeline:** The timeline has been **accelerated**. The H1 FY18 context indicated completion by January 2018. In the Q1 FY18 call, management stated, "we should be able to complete that sale by September 2017," implying an earlier closure.
*   **CAPEX Guidance:** Q1 FY18 CAPEX spending is tracking proportionally to the **FY18 guidance of INR 2,500-2,800 crores**, with management suggesting they might save INR 100-200 crores, indicating prudent capital allocation.
*   **Net Debt to EBITDA:** Management maintained that they are "well on course" with the previously given guidance for debt reduction, implying continued improvement from the H1 FY18 ratio of 2.64x.
