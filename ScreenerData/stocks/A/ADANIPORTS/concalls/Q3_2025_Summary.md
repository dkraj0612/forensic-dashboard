## Executive Overview

Adani Ports & Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a strong Q3 FY25, characterized by all-round growth and strategic execution. For the first nine months of FY25, revenue increased by 14%, EBITDA by 19%, and PAT by 32% year-on-year. EBITDA margins improved to 62% from 60% in the prior year. The company maintained excellent financial discipline, with the Net Debt to EBITDA ratio at 2.1x, down from 2.3x in FY24. This momentum is driven by market share gains, growth in logistics verticals, and operational efficiencies. APSEZ has upgraded its FY25 EBITDA guidance to ₹18,800-18,900 crore from the previous ₹17,000-18,000 crore. Key achievements include the closure of significant business initiatives such as Astro Offshore, Gopalpur, and Tanzania acquisitions, the divestment of Ennore, and the smooth commissioning of Vizhinjam Port. The company remains on track to commission its Colombo Port and has launched a new Trucking Management Solution.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility Model:** Continued focus on providing end-to-end logistics solutions, leveraging port network with inland logistics. Emphasis on technological integration and expanding virtual assets alongside physical ones.
*   **Market Share Gains & Volume/Price Mix:** Driving growth through increased market share, particularly in container business (+14.9% YoY), and strategic pricing. Diversified commodity mix helps mitigate risks from trade fluctuations.
*   **Logistics Vertical Traction:** Significant investment and expansion in logistics, including ICDs, warehousing, and a rapidly growing trucking business (92% YoY growth). Launch of Truck Management Solution (TMS) to enhance efficiency and customer offering.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Technology:** Implementation of technology to manage logistics and improve operational efficiency across ports and logistics operations.
*   **Global Expansion & Asset Integration:** Successful integration of new acquisitions (Gopalpur, Tanzania, Haifa) and progress on major projects like Vizhinjam and Colombo Port commissioning. Focus on bringing acquired assets to best-in-class performance.
