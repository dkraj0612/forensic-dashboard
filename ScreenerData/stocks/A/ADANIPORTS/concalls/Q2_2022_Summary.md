## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported strong performance for H1 FY23, demonstrating significant cargo volume growth and strategic acquisitions. The company handled a record 177.5 MMT of cargo, an 11% year-on-year increase, outperforming the overall Indian port growth. Dry cargo and containers led this growth, with non-Mundra ports contributing 54% of the total cargo basket. APSEZ continued its aggressive inorganic growth strategy with the acquisition of Haifa Port (Israel), Ocean Sparkle Ltd (marine services), and further consolidation of Gangavaram Port. The logistics segment also showed robust growth with a 24% rise in rail volume and significant expansion in warehousing capacity and rail network. Management remains focused on its transformation into a transport utility, integrating logistics and port operations to provide end-to-end supply chain solutions and expand its national footprint.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transport Utility Model:** Continued focus on integrating ports and logistics to offer end-to-end supply chain solutions, reaching the customer's gate.
*   **Inorganic Growth & Global Expansion:** Strategic acquisitions of Haifa Port (Israel), Ocean Sparkle Ltd, and further consolidation of Gangavaram Port to strengthen global and domestic presence.
*   **Diversification & Cargo Basket Expansion:** Growing non-coal dry cargo, containers, and expanding into new age cargo like LPG/LNG, while strategically managing cargo mix.
*   **Logistics Network Expansion:** Aggressive build-out of Multimodal Logistics Parks (MMLPs), warehousing capacity, and the largest private rail network in India.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Technology:** Digitization through solutions like RONC (Remote Operating Nerve Center) and investing in electrification of equipment to enhance efficiency and reduce costs.
*   **De-risking Portfolio:** National footprint with 13 ports across the coastline to mitigate concentration risk.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Leveraging partnerships in the container segment (MSC, CMA CGM) and with energy players (IOCL, TotalEnergies) to accelerate market share gains.
