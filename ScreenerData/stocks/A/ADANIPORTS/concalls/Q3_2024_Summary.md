## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a record-breaking Q3 FY2024 and a strong 9-month performance, driven by robust cargo volumes, revenue, and EBITDA growth. Operating revenue surged by 45% YoY to INR 6,920 crores for the quarter, with EBITDA (excluding forex impact) growing by 39% YoY to INR 4,186 crores. Cargo handling volume increased by a significant 44% YoY to approximately 109 million metric tons in Q3 FY2024, with growth across dry bulk, containers, and liquids. The company has revised its FY2024 cargo volume guidance upwards to over 400 million metric tons, exceeding the initial range of 370-390 million metric tons. APSEZ's net debt-to-EBITDA ratio improved to 2.5x as of December 31, 2023, from 2.8x at the beginning of the quarter, reflecting strong cash flow generation. S&P Global Ratings has revised APSEZ's credit outlook to stable.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transport Utility Model:** Continued integration of port and logistics services for end-to-end solutions.
*   **Logistics Network Expansion:** Commissioning of new ICDs (Loni, Valvada), addition of warehousing space, and expansion of the rail fleet.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Focus on improving operating margins, as evidenced by the increase in domestic port EBITDA margins.
*   **Cargo Diversification & Growth:** Strong performance across all major cargo categories, including coal, containers, and liquids. Significant growth in coastal coal movement.
*   **International Operations:** Haifa Port volumes remained steady with marginal increases; no major disruption observed due to Red Sea issues. Partnership with MSC extended for Ennore Container Terminal.
*   **Deleveraging:** Proactive cash flow generation has led to a further reduction in the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance:**
    *   **Cargo Volume Guidance:** Management has proactively revised the FY2024 cargo volume guidance upwards to over 400 million metric tons, demonstrating confidence and strong execution against initial targets. The 9-month performance already positions them to exceed the revised guidance.
    *   **Debt Reduction & Leverage:** The net debt-to-EBITDA ratio has been successfully managed down to 2.5x, meeting and even slightly exceeding the previously stated medium-term target. This reflects consistent execution on financial discipline.
    *   **Logistics Growth:** The logistics segment continues to show robust growth, with margins improving and MMLP/rake additions in line with expansion plans. Management is very bullish on the future growth of this segment.
    *   **Haifa Port Performance:** Management confirmed that Haifa Port has not seen significant negative impact from Red Sea disruptions, with volumes remaining steady and marginally increased.

*   **Consistency of Narrative:** The management maintained a consistent narrative emphasizing strong economic demand, capex cycle, manufacturing growth, and the integrated transport utility model as key drivers for cargo volumes. The bullish outlook on the logistics business and commitment to deleveraging remain core themes.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Logistics ROIC:** Expectation for logistics ROIC to move closer to the company-level ROIC within 3 years as assets ramp up.
    *   **Logistics Rake Count:** Target to increase rake count from 115 to 300 by FY2028.
    *   **New MMLPs:** Plans to add two more MMLPs in North India and the Mumbai region in the next 3-4 years.
    *   **Logistics EBITDA Margin:** Expectation for overall logistics segment EBITDA margins to improve towards 50% once stabilized, driven by agri silos and bulk trains.
    *   **Coastal Coal Volumes:**
        *   **Next Year (FY2025):** Coastal exports expected at 5-6 million tons.
        *   **Coastal Imports (Krishnapatnam & Karaikal):** Expected at 8-10 million tons.
        *   **Goa Terminal:** Expected around 1 million tons.
    *   **Tajpur Port (West Bengal):** Management reiterates waiting for the Letter of Award (LOA) from the West Bengal government.
    *   **Capex Guidance (FY2024):** Approximately INR 5,500 crores incurred in 9 months, full year on a pro-rata basis. FY2025 guidance to be provided at full-year results.
    *   **Red Sea Impact:** Approximately 10% of total container volume travels through the Red Sea. No major drop observed in January, but foresee potential container shortages and supply chain disruptions if the issue persists. Marginal impact on overall volumes, especially for India, is anticipated if prolonged.
    *   **JNPT vs. Mundra:** Management does not see a risk to Mundra volumes from JNPT's capacity addition post-DFC commissioning, as JNPT is expected to operate at sustainable utilization levels to meet demand.
    *   **Logistics Growth Pace:** Minimum next 5 years expected to continue growing at current or higher pace.
    *   **Integrated Services:** GPWIS business is fully integrated. Approximately 20% of container volume is fully integrated; the rest is port-dependent or uses other intermediaries.
    *   **Capital Return Policy:** Commitment to dividend policy of 22-25% of PAT. Will evaluate tax efficiency of buybacks vs. dividends.
