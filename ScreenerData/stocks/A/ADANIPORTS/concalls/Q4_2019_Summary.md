## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) demonstrated a landmark year in FY2019, surpassing 200 million metric tonnes (MMT) of cargo handled for the first time and achieving a Profit After Tax (PAT) of over Rs. 4,000 Crores. Despite global trade war fears and rupee depreciation, the company achieved 15% cargo volume growth, exceeding its guidance. APSEZ also announced significant corporate governance improvements, including enhanced board diversity and a new policy on related party transactions, alongside a new dividend and share buyback policy expected to be announced by June 4, 2019. The company's strategy of a multi-port network and diversified cargo mix continues to yield results, with increased market share in both overall cargo (21%) and container volumes (34%). APSEZ is poised for continued growth, with ambitious targets and strategic expansions in logistics and international markets.

*   **FY2019 Consolidated Operating Revenue:** Rs. 10,925 Crores (vs. Rs. 11,323 Crores in FY2018)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Exceeded guidance with 15% YoY growth in FY2019, driven by coal (17%), containers (13%), crude (31%), and other bulk cargo (9%).
*   **Market Share Expansion:** Increased market share in all India cargo volume to 21% (+200 bps) and container volume to 34% (+100 bps).
*   **Port Performance:** All operating ports except Dhamra registered double-digit growth. Mundra grew 13%, Dahej 30%, Kattupalli 18%, and Hazira 16%. Major ports (Tuna, Goa, Vizag, Ennore) saw over 127% growth.
*   **Dhamra Port Recovery:** Significant improvement in H2 FY2019 with 12 MMT cargo handled. Evacuation issues easing with increased rake availability (16.4 rakes/day in Q4 FY2019).
*   **Logistics Business Growth:** Adani Logistics Limited (ALL) is expanding its fleet (60+ rakes by end FY2020) and logistics parks. Acquisition of Adani Agri-Logistics Limited (AALL) and definitive agreement to acquire Innovative B2B Logistics Solution Private Limited aim to make ALL the largest private rail and logistics park operator.
*   **SEZ & Port Development Income:** Generated Rs. 769 Crores in FY2019, including Rs. 533 Crores from the sale of the Mundra LPG terminal.
*   **International Expansion:** Signed a 50-year BOT agreement for a Greenfield terminal at Yangon, Myanmar. Exploring opportunities in Indonesia and Bangladesh.
*   **Corporate Governance & Shareholder Returns:** Enhanced board diversity (20%), introduced a related party transaction policy, and announced a combined dividend payout and share buyback policy for FY2019.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of FY19 Performance and Narrative vs. Previous Guidance:**

*   **Cargo Volume:** Management's narrative of strong growth was validated. FY2019 cargo volume reached 208 MMT, exceeding the guidance of 200 MMT and demonstrating 15% YoY growth, significantly outperforming industry trends and reinforcing their market share gain strategy.
*   **Financial Performance:** Consolidated revenue and EBITDA were largely flat year-on-year, which contrasts with previous guidance that projected CAGRs. However, the EBITDA margin expanded by 200 bps to 65%, and PAT grew by 9% to Rs. 4,006 Crores, indicating operational efficiencies and improved profitability despite revenue nuances. The management's acknowledgment of global challenges like trade wars and rupee depreciation explains some of the revenue flatness.
*   **Dhamra Port:** The narrative shifted from previous concerns about evacuation issues to a positive outlook, with the port showing significant recovery in H2 FY2019. Improved rake availability and the introduction of higher value cargo contributed to this turnaround.
*   **Logistics Business:** Management proactively addressed concerns regarding rake availability impacting Adani Logistics Limited (ALL). The acquisition of AALL and the agreement for B2B Logistics Solution are key strategic moves to bolster the logistics segment, aligning with previous discussions on expanding service offerings and scale.
*   **SEZ Income:** The reported SEZ income of Rs. 769 Crores was lower than the projected Rs. 800-1000 Crores, primarily due to the complete sale of the LPG terminal at Mundra (Rs. 533 Crores). While the sale contributed significantly to cash flow, it impacted the recurring SEZ income.
*   **Debt Management:** The Net Debt to EBITDA remained within the targeted 3x, demonstrating consistent financial prudence.
*   **Forex Impact:** The company reported a forex mark-to-market loss of Rs. 476 Crores, consistent with management's practice of disclosing and managing these impacts.
