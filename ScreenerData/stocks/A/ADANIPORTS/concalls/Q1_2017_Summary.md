## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a strong Q1 FY17, demonstrating continued growth driven by strategic diversification of cargo mix, increased container volumes, and enhanced operational efficiencies. Total operating income saw a 7% year-on-year increase to ₹1,827 crores, with Profit After Tax (PAT) surging by 31% to ₹836 crores. The company maintained industry-leading EBITDA margins at 64% consolidated and 71% for ports. Cargo handled increased by 7% year-on-year to 42.33 million metric tons, with container volumes showing a remarkable 27% year-on-year growth to 1 million TEUs, now representing 35% of total cargo. Management reiterated confidence in achieving their full-year cargo volume guidance and highlighted significant progress in debt reduction.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Total Operating Income:** Increased by 7% YoY to ₹1,827 crores.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Grew by 5% YoY to ₹1,170 crores.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** Maintained at 64%.
*   **Ports EBITDA Margin:** Maintained at 71%.
*   **Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT):** Increased by 31% YoY to ₹836 crores.
*   **Port Revenue:** Grew by 10% YoY to ₹1,633 crores.
*   **Port Cargo Volumes:** Increased by 7% YoY to 42.33 million metric tons.
*   **Container Volumes:** Increased by 27% YoY to 1 million TEUs.
*   **Portions of Cargo:** Containers now account for 35% of total cargo (up from 29% in Q1 FY16).
*   **CAPEX Guidance (Revised):** Planned CAPEX for the fiscal year is expected to be reduced by another ₹200-300 crores from the initial ₹3,000 crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Achieved 7% YoY growth in cargo volumes, with a significant 13% quarter-on-quarter increase. Strong performance across major ports like Dhamra, Hazira, Kattupalli, and Goa.
*   **Container Cargo Focus:** Continued emphasis on container cargo, with new services at Kattupalli (34,000 TEUs in June 2016) and Hazira, and existing customer additions at Mundra.
*   **Coastal Shipping:** Demonstrated success with 1 million tons of coastal cargo handled, primarily from Dhamra to Tamil Nadu for the Electricity Board, on track to meet the annual target of 4 million tons.
*   **Diversification of Cargo:** Increased handling of other cargo, including iron ore (newly handled at Dhamra), fertilizer, and steel, contributing to a more balanced cargo profile.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Continued focus on improving profitability through operational excellence and cost management.
*   **SEZ Development:** Ongoing development with fertilizer handling facilities at Dahej and Dhamra planned for Q4. Limited SEZ revenue reported at ₹18 crores (continuing lease rentals).
*   **Logistics & Infrastructure:** Progress on CT4 terminal commissioning, expected by end of Q4 FY17 or early Q1 FY18.
