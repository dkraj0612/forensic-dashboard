## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a robust Q2 FY25, demonstrating continued growth and strategic execution. The company achieved a cargo volume of 220 million metric tons (MMT) in H1 FY25, a 9% year-on-year increase, despite disruptions from inclement weather at Gangavaram, Mundra, and Tuna, and cyclone Dana at Dhamra. Financially, APSEZ saw significant growth in Q2 FY25 with revenue up 6%, EBITDA up 13%, and PAT up 37%. For H1 FY25, revenue, EBITDA, and PAT increased by 13%, 21%, and 42% respectively, with a Net Debt/EBITDA ratio of 2.0x. The company continues to strengthen its integrated transport utility model, with key developments including the acquisition of Gopalpur Port, progress on Vizhinjam Port, and the acquisition of Astro Offshore to bolster its marine services. APSEZ is confident in achieving its full-year cargo guidance of 460-480 MMT and expects to hit the upper end of its FY25 EBITDA guidance. Credit ratings were further enhanced with CRISIL assigning AAA to its bank facilities and NCDs, making APSEZ the first private infrastructure operator in India to achieve AAA ratings from four domestic agencies.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility:** Continued focus on providing end-to-end logistics solutions, integrating port operations with extensive logistics networks. Emphasis on conversion from road to rail for domestic cargo.
*   **Global Expansion & Acquisitions:** Strategic acquisitions and development of international assets, including Gopalpur Port (acquired), Vizhinjam Port (progressing), Sri Lanka (on track for next year), Astro Offshore (acquired for marine business), and Tanzania (ramping up).
*   **Domestic Growth & Capacity Expansion:** Investments across the port portfolio to support cargo growth. Focus on enhancing efficiency and capacity at key ports like Mundra, Hazira, Kattupalli, Dhamra, Krishnapatnam, and Gangavaram.
*   **Logistics Business Growth:** Aggressive shift from road to rail, evidenced by significant growth in Adani Logistics' domestic volumes (+47% YoY) and an increase in rail coefficient at Mundra Port. Focus on last-mile connectivity through MMLPs, warehousing, and ICDs.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Diversification:** Balanced commodity mix and asset mix across the coastline. Growth in container handling is a key driver for improved EBITDA margins.
*   **Infrastructure Focus:** Leveraging India's growth story, particularly in infrastructure (cement, steel), industrialization (containers), energy, and food/fertilizers.

*   **Performance Against Guidance (Q2 FY25 Presentation Context):**
    *   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Management maintained confidence in hitting the full-year guidance of 460-480 MMT, citing strong performance in containers, expected recovery in agro and fertilizers, and contributions from new ports. While H1 saw disruptions, the narrative shifted to the resilience and strategic positioning for H2. The H1 volume growth of 9% is double the national trade growth, aligning with past strategies.
    *   **Financial Performance:** Q2 and H1 FY25 financial results showed strong year-on-year growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, indicating achievement of financial targets. Management is confident in hitting the upper end of the FY25 EBITDA guidance.
    *   **Leverage Management:** The Net Debt/EBITDA ratio of 2.0x in H1 FY25 is within manageable limits. Management acknowledged a projected increase to 2.2x-2.5x by year-end due to recent acquisitions (Gopalpur, Astro) and planned H2 capex, which is consistent with their stated long-term target of 2.5x.
    *   **Credit Ratings:** Further validation of financial strength with AAA ratings from CRISIL and India Ratings, alongside existing AAA ratings, demonstrating robust creditworthiness.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Gopalpur Port:** Expected to contribute 800,000 to 1 million tons per month from October onwards.
    *   **Vizhinjam Port:** Run rate expected around 50,000 to 60,000 TEUs per month (approximately 750,000 tons per month), with full operations of the full-length berth on track. Phase 2 investment of INR 20,000 crores is underway.

    *   **FY25 Capex:** Approximately 6,500 crores expected in the second half, contributing to the full-year guidance of INR 10,000 - 11,500 crores.
    *   **Container Handling Growth:** Confident in continued strong growth, especially from Mundra and Dhamra, with Gopalpur starting to contribute.
    *   **Port EBITDA Margin:** Medium-term potential for margins to reach 75-76% due to operational efficiency and increasing container mix.
    *   **International Operations:** Tanzania is above the ROCE threshold of 15% in rupee terms. Sri Lanka is expected to show returns within the next couple of years.
    *   **SEZ Income:** Annuity income from lease rentals is expected to remain consistent for future periods, with one-off income potentially occurring upon new port development transactions.
    *   **Net Debt Target:** Long-term target for debt leverage is 2.5x, with appropriate actions to be taken based on sustained performance over multiple quarters.
