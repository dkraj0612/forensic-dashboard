## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) demonstrated resilience and growth in Q1 FY19, with core operations showing strong performance despite a year-on-year revenue dip caused by the absence of SEZ port-led development income recognized in the prior year. Cargo volumes reached a record high for the quarter, outpacing all-India growth significantly. Container and coal volumes showed robust increases, while crude volumes recovered from a low base. The company is strategically diversifying its cargo mix and expanding its logistics network. Management reiterated confidence in achieving FY19 cargo volume guidance and improving EBITDA margins.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenue (Q1 FY19):** INR 2,411 crores (down 12% YoY, primarily due to no SEZ Port-led development income vs. INR 661 crores in Q1 FY18).
*   **Core Operations Revenue (Excluding SEZ income):** Grew 17% YoY to INR 2,411 crores.
*   **Port Revenue (Q1 FY19):** INR 1,966 crores (up 16% YoY).
*   **Port EBITDA (Q1 FY19):** INR 1,380 crores (up 17% YoY).
*   **Port EBITDA Margin (Q1 FY19):** 70% (up 100 bps YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (Q1 FY19, excluding MTM FOREX loss):** INR 1,588 crores.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q1 FY19, excluding MTM FOREX loss):** 66% (vs. 57% in Q1 FY18).
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT) (Q1 FY19):** INR 922 crores (lower YoY due to INR 383 crores MTM FOREX loss).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q1 FY19):** INR 691 crores (lower YoY due to MTM FOREX loss).
*   **APSEZ Cargo Volume (Q1 FY19):** 48.07 million metric tons (up 9% YoY, vs. 3% for All India Ports).
*   **Container Volume (Q1 FY19):** 1.42 million TEUs (up 16% YoY).
*   **Logistics Revenue (Q1 FY19):** Decline YoY due to rake availability issues.
*   **SEZ Lease Revenue (Q1 FY19):** INR 115 crores.
*   **CAPEX (FY19 Guidance):** INR 2,500 crores.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FY19 Guidance):** INR 1,750 - 2,000 crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Targeting 200 million metric tons for FY19, aiming for 1.5x All India cargo growth and 2x All India container growth.
*   **Port Development & Diversification:** Continued focus on handling diverse cargo types (e.g., salt, slags, specialty chemicals, steel coils, clinker, POS) to enhance revenue and EBITDA.
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Acquiring new rakes (10 on order, 10 more planned) and developing new Private Freight Terminals (PFTs) at Baroda, Bengaluru, Panipat, and Nagpur to counter rake shortages and expand network. JV with NYK Auto Logistics for automobile transport.
*   **SEZ Development:** Continued leasing of SEZ land (47 acres leased for INR 115 crores) and targeting INR 800-1000 crores in port-led development revenue for FY19.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Margin Expansion:** Aiming for 100 bps increase in port EBITDA margins annually, targeting 73-74% in the long term.
*   **Dhamra Port Strategy:** Addressing evacuation issues by initiating the "Own Your Wagon" scheme and handling high-value cargo via container trains and STS operations. Expects 20-25% growth from FY20 onwards.
*   **Balance Sheet Strengthening:** Generating free cash flow for debt reduction and strengthening the balance sheet.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Q1 FY19 Performance and Narrative vs. Previous Guidance:**

*   **SEZ Port-Led Development Income:** Management accurately reported the absence of SEZ port-led development income in Q1 FY19, a significant contributor to Q1 FY18 revenue, aligning with the shift in recognition timing. The prior year's Q3 FY18 summary noted CT4 income was "fully recognized," while the Q1 FY19 transcript clarifies that the CT4 income was "partially booked in this quarter" (referring to Q1 FY18) and further income was expected in subsequent quarters. This transcript confirms the prior year's Q1 had a significant SEZ income component (INR 661 crores).
*   **Cargo Volumes:** The company exceeded its growth targets, with 9% cargo volume growth against an All India average of 3%. This aligns with and strengthens the previous guidance of 1.5x All India growth. The 200 MMT FY19 guidance is reiterated and management expresses confidence in achieving it.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Port EBITDA margins at 70% met the previously stated target for Q1 FY19. The overall strategy to increase port EBITDA margins by 100 bps annually remains.
*   **Logistics Business Performance:** The decline in logistics revenue was explicitly attributed to a lack of leased rakes being called back by the lessor, which was a clear operational headwind. Management has proactively ordered new rakes to address this, projecting a recovery and 40-45% YoY revenue growth in FY20.
*   **Dhamra Port Evacuation Issues:** The narrative remains consistent regarding evacuation issues at Dhamra, with a reduction in available rakes from railways. The company's proactive steps via the "Own Your Wagon" scheme and handling of high-value cargo are consistent with stated strategies to mitigate this.
*   **FOREX Impact:** Management consistently explained the Mark-to-Market (MTM) loss on foreign currency loans, emphasizing the natural hedge from dollar-denominated revenues and the long-term perspective of their FX policy, which aims for P&L neutrality over a 5-year period. They clearly distinguished between the notional MTM loss and the realized impact.
*   **Kattupalli Acquisition:** The acquisition was completed, and the transcript notes the reduction in operating expenses due to IndAS adjustments, no longer charging interest as it's wholly owned.
*   **CAPEX Guidance:** The Q1 FY19 CAPEX spend is tracking in line with the FY19 guidance of INR 2,500 crores.
*   **Net Debt to EBITDA:** While not explicitly stated for Q1 FY19, the generation of free cash flow and focus on balance sheet strengthening indicates continued progress on deleveraging.
