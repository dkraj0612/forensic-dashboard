## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a strong Q1 FY25, exceeding previous performance benchmarks. The company achieved a cargo volume of 109 million metric tons (MMT), a notable 8% year-on-year increase, excluding disruptions at Gangavaram. This growth was underpinned by new concession agreements in Tanzania and Deendayal Port, an O&M contract at Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, and the deployment of marine services assets. The Logistics business also demonstrated strength with increased rail cargo and expanded warehousing. Financially, APSEZ recorded its highest-ever revenue (INR 7,560 crores, +21% YoY), EBITDA (INR 4,848 crores, +29% YoY), and PAT (INR 3,107 crores, +47% YoY). Credit ratings were further bolstered with CARE and ICRA upgrading APSEZ to AAA, and S&P revising its outlook to positive, acknowledging the company's expanding scale and diversification. A significant development was the resolution of auditor qualifications, now classified as an Emphasis of Matter (EOM).

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q1 FY25 Performance:**
    *   **Cargo Volume:** 109 MMT (+8% YoY, excluding Gangavaram non-operational days).
    *   **Operating Revenue:** INR 7,560 crores (+21% YoY).
    *   **EBITDA:** INR 4,848 crores (+29% YoY, excluding forex impact).
    *   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 3,107 crores (+47% YoY).
    *   **Domestic Ports EBITDA Margin:** 72% (+32 bps YoY).
    *   **Net Debt/EBITDA Ratio:** 2.1x (stable).
    *   **Cash Balance:** INR 9,000+ crores post-bond repayment.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility:** Continued focus on an end-to-end logistics solution, integrating port operations with an extensive logistics network.
*   **Global Expansion:** Active exploration of international opportunities, including new concession agreements and strategic reviews in Southeast Asia.
*   **Domestic Growth & Capacity Expansion:** Ongoing investments across the port portfolio (West and East coasts) to achieve the 1 billion metric ton cargo target by 2030. New projects like Vizhinjam (Phase 2) and capacity enhancements at existing ports are prioritized.
*   **Logistics Business Growth:** Significant capital expenditure allocated to land acquisition, warehousing, and rail infrastructure to enhance last-mile delivery capabilities.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Digitalization:** Investment in advanced technology platforms, including Navis software and in-house developed systems, to boost operational efficiency.
*   **Diversification:** Growth across key commodities (containers, liquid, dry cargo) is a strategic focus, with containers showing significant market share gains.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance (Q1 FY25 Presentation Context):**
    *   **Cargo Volume Growth:** The reported 8% YoY cargo growth significantly outpaced Indian trade growth (4.3%), aligning with the strategy to grow at least 2x the national rate. The upcoming contributions from Gangavaram and Gopalpur are expected to further enhance this.
    *   **Financial Performance:** Record revenue, EBITDA, and PAT demonstrate strong operational execution and achievement of financial targets.
    *   **Leverage Management:** The Net Debt/EBITDA ratio of 2.1x remains stable and within management's target range, showcasing financial discipline amidst investments.
    *   **Credit Ratings:** Upgrades to AAA by CARE and ICRA, and a positive outlook from S&P, validate the company's improving scale and diversification.
    *   **Auditor Qualification:** The successful resolution of auditor qualifications to an Emphasis of Matter (EOM) following an independent review addresses previous concerns.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Vizhinjam Port:** Phase 1 (1 MMT nameplate capacity) expected to be fully operational starting October 2024, with full capacity utilization anticipated in FY26. The Phase 2 investment is INR 20,000 crores.
    *   **Gopalpur Port:** Expected to contribute revenue from this quarter, with full impact from the next quarter onwards. The asset configuration and efficiency improvements are a focus, targeting an EBITDA margin increase from 38-42% to 54% in the short term, and eventually 65-70%.
    *   **Logistics Business Margin:** Expected to improve in the next quarter due to the resolution of busy season surcharges and the factoring in of step-down pricing on certain FCI contracts. Full-year margins are projected to be higher than current levels.
    *   **Capex Guidance (FY25):** INR 10,000 - 11,500 crores, broken down as: INR 7,300 cr for Ports, INR 400 cr for Marine, INR 2,300 cr for Logistics, and INR 1,500 cr for Renewables/Decarbonization. Q1 capex was INR 2,000+ crores.
    *   **Deendayal Port (LOI):** Anticipated investment of close to INR 600 crores, targeting a capacity of 1-1.2 MMT on a long-term lease.
    *   **International Operations:** Targeting 15% of the 1BMT cargo goal by FY30. Current EBITDA margins are around 10-11%, with expectations for improvement. The ROCE target of 16% in rupee terms is maintained.
    *   **Vietnam Project:** Currently in the study stage, exploring opportunities in Southeast Asia.
    *   **Haifa Port Operations:** Despite geopolitical sanctions impacting cargo to Israel (dry bulk -42%, containers -22%), car imports have increased significantly (+42%), demonstrating a shift in business mix. Operations continue 24/7.
    *   **Mundra Container Volumes:** Management confident in hitting the FY25 guidance of 460-480 MMT, with Mundra container growth being a key driver. Acknowledged potential for 9 MMT TEUs this year.
    *   **FY30 Capacity Target:** Capacity is projected to be around 1,200-1,400 MMT by FY30, including international operations, expansions, efficiency improvements, and new projects.
    *   **Port EBITDA Margin:** Medium-term potential for margins to reach 75-76% due to large land bank and digitalization focus.
    *   **Logistics Capex:** Significant investments in land parcels are mid- to long-term strategic plays for integrated logistics solutions.
    *   **Deendayal Port (LOI) Commercial Terms:** Long-term lease with an expected investment of close to INR 600 crores for 1-1.2 MMT capacity.
    *   **Vadhavan Port:** Welcomed as a complementary growth opportunity for India's increasing trade needs. Open to potential collaboration.
    *   **International Operations Margin Improvement:** Expectation of current 10-11% EBITDA margin to improve, with a ROCE target of 16% in rupee terms. Future tonnage contribution from international operations is projected at 15-20% by FY30.
