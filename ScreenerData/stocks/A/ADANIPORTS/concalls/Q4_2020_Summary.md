## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) has announced significant strategic moves, including the acquisition of a 31.5% stake in Gangavaram Port for Rs. 1954 Crores and the proposed acquisition of Sarguja Rail Corridor Private Limited (SRCPL) through a merger. These transactions are aimed at enhancing APSEZ's market share, expanding its logistics network, and creating synergistic value. The Gangavaram Port acquisition, at an implied multiple of 8.9x FY20 EBITDA, is expected to strengthen APSEZ's presence on the East Coast and potentially diversify its cargo mix. The SRCPL acquisition integrates a 70 km private rail corridor into APSEZ's growing logistics platform, reinforcing its end-to-end solution strategy. While the Gangavaram acquisition is currently as a minority stake, APSEZ is in discussions with the promoters for potential future acquisition. The company continues to emphasize its robust RPT policy for related party transactions.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Gangavaram Port Acquisition (Warburg Pincus Stake):**
    *   Consideration: Rs. 1954 Crores (for 31.5% stake)
    *   Implied Valuation: 8.9x FY20 EBITDA, 12x FY20 P/E
    *   Gangavaram Port FY20: Cargo volumes 34.5 MMT, Revenue Rs. 1082 Crores, EBITDA Rs. 634 Crores, EBITDA Margin 59%, PAT Rs. 516 Crores. Debt-free with Rs. 500+ Crores cash balance.

*   **Sarguja Rail Corridor Private Limited (SRCPL) Acquisition (Related Party Transaction):**
    *   Transaction Type: Merger
    *   Consideration: Issuance of 7.06 Crores APSEZ shares to promoters (based on VWAP of Rs. 675.18)
    *   Valuation (DCF by BDO Valuers & Deutsche Bank): Enterprise Value approx. Rs. 6000 Crores.
    *   SRCPL FY21 Expected: Cargo volumes 15 MMT, Revenue Rs. 456 Crores, EBITDA Rs. 395 Crores, EBITDA Margin 87%, Profit Rs. 121 Crores.
    *   SRCPL FY22 Expected (based on current contract): EBITDA Rs. 520 Crores.
    *   SRCPL Funding: Rs. 660 Crores debt + Rs. 278 Crores shareholder loans from sponsors, totaling ~Rs. 950 Crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Strategic Acquisitions for Market Share and East Coast Dominance:** The acquisition of Gangavaram Port is a key step to achieve East Coast and West Coast parity, increase hinterland reach, and expand market share to 30%.
*   **Integrated Logistics Solutions:** The SRCPL acquisition consolidates rail assets under a new entity, Adani Track Management Services Private Limited (ATMTS), aiming to enhance multimodal logistics capabilities and participate in future PPP track development.
*   **Cargo Diversification:** Gangavaram Port, currently focused on bulk and break bulk, presents opportunities for developing container and liquid businesses.
*   **Synergistic Value Creation:** Leveraging adjacencies and synergies between existing and acquired assets to improve operational efficiency and customer service.
*   **Focus on Annuity Income Streams:** The rail business, exemplified by SRCPL, is positioned to generate annuity income and participate in significant growth opportunities.
*   **Robust RPT Policy:** Continued adherence to a strong Related Party Transaction policy, with independent director committees and fairness opinions sought for related party deals.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Gangavaram Port Acquisition Rationale:** Management clarified that Warburg Pincus's exit was due to fund lifecycle. APSEZ's acquisition is seen as a strategic fit, opening new hinterlands and complementing existing assets like Krishnapatnam. While currently a minority stake, management expressed willingness to remain minority if majority acquisition doesn't materialize, citing the port's debt-free status, dividend payout potential, and underutilization.
*   **Sarguja Rail Corridor Acquisition Process:** Management detailed the rigorous RPT process, involving independent directors, external valuers (BDO, Deutsche Bank), and fairness opinion providers (JM Financial, JP Morgan), and EY for compliance. The use of APSEZ shares as consideration was highlighted, aligning with regulatory requirements.
*   **SRCPL Customer Concentration and Receivables:** Management confirmed that SRCPL's main customer is a state government entity but assured a consistent 30-day collection cycle for receivables and no unusual buildup.
*   **SRCPL Revenue and Capacity Realization:** Management stated that revenue is market-driven, with a 50% revenue share with RRVUNL on third-party volumes above 20 million tonnes. They project reaching 29 MMT by FY2024 and 40 MMT by FY2028, with existing rail capacity supporting up to 40 MMT.
*   **Gangavaram Port Profitability Improvement:** Management acknowledged that significant profit improvement for Gangavaram, similar to Krishnapatnam, would be possible upon gaining operational control, which is contingent on acquiring the majority stake from the promoters. Currently, with a minority stake, operational control remains with the promoters.
*   **Adani Track Management Services (ATMTS) Role:** Management clarified that ATMTS is distinct from Adani Logistics and is not a precursor for bidding on Container Corp. Its primary focus is to bid for track businesses and strategic connectivities to enhance port and logistics offerings.
*   **Funding for Rail Business:** ATMTS is expected to spend Rs. 200-300 Crores annually for growth in the rail sector, focusing on port connectivity, logistics connectivity, and freight traffic.
