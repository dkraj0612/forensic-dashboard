## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) delivered its strongest ever quarterly operating performance in Q1 FY2024, achieving record cargo volumes, revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, despite a six-day operational impact from Cyclone Biparjoy on its Gujarat ports. The company reported significant year-on-year growth across key financial and operational metrics, underscoring continued strong execution and market share gains. Acquisitions like Haifa and Karaikal are demonstrating strong ramp-up, and strategic investments in logistics infrastructure, including new trains and MMLPs, are supporting business growth. Management reaffirmed its FY2024 guidance and expressed confidence in meeting targets, while also highlighting ongoing efforts in deleveraging and capacity expansion.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q1 FY2024 Operational & Financial Highlights:**
    *   **Operating Revenue:** INR 6,248 Crores (+24% YoY)
    *   **EBITDA (incl. forex):** INR 3,765 Crores (+80% YoY)
    *   **EBITDA (excl. forex):** INR 3,754 Crores (+14% YoY, like-to-like)
    *   **Ports EBITDA Margin:** 72% (+150 bps YoY)
    *   **Logistics EBITDA Margin:** 28% (+150 bps YoY)
    *   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 2,119 Crores (+80% YoY)
    *   **Cargo Throughput:** 101.4 million metric tonnes (+12% YoY)
    *   **Indian Cargo Volumes:** +8% YoY (3x country's growth rate)
    *   **APSEZ Market Share (India):** Increased to 26% (+200 bps)
    *   **Mundra Container Volume:** 1.72 million TEUs (+12% vs. closest competitor)
    *   **Logistics Rail Volumes:** 131,420 TUs (+18% YoY)
    *   **Logistics Bulk Cargo:** 4.35 million metric tonnes (+40% YoY)
    *   **Total Trains:** 95 (43 container, 42 bulk)
    *   **Gross Debt:** INR 48,800 Crores
    *   **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** INR 9,800 Crores
    *   **Net Debt:** ~INR 39,000 Crores

*   **FY2024 Guidance Reaffirmed:**
    *   **Cargo Volume:** 370 to 390 million metric tons
    *   **Capex:** INR 4,000 to INR 4,500 Crores

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Transport Utility Model:** Continued integration of port and logistics services, offering end-to-end solutions.
*   **Acquisitions & Organic Growth:** Successful ramp-up of recently acquired assets (Haifa, Karaikal).
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Addition of new container and bulk trains, expansion of MMLPs, and growing ICD network.
*   **Capacity Augmentation:** Commissioning of new container berths (Mundra T3) and progress on Vizhinjam and Colombo ports.
*   **Cargo Diversification:** Focus on multi-commodity handling to de-risk from any single cargo segment slowdown.
*   **Deleveraging:** Commitment to maintaining debt levels and strategic debt repayments.
*   **Market Share Gains:** Consistently outperforming overall cargo growth and gaining share in key segments like logistics rail.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance:**
    *   **Q1 FY2024 Performance:** APSEZ not only met but exceeded expectations with its strongest ever quarterly performance, demonstrating robust operational execution. Cargo throughput of 101.4 million metric tonnes puts the company on track to meet or exceed the FY2024 guidance of 370-390 million metric tons. Revenue and EBITDA growth were strong.
    *   **Cyclone Biparjoy Impact:** Management quantified the impact of the cyclone as a loss of 2 million tonnes of handling volume. While this volume is expected to be partially compensated in subsequent quarters, the company did not revise its annual guidance, indicating confidence in its ability to absorb such short-term disruptions.
    *   **Logistics Growth:** The continued strong performance and margin improvement in the logistics business (28% EBITDA margin) align with previous reporting and peer comparisons.
    *   **Debt & Cash:** The reported gross debt of INR 48,800 Crores and cash of INR 9,800 Crores provide a clear picture of the company's financial position. The management is actively managing its debt profile.

*   **Consistency of Narrative:** Management maintained a consistent narrative emphasizing the strength of its integrated transport utility model, the benefits of diversification across commodities and geographies, and the strategic importance of logistics expansion. The focus on operational efficiency and market share gains remains a core theme.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Warehouse Expansion:** Aiming to add roughly 10 million square feet of warehousing space annually, with 5 million square feet currently under construction. Capex estimated at INR 1800-2000 per square foot.
    *   **Logistics Rail Volumes:** Expecting continued strong growth of 20-25% for rail volumes over the next two to three years, with new ICDs in Nagpur and Ahmedabad contributing to market share gains.
    *   **Haifa Port:** Expectation to clock 12 to 14 million tonnes of cargo by the end of FY2024. Management is in negotiations with unions to reduce manpower costs, aiming for margin improvement post settlement expected by December. Exact future margins are contingent on these negotiations.
    *   **Vizhinjam Port:** First vessel expected in October 2023, Phase I commissioning by March 2024, full commissioning by May 2024.
    *   **Colombo Port:** Phase I commissioning expected by December 2024.
    *   **Tajpur Port:** LOI signed; concession agreement, environmental clearance, and construction realistically 24 months away.
    *   **Logistics Trains:** Orders placed for 13 bulk and 24 container trains, to arrive during FY24. Total train count to increase to 93.
    *   **MMLPs:** Targeting commissioning of three more in FY24, taking the total to 9.
    *   **Agri-Silo Capacity:** On track to surpass the guided ambition of 2.5 million metric tons by FY26.
    *   **SEZ Income:** Guidance for FY24 revenue includes INR 500 Crores from SEZ.
    *   **Share Pledges:** Promoter share pledge at 4-5% is secondary collateral for operating assets with no outstanding loans.
    *   **Mundra Container Handling Capacity:** Expanding Terminal 2 to 1.2 million TEUs.
    *   **Liquidity:** INR 9,800 Crores cash on balance sheet as of March 31, 2023. No fresh fundraising anticipated in FY24.
    *   **Renewable Energy Share (SEZ):** 250 megawatts of renewable energy expected to be commissioned by April 2024, reaching approximately 90% of the renewable share target.
    *   **Dighi Port:** Working on railway line connectivity; land acquisition underway. Rejuvenating jetties and backup area, with four-lane road connectivity to the port completed.
    *   **Ocean Sparkle Acquisition:** The significant increase in tug and harbor business equipment is primarily due to the Ocean Sparkle acquisition.
    *   **Secured Debt:** Bonds are unsecured; secured debt comprises domestic bonds (debentures) of approximately INR 6,000 Crores.
    *   **Overseas Port Share:** Envisaged to be not more than 10-15% of the total port operator share by 2030.
    *   **CONCOR Privatization:** Management will continue to evaluate CONCOR if privatization occurs, and would "find a way to do it" if attractive, while adhering to overall leverage guidance.
    *   **Tanzania:** Currently operating and maintaining assets, with no further updates at this time.
    *   **Gujarat Port Concession Policy:** Engaged with the Gujarat government; awaiting policy formulation for private ports.
    *   **Price Hikes:** Price hikes have been taken, with full effect expected from July onwards, averaging around 2.5% of per tonne revenue.
