## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) demonstrated robust performance in Q2 FY17 and H1 FY17, exceeding previous growth trends. The company reported a 17% YoY increase in cargo volumes for Q2 FY17 and a 12% increase for H1 FY17, outperforming India's overall port growth. Revenue for Q2 FY17 grew by 21% YoY, and for H1 FY17 by 14% YoY. Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a significant surge of 46% YoY in H1 FY17. APSEZ continued its commitment to debt reduction, decreasing related party loans by ₹1,035 crores in H1 FY17. Strategic focus remains on container cargo growth, diversification, and SEZ development, with several new services added and infrastructure upgrades progressing.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Continued outperformance against India's port growth, driven by specialized cargo and strong performance at Kattupalli, Hazira, and Dhamra.
*   **Container Focus:** Significant 28% YoY growth in container volumes for H1 FY17, aiming to be India's highest container volume handler by FY17. New services added at Mundra and Hazira; Maersk Line shifted service to Kattupalli. Ennore port operations targeted for Q4 FY17, and container handling at Dhamra from Q4 FY17.
*   **Coastal Shipping:** On track to handle 4 million tons of coastal cargo annually, with initial focus on coal movement from Dhamra to Tamil Nadu.
*   **SEZ Development:** Secured key clients like Credo Minerals, Oilfield Warehousing Services, and Britannia Limited. Over 50 acres of land leased in FY17, with recurring SEZ revenue projected to exceed ₹100 crores annually from FY17 onwards, increasing over 15-30 years.
*   **Logistics Business:** Adani Logistics operates two ICDs and is the largest private rail operator. Achieved record terminal volumes in August 2016 and is expanding warehousing and supply chain solutions.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** Operationalized container terminal Berth 3 at Dhamra. CT4 terminal commissioning expected by end of Q4 FY17 or early Q1 FY18. Abbot Point Bulk Coal acquisition completed; results to be consolidated from Q3 FY17. Bid submitted for Greenfield portfolio in Andhra Pradesh (Bhavanapadu).
