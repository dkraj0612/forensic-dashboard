## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a strong Q2 FY'20, demonstrating significant cargo throughput growth of 9% year-on-year in H1 FY'20, outperforming the all-India average. This growth was driven by market share gains in both overall cargo (to 22%) and container volumes (to 35%). The company maintained healthy port EBITDA margins at 70%, with revenue and EBITDA growing by 13%. Mundra Port achieved a milestone in September 2019 by becoming India's largest container handling port, surpassing JNPT, and is on track to be the largest by year-end FY'20. The logistics business, particularly Adani Logistics Limited (ALL), saw substantial growth in rail volumes (79%) and revenue (43%). APSEZ also completed its first share buyback and strengthened its board with independent directors. The company reiterated its commitment to ESG principles and outlined ambitious growth plans for its logistics and port infrastructure, including new LPG and LNG terminals and strategic acquisitions.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Market Share Expansion:** Continued gains in market share for both overall cargo (to 22%) and containers (to 35%) driven by diversified cargo handling and port capabilities.
*   **Operational Enhancements:** Commissioning of new container terminal at Mundra, commencement of LPG operations at Mundra, and liquid terminal at Kattupalli.
*   **Cargo Diversification:** Ongoing efforts to handle diverse cargo types including project cargo, steel billets, naphtha exports, and coal for Grasim, reducing reliance on single cargo.
*   **Logistics Network Expansion:** Adani Logistics Limited (ALL) is on track to operate 60+ rakes by FY'20 end and expanding its logistics parks and warehouses. Adani Agri Logistics Limited (AALL) is also growing its capacity and market share.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Progress on Dighi Port acquisition (preferred bidder status) and continued evaluation of strategic acquisition opportunities in logistics and ports, particularly in Andhra Pradesh and Maharashtra.
*   **ESG Focus:** Commitment to efficient resource utilization, emission reduction, and zero tolerance for fatalities. Induction of an independent director with extensive shipping experience.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Successful completion of first share buyback and adherence to investment grade rating while exploring opportunities to elongate debt maturity profile.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Management successfully guided for and achieved strong cargo volume growth (9% in H1 FY'20), outpacing the all-India average and validating their strategy of market share gains. The revised guidance for FY'20 of 8-10% reflects a realistic assessment of the current macro slowdown, which is a consistent approach from previous guidance.
*   **Market Share:** The narrative of increasing market share in both overall cargo and containers was validated with concrete figures, demonstrating consistent execution.
*   **Port EBITDA Margins:** Management's ability to maintain 70% port EBITDA margins despite varying cargo mixes and volumes (e.g., lower crude impacting Mundra's margin) showcases operational efficiency and cost management. The explanation for margin variations across ports (Mundra, Hazira, Dahej) provides transparency.
*   **Logistics Performance:** The significant YoY growth in ALL's rail volumes (79%) and revenue (43%) confirms the positive outlook and execution in the logistics segment.
*   **Acquisitions:** Progress on Dighi Port acquisition is on track as per previous indications. The proactive evaluation of new port and logistics acquisitions aligns with their stated strategy.
*   **Financial Management:** The company successfully raised USD-denominated bonds and is actively working to elongate its debt maturity profile, a consistent strategic goal. The explanation for the increase in gross debt, considering forex restatement, acquisitions, and new bond issuance, is consistent with their financial disclosures.
*   **Corporate Tax Reduction Impact:** Management stated that the corporate tax reduction has not yet translated into new manufacturing activities or increased SEZ interest, indicating a cautious and realistic outlook on the immediate impact of this policy change on their business.
