## Executive Overview

Adani Ports & Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) concluded FY25 with a robust performance, exceeding guidance for revenue and EBITDA. The company achieved 16% year-on-year revenue growth and 20% EBITDA growth (excluding forex), surpassing its initial projections. Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a significant 37% YoY increase. Key strategic initiatives included the acquisition of Gopalpur Port, commencement of operations at Vizhinjam and Colombo Ports, and board approval for the acquisition of NQXT Australia. APSEZ's integrated transport utility model continues to be a core driver, with strong performance in its logistics segment, which saw a 39% revenue jump driven by asset accretion and new services like trucking and international freight networks. Financial discipline remains a priority, with the Net Debt to EBITDA ratio improving to 1.9x. The company has projected strong growth for FY26, with EBITDA guidance of ₹21,000-22,000 crore and revenue guidance of ₹36,000-38,000 crore.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility Model:** Continued focus on integrating logistics services (rail, ICDs, warehouses, trucking, freight forwarding) with port operations to capture more of the value chain and drive cargo to APSEZ ports.
*   **Logistics Segment Growth:** Hyper-growth trajectory with 39% YoY revenue increase, driven by asset accretion and expansion into capital-light, high-margin services like trucking and international freight networks. Strategic Command Centre launched to enhance efficiency.
*   **Marine Business Expansion:** Acquisition of Astro Offshore and fleet expansion aim to triple marine business revenue by FY27. Focus on diversified fleet, profitable operations, Tier-1 customers, and long-term contracts.
*   **Global Footprint Expansion:** Progress in international operations with commencement at Colombo Port, board approval for NQXT Australia acquisition, and signed concession agreement for Dar es Salaam Port. Haifa Port EBITDA increased 36% YoY with a new union agreement to improve productivity.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Implementation of new Terminal Operating Systems (Navis N4) and ongoing digitization to enhance efficiency and customer experience. Commitment to Net Zero by 2040.
*   **Focus on ROCE & ROE:** Management emphasizes ROCE and ROE as key performance indicators across all business segments, aiming for best-in-class returns.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

    *   **FY26 Revenue Guidance:** ₹36,000-38,000 Cr.

    *   **Tanzania Port:** 30-year concession agreement signed.
    *   **Haifa Port:** Union agreement signed until 2036 for increased productivity.
    *   **Logistics Margins:** Targeting above-industry margins in two years; expected to increase ROCE.
    *   **Capex for FY26:** ₹12,000 Cr total, including ₹2,000 Cr for Technology & Decarbonization.

    *   **Coal Volumes:** Management acknowledges the shift from coal to renewable energy but highlights that increased renewable energy growth benefits container cargo. Prepared to manage energy mix evolution.
    *   **Port Margins:** Directionally expanding, with potential to exceed 80% in the next five years due to brownfield expansions and IT focus.
    *   **Global Acquisition Risk:** Return expectations factor in geopolitical and currency risks; acquired assets are evaluated comprehensively before transactions.
