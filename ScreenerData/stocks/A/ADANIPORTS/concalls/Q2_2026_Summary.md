## Executive Overview

Adani Ports & Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported a strong Q2 FY26, showcasing robust double-digit growth across key financial and operational metrics. Revenue surged by 30% YoY to ₹9,167 crore, with EBITDA growing 27% YoY to ₹5,550 crore, and net profit up 29% YoY to ₹3,120 crore. The company achieved an all-time high in revenue and EBITDA for the quarter. Significant improvements were noted in ROCE across all business segments, reaching 16% in H1 FY26, up from 15% in FY25, reflecting enhanced capital efficiency. Free cash flow generation of over ₹3,000 crore in H1 FY26 underscores financial discipline. Strategic initiatives are yielding positive results, particularly in the Logistics, Marine, and International Ports businesses, validating the strategy launched last year. The Net Debt to EBITDA ratio improved to 1.8x, and Fitch Ratings revised its outlook on APSEZ to 'Stable' from 'Negative'.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility Model:** Continued focus on optimizing the value chain by integrating port operations with logistics services, driving cargo growth.
*   **Segmental Growth:** Strong performance in Logistics (79% revenue growth) driven by asset-light services and efficient utilization of hard assets. Marine business witnessed significant expansion (237% revenue growth) due to strategic vessel acquisitions.
*   **International Port Expansion:** Colombo Port achieved its third consecutive month of 100,000+ TEU, Tanzania operations are seeing growth, and Haifa operations are stable with improved productivity. NQXT Australia acquisition is progressing.
*   **Operational Excellence & Technology:** Implementation of new systems and technology is enhancing operational efficiency and customer experience across all segments.
*   **Capital Efficiency:** Emphasis on ROCE and ROE as key performance indicators, with improved metrics across Ports, Logistics, and Marine businesses.
*   **Strategic CAPEX:** Ongoing investment of ₹75,000 crores over 5 years, including port expansions (Dhamra, Vizhinjam Phase-2, Colombo Phase-2), logistics parks (Kochi), and marine vessel acquisitions.
*   **Sustainability Focus:** Continued commitment to sustainability, recognized by ESG rating agencies like S&P CSA and MSCI.
