## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) demonstrated robust operational performance in H1 FY19, with core port operations showing significant growth, offsetting the absence of SEZ port-led development income. Cargo volumes reached a historical high for the half-year, significantly outpacing the growth of major Indian ports. The company saw strong contributions from crude, container, and coal volumes across its port network. Logistics operations faced headwinds due to leased rake recall, but management has taken proactive steps to address this. The company reiterated its FY19 cargo volume guidance and expressed confidence in improving EBITDA margins and generating substantial free cash flow.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY19):** INR 5,019 crores (down 8% YoY, primarily due to INR 1,165 crores of SEZ port-led development income in H1 FY18).
*   **Core Operations Revenue (Excluding SEZ income):** Grew 17% YoY in H1 FY19.
*   **Port Revenue (H1 FY19):** INR 4,240 crores (up 21% YoY).
*   **Port EBITDA (H1 FY19, excluding FOREX MTM):** INR 2,975 crores (up 24% YoY).
*   **Port EBITDA Margin (H1 FY19):** 70% (up 100 bps YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (H1 FY19, excluding MTM FOREX loss):** Lower YoY due to no SEZ income, but core operations EBITDA grew 24%.
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT) & Profit After Tax (PAT) (H1 FY19):** Lower YoY due to INR 953 crores MTM FOREX loss on foreign currency loans.
*   **APSEZ Cargo Volume (H1 FY19):** Crossed 100 million metric tons (up 15% YoY). Q2 FY19 saw 22% YoY growth.
*   **Container Volume (H1 FY19):** 2.84 million TEUs (up 16% YoY).
*   **Logistics Revenue (H1 FY19):** Impacted by lessor recall of 8 leased rakes; rail volumes down YoY.
*   **Adani Power Coal Volumes Handled (H1 FY19):** 5.33 million metric tons.
*   **CAPEX (FY19 Guidance):** INR 2,300 - 2,500 crores.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FY19 Guidance):** INR 1,750 - 2,000 crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Reaffirmed FY19 guidance of 200 million metric tons, aiming for 1.5x All India cargo growth and 2x All India container growth.
*   **Port Development & Diversification:** Continued focus on handling diverse cargo types, with strong growth in crude (+52%), containers (+16%), and coal (+13% overall, +30% excluding APL/Tata Power). New commodity handling (copper slag at Dahej).
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Addressing rake shortages by ordering new rakes (11 ordered, 1 received; 10 more to be ordered in Q3 FY19; 3 auto rakes for JV). Aiming for expanded rail operations from FY20. Developing multi-modal logistics with warehouses (Taloja, Kattupalli) and Private Freight Terminals (PFTs) at Bengaluru (operational FY19), Vadodara (FY20), and land acquisition for Panipat, Nagpur.
*   **Dhamra Port Strategy:** Proactively resolving evacuation issues by proposing an "Own Your Wagon" scheme for 11 rakes to serve customers like Tata Steel, SAIL, JSPL. Commenced offshore ship-to-ship (STS) handling for POS and future LPG for Bangladesh/Haldia. Expects 20-25% growth from FY20 onwards.
*   **SEZ Development:** Confident of achieving INR 800-1000 crores in port-led development income for FY19, with significant visibility for INR 1000 crores in H2 FY19. Recurring lease income estimated at INR 150 crores annually.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Margin Expansion:** Aiming to increase port EBITDA margins by 100 bps annually, targeting 71% in FY19.
*   **Balance Sheet Strengthening:** Focus on generating free cash flow for debt reduction.
*   **JV with Total (Dhamra LNG):** APSEZ will transfer the Dhamra LNG terminal to the JV, retaining marine services, royalty, and port development income. This will remove significant CAPEX from APSEZ's books. JV expected to be signed between December 2018 and January 2019.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of H1 FY19 Performance and Narrative vs. Previous Guidance:**

*   **SEZ Port-Led Development Income:** Management accurately highlighted the absence of SEZ port-led development income in H1 FY19, which was a significant contributor in H1 FY18. This aligns with the previous quarter's explanation of revenue recognition timing. The H2 FY19 outlook for this income remains strong.
*   **Cargo Volumes:** The company significantly outperformed its historical trends and industry benchmarks, with 15% YoY growth in H1 FY19 against 5% for major ports. Q2 FY19 growth of 22% further validates this trend. The confidence in achieving the 200 MMT FY19 target is reiterated.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Port EBITDA margins at 70% met the targeted level for H1 FY19. Management reaffirmed the strategy of increasing margins by 100 bps annually, targeting 71% for FY19.
*   **Logistics Business Performance:** The decline in logistics revenue was clearly attributed to the lessor recalling leased rakes, a confirmed operational headwind. Management's proactive ordering of new rakes and plans for further acquisitions demonstrates a consistent strategy to counter this issue and drive future growth.
*   **Dhamra Port Evacuation Issues:** The narrative on rake availability remaining low (13 rakes vs. 15 in H1 FY18) is consistent. The proposed "Own Your Wagon" scheme and STS operations are consistent with previously stated strategies to mitigate these challenges and unlock growth.
*   **FOREX Impact:** Management consistently explained the MTM losses on foreign currency loans, differentiating them from realized impacts. The strategy to match dollar debt with dollar revenues remains a core principle, with reassurance that current debt levels are adequately covered. The long-term perspective of P&L neutrality over 5 years is maintained.
*   **Kattupalli Acquisition:** IndAS adjustments post-acquisition are noted, with no longer charging interest as it is wholly owned.
*   **CAPEX Guidance:** The H1 FY19 CAPEX spend is on track with the revised FY19 guidance of INR 2,300 - 2,500 crores.
*   **Trade Receivables (CT3 & CT4):** Management confirmed receipt of receivables from CT3 and expects CT4 receivables to be realized within the current six-month period, bringing the total to INR 450 crores (down from INR 15 billion) and expecting it to become zero by year-end.
*   **JV EBITDA:** JV EBITDA (excluding revenue share paid to APSEZ) grew by 59% YoY in H1 FY19 to INR 351 crores, demonstrating strong performance from these ventures.
