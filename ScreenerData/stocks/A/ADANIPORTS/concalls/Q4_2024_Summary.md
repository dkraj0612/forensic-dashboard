## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) concluded FY2024 with a robust performance, reporting record cargo volumes, revenue, and EBITDA. The company achieved a significant 24% YoY increase in cargo handling to 420 million metric tons, surpassing its revised guidance. Operating revenue surged by 28% YoY to INR 26,711 crores, with EBITDA (excluding forex impact) growing by 24% YoY to INR 15,864 crores. Domestic port EBITDA margins expanded by 150 basis points to 71%. Profit after tax climbed 50% YoY to INR 8,104 crores, despite a write-off related to a new tax regime.

APSEZ demonstrated strong financial discipline, reducing its net debt-to-EBITDA ratio to 2.3x by March 31, 2024, from 3.1x at the beginning of the year, despite substantial capex of over INR 7,400 crores. The company also secured a AAA credit rating from CARE and a stable outlook revision from S&P and ICRA. Strategic expansions included the acquisition of Gopalpur and Karaikal ports, a JV with MSC for Ennore, and a significant financing commitment for the Colombo terminal. For FY2025, APSEZ projects continued growth with cargo volumes of 460-480 million metric tons and revenue of INR 29,000-31,000 crores, backed by an ambitious capex plan of INR 10,500-11,500 crores, including significant investment in decarbonization.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility Model:** Continued focus on providing end-to-end logistics solutions by integrating port operations with a rapidly expanding logistics network.
*   **Logistics Network Expansion:** Significant investments in commissioning 3 new Multi-Modal Logistics Parks (MMLPs) taking the total to 12, adding 34 new rail rakes (total 127), expanding warehousing capacity to 2.4 million sq ft, and launching an asset-light trucking business for last-mile connectivity.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions & Partnerships:** Completion of Gopalpur and Karaikal Port acquisitions, joint venture with MSC for Ennore container terminal, and securing financing for the Colombo terminal.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Asset Utilization:** Driving economies of scale and improving margins, particularly at flagship ports like Mundra, by maximizing asset utilization and leveraging container terminal growth.
*   **Decarbonization Initiative:** Allocation of significant capex towards renewable energy projects (1000 MW solar/wind) for captive consumption, aiming for cost-competitive decarbonization.
*   **Balanced Growth:** Emphasizing a balanced commodity mix (dry bulk, container, liquids/gas) and port mix (west, east, and south coasts) to capture diverse growth opportunities.
*   **International Expansion:** Evaluating strategic acquisition and partnership opportunities in regions like the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, and the Mediterranean for profitable and sustainable cargo traffic.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance:**
    *   **Cargo Volume (FY2024):** Management had revised the FY2024 cargo volume guidance upwards to "over 400 million metric tons" in Q3. APSEZ reported 420 million metric tons for FY2024, successfully **meeting and exceeding** this target.
    *   **Net Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio:** The Q3 summary noted an improvement to 2.5x as of December 31, 2023. APSEZ further reduced this to 2.3x by March 31, 2024, **exceeding** the previous target and demonstrating continued financial discipline.
    *   **Logistics Growth & Expansion:** Q3 highlighted aggressive expansion plans for MMLPs and rail rakes. Q4 results confirm the commissioning of three new MMLPs (total 12) and the addition of 34 rakes (total 127), reflecting **consistent execution** on logistics expansion.
    *   **Haifa Port Performance & Red Sea Impact:** Management maintained its Q3 stance that "Haifa Port volumes remained steady with marginal increases; no major disruption observed." In Q4, they reiterated "absolutely, we are not facing any challenge, which should impact our growth" from Red Sea issues, demonstrating **consistency in narrative**.
    *   **FY2024 Capex:** Q3 indicated approximately INR 5,500 crores incurred in 9 months, implying a pro-rata full-year estimate. APSEZ reported a full-year capex of "over 7,400 crores," indicating that investment **exceeded** the implied earlier estimate, reflecting accelerated growth initiatives.
    *   **Capital Return Policy:** Q3 stated a "commitment to dividend policy of 22-25% of PAT." For FY2024, a dividend of INR 6 per share was recommended, translating to a payout of approximately INR 1,300 crores against a PAT of INR 8,104 crores (approx. 16% payout). While the absolute dividend per share increased from FY2023's INR 5, the **payout percentage for FY2024 falls below the previously stated 22-25% range**, despite management stating it was "in line with our capital allocation policy."

*   **Consistency of Narrative:** Management maintained a consistently positive outlook, attributing growth to strong economic demand, robust capex cycles, and the success of their integrated transport utility model. The narrative emphasized strategic positioning, operational excellence, and a diversified cargo and port mix. Concerns about Red Sea disruptions were consistently downplayed.
