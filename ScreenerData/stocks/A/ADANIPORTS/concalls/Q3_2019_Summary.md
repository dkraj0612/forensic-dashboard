## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) delivered a solid Q3 FY19, showcasing continued cargo volume growth and strong operational performance across its port network. Despite facing headwinds in logistics due to leased rake recall, the company demonstrated resilience by proactively ordering new rakes and expanding its multi-modal logistics capabilities. Management reiterated its confidence in achieving full-year cargo volume targets and highlighted progress on strategic initiatives, including port development, SEZ income generation, and the potential JV with Total. The company's focus on operational efficiency, margin expansion, and balance sheet strengthening remains evident.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenue (Q3 FY19):** INR 2,824 crores (grew 17% YoY).
*   **Port Revenue (Q3 FY19):** INR 1,585 crores (grew 17% YoY).
*   **Port EBITDA (Q3 FY19):** INR 1,059 crores (grew 27% YoY).
*   **Port EBITDA Margin (Q3 FY19):** 70% (stable YoY).
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT) (Q3 FY19):** INR 1,410 crores (grew 42% YoY, excluding FOREX MTM loss).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q3 FY19):** INR 992 crores (grew 26% YoY, excluding FOREX MTM loss).
*   **APSEZ Cargo Volume (Q3 FY19):** 53.27 million metric tons (grew 14% YoY).
*   **Container Volume (Q3 FY19):** 1.1 million TEUs (grew 9% YoY).
*   **Logistics Revenue (Q3 FY19):** Decreased 13% YoY due to leased rake recall.
*   **Adani Power Coal Volumes Handled (Q3 FY19):** 3.19 million metric tons (handled by ports).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Reaffirmed full-year guidance of 200 million metric tons, aiming for 1.5x All India cargo growth and 2x All India container growth. Q3 saw a 14% YoY increase in cargo volumes.
*   **Port Development & Diversification:** Continued focus on handling diverse cargo, with strong performance in coal (excluding captive power plants) and crude. New commodity handling initiatives and expansion of existing capacities at major ports.
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Addressing rake shortages by ordering new rakes (11 BOXN HL rakes ordered, 1 received; another 10 to be ordered in Q3 FY19). Aiming for expanded rail operations from FY20. Developing multi-modal logistics with new warehouses (Taloja, Kattupalli) and Private Freight Terminals (PFTs).
*   **Dhamra Port Strategy:** Proactively resolving evacuation issues through the "Own Your Wagon" scheme and commencing offshore ship-to-ship (STS) handling. Expects 20-25% growth from FY20 onwards.
*   **SEZ Development:** Confident in achieving INR 800-1000 crores in port-led development income for FY19, with strong visibility for H2 FY19. Recurring lease income estimated at INR 150 crores annually.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Margin Expansion:** Aiming to increase port EBITDA margins by 100 bps annually, targeting 71% in FY19.
*   **Balance Sheet Strengthening:** Focus on generating free cash flow for debt reduction.
*   **JV with Total (Dhamra LNG):** JV signing expected between December 2018 and January 2019. APSEZ will transfer the Dhamra LNG terminal to the JV, retaining marine services, royalty, and port development income.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Q3 FY19 Performance and Narrative vs. Previous Guidance:**

*   **SEZ Port-Led Development Income:** Management reiterated the confidence in achieving INR 800-1000 crores of port-led development income for FY19, with significant visibility for H2 FY19. This aligns with the strategy of monetizing assets.
*   **Cargo Volumes:** The company continued to outperform Indian ports with 14% YoY growth in Q3 FY19, reinforcing its commitment to achieving the 200 MMT FY19 target. The strategy of gaining market share in container volumes remains on track.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Port EBITDA margins were maintained at a healthy 70% in Q3 FY19, consistent with the strategy to achieve 71% for FY19. Management expressed confidence in further increasing margins.
*   **Logistics Business Performance:** The narrative around the negative impact of leased rake recall on logistics revenue (down 13% YoY) was consistent. Management's proactive approach to acquiring new rakes and developing multi-modal logistics infrastructure demonstrates a clear strategy to mitigate this headwind and drive future growth.
*   **Dhamra Port Evacuation Issues:** The "Own Your Wagon" scheme and STS operations were presented as ongoing solutions to Dhamra's evacuation challenges, aligning with previous discussions. Management anticipates 20-25% growth from FY20.
*   **FOREX Impact:** Management consistently explained the impact of FOREX MTM gains/losses on the P&L, differentiating them from operational performance. The strategy of matching dollar debt with dollar revenues and the long-term P&L neutrality outlook remain consistent.
*   **CAPEX Guidance:** Q3 FY19 performance and future plans are on track with the overall CAPEX strategy, with a focus on Dhamra, Kattupalli, and Vizhinjam.
*   **Trade Receivables:** Management reiterated the expectation that CT3 and CT4 receivables would be settled by year-end FY19, aligning with previous disclosures.
