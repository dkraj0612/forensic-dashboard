## Executive Overview

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) reported robust Q3 FY21 performance, demonstrating significant cargo volume growth and market share gains. The company's strategy of expanding its port network, integrating logistics, and improving operational efficiencies continues to drive strong results. Key highlights include the acquisition of Krishnapatnam Port and its successful integration, leading to improved EBITDA margins. APSEZ is also making strides in its logistics business, with expansion of logistics parks and an MOU with the Government of Gujarat for a multi-modal logistics park. The company reiterates its commitment to sustainable growth and achieving carbon neutrality by 2025. Financial performance was supported by strong revenue and EBITDA growth, though SEZ income was lower than expected for the quarter.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q3 FY21 Cargo Volumes:** 76 million metric tonnes, up 37% YoY (vs. 7% for All India ports).
*   **Q3 FY21 APSEZ Market Share (All India Cargo):** Increased from 22% to 28%.
*   **Q3 FY21 APSEZ Market Share (Container Segment):** Increased from 36% to 43%.
*   **Q3 FY21 Container Volumes:** 2.06 million TEUs, up 36% YoY.
*   **Q3 FY21 Mundra Port Container Volumes:** 1.59 million TEUs (vs. JNPT's 1.29 million TEUs).
*   **Q3 FY21 Dry Cargo Volumes:** 37 million metric tonnes, up 42% YoY.
*   **Q3 FY21 Liquid Cargo Volumes (incl. crudes):** 10 million metric tonnes, up 24% YoY.
*   **Q3 FY21 LPG Volumes:** 213,000 metric tons.
*   **Q3 FY21 LNG Volumes:** 567,000 metric tons.
*   **Krishnapatnam Port (Q3 FY21):** 10 million metric tons cargo, Rs. 473 crores revenue, Rs. 336 crores EBITDA (71% margin).
*   **Q3 FY21 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 3,746 crores, up 12% YoY (Port revenue up 35%, Logistics revenue up 8%).
*   **Q3 FY21 Consolidated EBITDA:** Not explicitly stated, but Port EBITDA increased by 38% YoY.
*   **Q3 FY21 Port EBITDA Margin:** 71.7%, improved by 140 bps YoY.
*   **9M FY21 Free Cash Flow (from operations):** Rs. 4,200 crores.
*   **Full Year FY21 Free Cash Flow Guidance:** Expected to be around Rs. 5,600 crores.
*   **Total US Dollar Debt:** $3.4 billion, representing 65-70% of the overall debt profile.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Integrated Transport Utility:** Continued focus on integrating ports with logistics and warehousing to provide end-to-end solutions.
*   **Cargo Volume Growth:** Driving growth through capacity expansion, strategic acquisitions (Krishnapatnam), and market share gains, particularly from JNPT diversion and new services.
*   **Logistics Expansion:** Operating 60 rail rakes, adding 16 more, and developing new logistics parks (Mundra, Nagpur). Partnership with the Government of Gujarat for a large multi-modal logistics park at Virochan Nagar. Scaling up Grade A warehousing capacity to cater to e-commerce.
*   **Diversification:** Adding LPG and LNG cargo to the portfolio, with significant growth expected.
*   **Port Development:** Continued development of ports like Dhamra (new contracts and expected double-digit growth) and Kattupalli (commissioning of liquid facility, seeking clearances for bulk commodities).
*   **Sustainability & ESG:** Commitment to carbon neutrality by 2025, with business and future investments aligned to sustainable growth.
*   **Capital Management:** Issuance of a $500 million US Dollar bond to extend debt maturity and refinance existing debt. Focus on judicious cash use, adequate liquidity, and prudent capital management. Guidance of Net Debt-to-EBITDA in the 3-3.5% range.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Performance Against Guidance:**
    *   **FY21 Cargo Volume Guidance (245-250 million metric tonnes):** The company is on track to achieve this guidance, with Q3 performance contributing significantly.
    *   **Port EBITDA Margins:** Achieved 71.7% in Q3 FY21, with a target of 72-73% going forward, aligning with previous expectations. Krishnapatnam Port's EBITDA margins reached 71% with a target of 78% by FY23.
    *   **SEZ Income:** SEZ income was significantly lower than anticipated for the quarter. While management stated they did not provide specific SEZ income guidance for this quarter, they expect SEZ land sales income to start driving growth from next year (FY22) due to economic recovery and strong interest from potential investors. They anticipate bunching up of SEZ income in FY22.
    *   **Logistics Business Performance:** The logistics business returned to the growth trajectory with revenue up 8% and EBITDA up 16% in Q3 FY21.
    *   **CAPEX Guidance (FY21):** Curtailed discretionary CAPEX to around Rs. 2,000 crores for FY21, as guided.

*   **Consistency of Narrative:** The core narrative of APSEZ's integrated transport utility vision, market share expansion, and focus on operational efficiency remains highly consistent. The company continues to emphasize its strong asset base and strategic acquisitions. The positive outlook on cargo recovery and logistics growth is maintained. The management explicitly addresses the global container shortage, positioning it as a short-term phenomenon that APSEZ is well-equipped to handle due to its asset strength and network connectivity.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**
    *   **Container Shortage Impact:** Not expected to significantly impact APSEZ's volumes due to its strong assets, ideal locations, and robust road/rail connectivity. Pricing is factored in by global manufacturers/importers.
    *   **LNG/LPG Volumes:** LPG volumes around 2.5-3 million tons in the next 2 years at Mundra. LNG volumes around 2.5 million tons at Mundra for the next two years. Dhamra LNG expected to be commissioned by end of calendar year 2022.
    *   **Dhamra Port Growth:** Continuous double-digit growth expected for the next five years, requiring capacity expansion.
    *   **Kattupalli & Ennore Market Share:** Expect to continue gaining market share in Q4 FY21.
    *   **Acquisition Expenses (KPCL related):** Around $3 million, including professional, legal, and M&A fees.
    *   **FY22 CAPEX Guidance:** Expected to be in the range of Rs. 2,000 crores to Rs. 3,000 crores. Logistics CAPEX likely Rs. 500-600 crores, with the balance for ports. International expansion CAPEX is included.
    *   **FY22 Free Cash Flow:** Expected to be more robust and higher than FY21's Rs. 5,600 crores.
    *   **Net Debt-to-EBITDA:** To be maintained within the 3-3.5% range, with potential for absolute debt to increase due to inorganic acquisitions.
    *   **Container Terminal Expansion (Mundra):** No immediate plans to expand CT3 and CT4, but utilization of T2 expected to increase sharply next year (FY22).
    *   **Dhamra Contracts:** 5 new contracts signed, kicking in from Q4 FY21, providing an annualized volume of 9 million metric tons over five-year agreements, with customers including JSPL, JSW, and Rungta.
    *   **Vizhinjam Port:** Construction in full force. Jetty and terminal ready. Breakwater expected to be completed by FY22/early FY23. Full CAPEX of Rs. 4,000 crores expected to be completed by FY23. Rs. 1,400 crores invested so far.
    *   **Myanmar Project:** Work continuing without disruption. Terminal expected to be commissioned by April 2021. Management is monitoring the situation.
    *   **Western Hinterland Container Growth:** Guidance remains at 2x the India trade growth rate.
    *   **Kattupalli Port:** Liquid facility commissioned. Seeking environment clearance for other commodities by September/October 2021.
    *   **Dighi Port Acquisition:** Awaiting Maharashtra Maritime Board approval, expected by end of the current month. Detailed strategy document to be released post-acquisition.
    *   **Other Terminal Margins:** Similar steady state EBITDA margins expected for other port terminals, especially Goa and Tuna, running at full capacity.
    *   **Consolidated Port EBITDA Margins:** Expected to be in the range of 72% to 73%.
    *   **Krishnapatnam Port Margins:** Tariffs are higher than others, which is the primary reason for its expected higher margins compared to other ports.
    *   **Mundra Extension (GMB):** Working with GMB, expecting policy clarity in the next 12-18 months. Pitching for as long a concession as viable, specific years not specified.
    *   **West Coast Capacity Outlook:** Expecting a shortage in container capacity in the next 5 years, with plans to expand capacity in Mundra and Hazira.
    *   **Palanpur Feeder Route:** Feeder line commissioned, awaiting DFC line commissioning.
    *   **Warehousing Target:** A big focus area, specific million square feet target not yet disclosed but plans are being finalized.
    *   **FY22 SEZ Revenue:** Comprehensive guidance for FY22 will be provided next quarter, currently positive and bullish but not putting a number out yet.
