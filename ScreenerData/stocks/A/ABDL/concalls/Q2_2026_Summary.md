## Executive Overview

Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) reported a strong Q2 FY26, marking their fifth consecutive quarter of profitable growth post-listing. The company demonstrated sustained improvement in portfolio premiumization and margin expansion, driven by its strategic focus on profitable growth, enhanced premium offerings, and backward integration for supply chain security and cost optimization. Consolidated income from operations grew 14.4% year-on-year to ₹995 crore. EBITDA increased by 23.6% to ₹130 crore, with an improved margin of 13.1%. Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a 32.3% rise to ₹63 crore. Sales volume for the quarter was 9 million cases, an 8.4% YoY increase, supported by a 3.8% rise in realization per case due to an improved product mix and price increases. The Prestige & Above (P&A) category continued its growth momentum with 28.8% YoY volume increase, lifting its salience to 47.1% from 39.7% in Q2 FY25. ICONiQ White was highlighted as a standout performer, achieving 2x volume growth YoY and being recognized as the fastest-growing spirits brand globally. The company also provided an update on its CAPEX program, including the commissioning of its PET bottle manufacturing facility, and highlighted progress on its balance sheet and cash flow generation.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABDL's strategy continues to focus on profitable growth through several key initiatives:

*   **Premiumization and Portfolio Diversification:** Accelerating the Prestige & Above (P&A) segment, with ICONiQ White showing exceptional growth. The company is also expanding its Super-Premium to Luxury category with new product launches and international market entries.
*   **Backward Integration:** The PET bottle manufacturing facility in Telangana has been commissioned, aimed at enhancing supply chain efficiency and reducing costs. The single malt distillery and ENA capacity expansion are on track.
*   **Brand Strength and Expansion:** Strengthening flagship brands like Officer’s Choice and ICONiQ White, while revitalizing Sterling Reserve B7 with a refreshed blend and nationwide campaign. International market expansion remains a key focus, targeting an increased footprint.
*   **Market Dynamics & Regulatory Environment:** Benefiting from premiumization trends, experience-led consumption, and favorable regulatory reforms in states like Andhra Pradesh. Management acknowledges state-level regulatory changes as a potential challenge.
*   **International Market Growth:** Rapid expansion from 14 to 30 countries in 18 months, with a focus on Africa, Western Hemisphere, and travel retail. The asset-light, high-margin export model is a key driver.
