## Executive Overview

Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) presented its Q2 FY25 results, demonstrating a strong sequential recovery and a positive year-on-year (YoY) performance, driven by premiumization efforts, operational efficiencies, and strategic initiatives. Income from Operations for Q2 FY25 stood at ₹870 Cr, a significant 14.5% increase Quarter-on-Quarter (QoQ) and a modest 2.1% YoY growth. EBITDA surged by 38.8% QoQ to ₹105 Cr, leading to an improved EBITDA margin of 12.1%, up 212 bps QoQ. Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a substantial increase of 135.9% QoQ to ₹48 Cr, benefiting from higher EBITDA and interest cost savings post-IPO. The company highlighted a 4.0% YoY increase in net realization per case to ₹998, attributed to an optimized state brand mix and premiumization. Sales volume for the quarter was 8.3 million cases. Key strategic moves include a partnership with Roust Corporation to introduce premium vodka and the development of a premium-to-luxury portfolio, supported by celebrity endorsement and a new venture company. Backward integration through ENA and PET plant investments is underway to enhance profitability.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABDL's strategy is focused on profitable growth through premiumization and operational excellence, underpinned by several key initiatives:

*   **Premiumization & Luxury Focus:** Accelerating the development of a premium-to-luxury portfolio through a new business venture, strategic partnerships (e.g., Roust Corporation for vodka), and brand extensions. The goal is to offer unique value propositions and cater to evolving consumer preferences.
*   **Portfolio Diversification:** Expanding beyond its traditional strength in whisky into categories like vodka and gin, leveraging market opportunities and partnerships.
*   **Backward Integration & Cost Optimization:** Investing in ENA units (Maharashtra), malt plants, and PET bottle manufacturing to secure raw material supply, enhance supply chain efficiency, and improve gross margins. These investments are expected to lead to substantial margin improvement over the next three years.
*   **Brand Building & Market Expansion:** Strengthening existing millionaire brands (Officer's Choice, Sterling Reserve, ICONiQ White) and introducing new products. The company is also expanding its export footprint, currently in 22 countries.
*   **Operational Excellence & Governance:** Implementing initiatives to improve profitability through enhanced backward integration and strengthening the governance framework with tools like Komrisk for compliance.

Industry drivers include the festive season, recovery in the Andhra Pradesh (AP) market, and a shift towards experience-driven consumption in the P&A category. Input costs for grain and ENA are expected to be inflationary, while glass and PET prices are anticipated to remain stable.
