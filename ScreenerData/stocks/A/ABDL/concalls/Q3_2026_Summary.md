## Executive Overview

Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) reported its sixth consecutive quarter of strong performance post-listing in Q3 FY26, demonstrating sustained profitable growth, portfolio premiumization, and margin expansion. Consolidated income from operations grew 2.8% year-on-year to ₹1,004 crore. EBITDA saw a significant 14.1% increase to ₹137 crore, with EBITDA margins improving to 13.6%, driven by a better product mix, operating leverage, and backward integration benefits. Profit After Tax (PAT) rose 10.9% to ₹63 crore. Sales volume for the quarter was 9 million cases, up 1.3% YoY, with realization per case improving by 0.7% due to an enhanced product mix and selective price increases. The Prestige & Above (P&A) segment continued its strong momentum, with volume growing 16.9% YoY, lifting its salience to 48.5% from 42% in Q3 FY25. ICONiQ White remains a standout performer, with impressive volume growth and progress towards a 10 million cases annual run rate. The company also highlighted progress in its backward integration program and international expansion, while navigating industry-specific headwinds in Telangana and Maharashtra. For the nine-month period ended Q3 FY26, consolidated income from operations was ₹2,929 crore (12.4% YoY growth), EBITDA was ₹386 crore (28.1% YoY growth), and PAT was ₹182 crore (57% YoY growth).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABDL's strategy remains focused on profitable growth driven by:

*   **Premiumization:** Continued acceleration of the P&A segment, with ICONiQ White as a key growth engine. Expansion of the luxury portfolio (ABD Maestro) with new product launches and international market penetration.
*   **Backward Integration:** Commissioning of the PET bottle manufacturing facility, and ongoing progress on the single malt distillery and ENA capacity expansion, expected to enhance gross margins by 300 basis points by FY28. New CAPEX in Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra for bottling and ENA capacity.
*   **Brand Strength & Expansion:** Officer's Choice maintains leadership and market share gains. ICONiQ White continues strong growth domestically and internationally. Sterling Reserve B7 is being revitalized with a refreshed blend and upcoming contemporary packaging. Expansion of CSD channel presence with approved brands.
*   **International Growth:** Aggressive expansion of export footprint, targeting 35 countries by Q4 FY26, leveraging an asset-light, high-margin model.
*   **Market Navigation:** Adapting to state-specific regulatory changes in Telangana and Maharashtra, while capitalizing on favorable trends like premiumization and evolving consumer preferences.
