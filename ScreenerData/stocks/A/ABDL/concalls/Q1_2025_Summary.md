## Executive Overview

Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) held its first conference call post-IPO to discuss its FY24 performance and provide an overview of its business. The company highlighted its strong market position as the third-largest spirits company in India by volume, with a diversified portfolio and a pan-India distribution network. Management emphasized the continuing trend of premiumization in the Indian spirits market, with a particular focus on growing the Prestige & Above (P&A) segment. Key strategic priorities for the upcoming year include accelerated participation in the luxury and premium segments, cost optimization beyond Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), and process automation and digitization. The company addressed past challenges, notably the working capital stress in Telangana during H2 FY24, which impacted volume growth, and outlined plans to leverage its deleveraged balance sheet post-IPO. Management expressed optimism regarding market expansion, product innovation, and margin improvement.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABDL's strategy remains centered on several key pillars:

*   **Premiumization & Luxury Segment Focus:** Driving growth and value through increased penetration in the Prestige & Above (P&A) and luxury segments. The company is launching new products and expanding its presence in these categories, with a specific focus on the luxury segment beyond the initial launch of Zoya Gin.
*   **Portfolio Enhancement:** Continuously developing and launching new products in premium and luxury segments, particularly in non-whisky categories to diversify from its current whisky-dominant portfolio.
*   **Cost Optimization & Efficiency:** Implementing cost-saving initiatives beyond COGS, focusing on areas like Logistics, Procurement & Bottling (LPB), forward logistics, and commodity costs. This also includes improving working capital efficiency through optimized procurement and replenishment models.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Enhancing operational efficiency through process automation, digitization of governance and compliance, and improving captive ENA usage to boost gross margins.
*   **Brand Building & Market Share:** Strengthening flagship brands like Officer's Choice and Officer's Choice Blue, while driving growth for emerging brands like ICONiQ White and Sterling Reserve. Emphasis on profitable growth in key markets and leveraging brand equity.
*   **Sustainability & Packaging Innovation:** Transitioning from glass bottles to PET and aseptic packaging (tetra) to improve gross margins and environmental impact, with positive consumer acceptance.

Key drivers for the industry and ABDL include: growing legal drinking age population, rising disposable incomes, increased social acceptance of alcohol consumption, and a consumer shift towards experience-driven purchases and premium offerings.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context (Baseline - FY24 Results & Guidance):**
The FY24 results call confirms several strategic focuses and expectations from previous discussions. The company reiterated its commitment to premiumization, growing the P&A segment, and enhancing profitability. Previous guidance pointed towards ICONiQ White reaching 4-5 Mn cases ARR in FY25, pan-India availability of ICONiQ White, and expansion in premium and luxury segments.

**Audit of Previous Promises vs. Current Transcript (FY24 Results Call):**

*   **Volume Growth:** While the transcript notes a slight de-growth of 1.7% in FY24 volumes (31.7 Mn cases vs. 32.2 Mn cases in FY23), management attributes this to strategic capital allocation favoring profitable brands (like ICONiQ) and states, and taking a backseat on lower-margin segments, particularly in H2 FY24 due to working capital constraints in Telangana. This is a shift from a blanket volume growth target, indicating a prioritization of profitability.
*   **ICONiQ White:** The brand achieved 2.3 million cases in FY24 and is projected to reach an Annual Run Rate (ARR) of 4-5 million cases in FY25, exceeding the previous guidance of 4-5 Mn cases ARR for FY25. It achieved million-case status within its first 12 months.
*   **Pan India Availability (ICONiQ White):** Management states the national rollout is "just about complete" and "many more markets that we have added post FY24," indicating significant progress towards or achievement of pan-India availability.
*   **P&A Salience:** P&A volume share increased to 37.3% in FY24, aligning with the strategy to grow this segment. Management expects this to grow to over 50% in volume and 60%-odd in value over the next few years.
*   **Profitability & Cost Savings:** EBITDA margin improved from 6.2% to 7.5% in FY24, with a stated goal of reaching industry-best-in-class margins (15%+) in the long term. The company is actively pursuing cost savings beyond COGS and expects improvements from renegotiated vendor terms and increased market bottle utilization.
*   **Working Capital Optimization:** The Telangana receivable issue significantly impacted H2 FY24 working capital, leading to strategic capital prioritization. While the issue is not fully resolved, it is improving, and management is focused on optimizing working capital going forward.
*   **Officer's Choice Market Leadership:** Officer's Choice maintained its position as the third-largest selling whisky brand globally and the flagship brand with the highest gross margin in the portfolio. Officer’s Choice Blue performed well in its regional strongholds.
*   **Premium Segment Presence:** The company has built a foundation for its luxury portfolio with a new division ("Prem Brand") and has launched Zoya Gin, with more luxury product launches planned. Sterling Reserve is also highlighted as a strong performer.
*   **Restructuring Costs:** Management clarified that the previously mentioned Rs. 93-95 crore cost saving relates to the chairman's transition to a non-executive role, the full benefit of which will accrue from FY25. Employee costs increased due to investment in building the luxury segment "Prem Brand" division.

**Management Narrative & Headwinds:**
The narrative remains consistent with a strong focus on premiumization and operational efficiency. The primary headwind discussed was the working capital issue in Telangana, which impacted H2 FY24 volumes. Management presented this as a strategic capital allocation decision rather than a persistent operational failure. Other industry headwinds like commodity prices and glass bottle prices are expected to soften. The company is actively seeking ways to improve gross margins, particularly through increased captive ENA usage.
