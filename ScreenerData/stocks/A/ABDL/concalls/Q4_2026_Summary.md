## Executive Overview

Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) has reported a robust Q4 FY26 and a record-breaking FY26, marking its seventh consecutive quarter of profitable delivery post-listing. The company achieved its highest ever EBITDA of ₹568 crore and Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹220 crore (adjusted for certain expenses) for FY26, demonstrating significant year-on-year growth of 25.8% and 36.3% respectively. Income from operations for FY26 grew by 11.5% to ₹3,949 crore. This strong performance was driven by accelerated premiumization, with the Prestige & Above (P&A) segment contributing 47.2% of overall volume and 57.3% of value. The company also saw successful execution of its backward integration projects and expansion of its ABD Maestro portfolio. ICONiQ White continues its remarkable growth trajectory, crossing 10.7 million cases in FY26. Management has recommended a dividend of 270% for FY26.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

ABDL's strategy is anchored on four key pillars:

*   **Premiumization:** Continued acceleration of the P&A segment, driven by ICONiQ White and the expansion of the ABD Maestro luxury portfolio across whisky, gin, vodka, and rum. The company is focusing on increasing P&A salience to over 50% by volume and 70-75% by value in the next three years.
*   **Backward Integration:** Progress on Phase 1 PET bottling facility (commissioned and EBITDA accretive) and the Telangana Malt Distillery (on track for H1 FY27). ENA distillery expansion in Maharashtra is slated for H1 FY28. These projects are expected to enhance structural cost efficiency and supply chain security, contributing up to 300 basis points to EBITDA margin enhancement by FY28. Phase 2 capex across UP, Maharashtra, and Andhra Pradesh will further strengthen capabilities.
*   **Brand Strength & Expansion:** Revitalization of Officer's Choice Blue with new packaging in Q2 FY27. Officer's Choice brandy is being launched in Andhra Pradesh. Expansion into the CSD and travel retail channels for key brands (ICONiQ, SRB7, Kyron, Jolly Roger Rum) to tap into profitable segments.
*   **International Growth:** Significant expansion of export footprint to 36 countries in FY26, with a focus on growing high-margin segments like value Scotch whisky.
