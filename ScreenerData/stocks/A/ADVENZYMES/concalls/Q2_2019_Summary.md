## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies (AETL) reported its financial results for the second quarter and first half of fiscal year 2020 (ended September 30, 2019). The company announced the approval of its unaudited financial results, including the appointment of an Additional Director, Dr. Sunny Sharma, and the significant decision to discontinue operations and initiate closure of its wholly-owned subsidiary in Malaysia, Advanced Enzymes (Malaysia) Sdn. Bhd. (AEM), due to unsatisfactory performance. The financial results for the quarter and half-year were approved by the Board of Directors and subjected to a limited review by the statutory auditors.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Mission:** To enable every human being to benefit from enzymes for well-being and healthy living.
*   **Vision:** To become the largest, enzyme-based, value provider to consumers and processors globally.
*   **Core Offering:** Replacing traditionally used chemicals with eco-friendly enzymatic solutions across a wide variety of industries.
*   **Key Industries Served:** Human health care and nutrition, animal nutrition, baking, fruit & vegetable processing, brewing & malting, grain processing, protein modification, dairy processing, specialty applications, textile processing, and others.
*   **R&D Focus:** Research-driven company with global leadership in enzyme manufacturing.
*   **Global Presence:** Operates in multiple regions including Asia (ex-India), with a significant market share in India.
*   **Regulatory Filings:** GRAS dossier evaluated by US FDA, Food Enzyme dossiers filed with EFSA.
