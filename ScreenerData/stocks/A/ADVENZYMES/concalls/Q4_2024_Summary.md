## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported a strong year-on-year performance for FY2024, with consolidated revenue growing 15% to ₹6,239 million and Profit After Tax (PAT) increasing by 32% to ₹1,370 million. EBITDA also saw a significant increase of ₹481 million, leading to an improved EBITDA margin of 33% (up from 29% in FY2023). The company highlighted a one-time exceptional item of ₹151 million in Q4 FY2024 due to an adverse court judgment in its US subsidiary, AST Enzymes, which impacted quarterly profitability.

Segment performance was mixed year-on-year. Human Nutrition, the largest segment (65% of Q4 revenue), grew 17% YoY in Q4 and 18% for the full year, driven by API and Probiotics. Animal Nutrition, however, declined 10% YoY in Q4 and 5% for the full year. Bioprocessing showed robust growth of 35% YoY in Q4 and 21% for the full year, with its food business growing 30% YoY. Specialized Manufacturing reported a 19% YoY growth for the full year, despite a marginal YoY decline in Q4.

The company also noted key developments including EFSA approval for two food enzyme dossiers and no-question letters for two GRAS filings with USFDA. They also increased their stake in JC Biotech to 95.72%. AETL initiated clean energy initiatives by installing solar power plants.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Growth in Core Segments:** Focus on continued growth in Human Nutrition, driven by API and Probiotics, and Bioprocessing, with strong traction in its food business.
*   **US Business Expansion:** Emphasis on the US market for driving growth, particularly in weight management and sugar management products. Aiming for continued growth in this region.
*   **Product Development & Approvals:** Obtaining regulatory approvals (EFSA, USFDA) for new food enzymes to expand product offerings and market reach.
*   **R&D and Innovation:** Strengthening R&D capabilities, with plans for a new, larger R&D center in Nashik.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions & Investments:** Continuing to explore strategic inorganic opportunities and increasing stake in subsidiaries like JC Biotech.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Initiating clean energy adoption with solar power installations.
*   **Margin Improvement:** Focus on improving margins through product mix, operational efficiencies, and growth in higher-margin businesses like the US market.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **FY2024 Revenue Growth:** The company achieved its stated goal of double-digit revenue growth, reporting 15% YoY for the full year.
*   **Profitability:** Full-year PAT growth of 32% and EBITDA margin improvement to 33% (from 29%) exceeded previous positive trends, demonstrating a strong recovery in profitability. However, Q4 PAT was negatively impacted by a one-time exceptional item.
*   **Segment Performance:** Human Nutrition delivered strong YoY growth as targeted. Animal Nutrition saw a decline, which management attributed to external factors like diseases and farming disruptions in specific regions. Bioprocessing and Specialized Manufacturing showed positive YoY growth for the full year.
*   **US Business:** Management indicated continued growth in the US, which is a key focus area. The transcript mentions US business improved and sees continued growth, though specific YoY figures for Q4 were not explicitly detailed as in previous periods.
*   **Product Approvals:** The company successfully obtained approvals from EFSA and USFDA, aligning with its strategy to expand its product portfolio globally.
*   **JC Biotech Investment:** The increase in stake in JC Biotech aligns with the strategy of strengthening subsidiary positions.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D expenditure as a percentage of revenue decreased slightly year-on-year, but absolute spend remains significant, supporting innovation. The planned new R&D center indicates continued commitment to R&D.

*   **FY2025 Revenue Growth Outlook:** The company projects revenue growth between 13% to 16%.
*   **EBITDA Margin Outlook:** Management expects the EBITDA margin to remain in the "33% range" annually, with potential for further improvement driven by product mix and US business growth. A 1% point increase in top-line growth can lead to a 2% increase in EBITDA margin.
*   **US Business Outlook:** Expecting continued growth from the US business, driven by weight management and sugar management products. Maintaining at least 10% growth in the US is targeted.
*   **Human Nutrition Contribution:** Expected to remain between 65% to 70% of the business mix.

*   **Probiotics Business:** While not tracked separately due to integrated formulations, the area is seen as growing and contributing to overall solutions.

*   **Logistics Costs:** While elevated due to Red Sea conflict, they are manageable and not impacting to the extent seen during COVID.
*   **Input Costs:** Expected to remain stable.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Continuing with interim dividends and retaining cash for strategic acquisitions and business expansion.
