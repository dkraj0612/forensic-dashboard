## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported a strong rebound in Q1 FY2024, with significant year-on-year growth across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT. Revenue grew 22% YoY to ₹1,473 million, driven primarily by the Human Nutrition and Bio-Processing segments. EBITDA increased by 42% YoY to ₹440 million, and PAT saw a substantial 67% YoY jump to ₹294 million. EBITDA margins stood at 30% and PAT margins at 20%, showing a marked improvement from the challenging Q1 FY2023. Management attributes the improved performance to stabilized raw material prices, though elevated power and fuel costs remain a concern.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cost Stabilization:** Raw material prices are largely stabilized, though power and fuel costs remain elevated. Management anticipates these higher costs to settle in coming quarters.
*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   **Human Nutrition:** Outperformed with significant YoY growth, driven by Pharma, API, and Biocatalyst in domestic markets and nutrition in international markets.
    *   **Animal Nutrition:** Showed modest YoY growth but a sequential decline, attributed to the lumpy nature of export shipments and potential sales reversals. No customer loss is indicated.
    *   **Bio-Processing:** Performed well with strong YoY and sequential growth.
    *   **Specialized Manufacturing:** Significant YoY growth, though a sequential decline.
*   **Geographic Focus:** "Rest of the world" revenue saw a jump, attributed to overall business increase rather than new geographies. Geographical sales remain lumpy quarter-to-quarter.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D spend as a percentage of revenue has decreased slightly in Q1 FY2024 compared to Q1 FY2023, with a focus on back-end R&D at the new Nasik facility.
*   **API Market Development:** Progressing with R&D and pilot stages, with anticipation of gradual commercialization and new product introductions.
*   **B2C Business (WELLFA):** Development is ongoing, with no revenue expected this year. Focus is on online platforms like Amazon.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Revenue Growth vs. Previous Outlook:** The Q1 FY2024 results directly contradict the challenging Q1 FY2023 narrative. The significant 22% YoY revenue growth is a strong positive reversal. Management had indicated in the prior call that Q1 and Q2 FY2024 would be more or less flat, with growth expected from Q3/Q4. The current performance surpasses this expectation, showing immediate improvement.
*   **Margin Improvement vs. Previous Expectations:** Q1 FY2024 EBITDA margins of 30% are an improvement from the 26% seen in Q1 FY2023 and are close to the 31% in Q4 FY2023. This aligns with management's prior expectation of margins returning to more normalized levels, though the goal of staying "above 40%" in a normalized world remains a future target. The current margins are close to the 30% mentioned as the Indian business margin in the previous call.
*   **Subsidiary Performance Contrast:** JC Biotech and Evoxx continued to show sequential and year-on-year declines in revenue and profitability, which aligns with previous quarters' trends. SciTech, however, showed a strong turnaround with positive revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, a significant improvement from its negative performance in Q1 FY2023.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Revenue Growth Outlook:** Management expects sustainable growth and still targets 15% to 20% annual sales growth in a normalized scenario, contingent on global stability. The first two quarters were expected to be flat, with growth from Q3/Q4, but Q1 has already shown strong growth.
*   **Margin Trajectory:** Aims to keep EBITDA margins "above 40%" in a normalized world. For FY2023, management expects aggregate EBITDA margin to be close to FY2022 levels, with definite improvement expected throughout the remaining quarters.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D expenditure is expected to remain at around 3-4% of consolidated revenue for the year, with the new Nasik R&D center expected to be completed by December.
*   **API Market Commercialization:** Anticipate gradual increases in orders and revenue from new products expected to be commercialized this year.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Future growth will be driven by Bio-processing, API development, Food Processing, Animal Feed, and B2C Human Nutrition.
*   **Nasik R&D Center:** First slab expected on September 15th, completion anticipated by December. Most back-end R&D will be housed here.
*   **Probiotics Sales:** Expects better sales this year compared to FY2023 (₹200 million), but this is not factored into current projections and would be a positive surprise. FY2023 probiotics sales were ₹200 million vs. ₹240 million in FY2022. Q1 FY2024 probiotics sales were ₹31 million.
*   **Dividend:** Continuing to provide dividends, indicating a strategy for excess cash alongside potential M&A.
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** Not explicitly mentioned, but implies management is comfortable with current levels.
*   **Mergers & Acquisitions:** Actively exploring M&A opportunities for excess cash, but significant opportunities have been elusive.
*   **Freight & Raw Material Expenses:** Outlook is for stabilization and softening, respectively, though power and fuel costs remain a concern. Planning for alternate energy sources to mitigate power costs.
