## Executive Overview

This transcript (Fallback_Transcript_87) details Advanced Enzyme Technologies' (AETL) acquisition of a 70% stake in JC Biotech, a manufacturer of the API enzyme Serratiopeptidase. This strategic move is expected to solidify AETL's market leadership in Serratiopeptidase to over 80% and marks a foray into the biopharma and Algal Omega-3 DHA markets. The acquisition, financed primarily through internal accruals and expected to close by December 2016, is projected to be EPS accretive for FY17. JC Biotech's capabilities in solvent-based permutations and recovery are complementary to AETL's existing processes. The company emphasizes leveraging its 27 years of fermentation expertise to improve JC Biotech's margins through cost reduction and market expansion. While JC Biotech's current revenue of Rs. 39 crores is solely from Serratiopeptidase, its pipeline includes biopharma molecules and Algal DHA, offering significant future growth potential. AETL plans to integrate JC Biotech's operations cautiously, focusing on maximizing synergies and market opportunities.
