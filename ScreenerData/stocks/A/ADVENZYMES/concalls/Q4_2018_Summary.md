## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies (AETL) announced its financial results for the fourth quarter and full year ended March 31, 2018. The company reported consolidated revenue growth of 20% year-on-year for Q4 FY18 and 15% for FY18, reaching ₹1,086 million and ₹3,957 million, respectively. Consolidated EBITDA saw a significant jump of 40% in Q4 FY18, reaching ₹490 million, with EBITDA margins expanding to 45%. Net Profit for Q4 FY18 also rose by 30% to ₹283 million. For the full year FY18, consolidated Net Profit saw a modest 1% increase to ₹936 million, with EBITDA margins contracting slightly to 42%. The company has recommended a dividend of 25% (₹0.50 per equity share). Key subsidiaries like Advanced Enzymes (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd and Evoxx Technologies GmbH were integrated during the fiscal year.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Mission:** To enable every human being to benefit from enzymes for well-being and healthy living.
*   **Vision:** To become the largest, enzyme-based, value provider to consumers and processors globally.
*   **Core Offering:** Replacing traditionally used chemicals with eco-friendly enzymatic solutions across a wide variety of industries.
*   **Key Industries Served:** Human health care and nutrition, animal nutrition, baking, fruit & vegetable processing, brewing & malting, grain processing, protein modification, dairy processing, specialty applications, textile processing, and others.
*   **R&D Focus:** Research-driven company with global leadership in enzyme manufacturing, operating 7 manufacturing facilities and 7 R&D centers.
*   **Global Expansion:** Exports to 45+ countries across 6 continents. Significant integration of international subsidiaries (Malaysia, Germany).
*   **Market Position:** Highest market share in India.
*   **Regulatory Filings:** GRAS dossier filed with US FDA and Food Enzyme dossiers filed with EFSA.
