## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies (AETL) released its Q4 FY2017 and full-year FY2017 financial results. The company reported consolidated net sales of ₹3,314 million for FY2017, a 13% year-on-year increase, and consolidated net profit of ₹924 million, up 20% YoY. For Q4 FY2017, net sales saw a significant QoQ increase of 43% to ₹860 million, with EBITDA up 69% QoQ to ₹333 million and net profit up 34% QoQ to ₹200 million. The company highlighted a strong YoY growth in net profit for FY2017, underscoring an improvement in profitability with a net profit margin of 28% compared to 26% in the prior year.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Business:** Global leadership in enzyme manufacturing, providing eco-safe solutions across various industries including human care, animal nutrition, baking, food processing, textiles, and biofuels.
*   **Growth Focus:** Continued expansion in international markets, with a broad portfolio of 400+ enzyme products and exports to 45+ countries.
*   **Research & Development:** Emphasis on a research-driven approach to develop new enzyme products and improve existing processes.
*   **Manufacturing Capacity:** Operates 6 state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities.
*   **Customer Solutions:** Provides customized enzyme solutions with strong technical service.
*   **Acquisition Strategy:** Chief Business Officer Piyush Rathi instrumental in the acquisition of JC Biotech (mentioned in prior context).
