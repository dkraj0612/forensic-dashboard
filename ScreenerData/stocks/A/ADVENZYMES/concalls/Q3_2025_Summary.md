## Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited - Q3 FY2026 Earnings Call - One-Page Executive Summary

**Date of Call:** February 04, 2026
**Period Covered:** Quarter ended December 31, 2025 (Q3 FY2026) and Nine Months ended December 31, 2025

---

### Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported mixed results for Q3 FY2026, with revenue showing modest YoY growth but a sequential decline. While several segments performed well, Human Healthcare experienced a YoY decline. Management acknowledged global market disruptions due to US tariffs, although noted a reduction in these levies. Despite some headwinds, the company remains focused on operational excellence and anticipates maintaining its full-year growth trajectory. Strategic initiatives in R&D and market expansion continue, with a new R&D center slated for partial activation.

---

### Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue (Consolidated):**
    *   Q3 FY2026: ₹1,719 Million (+2% YoY, -7% QoQ)
    *   9M FY2026: ₹5,424 Million (+15% YoY)
*   **EBITDA (Consolidated):**
    *   Q3 FY2026: ₹494 Million (-11% YoY, -18% QoQ)
    *   9M FY2026: ₹1,658 Million (+11% YoY)
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Consolidated):**
    *   Q3 FY2026: ₹432 Million (+11% YoY, -3% QoQ)
    *   9M FY2026: ₹1,284 Million (+20% YoY)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 29% (Q3 FY2026), 31% (9M FY2026)
*   **PAT Margin:** 25% (Q3 FY2026), 24% (9M FY2026)
*   **R&D Spend (Consolidated):** 3.2% of revenue (Q3 FY2026, after eliminations)
*   **Top Ten Customers Contribution:** 26% (Q3 FY2026), 23% (9M FY2026)
*   **Biocatalysis Segment Revenue:** $2.1 million (FY2025)

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Segmental Performance:** Human Healthcare saw a YoY decline due to lower sales in Pharma, API, and Nutrition. Animal Healthcare and Bioprocessing (driven by the food business) showed robust YoY and sequential growth. Specialized Manufacturing remained flat YoY.
*   **Geographic Focus:** Asia and "Rest of the World" are identified as key growth areas yielding results from past registrations and efforts. The US market continues to be impacted by tariffs, with management exploring strategies to mitigate these effects.
*   **Product Development & R&D:** Continuous investment in R&D, with new products and applications in development. A new R&D center in Nashik, focusing on strain development, protein engineering, and fermentation, is expected to be partially active by the end of Q2 FY2027.
*   **US Market Strategy:** Management is navigating tariff impacts by focusing on customer retention and potential price adjustments. The recent reduction in US tariffs is noted as a positive, but significant price increases remain unfeasible.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Management continues to focus on operational excellence to maintain momentum and margins amidst global uncertainties.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Q3 FY2026 Performance:** The reported Q3 FY2026 revenue of ₹1,719 million shows a modest 2% YoY growth, which is significantly lower than the "mid-double digit growth" stated as a full-year guidance and below the prior quarter's performance. EBITDA declined 11% YoY and 18% QoQ, missing the historical trend of strong EBITDA growth. However, PAT saw an 11% YoY increase. The segment-wise performance highlights a divergence from the consistent growth previously projected, with Human Healthcare showing a decline.
*   **US Tariff Impact:** Management reiterated the impact of US tariffs on sales and market dynamics, noting the uncertainty. They acknowledged the news of reciprocal tariff reductions but indicated that significant price increases in the US remain challenging. This aligns with the previous call's discussion on tariff headwinds.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D spend as a percentage of revenue remained consistent at around 3.2% (consolidated, after eliminations), indicating continued commitment to innovation.
*   **Full-Year Guidance:** Management stated they intend to maintain the previously stated guidance of "mid-double digit growth" for the full fiscal year, despite the mixed Q3 performance and ongoing global uncertainties.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Full-Year Growth:** Management aims to maintain "mid-double digit growth" for the full fiscal year.
*   **H2 FY2026 Outlook:** Specific guidance for H2 FY2026 will be provided once uncertainties become clearer, but efforts are focused on achieving the full-year target.
*   **EBITDA & PAT Margins:** Management expects to manage margins at current levels, with EBITDA margins between 30-32% and PAT margins of 21-22% expected in the worst-case scenario regarding tariffs.
*   **US Market Recovery:** Management anticipates seeing trending improvements in US sales reflecting from this year (FY2026), with potential improvements in margins due to higher sales volume.
*   **New R&D Center:** The new R&D center in Nashik is expected to be partially active by the end of Q2 FY2027, focusing on strain development, protein engineering, and fermentation.
*   **CapEx:** Greenfield or brownfield expansion CapEx is anticipated around FY2028-FY2029, with an estimated cost of ₹50 crore.
*   **Biocatalysis Segment:** Growth prospects are seen as very good. Management expects updates on product trials and potential outcomes by the first quarter of the next financial year (FY2027).
*   **Intermediate Business:** Management is still working on this area and will provide updates when the "right time comes," with market conditions expected to help establish a foothold.
*   **B2C Business (Nutrazyme):** This year's projected sales are between ₹1 crore to ₹1.5 crore, with significant results expected in the coming two to three years. The company is exploring various strategies including growing its own brands and potential partnerships.
*   **Industrial Enzymes:** The new R&D center will support entry into industrial enzyme spaces, requiring demonstration of performance and cost-effectiveness.
*   **European Business:** Revenue in Europe for Q3 FY2026 was INR 96 million, down from INR 105 million in Q3 FY2025, but management is working to re-align and grow product sales. EFSA registrations and novel food approvals are still pending, which could drive growth if materialized.
*   **JC Biotech:** A one-time, annual stores and spare consumption charge impacted Q3 FY2026 results, leading to a quarterly loss. The EBITDA margin for JC Biotech was positive.
