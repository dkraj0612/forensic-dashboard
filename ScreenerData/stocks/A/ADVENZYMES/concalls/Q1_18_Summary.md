## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies reported a strong Q1 FY19, with consolidated sales up 38% year-on-year to ₹1,043 million. EBITDA surged 64% to ₹487 million, leading to a 91% increase in net profit to ₹312 million. This robust performance was driven by broad-based growth across segments, including significant margin expansion in JC Bio due to the application of Advanced Enzyme's proprietary technology. The company is on track to achieve its annual guidance and is actively investing in R&D in the US to fuel future growth in areas like probiotics. Strategic acquisitions, such as Evoxx, are bolstering technological capabilities and market access. While the company faces product concentration risk with its key product Serratiopeptidase, it is mitigating this through cost competitiveness and continuous innovation. The palm oil extraction opportunity, a significant long-term prospect, is estimated at $450-$500 million, with commercial revenues expected from FY20.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Sales:** ₹1,043 million (up 38% YoY)
*   **EBITDA:** ₹487 million (up 64% YoY)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 47% (vs. 39% in Q1 FY18)
*   **Net Profit:** ₹312 million (up 91% YoY)
*   **Evoxx Revenue:** ₹30 million (Negative EBITDA of ₹25 million)
*   **JC Bio Revenue:** ₹125 million (EBITDA of ₹48 million)
*   **Top Account Contribution:** 16% of revenue (down from ~25% 2-3 years ago)
*   **Gross Debt:** ₹654 million (as of June 30)
*   **Cash on Balance Sheet:** ₹931 million (as of June 30)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Technological Advancement:** Leveraging its technology leadership to improve performance in acquired entities (e.g., JC Bio) and developing new applications.
*   **R&D Investment:** Significant planned R&D expansion in the US, focusing on probiotics and customer-oriented innovation for quicker results.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Evoxx acquisition provides access to directed evolution platforms, proprietary strains, bacterial engineering, and European market access.
*   **Market Diversification:** Reducing client concentration risk by growing non-top accounts, leading to a more diversified revenue mix.
*   **New Market Opportunities:** Exploring significant opportunities in palm oil extraction (estimated $450-$500 million market potential), with commercial revenues expected from FY20.
*   **Customized Solutions:** Business model focuses on developing customized enzyme solutions for business-oriented customers, driving growth through their success.
*   **Cost Competitiveness:** Maintaining a tight focus on cost structure, particularly for key products like Serratiopeptidase, to remain competitive.
