## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported a challenging Q2 FY2025, marking it as the lowest quarter of the fiscal year. Consolidated revenue declined 7% year-on-year to ₹1,461 million and 5% sequentially. EBITDA also saw a significant year-on-year and sequential drop of 17% to ₹424 million. Profit After Tax (PAT) decreased by 5% year-on-year to ₹334 million. The company attributed the downturn to several factors, including a higher revenue reversal of ₹83 million due to accounting criteria, a slowdown in export sales (excluding the US), and subdued EBITDA margins primarily caused by lower top-line performance. Despite these headwinds, management maintains confidence in the long-term business potential and anticipates a stronger second half of FY2025. The US business continues to be a bright spot, with year-to-date sales increasing by 24%.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Growth Outlook Shift:** Management has revised the full-year revenue growth guidance from mid-double digits (12-15%) to single digits. The second half of FY2025 is still expected to be stronger than the first half, but the overall annual target has been lowered.
*   **US Market Focus:** The US business remains a key growth driver, with a 24% year-to-date increase. Management is focusing on leveraging its branded ingredient names for finished consumer products to enhance customer-centricity and brand visibility.
*   **Product Development & Testing:** AETL is establishing a Level 3 independent testing facility for enzymes and probiotics to support US customers, thereby driving ingredient sales. The company is also developing new biocatalyst molecules and exploring agricultural offerings (biofertilizers, biopesticides).
*   **Addressing Competition:** Management acknowledges increased competition in specific areas like serratiopeptidase but states the molecule remains intact. The strategy is volume-driven, aiming to support customers through price volatility to gain market share.
*   **R&D Investment:** Significant resources are being allocated to R&D, with a new research center in Nashik planned to be operational by the end of next year. The goal is to expand R&D capabilities and introduce new products.
*   **Subsidiary Performance:** JC Biotech shows continuous improvement and profitability. SciTech is demonstrating a steady upward trend in sales and profitability. Evoxx continues to face challenges with negative EBITDA and PAT.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **FY2025 Revenue Growth Guidance:** The previous guidance of 12-15% growth has been revised down to "single-digit growth" for the full year. Management attributes this shift to delays in expected sales from biocatalysis and other areas, as well as the impact of the higher-than-anticipated revenue reversal. This indicates a significant deviation from the initial outlook.
*   **India vs. US Growth:** While management initially projected muted domestic sales for the year and stronger performance in the US, the overall company growth has been impacted more than anticipated. The US business is growing as expected, but the overall deceleration in India and other international markets has pulled down the consolidated guidance.
*   **Serratiopeptidase (Serra) Performance:** Management had guided for Serra sales to be down by 7-8% for the full year due to competition. The current quarter's performance and the expectation for H2 suggest this guidance is being met, with an expected full-year figure of INR115-120 Crores.
*   **EBITDA Growth:** The previously stated EBITDA growth being 2x-3x revenue growth appears to be challenged in the current quarter, with EBITDA declining at a steeper rate (17%) than revenue (7%) year-on-year. Management clarifies that this ratio holds true for incremental sales, but in a de-growth scenario, the impact is magnified.
*   **JC Biotech Normalization:** JC Biotech continues to show improvement and profitability, aligning with previous expectations.
*   **Evoxx Performance:** Evoxx continues to report negative EBITDA and PAT, not yet aligning with expectations of a turnaround. However, a significant customer-related R&D order is under negotiation.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D spend has increased both in absolute terms and as a percentage of revenue, aligning with stated intentions to bolster R&D capabilities.
