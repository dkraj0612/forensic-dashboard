## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported its Q2 FY2020 results, demonstrating steady revenue growth and a significant improvement in profitability. While revenue grew by 8% year-on-year to INR 1,114 million, EBITDA saw a stronger 12% increase to INR 473 million, and net profit rose by 23% to INR 318 million. This profit improvement was attributed to increased gross contribution from higher sales and reduced financial costs. The company also highlighted the strong performance of its subsidiary, EVOXX, which became EBITDA positive and achieved 59% sequential growth. Despite facing headwinds in the U.S. market and competitive pressures in India's human nutrition segment, AETL maintained its revenue growth guidance of 10% for the full year. The company anticipates a period of consolidation through FY2020, with expectations of better growth emerging in the latter half of FY2021.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Focus on Profitability & Consolidation:** While revenue growth is single-digit, the company is prioritizing bottom-line improvement through cost management and operational efficiencies. FY2020 is positioned as a year of consolidation to build capabilities for future growth.
*   **EVOXX Performance:** The subsidiary is a key growth driver, having achieved EBITDA positivity and strong sequential growth, indicating successful integration and operational improvements.
*   **Diversification & Market Focus:** AETL is actively diversifying its revenue streams to mitigate challenges in specific markets. Key growth areas for the next two years include probiotics, human nutrition, animal feed, and bio-catalysts.
*   **Geographic Focus:** India is expected to be the primary driver of better growth in the second half of the fiscal year.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** The company has significant underutilized capacity, with the potential to double revenue. The target is to achieve 100% capacity utilization within three years.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Revenue Growth:** AETL guided for 10-12% revenue growth for FY2020. The Q2 performance shows 8% YoY growth, leading to YTD growth of 7%. Management is maintaining the 10% revenue guidance despite challenges, indicating confidence in Q3 and Q4 performance.
*   **Profitability:** The significant uptick in the bottom-line (23% net profit growth in Q2) aligns with management's focus on improving profitability through operational efficiencies and cost control.
*   **EVOXX Performance:** Management's statement that EVOXX is "doing substantially well" and became EBITDA positive is validated by the reported 59% sequential growth and positive EBITDA contribution.
*   **Promoter Change Narrative:** Management clarified that the recent changes in the promoter group are due to an existing promoter exiting the company and another promoter subsequently resigning from management, while still holding a significant stake. This addresses a new investor's query regarding a perceived change in promotership.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Revenue Guidance:** Maintaining 10% revenue growth for FY2020.
*   **Margin Guidance:** Expectation to maintain current EBITDA margin range of 40-48% for FY2020, with potential for improvement as revenue grows. A year-over-year margin of approximately 43% is expected to be maintained.
*   **PAT Growth:** Possibility of 17% PAT growth in FY2020.
*   **Future Growth Drivers:** Probiotic, human nutrition, animal feed, and bio-catalyst segments are identified as key growth drivers.
*   **Capacity Utilization Target:** Targeting 100% capacity utilization within three years.
*   **Geographic Growth:** India expected to drive better growth in H2 FY2020.
*   **Future Growth Acceleration:** Expectation of better growth in the second half of FY2021, following a period of consolidation in FY2020.
*   **Investor Relations:** Management acknowledges past difficulties in reaching investor relations and commits to improving PR exercise going forward, with a specific commitment to be reachable next week.
