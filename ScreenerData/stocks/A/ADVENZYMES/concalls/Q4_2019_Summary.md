## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies (AETL) reported its Q4 FY19 and full-year FY19 results, highlighting a period of consolidation, strategic integration of subsidiaries, and progress in regulatory approvals. The company emphasized its commitment to growth without sacrificing margins and its focus on expanding its global presence. Key achievements include an uptick in US business, a decreasing reliance on top customers, and obtaining No Objection Letters from the US FDA for its GRAS filings, alongside numerous global registrations. The integration of Evoxx Technologies GmbH, acquired in 2017 for its technological expertise, has accelerated, aiming to leverage combined strengths for market innovation. For FY20, AETL anticipates continued double-digit organic growth and is actively exploring inorganic opportunities in probiotics, enzymes, and animal feed.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Focus:** Consolidating operations worldwide, achieving synergies, and becoming a leading player in enzymes and probiotics.
*   **Growth Drivers:**
    *   **Organic Growth:** Targeting low double-digit growth (10-13%) for FY20.
    *   **Inorganic Growth:** Actively exploring opportunities in probiotics, enzymes, and animal feed.
    *   **US Business:** Significant growth, with ex-top customer growth at 29% in USD terms.
    *   **Regulatory Approvals:** Achieved US FDA No Objection Letter for GRAS filings and filed numerous registrations globally (US, China, Europe, South America) for food and animal feed. These are expected to provide a competitive edge.
    *   **Evoxx Integration:** Accelerating integration to combine Evoxx's technological expertise with Indian R&D for market innovation. Evoxx aims for cash neutrality in FY20.
    *   **Green Energy/Eco-friendly Solutions:** Poised to capitalize on the global drive for green energy and eco-friendly solutions.
*   **Market Segmentation:** Primarily human healthcare (76%), animal nutrition (12%), and industrial bioprocessing (12%).
*   **R&D Investment:** ₹200 million spent on R&D (approx. 5% of topline), charged directly to P&L.
*   **Intellectual Property:** Increased patents filed, with one additional patent filed in the current fiscal year.
