## Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited - Q4 FY2026 Earnings Call - One-Page Executive Summary

**Date of Call:** May 12, 2026
**Period Covered:** Quarter ended March 31, 2026 (Q4 FY2026) and Full Year ended March 31, 2026 (FY2026)

---

### Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported its highest-ever quarterly and annual revenue for FY2026, demonstrating robust year-over-year growth across all divisions. Q4 FY2026 revenue reached INR 2,034 million (+22% YoY, +18% QoQ), and full-year revenue stood at INR 7,458 million (+17% YoY). EBITDA for Q4 FY2026 was INR 632 million (+39% YoY), and full-year EBITDA was INR 2,291 million (+18% YoY), with consistent EBITDA margins of 31%. Profit After Tax (PAT) for Q4 FY2026 surged by 69% YoY to INR 453 million, and full-year PAT grew by 30% to INR 1,736 million. Management attributed this strong performance to broad-based growth and effective navigation of global economic disruptions. The company continues to focus on innovation, regulatory advancements, and expanding its global market presence, with a new R&D center set to boost product development.

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Segmental Performance:** All business divisions, including Human Healthcare, Animal Healthcare, Bioprocessing, and Specialized Manufacturing, showed healthy growth in FY2026. Human Healthcare remains the largest segment (63% of revenue).
*   **Market & Regulatory Focus:** Filed two food enzyme dossiers with EFSA and three with US FDA. Anticipates approval for anti-inflammatory products in Europe this year, which could offer a significant market exclusivity.
*   **R&D and Innovation:** Investments in R&D are accelerating the innovation pipeline. The new R&D center in Nashik is on track for the latter half of FY2027, expected to triple product development capacity and include advanced technologies like AI.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Focus on expanding into new high-potential geographies, leveraging integrated end-to-end capabilities and customized solutions.
*   **Sustainability:** Continued investments in clean energy initiatives.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **FY2026 Performance:** The company delivered 17% revenue growth, exceeding the previously guided "mid-double-digit growth." EBITDA and PAT margins were in line with expectations, maintaining strong profitability.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** The 31% EBITDA margin for FY2026 was consistent with the previous year, demonstrating effective cost management despite global inflationary pressures.
*   **US Market:** Management acknowledged continued challenges in the US market, with revenue showing a decline in INR terms in Q4 FY2026 (-11% YoY). They are focusing on regulatory registrations and studies and expect "little growth" in FY2027 rather than negative growth.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D spend as a percentage of revenue remained stable at 5.3% for FY2026, consistent with the company's commitment to innovation.
*   **Segmental Performance:** The broad-based growth across all segments in FY2026 marks a positive shift from previous periods where some segments faced headwinds.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **FY2027 Growth:** Management anticipates "double-digit" overall growth in FY2027, with "little growth coming from the U.S." and healthy growth from the Indian market across all three segments (human nutraceutical, food, animal feed).
*   **EBITDA & PAT Margins:** Management expects to maintain similar margins as FY2026, targeting EBITDA margins around 30-31%.
*   **US Market (FY2027):** Expected to see "little growth" and not negative growth, with continued efforts to expand presence.
*   **Nashik R&D Center:** Expected to be fully operational in the latter half of FY2027, increasing capacity threefold. Incremental R&D expenses are projected at INR 50 million. Total R&D Capex is estimated at INR 130 crores, with INR 50 crores to be spent in FY2027.
*   **Serratiopeptidase:** Management has decided to stop sharing specific numbers due to competitive pressure but noted 45% YTD and 54% QoQ growth.
*   **Capital Allocation:** The Board is evaluating options for shareholder value enhancement, including buybacks. No interim dividend was declared.
*   **Emerging Areas:** Exploring opportunities in the broader protein category, including non-meat protein manufacturing via fermentation.
*   **Starya Labs:** Established independently, FY2027 will be its first year as a separate entity, with early positive responses but not yet significant numbers.
*   **Probiotics:** Management asserts the probiotic business is strong, with a strategic shift towards integrated solutions rather than commodity products.
