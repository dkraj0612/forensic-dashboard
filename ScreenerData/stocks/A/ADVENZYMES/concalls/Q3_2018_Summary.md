## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies (AETL) demonstrated a strong rebound in Q3 FY2018, with consolidated revenues surging by 73% year-on-year to ₹1,082 million, driven by robust performance across segments. EBITDA saw a significant increase of 136% YoY to ₹454 million, and net profit grew by 85% YoY to ₹265 million. The company reaffirmed its full-year revenue guidance of ₹380-400 Crores and EBITDA margin of 42%. Acquisitions, particularly Evoxx, continue to be a strategic focus, although Evoxx incurred a loss of ₹45 million in Q3 FY2018 due to its R&D investment orientation. The company is actively working to diversify its customer base to mitigate concentration risks.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenues (Q3 FY2018):** ₹1,082 million (up 73% YoY)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (Q3 FY2018):** ₹454 million (up 136% YoY)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q3 FY2018):** 42% (vs. 31% in Q3 FY2017)
*   **Consolidated Net Profit (Q3 FY2018):** ₹265 million (up 85% YoY)
*   **Nine-Month Revenues (FY2018):** ₹2,869 million (up 15% YoY)
*   **Nine-Month EBITDA (FY2018):** ₹1,166 million (down 2% YoY)
*   **Nine-Month EBITDA Margin (FY2018):** 41% (vs. 47% in FY2017)
*   **Nine-Month Net Profit (FY2018):** ₹652 million (down 8% YoY)
*   **Evoxx Loss (Q3 FY2018):** ₹45 million (primarily R&D investment)
*   **Serratiopeptidase Sales (Nine-Month FY2018):** ₹710 million
*   **R&D Expense (Q3 FY2018):** ₹25 million

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Acquisition Integration:** Continued focus on integrating Evoxx, which is being utilized as the R&D arm to strengthen technology platforms and expand product pipelines.
*   **Customer Diversification:** Proactive efforts to add more customers in the $5-10 million annual buying range to reduce reliance on a single large customer.
*   **Global Expansion:** Strategic focus on Europe, aiming for a contribution of 7-9% of overall revenue within two years, leveraging both organic and inorganic strategies.
*   **R&D Advancement:** Increased R&D investment is planned, with the R&D to sales ratio expected to grow from 3-4% to 5-6% of sales.
*   **New Application Areas:** Commercial revenues from palm oil processing in Malaysia are now expected in FY2020.
*   **Animal Healthcare:** Targeting geographical expansion and distribution network setup globally.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Q3 FY2018 Performance:** The company has demonstrated significant year-on-year growth in Q3 FY2018 for revenue, EBITDA, and net profit, largely exceeding the performance seen in Q3 FY2017. This indicates a strong recovery and execution in the quarter.
*   **Nine-Month FY2018 Performance:** While Q3 showed strong growth, the nine-month consolidated figures show a slight decline in EBITDA and net profit compared to the prior year. This suggests that the strong Q3 performance is crucial to meeting full-year targets and offsetting earlier headwinds.
*   **Evoxx Integration:** Management acknowledged that Evoxx is an R&D-focused acquisition and was expected to incur losses, which materialized in Q3. This is consistent with their prior communication about investing for long-term R&D capability.
*   **Top Customer Concentration:** Management reiterated the strategy to diversify by adding more mid-sized customers (above $5 million) to reduce the impact of any single customer's volatility, consistent with previous discussions.
*   **Palm Oil Commercialization:** The timeline for commercial revenues from palm oil in Malaysia has been pushed from FY2019 to FY2020, a slight delay from previous projections.

### **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **FY2018 Revenue Guidance:** Reaffirmed at ₹380-400 Crores.
*   **FY2018 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Maintained at approximately 42%.

*   **Europe Contribution:** Expected to contribute 7-9% of overall revenue within two years.
