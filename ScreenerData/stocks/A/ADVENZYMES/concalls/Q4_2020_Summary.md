## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported its Q4 FY2020 results, highlighting a robust full-year performance despite challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. For the full fiscal year, revenue grew by 6% to INR 4,440 million, while EBITDA saw an 11% increase to INR 2,023 million, achieving a 46% margin. Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by 15% to INR 1,330 million, representing a 30% margin. The company successfully reduced its dependency on its top customer, whose contribution fell to 9% of total revenue from 18% in FY2019. The German subsidiary, Evoxx, achieved standalone profitability for the first time in FY2020. Management acknowledged the difficulty in predicting FY2021 guidance due to the volatile COVID-19 situation but expressed confidence in achieving decent performance. The company confirmed its long-term goal of doubling revenue in 3-5 years, with a potential delay of 3-6 months due to the pandemic. R&D investments and capacity utilization remained consistent.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Consolidation and Focus:** Management reiterated a strategy of focusing on core strengths in three to four areas, moving away from a broad approach.
*   **Growth Verticals:** Continued focus on Human Nutrition, Animal Nutrition, and Bioprocessing (Food Processing). Biocatalyst and Probiotics are identified as key growth areas, with probiotics acting as a horizontal vertical across segments.
*   **De-risking Revenue:** Significant reduction in top customer dependency and an increase in the contribution of the top 10 customers, signifying a broader customer base.
*   **Geographic Diversification:** Growth in India (11% FY2020) and exports from India (45% growth) are compensating for the US market's top customer impact.
*   **Evoxx Integration:** Successful integration leading to standalone profitability, demonstrating successful M&A execution.
*   **R&D and Innovation:** Ongoing development of new enzymes (3-4 annually) and focus on application-based solutions. Plans for new R&D center deferred due to COVID-19.
*   **Eco-Friendly Solutions:** Continued strategy to replace traditional chemicals with enzymatic solutions.

*   **US Business Headwinds:** Acknowledged the significant impact of the top customer's decline, but highlighted that overall US business grew by 10% when this customer is excluded, demonstrating resilience and growth in other segments.
