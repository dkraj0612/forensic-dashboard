## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported its Q4 FY21 and full-year FY21 results, demonstrating resilience and continued growth despite the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic and the loss of a significant US customer. For FY21, revenue grew by 13% year-on-year to ₹ 5,018 million, driven by strong domestic performance and a robust US business that defied expectations. Profitability remained a key strength, with EBITDA margins at 46% and PAT margins at 30%, attributed to a favorable product mix. The acquisition of a 51% stake in SciTech Specialities Private Limited (SSPL) on January 11, 2021, contributed to inorganic growth and expanded the company's manufacturing and R&D capabilities. Management acknowledges the difficulty in providing precise guidance for FY22 due to the dynamic COVID-19 situation but remains optimistic for "decent growth."

*   **PAT Margin FY21:** 30%

*   **Segment Revenue Drivers (FY21):** Human Nutrition (77%), Animal Nutrition (9%), Industrial Bio-processing (14%).
*   **Geographical Revenue Split (FY21):** US business grew 12% despite customer loss ($5.37 million contribution lost). India business grew 20%.
*   **Subsidiary Performance (FY21):**
    *   Evoxx sales ₹ 282 mn (EBITDA ₹ 52 mn, PAT ₹ 13 mn)

    *   SSPL (partial year: Jan 11 - Mar 31, 2021) revenue ₹ 79 mn (EBITDA ₹ 22 mn, PAT ₹ 14 mn). Full year SSPL revenue ₹ 379 mn (EBITDA ₹ 93 mn, PAT ₹ 30 mn).
*   **Largest Enzyme (Anti-inflammatory) Revenue (FY21):** ₹ 1,134 mn (+10% Y-o-Y).
*   **Top 10 Customers Revenue Share (FY21):** 38% (vs. 35% in FY20).
*   **R&D Expense (FY21):** ₹ 179 mn (3.55% of revenue) (vs. ₹ 144 mn in FY20, 3.25% of revenue). Revenue expenditure: ₹ 125 mn. CAPEX: ₹ 54 mn.
*   **SSPL Acquisition:** 51% stake for ₹ 316 million, completed Jan 11, 2021. Contributed 1.8% inorganic growth. Added 2 manufacturing and 1 R&D facility.
*   **Depreciation & Amortization:** Increased by ~₹15 million due to SSPL addition (capitalized capex and identified intangible assets).
*   **Tax Impact:** Q4 FY21 saw higher tax due to MAT credit write-off (approx. ₹ 2.7 crore).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Core Focus:** Continued emphasis on Biocatalysis for API, Probiotics, and B2C Nutraceuticals. Expansion in the bakery solutions market in the US and Europe. Focus on Animal Nutrition globally.
*   **Market Expansion:** Aggressively pursuing B2C nutraceuticals in the US (web and Amazon) and planning for India and other industrial areas.
*   **Product Development:** Developing new delivery systems like effervescent tablets (via SSPL acquisition) for industrial applications.
*   **Growth Strategy:** Aiming for continued growth across key segments, leveraging acquisitions (like SSPL) and organic expansion.
*   **Capacity Management:** Current capacity utilization is around 55-60%. Management believes capacity can be increased through productivity improvements without immediate large-scale expansion.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Revenue Growth:** Management expressed optimism for FY21 and achieved the stated 13% Y-o-Y revenue growth. The 20% domestic growth and 12% US growth (despite a significant customer loss) demonstrate resilience and successful execution in key markets, validating management's confidence in their specialized business model.
*   **Profitability (EBITDA & PAT):** The reported EBITDA margin of 46% and PAT margin of 30% align with management's historical performance and their stated goal of maintaining high margins. The positive product mix was again cited as a driver for profitability.
*   **Margins:** Management's stated target EBITDA margin range of 40% to 48% was met, with FY21 EBITDA margins at 46%.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D expenses as a percentage of revenue increased slightly to 3.55% from 3.25%, showing a continued commitment to innovation. Revenue expenditure increased by ~10% YoY, while CAPEX nearly doubled.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Revenue Growth Outlook (FY22):** Management is **unable to provide specific guidance** for FY22 due to the dynamic COVID-19 situation. However, they expect "decent growth" and aim to remain "positive."
*   **Segment Growth Expectations (FY22):**
    *   **Biocatalyst:** Expected to see "decent growth."
    *   **Nutraceuticals:** Expected to see "decent growth," with positive impacts from increased awareness due to COVID-19. The nutraceutical industry is currently growing around 8.5%.
    *   **Probiotics:** Expected to play a "very good role" and contribute significantly to growth, particularly due to their immunity-boosting properties and positive trial results against COVID-19 related fatigue.
    *   **Animal Nutrition:** Expected to remain "under pressure" due to COVID-19 impacts (travel restrictions, logistics costs, impact on hospitality industry, raw material price hikes). Degrowth observed primarily in India.
    *   **Food Processing/Industrial Bio-processing:** Expected to be "under pressure" and potentially remain "neutral" for FY22, particularly due to COVID-19 impacting the food service sector and logistics challenges for export markets. Non-food bio-processing (textiles, detergents) saw a significant increase in US exports.
*   **SSPL Contribution (FY22 onwards):** Expected to contribute **10% to 12% of the total topline** going forward. Scope for margin improvement exists through integration and optimization.
*   **Margin Guidance:** Management maintains the target EBITDA margin range of **40% to 48%**. They anticipate a small impact (1-2%) from raw material price increases but believe it can be offset by capacity utilization and will wait to assess if price increases become a permanent feature before passing them on.
*   **R&D Investment (FY22):**
    *   **CAPEX:** Planned outlay of **₹ 100 million to ₹ 150 million**.
    *   **Revenue Expenditure:** Likely to remain in the **3.5% to 4% of revenue** range. Expansion plans for the Nashik R&D facility are resuming. Hiring has begun.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Current utilization is **55% to 60%**. Management believes this can be improved through productivity gains.
*   **SSPL Raw Material Sourcing:** Basic salt materials for SSPL are sourced indirectly from China, creating dependency on global supply chain cost increases.
*   **JC Biotech:** Management aims to make JC Biotech a **multi-product entity** beyond its current concentration on anti-inflammatory products, with improved capacity utilization.
*   **EFSA Registrations:** Timelines for the remaining **9 EFSA product registrations** are difficult to predict due to lengthy evaluation processes. Some questionnaires have been sent, and responses submitted.
