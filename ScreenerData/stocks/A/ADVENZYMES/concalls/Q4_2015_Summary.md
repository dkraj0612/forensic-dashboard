*   **R&D and Innovation:** Continued investment in R&D to develop new microbial systems, enhance fermentation and downstream processes, and create innovative enzyme solutions. Filing of GRAS Dossier with US FDA and Food Enzyme Dossiers with EFSA are highlighted.
*   **Market Expansion:** Focus on identifying product gaps and developing new enzyme segments, with a specific emphasis on the American and Asian markets.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Seeking acquisitions for key technologies, competencies, and skill sets to facilitate consolidation and market entry. The acquisition of JC Biotech is mentioned.
*   **Customer-Centric Solutions:** Providing customized enzyme solutions, enzyme blends, and enzyme products to diverse industries to optimize processes, improve product quality, and reduce costs for customers.
*   **Diversification:** Serving a broad range of industries, including Human Nutrition, Animal Nutrition, and Bio-Processing, with a diverse global customer base.
*   **Cost Competitiveness:** Emphasis on benchmarked Capex and Opex for R&D and manufacturing.
*   **Eco-Friendly Alternatives:** Promoting enzymatic solutions as sustainable replacements for traditional chemicals.
*   **Global Presence & Brand Building:** Actively participating in global trade shows to enhance corporate brand visibility and expand the international distributor network.
*   **Market Development:** Recognizing and capitalizing on the significant growth potential in the Indian enzyme market, alongside substantial opportunities in Asia and the Americas.
