## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported a challenging Q2 FY2023, with revenue growth slowing significantly and profitability metrics declining year-on-year, primarily due to persistent inflationary pressures on raw materials, power, fuel, and logistics. While sequential performance showed improvement, the year-on-year comparison revealed a contraction in both revenue and profitability. The company saw a mixed performance across its segments, with Human Nutrition facing softness due to inflation and Industrial Bio-Processing showing strong year-on-year growth, a reversal from previous trends. Strategic initiatives included an investment in Saiganesh Enzytech Solutions (SESL) for enhanced enzyme supply and an increased stake in JC Biotech. Management acknowledged the current quarter as the "worst quarter" for margin pressure and expects normalization to take a couple of quarters.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Strategic Investment:** Invested ₹60 million for a 50% stake in Saiganesh Enzytech Solutions (SESL) to secure intrastate supply of digestive enzymes and enhance offerings in human health, nutrition, and food industries.
*   **Subsidiary Stake Increase:** Acquired an additional 4.83% stake in JC Biotech, increasing ownership beyond the existing 85%.
*   **R&D Focus:** Increased R&D expenses to 6% of consolidated revenue, focusing on human nutrition, animal nutrition, new enzymes, biocatalysis, and animal feed formulations. Work on new biocatalyst products for API manufacturers is progressing.
*   **Market Strategy:** Management is selectively choosing its branded market in probiotics for long-term focus, while domestic probiotic markets remain soft. US market B2C sales are projected to be USD 6.5 million for FY23.
*   **Cost Management:** Management expects input cost and other operating expenses to normalize over the next two to three quarters.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Revenue Growth:** The H1 FY2023 revenue declined by 2% year-on-year, a significant deviation from the "at least positive" revenue growth anticipated for FY2022 in the previous earnings call. While Q2 FY2023 saw a 9% year-on-year increase, this was primarily driven by sequential improvement rather than a sustained upward trend from the prior year's comparable period.
*   **Profitability:** The EBITDA margin in Q2 FY2023 (29%) and H1 FY2023 (27%) represents a substantial decline from the 39% and 42% reported in Q2 FY2022 and H1 FY2022, respectively. This indicates that the impact of higher raw material and logistics costs, as well as other operating expenses, has been more persistent and severe than previously indicated. The management's previous expectation of maintaining EBITDA margins in the range of 42-48% for FY2022 has not been met, and the current year's margins are significantly depressed.
*   **Segment Performance:** While Industrial Bio-Processing showed strong YoY growth in Q2 FY2023 (+63%), the Human Nutrition segment saw a decline in H1 FY2023 (-4%), contrary to the positive outlook implied for that segment. The statement that "industrial segments do impact the margins definitely" and that "international sales particularly in US has impacted this year quite a bit" suggests a shift in the dominant margin-impacting factors compared to previous quarters where general cost inflation was the primary concern.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Margin Recovery:** Management aims to return to original EBITDA margins of between 40% and 48% over the next "two, three, four quarters."
*   **Normalization of Operating Costs:** Management expects input costs and other operating expenses to normalize over the "next two to three quarters."
*   **Biocatalyst Revenue Generation:** Expected to commence in 2-3 quarters (consistent with previous guidance).
*   **R&D Plant Construction:** Work to start in 1.5 to 2 months, with the first phase expected to be completed in approximately 1.5 years (consistent with previous guidance).
*   **SciTech Business Recovery:** Expected to recover in "another two quarters."
*   **Top 10 Customer Contribution:** Management expects stabilization of top customer relationships by the end of March or the first quarter of the next year (consistent with previous guidance).
*   **B2C US Sales Target (FY23):** Approximately USD 6.5 million.
*   **Lumpy Skin Disease Trials:** Trials are still ongoing, with no results yet.
