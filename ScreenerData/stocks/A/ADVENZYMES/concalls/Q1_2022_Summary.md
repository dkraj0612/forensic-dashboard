## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) demonstrated strong performance in Q1 FY2022, achieving robust year-on-year growth despite ongoing COVID-19 impacts. Revenue surged by 24% to ₹ 1,370 million, primarily driven by a significant 66% increase in the Industrial Bio-Processing segment. EBITDA grew by 21% to ₹ 628 million, and Profit After Tax (PAT) rose by 15% to ₹ 399 million. The company maintained healthy EBITDA margins at 46% and PAT margins at 29%. The newly integrated "Specialized Manufacturing" segment, stemming from the SciTech Specialties (SSPL) acquisition, contributed ₹ 134 million to revenue. International sales exhibited remarkable growth of 35%, accounting for 58% of total revenue, while domestic sales grew by 11%. The company is on the cusp of launching its B2C nutraceuticals platform in India.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **B2C Nutraceuticals:** The company is in advanced stages of launching its owned B2C nutraceutical online platform targeting Indian customers. This initiative is seen as a significant future growth driver, replicating the success seen in the US market under AST Enzymes and Dynamic Enzymes. The sales channels will include the company's own platform, other online channels, and potentially e-platforms like Amazon.
*   **Segment Focus & Growth:**
    *   **Human Nutrition:** Expected to remain constant for another quarter before picking up, impacted by reduced R&D focus in pharmaceutical companies on non-pandemic areas.
    *   **Animal Nutrition:** Exceptional performance driven by inflation in commodity prices (soya flour), with hopes for continued trends. However, international growth is hindered by travel restrictions.
    *   **Industrial Bio-Processing:** Overall segment expected to follow previous guidance. Food business saw strong growth (+101% Y-o-Y), while non-food business (detergents, textiles) was impacted by the pandemic.
    *   **Bio-Catalysis:** Focus on developing enzyme-based solutions for API manufacturers, aiming to reduce costs and chemical usage. Several products are in advanced trial stages.
    *   **Baking:** Expansion in Europe, Americas, and Rest of the World is targeted, offering value-based solutions.
*   **Product Development & Innovation:** Continued focus on developing new enzymes and probiotics for Human Nutrition (immunity, gut health), Animal Nutrition (efficacy, feed nutritional level), and Bio-Catalysis.
*   **Geographical Expansion:** Efforts to expand sales and marketing teams in the USA, MENA, and Asian markets for Animal Nutrition. Appointment of more distributors in overseas markets to strengthen geographical reach.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Continuously exploring inorganic expansion opportunities in Asia, Europe, and North America across various sizes, with a willingness to use debt financing if necessary. Acquisitions are viewed as a constant process.
*   **Specialized Manufacturing (SSPL):** The newly added segment, manufacturing effervescent technology products, contributes to nutraceutical, pharmaceutical, bakery, and other specialty applications, and will strengthen the B2C segment. SSPL contributed ₹ 134 million in Q1 FY2022, with ₹ 84 million from exports to European countries.
*   **Evoxx Technologies:** Efforts are ongoing to make this R&D company sustainable, which experienced negative EBITDA in Q1 FY2022 due to pandemic impacts on European activity, but has been EBITDA positive annually for the past two years.
