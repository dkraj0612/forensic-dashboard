## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies (AETL) reported a challenging Q1 FY2018 with consolidated revenues down 20% YoY to ₹755 million and consolidated EBITDA down 41% YoY to ₹299 million. Net profit also declined 41% YoY to ₹163 million. This decline was primarily attributed to a significant drop in revenue from a single top customer, which saw a YoY decrease from ₹375 million to ₹66 million in Q1 FY2018. Despite this, management expects this customer to recover and contribute ₹85-90 Crores for the full year, with an anticipated 10-15% growth in dollar terms. The company also highlighted the positive impact of the Evoxx acquisition, which strengthens its R&D capabilities in stain and microbial development and protein engineering. The full-year revenue guidance remains ₹380-400 Crores.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Revenues (Q1 FY2018):** ₹755 million (down 20% YoY)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (Q1 FY2018):** ₹299 million (down 41% YoY)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q1 FY2018):** 40% (vs. 54% in Q1 FY2017)
*   **Consolidated Net Profit (Q1 FY2018):** ₹163 million (down 41% YoY)
*   **Top Customer Revenue (Q1 FY2018):** ₹66 million (vs. ₹375 million in Q1 FY2017)
*   **JC Biotech Revenue (Q1 FY2018):** ₹93 million (new consolidation)
*   **Evoxx Acquisition Cost:** €7.65 million
*   **Full Year FY2018 Revenue Guidance:** ₹380-400 Crores

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Acquisition Integration:** Focus on integrating Evoxx for enhanced R&D (protein engineering, directed evolution) and JC Biotech for its production capabilities and market presence in India.
*   **Global Expansion:** Continued focus on international markets, leveraging Evoxx's European presence.
*   **R&D Advancement:** Strengthening R&D through Evoxx acquisition, aiming for expanded product portfolio and technological leadership.
*   **New Application Areas:** Pursuing opportunities in palm oil processing (Malaysia), biodiesel, and detergents, with initial revenue expected from FY2019 for palm oil and biodiesel, and FY2018 for detergents.
*   **Customer Diversification:** Efforts to increase multi-million dollar clients and reduce dependence on single large customers.
*   **Industry Vertical Focus:** Animal healthcare (domestic flattish, international growth), human healthcare, food (growing), and industrial processing (flat/declining).

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Q1 FY2018 Revenue:** The reported consolidated revenue of ₹755 million is significantly below the previous year's ₹941 million (a 20% YoY decline). This is a key deviation from the expected consistent growth, primarily driven by the substantial drop in revenue from the top customer.
*   **Q1 FY2018 Profitability:** EBITDA and Net Profit have seen a sharp decline of 41% YoY, directly impacting profitability margins (EBITDA margin down from 54% to 40%). This is a significant underperformance against the historical trend of strong profit growth seen in FY2017.
*   **Top Customer Performance:** Management's narrative consistently points to the top customer's buying cycle as the primary reason for the Q1 revenue slump. While they project recovery and full-year contribution, the magnitude of the QoQ and YoY decline for this specific customer is a stark miss for the quarter.
*   **JC Biotech Contribution:** The acquisition of JC Biotech added ₹93 million in revenue, which was not present in the previous year's comparable quarter. This inorganic growth partially offsets the decline in other segments.
*   **Evoxx Acquisition:** The acquisition of Evoxx is highlighted as a strategic move to bolster R&D, but its financial impact (revenue contribution) is not yet reflected in Q1 numbers as consolidation began mid-August.

### **New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **FY2018 Revenue Guidance:** Reaffirmed at ₹380 to ₹400 Crores, incorporating the Evoxx acquisition (partial year impact from August).
*   **Top Customer Outlook:** Expected to contribute ₹85 to ₹90 Crores for the full year, with a projected 10-15% growth in dollar terms. Buying cycle is expected to resume from Q2.
*   **JC Biotech Contribution:** ₹93 million revenue in Q1 FY2018, primarily contributing to human healthcare.
*   **Palm Oil Opportunity (Malaysia):**
    *   First patent holder for the technology.
    *   Malaysian subsidiary formed; trials conducted.

    *   Novozymes has also entered the market.
    *   Monetization expected from FY2019; revenue generation from FY2019. Initial clients expected to be added in FY2019.

*   **Detergent Enzymes:** Revenue expected in FY2018.
*   **R&D Expenses:** Expected to increase significantly due to Evoxx acquisition, potentially from ₹10 Crores to ₹30 Crores annually, though partially offset by Evoxx's existing revenue streams.
