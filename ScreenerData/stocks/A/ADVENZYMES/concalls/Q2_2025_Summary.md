## Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited - Q2 FY2026 Earnings Call - One-Page Executive Summary

**Date of Call:** November 13, 2025
**Period Covered:** Quarter ended September 30, 2025 (Q2 FY2026) and Half-Year ended September 30, 2025

---

### Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported strong financial performance for Q2 FY2026, demonstrating growth across all business segments. The company highlighted a significant YoY increase in revenue and EBITDA, driven by robust performance in Human Healthcare, Bioprocessing (specifically the food business), and Specialized Manufacturing. Management reiterated its commitment to R&D and anticipates continued growth, while acknowledging global economic uncertainties and the ongoing impact of US tariffs. The company is strategically expanding its international presence and product portfolio.

---

### Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue (Consolidated):**
    *   Q2 FY2026: ₹1,845 Million (+26% YoY, -1% QoQ)
    *   H1 FY2026: ₹3,704 Million (+32% YoY)
*   **EBITDA (Consolidated):**
    *   Q2 FY2026: ₹601 Million (+42% YoY, +6% QoQ)
    *   H1 FY2026: ₹1,165 Million (+25% YoY)
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Consolidated):**
    *   Q2 FY2026: ₹447 Million (+34% YoY, +11% QoQ)
    *   H1 FY2026: ₹852 Million (+25% YoY)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 33% (Q2 FY2026)
*   **PAT Margin:** 24% (Q2 FY2026)
*   **R&D Spend:**
    *   Q2 FY2026: 3% of revenue (Consolidated basis, excluding intercompany elimination)
    *   H1 FY2026: ~3% of revenue (Consolidated basis, excluding intercompany elimination)
*   **Top Ten Customers Contribution:** 27% (Q2 FY2026), 23% (H1 FY2026)
*   **B2C Segment:** $1.29 Million (Q2 FY2026)

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Segmental Growth:** Driving growth through strong performance in Human Healthcare (Pharma/API & Nutritional), Bioprocessing (Food business), and Specialized Manufacturing.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Focused efforts in Asia and Europe are yielding results, while US market growth is impacted by tariffs. Rest of World also shows positive growth.
*   **Product Development:** Continued investment in R&D, with new products and formulations in the pipeline across various segments, including probiotics and enzymes for specific applications.
*   **Biocatalysis:** Demonstrating traction with revenue growth, indicating ongoing development and commercialization efforts.
*   **Customer Relationships:** Expanding customer base and securing orders through long-term efforts and market presence.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Contributing to gross margin expansion, alongside product mix variations.
*   **Strategic Initiatives:** Exploring M&A opportunities, particularly in forward integration, animal feed, and probiotics for human nutrition.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **Q2 FY2026 Performance:** The reported Q2 FY2026 numbers (Revenue ₹1,845 Mn, EBITDA ₹601 Mn, PAT ₹447 Mn) significantly exceed the figures provided in the Q1 FY2026 historical summary for the *same* quarter (Revenue ₹1,680 Mn, EBITDA ₹400 Mn, PAT ₹220 Mn). This indicates a substantial revision in reported Q2 FY2026 figures within a short period, or a misinterpretation of the initial historical context's Q1 FY2026 figures. The Q2 FY2026 performance, however, aligns with the historical context's *projections* of strong growth.
*   **YoY Growth:** The reported +26% YoY revenue growth and +42% YoY EBITDA growth for Q2 FY2026 demonstrate strong execution and adherence to the previously anticipated growth trajectory.
*   **US Tariff Impact:** Management reiterated that US tariffs are impacting sales, particularly in the US market, leading to a slowdown in inquiries and affecting growth. The initial worst-case impact on EBITDA (₹10-11 Cr) and PAT (₹7-8 Cr) was confirmed as a potential scenario, with the company exploring mitigation strategies. The historical context's mention of US tariff impact is consistent with the current call's discussion.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D spend as a percentage of revenue remains consistent at approximately 3% consolidated, aligning with the historical emphasis on R&D.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Full-Year Growth Guidance:** Management intends to maintain the previously stated guidance of "mid-double digit growth" for the full fiscal year, despite global uncertainties and tariff impacts.
*   **H2 FY2026 Outlook:** Acknowledging uncertainties, management will wait for a clearer picture before providing more specific H2 guidance, but is working to achieve the full-year mid-teen growth target.
*   **EBITDA & PAT Margins:** Management expects to continue managing margins at current levels, with EBITDA margins between 30-32% and PAT margins of 21-22%, barring significant tariff impacts.
*   **US Market Strategy:** Exploring strategies to mitigate tariff impact, including potential price adjustments and other actions, with anticipation of price increases in the US market by next year.
*   **New R&D Center:** Expected to partially become active by the end of the current fiscal year (FY2026), with significant R&D expenses budgeted to increase by ₹10-12 crores in FY2027 compared to FY2026.
*   **Acquisition Targets:** Actively working on a few M&A opportunities, with interest in forward integration, animal feed, and probiotics for human nutrition.
*   **Biocatalysis Commercialization:** Expecting products to add to growth next year, with H1 FY2026 revenues at ₹128 Mn (vs. ₹104 Mn YoY).
*   **Pharma Intermediate:** Working on a few intermediates, with potential for further highlight on commercial orders in one to two quarters.
*   **Food Business Sustainability:** The food business revenue is considered more sustainable, not a one-time event, despite potential QoQ fluctuations.
*   **Asian Market Growth:** Driven by registrations and efforts over the past 1-2 years, materializing across animal feed and human nutrition segments.
*   **European Business:** Focus on food and nutraceutical areas, with pending EFSA registrations and novel food approvals that could drive significant growth if materialized in 6-8 months.
*   **High-Protein Food Enzymes:** Products are under development and customer testing phases for applications like lactase (neutral lactase under R&D) and proteases.
*   **B2C India Business:** Separated into a new company, aiming for INR 1-1.5 Cr revenue in FY2026.
*   **European Subsidiary:** Primarily R&D focused, but working on similar contracts with advanced stages, expecting growth if materialized in the next 1-2 years.
