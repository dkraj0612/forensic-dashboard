## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported its Q4 and full fiscal year FY2022 results, facing significant headwinds from raw material inflation, elevated energy prices, international freight escalation, and geopolitical conflicts. Revenue for Q4 FY2022 saw a marginal 1% year-on-year decline to ₹1,317 million, while full-year revenue grew by 6% to ₹5,294 million, partly driven by the full-year consolidation of SciTech. Profitability was significantly impacted, with Q4 EBITDA declining 27% year-on-year to ₹403 million and full-year EBITDA falling 13% to ₹2,014 million. This resulted in compressed EBITDA margins of 31% for Q4 and 38% for the full year, down from 38% and 46% respectively in the prior year. Management attributed the margin compression primarily to higher input costs, including raw materials, fuel, and logistics, as well as increased operating expenses such as consulting, CSR, and sales promotion. Despite these challenges, the company secured US NIH accreditation for systemic enzymes and probiotics, and saw clearance for two GRAS dossiers and two food enzyme dossiers from the European Food Authority.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **R&D & Regulatory Milestones:** Achieved US NIH accreditation for ImmunoSEB and ProbioSEB CSC3, and clearance for two GRAS dossiers (SEBtilis™ and SEBclausii™) and two European Food Authority food enzyme dossiers.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Doubled fermentation capacity at JC Biotech, with downstream capacity installation expected by early June. This expansion is aimed at substituting imports, particularly for animal feed enzymes requiring solvents.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Management noted increased customer interest for products due to ongoing geopolitical situations affecting competitors, particularly in the biocatalyst area.
*   **Product Development & Focus Areas:** Continuing to focus on human nutraceuticals, animal nutraceuticals, and food industries (baking and dairy). New product development can take 1-3 years for revenue generation.
*   **B2C Strategy:** Replicating the US market model in India, acknowledging a 3-4 year gestation period for market maturity.
*   **Cost Management & Margin Recovery:** Aiming to balance inflation through cost optimization on the raw material front and expecting prices to stabilize or decrease.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:

*   **Revenue Growth:** The full-year revenue growth of 6% for FY2022 was achieved, exceeding the previous year's performance. However, Q4 FY2022 revenue declined by 1% year-on-year, indicating continued pressure on top-line growth in the most recent quarter. The growth in FY2022 was significantly boosted by the full-year consolidation of SciTech, which had only an 84-day contribution in the previous fiscal year.
*   **Profitability:** The stated expectation for FY2022 was to maintain EBITDA margins. However, actual EBITDA margins for FY2022 (38%) and Q4 FY2022 (31%) saw a significant decline compared to FY2021 (46%) and Q4 FY2021 (38%). This indicates that the company was unable to fully offset the elevated input costs and increased operating expenses, contrary to prior expectations of managing and insulating margins. Management acknowledged "low gross margins" and "higher other expenses" as key drivers for the EBITDA decline.
*   **Segment Performance:** The significant decline in Probiotics revenue (from ₹551 mn to ₹172 mn) was a major detractor for FY2022 performance. Management attributed this to inventory pile-up at the customer end and loss of business for a key customer, which was a significant deviation from expected stability or growth in this segment. While Pharma (India) and Bio-Catalase showed growth, the overall impact of Probiotics was substantial.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Margin Recovery:** Management believes that as the business stabilizes and prices normalize, they should "come back to the margins." No specific timeline or target margin percentage was provided for the immediate future, but the expectation is for gradual improvement.
*   **Probiotics & Anti-inflammatory Segment Revival:** Management expects the situation in Probiotics to "become somewhat normal" over the next year, though not to the levels of two years prior. For anti-inflammatory products, registration processes in Europe may cause impact for another "year and two years," but efforts are ongoing to file necessary papers.
*   **Biocatalyst Area:** Prices are stabilizing and returning to normal, creating "lot of attraction" for this area. Management anticipates things will become "more better" within "one quarter or one and a half quarter."
*   **New Product Launch Revenue:** Products requiring solvents, particularly for animal feed areas, are expected to become available from "the second quarter of this year." Initial revenue additions from JC Biotech's expanded capacity are conservatively estimated at ₹5-10 Crores for FY2023, with potential for ₹15-20 Crores.
*   **R&D Centre:** Permissions for the new R&D Centre in Nasik are expected by the end of June, with construction spending potentially over five to six years.
*   **Double-Digit Revenue Growth:** Management hopes for "lower double digit growth" in FY2023, acknowledging uncertainty due to geopolitical situations and expecting more clarity by the next quarter.
*   **Five-Year Plan:** The pre-COVID five-year plan to double revenues is being re-evaluated. Management will regroup and rearrange the plan and inform stakeholders once finalized. They currently feel these goals should be achievable if normalization occurs more quickly.
