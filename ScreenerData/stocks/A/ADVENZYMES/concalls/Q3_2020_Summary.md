## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) presented its Q3 FY2020 results, showcasing continued top-line growth and improved profitability. Revenue increased by 10% year-on-year (YoY) to INR 1,119 million, driven by a favorable product mix and operational efficiencies. EBITDA grew by a significant 27% YoY to INR 529 million, resulting in a strong EBITDA margin of 47%. Net Profit After Tax (PAT) also saw a substantial increase of 37% YoY to INR 346 million. The company highlighted robust 15% growth in its India business and positive momentum from its German subsidiary, EVOXX, which achieved EBITDA positivity in the quarter. While the U.S. market faced headwinds from a major customer contraction, this was largely offset by growth in other customer segments. Management reiterated a strategy focused on consolidation and leveraging existing strengths, with a significant commitment to R&D for future growth. A slight adjustment was made to the full-year revenue guidance, while profitability guidance was maintained.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Consolidation and Focus:** Management emphasized FY2020 as a year of consolidation, focusing on strengthening existing market positions and applications.
*   **R&D Investment:** A significant commitment to R&D was highlighted, with plans for a new R&D center in Nasik over the next three years.
*   **Segmental Diversification:** The long-term vision (3-5 years) is to achieve a balanced revenue contribution (one-third each) from human nutrition, animal nutrition, and food processing segments, indicating a strategic push towards animal health and bioprocessing.
*   **EVOXX Growth:** The positive operational performance of EVOXX was presented as a key factor in the company's growth trajectory.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Manufacturing capacity is at approximately 50%, suggesting ample room for organic expansion.
*   **Eco-Friendly Solutions:** A core strategy remains replacing traditional chemicals with eco-friendly enzymatic solutions across various industries.
