## Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited - Q4 FY2025 Earnings Call - One-Page Executive Summary

**Date of Call:** May 14, 2025
**Period Covered:** Quarter ended March 31, 2025 (Q4 FY2025) and Full Year ended March 31, 2025 (FY2025)

---

### Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited (AETL) reported mixed results for Q4 FY2025 and the full fiscal year. While revenue saw a modest year-on-year increase in Q4 FY2025 and for the full year, EBITDA and Profit After Tax (PAT) experienced declines both sequentially and year-on-year for the quarter, and also year-on-year for the full fiscal year. Management attributed the EBITDA margin pressure to a change in product mix and year-end inventory adjustments. Despite these challenges, management expressed optimism for FY2026, anticipating mid-double-digit growth driven by ongoing traction in key markets and the potential emergence of new business segments. Significant progress was made on regulatory filings in Europe and the US, alongside patent applications and investments in renewable energy and R&D infrastructure.

---

    *   Q4 FY2025: 5.32% of revenue (excluding intercompany eliminations)

### Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Segmental Performance:**
    *   **Human Nutrition:** Revenue grew 1% YoY in Q4 FY2025, and declined 3% YoY for FY2025, primarily due to lower domestic sales, though US and international markets saw growth. It constituted 62% of Q4 FY2025 revenue and 64% of FY2025 revenue.
    *   **Animal Nutrition:** Strong performance with 13% YoY growth in Q4 FY2025 and 12% YoY growth for FY2025, contributing 12% of revenue.
    *   **Bio-Processing:** Grew 8% YoY in Q4 FY2025 but declined 13% QoQ. Food business grew 4% YoY to INR809 million (13% of revenue), and non-food business grew 12% YoY to INR208 million (3% of revenue) for FY2025.
    *   **Specialized Manufacturing:** Significant 39% YoY growth in Q4 FY2025 and 30% growth for FY2025, driven by full plant operations post-fire incident and added capacity. Contributed 9% to Q4 FY2025 revenue and 8% to FY2025 revenue.
*   **Market & Regulatory Focus:**
    *   Filed a food enzyme dossier with EFSA for baking and refined edible fats/oils.
    *   Filed 2 GRAS dossiers with US FDA for food processing enzymes in baking, brewing, starch processing, and oligosaccharide production.
    *   Filed 3 patent applications related to innovative sugar management technologies.
    *   Established Starya Labs in the US for testing enzyme and probiotic products.
*   **Sustainability:** Completed installation of a 350 kW solar power plant, increasing total clean energy capacity to 510 kW.
*   **R&D and Innovation:** Continuous focus on developing new products and molecules, particularly in biocatalyst and bioprocessing portfolios, to drive future revenue.

### Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Audit of Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance:**

*   **FY2025 Performance:** The full-year revenue growth of 2% was below previous expectations of mid-double-digit growth. FY2025 EBITDA and PAT saw a decline YoY, deviating from a consistent growth trajectory previously implied. The Q4 FY2025 EBITDA margin of 27% and PAT margin of 16% were significantly lower than historical trends and the prior year's performance, attributed by management to product mix and year-end inventory adjustments.
*   **US Market & Tariffs:** Management continues to navigate global economic challenges, including trade disputes. While previously noting a reduction in US tariffs, the current transcript does not explicitly mention tariffs as a major immediate headwind but acknowledges global volatility and potential surprises, particularly concerning US trade policies. The company's US facility's role (blending/final product) versus its reliance on India for fermentation raw materials means tariff impacts are nuanced.
*   **R&D Investment:** R&D spend as a percentage of revenue increased both quarterly (5.32% in Q4 FY2025 vs. 4.7% in Q4 FY2024) and annually (5.3% in FY2025 vs. 4.53% in FY2024), indicating sustained commitment to innovation.
*   **Segmental Performance Consistency:** While Animal Nutrition and Specialized Manufacturing showed strong growth, Human Nutrition saw a decline in FY2025, contrary to expectations for consistent growth across segments.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **FY2026 Growth:** Management expects a positive FY2026 with growth anticipated around "mid-double-digit numbers."
*   **EBITDA & PAT Margins:** For FY2026, management is projecting similar EBITDA margins (around 31%) and gross margins (around 76-77%) as FY2025. They are using current year numbers for calculation purposes.
*   **US Market Traction:** Good traction is expected in the US market, with growth primarily anticipated from existing businesses, while newer introductions will take more time to contribute significantly.
*   **New R&D Center:** Construction work is expected to finish by December 2025, with partial activation in the last quarter of FY2025-26.
*   **Intermediate Business:** Management is exploring an intermediate business segment, which could significantly boost the top line if successful.
*   **B2C Business (Wellfa):** Separating the B2C business into a subsidiary company to increase focus.
*   **Evoxx Business:** Expected to see a turnaround and reducing losses in FY2026, with an order already materialized and further discussions ongoing.
*   **Artificial Intelligence in R&D:** The company is exploring and plans to implement AI in R&D processes during FY2026.
*   **Biocatalyst Segment:** Expected to see good growth in FY2026, with management finding alternative solutions to previous cost challenges.
*   **Animal Feed:** Expected to perform well in FY2026, despite a low base.
*   **Food Business:** Expected to be normal in FY2026.
*   **Serratiopeptidase:** Expects a potential 2-5% decline in revenue from this product area in FY2026 due to competitive pressure.
*   **Specialized Manufacturing:** Expected to grow sustainably at over 20% in FY2026, driven by increased capacity and demand.
