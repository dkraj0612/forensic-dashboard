## Executive Overview

Advanced Enzyme Technologies (AETL) reported its nine-month results, showcasing a 15% increase in sales to ₹2453 million compared to ₹2133 million in the prior year. Consolidated EBITDA saw a significant 20.4% jump to ₹1180 million from ₹980 million, and profit grew by 35.1% to ₹70.85 Crores from ₹52.45 Crores. The company is strategically expanding into new avenues, including palm oil extraction, for which it secured an Indonesian patent, and is launching into the detergent enzymes market. Management addressed a dip in Q3 revenues, attributing it to a large US customer who overbought inventory in the first half of the year and consequently refrained from purchasing in Q3, resuming re-buying in the current quarter. This isolated incident skewed the quarterly numbers, but the nine-month performance remains robust. An Institutional Investor Day is scheduled for March 3, 2017, at their Indore factory.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **New Market Entry:** Launching into detergent enzymes and secured a patent for palm oil extraction in Indonesia, targeting expansion in palm oil producing countries.
*   **Customer-Centric Approach:** Emphasizing solutions and customized formulations over just product supply, especially for key clients.
*   **Product Development:** Continuously developing 4-5 new molecules annually through R&D.
*   **Acquisition Integration:** JC Biotech acquisition expected to boost profitability and sales in coming quarters, particularly in the pharma business.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** While the US remains a primary focus due to its mature market and high per capita GDP, the company is exploring opportunities in Indonesia, Malaysia, Africa, Middle East, and China.
*   **Margin Focus:** Management reiterates a commitment to maintaining mid-40s EBITDA margins, even for new ventures, prioritizing profitable growth.
