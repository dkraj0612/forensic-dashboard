## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) reported its FY25 full-year results, demonstrating robust growth driven by its incubated businesses and core infrastructure assets. The company's incubation model proved successful, with the emerging core infra businesses now surpassing AEL's consolidated FY23 results in income and EBITDA. Consolidated income grew by 2% to INR 1,00,365 crore, and consolidated EBITDA surged by 26% to INR 16,722 crore, with PBT increasing by 16% to INR 6,533 crore. A significant gain of INR 3,946 crore was realized from the 13.5% stake sale in Adani Wilmar.

The incubating portfolio showed remarkable growth, with income up 42% to INR 34,546 crore and EBITDA up 68% to INR 10,025 crore. The green hydrogen ecosystem, in particular, saw an EBITDA increase of 108% to INR 4,776 crore. Adani Airports delivered a strong performance with EBITDA growth of 43% to INR 3,480 crore. The mining services segment also reported substantial increases in dispatch volume (+40%) and EBITDA (+100%), with the Carmichael Mine operating near its rated capacity. AEL's commitment to ESG was further validated by an upgrade to an "A-" rating by CDP-CC, placing it in the "Leadership Category."

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Incubation Model Validation:** AEL's strategy of incubating large-scale utility and infrastructure assets, particularly in energy transition and logistics, continues to yield strong results, with incubating businesses becoming a significant contributor to overall financials.
*   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem Expansion:** Continued investment and capacity building in the green hydrogen ecosystem, including solar, wind, and electrolyzer manufacturing, remains a key focus.
*   **Airport Network Growth:** Expansion and operational enhancements in the airport business, including significant contributions from both Mumbai Airport (MIAL) and the broader AAHL ecosystem.
*   **Metals, Mining & Materials Focus:** Strengthening of the mining services business, growth in Integrated Resource Management (IRM), and the commissioning of the copper smelter are pivotal.
*   **Energy Transition Commitment:** Ongoing focus on renewable energy manufacturing and green hydrogen production aligns with India's energy transition goals.
*   **Strategic Capital Reallocation:** Proceeds from the Adani Wilmar stake sale are earmarked for reinvestment in core infrastructure, underscoring a strategic shift towards higher-return assets.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Target Achievement & Narrative Consistency Audit:**
    *   **Incubating Businesses Growth:** Management's narrative on the strength of incubating businesses is validated by their substantial growth, surpassing FY23 consolidated figures. The EBITDA growth of 68% for the incubating portfolio is a key highlight.
    *   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem:** The 108% EBITDA growth in this segment aligns with management's strategic focus and investment.
    *   **Airport Performance:** The 43% EBITDA growth in airports confirms continued momentum. Management acknowledged seasonality impacting Q4 EBITDA due to non-aero business, but confirmed all six PPP airports have achieved EBITDA break-even.
    *   **Mining Services & IRM:** Dispatch volume increase of 40% and EBITDA growth of 100% for mining services, alongside strong IRM performance, are consistent with prior positive commentary. The Carmichael Mine operating at capacity is also as expected.
    *   **Adani Wilmar Divestment:** The reported gain from the 13.5% stake sale is a tangible step in their capital allocation strategy.
    *   **ESG Performance:** The upgrade to "A-" by CDP-CC is a positive affirmation of AEL's ESG commitment, a consistent theme in their communications.
    *   **Copper Smelter Commissioning:** Management confirmed the copper smelter has been commissioned and provided a timeline for ramp-up, aligning with previous expectations.
    *   **Financial Reporting Clarity:** Management addressed concerns about elevated working capital and debt, attributing it primarily to inventory build-up in the copper segment and strategic asset deployment in airports and roads, which they presented as conservative accounting for emerging businesses.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance:**
    *   **Segmental Results Reporting:** AEL expects to formally report segmental results for the metals, materials, and mining business, likely starting in Q4 FY25, though it may not be material to AEL's overall financials.
    *   **Copper Smelter Ramp-up:** Ramp-up is expected over the next 180 days, with the full run rate anticipated around Q3 FY26. Ore sourcing is not an issue, and profitability is expected to remain within conservative estimates despite lower TCRC.
    *   **Airport EBITDA Breakup:** Approximately 45% of the INR 4,000+ crore EBITDA comes from Mumbai Airport (MIAL), with the rest from the AAHL ecosystem. A formal commentary on airport performance will be provided in the half-year results, with more granular reporting starting September 30th.
    *   **Wind Turbine Capacity:** Current capacity stands at 2.25 GW per annum. Production is expected to be around 450 sets (across 5.2, 3.0, and 3.3 MW) over the next three years. Management confirmed sufficient domestic capacity to meet the draft notification on domestic content.
    *   **Capex Guidance (FY26):** Approximately INR 36,000 crore, with major allocations to Green Hydrogen Ecosystem (~INR 5,500 crore), Airports (~INR 10,500 crore), Roads (~INR 6,200 crore), and PVC business (~INR 9,000 crore).
    *   **Mumbai Airport Tariff Order:** Expected by June 2025.
    *   **Navi Mumbai Airport:** Can commence operations under provisional tariffs. The total capitalized cost will be provided post-June 30th. Phase 2 will commence immediately post-stabilization of Phase 1, increasing capacity from 20 million to 60 million passengers.
    *   **Mining Services Volume (FY26-27):** Expected to be closer to 60 million tons over the next 18 months, based on current demand.
    *   **IRM Volumes:** Expected to remain flat.
    *   **Carmichael Mine:** Operating at rated capacity of 15 MMT per annum.
    *   **PVC Business Commissioning:** Timeline for commissioning remains FY28. Specific capex incurred to date will be provided in writing.
    *   **Mumbai Airport Terminal 1 Demolition:** Not a near-term event; will be planned post-stabilization of Navi Mumbai Airport operations and in consultation with airlines.
    *   **Mumbai Airport Traffic Potential:** Management anticipates latent demand in Mumbai to far exceed current numbers, potentially reaching over 100 million passengers in the long term.
    *   **Working Capital & Debt:** Elevated working capital is attributed to inventory build-up in the copper plant; this is expected to normalize as the ramp-up occurs. A significant portion of the debt is for airports and roads, which are operational or nearing operation.
    *   **Interest Expense:** FX volatility has a P&L impact but minimal cash flow impact.
    *   **Solar Export Uncertainty:** Management states they have visibility on sales due to contracts with key partners.
    *   **Solar Ecosystem Expansion:** The entire ecosystem aims for 10 GW capacity. Cell and module lines are expected by FY26 end to FY27.
    *   **Navi Mumbai Airport Phase 2:** Phase 2 will commence immediately post-stabilization of Phase 1, expanding capacity from 20 to 60 million passengers.
