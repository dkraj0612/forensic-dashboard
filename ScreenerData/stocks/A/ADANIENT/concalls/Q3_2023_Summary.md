## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) reported its Q3 FY2023 results, demonstrating resilience and progress despite market volatility. The company highlighted a significant contribution from its incubating businesses, which now account for over 33% of EBITDA, underscoring the success of its incubator model. While acknowledging market volatility, AEL reiterated its commitment to core infrastructure and utility projects, with a moderated approach to new capex commitments. The company crossed INR 1 lakh crore in total revenue and INR 6,000 crores in EBITDA for the first nine months of FY23.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Incubator Model:** Continues to be central, with incubating businesses (green hydrogen, airports, roads, data centers) now a significant contributor to EBITDA.
*   **Portfolio Diversification:** Focus on core infrastructure (energy and utilities, transport and logistics) while traditional businesses (mining services, trading) remain strong performers.
*   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem (ANIL):** Core to infrastructure plans; 2 GW cell and module line is operating, and a 5.2 MW onshore wind turbine is undergoing certification.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** Ongoing projects in airports (Navi Mumbai Airport completion on schedule), roads, and data centers are proceeding.
*   **Moderated Capex:** While core infra capex continues, new commitments will be deferred until market volatility subsides.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Target Achievement & Narrative Consistency Audit:

*   **Financial Performance:** The company achieved several financial milestones for the first nine months of FY23 (INR 1 lakh crore revenue, INR 6,000 crores EBITDA, INR 1,700 crores PAT), exceeding previous benchmarks and aligning with the narrative of robust growth. The increasing contribution from incubating businesses to EBITDA (over 33% in Q3 FY23) validates the incubator strategy.
*   **Incubating Businesses Progress:**
    *   **Green Hydrogen (ANIL):** The 2 GW cell and module line is operational, and the 5.2 MW wind turbine is progressing towards certification by June. This aligns with the previous commitment to developing the green hydrogen ecosystem.
    *   **Airports:** The significant increase in passenger movement (40%) and air traffic (21%) in Q3 FY23 is a strong indicator of recovery and growth in this segment, consistent with prior positive outlooks.
    *   **Roads:** Completion of 37 lane kilometers in Q3 FY23 shows continued execution on infrastructure development.
*   **Mining Services & Carmichael Mine:**
    *   Carmichael mine contributed INR 427 crores in EBITDA for Q3 FY23. Management plans to report it as a separate segment from next year, as it currently doesn't meet the 10% reporting threshold.
    *   MDO (Mine Developer and Operator) volumes for FY23 are now projected at around 30 million tons (down from a previous target of 40 million tons), and FY24 at 40 million tons (down from 50 million tons), attributed to delays in one or two mines. This represents a scaling down of targets.
    *   Commercial mining at the Dhirauli mine is still expected to start in FY24, consistent with prior guidance.
    *   MDO volumes were impacted by a one-time transition issue related to forest permissions for Parsa Kente, which has now been resolved, and Q4 FY23 is expected to be strong.
*   **Green Hydrogen Project (TotalEnergies Partnership):** Management clarified that the MoU with TotalEnergies is still signed and the due diligence work is continuing. They cautioned against interpreting media statements literally and reiterated that work on the new industries projects has been ongoing for two years. No revised commissioning schedule was provided, but the process is ongoing.
*   **Capex & Volatility:** Management explicitly stated a moderation in the pace of new capex commitments due to market volatility. Existing and committed projects (Navi Mumbai Airport, roads, data centers, Ganga Expressway) will continue as planned and are fully funded. This is a significant shift in guidance regarding the acceleration of new projects.
*   **Data Centers:** Scheduled projects will continue as planned. The Chennai facility is being developed into a 33 MW data center, with 17 MW currently operational.

### New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance:

*   **Adani New Industries (ANIL) / Green Hydrogen Ecosystem:**
    *   5.2 MW onshore wind turbine certification expected to complete by June.
    *   Commercial operations for wind turbine manufacturing to start shortly after testing concludes (expected by June/July this year).
    *   Wind turbine manufacturing capacity target maintained at 3 GW.
    *   Capex for the current year (FY23) will be within the range of $1-1.2 billion, and for next year (FY24) $2-2.5 billion, aiming for $50 billion by 2030. (Note: This range aligns with previous Q2 guidance but is reiterated in the context of moderated new commitments).
    *   Expected ROCE for the new energy business remains in the high teens to low twenties.
*   **Adani Airport Holdings:**
    *   Navi Mumbai Airport technical completion expected by December 2024, ready for commercial operations. Fully funded, construction continuing.
    *   EBITDA run-rate for airports is "just over INR 2,000 Crores" for the current year (FY23).
    *   Non-aero businesses (duty-free, parking, cargo, etc.) expected to contribute 20-25% of EBITDA by 2030-2031.
    *   City-side development (non-aero EBITDA contribution) expected to be 55-60% of airport EBITDA by 2030-2031.
    *   Aero business expected to contribute 10-15% of EBITDA by 2030-2031.
    *   Return on incremental investments in airports will comfortably exceed the cost of capital, potentially doubling that of other businesses.
    *   Revenue share provision for Mumbai International Airport commenced from October 1, 2022.
*   **Mining Services & Carmichael Mine:**
    *   MDO output target for FY2023 revised to closer to 30 million tons.
    *   MDO output target for FY2024 revised to 40 million tons.
    *   Carmichael mine is designed for 15 million tons per annum capacity.
    *   Annualized capacity from Carmichael is targeted to be reached by March or April.
    *   IRM targets are driven by market demand; foresee similar volumes as the current year (over 70 million tons) or slightly higher for FY24.
    *   Demand-supply gap in coal is expected to continue through CY'23.
    *   EBITDA per ton for IRM is around INR 540 and is considered sustainable, operating as a service business across the supply chain.
*   **Roads:**
    *   The Ganga Expressway project is proceeding as per schedule.
    *   No significant bidding expected for new road projects until the current volatility period settles.
    *   Total value of the Adani Road Portfolio is approximately INR 32,000 Crores.
*   **Data Centers:**
    *   Chennai facility to become a 33 MW data center; 17 MW currently operational.
    *   Scheduled projects will continue as planned.
*   **Copper Plant & Coal to PVC:**
    *   Copper plant construction is on schedule and will be completed as planned.
    *   Coal to PVC is considered a new commitment and will be reviewed post the settlement of the volatility period. No new commitments will be made for now.
*   **Solar:**
    *   2 GW solar cell and module capacity is operational.
    *   3.5 GW solar cell and module capacity expected by June.
    *   Current realization of $0.42/kilowatt is sustainable for existing contracts but will moderate to market levels for new contracts.
*   **Financials & Funding:**
    *   A formal plan for investment requirements over the coming 12 months will be presented with the March 31 results.
    *   New capex commitments will not be made until market volatility settles.
    *   Existing projects are fully funded and continuing as scheduled.
    *   Gross debt to EBITDA at AEL consolidated level is around 4.7x, with full numbers to be provided in the March quarter. Net debt to EBITDA at a portfolio level is just under 3.2x.
    *   The Navi Mumbai airport debt is included in the consolidated numbers.
    *   Sanctions for the Ganga Expressway project are complete, with conditions precedent to be met in the next two to three months.
