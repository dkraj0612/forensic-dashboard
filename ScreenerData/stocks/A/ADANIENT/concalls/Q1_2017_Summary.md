## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises (AEL) presented its Q1 FY17 results, showcasing resilience in its core businesses despite a softened commodity price environment. The company maintained its leadership in coal imports, significantly increasing volumes by 29.7% year-on-year to 24 million tons from 18.5 million tons. The Mine Developer and Operator (MDO) business emerged as a key growth driver, with dispatches more than doubling to 2.1 million tons from 1 million tons in the prior year's comparable quarter. AEL also made a notable debut in the renewable energy sector with its 648 MW solar project in Tamil Nadu, identified as the largest on Earth. Other businesses like city gas distribution and agri-business demonstrated robust volumes and margins, with the latter leveraging its brand for new product introductions under "Fortune." The company is strategically backward integrating its renewable energy operations with an upcoming 1.2 GW solar manufacturing facility for cells and modules, expected to be operational by the last quarter of FY17. AEL anticipates continued growth from its MDO, renewable, agri, and city gas segments.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Coal Imports Volume (Q1 FY17):** 24 million tons (Up 29.7% YoY from 18.5 million tons)
*   **MDO Coal Dispatched (Q1 FY17):** 2.1 million tons (Up 110% YoY from 1 million tons)
*   **Solar Project (Tamil Nadu):** 648 MW (Operational, billing commenced for 360 MW)
*   **Solar Manufacturing Facility:** 1.2 GW (Cells and modules, expected operational Q4 FY17)
*   **City Gas Distribution Volume (Q1 FY17):** 97 million standard cubic meters
*   **Agri Business Market Share:** 19%+
*   **Arbitration Award (Mining Contract):** Rs 195 crores (Favorable award recognized in Q1 FY17)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Coal Imports:** AEL continues to leverage its leadership position in coal imports, emphasizing that coastal and near-coastal power plants, along with cement and other industries, will continue to rely on imported coal due to freight cost advantages, despite government statements.
*   **MDO Business:** Identified as a significant growth driver, with strong volume increases reported.
*   **Renewable Energy:** AEL has made a strategic entry with its large-scale solar project in Tamil Nadu and has a pipeline of over 1600 MW across various states.
*   **Solar Manufacturing:** Backward integration is a key strategy with the development of a 1.2 GW solar cell and module manufacturing plant to support the renewable energy business.
*   **Agri Business:** Strengthening its position with increased storage capacity (75,000 metric tons for two new grain silo contracts) and brand expansion with new "Fortune" branded products.
*   **City Gas Distribution:** Maintaining robust volumes and margins.
*   **Financial Closure:** Completed for two new grain silo contracts (75,000 metric tons).
*   **Project Funding:** Solar manufacturing and renewable generation ventures are funded with a 70:30 debt-to-equity ratio, with AEL holding a 51% stake and promoters 49%.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Target Achievement:**
    *   **Coal Trading Volume:** Management achieved robust volume growth (29.7% YoY) in coal imports, exceeding the implied expectation of continued strong performance.
    *   **MDO Volume:** MDO dispatches more than doubled (110% YoY), significantly exceeding prior periods and indicating successful execution of growth plans in this segment.
    *   **Renewable Energy Debut:** The 648 MW solar project marks a significant step in entering the renewable energy sector, as previously indicated.
    *   **Solar Manufacturing:** Progressing as stated, with an expected operational start in Q4 FY17.
    *   **Agri Business:** Maintaining market share and expanding product lines, consistent with prior strategies.
    *   **EBITDA per Ton (Coal Trading):** Management acknowledged a significant reduction in EBITDA per ton to approximately 90 cents, down from previous levels (implied ~$1.5-$2 in prior context). While volumes are up, profitability per unit has decreased due to softening commodity prices.
*   **Narrative/Tone Shift Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   Management directly addressed the decline in EBITDA per ton for coal trading, attributing it to "price reduction" and "commodity prices have softened considerably."
    *   They continued to defend the import strategy for coal, citing freight cost advantages for coastal plants and reiterating the minister's statement that imported coal will remain viable as long as it offers cost benefits. This shows consistency in their market view.
    *   The narrative on coal imports remained analytical and defensive, aiming to justify the current business model.
*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance:**
    *   **Coal Trading Revival:** Management indicated a "sense of revival" setting in, suggesting that "things have bottomed out," but advised waiting for "one more quarter" for a definitive view.
    *   **Arbitration Award Continuation:** The favorable arbitration award of Rs 195 crores for the mining contract is considered largely a one-time event, though "some of it may continue if there is no offtake."
    *   **Renewable Energy Pipeline:** Details on the 1468 MW pipeline will be shared offline. Projects are typically completed within 12 months of award.
    *   **Indonesia Business:** Significant progress is expected with a "10% to 12% cost reduction" in the Indonesia business, though specific volume numbers for Q1 were not immediately available.
    *   **Related Party Funding:** Further details on changes in loans and advances from related parties will be available with September numbers.
