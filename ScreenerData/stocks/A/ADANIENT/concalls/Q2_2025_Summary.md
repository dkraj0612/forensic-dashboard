## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) presented its Q2 FY25 and H1 FY25 results, demonstrating robust growth driven by its emerging core infrastructure businesses. The company reported its highest ever H1 EBITDA of INR 8,654 crore, with incubating businesses contributing INR 5,233 crore, an impressive 85% year-on-year increase. Consolidated EBITDA grew by 47% to INR 8,654 crore, and consolidated profit before tax surged by 137% to INR 4,644 crore on a 14% increase in consolidated income to INR 49,263 crore. Key operational updates include progress in Adani New Industries' (ANIL) wind manufacturing and electrolyzer businesses, the Navi Mumbai airport's southern runway testing with expected completion early 2025, completion of two more road projects, and the addition of new iron ore and coal mine development agreements in the Mining Services portfolio.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Incubation Focus:** AEL continues its strategy of incubating large-scale infrastructure and new-age businesses, with a clear emphasis on emerging core infrastructure like green hydrogen, data centers, airports, and roads.
*   **Manufacturing Ecosystem Development:** Significant investment is being channeled into Adani New Industries (ANIL) to build a comprehensive manufacturing ecosystem for solar and wind energy, including electrolyzers.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:** The company is actively expanding its presence in airports, roads, and data centers, aiming to capitalize on India's growth trajectory.
*   **Mining Services Growth:** Expansion of Mining Services through new MDO contracts for iron ore and coal mines signifies a strong growth runway for this segment.
*   **Energy Transition Commitment:** ANIL's progress in wind manufacturing, electrolyzer capacity, and plans for green hydrogen generation underscore AEL's commitment to the energy transition.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Target Achievement & Narrative Consistency Audit:**
    *   **Incubating Businesses Contribution:** Management reiterated that incubating businesses are the primary growth drivers, with H1 FY25 EBITDA up 85% YoY to INR 5,233 crore and total income up 63% YoY to INR 17,287 crore, strongly aligning with the previous narrative of these businesses becoming the "emerging core infrastructure."
    *   **Asset Completion & Operationalization:** The Navi Mumbai Airport's southern runway testing and expected completion in early 2025 is a key milestone consistent with previous timelines. Two more road projects were completed, taking the total to six, and progress on the Ganga Expressway is reported as per schedule.
    *   **Airports Business Performance:** The company continues to handle a significant portion of India's air traffic (~23%) and added new routes, airlines, and flights, indicating continued growth momentum in the airport segment.
    *   **Mining Services (MDO) Growth:** The EBITDA for mining services grew by 65% YoY in Q2 FY25, supported by a 32% increase in dispatch volume and new contract wins, reinforcing the narrative of a long growth runway.
    *   **ANIL Progress:** Management confirmed the RLMM listing of wind turbines and applications for further certifications. The electrolyzer business has secured awards for manufacturing facilities, and the construction of the electrolyzer facility is progressing. The integrated manufacturing ecosystem for solar modules is nearing completion.
    *   **Trading Business Impact:** While not explicitly discussed in detail in this transcript, the focus has shifted heavily towards infrastructure and manufacturing segments, with less emphasis on the trading business's performance compared to previous periods.
