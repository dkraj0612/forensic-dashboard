## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) reported robust 9M FY25 results, demonstrating sustained growth and operational strength, particularly within its incubating businesses. Consolidated EBITDA saw a significant 29% year-over-year (YoY) increase to Rs. 12,377 crore, with incubating businesses contributing 62% of this total. Profit Before Tax (PBT) also rose by 21% YoY to Rs. 5,220 crore. The company highlights strong performance from the ANIL Ecosystem and Airports, underscoring the significant opportunities in their respective sectors. Gautam Adani, Chairman of the Adani Group, emphasized the company's focus on execution, operational excellence, innovation, and sustainability as key drivers of its core-plus portfolio's success and its commitment to creating long-term stakeholder value.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Total Income (9M FY25):** Rs. 72,763 crore (6% YoY growth)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (9M FY25):** Rs. 12,377 crore (29% YoY growth)

*   **Incubating Businesses EBITDA (9M FY25):** Rs. 7,674 crore (77% YoY growth)

*   **ANIL Ecosystem EBITDA (9M FY25):** Rs. 3,666 crore (77% YoY growth)

*   **WTG Supply (Sets) (9M FY25):** 1715.5 (6.5x growth, commercial production and supply started Q3 FY24)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Incubation Strategy Validation:** Emerging businesses, particularly the ANIL Ecosystem and Airports, continue to be the primary growth engines, contributing significantly to overall EBITDA.
*   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem (ANIL):** Strong solar module sales driven by export growth and domestic sales increase. Wind turbine manufacturing is progressing with four models listed in RLMM and the establishment of a Technology Design & Development Center in Germany.
*   **Data Centers (Adani Connex):** Phase I of the Hyderabad Data Center (9.6 MW) is operational. Noida and Pune data centers are progressing well. The goal is to build a 1 GW Data Center Platform by 2030.
*   **Airport Development (AAHL):** Navi Mumbai Airport is nearing operationalization after successful flight validation tests. Mumbai Airport achieved Level 5 Accreditation from ACI. The portfolio serves ~23% of the total passenger base and aims to leverage network effects.
*   **Roads Construction (ARTL):** A portfolio of 14 projects, with 6 operational and 8 under construction, is being developed.
*   **ESG Integration:** AEL demonstrates strong ESG performance, ranking among the top globally in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment and receiving various awards for sustainability and safety.
