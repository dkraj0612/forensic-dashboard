## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) announced a significant strategic restructuring: the demerger of its Renewable Power Business into a separate entity, Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL). This move aims to unlock shareholder value by creating a pure-play renewable energy Independent Power Producer (IPP). AGEL will be listed on BSE and NSE, with AEL shareholders receiving 761 AGEL shares for every 1,000 AEL shares held. The transaction is expected to conclude by Q1 FY2019. AGEL is poised to become India's largest listed renewable energy IPP with a fully funded portfolio of 2,148 MWAC (2.76 GWp). The company highlighted its strong execution capabilities, diversified portfolio, and long-term PPAs with sovereign off-takers. This demerger represents a pivotal step in AEL's strategy to focus on its core businesses while enabling its high-growth renewable segment to pursue dedicated capital and strategic development.

*   **AGEL Project Financing:** ~85% (2 GW) of renewable capacity has achieved financial closure.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Demerger for Value Unlocking:** To separate the renewable energy IPP business from AEL, removing any holding company discount and providing direct shareholder exposure to a high-growth segment.
*   **AGEL as a Pure-Play Renewable IPP:** AGEL will focus solely on renewable energy generation, aiming for stable cash flows and utilizing its flexibility in raising capital for growth.
*   **Scale and Market Leadership:** AGEL is positioned to be India's largest listed renewable energy IPP, leveraging Adani's execution capabilities.
*   **Execution Excellence:** Continued focus on proven project execution, in-house engineering, procurement, project management, and land acquisition.
*   **Strong Off-taker Relationships:** Long-term PPAs with sovereign entities like NTPC and SECI provide revenue visibility.
*   **Growth in India's Renewable Sector:** Capitalizing on India's significant renewable capacity addition drive, with solar PV expected to dominate.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** In-house O&M expertise aiming for industry-leading performance and cost efficiency.
*   **Supply Chain Resilience:** Strategic relationships with tier-1 global module and inverter suppliers.
*   **Expansion of Kamuthi Solar Plant:** The 648 MW Kamuthi, TN project is highlighted as the world's largest solar PV plant, completed in a record time.
