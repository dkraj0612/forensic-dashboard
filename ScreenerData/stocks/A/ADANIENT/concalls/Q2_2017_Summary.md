## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises (AEL) reported its Q2 FY17 results, showing continued growth across several key business segments. The company highlighted robust performance in its half-yearly mining and city gas distribution volumes, with a notable 48% and 5% increase respectively. Coal trading volumes also saw an increase of approximately 10% year-on-year for the half-year period. The renewable energy sector, although not yet a separately reported segment, is rapidly becoming a significant growth engine, with a combined 723 MW of solar projects (648 MW in Tamil Nadu and 75 MW in Punjab) now operational. AEL anticipates that renewable energy will soon become its largest business. The company is also nearing completion of its 1.2 GW solar manufacturing facility, expected to commence operations by the end of the calendar year or early next. The MDO (Mine Developer and Operator) business is projected to be a major growth driver, potentially reaching 100 million tons of peak capacity. AEL maintains its market leadership in edible oils and is expanding its agri-storage capacity. The company's debt-to-equity ratio remains healthy at 0.5:1, with a long-term debt of Rs. 8,000 crores and a net worth of Rs. 13,000 crores.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Renewable Energy as Next Growth Engine:** AEL reiterates its expectation that renewable energy, particularly solar generation, will become its largest business.
*   **Generation-Led Growth in Renewables:** Generation is identified as the primary driver of the renewable energy business, with manufacturing serving as a supporting strategy.
*   **Solar Manufacturing Expansion:** The 1.2 GW solar manufacturing facility (cells and modules) is nearing completion, with potential for 50-70% growth over five years. The company is exploring further backward integration opportunities.
*   **MDO Business Growth:** Projected to be a significant growth driver, with peak operational capacity potentially reaching close to 100 million tons.
*   **Coal Trading as Mature Business:** Remains an important contributor, with a focus on efficiency and cost management despite commodity price cycles. AEL believes dependence on imported coal for coastal and proximal plants will remain high.
*   **City Gas Distribution Expansion:** Continues to grow, supported by recent JV awards for projects in multiple cities.
*   **Agri-Storage Expansion:** Progressing well on new projects, increasing overall storage capacity.
*   **Market Penetration:** AEL expects to see growth in both overall market for coal imports and its own market share.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Target Achievement:**
    *   **Renewable Energy Operational Capacity:** Achieved 723 MW operational solar capacity, aligning with their stated progress. The 648 MW Tamil Nadu project is operational, and billing has commenced.
    *   **Solar Manufacturing Facility:** Nearing completion as stated in the previous quarter's outlook, with expected commencement of rolling out manufacturing by end of calendar year or beginning of next.
    *   **MDO Business:** Projected to be a "biggest growth driver," with management indicating a potential of "close to 100 million tons and more" in terms of operations over five years, reinforcing its importance.
    *   **Coal Trading Volumes:** H1 FY17 volumes showed ~10% growth YoY, which is strong but slightly less than the 29.7% growth in Q1 FY17. Management considers this as representative of overall coal imports into India, with both market growth and market share gains at play.
    *   **EBITDA per Ton (Coal Trading):** Management reported maintaining EBITDA per ton at "about $1.4 per ton," which is a decrease from the prior year's $2.25-$2.50 but shows stability compared to the previously mentioned "approximately 90 cents" in Q1 FY17. This suggests a partial recovery or stabilization.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   **Coal Trading Outlook:** Management moved from the Q1 FY17 sentiment of a "sense of revival" and "things have bottomed out" to describing coal trading as a "mature business" with its own commodity cycle challenges, which has "turned positive now." This indicates a more measured and less optimistic tone than the previous quarter, while still maintaining a positive outlook on volumes.
    *   **Renewable Energy Tariff Renegotiation:** Management directly addressed concerns about tariff renegotiations in renewable projects. They stated they are "not aware of anything having been negotiated down in Gujarat" for solar and clarified that Gujarat Utility's desire to negotiate solar tariffs was not allowed by the state regulator. They also differentiated this from wind projects where some states have refused to sign PPAs, indicating that while isolated issues exist, it's not a widespread regulatory challenge that clouds business decisions.
    *   **Tamil Nadu Solar Project Scheduling:** Management acknowledged stabilization in grid integration for the 648 MW Tamil Nadu project, stating it's "pretty good" but prefers to see a 3-6 month track record before drawing firm conclusions.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance:**
    *   **Renewable Energy Pipeline:** Total pipeline, including current operational assets, is approaching 2100 MW, encompassing about 100 MW of wind and 1250 MW of additional solar.
    *   **MDO Business Peak Capacity:** Potential peak operational capacity close to 100 million tons over five years.
    *   **Solar Manufacturing Facility Growth:** The 1.2 GW facility can "easily grow 50% to 70%" over five years.
    *   **Solar Manufacturing Integration:** Exploring opportunities to go "even further backwards" from cell and module level, though this is in the exploratory stage.
    *   **Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) Projects:** Identified as a "huge opportunity" for the solar manufacturing business.
    *   **Export Market for Solar:** Good prospects in the export market due to restrictions faced by Chinese players in Europe and US.
    *   **Tamil Nadu Solar Project (360 MW tariff):** The first 360 MW is confirmed at Rs. 7/unit, with the remaining capacity still in discussion with the regulator.
    *   **Coal Trading EBITDA per Ton:** Maintained at "about $1.4 per ton" for H1 FY17.
    *   **Indonesia Business Cost Reduction:** Specific volume numbers for Q1 were not available in the prior summary, but the current transcript mentions "significant progress is expected with a '10% to 12% cost reduction' in the Indonesia business," though this was stated in the context of the Q1 historical summary. No new update was provided in the Q2 transcript.
