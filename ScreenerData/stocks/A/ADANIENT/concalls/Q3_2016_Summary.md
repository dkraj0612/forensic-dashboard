## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises reported its Q3 FY16 results, highlighting a marginal decline in consolidated income to over Rs 33,000 crores for the nine months ended December 31, 2015, compared to Rs 35,700 crores in the prior comparable period. EBITDA saw a lesser decline of less than 5%, while Profit After Tax (PAT) decreased by 21% to Rs 874 crores from Rs 1110 crores. This performance was primarily attributed to the softening global commodity cycle impacting coal realizations, despite strong volume growth in coal mining (76% YoY for nine months to 3.7 million tons), coal trading (6% to 59 million tons), and agri-business (7%). Management indicated robust growth in the MDO (Mine Developer and Operator) business and other ventures like city gas distribution. The company is progressing on its 648 MW solar power project in Tamil Nadu and has a pipeline of 250 MW in other states. New agro storage capacity of 3 lakh tons has also commenced operations. Management anticipates renewable energy contribution in the coming quarters.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated Income (9M FY16):** > Rs 33,000 crores (Marginally down on comparable basis)
*   **EBITDA (9M FY16):** Less than 5% down (on comparable basis)
*   **PAT (9M FY16):** Rs 874 crores (Down 21% from Rs 1110 crores)
*   **Coal Mining Volume (9M FY16):** 3.7 million tons (Up 76% YoY)
*   **Coal Trading Volume (9M FY16):** ~59 million tons (Up 6% YoY)
*   **Agri Business Volume (9M FY16):** Up ~7% YoY
*   **City Gas Distribution Volume (9M FY16):** Nearly same levels as last year

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Coal Business:** Management believes that despite government statements on reducing coal imports, economic viability will continue to favor imports for coastal plants and those within a 500-600 km hinterland due to freight costs. They expect their business model to benefit from this continued import demand.
*   **MDO Business:** Identified as a key growth driver with robust performance in the first nine months.
*   **Renewable Energy:** Progressing on a 648 MW solar project in Tamil Nadu and a pipeline of 250 MW across other states. Expects contribution from this segment in upcoming quarters.
*   **Agri Business:** Addition of 3 lakh tons of agro storage capacity in Madhya Pradesh strengthens the agri-infrastructure business.
*   **Hedging Strategies:** Management highlighted well-placed hedging strategies for FOREX and procurement pricing to mitigate margin impact from volatility.
*   **CAPEX Plans (Next 12 Months):**
    *   Tamil Nadu Solar Project: ~Rs 4500 crores (of which ~Rs 3200 crores is debt).
    *   Next 250 MW Solar: Rs 17 billion to Rs 18 billion (at Rs 6-6.5 crores per MW), funded 70:30 debt:equity.
    *   Solar Manufacturing: 1.2 GW, Rs 1920 crores, funded 70:30 debt:equity, eligible for 20% central government subsidy.
    *   Wind Business: Building a small pipeline, but no ready numbers available.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Target Achievement:**
    *   **Coal Mining Volume:** Achieved robust volume growth (76% YoY), exceeding implied expectations for volume growth.
    *   **Coal Trading Volume:** Achieved moderate volume growth (6% YoY).
    *   **Agri Business Volume:** Achieved volume growth (~7% YoY).
    *   **Consolidated Income:** Marginally down on a comparable basis, missing implied growth targets if a flat or positive trend was expected.
    *   **EBITDA:** Down less than 5% on a comparable basis, showing resilience but still a decline.
    *   **PAT:** Down 21%, indicating a significant miss against any implied profit growth targets.
*   **Narrative/Tone Shift Regarding Headwinds:** Management directly addressed the impact of the "global commodity cycle softening" on "realization and consequently the profit per ton of coal" as the primary reason for EBITDA decline. They actively defended their coal import strategy, arguing for its continued economic viability despite potential shifts in government policy, indicating a consistent belief in their existing business model. The tone regarding coal imports was not evasive but rather defensive and analytical, presenting arguments for its continued relevance.
*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance:**
    *   **EBITDA per Ton:** Management suggests focusing on current profitability ($1.5 - $2 per ton) and potentially slightly improved margins, acknowledging it as a "new number to focus on" in the absence of other benchmarks.
    *   **Coal Imports:** Reaffirmed continued import demand for coastal and near-coastal hinterland plants over the next 12 months.
    *   **Renewable Energy Contribution:** Expects renewable business to be added "hopefully in the next quarter onwards, maybe some part of it in this quarter."
    *   **Australia Coal Project:** Decision on Australia coal project remains contingent on coal prices.
    *   **CAPEX:** Provided specific figures for solar projects and manufacturing for the next 12 months.
