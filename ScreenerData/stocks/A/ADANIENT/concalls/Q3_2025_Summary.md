## Executive Overview

Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) reported its Q3 FY25 results, showcasing strong performance primarily driven by its incubating businesses. The nine-month period ending December 2024 saw significant EBITDA growth, with the incubating segment contributing INR 7,674 crore, up 27% YoY. Adani New Industries (ANIL) saw a substantial 121% increase in EBITDA to INR 3,666 crore, with module sales reaching a 1 GW per quarter run rate. Adani Airports' EBITDA grew by 43% to INR 2,527 crore, supported by a 7% increase in passenger volume to 69.7 million. Consolidated income rose by 6% to INR 72,763 crore, and consolidated EBITDA increased by 29% to INR 12,377 crore, nearly matching the full-year FY24 EBITDA. A significant non-cash MTM adjustment related to the Australian mining business's shareholder loan led to a reported finance cost of INR 2,141 crore, with INR 750-770 crore being non-cash. The company also announced progress on the Adani Wilmar transaction, with a 13.5% stake reduction and plans for further divestment, expected to generate INR 14,200 crore in post-tax equity proceeds to reinvest in core infra businesses. AEL also highlighted its improved ESG performance, achieving a sector-leading S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment score of 63.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Incubation & Core Infrastructure Focus:** AEL continues to focus on incubating large-scale infrastructure and new-age businesses, particularly in energy and utility, transport and logistics, and primary industries. The emerging core infra businesses (ANIL, data centers, airports, roads) are highlighted as key growth drivers.
*   **Green Hydrogen Ecosystem (ANIL):** Significant investment and progress in building a comprehensive manufacturing ecosystem for solar, wind, and green hydrogen, including electrolyzers and wind turbine manufacturing.
*   **Airport Expansion:** Continued growth in airport passenger volumes and operational enhancements, with a focus on expanding capacity and services.
*   **Mining Services & IRM:** Leveraging its MDO model for coal and iron ore blocks, and strengthening its position in Integrated Resource Management (IRM) through digital platforms and diversified customer base.
*   **Energy Transition:** AEL remains committed to the energy transition through its investments in ANIL's green hydrogen and renewable manufacturing capabilities.
*   **Strategic Capital Allocation:** Proceeds from the Adani Wilmar stake sale are earmarked for reinvestment in core infrastructure businesses, signaling a strategic shift towards higher-return assets.

*   **Target Achievement & Narrative Consistency Audit:**
    *   **Incubating Businesses Growth:** Management consistently emphasized the strong performance of incubating businesses, which delivered robust EBITDA growth (27% YoY for 9M FY25). The narrative remains focused on these businesses becoming the "emerging core infrastructure."
    *   **ANIL Progress:** The reported EBITDA growth of 121% for ANIL and module sales at a 1 GW quarterly run rate aligns with management's strategy of building a comprehensive manufacturing ecosystem. The listing of additional wind turbine models also reinforces this.
    *   **Airport Performance:** The 43% EBITDA growth in Adani Airports and a 7% increase in passenger volume confirm continued momentum, aligning with prior commentary on airport expansion. Navi Mumbai Airport's commercial flight validation testing is a key milestone as per previous guidance.

    *   **Adani Wilmar Divestment:** The progress on the Adani Wilmar transaction, including stake reduction and proceeds earmarked for reinvestment, aligns with the strategic capital allocation plan. The expected INR 14,200 crore proceeds are positioned as a significant enabler for future infra investments.
    *   **ESG Performance:** The significant improvement in S&P Global ESG score to 63, placing AEL among the top global companies, is a positive affirmation of their ESG commitment.
    *   **Financial Reporting Clarity:** Management proactively addressed potential confusion regarding the non-cash MTM adjustment on the Australian mining business shareholder loan, providing clarity on actual cash interest costs and highlighting the disclosure on their presentation.
