## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) continues to reinforce its position as India's largest private thermal power producer, as evidenced by its Q1 FY 2024-25 earnings presentation. The company highlights outstanding financial performance driven by operational excellence and sound capital management, achieving significant EBITDA growth through higher dispatches and improved margins due to moderating import fuel prices. APL emphasizes its strategic advantages, enabling above-par capacity utilization and realizing the upside potential of growing demand by leveraging high plant uptimes and fuel management expertise. The company's robust growth trajectory is supported by a strategy focused on capacity expansion, operational efficiency, and a strong financial profile, as demonstrated by continued deleveraging and a healthy credit rating. APL's investment case is underpinned by a growing power demand, a high proportion of contracted capacity with assured fuel cost recovery, and a proven track record in project execution and asset turnaround.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **EBITDA:** Not explicitly stated as a consolidated figure for Q1 FY25, but growth is highlighted due to higher dispatches and improved margins from moderation in import fuel prices. "Continuing EBITDA (Adjusted for one-time income)" shows a 21% increase in the presented pie chart, while the "Revenue and EBITDA Bridge" indicates growth in "Continuing EBITDA" from Q1 FY24 to Q1 FY25 driven by "Change in volumes and tariffs" and "Change in fuel cost."
*   **Leverage:** Significant deleveraging continues. Net Debt / Continuing EBITDA is reported at **1.22x** as of June 30, 2024, a substantial improvement from 4.62x in March 2023. Senior Term Debt / Equity Ratio is also strong, though the specific Q1 FY25 figure is not explicitly stated in numerical form, the narrative emphasizes the "deleveraging of balance sheet and stronger net worth."
*   **Revenue Visibility:** **85%** of capacity is contracted under Long-Term/Medium-Term PPAs, of which **91%** has assured fuel cost recovery.
*   **Credit Rating:** Domestic credit rating of APL remains strong at **"AA-"**.
*   **Commercial Availability:** Consistently high performance on dispatch availability ensures full entitlement to capacity charges under PPAs.
*   **Regulatory Income:** Lower one-time income recognition is noted in Q1 FY25, attributed to "almost full resolution of regulatory matters."

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Power Generation:** Continues as India's largest private thermal power producer with an ultra-modern fleet and a strong growth pipeline. Current operating capacity is 15.25 GW, with 1.60 GW under construction. The total planned capacity reaches **22.08 GW**.
*   **Logistics:** Maintains embedded logistics for sourcing and disposal of fuel and fly ash, a key competitive advantage.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Aggressive expansion plans include **22,080 MW** of organic capacity (including 10,880 MW ongoing projects and 6,400 MW under construction/development) and **8,590 MW** of inorganic capacity (including 4,800 MW brownfield expansion, 3,120 MW ongoing acquisitions, and 1,100 MW under evaluation).
*   **Fuel Security & Cost Recovery:** **85%** of domestic fuel requirements are secured under LT/MT contracts. **91%** of contracted capacity has assured fuel cost recovery.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Thermal power is positioned as essential for India's growing base load demand, projected to grow from 445 GW to 900 GW by FY32. This necessitates significant coal-based capacity addition.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Leverages cutting-edge technologies, digitalization, and analytics for Reliability Centered Maintenance, ensuring full capacity charge recovery and maximizing dispatch.
*   **Project Execution:** Proven project management capabilities are highlighted, including advance ordering of equipment and pragmatic scope definitions to manage execution bottlenecks.
*   **New Project Development:** APL has initiated development of a 1600 MW Ultra-supercritical thermal power project (Phase-II expansion at Raipur, Chhattisgarh) and a greenfield 1600 MW Ultra-supercritical thermal power project at Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh.
*   **Subsidiary Amalgamation:** MEL (a subsidiary of APL) has filed for amalgamation with SMRPL (a subsidiary of AEL) which holds rights to the Dhirauli coal mine, securing a critical fuel source.
