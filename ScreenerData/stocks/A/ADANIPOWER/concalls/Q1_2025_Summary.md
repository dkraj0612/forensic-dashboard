## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) reported a robust Q1 FY25 performance, marked by significant year-on-year growth in PLF (to 78%) and power dispatch (to 26 billion units). This strong operational showing was driven by a vibrant power market, improved fuel cost management, and high plant uptimes capitalizing on peak demand. The company highlighted the substantial reduction in one-time revenue recognition from prior period items, allowing for a clearer view of ongoing business performance. Continuing EBITDA saw a sharp 53% increase, supported by a pragmatic approach to cash flow and leverage that reduced finance costs. Profit Before Tax nearly doubled on a recurring basis. APL is actively pursuing capacity expansion, with several organic and inorganic projects underway or in evaluation, positioning itself to meet the projected surge in India's power demand. The company reiterated its commitment to a balanced strategy of long-term PPAs for stability and a portion of capacity for merchant sales to optimize returns.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Organic Growth:** Mahan Energen Phase II (1600 MW) on track for June 2027. Advanced preparation for three new 1600 MW projects (Raipur, Raigarh, Mirzapur). Total project pipeline projected to reach 30.67 GW by FY29-30, with BTG equipment ordering for 9.6 GW brownfield and 1.6 GW greenfield projects.
    *   **Inorganic Growth:** Awaiting NCLT approval for Lanco Amarkantak (1800 MW) and Coastal Energen (1320 MW). KSK Mahanadi is under bidding. Evaluating further brownfield expansion and inorganic opportunities (>1,000 MW).
    *   **PPA Strategy:** Targeting 25-year PPAs for new capacities. States like Maharashtra (1600 MW), UP (1600 MW), and Rajasthan (3200 MW) have invited thermal power bids. Expectation of significant bid opportunities due to increasing resource adequacy obligations.
*   **Merchant Strategy:** Maintaining approximately 20% of capacity for merchant sales to capitalize on market opportunities and optimize returns, while tying up 80% under long-term PPAs for stable revenue.
*   **Fuel Security & Cost Management:** Improved fuel cost management, with lower import coal prices contributing to higher merchant contributions. Emphasis on domestic coal availability and in-house logistics.
*   **Operational Excellence:** High PLF (78%) achieved through O&M excellence and capitalizing on peak demand opportunities. Commissioning of the second 800 MW unit at Godda contributed to higher volumes.
*   **Deleveraging & Financial Strength:** Prudent management of cash flows and leverage has led to reduced finance costs. Credit ratings have improved (implied by mention of "AA family" and previous reports).
*   **ESG & Innovation:** Exploring co-firing of green ammonia with coal at Mundra site as a pilot study to reduce carbon footprint.
