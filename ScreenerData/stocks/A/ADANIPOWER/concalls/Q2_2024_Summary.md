## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) presented its Q2 FY25 results, showcasing strong operational and financial performance driven by increased dispatch volumes, capacity additions, and effective fuel cost management. The company highlighted its strategic advantages, including robust PPA coverage, fuel security, and operational efficiencies, positioning it to capitalize on India's growing power demand. Key financial highlights include revenue and EBITDA growth, with a continued focus on deleveraging its balance sheet. The presentation also reiterated APL's commitment to ESG principles and strong corporate governance.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Operating Capacity:** 17.55 GW (operating assets)
*   **Growth Pipeline:** 13.12 GW (targeting 30.67 GW total capacity)
*   **Contracted Capacity:** 83% of capacity under LT/MT PPAs, with 92% having assured fuel cost recovery.
*   **Fuel Security:** 88% of domestic fuel requirements secured under LT/MT contracts.
*   **Revenue Growth:** 9.1% (QoQ Continuing Revenue) driven by volume growth (21%) and capacity addition, partially offset by lower tariff realization.
*   **EBITDA Growth:** Strong growth in Continuing EBITDA driven by higher volumes, lower fuel cost per unit, and expense control.
*   **Profitability:** Continuing PBT growth due to higher EBITDA and controlled finance costs.
*   **Balance Sheet:** Outstanding UPS reduced to ₹4,643 Crore. Net Senior Debt to Continuing EBITDA at 1.40x (Sep-24). Senior Term Debt to Equity at 0.56x (Sep-24).
*   **Credit Rating:** Improved to "AA".

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting a total capacity of 30.67 GW, with 13.12 GW in the growth pipeline (including ongoing projects, brownfield opportunities, and acquisitions).
*   **Fuel Security & Management:** Leveraging in-house logistics and securing fuel contracts to ensure competitive cost advantage.
*   **PPA & Revenue Visibility:** High PPA coverage provides stable revenue streams, with a focus on remunerative long-term tie-ups and opportunistic merchant power.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Utilizing advanced systems like ENOC for optimized capacity utilization and dispatch.
*   **Acquisitions & Turnarounds:** Demonstrated success in acquiring and integrating stressed assets, such as CEPL and LAPL.
*   **ESG Commitment:** Focus on sustainability, including reducing emission intensity, water management, and waste utilization. Recognized for sustainability efforts.
*   **Corporate Governance:** Emphasis on independent board oversight, management ownership, and robust review processes for related-party transactions.
