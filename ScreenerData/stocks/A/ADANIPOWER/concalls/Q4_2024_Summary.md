## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) has presented an updated investor presentation, highlighting its strategic positioning for growth and strong operational and financial performance. The company continues to leverage India's rising power demand, emphasizing its robust platform with stable profitability, substantial contracted capacity, and a growing pipeline. APL is focused on capacity augmentation, enhancing fuel security, and maintaining operational excellence. The presentation underscores the company's deleveraging efforts, improved creditworthiness, and strong liquidity, positioning it to capitalize on future growth opportunities. ESG initiatives remain a focus, with progress reported on environmental compliance and sustainability.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Continuing EBITDA (TTM):** INR 20,792 crores (as per previous summary, likely from FY24)
*   **Continuing EBITDA (Q3 FY25):** Not explicitly stated, but growth in Profit Before Tax (PBT) by 26.4% YoY driven by one-time income.
*   **Net Debt / Equity Ratio (9MFY25):** 1.33x (down from 1.41x in FY24 and 4.62x in FY23)
*   **Net Senior Debt to Continuing EBITDA (Dec '25 TTM):** Projected to be 1.33x (an improvement from prior periods).
*   **Total Debt (Dec 2024):** INR 64,331 crores (includes senior secured loans, under-construction project debt, and other unsecured loans).
*   **Ash Utilization (FY24):** 89%
*   **Water Intensity (FY24):** 2.35 m3/MWh (33% lower than statutory limit for Hinterland plants).

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting a total capacity of 30.67 GW, with 17.55 GW operating assets and a 13.12 GW further growth pipeline. This includes:
    *   **Organic Growth:** Significant pipeline with 1,600 MW under construction and development initiated for further expansions.
    *   **Inorganic Growth:** Operational control over acquired assets (Vidarbha Industries Power Ltd. - 600 MW, acquired Sept 2024 for Rs. 4,101 Crore; another asset acquired Sept 2024 for Rs. 3,336 Crore). Continues to evaluate other opportunities.
*   **Fuel Security & Cost Management:** 91% of domestic fuel requirements secured under long-term/medium-term contracts. Enhancement of fuel availability and cost control through commercial mining licenses. Expects fuel costs to stabilize around $90-$100 per ton for imported coal.
*   **PPA & Revenue Visibility:** 87% of capacity contracted under Long Term (LT) / Medium Term (MT) PPAs. 92% of contracted capacity has assured fuel cost recovery. Strong demand for dispatchable capacity from thermal power plants. Merchant power demand remains high.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Maintaining optimum availability (consistently more than 90%) and dispatch capability. Leveraging ENOC (Energy Network Operation Center) for remote monitoring, diagnosis, and centralized power scheduling. Focus on ultra-supercritical/supercritical technologies for efficiency and emission reduction.
*   **Deleveraging:** Significant reduction in senior term debt through prepayment and regular repayments. Improvement in credit rating to AA in 2024 from BB- in 2018. Strong credit profile with high liquidity.
*   **ESG Focus:** 7 out of 9 operating locations certified SUP Free. Aims for 100% certification by FY25. Targets reduction in GHG emission intensity to 0.84 tCO2e/MWh by FY25. Maintained B Score from CDP for Climate Change and Water Security.
