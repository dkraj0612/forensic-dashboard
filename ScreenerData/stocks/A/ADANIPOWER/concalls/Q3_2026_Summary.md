## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) demonstrated resilience in Q3 FY26, navigating a softer demand environment influenced by extended monsoons and cooler temperatures, which also impacted merchant prices. Despite these headwinds, the company achieved stable operational performance, evidenced by a slight increase in power sales year-on-year due to higher operational capacity, particularly from the recently operationalized 600 MW Butibori plant. Revenue saw a marginal year-on-year decline, primarily driven by lower power selling rates in the merchant market and reduced energy charges linked to lower imported coal prices. However, improved EBITDA contribution from acquired assets and effective cost control cushioned the impact, resulting in a slight YoY decrease in EBITDA but a YoY improvement in Profit Before Tax due to lower finance costs. The company secured a significant 3,200 MW PPA in Assam and continues to focus on increasing its contracted capacity to over 90%, thereby reducing exposure to market volatility. APL also successfully raised INR 7,500 crores through AA-rated NCDs to support expansion and working capital, maintaining its AA stable credit rating. Project execution for its substantial thermal expansion program is progressing as per schedule, with phased commissioning expected from FY27 onwards.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Operational Resilience:** Maintained stable power sales despite weaker demand and lower PLF (62.6%) due to extended monsoons and cooler temperatures. Operational capacity increased due to the Vidarbha acquisition and the Butibori plant becoming fully operational.
*   **Capacity Expansion & PPA Strategy:** Secured a 3,200 MW PPA in Assam for a greenfield project. Increased contracted capacity to over 90% of operating fleet (18.15 GW), reducing merchant exposure to 10%. New PPAs offer higher capacity charges, contributing to better per MW EBITDA.
*   **Revenue & Cost Management:** Revenue impacted by lower merchant prices and reduced energy charges from lower imported coal. Fuel costs decreased YoY due to lower coal prices. Operating expenses increased due to operationalizing acquired assets, overhauling expenses, and higher CSR spend.
*   **Financial Prudence:** Raised INR 7,500 crores via AA-rated NCDs for expansion and working capital. Maintained a strong credit rating (AA Stable). Debt increased due to bridge financing but leverage remains comfortable, supported by strong cash generation.
*   **Long-Term Outlook:** Continued confidence in India's power demand growth. Focus on securing long-term PPAs for new capacities to ensure stable earnings and visibility. Expectation of returning strong power demand in the ensuring year.
