## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) reported strong Q3 FY24 results, driven by significant growth in PLF and power sales volumes, complemented by favorable fuel prices and resolved regulatory issues. The company demonstrated robust financial performance with substantial increases in recurring revenue and EBITDA. APL continues its aggressive deleveraging strategy and provides updates on its substantial capacity expansion pipeline, including ongoing greenfield/brownfield projects and strategic acquisitions. The management emphasized operational efficiency through digital transformation and reiterated its commitment to sustainable operations and corporate governance.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Consolidated PLF:** 68.6% (Q3 FY24) vs. 42.1% (Q3 FY23)
*   **Consolidated Power Sales Volume:** 21.5 billion units (Q3 FY24) vs. 11.8 billion units (Q3 FY23)
*   **Year-to-Date (9M FY24) PLF:** 62.4% vs. 46.6% (9M FY23)
*   **Year-to-Date (9M FY24) Power Sales Volume:** 57.1 billion units vs. 39.1 billion units (9M FY23)
*   **Continuing Consolidated Revenue (Q3 FY24):** INR 13,405 crores (72% YoY growth)
*   **Continuing Consolidated Revenue (9M FY24):** INR 37,173 crores (40% YoY growth)
*   **Continuing Recurring EBITDA (Q3 FY24):** INR 5,059 crores (242% YoY growth)
*   **Continuing Recurring EBITDA (9M FY24):** INR 13,516 crores (118% YoY growth)
*   **Reported EBITDA (Q3 FY24):** INR 5,009 crores (151% YoY growth)
*   **Reported EBITDA (9M FY24):** INR 22,743 crores (92% YoY growth)
*   **Finance Cost (Q3 FY24):** INR 797 crores (16% YoY reduction)
*   **Continuing Profit Before Tax (PBT) (Q3 FY24):** INR 3,261 crores vs. Loss of INR 305 crores (Q3 FY23)
*   **Continuing Profit Before Tax (PBT) (9M FY24):** INR 8,006 crores vs. INR 1,136 crores (9M FY23)
*   **Reported Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q3 FY24):** INR 2,738 crores vs. INR 9 crores (Q3 FY23)
*   **Reported PAT (9M FY24):** INR 18,092 crores vs. INR 5,484 crores (9M FY23)
*   **Perpetual Securities Outstanding (as of Q3 FY24):** INR 7,435 crores (reduced from INR 9,180 crores in Q2 FY24)
*   **Total Debt Reduction (Q3 FY24):** INR 809 crores (senior debt) + INR 2,000 crores (perpetual including yield)
*   **Senior Debt per Megawatt:** INR 1.65 crores (approx.)

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 21,150 MW from current 15,250 MW, with a planned addition of approximately 6,000 MW, comprising greenfield, brownfield, and potential acquisitions.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Implementing digital transformation and analytics through an Analytics Center of Excellence to identify over 90 projects for efficiency, predictability, and safety.
*   **Fuel Security & Cost Management:** Benefiting from lower imported fuel prices and effective management of domestic coal sourcing (mix of imports and e-auctions for Godda). Merchant capacity benefits from proximity to coal belts.
*   **PPA & Revenue Visibility:** High PLFs achieved across PPA-based and merchant capacities. Godda plant is firmly established in the Bangladesh power mix.
*   **Acquisitions & Turnarounds:** Mahan Power plant successfully turned around post-acquisition and repaid its sustainable debt. Moving forward with the acquisition of Coastal Energen.
*   **Deleveraging:** Consistent use of surplus cash flows for debt reduction, including prepayment of senior debt and perpetual securities.
*   **Responsible & Sustainable Operations:** Adhering to statutory limits for water consumption and CO2 emissions, with the 9th ESG report published following GRI standards.
