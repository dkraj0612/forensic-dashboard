## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) presented its Q4 FY25 earnings, highlighting strong operational performance driven by increased power demand and strategic capacity utilization. The company emphasized its robust core profitability and sound capital management. While the transcript notes tempered revenue growth due to lower average tariff realization (influenced by reduced import fuel prices and merchant tariffs), EBITDA performance remained strong, supported by volume growth. APL showcased its deleveraged balance sheet and robust cash flow generation, deploying surplus to fund capacity expansion. The company also received significant credit rating upgrades and highlighted improved ESG performance.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Q4 FY25 Continuing Revenue:** Not explicitly stated, but revenue growth was tempered by lower average tariff realization.
*   **Q4 FY25 EBITDA:** Not explicitly stated, but quarterly Continuing EBITDA was affected by lower merchant contribution and higher operating costs of acquisitions.
*   **FY25 Continuing Revenue:** INR 56,473 Cr (+11% YoY).
*   **FY25 Continuing EBITDA:** INR 21,575 Cr.
*   **FY25 Net Debt to Continuing EBITDA:** 1.44x (Mar '25).
*   **FY25 Water Intensity:** 2.21 m³/MWh (36% below statutory limit).
*   **FY25 Ash Utilization:** 102%.
*   **FY25 Average Emission Intensity:** 0.85 tCO2e/MWh.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Operational Excellence & Capacity Utilization:** Achieved strong dispatch availability, leading to full capacity charge recovery under PPAs. Udupi plant achieved 100% O&M availability in Q4 FY25, and Raigarh plant achieved 99% in Q4 FY25.
*   **Revenue & Volume Growth:** Driven by higher operating capacity and growing power demand. However, tempered by lower average tariff realization due to reduced import fuel prices and merchant tariffs affected by cold weather.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions & Integrations:** Adani Power (Jharkhand) Ltd. amalgamated with APL on April 25, 2025. Resolution Plan for Vidarbha Industries Power Ltd. (VIPL), a 600 MW plant, approved; Letter of Intent received.
*   **Financial Discipline & Deleveraging:** Deleveraged balance sheet with increasing long-term borrowings for acquisitions. Net Debt to Continuing EBITDA improved to 1.44x. Robust cash flow generation deployed in capacity expansion. Redemption of Unsecured Perpetual Securities.
*   **Credit Profile Enhancement:** Significant credit rating upgrades received from CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, and India Ratings, with AA/Stable ratings assigned to facilities.
*   **ESG Performance:** Achieved S&P Global CSA score of 67 (above industry average). Maintained B score from CDP for Climate Change and Water Security. 7 out of 9 operating locations certified SUP Free. Zero health and safety-related injuries reported. Reduced GHG emission intensity to 0.84 tCO2e/MWh by FY25.

The previous summary also noted ongoing discussions regarding outstanding dues from the Bangladesh power plant.
