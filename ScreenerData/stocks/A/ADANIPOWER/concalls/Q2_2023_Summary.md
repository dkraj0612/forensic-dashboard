## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Power Generation:** Focus on being India's largest private thermal power producer with a strong growth pipeline and an ultra-modern fleet.
*   **Logistics:** Leveraging in-house, mine-to-plant logistics capability for efficient fuel and fly ash management, handling approximately 60 MMTPA of coal and 13 MMTPA of fly ash.
*   **Development:** Emphasis on high-quality manpower development and knowledge dissemination, alongside critical spares and vendor development.
*   **Waste Management:** Creating value from waste products and strengthening the local industrial base.
*   **Technology & Efficiency:** Utilization of ultra-supercritical and supercritical technologies for high efficiency and lower emissions, with a significant portion of capacity equipped with Flue Gas Desulphurisers (FGDs).
*   **Capacity Expansion:** A clear path to 21.15 GW from the current 16.85 GW of operating and upcoming capacity.
*   **Fuel Security & Cost Recovery:** Majority of domestic fuel requirements tied up under long-term/medium-term Fuel Supply Agreements (FSAs). 74% of capacity ensures margin protection through fuel cost pass-through mechanisms in Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs).
*   **Market Dynamics:** Capitalizing on India's accelerating power demand and the role of the thermal fleet in underpinning renewable energy penetration.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Employing the Energy Network Operating Centre (ENOC) for remote monitoring, diagnosis, and troubleshooting, enhancing plant uptime and optimizing capacity utilization through data analytics and predictive maintenance.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Demonstrating success in turning around stressed assets through strategic acquisitions and effective operational management.
*   **Transnational Power Supply:** Successfully commissioned the 1600 MW Godda Ultra-Supercritical power project, supporting Bangladesh's energy needs.
