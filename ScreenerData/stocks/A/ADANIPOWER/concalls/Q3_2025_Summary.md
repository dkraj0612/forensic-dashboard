## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) presented a highly optimistic outlook for its role in India's robust economic growth, positioning itself as a market leader within a multi-decade infrastructure super-cycle. The company underscored India's strong GDP growth, massive electricity demand projected to reach ~1,000 GW by FY32, and the critical need for 80 GW of additional coal-based capacity, which APL is strategically addressing. APL reiterated its aggressive capacity expansion plans, aiming for 23.7 GW by FY32, with significant progress in derisked project execution and supply chain assurance (22.4 GW BTG sets ordered). The presentation emphasized APL's core strengths: an effectively unlevered capital structure, strong internal accrual generation capable of self-funding $14 billion in capex over the next seven years, and industry-leading operational excellence driven by AI/ML technologies. APL also highlighted its unparalleled in-house mine-to-plant logistics, robust fuel security strategy, and consistent commitment to ESG principles, reflected in improved ratings. While focusing on macro trends and strategic positioning, the presentation did not provide specific Q3_2025 quarterly financial results, instead offering full FY25 figures and TTM June'25 data. A behavioral analysis warning of "High Evasion Density" was noted, which could be attributed to this strategic focus on broader themes over granular current-quarter financials in a prepared presentation.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion & Market Leadership:** Targeting 23,720 MW operational capacity by FY32 (currently 18,150 MW operational). Positioned as India's largest private thermal IPP, poised to meet a significant portion (c. 30%) of India's 80 GW additional coal capacity requirement by FY32. Over 22.4 GW of Boilers, Turbines, and Generators (BTG) sets have been advance-ordered, with 92% land availability for future projects, ensuring derisked and timely execution.
*   **Derisked Execution Model:** Emphasis on brownfield development (69% of upcoming capacity) for faster execution, reduced delays in land acquisition/clearances, and direct control over vendors and suppliers. Project Management and Assurance Group (PMAG) leads institutionalized project execution.
*   **PPA Strategy & Revenue Certainty:** 88% of existing capacity tied up in long-term and medium-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) with leading states, ensuring stable revenue streams through two-part, availability-based tariff structures and fuel cost pass-through. Actively pursuing new PPA bids from states (e.g., Madhya Pradesh, Bihar) with attractive tariffs.
*   **Fuel Security & Logistics:** Unique in-house, mine-to-plant logistics capability, handling approx. 74 MTPA coal. Entry into commercial mining with 14 MTPA capacity from four owned coal mines to enhance fuel security for untied capacities. Advocates for coal as critical for India's baseload power, supported by vast domestic reserves and policy.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Consistent 90%+ plant availability, achieving the highest EBITDA margins in the thermal power sector (38%). Leverages an Energy Network Operations Center (ENOC) with AI/ML-based analytics for predictive maintenance, fuel optimization, and anomaly detection.
*   **Financial Strength:** Maintains an effectively unlevered capital structure with robust profitability and strong cashflow generation. FY25 FFO of $2.4 Bn is projected to fund ~$14 Bn capex over the next 7 years primarily through internal accruals, minimizing reliance on interim debt. Rated AA/Stable by four leading domestic rating agencies, indicating an improved credit profile.
*   **ESG Commitment:** Achieved a B Score from CDP for climate change and water security, and an S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment score of 68/100 (above peer average). High ash utilization (102% in FY25) and low water intensity (2.21 m3/MWh in FY25) demonstrate environmental stewardship.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Historical Context (from Q3 FY25 Summary):** APL targeted 23,720 MW capacity by FY32, focused on securing long-term PPAs (88% of capacity), pursuing organic and inorganic expansion, ensuring fuel security, maintaining operational excellence, prudent financial management (deleveraging), and ESG principles. The previous summary also noted ongoing discussions regarding outstanding dues from the Bangladesh power plant.

**Audit:**

*   **Capacity Expansion:** **Consistent.** Management explicitly re-affirmed the target of 23,720 MW by FY32, with current operational capacity at 18,150 MW. Progress is evidenced by the advanced ordering of 22.4 GW of BTG sets and active development of brownfield projects like Mahan, Raipur, and Raigarh Phase-II. This demonstrates continued, strong execution towards the stated goal.
*   **PPA Strategy:** **Consistent.** The presentation directly states "88% PPAs Tied up," confirming the previous commitment to long-term revenue visibility. The emphasis on derisked PPAs with fuel pass-through and active pursuit of new state bids reinforces this strategy.
*   **Expansion (Organic & Inorganic):** **Consistent.** The presentation details both existing organic capacity growth and the successful turnaround of acquired assets (Mahan, Raipur, Raigarh plants), aligning with the strategy of diversified expansion.
*   **Fuel Security & Cost Management:** **Consistent and strengthened.** The acquisition of the Dhirauli mine was mentioned previously, and this presentation elaborates on APL's "only IPP in India with in-house, mine-to-plant logistics capability" and "Entry in commercial mining with 14 MTPA capacity," which are significant advancements in fuel security. The continued focus on domestic coal and cost efficiency remains central.
*   **Operational Excellence:** **Consistent.** Management continues to highlight "Consistent 90%+ plant availability" and "Highest EBITDA margin in the sector," now supported by detailed descriptions of AI/ML-enabled O&M through ENOC and other digital tools.
*   **Financial Strength & Deleveraging:** **Consistent but with a shift in emphasis.** While the previous summary noted deleveraging, the current narrative emphasizes a "low leverage and high liquidity" profile that enables "fully funded growth" primarily through internal accruals ($14 Bn capex over 7 years). The TTM Net Debt to EBITDA of 1.78x demonstrates continued strong financial health, allowing for aggressive, self-funded expansion rather than a primary focus on further deleveraging.
*   **ESG Focus:** **Consistent and progressing.** Improved ESG ratings from S&P Global and CDP, along with specific metrics (102% ash utilization, reduced water intensity), demonstrate tangible progress and commitment to previous statements.
*   **Bangladesh Power Plant:** **No mention.** The previous summary mentioned "Outstanding dues around USD 800 million" and ongoing reconciliation. This current investor presentation *does not address* the status of the Bangladesh Power Plant outstanding dues, which represents an omission from the previous quarter's forward-looking statements. The behavioral analysis warning of "High Evasion Density" could be partly reflected in this omission, and the general lack of specific Q3_2025 financial performance data, favoring broader strategic communication.
