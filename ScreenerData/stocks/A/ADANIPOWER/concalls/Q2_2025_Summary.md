## Executive Overview

Adani Power Limited (APL) presented an investor presentation for September 2025, underscoring India's strong economic growth and its positioning to benefit from the resulting infrastructure and energy demand surge. While the transcript primarily focuses on future outlook and strategic positioning rather than historical quarterly performance, it reiterates APL's role as India's largest private thermal IPP. The company emphasizes its robust expansion plans, secured long-term PPAs, focus on fuel security, and operational excellence driven by technology. The presentation highlights a significant increase in the projected operational capacity by FY32 and substantial progress in securing new PPAs. APL also stresses its robust financial health, with a self-funded capex plan and a strong cash flow generation capability, supported by an improved credit profile.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Target:** 23,720 MW operational capacity by FY32, with 13 projects.
    *   **Existing Operational Capacity:** 18,150 MW.
    *   **Greenfield Projects:** 2,920 MW under development (e.g., 2,400 MW in MP, 2,400 MW in Bihar, 570 MW in Karnataka).
    *   **Acquired/Revived Assets:** Focus on integrating and expanding acquired assets like VIPL (1,600 MW, PPA starting Nov 2025).
    *   **Future Growth:** Over 16,000 MW in ongoing bids across various states.
    *   **BTG Ordering:** 100% BTG ordering for 23.7 GW of upcoming capacity.
*   **PPA Strategy:**
    *   **Current Tie-up:** 88% of existing 18.15 GW capacity tied up under long-term and medium-term PPAs.
    *   **New Wins:** Secured PPAs for 7,720 MW of upcoming capacity, with significant new awards in Bihar (2,400 MW), MP (3,200 MW + 1,320 MW), and Karnataka (570 MW).
    *   **PPA Structure:** New PPAs feature a 25-year term and a two-part, availability-based tariff structure ensuring capital charge recovery and fuel cost pass-through.
*   **Fuel Security & Cost Management:**
    *   **Domestic Coal Focus:** 81% of domestic coal-based capacity has fuel security via long-term FSAs.
    *   **Mine Operations:** Approval received to commence operations at Dhirauli Mine; coal production expected from next year. APL is developing four coal mines with 14 MTPA production capacity.
    *   **Logistics:** In-house mine-to-plant logistics capability, handling approx. 74 MTPA coal.
*   **Operational Excellence:**
    *   **Plant Availability:** Consistently maintained over 90% plant availability.
    *   **Technology Integration:** Real-time monitoring via Energy Network Operations Center (ENOC), predictive maintenance, AI/ML for anomaly detection, and digital asset management systems.
*   **Financial Strength:**
    *   **Self-funded Growth:** Majority of expansion capex to be funded through internal accruals.
    *   **Debt Management:** Strong FFO generation covers debt maturities; low leverage and high liquidity.
    *   **Credit Profile:** 8 notches upgrade in credit rating over 6 years to AA/Stable.
*   **ESG Focus:**
    *   **Climate Change:** Target to reduce GHG emission intensity to 0.84 tCO2e/MWh by FY26.
    *   **Water Management:** Achieved 36% lower water intensity than statutory limits.
    *   **Waste Management:** 100% ash utilization for FY25; aiming for 100% SUP-free locations by FY26.
    *   **ESG Ratings:** Maintained B score from CDP, strong scores from S&P Global, CSR HUB, and FTSE.
