## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) reported a steady Q1 FY26 performance, marking its first earnings announcement as an independently listed entity post-demerger. The company achieved a 6% revenue growth to INR 1,570 crores, primarily driven by a strong 15% like-to-like (LTL) growth in its Lifestyle Brands segment. Emerging Businesses revenue saw slower growth due to the closure of Forever 21. Consolidated EBITDA was INR 286 crores, a marginal increase year-on-year, with EBITDA margins at 15.5%, reflecting increased marketing investments. Profitability improved with PAT at INR 24 crores, up 5% year-on-year. The company continues to expand its retail footprint, with over 40 new gross store additions in Q1 FY26, bringing the total to over 3,200 stores covering 4.6 million square feet. Management highlighted the resilience of their brand portfolio and execution amidst a sluggish consumption environment, with a strategic focus on accelerating store additions and strengthening distribution channels.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** INR 1,570 crores (up 6% YoY)
    *   Lifestyle Brands: INR 1,570 crores (up 6% YoY)
    *   Emerging Businesses: Slower growth impacted by Forever 21 closure; underlying brands showed strong LTL.
*   **EBITDA:** INR 286 crores (up marginally YoY)
    *   EBITDA Margin: 15.5% (decreased by 40 bps YoY, due to amplified marketing investment)
*   **PAT:** INR 24 crores (up 5% YoY)
    *   Adjusted PAT (excluding incremental ad spend): INR 54 crores
*   **Retail Like-to-Like (LTL) Growth:**
    *   Overall Brands Portfolio: Strong double-digit LTL growth.
    *   Lifestyle Brands: 15% LTL growth (fourth consecutive quarter of strong performance).
    *   Youth Brands & Innerwear: 10% LTL growth.
*   **Retail Expansion:** 40+ gross store additions in Q1 FY26.
    *   Total Stores: 3,200+
    *   Total Retail Area: 4.6 million sq ft

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Retail Expansion Acceleration:** Plan to accelerate the pace of store additions over the next three quarters and beyond to expand physical presence and reach.
*   **Brand Portfolio Strength & Execution:** Leveraging iconic brands with a focus on high-quality retail execution and curating high-impact merchandise.
*   **Channel Calibration & Profitability:** Focus on discount correction, especially in e-commerce, to improve profitability profiles.
*   **Emerging Businesses Growth:** Positioning youth brands and innerwear portfolio for strong and profitable growth, with initiatives to enhance store productivity and consumer engagement.
*   **Marketing Investment:** Conscious decision to invest significantly in advertising, including associating with events like IPL, to enhance brand equity and visibility.
*   **Digitization & Technology:** Continued digitization across operations and supply chain, with ongoing investment in technology.
*   **Product Portfolio Enhancement:** Driving growth through product portfolio upgrades, innovation, and a focus on fashionability and quality.
*   **Consolidation of Non-Retail Channels:** Heavy lifting in network correction for department stores and e-commerce is largely complete, with expectations of stabilization and improvement.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Baseline - Q1 FY26):**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Management stated "strong growth momentum" and "industry-leading, strong double-digit same-store sales growth". LTL growth was indeed strong at 15% for Lifestyle Brands. However, overall revenue growth was 6%, not double-digit as implied for the overall business, though it met the segment-specific expectation for Lifestyle Brands.
    *   **Profitability:** Management highlighted "resilient performance" and "EBITDA was higher year-on-year". EBITDA saw a marginal increase, and PAT grew by 5%. The reported EBITDA margin decreased slightly due to increased marketing spend, which was a conscious strategic choice. Adjusted PAT provides a better view of underlying profitability improvement.
    *   **Retail Expansion:** Management reported adding "40-plus gross stores addition" which aligns with their ongoing expansion strategy. The total store count and footprint are consistent with previous reporting.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   **Consumption Environment:** Management acknowledged a "sluggish consumption environment" with apparel market displaying "selective pockets of growth, largely fueled by wedding season." This is consistent with prior observations of a cautious market.
    *   **E-commerce Correction:** Management confirmed that "large part of the corrections around e-commerce are more or less done" and expects positive trajectory after "a little bit more in Q2." This validates the previous mention of recalibrating other channels to a more profitable base.
    *   **Forever 21 Closure:** Identified as a primary reason for slower growth in Emerging Businesses, consistent with previous explanations.
    *   **Marketing Spend:** Explained the higher marketing spend in Q1 as a conscious investment for brand equity and visibility, particularly due to IPL association, and stated it won't be as dramatic a shift in future quarters.
    *   **Store Closures:** Explicitly stated by management that store closures have "practically no impact on delivered LTL of 3,000 store network," clarifying its minimal effect on LTL calculations.
    *   **GST Transition:** Not explicitly mentioned in this transcript, suggesting the impact from the previous quarter is now behind them.
    *   **Reebok Transition:** Management detailed the complexities of the Reebok transition, including initial store numbers, repair work, and BIS compliance, explaining the gradual growth trajectory rather than an immediate takeoff.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance (To Track Next Quarter):**
    *   **E-commerce Trajectory:** Expectation of a positive trajectory for e-commerce after a potential slight dip in Q2, with corrections largely done.
    *   **Marketing Spend:** While significant investment will continue, the dramatic shift seen in Q1 is not expected to repeat. Full-year marketing spend is likely to be around 3-3.5% of revenue.
    *   **Store Additions:** Positive outlook for quarterly store additions and continued expansion across the portfolio (Lifestyle Brands, Reebok, etc.).
    *   **Innerwear Profitability:** Expected to reach breakeven on a full-year basis in FY '27.
    *   **Lifestyle Brands Growth:** Expected to deliver double-digit growth for the Lifestyle Brands portfolio in the medium-term.
    *   **Emerging Businesses Growth:** Expected to grow at slightly higher double-digits than Lifestyle Brands.
    *   **Debt Reduction:** Expectation of INR 200-300 crores reduction in debt each year, aiming for debt-free status in 2.5 to 3 years.
    *   **Working Capital:** Expected to operate within a range of 13-17% of sales, with potential for improvement as smaller businesses scale.
    *   **Capex:** Annual capex guidance remains around INR 250 crores, primarily for retail expansion (new stores and refurbishments), warehouse infrastructure, manufacturing, and technology.
    *   **Reebok Growth Target:** Long-term target of in excess of 20% growth for the Reebok portfolio.
    *   **Brand Recognition:** Initiatives are ongoing to strengthen brand recognition for all brands, with a focus on product quality, fashionability, and consumer experience.
    *   **Gross Margin:** While impacted by channel mix and discounting, management is focused on maintaining price parity for new merchandise.
    *   **Channel Mix (Non-Reebok):** Primarily a consignment model for Lifestyle Brands, with inventory managed by the company. Peter England has a small portion of franchisee billing.
