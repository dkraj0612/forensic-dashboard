## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) reported a strong Q3 FY26 performance, showcasing accelerated growth momentum and improved profitability. Revenue grew 10% year-on-year (YoY) to INR 2,343 crores, driven by robust performances across both Lifestyle Brands (9% growth) and Emerging Businesses (13% growth). The company achieved a consolidated EBITDA of INR 431 crores, up 21% YoY, with an impressive EBITDA margin expansion of 180 basis points to 18.4%. Profitability significantly improved with Profit After Tax (PAT) at INR 100 crores, a 66% increase YoY, benefiting from operating leverage and a one-time exceptional item related to labor code impact. The company continued its aggressive retail expansion, adding over 90 stores in the quarter, bringing the total to over 3,300 stores. Management highlighted strong execution amidst a slightly moderated December due to wedding date shifts, with a focus on sustained double-digit growth and further margin expansion driven by product upgrades, enhanced retail experiences, and disciplined expansion.

## Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue:** INR 2,343 crores (up 10% YoY)
    *   Lifestyle Brands: INR 2,002 crores (up 9% YoY)
    *   Emerging Businesses: INR 341 crores (up 13% YoY)
*   **EBITDA:** INR 431 crores (up 21% YoY)
    *   EBITDA Margin: 18.4% (expanded by 180 bps YoY)
*   **PAT:** INR 100 crores (up 66% YoY)
    *   Reported PAT (incl. exceptional item): INR 69 crores
*   **Retail Like-to-Like (LTL) Growth:**
    *   Overall Business: 6% LTL growth
    *   Lifestyle Brands: 5% LTL growth (sixth consecutive quarter of positive LTL)
    *   Emerging Businesses: 16% LTL growth (excluding Forever 21, growth at 19%)
*   **Retail Expansion:** 90+ gross store additions in Q3 FY26.
    *   Total Stores: 3,300+
    *   Total Retail Area: 4.8 million sq ft

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Accelerated Growth Momentum:** Focus on sustaining double-digit growth driven by strong execution across brands and channels.
*   **Margin Expansion:** Leveraging operating leverage from sales growth and disciplined cost management to further improve EBITDA margins.
*   **Product Portfolio & Retail Experience:** Continued investment in product upgrades, especially in wedding-related categories, and enhancing in-store customer engagement and salience.
*   **Aggressive Retail Expansion:** Continued momentum in store additions with larger, more impactful stores featuring sharper assortments.
*   **Emerging Businesses Growth:** Positioning emerging brands (Reebok, Van Heusen Innerwear, American Eagle) for robust growth, with a target to contribute one-fourth of overall ABLBL business in 4-5 years.
*   **Channel Calibration:** Stabilization and rebound in other channels like trade and e-commerce, with ongoing efforts to sharpen models for profitability.
*   **Brand Strength:** Leveraging iconic brands, including the continued scaling of Reebok and recovery in Van Heusen Innerwear.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Baseline - Q3 FY26):**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Management indicated "strong double-digit growth" and expected "growth momentum to remain strong and profitable." The reported 10% YoY revenue growth aligns with this expectation, though the description "sharply focused and disciplined execution... delivered strong double-digit growth" could imply slightly higher if not for the festive calendar shift.
    *   **Profitability:** Management highlighted "strong operating leverage and further margin expansion" and stated "Consolidated EBITDA up 21%." Actual EBITDA growth of 21% and EBITDA margin expansion of 180 bps met these expectations. The PAT growth of 66% was significantly boosted by an exceptional item, but underlying profitability also improved.
    *   **Retail Expansion:** Management stated "adding around 90-plus stores during the quarter, as expansion momentum gained pace." The actual addition of over 90 stores aligns with this statement.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   **Festive Season Impact:** Management acknowledged that the festive season's impact moderated in December due to a shift in wedding dates, impacting sales growth. This is a clarification of a known headwind.
    *   **GST Impact:** The impact of GST-related disruptions in Q2 was confirmed to be "pretty much digested through the quarter," with no significant restocking effects in Q3.
    *   **Wedding Calendar:** The moderation in December was directly attributed to the wedding calendar shifting away earlier than in the previous year, a cyclical factor.
    *   **E-commerce & Wholesale Correction:** Management reiterated that "Most of that is done" regarding corrections in wholesale and e-commerce, leading to a rebound in these channels with double-digit growth, confirming the completion of earlier strategic adjustments.
    *   **Innerwear Turnaround:** The strong double-digit growth in Innerwear after subdued quarters was attributed to improved retail LTL (over 30%), product innovation, inventory correction being behind, and leveraging GST benefits. This marks a significant positive shift in narrative for this segment.
    *   **Reebok Growth:** The narrative around Reebok remains highly positive, with network doubling and strong growth, reinforcing past positive commentary.
    *   **Rental Expenses:** The decline in rent expense as a percentage of sales was explained by operating leverage from higher LTL sales, rather than a shift from franchisee to company stores, a clarification of a specific P&L item.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance (To Track Next Quarter):**
    *   **Sustained Double-Digit Growth:** Expectation of continued "steady double-digit growth" and "momentum should continue over the next few quarters."
    *   **EBITDA Margin:** Aiming for "steady EBITDA 11%-12% pre Ind AS margins" in the medium term.
    *   **Innerwear Profitability:** Hopeful for "at least one profitable quarter next year" for the Innerwear business, marking the beginning of a turnaround.
    *   **Emerging Businesses Contribution:** Target to become "one-fourth of overall ABLBL business in next 4-5 years."
    *   **Reebok Growth:** Significant headroom for "aggressive growth" in Reebok, with expectations of exiting FY26 with 230+ stores and considerable market opportunities remaining.
    *   **Lifestyle Brands Network Expansion:** Headroom for network expansion for at least three-four years, adding ~200 stores annually.
    *   **Steady State Growth:** Expectation of 6%-7% LTL growth for Lifestyle Brands and the rest from expansion. Overall, aiming for ~12% steady state growth for the business.
    *   **Store Addition Pipeline:** Expectation of adding "another 90 plus net stores in the next quarter," leading to an estimated 150-store net addition for FY26.
    *   **Capex:** FY26 capex expected to be north of INR 300 crores (around INR 320-330 crores), with the lion's share (over 80%) for Lifestyle Brands, including expansions and renovations.
    *   **Net Debt Reduction:** Plan to reduce net debt by INR 75-100 crores in Q4, continuing the trend of deleveraging.
    *   **Van Heusen Innerwear:** Losses halved in Q3, with initiatives around retail, product, and replenishment expected to drive further improvement.
    *   **Gross Margins:** Management is confident that gross margins have stayed "fairly healthy" and any perceived decline is due to business mix.
    *   **Reebok Apparel Contribution:** Aiming to increase apparel contribution in Reebok from the current 36% to higher levels.
    *   **Small Town India Performance:** Noted strong performance in smaller towns with LTL growth of 13-14% YTD for Lifestyle Brands.
