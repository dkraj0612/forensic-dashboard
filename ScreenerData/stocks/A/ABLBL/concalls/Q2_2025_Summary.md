## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) reported a mixed but resilient Q2 FY26 performance. Revenue grew by 4% year-on-year to INR 2,038 crores, driven by a robust 7% growth in Lifestyle Brands. While Emerging Businesses saw a reported decline, this was primarily due to the phasing out of Forever 21. Retail like-to-like growth remained strong at double-digits for both Lifestyle Brands (12%) and Emerging Businesses (11%). Consolidated EBITDA saw a healthy 12% growth to INR 338 crores, with margins expanding by 125 basis points to 16.6%, reflecting improved operating efficiency and cost discipline. Profitability improved significantly with Consolidated PAT at INR 23 crores, a notable recovery from a loss in the previous quarter, partly due to an exceptional loss in the prior period. The company continued its aggressive retail expansion, adding over 75 new stores, bringing the total footprint to 4.7 million square feet across approximately 3,250 stores. Management acknowledged challenges like the GST transition and localized issues in the East, which moderated momentum, but highlighted the strength of their brand portfolio and execution.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Retail Expansion:** Continued aggressive store additions to expand physical presence and reach, particularly in smaller towns.
*   **Brand Portfolio Strength:** Leveraging a strong portfolio of lifestyle and emerging brands, with consistent double-digit like-to-like growth in retail.
*   **Operational Efficiency & Cost Management:** Focus on disciplined discounting and cost management, leading to EBITDA margin expansion.
*   **Channel Calibration:** Calibrating other channels to a more profitable base.
*   **Emerging Businesses Growth:** Positioning emerging businesses for profitable and scalable growth, with focus on brands like Van Heusen Innerwear, American Eagle, and Reebok.
*   **Product Portfolio Enhancement:** Driving growth through product portfolio upgrades and innovation.
*   **Digitization:** Continued digitization across operations and supply chain.
*   **Store Renovations:** Aggressive store renovation program (~65 stores in Q2) to upgrade retail identity and consumer experience, which is expected to continue and drive stronger like-for-likes going forward.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Baseline - Q2 FY26):**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Management stated "delivering yet another quarter of double-digit retail like-to-like growth" and "revenue growing to 4% Y-o-Y". While retail LTL growth was strong (12% for Lifestyle, 11% for Emerging), overall revenue growth was 4%. This is a slight divergence as overall revenue growth was not double-digit, though driven by strong retail performance.
    *   **Profitability:** Management highlighted "sustained profitable growth" and "EBITDA registered a healthy growth of 12%". The 12% EBITDA growth and 125 bps margin expansion to 16.6% align with this narrative. The PAT recovery from a loss to positive INR 23 crores also supports this.
    *   **Retail Expansion:** Management reported adding "75-plus new stores" and a total footprint of "4.7 million square feet across 785-plus cities and towns, with a network of around 3,250 stores". This met their stated expansion goals.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   Management acknowledged **challenges** including "early onset of the Pujo season provided a healthy boost to our positive demand. However, the GST transition and localised issue in the East did moderate overall momentum to some extent." This is a consistent acknowledgement of external factors impacting momentum.
    *   The **GST transition** was repeatedly cited as a factor impacting wholesale businesses due to IT configurations and purchase order system adjustments, causing a loss of sales in September. Management indicated this impact is largely behind them.
    *   **Forever 21** phasing out was cited as the primary reason for the decline in the Emerging Businesses segment's reported revenue, with management quantifying its impact on overall growth at approximately 1%.
    *   **Pipeline inventory issues in Reebok** were mentioned as a factor impacting revenue recognition.
    *   **Store renovations** (65 stores in Q2) were highlighted as an initiative impacting reported growth due to store closures, but expected to drive stronger LTL going forward.
    *   **Demand trends:** Management characterized Diwali as "okay" and is awaiting the wedding season to gauge full festive impact. They noted a "decent Diwali" but refrained from calling it exceptionally good, awaiting further data.

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance (To Track Next Quarter):**
    *   **Emerging Businesses:** Expected to "deliver strong profitable growth in the coming quarters" and are "well-positioned to become levers of profitable and meaningful growth in the coming quarters."
    *   **Lifestyle Brands:** Expected to "report strong growth in the coming quarters" for primary sales.
    *   **Overall ABLBL:** Expects "steady net addition of stores going forward" after completion of consolidation exercise.
    *   **GST Impact:** Management believes the GST transition impact is behind them, and now it's about consumer response to revised prices. A good test would be the wedding season.
    *   **Reebok:** Expected to "keep building on the momentum" with streamlined sourcing and store additions. Customer level growth is expected to translate into reported revenues.
    *   **Depreciation & Interest:** Management considers current depreciation and interest costs as "normalized" for now, but expects finance cost to reduce in H2.
    *   **Working Capital:** Expects working capital to return to similar levels by the end of Q3 FY26, barring seasonal inventory build-up.
    *   **Debt Levels:** Expected to "go down" by the end of FY26.
    *   **Wholesale/E-commerce Channels:** Expects the gap between primary and secondary sales to translate into higher growth in coming quarters as channel is "lighter".
    *   **Small Towns:** Expects continued strong performance in small towns, with management improving their methods of managing these formats.
    *   **Innerwear:** Expected to see "exponential sales growth" and improved profitability with deeper investments and consumer stickiness.
    *   **Overall Consumption:** Management stated "not yet visible" broader consumption uptick, but sees strong growth in small towns as a clear indicator of some positive impact.
    *   **ABLBL Direction:** Expects "double-digit growth" from these brands in the medium-term, with ups and downs per quarter.
    *   **Profitability Protection:** Focus on rent leverage and sales per square foot as key drivers of margin expansion, alongside continuous cost programs.
    *   **E-commerce:** Management acknowledges that their e-commerce business "is something which we could do better in" and they need to find "own methods and recipes."
    *   **Market Consumption:** Management stated the "most crucial pinpoint" for the industry is the desire for "more consumption in the market."
