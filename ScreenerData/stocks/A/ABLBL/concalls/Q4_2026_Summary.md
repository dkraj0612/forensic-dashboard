## Executive Overview

Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited (ABLBL) delivered a robust Q4 FY26, showcasing continued double-digit revenue growth and improved profitability despite a slightly moderated consumption environment influenced by a softer wedding calendar and geopolitical uncertainties. Revenue increased by 12% year-on-year (YoY) to INR 2,174 crores, with Lifestyle Brands growing 11% and Emerging Businesses accelerating by 18%. The company achieved a consolidated EBITDA of INR 375 crores, up 14% YoY, with a 20 basis point (bps) expansion in EBITDA margin to 17.2%. Normalized Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a significant 58% YoY increase to INR 60 crores. The full-year revenue stood at INR 8,396 crores (up 7% YoY), with EBITDA growing 13% to INR 1,429 crores and margins improving by 90 bps to 17%. ABLBL continued its aggressive retail expansion, adding over 300 stores during the year, bringing the total store count to 3,348. The company also declared a dividend of INR 50 paisa per equity share. Management emphasized strong execution across brands and channels, with a focus on sustained double-digit growth and further margin expansion driven by product innovation, retail experience enhancement, and disciplined expansion.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Sustained Double-Digit Growth:** Management is focused on continuing the double-digit revenue growth trajectory, supported by strong performance across brands and channels.
*   **Margin Improvement:** Leveraging operating leverage from sales growth and disciplined cost management to drive further EBITDA margin expansion.
*   **Product & Consumer Relevance:** Continued focus on sharpening consumer proposition, product innovation, and adapting to changing consumer needs.
*   **Aggressive Retail Expansion:** Maintaining momentum in store additions across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 cities, with a robust pipeline.
*   **Emerging Businesses:** Positioning emerging brands for robust growth and scaling them into significant contributors, with a target to contribute one-fourth of overall ABLBL business in 4-5 years.
*   **Channel Optimization:** Continued strong performance in e-commerce and departmental stores, and stabilizing other channels.
*   **Brand Strength & Innovation:** Leveraging iconic brands and driving growth through product innovations, particularly in categories like women's lines and performance footwear.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

*   **Stated Targets vs. Actual Performance (Q4 FY26):**
    *   **Revenue Growth:** Management anticipated "double-digit growth" and reported 12% YoY growth, aligning with expectations.
    *   **Profitability:** Management indicated "stronger operating performance" and reported 14% EBITDA growth and 20 bps margin expansion, meeting expectations for continued strength. Full-year EBITDA growth of 13% and margin improvement of 90 bps also met projections.
    *   **Retail Expansion:** Management highlighted "expansion momentum also remained robust" and the addition of "300+" stores for the full year aligns with ongoing aggressive expansion plans.
    *   **Innerwear Turnaround:** Management stated Van Heusen Innerwear was "on track to achieve break-even quarter by Quarter 4 of current fiscal year" and reported improved profitability and reduced losses, indicating progress.

*   **Narrative/Tone Shift Regarding Headwinds:**
    *   **Consumption Environment:** Management acknowledged "near-term softness" due to geopolitical uncertainties and market volatility, a slight moderation from the previously described stable consumption trends. The wedding calendar's impact was reiterated as uneven.
    *   **Channel Performance:** The strong growth in "other channels" was clarified to include contract manufacturing, not just e-commerce, with additional capacity driving this segment.
    *   **Margin Commentary:** Management clarified that while some one-off benefits (PLI) influenced specific quarters, the underlying Lifestyle business margin expanded by 20 bps for the full year, and overall Emerging Business margins showed significant improvement. The previous year's GST impact was confirmed as "digested."

*   **New Forward-Looking Promises/Guidance (To Track Next Quarter):**
    *   **Dividend Policy:** Confidence in cash flow generation to pay dividends in the range of "15% to 25% of net profit" annually, with a commitment to starting from year one.
    *   **Debt Repayment:** Aim to become "debt-free in the next three years," though not a primary chase if reasonable debt levels are maintained.
    *   **CAPEX:** FY27 CAPEX expected to be around INR 300 crores, with ~INR 200 crores allocated to retail expansion and renovations.
    *   **Net Debt Reduction:** Plan to reduce net debt by INR 50-60 crores in Q4 FY26. Full-year FY26 net debt reduction was INR 55 crores.
    *   **Innerwear Profitability:** Targeting "at least one profitable quarter in FY27," with potential for Q3 FY27 to be a breakeven quarter.
    *   **Emerging Businesses Contribution:** Target to contribute "one-fourth of overall ABLBL business in next 4-5 years."
    *   **Reebok Network Expansion:** Aiming for 40-50 stores per year for the next few years.
    *   **Lifestyle Brands LTL Growth:** Projected steady-state LTL growth of "about 7%" for the next few years.
    *   **Store Addition Pipeline:** Expectation of "stronger net expansion" in FY27 due to lesser closures.
    *   **Gross Margins:** Management confident gross margins have remained "fairly healthy," and perceived declines are due to business mix.
    *   **Small Town India Performance:** Peter England Red saw "significant double-digit growth" in small towns.
    *   **Contract Manufacturing:** Contract manufacturing business is a significant growth driver, with additional capacity and good run rate.
    *   **Raw Material Costs:** Management is monitoring crude derivative impacts and will respond as needed, noting that autumn-winter buying prices are already locked.
    *   **Rental Costs:** Decline in rental costs is due to an IndAS impact, moving towards more fixed than variable rentals.
    *   **Van Heusen Innerwear:** Losses have come down significantly, with focus on retail, product, and replenishment driving improvement.
    *   **Brand Expansion Focus:** Reebok has significant whitespace for expansion. All SBUs have aggressive ambitions for their respective white spaces.
    *   **COCO Store Ratio:** Approximately 36-37% of the network (1,200-1,250 stores) are COCO stores.
    *   **PLI Scheme:** The INR 20 crores recognized in Q4 FY26 was a one-time benefit related to PLI for manufacturing capacity, not expected to recur annually.
    *   **Emerging Business Margin:** Expected to keep improving, moving from ~4% EBITDA in FY26 towards higher levels.
