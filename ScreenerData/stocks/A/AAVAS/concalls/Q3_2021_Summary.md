## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported Q3 FY2021 performance showing signs of recovery and consistent growth amidst ongoing economic adaptation. While disbursements for 9M FY21 were down 21% YoY, Q3 FY21 saw a 2% YoY and 15% QoQ increase in disbursements. Assets Under Management (AUM) grew 23% YoY to Rs. 88,226 Mn as of December 2020. Profit After Tax (PAT) for 9M FY21 increased by 7% YoY (Ind-AS) to Rs. 2,022 Mn. Asset quality saw an improvement in collection efficiency, rising from 95% in September to 98.8% in December, nearing pre-COVID levels. The company maintained its focus on its core customer segment in semi-urban and rural areas, with home loans forming 73.4% of the portfolio. AAVAS continued its branch network expansion, adding 18 new branches in the last 12 months, bringing the total to 263.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continues to serve low- and middle-income customers in semi-urban and rural areas. Home loans constitute 73.4% of the portfolio, with self-employed customers making up 60.7% (a slight increase in salaried proportion observed in Q3 FY21).
*   **Network Expansion:** Added 18 branches in the past 12 months to reach 263 branches. Plans to continue expansion at a similar pace to serve its target market.
*   **Risk Management:** Maintaining zero exposure to commercial paper. Increased focus on underwriting and collection efficiency. Full provisioning for Stage 3 assets, including additional COVID-19 provisions. Portfolio is secured by mortgage, predominantly self-occupied residential property, with a granular customer profile and low LTV.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Access to diversified and cost-effective long-term financing with stable credit ratings (AA-). Prudent borrowing practices maintained, including zero commercial paper exposure.
*   **Operational Resilience & Efficiency:** Continued investment in manpower and distribution. Focus on maintaining a healthy spread.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q3 FY2021 & 9M FY2021):

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 23% YoY growth, aligning with historical growth trajectories.
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported 7% YoY growth for 9M FY21 (Ind-AS), indicating resilience despite the challenging environment.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD):** The 1+DPD number rose to 8.21%, which management attributes to the lag effect post-moratorium periods and the impact of the Supreme Court order. However, collection efficiency improved significantly to 98.8% in December, demonstrating borrower repayment behavior recovery. Management expects 1+DPD to improve.
*   **ROA/ROE:** Achieved ROA of 3.9% and ROE of 15.1% in Q3 FY21, indicating strong profitability on a quarterly basis.
*   **Spread:** Maintained a healthy spread of 5.74%, exceeding the previous target.

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:

*   **COVID-19 Impact:** Management's tone is cautiously optimistic, highlighting the "new normal" and the gradual improvement in economic activity. The increase in 1+DPD is explained as a natural consequence of payment cycles post-moratorium and the Supreme Court order, rather than a severe behavioral shift. The focus is on improved collection efficiency.
*   **Moratorium & Collections:** The significant improvement in collection efficiency from 95% to 98.8% is a key positive narrative, indicating a return to normalcy for a large portion of the customer base.
*   **Economic Environment:** Management sees continued demand for affordable housing. The granular nature of the portfolio and strong LTV ratios provide comfort.
*   **Funding Environment:** Stable credit ratings and diversified funding sources continue to provide comfort.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **1+DPD Target:** Management expects the 1+DPD to improve from current levels, with a target to return to normal 5-6% levels in one to two quarters. The Supreme Court order's resolution is expected to further help this roll-down.
*   **Disbursement Growth:** Expects the second half of FY21 to see stronger disbursement trends, aligning with the 20-25% AUM growth target for the year.
*   **Salaried vs. Self-Employed Mix:** While the salaried proportion increased due to higher disbursements in that segment and reclassifications, management indicated they will continue to choose segments that allow them to maintain asset quality, spread, and growth, suggesting a dynamic approach rather than a fixed target for the mix.
*   **Interest Rate Pass-on:** A 10 bps reduction in the prime lending rate was implemented from January 1, 2021, with potential for further reductions based on board decisions and market conditions.
*   **Liquidity:** Management plans to reduce excess liquidity from 5-6 months of disbursements to a more traditional 3-4 months as normalization continues.
*   **Yield Pressure:** Acknowledged potential yield compression of 50-75 bps over the next two to three years due to competition, but expects to offset this with opex reduction to sustain a 2.5% ROA.
*   **Restructuring:** No accounts have been restructured to date, and management has rejected a significant number of requests, reinforcing a clean asset quality narrative. ECLGS and Supreme Court-related numbers are not impacting reported figures.
*   **Opex Reduction:** Continues the journey of reducing opex by 30-40 bps annually, leveraging technology.
