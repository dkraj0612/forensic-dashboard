## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported strong Q2 FY2020 and H1 FY2020 performance, characterized by robust AUM growth and significant PAT expansion. The company maintained its focus on the low- and middle-income, self-employed customer segment in semi-urban and rural areas, specializing in small-ticket housing loans. Management highlighted proactive efforts in diversifying borrowing profiles and expressed confidence in scaling operations while maintaining asset quality. Regulatory changes, such as enhanced priority sector lending limits for banks to NBFCs/HFCs, are expected to positively impact the affordable housing sector and AAVAS.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continued emphasis on low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas. Home loans constitute 74.6% of the portfolio, and other mortgage loans 25.4%. Self-employed customers represent 64.8% of the client base.
*   **Network Expansion:** Added 6 branches in H1 FY2020, with approximately 30 more planned for H2 FY2020, aiming for a total of around 40 new branches in the fiscal year.
*   **Risk Management:** Robust in-house underwriting and analytics framework. Management aims to maintain Gross NPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%. Zero exposure to commercial papers as a prudent measure.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Secured term loan from SBI Life Insurance (Rs. 100 Crores) and NCDs from IFC (Rs. 345 Crores). Diversified borrowing mix including term loans, securitization, NHB, and debt capital markets.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Focus on leveraging technology and data analytics for continuous growth and efficiency.
*   **Product Mix:** Home loans represented 74.6% of H1 FY21 disbursements, consistent with prior periods.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q2 FY2020 & H1 FY2020):

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 42% YoY growth, significantly exceeding previous guidance and demonstrating strong market penetration.
*   **Disbursement Growth (H1 FY2020):** Reported 21% YoY growth, indicating a healthy recovery and expansion. Management noted that Q2 traditionally sees slower growth due to monsoon season impacting construction activities in their target segment.
*   **Asset Quality (Gross NPA < 1%):** Maintained Gross NPA at 0.62%, well within target. Gross NPA for "other mortgage loans" saw a slight increase to 0.47% but remained within acceptable limits and management attributed this to vintage effects in a relatively new segment.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD < 5%):** Reported 1+DPD at 3.92%, marking the best-ever performance for a Q2 in eight years, exceeding the stated target. Management highlighted strong collection efficiency of over 98%.
*   **ROA Target (2.5% and above):** Reported 4.03% ROA for H1 FY2020, significantly exceeding the stated target. Management noted that effective tax rate reduction also contributed positively.
*   **Spread:** Maintained spreads at 5.03%, meeting the target of over 5%. Management indicated potential future compression but expects to offset it with operational efficiency improvements.

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:

*   **Economic Environment:** While acknowledging concerns in media about the broader economy, management expressed confidence in the resilience of their target customer segment. They cited strong demand for home loans, stable asset quality, and positive ground-level indicators like good rainfall as signs of stability. They differentiate their segment from mid-level activities like distributors and stockists, focusing on the "last mile guy."
*   **Competition:** Management acknowledged that larger HFCs are showing increased interest in the EWS/LIG segment. However, they emphasized AAVAS's deep reach into Tier-2 to Tier-6 towns (covering over 1300 towns) and focus on individual loans, which differentiates them from larger players catering to builder-led supply and top cities. They believe their existing distribution and sourcing capabilities are sufficient for future growth.
*   **Funding Environment:** Management noted regulatory efforts to improve liquidity in the market and expressed confidence in their ability to access diversified and cost-effective long-term financing.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Credit Rating:** Continue to maintain long-term credit ratings of AA- from CARE and A+ with a positive outlook from ICRA.
*   **Opex Ratio:** Management indicated a continuous effort towards improving business efficiency through technology and data analytics, which will help offset potential spread compression and maintain a healthy ROA.
*   **Borrowing Cost:** Expects to maintain borrowing costs at current levels or see further reduction, with a focus on diversified and cost-effective long-term financing.
*   **Asset Quality:** Endeavor to maintain GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD around 5%.
*   **ROA:** Aim to consistently deliver ROA of 2.5% and above.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to open around 40 new branches in H2 FY2020, continuing the strategy of network expansion.
*   **Technology Investment:** Continued investment in technology, business process, and analytics to improve operational efficiency and customer acquisition.
*   **Securitization & Assignment Volumes:** Intend to keep securitization and assignment volumes around 25% of the loan book in the near term.
*   **Aavas Mitra:** Aspire to generate 15% of total leads from the Aavas Mitra platform in the next two to three years.
*   **Future Business Activity:** While currently focused on housing finance, AAVAS Finserv (a dormant subsidiary) may be opened in the future to serve existing customers with other products, demonstrating lending ability in the housing business.
*   **CAGR Growth:** Expect to grow AUM at 25% CAGR over the next three to five years.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Currently operating in nine states and may explore three more states after three years.
*   **Collection Efficiency:** Normal collection efforts continue, with collection efficiency remaining high.
*   **Loan Book from Tier-1 Locations:** Currently around 25% to 30% of the outstanding loan book.
