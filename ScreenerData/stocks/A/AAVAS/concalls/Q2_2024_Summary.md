## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported Q2 FY'24 results, showcasing continued growth and resilience, driven by a focus on calibrated expansion and digital transformation. The company achieved AUM growth of 22.1% year-on-year, reaching INR 153,195 million as of September 30, 2023. Profit After Tax (PAT) for H1 FY'24 grew by 17.9% year-on-year to INR 2,313 million. Disbursements in Q2 FY'24 demonstrated a Q-o-Q growth of 18% and a Y-o-Y growth of 10%, totaling INR 12,585 million. Significant progress has been made on technology implementation with Phase 1 of Salesforce (LOS) stabilized and Oracle Fusion ERP-GL going live. The Oracle FLEXCUBE Loan Management System (LMS) is currently under testing and migration. Management reiterated its commitment to the Aavas Vision 3.0 framework, emphasizing technology, people, and sustainable growth. The Opex ratio has seen gradual moderation. The company maintains a strong liquidity position and a healthy capital adequacy ratio.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Technology Transformation:** Phase 1 of Salesforce (LOS) is stabilized. Oracle Fusion ERP-GL is live, and Oracle FLEXCUBE LMS is under testing and migration. The company views FY'24 as a full transformation year, aiming to reduce TAT from ~13-14 days to 6-7 days post-completion.
*   **Growth Focus:** Reaffirmed AUM growth guidance of 20-25% for the fiscal year. Management aims for calibrated, moderated growth in coming quarters due to ongoing transitions, prioritizing quality.
*   **Product Mix:** Home loan share is stable at 69.7%, and Non-Home Loan share is at 30.3%. Focus remains on cash flow-based underwriting backed by mortgages, with no reliance on volatile income or subsidy-based funding.
*   **Geographical Diversification:** Growth in regions like Karnataka, Maharashtra, and central regions is contributing to an increase in average ticket size.
*   **Underwriting Philosophy:** Core philosophy remains cash flow-based underwriting with a focus on risk-adjusted returns and asset quality. LTV ratio remains stable at around 54.8%.
*   **Talent Management:** Addressing front-line attrition and focusing on development and career progression for sales officers to improve productivity.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 22.1% YoY growth, aligning with the 20-25% guidance.
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported 17.9% YoY growth for H1 FY'24, indicating sustained profitability.
*   **Disbursements:** While Q-o-Q growth was 18%, the YoY growth of 10% in Q2 FY'24 was softer than anticipated, lower than the prior quarter's recovery momentum. Management attributed this to a lagged impact of sanctions not converting to disbursements (Sanction-to-Disbursement ratio dropped from 90% to 80%) and the ongoing second phase of technology transformation (LMS and ERP). This narrative is consistent with the prior quarter's emphasis on technology impacting operations.
*   **Asset Quality:** Gross Stage 3 at 1.04% and Net Stage 3 at 0.76% remain robust and well-managed, consistent with historical performance. One Day Past Dues improved YoY.
*   **Spread Management:** The spread remains healthy at 5.29%. Management expressed a commitment to maintaining it above 5% despite competitive pricing pressures and rising borrowing costs. The decline in yield (11-12 bps) was attributed to customer retention efforts and pricing pressure on new acquisitions.
*   **Technology Implementation:** Salesforce (LOS) is stabilized. Oracle Fusion ERP-GL is live. LMS is under testing. Management reiterates FY'24 as a full transformation year.
*   **Opex Ratio:** Opex to AUM ratio moderated to 3.65% in H1 FY'24, showing a positive trend from Q1 FY'24. Management anticipates further reduction.
*   **Employee Expenses:** Employee count is stable. Reduction in employee cost QoQ was attributed to one-off costs in the previous quarter (incentive, bonus, senior management resignation). ESOP expenses are subject to accounting principles and attrition.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Technology Transformation Completion:** Targeted by the end of FY'24, including LMS and ERP.
*   **TAT Reduction:** Target of 6-7 days from the current ~13-14 days post full technology implementation.
*   **Opex Ratio Improvement:** Expectation of 20-25 bps annual savings on Opex for 2-3 years starting next fiscal, aiming for less than 3% of AUM.
*   **AUM Growth Guidance:** Reaffirmed 20-25% growth for the fiscal year. Calibrated, moderated growth expected in coming quarters due to ongoing transitions.
*   **Disbursement Momentum:** Confidence in continued growth in August and September, with a commitment to increase non-conventional sourcing channels.
*   **Data Science & Analytics:** Systems expected to go live in approximately one quarter, aiding efficiency and risk-based pricing.
*   **Borrowing Cost Management:** Incremental borrowing expected around 8% to 8.1% in the next two quarters. Strategies include utilizing NHB sanctions, targeting DFIs, and leveraging cheaper Pass-Through Certificates (PTCs).
*   **Spread:** Endeavor to maintain spreads around 5% or above.
*   **Branch Addition:** Plan to add around 20 to 25 branches in H2 FY'24.
*   **Rating Upgrade:** Expectation of a rating upgrade within 12-18 months, potentially opening access to larger domestic institutional investors.
*   **Medium-term Market Share:** Management is comfortable with potentially losing some market share in the short term to focus on quality and risk-adjusted returns, aiming to regain momentum post-transition.
*   **MSME Product Focus:** Continues to maintain a significant portion of the non-home loan book as MSME loans backed by mortgages, with no red flags.
*   **Sanction-to-Disbursement Ratio Normalization:** Expected to improve from current 80% levels over the next couple of quarters.
*   **Development Financial Institutions (DFIs) Funding:** Targeting to maintain 10-15% of total borrowing from DFIs.
*   **Future Funding:** Expectation of a rating upgrade within 12-18 months to access funds from domestic insurance companies, provident funds, and retirement funds.
*   **BT-Out:** Flat at approximately 0.5% per month (6% annualized).
*   **Productivity Improvement:** Expectation of sales officer productivity to increase as front-line stability is achieved post-attrition and onboarding.
*   **Repayment Rates:** Management does not see any unusual trend in repayment rates for non-home loans, indicating them to be similar to home loans.
