## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported a resilient Q1 FY2021 performance amidst the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic. The company demonstrated steady operational metrics, achieving AUM growth of 25% YoY and PAT growth of 11% YoY (Ind-AS) and 33% YoY (IGAAP). Gross NPA remained stable at 0.46% as of June 2020. Management highlighted proactive engagement with borrowers, with a reduction in the moratorium book from 24.0% in April to 17.8% in June. While discretionary expenses were deferred and fixed salaries rationalized, the company maintained a strong liquidity position, with cash and equivalents of Rs. 15,050 million as of June 30, 2020. The focus remains on the underserved semi-urban and rural markets, with continued expansion in branch network and employee base.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continues to target low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas. Home loans constitute 73.4% of the portfolio, and other mortgage loans 26.6%. Self-employed customers remain 65% of the client base.
*   **Network Expansion:** Added 7 branches in Q1 FY2021, with plans to open 20-30 more by year-end, contingent on the COVID-19 situation. Employee count increased to 3,536.
*   **Risk Management:** Maintaining zero exposure to commercial paper. Proactive customer engagement and daily monitoring of collections. Refined underwriting process with video personal discussions and enhanced analytics.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Secured a significant disbursement of Rs. 366 Crores at 4.95% from NHB. Maintains access to diversified and cost-effective long-term financing, with a focus on maintaining current borrowing costs.
*   **Operational Resilience:** Management is rationalizing fixed salaries for a few months and deferring discretionary expenses. Maintaining high liquidity levels to navigate uncertainty.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q1 FY2021):

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 25% YoY growth, consistent with previous periods' growth trajectory.
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported 11% YoY growth (Ind-AS), meeting guidance.
*   **Asset Quality (Gross NPA < 1%):** Maintained Gross NPA at 0.46%, well within the target.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD < 5%):** Reported 1+DPD at 1.50%, a significant improvement from the previous quarter's 2.43% (excluding moratorium impact).
*   **ROA Target (2.5% and above):** Reported 2.55% ROA for Q1 FY2021, meeting the stated target.
*   **Spread:** Maintained spreads at 5.53%, exceeding the target of over 5%.

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:

*   **COVID-19 Impact:** Management acknowledged continued disruption but expressed confidence in the business model's resilience. The tone shifted from cautious optimism in the previous quarter to more confidence in recovery, highlighted by a significant reduction in moratorium and increased collections.
*   **Moratorium Impact:** Management provided detailed breakdowns of moratorium by customer segment and loan type, showing a consistent decline. They emphasized that the moratorium was a deferment and not a waiver, with interest continuing to accrue. The company proactively engaged with customers, with 40% of sticky customers (those not paying in April-June) making payments in the first 42 days of July.
*   **Economic Environment:** Management remains confident in the demand for affordable housing. They highlighted the robust security levels (200% on average) and small average loan installments (Rs. 12,000) as buffers against economic downturns, reducing the need for restructuring.
*   **Funding Environment:** Management highlighted their access to cost-effective funding, including a recent favorable disbursement from NHB. They are comfortable with their current borrowing costs and liquidity position.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Collection Efficiency:** Expects collections to further improve, with a significant portion of the 'sticky' customer base making payments. Management views the current ECL provisioning as sufficient.
*   **Asset Quality:** Aims to maintain Gross NPA below 1% and 1+DPD around 5%.
*   **ROA:** Endeavor to consistently deliver ROA of 2.5% and above.
*   **Cost of Borrowings:** Hopeful to maintain current average cost of borrowing or see further reduction, with ongoing access to cost-effective financing.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to open 20-30 more branches in FY2021, contingent on the COVID-19 situation.
*   **Liquidity:** Plans to maintain current liquidity levels for at least 1-2 quarters before reviewing a potential reduction.
*   **Restructuring:** Not considering restructuring at this point, citing strong collateral, low ticket sizes, and customer behavior.
*   **Credit Costs:** Detailed ECL provisioning is based on granular data and risk assessment, with current provisions deemed sufficient. Reassessments will occur quarterly.
*   **Disbursements:** Disbursements have picked up in July compared to June and are not constrained by liquidity.
*   **Tax Rate:** Expect effective tax rate of 20%-21% for FY21.
*   **Moratorium End:** The moratorium officially ends on September 1, 2020, after which the company will have 90 days to engage with customers before accounts become NPA.
*   **Underwriting:** Introduced enhanced underwriting processes including video personal discussions, time stamping, and analytics for cash flow assessment, with a focus on 'less affected' business profiles.
*   **Customer Profiling (Sticky Accounts):** 60-65% of the 7.7% 'sticky' customer pool (as of June 30) have CIBIL scores above 650. 75% of this 7.7% are self-employed.
