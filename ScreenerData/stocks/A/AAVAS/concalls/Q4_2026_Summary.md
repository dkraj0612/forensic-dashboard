## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) concluded FY'26 with a strong Q4, demonstrating robust growth and improved operational efficiencies. The company reported a 15% YoY AUM growth to INR 234.5 billion for the full year, driven by a 16% YoY increase in Q4 disbursements to INR 23.5 billion and 11% growth for the full year to INR 67.8 billion. Net profit saw an 18% YoY increase in Q4 to INR 1.82 billion, with a 17% YoY growth in Net Interest Income (NII). Net Worth also continued to grow robustly by 16% YoY. Aavas maintained its strong asset quality, with 1+ DPD improving significantly to 3.17% and Gross NPA at 1.05%. The company expanded its network to 435 branches and successfully raised INR 975 crore from a multinational financial institution, reinforcing external confidence. The new CEO, Manu Singh, outlined a strategic focus on responsible scaling, enhanced operating efficiency, and delivering sustainable risk-adjusted returns, anchored by a customer-first and credit-first philosophy.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Leadership Transition & Vision:** New CEO Manu Singh emphasizes responsible scaling, operating efficiency, risk-adjusted returns, customer-centricity, and a credit-first philosophy.
*   **Growth Aspirations:** Target of 20%+ AUM growth and high teens ROE in the long term. Focus on sharp execution across product, channel, and geographies.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Continued focus on high-potential states like Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu for branch additions, alongside strengthening presence in established markets.
*   **Product & Pricing Strategy:** Emphasis on risk-adjusted pricing and disciplined underwriting within the assessed income segment. Aim to optimize yields through strategic product placement.
*   **Distribution Network:** Rebuilding and strengthening the direct sourcing muscle through branch-driven models, while acknowledging the importance of indirect sourcing channels.
*   **Digital & Technology Integration:** Continued investment in technology for underwriting, acquisitions, and collections. Focus on AI/GenAI for enhanced efficiency and cost reduction.
*   **Liability Management:** Strategic shift towards EBLR-linked instruments and market benchmarks for faster repricing and cost optimization. Successful INR 975 crore NCD placement.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Focus on improving per-person productivity and reducing leakages in the sourcing funnel. Aim to bring Opex to AUM ratio below 3% in the medium term.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** The reported 15% YoY AUM growth for FY'26 aligns with previous expectations. The company has set a higher aspiration of 20%+ for the medium term.
*   **Disbursements:** FY'26 disbursements grew 11% YoY, slightly below the 15%+ pace some peers achieved, but the Q4 growth of 16% YoY and 36% QoQ indicates an improving operating rhythm. Management attributes the full-year performance to transformation initiatives and a calibrated approach.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management's narrative of maintaining pristine asset quality remains consistent and is supported by the improved 1+DPD (3.17%) and GNPA (1.05%) figures, well within guided ranges.
*   **Profitability:** Net profit growth and NIM expansion (44 bps sequential in Q4) demonstrate profitable growth driven by NII improvements. ROE has shown sequential improvement.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** The Opex to Assets ratio increased YoY in FY'26 due to investments in branch expansion, ESOPs, and a slower-than-anticipated growth in FY'26. Management acknowledges this and expects improvement with scaled growth.
*   **Borrowing Costs & Liability Management:** The strategy to shift to EBLR-linked instruments has yielded benefits, with a 62 bps improvement in the overall cost of funds for FY'26. The successful INR 975 crore NCD placement highlights strong liability management.
*   **Spread Management:** The FY'26 spread of 5.20% is stable year-on-year, with management confident in maintaining spreads above 5%+.
*   **Branch Expansion:** The addition of 31 branches in Q4 FY'26 and reaching 435 branches by year-end aligns with the strategy of geographical expansion.
*   **Technology Implementation:** Investments in core systems are complete, with ongoing focus on AI/GenAI for collections and underwriting efficiency.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **AUM Growth Guidance (Medium-term):** Aspiration to consistently deliver 20%+ AUM growth.
*   **ROE Target (Long-term):** Aiming for high teens ROE.
*   **Disbursement Growth (FY'27 onwards):** Focus on sharper execution and rebuilding direct sourcing muscle is expected to drive improved volume growth.
*   **Spread Outlook:** Confident of maintaining spreads above 5%+.
*   **Credit Costs Guidance:** Continued guidance to keep credit costs below 25 bps on a sustainable basis.
*   **Opex to AUM Ratio Target:** Aiming for below 3% in a 2-to-3-year horizon.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Continued focus on adding branches in UP, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu, and strengthening presence in other high-potential states.
*   **Technology Investment:** Ongoing small investments in AI/GenAI for sharpening underwriting, acquisitions, and collections, expected to reduce manpower opex.
