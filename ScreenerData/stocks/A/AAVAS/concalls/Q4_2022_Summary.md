## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported a strong Q4 FY22 and full-year performance, marked by the highest-ever quarterly disbursement in Q4 FY22 (Rs. 1,287.2 crore, 27% YoY growth). Despite a cautious approach, the company demonstrated robust AUM growth of 20% YoY to Rs. 113,502 million as of March 31, 2022. A significant highlight was the sharp improvement in asset quality, with 1+ Days Past Due (DPD) reducing to 4.47% in March 2022, down from 6.45% in December 2021, surpassing previous targets. The Gross Stage 3 ratio stood at a healthy 0.99% as of March 31, 2022, though this includes 0.31% due to the RBI's IRACP norms harmonization. The company maintained its long-term credit rating of AA- with a Positive outlook from both ICRA and CARE. Management reiterated its commitment to sustainable growth, asset quality, and operational efficiency, while also focusing on digital transformation for scalability.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Cautious Growth with Quality Focus:** Continues to prioritize asset quality and sustainable operating metrics, balancing growth with prudence.
*   **Customer Segmentation:** Home loans remain dominant (72.1%), other mortgage loans at 27.9%. Salaried (40.0%) and self-employed (60.0%) customer mix remains consistent.
*   **Network Expansion:** Ongoing branch network expansion, adding 34 branches in FY22 to reach 314, supporting future growth.
*   **Risk Management:** Maintains zero exposure to commercial paper and emphasizes robust collection efforts to manage asset quality.
*   **Funding Strategy:** Leverages diversified and cost-effective long-term financing, with a focus on maintaining a stable borrowing cost.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Investing significantly in digital and technology infrastructure to enhance scalability, improve customer/employee journey, reduce Turnaround Time (TAT), and achieve operational leverage.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q3 FY22 Context & New Targets):

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 20% YoY growth for FY22, consistent with previous indications and the stated target of 20-22%. **Consistent.**
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported 29% YoY growth for FY22 (like-for-like), exceeding the previous quarter's growth and indicating strong profitability. **Exceeded expectations.**
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD):** Achieved the target of improving 1+DPD to below 5% by March 2022, reaching 4.47%. This represents a significant improvement from 6.45% in December 2021. **Exceeded expectations.**
*   **Asset Quality (Gross Stage 3):** Maintained a low Gross Stage 3 ratio, managing the impact of the RBI circular. **Consistent.**
*   **Spread:** Maintained a healthy spread of 5.77%, consistent with previous levels, though management indicated plans to pass on benefits to customers as rates rise and costs normalize. **Consistent.**
*   **Disbursements:** Q4 FY22 disbursements (27% YoY) and FY22 disbursements (36% YoY) show strong momentum, exceeding previous growth rates and indicating successful business execution. **Exceeded expectations.**
*   **ROA/ROE:** FY22 ROA of 3.6% and ROE of 13.7% are strong and improving, with management guiding for further increases. **Exceeded expectations.**
*   **Opex:** While Opex-to-ATA has been elevated due to investments, management expects cost efficiencies to emerge, aligning with previous discussions on operating leverage. **Consistent with stated investment plans.**

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:

*   **Asset Quality Improvement:** Management's tone on asset quality improvement is highly positive, highlighting the proactive approach to collections and managing delinquencies.
*   **RBI Circular Impact:** The impact of the RBI notification on IRACP norms continues to be transparently reported, with specific figures provided for GNPA reclassification.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Management acknowledges increasing competitive intensity, particularly in balance transfers (BTs), but remains confident in their strategy and niche.
*   **Inflation Impact:** Management addressed concerns regarding rising construction material costs, stating that the impact on affordability is managed by increased customer income growth and efficiencies in labor costs.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Disbursement Growth:** Aiming for 20-22% YoY growth sustainably.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD):** Target to maintain 1+DPD below 5%.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Significant investment in digital and technology infra for scalability, aiming to reduce TAT by 50-75% further and complete the journey in the next 12-15 months. Expected to double technology opex/depreciation to approx. Rs. 20 crore per year for the next 3-4 years.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** Expecting 30-35 bps cost efficiency (opex moderation) in the current fiscal year.
*   **Spread Management:** Aiming to maintain a long-term spread of around 5%, with decisions on passing on borrowing cost benefits made based on ALCO and Board guidance and real impact on borrowing costs.
*   **ROA/ROE:** Guidance for continued improvement in ROE by 100-150 bps YoY. Sustainable ROA target of 2.5%-2.6% and ROE of 15-16% anticipated.
*   **Branch Productivity:** Expecting normal business functioning from branches, with a 3-year lag typically required for 100% efficiency. New branches are structured for specific business potential (e.g., C and D category branches for yield and new-to-customer focus).
*   **Subsidiary Business (Aavas Finserv):** Applied for NBFC license; intend to focus on secured MSME and customer lifestyle products.
*   **Affluent Segment:** The vertical has existed for 4 years, contributing 30-35 crore out of 400-450 crore business, with no strategic shift towards mid or affluent segments planned.
*   **Balance Sheet Matching:** Maintaining perfectly matched balance sheet on fixed and floating rates for interest rate risk management.
*   **Credit Cost:** Expects sustainable credit cost around 0.5% (historical guidance).
