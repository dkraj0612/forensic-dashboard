## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported solid Q3 FY'25 results, demonstrating resilience and continued growth momentum. Assets Under Management (AUM) grew by 20% YoY to INR 192 billion, maintaining the company's guided growth trajectory. Disbursements saw a significant sequential pick-up, growing 17% YoY to INR 16 billion for the quarter and INR 41 billion for the first nine months of FY'25. This performance indicates a strong recovery from the earlier disbursement slowdown attributed to LMS implementation. Net profit for 9MFY'25 grew by 21% YoY to INR 4.20 billion. The company has successfully completed the upgrade of its core technology platforms, establishing a foundation for future scalability and efficiency. Asset quality remains a key strength, with 1+DPD at 3.85% and Gross NPAs at 1.14%, staying within management's guided ranges. Management remains focused on cost optimization and operational efficiency, evidenced by a 42 bps YoY improvement in the Opex-to-Assets ratio to 3.21% in 9MFY'25. The company is also accelerating its branch expansion, opening 6 new branches in 9MFY'25 and planning over 20 more in the current quarter, predominantly in Karnataka and UP.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Digital Transformation & Efficiency:** Completed major tech platform upgrades (Oracle Flexcube, Salesforce), now stabilizing and expected to drive sustainable, scalable, and profitable growth. Focus on leveraging these platforms for strengthened governance, scale, cost optimization, and operational efficiencies. Login-to-sanction TAT improved to 7 days.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Accelerated branch expansion, opening 6 new branches in 9MFY'25 and planning 20+ more in Q4 FY'25, with a focus on Karnataka and UP. This is part of a strategy to strengthen presence in Tier 3 to Tier 5 locations.
*   **Product & Yield Enhancement:** Continued focus on risk-adjusted returns, with incremental business yields increasing by over 25 bps across products. Increased focus on micro-MSME loans and loans below INR 10 lakhs ticket size to improve yields and spreads. Home loan yields are in the 11.5%-12% range, with MSME and LAP yielding higher.
*   **Liability Management:** Successfully raised INR 6.3 billion NCDs from IFC, the largest NCD raised to date, earmarked for promoting green homes. Maintaining a diversified liability profile (50.3% term loans, 24.8% assignment/securitization, 15.9% NHB, 9.0% debt capital markets). Progressing on a co-lending tie-up with a PSU Bank. Approximately 51% of bank borrowings are linked to external benchmarks (Repo, TB, MIBOR, <3-month MCLR), allowing for faster repricing.
*   **Asset Quality Maintenance:** Pristine asset quality remains a priority, with 1+DPD below 4% and GNPA below 1.25%. Vintage states and emerging states show strong performance. Risk management practices are continuously monitored and refined.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Remarkable improvement in Opex to Assets ratio, driven by cost optimization strategies and operating leverage from technology implementation.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 20% YoY AUM growth, aligning with the previous guidance. Management reiterated a long-term guidance of 20-25% AUM growth.
*   **Disbursements:** The narrative around disbursements shifted positively, highlighting a strong sequential pick-up (23% sequentially, 17% YoY) and confidence for H2 FY'25. This addresses concerns from the previous quarter regarding the impact of LMS implementation. January's performance is also tracking at 15-20% growth.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management consistently highlighted the pristine asset quality, with 1+DPD at 3.85% and GNPA at 1.14%, well within guided ranges. The CRO's commentary indicated no alarming trends across geographies or ticket sizes.
*   **Profitability:** Net profit growth of 21% YoY for 9MFY'25 was strong, driven by NII and operating leverage. ROA and ROE showed incremental improvements.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** The Opex-to-Assets ratio improvement of 42 bps YoY to 3.21% in 9MFY'25 significantly exceeded the previously stated annual guidance of ~20 bps, demonstrating strong cost control and operating leverage benefits from technology.
*   **Borrowing Costs & Liability Management:** While the average borrowing cost increased slightly to 8.41% (9MFY'25), the company has successfully raised funds at competitive rates from IFC and maintains diversified funding. The increasing proportion of benchmark-linked borrowings positions them well for potential rate cuts.
*   **Spread Management:** The calculated spread improved sequentially by 4 bps to 5.72% in Q3 FY'25. Management is focused on increasing disbursement yields to sustain and improve spreads.
*   **BT-out Rate:** Management reported a best-in-class BT-out rate of approximately 5.4%, aided by predictive models and technology. This aligns with their stated focus on customer retention.
*   **Technology Implementation:** The successful go-live and stabilization of key tech platforms (Oracle Flexcube, Salesforce) were highlighted as a significant achievement enabling future efficiencies.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **AUM Growth Guidance:** Reiterated guidance of 20-25% AUM growth on a long-term basis. Confident in delivering guided AUM growth of ~20% for the current fiscal year (FY'25).
*   **Disbursement Momentum:** Confident in maintaining the Q3 disbursement run rate and pushing for growth quarter-on-quarter, building scale with quality.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plans to accelerate branch expansion, opening over 20+ branches in Q4 FY'25, predominantly in Karnataka and UP. Aspiration to become a Pan-India player.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** Continued commitment to cost optimization strategy. Aspiration to reach INR 500 billion in AUM in the next 4-5 years, implying continued focus on scaling efficiently.
*   **Spread Outlook:** Aiming to improve spreads, with a realistic possibility of crossing 5.20-5.30% in the future, dependent on the macroeconomic environment and rate cuts benefiting borrowing costs.
*   **Yield Improvement:** Target to increase the share of loans below INR 10 lakhs ticket size to around 45% (currently 30-35%), which typically carry higher yields and spreads.
*   **Credit Costs:** Guidance remains below 25 bps on a sustainable basis, with current levels around 15 bps for 9MFY'25. Confident in maintaining this due to strong underwriting and collection practices.
*   **FY'26 Disbursements:** Expecting disbursement growth to be higher than the usual run rate of 17-18% in FY'26, due to improved efficiencies and capacity building.
*   **Provisioning:** ECL provisioning model has been updated to the Deloitte model, which incorporates monthly data and macroeconomic factors. Current ECL provisioning is around 65 bps of total AUM and is expected to remain at this level based on current assessment.
*   **M&A/Subsidiaries:** Applied to close one subsidiary due to operational difficulties.
*   **Technology Capitalization:** IT project spends for LOS, LMS, and data warehouse transformation are being capitalized, totaling approximately INR 60 crores to be amortized over seven years.
