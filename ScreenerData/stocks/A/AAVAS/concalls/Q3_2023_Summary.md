## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) is embarking on its "Aavas 3.0" journey, signifying a strategic evolution into a long-term, institutionalized affordable housing finance player. This new phase is driven by a strong emphasis on people and technology. A key development is the restructuring of leadership, splitting the Managing Director (MD) and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) roles to enhance focus on long-term strategy and day-to-day execution, respectively. Mr. Sushil Kumar Agarwal will continue as MD, focusing on overall business strategy, while Mr. Sachinder Bhinder, previously CEO of Aavas MSME, takes on the CEO role to drive core execution, leveraging his experience from Kotak Mahindra Bank. The company is also elevating other key leaders to reinforce its institutional framework. A significant investment in digital transformation (INR 120-150 crores) is underway, including the implementation of Salesforce for Loan Origination System (LOS), Flexcube for Loan Management System (LMS), and Oracle Fusion for ERP. These initiatives are aimed at achieving non-linear growth, improving customer experience, and enhancing operational efficiency. The company continues to focus on its core business while strategically expanding its MSME portfolio and distribution network.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aavas 3.0 Transformation:** A strategic shift to institutionalize the business and become a leading affordable housing finance player, powered by people and technology.
*   **Leadership Restructuring:** Splitting MD and CEO roles to allow for distinct focus on long-term strategy and day-to-day execution.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Significant investment (INR 120-150 crores) in core IT systems including Salesforce (LOS), Flexcube (LMS), and Oracle Fusion (ERP) to drive scalability, reduce TAT, improve customer experience, and enhance profitability insights.
*   **MSME & Non-Home Loan Focus:** Continuation of building the MSME portfolio within the non-home loan segment, offering longer tenures (7 years) and targeting business owners with incomes of INR 20,000 to INR 1 lakh per month.
*   **Distribution Network Expansion:** Maintaining an annual branch expansion strategy of 30-35 branches, with plans to enter 4 new states over 5 years. Focus on contiguous distribution and digitized land records.
*   **Green Housing Initiatives:** Achieved EDGE certification for affordable green self-build homes in collaboration with IFC, aiming for 15-20% of funded homes to be green-certified within 5 years.
*   **Customer Segmentation:** Focus on direct-to-customer (D2C) businesses for MSME loans.
*   **Product Diversification (Future Potential):** Readiness to offer additional product lines like car against property or overdraft against property if regulator approvals are obtained.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q3 FY23 Context & Previous Guidance)

*   **Disbursement Growth:** Achieved strong YoY growth in disbursements for Q3 FY23 (+26.4%) and 9M FY23 (+48.7%), aligning with and exceeding previous growth trajectories.
*   **AUM Growth:** Maintained healthy AUM growth at 23.3% YoY, consistent with historical growth focus.
*   **Asset Quality:** Gross Stage 3 at 1.13% is as reported, with management clearly explaining the impact of the RBI notification on up to 90 DPD assets (0.19%). Net Stage 3 remains stable at 0.87%.
*   **Spread Management:** The spread of 5.75% demonstrates effective management in a rising interest rate environment.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Continued with branch expansion, adding 23 branches in 12 months, consistent with stated strategy.
*   **Digital Transformation Progress:** Significant investment and progress reported on key IT projects, indicating strong commitment to stated digital transformation goals.
*   **MSME Portfolio Growth:** MSME now constitutes 40% of the non-home loan portfolio, demonstrating strategic focus and execution in this segment.
*   **Loan Break-even:** Management reiterates that loans are profitable from the first year due to upfront fees, a consistent narrative.

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds

*   **Interest Rate Environment:** Management acknowledges continued repo rate increases and has passed on rate hikes to customers (125 bps over 9 months, with an additional 35 bps in January 2023). The focus remains on maintaining stable margins.
*   **Asset Quality Classification:** The impact of the RBI notification on classifying assets up to 90 DPD as GNPA is explicitly stated as influencing the Gross Stage 3 figures.
*   **Opex Elevated Due to Investment:** The narrative continues to acknowledge elevated opex ratios (around 3.5%-3.6%) due to ongoing technology transformation and capacity building, with a forward-looking expectation of decrease.
*   **Leadership Transition:** The announcement of "Aavas 3.0" and the split of MD and CEO roles is presented as a proactive step to institutionalize growth and manage increasing complexity.
*   **NBFC License Application:** Management clarified that the NBFC license application for Aavas Finserv was returned by RBI, and the focus remains solely on Aavas Financiers business.
