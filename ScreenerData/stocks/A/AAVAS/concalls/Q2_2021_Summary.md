## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) demonstrated a robust recovery and growth in Q2 FY2022, with significant improvements in disbursements and profitability. Following the disruptions of the second COVID-19 wave, the company has seen a marked improvement in economic activity and borrower repayment behavior. AUM grew 21% YoY to Rs. 101,481 million, and Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a substantial increase of 31% YoY (Ind-AS) for H1 FY2022. The company is actively expanding its reach, adding 38 new branches in the last 12 months, bringing the total to 297. Asset quality has also shown improvement, with 1+ DPD declining to 8.88% from 12.67% in the previous quarter. Management expressed confidence in continued growth and asset quality improvement, aiming to return to pre-COVID levels of delinquency by March 2022, barring any third wave.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continues to serve low- and middle-income customers in semi-urban and rural areas. Home loans constitute 72.1% of the portfolio, with self-employed customers making up 60.2%.
*   **Network Expansion:** Added 38 branches in the past 12 months to reach 297 branches. Plans to continue this expansion, opening 30-35 branches annually. Focus on entering new states (Karnataka and Odisha in FY22) while also expanding in existing ones.
*   **Risk Management:** Maintaining zero exposure to commercial paper. Proactive customer engagement and daily collection monitoring. Enhanced underwriting processes, including video personal discussions and analytics. Implementing RBI's Resolution Framework 2.0 for specific borrower accounts.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Access to diversified and cost-effective long-term financing, with a focus on maintaining current borrowing costs.
*   **Operational Resilience & Efficiency:** Continued investment in technology, manpower, and distribution. Aiming to improve opex percentage to total assets by 25-30 bps YoY over the next two to three years.
*   **Product Mix Focus:** Shifting focus within non-housing loans towards MSME loans over LAP, due to better end-use control and shorter tenures, while maintaining a 70-75% home loan to 25-30% non-home loan ratio.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q2 FY2022 & H1 FY2022):

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 21% YoY growth, aligning with historical 20-25% trajectory.
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported significant YoY growth (31% Ind-AS for H1 FY22), exceeding previous periods and indicating a strong recovery.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD):** Marked improvement from 12.67% to 8.88%, moving towards the target of reaching Mar-21 levels by Mar-22. Management is targeting a monthly reduction of ~0.5% in 1+DPD.
*   **ROA/ROE:** Achieved ROA of 3.9% and ROE of 14.66% in Q2 FY22, and 3.25% ROA and 12.25% ROE in H1 FY22, which is at or above historical target levels.
*   **Spread:** Maintained spreads at 5.73%, exceeding the previous target of over 5%, even after passing on 25 bps due to COVID.

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:

*   **COVID-19 Impact:** Management expressed strong confidence in the business model's resilience and the improving economic environment due to calibrated reopening and vaccination drives. The tone is significantly more optimistic than in previous quarters, highlighting a robust and continuous improvement in economic activity.
*   **Moratorium & Collections:** The reduction in 1+DPD is seen as a direct result of improved borrower behavior and proactive engagement, with a significant portion of the customer base now making current month installments.
*   **Economic Environment:** Management remains confident in the demand for affordable housing and sees robust security levels and small average loan installments as buffers.
*   **Funding Environment:** The company has secured improved credit ratings (AA- / Positive from CARE) and maintains access to cost-effective funding, comfortable with current borrowing costs.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **1+DPD Target:** Aiming to reach Mar-21 levels (around 6.4%) by Mar-22, with an anticipated monthly reduction of approximately 0.5%, assuming no third wave.
*   **Disbursement Growth:** Expects the second half of FY22 to be stronger, potentially exceeding the typical 60-65% share of annual disbursements.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to continue opening 30-35 branches annually, with 20-25% of these in new states and the remainder in existing states.
*   **AUM Growth:** Committed to 20-25% AUM growth trajectory over the next three years, doubling the book from Rs. 10,000 Crores to Rs. 20,000 Crores.
*   **Spread Target:** Aiming to maintain a spread of around 5% in the medium term, with cost savings in opex to offset potential spread compression.
*   **Credit Cost:** Hopeful to contain NPA around 1% and credit cost at 20-25 bps.
*   **Opex to AUM:** Expects to improve opex percentage to total assets by 25-30 bps YoY over the next two to three years.
*   **ROE Target:** Aiming for a consistent ROE of 15-18% over the next two to three years.
*   **New States:** Continued expansion philosophy of developing 4 new states every 5 years; currently have entered Karnataka and Odisha.
*   **MSME Lending:** Increasing focus on MSME loans within the non-housing segment, characterized by shorter tenures and better end-use verification.
*   **Employee Growth:** Anticipates employee growth to be around 15-20% vis-à-vis AUM growth of 20-25%, with increasing leverage of technology.
*   **Restructuring:** Implementing restructuring as per RBI's Resolution Framework 2.0, with approximately Rs. 148.2 Crores classified as Stage 2 with associated provisioning.
*   **Tax Rate:** Expects an effective tax rate of 20-21% for FY22.
*   **Branch Maturity:** Considers a branch mature when it achieves positive ROE, with most branches achieving this within 12 months (metro branches taking 2-3 years). Over 85% of branches are currently ROE positive.
