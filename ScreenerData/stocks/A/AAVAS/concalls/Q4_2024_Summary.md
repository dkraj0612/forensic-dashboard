## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) delivered a strong Q4 FY'24 and full-year performance, demonstrating robust AUM growth and maintaining excellent asset quality. AUM grew by 22% year-on-year to INR 173 billion, with Q4 FY'24 disbursements reaching an all-time high of INR 18.93 billion, a significant 20% YoY and 39% QoQ increase. Full-year net profit for FY'24 was INR 4.91 billion, a 15% YoY rise. The company achieved an all-time low 1+DPD of 3.12% and a GNPA of 0.94%, reinforcing its commitment to asset quality. Key technology initiatives, including Salesforce LOS stabilization and Oracle Fusion ERP go-live, are progressing, with the Lead Management System (LMS) and Loan Management System (LMS) nearing completion. This has led to a notable reduction in login-to-sanction TAT to approximately 8 days in Q4 FY'24. Management reaffirmed its FY'25 AUM growth guidance of 22-25% and reiterated its commitment to doubling AUM in three years. Liability management remains a focus, with a diversified borrowing mix and innovative funding tie-ups.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Technology Transformation:** Salesforce LOS stabilized with over 1.6 lakh applications processed. Oracle Fusion ERP is live. Lead Management System (LMS) on Salesforce and Loan Management System (LMS) on Oracle Flexcube are in advanced testing and nearing go-live, targeting further TAT reduction.
*   **Growth Focus:** Reaffirmed AUM growth guidance of 22-25% for FY'25. Aim to double AUM in three years. Focus on diversified geographic growth with emphasis on Karnataka and UP.
*   **Opex-Light Sourcing:** Increasing contribution from channels like eMitra, RRO, and Mitra, reaching 13% of disbursements.
*   **Productivity & Efficiency:** Leveraging technology to improve productivity and achieve an Opex-to-Asset ratio below 3%.
*   **Asset Quality:** Continued focus on risk-based underwriting and granular collections, leveraging predictive analytics to maintain industry-leading asset quality.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 22% YoY growth in FY'24, aligning with previous guidance and reaffirming future growth targets. Management expressed confidence in sustaining H1 FY'25 momentum.
*   **Disbursements:** Demonstrated a strong rebound in Q4 FY'24 with the highest ever quarterly disbursement, validating previous expectations and showing strong sequential improvement.
*   **Technology Implementation:** Significant progress reported with Salesforce LOS and Oracle Fusion ERP stabilization. Upcoming LMS go-lives are on track with previous timelines for full transformation benefits, leading to login-to-sanction TAT improvement to approximately 8 days in Q4 FY'24.
*   **Asset Quality:** Maintained pristine asset quality with all-time low 1+DPD and improved GNPA, consistently meeting and exceeding targets. Management reiterated guidance to keep 1+DPD below 5% and GNPA around 1%.
*   **Spread Management:** Maintained spreads around 5% (5.06% in FY'24), in line with guidance, despite competitive pricing. Management reiterated commitment to remain in this range and focus on improving spreads by increasing the contribution of sub-Rs 10 lakh ticket size products.
*   **Opex Ratio:** Improved by 10 bps in FY'24 to 3.58%, showing progress towards the targeted reduction. Management aims to bring the Opex-to-Asset ratio below 3% in the near-to-medium term.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Incremental borrowing costs remain slightly above 8%, indicating that borrowing costs have likely peaked, consistent with prior expectations. Management anticipates positive impact from potential rate cuts in Q2 or early Q3 FY'25.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Technology Transformation Completion:** Lead Management System (LMS) on Salesforce to be live in Q1 FY'25, fully operational by Q2 FY'25. Loan Management System (LMS) on Oracle Flexcube to go live in the first week of July.
*   **AUM Growth Guidance:** Reaffirmed 22-25% AUM growth for FY'25. Guidance to double AUM in the next three years.
*   **Disbursement Momentum:** Confidence in sustaining H1 FY'25 momentum due to strong logins and tech transformation.
*   **Opex Ratio Improvement:** Aim to bring Opex-to-Asset ratio below 3% in the near-to-medium term, with an anticipated 20+ basis point saving in FY'25 on a full-year basis.
*   **ROA Target:** Delivering a sustainable ROA of 3%.
*   **Productivity Improvement:** Expected to improve due to technology adoption and optimization of manpower.
*   **Spread Maintenance:** Commitment to maintain spreads in the range of around 5% on an overall AUM basis. Focus on increasing the contribution of the sub-Rs 10 lakh ticket size product.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to add approximately 10% more branches in FY'25, with a specific focus on Karnataka and UP, while exploring opportunities in existing markets and leveraging RRO models.
*   **Borrowing Cost Management:** Expectation that borrowing costs have peaked. Rate cuts by RBI and Fed are anticipated to provide positive impact from Q2 or early Q3.
*   **Asset Quality:** Maintain 1+DPD below 5% and GNPA around 1%.
*   **Yield Management:** Focus on pricing risk correctly and achieving risk-adjusted returns, not state-based pricing.
*   **MSME & LAP Yield:** Approximately 2.50% to 3.00% difference between home loan yields (around 12.5%) and MSME/LAP yields (around 15%).
*   **Geographical Concentration:** Top four states account for around 82% of AUM.
