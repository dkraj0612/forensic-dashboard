## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported a solid Q1 FY2020, demonstrating resilience and consistent delivery in a turbulent market for housing finance. The company achieved a 46% year-on-year growth in Assets Under Management (AUM) to Rs. 63,623 million, and a 23% year-on-year increase in disbursements to Rs. 6,710 million. Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a robust 50% year-on-year growth to Rs. 453 million. Management highlighted the company's strong liquidity buffers, diversified borrowing profile, and robust capital base as key strengths in navigating the challenging environment. AAVAS continues its strategic focus on the low- and middle-income, self-employed customer segment in semi-urban and rural areas, specializing in small-ticket housing loans. The company reiterated its commitment to maintaining asset quality, with Gross NPA at 0.58% and 1+DPD at 4.25%.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continued emphasis on low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas, with an average home loan ticket size of Rs. 8.99 lakhs and non-home loan ticket size of Rs. 7.16 lakhs.
*   **Network Expansion:** Opened 1 new branch in Q1 FY2020, with plans to add approximately 40 branches annually, typically in Q2 and Q3. Branches are expected to breakeven within 6-12 months.
*   **Risk Management:** Commitment to maintaining asset quality with targets of GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%. Robust in-house underwriting with a significant number of chartered accountants and civil engineers, alongside external legal and engineering firms.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Access to 35 lenders, with 89% of borrowings from long-term term loans, assignments, and NHB refinancing. Zero exposure to commercial paper. New sanctions of Rs. 10,000 million received at an average rate of 9%.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Expectation of continued improvement in operating efficiencies through manpower utilization, technology, and data analytics, leading to opex reduction.
*   **Product Mix:** Home loans constitute approximately 75% of the portfolio, and other mortgage loans 25%. Self-employed customers represent approximately 64.9% of the client base.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Performance Against Stated Targets (Q1 FY2020):**

*   **Disbursement Growth:** Achieved 23% YoY growth, which management stated is on track for the full year target of 25-30%, citing historical Q1-H2 business distribution (40%-60%). Q1 budget was met.
*   **Profitability (PAT Growth):** Reported 50% YoY growth, exceeding expectations.
*   **Asset Quality (GNPA < 1%):** Maintained GNPA at 0.58%, well within target.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD < 5%):** Achieved 1+DPD of 4.25%, meeting target. Management noted this has improved from 4.9% in Q1 FY2019.
*   **ROA Target (2.5% and above):** Reported 3.17% ROA for Q1 FY2020, exceeding target.
*   **Spread:** Maintained spreads above 5% at 5.06%, consistent with historical performance. Management compensated for a sequential NIM reduction (Q4: 8.52% to Q1: 8.10%) due to higher assignment income in Q4 by focusing on opex reduction.

**Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:**

*   Management acknowledged the "turbulent phase" and "prolonged liquidity crisis" in the housing finance sector.
*   Despite a general slowdown and increased competition from larger players (SBI, HDFC), AAVAS noted that competition from smaller peers has reduced.
*   Management indicated they are increasing filtering levels at the ground level to be cautious despite increased lead flow, emphasizing consistency in business and disbursement numbers.
*   The tone remained confident and consistent with their risk-led, customer-centric approach, emphasizing their ability to navigate challenges due to strong liquidity, diversified funding, and a robust capital base.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to open approximately 40 branches annually, with additions typically in Q2 and Q3.
*   **Opex Ratio Moderation:** Expect to improve operating efficiencies and achieve 40-50 basis points opex improvement year-on-year. Expect opex growth to be less than 8-10% of income growth, indicating positive jaws.
*   **Borrowing Cost:** Fresh sanctions received at an average rate of 9%, a marginal 20-30 bps increase compared to the previous year's fresh borrowing.
*   **ALM Management:** Maintaining a positive ALM profile with sufficient liquidity, with around Rs. 2,000 crores available fund after meeting obligations as of June 30.
*   **Asset Quality:** Endeavor to maintain GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%.
*   **ROA:** Aim to consistently deliver ROA of 2.5% and above.
*   **Customer Retention:** BT out rate reduced to 0.3% per month from 0.8% per month in Q4 FY19.
*   **Loan Yields:**
    *   Home Loan Incremental Yield: 13.3%
    *   Non-Home Loan Incremental Yield: 15.5%
    *   Average Portfolio Yield: 13.85%
