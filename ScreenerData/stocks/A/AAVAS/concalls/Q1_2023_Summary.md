## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) demonstrated strong performance in Q1 FY23, characterized by significant disbursement growth and robust AUM expansion. The company reported a 136% YoY increase in disbursements to Rs. 10,936 million, reaching 85% of the previous year's seasonally strong Q4. AUM grew by 24% YoY to Rs. 118.94 billion as of June 30, 2022. Profitability saw a substantial boost with a 49% YoY increase in PAT to Rs. 892 million. Asset quality remained a focus, with 1+ DPD at 4.67% and 90+ DPD at 0.82%. The Gross Stage 3 ratio stood at 1.08%, including 0.26% reclassified due to RBI's IRACP norms. A significant highlight was the upgrade in its long-term credit rating by ICRA from AA- (Positive outlook) to AA (Stable outlook). Management emphasized continued investment in technology and distribution to drive sustainable growth and operational efficiency.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Calibrated Growth with Quality Focus:** Continues to prioritize controlled growth, maintaining operating metrics and asset quality.
*   **Customer Segmentation:** Home loans constitute 71.1%, and other mortgage loans 28.9%. The salaried to self-employed mix remains consistent at 39.9% and 60.1% respectively.
*   **Network Expansion:** Ongoing branch network expansion continues, with 34 new branches added in the last 12 months to reach 318, supporting future business development.
*   **Risk Management:** Maintains zero exposure to commercial papers and focuses on robust collection mechanisms.
*   **Funding Strategy:** Diversified and cost-effective long-term financing remains a priority, with a focus on managing borrowing costs.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Significant investments are being made in digital and technology infrastructure to enhance scalability, customer experience, and reduce turnaround times.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q1 FY23 Context & Previous Guidance)

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 24% YoY growth for Q1 FY23, aligning with the previously stated sustainable growth target of 20-25%. **Consistent.**
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported 49% YoY growth for Q1 FY23, indicating strong profitability and exceeding expectations for the quarter. **Exceeded expectations.**
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD):** Maintained 1+DPD below 5% (4.67%), consistent with the target of keeping it below 5%. **Consistent.**
*   **Asset Quality (90+ DPD):** Maintained 90+ DPD below 1% (0.82%), consistent with the target. **Consistent.**
*   **Gross Stage 3:** Maintained a low ratio (1.08%), managing the impact of the RBI circular on IRACP norms. **Consistent.**
*   **Spread:** Maintained a healthy spread of 5.81%, demonstrating effective management of borrowing costs and portfolio yields. **Consistent.**
*   **Disbursements:** Q1 FY23 disbursements showed a significant 136% YoY growth, indicating strong business momentum and effective capacity utilization. **Exceeded expectations.**
*   **ROA/ROE:** Q1 FY23 ROA of 3.2% and ROE of 12.5% are strong, with management indicating a trajectory for further improvement. **Consistent with stated investment plans.**
*   **Opex:** Operating expenses remain elevated due to ongoing investments in technology and distribution, as previously communicated. Management expects this to lead to cost efficiencies in the medium to long term. **Consistent with stated investment plans.**

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds

*   **Interest Rate Environment:** Management acknowledged the rising interest rate environment, with the RBI hiking repo rates. They have proactively increased their Prime Lending Rate (PLR) in response.
*   **Asset Quality:** While overall asset quality metrics are strong, management noted a sequential deterioration in the 90+ DPD bucket, attributing it to a Rs. 16 crore slip from the restructured book, which was anticipated.
*   **Competition:** Management views the increasing competition from larger, highly-rated players as manageable, believing their niche in specific geographies and use of technology will help maintain their competitive advantage. They see ample room for growth for all players.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Incremental borrowing costs were noted to be lower (5.65%) in the quarter due to specific negotiated deals and NHB borrowing. However, management anticipates these costs will rise in line with the general market trend.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines

*   **Disbursement Growth:** Aiming for 23-25% growth for FY23, with a run-rate of approximately Rs. 400 crores per month.
*   **Asset Quality:** Target to maintain 1+DPD below 5% and 90+ DPD below 1%.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Continued significant investment in technology, with projected doubling of technology opex/depreciation to approximately Rs. 20 crore per year for the next 3-4 years. Completion of the digital journey is expected in the next 12-15 months.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** Expecting 20-30 bps incremental reduction in opex YoY basis in the next 3-5 years, after the current investment phase.
*   **Spread Management:** Aiming to maintain a long-term spread of around 5%. With recent and planned rate hikes (25 bps in June, 50 bps from August 5th), management is confident in maintaining spreads over the next 2-3 quarters.
*   **ROA/ROE:** Guidance for ROA to remain above 2.5% sustainably. ROE is expected to improve sustainably in the next 3-5 years driven by ROA improvements and increased leverage.
*   **Branch Productivity:** Branches are expected to become ROE positive within 12 months. Focus remains on unit economics rather than simply increasing AUM per branch.
*   **MSME Book:** Incremental non-housing loan book comprises 40% MSME and 60% LAP/other loans. Average ticket size for MSME is Rs. 8-10 lakhs, tenure up to 7 years, and yields up to 15%.
*   **Balance Sheet Matching:** Management maintains a perfectly matched balance sheet on fixed and floating rates for interest rate risk management.
*   **Credit Cost:** Expects sustainable credit cost around 0.5% (historical guidance).
*   **Fee-Based Income:** No current aspiration to venture into new fee-based income products.
