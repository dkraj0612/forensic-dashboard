## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) demonstrated resilience and growth in Q4 FY2021, concluding a challenging fiscal year on a strong note. Despite economic headwinds, the company achieved a 21% YoY growth in Assets Under Management (AUM) to Rs. 94,543 Mn as of March 2021. Disbursements for Q4 FY21 surged 17% YoY and 32% QoQ to Rs. 10,127 Mn, marking a historical high. Profit After Tax (PAT) for FY21 saw a substantial increase of 17% YoY (Ind-AS) to Rs. 2,903 Mn and 34% YoY (IGAAP) to Rs. 3,078 Mn. Asset quality showed significant improvement, with 1+DPD declining to 6.37% from 8.21% in the previous quarter, nearing pre-moratorium levels. The company continued its focus on its core customer segment in semi-urban and rural areas, with home loans constituting 73.5% of the portfolio. Aavas further expanded its reach, adding 30 new branches in FY21, bringing the total to 280.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continues to serve low- and middle-income customers in semi-urban and rural areas. Home loans remain the dominant product (73.5%), with a slight increase in the salaried customer proportion (39.6% vs. 60.4% self-employed).
*   **Network Expansion:** Accelerated branch network expansion, adding 30 branches in FY21 to reach 280. Plans to continue this growth trajectory.
*   **Risk Management:** Maintains a prudent approach with zero exposure to commercial papers. Focus on enhanced underwriting practices and improved collection efficiency. Full provisioning for Stage 3 assets, including COVID-19 provisions. Portfolio is secured by granular retail mortgages with low Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratios.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Access to diversified and cost-effective long-term financing, supported by stable credit ratings (AA-).
*   **Operational Resilience & Efficiency:** Investment in manpower and technology to enhance operational efficiency and customer service, ensuring a smooth work-from-home transition during lockdowns.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q4 FY2021 & FY2021):

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 21% YoY growth, consistent with the target of 20-25% for the year.
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported strong YoY growth for FY21 (17% Ind-AS, 34% IGAAP), exceeding expectations.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD):** Demonstrated significant improvement, reducing 1+DPD to 6.37% from 8.21% in Q3 FY21. This aligns with management's expectation of improvement post-moratorium and Supreme Court order.
*   **ROA/ROE:** Achieved ROA of 3.9% and ROE of 15.0% in Q4 FY21, indicating robust quarterly profitability. FY21 ROA and ROE stood at 3.5% and 12.9% respectively.
*   **Spread:** Maintained a healthy spread of 5.76%, in line with previous performance and expectations.
*   **Disbursements:** Q4 FY21 disbursements hit a historical high of Rs. 10,127 Mn, surpassing the anticipated growth.

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:

*   **COVID-19 Impact:** Management acknowledges the resurgence of COVID-19 and localized lockdowns, emphasizing a cautious yet confident stance based on learnings from the first wave. They highlight improved customer resilience and repayment behavior.
*   **Moratorium & Collections:** The significant improvement in 1+DPD is a key positive narrative, indicating a return to normalcy and strong borrower behavior. Collection efficiency is noted as being over 100% due to the improvement in 1+DPD.
*   **Economic Environment:** Management perceives continued demand for affordable housing and expresses confidence in navigating the current economic conditions due to the company's granular customer profile and secured portfolio.
*   **Funding Environment:** Stable credit ratings and diversified funding sources continue to provide comfort.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **1+DPD Target:** Management expects 1+DPD to continue improving, aiming for levels around 5-6% within one to two quarters post-December. They anticipate further improvement once the Supreme Court order's impact fully resolves.
*   **Disbursement Growth:** While the capacity exists for approximately Rs. 400 Crores per month, management aims for consistent and conservative underwriting. They possess sufficient capacity to support growth in line with their long-term AUM growth targets.
*   **Yield Pressure:** Acknowledged potential yield compression of 10 bps in Q4 FY21 due to a PLR reduction and a shift in the customer mix towards salaried individuals. Further yield compression of 50-75 bps is anticipated over the next two to three years due to competition, to be offset by Opex reduction to sustain a 2.5% ROA.
*   **Liquidity:** Plans to reduce excess liquidity from 5-6 months to a more traditional 3-4 months as the economy normalizes.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Continues a consistent branch opening strategy, aiming for 30-40 branches annually, with approximately 40-50% in new states and 60% in existing states.
*   **Opex Reduction:** Commitment to improving Opex to AUM by 25-30 bps annually over the next two to three years.
*   **Taxation:** The lower tax rate in Q4 FY21 was due to a tax refund of Rs. 20 Crores related to FY17-18 and FY18-19, and the recomputation of tax based on assessment orders.
*   **Provisioning:** Confident that existing COVID-19 provisions of Rs. 19 Crores are sufficient, with no additional provisioning deemed necessary at present. Management will continue to assess the situation.
*   **Direct Assignment Income:** The upfront booking of income from direct assignments under Ind-AS is noted as a point of discussion, with management open to further improving disclosures on this aspect. They currently provide reconciliations between IGAAP and Ind-AS numbers.
*   **ECLGS:** Minimal exposure to the ECLGS scheme, with only 10-15 cases and an absolute exposure of Rs. 54 lakh.
*   **New State Contribution:** New states contribute conservatively for the first 30-36 months post-branch opening, with approximately 100 branches currently in new states. Future growth will be driven by continued expansion in both new and existing states.
*   **Capacity Addition:** Management maintains a bench strength of 30-40% higher than immediate needs to ensure consistent growth and the ability to pick and choose opportunities while maintaining asset quality.
*   **Rejection Rate:** Acknowledges an increase in rejection rates, indicating a focus on high-quality customer additions. This practice, combined with existing capacity, provides confidence in maintaining current disbursement run rates.
