## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) concluded Q3 FY'26 with AUM crossing INR 222 billion, marking a significant milestone. The company reported a robust 15% YoY AUM growth, driven by a 10% QoQ increase in disbursements to INR 17.2 billion in Q3 FY'26. Net profit grew 16% YoY to INR 1.70 billion, bolstered by a 17% YoY increase in Net Interest Income (NII) and a healthy improvement in spreads. NIM expanded by 27 bps YoY to 8.01%. Asset quality remained a strong point, with 1+ DPD improving sequentially to 3.80% and Gross NPA at 1.19%. The company successfully raised approximately INR 975 crore from a multilateral institution, underscoring strong external confidence. Management is focused on leveraging digital platforms, expanding distribution, and optimizing costs to drive sustainable, quality-led growth, with an emphasis on strengthening the operating backbone through initiatives like Project Neev, Nipun, Sampoorn, Setu, and RISE.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Digital Transformation & Efficiency:** Continued focus on leveraging digital platforms (Project Neev, Nipun, Sampoorn, Setu, RISE) to enhance operational backbone, improve login quality, reduce rework, optimize channel management, and strengthen employee engagement. Increased customer visits by 30% through direct channels.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Accelerated branch expansion, reaching 410 branches by Jan 2026, with plans to add 20-25 more in Q4 FY'26 and 50 new branches in FY'27. Focus on deepening presence in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities via RRO (Resident Relationship Officer) model and converting them to model branches. Entry into Tamil Nadu with 10 branches.
*   **Product & Yield Enhancement:** Emphasis on risk-adjusted returns and prudent underwriting. Transition to new disbursement recognition framework normalized operations. Continued focus on granular loans and risk-based pricing.
*   **Liability Management:** Diversified liability profile with 69% of borrowings linked to external benchmarks or sub-3-month MCLR, enabling faster repricing. Raised INR 975 crore from a multilateral institution at competitive rates. Anticipating potential softening in interest rates and shifting to EBLR-linked instruments.
*   **Asset Quality Maintenance:** Commitment to pristine asset quality, with 1+ DPD and GNPA within guided ranges. Proactive risk management framework and rigorous credit assessment process.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Improvement in Opex-to-Assets ratio sequentially. Focus on operating leverage benefits from technology and scale.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** The reported 15% YoY AUM growth for 9MFY26 is below the previously stated guidance of ~18-20% for FY'26. Management attributed this to a "calibrated tightening of credit" in H1 FY'26 due to transformation initiatives and a "relatively subdued macro and credit environment," prioritizing portfolio quality over headline growth. They noted a pickup in business momentum post-Diwali and expect Q4 FY'26 to exceed Q4 FY'25 disbursements.
*   **Disbursements:** The 10% QoQ growth in Q3 FY'26 was described as a normalization after the transition to the new disbursement recognition framework in Q1 FY'26. The sanction-to-disbursement ratio has recovered to >80%. Management acknowledges that full-year disbursements might fall short of previous implicit targets due to the H1 drag but are confident in exceeding Q4 FY'25 levels and achieving accelerated growth in FY'27.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management consistently highlighted strong asset quality, with 1+DPD improving sequentially to 3.80% and GNPA at 1.19%. The narrative remains consistent with maintaining pristine asset quality, well within guided ranges.
*   **Profitability:** Net profit growth of 16% YoY and NIM expansion of 27 bps YoY align with expectations of profitable growth driven by NII and spread improvement. ROE saw a marginal YoY increase.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** Opex-to-Assets ratio saw a sequential improvement, and the Cost-to-Income ratio declined significantly, reflecting efficiency gains from technology and operational improvements.
*   **Borrowing Costs & Liability Management:** The company successfully raised INR 975 crore at competitive rates and highlighted a 16 bps sequential and 56 bps YoY improvement in the cost of funds. The strategy of shifting to EBLR-linked instruments is yielding benefits.
*   **Spread Management:** The spread improved sequentially by 40 bps YoY to 5.34%, exceeding previous levels. Management is focused on maintaining spreads within a sustainable range.
*   **Technology Implementation:** The stabilization of operations post new disbursement recognition framework is confirmed, with operations normalized.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Management confirmed 410 branches as of Jan 2026 and plans to add 20-25 in Q4 FY'26 and 50 in FY'27, continuing the trajectory of geographic expansion.
