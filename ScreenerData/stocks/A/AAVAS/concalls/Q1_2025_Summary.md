## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported Q1 FY'26 results marked by a significant strategic shift to a realization-based disbursement recognition model, a move aimed at enhancing transparency and conservatism. This transition, while impacting reported disbursement growth, underscores the company's commitment to quality. The company welcomed CVC Capital Partners as its new promoter, signifying strong institutional confidence. Despite a reported dip in disbursement growth due to the accounting change, AUM growth remained robust at 16% YoY, reaching INR 207 billion. Net profit grew by 10% YoY to INR 1.4 billion, driven by a 16% YoY increase in Net Interest Income (NII), supported by improved spreads. Asset quality metrics remain strong, with 1+DPD at 4.15% and GNPA at 1.22%, showing signs of normalization after a seasonal uptick. Management reaffirmed its FY'26 AUM growth guidance in the 18-20% range and anticipates returning to a 20-25% growth trajectory in FY'27 post stabilization.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Promoter Transition:** Welcomed CVC Capital Partners as the new promoter, signaling a new phase of growth and innovation.
*   **Disbursement Recognition:** Shifted to a realization-based model for disbursement recognition, prioritizing transparency and conservative accounting.
*   **Demand Traction:** Logins grew 17% YoY, indicating robust underlying housing demand, with healthy real estate price appreciation in core markets.
*   **Calibrated Risk Strategy:** Deliberately adjusted sanction ratio to optimize yield and credit quality, focusing on the self-employed segment and loans > INR 5 lakhs.
*   **Distribution Diversification:** Leveraging digital partners (CSC, eMitra, India Post Payment Bank) showing strong traction, alongside plans for new branch openings.
*   **Technology & Efficiency:** Continued focus on digital and operational platforms to drive scale efficiently, optimize costs, and enhance productivity.
*   **Asset Quality Focus:** Maintaining a disciplined underwriting approach, leveraging predictive analytics and a robust risk management framework to preserve industry-leading asset quality.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **Promoter Transition:** Successfully concluded the promoter change with CVC Capital Partners, a key strategic development.
*   **Disbursement Accounting Change:** Implemented a realization-based model, a proactive and conservative shift that impacted Q1 FY'26 disbursement figures, a deviation from the previously implied growth trajectory based on cheque issuance. Management attributed the lower reported disbursement to this accounting change and expects a "rollover effect" into Q2 FY'26.
*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 16% YoY AUM growth, below the previous guidance of 22-25% for FY'25 (now FY'26), largely due to the disbursement recognition change and its impact on the Q1 run rate. Management now expects 18-20% AUM growth for FY'26.
*   **Asset Quality:** Maintained strong asset quality, with 1+DPD at 4.15% and GNPA at 1.22%. While a seasonal uptick was observed in Q1, management highlighted a moderation in July, indicating a return to normalization. The narrative remains consistent with a focus on maintaining high asset quality.
*   **Yield & Spread:** Reported improved spreads (5.11% sequentially) and NIMs (7.48%), with a 35 bps increase in incremental business yields, aligning with the strategy to optimize portfolio mix and pricing.
*   **Opex Ratio:** Opex to asset ratio declined sequentially to 3.46%. Management acknowledged a Y-o-Y increase driven by ESOP costs but highlighted efforts towards operational efficiency.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Proactively shifted to EBLR-linked instruments, leading to a 22 bps Q-o-Q reduction in the cost of borrowing. Management anticipates further benefits from potential rate cuts.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **AUM Growth Guidance:** Reaffirmed FY'26 AUM growth guidance in the range of 18-20%. Expects to return to 20-25% AUM growth in FY'27 after stabilization.
*   **Disbursement Momentum:** Expects Q2 FY'26 to show a "rollover effect" from Q1, leading to a rebound in disbursements. Confident in achieving double-digit growth in disbursements for July, August, and September.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plans to open 10 new branches in September, focusing on Tamil Nadu, front-loading expansion in H1 FY'26.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** While acknowledging current investment in manpower for new channels and branches, management expects productivity to improve over the coming quarters and remains committed to cost optimization led by technology.
*   **Credit Cost Guidance:** Maintains guidance of keeping credit costs below 25 bps on a sustainable basis.
*   **Productivity Improvement:** Expects improved productivity from new channels and branches as they mature.
*   **Spread Maintenance:** Focus on optimizing yield and credit quality.
*   **Borrowing Cost Management:** Anticipates continued downward pressure on cost of funds due to proactive liability management and potential rate cuts.
*   **Asset Quality:** Expects 1+DPD to normalize and remain within the guided range of less than 5%, with July numbers showing a positive trend.
*   **Credit Rating:** Engaging with rating agencies (CARE and ICRA) for a positive rating review, citing strong asset quality, lifetime write-offs, and liability franchise.
*   **PLR Review:** Likely to review PLR in Q2 end or Q3, depending on the overall interest rate transition.
*   **Employee Per Branch:** Acknowledged the higher employee-per-branch ratio (18) compared to peers, attributing it to a direct distribution model and investment in digital channels and manpower for growth. Expects this to monetize in coming quarters.
