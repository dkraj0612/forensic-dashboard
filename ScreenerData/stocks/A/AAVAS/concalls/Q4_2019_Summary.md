## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported strong performance for Q4 FY19 and the full fiscal year, demonstrating resilience amidst a challenging market characterized by tight liquidity. The company achieved a 30% YoY growth in disbursements for FY2019, reaching Rs. 2,672.4 Crores, and saw an 89% YoY growth in PAT to Rs. 176.1 Crores (Ind-AS accounting). AAVAS further strengthened its market position with the opening of 45 new branches during the fiscal year, bringing the total to 210 branches across 122 districts in 10 states. The company reiterated its strategic focus on low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas, specializing in small-ticket housing loans. A rating upgrade by CARE to AA- Stable outlook underscored the company's robust business model and asset quality. AAVAS continues to emphasize its risk-led approach, aiming to maintain GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continued emphasis on low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas, with an average ticket size sub-Rs. 10 lakhs.
*   **Network Expansion:** Opened 45 new branches in FY2019, totaling 210 branches across 122 districts in 10 states.
*   **Risk Management:** Commitment to maintaining asset quality with targets of GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%, supported by robust in-house credit and collection systems.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Strong relationships with 37 lenders, 89% of borrowings from long-term loans, assignments, and NHB refinancing. No commercial paper outstanding.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Continued investment in technology and data analytics; Opex reduced by approximately 100 basis points to 3.81% of AUM in FY19 from 4.86% in FY18.
*   **Product Mix:** Home loans constitute approximately 75.5% of the portfolio, and other mortgage loans 24.5%. Self-employed customers represent approximately 64.9% of the client base.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Performance Against Stated Targets (FY2019):**

*   **Disbursement Growth:** Achieved 30% YoY growth, meeting guidance.
*   **Profitability (PAT Growth):** Reported 89% YoY growth (Ind-AS), significantly exceeding expectations.
*   **Asset Quality (GNPA < 1%):** Maintained GNPA at 0.47%, well within target.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD < 5%):** Achieved 1+DPD of 3.4%, meeting target.
*   **ROA Target (2.5% and above):** Reported 3.64% ROA for FY2019, exceeding target, though influenced by Ind-AS and excess capital.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Added 45 new branches in FY2019, consistent with expansion plans.
*   **Spread:** Maintained spreads above 5% (approx. 5.01%), despite liquidity challenges and a slight decrease from the prior year. Management compensated for potential spread compression through Opex reduction.

**Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:**

*   Management acknowledged a challenging market and tight liquidity but highlighted their ability to maintain borrowing costs and raise funds, validating their business model.
*   The tone remained consistent with their risk-led, customer-centric approach.
*   The rating upgrade to AA- was a significant positive validation of their strategy.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to open around 40 branches in the current fiscal year (FY2020).
*   **Opex Ratio Moderation:** Management expects further improvement in operating efficiencies through manpower utilization, technology, and data analytics.
*   **Borrowing Cost:** Anticipates a potential 25-50 basis points hike in borrowing costs in the next two quarters, but expects to manage this through prudent liquidity management and cost efficiencies.
*   **ALM Management:** Maintaining a positive ALM profile with sufficient liquidity.
*   **Asset Quality:** Endeavor to maintain GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%.
*   **ROA:** Aim to consistently deliver ROA of 2.5% and above on a steady-state basis.
*   **Leverage:** Expects leverage to remain around four times in FY2020, gradually increasing to 6-7 times over five to six years with a AA rating.
*   **Funding Mix:** Anticipates a shift in borrowing reliance: bank funding to decrease from 42% to 30-35%, multilateral institutions to be around 10%, and insurance/provident fund sector to increase from 10% to 20%.
*   **NHB Funding:** Expects to continue receiving support from NHB, with an average borrowing cost of 7.5% on Rs. 900 Crores outstanding.
*   **Write-offs:** Q4 FY19 write-offs were Rs. 1.14 Crores, and full-year write-offs were Rs. 3.25 Crores, largely due to a conservative approach to NPA asset re-evaluation.
*   **Incremental Yields:** Housing loan incremental yield at 12.6%, mortgage loan incremental yield at 15.3%.
*   **Employee Strength:** Total employee strength of 2384.
