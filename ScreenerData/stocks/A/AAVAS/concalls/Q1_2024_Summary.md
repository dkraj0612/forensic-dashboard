## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported Q1 FY'24 results amidst the stabilization of its comprehensive technology transformation initiative. The company posted AUM growth of 23.2% and PAT growth of 23% year-on-year. Disbursements were softer in the quarter due to the PAN-India rollout of the Salesforce (LOS) platform, which commenced in April 2023. However, the system stabilized by May and demonstrated strong month-on-month growth in June, indicating a return to momentum. The company remains committed to its Aavas 3.0 framework, focusing on technology, people, and sustainable growth. New core systems (LMS and ERP) are slated to go live by Q4 FY'24. Management reiterated its confidence in achieving the previously guided AUM growth of 20-25% for the fiscal year and highlighted continued commitment to robust governance and asset quality.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Technology Transformation:** PAN-India Salesforce (LOS) rollout completed and stabilized, with full benefits expected post-stabilization. LMS and ERP systems to go live by Q4 FY'24. This aims to enhance efficiency, reduce Turnaround Time (TAT), and improve customer experience.
*   **Growth Focus:** Reaffirmed 20-25% AUM growth guidance. Emphasis on leveraging technology for increased distribution productivity and exploring new sourcing channels, including digital.
*   **Product Mix:** Aim to maintain Non-Home Loans between 30-35%, with a preference for MSME products backed by mortgages. Home loan component saw an increase in Q1 FY'24 compared to the previous year.
*   **Geographical Diversification:** Continued diversification away from Rajasthan, with Maharashtra, MP, and Gujarat contributing around 15-16% each. Karnataka showing strong growth.
*   **Underwriting Philosophy:** Core underwriting model remains decentralized ("touch and feel"), with technology augmenting efficiency and data insights. Cash flow-based underwriting for MSME loans, backed by residential mortgage security, continues to perform well with low NPAs.
*   **Talent Management:** Focus on investing in people and technology. Leadership development programs are in place. Frontline attrition is noted as normal industry trend.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 23.2% YoY growth, aligning with the 20-25% guidance.
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported 23% YoY growth, indicating healthy profitability.
*   **Disbursements:** Softer disbursements in Q1 FY'24 were explicitly attributed to the PAN-India Salesforce (LOS) rollout and subsequent stabilization, a narrative consistent with the prior quarter's announcement of the platform going live. The company highlighted a 17% MoM growth in June and continued growth in July, demonstrating recovery and bouncing back from the temporary disruption.
*   **Asset Quality:** Gross Stage 3 at 1.00% and Net Stage 3 at 0.73% remain well-managed and at industry-leading levels, consistent with stated commitments. One Day Past Dues improved YoY.
*   **Spread Management:** Spread of 5.60% remains healthy, with management expressing confidence in maintaining margins despite some borrowing cost fluctuations.
*   **Technology Implementation:** Salesforce (LOS) rollout completed and stabilized, with positive early indicators of improved efficiency. LMS and ERP implementation remain on track.
*   **Opex Ratio:** Opex to AUM ratio was elevated at 3.79% in Q1 FY'24, attributed to investment in technology and incremental headcount. Management reiterated that this is a peak investment year and expects a reduction in subsequent years.
*   **Employee Attrition:** Management addressed concerns by stating that attrition is primarily at the frontline, in line with industry norms, and there is no significant attrition at senior management levels.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **TAT Reduction:** Target reduction in overall lead time from ~13-14 days to 6-7 days post full technology implementation.
*   **LMS and ERP Go-Live:** Expected by Q4 FY'24.
*   **Opex Ratio Improvement:** Expectation of 20-25 basis points savings on Opex annually for 2-3 years starting from next year, aiming to bring it back to less than 3% of AUM.
*   **AUM Growth Guidance:** Reaffirmed 20-25% growth for the fiscal year.
*   **Disbursement Momentum:** Confidence in continued growth in August and September, with a target of significantly increasing non-conventional sourcing channels beyond the current 10%.
*   **Data Science & Analytics:** Robust systems expected to go live in approximately one quarter, aiding efficiency, risk-based pricing, and lead funnel prioritization.
*   **Opex Peak:** Management indicated that the current quarter's Opex to AUM ratio is at peak levels due to investment year.
*   **ROE:** Long-term direction remains at 15% to 20% ROE, with current ROA trajectory of 2.5% to 3% at a leverage of 7-8.
*   **MSME Product Focus:** Intent to maintain 30-35% non-home loan mix, with over 50% of that being MSME loans.
*   **New State Entries:** (From previous context) Plan to add 2 more states over the next 3-5 years.
