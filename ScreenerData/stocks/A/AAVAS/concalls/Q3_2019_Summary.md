## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported a strong Q3 FY19, demonstrating consistent growth and profitability despite a challenging market environment characterized by tight liquidity. The company achieved significant year-on-year growth in disbursements and profits, underscoring its robust business model and disciplined execution. AAVAS continues to expand its distribution network, adding 45 new branches in the current fiscal year, bringing the total to 210. The company maintains its strategic focus on serving low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas with small-ticket housing loans, prioritizing asset quality and a risk-led approach. Diversified funding sources, including a maiden masala bond issuance and NHB refinance, have been secured to support ongoing growth.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continued focus on low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas, specializing in single-unit dwelling financing with an average ticket size below Rs. 10 lakhs.
*   **Network Expansion:** Expansion of branch network to 210 branches across 122 districts in 10 states, with a plan to add 30-40 new branches annually for the next three years.
*   **Risk Management:** Commitment to maintaining asset quality with targets of GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%, supported by a robust, in-house credit and collection system.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Successful fundraising through a maiden Masala Bond (Rs. 200 Crores) with CDC Group and NHB refinance (Rs. 500 Crores), alongside term loans and assignment transactions, to ensure cost-effective and long-term funding.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Investment in technology and data analytics to improve operational efficiencies and customer experience. Opex to AUM ratio is expected to moderate by 25-50 basis points in the medium term.
*   **Product Mix:** Home loans constitute 75% of the portfolio, and other mortgage loans 25%. Self-employed customers represent 65% of the client base.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

**Performance Against Stated Targets (Q3 FY19 & 9M FY19):**

*   **Disbursement Growth:** Achieved 38% YoY growth in Q3 FY19 and 37% YoY growth in 9M FY19, aligning with their strategy of consistent growth.
*   **Profitability (PAT Growth):** Reported a strong 66% YoY growth in PAT for 9M FY19, exceeding expectations.
*   **Asset Quality (GNPA < 1%):** Maintained GNPA at 0.58%, well within their target.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD < 5%):** Achieved 1+DPD of 3.9%, meeting their target.
*   **ROA Target (2.5% and above):** Reported 3.58% ROA for 9M FY19, exceeding the stated target, though management noted this is influenced by recent capital infusion.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Added 45 new branches in 9 months of FY19 and 71 in the last 12 months, consistent with their expansion plans.
*   **Customer Acquisition:** Customer acquisition increased by 39.6% in 9M FY19, supporting disbursement growth.

**Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:**

*   Management acknowledged a challenging market and tight liquidity but emphasized their consistent approach and ability to raise funds.
*   They reiterated their "credit-led, not sales-led" DNA, highlighting a conservative approach to customer selection, avoiding rejected cases from other financiers.
*   Regarding balance transfers, management indicated a focus on acquiring new customers rather than aggressively pursuing portfolio takeovers, with BT-in constituting around 5% of the total book.

**New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:**

*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to add 30-40 new branches annually for the next three years, reaching over 300 branches.
*   **Opex Ratio Moderation:** Expect 25-50 basis points improvement in opex to AUM ratio in the next six to nine months.
*   **Borrowing Cost:** Acknowledged potential increase in borrowing costs due to the rising interest rate scenario, but expect to maintain similar levels as Q3 FY19 in Q4.
*   **ALM Management:** Will continue to maintain a positive ALM profile with sufficient liquidity and surplus of assets over liabilities.
*   **Asset Quality:** Endeavor to maintain GNPA below 1% and 1+DPD below 5%.
*   **ROA:** Aim to consistently deliver ROA of 2.5% plus on a steady-state basis, even with increased leverage.
*   **New Employee Addition:** Added 300 employees in sales and 160 in credit in the last nine months, primarily for new branch distribution.
*   **PLR Increase:** Increased PLR by 35 basis points effective November 1, 2018.
*   **Static Pool Data:** Will share static pool analysis for 1+DPD performance.
*   **Provision Coverage:** While operating under Ind-AS, management indicated a capacity to improve provision coverage if deemed necessary, currently having Rs. 11.2 Crores provision against Rs. 25.1 Crores NPA.
