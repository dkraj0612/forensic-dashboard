## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported Q2 FY'25 results marked by a 20% YoY growth in Assets Under Management (AUM) to INR 184 billion. Despite a muted disbursement figure of INR 12.94 billion for the quarter (down 8% YoY for H1 FY'25 at INR 25.05 billion), management is confident of a stronger second half, noting a 22% YoY growth in cumulative disbursements for September and October. This disbursement slowdown was attributed to the loan management system (LMS) implementation and an extended monsoon season. Net profit grew by 22% YoY to INR 1.48 billion, driven by net interest income growth and improved operating leverage. Asset quality remains a key focus, with 1+DPD at 3.97% (below 4%) and GNPA at 1.08%. The company has launched its new loan management system, Oracle Flexcube, and a new lead management system, expecting improved operational efficiencies and TAT reduction. The Opex-to-assets ratio saw a significant improvement of 40 bps YoY to 3.25% in H1 FY'25, with continued guidance for further reduction. The company entered Tamil Nadu with one branch in Hosur as part of its geographic expansion.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Digital Transformation:** Successful implementation of Oracle Flexcube (LMS) and Salesforce (LMS) to improve operational efficiency, reduce TAT (login to sanction improved to 8 days), and enhance customer service.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Opened one new branch in Tamil Nadu (Hosur) as part of its contiguous geographic expansion strategy, aiming to strengthen presence in southern states.
*   **Product Strategy:** Focus on risk-adjusted returns and increasing disbursement yields. Incremental business yield increased by 25 bps across products. Increased focus on micro-MSME loans backed by self-occupied residential properties for value addition and securitization benefits.
*   **Liability Management:** Successfully raised INR 6.3 billion NCDs from IFC. Maintaining a diversified liability profile with ~30% linked to external benchmarks for faster repricing. Exploring co-lending tie-ups with PSU Banks.
*   **Asset Quality Maintenance:** Continued focus on disciplined underwriting, with robust performance in existing states and emerging states. Maintaining portfolio quality through a predictive model for customer retention.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Consistent efforts to optimize costs leading to improved opex-to-asset ratios, with continued guidance for further reduction.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 20% YoY growth in AUM, aligning with the previously guided "20% plus" for the current year.
*   **Disbursements:** The narrative around disbursements shifted from previous expectations to acknowledging the impact of LMS implementation and extended monsoon. Management attributed the Q2 FY'25 disbursement slowdown to these specific, identifiable factors, rather than a fundamental demand issue. The observed 22% YoY growth in September and October disbursements provides confidence in achieving H2 targets.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management reiterated its commitment to pristine asset quality, with 1+DPD at 3.97% and GNPA at 1.08%, well within guided ranges. The sequential improvement in 1+DPD in October (3.84%) indicates proactive management of any temporary uptick.
*   **Profitability:** Net profit growth of 22% YoY was strong, supported by net interest income and improved operating leverage.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** The Opex-to-assets ratio improvement of 40 bps YoY to 3.25% in H1 FY'25 exceeded the guided 20-30 bps annual improvement, demonstrating significant progress. Management highlighted that even excluding the ESOP cost reversal, there was a 30 bps YoY improvement.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** The company has successfully raised funds at competitive rates (IFC NCDs at sub-8%). The average borrowing cost of 8.15% and efforts to pass on cost increases via BPLR are consistent with liability management strategies.
*   **Spread Management:** The spread compression to 4.89% was acknowledged and attributed to a lag in asset yield adjustment and rising cost of funds. Management is actively addressing this through increasing disbursement yields and expects the spread to improve towards the 5% target. The statement that ~30% of liabilities are benchmarked provides flexibility in a falling rate scenario.
*   **BT-out Rate:** The BT-out rate remained stable at 5.2% annualized, consistent with previous periods, with management highlighting the effectiveness of their retention models.
*   **Technology Implementation:** The successful go-live of Oracle Flexcube and Salesforce LMS are key achievements, aligning with the strategic focus on technology upgradation and operational efficiency.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **AUM Growth Guidance:** Reiterated guidance of "20% plus" for the current year (FY'25).
*   **Disbursement Run Rate:** Confident in achieving H2 FY'25 disbursement targets, with positive green shoots observed in September and October.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Opened one branch in Tamil Nadu in H1 FY'25, and plans to expand within southern states as part of its contiguous geographic strategy.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** Continued commitment to bringing the Opex-to-Asset ratio below 3% over the medium term. Confirmed achieving almost the full year's guided savings of 25-30 bps in H1 FY'25.
*   **Spread Target:** Aiming to maintain spreads in the range of "5% plus minus 20 bps" for the current year. Expects improvement in yields and recouping spread in coming months.
*   **PLR Strategy:** Raising BPLR by 25 bps effective October to pass on cost of fund increases and improve yields.
*   **Yield Sustainability:** Continuing focus on lower ticket size, self-construction individual houses, and micro-MSME loans for risk-adjusted returns.
*   **Credit Costs:** Guidance remains below 25 bps sustainable basis, with current levels around 11-12 bps. Management is confident of maintaining this due to strong underwriting and collection practices.
*   **Employee Growth:** Employee count has remained largely flat for the past three years, with minimal addition expected, aided by digital initiatives and operating leverage.
*   **FY'33 AUM Target:** Aspires to reach INR 1 trillion in AUM by 2033, broadening horizon as a Pan India player.
*   **Intangibles Capitalization:** IT project spends for LOS, LMS, and data warehouse transformation are being capitalized, totaling approximately INR 60 crores to be amortized over seven years.
