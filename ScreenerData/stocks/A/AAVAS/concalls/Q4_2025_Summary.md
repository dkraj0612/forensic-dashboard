## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) concluded FY'25 with a strong Q4, demonstrating robust growth and operational efficiency, notably surpassing the INR 200 billion Assets Under Management (AUM) milestone. AUM grew 18% YoY to INR 204 billion. Disbursements saw a significant sequential acceleration, growing 27% QoQ to a record INR 20 billion in Q4 FY'25, contributing to INR 61.2 billion for the full year (10% YoY growth). Net profit for FY'25 rose 17% YoY to INR 5.74 billion. The company has successfully completed major technology platform upgrades, now stabilizing and poised to drive future efficiency and scalability. Asset quality remained a key strength, with 1+DPD at 3.39% and Gross NPAs at 1.08%. Management successfully reduced the Opex-to-Assets ratio by 26 bps YoY to 3.32% in FY'25, exceeding their guidance. The company also accelerated its branch expansion, opening 30 new branches in FY'25.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Digital Transformation & Efficiency:** Completed and stabilizing of major tech platforms (Oracle Flexcube, Salesforce) are expected to drive scalable and profitable growth. Focus is on leveraging these for strengthened governance, scale, cost optimization, and operational efficiencies. Login-to-sanction TAT improved.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Accelerated branch expansion with 30 new branches opened in FY'25, with a plan to front-end expansion in H1 FY'26. Tamil Nadu is slated for entry within the next 3-4 years. Aspiration to reach INR 500 billion AUM in 5 years and become Pan-India.
*   **Product & Yield Enhancement:** Continued focus on risk-adjusted returns, with incremental business yields increasing by 22 bps in FY'25. Emphasis on product mix and granular loans (less than INR 10 lakhs, currently 32-33%) to improve yields and spreads. Risk-based pricing through BRE engines is being utilized.
*   **Liability Management:** Diversified liability profile maintained (51% term loans, 25% assignment, 14% NHB, 10% debt capital markets). Raised INR 6.3 billion NCDs from IFC. Progress on a co-lending tie-up with a PSU Bank. Approximately 56% of borrowings are linked to external benchmarks (Repo, TB, <3-month MCLR), allowing for faster repricing.
*   **Asset Quality Maintenance:** Pristine asset quality remains a priority with 1+DPD at 3.39% and GNPA at 1.08%. Management confident of returning GNPA to below 1% in 1-2 quarters.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Delivered a 26 bps YoY reduction in Opex to Assets ratio in FY'25, exceeding guidance. Continued commitment to cost optimization and operating leverage benefits. Expecting 10-20 bps saving on Opex to Assets in FY'26.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 18% YoY AUM growth in FY'25, falling slightly short of the previously stated 20% guidance for the year, citing a cautious underwriting approach. However, management reiterated a long-term guidance of 20% AUM CAGR.
*   **Disbursements:** The narrative shifted positively, highlighting a strong sequential pick-up (27% QoQ) and a record quarter. While full-year growth (10% YoY) was below historical trends, management expressed confidence in accelerating disbursements in FY'26, expecting higher than the usual run rate.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management consistently highlighted strong asset quality, with 1+DPD at 3.39% and GNPA at 1.08%, well within guided ranges. The CRO indicated no alarming trends across geographies or ticket sizes and expressed confidence in bringing GNPA back below 1% within 1-2 quarters.
*   **Profitability:** Net profit growth of 17% YoY for FY'25 was strong, driven by NII and operating leverage. ROE showed an incremental improvement.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** The Opex-to-Assets ratio improvement of 26 bps YoY to 3.32% in FY'25 met their guided reduction of 25 bps, demonstrating strong cost control and operating leverage.
*   **Borrowing Costs & Liability Management:** Average borrowing cost was 8.42% for FY'25. Management highlighted the benefit of benchmark-linked borrowings for faster repricing in a falling rate environment.
*   **Spread Management:** The calculated spread improved sequentially by 15 bps to 5.87% in Q4 FY'25. Management is focused on increasing disbursement yields to sustain and improve spreads, with a long-term target of 5%+.
*   **Technology Implementation:** Successful go-live and stabilization of key tech platforms were emphasized as a significant achievement enabling future efficiencies.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Opened 30 new branches in FY'25, with a focus on front-ending expansion in H1 FY'26.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **AUM Growth Guidance:** Reiterated guidance of 20% AUM CAGR on a long-term basis. Confident in delivering guided AUM growth of ~20% for FY'26.
*   **Disbursement Momentum:** Confident in accelerating disbursement growth in FY'26, expecting it to be higher than the usual 17-18% run rate due to improved efficiencies and capacity.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plans to accelerate branch expansion, with a focus on front-ending in H1 FY'26. Tamil Nadu entry planned within 3-4 years.
*   **Opex Efficiency:** Expecting a saving of 10-20 bps on Opex to Assets ratio in FY'26.
*   **Spread Outlook:** Aiming to sustain and improve spreads, with a target of crossing 5%+. The company had achieved a 5% spread in a falling interest rate scenario in 2019.
*   **Yield Improvement:** Continued efforts to increase incremental disbursement yields, which increased by 22 bps in FY'25. Aiming to bridge the gap between disbursement yields and AUM yields over the next 3-4 quarters.
*   **Credit Costs:** Guidance remains below 25 bps on a sustainable basis, with current levels at 15 bps for FY'25.
*   **GNPA Target:** Confident in bringing GNPA back to below 1% in the next 1-2 quarters.
*   **Provisioning:** ECL provisioning methodology updated. Stage 3 provision coverage expected to stabilize between 30% to 34%. Overall ECL provisioning expected to remain less than 0.7%.
*   **AUM Milestone:** Aspiration to reach INR 500 billion in AUM within the next 5 years.
*   **Home Loan/MSME/LAP Mix:** Endeavor to maintain a 65%:35% ratio for Home Loans versus MSME and LAP loans at the loan book level.
*   **Fee Income:** Focus on credit insurance from a customer protection perspective rather than a pure revenue source, expecting it to grow in line with loan growth.
