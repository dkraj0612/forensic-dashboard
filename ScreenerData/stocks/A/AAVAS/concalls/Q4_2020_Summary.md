## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) demonstrated resilience in Q4 FY2020 and FY2020, navigating the unprecedented challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic and nationwide lockdown. Despite the disruptions, the company reported strong year-on-year growth in Assets Under Management (AUM) of 31% and Profit After Tax (PAT) of 41% for the full year. Management highlighted proactive measures taken to ensure business continuity, maintain liquidity, and support customers through moratorium policies. The company emphasized the continued strength of demand in its core affordable housing segment, driven by fundamental needs and specific demographic trends. AAVAS maintained a conservative approach to risk management and asset quality, with Gross NPA at 0.46% and a focus on its well-defined, low-risk customer base in semi-urban and rural areas.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Customer Focus:** Continued emphasis on low- and middle-income, self-employed customers in semi-urban and rural areas. Home loans constitute 73.5% of the portfolio, and other mortgage loans 26.5%. Self-employed customers represent 65% of the client base.
*   **Network Expansion:** Added 40 branches in FY2020, bringing the total to 250. Strategy to continue opening 30-40 branches annually, with timing dependent on COVID-19 situation.
*   **Risk Management:** Robust in-house underwriting and analytics framework. Maintained zero exposure to commercial papers. Proactive engagement with customers regarding moratorium policy.
*   **Funding Diversification:** Secured significant funding from Asian Development Bank (ADB) through NCDs. Maintained access to diverse and cost-effective long-term financing.
*   **Operational Resilience:** Swift implementation of Business Continuity Plans (BCP) to ensure continuity of operations during lockdowns. Operated up to 80% of branches during lockdown phases.
*   **Product Mix:** Home loans remain the primary product (73.5%), with other mortgage loans forming 26.5%.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets (Q4 FY2020 & FY2020):

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 31% YoY growth for FY2020, aligning with expectations for consistent growth.
*   **PAT Growth:** Reported 41% YoY growth for FY2020 (Ind-AS), exceeding prior periods and demonstrating resilience.
*   **Asset Quality (Gross NPA < 1%):** Maintained Gross NPA at 0.46%, well within the target.
*   **Asset Quality (1+DPD < 5%):** Reported 1+DPD at 2.43%, marking a strong performance considering the challenging environment and moratorium impact. Management noted that without moratorium, 1+DPD would have been 7.01%.
*   **ROA Target (2.5% and above):** Reported 3.75% ROA for FY2020, significantly exceeding the stated target.
*   **Spread:** Maintained spreads at 5.19%, meeting the target of over 5%. Management noted the impact of excess liquidity on interest costs but highlighted the improvement in overall spread.

### Narrative/Tone Shift & Headwinds:

*   **COVID-19 Impact:** Management acknowledged the significant disruption caused by the pandemic and lockdowns, particularly in the last 10 days of March. The tone was cautiously optimistic, emphasizing the company's preparedness and the resilience of its customer segment.
*   **Moratorium Impact:** The narrative clearly articulated the operational and financial impact of the moratorium, including the collection efficiency figures for March and April. Management proactively communicated that this was a deferment, not a waiver, and that interest would continue to accrue.
*   **Economic Environment:** Management expressed confidence in the continued demand for affordable housing, citing demographic trends and the fundamental "Roti, Kapda aur Makaan" priority. They differentiated their resilient customer segment (kirana stores, essential services) from more volatile sectors.
*   **Funding Environment:** While acknowledging the general market tightness, management highlighted their strong liquidity position and continued access to diverse funding sources, including a significant NCD issuance from ADB.
*   **Disbursements:** Management noted a significant impact on disbursements in March due to lockdown restrictions, causing an estimated loss of Rs. 150-200 crores. However, disbursements resumed in Lockdown 3 at a run rate of Rs. 2-2.5 crores per day.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Collection Efficiency Improvement:** Confident that collection efficiency, which stood at 76.3% in April, will improve by end-May as lockdown restrictions ease and customers' cash flows normalize. Expected to return to pre-demonetization levels (around 7-8% 1+DPD without moratorium) within the next two quarters.
*   **Asset Quality:** Endeavor to maintain Gross NPA below 1% and 1+DPD around 5%. COVID-19 provisioning will be monitored and adjusted quarterly.
*   **ROA:** Aim to consistently deliver ROA of 2.5% and above.
*   **OpEx Ratio:** Expect to reduce cost to assets by 25 basis points in FY21, offsetting discretionary expense increases through efficiency gains and a variable cost structure.
*   **Borrowing Cost:** Expects to maintain borrowing costs at current levels or see further reduction, with a focus on diversified and cost-effective long-term financing.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Plan to open around 30-40 new branches in FY2021, contingent on the COVID-19 situation improving in Q1.
*   **Technology Investment:** Continued investment in technology, business processes, and analytics to improve operational efficiency and customer acquisition.
*   **AUM Growth:** Reiterate aspiration for 25% CAGR growth over the next three to five years, expecting to be near these numbers in FY21 if the business situation normalizes after Q1.
*   **Demand Outlook:** Expect sustained demand for affordable housing driven by fundamental needs, government incentives, and demographic shifts.
*   **Customer Engagement:** Highlighted the strategy of talking to 100% of customers (105,000+) as a key differentiator and a factor in maintaining collection efficiency.
*   **Credit Loss:** Throughout the company's 10-year history, credit losses have been approximately 7-8 basis points on Rs. 11,000 crore disbursements, with no significant deviation during demonetization.
*   **COVID-19 Provisioning:** The Rs. 44.375 million provision has been fully recognized in the current quarter and will be monitored and reassessed quarterly.
*   **Disbursements:** Currently disbursing Rs. 2-2.5 crores per day, with a target to return to pre-lockdown levels if the lockdown does not extend beyond Q1.
*   **LTV and FOIR:** Maintain low portfolio LTV (around 47%) and average FOIR (around 40%), which provides a strong buffer against stress.
*   **Salaried vs. Self-Employed:** In April, 82% of customers who paid were salaried and 73% were self-employed. Management anticipates further improvement in collections in May.
*   **M&A/LAP Queries:** Observing a steady trend in queries, with 70% for home loans and 30% for non-home loans, even during lockdown.
*   **Future Business Activity:** Potential to open AAVAS Finserv (dormant subsidiary) in the future to offer other products to existing customers.
*   **Securitization:** Will remain a funding tool, with volumes depending on pricing and overall funding requirements.
*   **Geographic Performance:** Consistent performance across states, with newer states showing better trends due to a higher proportion of salaried customers.
*   **Portfolio Composition:** 99% of mortgages are against self-occupied residential property.
*   **Customer Base:** 40% of customers are solely financed by AAVAS; 60% have other loans.
*   **Affordable Housing Demand:** Remains robust, with 4,500+ new customer KYC submissions in April alone.
*   **Moratorium Uptake:** By value, approximately 6,000 customers out of 105,000 requested moratorium, with exact figures to be finalized post-May 31st.
*   **Long-Term Survival:** Management emphasizes the company's proven ability to survive various crises over the past decade, citing a conservative, agile, and risk-aware approach as key to sustained performance.
