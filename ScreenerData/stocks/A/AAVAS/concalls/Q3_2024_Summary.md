## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) demonstrated resilience and strategic progress in Q3 FY'24, with a focus on technological transformation and calibrated growth. The company's Assets Under Management (AUM) grew by 23% year-on-year to INR 160 billion, exceeding the previously stated guidance. Disbursements saw a notable pickup, with an 8% QoQ and 13% YoY growth, reaching INR 13.62 billion, indicating a positive trend. Profit after Tax (PAT) for the nine months ended December 31, 2023, grew by 14.6% YoY to INR 3.48 billion. A key highlight is the ongoing technology implementation, with Phase II of Salesforce (LOS) rolled out and Oracle Fusion ERP live. The Loan Management System (LMS) on Flexcube is under testing and migration, with a projected completion of major business system transformations within the next three to four months. This technological advancement is already showing results, with login to sanction TAT reducing from 13 days to 9 days. The company reaffirmed its AUM growth guidance of 20-25% for the fiscal year and anticipates doubling its AUM in the next three years. Asset quality remains robust, with 1+ DPD at 3.75%. Management also indicated that borrowing costs have likely peaked.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Technology Transformation:** Phase II of Salesforce (LOS) is live, integrating account aggregator and upcoming modules. Oracle Fusion ERP is live, and Oracle FLEXCUBE LMS is under testing/migration, with full transformation expected in 3-4 months. This is aimed at reducing login-to-sanction TAT to 6-7 days. GenAI Chatbot piloted.
*   **Growth Focus:** AUM growth guidance of 20-25% for FY'24 reaffirmed. Expectation to double AUM in three years. Disbursements showing month-on-month improvement and confidence in meeting guidance. Geographical diversification continues with growth in Karnataka, UP, and Maharashtra, and plans for expansion in Southern India.
*   **Product Mix:** Focus on enhancing the contribution of the core Rs. 7.5 lakh product to improve yields. Plans to increase its share in the disbursement mix by 5%. Targeting opex-light sourcing channels like eMitra in Rajasthan and others.
*   **Underwriting Philosophy:** Continued focus on cash flow-based underwriting for unserved, underserved, and unbanked customers in Tier-3 to Tier-5 towns. Emphasis on risk-adjusted returns and maintaining asset quality.
*   **Talent Management:** Employee count stable around 6,000. Productivity improvements expected due to technology adoption and stabilization of frontline staff.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 23% YoY growth in Q3 FY'24, aligning with the 20-25% guidance. Management reiterated confidence in meeting this range for the fiscal year.
*   **Disbursements:** Q3 FY'24 saw an 8% QoQ and 13% YoY growth, a positive pickup compared to previous quarters, addressing concerns about the disbursement engine. Management attributed previous softness to technology implementation lags and sanction-to-disbursement ratio fluctuations, a narrative consistent with prior calls. The current improvement and January's positive trend support this.
*   **Technology Implementation:** Significant progress reported with Salesforce LOS (Phase II live, 1 lakh+ applications processed) and Oracle Fusion ERP (live). LMS is under testing. The projected completion of major systems within 3-4 months aligns with previous timelines for full transformation benefits.
*   **Asset Quality:** Remained strong with 1+ DPD at 3.75% and Gross NPA at 1.09%, consistent with historical performance and management commentary. Credit cost remained low.
*   **Spread Management:** Maintained spreads above 5% (5.12% in Q3 FY'24), in line with guidance, despite competitive pressures. Management indicated that the current spread level is a normalization.
*   **Opex Ratio:** Moderated to 3.49% in Q3 FY'24, showing progress towards the targeted reduction. Management anticipates further improvements.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Management indicated that borrowing costs have likely peaked, with incremental costs showing a slight decline. This aligns with previous expectations of stabilization.
