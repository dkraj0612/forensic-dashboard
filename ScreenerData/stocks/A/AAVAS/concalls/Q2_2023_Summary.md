## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) continues its trajectory of growth and operational enhancement in Q2 FY24, as detailed in their investor presentation. The company is deeply invested in a digital transformation initiative, "Project GATI and UNNATI," aimed at achieving significant scale in loan origination and customer service through platforms like Salesforce. This strategic push, alongside the implementation of Oracle Fusion for ERP, is expected to drive efficiency and improve customer experience, with early results showing successful processing of over 67,000 login cases and sanctioning of Rs. 3,000+ Cr through the new LOS. Financially, AAVAS maintained a healthy net interest margin (NIM) of 6.87% for H1 FY24, despite a 17 bps decline quarter-on-quarter. Assets Under Management (AUM) are on an upward trend, and the company highlights comfortable liquidity and a well-diversified shareholding base. Asset quality metrics remain robust, with Gross Stage 3 (DPD <= 90) at 0.80% and Gross Stage 3 (DPD > 90) at 0.11%. Management is proactively managing borrowing costs and maintaining spreads in a dynamic interest rate environment. The company is also demonstrating a strong commitment to ESG principles, with various initiatives in green housing, community empowerment, and responsible lending.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Digital Transformation (Project GATI and UNNATI):** Focus on a complete digital journey from origination to servicing using Salesforce for Loan Origination System (LOS) and Oracle Fusion for Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP). Aims for 10x scale, minimized TAT, and improved team productivity.
*   **In-house Execution Model:** Continues to leverage in-house capabilities for lead generation, risk management, underwriting, and collections, emphasizing a focused approach and technology utilization.
*   **Customer Experience Enhancement:** Implementing new LOS and Loan Management Solution (LMS) to enable scalability and best-in-class customer experience. The Enterprise GL system aims for back-office automation and improved financial controls.
*   **Data-Driven Operations:** Utilizing analytics for optimized lead generation, credit risk assessment, pricing, and loan lifecycle supervision.
*   **Branch Network Expansion:** Maintaining a significant retail network of 350 branches to support business development and customer reach.
*   **Responsible Lending & ESG:** Commitment to green housing initiatives, community empowerment, responsible lending practices, and secure customer data.
*   **Diversified Funding:** Prudent management of borrowing costs and maintaining a comfortable liquidity position.
