## Executive Overview

Aavas Financiers Limited (AAVAS) reported its strongest quarter ever for Q4 FY'23 under the new "Aavas 3.0" framework. The company achieved record disbursements and continued its strategic focus on growth, technology implementation, and institutionalization. A significant development was the transition in leadership, with former MD Sushil Kumar Agarwal stepping down. Mr. Sachinder Bhinder, now MD & CEO, reiterated the commitment of promoter shareholders to the long-term vision and highlighted the company's strong management bench. The company has gone live with its Salesforce platform, a key step in its digital transformation, and is well-capitalized with robust liquidity.

## Business Strategy & Drivers

*   **Aavas 3.0 Framework:** First quarter completed under the new framework, marked by record performance.
*   **Leadership Transition:** Acknowledged Sushil Kumar Agarwal's departure and emphasized the commitment of the Board and promoter shareholders to institutionalization.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Salesforce platform is now live, with Oracle FlexCube and Oracle Fusion in implementation for back and middle office. This is expected to significantly reduce Turnaround Time (TAT) and enhance efficiency.
*   **Growth Strategy:** Continued focus on geographical expansion (Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh) and deepening presence in existing markets.
*   **Product Mix:** Maintaining a long-term non-home loan (MSME/LAP) book between 30-35%.
*   **Credit Philosophy:** Commitment to core DNA of underwriting in Tier 3-5 towns, self-employed/non-professional (SENP) customers, and affordable housing, with a strong focus on governance and risk-adjusted returns.

## Management Accountability & Consistency Audit

### Performance Against Stated Targets & Narrative Audit:

*   **Disbursement Growth:** Q4 FY'23 and FY'23 disbursements exceeded previous growth rates and set historical records, aligning with strong execution under the new framework. Management highlighted that new business growth was around 39-40%.
*   **AUM Growth:** Achieved 24.8% YoY growth, consistent with stated growth ambitions.
*   **Asset Quality:** Gross Stage 3 (0.92%) and Net Stage 3 (0.68%) remain low and are well-explained in the context of RBI notification. The company continues to demonstrate industry-leading asset quality.
*   **Spread Management:** The spread of 5.51% reflects effective management, though there was a marginal reduction driven by a lag in passing on increased borrowing costs to assets. Management expressed confidence in maintaining spreads above 5%.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Continued branch expansion with 32 new branches, aligning with the strategy to build physical presence.
*   **Digital Transformation Progress:** Salesforce platform has gone live, marking a significant milestone in the technology transformation journey. The full benefits on TAT reduction are expected to materialize after stabilization.
*   **MSME & Non-Home Loan Focus:** While the overall home loan segment saw deceleration in disbursement value (due to prioritization of MSME/LAP), the company churned out a significant portion of its MSME and LAP book via assignments, creating a cushion to continue lending in this segment. The overall strategic mix is intended to remain within 30-35% non-home loans.
*   **Leadership Transition:** The departure of Sushil Kumar Agarwal, a founding member, occurred within three months of the MD/CEO role split announcement. Management clarified this was a personal decision and a planned separation with the Board, emphasizing that the Aavas 3.0 journey and institutionalization continue. Promoter shareholders remain committed and have not sold shares.
*   **Opex Ratio:** No specific update on opex ratio in this transcript, but historical context suggests ongoing investments are driving elevated levels, with a forward expectation of decrease.

### New Forward-Looking Promises, Guidance, Margins, or Timelines:

*   **Salesforce TAT Reduction:** Target TAT reduction to an average of 6-7 days from the current 11-12 days, with real results expected after a 3-month stabilization period.
*   **Oracle FlexCube & Fusion Implementation:** Expected to be fully up and running by the end of Q1 FY'24, with teething issues resolved and no hindrance to business operations.
*   **Opex Ratio Improvement:** (From previous context) Expected decrease of 25-30 bps YoY from Q3 FY'24 onwards.
*   **Margin Stability:** Hopeful of stable margins for at least the next quarter. Potential for 25-50 bps reduction if interest rates decrease.
*   **FY'24 Guidance:** Management will monitor interest rate movements. Efficiency improvements expected to offset potential margin pressures.
*   **Growth Guidance:** Reaffirms 20%-25% growth guidance, with potential to exceed if market conditions are favorable. Growth trajectory will be a mix of deeper penetration in existing geographies and accelerated growth in new territories.
*   **New State Entries:** Plan to add 2 more states over the next 3-5 years.
*   **Branch Break-even:** 90% of branches achieve break-even within 12 months, with 1-year-old branches giving ROE >12% and 3-year-old branches ROE ~20%.
*   **Investor/Analyst Day:** Scheduled for May 24, 2023, in Mumbai, to engage with the broader leadership team.

*   **Disbursement Run Rate Target:** Once teething issues are behind and software implemented, a target of INR 800 crores to INR 1,000 crores monthly disbursement run rate is implied.
*   **Geographical Expansion Strategy:** Continued focus on Tier 3 to Tier 5 towns and self-individual construction loans. Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are key growth geographies.
*   **Promoter Commitment:** Reaffirmed no plan to sell shares in the coming year.
